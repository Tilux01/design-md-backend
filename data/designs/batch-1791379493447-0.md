# 🎨 Design & Layout Specification

## 1. Global Viewport & Background
*   **Layout:** Full-screen (`width: 100vw`, `height: 100vh`), centered flex container.
*   **Background Base Color:** `#0a0608` (Deep near-black with a warm violet-brown undertone).
*   **Lighting/Glow Effect (Pseudo-elements):**
    *   **Top-Left Orange Flare:** A soft radial gradient transitioning from vibrant orange (`#ff5500`) to transparent, positioned at `top: -10%`, `left: -20%`, blurred heavily via `filter: blur(80px)`.
    *   **Bottom-Right Warm Flare:** A deep amber-to-orange glow positioned at `bottom: -15%`, `right: -15%`, blurred via `filter: blur(100px)`.
*   **Texture Overlay:** A subtle noise/grain texture overlay set to low opacity (`opacity: 0.15`, `mix-blend-mode: overlay`) to emulate the vintage analog film grain visible in the source image.

---

## 2. Typography & Font Stack

### **Font 1: "Use these" & "to make your next design go viral"**
*   **Primary Font:** **"Montserrat"** or **"Inter"** (Geometric Sans-Serif).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Montserrat', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif;`
*   **Styling Details:**
    *   *Top line ("Use these"):* Uppercase, tracked out slightly (`letter-spacing: 1px`), weight: Medium (`500`), color: `#ffffff` (Pure white).
    *   *Bottom line ("to make your next design go viral"):* Lowercase, with the word **"your"** styled in **Italic Serif** for emphasis. Weight: Regular (`400`).

### **Font 2: "color" (The Main Hero Word)**
*   **Primary Font:** **"Playfair Display"**, **"Bodoni Moda"**, or **"Instrument Serif"** (High-contrast, elegant modern serif).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Instrument Serif', 'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif;`
*   **Styling Details:**
    *   Italicized (`font-style: italic`).
    *   Extreme weight and vertical slant. The letter **"l"** stretches dramatically upward, overlapping the line above.
    *   Color: Vibrant Neon-Orange (`#ff4500`).

### **Font 3: "combos"**
*   **Primary Font:** **"Clash Display"**, **"Syne"**, or **"Montserrat Black"** (Ultra-bold geometric/sans-serif).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Clash Display', 'Montserrat', sans-serif;`
*   **Styling Details:**
    *   Lowercase, heavy bold tracking (`font-weight: 800`), uppercase/tight letter-spacing.
    *   Color: Matching Vibrant Orange (`#ff4500`).

---

## 3. Visual Elements & Accents
*   **Underline Stroke:** Located directly beneath the bottom text line. A hand-drawn/brush-style horizontal stroke rendered in matching orange (`#ff4500`), slightly tilted (~-2 degrees) with tapered ends.
*   **Watermark / Logo:** Positioned at the bottom center (`"DGTL DSNR"` stylized monogram). Rendered in a monospace/cyberpunk geometric font at a small size with low opacity (`opacity: 0.7`).

## 🎬 Animation Specifications (Optional for Motion Graphics / Reels)

If you are turning this into an animated short/Reel or motion graphic:
1.  **Entrance (0.0s – 0.6s):** 
    *   The background glows scale up from `0.8` to `1.0` with an ease-out cubic curve.
    *   The hero words (`color` and `combos`) fade up from y-offset `+30px` with a stagger effect (`color` leads by 0.1s).
2.  **Emphasis (1.2s):** 
    *   A quick scale pulse (`scale: 1.02`) on the orange text to catch the viewer's eye instantly.
3.  **Exit / Loop (3.0s+):** Hold static for optimal readability (ideal for short-form content loops).
