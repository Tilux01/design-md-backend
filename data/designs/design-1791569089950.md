# Flowblox – SaaS Landing Page Clone Design Specification

## 1. Global Styles & Design Tokens

### 1.1. Color Palette
* **Background Primary (Cream/Sand):** `#F7F4EE`
* **Text Primary (Dark Espresso/Charcoal):** `#1E1C1A` (Used for headings, main titles)
* **Text Secondary (Muted Brown/Gray):** `#6B6560` (Used for subtext, descriptions, nav links)
* **Button Primary (Dark/Black):** `#1E1C1A`
* **Button Text:** `#FFFFFF`
* **Card Fills (Neutral/Tan):** `#E4DDD3` (Used for placeholder/feature cards)
* **Card Fills (Accent Green):** `#536853` (Used for Progress Tracking feature card)

### 1.2. Typography
* **Primary Font Family (Headings):** *Playfair Display* (Serif, elegant, high contrast)
  * *Fallback:* `Georgia`, `Cambria`, `Times New Roman`, `serif`
* **Secondary Font Family (Body & UI):** *Plus Jakarta Sans* or *Inter* (Clean sans-serif)
  * *Fallback:* `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Components

### 2.1. Navigation Bar (Sticky/Fixed Top)
* **Container:** Full width, padded horizontally (`~64px` desktop).
* **Left Section (Nav Links):**
  * Links: `Services`, `Features`, `Blog`, `Services`
  * Font: Sans-serif, 14px, `#6B6560`, medium weight.
* **Center Section (Logo):**
  * Text/Brand: **Flowblox** (Bold Serif, ~18px).
* **Right Section:**
  * Links: `About`, `Pricing`, `Contact`
  * CTA Button: **Get started →** (Dark pill-shaped button, rounded corners, white text).

---

### 2.2. Hero Section
* **Layout:** Centered content alignment, generous vertical padding.
* **Heading (H1):**
  * Text: *"Streamline Your Team,"* / *"Supercharge Your Workflow"*
  * Typography: Serif font, large display scale (`~48px` to `60px`), centered, dark espresso color.
* **Subheading:**
  * Text: *"All-in-one platform to plan, collaborate, and deliver — faster and smarter."*
  * Typography: Sans-serif, 16px, muted gray `#6B6560`.
* **Hero CTA Button:**
  * Text: `Get started for Free →`
  * Style: Pill-shaped dark button (`#1E1C1A`) with white text and a right-arrow icon.

#### Carousel / Team Member Cards Row
* **Layout:** A horizontal curved/fanned card layout showcasing diverse team members with distinct colored backgrounds/clothing (pinks, purples, browns, greens).
* **Cards Structure:**
  * Rounded portrait cards arranged in a subtle perspective curve.
  * Below each card cluster, small descriptor text blocks:
    1. **Real-Time Collaboration:** *"Communicate seamlessly and keep everyone in sync with built-in messaging, file sharing, and live updates."*
    2. **Task & Project Tracking:** *"Assign tasks, set deadlines, and visualize progress with boards, lists, and timelines tailored to your team's style."*
    3. **Performance Insights:** *"Make smarter decisions with analytics that show productivity trends, bottlenecks, and team workload balance."*

---

### 2.3. Value Proposition / Section 2
* **Section Heading (H2):**
  * Text: *"Everything Your Team Needs to Work Smarter"*
  * Typography: Serif, centered, prominent size.
* **Section Subtext:**
  * Text: *"From task tracking to real-time chat, our features are built to keep your team connected, organized, and moving forward—together."*

#### Bento Grid Feature Layout
* **Card 1 (Built-In Team Chat):**
  * **Image Description:** Office setting showing a female colleague giving a high-five to a male team member over a desk setup with monitors and natural window light.
  * **Overlay Text:** "Built-In Team Chat" + short description.
* **Card 2 (Task Assignment):**
  * **Background:** Solid neutral tan (`#E4DDD3`).
  * **Text Content:** "Task Assignment" + descriptive body text.
* **Card 3 (Real-Time Scheduling):**
  * **Background:** Solid neutral tan (`#E4DDD3`).
  * **Text Content:** "Real-Time Scheduling" + descriptive body text.

  * **Background:** Solid olive/forest green (`#536853`).

  * **Text Content:** "Progress Tracking" + descriptive body text.

---

### 2.4. Section 3: Proven Results, Real Impact
* **Section Heading (H2):**
  * Text: *"Proven Results, Real Impact"*

  * Text: *"See how teams around the world are working faster, communicating better, and getting more done with our all-in-one management platform."*

  * Cards previewing product UI screenshots, mobile app mockups, and analytical dashboards.

---

## 3. Responsive Behavior Guidelines
* **Desktop (>1024px):** Multi-column bento grid, expansive hero layout with fanned profile cards.
* **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 2-column bento grid adaptation, stacked profile images.
* **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column vertical stack for all cards, hidden or simplified navigation menu behind a hamburger toggle.
