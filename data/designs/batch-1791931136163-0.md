# Design Specification: "Pal" Product Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Color Palette
Full-screen, minimal dark-mode editorial landing page for a concept personal electric vehicle.

- **Background Base:** Slate Grey (`#48525b`)
- **Text Primary:** Pure White (`#ffffff`)
- **Text Secondary / Accents:** Muted Light Grey (`#a0aab2`)
- **Accent Highlight:** Soft Salmon / Rose Gold (`#d89b88`)
- **Overlay/Card Dark:** Deep Charcoal (`#2b3036`)

---

## 2. Typography
- **Primary Headings & Display:** Neo-grotesque sans-serif (e.g., *Helvetica Neue*, *Inter*, or *Haas Grotesk*).
  - *Weights:* Bold (700), Heavy/Black (900).
- **Body & Metadata:** Technical geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Aktiv Grotesk*, *Inter*, or *Roboto*).
  - *Weights:* Regular (400), Medium (500).
- **Fallback Font Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Arial, sans-serif`

---

## 3. Layout Structure

### Top Navigation Bar
- **Position:** Fixed top, full width, padding: `40px 60px`.
- **Left:** Brand mark `✱ Phenomenon` (Font size: 16px, Bold).
- **Center-Left:** `:: Projects` link (Font size: 13px, Medium).
- **Top Right:** 
  - Slide counter: `01 —— 05` (Thin line divider, 13px).
  - Menu: Two-line horizontal hamburger icon (`#ffffff`, 20px wide).

### Center Hero Section
1. **Background Text ("Pal"):**
   - Extra-large bold sans-serif text (`Pal`) rendered in solid white (`#ffffff`).
   - Sits directly on the floor level, behind the foreground vehicle.
   - Size: ~30vw, Font Weight: 900.
2. **Foreground Hero Render:**
   - **Asset:** Backgroundless (transparent PNG) 3D render of the "Pal" smart scooter.
   - **Visual Details:** 4-wheel compact personal transport with light-wood deck, dark grey matte stem/bag module, dual front LED horizontal lights, and circular salmon/white rim glow on wheels.
   - **Angle & Placement:** 3/4 isometric view facing slightly forward-left; center-aligned, overlapping the giant "Pal" background typography.

### Left Sidebar / Metadata
- **Padding Left:** `60px`, vertical flex layout:
  - **Year Indicator:** `Year —— 2021` (13px, regular, muted grey).
  - **Mission Statement:** `"We believe the future is autonomous and sustainable ————"` (13px, regular, white, max-width: 200px).
  - **Video Thumbnail Card:** 
    - Small dark rounded rectangle (aspect ratio ~16:9, ~180px wide).
    - Image inside shows a faint profile render of the scooter.
    - Centered circular translucent play button with an orange play triangle.
  - **Bottom Left:** Vertical or horizontal indicator `Scroll` (13px, muted).

### Right Sidebar / Content
- **Padding Right:** `60px`, vertical alignment starting mid-screen:
  - **Pagination Dots (Top Right of block):** 
    - 5 indicators: Active dot has an outer salmon-tinted concentric ring; followed by 3 solid small dots, ending with 1 outlined circle.
  - **Headline:** `A SMARTER LAST MILE` (Uppercase, 20px, Bold, tracking: 1px).
  - **Body Copy:** Two concise paragraphs (~12px, regular, leading: 1.6, muted grey, max-width: 280px).
    - *Text covers:* AI/machine learning last-mile prototype for NIO; modular accessories (bag, basket, cart).
  - **Bottom Right:** Social links `Fb  •  In  •  Tw` (13px, inline, separated by bullets).

---

## 4. Assets & Imagery Specifications
- **Main Hero (`pal-hero.png`):** Transparent PNG, 2000×1600 minimum. Ground contact shadows included in alpha channel to integrate cleanly over `#48525b`.
- **Video Preview Card:** Dark grey box (`#2b3036`), 8px border radius, containing thumbnail and CSS/SVG play overlay.
- **Icons:** Inline SVG for asterisk (`✱`), menu icon (`=`), and colon markers (`::`).
