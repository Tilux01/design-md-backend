# Design Specification: Shōgun Media Interface

## 1. Layout & Composition
*   **Viewport:** Full-screen (100vw, 100vh), overflow hidden.
*   **Grid System:** 12-column grid with wide margins (approx. 80px on left/right).
*   **Alignment:** 
    *   The main content block (Title, Metadata, Synopsis, Buttons) is center-aligned vertically and positioned in the right two-thirds of the screen.
    *   The primary character subject is positioned on the left third, bleeding off the bottom and left edges.
*   **Background:** A full-screen atmospheric gradient/image.

## 2. Color Palette
*   **Primary Red:** `#FF0D0D` (Used for "WATCH NOW" button, "SEASONS 01" text, and the accent line).
*   **Background Teal:** A dark, desaturated teal gradient ranging from `#0A2A2E` (bottom) to `#1B4D54` (top).
*   **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
*   **Text Secondary:** `#CCCCCC` (Light Grey for synopsis).
*   **Accent Gold:** Found on the character's armor (approx. `#B8860B`).

## 3. Typography
### Primary Title ("SHŌGUN")
*   **Font:** A high-contrast, elegant Serif. 
*   **Fallback:** `Cinzel`, `Trajan Pro`, or `serif`.
*   **Styling:** Uppercase, wide letter-spacing (approx. 0.2em). The "O" features a macron (horizontal bar) above it.
*   **Color:** White.

### Sub-headers ("SEASONS 01", "WATCH NOW")
*   **Font:** Geometric Sans-Serif.
*   **Fallback:** `Montserrat Bold`, `Inter`, or `sans-serif`.
*   **Styling:** Uppercase, bold, letter-spacing 0.1em.

### Body Text (Synopsis & Metadata)
*   **Font:** Clean Sans-Serif.
*   **Fallback:** `Inter`, `Roboto`, or `sans-serif`.
*   **Size:** Metadata (14px), Synopsis (16px).
*   **Line Height:** 1.6 for readability.

## 4. Visual Assets & Imagery

### Asset 1: The Main Subject (Samurai)
*   **Description:** A high-resolution photo of a Samurai (Lord Toranaga) in profile, looking slightly toward the viewer. He wears traditional black and gold ornate armor with a prominent gold crest on his kabuto (helmet). He holds a katana.
*   **Transparency:** **Backgroundless (PNG).** The image should be cut out cleanly.
*   **Placement:** Positioned on the left side of the screen. The bottom of the torso should be cut off by the bottom of the viewport or a dark "mist" overlay.
*   **Rotation:** 0 degrees, but slightly scaled up to dominate the left-hand side.

### Asset 2: Red Brushstroke
*   **Description:** A stylized, vertical, "bloody" or ink-style red brushstroke. It has a rough, organic texture.
*   **Transparency:** **Backgroundless (PNG).**
*   **Placement:** Directly behind the Samurai, slightly to his left, following the curve of his back. It acts as a visual "pop" against the teal background.

### Asset 3: Japanese Kanji
*   **Description:** Red vertical calligraphy for "Shōgun" (将軍).
*   **Placement:** To the immediate left of the "S" in the main title.
*   **Color:** `#FF0D0D`.

### Asset 4: Background Landscape
*   **Description:** A moody, foggy landscape featuring silhouettes of jagged mountains at the very bottom. The atmosphere is thick with mist or smoke, rendered in teal and dark blue tones.

## 5. UI Components

### Logo (Top Right)
*   **Content:** "Night Owls" with an owl-head icon.
*   **Placement:** Top right corner, 40px padding from top/right.

### Metadata Row
*   **Format:** `10 EPISODES • WAR | DRAMA | POLITICS • 2024`
*   **Styling:** White, uppercase, small font size.
*   **Accent:** A horizontal red line (3px height) sits directly above this text, spanning roughly the width of the "SHŌGUN" title.

### Action Buttons
1.  **Watch Now Button:**
    *   **Background:** Primary Red (`#FF0D0D`).
    *   **Shape:** Rectangular, no border-radius.
    *   **Icon:** White "Play" triangle to the left of the text.
2.  **IMDb Button:**
    *   **Background:** Transparent.
    *   **Border:** 2px Solid White.
    *   **Icon:** Yellow/Gold star icon to the left of the text.
    *   **Content:** "8.6 IMDb".

## 6. Effects & Overlays
*   **Vignette:** A subtle dark vignette around the edges of the screen to draw focus to the center.
*   **Particle Effect:** A subtle overlay of "ash" or "dust" particles (light blue/white) concentrated around the Samurai to give a sense of depth and motion.
