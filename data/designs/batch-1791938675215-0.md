This `design.md` document provides a comprehensive technical specification for recreating the provided spatial UI design. The goal is to achieve a pixel-perfect clone of the interface, optimized for a full-screen, glass-morphism environment.

# Design Specification: Spatial Media Interface (Netflix Concept)

## 1. Global Environment & Layout
*   **Canvas:** Full-screen immersive view.
*   **Background:** The UI elements should float in a 3D space. The background is a blurred, high-fidelity interior scene (living room).
*   **Glassmorphism Effect:** All panels use a semi-transparent, frosted glass effect.
    *   **Background Blur:** 30px - 50px.
    *   **Border:** 1px solid white with 10% - 15% opacity to create a "specular edge" light reflection.
    *   **Shadows:** Soft, deep ambient occlusion shadows to give the floating panels depth.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **SF Pro Display** (or **Inter** as a web-safe alternative).
*   **Secondary Font:** **SF Pro Text** (for smaller body copy).
*   **Fallbacks:** `System-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Color:** Primary text is `#FFFFFF` (Pure White). Secondary text (metadata) is `#FFFFFF` with 60% opacity.

---

## 3. Sidebar Navigation (Left)
*   **Dimensions:** Vertical pill-shaped container. Height covers roughly 80% of the viewport height.
*   **Visual Style:** Frosted glass with a subtle vertical gradient.
*   **Elements (Top to Bottom):**
    1.  **Netflix Logo:** The iconic "N" in Netflix Red (`#E50914`). Placed at the very top.
    2.  **Home Icon:** Outline style. Highlighted with a small red vertical indicator pill on the far left edge of the sidebar.
    3.  **Search Icon:** Outline magnifying glass.
    4.  **Movies Icon:** Outline film strip.
    5.  **TV Shows Icon:** Outline television set.
    6.  **Trending Icon:** Outline rising graph line.
    7.  **Categories Icon:** Outline grid/four-square.
    8.  **User Profile:** Outline person icon at the bottom.

---

## 4. Hero Section (Top Center)
*   **Title:** "Extraction 2" (Large, Bold, SF Pro Display).
*   **Description:** A two-line synopsis in medium-weight white text.
*   **Metadata:** "2023" and a 5-star rating system (4.5 stars filled in gold/yellow).
*   **Action Buttons:**
    *   **Play Button:** Large red rectangle (`#E50914`) with rounded corners (12px). Contains a white "Play" triangle icon and the text "WATCH NOW". Positioned at the bottom right of the hero image.
    *   **Info/Add Buttons:** Two small, semi-transparent pill buttons below the description.
*   **Hero Image Description:** 
    *   **Content:** A high-resolution close-up of Chris Hemsworth as Tyler Rake. He is looking slightly to the left, appearing rugged with dirt/sweat on his face, wearing tactical gear. The background of the image is a blurred, warm-toned combat zone.
    *   **Treatment:** The image has a subtle inner glow and rounded corners (24px). It is integrated into the glass panel with a slight dark gradient overlay at the bottom to make the text pop.

---

## 5. "Popular on Netflix" Grid (Bottom)
*   **Heading:** "Popular on Netflix" in semi-bold white text, positioned above the row.
*   **Card Style:** Vertical rectangles with 16px rounded corners. Each card features a movie poster, a title at the bottom, and a star rating.
*   **Image Descriptions (Left to Right):**
    1.  **Card 1 (Through My Window):** A romantic, warm-toned shot of a couple (man and woman) looking at each other closely.
    2.  **Card 2 (The Mother):** A dark, moody close-up of Jennifer Lopez looking intensely toward the camera.
    3.  **Card 3 (Blood & Gold):** A gritty, high-contrast close-up of a man's face covered in dirt and blood.
    4.  **Card 4 (The Mother-in-Law):** A medium shot of a man and woman in a tense or dramatic conversation.
    5.  **Card 5 (Beef):** A stylized shot of a man (Steven Yeun) looking distressed, with warm, orange-tinted lighting.
    6.  **Card 6 (Take Care of Maya):** A documentary-style portrait of a young girl looking directly at the camera with a neutral expression.
*   **Placement:** The cards are arranged in a horizontal row with even spacing (approx. 20px). The last card (Maya) is slightly offset/rotated to the right, suggesting a 3D carousel effect that follows the curve of the user's field of vision.

---

## 6. Interactive Elements
*   **Search Floating Button:** A small, circular glass button with a magnifying glass icon, floating independently in the top right corner area.
*   **Hover States:** When a movie card is focused, it should scale up slightly (1.05x) and the border brightness should increase.
*   **Z-Index/Depth:** 
    *   The Sidebar is on the closest plane to the user.
    *   The Main Content Panel is slightly behind the sidebar.
    *   The Search Button floats at the same depth as the Main Panel.

---

## 7. Image Specifications
*   **Backgroundless Elements:** The UI icons (Home, Search, etc.) and the Netflix "N" must be backgroundless PNGs or SVGs.
*   **Poster Images:** High-aspect-ratio (2:3) images with the bottom 20% featuring a dark gradient overlay to house the white text titles.
*   **Rotation:** The entire main panel has a very slight "yaw" rotation (approx 2-3 degrees) to the left to simulate a curved display wrapping around the user's perspective. The far-right card has an additional 5-degree Y-axis rotation to enhance the 3D depth.
