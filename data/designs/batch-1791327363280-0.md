# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist Real Estate Discovery & FinTech Companion App (Warm Neo-Editorial Glassmorphism).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio on hero screens; structured, card-dense layout in dashboard and interactive filter sheet views. 
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered depth architecture utilizing dark tinted glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), solid white/dark structural panels, floating pill navigation bars, and subtle multi-layered drop shadows (`0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-contrast location badges with blur backdrop, gradient overlay on property imagery, interactive price-histogram charts, and floating glass navigation docks.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 12px`, Font-size `12px`, Font-weight `500`, Tracking `0.04em`, Border-radius `9999px`, Background translucency `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, Border width `1px` solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> `&#9679;` or `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`
