# Design Specification: Apple Watch Landing Page Clone

## 1. Global Setup & Theming

### Color Palette
* **Background Dark:** `#0E1013`
* **Surface Overlay:** `#1A1C20` (opacity 70–85%)
* **Border Subdued:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
* **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF`
* **Text Secondary:** `#7D828D`
* **Text Muted:** `#484D56`

### Typography
* **Primary Font:** `SF Pro Display`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`
* **Fallbacks:** `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, `sans-serif`
* **Weights:** Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600), Bold (700)

---

## 2. Layout Architecture

A full-screen, fixed-viewport layout (`100vw` × `100vh`, `overflow: hidden`) split into:
1. **Left Sidebar:** Vertical navigation rail (width: `72px`).
2. **Main Canvas:** Hero content, background imagery, top nav, and floating widgets.
3. **Right Edge Rail:** Social links and slide indicators.

```
+----+-----------------------------------------------------+
|    | Top Nav (Links left, Hamburger right)               |
| L  |-----------------------------------------------------|
| E  | Hero Text             | Hero Image: Wrist & Watch   |
| F  | (Heading, Copy, CTA)  | showing music player        |
| T  |                       |                             |
|    |-----------------------------------------------------|
|    | Feature Columns       | Video Card                  |
+----+-----------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 3. Left Sidebar (Vertical Rail)

* **Dimensions:** Width `72px`, height `100%`, border-right `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
* **Top Component:** White Apple logo SVG (`height: 20px`), centered horizontally, `top: 32px`.
* **Center Pagination:**
  * 4 vertical dots spaced `14px` apart.
  * Dot 1 (Active): Filled white circle (`4px`) enclosed by an outer stroked circle (`16px` diameter, `border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.6)`).
  * Dots 2–4: Muted gray filled circles (`4px`, `#484D56`).
* **Bottom Component:**
  * Rotated label `← SCROLL` (`transform: rotate(-90deg)`), font size `11px`, letter-spacing `2px`, uppercase, color `#7D828D`.

---

## 4. Top Navigation Bar

* **Placement:** `top: 32px`, `left: 112px`, `right: 48px`, height `40px`.
* **Left Links:** Inline list with `32px` gaps.
  * Items: "Welcome", "Features", "Specification", "Buy now".
  * Font: `13px`, medium weight.
  * Inactive items: `#7D828D`.
  * Active item ("Buy now"): `#FFFFFF` with a `2px` white underline placed `6px` below.
* **Right Icon:** Hamburger menu icon.
  * Two horizontal white bars (`width: 22px`, `height: 2px`, vertical gap `6px`), aligned right.

---

## 5. Main Hero Section

### Left Content Group
* **Placement:** `top: 26%`, `left: 112px`, max-width `460px`.
* **Headline:**
  * Text: `Innovative`<br>`Media Watch.`
  * Size: `56px`, line-height `1.1`, font-weight `700`, color `#FFFFFF`.
* **Body Copy:**
  * Text: *"Take a call when you're out on the water. Ask Siri to send a message. Stream your favorite songs on your run. And do it all while leaving your phone behind. It is Apple Watch Series 3."*
  * Size: `13px`, line-height `1.6`, font-weight `400`, color `#7D828D`, margin-top `24px`.
* **CTA Button:**
  * Pill outline: `border: 1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`, padding `10px 24px`, border-radius `24px`.
  * Content: `BUY NOW` (size `11px`, tracking `1.5px`, bold, white) followed by a right arrow `→`. Margin-top `32px`.

### Hero Visual Assets & Positioning
* **Primary Image:**
  * **Description:** Low-key, dramatic photograph of a human wrist and forearm entering from the right, wearing a dark metallic Apple Watch Series 3.
  * **Screen Display:** Brightly lit UI showing Apple Music playback (song: "Buck Shots - A$AP Rocky-TESTING", play/pause/skip icons, red volume accent).
  * **Treatment:** Right-aligned, dark gradient fade mask (`mask-image: linear-gradient(to left, black 60%, transparent 100%)`) merging seamlessly with `#0E1013` on the left.
