# Design System & Specification: Alex Morgan Editorial Portfolio

This document provides a comprehensive, pixel-accurate blueprint to reconstruct the editorial magazine-style portfolio layout as a responsive, full-screen digital interface.

---

## 1. Design Overview & Aesthetic DNA

- **Style:** Brutalist Editorial / High-Fashion Print Magazine aesthetic adapted for modern web.
- **Core Visual Principles:**
  - High-contrast editorial typography (giant condensed display sans paired with sharp classical serif headlines).
  - Warm, tactile, non-pure-white canvas mimicking premium matte uncoated paper.
  - Terracotta/burnt-orange accent color highlighting primary verbs, headings, percentages, and icons.
  - Multi-layered depth in the hero section: display text behind graphic shapes and foreground cutout imagery.
  - Fine 1px architectural hairline dividers separating sections with balanced whitespace.

## 3. Typography System

### 3.1 Font Families & Fallback Stacks

1. **Display Condensed Sans (Giant Headline):**
   - **Primary:** `Oswald` (700) or `Bebas Neue` (400) or `Druk Condensed`
   - **Fallback:** `Impact, Arial Narrow, sans-serif`
2. **Editorial Display Serif (Headings & Titles):**
   - **Primary:** `Playfair Display` or `Cormorant Garamond` (600 / 700)
   - **Fallback:** `Didot, Bodoni MT, Georgia, serif`
3. **Primary Body & Technical Sans:**
   - **Primary:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter` (400 / 500 / 600 / 700)
   - **Fallback:** `Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif`
4. **Signature Font (Script):**
   - **Primary:** `La Luxes Script` or `Caveat` or `Creattion` (400)
   - **Fallback:** `Brush Script MT, cursive`

## 4. Layout Architecture & Container Metrics

- **Max Canvas Width:** `1280px` centered, or full bleed with minimum `36px` side margins.
- **Side Gutters (Left & Right Margins):**
  - Left & right gutters (`48px` wide) reserved for vertical rotated labels, framing the layout like a publication page.
- **Section Spacing:**
  - Vertical padding between section lines: `40px` to `48px`.
  - Divider lines: Full-width `1px solid #DFCFC2`.

### 5.2 Hero Section (Layered Editorial Composition)

The hero section uses a 4-layer stacking order to achieve physical depth:

#### Layer Breakdown (`z-index` Order):
- **Layer 1 (`z-index: 1`): Display Headline ("PORTFOLIO")**
  - Ultra-condensed, bold, ink-black text spanning 100% of the content container width.
  - Sits flush near the top, partially occluded by the foreground portrait.
- **Layer 2 (`z-index: 2`): Terracotta Geometric Sun Circle**
  - Perfect circle (`width: 380px; height: 380px; border-radius: 50%;`).
  - Solid fill: `#C26144`.
  - Position: Horizontally centered or shifted slightly to the right (`left: 52%`, `top: 35%`), sitting directly behind the portrait cutout.
- **Layer 3 (`z-index: 3`): Model Portrait Cutout**
  - **Asset Description:** Transparent PNG cutout of a young stylish male model with dark wavy hair, wearing vintage round tortoise/dark sunglasses, a black textured corduroy overshirt, and a dark t-shirt. He is captured from mid-chest up, angled in a three-quarter turn gazing towards the upper right.
  - **Positioning:** Overlaps the bottom of the "T", "F", "O", "L" in "PORTFOLIO", overlapping the terracotta circle.
  - **Base crop:** The torso is cleanly clipped at the horizontal divider line below the hero.
- **Layer 4 (`z-index: 4`): Rotating Circular Stamp Badge**
  - Placed at the upper right of the model portrait (`top: 40%`, `right: 8%`).
  - Exterior: Dashed circular ring with curved circular SVG text: `AVAILABLE FOR NEW PROJECTS • WORLDWIDE • AVAILABLE WORLDWIDE •`.
  - Center: Three lines of stacked uppercase text:
    ```
    OPEN
    FOR NEW
    PROJECTS
    ```
  - Color: `#C26144`.
- **Layer 5 (`z-index: 4`): Left Content Block**
  - Positioned on the left side of the hero (`width: 38%`):
    - `HELLO, I'M` (small caps serif, `#141414`).
    - `ALEX` / `MORGAN` (giant 2-line high-contrast serif).
    - `WEB DESIGNER & DIGITAL CREATIVE` (tracked sans-serif, `#C26144`).
    - Short Bio: `"I craft clean, modern, and user-focused websites that help brands stand out and connect with the right audience."`
    - Handwritten Signature: `"Alex Morgan"` drawn in dark ink directly beneath the bio.

## 6. Implementation Notes & Micro-interactions

1. **Layering Performance:**
   - Ensure the cutout portrait uses transparent WebP/PNG format with clean alpha masking along the hair edges.
   - Maintain stacking context with explicit `z-index` values on the `.hero-title`, `.hero-sun-shape`, and `.hero-image-cutout` elements.
2. **Rotating Stamp Animation:**
   - Add a subtle continuous CSS keyframe rotation (`animation: spin 20s linear infinite;`) to the circular SVG text badge.
3. **Hover States:**
   - **Project Cards:** Subtle image zoom (`transform: scale(1.03); transition: transform 0.4s ease;`) inside an `overflow: hidden` container.
   - **Action Buttons (`→`):** Arrow shifts right by `4px` on hover (`transform: translateX(4px); transition: transform 0.2s ease;`).
   - **Skill Bars:** Progress bars animate smoothly from `width: 0%` to their target percentages upon entering the viewport.
4. **Dividers:**
   - All horizontal divider rules are precisely `1px solid #DFCFC2`, stretching edge-to-edge between the left and right vertical marginalia text columns.
