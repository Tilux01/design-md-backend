# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: High-End 3D Generative / Surreal Tactile Design System (Dark Canvas Presentation & Soft Organic Form Architecture).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Extreme focal centering. The entire canvas acts as a negative-space showcase with absolute black background framing high-density tactile volume.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Volumetric self-shadowing, soft subsurface scattering illusion, tactile fiber-pile displacement texture model with zero hard geometric outlines.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Subsurface scattering simulations, high-density fiber displacement texture overlays, obsidian framing, ambient volumetric glow.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: 6px 16px, Font-size: 0.875rem, Font-weight: 500, Tracking: 0.05em, Border-radius: 9999px, Background: rgba(255, 107, 107, 0.1), Border width: 1px.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-[#FF6B6B] inline-block"></span>`.
