# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: High-contrast digital art installation, Dark Mode Luminescent Pixelation, Editorial Luxury.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High ambient visual density dominated by a golden micro-dot/pixel matrix backdrop, contrasted against an absolute dark silhouette focal point.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Monolithic flat background canvas with atmospheric depth achieved via backlighting gradients (golden starlight illumination against deep charcoal/black void).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Generative pixel-grid starlight mapping, high-contrast dark silhouette isolation, luminous gold illumination dispersion.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;`
