# Design Specification: "Finest Aroma" Coffee Website

This document provides the precise layout, typography, color palette, asset arrangement, and animation specifications to clone the hero section shown in the media.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

*   **View:** Full-screen responsive hero section (`width: 100vw; height: 100vh;` or min-height 800px).
*   **Background:** 
    *   Base color: Warm cream/off-white (`#F9F4EE`).
    *   Pattern: Vertical pinstripe lines in a slightly darker terracotta/beige tone (`#E8D7CA`), spaced evenly across the viewport width.
*   **Layout Wrapper:** Max-width container centered with generous padding (e.g., `max-width: 1440px; margin: 0 auto; padding: 24px 60px;`).

---

## 2. Navigation Header

Positioned fixed or absolute at the top of the hero viewport.

*   **Left Element:** Brand logo (Minimalist coffee bean icon in dark brown).
*   **Center Links (Nav Menu):** 
    *   `shop`
    *   `flavours`
    *   `our roast`
    *   `our stores`
    *   `sustainability`
    *   *Typography:* Sans-serif, lowercase, medium weight, dark brown (`#3A2618`).
*   **Right Element:** Pill-shaped CTA button ("buy now")
    *   Background: Dark brown (`#3A2618`).
    *   Text: White (`#FFFFFF`), lowercase.
    *   Border-radius: Fully rounded (pill).

---

## 3. Typography Specification

The design relies on a bold, chunky, rounded serif/display font for the main hero headline, paired with a clean geometric sans-serif for UI elements.

### Primary Headline ("finest aroma")
*   **Font Family:** `Fraunces`, `Clash Display`, or a heavy rounded serif (e.g., `Recoleta` or `Cooper Black`).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `Georgia, serif`
*   **Font Weight:** Bold (700 or Black)
*   **Text Transformation:** Lowercase (`finest aroma`)
*   **Color:** Warm white / light cream (`#FDFBF7`) with a subtle soft drop shadow for depth against the background.
*   **Layout/Structure:** 
    *   Line 1: "finest" (integrated with the coffee beans replacing letters or overlapping them).
    *   Line 2: "aroma".
    *   *Note:* The coffee beans visually interact with the text (acting as the dot on the 'i' or seamlessly floating behind/in front of letterforms).

---

## 4. Color Palette

*   **Background Cream:** `#F9F4EE`
*   **Stripe Accent:** `#E8D7CA`
*   **Primary Dark Brown (Text & Buttons):** `#3A2618`
*   **Coffee Bean Highlights/Midtones:** Rich warm browns ranging from `#8C4A2F` to `#2C1810`
*   **Text Highlight (Headline):** `#FDFBF7`

---

## 5. Assets & Imagery

*   **Coffee Beans:** Multiple high-resolution 3D-rendered coffee beans floating dynamically across the screen at various angles and scales.
    *   Top-left: Large bean positioned over the 'f' / 'i' of "finest".
    *   Top-right: Medium bean angled downward.
    *   Center-right: Another floating bean near the right edge.
    *   Bottom-left: Small and medium beans floating upward.
    *   Bottom-right: Large prominent bean overlapping the word "aroma".

---

## 6. Animation & Interaction Specifications (Parallax / Scroll Narrative)

As referenced by the caption ("Your website tells a story"), this layout is designed for interactive scroll-triggered animations (using libraries like *GSAP ScrollTrigger* or *Framer Motion*):

1.  **Initial Load (Entrance Animation):**
    *   Background stripes fade in softly.
    *   Navigation slides down from the top.
    *   Headline text ("finest aroma") scales up from 0.9 to 1.0 with a smooth fade-in.
2.  **Parallax Floating Effect (Mouse Move / Scroll):**
    *   The individual 3D coffee beans float independently at different speeds relative to the cursor position (parallax mouse move) or scroll position, creating a tactile, immersive depth-of-field effect.
3.  **Hover States:**
    *   Navigation links subtly shift color or underline on hover.
    *   "buy now" button scales up slightly (`transform: scale(1.05)`) with a smooth transition.
