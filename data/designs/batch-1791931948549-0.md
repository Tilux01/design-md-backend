# Design System Specification: Planet Desert Hero Landing Page

---

## 1. Overview & Viewport Architecture

This document specifies the exact visual design, layout rules, color palettes, typography, and asset requirements to clone the full-screen desktop hero landing page for **Planet Desert**.

* **Target Viewport:** 100vw × 100vh (Full-screen, non-scrolling single-screen desktop hero experience).
* **Base Resolution Reference:** 1440px × 900px / 1920px × 1080px (scalable via fluid units `vw`, `vh`, `rem`).
* **Frame Removal Notice:** The outer dark background, mockup shadows, and card borders present in the source media must be **completely omitted**. The inner layout expands to fill `100vw` by `100vh` edge-to-edge.

## 6. Asset & Imagery Specifications

### 6.1. Hero Visual Asset (Central Cactus)
* **File Type:** Backgroundless transparent PNG or WebP with crisp anti-aliasing.
* **Dimensions:** Minimum `900px × 1200px` at `@2x`.
* **Subject:** Mexican Saguaro / Pachycereus Pringlei columnar cactus.
  * **Morphology:**
    * Thick, ribbed vertical structure with sharp, prominent ridges.
    * Multiple areoles with pale ivory-to-brownish spiky needles.
    * Central dominant tall columnar stem extending vertically.
    * Three branching secondary arms:
      * Left arm: Shorter, curving slightly outward to the left at a ~25-degree angle.
      * Middle-front arm: Vertical, slightly lower than the central trunk.
      * Right arm: Branching outward to the right side, balanced height.
  * **Coloration:** Vibrant natural forest/olive green with photorealistic sunlight highlights along the left ribs and subtle cast shadows in the right grooves.
* **Placement & Layering:**
  * Placed directly in front of the pastel arch (`z-index: 3`).
  * Anchored near the bottom, extending upwards beyond the arch top and breaking through the top half of the screen.
  * Position: Horizontal offset `~58%` from left viewport, vertical offset bottom `~60px`.

### 6.2. Secondary Visual Asset (Bottom Bar Thumbnail)
* **File Type:** Backgroundless transparent PNG or WebP.
* **Subject:** Clustered miniature *Mammillaria* or *Echinocactus* ("Thimble Cacti").
  * **Morphology:** Dense rounded spherical/cylindrical clustering succulents covered in dense fine white star-shaped spines/areoles.
* **Placement:**
  * Sits inside the mustard-yellow rounded-corner box (`border-top-left-radius: 36px`).
  * Positioned near the bottom-left of the bar, with the tops of the cacti extending slightly upwards.

## 8. Micro-Interactions & State Details

1. **Center Navigation Links:**
   * Inactive links have `opacity: 0.8`.
   * On hover: `opacity: 1.0`, smooth color shift to `#FFFFFF` with transition `0.2s ease`.
2. **Shop Now CTA Button:**
   * On hover: Border turns `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`, and the nested amber circle scales up slightly (`transform: scale(1.08)`).
3. **Cart Button (`0 🛒`):**
   * On hover: Amber background lightens to `#EEB655` (`color-accent-amber-light`).
4. **Social Icon Buttons:**
   * On hover: Background transitions to `#FFFFFF`, and icon glyph color flips to `#9EB88D`.
5. **Right Vertical Dots:**
   * Cursor pointer on hover; dots scale from `4px` to `6px` with transition `0.2s ease`.
