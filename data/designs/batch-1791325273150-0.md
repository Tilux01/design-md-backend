# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Dark Luxury, Classical Fusion & Modern Minimalism.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio in the left editorial column; asymmetric, focused spatial layout prioritizing artistic focal points and strong typographic hierarchy.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered composition using a deep charcoal/near-black background surface (`#0d0d0f`), elevated circular atmospheric glow surfaces (`#a32e2e`), subtle glass card containers, and sharp geometric vector outlines.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient crimson radial gradient backdrops, high-contrast high-tracking category labels, asymmetrical sculptural focal art integration, dark-mode glassmorphic control tokens.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Arrow Icon (&rarr;) -> Use `&rarr;` or SVG chevron icon component.
  - Status Indicator (●) -> Use `<span class="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-brand"></span>`.
