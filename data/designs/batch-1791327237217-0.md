# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist Eyewear Atelier. Clean, sophisticated, high-end optical brand aesthetic combining editorial imagery with architectural precision.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace focus, generous vertical padding, asymmetric and balanced grid layouts allowing product imagery to breathe.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean flat surfaces, subtle borders (`#E2E8F0` / `#27272A`), minimalist card containers with understated hover states and zero aggressive drop-shadows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase navigation links, interactive slide pagination indicators, editorial full-bleed imagery overlays, minimalist interactive state transitions.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
