# BODRIN - Coffee Shop Website Design Specification

This document outlines the precise design and layout specifications for the **BODRIN** coffee shop landing page, designed to be built as a full-screen, responsive web experience.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

### Color Palette
*   **Primary Background (Dark Brown / Espresso):** `#231815` (Deep, rich dark brown base used throughout the body).
*   **Card Backgrounds / Elevated Surfaces:** `#302320` or translucent dark tones with backdrop blur (used for menu cards and sections).
*   **Accent / Brand Color (Teal / Sage Green):** `#4A8B82` (Used for primary CTA buttons, badges, and accents).
*   **Primary Text (White/Off-White):** `#FFFFFF` / `#F8F5F2`
*   **Secondary Text (Muted Sand/Beige):** `#B5A8A0` (Used for body copy, descriptions, and metadata).
*   **Price / Highlight Text:** `#FFFFFF`

### Typography
*   **Headings & Titles:**
    *   *Font Family:* Modern Serif (e.g., *Playfair Display*, *Cormorant Garamond*). Fallbacks: `Georgia, serif`.
    *   *Weight:* Bold (700) / Semi-bold (600).
*   **Body & UI Text:**
    *   *Font Family:* Clean Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Roboto*, *Montserrat*). Fallbacks: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.
    *   *Weight:* Regular (400), Medium (500).

---

## 2. Header / Navigation Bar

*   **Layout:** Fixed top navigation bar spanning the full width of the viewport.
*   **Logo:** Left-aligned "BODRIN" logotype in white uppercase serif font with a coffee bean icon dotting the 'I'.
*   **Navigation Links (Center):**
    *   `Главная` (Home)
    *   `Меню` (Menu)
    *   `О нас` (About Us)
    *   `Отзывы` (Reviews)
    *   `Акции` (Promotions)
*   **Utility & Actions (Right):**
    *   Search/User icon.
    *   Primary CTA Button: `Сделать заказ` (Place an order) styled with a teal/sage pill background.

---

## 3. Hero Section

### Layout & Content
*   **Left Column (Copywriting):**
    *   **Headline:** *"Открой для себя превосходный вкус в каждом глотке!"* (Discover exquisite taste in every sip!) — Large serif heading.
    *   **Subheadline:** *"У нас кофе – не просто напиток, а искусство..."* (Coffee for us is not just a drink, but an art...) — Muted sans-serif body copy.
    *   **CTA Button:** `Выбрать кофе` (Choose coffee) — Pill-shaped button with a solid teal background `#4A8B82`.
*   **Right Column (Visuals):**
    *   Dynamic, explosive hero graphic featuring coffee beans, splashing liquid coffee, and two stacked takeaway coffee cups branded with the "BODRIN" logo and textured cardboard sleeves.

---

## 4. Popular Menu / Product Cards Section

*   **Section Heading:** Positioned centrally below the hero (represented by a subtle "TOP" watermark in the background).
*   **Card Grid:** 3 columns displaying popular coffee drinks.
    *   **Card 1:** Cappuccino
        *   *Image:* Takeaway cup with sleeve.
        *   *Rating:* ⭐ 4.9
        *   *Subtitle/Recipe:* 30% espresso, 40% milk, 40% dense foam.
        *   *Price:* `120 р.`
        *   *Action:* Circular `+` button in teal.
    *   **Card 2:** Latte
        *   *Image:* Takeaway cup with sleeve.
        *   *Rating:* ⭐ 5.0
        *   *Subtitle/Recipe:* 30% espresso & 70% fresh milk.
        *   *Price:* `140 р.`
        *   *Action:* Circular `+` button.
    *   **Card 3:** Mocha
        *   *Image:* Takeaway cup with sleeve.
        *   *Rating:* ⭐ 4.7
        *   *Subtitle/Recipe:* 20% espresso, 50% hot milk & 30% hot chocolate.
        *   *Price:* `140 р.`
        *   *Action:* Circular `+` button.
*   **Bottom Link:** `Перейти в меню` (Go to menu).

---

## 5. "BODRIN Это" (Why Choose Us / Features) Section

*   **Background Typography:** Large background watermark numbers (`1`, `2`, `3`, `4`) interlaced with the grid layout.
*   **Central Element:** Large central image of a signature BODRIN takeaway cup with a CTA button `Заказать` (Order) overlaid directly below it.
*   **Grid Points (2x2 layout surrounding the center):**
    1.  **Высокое качество зерен** (High grain quality) — Description about meticulous bean selection.
    2.  **Индивидуальный подход к каждому гостю** (Individual approach to each guest) — Custom-crafted drinks.
    3.  **Атмосфера вдохновения и уюта** (Atmosphere of inspiration and comfort) — Cozy interior and welcoming ambiance.
    4.  **Профессиональная команда бариста** (Professional barista team) — Skilled experts in coffee preparation.

---

## 6. Interactive Quiz Banner ("Coffee Finder")

*   **Card Layout:** Full-width container with a dark gradient background, accented by scattered coffee beans and a wooden scoop filled with roasted beans in the bottom-right corner.
*   **Heading:** *"Узнай какой кофе подходит тебе больше всего"* (Find out which coffee suits you best).
*   **CTA Button:** `Пройти тест` (Take the test) — Centered pill button.

---

## 7. Footer

*   **Content:** Copyright notice, social media links (`Новости кофейни телеграм инстаграм`).

---

## 8. Animation & Interaction Specifications

*   **Hover States:**
    *   *Buttons:* Subtle scale up (`transform: scale(1.03)`) and brightness increase.
    *   *Product Cards:* Elevation lift with a subtle box-shadow increase and slight zoom on product imagery.
*   **Scroll Animations (AOS / Intersection Observer):**
    *   *Hero Elements:* Fade-in and slide-up on page load.
    *   *Floating Elements:* Parallax effect on scattered coffee beans and liquid splashes during vertical scrolling.
    *   *Feature Numbers:* Subtle fade-in as the user scrolls into the "BODRIN это" section.
