# eTrade - E-Commerce Website Design Specification

## Global Styles & Variables

- **Background Color (Body):** `#FDFDFD` (Off-white / Light gray)
- **Primary Text Color:** `#1A1A1A` (Dark Charcoal / Black)
- **Secondary Text Color / Muted:** `#717171` (Gray)
- **Primary Accent / Brand Color:** `#0066FF` (Vibrant Blue)
- **Secondary Accent / Sale Badge Color:** `#FF4D4D` (Red/Coral)
- **Rating Star Color:** `#FFC107` (Gold/Yellow)
- **Font Family:** System-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif

---

## 1. Top Header Bar
- **Background:** `#F8F8F8` or `#FFFFFF` with a subtle bottom border (`#E5E5E5`).
- **Layout:** Flexbox, space-between.
- **Left Side:** Language switcher ("English" with dropdown arrow) & Currency switcher ("USD" with dropdown arrow).
- **Right Side:** Promotional text / Help links ("Need Help?", "Sign In / Register").

---

## 2. Main Navigation Header
- **Layout:** Flexbox, aligned horizontally.
- **Logo:** 
  - Text: `eTrade`
  - Icon: Shopping bag or stylized "e" in brand blue (`#0066FF`).
- **Nav Links:** `Home`, `Shop`, `Pages`, `About`, `Blog`, `Contact`. (Active state on "Home" or default hover states).
- **Action Icons (Right):** Search, Wishlist (with counter badge), Shopping Cart (with counter badge), User Account.

---

## 3. Hero Section
- **Background:** Soft gradient fade from white to pale pink/peach (`#FFF0F0` to `#FFFFFF`).
- **Layout:** Two-column grid (Left: Text & CTA, Right: Product Imagery).
- **Left Column:**
  - Small Subtitle: `Hot Deal In This Week` (with a small red icon/dot).
  - Main Heading (H1): `Roco Wireless Headphone` (Bold, large font).
  - Buttons / Links: `Shop Now` (Primary button) + user review avatars and 5-star rating widget.
- **Right Column:**
  - Main Product Image: Over-ear wireless headphones (silver/black futuristic design floating).
  - Floating Price Badge: `$49.00` in a small circular pill.
  - Secondary floating product thumbnail (Smartwatch).

---

## 4. Browse by Category Section
- **Section Title:** `Browse by Category`
- **Layout:** Horizontal scroll or evenly spaced flex grid containing category cards.
- **Categories (Icons + Labels):**
  1. Phones
  2. Computers
  3. Accessories
  4. Laptops
  5. Monitors
  6. Networking
  7. PC Gaming
- **Card Styling:** Minimalist white cards with light grey borders (`#EAEAEA`), subtle drop shadows on hover, featuring a line icon centered above the category name.

---

## 5. Promotional Banner / Countdown Section
- **Background:** Soft neutral/white container with a centered layout.
- **Left Content:**
  - Subtitle: `Don't Miss!`
  - Heading: `Enhance Your Music Experience`
- **Countdown Timer Blocks:** Four distinct boxes showing:
  - `15` Days
  - `10` Hrs
  - `56` Mins
  - `54` Secs
- **Right Content:**
  - Large product visual: Apple AirPods Max style silver over-ear headphones floating centrally.
- **CTA Button:** `Check It Out` (Primary Blue button).

---

## 6. Explore Our Products Section
- **Section Title:** `Explore our Products`
- **Layout:** 4-column product grid with 2 rows (Total 8 products).

### Product Card Common Elements:
- **Top Badge:** `30% Off` (Blue badge on top-left).
- **Product Image:** Centered high-quality product render on a clean white background.
- **Rating:** 5 yellow stars + review count.
- **Product Name:** Bold secondary text.
- **Price:** Current price (e.g., `$29.99`) in bold, strikethrough original price (`$49.99`).
- **Color Swatches:** Small circular color dots at the bottom of the card.

### Product Grid Items:
1. **Row 1:**
   - White Wireless Gaming Controller
   - RGB Gaming Keyboard
   - Webcam / Streaming Camera
   - 3.0 Wireless Speaker System
2. **Row 2:**
   - Cylindrical Smart Speaker
   - Ergonomic Wireless Mouse
   - Professional Studio Headphones
   - Blue Dual-Shock Game Controller

---

## 7. Footer / Pagination Action
- **Button:** `View All Products` centered below the product grid (Outlined or flat style).
