This is a detailed `design.md` specification for recreating the provided Peaky Blinders streaming interface. This document focuses on a full-screen, edge-to-edge implementation, omitting the external presentation frame.

---

# Design Specification: Peaky Blinders UI Clone

## 1. General Overview
*   **Theme:** Dark, cinematic, moody, and historical.
*   **Layout:** Single-page application (SPA) style with a hero background, left-aligned content overlay, and a bottom navigation/thumbnail bar.
*   **Background:** The entire viewport is covered by a high-resolution, slightly darkened cinematic still.

## 2. Typography
The design uses a modern, clean Sans-Serif typeface to contrast with the 1920s setting of the show.

*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Roboto`.
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `Segoe UI`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Title:** 48px - 56px, Bold, White.
    *   **Metadata (Sub-header):** 14px, Regular, Light Gray (`#CCCCCC`).
    *   **Body Text:** 16px, Regular, White with 0.8 opacity. Line height: 1.5.
    *   **Button Text:** 14px, Uppercase, Bold, Black.
    *   **Nav Links:** 12px, Uppercase, Letter-spacing: 1px, White.

## 3. Color Palette
*   **Primary Background:** `#000000` (Black) with image overlays.
*   **Accent/Buttons:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
*   **Text (Primary):** `#FFFFFF` (White).
*   **Text (Secondary/Metadata):** `#A0A0A0` (Medium Gray).
*   **Overlays:** Linear gradient from `rgba(0,0,0,0.8)` on the left to `rgba(0,0,0,0)` on the right. A bottom-up gradient `rgba(0,0,0,1)` at the footer to `rgba(0,0,0,0)` at the center.

## 4. Layout Components

### A. Header (Top Navigation)
*   **Placement:** Fixed or Absolute at the top. Padding: 40px (Left/Right), 30px (Top).
*   **Logo (Left):** The words "PEAKY BLINDERS" in a condensed, slightly weathered serif font (resembling the show's official branding). Color: Off-white/Silver.
*   **Nav Menu (Right):** A horizontal list of links: `CAST`, `EPISODES`, `CHARACTERS`, `PHOTOS`.
    *   **Styling:** White text, low opacity (0.6) except on hover. Pill-shaped translucent background behind the entire nav group.

### B. Hero Section (The Main Image)
*   **Image Description:** A wide-angle shot of a grassy, overcast field. Three men (The Shelby brothers) are walking toward the camera.
    *   **Subject 1 (Center-Left):** Thomas Shelby (Cillian Murphy). He is the largest figure, positioned slightly left of center. He wears a dark overcoat, a three-piece suit, and his signature newsboy cap. He is looking slightly to the right with a neutral, intense expression.
    *   **Subject 2 (Center-Right):** Arthur Shelby. He is positioned further back, carrying a long-barreled rifle over his right shoulder. He wears a similar dark overcoat and cap.
    *   **Subject 3 (Far Right):** John Shelby. Positioned in the background, hands at his sides, wearing a dark suit and cap.
*   **Image Treatment:** Not backgroundless. This is a full-frame photo. It has a cool, desaturated color grade (blues and greens are muted).

### C. Content Overlay (Left Side)
*   **Placement:** Vertically centered, aligned to the left with 60px padding.
*   **Title:** "Peaky Blinders" in white bold sans-serif.
*   **Metadata Row:** "Language: English | 6 Seasons | 2013-2019". Small font size, separated by vertical pipes.
*   **Synopsis:** A paragraph of roughly 3-4 lines. Text is white with reduced opacity for readability against the background.
*   **Credits:** "Starring: Cillian Murphy, Tom Hardy, Helen McCrory" followed by "Created by: Steven Knight".
*   **Call to Action (Button):**
    *   **Shape:** Rounded Pill (Stadium).
    *   **Color:** White background, black text.
    *   **Content:** A "Play" icon (triangle) followed by "WATCH FROM START".
    *   **Placement:** 20px below the credits.

### D. Season Selector (Center Divider)
*   **Placement:** Located about 80% down the screen.
*   **Visual:** A thin, horizontal gray line spanning the width (with margins).
*   **Selector:** In the dead center of the line, a black pill-shaped button with a white border containing the text "Season 1" and a small downward chevron icon.

### E. Thumbnail Gallery (Bottom)
*   **Placement:** Bottom of the screen, below the Season Selector.
*   **Layout:** Three rectangular thumbnails with slightly rounded corners (approx 8px radius).
*   **Thumbnail 1 (Left):** A shot of the three brothers standing together in a dark, smoky industrial setting.
*   **Thumbnail 2 (Center):** A close-up profile shot of Thomas Shelby in a dark room, looking downward.
*   **Thumbnail 3 (Right):** A dynamic shot of two men in a confrontation/fight in a dimly lit interior.
*   **Effect:** The thumbnails should have a slight "glow" or border if selected.

## 5. Detailed Image Specifications
1.  **Main Background:** Full-bleed, 100vw, 100vh. Aspect ratio 16:9 or 21:9. The image is not rotated. It is static.
2.  **Left Gradient:** A CSS linear gradient `to right, black 0%, transparent 50%` must be applied over the background to ensure the white text is legible over the field imagery.
3.  **Bottom Shadow:** A CSS linear gradient `to top, black 0%, transparent 30%` to ground the thumbnails and the season selector.

## 6. Interactive States
*   **Navigation Links:** Opacity shifts from 0.6 to 1.0 on hover.
*   **Watch Button:** Scale up slightly (1.05x) and increase drop shadow on hover.
*   **Thumbnails:** Brightness increases by 20% on hover to indicate they are clickable.
