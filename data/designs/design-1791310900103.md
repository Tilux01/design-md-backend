# Production-Grade Design Specification: Audiophile Web Experience

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
*   **Primary Mood:** Minimalist luxury, high-end consumer electronics showcase, sleek, immersive, and precise.
*   **Brand Archetype:** Premium / Sophisticated E-Commerce & Product Showcase.
*   **Visual Density:** Clean and airy, prioritizing a large central hero product imagery with balanced whitespace.
*   **Whitespace Balance:** Generous outer padding with tightly controlled inner grid alignments. Asymmetric horizontal layout framing product imagery over a neutral display block.
*   **Theme Architecture:** Light-mode-first aesthetic. Surfaces rely on stark whites (`#FFFFFF`), neutral light grays (`#F4F4F4`), high-contrast dark charcoal text (`#1A1A1A`), and subtle warm orange accent points (`#F2994A`).

---

## 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

### Core Colors
*   **Primary (Brand):** `#111111` (Deep Charcoal / Near Black)
*   **Primary Hover:** `#333333`
*   **Accent / Rating:** `#F2994A` (Vibrant Warm Orange)
*   **Base Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
*   **Surface Background (Hero Card):** `#EAEAEA` to `#F1F1F1` (Subtle Neutral Gray)
*   **Thumbnail Card Background:** `#FFFFFF` with `#E0E0E0` borders

### Borders & Dividers
*   **Border Light:** `#EAEAEA`
*   **Border Active / Selected:** `#111111` (or `#F2994A` for accent highlights)

### Text Colors
*   **Primary Text:** `#1A1A1A`
*   **Muted Text / Subtitles:** `#7A7A7A`
*   **Inverse Text:** `#FFFFFF`
*   **Accent Text:** `#F2994A`

---

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy

### Font Families
*   **Headings:** `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, or condensed modern sans-serif (Bold, tracked-out styling).
*   **Body:** `Inter`, system-ui, `-apple-system`, sans-serif.

### Scale Breakdown
*   **Logo/Brand Title:** 18px / Line-height: 1.2 / Weight: 700 / Tracking: 0.05em
*   **Heading 1 (Product Title):** 36px - 42px / Line-height: 1.1 / Weight: 800 / Tracking: -0.02em
*   **Section Nav / Links:** 13px / Line-height: 1.0 / Weight: 500
*   **Body Copy (Description):** 13px - 14px / Line-height: 1.6 / Weight: 400 / Color: `#7A7A7A`
*   **Button Text:** 12px / Line-height: 1.0 / Weight: 600 / Tracking: 0.1em (Uppercase)

---

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid

### Grid Structure
*   **Container Max-Width:** `1200px` centered with auto margins.
*   **Layout Structure:** Fixed top header, expansive central showcase viewport featuring an offset product display card, bottom thumbnail carousel switcher, and vertical side utility indicators.

### Spacing Scale
*   `--space-xs`: `4px`
*   `--space-sm`: `8px`
*   `--space-md`: `16px`
*   `--space-lg`: `24px`
*   `--space-xl`: `48px`

### Stacking Order (Z-Index)
*   `z-0`: Background Canvas (`#FFFFFF`)
*   `z-10`: Hero Display Card (`#EAEAEA`)
*   `z-20`: Product 3D Renders / Imagery & Typography
*   `z-30`: Navigation Bars, Floating Controls & Side Socials

---

## 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement

### Top Navigation Bar
*   **Layout:** Flexbox, space-between alignment.
*   **Left Element:** Hamburger menu icon (`#1A1A1A`) paired with uppercase brand logotype `AUDIOPHILE`.
*   **Center/Right Links:** Feature, Sound, Review (hover state applies subtle opacity shift).
*   **Far Right:** Cart/Bag icon with indicator dot.

### Hero Showcase Card
*   **Dimensions:** Fluid width spanning roughly 75% of container bounds, generous vertical padding.
*   **Background:** Neutral soft gray container with subtle inner shadow vignette.
*   **Product Visual:** Centered studio-lit headphones casting a soft ambient drop shadow onto the card surface.
*   **Typography Block:** Right-aligned or neatly stacked typographic column containing:
    *   Category / Product Line
    *   Model Designation (`VK-2 LEGACY`)
    *   Descriptive paragraph block
    *   Star-rating component (`★★★★★` in `#F2994A`)
    *   **Action Button:** Dark charcoal rectangular button (`#111111`) reading `DISCOVER MORE >` with right-pointing arrow icon.

### Carousel & Navigation Controls
*   **Side Controls:** Vertical pagination indicators (`01`, `03`) on the right margin; left/right navigation chevrons (`<` `>`) anchored to the center-left edge of the hero canvas.
*   **Thumbnail Switcher:** Row of three preview cards positioned at the bottom right of the hero container.
    *   Active thumbnail features a solid bottom orange border indicator (`#F2994A`).
    *   Inactive thumbnails feature neutral subtle borders.

### Social Sidebar
*   Pinned vertically along the far-right edge featuring monochromatic social icons (Twitter, Pinterest, Facebook, Instagram) rendered in muted gray (`#999999`).

---

## 6. Motion Mechanics & Interaction Specifications

### Animation Triggers
*   **Page Load / Entrance:** Staggered fade-in and upward translation (`translateY(20px)` to `0`) for the hero card, typography, and product render.
*   **Thumbnail Switch:** Smooth cross-fade and scale transition of the central product imagery coupled with text content sliding in horizontally.

### Transition Physics
*   **Standard Transition:** `300ms cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)` (Ease-out snap).
*   **Hover States:** `150ms ease-in-out` for color shifts and subtle button scale effects (`transform: scale(1.02)`).
