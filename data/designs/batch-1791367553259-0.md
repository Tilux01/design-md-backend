```markdown
# Design Specification: E-Commerce Landing Page (Tech/Gadgets)

## 1. Global Styles & Variables

### Color Palette
*   **Background (Primary):** `#FFFFFF` (White)
*   **Background (Dark Sections/Header):** `#111111` (Very Dark Gray / Off-Black)
*   **Background (Secondary Card/Banner):** `#1A1A1A` (Dark Charcoal)
*   **Text (Primary Dark):** `#111111` (Off-Black)
*   **Text (Primary Light):** `#FFFFFF` (White)
*   **Text (Muted/Gray):** `#777777` (Medium Gray)
*   **Brand / Action Color (Green):** `#2ECC71` (Vibrant Green)
*   **Accent Color (Sale Badge - Pink/Red):** `#E74C3C` (Reddish Pink)
*   **Border / Divider Color:** `#E0E0E0` (Light Gray)

### Typography
*   **Font Family:** Inter, sans-serif (or system-ui fallback)
*   **Headings:** Bold, 600–700 weight
*   **Body:** Regular, 400 weight

---

## 2. Layout & Structure

### Overall Structure
*   **Page Width:** Full-width (`100%`), content centered inside a max-width container (e.g., `1200px`).
*   **Grid System:** Responsive grid for product cards (4 columns on desktop, 2 on tablet, 1 on mobile).

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. Header & Navigation
*   **Background:** Dark (`#111111`)
*   **Top Bar:**
    *   Left: Logo mark (Green/White text/icon block).
    *   Right: Search icon, cart icon, user account icon.
*   **Sub-Navigation / Category Bar:**
    *   Horizontal list of links (e.g., Audio, Wearables, Gaming, Smart Home, Accessories).
    *   Active/Hover state: Green underline or text color change.

---

### B. Section 1: Featured Products ("Trending Items")
*   **Section Header:**
    *   Title: "Featured Products" (Centered, bold, medium size).
*   **Product Grid (4 items shown):**
    *   **Card Styling:** Clean white background, thin border or subtle shadow, rounded corners (`8px`).
    *   **Badges:**
        *   "NEW" badge (Green pill badge, top right of card).
        *   "SALE" badge (Pink/Red pill badge, top right of card).
    *   **Product Elements (Top to Bottom):**
        1.  **Image:** High-contrast product render (Earbuds, Smartwatch, Camera/Lens, Headset).
        2.  **Title:** 2-line product name (e.g., "Wireless ANC Earbuds").
        3.  **Price:** Bold dark text (e.g., "$149.00").
        4.  **CTA Button:** Solid Green (`#2ECC71`), rounded, white text: "Add to Cart".

---

### C. Section 2: Promotional Banner
*   **Container:** Full-width banner with dark gradient background (`#1A1A1A` to `#2C2C2C`), rounded corners.
*   **Layout:**
    *   **Left Side:**
        *   Heading: "Next-Gen Productivity" (White, Bold, Large).
        *   Subtext: Short descriptive copy about tech/productivity tools.
        *   Button: Solid Green outline/fill "Shop Now".
    *   **Right Side:** Product lifestyle render/mockup (e.g., mechanical keyboard / multi-device setup).

---

### D. Section 3: Category Highlights / Secondary Grid
*   **Layout:** 2-column split banner or dual category grid (e.g., "VR & AR" and "Pro Audio").
*   **Card Elements:**
    *   Dark background card with product imagery on the right/center.
    *   Category title overlaid clearly.
    *   Sub-link: "Explore More".

---

## 4. Responsive Behavior
*   **Desktop (> 1024px):** 4 columns for products, 2 columns for banners.
*   **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 2 columns for products, stacked banners.
*   **Mobile (< 767px):** 1 column for products, full-stack vertical layout, hamburger menu for navigation.
```
