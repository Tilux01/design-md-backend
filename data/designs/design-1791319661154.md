# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist Dark-on-Light / Modern Portfolio Showreel with High-Contrast Typography.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio, clean asymmetric hero balancing massive character portrait imagery against sparse typography and micro-HUD elements.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, ultra-clean canvas with subtle floating micro-cards (glassmorphism/translucency with thin borders for contextual stat callouts).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Floating HUD cards anchored to focal points, high-contrast monochrome hero treatment, custom dot-pagination scroll indicator, brand logo pill framing.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border width `1px` (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-slate-900 inline-block"></span>`.
