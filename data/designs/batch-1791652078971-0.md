# Design Specification: Aero Luxe - Private Flight Landing Page

## 1. Overall Layout & Canvas Properties
- **Viewport:** Full-screen responsive layout (designed for desktop, minimum recommended width: 1440px).
- **Background Color:** Deep charcoal/black gradient (`#050505` to `#111111`). The entire canvas features a moody, high-contrast, cinematic lighting setup with dark ambient occlusion.
- **Grid Structure:** Asymmetric layout featuring a sticky/fixed top navigation, a hero central display area split across multiple depth layers, and a structured lower content block.

---

## 2. Typography & Font Specifications
*Note: If custom luxury fonts cannot be loaded, standard professional geometric and serif sans-serif/script fallbacks should be used.*

### Primary Logo & Branding Font
- **Family:** Custom geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Montserrat* or *Futura*).
- **Fallback:** `sans-serif`.
- **Styling:** Uppercase, wide letter-spacing (tracking).

### Hero Headline ("Private Flight")
- **Word "Private":**
  - **Font Family:** Elegant high-contrast serif script or display font (e.g., *Playfair Display*, *Cinzel*, or *Didot*).
  - **Fallback:** `serif`.
  - **Styling:** Sentence case, light font weight, smooth anti-aliasing.
- **Word "Flight":**
  - **Font Family:** Matching the high-contrast serif/display font style.
  - **Fallback:** `serif`.
  - **Styling:** Sentence case, light font weight, perfectly balanced with "Private".

### Body & Navigation Text
- **Font Family:** Clean, modern sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*, *Arial*).
- **Fallback:** `sans-serif`.
- **Sizes:** 
  - Navigation links: ~13px, uppercase, medium weight.
  - Microcopy / Stats (e.g., "CLIENTS 12.8K"): ~11px, tracking expanded, light weight.
  - Paragraph description ("WE PROVIDE A TAILORED..."): ~14px, regular weight, high line-height for readability.

---

## 3. Header & Navigation Bar
- **Position:** Fixed or sticky top bar spanning the full width with subtle horizontal padding.
- **Left Element:** 
  - Brand Logo: "AEROLLUXE" in bold tracked uppercase letters, accompanied by a smaller tagline underneath: "YOUR SKY, YOUR TRIP".
- **Right Elements (Navigation Links):**
  - Items: `AEROPLANES`, `SERVICES`, `BLOG`.
  - **CTA Button:** `CONTACT` — styled with a solid warm gold/amber background (`#d4af37`), dark text, rounded or sharp rectangular borders, prominent hover state.

---

## 4. Hero Section & Central Imagery

### Central Jet Image
- **Description:** A sleek, high-end private luxury jet viewed from a three-quarters front angle, parked inside a dimly lit, professional hangar. Dramatic spotlights hit the fuselage and wingtips.
- **Properties:** Backgroundless asset (transparent PNG/WebP cutout placed over the dark gradient background).
- **Placement & Rotation:** Centered horizontally in the upper-middle portion of the screen, perfectly balanced between the "Private" and "Flight" text. Zero or near-zero rotational tilt (horizontal alignment).

### Foreground Metallic Ribbon / Wave
- **Description:** A hyper-realistic, highly polished liquid chrome/silver abstract ribbon that swoops dynamically across the lower-middle section of the screen from left to right. It features dramatic specular highlights, soft reflections, and deep shadows.
- **Placement:** Positioned in the foreground, overlapping the lower half of the jet and text, creating a strong sense of depth (parallax layering effect).

### Central Interactive Element ("BOOK NOW")
- **Description:** A circular badge/button embedded directly into the metallic ribbon wave. 
- **Styling:** Concentric glowing metallic rings with a dark circular center reading `BOOK` on the top line and `NOW` on the bottom line in crisp, uppercase white text.

---

## 5. Floating UI Data Callouts & Badges

- **Left Data Point ("CLIENTS 12.8K"):** Positioned to the left of the jet, connected via a thin white tracking line and dot pointing toward the standing silhouette.
- **Top Center Data Point ("FLIGHTS 6.1K"):** Positioned above the jet nose, connected via a thin angular line pointing directly to the cockpit windshield.
- **Top Right Badge ("150 COUNTRIES"):** A small square card featuring a subtle globe icon and the bold text `150 COUNTRIES`.
- **Bottom Right Badge ("APEX+ COMPANY OF THE YEAR"):** A circular golden/bronze award seal featuring star ratings and the official typography marking it as the company of the year.

---

## 6. Lower Content Section

### Left Column ("CONCIERGE SERVICE")
- **Heading:** `CONCIERGE SERVICE` in clean, tracked uppercase sans-serif.
- **Accents:** Minimalist horizontal accent line underneath the title.

### Center Column (Luggage Imagery & Description)
- **Luggage Images:** Two high-end, ribbed carbon-fiber/metallic designer suitcases (one larger upright case angled, one smaller vanity case next to it). Both are backgroundless cutouts with realistic drop shadows onto the floor surface.
- **Paragraph Text:** 
  > WE PROVIDE A TAILORED APPROACH TO MAKE YOUR FLIGHT A SEAMLESS AND LUXURIOUS EXPERIENCE.
- **Styling:** Multi-line text block aligned neatly with professional typography hierarchy.

---

## 7. Interactive States & Responsive Behavior
- **Hover Effects:** Navigation links and the "BOOK NOW" badge should feature smooth scaling or luminosity shifts on hover.
- **Responsiveness:** On smaller viewports (tablets/mobile), the horizontal layout should stack vertically: Logo/Nav collapse into a hamburger menu, hero typography scales down, data points reposition, and the lower content grid stacks into a single column.
