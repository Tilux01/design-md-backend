# Design Specification: "Dream trip" Aerodynamic Bicycle Product Landing Page

---

## 1. Overview & Canvas Setup

This specification details a modern, dark-themed, minimalist e-commerce showcase page for a high-performance aerodynamic carbon fiber bicycle. 

* **Target Viewport**: Fullscreen single-page application (`100vw` × `100vh`), with `overflow: hidden`.
* **Outer Presentation Frame**: Excluded. Render edge-to-edge with no mock browser frames or outer padding.
* **Layout Model**: CSS Grid / Flexbox overlay structure with an absolute visual layering stack for the background, hero image, and floating controls.

## 6. Bicycle Hero Imagery (Visual Asset)

### Image Specifications
* **Format**: Isolated, backgroundless cutout (transparent PNG or WebP).
* **Placement**: Absolute positioned on the right half of the canvas.
* **Scale**: Large, dominating the right 60% of the screen.
* **Vertical Positioning**: Resting just above the bottom margin line, tires planted on the baseline.
* **Rotation / Angle**: Strict side profile view facing left toward the content block.

### Visual Description of Asset:
* **Frame**: Ultra-sleek, aerodynamic matte black/charcoal carbon fiber monocoque frame.
* **Handlebars**: Low-profile integrated flat bullhorn/aerobar design extending horizontally.
* **Branding on Down Tube**: Small white stylized typography reading `"sidoor"` angled parallel to the down tube.
* **Seat Post Accent**: Distinct neon cyan/electric blue luminous glow along the vertical seat mast/post section connecting to the saddle.
* **Wheels**:
  * Solid carbon disc wheels (aero dish/disc style) front and rear.
  * Deep charcoal/graphite texture with a visible rim track.
  * Central hubs: Small red circular accent rings around the hub centers.
* **Drivetrain**: Minimalist single front chainring and crankarm in dark matte alloy.
* **Ground Shadow**:
  * Linear ground baseline: A single continuous `1px` subtle horizontal line (`#343F52`) under the wheels extending toward the right.
  * Soft elliptical contact shadow directly beneath both tires to ground the asset.

## 9. Animation & Interaction Specifications

1. **CTA Hover**:
   * Scale `1.02` with an intensified drop shadow glow (`0 6px 20px rgba(0, 194, 203, 0.5)`).
2. **Carousel Arrows Hover**:
   * Segment background transitions to `#3A475C` with active click feedback.
3. **Bicycle Image Load**:
   * Subtle fade-in and slight slide-in from right (`transform: translateX(40px)` to `translateX(0)` over `800ms ease-out`).
4. **Pagination Item Click**:
   * Active cyan ring animates smoothly between index numbers with a fade transition.
5. **Seat Tube Glow**:
   * Subtle breathing pulse animation on the electric blue gradient (`opacity: 0.85` to `1.0`).
