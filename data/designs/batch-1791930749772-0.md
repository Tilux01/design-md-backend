# Design Specification: Dark Matter Landing Page

## 1. Overall Layout & Canvas
* **Layout Structure:** Full-screen 50/50 vertical split.
  * **Left Column (50% width):** Solid warm orange (`#F58F1E`).
  * **Right Column (50% width):** Solid dark charcoal (`#2B2C2E`).
* **Frame:** Edge-to-edge, responsive viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), no external borders or drop shadows.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Primary Accent (Left):** `#F58F1E` (Vibrant Amber/Orange)
* **Primary Dark (Right):** `#2B2C2E` (Matte Charcoal)
* **Text & Icons:** 
  * Main text/white elements: `#FFFFFF`
  * Secondary text/accents: `#F0F0F0` / `#A0A0A0`
* **Play Button:** `#FFFFFF` background with `#2B2C2E` play icon.

---

## 3. Typography
* **Primary Font Family:** *Montserrat*, *Futura PT*, or *Inter*.
* **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `sans-serif`.

### Type Hierarchy
1. **Hero Title ("DARK MATTER"):**
   * Case: Uppercase
   * Weight: `300` (Light)
   * Size: `2.5rem` – `3rem`
   * Letter Spacing: `0.75em` (Extremely wide tracking)
   * Color: White (`#FFFFFF`)
2. **Hero Subtitle ("by Alberto Seveso"):**
   * Case: Mixed case
   * Weight: `400` (Regular)
   * Size: `0.75rem`
   * Letter Spacing: `0.15em`
   * Color: Light Gray (`#CCCCCC`)
3. **Navigation & Interactive Labels:**
   * Case: Lowercase
   * Weight: `400`
   * Size: `0.8rem`
   * Letter Spacing: `0.05em`
   * Color: White (`#FFFFFF`)

---

## 4. Visual Asset: Center Artwork
* **Type:** High-resolution transparent PNG (backgroundless).
* **Description:** Abstract, highly detailed plume of billowing, volumetric black liquid ink dispersing in water (Alberto Seveso style). Dense center with smooth, undulating organic folds and glossy shadow contours.
* **Placement:** Dead-center horizontally and vertically (`top: 50%`, `left: 50%`, `transform: translate(-50%, -50%)`).
* **Dimensions:** Approximately `45vw` width, preserving natural aspect ratio.
* **Rotation:** `0deg` (upright as shown).

---

## 5. UI Components

### Top Navigation Bar
* **Position:** Fixed or absolute, `padding: 2.5rem 3rem`, spans full width.
* **Left Section:**
  * **Logo Mark:** Three small overlapping multifaceted triangles (red, orange, black).
  * **Brand Text:** `darkmatter.` in bold sans-serif, followed by micro-subtext below it.
* **Right Section:**
  * Horizontal layout aligned right.
  * Thin horizontal dash (`—`) active indicator.
  * Nav links: `discover`, `works`, `statements` with `1.5rem` spacing.
  * Menu Icon: 3×3 square grid of white circular dots.

### Hero Center Overlay
* Centered on top of the ink artwork.
* Main title `D A R K   M A T T E R` centered over the image.
* Subtitle `by Alberto Seveso` placed directly beneath with a `1.5rem` top margin.

### Bottom Interactive Bar
* **Position:** Bottom center, `bottom: 3rem`.
* **Play Control:**
  * Centered circular button (`~40px` diameter), background pure white, subtle elevation shadow.
  * Centered right-pointing triangle play icon (Charcoal, `10px`).
* **Connecting Line & Labels:**
  * Thin horizontal line (`1px`, `#FFFFFF` with low opacity) passing horizontally behind or through the button.
  * Left label (`play intro`): Located to the left of the button, connected via line.
  * Right label (`take survey`): Located to the right of the button, connected via line.
