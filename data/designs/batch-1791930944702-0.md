# Design Specification: Peugeot Future Bicycle Concept UI

## 1. Typography

* **Primary Font:** Inter, Helvetica Neue, or Roboto.
* **Fallbacks:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`.
* **Weights & Styles:**
  * **Brand Logo ("FTR"):** Bold (700), ~20px, uppercase, tracking-wider.
  * **Headings ("Peugeot future bicycle"):** Semi-Bold to Bold (600–700), ~44px, line-height 1.15, text-white.
  * **Nav Links:** Regular (400), ~13px, color `#8a93a5`.
  * **Body Description:** Regular (400), ~13px, line-height 1.6, color `#6e7787`.
  * **Price Tag:** Light to Regular (300–400), ~22px, text-white.
  * **Card Labels:** Medium (500), ~12px, text-white.
  * **Social Rail Links:** Medium (500), ~11px, tracking-widest, uppercase, vertical orientation.

---

## 2. Color Palette

* **Background:** Deep dark navy/slate gradient (`#0d1017` to `#161b24`) with a soft radial spotlight behind the main bike.
* **Left Floating Card:** Solid matte blue-grey `#2c3340`.
* **Right Sidebar:** Deep charcoal/navy `#12161f` with subtle border-left `#1e2430`.
* **Text & Accents:**
  * Primary Text: `#FFFFFF`
  * Secondary / Muted Text: `#7A8494`
  * Button Border: `#555D6D` (hover: `#FFFFFF`)
  * Accent Highlight (on bike model): Metallic Bronze / Rose Gold (`#C59B7E`)

---

## 3. Layout Structure

* **Viewport:** Fullscreen (100vw, 100vh), overflow hidden.
* **Three-Column Grid/Flex Layout:**
  1. **Left Card Panel:** Floating overlay or fixed column (`~240px` wide).
  2. **Central Hero Section:** Flex-grow product showcase (Navbar, Hero Copy, Hero Image, Price).
  3. **Right Action Rail:** Thin persistent sidebar (`~60px` wide).

---

## 4. Components

### A. Navigation Bar (Top Center)
* **Left:** Brand label `FTR`.
* **Center:** Horizontal links with even spacing (`gap-8`): `About`, `Product`, `Guarantee`, `Reviews`, `Cart`.
* **Right:** Minimalist search icon (`outline`, 18px).

### B. Left Floating Panel
* **Container:** Floating card positioned on the left margin, `border-radius: 16px`, background `#2c3340`, vertical layout with 3 product cards.
* **Card Items (Stacked vertically):**
  * Top: Cruxon model A
  * Middle: Cruxon model S
  * Bottom: Cruxon model D
  * *Each card:* Dark inset preview box (`border-radius: 8px`, `#12151b`), centered bike thumbnail, labeled below with clean white text.

### C. Hero Content (Center-Left)
* **Heading:** Two-line heading `Peugeot` / `future bicycle`.
* **Body Copy:** 3 lines of muted placeholder text (`max-w-sm`).
* **Button ("Add to cart"):** Pill-shaped outline button (`rounded-full`), transparent background, 1px border `#555D6D`, white text, padding: `10px 32px`.

### D. Main Product Showcase (Center-Right)
* **Hero Image:** High-resolution futuristic bicycle positioned overlapping the bottom right floor plane.
* **Price Indicator:** Horizontal dash followed by price `— $1 250`, placed bottom-center near the bottom bracket/crankset.

### E. Right Vertical Rail
* **Top:** Custom menu icon (three horizontal stacked bars of variable lengths: top medium, middle short, bottom long).
* **Bottom:** Vertical social links stacked (`TWITTER`, `INST`, `FB`), rotated `-90deg`, uppercase, tracked.

---
