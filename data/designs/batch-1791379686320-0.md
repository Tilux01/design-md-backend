# UI Design Specification: Nike Air Force 1 Interactive Product Page

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
- **View:** Fullscreen (100vw, 100vh), immersive single-page layout.
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Primary Background:** Solid monochromatic gradient / flat ice blue (`#73A7E3` to `#8CB6ED` range, or uniform pastel blue base `hsl(215, 65%, 72%)`).
  - **Accents & Text:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for typography and iconography, translucent white overlays (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` to `0.8`) for background watermark typography and UI chrome.
- **Spacing:** Generous, centered composition with floating glassmorphism/card UI container keeping content safely padded from screen edges.

---

## 2. Typography & Fallbacks
The design relies heavily on heavy, modern, bold grotesque sans-serif typefaces typical of high-end Nike digital experiences (similar to *Futura Bold*, *Nike Futura*, or *Helvetica Neue Condensed*).

### Typography Hierarchy:
1. **Watermark / Background Typography ("FORCE AIR"):**
   - **Font-Family:** `Futura Condensed Bold`, `Helvetica Neue Bold`, *Fallbacks:* `Impact`, `Arial Black`, `sans-serif`.
   - **Style:** Uppercase, extra-bold weight, huge scale spanning the central viewport width.
   - **Opacity:** Low opacity (`0.15` - `0.25`) sitting behind the sneaker product asset.
2. **Global Navigation & Utility Text ("ADD TO CART", Social handles "FB", "TW", "IG"):**
   - **Font-Family:** `Helvetica Neue`, `Inter`, `system-ui`, *Fallbacks:* `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
   - **Style:** Medium/Semi-bold, tracked/letter-spaced (approx. `1px` to `1.5px`), uppercase.

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. Header / Navigation Bar
- **Position:** Absolute, top-aligned within the inner canvas boundary.
- **Left:** Nike "Swoosh" logo in solid white (`#FFFFFF`), scaled to approx. `48px` width.
- **Right:** Utility icons styled with crisp white line-art:
  - **Search Icon (Magnifying Glass)**
  - **Divider Pipe (`|`)**
  - **Menu/Hamburger Icon (Three horizontal bars)**

### B. Center Stage: Product & Typography Watermark
- **Background Watermark:**
  - Giant block text spelling **"FORCE"** with an **"AIR"** badge embedded inside the letter "C".
  - Faded into the blue backdrop.
- **Product Asset (Sneaker):**
  - Nike Air Force 1 Low (University Blue / White colorway).
  - Rotated at a dynamic 3/4 floating isometric perspective (angled upwards facing right).
  - **Drop Shadow:** Deep, soft diffused oval drop shadow floating directly beneath the sole to create weightless depth/levitation.

### C. Left Navigation & Pagination
- **Pagination Dots:** Vertical stack of four dots indicating slider/carousel states.
  - Active state: Solid white pill/dot.
  - Inactive states: Semi-transparent white dots.
- **Interactive Cursor/Pan Tool:**
  - Floating circular UI element at the bottom-left corner with crosshairs (`+`) and directional arrows, simulating a 360-degree product inspection tool.

### D. Right Side Utilities
- **Vertical Social Links:** Stacked vertically on the mid-right edge:
  - `FB` (Facebook)
  - `TW` (Twitter)
  - `IG` (Instagram)
  - Styled in muted, high-legibility white text.

### E. Bottom Right Actions
- **CTA Button:** 
  - Text: `ADD TO CART ↗`
  - Styled with a subtle diagonal arrow icon pointing up-right, minimalist borderless or subtle border treatment.

---

## 4. Animations & Micro-Interactions (Motion Design Specification)
- **Product Float (Idle State):**
  - The sneaker utilizes a continuous sine-wave vertical floating animation (`transform: translateY(-10px)` transitioning back and forth over a 4-second duration).
- **360-Degree Interaction:**
  - Dragging anywhere on the canvas or using the bottom-left cursor tool triggers rotational scrubbing of the 3D model.
- **Hover States:**
  - **CTA ("ADD TO CART"):** Shifts opacity from `0.8` to `1.0` with a slight scale-up (`scale(1.02)`).
  - **Nav Icons:** Subtle brightness bloom on hover.
