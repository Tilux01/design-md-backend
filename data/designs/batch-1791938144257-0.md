# Cinema App Design Specification

## 1. General Theme & Style
*   **Background Color:** Pure Black (`#000000`).
*   **Primary Text Color:** White (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Secondary Text Color:** Light Gray (`#A1A1A1`).
*   **Accent Color:** Glassmorphism/Translucent White (Alpha 10-20%).
*   **Corner Radii:** 
    *   Main Cards: `32px`
    *   Buttons/Badges: `20px`
    *   Cast Images: `16px`
*   **Status Bar:** iOS standard style (Time at 9:41, Signal, Wi-Fi, and Battery icons in white).

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Inter** or **SF Pro Display**.
*   **Fallback Font:** Sans-serif (Helvetica, Arial).
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Date Header:** `700` (Bold), approx `48px`, Uppercase.
    *   **Section Headers:** `700` (Bold), approx `24px`, Uppercase.
    *   **Body Text:** `400` (Regular), approx `14px`, Line height `1.5`.
    *   **Badges/Labels:** `600` (Semi-bold), approx `12px`.
    *   **Nav Items:** `500` (Medium), approx `14px`.

## 3. Screen 1: Home / Now Playing
### Top Navigation (Tabs)
*   **Layout:** Horizontal flex container with three items.
*   **Items:** "Coming Soon", "Now Playing", "Tomorrow".
*   **"Now Playing" State:** Active. Encased in a pill-shaped container with a subtle white border and a semi-transparent background.
*   **"Tomorrow" State:** Includes a small chevron-right icon.

### Main Content
*   **Date Text:** "12 JUN" centered at the top, bold and large.
*   **Movie Card:** 
    *   **Placement:** Centered, taking up ~70% of the screen height.
    *   **Image:** Poster for "Nobody 2".
    *   **Overlay Badges (Top Left):** 
        *   Pill 1: "1h 30m" (Black background, white text).
        *   Pill 2: "Action" (Black background, white text).
    *   **Visual Effect:** The card has a subtle blue/cyan glow emanating from behind the top edge.

### Bottom Navigation Bar
*   **Shape:** Floating pill-shaped bar, centered horizontally.
*   **Background:** Translucent dark gray (Glassmorphism effect).
*   **Icons:** 
    *   Home (Outline)
    *   Movies (Play button inside a square - Active state with a white circle background)
    *   Notifications (Bell outline)
    *   Profile (User circle outline)

## 7. Interaction & Effects
*   **Gradients:** A soft radial gradient (Black to `#1A1A1A`) is used behind the main movie cards to create depth.
*   **Shadows:** No heavy drop shadows; depth is achieved through color layering and translucency (Glassmorphism).
*   **Transitions:** The transition from Screen 1 to Screen 2 should feel like the movie card expanding to fill the top half of the screen.
