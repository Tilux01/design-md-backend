# Design Specification: Deadpool 2 Landing Page

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Layout Style:** Split-screen asymmetrical layout with a floating central hero character.
*   **Canvas:** Full-screen (100vw, 100vh), no borders, no scrollbars (overflow hidden).
*   **Primary Background:** The screen is divided into two main sections:
    *   **Left Section (65% width):** Off-white/Light gray gradient (#FFFFFF to #F5F5F5).
    *   **Right Section (35% width):** Deep Crimson Red (#A7000E) with a subtle dark vignette.
*   **Overlay Element:** A large, semi-transparent "DP2" watermark sits in the background behind the main character, spanning the center.

## 2. Color Palette
*   **Deadpool Red:** `#BE1E2D` (Used for primary headings and buttons).
*   **Deep Crimson:** `#A7000E` (Right sidebar background).
*   **Pure Black:** `#000000` (Main text).
*   **Dark Gray:** `#1A1A1A` (Sub-headings and secondary text).
*   **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF` (Navigation and button backgrounds).
*   **Muted Gray:** `#8E8E8E` (Secondary info and icons).

## 3. Typography
*   **Primary Heading Font:** *Montserrat Bold* or *Gotham Ultra*. 
    *   *Fallback:* Helvetica Neue, Arial Black, sans-serif.
    *   *Style:* All-caps, tight letter spacing, heavy weight.
*   **Secondary/Body Font:** *Inter* or *Roboto*.
    *   *Fallback:* Segoe UI, San Francisco, sans-serif.
    *   *Style:* Medium weight for navigation, Regular for sub-text.
*   **Numerical Font:** *DIN Condensed* or *Oswald*.
    *   *Style:* Tall, narrow, and bold (used for the viewer count).

## 4. Header & Navigation
*   **Logo:** Top-left corner. Deadpool circular mask icon (Red and Black).
*   **Nav Links:** Centered at the top. 
    *   Items: "About", "Get tickets", "Videos", "Share", "#Deadpool".
    *   **Active State:** "Get tickets" has a thin red underline (#BE1E2D) and slightly bolder weight.
    *   **Font Size:** 14px, Uppercase.

## 5. Main Hero Content (Left Side)
*   **Tagline:** "#1 MOVIE IN THE WORLD" 
    *   Placement: Above the main title.
    *   Style: Small caps, spaced out (letter-spacing: 2px), dark gray.
*   **Main Title:** 
    *   "WATCH NEW" (Black)
    *   "DEADPOOL 2" (Red - #BE1E2D)
    *   "ONLINE" (Black)
    *   **Style:** Massive font size (~80pt), line-height 1.1, stacked vertically.
*   **CTA Button:** 
    *   Text: "BUY ACCESS"
    *   Shape: Pill-shaped (rounded corners).
    *   Background: White with a light gray drop shadow.
    *   Icon: A small red arrow pointing right.
*   **Vertical Nav (Far Left):** 
    *   "01" at the top.
    *   A vertical line with a red active indicator.
    *   "NEXT" written vertically at the bottom.

## 6. Central Character Image (Deadpool)
*   **Image Description:** A high-resolution, backgroundless (PNG) cutout of Deadpool.
*   **Pose:** Front-facing, arms crossed over his chest. Two katana handles are visible protruding over his shoulders.
*   **Placement:** Placed exactly in the center of the screen, overlapping the seam between the white and red backgrounds.
*   **Rotation:** The character is slightly rotated approximately 2-3 degrees counter-clockwise to create a dynamic feel.
*   **Detail:** The bottom of the image is cut off at the waist, aligned with the bottom of the viewport.

## 7. Right Sidebar (Info Panel)
*   **Top Card:** 
    *   Text: "20TH CENTURY FOX" (Small, white).
    *   Title: "Behind the scenes of Ashes with Celine Dion".
    *   Thumbnail: A small rectangular video preview showing a man (Cable) in a dark setting.
*   **Middle Stats:**
    *   **Viewer Count:** "32 078 138" in large white condensed font.
    *   **Rating:** 5 gold stars with a "4.8" numerical value next to it.
    *   **Poster:** A tiny thumbnail of the Deadpool 2 movie poster.
*   **Bottom Section ("About Film"):**
    *   Background: A dark, desaturated close-up image of Deadpool's utility belt and handgun holster.
    *   Text: "\BOUT FILM" (Stylized with a backslash).
    *   Icon: A small arrow inside a thin white circle.

## 8. Footer (Media Controls)
*   **Location:** Spans the bottom 10% of the screen.
*   **Play Button:** A solid red circle with a white "play" triangle icon, located on the far left.
*   **Progress Bar:** A thin gray horizontal line stretching across the bottom, with a white indicator showing progress.
*   **Volume Icon:** A small speaker icon located on the right side of the progress bar.

## 9. Image Asset Requirements
1.  **Deadpool Hero:** PNG, transparent background. High detail on suit texture. Centered, waist-up.
2.  **Cable Thumbnail:** 16:9 aspect ratio, low brightness, placed in the top-right red section.
3.  **Holster Detail:** Dark, low-contrast image of a tactical belt/gun, used as a background for the "About Film" button in the bottom right.
4.  **DP2 Watermark:** Large "DP2" text, light gray, 5% opacity, placed behind the hero character.
5.  **Deadpool Icon:** Circular mask logo, 40px x 40px, top left.

---
**Implementation Note:** Ensure the red sidebar uses a `mix-blend-mode` or careful layering so Deadpool’s arm appears to naturally cast a slight shadow onto the red surface.
