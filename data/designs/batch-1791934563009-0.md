# Design System Specification: Creator Profile Card

## Overview
A profile card featuring a background landscape photo, white readability overlays, avatar, bio data, stats grid, software tool icons, and a call-to-action button. Covers two layout variants: **Top** (balanced white fade, dark CTA) and **Bottom** (corner vignette, light CTA).

---

*   **Primary Font**: `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`
*   **Fallbacks**: `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica Neue`, `sans-serif`

---

## Image Assets

### 1. Landscape Backdrop
*   **Description**: Misty, cinematic view of lush green tropical mountains with cloud valleys under diffused sky lighting. Not backgroundless.
*   **Position**: Full-bleed background (`object-fit: cover`).
*   **Rotation**: `0deg`.

### 2. User Avatar
*   **Description**: Stylized 3D animated portrait of a young woman wearing a dark baseball cap and jacket. Warm background studio light inside the circle.
*   **Framing**: Circular container with a `3px` solid `#FFFFFF` border and soft drop shadow.
*   **Size**: `64px × 64px`.

### 3. Tool Logos
*   **Icons**: Notion, Webflow, WordPress, Figma.
*   **Format**: Flat monochrome for Notion/Webflow/WordPress (`#111827`); multi-color for Figma.
*   **Size**: `16px × 16px` each, with `6px` spacing.

---

## Layout Variations

### Variant 1: "Top"

1.  **Background Overlay**:
    *   Linear gradient: `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255,255,255,0) 25%, rgba(255,255,255,0.85) 60%, #FFFFFF 85%)`.
2.  **Top Right Action**:
    *   Floating circular button (`36px × 36px`), `#FFFFFF` background, subtle shadow, containing an outline bookmark icon (`18px`).
3.  **Middle Row**:
    *   Left: Avatar (`64px`), Name, Tagline, and Meta tags (`Suitcase + "Product designer"`, `Map Pin + "In the feedback loop."`).
    *   Right: Software tool icons + "Tools" text label, aligned horizontally.
4.  **Bottom Row**:
    *   Left: 3-column stats table with vertical borders:
        *   Rating: `★ 4.6` / `rating`
        *   Duration: `7 days` / `duration`
        *   Rate: `$44/hr` / `rate`
    *   Right: Dark CTA button (`background: #1F242F`, `color: #FFFFFF`, pill shape `rounded-full`, padding `12px 24px`).

---

### Variant 2: "Bottom"

1.  **Background Overlay**:
    *   Angled radial wash: `radial-gradient(circle at 15% 65%, #FFFFFF 35%, rgba(255,255,255,0.85) 55%, rgba(255,255,255,0) 80%)`.
2.  **Top Right Action**:
    *   Same floating white bookmark button.
3.  **Middle-Left Block**:
    *   Avatar, Name, Tagline, and Meta row stacked vertically over the illuminated white pocket.
    *   Stats row placed directly underneath the meta info.
4.  **Bottom Row**:
    *   Left: Tool icons + "Tools" label positioned at the bottom-left corner.
    *   Right: Light CTA button (`background: #FFFFFF`, `color: #111827`, subtle shadow, pill shape `rounded-full`, padding `12px 24px`).

---

## Card Dimensions & Radius

*   **Width**: Responsive, optimal at `520px` to `600px`.
*   **Aspect Ratio**: `~16:11`.
*   **Corner Radius**: `24px` (`overflow: hidden`).
*   **Padding**: `24px` internal safe padding.
