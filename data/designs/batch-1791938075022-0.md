# Design Specification: Marvel Character Selection Screen

## 1. Global Styles & Layout
*   **Viewport:** Full-screen (100vw, 100vh). No margins or external padding around the main container.
*   **Background:** 
    *   **Primary:** A deep radial gradient. 
    *   **Colors:** Center-bottom starts at `#163075` (Royal Blue) and fades out to `#0a1429` (Dark Navy) at the edges.
    *   **Floor Detail:** A subtle perspective grid or series of thin, vertical light-blue lines emanating from the bottom center to create a "stage" effect for the characters.
*   **Container Padding:** 5% horizontal padding, 4% vertical padding.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Heading ("CHOOSE CHARACTER"):**
    *   **Font:** *Archivo Black* (Italic) or *Impact*.
    *   **Fallback:** `Arial Black, sans-serif` (set to italic).
    *   **Styling:** Uppercase, Bold, Italic.
    *   **Letter Spacing:** -2px (tight).
    *   **Color:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
*   **Marvel Logo:**
    *   **Font:** *Universe 57 Condensed* (Bold).
    *   **Fallback:** `Impact, sans-serif`.
    *   **Color:** `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Sub-headings ("AVENGERS"):**
    *   **Font:** *Roboto* or *Inter* (Bold).
    *   **Fallback:** `sans-serif`.
    *   **Letter Spacing:** 1px.
    *   **Color:** `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Body Text:**
    *   **Font:** *Roboto* or *Inter* (Regular).
    *   **Size:** 12px - 14px.
    *   **Line Height:** 1.4.
    *   **Color:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` (Off-white/Translucent).

## 3. Header Section
*   **Position:** Top of the screen, flexbox layout (space-between).
*   **Left Element:** Hamburger Menu Icon. Three horizontal white lines of equal thickness.
*   **Center Element:** The word "MARVEL" in white.
*   **Right Element:** User Profile Icon. A simple white outline of a head and shoulders (circular head, curved base).

## 4. Main Title Section
*   **Text:** "CHOOSE CHARACTER" stacked in two lines.
*   **Alignment:** Left-aligned.
*   **Accent Line:** Directly beneath the text, there is a thin horizontal line (1px to 2px height). 
    *   **Color:** `#e23636` (Marvel Red).
    *   **Width:** Spans approximately 80% of the screen width.

## 5. Character Carousel (The Assets)
*   **General Requirement:** All images must be high-resolution PNGs with **transparent backgrounds**.
*   **Character Placement (Left to Right):**
    1.  **Black Panther:** Posed in a low, athletic crouch. Facing toward the right. Left hand touching the ground.
    2.  **Iron Man:** Standing in the Mark 85 suit. Facing slightly left but looking forward. Right arm slightly extended.
    3.  **Captain America:** Standing upright, facing forward. Holding the circular shield on his right arm.
    4.  **Hulk (Center Focus):** The largest asset. Standing tall, muscular, facing directly forward with arms slightly flared at his sides.
    5.  **Black Widow:** Standing straight, facing slightly left. Arms at her sides.
    6.  **Falcon:** Standing with mechanical wings partially deployed behind him. Facing forward/left.
    7.  **Hawkeye (Partial):** Positioned at the far right edge, partially cropped by the screen border. Holding a bow.
*   **Visual Treatment:** Characters should have a subtle drop shadow or a soft "glow" at their feet to anchor them to the floor lines.

## 6. Footer & Navigation
*   **Position:** Bottom left area.
*   **Navigation Buttons:**
    *   Two circular buttons side-by-side.
    *   **Style:** White 2px border, transparent background.
    *   **Icons:** Thin white arrows (Left `←` and Right `→`).
*   **Description Block:** Located to the right of the navigation buttons.
    *   **Title:** "AVENGERS" (Bold, White).
    *   **Paragraph:** "Earth's Mightiest Heroes stand as the planet's first line of defense against the most powerful threats in the universe."
    *   **Max-width:** 300px to ensure the text wraps into three lines.

## 7. Color Palette Summary
*   **Background Dark:** `#0a1429`
*   **Background Light:** `#163075`
*   **Accent Red:** `#e23636`
*   **Text White:** `#FFFFFF`
*   **Text Muted:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`

## 8. Interactive Elements (States)
*   **Hover State (Buttons):** The circular arrow buttons should fill with white, and the arrow icon should turn blue (`#163075`) when hovered.
*   **Character Selection:** The character currently in the center (Hulk in the mockup) should be 10-15% larger than the characters at the far edges to create a sense of depth.
