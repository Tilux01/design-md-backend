# Design Specification: NeoVision Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Layout Structure
- **Layout Type:** Full-width, single-column scrolling dark-mode landing page.
- **Background Color:** Solid Deep Black (`#0A0A0A` or `#000000`).
- **Primary Text Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for main headings and titles.
- **Secondary Text Color:** Muted Gray (`#9CA3AF`) for body copy and descriptions.
- **Accent Color:** Soft White / Light Gray for interactive borders and subtle UI highlights.

### Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** Inter (Fallback: `sans-serif`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`).
- **Typography Hierarchy:**
  - **Hero Header / Section Titles ("OUR SERVICE", "LIMITLESS POSSIBILITIES..."):** Extra-bold, uppercase where specified, tracking adjusted for modern tech aesthetic.
  - **Card Headings ("Reality Development", etc.):** Semi-bold, medium size, clear hierarchy over body text.
  - **Body Copy / Descriptions:** Regular weight, small to medium sizing (`14px` to `16px`), high line-height for readability against dark backgrounds.

---

## 2. Section-by-Section Breakdown

### A. Navigation & Hero Section
- **Top Navigation Bar:**
  - Minimalist layout.
  - Centered pill-shaped floating navigation links: **Digital**, **Reality**, **Next**.
- **Hero Content:**
  - **Subheading/Tagline:** *"Step into the Digital Frontier, where the boundaries between reality and virtual innovation disappear. This is the next era of immersive technology."*
  - **Call to Action (CTA) Buttons:**
    - Primary Button: Solid or outlined pill button labeled **"Learn More"**.
    - Secondary Action: **"Watch a Video"** accompanied by a play icon inside a circle.
- **Hero Image (Visual Asset):**
  - **Description:** A futuristic, high-contrast, monochrome (black and white) portrait of a person wearing an advanced, sleek virtual reality helmet/visor with a reflective, glossy dark faceplate and textured tactical collar/clothing.
