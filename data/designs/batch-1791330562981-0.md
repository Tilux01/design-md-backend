# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Playful Neo-Morphic Media Streaming / 3D Cartoon Landing Page (Vibrant, high-saturation, friendly entertainment platform UI).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace focus with asymmetric layout centering and large 3D character hero illustration anchored to the bottom-right.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean flat background canvas with 3D inset buttons, smooth pill badge borders, and soft drop shadows on interactive CTA elements and floating clouds.

- **Primary Brand Color & Variants**: Default `#00A2FF`, Hover `#008CE6`, Active `#0073C2`, Subdued `rgba(0, 162, 255, 0.15)`.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: 3D volumetric cartoon assets, floating cloud elements, glassmorphic pill containers, high-contrast white bold titles against sky-blue gradients.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `700`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Star Icon (★) -> Use `&#9733;` or inline SVG star.
  - Play Icon (▶) -> Use `&#9654;` or inline SVG play triangle.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-white inline-block"></span>`.
