# UI/UX Specification & Replication Blueprint: Zentora Agency Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Document Setup
- **View:** Full-screen edge-to-edge layout (`100vw`, continuous vertical scrolling canvas).
- **Base Color Scheme:** Deep obsidian black (`#000000` to `#050505`) backdrop establishing a futuristic, immersive cyberpunk/high-end tech agency aesthetic.
- **Accents:** Electric blue glows (`#0055ff`, `#0099ff`), stark high-contrast white text (`#ffffff`), and subtle neutral grays (`#888888`, `#333333`) for secondary elements, borders, and tags.

---

## 2. Typography Strategy & Fallbacks
The design relies on ultra-clean, modern geometric sans-serif typefaces with massive scale contrasts (hyper-bold hero statements paired with minuscule technical metadata tags).

1. **Primary Display Font (Hero Headings & Branding):**
   - *Target:* **Syne** or **Clash Display** (Ultra-Bold / Extra-Bold weights).
   - *Characteristics:* High visual impact, architectural form, slight futuristic curvature.
   - *CSS Fallback Stack:* `font-family: 'Syne', 'Clash Display', 'Helvetica Neue', Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;`

2. **Secondary UI & Body Font (Navigation, Metadata, Subheaders):**
   - *Target:* **Inter** or **Plus Jakarta Sans** (Light, Regular, Medium, Semi-Bold weights).
   - *Characteristics:* Highly legible at micro-sizes (e.g., `#UserFirst` tags rendered at 10-12px), crisp rendering on dark backgrounds.
   - *CSS Fallback Stack:* `font-family: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif;`

---

## 4. Interaction & State Notes (For Developers)
- **Buttons & Links:** All primary action pills (`CONTACT US`, `BOOK A CALL`, `LET'S TALK`) feature micro-interactions (hover state: background color inversion or scale-up on the circular arrow icon).
- **Images:** All embedded imagery (cyber-samurai render, project thumbnails) must be optimized WebP/PNG assets with transparent or deep-black matching backgrounds to preserve the seamless obsidian aesthetic.
