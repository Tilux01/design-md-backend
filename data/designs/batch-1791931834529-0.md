# Design Specification: Ocean Wave Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Layout Strategy
* **Layout**: Full-screen 100vh/100vw single-view hero section (no scroll).
* **Grid/Structure**: Two-column layout over an absolute full-bleed background.
  * **Left Column**: Branding/accent icon, secondary title, description, ghost CTA button.
  * **Right Column**: Massive vertical text stack, pagination counter.
  * **Header**: Top-right horizontal navigation.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Deep Ocean Teal (Background base)**: `#0B3B49` to `#06242E`
* **Foam White**: `#FFFFFF`
* **Muted Aqua Text**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`
* **Active Accent / Underline**: `#FFFFFF`

---

## 3. Typography & Fallbacks
* **Primary Display & Headings**: 
  * *Font*: `Montserrat`, `Oswald`, or `Arial Black`
  * *Fallback*: `Impact`, `Helvetica Neue`, `sans-serif`
* **Body & Navigation**:
  * *Font*: `Inter`, `Poppins`, or `system-ui`
  * *Fallback*: `Arial`, `sans-serif`

---

## 4. Assets & Imagery
* **Background Image**:
  * **Type**: Full-bleed photographic image (not backgroundless).
  * **Description**: Aerial drone view looking directly down onto deep teal-blue ocean water. A heavy band of churning, frothy white wave foam runs vertically down the left edge, fading into calmer deep water toward the right.
  * **Styling**: `width: 100vw; height: 100vh; object-fit: cover; object-position: center;`.
* **Masked Text Texture**:
  * **Description**: Frothy seafoam/water spray texture used as an internal clipping mask for the large display text on the right.

---

## 5. Navigation Bar
* **Position**: Top-right, fixed/absolute (`top: 40px; right: 60px; z-index: 10`).
* **Display**: Horizontal flexbox (`gap: 32px; list-style: none;`).
* **Links**:
  1. **HOME**: Bold, uppercase, white, small underline bar positioned ~6px below.
  2. **ABOUT**: Uppercase, opacity `0.75`.
  3. **PRODUCTS**: Uppercase, opacity `0.75`.
  4. **SERVICE**: Uppercase, opacity `0.75`.
  5. **CONTACT**: Uppercase, opacity `0.75`.
* **Specs**: Font size `12px`, letter spacing `1.5px`, font weight `600`.

---

## 6. Left Content Section
* **Placement**: Vertically centered, positioned left of center (`left: 20%`, `top: 55%`, `transform: translateY(-50%)`).
* **Elements**:
  * **Accent Icon**: Two small diagonal parallel rectangles tilted at ~45 degrees, colored white.
  * **Title**:
    * Text: `"Happiness\ncomes\nin waves"`
    * Styling: `font-size: 36px`, `line-height: 1.15`, `font-weight: 700`, color `#FFFFFF`.
  * **Body Paragraph**:
    * Text: 2–3 lines of descriptive text.
    * Styling: `font-size: 13px`, `line-height: 1.6`, color `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`, max-width `260px`, margin `16px 0 24px 0`.
  * **Button**:
    * Text: `"PLAN NOW"`
    * Style: Ghost button; `border: 1px solid #FFFFFF`, `border-radius: 4px`, `background: transparent`, `color: #FFFFFF`, padding `8px 22px`, font size `11px`, tracking `1.5px`, uppercase, bold.

---

## 7. Right Hero Typography
* **Placement**: Center-right (`right: 8%`, `top: 50%`, `transform: translateY(-50%)`).
* **Text**:
  ```text
  FLOW
  WITH
  IT
  ```
* **Styling**:
  * **Font Size**: `~120px–140px` (or `10vw`).
  * **Font Weight**: `900` (Ultra-Bold / Black).
  * **Line Height**: `0.85`.
  * **Fill**: Masked with white wave foam texture (`background-clip: text; -webkit-background-clip: text; color: transparent; opacity: 0.9;`).
  * **Alignment**: Left-aligned stack.

---

## 8. Pagination Indicator
* **Placement**: Bottom right (`bottom: 40px`, `right: 8%`).
* **Content**: `"01 / 03"`
* **Styling**:
  * `"01"`: `font-size: 24px`, `font-weight: 700`, color `#FFFFFF`.
  * `"/ 03"`: `font-size: 14px`, `font-weight: 400`, color `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`.
