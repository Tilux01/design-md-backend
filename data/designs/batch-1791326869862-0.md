# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: High-Performance Tech / Futuristic Automotive Dark Mode Glassmorphism.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High visual impact centered around a hero product focal point; spacious outer container padding with dense, information-rich metric callouts and glassmorphic micro-cards.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-tier elevation utilizing subtle backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), translucent dark gradients, glowing electric-blue accent borders (`rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.4)`), and radial ambient illumination orbs.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient radial glow orbs, backdrop blur frosted glass, floating technical telemetry counters.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `4px 12px`, Font-size: `11px`, Font-weight: `600`, Tracking: `0.08em`, Border-radius: `9999px`, Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-cyan-400 inline-block"></span>`.
