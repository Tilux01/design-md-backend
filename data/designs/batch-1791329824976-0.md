# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Futuristic Minimalist Glassmorphism with Liquid Metallic 3D Aesthetics. Clean enterprise-grade SaaS landing page.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio focused on centering attention around the central 3D liquid metal sphere and floating navigation header.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered translucent surfaces featuring glassmorphism backdrop blur (`blur-md`), hairline borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), and smooth floating pills.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill navigation, ambient liquid surface ripples, centered glassmorphism branding.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `8px 24px`, font-size `14px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
