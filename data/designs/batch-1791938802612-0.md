# Design Specification: "Tinecoo" Media Streaming Dashboard

## 1. General Overview
*   **Theme:** Dark-mode, modern, cinematic.
*   **Layout:** Fixed-width left sidebar with a fluid main content area.
*   **Visual Style:** Glassmorphism (frosted glass) effects, high-contrast yellow accents, and rounded corners (approx. `12px` to `20px` for containers).
*   **Background:** Full-screen hero image with a dark linear gradient overlay (bottom-to-top and left-to-right) to ensure text legibility.

---

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Montserrat` or `Poppins` (Geometric Sans-Serif).
*   **Secondary/Body Font:** `Inter` or `Roboto`.
*   **Fallbacks:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.

### Text Styles:
*   **Hero Title:** Font-size: `48px`, Weight: `700` (Bold), Color: `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Section Headers ("Popular"):** Font-size: `24px`, Weight: `600`, Color: `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Sidebar Labels:** Font-size: `14px`, Weight: `500`, Color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`.
*   **Body Text/Description:** Font-size: `16px`, Weight: `400`, Line-height: `1.5`, Color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`.
*   **Poster Titles:** Font-size: `12px`, Weight: `600`, Color: `#FFFFFF`.

---

## 3. Color Palette
*   **Primary Accent (Yellow):** `#FFD200` (Used for active states, primary buttons, and logo).
*   **Background (Deep):** `#0A0A0A` (Base layer).
*   **Sidebar/Overlay:** `rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.6)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.
*   **Text (Primary):** `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Text (Secondary):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.

---

## 4. Layout Structure

### A. Left Sidebar (Navigation)
*   **Width:** `240px`.
*   **Background:** Semi-transparent dark blur.
*   **Logo:** Top-left, "Tinecoo" in Yellow bold text.
*   **Active Indicator:** A solid yellow vertical bar (`4px` wide) on the far left of the active menu item.
*   **Menu Items:** Vertical list with icons (approx. `20px` size) followed by text.
    *   *Items:* Home (Active), Movies, Favorites, Categories, About, Settings (at the bottom).
*   **Active State:** Text and icon turn white; background of the item has a subtle yellow tint or highlight.

### B. Top Header
*   **Search Bar:** Located at the top right. A pill-shaped input with a glass effect.
*   **User Profile:** Round avatar icon at the far top right, next to a yellow notification/status dot.

### C. Hero Section (Feature Content)
*   **Placement:** Upper-middle section, left-aligned.
*   **Content:**
    *   **Title:** "The Garfiled Movie 2024" (Note: Keep the typo "Garfiled" if a literal clone is required).
    *   **Description:** 3-4 lines of text describing the plot.
    *   **Buttons:** 
        1.  **Watch Now:** Solid Yellow pill-shaped button with black text.
        2.  **Play Icon:** A circular button with a white border and a "Play" triangle icon inside, placed to the right of the "Watch Now" button.

### D. "Popular" Gallery Row
*   **Header:** "Popular" text with two small circular navigation arrows (Left/Right) on the far right of the row.
*   **Poster Cards:**
    *   **Aspect Ratio:** `2:3` (Standard movie poster ratio).
    *   **Border Radius:** `12px`.
    *   **Hover Effect:** Slight scale-up (`scale(1.05)`) and increase in brightness.
    *   **Caption:** Title of the movie placed directly below each poster.

---

## 5. Image Descriptions & Placement

### Hero Background Image
*   **Subject:** A high-definition close-up of Garfield (the orange cat) on the left and Odie (the yellow dog) on the right.
*   **Composition:** Garfield is looking slightly downward/sideways with a grumpy expression. Odie is looking towards Garfield with a curious/happy expression.
*   **Placement:** Full-screen background.
*   **Rotation:** `0 degrees`.
*   **Transparency:** No transparency on the image itself, but covered by a `linear-gradient(to right, rgba(0,0,0,0.9) 20%, rgba(0,0,0,0) 80%)`.

### Movie Posters (The Row)
1.  **Poster 1 (Active):** "The Garfield Movie" - Orange background, close up of Garfield's face.
2.  **Poster 2:** "Despicable Me 4" - Purple/Blue background featuring a Minion.
3.  **Poster 3:** "Spider-Man" - Blue/Red background featuring Spider-Man in an action pose.
4.  **Poster 4:** "HOME" - Bright orange background with the character "Oh" (purple alien).
5.  **Poster 5:** "Little Mermaid" - Deep blue underwater scene.
6.  **Poster 6:** "IF" - Purple background with a large furry creature.
7.  **Poster 7:** "Kung Fu Panda 4" - Green lush background with Po in a fighting stance.
8.  **Poster 8:** "Madagascar" - Jungle background with the main animal cast.

*Note: All posters should have the background removed from their container and be placed inside the `12px` rounded-corner card frames.*

---

## 6. Interactive Elements
*   **Buttons:** Should have a `transition: all 0.3s ease`.
*   **Sidebar Items:** Should have a `cursor: pointer` and a subtle background color change on hover.
*   **Scrollbar:** Hidden or styled as a very thin, dark-grey line to maintain the clean aesthetic.
