# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise SaaS & Financial CRM Dashboard, featuring high-contrast dark spatial styling with glowing lime accents and glassmorphism.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-density operational view balanced with generous inner card padding (16px - 24px) and rounded container geometry.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered depth architecture. Surface 0 (Deep Black Canvas `#000000`), Surface 1 (Light Neutral App Frame `#E6E6E6`), Surface 2 (White Card Containers `#FFFFFF`), and Surface 3 (Floating Interactive Dock `#0D0D0D`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-contrast dark floating navigation dock, vibrant lime highlight cards (`#C6FF36`), avatar stacks, and clean rounded card geometry (`rounded-3xl`).
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `4px 12px`, font-size: `0.75rem`, font-weight: `600`, tracking: `0.05em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `rgba(198, 255, 54, 0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-accent inline-block"></span>`
