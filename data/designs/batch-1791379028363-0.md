# UI/UX Specification & Design Document: Astro Winter Armor II E-Commerce Page

This document provides a comprehensive blueprint for building a full-screen, responsive, pixel-perfect clone of the **Astro Winter Armor II** product page. 

---

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Properties

*   **View Style:** Full-screen immersive viewport (100vw × 100vh), eliminating any outer decorative frames, mockups, or dropshadow wrappers.
*   **Grid System:** 12-column layout with a generous max-width container (`max-width: 1440px`) centered horizontally.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   `--bg-primary`: `#F2ECE4` (Warm vintage off-white / parchment texture base)
    *   `--bg-card`: `#FFFFFF`
    *   `--text-main`: `#1A1A1A` (Near black for high-legibility headings)
    *   `--text-muted`: `#666666` (Subdued body copy)
    *   `--accent-orange`: `#E65F2B` (Vibrant tactical orange for highlights, active states, and CTAs)
    *   `--border-color`: `#D9D0C7` (Soft grey-beige structural lines)
*   **Typography Stack:**
    *   *Primary Heading Font:* **Syne** or **Space Grotesk** (Fallback: `sans-serif`) – Geometric, futuristic display sans.
    *   *Body Copy Font:* **Inter** or **Plus Jakarta Sans** (Fallback: `system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`) – Clean, highly legible UI sans.

*   **Position:** Sticky top, full width, border-bottom separator (`1px solid var(--border-color)`).

## 5. Micro-Interactions & Animations

*   **Color Swatch Selection:** Smooth state change where clicking an alternate color swatch morphs the central jacket image via a soft crossfade transition.
*   **Size Interaction:** Clicking sizing pills (`S`, `M`, `L`, `XL`) instantly updates the active state styling (filling background with `--accent-orange` and turning text white).
*   **Add to Bag Feedback:** On-click scaling effect (`transform: scale(0.98)` active state) with potential micro-loading spinner or checkmark transition.
*   **Accordions:** Smooth CSS height transition (`max-height` toggle) when expanding `LOREM IPSUM DOLOR` dropdowns.
*   **Hover States:** Subtle parallax or lift effect on the central product imagery on desktop viewports.
