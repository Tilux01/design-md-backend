# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Glassmorphism & High-Contrast Neomorphic Minimalism.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High contrast with compact card padding, centered interactive zones, balanced margins, and focused visual hierarchy emphasizing travel metrics.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-layered dark surface architecture utilizing absolute dark backgrounds (`#0A0A0A`), semi-translucent card layers (`#141414` with 80% opacity), subtle 1px border strokes (`#262626`), and soft inner glow highlights.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-contrast dark background, lime-green neon accent points, pill-shaped UI toggles, blurred photo-mask card containers.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#1E1E1E`, border `1px solid #2A2A2A`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> `&mdash;`
  - Airplane Icon (✈) -> `<svg>` inline asset
  - Status Dot (●) -> `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-[#A3E635] inline-block"></span>`.
