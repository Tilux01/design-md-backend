# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Web3 Financial SaaS, Futuristic Crypto-Platform, Immersive Spatial 3D UI.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-contrast sparse layout, expansive asymmetric spacing, absolute-positioned floating 3D assets juxtaposed against a deep immersive canvas.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered obsidian carbon surfaces featuring subtle inner borders, volumetric neon purple-to-magenta radial glow effects, and blurred backdrop glassmorphism containers.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient neon purple glow orbs, isometric crypto token rendering with neon rims, glassmorphic floating CTA pills, high-contrast dark spatial environment.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `6px 14px`, font-size: `12px`, font-weight: `500`, tracking: `0.05em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.1)`, border: `1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-purple-500 inline-block shadow-glow"></span>`.
