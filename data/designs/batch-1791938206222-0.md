# Design Specification: VC Horror Streaming Interface

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Background:** The entire application should have a deep charcoal/black background. The primary visual layer is a full-screen cinematic image (see Section 4).
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Accent:** `#E89C5E` (Muted Orange/Amber) - Used for the "2", Play button, and "Add to favorites" button.
    *   **Text (Primary):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
    *   **Text (Secondary/Metadata):** `#D1D1D1` (Light Grey).
    *   **Overlays:** Semi-transparent black gradients (`rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`) to ensure text readability over the background image.
*   **Border Radius:** 12px for buttons and 20px for container cards (like the "What's Next" section).

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Heading (Title):** 
    *   *Font:* Impact, Oswald, or a similar bold, condensed sans-serif.
    *   *Fallback:* `sans-serif-condensed`, `Arial Narrow`.
    *   *Style:* All caps, tight letter spacing, white color.
*   **Secondary Heading (Logo/Navigation):**
    *   *Font:* Montserrat or Inter.
    *   *Fallback:* `sans-serif`.
*   **Body Text (Description):**
    *   *Font:* Roboto or Open Sans.
    *   *Fallback:* `sans-serif`.
    *   *Size:* 14px - 16px.
*   **The "2" Graphic:**
    *   *Font:* Custom brush-stroke font (e.g., "Road Rage" or "Brush Script").
    *   *Fallback:* Hand-written style script.

## 3. Layout Structure

### A. Top Navigation Bar
*   **Logo (Left):** "VC" in a stylized, sharp-edged white font.
*   **Menu Links (Center-Left):** "Home", "Category", "Series", "Movies". White text, approx 14px, medium weight.
*   **Brand Mark (Center):** The word "HORROR" in a stylized, jagged, "bleeding" or "slasher" style font, centered horizontally.
*   **Search Bar (Right):** 
    *   Pill-shaped (rounded ends) with a thin white border.
    *   Placeholder text: "Search your episode..."
    *   Icon: Magnifying glass icon on the right side of the pill.
*   **User Profile (Far Right):** A circular outline icon containing a simplified person silhouette.

### B. Hero Section (Main Content)
*   **Left Column:**
    *   **Label:** "HORROR SERIE:" in small, white, all-caps sans-serif.
    *   **Title:** "THE SKIN MAKER" stacked vertically.
    *   **The "2":** A large, orange brush-stroke "2" layered behind the word "MAKER" but in front of "SKIN". It is rotated roughly 15 degrees counter-clockwise.
    *   **Button:** "Add to favorites" button below the title. It features a hollow star icon and a solid orange background with black text.
*   **Right Column:**
    *   **Tagline:** "DON'T COME TO HIM!" in bold white caps.
    *   **Metadata:** "2023 | +18 | 1h 27min | Horror". The "+18" is contained in a small orange square.
    *   **Description:** A paragraph of white text describing the plot.
    *   **Action Buttons:**
        *   **Play:** Solid orange rectangle with a black play triangle icon and the word "PLAY".
        *   **Watch Trailer:** Transparent background with a thick white border and white text "WATCH TRILER" (Note: maintain the original's typo "TRILER" if an exact clone is desired).

### C. Bottom UI Elements
*   **Footer (Left):** "© 2023 WolvesClub Studio. All rights reserved." in tiny grey text.
*   **Social Icons (Center):** Small white icons for Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube.
*   **"What's Next" Section (Bottom Right):**
    *   A floating dark-glass container (semi-transparent blur).
    *   **Header:** "WHAT'S NEXT?" in white caps.
    *   **Thumbnails:** Two horizontal thumbnails with rounded corners. 
        *   *Thumbnail 1:* A dark, misty forest scene.
        *   *Thumbnail 2:* A person in a cave-like setting looking up.
    *   **Close Button:** A small white "X" in a circle sits just above the left corner of this container.

## 4. Image Descriptions & Placement

### Main Background Image (The Hero)
*   **Description:** A high-resolution cinematic shot of a horror setting. A man sits centrally, facing the camera. He wears a pale, stitched-together leather mask (resembling a skull/skin), a light grey button-down shirt, and dark suspenders. He is in a cluttered workshop or basement filled with dozens of lit white pillar candles of varying heights. The lighting is warm from the candles but surrounded by deep, vignetted shadows.
*   **Placement:** Full screen, centered on the character.
*   **Background Status:** This is a full-bleed photographic background (not backgroundless).

### The "2" Graphic
*   **Description:** A thick, textured orange brush stroke forming the number "2". It has "dry brush" effects at the edges.
*   **Placement:** Positioned behind the main title text.
*   **Background Status:** Backgroundless (PNG/SVG) to allow the background image and text to layer correctly.
*   **Rotation:** Rotated ~15° counter-clockwise.

### Thumbnail Images (What's Next)
*   **Thumbnail 1:** Dark, desaturated image of a foggy woods. Backgroundless: No (framed within the UI).
*   **Thumbnail 2:** A character in a dark brown environment reaching toward a light source. Backgroundless: No (framed within the UI).

## 5. Interaction Details
*   **Hover States:** Navigation links should increase in brightness or show a small orange dot underneath.
*   **Buttons:** The "Play" button should have a slight scale-up effect (1.05x) on hover.
*   **Search Bar:** Should expand slightly or glow when focused.
