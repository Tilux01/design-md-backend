# Design Specification: Organic Kohaku Water Product Page

## 1. Global Setup & Color Palette

*   **Background Base:** Fullscreen solid dark charcoal `#131417`
*   **Primary Text:** Pure White `#FFFFFF`
*   **Secondary / Muted Text:** Slate Gray `#7E828A`
*   **Accent Colors:**
    *   Neon Mint Green: `#3EE8B5` (hand-drawn circle, arrows)
    *   Coral Orange: `#FF5745` (lightning badge)
    *   Arch Backdrop: Muted Slate Cyan `#7F9398`
    *   Card / Pill Dark Background: `#1F2127` (with subtle border `#2C2E36`)

---

## 2. Typography

*   **Primary Display / Headings:** *Plus Jakarta Sans* or *Clash Display* (Fallback: `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif`)
    *   Main Product Title: `700` Bold, ~48px.
    *   Price: `700` Bold, ~32px.
*   **Body & Navigation:** *Plus Jakarta Sans* or *Inter* (Fallback: `sans-serif`)
    *   Nav Items: `500` Medium, 14px.
    *   Body Description: `400` Regular, 14px, line-height 1.5.
    *   Accordion Headers: `600` Semi-Bold, 18px.

---

## 3. Layout Structure

Full-screen 3-column grid inside a top-level flex container with generous padding (`40px 60px`).

### 3.1. Top Navigation Bar
*   **Left Section:** Horizontal links: `Category`, `Lifestyle`, `Books` (White, 14px, spaced 32px apart).
*   **Center Section:**
    *   A pill-shaped theme toggle switch (dark base, white active knob).
    *   A subtle, large decorative thin circular stroke background intersecting the top border.
*   **Right Section:**
    *   Heart icon (outlined, white).
    *   Shopping bag icon with red badge indicator showing count `2`.
    *   Circular avatar/profile icon with an abstract geometric blue motif.

---

### 3.2. Main Content (Three Columns)

#### Column 1: Product Details (Left, ~30% width)
*   **Headline Unit:**
    *   Line 1: "Organic" enclosed in a sketchy neon mint oval stroke.
    *   Line 2: "kohaku" followed by a neon mint diagonal arrow (`↗`).
    *   Line 3: "water" followed by a circular coral badge containing a white lightning bolt.
*   **Price:** `£39.00` placed directly below headline with `24px` spacing.
*   **Flavor Description:** "Fresh, tangy and zesty lime with refreshing minty notes" (Muted Gray `#7E828A`, 14px).
*   **Action Row:**
    *   "Add to cart" Button: Full pill shape, solid white background `#FFFFFF`, black text, checkmark icon prefix (`✓`).
    *   Favorite Button: Circular outline button with white heart icon.

---

#### Column 2: Center Showcase & Imagery (Center, ~40% width)
*   **Backdrop:** Tall vertical arch (rounded top, flat bottom) filled with `#7F9398`.
*   **Hero Image (Bottle):**
    *   *Description:* High-resolution 3D render of a matte, chalk-white reusable water bottle with a textured dark-charcoal cap.
    *   *Asset Specs:* Isolated/backgroundless PNG, centered vertically and overlapping the arch.
    *   *Decorative Overlays:*
        *   White 3D spiral line looping around the bottle's neck.
        *   Floating blurred white spheres (depth-of-field effect) on the left and right sides.
*   **Social Activity Popups (Floating Pills):**
    *   *Top-Right Pill:* Dark pill container containing a circular user avatar (`Kohaku`), text "Kohaku has liked this item", and a floating red heart badge.
    *   *Bottom-Left Pill:* Dark pill container containing a circular user avatar (`Moyo Shiro`), text "Moyo Shiro has just purchased", and a green lock/purchase badge.
*   **Scroll Indicator:**
    *   Centered at the bottom base of the bottle.
    *   Circular outline with a downward arrow (`↓`).

---

#### Column 3: Specs & Accordion (Right, ~30% width)
*   **Top Navigation:** Carousel controls with left arrow (`←`) and right arrow (`→`).
*   **Accordion List:**
    1.  **Description:** Header with `+` expandable icon, divider line below.
    2.  **Nutrition:** Header with `+` expandable icon, divider line below.
    3.  **Ingredients (Expanded State):**
        *   Header with `—` collapse icon.
        *   Content text: "Fresh, tangy and zesty lime with refreshing minty notes" (Muted gray, 13px).

---

## 4. Asset Summary

1.  **Bottle Image:** Centerpiece, backgroundless transparent PNG, matte white cylindrical bottle with textured cap.
2.  **Avatars:** Two 32x32px circular portrait photos for floating feedback pills.
3.  **Vector Icons:** Heart, Shopping Bag with notification badge, Lightning bolt, Navigation arrows (`←`, `→`, `↓`), Accordion triggers (`+`, `—`).
