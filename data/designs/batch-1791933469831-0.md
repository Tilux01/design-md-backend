# Design Specification: Darwin Hero Section

## 1. Typography & Hierarchy

*   **Primary Font:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter` (Fallback: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`)
*   **Headline (H1):**
    *   Text: "Hi, I'm Darwin —\nyour new BDR colleague"
    *   Weight: 600 (Semi-Bold) | Size: ~56px–64px | Line-height: 1.15 | Tracking: -0.03em
    *   Color: `#111827` (Near Black)
*   **Subheadline:**
    *   Text: "Darwin will continuously source leads, craft personalized\n& persuasive messages that book meetings with buyers."
    *   Weight: 400 (Regular) | Size: ~18px | Line-height: 1.5 | Max-width: 580px
    *   Color: `#6B7280` (Muted Slate)
*   **Navigation & Microcopy:**
    *   Weight: 500 (Medium) | Size: 13px–14px | Color: `#374151`

---

## 2. Color Palette & Background

*   **Page Background:**
    *   Base: `#FFFFFF`
    *   Top Left Radial Gradient: `#FCE7F3` (Pale Pink / Lavender, blur: 120px)
    *   Center / Right Ambient Gradient: `#E0F2FE` (Soft Cyan/Sky Blue, blur: 140px)
*   **Grid Backdrop (Behind Hero Image):**
    *   Subtle white/light-blue segmented grid lines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`).
    *   Diamond-shaped accent glyphs at select intersections (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).
*   **Primary CTA Button:**
    *   Gradient Fill: Linear from `#2D3A54` (top) to `#1D263B` (bottom)
    *   Inner Highlight: 1px top border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`
    *   Text Color: `#FFFFFF`

*   **Container:** Centered floating pill; background `#FFFFFF`; border `1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`; shadow `0 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
*   **Padding & Radius:** `8px 24px` | Border Radius: `9999px`.
*   **Items (Left to Right):**
    *   `Darwin` (Bold, 600 weight)
    *   `Product`, `Features`, `Pricing`, `About` (Gap: 24px)

## 5. Main Subject Image

*   **Subject Description:** Young Caucasian male with wavy dark brown hair, smiling broadly. Wearing an unbuttoned white collared shirt under a light beige/oatmeal zip-up fleece vest with a small purple chest logo patch. Both hands lightly holding the vest lapels.
*   **Format:** Cutout portrait (100% transparent background, PNG/WebP).
*   **Placement:** Centered horizontally, anchored to the bottom screen edge.
*   **Z-Index:** Layered above the background grid and beneath floating overlay cards.
