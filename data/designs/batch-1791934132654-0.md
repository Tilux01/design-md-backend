# Design Specification: "Choose Your Best Happy Land" Real Estate Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Layout Architecture
- **Canvas**: Full viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), no external mockup frame.
- **Color Scheme**: 
  - Primary Dark: `#1B1E24` (Deep charcoal/navy)
  - Surface Accent: `#2B303A` (Translucent glass card)
  - Primary Accent: `#5D45D8` (Vibrant purple for CTA and badges)
  - Coral Accent: `#E58B79` (Peach badge)
  - Light Ground: `#FFFFFF` (Bottom section)
  - Text: White (`#FFFFFF`), Muted Slate (`#9CA3AF`), Dark Charcoal (`#111827`)
- **Structure**:
  - **Upper Section (75% height)**: Dark theme containing navigation, hero text, search bar, floating cards, and skyscraper background image.
  - **Bottom Section (25% height)**: Light theme containing a floating property card on the left and client logos on the right, integrated via a curved mask/asymmetric cut-out.

---

## 2. Typography
- **Primary Font**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, fallback to `Inter`, `system-ui`, `sans-serif`.
- **Weights & Sizes**:
  - Nav Links: 14px, Regular (400) / Semi-bold (600)
  - Hero Headline: 52px, Bold (700), line-height 1.15
  - Subheadings / Card Titles: 18px–20px, Semi-bold (600)
  - Body / Captions: 12px–14px, Regular (400)

---

## 3. Header Navigation
- **Left**: Minimalist square logo with rounded corners (`1px solid #4B5563`), containing stacked text `"My / Home"`.
- **Center**: Navigation items (`Home`, `Properties`, `Members`, `Pages`, `Blogs`). `Home` features a small horizontal indicator dot/line underneath.
- **Right**: Text link `Log In` and pill button `Sign Up` (transparent background, thin white border, rounded-full).

---

## 4. Hero Section (Dark Area)

### Left Column:
1. **3D Asset Badge**:
   - Small purple rounded rectangle (`#5D45D8`).
   - *Image Description*: Backgroundless 3D render of a tilted white matte cylinder with a bright orange sphere resting on top. Placed center-aligned within the badge.
2. **Headline**: `"Choose Your / Best Happy Land"` in stark white.
3. **Sub-copy**: `"Real Estate & Properties For Sale Or Rent / In 12+ Country"` in soft muted grey.
4. **Search Filter Bar**:
   - Floating white horizontal card with fully rounded pill corners.
   - Column 1: House icon, label `"Property type"`, value `"Show all ▾"`.
   - Column 2: Tag icon, label `"Price range"`, value `"Any price ▾"`.
   - Action: Rounded purple button (`#5D45D8`) with centered text `"Search"`.
5. **Quick Filters**: Small white-pill toggle triggers beneath search bar: `"Buy a home ▾"` and `"Rent a home ▾"`.

### Right Column:
1. **Background Architecture Graphic**:
   - *Image Description*: Dramatic low-angle architectural perspective looking up at a modern dark glass-and-steel skyscraper against an overcast moody sky. Integrated seamlessly into the right side with soft gradient falloff to the dark background.
2. **Pill Badge**:
   - Coral/peach background (`#E58B79`), rotated slightly or rounded pill frame labeled `"Our Special / Service"`.
3. **Floating Service Cards**:
   - Semi-transparent dark frosted glass (`rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`, `backdrop-blur: 16px`, rounded corners).
   - **Card 1 ("Comfortable")**: Small neon green circular icon with megaphone, bold title `"Comfortable"`, description listing channels (`Facebook Ads, Google Ads, Linkedin Ads,`).
   - **Card 2 ("Luxury")**: Subtle muted green building icon, bold title `"Luxury"`, description (`Instagram Captions...`).
4. **Footer Micro-text**: Small contact text: `"Give us a Call 1-888-498-9240 and we can set you up, or check our pricing plans"`.

---

## 5. Bottom Section (White Ribbon)
The dark background forms a smooth, rounded inverted scallop cutout on the bottom-left, revealing the white base section.

### Left Floating Card (Property Preview):
- Elevated card containing:
  - *Image Description*: Thumbnail photo of a modern cubic two-story luxury villa with warm interior lighting and warm exterior accents; no rotation; cropped inside a rounded rectangle.
  - Details: Location pin with `"Jakarta Barat, Indonesia"`.
  - Specs: Bed icon `"4 bed"`, area icon `"10x10 m"`.
  - Price: Bold text `"$5,200,000"`.
  - CTA: Small light-green pill button `"Book Now"`.

### Right Brand Logos:
- Uniform rounded light-grey pill panels (`#F3F4F6`), each housing a vector monochrome logo:
  1. **Airbnb**
  2. **Cisco**
  3. **eBay**
  4. **Microsoft**
