# Design Specification: Japan Tours Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Color Palette
- **Primary / Background Dark:** `#0E0E0E` (Deep charcoal/black used for the main content areas)
- **Primary Text:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for main headings and titles)
- **Secondary Text:** `#B0B0B0` (Muted gray for body text, descriptions, and metadata)
- **Accent Highlight:** `#E2C392` (Soft warm gold used for specific highlighted text like "Osaka, Kyoto, and Tokyo" and general branding warmth)
- **UI Elements / Cards Border:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` (Subtle white borders with transparency for cards and form fields)
- **Form/Card Background:** `rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.6)` (Semi-transparent dark overlay for background blending)

### Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** Clean, geometric Sans-Serif (Recommended Google Fonts fallback stack: `Montserrat`, `Inter`, `sans-serif`)
- **Heading Styles:**
  - Hero Title ("JAPAN"): Extra-bold, uppercase, ultra-large tracking (letter-spacing), semi-transparent white gradient blend over background landscape.
  - Section Titles ("ABOUT THE TOUR", "WHAT'S INCLUDED"): Medium/Bold weight, uppercase, flanked by subtle horizontal rule separators.
- **Body Styles:** Regular weight, clean legibility at small scale (`14px` - `16px`), line height `1.5`.

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Sections

### Navigation Bar (Header)
- **Position:** Fixed / Absolute at the top of the viewport.
- **Left:** Globe icon + "JAPAN TOURS" (White, uppercase, bold).
- **Center:** Navigation links (`About`, `Included`, `Contacts`) in light gray/white.
- **Right:** 
  - "Book" button: Pill-shaped, outlined with a thin white border, transparent background, white text.
  - Social media icon stack (Instagram, Facebook/Messenger, Telegram) arranged vertically on the far right edge of the hero area.

---

### Section 1: Hero Section
- **Background:** High-resolution landscape photo of a Japanese sunrise/sunset over misty mountains, cherry blossoms in full bloom on the right, and a traditional Japanese pagoda silhouetted.
- **Overlay:** Subtle vertical gradient fading into the dark `#0E0E0E` section below.
- **Hero Title:** Giant typography spelling "JAPAN" centered horizontally across the landscape.
- **Feature Cards Row (Floating carousel / horizontal list):**
  - Five distinct portrait-oriented rectangular cards showcasing trip highlights with dark semi-transparent backdrops and bottom-aligned white text:
    1. `3 cities in Japan` (with a photo of a Tokyo street/pagoda)
    2. `10 days` (with misty mountain landscape)
    3. `gigabytes of photos` (with pagoda view)
    4. `eat ramen` (with a cozy indoor ramen shop scene)
    5. `enjoy the vibe` (with atmospheric evening alleyway)
- **Call-to-Action:** A solid/semi-transparent "Book" button placed just below the rightmost card, near the figure of a woman wearing a traditional kimono looking out over the landscape.
