# Design Specification: Multi-Style Hero Concepts

This document outlines the visual structure, layout, typography, and asset requirements to clone the three portfolio landing designs shown in the reference.

---

## 1. Design Variant 1: "Lilien" (High-Contrast Editorial Red)

### Layout & Background
* **Background**: Vibrant radial gradient transitioning from intense bright orange (`#FF4D00`) at the top right to deep crimson red (`#8A0B00`) at the bottom.
* **Layout Structure**: 3-column asymmetric layout with centered overlapping typography.

### Typography & Fonts
* **Primary Display (Heading)**: High-contrast transitional serif (e.g., *Canela*, *Ogg*, fallback: *Playfair Display*, *Georgia*, serif).
  * Text: `"Let's Create / Portraits That / Tell Your Story"`
  * Style: Regular weight, white, left-aligned.
* **Large Signature Logo**: Custom 70s-style fluid script lettering (`"Lilien"`).
  * Layering: Sandwiched between portrait layers; solid white fill on outer letters, thin white outline (`1.5px`) where overlapping the subject.
* **Body / Meta Text**: Clean neo-grotesque sans-serif (e.g., *Neue Haas Grotesk*, fallback: *Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*, sans-serif).
  * White, opacity `0.85`, right-aligned text block.

### Visual Assets & Subject
* **Subject Image**: Backgroundless PNG cutout of a person in sharp profile looking upward, wearing round glasses.
* **Lighting/Grade**: Monochromatic high-contrast red rim light with deep shadows matching the background gradient.
* **Position**: Centered, base extending to bottom edge, overlapping the large `"Lilien"` script.

### UI Components
* **Top Header**: Text `"Hi, I'm Lilien"` (Top-left, small sans-serif).
* **CTA Button**: Pill-shaped button (black background `#000000`, white text `"See my works"`), containing an inline white circle with a black diagonal arrow (`→`).

## 2. Design Variant 2: "Dominic" (Dark Minimalist Streetwear Style)

### Layout & Background
* **Background**: Matte dark charcoal to off-black (`#111215`).
* **Layout Structure**: Centered hero subject with balanced two-sided information hierarchy and oversized backdrop typography.

### Typography & Fonts
* **Hero Backdrop Text**: Bold geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Syne*, *Clash Display*, fallback: *Montserrat*, *Arial Black*, sans-serif).
  * Text: `"Dominic"` (Massive, tracking `-0.04em`, pure white).
  * Layering: Rendered behind the subject's chest and shoulders.
* **Section Headings**: Modern grotesque sans-serif (e.g., *General Sans*, fallback: *Inter*, sans-serif).
  * Left Heading: `"Brand & UI/UX / Designer based / in London"` (Semi-bold, white).
* **Body Text**: Neutral sans-serif, regular weight, light gray (`#A0A0A0`).

### Visual Assets & Subject
* **Subject Image**: Cutout portrait of a male designer with afro hair, round red-tinted reflective sunglasses, silver chain, and a black puffy winter jacket.
* **Lighting**: Moody studio lighting with amber reflections in the sunglasses lenses.
* **Position**: Dead center, torso fading/cutting off cleanly at the bottom container edge.

### UI Components
* **Navigation Bar**: Floating capsule (`#1F2127`, subtle border), containing a brand mark icon, label `"Dominic"`, and a hamburger menu icon.
* **Status Badge**: Capsule pill with a red-orange active indicator dot + `"Available for Work"`.
* **CTA Button**: High-visibility neon orange pill (`#FF602E`) with a white circular icon housing an arrow (`→`) and label `"See my works"`.

## 3. Design Variant 3: "Solt" (Warm Minimalist Editorial)

### Layout & Background
* **Background**: Warm dual-tone muted sage/linen split (`#D0D5CC` left, `#C3C8BD` right).
* **Layout Structure**: 3-zone split: Left bio & CTA, Center framed portrait, Right key metrics, Bottom 4-column feature row.

### Typography & Fonts
* **Headline Titles**: Mixed editorial pairing:
  * Serif font: Elegant italic serif (e.g., *Cormorant Garamond*, fallback: *Baskerville*, serif) for `"Hey. I'm Solt,"` and `"& Brand"`.
  * Display Sans: Condensed bold sans-serif (e.g., *Bebas Neue*, *Oswald*, fallback: *Impact*, sans-serif) for `"A UI/UX"` and `"DESIGNER"`.
* **Metrics**: Serif numbers (`15+`, `280+`, `*99%`, `50`) paired with light sans-serif descriptors.
* **Footer Row**: Compact uppercase sans-serif headings with small subtext.

### Visual Assets & Subject
* **Subject Image**: Young male with round tortoiseshell glasses, light mustache/beard, folded arms, wearing a tan chore jacket over an olive shirt.
* **Framing/Mask**: Subject is placed inside a tall arched/capsule frame with an olive-gray background fill; the head and shoulders slightly break through the frame boundary.

### UI Components
* **CTA Button**: Pill-shaped dark slate button (`#1D201F`) with white text `"CONTACT ME"` and an encircled right arrow icon.
* **Metrics List**: Vertical stack aligned to the right (Years Experience, Projects Delivered, Client Satisfaction, Clients worldwide).
* **Footer Features Grid**: 4 equal-width horizontal columns across the bottom:
  1. *User-Centered Design*
  2. *Brand Identity & Strategy*
  3. *Responsive & Modern UI*
  4. *Seamless Prototyping*
