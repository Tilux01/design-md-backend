# Design System Specification: LAB. WORKS™ UI

## 1. Overview & Layout Architecture
- **Screen Bounds:** Fullscreen viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), `overflow: hidden`.
- **Base Grid:** 12-column fluid grid, 40px outer padding.
- **Theme:** Minimalist technical/utilitarian brutalism with high-contrast monochrome and pixel-art typographic accents.

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Background Base:** `#F2F2F2` (Soft off-white / light neutral gray)
- **Text & Elements (Primary):** `#111111` (Deep charcoal/black)
- **Text & Elements (Secondary/Muted):** `#7E7E7E` (Mid-tone cool gray)
- **Watermark Layer:** `#E5E5E5` / `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)` (Very light ghost gray)
- **Accent Color:** `#10B981` / `#00E676` (Electric neon green indicator dot)
- **Frosted Overlay:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`

---

## 3. Typography & Fallbacks
- **Primary Technical Body & Captions:**
  - *Font:* `Space Mono`, monospace.
  - *Fallback:* `Consolas`, `Menlo`, `monospace`.
  - *Usage:* Metadata, disclaimers, sub-labels, navigation links.
  - *Attributes:* Uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.08em - 0.15em`, `font-size: 9px - 11px`.
- **Primary Display Headings:**
  - *Font:* `Syne` (Bold 800) or `Druk Wide`.
  - *Fallback:* `Arial Black`, `Impact`, `sans-serif`.
  - *Usage:* `LAB. WORKS™`, `CRAFTED CREATIONS — ©2020`.
  - *Attributes:* Heavy weight, uppercase, `letter-spacing: -0.01em`.
- **Pixel Background Watermarks:**
  - *Font:* `Silkscreen` or `VT323`.
  - *Fallback:* `monospace`.
  - *Usage:* Large background numerals ("20") and vertical "LAB".

---

## 4. Visual Assets & Media

### 4.1. Center Hero Asset (Tactical Mask)
- **Asset Type:** Transparent PNG (backgroundless).
- **Description:** Matte black tactical gas mask/respirator with wide-angle dark tinted visor, central grill intake, and dual circular side filter canisters.
- **Position:** Dead center (`top: 50%`, `left: 50%`, `transform: translate(-50%, -50%)`).
- **Rotation:** `0deg` (frontal straight-on view).
- **Scale:** ~45vh to 50vh height.

### 4.2. Video Module Thumbnail
- **Asset Type:** Embedded rectangular component (`~180px × 54px`).
- **Left Side:** Solid black block with a white circular play icon (`▶`).
- **Right Side:** High-contrast grayscale portrait crop of an individual wearing a technical hooded storm jacket and face mask.
- **Position:** Bottom-left quadrant.

### 4.3. Graphic Marks & Icons
- **Logo:** `lab.®` in geometric lowercase bold sans-serif.
- **Badges (Left Side):** Small custom circular biohazard-like glyph + enclosed pill label `(LAB)` + small data mark.
- **World Map:** Pixelated mini-world graphic, top-right header.
- **Scroll Indicator:** Minimal down arrow (`↓`) inside border box at bottom-right.

---

## 5. UI Component Hierarchy

### 5.1. Header Bar
- **Position:** Fixed top, spanning full width with `padding: 32px 40px`.
- **Left:** Logo `lab.®` (`font-size: 20px`, bold).
- **Center-Left:** Navigation links `SHOP`, `TOUR`, `EXPLORE` (small uppercase monospace, tracking `0.15em`) followed by an extended thin line (`#D0D0D0`).
- **Right Area:** Pixelated map icon, followed by language selector `EN` (solid black, active) and `FR` (muted gray).

### 5.2. Background Watermarks
- **Pixelated "20":** Giant cropped pixel font located top-right behind the main asset.
- **Pixelated/Block "LAB":** Giant vertical cropped typography on the center-left behind the mask.

### 5.3. Hero Left Block
- **Title Block:**
  - Line 1: `LAB. WORKS ™` (Large, heavy sans).
  - Line 2: `CRAFTED CREATIONS — ©2020` (Medium heavy sans).
- **Sub-Badges:** Biohazard icon, `(LAB)` pill stamp, and miniature technical copyright text.
- **Slider Control:** Small active black dot `•` with trailing horizontal dash `—`.
- **Video Card:** Play button split with grayscale portrait.
- **Micro-copy (Below Video):**
  - Text: `FOR AUTHENTICATION OF LAB. UNVEIL SUMMER COLLECTION AND PROTECTION KEEP UNSEEN ALL RIGHTS RESERVED` (4 lines, `font-size: 7px`, monospace).

### 5.4. Hero Right Block
- **Collection Tag (Upper Right):** Neon green dot `•` with adjacent text `LAB SUMMER COLLECTION 2020` (2 lines).
- **Middle Feature Spec:** `SURVIVALIST CRAFTED ELEMENTS & VISUAL LANGUAGE` (3 lines, muted, right-aligned).
- **Index Gauge:** Vertical stack `20` — `20` separated by a thin vertical line.

### 5.5. Center Pill Tag (Overlay)
- **Style:** Frosted glass horizontal pill (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), positioned slightly overlapping the bottom-right canister of the gas mask.
- **Content:** Technical micro-spec lines, radio dots, and bold section marker `01`.

### 5.6. Bottom Navigation Anchor
- **Position:** Bottom-right corner (`bottom: 0`, `right: 40px`).
- **Element:** Square boundary box with downward direction arrow icon (`↓`).
