# Design Specification: 11x Landing Page ("Alice")

## 1. Global Setup & Theming

### Color Palette
*   **Background Base:** Desert ochre gradient ranging from `#6F4E37` (sky/top) to `#3B2219` (rocky terrain/bottom).
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   Primary: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
    *   Secondary/Muted: `#D6C5B3` (Warm Sand)
    *   Dark Text (on light buttons): `#111111`
*   **UI Accents & Overlays:**
    *   Glassmorphism Dark: `rgba(18, 14, 12, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`
    *   Card Inner Badges: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)` with subtle `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`

### Typography
*   **Primary Font:** *Inter*, *Aeonik*, or *SF Pro Display*
*   **Fallbacks:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
*   **Scale:**
    *   Hero Title (`H1`): 68px, Bold (700), line-height 1.1, letter-spacing -0.02em.
    *   Section Subtitle: 15px, Medium (500), uppercase tracking.
    *   Body Copy: 15px, Regular (400), line-height 1.6, max-width 420px.
    *   Nav & Labels: 13px–14px, Medium (500).

---

## 2. Page Structure & Layout

### Background
*   **Type:** Full-screen static image covering `100vw` by `100vh` (`object-fit: cover`).
*   **Description:** A Martian or desolate desert terrain featuring sharp, dark-brown rocky ridges in the foreground and lower midground, blending into a thick, dusty sepia/terracotta atmospheric haze toward the top. No rotation.

---

### Top Navigation Bar
*   **Layout:** Fixed or absolute pinned to top, `width: 100%`, flexbox layout with items centered vertically. Height: ~72px. Padding: `0 48px`.
*   **Elements:**
    *   **Left:** `11x` brand logo (stylized four-petal flower mark followed by "11x" in white).
    *   **Center:** Nav links with 24px gap: `Alice`, `Julian`, `Company`, `Blog`, `Partners`, `Opportunities`. Color: `#D6C5B3`, hover: `#FFFFFF`.
    *   **Right:** "Get started" button. Pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`), background `#FFFFFF`, text `#111111`, padding `8px 18px`, font size 13px, font weight 600.

---

### Hero Section
*   **Grid:** 2-column layout (`50% / 50%`), vertically centered within `min-height: calc(100vh - 140px)`, horizontal padding `80px`.

#### Left Column (Copy & CTA)
*   **Title:** "Hi, I’m Alice."
*   **Divider:** Thin horizontal line beneath the title (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`), spanning approximately 360px.
*   **Subtitle:** "The world's best SDR." (Color: `#D6C5B3`, margin-top 20px).
*   **Paragraph:** "I track every data point of your market, identify your ideal buyers, and engage decision-makers 24/7 to book you meetings on autopilot." (margin-top 12px, margin-bottom 28px).
*   **CTA Button:**
    *   Label: "Hire me"
    *   Style: White pill button (`border-radius: 9999px`), color `#111111`, font size 14px, padding `10px 24px`, bold.

#### Right Column (Avatar Card)
*   **Card Container:**
    *   Dimensions: Width ~420px, Height ~540px.
    *   Styling: Rounded corners (`border-radius: 28px`), overflow hidden, subtle drop shadow.
*   **Avatar Image:**
    *   **Description:** High-resolution portrait of "Alice", a woman with shoulder-length wavy brunette hair, blue eyes, and a neutral, friendly expression. Wearing a navy blue blazer over a blue-striped blouse. The studio backdrop is a warm beige tone that seamlessly matches the card background. Non-transparent background, 0° rotation.
*   **Top Badges (Overlay):**
    *   Position: Absolute, top 16px, left 16px. Flex column with 6px gap.
    *   Pill 1: "Alice the SDR" (glassmorphism pill, text size 11px, white).
    *   Pill 2: "⚡ Autopilot activated" (glassmorphism pill, text size 11px, white).
*   **Notification Card (Bottom Overlay):**
    *   Position: Absolute, bottom 16px, left 16px, right 16px.
    *   Style: Frosted dark glass (`rgba(10, 10, 15, 0.7)`, blur 20px, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, `border-radius: 16px`), padding `12px 16px`.
    *   Content: Row layout. Blue rounded mail icon on the left. Text right:
        *   Row 1: "Alice the SDR" (bold, 12px, white).
        *   Row 2: "Hey James, have you tried hiring an AI SDR?" (12px, `#D6C5B3`).
