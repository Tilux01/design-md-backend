# Design Specification: Coffee Menu Application

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
- **Primary Mood:** Warm, artisanal, contemporary boutique cafe experience with a modern minimalist execution.
- **Brand Archetype:** Premium, natural, earthy, and clean.
- **Visual Density:** Medium-high spatial density optimized for quick scanning of menu catalogs and item categories.
- **Whitespace Balance:** Generous padding in header and section containers balanced with tightly organized grid items for product cards.
- **Theme Balance:** Light-mode dominant design utilizing warm beige/cream tones (`#F7F4EF`), dark espresso brown typography (`#36281B`), and clean white product card surfaces.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

- **Primary Color:** Deep Espresso (`#36281B`, Hover: `#221810`, Active: `#140E0A`)
- **Secondary & Accent Colors:** Warm Caramel Gold (`#C28E58`, Hover: `#B17D46`)
- **Base Background:** Creamy Off-White (`#F7F4EF`)
- **Surface Backgrounds:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for cards, Semi-transparent backdrop blur for sticky elements.
- **Border & Divider Colors:** Soft Muted Sand (`#E5DFD3`)
- **Text Colors:** Primary Dark (`#36281B`), Muted Gray-Brown (`#7A6E63`), Inverse Light (`#FFFFFF`), Accent Gold (`#C28E58`)

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy
- **Font Families:**
  - *Headings:* Serif, elegant display font (e.g., *Playfair Display* or *Lora*)
  - *Body / UI:* Clean Grotesk Sans-serif (e.g., *Inter* or *Plus Jakarta Sans*)

### Scale Breakdown
| Level | Font Size | Line Height | Weight | Tracking (Letter-Spacing) |

| **Caption / Price** | 0.8125rem / 13px | 1.4 | Regular (400) | Normal |

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
- **Container Max-Width:** `1200px` centered with fluid horizontal padding (`1.5rem` on mobile, `3rem` on desktop).
- **Grid Layout:** 4-column responsive grid for product items (`grid-template-columns: repeat(4, minmax(0, 1fr))`).
- **Gaps:** `1.5rem` gap between grid cards horizontally and vertically.
- **Section Sequence:**
  1. Header Bar (Logo / Branding left, Action/Cart right)
  2. Category Navigation (Horizontal scrollable pill menu)
  3. Product Catalog Grid (Two rows of 4 items)
  4. Footer Action Bar (Pagination or secondary CTAs)

---

## 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Special Glyphs
- **High-Tracking Category Tabs:** Uppercase tracking headers with active state indicator underlines.
- **Micro-Badges:** Rounded rectangle tags (`border-radius: 4px`) displaying item properties (e.g., *Iced*, *Signature*).
- **Glyphs:** Unicode separators (—) and dot indicators (●) used for metadata and status.

## 7. Motion Mechanics, Framer Motion & CSS Animation Code
- **Animation Triggers:** Staggered entrance on page load, hover scaling on product imagery, smooth color transition on buttons.
- **Easing Physics:** `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (Swift out / smooth deceleration).

## 8. Accessibility (a11y) & WCAG Contrast Audit
- **Contrast Ratios:**
  - `#36281B` (Primary Dark) on `#FFFFFF` (White Background): **14.2:1** (Passes WCAG AAA)
  - `#7A6E63` (Muted Text) on `#FFFFFF`: **5.1:1** (Passes WCAG AA)
- **Focus Rings:**
  - `focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-[#C28E58] focus-visible:ring-offset-2`
