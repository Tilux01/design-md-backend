# UI/UX Design Specification: NEXORA Fashion E-Commerce Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Design Tokens

### 1.1 Color Palette
*   **Primary / Brand Purple:** `#6C4EE0` (Main banner background, headings, accents)
*   **Light Lilac / Soft Purple:** `#9A7CEB` or `#B89FF5` (Secondary purple accents, sticker backgrounds, wavy shapes)
*   **Dark Neutral / Text Primary:** `#1A1A1A` (Primary typography, nav items, headings)
*   **Muted Text / Secondary:** `#555555` / `#666666` (Subtitles, body copy)
*   **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF` (Card backgrounds, button text, primary product surfaces)
*   **Off-White / Canvas Background:** `#F4F2FA` (Overall website background tone)

### 1.2 Typography
*   **Font Family:** Modern Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, or *Outfit*)
*   **Headings (Display):** Bold, heavy weights (700-900), tight letter-spacing for large stylistic text.
*   **Body Copy:** Regular weight (400), clean readability, optimized for web.

---

## 2. Header & Navigation Bar
*   **Position:** Fixed/Sticky top bar, full width with generous horizontal padding.
*   **Left Section (Navigation Links):**
    *   `Shop`, `New In`, `Collections`, `About`, `Contact`
    *   *Typography:* Medium weight, dark neutral color, subtle hover state.
*   **Center Section (Logo):**
    *   `NEXORA` in a bold, stylized, uppercase sans-serif logotype.
*   **Right Section (Actions):**
    *   Search icon (magnifying glass)
    *   User Profile Avatar
    *   Cart Button: Pill-shaped button with shopping bag/cart icon and badge counter (`1 item`), styled with a subtle grey/white border.

---

## 3. Hero Section

### 3.1 Top Headline Area
*   **Left-Aligned Text:** *"Define Your"* (Black, regular weight) + *"STYLE"* (Large, bold, primary purple fill).
*   **Right-Aligned Text:** *"Own Your"* (Black, regular weight) + *"WORLD"* (Large, bold, primary purple fill) + Star/Sparkle vector accent (`✦`).

### 3.2 Main Hero Banner Card
*   **Container:** Large rounded rectangle card filled with `#6C4EE0` (Primary Purple), featuring organic wavy/curved background pattern overlays in lighter shades of purple.
*   **Center Visual (Model):**
    *   High-fashion portrait of a female model wearing a lavender beanie and an oversized white graphic tee (`NEXORA` branding on the back featuring a purple globe graphic).
    *   The model breaks the container bounds slightly, creating depth.

*   **Left Content Area (Inside Banner):**
    *   **Tag:** `✦ New Collection` (White text with star icon).
    *   **Main Headline:** *"Where Comfort Meets Confidence"* (White, large, bold).
    *   **Sub-copy:** *"Elevate your everyday look with pieces that speak you."* (Light grey/white, medium size).
    *   **Primary CTA Button:** Pill-shaped, solid black background, white text *"Explore Now"* with a right-arrow icon in a circle.
    *   **Social Proof Widget:** Rounded semi-transparent pill containing overlapping circular avatars of users + text *"Loved by 20K+ Trendsetters"* and a white heart icon.

*   **Right Content Area (Inside Banner):**
    *   **Value Proposition Icons:** Three vertical or horizontal icons with labels:
        *   T-shirt icon: *"Premium Quality"*
        *   Leaf icon: *"Sustainable Fashion"*
        *   Diamond/Gem icon: *"Limited Edition"*
    *   **Featured Look Card:**
        *   Floating card titled *"Featured Look"*.
        *   Product image: Male model wearing a lavender hoodie.
        *   Product Title: *"Graffiti Hoodie"*
        *   Subtitle: *"Streetwear Essential"*
        *   Action Button: Purple pill button with cart icon and price (`$79.99`).

---

## 4. Layout & Grid Specifications
*   **Container Max-Width:** `1440px` (Desktop view)
*   **Border Radius (Cards):** `24px` to `32px` for major containers.
*   **Shadows:** Soft, diffused purple-tinted drop shadows (`rgba(108, 78, 224, 0.15)`) to give depth to floating elements.
