# UI/UX Specification: E-Commerce Product Page (Minimalist Green Theme)

## 1. Overview & Layout Strategy
- **Design Paradigm**: Clean, modern e-commerce product detail page (PDP) optimized for desktop viewport.
- **Grid Layout**: Two-column split layout contained within a centered modal-like card (`max-w-[900px]`).
  - **Left Column**: Product gallery with primary image display, thumbnail navigation, and zoom indicators.
  - **Right Column**: Product metadata, variant selectors, purchase actions, and accordion modules.
- **Viewport Handling**: Full-bleed background (`bg-[#f4f7ee]` / pale sage green) with a centered white card container (`bg-white rounded-2xl shadow-sm`) simulating a high-end web application modal.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Typography

### Colors
- **Backgrounds**:
  - Page Background: `#f4f7ee` (Soft Sage)
  - Card/Container Background: `#ffffff` (Pure White)
  - Secondary Accents / Badges: `#eef3e8` (Light Sage)
- **Primary / Brand**:
  - Deep Forest Green: `#123c28` (Used for primary CTA buttons, active state highlights, and primary text headers).
- **Text & Borders**:
  - Primary Text (Dark): `#1a1a1a`
  - Secondary Text (Muted): `#6b7280` (Gray)
  - Borders/Dividers: `#e5e7eb` (Light Gray)

### Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter, sans-serif
- **Scale**:
  - Product Title: `text-2xl font-normal text-[#123c28]`
  - Price: `text-lg font-medium text-[#1a1a1a]`
  - Body / Subtext: `text-sm text-[#6b7280]`
  - Button Text: `text-sm font-medium tracking-wide text-white`

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### 3.1. Header / Navigation (Implicit)
- **Top Bar**: Minimalist header with left-aligned brand logo/name and right-aligned user actions (search, cart indicator).

### 3.2. Left Column: Product Gallery
- **Main Image Container**:
  - Aspect ratio: `1:1` square.
  - Background: Soft neutral grey (`#f1f0f4`).
  - Content: High-resolution centered product render (Green patterned bottle/can with cable accent).
- **Thumbnail Navigation**:
  - Horizontal row of 3–4 small square thumbnail buttons positioned below the main image.
  - Active thumbnail highlighted with a subtle border.
- **Gallery Controls**:
  - Pagination indicators (dots) or scroll arrows for additional angles/views.

### 3.3. Right Column: Product Information & Purchase Flow
- **Product Header**:
  - Title: *"Sparkling Green Refresh"* (or similar product identifier).
  - Subtitle/Flavor note: *"Natural Botanical Extract"*
- **Pricing**:
  - Currency/Amount: `$24.00` (clean, bold typeface).
- **Variant Selector (Pack Size / Style)**:
  - Label: `Pack Size`
  - Options: Horizontal pill-shaped radio buttons or cards representing quantities (e.g., *4-pack*, *8-pack*, *12-pack*).
  - Interactive state: Selected option outlined with a dark forest green border (`border-[#123c28]`).
- **Purchase Option (One-Time vs. Subscription)**:
  - Segmented control or radio selector:
    - *One-time purchase*: `$24.00`
    - *Subscribe & Save (15% off)*: `$20.40`
- **Quantity Selector & Add to Cart**:
  - Counter component: `- [ 1 ] +` bordered pill container.
  - Primary CTA Button: Full-width button with deep forest green background (`bg-[#123c28]`), rounded corners (`rounded-full`), center-aligned text *"Add to Cart"*.
- **Accordion / Expandable Sections**:
  - Collapsible rows for *"Ingredients"*, *"Shipping & Returns"*, and *"Nutrition Facts"* with subtle chevron indicators.

---

## 4. Interactive States & Micro-Interactions
- **Buttons**: Scale transition on hover (`scale-[1.02]`) with subtle shadow enhancement.
- **Selectors**: Immediate border color shift from `#e5e7eb` to `#123c28` on selection.
- **Thumbnails**: Opacity shift on hover (`opacity-80` to `opacity-100`).
