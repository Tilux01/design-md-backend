# Design Specification: Playcloud Music Interface

## 1. General Theme & Colors
*   **Background:** Deep Black (`#000000`).
*   **Surface/Sidebar Background:** Slightly lighter black/dark grey (`#0A0A0A`).
*   **Primary Accent:** Vibrant Red (`#FF0000` or `#D60017`). Used for the main Play button and the artist's background glow.
*   **Primary Text:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) at 100% opacity.
*   **Secondary Text/Icons:** Light Grey (`#B3B3B3`).
*   **Dividers/Borders:** Dark Grey (`#282828`).

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Circular Std`.
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:** 
    *   Regular (400) for body text and metadata.
    *   Medium (500) for navigation items and track titles.
    *   Bold (700) for section headers and the main Artist title.

## 3. Layout Structure
The application uses a three-panel layout:
1.  **Sidebar (Left):** Fixed width (approx. 240px).
2.  **Main Content Area (Center/Right):** Scrollable area containing the Header, Hero section, and Tracklist.
3.  **Player Bar (Bottom):** Fixed height (approx. 90px) at the bottom of the viewport.

---

## 4. Component Details

### A. Top Navigation Header
*   **Logo:** "Playcloud" in white, medium weight, left-aligned.
*   **Breadcrumbs:** `< Artists > The Weeknd`. The arrows are grey; "The Weeknd" is white.
*   **Center Nav:** Search Icon, "New Releases", "News Feed", "Shuffle Play". Spaced evenly with grey text.
*   **User Profile (Right):** A small circular avatar, the name "Matias Alexander", and a hamburger menu icon (`≡`).

### B. Sidebar
*   **Section 1: Browse Music**
    *   Items: Home, Albums, Tracks, Genres.
    *   *Active State:* "Tracks" is highlighted. The icon and text are white; others are grey.
*   **Section 2: Library**
    *   Items: Recently Played, Favorite Tracks, Charts, Radio.
    *   Text is slightly smaller and grey.

### C. Hero Section (Artist Profile)
*   **Text Content:**
    *   Label: "PLAYLIST" (Uppercase, small, grey).
    *   Title: "The Weeknd" (Large, bold, white, approx 48pt).
    *   Description: Two lines of grey text describing the artist.
    *   Metadata: "Spotify • 860,305 likes • 100 songs, 5 hr 5 min" (Grey).
*   **Action Buttons:**
    *   Large circular red button with a white "Play" triangle icon.
    *   Outline heart icon (grey).
    *   Horizontal ellipsis icon (`...`) for more options.
*   **Artist Image:**
    *   **Description:** A high-resolution profile shot of The Weeknd looking to the left. He is wearing dark sunglasses.
    *   **Visual Style:** The image itself is **backgroundless (transparent PNG)**.
    *   **Placement:** Positioned to the right of the text.
    *   **Background Element:** Behind the artist is a large, soft-edged **Red Radial Gradient Circle**. The glow is brightest in the center and fades into the black background. The artist's silhouette overlaps the right side of this red glow.

### D. Tracklist Table
*   **Headers:** `#`, `Title`, `Album`, `Number of Auditions`, `Clock Icon`. (All grey, uppercase, small font).
*   **Row Content:**
    *   **# Column:** Small play icon (appears on hover) or track number.
    *   **Title Column:** Small square album art thumbnail (approx 40x40px), followed by the Song Title (White) and Artist Name (Grey) stacked vertically.
    *   **Album Column:** Album title in grey.
    *   **Auditions Column:** A small headphone icon followed by the count (e.g., `460,228,511`) in grey.
    *   **Actions:** Outline heart icon and duration (e.g., `2:45`).
*   **Rows:** "Save Your Tears", "Blinding Lights - Single", "Starboy", "The Hills", "I Feel It Coming".

### E. Bottom Player Bar
*   **Track Info (Left):** Thumbnail of "Blinding Lights", Title "The Weeknd", Subtitle "Blinding Lights".
*   **Controls (Center):** 
    *   Top row: Shuffle, Previous, Play (circular white button with black icon), Next, Repeat.
    *   Bottom row: Current time (`2:49`), Progress bar (thin line, white for elapsed, grey for remaining), Total time (`3:37`).
*   **Utilities (Right):** Queue icon, Device icon, Volume icon, and a volume slider.

## 5. Image Asset Descriptions

1.  **Main Artist Image:** 
    *   **Subject:** The Weeknd (Abel Tesfaye).
    *   **Pose:** Side profile, facing left.
    *   **Attributes:** Afro-style hair, beard, wearing dark-tinted sunglasses.
    *   **Format:** Transparent background (no box).
    *   **Rotation:** 0 degrees (upright).

2.  **Track Thumbnails:**
    *   Small 1:1 aspect ratio squares.
    *   Images correspond to the album covers for *After Hours* (red/bloody face) and *Starboy* (blue/neon cross background).

3.  **User Avatar:**
    *   Small circular crop of a portrait.

## 6. Iconography
*   Use a consistent thin-stroke icon set (like **Feather Icons** or **Phosphor Icons**).
*   **Icons needed:** Play (filled and outline), Heart (outline), Ellipsis (horizontal), Headphone, Clock, Search, Chevron (Left/Right), Shuffle, Repeat, Volume, List/Queue.
