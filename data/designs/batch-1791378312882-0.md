# UI/UX Specification & Replication Guide: Oryzo Product Landing Page

This document provides the exact design specifications, layout structure, typography, and animation logic needed to build a pixel-perfect, full-screen clone of the Oryzo product website shown in the media.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

The interface uses a **full-bleed, asymmetrical grid layout** mimicking a physical maker's desk/blueprint workspace. 

*   **View:** Full screen (`100vw`, `100vh`), non-scrolling or single-page vertical scroll depending on interaction state.
*   **Background:** 
    *   Base color: Deep charcoal/black (`#111111`).
    *   Overlay Pattern: A green technical cutting mat grid (`#1c2826` or `#1b2623`) with precise white/light-green grid lines and metric measurement numbers along the X and Y axes.
*   **Layout Regions:**
    *   **Header / Navigation:** Floating top bar with branding and anchor links.
    *   **Hero Left:** Large bold typography, studio credit, and product descriptor tagline.
    *   **Hero Center / Focal Point:** The interactive/photorealistic cork coaster centered on the cutting mat grid, surrounded by prop elements (drafting pencil, utility knife).
    *   **Hero Right / Info Panel:** Product copy highlighting design philosophy ("Designed to lift, insulate, and grip...").
    *   **Footer / Micro-copy:** Scroll prompt at the bottom center.

---

## 2. Typography & Font Stack

The aesthetic relies on a mix of ultra-bold heavy headlines and clean, industrial sans-serif body copy.

### Primary Font Family (Headlines & Branding)
*   **Font Name:** *Inter* / *Helvetica Neue* / *Arial Black* (Heavy weight variant, uppercase).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Usage:**
    *   `ORYZO` Logo: `font-weight: 900`, `letter-spacing: -0.04em`, uppercase.

### Secondary Font Family (Body & UI Elements)
*   **Font Name:** *Inter* / *SF Pro Text* (Regular & Light weights).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif`.
*   **Usage:**
    *   Subtitles, navigation links (`INTRO`, `FEATURES`, `PRODUCT`, `CONTACT`), and body text.

## 4. Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation Bar (Top)
*   **Left:** Small caption text: `MADE FOR MUGS. BUILT FOR TABLES.` (Uppercase, tracked out, small font size ~11px, muted white).
*   **Right:** Anchor links: `INTRO` | `FEATURES` | `PRODUCT` | `CONTACT`. 
*   **Far Right Edge:** Vertical tag element reading `ORYZO-1 MODEL` set inside a dark badge or border.

### B. Left Content Block (`DESIGNED BY LUSION...`)
*   **Main Headline:** `ORYZO` (Massive scale, left-aligned, occupying ~30% of viewport width).
*   **Studio Credit Box:** 
    *   Text: `DESIGNED BY LUSION, THE AWARD-WINNING DESIGN STUDIO.`
    *   Secondary sub-copy underneath: `The world's most unnecessarily sophisticated cork coaster.`
    *   Styling: Contained within a subtle semi-transparent dark container (`background: rgba(0,0,0,0.4)` or backdrop blur) with thin structural borders.

### C. Center Visual (The Cutting Mat & Coaster)
*   **Asset:** A photorealistic top-down render of a circular cork coaster resting on the green grid mat.
*   **Props (Decorative Elements):**
    *   A silver mechanical drafting pencil positioned diagonally in the top-right corner.
    *   A utility/craft knife (yellow handle with metallic blade casing) positioned in the bottom-right corner.
    *   A pencil tip visible on the bottom-left edge.
*   **Ruler Markings:** Vertical ruler running down the left side of the grid (`0`, `10`, `20`... up to `150+`) and horizontal markings along the bottom.

### D. Right Info Panel
*   **Copy:** `Designed to lift, insulate, and grip in all the right ways. Oryzo makes the simplest moment feel considered.`
*   **Styling:** Right-aligned or left-aligned within a max-width block (~300px), clean sans-serif typography, regular weight, high contrast against the dark background.

### E. Bottom Scroll Indicator
*   **Text/Icon:** `↓ SCROLL TO CONTINUE` (Centered at the bottom of the viewport with a subtle bouncing or pulsing animation cue).

## 5. Animation & Interaction Specification

To match the high-end interactive studio feel (characteristic of agencies like Lusion):

1.  **Parallax / Mouse Movement Effect:**
    *   As the user moves their cursor across the screen, the background grid, props (pencil, knife), and central coaster should exhibit a subtle 3D parallax shift (utilizing `transform: translate3d()` driven by mouse coordinates).
2.  **Scroll Animations (GSAP / Framer Motion):**
    *   On load, elements fade in sequentially (`opacity: 0` to `1` with `translateY` offset).
    *   The central coaster should feature a micro-scale or lighting shift as the page settles.
3.  **Hover States:**
    *   Navigation items subtly brighten on hover (`#FFFFFF` from `#A0A0A0`).
    *   The central product may react dynamically to cursor proximity with a slight tilt or shadow elevation.
