# Design Specification: Smart Home Mobile Application

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

*   **Platform:** iOS / Android (Modern Smartphone Aspect Ratio)
*   **Theme:** Light Mode default with dark mode elements (e.g., Smart Speaker card)
*   **Corner Radius (App Frame):** Smooth rounded corners (approx. `40px` outer container radius)
*   **Layout Style:** Asymmetric multi-screen view showcasing two distinct states:
    1.  **Detail/Control Screen:** Left side (Smart Light controls)
    2.  **Dashboard Screen:** Right side (Home overview)

---

## 2. Color Palette & Typography

### Colors
*   **Background (Canvas):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
*   **Surface Cards/Containers:** `#F8F9FA` or `#F4F5F7` (Very Light Gray)
*   **Primary Accent / Interactive:** `#000000` (Pitch Black for active states, toggles, and main text)
*   **Secondary / Muted Text:** `#8E8E93` (System Gray)
*   **Active Dark Card:** `#1A1A1A` to `#242424` (Gradient/Solid Dark Gray for active device states like the Smart Speaker)
*   **Borders / Dividers:** `#E5E5EA` (Light border gray)

### Typography
*   **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, or `Roboto` (Sans-Serif, geometric, highly legible).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
*   **Scale:**
    *   **Screen Title (Dashboard):** 28px, Bold (`700`), `#000000`
    *   **Section Header (Devices):** 16px, Semi-Bold (`600`), `#000000`
    *   **Card Title / Device Name:** 14px, Medium (`500`), `#000000`
    *   **Body / Subtitle Text:** 12px, Regular (`400`), `#8E8E93`
    *   **Microcopy / Labels:** 10px, Regular (`400`), `#8E8E93`

---

## 3. Screen 1: Smart Light Detail View (Left Component)

### Header Section
*   **Back Button:** Standard iOS-style chevron left `<` icon, size 20px, color `#000000`, placed at top-left.
*   **Title:** "Smart Light" positioned below the back button. Font size: 24px, Bold (`700`), `#000000`.

### Main Power Toggle
*   **Component:** Custom pill-shaped toggle switch.
*   **State:** ON (Thumb aligned to the right side inside a dark capsule `#000000`).
*   **Position:** Directly below the screen title.

### Brightness Arc Control (Center-Left)
*   **Visual Graphic:** A quarter-circle arc graph spanning from "Low" to "High".
*   **Curve:** Smooth curved line starting from the bottom-left curving outward to the top-right.
*   **Interactive Handle:** A solid black circular pin indicator at the 47% mark along the arc path.
*   **Labels:** 
    *   "High" (Top-left, small gray text)
    *   "Low" (Bottom-left, small gray text)

### Product Illustration (Center-Right)
*   **Image Description:** A modern, minimalist black industrial pendant lamp (dome style) hanging from a braided black electrical cord attached to the ceiling. 
*   **Background:** Transparent / Backgroundless cutout.
*   **Placement:** Positioned in the upper-right portion of the screen.
*   **Rotation:** 0 degrees (Upright).

### Brightness Percentage Display
*   **Typography:** Large numbers "47%" in 36px Bold (`700`), `#000000`.
*   **Subtitle:** "Brightness" in 12px Regular, `#8E8E93`, centered below the percentage.

### Schedule Section
*   **Header:** "Schedule" in 14px, Semi-Bold (`600`).
*   **Inputs:** Two inline dropdown pill selectors:
    *   `From [ 12:00 AM v ]`
    *   `To [ 18:00 PM v ]`
*   **Styling:** Light gray background pill containers with downward chevron icons.

### Intensity Slider
*   **Header:** "Intensity" (Left) | "70%" (Right, Bold).
*   **Component:** Horizontal slider track with discrete step ticks.
*   **Track Color:** Light gray with a solid black filled track up to the 70% marker.
*   **Thumb:** Solid black circular knob at 70%.
*   **Labels:** "Off" on the far left, small tick marks along the line, "100%" on the far right.

---

## 4. Screen 2: Smart Home Dashboard (Right Component)

### Top Navigation Bar
*   **Left Element:** Menu icon (three horizontal lines of varying lengths), color `#000000`.
*   **Right Element:** Circular profile avatar image showing a smiling man with short brown hair wearing a black shirt. Transparent/backgroundless or circular crop.

### Greeting Header
*   **Title:** "Hi Mark" (24px, Bold, `#000000`).
*   **Subtitle:** "Welcome to your smart home" (12px, `#8E8E93`).

### Room Category Filter (Horizontal Scrollable Icons)
*   A row of rounded square category buttons with icons representing different rooms:
    1.  **Living room (Active):** Inverted colors—solid black background (`#000000`) with a white couch icon and white text below.
    2.  **Bedroom:** Light gray background (`#F4F5F7`), dark gray armchair/bed icon, dark gray text.
    3.  **Kitchen:** Light gray background, kitchen appliance icon, gray text.
    4.  **Bathroom:** Light gray background, bathtub/toilet icon, gray text.
    5.  **Garage:** Light gray background, garage door icon, gray text.

### Devices Section Header
*   **Title:** "Devices" (16px, Bold, `#000000`).
*   **Action:** Three horizontal dots (`...`) menu icon on the right.

### Device Cards Grid (2x2 Layout)

#### Card 1: Smart Speaker (Active State)
*   **Background:** Dark solid color (`#1A1A1A`).
*   **Image:** A cylinder-shaped smart speaker (Amazon Echo-style fabric mesh) with a glowing cyan/blue light ring active along the top edge. Backgroundless cutout.
*   **Text:** 
    *   "Smart speaker" (White, Medium weight)
    *   "60%" (Muted gray text underneath)
*   **Toggle Switch:** Active state (white thumb on dark pill track).

#### Card 2: Thermostat
*   **Background:** `#FFFFFF` with a subtle light gray border (`#E5E5EA`).
*   **Image:** A round Nest-style smart thermostat showing digital temperature "20°C" inside a dark circular interface with a green target indicator arc. Backgroundless cutout.
*   **Text:**
    *   "Thermostat" (Black text)
    *   "25°C" (Gray status text)
*   **Toggle Switch:** Inactive state (gray track, white thumb on left).

#### Card 3: Smart Light
*   **Background:** `#FFFFFF` with a subtle light gray border.
*   **Image:** Identical black pendant dome lamp (as seen on the left detail screen), scaled down. Backgroundless cutout.
*   **Text:**
    *   "Smart Light"
    *   "47%"
*   **Toggle Switch:** Inactive state.

#### Card 4: WiFi Router
*   **Background:** `#FFFFFF` with a subtle light gray border.
*   **Image:** A modern multi-antenna black gaming/mesh Wi-Fi router. Backgroundless cutout.
*   **Text:**
    *   "WiFi Router"
    *   "200 Mbps"
*   **Toggle Switch:** Inactive state.
