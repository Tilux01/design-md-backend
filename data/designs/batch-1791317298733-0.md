# UI/UX Design System Specification: Bluneo.ai

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
*   **Primary Mood:** Futuristic, high-performance, developer-focused artificial intelligence.
*   **Brand Archetype:** Cutting-edge infrastructure, sophisticated, high-tech B2B SaaS.
*   **Visual Density:** Medium-low density with a generous whitespace balance in the hero viewport, allowing the 3D abstract illustrative centerpiece to dominate visual gravity.
*   **Theme Architecture:** Pure dark mode interface (`#000000` base) paired with vivid electric cyan/blue lighting effects (`#2563EB`, `#3B82F6`), rich radial gradients, and subtle glassmorphic surface depth.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

### Color Inventory
*   **Primary Accent:** Blue 600 (`#2563EB`) & Electric Blue (`#3B82F6`)
*   **Primary Hover:** Blue 500 (`#3B82F6`) / Bright Sky (`#60A5FA`)
*   **Base Background:** True Black (`#000000`)
*   **Surface Layers:** 
    *   Glass Pill BG: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` with border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

    *   Primary Headings: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy
*   **Font Families:** 
    *   Headings & Body: Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
    *   Monospace/Code: JetBrains Mono, Fira Code, monospace

### Scale Breakdown
*   **Nav Links:** 14px (0.875rem), Font-Weight: 400, Line-Height: 1.2, Tracking: normal (`0em`)
*   **Hero Badge:** 12px (0.75rem), Font-Weight: 500, Line-Height: 1.0, Tracking: `0.05em` (Uppercase)
*   **Hero H1:** 56px–64px (3.5rem–4rem), Font-Weight: 700, Line-Height: 1.08, Tracking: `-0.03em`
*   **Hero Subtitle:** 16px (1rem), Font-Weight: 400, Line-Height: 1.6, Tracking: `-0.01em`
*   **CTA Button Text:** 14px (0.875rem), Font-Weight: 500, Line-Height: 1.0, Tracking: `0.01em`

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
*   **Container Max-Width:** `1280px` (Centered with responsive side padding `clamp(20px, 5vw, 64px)`)
*   **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** 8px base grid unit (8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128px)
*   **Section Sequence:**
    1.  Global Sticky Header (Logo, Nav links, Contact CTA)
    2.  Hero Section (Left: Badge, H1, Subtitle, CTA; Right: 3D Isometric Composition)
    3.  Social Proof / Partner Logo Bar (Monochrome low-opacity brand marks)
*   **Z-Index Hierarchy:**
    *   Background Ambient Glow: `z-0`
    *   Main Content & 3D Elements: `z-10`
    *   Navigation Bar: `z-50`

## 7. Accessibility (a11y) & WCAG Contrast Audit
*   **Pure White Headings (`#FFFFFF`) on Pure Black (`#000000`):** Contrast Ratio **21:1** (Passes WCAG AAA).
*   **Muted Body Text (`#9CA3AF`) on Pure Black (`#000000`):** Contrast Ratio **7.4:1** (Passes WCAG AAA).
*   **Primary Button Text (`#FFFFFF`) on Blue 600 (`#2563EB`):** Contrast Ratio **4.6:1** (Passes WCAG AA).
*   **Keyboard Focus Indicator:** 
    ```css
    *:focus-visible {
      outline: 2px solid #3B82F6;
      outline-offset: 2px;
    }
    ```
