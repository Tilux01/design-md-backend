# Design Specification: Quest Room Hero Landing Page

---

## 1. Overview & Visual Aesthetic

A minimalist, high-contrast, dark-mode hero landing page inspired by the *Squid Game* visual identity for an escape/quest room venue named **Quest Room**.

* **Tone & Mood:** Mysterious, cinematic, immersive, tension-driven, premium.
* **Layout Paradigm:** Full-viewport viewport-locked landing section (`100vw` × `100vh` / `100dvh`), zero vertical scrollbar on initial load, absolute/sticky floating header, and a dramatic, centered focal photographic figure emerging from total darkness.

## 3. Typography & Fallback Stacks

The design uses a clean, modern, slightly wide geometric grotesque sans-serif with rounded terminals matching contemporary sci-fi/thriller aesthetics.

### Primary Typography Stack
```css
font-family: "Space Grotesk", "Montserrat", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif;
```

### Font Hierarchy & Styles

1. **Logo ("Quest Room"):**
   * **"Q" Symbol:** Custom geometric glyph (circular magnifying glass / target loop with angled lower-right spur) in hot pink (`#E60067`).
   * **"uest":** Uppercase or lowercase stylized geometric sans, White (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 700`, `font-size: 18px`, `letter-spacing: -0.02em`.
   * **"room":** Positioned directly beneath or adjacent in all-lowercase, White (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 500`, `font-size: 9px`, `letter-spacing: 0.25em`, `text-transform: uppercase`.

2. **Navigation Items ("About the Game", "Pricing", "Contact"):**
   * `font-size: 13px` (0.8125rem)
   * `font-weight: 500`
   * `line-height: 1.2`
   * `letter-spacing: 0.02em`
   * Color: `#D1D1D1` (Transitions to `#FFFFFF` on hover)

3. **CTA Button ("Play Game"):**
   * `font-size: 13px` (0.8125rem)
   * `font-weight: 600`
   * `letter-spacing: 0.02em`
   * Text Color: `#FFFFFF`

## 5. Component Breakdown

### 5.1. Navigation Header
* **Positioning:** Fixed or absolute pinned to the top:
  * `top: 0; left: 0; right: 0; width: 100%;`
  * `padding: 32px 56px;` (Responsive: `20px 24px` on tablet/mobile)
  * `display: flex; align-items: center; justify-content: space-between;`
  * Background: Fully transparent (`background: transparent`).

#### Left: Brand Identity
* Container: Flexbox, vertical alignment center.
* Mark: A bold circular magnifying glass / loop stylized as the letter `Q` in `#E60067`, followed by clean typographic logotype.

#### Center / Right-Aligned Navigation Cluster
* Navigation menu items and the CTA button are grouped toward the right side.
* Nav List Container:
  * `display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 36px;`
  * Links: `About the Game`, `Pricing`, `Contact`.
  * Transition: `color 0.2s ease-in-out`.

#### CTA Button ("Play Game")
* Pill-shaped button:
  * `border-radius: 9999px`
  * `background-color: #E60067`
  * `padding: 10px 26px`
  * `border: none; cursor: pointer;`
  * Hover State: `background-color: #FF1A7D; box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(230, 0, 103, 0.45); transform: translateY(-1px);`
  * Transition: `all 0.25s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.

---

### 5.2. Hero Image Asset Specification

* **Subject:** A Squid Game "Soldier" character (guard in pink jumpsuit with black face fencing-style mask).
* **Composition & Framing:** 
  * Half-body portrait (from the waist/beltline upward to the top of the hood).
  * Direct frontal symmetrical orientation (`rotation: 0deg`, no tilt, perfectly straight).
  * Arms hang naturally down along the sides of the torso.
* **Costume Details:**
  * **Jumpsuit:** Vibrant magenta/hot pink nylon-like windbreaker fabric with natural atmospheric shadow creasing, central vertical black zipper running from chest to neck, elastic waist gather/black harness belt visible at the bottom.
  * **Hood:** Pulls up over the head, framing the mask tightly.
  * **Mask:** Matte black protective mesh face shield featuring a solid, crisp, equilateral white triangle (`△`) emblem centered precisely over the facial area.
* **Asset Format & Background:**
  * **Transparency:** Backgroundless PNG or WebP with smooth antialiased alpha edges, seamlessly dropping into the `#000000` canvas.
  * Alternatively, deep black background with soft feathering/vignette gradient fading into pure `#000000` around the waist and outer torso edges.
* **Placement & Sizing:**
  * `position: absolute;`
  * Horizontal Alignment: Dead center (`left: 50%; transform: translateX(-50%);`)
  * Vertical Alignment: Anchored to the lower half. The head sits approximately in the vertical middle of the screen (`top: 48% - 52%` region), with the torso extending to the bottom edge.
  * Scale / Height: Approx. `58vh` to `65vh` of viewport height (retaining natural aspect ratio, approximately `3:4` or `1:1` cropped).
  * `pointer-events: none;` (to ensure clicks pass through if needed).

## 7. Responsive Behavior Guidelines

* **Desktop (> 1024px):** Exactly as pictured: standard full-width header (`padding: 32px 56px`), centered hero soldier height at `62vh`.
* **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** Header padding drops to `24px 32px`, nav gap reduces to `24px`, hero figure scales to `55vh`.
* **Mobile (< 768px):** 
  * Navigation links (`About the Game`, `Pricing`, `Contact`) collapse into a mobile slide-out/hamburger overlay.
  * The "Play Game" button remains visible or moves inside the menu bar.
  * Soldier graphic centers at `width: 85vw; height: auto; bottom: 0;`.
