# Design Specification: Dark Mode Culinary Mobile Application

This document provides the exact design specifications for building a high-fidelity clone of the provided mobile application screens. The design features a cohesive dark mode aesthetic tailored for a culinary, recipe, or food-ordering platform.

---

## 1. Global Design System & Tokens

### Color Palette
*   **Background (Primary):** `#121212` (Deep Charcoal / Near Black)
*   **Background (Surface / Cards):** `#1A1A1A` to `#222222` (Slightly lighter dark surfaces used for buttons, containers, and cards)
*   **Primary Accent / Interactive:** `#F0A500` (Warm Amber / Golden Yellow)
*   **Text (Primary / Headings):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
*   **Text (Secondary / Body):** `#B0B0B0` (Muted Light Gray)
*   **Text (Disabled / Tertiary):** `#707070` (Dark Gray)

### Typography
*   **Headings / Brand Titles:** 
    *   *Style:* Elegant, high-contrast serif script with organic stroke variations (reminiscent of bespoke display serifs like *Playfair Display*, *Bodoni*, or *Cinzel*).
    *   *Fallback Stack:* `Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif`
*   **Body / UI Labels / Metadata:**
    *   *Style:* Clean, highly legible geometric sans-serif (reminiscent of *Inter*, *SF Pro Display*, or *Roboto*).
    *   *Fallback Stack:* `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`

## 2. Screen-by-Screen Layout & Content Specification

### Screen 1: Login / Welcome Screen (Top Left)
*   **Header:** Brand title "Logina" centered at the top in large, stylized serif typography.
*   **Hero Image:** 
    *   *Description:* A top-down macro photograph of a gourmet savory dish (molded rice/grain stack garnished with fresh herbs and a vibrant yellow flower petal).
    *   *Background:* Backgroundless (isolated on the dark app background).
    *   *Placement:* Centered horizontally in the upper-middle portion of the screen.
*   **Call-to-Action (CTA):** A rounded pill-shaped button filled with the primary amber accent (`#F0A500`) containing bold dark text.
*   **Footer:** Minimalist secondary text link centered at the bottom.

### Screen 2: Recipe Detail - View 1 (Top Center)
*   **Header:** "Viplato" centered in stylized serif type.
*   **Thumbnail Image:** 
    *   *Description:* A circular top-down thumbnail of a mixed grain salad with red tomatoes and green herbs.
    *   *Placement:* Centered horizontally just below the header.
*   **Body Content:** 
    *   *Description:* A multi-line block of secondary gray text representing recipe description or ingredients overview, aligned left-to-center.
*   **CTA:** A wide, rounded amber pill button (`#F0A500`) towards the lower third.

### Screen 3: Recipe Feed / Detail - View 2 (Top Right)
*   **Header:** Navigation/Brand title left-aligned with a hamburger menu icon on the far left.

*   **CTA & Actions:** Amber pill button for primary action; secondary text link below.

### Screen 4: Recipe Detail - View 3 (Middle Left)
*   **Header:** "Trumada" stylized serif title on the left, heart/favorite icon on the right.

    *   *Description:* A dark ceramic bowl filled with a colorful grain and vegetable mix (red tomatoes, greens). Scattered around the bowl on the dark surface are whole red tomatoes, peppercorns, and fresh herbs (artfully placed as realistic props).

## 3. Asset & Imagery Guidelines
*   **Photography Style:** High-end culinary photography shot from a direct top-down (flat lay) or 45-degree angle. Lighting is moody and dramatic (chiaroscuro), featuring high contrast against pitch-black backgrounds.
*   **Color Grading:** Warm tones emphasizing rich reds (tomatoes, paprika), vibrant greens (herbs), and golden grains, contrasted sharply against deep blacks and charcoals.
*   **Integration:** All food images are rendered **without rectangular borders or backgrounds** (alpha channel/isolated), blending seamlessly into the `#121212` app canvas.
