# UI/UX Specification: Nike Pegasus Plus Product Page

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas

*   **View Mode:** Full-screen immersive layout (ignoring the previewer frame and external presentation padding).
*   **Background:** 
    *   Linear Gradient filling the entire viewport.
    *   **Colors:** Top-left transitioning down-right through soft, warm sunset/peach tones. 
    *   Approximate color stops: 
        *   Top/Left: Light peach (`#fcdcd6`)
        *   Bottom/Right: Warm pink/magenta-tinted gradient (`#de879f` to `#e6abb5`)
*   **Typography Base:** Clean, modern sans-serif typography.
    *   *Primary Font:* **Inter** (Fallback: `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`).
    *   *Secondary/Brand Font:* **Futura** or **Nike Futura Condensed** for heavy uppercase headers (Fallback: `Arial Black, Impact, sans-serif`).

---

## 2. Header / Navigation Bar

Positioned at the top with generous horizontal padding.

*   **Left Section:**
    *   Brand Logo: Minimalist abstract mark (black circular head shape over a horizontal pill) followed by the text `uiuxzaid` in lowercase, medium weight.
*   **Right Section (Pill-shaped Navigation Links):**
    *   Links styled as rounded outline pills with subtle white translucent fills:
        *   `TUTORIAL`
        *   `FIGMA`
        *   `EFFECT`
        *   `ANIMATION`
    *   *Typography:* Uppercase, 11px, tracking/letter-spacing wide, semi-bold.

---

## 3. Product Display Card / Hero Section

Centered within the viewport as a wide glassmorphism/gradient container with rounded corners (`border-radius: 24px`), blending softly into the background.

### 3.1 Inner Header (Product Context)
*   **Top-Left:** Nike Swoosh logo in solid white.
*   **Top-Center (Category Switcher):**
    *   Segmented control pill with white background active state (`Women`) and inactive text elements (`Men`, `Kids`, etc.).
*   **Top-Right:** 
    *   User profile icon and Shopping Bag icon containing a red notification badge with the number `2`.

### 3.2 Typography & Product Details (Left Column)
*   **Product Title:** `NIKE PEGASUS PLUS`
    *   *Style:* Bold, uppercase, tracked out (condensed sans-serif). White color.
*   **Metadata Blocks:**
    *   `RELEASE DATE:` / `2024-10-06`
    *   `COLOR WAY:` / `SAIL/STARFISH-BLACK`
    *   *Style:* Small uppercase labels in muted white/translucent text; values in regular white.

### 3.3 Size Selector Matrix
*   **Label:** `SELECT SIZE (US)`
*   **Grid:** 5 columns × 2 rows of interactive size boxes.
    *   *Sizes available:* `8`, `8.5`, `9`, `9.5`, `10`, `10.5`, `11`, `11.5`, `12`, `12.5`.
    *   *Style:* Thin white borders, transparent background, centered white numbers. Active state highlights with a solid white fill and dark text.

### 3.4 Color Selector
*   **Label:** `SELECT COLOR`
*   **Swatches:** Three circular color options:
    1.  Magenta/Pink (Active ring around it)
    2.  Golden Yellow/Mustard
    3.  Dark Teal/Green

### 3.5 Central Hero Visual (3D Shoe Render)
*   **Visual Asset:** Photorealistic 3D render of the **Nike Pegasus Plus** running shoe suspended mid-air at a dynamic 3/4 angle.
    *   *Colors:* Vibrant pink upper, bright orange/coral midsole accents, white sole with speckled multicolour pattern and black rubber tread.
*   **Background Typography:** 
    *   Massive, subtle watermark text running horizontally across the background of the card: `PEGASUS` in large, low-opacity bold block letters.
*   **Scroll / Interaction Indicator:**
    *   Subtle animated double chevron (`>>`) pointing downwards at the bottom center of the inner container.

### 3.6 Pricing & Action Panel (Bottom Right)
*   **Price Pill:**
    *   Label: `Price` (small, muted)
    *   Value: `$298.00` (bold, larger white text)
    *   Contained within a rounded rectangle with a translucent glass background.
*   **CTA Button:**
    *   Text: `Pre Order`
    *   Style: Solid white background, rounded pill shape (`border-radius: 50px`), dark text, high contrast for primary conversion.

---

## 4. Footer

*   Positioned at the bottom of the full screen outside the main product card.
*   **Left:** `© 2024 All rights reserved` (Muted white/pinkish text).
*   **Center/Bottom Branding:** Repeated creator signature (`uiuxzaid` with the logo mark above it).

---

## 5. Animation & Interaction Specifications

*   **Mouse Cursor:**
    *   A custom hand/pointer cursor interacting directly with the floating shoe asset.
*   **Shoe Floating Animation:**
    *   *Effect:* Continuous subtle floating (y-axis translation of ±10px combined with a gentle 2-degree rotation loop) to simulate weightlessness.
    *   *Duration:* 4 seconds ease-in-out infinite loop.
*   **Hover States:**
    *   *Size Buttons:* Smooth background color transition to solid white with a scale-up of `1.05`.
    *   *Pre-Order Button:* Drop shadow elevation increase and slight scaling on hover (`scale(1.02)`).
    *   *Navigation Pills:* Backdrop blur intensity increase on hover.
