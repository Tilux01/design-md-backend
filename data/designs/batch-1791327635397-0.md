# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Healthcare Enterprise SaaS / Clinical Dashboard.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High structural discipline with generous whitespace padding in content cards, balanced against a dense, high-hierarchy navigation sidebar.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered SaaS UI utilizing flat surface contrast with crisp 1px borders (`#e2e8f0` equivalent) and subtle drop-shadows on stat cards (`0 4px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, emerald active indicators, clean SaaS borders, subtle pill avatar badges.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `2px 10px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#d1fae5`, text `#065f46`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
