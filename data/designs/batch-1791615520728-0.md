# Design System & Implementation Specification: Noomo Labs Hero Page

---

## 1. Overview & Aesthetic Direction

* **Visual Style:** Neo-brutalist tech meets organic surrealism (Creative Technologist / WebGL Portfolio).
* **Composition:** Monolithic, hyper-condensed typography anchored by a centered, high-fidelity bioluminescent 3D organism, framed by minimalist technical HUD (Heads-Up Display) navigation elements.
* **Viewport Target:** Fullscreen edge-to-edge canvas (`100vw` × `100vh`), no external window frames, no native scrollbars (`overflow: hidden`).

## 3. Typography & Font Specifications

### 3.1 Massive Headline (`NOOMO LABS`)
* **Primary Font:** *Druk Condensed Super*, *Impact*, or *Bebas Neue*.
* **Fallback Stack:** `"Druk Condensed", "Impact", "Arial Narrow Bold", sans-serif`.
* **Style:** All-Caps, Ultra-Condensed, Sans-Serif.
* **Weight:** 900 (Heavy/Black).
* **Font Size:** Fluid viewport calculation: `clamp(7rem, 21vw, 24rem)`.
* **Letter Spacing:** `-0.01em` (tightly kerned).
* **Line Height:** `0.85` (optical baseline centering).

### 3.2 Logo Typography (`noomo / labs`)
* **`noomo` Part:**
  * **Font:** Custom geometric bold lowercase sans (e.g., *GT America Expanded Black*, *Syne Bold*, or *Montserrat Black*).
  * **Weight:** 800 / 900.
  * **Size:** `1.15rem` (`18px`).
  * **Letter Spacing:** `-0.03em`.
* **Slash (`/`)**:
  * Light geometric slant, `#000000`.
* **`labs` Part:**
  * **Font:** Expressive, fast script/signature font (e.g., *Brittany Signature*, *Caveat*, *Reenie Beanie*, or *Segoe Script*).
  * **Style:** Italic, handwritten cursive cursive style, tilted upward ~8°.
  * **Weight:** 400.
  * **Size:** `1.35rem` (`21px`).

### 3.3 Technical HUD & Interface Elements
* **Primary Font:** *PP Neue Montreal Mono*, *Space Mono*, or *JetBrains Mono*.
* **Fallback Stack:** `"Space Mono", "Courier New", monospace`.
* **Style:** Uppercase.
* **Weight:** 500 (Medium).
* **Font Size:** `0.7rem` (`11px`).
* **Letter Spacing:** `0.08em` to `0.12em`.

### 3.4 Bottom Center Tagline
* **Primary Font:** *Druk Wide Bold*, *Monument Extended*, or *Eurostile Bold Condensed*.
* **Fallback Stack:** `"Arial Narrow Bold", "Helvetica Condensed", sans-serif`.
* **Style:** Uppercase, Condensed, Bold.
* **Weight:** 700 / 800.
* **Font Size:** `clamp(0.75rem, 1.1vw, 1.05rem)`.
* **Line Height:** `1.2`.
* **Letter Spacing:** `0.02em`.

## 4. Background & Surface Texture

1. **Base Layer:** Solid `#E5E5E8`.
2. **Radial Lighting:** Centered radial gradient behind the text:
   `radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 45%, #F4F4F6 0%, #E5E5E8 75%)`.
3. **Fine Micro-Grid / Noise Texture:**
   * CSS micro-dot grid pattern:
     ```css
     background-image: radial-gradient(rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08) 1px, transparent 1px);
     background-size: 16px 16px;
     ```
   * Subtle SVG film grain / noise overlay at `2.5%` opacity with blend mode `multiply`.
4. **Ambient Dust Particles:**
   * Single soft-white circular orb floating at coordinates `X: 36%`, `Y: 82%`, size `14px × 14px`, `opacity: 0.85`, soft blurred edges (`filter: blur(0.5px)`).

## 6. Central Visual Asset: The Jellyfish

### 6.1 Visual Description
* **Asset Format:** Transparent background (Alpha channel PNG/WebP or WebGL dynamic 3D model).
* **Coloration:**
  * **Umbrella / Bell:** Highly glossy, translucent bulbous cap in rich orchid pink / neon magenta (`#D952A5` to `#F472B6`).
  * **Mantle Surface:** Distinct radial organic tiger-stripes / veining in deep purple/black (`#3B0764`), running from the top apex downward toward the rim.
  * **Oral Arms & Core:** Bioluminescent crystalline cyan, soft periwinkle blue (`#82B2DE`), and violet core filaments.
  * **Tentacles:** Ultra-fine, hair-thin wavy filaments extending downwards with translucent pink-to-blue gradient tips.
* **Material Properties:** Wet specular reflections, subsurface scattering (SSS) allowing light to transmit through the translucent edges.

### 6.2 Placement & Alignment
* **Anchor Point:** Exact center of the viewport horizontally (`left: 50%`, `transform: translateX(-50%)`).
* **Vertical Position:**
  * The top of the bell sits at approximately `22%` from the top of the viewport.
  * The bell's lower rim hovers just above the vertical midpoint of the `NOOMO LABS` letters.
  * The long trailing tentacles drape straight down through the center, overlapping the inner strokes of the letters `M`, `O`, and `L`, terminating at roughly `82%` viewport height (hovering just above the bottom lightning bolt icon).
* **Rotation:** `0deg` tilt (upright, swimming straight toward the viewer with a minor upward perspective angle).
* **Z-Index Layering:**
  * Background layer: `z-index: 1`
  * Hero Text (`NOOMO LABS`): `z-index: 2`
  * Jellyfish: `z-index: 3` (renders in front of the text, creating depth).

## 8. Responsive Behavior

| Breakpoint | Typography Adjustments | Layout Adjustments |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Desktop (> 1024px)** | Full scale as specified above. | Fixed HUD elements along all 4 viewport edges. |
| **Tablet (768px - 1024px)** | Hero font drops to `16vw`; bottom copy scales to `0.8rem`. | Side HUD rails move closer to edges (`16px`). |
| **Mobile (< 768px)** | Hero text breaks onto two stacked lines (`NOOMO` on line 1, `LABS` on line 2) to maintain massive scale; side rails (HUD and ruler) hide. | Jellyfish scales to `65%` of original width, centered directly over the stacked words. |
