# Design Specification: iPhone 12 Pro Hero Page

## 1. Layout & Viewport
* **Display Mode:** Fullscreen (100vw, 100vh), overflow hidden.
* **Base Theme:** Dark, minimalist modern tech aesthetic.
* **Background:** Radial/linear gradient from deep slate cyan/blue (`#23353e`) at top-left to dark navy charcoal (`#16222a`) at bottom-right.

---

## 2. Typography
* **Primary Font:** SF Pro Display / SF Pro Text
* **Fallback Stack:** `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`
* **Hierarchy:**
  * **Product Eyebrow:** Small, regular weight, muted silver (`#9cb3c2`), ~14px.
  * **Hero Headline:** Medium/bold weight, pure white (`#ffffff`), ~36px–42px, tight letter spacing.
  * **Sub-navigation:** Small regular, light muted gray (`#b0c4cf`), ~12px.
  * **Footer/Legal Text:** Extra small, muted gray (`#8a9ea8`), ~11px.

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### Header Navigation
* **Placement:** Fixed at top, full width, padded (~40px padding-x, ~30px padding-y).
* **Left Group:**
  * Apple Icon: White/light silver, ~18px height.
  * Hamburger Menu: Three horizontal lines, ~20px width, placed adjacent to Apple logo.
* **Right Group:**
  * Search Icon: Minimalist magnifying glass icon, white/light silver, ~18px.

### Hero Text & Action Area
* **Placement:** Left-aligned, positioned around 35% from the top, 10% from the left.
* **Content:**
  * Text line 1: `iPhone 12 Pro`
  * Text line 2: `It’s a leap year.`
* **CTA Group (Inline/Offset to the right of headline):**
  * Text Link: `Overview`
  * Text Link: `Tech Specs`
  * Button: `View pricing` (Pill shape, background `#0071e3`, text color `#ffffff`, compact padding ~6px 14px, rounded ~20px).

### Bottom-Left Metadata
* **Index/Metric:** `115` displayed in medium weight, light blue-gray (~16px), positioned at ~70% vertical height.
* **Pricing Note:** Positioned at bottom-left corner (~40px from bottom, ~10% from left).
  * Text: `From $41.62/mo. or $999 before trade-in.¹`

---

## 4. Hero Imagery & Asset Specs

* **Asset Type:** Transparent PNG (backgroundless).
* **Subject:** Dual iPhone 12 Pro units in Pacific Blue finish.
  * **Front Phone (Left):** Display angled toward the viewer (~35° tilt), showing the Pacific Blue dynamic ribbon wallpaper and flat stainless steel edge.
  * **Rear Phone (Right):** Overlapping behind the front phone, matte frosted glass back visible with triple-camera bump, LiDAR sensor, and polished Apple logo.
* **Placement & Size:**
  * Anchored to bottom-right quadrant.
  * Extends from bottom-center toward top-right corner.
  * Covers approximately 55–60% of total viewport area.
* **Lighting/Shadows:** High-contrast studio rim lighting on steel edges; soft ambient drop shadow beneath the devices.
