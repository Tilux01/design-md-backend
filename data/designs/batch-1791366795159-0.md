# Design Specification: "LONS" Camera E-Commerce Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Layout

### Color Palette
*   **Background (Canvas):** `#121212` (Dark Charcoal / Near Black)
*   **Primary Text (Headings):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
*   **Secondary Text (Body/Descriptions):** `#B3B3B3` (Light Muted Gray)
*   **Accent/Highlight Text (Orange-Brown Specs):** `#C28B5C` (Muted Warm Brown / Tan)
*   **Navigation & UI Elements:** `#FFFFFF` (Active), `#808080` (Inactive/Hover)

### Typography
*   **Font Family:** Inter, Roboto, or system sans-serif equivalent.
*   **Logo:** Bold, clean sans-serif. The letter "O" is replaced with a camera aperture icon.
*   **Navigation Menu:** Regular weight, ~14px, tracked out slightly.
*   **Hero Heading ("Canon Lens"):** Bold weight, ~42px, tight line-height.
*   **Body Copy:** Regular weight, ~14px, relaxed line-height (~1.5).
*   **Product Specs (Annotations):** Regular weight, ~11-12px, high tracking/line-height for technical aesthetic.

### Layout Structure
*   **View:** Full-screen responsive viewport (1440px standard desktop width assumed).
*   **Grid:** Asymmetrical layout split roughly into two halves:
    *   **Left Column:** Branding, navigation, primary hero text, and a carousel/card preview of lenses.
    *   **Right Column:** Hero photography featuring a camera body, standalone lenses, and technical leader lines (callouts).

---

## 2. Component Specifications

### A. Header & Navigation Bar
*   **Position:** Fixed top, aligned with main content padding (approx. 60px side margins).
*   **Left:** 
    *   **Logo:** Text reads `L O N S` where the `O` features an internal 6-blade aperture graphic in white.
*   **Center:**
    *   **Nav Links:** `Home` (with a curved white underline indicator), `Products`, `Gallery`, `About Us`. Spacing between links is generous (~32px).
*   **Right:**
    *   **Search Icon:** Minimalist magnifying glass outline (`#FFFFFF`).

---

### B. Left Section (Hero Content)
*   **Main Heading:**
    *   `Canon Lens`
*   **Paragraph Copy:**
    *   `For sharper shots, more detail and more zoom.`
    *   `feel the millions of colors in your eyes` (The word "colors" or the secondary line uses the accent color `#C28B5C`).
*   **Carousel Component (Bottom Left):**
    *   A horizontal carousel showing lens cards against a dark gray container (`#1A1A1A`).
    *   **Active Card:** Displays a top-down view of a blue-tinted lens element with text overlay:
        *   `77 mm`
        *   `24mm`
        *   `1:1.4 L`
    *   **Pagination Dots:** Three small dots below the carousel (Active dot is white/pill-shaped, inactive dots are small gray circles).

---

### C. Right Section (Visuals & Annotations)
*   **Primary Camera Object:**
    *   A modern DSLR/Mirrorless camera body (resembling a Sony Alpha style body with "SONY" branding visible on the prism housing) fitted with a Canon EF lens. Positioned at an isometric/dynamic angle in the upper-right quadrant.
*   **Standalone Lens Object:**
    *   A secondary Canon EF 50mm lens floating/positioned lower in the right section, angled slightly upward.
*   **Technical Callouts (Leader Lines):**
    *   **Top Callout (Camera Lens):**
        *   A thin white line extends from the camera lens barrel to a text block on the left.
        *   **Text:**
            ```text
            Canon Lens
            EF 40mm
            1:2.8
            Made in Japan
            ```
    *   **Bottom Callout (Standalone Lens):**
        *   A thin white line extends from the 50mm lens edge to a text block on the right.
        *   **Text:**
            ```text
            Canon Lens
            EF 50mm
            1:1.4
            Made in Japan
            ```

---

## 3. Responsive Behavior & Interaction Notes
*   **Hover States:** Navigation links shift from gray to pure white. Carousel elements feature subtle drop shadows and depth layers.
*   **Background Depth:** Subtle radial gradients or vignettes centered behind the camera and lenses to create depth against the flat `#121212` background.
