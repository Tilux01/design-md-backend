# Design Specification: Mtouch Music App

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Theme:** Dark Mode.
*   **Primary Background:** Deep Black (`#000000`) to Dark Charcoal Gradient (`#121212`).
*   **Accent Color:** Vibrant Purple (`#8A2BE2` or `#7B2CBF`).
*   **Secondary Accent:** Warm Gold/Orange (`#FFB703`) used for the fingerprint icon and specific highlights.
*   **Text Colors:** 
    *   Primary: White (`#FFFFFF`)
    *   Secondary: Light Grey (`#B3B3B3`)
    *   Muted: Dark Grey (`#535353`)

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Heading Font:** A high-contrast, elegant Serif font (e.g., *Playfair Display*, *Cormorant Garamond*, or *Bodoni*).
    *   *Fallback:* Times New Roman, Serif.
*   **Body/UI Font:** A clean, modern Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Montserrat*, or *SF Pro Display*).
    *   *Fallback:* Arial, Helvetica, Sans-serif.
*   **Text Styles:**
    *   Hero Headings: Large, thin weight, Serif.
    *   Section Headers: Semi-bold, Sans-Serif, size 18-20px.
    *   Body Text: Regular, Sans-Serif, size 14px.

## 3. Visual Assets (Images & Icons)
*   **Logo (Mtouch):** 
    *   *Description:* A stylized purple musical note (quaver) where the bottom "head" of the note is replaced by an orange/gold fingerprint icon. 
    *   *Properties:* Backgroundless (PNG/SVG). The fingerprint has concentric circular ridges.
*   **Album Art:** 
    *   High-resolution square images with subtle rounded corners (approx. 8px).
    *   *Specific Image:* A dark, moody photo of a person in profile, bathed in warm orange light, used for the "Losing Less" track.
*   **Artist Avatars:** Circular crops of artist portraits.
*   **Icons:** Thin-stroke line icons for navigation (Home, Search, Library, Profile).

---

## 4. Screen-by-Screen Breakdown

### A. Splash Screen
*   **Background:** Pure black.
*   **Centerpiece:** The Mtouch Logo (Musical note + Fingerprint) centered vertically and horizontally.
*   **Text:** "Mtouch" in a clean Sans-Serif font below the logo.
*   **Detail:** A very faint, dark purple nebula-like glow in the bottom right corner.

### B. Hero/Welcome Screen ("The clean energy hub...")
*   **Background:** Dark gradient.
*   **Top Section:** Large Serif text: "The clean energy hub of the future".
*   **Subtext:** Small Sans-Serif text: "Why aren't more people investing in African green energy?"
*   **Featured Card:** 
    *   A large purple rounded rectangle (`border-radius: 30px`).
    *   Contains a floating album art image rotated approximately 5 degrees clockwise.
    *   Text: "Losing Less - Obama" (Serif font).
    *   Bottom Right: A circular play button with a purple outline and a small white arrow.

### C. Home/Discovery Screen
*   **Header:** "Good Evening" (Sans-Serif) with a small profile avatar in the top left and a notification bell in the top right.
*   **Horizontal Scroll (Artists):** Circular images of artists with names underneath.
*   **Grid (Recently Played):** 2x2 grid of square album covers with titles.
*   **Bottom Section:** "Top Indie Playlist" header with a large rectangular card showing a playlist cover.
*   **Bottom Nav:** Dark bar with icons for Home (active), Search, Library, and Profile.

### D. Now Playing Screen
*   **Background:** A blurred version of the current album art.
*   **Top:** "Now Playing" label and a chevron-down icon.
*   **Center:** Large, high-quality album art (Square).
*   **Track Info:** Title (Serif, large) and Artist (Sans-Serif, medium).
*   **Controls:** 
    *   Progress bar: A thin purple line with a small circle indicator.
    *   Playback: Large circular purple Play/Pause button in the center, flanked by "Skip Back" and "Skip Forward" icons.
*   **Queue:** A "Coming Up Next" list at the bottom with small thumbnails.

### E. Profile Screen
*   **Header:** "Profile" centered at the top.
*   **User Info:** Circular profile picture on the left, Name ("Sandy Cohen") and "Pro Member" badge on the right.
*   **List Items:** "Customer ID", "Change Password", "Change Email", "Email Preferences". Each has a thin divider line and a chevron-right icon.
*   **Toggle:** "Connect with Facebook" featuring a purple toggle switch in the 'On' position.
*   **Footer Links:** Red "Delete Account" link at the bottom.

### F. Settings/Playback Screen
*   **Header:** "Playback" centered.
*   **Toggles:** 
    *   "Offline", "Display Playback", "Autoplay", "Show Unplayable Songs", "Enable Audio Normalization".
    *   All toggles are purple when active.
*   **Volume Slider:** 
    *   Label: "Volume Level".
    *   A gradient slider bar (Purple to Pink).
    *   Markers underneath: "Loud", "Normal", "Quiet".

---

## 5. Layout & Spacing
*   **Padding:** Standardized 20px horizontal padding for all screens.
*   **Corner Radius:** 
    *   Large Cards: 24px - 32px.
    *   Buttons/Small Cards: 12px.
    *   Avatars: 50% (Circle).
*   **Transitions:** Smooth fades between screens; the Now Playing screen should feel like an overlay sliding up from the bottom.
