# Design Specification: TECHWEAR Landing Page

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Theme:** Dark, futuristic, techwear-aesthetic.
*   **Layout:** Full-screen (100vw, 100vh) single-page hero section.
*   **Container:** Overflow hidden to prevent scrolling. The design uses a grid/flex layout with absolute positioning for decorative elements.
*   **Background:** 
    *   The base background is a deep charcoal/black (`#121212`).
    *   There is a subtle "glitch" texture overlay consisting of horizontal scan lines and noise.
    *   Large, low-opacity background letters: A large "P" (top left-center), "E" (top right), and "R" (bottom left) in a dark grey, slightly lighter than the background.

## 2. Color Palette
*   **Primary Dark:** `#121212` (Main background)
*   **Accent Orange:** `#FF5722` (Used for the sun, the cap, and the split background in the preview)
*   **Primary White:** `#FFFFFF` (Main text and logo)
*   **Secondary Grey:** `#888888` (Vertical navigation and social icons)
*   **Glitch Accents:** Cyan (`#00FFFF`) and Red (`#FF0000`) used sparingly in thin horizontal lines.

## 3. Typography
*   **Main Title ("TECHWEAR"):**
    *   *Font:* A wide, futuristic, blocky sans-serif. 
    *   *Recommended:* **"Michroma"** or **"Syncopate"** (Bold).
    *   *Fallback:* `Impact`, `Arial Black`, `sans-serif`.
    *   *Styling:* Uppercase, high letter-spacing (approx 5px).
*   **Logo ("TECH"):**
    *   *Font:* Custom geometric stencil font.
    *   *Recommended:* **"Orbitron"** (Black weight).
*   **Navigation & UI Elements:**
    *   *Font:* Clean, geometric sans-serif.
    *   *Recommended:* **"Rajdhani"** or **"Montserrat"**.
    *   *Fallback:* `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`.

## 4. Image Assets & Placement

### A. The "Sun" Background Element
*   **Description:** A large, perfectly circular disc. It has a grainy, stippled texture (noise) and a slight orange-to-dark-orange radial gradient.
*   **Placement:** Centered horizontally and vertically.
*   **Z-index:** Behind the character and the "TECHWEAR" text.

### B. The Character (Main Subject)
*   **Description:** An anime-style illustration of a male character. 
    *   **Hair:** Messy, vibrant blue hair.
    *   **Headwear:** A bright orange baseball cap with a white stylized "YS" or "75" logo. The cap is tilted forward, obscuring the eyes.
    *   **Clothing:** A black techwear-style oversized hoodie with white vertical stripes on the sleeves and a grey/white patterned front panel. Orange backpack straps are visible over the shoulders.
    *   **Style:** High-contrast cel-shading with clean line art.
*   **Transparency:** The image must be a **backgroundless PNG**.
*   **Placement:** Centered horizontally. The bottom of the character should be cut off by the bottom of the viewport.
*   **Rotation:** 0 degrees (straight vertical).
*   **Z-index:** In front of the "TECH" and "EAR" text (the text "TECHWEAR" is layered *behind* the character's head/torso but *in front* of the sun).

## 5. UI Components

### Top Navigation (Header)
*   **Logo (Top Left):** "TECH" in white. 24px padding from top and left.
*   **Buttons (Top Right):**
    *   **Sign In:** A pill-shaped button with a dark grey semi-transparent background (`rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`) and white text.
    *   **Language Toggle ("EN"):** A circular button to the right of "Sign In" with the same styling.

### Left Sidebar (Vertical Navigation)
*   **Orientation:** Rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise.
*   **Items:** HOME, GALLERY, TRENDS, SHOP, WORKSHOP, ABOUT.
*   **Styling:** All caps, secondary grey color, approx 12px font size, wide letter spacing.
*   **Placement:** Vertically centered on the far left edge.

### Right Sidebar (Pagination)
*   **Elements:** Four vertical dots. The second dot from the top is highlighted (larger or brighter white), indicating the current section.
*   **Placement:** Vertically centered on the far right edge.

### Footer Elements
*   **Bottom Left:** "R-18" text in white, clean sans-serif. 24px padding from bottom and left.
*   **Bottom Right:** Social Media Icons (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X). 
    *   *Styling:* Small, white, monochromatic icons.
    *   *Placement:* 24px padding from bottom and right.

## 6. Decorative Effects
*   **Text Layering:** The word "TECHWEAR" is split. "TECH" is on the left of the character, "EAR" is on the right. The character's body overlaps the middle of the word.
*   **Glitch Lines:** Randomly placed, very thin (1px to 3px height) horizontal bars in orange, cyan, and white. These should be placed both behind and in front of the character to create depth.
*   **Grain:** A global CSS noise filter or a transparent PNG grain overlay at low opacity (3-5%) to give the design a "printed" or "retro-tech" feel.
