# Design Specification: Jacket Masters E-Commerce Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport Settings
* **Layout:** Full-screen responsive single-page web app / e-commerce landing page.
* **Orientation:** Landscape / Desktop (Min-width: 1200px recommended).
* **Grid Structure:** CSS Flexbox or CSS Grid utilizing a 12-column layout with a central content offset to accommodate the floating hero product.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Primary Background (Gradient):**
  * Top Center / Radial Center: `#E88126` (Bright vibrant orange)
  * Mid-Tone / Transition: `#C76013` (Rich burnt orange)
  * Bottom/Outer Edges: `#8B3608` / `#5C2003` (Dark deep amber-brown vignette)
  * *CSS Representation:* `background: radial-gradient(circle at 55% 45%, #F49129 0%, #B8530B 50%, #5E2003 100%);`
* **Text Colors:**
  * **Primary Headings & Key Accents:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
  * **Body Copy & Secondary Subtitles:** Soft Off-White / Muted Cream (`#E2D5CD` or `#D4C4BC` at `0.85` opacity)
  * **Strikethrough Price:** Muted Warm Gray (`#A39087`)
* **UI Elements / Interactive Backgrounds:**
  * **Navigation Pill Background:** Translucent black overlay with heavy blur (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`)
  * **Active Pill / Button Background:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with dark text (`#1A1A1A`)
  * **Inactive / Standard Buttons:** Dark semi-transparent pill (`rgba(0,0,0,0.15)`)

---

## 3. Typography
* **Primary Font Family:** Clean, modern geometric sans-serif.
  * **Primary Stack:** `'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
  * **Fallback:** Any system sans-serif font matching modern geometric aesthetics (e.g., Arial, sans-serif).
* **Type Hierarchy:**
  * **Brand Logo ("JACKET MASTERS"):** 
    * Weight: Semi-Bold / Bold (`600`)
    * Size: `14px`
    * Letter-spacing: `0.05em`
    * Uppercase.
  * **Navigation Links ("PUFFER JACKET", "ALL PRODUCTS", etc.):**
    * Weight: Medium (`500`)
    * Size: `11px`
    * Letter-spacing: `0.06em`
    * Uppercase.
  * **Main Hero Headline ("Stand out Without trying"):**
    * Weight: Bold (`700`)
    * Size: `48px` – `56px`
    * Line-height: `1.1`
    * Letter-spacing: `-0.02em`
  * **Body Copy:**
    * Weight: Regular (`400`)
    * Size: `13px` – `14px`
    * Line-height: `1.6`
    * Color: `#E2D5CD`
  * **Pricing:**
    * Current Price (`$149`): Weight `700`, Size `32px`, Color `#FFFFFF`.
    * Original Price (`$199`): Weight `500`, Size `20px`, Color `#A39087`, `text-decoration: line-through`.
  * **Size Selector Numbers (36, 38, 40):**
    * Weight: Medium (`500`)
    * Size: `12px`
    * Color: `#FFFFFF`

---

## 4. Component Breakdown & Structure

### A. Header & Navigation
* **Top Left:** 
  * Logo container featuring a dark square box with the white lettermark `"JM"` and the text `"JACKET MASTERS"` adjacent in uppercase white.
* **Top Center (Floating Pill Navigation):**
  * A pill-shaped container (`border-radius: 50px`, frosted glass effect).
  * Contains navigation links: 
    * `PUFFER JACKET` (Highlighted with a white pill background and dark text).
    * `ALL PRODUCTS`
    * `ABOUT US`
    * `CONTACT`
* **Top Right:**
  * Icon set aligned horizontally: Cart icon (shopping bag) and Wishlist icon (heart) in clean white vector strokes (`18px` size).

### B. Left Content Area (Hero Copy & Actions)
* **Carousel Indicators:**
  * Two small circular arrow buttons (`<` and `>`) stacked horizontally above the headline to indicate image sliding functionality.
* **Headline:**
  * Two-line layout:
    * `"Stand out"` (White)
    * `"Without trying"` (White)
* **Paragraph Description:**
  * *"It’s not just about staying warm. It’s about stepping outside and instantly feeling confident, comfortable, and completely yourself. Designed to elevate even the simplest outfit, this jacket wraps you in lightweight warmth."*
* **Call-to-Action (CTA):**
  * Pill button reading `"Get the look >"` with a white background, dark bold text, and a rounded arrow indicator.

### C. Center Stage (Hero Product)
* **Primary Product Image:**
  * **Description:** High-resolution 3D-rendered or studio-photographed glossy metallic orange puffer jacket facing forward. The jacket features distinct horizontal baffling, a high stand-up collar, cuffed sleeves, and a rich, light-reflecting sheen with soft ambient drop-shadow underneath on the surface.
* **Sub-caption:**
  * Centered underneath the product: `"Confidence, wrapped in warmth"` in a clean, italicized or soft-weight body font.

### D. Right Side Panel (Product Details & Purchasing)
* **Pricing Block:**
  * Large prominent current price: **`$149`**
  * Strikethrough comparative price: **`$199`**
* **Size Selection Section:**
  * Label: `"Choose your size:"` (Small, muted uppercase text).
  * Size Options: Interactive circular buttons for **`36`**, **`38`**, and **`40`**. 
    * *Default state:* Dark translucent background with white text.
    * *Active state:* Solid white background with dark text (applied to size `36` by default).
* **Thumbnail Carousel / Alternate View:**
  * A small square thumbnail preview card at the bottom right showing a dark/charcoal variant of the jacket from a different angle, complete with rounded corners and subtle drop-shadow.

### E. Footer Area (Bottom Left)
* **Social Media Icons:**
  * Horizontal row of minimalist icons: Instagram, Facebook, Globe/Web icon, and Behance (`Be`), styled in semi-transparent white.

---

## 5. Interactive States & Micro-Interactions
* **Hover Effects:**
  * Navigation links shift opacity from `0.7` to `1.0`.
  * Size selector buttons scale up slightly (`transform: scale(1.05)`) and brighten on hover.
  * Primary CTA button shifts background or gains a soft outer glow.
* **Transitions:**
  * Smooth CSS transitions (`transition: all 0.3s ease`) applied to all interactive buttons, hovers, and state changes.
