# Visual Style & Design Specification
<!-- Exact 1:1 Production Clone for Coca-Cola Zero Dark Aesthetic -->

---

## 1. Global Scene & Canvas Architecture
* **Color Palette:** True Absolute Black (`#000000`) dominating the entire viewport.
* **Canvas Dimensions:** Full-screen edge-to-edge layout (`100vw` / `100vh`).
* **Composition:** A single, isolated floating object positioned centrally-diagonally, tilted at approximately **15° to 20° counter-clockwise** off vertical.
* **Depth & Layering:** Infinite black void background with a single foreground asset floating in mid-air. No grounding shadows on a surface—pure atmospheric suspension.

---

## 2. Typography & Branding Elements
* **Main Logotype:** 
  * **Text:** "Coca-Cola" (written in the signature Spencerian script).
  * **Layout Orientation:** Vertical running text (read bottom-to-top, tilted matching the can's angle).
  * **Rendering Style:** Embossed / Glossy Debossed overlay. The letters are styled with a wet gloss texture against the matte-satin black can body, creating a subtle tonal contrast (`#111111` gloss reflection on `#0d0d0d` matte).
* **Sub-branding / Variant Tag:**
  * **Text:** "ZERO"
  * **Font Family:** Clean, bold, ultra-modern sans-serif, all-caps, tracked/spaced out slightly.
  * **Container:** Enclosed inside a clean, thin white outlined pill/oval shape (`border: 1px solid #FFFFFF`).
  * **Placement:** Positioned to the right of the main logotype, vertically aligned with the upper half of the can.
  * **Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).

---

## 3. Core Product Asset: The Can
* **Form Factor:** Sleek, tall, modern cylindrical beverage can (slender "slim" can profile).
* **Body Finish:** Deep matte black / dark charcoal finish (`#0a0a0a`) with a soft-touch satin texture that catches minimal ambient light.
* **Top Ring & Lid:** 
  * **Color:** Vibrant, glossy electric red (`#E31937`).
  * **Finish:** Highly polished metallic sheen with subtle radial light reflections tracing the rim.
* **Lighting & Shading (The 3D Form):**
  * **Key Light:** Soft, diffused edge-lighting tracing the left contour of the can to define its cylindrical volume.
  * **Ambient Occlusion:** Deep shadow gradients concentrating heavily along the right side and bottom edges to sell the pitch-black environment.
  * **Highlights:** Subtle vertical specular streak running parallel to the body, emphasizing the smooth metallic curvature.

---

## 4. Animation & Motion Parameters (For Interactive/Video Implementation)
* **Float Physics:** Slow, gentle sine-wave floating motion (bobbing up and down by ~10px) combined with a microscopic rotational sway.
* **Lighting Interaction:** Specular highlights shift subtly across the glossy logotype and red rim as the asset rotates.
