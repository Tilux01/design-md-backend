# Design Specification: Edem Restaurant Landing Page

## 1. Canvas & Global Layout
- **Viewport**: 100vw × 100vh (Full-screen desktop layout, no device borders).
- **Base Background**: Deep matte black (`#0C0D0E`).
- **Texture**: Fine white salt grains dusted around the right-center region under the skillet.

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Background**: `#0C0D0E`
- **Text Primary (Headings)**: Off-white / Cream (`#F5F5F3`)
- **Text Secondary (Nav Links)**: Light neutral grey (`#C5C6C7`)
- **Brand Accent (Logo)**: Muted Champagne Gold (`#D4C39E`)
- **Button Border/Text**: Crisp light grey (`#E0E0E0`)

---

## 3. Typography
- **Brand / Hero Display Font**:
  - *Primary*: Artistic Display Serif (e.g., *Italiana*, *Cormorant Garamond*, or *Roslindale*)
  - *Fallback*: `Didot, "Bodoni MT", serif`
  - *Styling*: High-contrast strokes, fluid ligatures, italicized aesthetic.
- **Navigation & UI Font**:
  - *Primary*: Minimal Geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Montserrat*)
  - *Fallback*: `"Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`
  - *Styling*: Regular weight, slightly expanded letter spacing (`tracking-wide`).

---

## 4. Navigation Bar
- **Positioning**: Fixed or static top bar, full width with standard horizontal padding (~80px left/right, 40px top).
- **Structure**:
  - **Left**: "Edem" logo (Font size: ~28px, Color: `#D4C39E`, font-style: italic/display serif).
  - **Right**: Horizontal navigation links spaced evenly (~32px apart):
    - Items: `Home`, `About Us`, `Menu`, `Events`, `Reservation`
    - Font size: ~14px, Color: `#C5C6C7`, Weight: 400.

---

## 5. Hero Section

### Left Column: Text & CTA
- **Alignment**: Left-aligned, vertically centered.
- **Padding Left**: ~80px.
- **Main Heading**:
  ```text
  Welcome
  to Edem
  restaurant
  ```
  - *Font*: Display Serif
  - *Font Size*: ~72px–80px
  - *Line Height*: 1.15
  - *Color*: `#F5F5F3`
- **CTA Button**:
  - **Label**: "Reservation"
  - **Style**: Ghost/Outlined button.
  - **Border**: 1px solid `#E0E0E0`
  - **Corner Radius**: 6px
  - **Padding**: 10px 28px
  - **Font Size**: 14px, Sans-Serif
  - **Margin Top**: ~36px below the heading

---

### Right Column: Focal Image
- **Image Description**:
  - Top-down/overhead shot of a seasoned, golden-brown seared pork chop inside a seasoned black cast-iron skillet.
  - Garnish: Fresh rosemary sprigs and coarse cracked peppercorns/sea salt inside the pan.
  - Exterior: Fine sea-salt crystals scattered across the dark surface beneath and around the pan.
- **Format**: Backgroundless (transparent PNG) or seamlessly blended into `#0C0D0E`.
- **Placement**:
  - Vertically centered, positioned toward the right edge.
  - Handle orientation: Angled downward toward the bottom-right corner (~135° angle / 4 o'clock position).
  - Size: Occupies approximately 45–50% of the screen width.
