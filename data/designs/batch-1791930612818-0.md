# Design Specification: Reimagined History Landing Page

## 1. Typography & Colors

### Color Palette
- **Background:** `#F9F9FB` (Light clean grey/off-white)
- **Primary Text:** `#111111` (Deep black)
- **Muted Text:** `#737373` (Neutral gray)
- **Dark Accent Panel:** `#0A0A0A` (Near black)
- **Border / Lines:** `#E5E5E5` (Light neutral)
- **Flame Accent:** Warm orange/yellow tones (`#FFA726`)

### Typography
- **Primary Font:** *Neue Haas Grotesk*, *Inter*, or *Helvetica Neue*
- **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
- **Text Styles:**
  - **Logo ("Si"):** 22px, Bold (700)
  - **Navigation Links:** 12px, Regular (400), tracking slightly wide
  - **Eyebrow ("realreason."):** 20px; "real" (Bold 700), "reason." (Regular 400)
  - **Hero Headline:** 56px–64px, Bold (700), line-height: 1.05
  - **Dark Panel Title ("JACKSON WAGNER"):** 10px, Bold (700), uppercase, tracking: 1px
  - **Dark Panel Subtext:** 9px, Regular (400), color: `#A0A0A0`
  - **Pagination / ".more":** 11px, Bold (700)

---

## 2. Page Layout

- **Viewport:** Full screen (`100vw`, `100vh`), non-scrolling single viewport.
- **Structure:** 
  - Top: Global Header (fixed height ~70px).
  - Center: Split Hero Content (Left: Copy, Right: Candle visual).
  - Bottom: Split Footer (Left: Dark Card, Right: Navigation controls).

---

## 3. Component Details

### Header
- **Container:** Full width, horizontal flex layout, padding: `32px 48px`.
- **Left:** Logo `Si`.
- **Nav Center-Left:** Links spaced at 32px intervals: `Ghana`, `Canada`, `United States`.
- **Nav Center-Right:** `United Arab Emirates`.
- **Right Icons:** Search (magnifying glass) and Hamburger Menu (two horizontal bars), spaced 24px apart.

### Hero Section
- **Left Text Column:**
  - Position: Left: `10%`, Top: `40%`.
  - Eyebrow text: `realreason.`
  - Main Heading: Two-line stack:
    ```
    Reimagined
    History.
    ```
- **Right Visual Column:**
  - Position: Right: `15%`, vertically aligned to bottom baseline.
  - Features the featured candle asset and its grounded horizontal contact shadow extending slightly leftward.

### Bottom Section

#### Left: Black Info Card
- **Dimensions:** Width `46%`, Height ~80px. Fixed to the bottom-left corner.
- **Background:** `#0A0A0A`, text color white.
- **Internal Elements:**
  - **Avatar:** Circular (36px diameter), grayscale portrait engraving of a classical historical bust/philosopher.
  - **Text Group:** 
    - Heading: `JACKSON WAGNER`
    - Description: `Looking For Your Own Printing Solution / right for you?`
  - **Divider / Progress Bar:** 1px horizontal line with a slightly highlighted middle segment.
  - **Social / Action Icons:** Instagram icon and Share branching icon grouped on the far right.

#### Right: Carousel Controls
- **Alignment:** Aligned horizontally with the bottom card on the right side.
- **Elements (Left to Right):**
  - **Pagination Dots:** Three small dots (two hollow `#CCCCCC`, one solid `#111111`).
  - **Navigation Buttons:** Two circular outlined buttons (`32px` diameter) containing left (`<`) and right (`>`) chevron icons.
  - **Text Link:** `.more` placed adjacent to the carousel buttons.

---

## 4. Imagery & Assets

### 1. Scented Candle Asset
- **Type:** PNG cutout (transparent background).
- **Placement:** Positioned on the right third of the screen, resting on the floor plane.
- **Description:** A short, wide amber-brown glass jar containing white wax with a centrally burning, glowing flame. The jar body is wrapped in a thick, rugged cylinder of natural tree bark, secured around the center with a thin, double-strand rustic twine rope tied into a bow.
- **Shadow:** Soft directional ground shadow cast subtly underneath and toward the left.

### 2. Historical Figure Avatar
- **Type:** PNG / circular-cropped raster graphic.
- **Placement:** Inside the bottom-left black bar, vertically centered.
- **Description:** High-contrast, monochromatic circular line engraving of a bearded classical thinker (resembling a Greek philosopher bust) wearing a monocle or eyeglass.
