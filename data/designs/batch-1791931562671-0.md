# Design Specification: Dark Minimalist Portfolio / Agency Landing Page

## 1. Overall Layout & Architecture

* **Type**: Fullscreen single-page hero section (100vw, 100vh, `overflow: hidden`).
* **Grid**: Asymmetrical two-column split.
  * **Left Column (~58%)**: Darkest tone, navigation, typography, branding accents, CTA.
  * **Right Column (~42%)**: Photography section with integrated floating CTA.
* **Base Color Palette**:
  * Primary Dark (Left BG): `#191A1D`
  * Secondary Dark (Right BG): `#222428`
  * Accent Red/Orange: `#F24E1E`
  * Primary Text: `#FFFFFF`
  * Secondary Text (Muted): `#8D9096`
  * Subdued Accents: `#2E3136`

---

## 2. Typography

* **Primary Font Family**: *Gilroy*, *Montserrat*, or *Poppins*.
  * *Fallback*: `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
* **Type Hierarchy**:
  * **Category Subtitle**: 13px, Medium (500), Color: `#8D9096`, tracking: `0.5px`.
  * **Hero Title ("Design Technology.")**: 36px–40px, Bold (700), Color: `#FFFFFF`.
  * **Outline Display Text ("DESIGN")**: 72px–80px, Font-weight 800, Transparent fill with a 1.5px stroke (`-webkit-text-stroke`).
    * "D", "E", and "N": Stroke color `#F24E1E`.
    * "S", "I", "G": Stroke color `#FFFFFF`.
  * **Vertical Title ("Brands.")**: 84px–96px, Extra Bold (800), Color: `#FFFFFF`.
  * **Vertical Date**: 10px–11px, Regular (400), Color: `#63666D`, tracking: `2px`.
  * **CTA ("About us")**: 12px–13px, Semi-Bold (600), Color: `#FFFFFF`.

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation & Social Sidebar (Left Rail)
* **Menu Toggle (Top-Left)**:
  * 3 horizontal lines (hamburger) with staggered widths (full, medium, short) in `#585A60`.
  * Margin: `40px` from top/left borders.
* **Social Links (Vertical Stack, Far Left)**:
  * Icons: Instagram, Behance, Dribbble, Pinterest.
  * Layout: Centered vertically, stacked with `24px` spacing.
  * Color: `#8D9096`, hover: `#FFFFFF`.
  * Size: `16px × 16px`.

### B. Left Content Area
* **Top Accent**: Small horizontal orange dash (`#F24E1E`, `24px × 2px`) positioned above subtitle.
* **Content Stack**:
  * "Love on UI & UX" placed `16px` below dash.
  * "Design Technology." directly below subtitle (`8px` gap).
  * Outlined "DESIGN" text with a trailing horizontal line (`32px × 1.5px`, `#F24E1E`) aligned to its baseline.
* **CTA Block ("About us")**:
  * Horizontal row positioned below main headline group.
  * "About us" text followed by a horizontal dotted leader (`......`) in `#44474D`.
  * Terminal element: A circle (`18px` diameter) with a 1.5px border in `#F24E1E` containing an orange solid dot in the center.

### C. Center Division & Texture
* **Dot Grid**: Subtle pattern of tiny dots (`#282A2E`) filling the vertical seam area.
* **Vertical Date**: `"28:03:2018"` rotated `-90deg` (reading upwards), positioned near bottom-center.
* **Vertical Main Feature Text ("Brands.")**:
  * Rotated `-90deg` (bottom-to-top orientation).
  * Aligned precisely on the dividing axis between columns.
  * Includes a solid orange period (`#F24E1E`) at the end.

### D. Right Visual Section
* **Hero Image**:
  * **Subject**: Close-to-mid portrait of a Black man with a beard, glasses, and hair in a neat top knot, dressed in a tailored suit and tie with arms crossed, facing right.
  * **Style**: Monochromatic / desaturated, low-key lighting, dark charcoal background seamlessly blended into `#222428`.
  * **Placement**: Full-bleed within the right 42% column, vertically centered.
* **Floating Action Button (Bottom-Right)**:
  * Size: `56px × 56px` square.
  * Background: Solid vibrant orange (`#F24E1E`).
  * Icon: Thin rightward-pointing arrow (`→`) in `#FFFFFF`, centered.
  * Placement: Floats `40px` from bottom-right corner.
