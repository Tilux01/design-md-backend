# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Neo-Surrealism & Gen-Z E-Commerce.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-impact editorial whitespace, oversized bubbly typography mixed with soft pastel atmosphere.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean flat design layered with subtle translucent glassmorphic navigation and pill inputs.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Inflated bubble 3D text effects, soft cloud atmospheric gradients, rounded organic pill inputs.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Arrow Icon (→) -> Use `&rarr;` or SVG.
  - Bullet (•) -> Use `&bull;`.
