# UI Design Specification: DOMINGO Juice Product Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
- **View:** Full-screen responsive web layout.
- **Background Color:** Vibrant Royal Blue (`#2563eb` or approximate Tailwind `blue-600`).
- **Layout Architecture:**
  - **Header:** Fixed top navigation bar containing branding, category links, and utility links.
  - **Hero Section:** Centered product presentation featuring asymmetric typography, floating imagery elements (oranges and tomatoes), promotional copy, interactive disclosure tabs on the right, and a numbering indicator ("01").
  - **Transition Area:** Organic/torn-edge white paper texture separating the hero section from the sub-content below.
  - **Sub-Section:** "Our daily bundles" teaser featuring circular thumbnail representations grouped with addition (`+`) operators.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
- **Primary Headings & Brand Logo:** 
  - Style: Clean, geometric, high-impact Sans-Serif (reminiscent of *Montserrat*, *Helvetica Neue*, or *Inter* bold weights).
  - Letter spacing: Generous tracking on large background text (`RENEW`).
- **Body Text:** 
  - Style: Neutral Sans-Serif (*Inter* or *Roboto* regular weight).
- **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation Header
- **Logo ("DOMINGO"):**
  - Position: Top-left.
  - Appearance: Bold, uppercase sans-serif text in white.
- **Nav Links:**
  - Items: `Shop now`, `Cleanses`, `Daily Bundles`, `Collections`.
  - Appearance: Medium weight, white text, subtle hover states.
- **Utility Actions:**
  - Items: `Account`, Shopping Bag Icon (`🛍️`).
  - Position: Top-right.

---

### B. Hero Section
- **Background Typography ("01" & "RENEW"):**
  - "01": Small numeric prefix displayed above the main title in light blue/cyan.
  - "RENEW": Massive, semi-transparent background display text rendered in a light cyan/mint tone (`#a5f3fc`), sitting behind the central bottle.
- **Central Product Imagery (Juice Bottle):**
  - Description: A clear amber-brown glass bottle with a black screw cap, filled with an amber-orange juice. Features a colourful, modern illustrative label showcasing a stylized orange sphere and geometric blocks of blue and pink.
- **Floating Visual Accents:**
  - **Tomatoes:** Small, vibrant red cherry tomatoes floating on the left side of the bottle, adding a visual cue to the "spiced/savory" combination.
  - **Orange Slice:** A fresh, juicy half-orange wedge floating on the right side of the bottle.
- **Promotional Copy (Left Column):**
  - Headline/Description text: *"The perfect combination of fresh ingredients to spice up your morning"*
  - Font size: Medium, clean left alignment.
- **Interactive Accordion / Options (Right Column):**
  - Row 1: `How to consume` + (Expand icon)
  - Row 2: `Benefits` + (Expand icon)
  - Row 3: `Buying options` + (Expand icon)
  - Layout: Clean horizontal divider lines extending from the right edge toward the center.

---

### C. Transition & Lower Section
- **Torn Paper Divider:**
  - Appearance: A distressed, white, organic torn-edge background band spanning the bottom width of the viewport, creating depth.
- **"Our daily bundles" Section:**
  - Heading: Bold dark text (`#1e293b`) reading "Our daily bundles".
  - Bundle preview circles: Three circular avatar-style containers set against pastel backgrounds (purple, blue, orange) showing miniature product/ingredient graphics separated by `+` symbols.
