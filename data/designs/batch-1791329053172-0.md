# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Fintech & Multi-Currency SaaS Dashboard with Soft Neomorphism / Modern Enterprise Polish.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High functional density with balanced component padding (12px–24px), organized in modular card containers separated by 16px–24px gaps, maintaining a calm, data-forward workspace.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered depth using crisp white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) over a soft off-white/light grey canvas (`#F4F5F7`), with subtle 1px border lines (`#E2E8F0`) and muted emerald accent glows for financial indicators.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking status tags, dynamic chart gradient bars, multi-currency flag pills, live ambient currency tickers.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding (`4px 10px`), font-size (`11px`), font-weight (`600`), tracking (`0.05em`), border-radius (`9999px`), background translucency (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`), border width (`1px`).
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Up Arrow (↑) -> Use `&uparrow;` or SVG
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
