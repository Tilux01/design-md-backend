# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist Neo-Brutalism with high-contrast monochrome framing, stark vertical structural dividers, and tactile typography integration.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Generous horizontal and vertical whitespace with precise grid alignment, zero extraneous decorations, focusing attention entirely on core typographic features and product photography.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, border-defined architectural surfaces with razor-sharp structural dividers rather than heavy drop-shadows or multi-layered modals.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Vertical typographic side-labels, structural partition borders, giant stylized background typography, overlapping product cutouts.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(10,10,10,0.05)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
