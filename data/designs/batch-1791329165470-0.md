# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise SaaS Dashboard Metric Widget.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-data ratio with structured padding (`24px` to `32px`), emphasizing clean visual hierarchy and focus on core financial metric representation.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Single-layer container card elevation. Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) on neutral light gray app canvas (`#F1F3F6`), framed with subtle inner border separation and soft ambient card boundary.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Clean rounded pill dropdown triggers (`Monthly`, `Filter`, `Add widget`), percentage increase indicator badges, rounded bar charts with muted projection tracks.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 10px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#F8FAFC`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Currency ($) -> Use `$` or `&#36;`.
  - Up Arrow (▲ / ↗) -> Use SVG path.
