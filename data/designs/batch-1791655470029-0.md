# UI/UX Design Specification: Dark Neumorphic Chat Application

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
- **View Type**: Full-screen split view showcasing two distinct screens simultaneously (Main Chats List on the left, Active Chat Conversation on the right).
- **Global Background Color**: Dark charcoal/off-black (`#16191D`).
- **Surface Elevation Strategy**: Neumorphic / Skeuomorphic inset and drop shadows utilizing dual-layer shadow rendering (Dark directional shadow bottom-right: `#0B0C0E`, Inner/Top-left soft highlight: `#22272E`).
- **Corner Radius**: 
  - Device container: `44px` (Rounded rectangle).
  - Internal cards/elements: `16px` to `24px`.
- **Status Bar**: 
  - Standard iOS-style top bar.
  - Time: `9:41` (Font: SF Pro Display / Inter, Regular, White, ~14px).
  - Icons: Cellular signal, WiFi, and battery indicator (Right-aligned, pure white icons, ~12px height).

  - Surface / Card Dark: `#1F232A`

  - Secondary Text / Muted: `#8E959F`
  - Dark Shadow: `#0B0C0E`
  - Light Highlight: `#22272E`

---

## 5. Asset & Image Descriptions
All avatar images are circular (`border-radius: 50%`), featuring realistic photographic portraits rendered with dark cinematic grading, high contrast, and soft ambient shadow overlays to match the neumorphic aesthetic. No background-less transparent cutouts are used; all avatars fill their circular containers entirely.
