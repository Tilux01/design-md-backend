# Design Specification: Tesla Roadster Concept & Car Showcase App

This document outlines the exact design specifications for the provided mobile app UI concept. The goal is to build a full-screen, responsive, pixel-perfection clone of the application interface, stripping away the device mockup frame, ambient background, and creator watermark.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

### Color Palette
*   **Primary / Brand Red:** `#E31B23` (Used for active states, logos, key headers, and buttons)
*   **Deep Background / Surface Dark:** `#0E0E0E` or `#121212` (Base canvas color)
*   **Card Background / Surface Light:** `#1A1A1A` (Inner cards for car showcases)
*   **Secondary Surface (Elevated):** `#222222` (Interactive selection wells, logo grids)
*   **Primary Text (High Contrast):** `#FFFFFF` (Headings, car names, primary labels)
*   **Secondary Text (Muted):** `#A0A0A0` or `#888888` (Descriptions, specs, prices, subtext)
*   **Border / Divider Tints:** `#2C2C2C` (Subtle strokes separating sections)

### Typography & Fonts
The design relies on a modern, geometric sans-serif typeface family suitable for high-end automotive UI (similar to Tesla’s corporate UI or Apple's San Francisco).

*   **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `Montserrat`, `sans-serif`
*   **Font Weights Used:**
    *   **Light / Regular (300/400):** Body copy, sub-specs, brand lists.
    *   **Medium (500):** Navigation items, tabs, subheadings.
    *   **Bold / Heavy (700/800/900):** Hero headings (`ROADSTER`), car titles, price tags.

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Sections

### Section A: Header Navigation Bar
*   **Background:** Transparent/Blurred over the red hero header.
*   **Left Element:** Tesla "T" Logo (`#FFFFFF` or matching theme).
*   **Right Elements:** 
    *   Language indicator (`EN`).
    *   Search Icon (`🔍`).
    *   Menu/Hamburger Icon (`≡`).

### Section B: Hero Showcase (Tesla Roadster)
*   **Background Banner:** Radial gradient fading from a vibrant `#E31B23` red at the top to dark `#121212` at the bottom.
*   **Main Title:** 
    *   Text: `ROADSTER`
    *   Style: Extra bold, uppercase, tracked/letter-spaced wide, centered.
*   **Hero Image:** Top-down/aerial perspective view of a red Tesla Roadster floating dynamically.
*   **Arc Feature Navigation (01 – 05 Indicators):**
    *   A curved arc line underlying the car.
    *   Numbered nodes (`01`, `02`, `03`, `04`, `05`) marking spec pivots.
    *   Icons and brief label hints: `TRI-MOTOR AWD SYSTEM`, `620MI DRIVING RANGE`, `0-60 MPH IN 1.9 SEC`, `250+ MPH TOP SPEED`, `NEXT-GEN BATTERY TECH`.
*   **Interior Preview Cards (Dual Split Carousel/Grid):**
    *   Two side-by-side or horizontally scrollable preview windows displaying interior angles (steering wheel, cockpit).
    *   **Card 1:** `BUILT FOR PERFORMANCE` — Subtitle: *Tesla Roadster is engineered with cutting-edge technology to deliver...*
    *   **Card 2:** `SMART . INTUITIVE . FUTURISTIC.` — Subtitle: *mind-blowing speed, insane range and pure driving excitement.*

### Section C: Brand Selector ("SELECT YOUR CAR BY")
*   **Section Header:**
    *   Left: `SELECT YOUR CAR BY` with a vertical red indicator line.
    *   Right Switcher Tabs: `BRANDS` (Active, colored red) / `TYPES`.
*   **Brand Logo Grid:**
    *   A horizontally scrollable or cleanly wrapped grid of luxury automotive brand badges set inside dark, rounded container pills (`#1A1A1A`):
        *   Mercedes-Benz
        *   Bentley
        *   Jaguar
        *   Audi
        *   Aston Martin
        *   BMW
        *   Lamborghini
        *   Porsche

### Section D: Car Catalog Feed ("CAR BY")
*   **Section Header:**
    *   Left: `CAR BY` with a vertical red indicator line.
    *   Right Filters: `Latest` | `Featured` | `POPULAR` (Active pill highlighted in solid `#E31B23`).
*   **Car Card Grid (2-Column Responsive Layout):**
    *   Each card features a dark surface background (`#1A1A1A`) with subtle border radii (approx. 12-16px).
    *   **Card Contents:**
        1.  **Car Image:** Clean studio profile cut-out of the vehicle.
        2.  **Title / Model:** e.g., `MERCEDES-BENZ`, `S-CLASS 2025`.
        3.  **Specifications block:**
            *   *Price:* `Price: Starting from Around $125,000`
            *   *Type:* `Type: Luxury Sedan`
            *   *Engine:* `Engine: Twin-Turbo V8`
        4.  **Action Button:** Full-width rounded button at the bottom of the card labeled `EXPLORE PRODUCT` (Dark border style with white text).

## 3. Micro-Interactions & Animations (For Developer Implementation)

1.  **Hero Car Floating Animation:**
    *   *Effect:* Subtle, continuous floating/hover loop using CSS keyframes (`transform: translateY(0px)` -> `translateY(-8px)` -> `translateY(0px)` over 4 seconds, ease-in-out).
2.  **Arc Navigation Nodes:**
    *   *Effect:* Hovering or tapping indicators (`01`-`05`) shifts active state, updating the hero description text and smoothly shifting focus to the corresponding feature.
3.  **Brand & Filter Tabs:**
    *   *Effect:* Active tabs (`BRANDS`/`TYPES`, `POPULAR`) feature an instant background fill transition with scale feedback (`scale(0.98)` on click).
4.  **Card Hover State:**
    *   *Effect:* Car product cards subtly elevate with a soft drop shadow and border brightness increase (`border-color: #E31B23`).
5.  **Smooth Scrolling:**
    *   *Effect:* Horizontal sliders (Brands and Interior views) should have momentum scrolling enabled (`overflow-x: auto; scroll-snap-type: x mandatory;`).
