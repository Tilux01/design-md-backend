# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise Healthcare SaaS Dashboard. High-contrast clinical green surfaces paired with ultra-clean white cards, minimal iconography, and subtle surface depth.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High structural discipline. Generous internal padding (16px–24px) within structured card blocks, maintaining high clarity for critical medical data.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered SaaS UI utilizing a solid dark clinical green primary sidebar, a bright white application canvas, and subdued pale green/gray card containers (`#f8fafc`, `#e2e8f0`) with soft boundary contrast.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-contrast health green theme, soft status pill badges with pulsing indicator dots, clean card containers with subtle rounded corners (`16px`), modern data visualization bar charts with highlighted active states.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#10b981/15`, text `#047857`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`

## 1. Sidebar Navigation Component
- **Layout & Positioning**: `flex`, `flex-col`, position `relative`, width `260px`, height `100vh`, background `#047857`.
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Padding `24px 16px`, gap between navigation groups `32px`.
- **Borders, Radii & Shadows**: None (solid block).
- **Typography**: Font Family `"Plus Jakarta Sans"`, Font Size `14px`, Font Weight `500` (active `600`), Color `#ffffff` (active) / `#a7f3d0` (inactive).

- **Box Model & Spacing**: Padding `0px 24px`.
- **Borders, Radii & Shadows**: Border-bottom `1px solid #e2e8f0`.
- **Typography**: Search placeholder `14px` color `#64748b`.

- **Layout & Positioning**: `grid`, grid-template-columns `repeat(2, minmax(0, 1fr))`, gap `16px`.
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Card internal padding `20px`.
- **Borders, Radii & Shadows**: Border-radius `16px`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, background `#ffffff` (and special emerald variant `#059669` for "Upcoming Today").
- **Typography**: Metric Label `13px` font-weight `500` color `#64748b` (white for emerald card), Metric Value `32px` font-weight `700`.

## 4. Analytics Bar Chart Component
- **Layout & Positioning**: `flex`, `flex-col`, background `#ffffff`, border-radius `16px`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`, padding `20px`.
- **Elements**: Daily appointment count breakdown, period filter pills ("Today", "Monthly", "Weekly" active state in emerald `#047857`), vertical bar graph with active highlighted column (`#047857`).
