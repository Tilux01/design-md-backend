# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Creative Editorial / Immersive Digital Journal. Combines dark mode glassmorphism with high-impact visual media presentation (photographic memories, scrapbook collage aesthetic).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Highly immersive, media-forward layout with generous viewport utilization for content cards, framed by clean navigation UI bars with floating capsule action controls.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-layered depth utilizing semi-transparent dark slates (`rgba(20, 30, 45, 0.75)`), crisp white paper/polaroid textures with subtle drops, and a floating frosted glass control pill at the bottom (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Glassmorphic floating control dock, subtle translucent page-turn carousels, layered scrapbook collage composition, high-contrast white typographic branding against dark slate backgrounds.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.02em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-400 inline-block"></span>`.
