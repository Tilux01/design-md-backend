# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Futuristic Cyber-Minimalist / AI Tech Startup Dark Mode.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace balance, utilizing a stark black canvas (`#0A0A0A`) with generous padding, asymmetric layout blocks, and floating wireframe 3D holographic graphics.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered obsidian panels featuring subtle dark border rings (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), high-contrast glowing typography accents, and soft atmospheric glow lighting without heavy drop shadows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Geometric brutalist headers mixed with clean cybernetic wireframe grids, high-tracking uppercase badges, floating orbital graphics, monochrome aesthetic.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `6px 16px`, font-size: `12px`, font-weight: `600`, tracking: `0.1em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background translucency: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border width: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-white inline-block"></span>`.
