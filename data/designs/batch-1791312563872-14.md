# Production-Grade Design Specification: Panerai Submersible Automatic

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic

- **Primary Mood & Archetype:** Ultra-luxury, high-precision technical horology. Minimalist yet aggressive industrial luxury.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace:** Controlled high-contrast density with generous margins flanking the core content container.
- **Theme Balance:** 95% Dark Mode architectural foundation accented by earthy, military-grade tactical olive green (`#5F6649`) to mirror the Submersible Carbotech aesthetic. Flat depth accented by deep photographic shadows and subtle multi-stop linear gradients.

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid

- **Container Max-Width:** 1280px fluid viewport container with fixed outer horizontal gutters (32px desktop).
- **Spatial Scale (8pt Grid derivative):** `4px`, `8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`.
- **Layout Structure:**
  - **Header Region:** Fixed top bar containing Left Logo, Central Navigation Links, and Right-hand Utility Icons.
  - **Main Hero Split Grid:**
    - Left Column (Dark Media Showcase): 55% width.
    - Right Column (Olive Accent Callout): 45% width.
  - **Footer Action Strip:** Floating pagination indices and directional arrow navigation.

## 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement

### Navigation Header
- **Height:** 64px
- **Elements:**
  - Left: Brand logotype (`PANERAI`).
  - Center: Horizontal navigation links (`COLLECTIONS`, `ACCESSORIES`, `KNOW HOW`, `SERVICES`, `BOUTIQUES`, `ABOUT PANERAI`). Spacing: 32px gap.
  - Right: Action icons (`Search`, `Wishlist`, `Profile`). Gap: 20px.

### Hero Product Showcase Container
- **Border-Radius:** 0px (Sharp flush layout against viewport).
- **Background:** Multi-stop radial dark vignette fading to pitch black (`#0A0A0A`).
- **Product Imagery:** Angled 3D isometric cutout of Panerai Submersible Carbotech positioned centrally within the dark quadrant, dropping subtle soft black drop shadows (`box-shadow: 0px 24px 48px rgba(0,0,0,0.8)`).
- **Typography Block (Right Side of Dark Quadrant):**
  - **H1:** `SUBMERSIBLE AUTOMATIC`
  - **H2:** `42MM CARBOTECH™`
  - **Video Trigger Button:** Circular play icon (`36px` diameter, solid dark grey with white icon) followed by text link `Official Campaign - WATCH VIDEO`.
  - **Product SKU / Special Edition Text:** `PAM01661 SPECIAL EDITION` (10px, Muted).
  - **Footer Link:** `DISCOVER LUMINOR` with rightward arrow animation cue.

### Action Controls & Pagination
- **Pagination Indicators:** Bottom-left circular dot indicators (1 active filled dot, 4 inactive stroked dots).
- **Carousel Arrows:** Bottom-right fixed chevron pair (`<` and `>`) housed within 32px square dark hit-areas.

---

## 6. Motion Mechanics & Interactions

- **Hover States:**
  - Navigation links: Color transition from `#8C8C8C` to `#FFFFFF` over `150ms cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`.
  - Buttons / Play Icons: Scale transform `scale(1.05)` with background luminosity shift.
- **Page Transitions & Carousel Navigation:**
  - Slide transition for product showcases: Horizontal translation (`translateX(100%)` to `translateX(0%)`) driven by `400ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (Custom Spring-like ease-out).
- **Video Trigger Interaction:**
  - On click, smooth modal overlay fade-in (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`, background color `rgba(0,0,0,0.85)` over `250ms`).
