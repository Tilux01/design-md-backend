# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist Architecture Portfolio / Modernist Design System Showroom.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Highly balanced, restrained composition emphasizing stark dark contrast, structural typography scale, and curated architectural photography. Generous padding and clear content zoning.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, monolithic dark theme canvas utilizing subtle tonal variations (`#121212`, `#1E1E1E`), clean 1px structural borders, and sharp photographic framing without excessive drop shadows or pseudo-skeuomorphic depth layers.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase architectural labels, monospaced hex color codes, editorial image grid layouts, clean border separation.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `4px 12px`, font-size: `11px`, font-weight: `600`, tracking: `0.05em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-[#E85D4C] inline-block"></span>`.
