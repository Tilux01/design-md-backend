# Design Specification: TIDY — Team Command Center

This document specifies the exact visual clone, design system, typography, layout architecture, and asset descriptions to reproduce the **TIDY** landing page as a modern, full-screen responsive web experience (without external mockup bezels or tablet frames).

---

## 1. Global Visual & Atmosphere Overview

* **Design Aesthetic:** Neo-Swiss Technical Minimalism with a high-end 3D sculptural centerpiece.
* **Canvas Style:** Clean, high-key, matte off-white/warm industrial gray background with high-contrast monochrome typography and a single vibrant focal asset.
* **Viewport Scope:** Full viewport height and width (`100vw`, `100vh`) with fixed/pinned layout mechanics (no scrolling necessary on desktop viewports above 800px height; fluid vertical stack on mobile/smaller screens).

## 3. Typography & Font Stacks

### 3.1 Primary Font: Neo-Grotesque Display & Body
* **Target Font:** `PP Neue Montreal`, `Neue Haas Grotesk Display Pro`, or `Geist Sans`
* **Fallback Stack:** `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
* **Characteristics:** Tight tracking on headings, sharp geometric uppercase forms, high legibility.

### 3.2 Monospace / Technical Font
* **Target Font:** `JetBrains Mono`, `Space Mono`, or `SF Mono`
* **Fallback Stack:** `ui-monospace, "Cascadia Code", "Source Code Pro", Menlo, Consolas, monospace`
* **Usage:** Index labels, version tags, bracketed navigation items.

### 5.3 Central 3D Graphic Asset Specification

* **Asset Type:** Transparent backgroundless PNG / WebP or WebGL/Three.js Canvas (`width: 100%; height: auto; max-height: 48vh; object-fit: contain; pointer-events: none;`).
* **Placement:** Centered horizontally (`margin: 0 auto;`), vertically positioned in the mid-layer between the hero headline and bottom CTA.
* **Visual Description of the 3D Asset:**
  1. **Structure & Form:**
     * A dynamic, sinuous, horizontal S-curve "wave" constructed from a continuous row of dozens of thick rectangular domino-like tiles/slabs.
     * The slabs start on the far-left at a slight downward tilt, arc upward towards the center-left, twist sharply into an upright twisting crest in the center (exposing both front and rear facets), and sweep downward to the right before leveling off.
  2. **Color & Lighting:**
     * **Front Faces:** Iridescent gradient shifting smoothly from warm dusty rose/magenta (`#B05B86`) on the left to vibrant lilac/violet (`#955799`) in the mid-curve, transitioning into soft slate/cyan blue (`#5D88A6`) on the right segment.
     * **Back Faces / Sides:** Deeper saturated plum-indigo and dark shaded tones providing dimensional depth.
     * **Lighting:** Studio key light from the top-left casting soft specular highlights along the top edge of each tile, with realistic ambient occlusion and drop shadows cast between adjacent tiles.
  3. **Floating Micro-Cards / Data Floaters:**
     * Scattered above and below the undulating wave are approximately 15–25 tiny floating rectangular chips/markers.
     * Visuals: Pale silver/gray matte tiles, hovering in 3D perspective at varying angles and distances.
     * Several floaters feature micro-labels/numbers (e.g., `21`, `42`, tiny dots) resembling modular floating data points or task cards.
  4. **Transparency & Blend Mode:**
     * Absolutely clean alpha channel with no background halo. Sits naturally over the `#ECECEE` canvas.


  * Label: `GET STARTED`
  * Geometry: Fully rounded pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px; padding: 10px 24px;`).
