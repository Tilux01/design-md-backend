# Design Specification: Dark-Themed Portfolio & Branding Landing Page

This document outlines the precise specifications required to build a full-screen, responsive, pixel-perfect clone of the provided UI design.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Variables

### Color Palette
*   **Background (Canvas):** `#18181a` (Dark charcoal grey)
*   **Primary Text (Headings):** `#ffffff` (Pure white)
*   **Secondary Text (Subheadings/Body):** `#a0a0a0` (Light grey)
*   **Accent Color (Orange):** `#ff4500` (Vibrant orange, used for dots, outlines, and buttons)
*   **Image Overlay Tint:** Monochromatic / Black-and-white high contrast with dark neutral grading.

### Typography
*   **Font Family:** Modern Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Roboto*, or *Helvetica Neue*).
*   **Weights Used:** Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (700).

---

## 2. Layout & Structure

*   **View:** Full-screen viewport (`width: 100vw`, `height: 100vh`, `overflow: hidden`).
*   **Grid Division:** 
    *   **Left Section (Content Area):** ~60% width. Dark background (`#18181a`).
    *   **Right Section (Hero Image Area):** ~40% width. Features a portrait image with a vertical title overlay.

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation & Left Sidebar
1.  **Menu Icon:** 
    *   Located at the top-left corner.
    *   Three horizontal lines (hamburger style) with a small accent dot/dash on top.
2.  **Social Links (Left Edge):**
    *   Vertically stacked icons aligned to the middle-left.
    *   Icons: Instagram, Behance, Dribbble, Pinterest.
    *   Color: White/Light Grey, transitioning to accent orange on hover.

### B. Main Content Area (Left)
1.  **Top Micro-copy:**
    *   Text: `"Love on UI & UX"`
    *   Font size: Small (approx. 12px), tracked out, muted grey.
2.  **Main Headline:**
    *   Text: `"Design Technology."`
    *   Font size: Large, bold, pure white.
3.  **Outline/Stylized Typography Effect:**
    *   Text: `"D E S I G N"`
    *   Style: Large hollow/stroked outline typography using the accent orange and white colors.
4.  **Footer Link ("About us"):**
    *   Located at the bottom left.
    *   Includes a dotted horizontal pagination/indicator line and an orange circular icon/button.

### C. Central Vertical Divider Element
1.  **Vertical Title:**
    *   Text: `"Brands."`
    *   Orientation: Rotated 90 degrees (vertical reading direction, bottom-to-top or top-to-bottom as depicted).
    *   Dot: A solid orange dot (`#ff4500`) positioned directly above the word "Brands."
    *   Metadata: Small vertical date text next to it reading `"28.03.2018"` in a subtle grey tone.

### D. Right Hero Section & Call-to-Action
1.  **Portrait Image:**
    *   Subject: A professional African American man with a beard and glasses, wearing a suit, arms crossed, looking away from the camera.
    *   Treatment: Desaturated (Black and White) with a subtle dot-matrix/halftone pattern overlay visible on the dark areas.
2.  **Next/Slider Button:**
    *   Position: Floating on the right edge, overlapping the image container.
    *   Shape: Square with rounded corners or sharp edges (solid orange background).
    *   Icon: White right-pointing arrow (`→`).

---

## 4. Responsive & Interactive Behavior

*   **Hover States:** 
    *   Social icons brighten to pure white or accent orange.
    *   The "Next" button shifts slightly or increases opacity.
*   **Responsiveness:** On mobile viewports, stack the left content area above the image container and adjust font sizes accordingly while maintaining the stark dark aesthetic.
