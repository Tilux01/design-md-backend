### C. Image Asset Descriptions & Positioning Specs

#### Image Asset 1: Central Vineyard Window (Landscape Graphic)
* **Type:** High-resolution photograph inside a cropped container.
* **Aspect Ratio / Dimensions:** Vertical rectangle, approximately `340px` wide × `580px` tall.
* **Shape/Border:**
  * Chamfered / angled top corners (cut-corner arch style) or subtle arched top styling.
  * Border: `1px solid #E0C488` with an inset offset border of `3px`.
* **Image Description:**
  * Photorealistic image of rolling vineyard hills in Tuscany or France during golden hour / late afternoon.
  * Green vines arranged in rows leading into distant hills under a warm, hazy sky with soft orange/yellow lighting.
* **Positioning:** Centered horizontally (`left: 50%; transform: translateX(-50%);`) and vertically aligned in the composition.
* **Rotation:** `0deg`.

#### Image Asset 2: Overlay Bottle & Glass Sculpture
* **Type:** Transparent PNG (Backgroundless cutout with alpha transparency for glass refractions).
* **Image Description:**
  * A sleek, ultra-modern clear glass wine bottle fused into a tall, slender stemware shape.
  * The bottom bowl of the glass contains a golden-amber wine liquid.
  * Clean white reflections along the left and right edges of the glass contours to simulate light hit.
  * Fully transparent glass body allowing the background landscape image behind it to show through with refraction.
* **Positioning:**
  * Centered directly over the Central Vineyard Window.
  * **Rotation:** `0deg`.
  * **Scale:** Extends slightly taller than the central landscape frame, overlapping both top and bottom edges.
