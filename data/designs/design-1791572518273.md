# UI/UX Design Specification & Clone Blueprint

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport Setup
* **Target Viewport:** Full-screen desktop application/web layout (1440px × 900px minimum recommended).
* **Base Container:** A central application card or viewport container spanning the inner canvas with rounded corners (`border-radius: 16px` to `24px`), floating atop a darkened ambient background.
* **Background Architecture:** 
  * Outer application canvas: Dark charcoal/black radial gradient (`#121316` to `#0d0e11`).
  * Inner Card Split Background:
    * Left side (Dark Zone): Deep slate/charcoal gradient (`#1a1c23` to `#16181f`).
    * Right side (Accent Zone): Vibrant magenta/hot-coral diagonal slash/block (`#ff2e51` to `#e61e41`).
* **Watermark Typography:** A large translucent background numeral "6" overlaid across the center split (`opacity: 0.05` to `0.08`, massive sans-serif weight).

---

## 2. Typography & Font Stack
* **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, sans-serif.
* **Font Fallbacks:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.
* **Type Hierarchy:**
  * **Navigation Links:** 11px–12px, Uppercase, Medium weight (`500`), letter-spacing: `1px`. Color: White/Light Gray (`#d1d5db`). Active state underline indicator.
  * **Price Tag:** 20px–22px, Bold (`700`), color: Pure White (`#ffffff`).
  * **Product Title ("Air Jordan 6 Retro SP"):** 36px–42px, Extra Bold (`800`), line-height: `1.1`, tracking/letter-spacing: tight (`-0.5px`). Color: Pure White (`#ffffff`).
  * **Body Copy / Description:** 12px–13px, Regular (`400`), line-height: `1.5`, color: Muted Light Gray (`#9ca3af`).
  * **Action Button Text:** 11px, Uppercase, Semi-Bold (`600`), tracking: `0.5px`, color: Pure White (`#ffffff`).
  * **Utility / Footer Labels:** 10px–11px, Regular (`400`), color: Muted Gray (`#9ca3af`).

---

## 3. Header & Navigation Bar
* **Position:** Absolute/Sticky top, spanning the full width of the internal card with horizontal padding (approx. `40px`).
* **Left Element:** Nike "Swoosh" logo in pure white (`width: 24px` approx).
* **Center Navigation:** 
  * Links: `NEW RELEASES`, `MEN`, `WOMEN`, `KIDS`, `CUSTOMIZE`.
  * Active Indicator: A subtle white underline pill beneath `NEW RELEASES`.
* **Right Elements:**
  * Search Bar: Magnifying glass icon + placeholder text `Search` (muted gray style with subtle border/background pill).
  * Utility Icons: User profile / avatar indicator, Shopping Bag icon with counter badge (`0`).

---

## 4. Content Architecture (Left Column)
* **Price Element:** Displayed prominently above the main title: `$225`.
* **Product Title:** 
  * Line 1: `Air Jordan 6`
  * Line 2: `Retro SP`
* **Rating Stars:** 
  * 5 filled star icons in vibrant gold/yellow (`#facc15`), aligned horizontally beneath the title.
* **Product Description Paragraph:**
  * *Text:* "Originally released the year MJ won his professional championship, the Air Jordan 6 Retro SP features a sculpted midsole for cushioned stability, with overlays designed to abstractly depict the number 23."
* **Primary CTA Button ("ADD TO CART"):**
  * Shape: Rounded pill button (`border-radius: 20px`).
  * Fill: Vibrant coral/red (`#ff2e51` matching the background accent).
  * Typography: Uppercase, bold, white text with letter-spacing.
* **Author / Social Footer:**
  * Left: User avatar thumbnail (small circular image of a person) + handle `@sa_arteaga`.
  * Right: Social media icon set (Twitter/X, Facebook, Dribbble or similar minimalist vector glyphs in white/gray).

---

## 5. Visual Asset Specifications
* **Main Product Image (Sneaker):**
  * **Description:** High-resolution product photography of the *Air Jordan 6 Retro SP* (Silver/Grey metallic upper, black midsole, translucent icy-blue outsole accents, vibrant infrared/red lace locks and heel pull-tab details).
  * **Placement:** Positioned dynamically breaking out of the dark container into the vibrant magenta/red accent zone on the right. Tilted at a dynamic 30–45 degree angle to emphasize motion and depth. Drop shadow applied underneath to elevate it off the background.
* **Secondary Interactive Element ("WATCH TRAILER"):**
  * **Description:** Circular play button icon (white play triangle inside a translucent/bordered circle) paired with the stacked label `WATCH` / `TRAILER` in crisp white uppercase text. Placed in the bottom-right corner of the red accent zone.

## 6. Color Palette
* **Background Dark:** `#121316` (Canvas), `#1a1c23` (Card Left)
* **Primary Accent (Red/Coral):** `#ff2e51`
* **Pure White (Text/Logo):** `#ffffff`
* **Muted Gray (Body/Subtext):** `#9ca3af`
* **Rating Gold:** `#facc15`
