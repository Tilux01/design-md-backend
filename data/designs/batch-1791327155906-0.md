# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Glassmorphism & Modern Fintech Dashboard.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-contrast information density combined with soft rounded surface cards, ambient radial lighting, and subtle multi-layered borders to maintain hierarchy.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-tier dark depth layers utilizing pure obsidian black backgrounds, slate glass cards (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`), frosted backdrop-filters (`blur(16px)`), cyan primary action pills, and subtle cyan/blue linear gradient borders.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient radial backlighting gradients, glowing chart lines with area gradient fills, high-tracking uppercase pill badges, backdrop-filtered glass containers.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `0.75rem`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(30, 228, 255, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(30, 228, 255, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-[#1EE4FF] inline-block"></span>`.
