# Design Specification: Swirl Creamery Advertisement

## 1. Overview & Layout Strategy
* **Aspect Ratio:** Optimized for Instagram Story / Vertical Ad (approx. 9:16 vertical container).
* **Color Palette (Background & Theme):**
  * **Main Background:** Soft Pastel Pink (`#F3C1C6` or similar soft blush pink).
  * **Brand Logo Text & Headings:** Deep Berry / Dark Chocolate Brown (`#3A1E22`).
  * **Accents:** Vibrant Yellow (`#F3B135` for Mango Madness tub), Sage Green (`#88A771` for Pistachio/Matcha tub), Berry Pink (`#D2647E` for Strawberry tub), Rich Brown (`#5A3B34` for Chocolate tub).
* **Layout Structure:**
  * **Top Left:** Brand Logo ("Swirl CREAMERY").
  * **Center-Left Floating Elements:** A dynamic, diagonal stack of 5 ice cream pints floating upwards, creating depth.
  * **Right Side:** Large bold typography statement ("ICE CREAM JUST THE WAY YOU LIKE IT") followed by a color swatch/flavor indicator row and "MADE WITH LOVE" badge.
  * **Bottom Left:** Descriptive tagline ("PREMIUM ICE CREAM. PLAYFUL FLAVORS. MADE TO SWIRL.") and a call-to-action (CTA) button ("SHOP NOW").
  * **Top Right:** Social media icon links (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok).
  * **Footer Watermark:** `@RILEY_LUXE_STUDIO` centered at the very bottom on the outer container.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### **Primary Brand Font (Logo):**
* **Style:** Bold, script/hand-lettered aesthetic with connected strokes, rounded terminals, and a playful, bouncy baseline.
* **Fallback Stack:** `Brush Script MT`, `Pacifico`, `cursive`.

### **Secondary Heading Font (Right Side Statement):**
* **Style:** Heavy, geometric, ultra-bold sans-serif with slight retro/condensed proportions. Clean edges and high impact.
* **Fallback Stack:** `Impact`, `Arial Black`, `sans-serif`.

### **Body Copy & UI Font (Taglines & Buttons):**
* **Style:** Clean, highly legible, geometric sans-serif (medium/semibold weight for buttons, regular weight for descriptions).
* **Fallback Stack:** `Montserrat`, `Helvetica Neue`, `sans-serif`.

---

## 3. Element-by-Element Breakdown

### A. Background & Framing
* **Canvas:** Solid soft pink background filling the entire viewport.
* **No internal frames:** The layout extends edge-to-edge as requested.

### B. Header (Top Left & Right)
* **Logo:** 
  * Text: "Swirl" in large script font, stacked above "CREAMERY" in a tracked-out uppercase sans-serif.
  * Color: Deep Berry/Brown (`#3A1E22`).
* **Social Icons:**
  * Minimalist white line art icons for Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok aligned horizontally in the top right corner.

### C. Floating Product Showcase (Center-Left)
* **Arrangement:** An isometric, cascading stack of 5 ice cream pints floating diagonally from bottom-center to top-center.
* **Pint Flavors & Colors:**
  1. **Mango Madness (Foreground/Bottom):** Bright yellow tub (`#F3B135`) with bold white branding.
  2. **Vanilla Bean (Middle-Low):** Cream/off-white tub (`#E8DFC9`) with vanilla pod illustration.
  3. **Pistachio/Green Tea (Middle-Left):** Sage green tub (`#88A771`).
  4. **Strawberry/Berry (Middle-Top):** Dusty pink tub (`#C87884`).
  5. **Chocolate (Top-Right):** Deep chocolate brown tub (`#5A3B34`).

### D. Typography & Callouts (Right Side)
* **Main Headline:**
  * Text: 
    * `ICE CREAM` (Large, bold)
    * `JUST THE WAY` (Large, bold)
    * `YOU LIKE IT` (Large, bold)
  * Color: Deep Berry/Brown (`#3A1E22`).
  * Alignment: Right-aligned block.
* **Sub-Badge:**
  * Text: "MADE WITH LOVE" in small uppercase tracking.
* **Flavor Swatch Row:**
  * A horizontal row of 5 circular color indicator pips matching the flavors of the 5 tubs (Brown, Yellow, Green, Pink, Dark Brown).

### E. Footer Area (Bottom Left)
* **Tagline Block:**
  * Text: 
    * `PREMIUM ICE CREAM.`
    * `PLAYFUL FLAVORS.`
    * `MADE TO SWIRL.`
  * Font: Clean uppercase sans-serif, deep brown color.
* **CTA Button ("SHOP NOW"):**
  * Shape: Pill-shaped capsule button with a shopping cart icon on the left.
  * Color: Deep Berry/Brown background (`#3A1E22`) with white text.

---

## 4. Animation Specifications (Motion Design)

If rendered as an animated graphic or video ad:
1. **Entrance (0.0s – 1.0s):**
   * The background fades in instantly.
   * The 5 ice cream pints **float in** sequentially from bottom to top with a smooth spring physics effect (paralax floating motion, subtle bobbing up and down continuously).
2. **Typography Reveal (0.5s – 1.5s):**
   * The right-side headline (`ICE CREAM JUST THE WAY YOU LIKE IT`) slides in from the right with a smooth fade-in (`ease-out`).
   * The "SHOP NOW" button scales up from 0 to 1 with a subtle bounce.
3. **Continuous Loop:**
   * The ice cream tubs maintain a gentle, organic floating/hovering motion to simulate depth and freshness.
