# Design & Implementation Specification: `puren.` Juice Landing Page

*   **View Mode:** Full-screen viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), responsive design with a centered layout container.
*   **Grid System:** Asymmetric 2-column or 3-column flex/grid container depending on viewport width.

## 5. Animations & Micro-Interactions

1.  **Page Load Entrance Animation:**
    *   *Staggered Fade-In:* Nav items load first, followed by the hero heading, description, and bottle imagery sliding in from the right (`transform: translateX(50px)` to `0`).
2.  **Hover States:**
    *   *Navigation Links:* Subtle color opacity shift (`1` to `0.8`).
    *   *CTA Button:* `transition: all 0.3s ease;` with box-shadow expansion.
    *   *Next Button:* Border scale or background fill on hover to indicate interactivity.
3.  **Carousel Transition (State Change):**
    *   Smooth slide-and-fade transition when clicking `NEXT`, moving the current bottle to the left and bringing the next flavor into center stage.
