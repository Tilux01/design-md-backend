# Design Specification: "Today" Habit Tracker & Pet App

## 1. Global & Screen Layout

*   **Screen Type:** Full-Screen Mobile View (Vertical, Portrait orientation).
*   **Dimensions (Reference):** Designed for a standard modern smartphone viewport (e.g., iPhone 14/15 proportions, approx. 390px × 844px).
*   **Background Color:** 
    *   Primary base: Very pale soft periwinkle / lavender white (`#F4F2FC` or `#F7F6FF`).
    *   Ambient gradient/mesh: Soft diffused pastel purple/violet clouds in the upper-middle area behind the central character.
*   **Layout Structure (Top to Bottom):**
    1.  **Header:** Title ("Today") on the left, Menu/Settings icon on the right.
    2.  **Date/Streak Ribbon:** A horizontal scrollable row of 7 circular date/status indicators representing days of the week.
    3.  **Hero Illustration Section:** A central illustration container featuring a purple mascot character ("Thriving (75%)") with floating ambient icons/decorations.
    4.  **Dashboard Cards (Bottom Grid):** Two side-by-side white rounded cards displaying metric stats (e.g., Water intake on the left, Coins/Points on the right).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Font Family
*   **Font:** **Inter** (Fallback: `SF Pro Display`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`).
*   **Secondary Font (for playful headers/status):** **Poppins** or **Quicksand** (Fallback: `Nunito`, `Rounded sans-serif`).

### Text Styles Hierarchy
*   **App Title ("Today"):**
    *   Font Size: `28px` - `32px`
    *   Font Weight: Bold (`700`)
    *   Color: Deep navy/purple (`#26223B`)
*   **Ribbon Dates (e.g., numbers below circles):**
    *   Font Size: `11px` - `12px`
    *   Font Weight: Medium (`500`)
    *   Color: Muted grayish-purple (`#8C85A0`)
*   **Status Label ("Thriving (75%)"):**
    *   Font Size: `14px`
    *   Font Weight: Semi-bold (`600`)
    *   Color: Dark slate purple (`#3A3251`)
*   **Card Headers & Metrics:**
    *   Titles: `12px` (`#8C85A0`), Regular/Medium.
    *   Values (e.g., `10 / 64 oz`): `16px` - `18px`, Bold (`700`), (`#26223B`).

---

## 3. Component Breakdown & Styling

### A. Header
*   **Alignment:** Flexbox, Space-Between.
*   **Left Element:** Text string "Today".
*   **Right Element:** Circular icon button (Menu / Settings / Profile icon with a notification dot), light semi-transparent white background with a subtle purple border/shadow.

### B. Date/Streak Ribbon (7-Day Carousel)
*   **Container:** Horizontal flex layout with even spacing (`space-between`).
*   **Items:** 7 vertical stacks representing days.
    *   *Active/Completed Day (e.g., days 3, 4, 5):* Solid pastel purple rounded-square or capsule badge containing an icon (check/streak flame) with a glowing soft shadow. Background color: `#D4CCF9`.
    *   *Inactive/Future Day (e.g., days 1, 6, 7):* Outlined transparent container with a thin grey-purple border and a muted icon inside.
    *   *Labels:* Small numeric date below each pill (e.g., `12`, `13`, `14`, etc.).

### C. Hero Illustration Section
*   **Background Elements:** An organic, soft-edged purple watercolor/cloud shape (`#D8D2F8` with low opacity) centered behind the mascot. Scattered tiny white/yellow star and sparkle particles.
*   **Central Mascot Image (Asset):**
    *   **Description:** A cute, stylized, fluffy, cartoonish creature (resembling an otter or bear-like monster) rendered in soft shades of periwinkle and deep purple. It is sitting upright, facing slightly right, with a white belly patch, a small black nose/eye, and a relaxed, content posture.
    *   **Background:** Backgroundless (Transparent PNG/SVG).
    *   **Placement & Rotation:** Centered horizontally in the upper-middle frame. No rotation (`0deg`), standing straight on an implicit baseline.
*   **Status Text:** Positioned directly beneath the mascot image: *"Thriving (75%)"*.
*   **Floating Decorative Elements (Surrounding Mascot):**
    *   Left side: A subtle pastel pink/orange glowing item/glass.
    *   Right side: A small floating hourglass and sparkle icons positioned near the top right of the illustration box.

### D. Dashboard Cards (Bottom Grid)
*   **Layout:** 2-column grid with a gap of `12px` - `16px`.
*   **Card Container Style:**
    *   Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
    *   Border-radius: `16px` - `20px`.
    *   Shadow: Soft, diffused drop shadow (`0px 8px 24px rgba(140, 133, 160, 0.08)`).
    *   Padding: `16px`.
*   **Left Card (Water / Hydration Tracker):**
    *   Top Header: Small icon + text (e.g., Water droplet icon + "Water").
    *   Main Stat: `10 / 64 oz` (Bold).
    *   Visual: Small progress indicator bar at the bottom.
*   **Right Card (Coins / Rewards Tracker):**
    *   Top Header: Ticket/Coin icon + text (e.g., "Tickets" or "To-Dos").
    *   Secondary row: "To-Dos" label.
    *   Main Stat: Numerical value with a small action chevron icon on the top right.
