# Design Specification: Portfolio Homepage Hero

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
* **Layout Mode:** Full-screen viewport (`width: 100vw; height: 100vh;` or `min-height: 100vh;`).
* **Background Color:** Deep charcoal / near black (`#0B0E0D` or `#0D1110`).
* **Padding/Margins:** Generous padding around the inner container to let the typography breathe (approx. `40px` to `60px` padding from the viewport edges).
* **Grid/Alignment:** Left-aligned text hierarchy with an interactive 3D/abstract graphic element occupying the mid-to-left portion of the screen.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Headings & Brand Text
* **Font Family:** Modern, high-impact Sans-Serif (e.g., **Syne**, **Clash Display**, or **Helvetica Neue**).
* **Weight:** Bold / Extra Bold (`700` to `800`).
* **Fallback Stack:** `sans-serif, Arial, Helvetica`.

### Hierarchy
1. **Brand / Small Header ("SPARKK"):**
   * **Text:** `SPARKK`
   * **Size:** Small (approx. `12px` - `14px`).
   * **Letter Spacing:** Wide (`2px` to `3px`).
   * **Color:** Off-white / Muted gray.

2. **Main Headline ("LET US PLAY WITH OUR ideas"):**
   * **Structure:** Multi-line stack with mixed font styles.
   * **Line 1 ("LET US"):** Large, bold, uppercase sans-serif in white (`#FFFFFF`).
   * **Line 2 ("PLAY WITH"):** Large, bold, uppercase sans-serif in white (`#FFFFFF`).
   * **Line 3 ("OUR ideas"):** 
     * **"OUR":** Bold, uppercase sans-serif in white (`#FFFFFF`).
     * **"ideas":** Italicized serif or handwritten-style script font (e.g., **Instrument Serif Italic**, **Playfair Display Italic**, or **Georgia Italic**) rendered in a vibrant lime/neon green (`#A3E635` or `#7BE113`).

---

## 3. Color Palette
* **Background:** Dark Charcoal (`#0B0E0D`)
* **Primary Text:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`)
* **Accent / Interactive Elements:** Neon Lime Green (`#7BE113` or `#A3E635`)
* **Muted Text / Secondary UI:** Muted Gray (`#6B7280`)

---

## 4. Visual Elements & Graphics

### Abstract 3D Graphic (Left/Center Area)
* **Description:** A dynamic, floating cluster of geometric, hexagonal-faced blocks or particles grouped together in a cloud formation. 
* **Color:** Monochromatic neon green gradients, ranging from bright lime highlights to deep forest green shadows.
* **Implementation Note:** Can be rendered using WebGL (Three.js), a high-resolution SVG illustration, or a transparent PNG/WebP asset positioned absolutely within the layout.

### UI Navigation & Controls (Right / Corners)
* **Top-Right Controls:** A solid neon green square/pill indicator button (`#7BE113`) acting as a status or menu toggle.
* **Right-Center Pagination / Scroll Indicators:** A vertical stack of small dot/line indicators showing current scroll position or carousel status.

---

## 5. Responsive Behavior
* **Desktop (>1024px):** Full-screen split view with the 3D abstract graphic floating centrally beneath/beside the large typography.
* **Mobile (<768px):** 
  * Stack the typography vertically.
  * Scale down headline font sizes (`clamp(2.5rem, 8vw, 6rem)`).
  * Scale down or reposition the abstract 3D graphic behind the text with reduced opacity to maintain readability.
