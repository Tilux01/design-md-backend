# Design Specification: Cyberpunk Games Dashboard

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Background:** Solid Deep Black (`#080808`).
*   **Primary Accent:** Neon Lime Green (`#B3FF00`).
*   **Secondary Accent:** Electric Blue (`#2B59FF`).
*   **Surface Color:** Dark Charcoal Grey (`#1A1A1E`) for containers and cards.
*   **Border Radius:** Highly rounded corners (ranging from `24px` to `40px`) to create a "pill" or "organic" feel.
*   **Layout:** Full-screen responsive grid. Left/Center (70% width) for Hero/Navigation; Right (30% width) for the Cart Sidebar.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Roboto`.
*   **Heading Font:** `Montserrat` (Bold/Black weights) or `Orbitron` for a more "tech" feel.
*   **Fallbacks:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   Headings: White (`#FFFFFF`)
    *   Body: Light Grey (`#A0A0A0`)
    *   Accents: Neon Lime Green (`#B3FF00`)

---

## 3. Header Section (Top Navigation)
*   **Logo (Top Left):** Text "CYBERPUNK GAMES" in a stacked, heavy sans-serif font. "CYBERPUNK" in white, "GAMES" in Neon Green.
*   **Search Bar:** A pill-shaped container. The left half is a Neon Green circle containing a dark magnifying glass icon. The right half is a dark grey field.
*   **Nav Links:** "Community", "Gallery", "News". Small font size (`12px-14px`), uppercase, light grey color.
*   **User Profile (Top Right):** 
    *   A pill-shaped dark grey badge showing a currency icon and balance (e.g., "1200.00").
    *   A circular profile avatar with a thin pink/red border.

---

## 4. Hero Section (Main Content)
*   **Main Character Image:**
    *   **Description:** A high-detail digital painting of a cyberpunk ninja. The character has spiky white/silver hair, a high-tech metallic face mask/respirator covering the nose and mouth, and is wearing a dark, bulky tactical jacket with a textured collar.
    *   **Placement:** Positioned on the far left, slightly overlapping the bottom edge. The character is facing right.
    *   **Background:** Backgroundless (transparent PNG).
    *   **Rotation:** 0 degrees, but the character's body is angled in a 3/4 view.
*   **Text Block:**
    *   **Title:** "ULTIMATE **NINJA** WARS". "ULTIMATE" and "WARS" are white; "NINJA" is Neon Green. Large, bold, all-caps.
    *   **Description:** 3-4 lines of small, justified light grey text describing the game.
*   **Circular Previews:** Three small circles below the description. Each contains a different stylized character portrait (one blue-toned, one orange-toned, one dark-toned).
*   **Genre Badge (Bottom Center):** A floating dark grey rounded square containing a stylized "S" logo and the text "Game Genre / Action".

---

## 5. Cart Sidebar (Right Side)
*   **Container:** A vertical dark grey panel with a very large border-radius on the top-left corner.
*   **Title:** "CART" in large white caps.
*   **Cart Items (The "Wave" Stack):**
    *   **General Shape:** Each card has a "wavy" or "liquid" top and bottom edge that nests into the card above/below it.
    *   **Item 1 (Top):** Neon Green background. Text: "ANGRY WARS CYBERPUNK". Includes a small lightning bolt icon and a red circular "remove" button.
    *   **Item 2 (Middle):** Electric Blue background. Text: "ATTACK OF THE TITANS LIMITED EDITION". Includes a shield/helmet icon and a red circular "remove" button.
    *   **Item 3 (Bottom):** Dark Grey background (matching the sidebar). Text: "NARUTO CYBERPUNK". Includes a circular swirl icon and a red circular "remove" button.
*   **Footer:**
    *   **Total Price:** A pill-shaped dark grey bar at the bottom showing "Total Price: $1,200".
    *   **Checkout Button:** A small Neon Green circle with a dark arrow or shopping bag icon, placed at the end of the total price bar.

---

## 6. Visual Effects
*   **Glow:** Apply a subtle `drop-shadow` or `box-shadow` to the Neon Green elements (Search icon, Cart item 1, "NINJA" text) to simulate a neon glow.
*   **Depth:** The sidebar should have a slight inner shadow to appear recessed, while the Hero character should appear to pop forward using a subtle drop shadow against the black background.
*   **Masking:** The "wavy" cards in the cart require a `clip-path` or a custom SVG background to achieve the organic, interlocking curves.
