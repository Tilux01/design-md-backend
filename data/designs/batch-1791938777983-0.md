# Design Specification: STREAMPULSE Web Interface

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Background Color:** Solid Black (`#000000`) for the main body, with deep charcoal (`#121212`) sections for depth.
*   **Primary Accent Color:** Vibrant Orange (`#FF4D00`) used for primary buttons, progress bars, and active states.
*   **Text Color:** Primary White (`#FFFFFF`), Secondary Grey (`#A0A0A0`) for metadata.
*   **Layout Style:** Full-screen, edge-to-edge responsive grid. No outer margins or browser-style frames.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Inter** or **Montserrat** (Sans-serif).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:** 
    *   Logo: Bold (700)
    *   Section Headers: Semi-Bold (600)
    *   Hero Title: Extra Bold (800)
    *   Body/Meta: Regular (400)

---

## 3. Header (Navigation Bar)
*   **Position:** Fixed at top, transparent background with a slight dark gradient overlay to ensure visibility against hero images.
*   **Logo (Left):** "STREAMPULSE" in all-caps. The "STREAM" is white, "PULSE" is orange. To the left is a play-button icon (orange triangle inside a white-outlined hexagon).
*   **Nav Links (Center-Left):** "Browse", "TV Shows", "Movies", "New & Popular", "My List". Font size: 14px, Color: White.
*   **Utility Icons (Right):** 
    *   Search Icon (Magnifying glass).
    *   Notification Icon (Bell with a small orange dot).
    *   Profile: Circular avatar of a woman (Sarah), followed by the name "Sarah" and a downward chevron.

---

## 4. Hero Section (Featured Content)
*   **Featured Title:** "THE VANGUARD" in large, white, all-caps sans-serif.
*   **Metadata:** "Action | Thriller | Drama | ⭐ 4.8" (14px, white).
*   **Description:** "A global crisis threatens. One team must navigate darkness. Stream now." (16px, light grey).
*   **Buttons:**
    *   **Play Now:** Orange background (`#FF4D00`), black text, rounded corners (4px). Includes a black play triangle icon.
    *   **More Info:** Semi-transparent grey background (`rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`), white text, rounded corners (4px). Includes a white "+" icon.
*   **Carousel Indicators:** Two horizontal bars at the bottom center. The active one is orange, the inactive is dark grey.

---

## 5. Content Rows (General Styles)
*   **Row Header:** White text, 20px, semi-bold.
*   **Navigation:** Small white left/right chevrons at the far right of each row header.
*   **Card Spacing:** 15px gap between items.

### A. "Continue Watching for Sarah"
*   **Card Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (Landscape).
*   **Visuals:** The card features the show logo at the bottom left and a progress bar at the bottom.
*   **Progress Bar:** A thin line at the bottom of the image. The completed portion is Orange (`#FF4D00`), the remaining is Dark Grey.
*   **Metadata:** Title (White, 14px), Season/Episode (Grey, 12px), "% complete" (Grey, 12px).

### B. "Trending Now" & "StreamPulse Originals"
*   **Card Aspect Ratio:** 2:3 (Portrait/Poster style).
*   **Metadata (Below Image):** 
    *   Title: White, 14px, Bold.
    *   Sub-line: Year | ⭐ Rating | Duration (e.g., "2023 | ⭐ 4.9 | 12m") in 12px Grey.
