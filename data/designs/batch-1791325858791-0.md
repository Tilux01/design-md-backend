# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Outdoor / Premium E-Commerce Minimalist.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-end editorial whitespace with expansive 100vw immersive photography backgrounds, low visual clutter, and strict typographic hierarchy.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Dark mode glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), subtle translucent overlays (`rgba(0,0,0,0.4)`), and razor-thin borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).

# 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient landscape photography background, floating translucent top bar, high-tracking uppercase pill indicators (`ITEM #01`), subtle interactive states.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, backdrop-filter `blur(8px)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-cyan-400 inline-block"></span>`
