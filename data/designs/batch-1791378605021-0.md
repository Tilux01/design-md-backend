# Design Specification: Honey Haven Landing Page Clone

## 1. Global & Layout Settings

*   **View:** Full-screen responsive viewport (`100vw` width, `100vh` height), strictly edge-to-edge. No outer framing, mockups, or browser chrome.
*   **Background:** 
    *   **Base:** Deep dark-chocolate/near-black (`#0B0806`).
    *   **Overlay Element:** A dramatic, macro photography background of a honeycomb structure with glowing warm amber/golden lighting gradients and dark smoky transitions (`radial-gradient` or blurred image layer).
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   `Primary Amber`: `#F5A623` (CTA Button background, highlight accents).
    *   `Primary Text`: `#FFFFFF` (Headings, brand name).
    *   `Secondary Text`: `#CCCCCC` or `#A3A3A3` (Paragraph descriptions, nav links).
    *   `Dark Overlay`: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)` for text readability.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

To replicate this exact luxury aesthetic, use the following font stack:

*   **Primary Font (Headings & Large Display - "HONEY"):** 
    *   *Family:* `Cinzel`, `Didot`, or `Playfair Display` (Ultra-spaced, high-contrast serif).
    *   *Fallback:* `Georgia`, `serif`.
    *   *Styling:* Uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.15em` to `0.2em`.
*   **Body Text & Navigation (Clean UI):**
    *   *Family:* `Inter`, `Montserrat`, or `Helvetica Neue`.
    *   *Fallback:* `sans-serif`.
    *   *Weights:* Regular (`400`) for paragraphs, Medium (`500`) for navigation and button labels.

---

## 3. Section-by-Section Breakdown

### A. Navigation Bar (Header)
*   **Layout:** Flexbox, space-between, fixed at the top with a subtle transparent backdrop blur.
*   **Left Element:** Logo icon (minimalist hexagon/honeycomb icon in white, approx. `32px` size).
*   **Center/Right Links:** 
    *   Items: `shop`, `honey`, `bee pollen`, `about us`, `contact`.
    *   Styling: Lowercase, tracking/letter-spacing applied, color transitions to white on hover.
*   **Far Right Element:** Shopping bag / cart icon with badge indicator (`0`).

### B. Hero Section (Left-Hand Content Block)
*   **Layout:** Absolute/Flex positioning aligned vertically center-left. Max width ~450px.
*   **Sub-Headline / Eyebrow:** 
    *   *Text:* "You’ve arrived at the Haven for"
    *   *Size:* ~16px, medium weight.
*   **Main Headline:** 
    *   *Text:* "100% purest honey delights"
    *   *Size:* ~32-38px, bold serif font.
*   **Paragraph Description:** 
    *   *Text:* `"Welcome to Honey Haven, where we bring you a delectable array of 100% pure honey products. From golden liquid honey to exquisite honey-infused creations, our offerings are a celebration of nature's finest sweetness, meticulously crafted to delight your senses and nourish your well-being"`
    *   *Size:* ~13-14px, line-height 1.6, color `#D1D1D1`.
*   **Call to Action (CTA) Button:**
    *   *Text:* "SHOP NOW"
    *   *Shape:* Pill-shaped / rounded rectangle (`border-radius: 50px`).
    *   *Background:* Solid Amber (`#F5A623`).
    *   *Text Color:* Dark Brown/Black (`#1A1410`), bold weight.
    *   *Hover State:* Slight scale up (`transform: scale(1.05)`) with enhanced drop shadow/glow.

### C. Visual Elements (Right-Hand Side)
*   **Typography Background Element ("HONEY"):**
    *   Large, outlined/stroked text reading **`HONEY`** positioned in the background center-right.
    *   Styling: Heavy serif font, transparent fill with a thin white outline (`-webkit-text-stroke: 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).
*   **3D / Floating Bee Graphic:**
    *   A high-definition 3D render or transparent PNG of a honeybee in mid-flight facing left toward the text block.
    *   *Position:* Central-right, overlapping the background typography.

---

## 4. Animations & Interactivity

*   **Page Load (Entrance Animations):**
    *   *Staggered Fade-in:* The header loads instantly, followed by a smooth upward fade (`translateY` from `20px` to `0`) for the left-hand text block.
    *   *Background Honeycomb:* Subtle slow zoom (Ken Burns effect) or floating gradient shift to create depth.
*   **Bee Animation:**
    *   *Floating/Hover Loop:* A continuous, gentle vertical bobbing motion (using CSS keyframes: `transform: translateY(0px)` to `translateY(-10px)` over 3–4 seconds) simulating natural flight/hovering.
    *   *Wing Blur:* Rapid subtle opacity/scale oscillation on the wings to simulate motion blur.
*   **Interactive States:**
    *   Navigation items show a subtle color glow/underline on hover.
    *   The "SHOP NOW" button features smooth transition physics (`transition: all 0.3s ease`).
