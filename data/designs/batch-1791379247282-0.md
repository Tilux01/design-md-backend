# Design Specification: Glass Effect UI Overlay

This specification outlines the exact visual design, layout hierarchy, typography, color palette, and animation for cloning the provided media into a fully responsive, full-screen digital interface.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

*   **View Mode:** Full-Screen (`100vw` width, `100vh` height).
*   **Background:** 
    *   An immersive, cinematic visual featuring a futuristic portrait of a person inside a transparent spherical helmet/pod.
    *   Lighting dynamics: Strong yellow/amber warm light source on the left side, contrasted with cool cyan/blue atmospheric lighting from the top and bottom.
*   **Central Content Card (Glassmorphism):**
    *   **Position:** Centered horizontally, vertically positioned in the lower-middle portion of the viewport.
    *   **Dimensions:** Fluid width (approx. `85%` max-width `540px`), auto-height based on content padding.
    *   **Border Radius:** Rounded corners (`24px` to `32px`).
    *   **Border:** Subtle inset border with a gradient stroke transitioning from semi-transparent white/gold to dark grey.
    *   **Glass Effect (Frosted Glass / Glassmorphism):**
        *   `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` (simulates optical light scattering).
        *   `background: rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.35)` (dark semi-transparent tint to ensure text contrast).
        *   Textured overlay: A subtle high-frequency noise or pebbled glass displacement map to create the rippled distortion effect seen over the yellow garment.

---

## 2. Typography & Hierarchy

### Primary Font Stack
*   **Heading Font:** **Plus Jakarta Sans**, **Inter**, or **SF Pro Display** (Geometric Sans-Serif).
*   **Body Font:** **Inter**, **Roboto**, or **Helvetica Neue** (Clean, highly legible sans-serif).

> **Fallback Strategy:**
> ```css
> font-family: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;
> ```

### Text Specifications

1.  **Main Heading ("Glass Effect")**
    *   **Font Size:** `40px` (Desktop) / `32px` (Mobile)
    *   **Font Weight:** Bold (`700`)
    *   **Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
    *   **Line Height:** `1.1`
    *   **Letter Spacing:** Tight (`-0.02em`)

2.  **Body Text (Lorem Ipsum paragraph)**
    *   **Font Size:** `15px` (Desktop) / `14px` (Mobile)
    *   **Font Weight:** Regular (`400`)
    *   **Color:** Light Off-White / Silver (`#E0E0E0`)
    *   **Line Height:** `1.5`
    *   **Margin Top:** `12px`
    *   **Margin Bottom:** `20px`

3.  **Horizontal Accent Divider Line**
    *   **Width:** Exactly aligned with the text block start, roughly `120px` wide.
    *   **Height:** `2px`
    *   **Color:** Solid White (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle soft outer glow.

---

## 3. Color Palette & Lighting

*   **Warm Accent (Yellow/Gold):** `#FFB703` (Primary light bleed from the left).
*   **Cool Accent (Cyan/Blue):** `#00B4D8` (Atmospheric rim light).
*   **Deep Dark (Base Shadows):** `#0B0F19` (Cinematic dark background tone).
*   **Glass Overlay Tint:** `rgba(15, 15, 15, 0.45)` with dynamic refraction.

---

## 4. Animation & Interaction Specifications

To bring this exact design to life, implement the following micro-interactions:

1.  **Initial Load (Entrance Animation):**
    *   **Background:** Scales in smoothly from `1.05` to `1.0` scale over `1.2s` using `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
    *   **Glass Card:** Fades in and slides up from `Y: +30px` to `Y: 0px` with an opacity transition from `0` to `1` over `0.8s` (Delayed by `0.3s`).

2.  **Hover State (Parallax Glass Card):**
    *   Subtle mouse-following tilt effect (Vanilla JS or Framer Motion `useMotionValue`) giving a true 3D depth impression as the cursor moves across the screen.
    *   Border gradient shifts slightly to mimic light catching the edge of physical glass.
