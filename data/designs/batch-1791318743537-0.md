# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial E-Commerce / Modern Minimalist Luxury Sportswear.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace with clean grid separation. Balanced padding (16px to 32px card padding) ensuring clear visual hierarchy and breathing room around high-impact product imagery.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean, flat white/light-gray background canvas (`#FFFFFF` to `#F9FAFB`) with subtle product cards featuring minimal 1px borders (`#E5E7EB`) and soft hover elevations (`shadow-lg`). Dark mode contrast utilized strictly for top announcement bar, promotional banners, and the footer architecture (`#0B0B0C`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, minimalist category icons, subtle glassmorphism on promotional dark overlays, star rating indicators.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: 4px 12px; font-size: 12px; font-weight: 600; tracking: 0.05em; border-radius: 9999px; background translucency: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1);` border width: 1px.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Star (★) -> Use `&#9733;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
