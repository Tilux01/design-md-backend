# Design Specification: RENTRA Car Rental App

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Color Palette
- **Primary Dark (Backgrounds & Text):** `#111111` (Near-black, sleek automotive aesthetic)
- **Secondary Dark:** `#1D1D1D` (Card backgrounds, buttons)
- **Neutral Light (App Background):** `#E8E8E8` / `#F0F0F0` (Soft light grey for screen backgrounds)
- **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF` (Text, highlights, container backgrounds)
- **Accent Orange/Red:** `#FF6A00` / `#FF3B00` (Tail light glows, highlight badges)
- **Border/Divider Gray:** `#D0D0D0`

### Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif
  - *Fallback Strategy:* `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, sans-serif`
- **Font Weights Used:**
  - Regular (`400`): Body descriptions, metadata
  - Medium (`500`): Navigation labels, secondary buttons
  - Bold (`700` / `800`): Brand headers, titles, primary pricing/actions

---

## 2. Screen 1: Welcome / Onboarding Screen

### Layout & Structure
- **Background:** Solid light gray (`#E8E8E8`).
- **Header / Brand:** 
  - Top-left placement of the **RENTRA Logo mark** (A geometric, stylized 'R' monogram in solid black).
- **Hero Text Content (Top to Bottom):**
  - Subtitle: `"WELCOME TO"` (Uppercase, smaller font, muted gray, weight `500`).
  - Title: `"RENTRA"` (Large, bold, dark text, tracking tight).
  - Description Paragraph: `"Find, Book, and Ride the perfect vehicle for every journey."` (The words *"Find"*, *"Book"*, and *"Ride"* are styled in bolder weight `700`).
- **Hero Image (Vehicle):**
  - **Description:** A rear three-quarter view of a vintage/cyberpunk-inspired high-performance black sports car (reminiscent of a classic Porsche or Group B rally car). Prominent wide body kit, deep dish wheels, dual exhaust pipes, and glowing vibrant orange-red horizontal tail lights illuminating the rear grille.
  - **Background State:** Backgroundless/isolated render on a clean white/light gradient floor.
  - **Placement:** Anchored towards the lower center, overlapping the bottom CTA area.
- **Call-to-Action (CTA) Button:**
  - **Container:** Dark pill-shaped container (`#111111`) stretching across the bottom with generous padding.
  - **Left Text:** `"GET START FOR RIDE"` (White, uppercase, bold, tracking wide).
  - **Right Icon:** A circular white button holding a black right-arrow icon (`→`).

---

## 3. Screen 2: Dashboard / Home Screen

### Status Bar & Header
- **Status Bar:** Standard iOS layout featuring time (`16:26`), cellular signal, WiFi, and battery indicator in dark monochrome.
- **Top Bar:**
  - Left: RENTRA logo mark.
  - Center: `"RENTRA"` wordmark (bold, centered).
  - Right: User profile avatar icon (circular outline with silhouette).

### Hero Banner / Search Section
- **Banner Image:**
  - **Description:** A cinematic side-profile shot of a dark luxury sports car (e.g., Porsche) parked in a minimalist showroom environment. A bright, glowing yellow/lime ring light or reflection highlights the front wheel arch.
  - **Placement:** Edge-to-edge curved header card with dark gradient overlay.
- **Search Bar:**
  - **Style:** Floating translucent/glassmorphism pill bar overlapping the bottom of the hero banner.
  - **Content:** Search icon on the left, placeholder text `"search your destination"`, and a filter icon button on the far right inside a white circular badge.

### Section 1: Top Choice Cars
- **Section Header:**
  - Left: `"Top Choice Cars"` (Bold, size `18px`). Subtitle below: `"Planning your trip?"` (Muted gray, `14px`).
  - Right: `"View all"` link (Muted gray, medium weight).
- **Horizontal Car Cards:**
  - **Card Container:** Pure white rounded card (`#FFFFFF`) with soft drop-shadow.
  - **Top-Right Badge:** Rating badge (`★ 4.9`) with white background and dark text.
  - **Image:** Front-facing view of a classic black sports car (Porsche 816) with illuminated red tail lights facing the camera, isolated on white.
  - **Car Title:** `"Porsche 816"` (Bold, `16px`).
  - **Metadata Pills:**
    - Location pill: Pin icon + `"Spain"`
    - Performance pill: Speedometer icon + `"262km/h"`
  - **Pricing:** `" $1200"` (Large, bold, dark text aligned to the bottom-right).

### Section 2: New Cars
- **Section Header:**
  - Left: `"New Cars"`
  - Subtitle: `"Find perfect car for your trip"`
- **Thumbnail Carousel:**
  - A horizontal row of preview cards showing alternative vehicles (e.g., modern muscle cars, orange sports cars) set against scenic backdrops (desert highways, winter roads).

### Bottom Navigation Bar
- **Style:** Floating pill-shaped navigation dock with a solid dark background (`#111111`).
- **Icons (Left to Right):**
  1. Home icon (Highlighted inside an active white circular container).
  2. Search / Explore icon (Magnifying glass).
  3. Wishlist / Favorites icon (Heart).
  4. Messages / Chat icon (Speech bubble).
  5. Profile icon (User silhouette).
