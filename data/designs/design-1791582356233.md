# UI/UX Specification: E-Commerce Landing Page (American Tall Water Bottle)

This document provides a detailed specification for recreating the provided e-commerce landing page design. The layout is structured as a modern Bento-box style grid with a monochrome palette (black, white, and shades of gray).

---

## 1. Global Styles & Layout Structure

### **Canvas & Viewport**
- **Layout Type:** Full-screen responsive grid container (no outer mockup frames, mockups, or drop shadows from the presentation view).
- **Aspect Ratio / Grid:** Fluid grid spanning 100vw and 100vh.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Background: `#EAEAEA` (Light gray canvas background)
  - Primary UI & Text: `#000000` (Pitch black)
  - Surface Blocks: `#FFFFFF` (White) and `#EAEAEA` (Light gray block fills)
  - Accent / Hover States: `#000000`

### **Typography**
- **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, sans-serif.
- **Fallback Stack:** `sans-serif`
- **Weights Used:** Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (700), Heavy/Black (900).

- **Background:** White (`#FFFFFF`) with a clean black bottom border.
- **Height:** ~60px

  1. **Hamburger Menu Icon:** Left aligned. Three horizontal black lines.
  2. **Navigation Links:** Center/Left aligned.

     - Placeholder text: `SEARCH` (All caps, small, gray).
