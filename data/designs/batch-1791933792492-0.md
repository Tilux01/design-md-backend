# Design Specification (`design.md`)

## 1. Overview & Layout Strategy
A full-screen split/layered portfolio hero section combining a rich, dark amber/orange aesthetic on the left with a clean, high-contrast white card container on the right. 

* **Viewport:** 100vw, 100vh, `overflow: hidden`.
* **Grid/Structure:** Two-column asymmetric layout with a centered, overlapping cutout hero portrait.
* **Background:** Deep radial and angular gradients transitioning from `#0A0502` to `#D35400` and `#E67E22`, overlaid with faint, large geometric circular shapes.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Accent Warm:** `#FF7A00` / `#E65100` (Gradients and text accents)
* **Dark Base:** `#0F0A06` (Hero background and dark texts)
* **Light Base:** `#FFFFFF` (Right panel, card surfaces, CTA button)
* **Muted Gray:** `#666666` (Subtitles, list items)
* **Border/Outline:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` (Dark mode pills/icons)

---

## 3. Typography
* **Primary Font:** *Plus Jakarta Sans* or *Outfit* (Geometric Sans)
* **Fallback Stack:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
* **Hierarchy:**
  * **Main Headline (Left):** 72px–88px, Weight: 700, Leading: 1.05, Tracking: -0.03em, Color: `#FFFFFF`.
  * **Bio Heading (Right):** 28px–32px, Weight: 600, Leading: 1.25, Color: `#1A1A1A` (Key phrases colored `#E65100`).
  * **Body / Feature List:** 13px–14px, Weight: 500, Leading: 1.4, Color: `#4A4A4A`.
  * **Numbers / Metrics:** 24px–28px, Weight: 700, Color: `#0F0A06`.
  * **Card Labels / Badges:** 11px–12px, Weight: 600.

---

## 4. Imagery & Asset Specifications

### A. Main Hero Portrait
* **Format:** Cutout PNG with a transparent background.
* **Subject:** Young South Asian male with short dark hair, light beard, black modern sunglasses, and a black hoodie, facing slightly right and looking upward.
* **Lighting:** High-contrast studio rim light with strong orange/amber highlights along hair, face profile, and shoulder edges.
* **Placement:** Bottom-center/left-center, cropped at chest level, layered between background graphics and foreground UI cards.

### B. Mini Testimonial Avatar
* **Format:** Rounded square image (`border-radius: 10px`).
* **Subject:** Close-up thumbnail matching the main hero portrait.

---

## 5. Component Breakdown

### Top Navigation & Branding
* **Brand Mark (Top-Left):** Minimalist abstract white glyph (two mirrored semicircular brackets forming an open oval).
* **Navigation Links (Top-Right of white panel):** Horizontal inline list (`About`, `Projects`, `Services`, `Contact`), 14px, Weight: 500, Color: `#333333`.

### Left Hero Section
1. **Role Pill Badge:** Bordered pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`), text: `Lovish • Web Developer`.
2. **Headline:** Multi-line text reading `Build / Modern / Websites`.
3. **CTA Button:**
   * Style: Solid white pill, black text (`View My Work`).
   * Shadow: Soft radial ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 25px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`).
4. **Metric Stat Cards (Bottom-Left):**
   * Two adjacent white cards (`border-radius: 14px`, padding: 12px 16px).
   * Include mini orange trend icon in the top right.
   * **Card 1:** Label: `Projects Built`, Value: `25+`.
   * **Card 2:** Label: `Client Support`, Value: `24/7`.

### Center-Bottom Social Links
* Three floating glassmorphism/pill circles containing Instagram, LinkedIn, and Globe/Website icons.
* Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, Background: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`.

### Right Panel (White Section)
* **Container:** Large white surface with rounded top-left corner (`border-radius: 36px 0 0 36px`), occupying the upper and right viewport.
* **Status Badge:** Small pill reading `Available` (Light gray border, 11px).
* **Bio Section:** Headline text emphasizing `"modern, fast websites"` in orange.
* **Feature Bullet List:** Unstyled vertical list:
  * `Clean design.`
  * `Smooth animations.`
  * `Responsive builds.`
* **Testimonial Card (Bottom-Right):**
  * Soft drop-shadow card (`background: #FFFFFF`, `border-radius: 16px`, padding: 16px).
  * Header: Mini avatar + Name (`Lovish`) + Subtitle (`Website Developer`).
  * Body: `“I create premium websites that look clean and convert.”` (15px, Weight: 600).
