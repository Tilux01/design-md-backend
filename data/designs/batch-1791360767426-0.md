# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Cinematic Dark Mode Editorial & Digital Entertainment Portal (Neo-Brutalist & High-Contrast Media Branding).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High visual impact centered around bold oversized typography (`DEADPOOL`), asymmetrical balancing, immersive layered character imagery, and compact telemetry navigation bars.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Deep layered stacking architecture utilizing absolute-positioned character artwork layered between background typography masks and foreground navigation HUDs. Subtle backdrop blurs, high-contrast drop shadows (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`), and hairline crimson borders (`#EF4444`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, cinematic backdrop blur panels, glowing badge indicators (`animate-pulse`), monochromatic dark mode surface hierarchies.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `700`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-red-500 inline-block"></span>`.
