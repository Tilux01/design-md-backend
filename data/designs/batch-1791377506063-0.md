# Visual Style & Design Specification
**Media Reference:** Custom Tailored Double-Breasted Suit Display  
**Layout:** Full-Screen Live-Action Video / Photography Composition

---

## 1. Overall Layout & Composition
*   **Viewport:** Full-screen immersive display (`100vw` × `100vh`).
*   **Perspective:** Eye-level, medium-full shot capturing a subject in a boutique setting.
*   **Focal Point:** The human subject wearing a custom grey-green double-breasted suit, gesturing forward to reveal an elaborate inner-sleeve lining and matching tie.
*   **Depth of Field:** Shallow-to-medium depth; the subject is crisp in the foreground, while the background (mannequins, clothing racks, retail shelving) exhibits a soft, natural optical blur.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Lighting
*   **Primary Subject Palette:**
    *   **Suit (Sage/Slate Green):** `#4A6055` (Double-breasted jacket and trousers).
    *   **Shirt (Crisp White):** `#FFFFFF` (Visible cuffs and collar).
    *   **Tie & Sleeve Lining Accent:** Deep charcoal grey/black base (`#2B2B2B`) interwoven with intricate metallic gold/copper floral scrollwork (`#C5A059` to `#8C6737`).
*   **Background Environment Palette:**
    *   **Mannequin Suit:** Warm amber-ochre/camel brown (`#C67D3B`) with a silver metallic mirror-finish head.
    *   **Background Suits:** Navy blue (`#1B2A4A`), charcoal (`#333333`), and beige (`#D4C5B9`) on hanging rails.
    *   **Flooring:** Polished white marble/tile with subtle grey veining (`#F0F2F5`).
*   **Lighting Scheme:**
    *   **Source:** Modern commercial retail downlighting (recessed ceiling spotlights).
    *   **Quality:** Crisp, neutral-white overhead lighting with subtle highlights reflecting off the glossy mannequin head and polished tile floor.

---

## 3. Typography & Text Styling
*(Note: There is no embedded digital UI typography in the source media; however, this specification defines the stylistic typography system if text elements—such as branding, captions, or product callouts—were to be overlaid.)*

*   **Primary Display / Branding Font:**
    *   **Family:** *Cinzel* or *Playfair Display* (High-end, elegant serif reflecting bespoke tailoring).
    *   **Fallback Stack:** `Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif`.
*   **Secondary / Body Font:**
    *   **Family:** *Montserrat* or *Inter* (Clean, modern sans-serif for legibility).
    *   **Fallback Stack:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.
*   **Styling Rules:**
    *   **Headings:** Uppercase, letter-spaced (`tracking-wider`), colored in warm gold (`#C5A059`) or crisp white (`#FFFFFF`).
    *   **Body Text:** Regular weight, anti-aliased, in soft off-white (`#E5E5E5`) for high contrast against dark retail backgrounds.

---

## 4. UI Elements & Components (If Applicable)
*   *None present in the source media.* The composition functions purely as a cinematic visual display or promotional video frame.

---

## 5. Animation & Motion Specification (For Video Context)
*   **Camera Movement:** Static tripod shot with subtle handheld micro-movements to maintain organic realism.
*   **Subject Motion:**
    *   **Dynamic Reveal:** The subject pulls or extends their arm forward to deliberately showcase the hidden decorative inner lining of the sleeve.
    *   **Secondary Action:** Holding a matching patterned tie across the lower left foreground, introducing depth and texture continuity.
*   **Transitions:** Smooth continuous cut / live-action feed without digital transition effects.
