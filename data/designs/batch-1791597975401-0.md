# Design Specification: Aurelia Residences Hero Section

## 1. Overview
This design is a high-end, luxury real estate website hero section. It features a full-bleed, high-resolution background image, minimalist typography, and a refined color palette utilizing deep blues, stark whites, and a signature gold accent. The layout is asymmetrical, balancing a heavy left-aligned typographic block with delicate right-aligned navigational elements.

## 2. Global Styles

### 2.1 Color Palette
*   **Primary Text:** White (`#FFFFFF`)
*   **Secondary Text (Faded):** Light Gray with reduced opacity (approx. `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`)
*   **Accent Color (Buttons/Icons):** Muted Gold/Champagne (approx. `#D2A765` or `#C5A059`)
*   **Background:** The background is entirely driven by the hero image (deep twilight blues and warm architectural lighting).

### 2.2 Typography
The design relies on a classic high-contrast pairing of an elegant Serif for display and a clean Sans-Serif for utility.

*   **Primary Font (Display/Headings):**
    *   *Visual Match:* A high-contrast transitional or modern serif.
    *   *Suggested Font:* **Playfair Display** or **Cinzel**.
    *   *Fallback Stack:* `'Playfair Display', 'Didot', 'Bodoni MT', 'Times New Roman', serif`
    *   *Usage:* Logo primary text, Main Hero Headline.
*   **Secondary Font (UI/Body/Navigation):**
    *   *Visual Match:* A geometric, highly legible sans-serif with wide letter-spacing.
    *   *Suggested Font:* **Montserrat** or **Lato**.
    *   *Fallback Stack:* `'Montserrat', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif`
    *   *Usage:* Top utility bar, Main navigation links, Logo subtext, Buttons, Vertical indicators, Subheadings.

## 3. Image Specifications

### 3.1 Main Background Image
*   **Description:** A high-quality, photorealistic architectural rendering or photograph of a luxury high-rise residential building. The perspective is a dramatic low-angle shot looking up at the building. The time of day is twilight/dusk, resulting in a rich, dark blue gradient sky. The building features a modern glass facade with vertical ribs and balconies. Warm, golden interior lights are glowing from various windows, creating a striking contrast against the cool blue sky.
*   **Type:** Full photographic background. It is **not** backgroundless/transparent.
*   **Placement & CSS Behavior:** Spans `100vw` and `100vh`. Should be implemented as a background image with `background-size: cover;`, `background-position: center center;`, and `background-repeat: no-repeat;`.
*   **Rotation:** 0 degrees (Standard).

## 4. Layout & Component Details (Top to Bottom, Left to Right)

### 4.1 Utility Bar (Absolute Top edge)
A very thin, low-opacity row sitting flush at the top of the viewport.
*   **Left Side:** Breadcrumb or minimal navigational text (e.g., `HOME >`). Font: Sans-serif, extremely small (approx 10px), uppercase, white at 50% opacity.
*   **Right Side:** Utility icons and selectors.
    *   Search Icon (magnifying glass)
    *   Text: `5000 DW v` (Dropdown indicator next to it).
    *   Globe Icon.
    *   Text: `EN`
    *   *Styling:* Sans-serif, approx 10px, uppercase, white at 70% opacity. Spacing between items is approximately 15px.

### 4.2 Main Header Navigation
Located directly below the utility bar. Spans the full width with substantial left and right padding (approx. 5% of viewport width).

*   **Logo (Left Aligned):**
    *   **"AURELIA"**: Serif font, Uppercase, approx 24px-28px, White, normal weight.
    *   **"RESIDENCES"**: Positioned directly underneath "AURELIA". Sans-serif font, Uppercase, approx 10px, wide letter-spacing (approx. `3px`), White at 70% opacity.
*   **Navigation Links (Center Aligned):**
    *   Items: `Residences` | `Amenities` | `Architecture` | `Location` | `Gallery`
    *   Styling: Sans-serif font, Title Case, approx 14px, White, normal weight. Spacing between links is wide (approx. 30px).
*   **Right Actions (Right Aligned):**
    *   **"BOOK A VIEWING" Button:** Rectangular button with sharp corners. Background color: Muted Gold (`#D2A765`). Text: Sans-serif, Uppercase, approx 12px, White, bold weight. Padding: approx 10px top/bottom, 20px left/right.
    *   **Hamburger Menu:** Located to the right of the button. Three distinct horizontal white lines, equal width, stacked vertically.

### 4.3 Left Content Block (Hero Typography)
Vertically centered within the viewport, aligned to the left (respecting the same left padding as the logo).

*   **Subheading:**
    *   Text: "Central District"
    *   Styling: Serif font, Title case, approx 18px, White, slightly italicized or lighter weight. Margin-bottom: approx 15px.
*   **Main Headline:**
    *   Text: "YOUR LIFE." (Line 1) <br> "ELEVATED." (Line 2)
    *   Styling: Serif font, Uppercase, approx 56px-64px, White, normal weight. Line-height is tight (approx 1.1).
*   **CTA Button (Ghost Button):**
    *   Text: "EXPLORE THE RESIDENCES"
    *   Styling: Transparent background, thin (1px) solid White border. Text is Sans-serif, Uppercase, approx 12px, White, wide letter-spacing. Padding: approx 15px top/bottom, 25px left/right. Margin-top: approx 30px.
*   **Left-edge Slide Indicators (Absolute Left):**
    *   Located to the left of the main typography block, very close to the screen edge.
    *   Three vertically stacked small circular dots.
    *   Top dot: Hollow (white border, transparent center).
    *   Middle dot: Solid white (indicates active state).
    *   Bottom dot: Hollow (white border, transparent center).

### 4.4 Right Vertical Slider Indicator
Positioned on the far right side of the viewport (respecting the right padding), vertically spanning the middle section.

*   **Top Number:** `02` (Sans-serif, approx 12px, White, normal weight).
*   **Connecting Line:** A thin, solid white vertical line connecting the top number to the bottom text/number.
*   **Rotated Text:** Located along the vertical line.
    *   Text: `AMENITIES`
    *   Styling: Sans-serif, Uppercase, approx 10px, White, wide letter-spacing.
    *   **Rotation:** Rotated exactly **-90 degrees** (counter-clockwise), so it reads from bottom to top.
*   **Bottom Number & Indicator:**
    *   Number: `01` (Sans-serif, approx 14px, White, bolder weight than '02').
    *   Indicator: A solid white circle placed immediately to the right of the "01".

### 4.5 Bottom Corner Elements
*   **Bottom Left Corner:** A very small, faint circular icon or badge (looks like a minimalist copyright or secondary logo). White outline, highly transparent.
*   **Bottom Right Corner:**
    *   Text: "STREET LEVEL"
    *   Styling: Sans-serif, Uppercase, approx 12px, White, bold weight, aligned to the right.
    *   Icon: Placed to the right of the text. A perfect circle with a solid Muted Gold (`#D2A765`) background. Inside the circle is a white icon (resembles a building, a list, or a directional arrow). Vertically centered with the text.
