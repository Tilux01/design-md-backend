# Design Specification: Portfolio Landing Page (Olympus Mons)

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
- **Primary Mood & Archetype:** Cyberpunk-minimalist, mysterious, high-contrast, modern creator portfolio.
- **Visual Density:** Low density, high negative space, centered focal point with strategic lateral anchors.
- **Theme Balance:** 100% Dark Mode. The interface relies on absolute black (`#000000`) paired with vibrant electric green neon glows to establish stark hierarchy and immediate visual focus on the center portrait subject.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

### Core Colors
- **Brand Neon Green (Primary Accent):** `#00FF66` (Used for logo highlight, glowing background circle ring, and location label).
- **Absolute Black (Base Background):** `#000000` (Canvas/Viewport background).
- **Primary Pure White (Text/Icons):** `#FFFFFF` (Used for headings, main navigation items, and social icon glyphs).
- **Muted Gray (Secondary Text):** `#A0A0A0` (Used for sub-labels and secondary metadata).

### UI Tokens
- **Surface Layer / Card Background:** Transparent over black base.
- **Border/Divider Color:** None (relying entirely on typographic weight and spatial separation).

---

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy

- **Primary Font Family (Headings & UI):** Inter / Sans-serif geometric typeface.
- **Monospace/Logo Font:** Custom geometric brand font for "Olympus Mons".

### Type Scale Breakdown
- **Logo / Brand Header:** `20px`, Font-Weight: 700 (Semi-bold), Tracking: +1.5px.
- **User Name (`Jane Doe`):** `14px`, Font-Weight: 500 (Medium), Color: `#FFFFFF`.
- **Primary Navigation Links (`Home`, `About`, `Contact`):** `14px`, Font-Weight: 400 (Regular), Vertical Orientation (Rotated / Stacked).
- **Role Subtitle (`Graphics Designer`):** `14px`, Font-Weight: 400 (Regular), Color: `#FFFFFF`.
- **Location Accent (`BAY AREA`):** `14px`, Font-Weight: 700 (Bold), Color: `#00FF66`, Tracking: +1px.

---

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid

- **Container Structure:** Fixed aspect-ratio card interface bounded inside a dark desktop window frame (representative of a web viewport).
- **Layout Model:** Absolute/Flex hybrid layout with a strong central hero focus and peripheral edge alignment.
- **Padding & Margins:**
  - Outer viewport padding: `40px` top/left/right/bottom.
  - Corner radius of container card: `24px`.
- **Section Sequence (Hero View):**
  - **Top Left:** Minimalist vertical line art logo + `Jane Doe`.
  - **Top Right:** Brand text `Olympus Mons`.
  - **Center:** Glowing electric green circular ring framing a portrait masked in a dark aesthetic.
  - **Bottom Left:** Profession title & region indicator (`Graphics Designer` / `BAY AREA`).
  - **Bottom Right:** Social links row (Dribbble, Twitter, Instagram, Facebook).
  - **Middle Right Edge:** Vertical navigation stack (`Home`, `About`, `Contact`).

---

## 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement

### Top-Left Brand Identity
- **Logo Icon:** Series of 5 vertical bar lines varying slightly in height, finished in pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Spacing:** `12px` gap between the bar icon and `Jane Doe` text string.

### Central Hero Frame
- **Graphic Element:** Perfect neon green circular stroke (`#00FF66`) with an outer atmospheric glow blur effect.
- **Portrait Subject:** Centered precisely inside the green ring, utilizing dark-clothed contrast against the absolute black background.

### Navigation Components
- **Right Edge Sidebar:** 
  - Items stacked vertically with generous vertical spacing (~`24px` gap).
  - Text alignment: Right-aligned relative to the right margin.
- **Footer Social Icons:**
  - Row of 4 monochrome white line icons (Dribbble, Twitter, Instagram, Facebook).
  - Icon container size: `24px` × `24px`.
  - Gap between icons: `16px`.

---

## 6. Motion Mechanics & Interaction States

- **Hover States:**
  - Navigation links: Transition opacity from `1.0` to `0.6` or shift color to `#00FF66`.
  - Social icons: Subtle scaling (`scale(1.1)`) with color fill transitioning to `#00FF66`.
- **Animation Triggers:**
  - Neon green circle features a subtle breathing/pulsing glow loop (`cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.6, 1)`, duration: `3s`).
  - Page load sequence: Fade-in blur for portrait image combined with slide-up for typography elements.
