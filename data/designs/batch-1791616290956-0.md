# UI Design Specification: Tangelo Breeze Smoothie App

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Configuration
* **Viewport:** Fullscreen responsive layout with a centered, rounded glassmorphism card container.
* **Canvas Background:** A soft, blurred, ambient gradient transitioning from deep blues at the bottom/left to warm peach, orange, and pale yellow tones at the top/right (simulating sunset/sunrise lighting).
* **Main Card Container:**
  * **Shape:** Rounded rectangle with high border-radius ($\approx 32px$).
  * **Material Effect:** Frosted glassmorphism (semi-transparent white overlay with backdrop-blur, subtle inner shadow, and a thin 1px white/transparent border).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
* **Primary Typeface:** Clean, geometric sans-serif (e.g., **Inter**, **SF Pro Display**, or **Poppins**).
* **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
* **Text Styles & Hierarchy:**
  * **Main Title ("Tangelo Breeze"):**
    * *Font Weight:* Bold / Extra Bold ($\approx 700-800$).
    * *Color:* Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
    * *Size:* Large display size ($\approx 36px - 44px$).
    * *Letter Spacing:* Tight.
  * **Button Label ("Squeeze →"):**
    * *Font Weight:* Medium ($\approx 500$).
    * *Color:* White / Light Blue (`#E0F2FE`).
    * *Size:* $\approx 14px$.
  * **Bottom Carousel / Option Labels (e.g., "Mandarin Smoothie"):**
    * *Font Weight:* Regular / Medium ($\approx 400-500$).
    * *Color:* White with high opacity ($\approx 80%-90%$).
    * *Size:* $\approx 11px - 12px$, centered alignment below icons.

---

## 3. Component Breakdown & Placement

### A. Top Navigation Bar (Left Side)
* **Trash/Bin Icon:** Placed in a rounded white pill/square container with low opacity background and blur.
* **Lollipop/Swirl Icon:** Positioned to the right of the trash icon.

### B. Top Navigation Bar (Right Side)
* **Utility Icons:** A vertical or clustered group of outline icons (Home, Settings, Profile/Preferences) rendered in a subtle white stroke on frosted buttons.

### C. Hero Section (Left)
* **Text Block:** Positioned on the mid-left area.
  * Line 1: "Tangelo"
  * Line 2: "Breeze"
* **Action Button ("Squeeze →"):**
  * *Shape:* Pill-shaped button (`border-radius: 50px`).
  * *Background:* Vibrant cyan/light-blue gradient (`#38BDF8` to `#0EA5E9`).
  * *Shadow:* Soft cyan drop shadow for a glowing effect.
  * *Content:* White text "Squeeze" followed by a right arrow icon (`→`).

### D. Central Hero Illustration (Cup & Floating Fruit)
* **Central Cup ("Tangelo Breeze"):**
  * *Description:* A clear, tall plastic cup with a dome lid and two black straws sticking out.
  * *Content:* Filled with a pale pink/lavender creamy smoothie.
  * *Facial Expression:* A minimalist, cute kawaii face printed on the front (closed happy curved eyes and a smiling mouth in dark charcoal/black).
  * *Spill/Splash:* A white, organic liquid splash shape pooling beneath the base of the cup.
* **Floating Fruit Elements (Backgroundless PNGs):**
  * *Top-Left Wedge:* A sliced segment of pink grapefruit/tangelo, angled diagonally with the red-pink pulp facing up and a soft drop shadow beneath it.
  * *Top-Right Wedge:* Another pink citrus wedge floating near the upper-right corner of the cup.
  * *Bottom-Left Wedge:* A rich orange-pink citrus segment positioned diagonally below the left side of the cup.
  * *Bottom-Right Wedge:* A bright orange citrus segment floating to the lower-right of the cup, casting a soft shadow onto the blue gradient surface below.

### E. Right-Side Ingredient Selector Grid
* **Layout:** A 2-column or 3-column grid of frosted glass tiles representing different ingredients/flavors.
* **Active State Tile:** One tile is highlighted with a solid white background and a subtle drop shadow (containing a citrus slice outline icon).
* **Inactive State Tiles:** Semi-transparent glass tiles with minimalist white outline icons representing various fruits, leaves, and smoothie options.

### F. Bottom Smoothie Carousel / Menu Bar
* **Layout:** Horizontal row of 4-5 option columns evenly spaced across the bottom third of the card.
* **Content per Item:**
  * Top: A minimalist line-art icon (e.g., citrus segments, ice crystals, droplets).
  * Middle: Two-line text label (e.g., "Mandarin / Smoothie").
  * Bottom: Indicator dots (e.g., active blue dot indicator under the selected item).

---

## 4. Color Palette & Effects
* **Background Gradient:** Ambient blend of `#1E3A8A` (Deep Blue), `#3B82F6` (Royal Blue), `#F43F5E` (Rose/Coral), and `#FBBF24` (Amber).
* **Card Surface:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.
* **Primary Accent:** Cyan (`#0EA5E9`).
* **Text Primary:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Text Secondary:** Muted Ice Blue (`#CBD5E1`).
