This document provides a comprehensive design specification to recreate the provided UI design for **SUKOYA**. The goal is a pixel-perfect, full-screen implementation.

---

# Design Specification: SUKOYA Landing/Footer

## 1. General Styles
*   **Background Color:** The base layer is a deep charcoal/black (`#050505`).
*   **Global Texture:** A subtle film grain/noise overlay is applied across the entire screen to give it a tactile, cinematic feel.
*   **Layout:** A full-height, full-width container with a flex or grid structure, ensuring the "SUKOYA" text remains anchored to the bottom.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Brand Font (SUKOYA):** 
    *   **Style:** Ultra-bold, extended (wide) geometric sans-serif.
    *   **Recommended:** *Monument Extended (Ultrabold)* or *Integral CF (Heavy)*.
    *   **Fallback:** `Impact, "Arial Black", sans-serif` (with `letter-spacing: -2px` and `transform: scaleX(1.2)`).
*   **UI/Navigation Font:** 
    *   **Style:** Clean, medium-weight geometric sans-serif.
    *   **Recommended:** *Inter*, *Montserrat*, or *Helvetica Neue*.
    *   **Fallback:** `system-ui, sans-serif`.
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   Primary: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
    *   Secondary/Muted: Light Grey (`#A0A0A0`).
    *   Accent: Peach/Soft Orange (`#FFB88C`) for the "WORK FAST" text.

---

## 3. Background Image Description
Instead of a solid color, the top two-thirds of the screen features a high-art illustration.

*   **Content:** A cinematic, painterly landscape at twilight.
    *   **Subject:** A lone silhouette of a person sitting cross-legged in the center-middle, facing away from the viewer.
    *   **Foreground:** A dark, grassy meadow with tiny, indistinguishable wildflowers.
    *   **Midground:** A vast valley leading to a glowing, dense city skyline in the far distance, nestled between two dark mountain ranges.
    *   **Sky:** A deep navy blue sky filled with stars. A single, bright shooting star streaks from the top left toward the center.
    *   **Clouds:** Massive, stylized cumulus clouds frame the left and right sides. The clouds are caught in the "golden hour" light, with bright peach/orange highlights on their tops and deep blue shadows underneath.
*   **Placement:** The image should be `background-size: cover` and `background-position: center 20%`. It should fade into the black background of the bottom section using a long linear gradient mask (`rgba(0,0,0,0)` to `rgba(0,0,0,1)`).

---

## 4. Header Section
*   **Logo (Top Left):** 
    *   A minimalist white circular icon. It consists of a thin white ring. Inside, a horizontal line sits slightly above the center, with a small downward curve attached to it, resembling a stylized "S" or an eye.
*   **Slogan (Top Right):** 
    *   Text: "WORK FAST. LIVE SLOW."
    *   "WORK FAST." is colored in a peach-to-white gradient or solid peach (`#FFB88C`).
    *   "LIVE SLOW." is solid white.
    *   Font weight: Bold. All caps.

---

## 5. Navigation & Links Section
This section sits in the middle-bottom area, overlaid on the dark transition of the background.

*   **Structure:** Three columns with a horizontal divider line above the links.
*   **Column 1 (MENU):**
    *   Header: "MENU" (Uppercase, Bold, White).
    *   Links: Home, Services, Work, About, Contact (Stacked vertically, White, regular weight).
*   **Column 2 (SOCIALS):**
    *   Header: "SOCIALS" (Uppercase, Bold, White).
    *   Links: X, Instagram, LinkedIn.
*   **Column 3 (RESOURCES):**
    *   Header: "RESOURCES" (Uppercase, Bold, White).
    *   Links: Weekstack App, Newsletter.
*   **Action Button (Right Side):**
    *   A pill-shaped button (rounded-corners).
    *   Border: 1px solid White.
    *   Background: Transparent.
    *   Text: "Send a message" (White, centered).

---

## 6. The "SUKOYA" Hero Text
This is the most prominent visual element at the bottom.

*   **Placement:** Positioned at the absolute bottom of the viewport. The bottom of the letters is slightly "cut off" by the edge of the screen.
*   **Size:** Massive (approx 25-30vw).
*   **Styling:** 
    *   **Gradient Fill:** The text uses a vertical linear gradient. 
        *   Bottom: Solid White (`#FFFFFF`).
        *   Top: Fades into the background color (`#050505`), making the top of the letters appear to vanish into the darkness.
    *   **Letter Spacing:** Tight (approx `-0.05em`).

---

## 7. Layout Measurements (Proportional)
*   **Outer Padding:** 5% padding on left and right sides.
*   **Top Padding:** 40px.
*   **Navigation Gap:** The space between the Menu, Socials, and Resources columns is roughly 15% of the screen width.
*   **Divider Lines:** 1px thick, spanning the width of their respective columns, positioned roughly 10px below the column headers.
