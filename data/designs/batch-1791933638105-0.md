# Design Specification: "Que" Portfolio Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Color Palette
Modern, atmospheric editorial portfolio layout featuring a dark slate palette, high-contrast typography, and subtle ochre accents.

* **Primary Background:** Muted slate teal (`#424F52`) with subtle grain/noise.
* **Panels / Overlays:** Semi-transparent dark teal (`rgba(30, 42, 44, 0.45)`) with backdrop blur.
* **Text Primary:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Text Secondary:** Cool Muted Gray (`#9EADB0`).
* **Accent Color:** Ochre / Warm Gold (`#D6B265`).

---

## 2. Typography
* **Primary Display (Headings, Logo):** Geometric Sans-serif (e.g., *Gilroy Bold*, *Montserrat Bold*, or *Poppins*). 
  * *Fallback:* `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.
* **Body & Navigation:** Clean Modern Sans (e.g., *Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*).
  * *Fallback:* `Arial, sans-serif`.

---

## 3. Page Layout & Components

### A. Left Sidebar Rail
* **Width:** ~60px fixed width, full height (`100vh`).
* **Top:** Hamburger menu icon (three short horizontal white lines).
* **Bottom:** Vertical social links rotated 90° counter-clockwise:
  * Order (bottom to top): `dribbble`, `behance`, `twitter`.
  * Style: Small, uppercase/lowercase sans-serif, muted gray (`#9EADB0`).

### B. Header / Top Navigation
* **Placement:** Top horizontal bar, padded 40px left/right.
* **Logo (Left):** "Que" in bold white display type (~24px).
* **Links (Right):** Inline horizontal list: `about`, `works`, `services`, `contacts`.
  * Style: 13px, lowercase, muted gray, spaced evenly (~30px gap).

### C. Hero Section
* **Hero Portrait (Background/Focal Asset):**
  * **Subject:** Profile view of a man facing left with a focused expression, close-cropped buzz/fade hairstyle, wearing a white turtleneck and a dark tailored blazer.
  * **Integration:** Natural portrait blended directly into the dark slate background (seamless edge fading into `#424F52`). No hard image borders. Positioned center-right, head level with the upper third.
* **Content Left (Carousel Controls):**
  * Pagination: `01 / 06` (white active number, muted total).
  * Slider Line: Thin horizontal rule (active white bar left, muted line right).
  * Controls: `< | >` arrows aligned right of the indicator line.
  * Action: `view work` in ochre/gold (`#D6B265`) below the line.
* **Content Right (Hero Typography):**
  * Layered partially over the hero portrait.
  * Heading:
    ```text
    Travis
    Garlem
    ```
    *Style:* Large bold white sans-serif (~72px), tight line-height.
  * Description:
    *Text:* *"Perceived end knowledge certainly day sweetness why cordially Ask quick six seven offer see among. Handsome met debating sir dwelling age material."*
    *Style:* Small (~12px), light gray, max width ~320px.

### D. Bottom "Blog" Drawer
* **Dimensions:** Spans full width from sidebar to right edge, height ~120px.
* **Background:** Frosted glass overlay (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)`).
* **Content Columns:**
  * **Column 1 (Meta & Post 1):**
    * Label: `BLOG` (uppercase, bold, tracking +2px).
    * Author & Time: `@QueAgency · 10 min`
    * Snippet: *"Yesterday you brought up the heat with over a thousand mentions of #FontSunday dedicated to fire typography ."* (`#FontSunday` highlighted in ochre).

* **Far Right Action Panel:**
