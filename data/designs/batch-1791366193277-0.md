# THE GREATEST COUNTDOWN - Design System & Specification

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas

*   **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 widescreen presentation format.
*   **Background:** 
    *   **Type:** Radial gradient with heavy fine-grain noise texture.
    *   **Color Palette:** Centered around `#141414` (deep charcoal) radiating outward to `#000000` (pure black) at the outer edges.
    *   **Texture:** Subtle, high-frequency monochrome film grain overlay applied globally across the entire canvas to break up solid banding and add a cinematic grit.

---

## 2. Typography Hierarchy

*   **Primary Font Family:** Clean, geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Montserrat, Helvetica Neue, or Roboto Mono* variant).

### Element A: Header Tagline
*   **Text:** `THE GREATEST } } } }` (Note: The closing chevrons are distinct triple-angle markers).
*   **Font Weight:** Light / Regular (300)
*   **Letter Spacing (Tracking):** Wide/Expanded (`0.25em` to `0.3em`)
*   **Color/Opacity:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) at roughly `85%` opacity.
*   **Position:** Top-right aligned, floating above the central composition.

### Element B: Core Numbering ("01")
*   **Text:** `01`
*   **Scale:** Massive, dominating the central and right portion of the canvas.
*   **Font Weight:** Ultra-Bold / Black (900)
*   **Styling:** 
    *   Text fill is a rich, textured gradient transitioning from light silver (`#D0D0D0`) at the top to a darker gunmetal grey (`#3A3A3A`) at the bottom.
    *   Heavy internal grain/stipple texture mapped to the glyph shapes.
    *   Semi-transparent cutout effect where overlapping elements intersect.

### Element C: Interactive/Bracketed Subtext
*   **Text:** `{ C O U N T D O W N }`
*   **Font Weight:** Regular (400)
*   **Letter Spacing:** Extremely wide character tracking to space out individual letters uniformly.
*   **Formatting:** Enclosed horizontally by curly brackets `{` and `}` on the far left and right.
*   **Color:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) at `90%` opacity.
*   **Position:** Centered horizontally across the middle axis, intersecting horizontally with the large background numeral.

---

## 3. Structural Graphics & Symbols

*   **Line Weight:** Ultra-thin, precise vector stroke weights (approx. `1px` to `1.5px`).
*   **Color:** Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with slight outer glow or sharp anti-aliasing.

### Key Graphical Markers:
1.  **Double Chevrons (`>>`):** Placed directionally above the left side of the main typography block and beside the top-right header text.
2.  **Audio/Signal Waves (`(( ))`):** Symmetrical curved bracket lines framing the central point between the letters 'D' and 'O' in the word `COUNTDOWN`.
3.  **Horizontal Division Bars:** Clean rule lines breaking up spatial areas on the middle-left axis.

---

## 4. Composition & Spatial Arrangement

*   **Visual Weight Balance:** Heavy asymmetric composition. The massive `01` anchors the right-center, while the left side balances it with the bracketed sequence `{ C O U N T D O W N }`.
*   **Layering / Z-Index Depth:**
    1.  *Background:* Radial dark gradient + noise (Layer 0)
    2.  *Secondary Large Numeral:* Faded dark grey `01` on the mid-left (Layer 1)
    3.  *Foreground Giant Numeral:* Textured bright `01` on the right (Layer 2)
    4.  *UI/Vector Accents:* Brackets, chevrons, and text labels (Layer 3)
