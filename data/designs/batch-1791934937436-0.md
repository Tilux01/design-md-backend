# Design System & Implementation Specification: Signalis Venture Capital Landing Page

This document provides the exact technical and visual design specification required to reproduce the full-screen monochromatic web interface with absolute fidelity. All measurements, typography hierarchies, visual layering, and geometry overlays are detailed below.

---

## 1. Global Canvas & Viewport Architecture

* **Dimensions:** Fullscreen viewport bleed (`100vw` × `100vh`), non-scrollable base canvas (`overflow: hidden`).
* **Background Color Palette:**
  * Dark Base: Deep charcoal-black `#0A0B0D` to `#111215`.
  * Highlight Core: Brushed platinum-silver `#E5E7EB` to `#F3F4F6`.
  * Midtones / Shadow Gradients: Muted graphite `#2A2D34` and slate `#4B515D`.
* **Surface Lighting Effect (Specular Sheen):**
  * A broad, high-contrast, linear directional sheen sweeps from the top-right quadrant down towards the right-center horizon.
  * **Angle:** Approximately `130deg` to `135deg`.
  * **Gradient Stoppages:**
    * `0%` to `40%`: Solid deep obsidian `#0A0B0D`
    * `58%`: Transitioning charcoal `#282A30`
    * `78%`: Luminous brushed titanium highlight `#D4D7DC`
    * `92%`: Diffused platinum `#8A8E98`
    * `100%`: Tapering dark steel `#1C1D21`
* **Texture / Grain Overlay:**
  * High-frequency, monochromatic micro-film noise texture layered across the entire viewport.
  * **Opacity:** `3.5%` to `5%`.
  * **Blend Mode:** `overlay` or `soft-light`.

## 2. Typography System & Fallbacks

### 2.1 Primary Editorial Display (Hero & Arc Headlines)
Used for **"Capital in motion"** and **"Shaping what comes next"**.

* **Primary Typeface:** *PP Editorial New* (Italic) or *Canela Deck / Fine* (Light Italic).
* **Fallback Cascade:**
  1. *Playfair Display* (Italic, weight 400)
  2. *Cormorant Garamond* (Light Italic, weight 300/400)
  3. *Didot* / *Bodoni MT* (Italic)
  4. *serif*
* **Styling Characteristics:**
  * High-contrast transitional/modern serif with extended ascenders, graceful teardrop terminals, and tight tracking.
  * **"Capital in motion"**:
    * Font Size: `clamp(4.5rem, 8.5vw, 8.8rem)`
    * Line Height: `0.92` (extremely tight vertical stacking)
    * Letter Spacing: `-0.035em`
    * Weight: `300` / `400` (Light to Regular)
    * Text Alignment: Center
    * Layer Blending: Uses `mix-blend-mode: difference` or an internal linear gradient mask reflecting the background lighting (letters on the left are light silver `#EAEAEA`; letters crossing into the right light streak become deep graphite `#161719`).
  * **"Shaping what comes next"**:
    * Font Size: `clamp(2.5rem, 4.2vw, 4.5rem)`
    * Line Height: `1.0`
    * Letter Spacing: `-0.02em`
    * Alignment: Curved along SVG circle path.

### 2.2 Secondary Interface Sans-Serif (Technical UI & Navigation)
Used for all UI microcopy, navigational links, labels, and metadata.

* **Primary Typeface:** *PP Neue Montreal* or *Söhne* (Buch / Halbfett).
* **Fallback Cascade:**
  1. *Inter* (weight 400, `font-feature-settings: "cv02", "cv03", "cv04", "cv11"`)
  2. *Helvetica Neue*
  3. *Arial*
  4. *sans-serif*
* **Styling Characteristics:**
  * Clean, geometric neo-grotesque with neutral proportions and wide tracking when uppercase.

## 3. Geometric Wireframe & Blueprint Overlay Layer

Rendered as an absolute fullscreen SVG/Canvas element (`pointer-events: none`) positioned between the background gradient and the typographic elements.

* **Stroke Color:** Semi-transparent crisp white/silver `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`.
* **Stroke Width:** `0.75px` to `1px` crisp.
* **Stroke Dash Array:**
  * Dotted rings: `stroke-dasharray="1.5 4"`
  * Thin solid reference lines: `stroke-dasharray="none"`

### Geometric Components:
1. **Central Coordinate Circle:**
   * Centered behind the hero headline at `(x: 50vw, y: 38vh)`.
   * Radius: `~26vw` (diameter `~52vw`).
   * Subtle dashed stroke (`1.5 4`).
2. **Auxiliary Overlapping Rings (Venn/Orbital Construction Grid):**
   * **Left Circle:** Center `(x: 18vw, y: 38vh)`, radius matching central circle (`~26vw`), overlapping the left hemisphere.
   * **Right Circle:** Center `(x: 82vw, y: 38vh)`, radius matching central circle (`~26vw`), overlapping the right hemisphere.
3. **Axis Crosshairs:**
   * Hairline vertical line passing precisely through `50vw` from top to bottom edge (`stroke-width: 0.5px`, opacity `0.1`).
   * Hairline horizontal line intersecting at `y: 38vh`.
4. **Lower Arc & Orbital Text Path:**
   * Large dome arc emerging from bottom center.
   * Center point: `(x: 50vw, y: 125vh)`, Radius: `~55vw`.
   * Multiple concentric dashed guide curves at radii `54vw`, `55vw`, and `56.5vw`.
   * Micro-metadata text on path running along the outer concentric ring: `SIGNALIS CAPITAL • 2026 • INVESTING IN FOUNDERS • SCALED INFRASTRUCTURE...` in `7px` monospace uppercase.

## 4. Layout Grid & UI Element Manifest

The screen is structured using a multi-quadrant anchor system with a standard padding inset of `32px` (`2rem`) from all viewport borders.

```

| [NAV LIST + ARROWS]           [TOP-CENTER TITLE]               [LANGUAGES]       |
|                                                                                   |
|                                                                                   |
|                                ( Capital )                                        |
|                                (in motion)                                        |
|                                                                                   |

| [COPYRIGHT & YEAR]             (  what comes )                          [SUPPORT] |
```

### 4.1 Top Left: Primary Navigation
* **Placement:** `top: 32px`, `left: 32px`.
* **Layout:** Vertical stack of 4 items, flex column, gap `4px`.
* **Content:**
  * `HOME` `↗`
  * `ABOUT` `↗`
  * `PORTFOLIO` `↗`
  * `CONTACT` `↗`
* **Typography:** Uppercase, font-size `10px`, letter-spacing `0.08em`, weight `400`.
* **Arrow Glyph:** North-East Arrow (`↗` / `U+2197`), aligned to a tabbed column `24px` to the right of the longest label (`PORTFOLIO`).
* **Color:** Text: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`; Arrows: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`.

### 4.2 Top Center: Platform Description
* **Placement:** `top: 32px`, `left: 50%`, `transform: translateX(-50%)`.
* **Layout:** Two-line centered stack.
* **Content:**
  * Line 1: `VENTURE CAPITAL PLATFORM`
  * Line 2: `INVESTING IN THE NEXT GENERATION`
* **Typography:** Uppercase, font-size `9px`, letter-spacing `0.12em`, line-height `1.4`.
* **Color:** Muted graphite `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`.

### 4.3 Top Right: Language Selector
* **Placement:** `top: 32px`, `right: 32px`.
* **Layout:** Vertical stack, text-align right, gap `3px`.
* **Content:**
  * `ENGLISH` (Active / Bright: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`)
  * `UKRAINIAN` (Inactive: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`)
  * `GERMAN` (Inactive: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`)
* **Typography:** Uppercase, font-size `10px`, letter-spacing `0.08em`.

### 4.4 Center Canvas: Hero Headline
* **Placement:** Horizontally centered at `50%`, vertically positioned at `~35vh`.
* **Content:**
  * Line 1: `Capital`
  * Line 2: `in motion`
* **Visual Treatment:**
  * Line 1 has an initial capitalized `C`, remaining characters lowercase.
  * Line 2 is completely lowercase.
  * The text overlays the specular light streak: the left half displays bright silver-white letters against black, while the right half transitions into dark obsidian characters against the platinum light cone.

### 4.5 Center Lower: Call-to-Action & Thesis Statement
* **Placement:** Horizontally centered at `50%`, vertically positioned at `~66vh`.
* **Layout:** Centered flex column, gap `12px`.
* **Elements:**
  1. **Primary Link:**
     * Text: `Get in touch`
     * Font: Sans-serif, title case, font-size `15px`, weight `400`, letter-spacing `-0.01em`.
     * Underline: Solid `1px` underline with `4px` underline offset (`text-underline-offset: 4px; text-decoration-thickness: 1px`).
     * Color: `#FFFFFF`.
  2. **Thesis Microcopy:**
     * Line 1: `BACKING FOUNDERS FROM DAY ONE`
     * Line 2: `FOCUSED ON WHAT LASTS.`
     * Font: Sans-serif, uppercase, font-size `8.5px`, tracking `0.14em`, line-height `1.5`.
     * Color: Muted slate-gray `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`.

### 4.6 Bottom Arc: Secondary Section Hook
* **Placement:** Centered at `50%` horizontally, curving across the bottom boundary (`y: 84vh` to `94vh`).
* **Content:** `Shaping what comes next`
* **Rendering Method:** Rendered along an SVG `<path>` (`<textPath href="#arc">`) defining an upward circular curve:
  * Path `d="M [leftX] [bottomY] A [r] [r] 0 0 1 [rightX] [bottomY]"`
* **Typography:** Serif, Title Case ("Shaping what comes next" with "what comes next" remaining lowercase or mixed italic), font size `clamp(2.5rem, 4vw, 4.2rem)`.

### 4.7 Bottom Left: Brand Identification & Legal
* **Placement:** `bottom: 32px`, `left: 32px`.
* **Layout:** Two-line left-aligned stack, gap `2px`.
* **Content:**
  * Line 1: `SIGNALIS`
  * Line 2: `©2026 ALL RIGHTS RESERVED.`
* **Typography:** Uppercase, font-size `9px`, tracking `0.1em`, font-weight `500`.
* **Color:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`.

### 4.8 Bottom Right: Support Portal
* **Placement:** `bottom: 32px`, `right: 32px`.
* **Content:** `SUPPORT`
* **Typography:** Uppercase, font-size `9px`, tracking `0.1em`.
* **Color:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`.

- [x] Inverted contrast behavior: characters over the light sheen appear dark; characters over the dark ground appear light.
