# Design Specification: Fitness Dashboard UI

## 1. Global Styles & Environment
*   **Canvas:** Full-screen layout (100vw, 100vh).
*   **Background Color:** `#F4F7FE` (A very light, cool-toned grey-blue).
*   **Border Radius:** Standardized at `24px` for all major containers and `12px` for smaller elements.
*   **Shadows:** Soft, diffused shadows for cards: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05) 0px 10px 30px`.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Poppins` (Sans-serif).
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `Oxygen`, `Ubuntu`, `Cantarell`, `"Open Sans"`, `"Helvetica Neue"`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:**
    *   **Bold:** 700 (Headers, "Day 1", "Exercise 1/5").
    *   **Medium:** 500 (Sub-headers, stats labels).
    *   **Regular:** 400 (Descriptions, timestamps).
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Text:** `#1A1C1E` (Deep charcoal).
    *   **Secondary Text:** `#7C8087` (Muted grey).
    *   **Accent Orange:** `#FF6B35` (Active states, play button).

## 3. Layout Structure

### A. Left Navigation Sidebar (Narrow)
*   **Width:** 80px.
*   **Background:** White (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Border:** `1px solid #E8EBF0` (Right side).
*   **Icons:**
    *   Top: Lightning bolt logo (Black).
    *   Middle Group: 5 icons (Home, Exercise, Community, Stats, Settings).
    *   Bottom: User profile avatar (Male illustration, circular crop).
*   **Active State:** The second icon (Exercise) is encased in an orange circle (`#FF6B35`) with white icon color.

### B. Top Navigation (Horizontal)
*   **Items:** "Day 1", "Day 2", "Day 3", "Day 4".
*   **Active State:** "Day 1" is bold, black, with a small yellow baby emoji 👶 next to it.
*   **Inactive State:** "Day 2-4" are muted grey (`#B0B5BB`).

### C. Main Content Area (Center-Left)
#### 1. Video Player Card
*   **Background:** Light blue-grey gradient or solid `#D9E8F1`.
*   **Main Image:**
    *   **Subject:** A woman in white leggings and a grey sports bra performing a Russian twist.
    *   **Properties:** Backgroundless (PNG), crisp edges.
    *   **Placement:** Centered, slightly towards the bottom.
    *   **Object:** She is holding a teal/cyan medicine ball.
*   **Overlays:**
    *   **Top Left:** "Exercise 1/5" (Bold) and "Abdominal muscles" (Grey).
    *   **Top Right:** Red heart icon button (Small, rounded square).
    *   **Right Side:** Vertical volume slider (White semi-transparent track, black handle, speaker icon at bottom).
    *   **Bottom Left:** Large circular "Pause" button. White outer ring, orange progress arc (approx. 40% complete), white center with two black vertical bars.

#### 2. Stats Bar (Below Video)
*   Four distinct modules:
    1.  **Stretching:** Yellow icon, "Stretching" (Bold), "EXERCISE" (Small caps).
    2.  **Difficulty:** Purple icon, "2" (Bold), "DIFFICULT" (Small caps).
    3.  **Total Time:** Green icon, "45sec" (Bold), "TOTAL TIME" (Small caps).
    4.  **Real Time:** Pink icon, "30sec" (Bold), "REAL TIME" (Small caps).

#### 3. Description Card
*   **Background:** White.
*   **Content:**
    *   "DESCRIPTION" header (Small caps, grey).
    *   Numbered list: "1. Start point:" followed by instructional text.
    *   "Actions" sub-header followed by instructional text.

### D. Right Sidebar (Widgets)
#### 1. Exercises List
*   **Title:** "EXERCISES" (Small caps, grey).
*   **Items:** 4 list items.
    *   Each item contains: Small thumbnail image (backgroundless person on colored background), Title, Time, Calories, and Status Icon.
    *   **Status Icons:** 
        *   Item 1: Orange loading ring.
        *   Item 2 & 3: Green checkmark circle.
        *   Item 4: Red "X" circle.

#### 2. Level Card (Bottom Right)
*   **Background:** Very light lavender (`#F2EEFF`).
*   **Content:** Yellow baby emoji, "BEGINNER" (Bold), "YOUR LEVEL" (Small caps), and a kettlebell icon with the number "1021".

#### 3. Friends Section
*   **Title:** "MY FRIENDS" (Small caps, grey).
*   **Content:** 5 circular avatars overlapping slightly.
*   **Action:** A "+10" bubble and an orange circular button with a white "add user" icon.

## 4. Specific Asset Descriptions

*   **Main Exercise Image:** A high-resolution PNG of a female athlete. She is sitting on the floor, legs bent and elevated, torso rotated to her left. She holds a teal medicine ball with both hands. Her hair is in a braid. She wears white athletic leggings and a grey top. No background.
*   **Exercise Thumbnails:**
    1.  **Abdominal:** Woman on white floor with teal ball (Light blue background).
    2.  **Jumping on ball:** Woman sitting on a large purple exercise ball (Light purple background).
    3.  **With dumbbells:** Woman standing, holding red dumbbells (Light blue background).
    4.  **Jumping:** Woman in mid-air jump (Light pink background).
*   **Icons:** All icons are "Line Art" style with a stroke weight of approx 2px.

## 5. Spacing & Alignment
*   **Outer Padding:** 40px from the edges of the screen.
*   **Gutter:** 24px between the main content area and the right sidebar.
*   **Vertical Rhythm:** 20px spacing between cards in the right sidebar.
