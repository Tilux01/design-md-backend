# Design System & Specification: Triplio Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Layout Strategy
A modern, immersive travel landing page featuring a full-viewport hero section with atmospheric mountain photography and high-contrast, rounded UI components over a clean white content section below.

---

## 2. Color Palette

*   **Primary Deep Navy:** `#0A1931` (Hero lower gradient, primary buttons, main text)
*   **Accent Sky Blue:** `#3B82F6` / `#60A5FA` (Active links, subtle glow effects)
*   **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF` (Primary text in hero, card backgrounds, content section)
*   **Muted Light Gray:** `#F3F4F6` (Input backgrounds, secondary pills)
*   **Border Gray:** `#E5E7EB` (Subtle card borders and dividers)
*   **Subtle Text Gray:** `#6B7280` (Secondary labels, metadata)

---

## 3. Typography & Fallbacks

*   **Primary Font Family:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, fallback to `Inter`, `system-ui`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Massive Hero Watermark ("TRIPLIO"):**
    *   Weight: 900 (Extra Bold/Black)
    *   Size: ~`12vw` - `14vw`
    *   Color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a subtle masked blend layer behind foreground peaks.
*   **Hero Heading:**
    *   Weight: 700 (Bold), Size: `2.75rem` (`44px`), Color: `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Section Heading:**
    *   Weight: 700 (Bold), Size: `2.25rem` (`36px`), Color: `#0A1931`.
*   **Body & Navigation:**
    *   Weight: 500 (Medium), Size: `0.9rem` - `1rem` (`14px - 16px`).

---

## 4. Image Specifications

### Hero Background Image
*   **Description:** Full-bleed panoramic shot of sharp, snow-dusted alpine mountain peaks beneath a gradient sky transitioning from deep cobalt blue at the top to a rich dark navy at the lower base.
*   **Treatment:** Full-bleed coverage (`object-fit: cover`), zero angle rotation, vertical linear overlay (`to bottom, rgba(10,25,49,0) 40%, rgba(10,25,49,0.95) 100%`). Not transparent.

### Destination Card Images
1.  **Namib Desert (Left):**
    *   *Description:* Golden yellow desert dunes in the foreground leading up to rugged rock spires under a vivid blue sky.
    *   *Treatment:* Aspect ratio ~`4:5`, rounded corners (`16px`), slight dark gradient overlay at the bottom for text legibility.
2.  **Tropical Atoll (Center):**
    *   *Description:* Top-down aerial view of a lush green islet surrounded by pure white sand and clear turquoise waters.
    *   *Treatment:* Aspect ratio ~`4:5`, rounded corners (`16px`), bottom gradient overlay.
3.  **Canyon Sunset (Right):**
    *   *Description:* Sweeping warm sunset light over undulating desert canyon ridges with small silhouette figures walking along the crest.
    *   *Treatment:* Aspect ratio ~`4:5`, rounded corners (`16px`), bottom gradient overlay.

---

## 5. Component Breakdown

### Header / Navigation Bar
*   **Position:** Absolute top, floating, full width with horizontal padding (`px-12`).
*   **Logo:** Left-aligned. Abstract geometric white icon + "Triplio" text (White, Bold, 20px).
*   **Navigation Links:** Centered, inline flex. Items: *Home, Destination, About, Community, FAQ, Blog*. White text, hover opacity transition.
*   **Auth Button:** Right-aligned pill shape (`border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.4)`), text "Sign Up / Log In" with adjacent user icon.

### Hero Section
*   **Title Placement:** Bottom-left quadrant: "Uncover the World's Natural Wonders."
*   **Search Floating Card:**
    *   Right-aligned inline bar with rounded pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`), white background (`#FFFFFF`), shadow elevation.
    *   Three input columns: *Where* (location icon), *Date* (calendar icon), *Guest* (person icon).
    *   Action: Dark navy pill button labeled "Search Now" nested on the right.
*   **Hero Footer Social Proof:**
    *   Overlapping circular traveler avatars (4 avatars, 32px diameter).
    *   Rating text: "4.9/5 Rating from 10k+ reviews" (White, regular).
    *   Divided by a fine horizontal rule (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).

### Destinations Section
*   **Background:** Clean `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Section Header:**
    *   Small uppercase pill badge: `DESTINATIONS`.
    *   Main Heading (Left): "Your Journey to the Perfect Destination Begins Here".
    *   Action Button (Right): Rounded pill "See All Destinations" paired with a dark circular arrow icon button.
*   **Grid Layout:**
    *   3-column CSS Grid / Flex row (`gap: 24px`).
    *   Cards feature an embedded top-right floating circular action button (white circle, dark bookmark/favorite icon).
    *   Bottom overlay content: Destination Name (Bold, 18px, White) and Region Subtitle (Regular, 14px, Light Gray).
