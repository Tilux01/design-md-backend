# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Dark Luxury & Bespoke Automotive Atelier.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High cinematic whitespace ratio with atmospheric darkness, focused entirely on high-impact typography and photorealistic luxury automotive imagery.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Deep obsidian glassmorphism, matte dark textures, subtle inner glows, and crisp hairline highlights (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase navigational tags, subtle brushed-metal surface highlights, editorial serif headings paired with clean sans-serif bodies.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `11px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.15em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-white inline-block"></span>`.
