# Design System & Implementation Specification: Digital Stream Showcase

## 1. Executive Summary & Canvas Setup
* **Target Viewport:** 100vw × 100vh (Full-screen canvas, edge-to-edge layout, strictly omitting any mockup device frames or ambient outer background blur).
* **Overall Theme:** Ultra-modern dark-meets-light digital gallery platform. Combines a pristine white left control canvas with a dark 3D virtual exhibition viewport on the right.
* **Visual Tone:** Minimalist, high-tech, spatial UI, glossy fluid glassmorphism, hyper-clean typography.

---

## 3. Color Palette & Gradients

### Base Surfaces
* **Left Screen Background:** Pure White `#FFFFFF`
* **Ambient Top-Left Accent Blur:** Soft radial gradient (`rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.25)` to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`) blurred heavily (`filter: blur(80px)`).
* **3D Exhibition Room Walls:** Deep Dark Charcoal `#121316` / Matte Obsidian `#1A1C20`
* **Exhibition Room Floor:** Dark glossy wood/epoxy with subtle ambient reflection `#16181D`.

### Accent & Dynamic Art Colors
* **Vibrant Pink:** `#FF2A7A`
* **Vibrant Yellow:** `#FFD600`
* **Cyan-Blue Dynamic Fluid:** `#00E5FF` blending to `#7C3AED`

---

## 4. UI Layout Architecture

The screen is split into two primary structural regions:
1. **Header Bar:** Full-width floating navigation across the top.
2. **Main Split Body:**
   * **Left Side (~40% Viewport Width):** 2D Grid Cards + Hero 3D Chair Showcase + Bottom Feature Metrics.
   * **Right Side (~60% Viewport Width):** Interactive 3D Virtual Gallery Room with spatial posters.

---

## 5. Component Detailed Specifications

### A. Top Navigation Bar
* **Position:** Fixed top, `height: 70px`, `padding: 0 40px`, Flexbox distribution (`justify-content: space-between`, `align-items: center`).
* **Left Segment:**
  * Logo Icon: Small square icon composed of two vertical purple/blue gradient pill bars.
  * Logo Text: "Digital Stream" in bold 18px text.
* **Center Segment:** Horizontal menu items (`Showcase`, `Products`, `Vision`, `Solutions`).
  * `Showcase` has a small dark indicator line (`height: 2px`, `width: 100%`) positioned 4px underneath it.
* **Right Segment:**
  * Single line-art user profile icon inside a thin border circle (28px diameter).

---

### B. Left Content Column

#### 1. Preview Card Grid (Top Left Area)
* Layout: 2×2 grid of rectangular media preview cards (`border-radius: 8px`).
* **Card 1 (Top Left):** Abstract geometric pattern with cyan/purple gradient mesh.
* **Card 2 (Top Right):** Label "3D" in bold grey text, accompanied by two lines of muted micro-text underneath.
* **Card 3 (Bottom Left):** Deep red/magenta abstract glassmorphic render.
* **Card 4 (Bottom Right):** Label "2D" in grey text, above a soft grey/white abstract cloud thumbnail.

#### 2. Hero Object: Modern Black Armchair
* **Type:** High-end 3D modern lounge chair model / PNG render.
* **Background:** **Fully Transparent (Backgroundless PNG)**.
* **Object Description:** Sleek, minimalist black leather/matte cushion lounge armchair with thin dark tubular metallic frame legs.
* **Orientation / Rotation:**
  * Positioned floating in the lower-left section.
  * **Angle:** Rotated to a 3/4 rear-profile perspective facing upward towards the upper right (`Y-axis rotation: ~135°`, `X-axis tilt: ~10°`).
* **Shadow:** Soft directional ground contact shadow casting towards the bottom-right floor area (`rgba(0,0,0,0.15)` blurred).

#### 3. Bottom Feature Footer Bar
Horizontal bar aligned beneath the armchair containing 4 feature nodes:

1. **Showcase Control:**
   * Circle button (`32px`, dark fill) with a white inline Play icon (`▶`), followed by text "Showcase".
2. **HDR 10+ Spec:**
   * Title: "HDR 10+"
   * Subtitle: "Dynamic Range Display"
3. **Visual Effects:**
   * Icon: Minimalist eye / aperture icon (`16px`).
   * Title: "Visual Motion Effect"
4. **Adaptive Color:**
   * Icon: Sliders / toggle icon (`16px`).
   * Title: "Adaptive Color Configuration"

---

### C. Right Content Column: 3D Virtual Exhibition Room

A spatial 3D architectural booth room rendered at an oblique perspective angle (~30° rotated isometric view).

#### Architectural Structure
* Dark interior room box (`#121316`) open to the front and top.
* Light Source: Soft directional spot lighting from above, creating ambient glows on inner walls and floor reflections.

#### Wall Art & Digital Displays Breakdown

1. **Center Wall - Fashion Portrait (Main Focus):**
   * **Placement:** Centered on the main back wall inside the room.
   * **Content:** High-fashion black and white / soft warm portrait of a woman looking straight ahead with pale clean lighting.
   * **Aspect Ratio:** Vertical 3:4 rectangle with bright white edge illumination.

2. **Top-Left Hung Screen:**
   * **Placement:** Suspended higher on the left interior wall.
   * **Content:** Abstract fluid sand dune texture in warm orange/terracotta tones.

3. **Bottom-Left Floating Panel:**
   * **Placement:** Floating lower down on the left outer edge.
   * **Content:** Vibrant hot-pink card (`#FF2A7A`) with a white stylized wireframe/logo graphics.

4. **Lower-Center Wave Panel:**
   * **Placement:** Vertical rectangular panel hanging near the floor.
   * **Content:** Iridescent pastel vertical wave/ribbon pattern (yellow, teal, purple gradient lines).

5. **Right Exterior Wall - Yellow Banner:**
   * **Placement:** Fixed vertically to the outer right wall surface.
   * **Content:** High-contrast bright yellow background (`#FFD600`) featuring a bold, heavy black letter **"X"** centered.

6. **Right Edge - Blue Display:**
   * **Placement:** Far right corner edge panel.
   * **Content:** Glowing neon blue vertical rectangle panel.

7. **Foreground Liquid Art Object:**
   * **Placement:** Resting on the floor of the room, extending forward into the bottom-right space.
   * **Content:** A glossy 3D fluid organic liquid shape with a bright purple-to-cyan gradient (`#7C3AED` to `#00E5FF`).

## 7. Responsive Behavior & Canvas Rules
* **Aspect Ratio:** Optimized for 16:9 and 16:10 desktop screen proportions.
* **Canvas Constraints:** No scrollbars (`overflow: hidden` on main body).
* **Scale Behavior:** On narrower viewports, the right 3D viewport scales proportionally using CSS `transform: scale()` or WebGL responsive camera adjustments, keeping the split view intact.
