# Design System & Specification: SOHO Product Hero

## 1. Overview & Layout Architecture
Full-screen, minimal e-commerce product landing page layout.

- **Screen Dimensions:** 100vw × 100vh, fixed viewport, no scroll.
- **Canvas Base Color:** Off-white/pale gray-lavender (`#EAE8EB`).
- **Grid / Layout Structure:** 
  - **Header:** Sticky top bar, height 80px, horizontal padding `60px`.
  - **Main Display:** 3-column asymmetric layout (Left: Product copy; Center: Model visual; Right: Variant selector card).
  - **Bottom Dock:** Row of floating cards pinned to the bottom viewport with `40px` bottom margin and `60px` horizontal padding.

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Background:** `#EAE8EB`
- **Surface / Cards:** `#FFFFFF` (opacity 85%–100%, soft blur `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`)
- **Primary Text:** `#1A1A1A`
- **Secondary / Muted Text:** `#7A787E`
- **Card Shadows:** `0 8px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`
- **Swatches:**
  - White: `#FFFFFF` (with `#D0CFD2` border)
  - Black: `#111111`
  - Yellow: `#ECA828`
  - Blue: `#1855DE`
  - Red: `#E42D2D`

---

## 3. Typography & Fallbacks
- **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, Arial, sans-serif.
- **Weights & Hierarchy:**
  - **Logo:** 700 (Bold), 14px, tracking `1px`.
  - **Hero Title:** 700 (Bold), 36px–40px, leading 1.2.
  - **Subheadings & Labels:** 600 (Semi-bold), 12px–14px.
  - **Body Copy:** 400 (Regular), 12px–13px, leading 1.6.
  - **Product Specs / Tags:** 500 (Medium), 11px–12px.
  - **CTA ("Shop"):** 700 (Bold), 18px.

---

## 4. Component Breakdown

### 4.1 Navigation Bar
- **Logo (Left):** Square box with line-art borders enclosing stacked text `"SO"` over `"HO"`.
- **Navigation Links (Center-Left):** Horizontal menu (`gap: 32px`): `"man"`, `"women"`, `"Children"`, `"Explore"`. Muted text styling, lowercase/mixed case.
- **Utility Icons (Right):** Volume/Speaker, Shopping Bag, Search. Thin stroke icons (`20px × 20px`), black.

### 4.2 Hero Section (Left Column)
- **Category:** `"Summer collection"` in 12px semi-bold muted text.
- **Title:** `"Striped shirt dress"` in 38px bold.
- **Description:** 3-line truncated Latin body text (`#7A787E`).
- **Interaction Row:** Share icon + Favorite/Heart icon with counter label `"143 K"`.

### 4.3 Center Model Visual
- Centered horizontally, anchored to the bottom. Overlaps header space slightly.

### 4.4 Right Product Configuration Card
- Floating white card (`width: 280px–300px`, `border-radius: 8px`, `padding: 24px`, shadow).
- **Color Selection:** Label `"Colour: White"` on the left; five `14px` circular color dots on the right (`gap: 8px`).
- **Divider:** Ultra-thin light gray line (`#E2E0E4`).
- **Model Metadata:** Vertical stack (`gap: 8px`, 12px font):
  - `"REF 7682/256"`
  - `"The model size: S"`
  - `"Height: 5.81 ft / 177 cm"`
  - `"Select your size:"`
- **Size Selector:** Segmented outline button group: `[ S | M | L | XS | XXL ]` (equal width cells, 11px uppercase).

### 4.5 Bottom Carousel & CTA Dock
Horizontal flex container, `gap: 20px`, aligned to bottom.
- **Nav Button:** Square white card with centered back arrow (`<`), `border-radius: 8px`, `width: 56px`, `height: 80px`.
- **Carousel Card 1:** White rounded card (`padding: 16px 20px`). Title: `"Praesent ut maximus"`; Subtext: 2 lines muted text.
- **Carousel Card 2:** White rounded card (`padding: 16px 20px`). Title: `"Bandeau bodysuit"`; Subtext: 2 lines muted text.
- **Shop CTA Card:** White rounded card matching carousel height, width ~260px. Centered `"Shop"` label in 18px bold black.

## 5. Imagery Specifications

### Central Model Photograph
- **Subject:** Young female fashion model standing upright, facing directly forward.
- **Hair/Features:** Brunette bob cut with full straight bangs.
- **Attire:** Oversized, long-sleeve, knee-length beige/tan utility button-up shirt dress over white trousers.
- **Accessories:** Small, crescent/pleated off-white crossbody pouch worn high across the chest with a thin strap.
- **Treatment:** Fully backgroundless (isolated PNG/transparent cutout).
- **Positioning:** Grounded at bottom center-right; extends vertically from the lower chest of the layout past the top nav level. No rotation.
