# Design Specification: Drexel E-commerce Furniture Catalog

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Colors
*   **Primary / Text:** `#222222` (Dark charcoal for body text and headers)
*   **Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white canvas)
*   **Off-White / Light Gray (Banners, Background fills):** `#F7F7F7`
*   **Borders & Dividers:** `#E5E5E5`
*   **Interactive / Accent (Active states, pagination, price range):** `#222222` (Monochromatic high-contrast UI)
*   **Tag Backgrounds:** 
    *   `HOT` Tag: `#222222` (Black) with white text
    *   `SALE` Tag: `#666666` or `#222222` with white text
*   **Ratings:** `#222222` (Filled star color)

### Typography
*   **Font Family:** Sans-serif (Clean, modern geometric sans like Montserrat, Open Sans, or Lato)
*   **Header / Logo:** Bold, uppercase, elegant serif or stylized clean sans-serif (`DREXEL` with a stylized bird/dragon monogram).
*   **Navigation / Menu Links:** 12-14px, uppercase or sentence case, medium weight.
*   **Product Titles ("WATER REPELLENT PARKA"):** 11-13px, uppercase, letter-spacing tracked out (`1px - 2px`).
*   **Prices:** 12-14px, bold for current price (`$229.9`), lighter/strikethrough (`$239.9`) for old prices.
*   **Sidebar Category Headings:** 11-12px, bold, uppercase, letter-spacing tracked out.

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Grid

*   **Max Width:** `1440px` (Full-width responsive container with generous padding).
*   **Layout Style:** Classic E-commerce Split View
    *   **Left Sidebar:** Filters, Categories, Manufacturers, Price Slider, Color Options, Subcategory, Size Options, Compare section (~`260px` width).
    *   **Main Content Area:** Promotional Banner + Sorting controls + 3x3 Product Grid (~`1100px` width).

---

## 3. Component-by-Component Specifications

### A. Top Navigation Bar
*   **Layout:** Two-tier or single-line comprehensive header.
    *   **Left:** Links (`HOME`, `FEATURE`, `BLOGS`, `ABOUT US`) with dropdown carets.
    *   **Center:** Logo (`DREXEL`) with icon.
    *   **Right:** Utility links (`LOGIN`, `♥ (0)`, `CART (0)`, Search icon `🔍`).
*   **Breadcrumbs:** `Home / Furniture` positioned directly below the top utility nav, small text (`11px`, gray).

### B. Left Sidebar (Filters)
*   **Categories:**
    *   `FURNITURE` (Dropdown/Expand indicator `-`)
        *   Chairs, Tables, Office, Storage, Stools & Benches
    *   `CHAIRS & SOFAS`
        *   Sofas, Lounge chairs & ottomans, Sofa armchairs, Sofa beds
    *   `LIGHTING` (`+`)
    *   `ALL PRODUCT` (`+`)
*   **Shop By Manufacturer:**
    *   Adidas (15), Nike (09), Converse (12), Chanel (16), Gucci (05)
*   **Price Filter:**
    *   Dual-handle range slider spanning min to max.
    *   Inputs: `$50` ↔ `$450` with a black **`Search`** button next to it.
*   **Color Options:**
    *   Black (15), White (09), Blue (12), Red (16), Screen (05)
*   **Subcategory:**
    *   Material Bag (15), Arreglos (09), Dresses (12), Headphone (16)
*   **Size Options:**
    *   L (15), M (09), S (12), XL (16)
*   **Compare:**
    *   "You have no items to compare." (Empty state text)

### C. Main Content Area

#### 1. Promotional Hero Banner
*   **Background:** `#F4F4F4` light gray container.
*   **Content:**
    *   Subtitle: `NEW STYLE 2017` (Small, uppercase, tracked)
    *   Main Title: `BLACK` (Large, bold, serif/sans-serif)
    *   Body Text: *It is a long established fact that a reader will be distracted by the readable content of a page when looking at its layout.* (Centered, max-width constrained).

#### 2. Sorting & View Controls Bar
*   **Left:** `Show Sidebar` toggle with View Mode icons (List view vs. Grid view `☷ ⊞`).
*   **Center:** Dropdown `Default sorting ⌵`.
*   **Right:** Result count text: `SHOWING 1-12 OF 30 RESULTS`.

#### 3. Product Grid (3 Columns)
*   **Card Structure:**
    *   **Image Container:** Square aspect ratio, clean white background, minimalist product photography (monochrome/black theme objects: pendant lamp, wire basket, dustpan, geometric wireframe, tumbler, wall clock, minimalist bowl, minimalist clock, modern wall clock).
    *   **Badges:** Top-right or top-left absolute tags (`HOT`, `SALE`).
    *   **Hover Actions (e.g., first item):** "ADD TO CART" button overlay or action bar.
    *   **Product Info:**
        *   Title: `WATER REPELLENT PARKA` (Uppercase, small, centered).
        *   Rating Stars: `★★★★★` (Filled black stars).
        *   Pricing: `$229.9` (and old price `$239.9` struck through where applicable).

#### 4. Pagination
*   Centered pagination links at the bottom of the grid:
    *   `[01]` (Active, styled with dark background/white text), `02`, `03`, `>`, `>` (Last page).
*   Repeated result count below: `SHOWING 1-12 OF 30 RESULTS`.

---

### D. Footer Section
*   **Four Column Layout:**
    1.  **ABOUT:** News & Stories, History, Our Studio, Showrooms, Stockists
    2.  **CUSTOMER SERVICES:** Contact Us, Trade Services, Login/Register, Delivery & Returns, FAQs
    3.  **FURNITURE:** Tables, Chairs, Storage
    4.  **ACCESSORIES:** Candles & Fragrance, Stationery, Kitchen & Dining, Textiles, Gift Sets
*   **Bottom Utility Bar:**
    *   Left: `ABOUT US` | `BLOG` | `FAQS` | `ORDER TRACKING` | `CONTACT`
    *   Right: `CONNECT WITH US:` + Social media icons (Twitter/X, Pinterest, Instagram, Facebook).
