# Comprehensive UI/UX Design Specification & Replication Blueprint

This specification provides a pixel-for-pixel blueprint to replicate the mobile application UI shown in the reference media. It covers both views displayed: the **Home/Product Catalog Screen** (Left) and the **Product Detail Screen** (Right).

---

## 1. Global Layout & Styling System

### 1.1 Color Palette
*   **App Background (`#F8F9FA`):** Clean, off-white background used for the app canvas.
*   **Surface Containers (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white used for cards, floating buttons, and content panels.
*   **Primary Text (`#111827` / Dark Charcoal):** Used for titles, primary headings, and high-emphasis labels.
*   **Secondary Text (`#6B7280` / Cool Gray):** Used for subtitles, descriptions, and metadata.
*   **Accent / Brand Color (`#1E293B` / Dark Slate):** Used for primary call-to-action (CTA) buttons and selected states.
*   **Image Placeholders/Backgrounds (`#E2E8F0` to `#CBD5E1`):** Neutral gray-blue tones used inside product cards and photo backdrops.

### 1.2 Typography & Font Stack
*   **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, sans-serif.
*   **Fallback Font:** `sans-serif` system default.
*   **Hierarchy:**
    *   **App Header / Logo Text:** `18px`, Bold (Weight: 700), Letter spacing: `-0.02em`.
    *   **Section Headers / Product Titles:** `14px` - `16px`, Semi-bold / Bold (Weight: 600/700).
    *   **Body Copy / Descriptions:** `12px` - `13px`, Regular (Weight: 400), Line height: `1.5`.
    *   **Captions / Prices:** `11px` - `12px`, Medium (Weight: 500).

---

## 2. Screen 1: Home / Product Catalog Screen (Left)

### 2.1 Navigation Bar (Header)
*   **Layout:** Flexbox, space-between alignment with horizontal padding (`16px`).
*   **Left Element:** Brand Name/Logo ("beauty...") rendered in bold lowercase/mixed case with stylized typography.
*   **Right Element:** Search/Filter or Profile icon (minimalist outline icon, `24x24px`).

### 2.2 Product Grid Layout
*   **Structure:** 2-column masonry or equal-width grid.
*   **Spacing:** `12px` column and row gap.
*   **Card Container:**
    *   Background: `#FFFFFF`
    *   Border Radius: `16px` to `24px` (Pill-shaped/Rounded container).
    *   Padding: `12px` inner spacing.

#### Card 1 (Top-Left: Facial Massager / Jade Roller)
*   **Image Style:** Inside a rounded vertical pill container with a neutral cool-gray background (`#E5E7EB`).
*   **Image Description:** A dark green / jade Gua Sha or facial massaging tool. Backgroundless object (isolated on a soft gray-green gradient backdrop). Positioned centrally with a slight vertical tilt.
*   **Typography:**
    *   Title: "Jade facial roller" (`12px`, Bold).
    *   Price: "$24" (`11px`, Regular/Muted).

#### Card 2 (Top-Right: Face Cream / Skincare Jar)
*   **Image Style:** Rounded vertical pill container with a warm pastel background (`#F3E8E8`).
*   **Image Description:** A round, metallic-rose gold jar of face cream or cosmetic product, viewed from a top-down/angled perspective. Backgroundless object.
*   **Typography:**
    *   Title: "Rose gold cream" (`12px`, Bold).
    *   Price: "$35" (`11px`, Regular/Muted).

#### Card 3 (Bottom-Left: Serum / Skincare Bottle)
*   **Image Style:** Rounded vertical pill container with a soft purple/lilac background (`#EDE9FE`).
*   **Image Description:** A dark purple, cylindrical dropper bottle containing facial serum. Backgroundless object, perfectly centered.
*   **Typography:**
    *   Title: "Hydrating serum" (`12px`, Bold).
    *   Price: "$42" (`11px`, Regular/Muted).

#### Card 4 (Bottom-Right: Facial Cleanser / Tool)
*   **Image Style:** Rounded vertical pill container with a cool slate-blue background (`#E2E8F0`).
*   **Image Description:** A sleek, dark gray cylindrical skincare device or cleanser tube with a nozzle applicator. Backgroundless object, oriented vertically.
*   **Typography:**
    *   Title: "Cleansing device" (`12px`, Bold).
    *   Price: "$50" (`11px`, Regular/Muted).

### 2.3 Bottom Navigation Bar
*   **Layout:** Fixed bottom bar with 4 minimalist outline icons (Home, Search, Wishlist, Profile).
*   **Active Indicator:** Subtle dot or bolded icon state on the active tab (Home).

---

## 3. Screen 2: Product Detail Screen (Right)

### 3.1 Top Media Showcase (Hero Section)
*   **Layout:** Occupies the upper 40% of the screen height.
*   **Background:** Neutral light gray gradient (`#F1F5F9`).
*   **Image Description:** A rectangular product box/package in dark slate blue (`#334155`), featuring embossed or printed minimalist branding/logo graphics on the front face. The box is shot from a top-down, slightly angled perspective with soft drop shadows creating realistic depth. Backgroundless product package rendered on a clean studio backdrop.

### 3.2 Content Panel (Bottom Sheet Style)
*   **Layout:** Overlaps the bottom of the hero image with a clean white card background (`#FFFFFF`) featuring rounded top corners (`Radius: 24px`).
*   **Padding:** `20px` horizontal and vertical.

#### Product Information
*   **Product Title / Heading:** Bold, dark charcoal (`16px`, Weight: 700). Example: *Advanced Skincare Treatment Box*.
*   **Description Text:** Multi-line paragraph in muted gray (`12px`, Line height: `1.6`). Explains product benefits, ingredients, and usage instructions in a clean, readable layout.
*   **Variant / Options Selectors:** Small circular color/edition swatches or sizing pills located beneath the description.

### 3.3 Sticky Bottom CTA Bar
*   **Container:** Floating white container pinned to the bottom of the screen.
*   **Primary Button:**
    *   Background: Dark Slate / Black (`#111827`).
    *   Border Radius: Full pill shape (`9999px`).
    *   Height: `50px`.
    *   Text: "Add to cart - $45" (Centered, White text, `14px`, Medium weight).
    *   Icon: Small shopping bag or arrow icon aligned to the right inside the button.

---

## 4. Implementation Checklist for Developers
1.  **Container Setup:** Ensure screens are simulated with mobile frame dimensions (e.g., iPhone 14/15 Pro proportions: `390px` width) with standard status bar padding at the top.
2.  **Asset Handling:** Replace placeholder images with high-resolution PNG/WebP cutouts (transparent backgrounds) for the jade roller, cream jar, serum bottle, and product box.
3.  **Border Radii:** Maintain consistent, highly rounded corners (`16px` to `24px`) across all cards and interactive elements to match the soft, modern aesthetic of the design.
