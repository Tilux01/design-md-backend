# Design Specification: Daredevil UI Clone

## 1. General Layout & Theme
- **Theme:** Dark Mode.
- **Background:** A full-screen hero image (described in Section 7) with a dark overlay.
- **Overlays:** 
    - A linear gradient from the left (`rgba(0,0,0,0.9)`) to the right (`rgba(0,0,0,0)`) covering the left 50% of the screen to ensure text legibility.
    - A subtle bottom-to-top dark gradient to blend the thumbnail gallery into the background.
- **Dimensions:** Full viewport width (`100vw`) and height (`100vh`). No margins or external frames.

## 2. Typography
- **Primary Font:** `Netflix Sans` (or fallback to `Inter` or `Montserrat`).
- **Secondary/Body Font:** `Helvetica Neue` or `Arial` (fallback to `sans-serif`).
- **Text Styles:**
    - **Main Title ("Daredevil"):** Font-weight: 800 (Extra Bold), Size: `64px`, Color: `#FFFFFF`, Letter-spacing: `-2px`.
    - **Navigation Links:** Font-weight: 500, Size: `14px`, Color: `#FFFFFF`. Active link ("TV shows") color: `#E50914` (Netflix Red).
    - **Metadata (Year/Seasons):** Font-weight: 400, Size: `14px`, Color: `#FFFFFF`.
    - **Description Text:** Font-weight: 300, Size: `16px`, Color: `#CCCCCC` (muted white), Line-height: `1.6`.
    - **Starring Label:** Font-weight: 400, Size: `14px`, Color: `#808080`.

## 3. Color Palette
- **Netflix Red:** `#E50914` (Used for the logo, active nav link, and primary button).
- **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF` (Primary text).
- **Muted Gray:** `#B3B3B3` (Secondary text and icons).
- **Deep Black/Background:** `#050505`.
- **Button Hover Red:** `#B20710`.

## 4. Navigation Bar (Top)
- **Position:** Fixed at top, `100%` width, padding `30px 60px`.
- **Left Section:**
    - **Logo:** The Netflix "N" symbol in Red.
    - **Menu:** "Movies", "TV shows" (Red text), "Categories" (with a small downward chevron).
- **Center Section:**
    - **Search Bar:** A transparent input field with a thin bottom border (`1px solid #333`). Includes a magnifying glass icon on the left and placeholder text "e.g. Stranger things".
- **Right Section:**
    - **User Profile:** Text "Lucas Green", followed by a circular profile image (white male, short hair), and a power/standby icon.

## 5. Hero Content (Left-Aligned)
- **Positioning:** `20%` from the top, `60px` from the left.
- **Breadcrumb:** "< back to all TV shows" in small, muted uppercase text.
- **Rating Badge:** "16+" inside a rounded rectangle with a thin white border.
- **Metadata Row:** "2016 | 2 seasons | 51 minutes" separated by thin vertical lines.
- **Action Buttons:**
    - **Primary:** "Go to season 1" - Solid Red background, rounded corners (pill shape), white text.
    - **Secondary:** "Watch trailer" - Transparent background, `2px` white border, pill shape. Includes a "Play" triangle icon inside a circle.

## 6. Thumbnail Gallery (Bottom)
- **Position:** Fixed at the bottom, `60px` from the left and right.
- **Structure:** A horizontal row of 4 rectangular images.
- **States:**
    - **Thumbnail 1:** Active/Selected. Features a magnifying glass icon in the center and a thick white progress bar at the very bottom of the image.
    - **Thumbnails 2-4:** Standard state, slightly dimmed.
- **Dimensions:** Aspect ratio `16:9`.

## 7. Image Descriptions

### A. Main Background Image
- **Subject:** A man (Daredevil) seen from the back, slightly angled to the right.
- **Appearance:** He is wearing a dark red, textured tactical superhero suit with a cowl (horns visible). The suit looks wet, as if from rain.
- **Setting:** He is standing on a rooftop overlooking a dark, moody city (Hell's Kitchen). To the left, a water tower structure is visible.
- **Lighting:** Dark purple and blue hues dominate. A faint red glow comes from the bottom right, suggesting city lights. The atmosphere is foggy/rainy.
- **Placement:** The character is positioned in the right two-thirds of the screen, looking away from the text.

### B. Thumbnail 1 (Leftmost)
- **Content:** A dark, close-up shot of Daredevil in a dimly lit hallway or room.
- **Overlay:** A white magnifying glass icon centered. A white progress bar at the bottom indicates the user is currently at this point in the video.

### C. Thumbnail 2
- **Content:** A medium shot of Daredevil in his red suit, standing in a room with heavy pink/purple neon lighting.

### D. Thumbnail 3
- **Content:** A scene inside a bar ("Josie's"). Two men (Foggy Nelson and Matt Murdock in civilian clothes) are sitting at a table talking. Warm, yellowish indoor lighting.

### E. Thumbnail 4 (Rightmost)
- **Content:** A close-up of two characters in a dark setting, likely Matt and Karen, with soft red ambient lighting hitting their faces.

## 8. Icons & Assets
- **Search Icon:** Simple vector magnifying glass, `#B3B3B3`.
- **Power Icon:** Standard UI standby symbol, `#B3B3B3`.
- **Play Icon:** Small white triangle inside the "Watch trailer" button.
- **Chevron:** Small downward-pointing arrow next to "Categories".
