# Design Specification: Lukas Rudrof Portfolio Hero

## 1. Color Palette

* **Background:** Deep Charcoal Gray (`#4E5052`)
* **Text Primary:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
* **Text Muted/Secondary:** Light Gray / 70% White (`#C2C4C6`)
* **Text Dimmed (Labels):** Medium Gray / 40% White (`#8C8F92`)
* **Accent / Button Background:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
* **Button Text:** Near Black (`#111111`)
* **Borders / Separators:** Subtle White Stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`)

---

## 2. Typography

* **Primary Font:** Inter, Neue Haas Grotesk, or Plus Jakarta Sans
* **Fallback Fonts:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`
* **Hierarchy:**
  * **Site Title / Logo:** 18px, Weight: 500, White.
  * **Nav Links:** 11px, Uppercase, Weight: 400, Letter-spacing: +0.05em, Dimmed White.
  * **Main Heading (H1):** 52px–56px, Line-height: 1.15, Weight: 500, White.
  * **Body Copy:** 15px, Line-height: 1.5, Weight: 400, Light Gray.
  * **Label ("FEATURED ON"):** 10px, Uppercase, Weight: 600, Letter-spacing: +0.08em, Dimmed Gray.
  * **Button Text:** 13px, Weight: 500, Near Black.

---

## 3. Layout & Structure

* **Viewport:** Fullscreen (`100vw`, `100vh`), `overflow: hidden`, fixed frame.
* **Grid:** 2-column hero layout with pinned header and floating bottom-right CTA.

### Header (Navbar)
* **Position:** Top, full width, padded (`24px 48px`).
* **Border:** 1px solid bottom border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
* **Flex Layout:** Space-between.
  * Left: "Lukas Rudrof".
  * Right: Nav items row with ~28px gap ("LEISTUNGEN", "PROJEKTE", "ABOUT", "BLOG", "KONTAKT").

### Left Column (Content)
* **Alignment:** Vertically centered, left-aligned, padded (`0 0 0 64px`), max width ~560px.
* **Vertical Spacing:**
  * **H1:** "Webdesign, Webflow & SEO aus Nürnberg." (Line break after "Webflow").
  * **Body Margin:** 24px below H1.
  * **Trust Section Margin:** 56px below body copy.
* **Trust Badges ("FEATURED ON"):**
  * Tiny label followed by a horizontal row of 4 monochrome white logos: **Malt**, **t3n**, **Landbook**, **Sortlist**. Height ~18px each, spaced 24px apart.

### Right Column (Imagery)
* **Image Description:** Cutout (transparent background PNG), high-contrast black-and-white photographic portrait of a young Caucasian man with dark short hair, light stubble/beard, wearing a dark button-up shirt.
* **Pose & Framing:** Profile/three-quarter view facing left and looking slightly upward.
* **Placement:** Anchored to the bottom and right edges (`bottom: 0`, `right: 5%`). Scaled to take up roughly 70–80% of the viewport height.

### Interactive Elements (CTA)
* **Pill Button ("Projekt anfragen →"):**
  * **Position:** Floating at the bottom right (`bottom: 32px`, `right: 48px`), overlapping the lower torso of the portrait.
  * **Dimensions & Style:** Fully rounded border-radius (`999px`), solid white fill, black text, padding (`12px 24px`), display flex with right-pointing arrow icon (`→`).
