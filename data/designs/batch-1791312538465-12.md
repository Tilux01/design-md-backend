# Design Specification: Coffee Flavours Web Application

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
- **Primary Mood:** Sophisticated, warm, organic, editorial, artisanal.
- **Brand Archetype:** Premium artisan cafe / specialty beverage brand.
- **Visual Density:** Airy, minimalist with generous whitespace balanced by textured, tactile photography.
- **Theme Balance:** Light-mode dominant design utilizing soft, warm neutral off-whites, organic earthy browns, and tactile coffee-bean/latte textures.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

### Primary & Accent Colors
- **Primary Brand / Accent (Gold/Tan):** `#C8A885`
- **Primary Accent Hover:** `#B59470`
- **Primary Accent Active:** `#A2815C`

### Backgrounds & Surfaces
- **Base Background (Page):** `#EFECE7` (Warm cream/oatmeal)
- **Left Panel Surface (Media Container):** `#E4DFD8`
- **Right Panel Surface (Content Container):** `#EBE7E1`
- **Button Surface:** `#C8A885`

### Text Colors
- **Primary Headings & Text:** `#1A1A1A` (Near Black)
- **Body & Secondary Copy:** `#63615E` (Warm Muted Charcoal)
- **Inverse Text (on accent buttons):** `#1A1A1A` or `#FFFFFF`

### Borders & Dividers
- **Subtle Dividers / Indicators:** `#C8C2B8`
- **Active Pagination Dots:** `#1A1A1A`
- **Inactive Pagination Dots:** `#D4CFC7`

---

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy

### Font Families
- **Headings:** Editorial Serif (e.g., *Playfair Display*, *Bodoni Moda*, or *Cinzel*)
- **Body / UI:** Geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Montserrat*, or *Helvetica Neue*)

### Scale Breakdown
| Level | Font Size | Line Height | Letter Spacing (Tracking) | Font Weight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brand Wordmark** | 13px | 1.0 | +0.05em | 600 (Bold) |
| **H1 (Coffee Flavor Name)** | 48px | 1.1 | -0.02em | 500 (Medium Serif) |
| **Pagination Indicator** | 11px | 1.0 | +0.1em | 400 (Regular) |
| **Body Copy** | 12px | 1.6 | normal | 400 (Regular) |
| **Button Label** | 11px | 1.0 | +0.15em | 600 (Semi-Bold) |
| **Caption / Copyright** | 10px | 1.0 | +0.05em | 400 (Regular) |

---

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid

### Container Layout
- **Split-Screen Asymmetric Layout:** 50/50 horizontal division.
  - **Left Half:** Visual/Media viewport (Product photography, ambient elements).
  - **Right Half:** Editorial content & interactive typography.
- **Padding & Margins:**
  - Outer viewport framing with generous outer margins.
  - Internal padding scale: `24px`, `32px`, `48px`.

### Section Sequence Breakdown
1. **Header Navigation:**
   - Top-left: Brand logo + wordmark ("coffee flavours").
   - Top-right: Social links (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube).
2. **Main Split Hero Area:**
   - **Left:** Top-down photographic composition featuring a latte cup, spoon, scattered coffee beans, and dried botanical elements. Vertical copyright badge on the far left edge.
   - **Right:** Numerical carousel indicator (`01 / 05`), dynamic H1 title ("Latte"), explanatory descriptive text block, primary Call-to-Action button ("ORDER NOW").
3. **Interactive Carousel Controls:**
   - Vertical step-indicator dots positioned centrally along the split boundary.
   - Vertical arrow stepper (`↑` / `↓`) anchored to the bottom-right corner.

---

## 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement

### Cards & Containers
- **Border Radius:** `0px` (Sharp, editorial print-like aesthetic).
- **Shadows:** Minimal soft drop shadows on floating elements (e.g., spoon casting subtle shadow onto surface).

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary CTA ("ORDER NOW"):**
  - Shape: Rectangular, sharp edges (`border-radius: 0px`).
  - Background: `#C8A885`.
  - Padding: `12px 24px`.
  - Typography: Uppercase, tracking wide (`+0.15em`), 11px.
  - Hover State: Background shifts to `#B59470`.

### Navigation & Pagination Elements
- **Vertical Step Indicator:**
  - 5 circular dots aligned vertically on the center split line.
  - Active dot: Solid dark fill (`#1A1A1A`) with a slightly expanded scale or ring outline (`#C8A885`).
  - Inactive dots: Muted neutral circles (`#D4CFC7`).
- **Social Icons:** Minimalist vector glyphs (F, IG, YT), 14px size, dark gray (`#1A1A1A`).

---

## 6. Motion Mechanics & Interactions

### Animation Triggers
- **Slide Transitions:** Triggered via vertical pagination clicks or scroll wheel action.
- **Slide Transition Physics:** Smooth horizontal translation (`transform: translateX()`) paired with cross-fade opacity transitions for typography layers.
- **Estimated Duration:** `600ms` to `800ms`.
- **Easing Function:** `cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)` (Smooth artisanal ease-out).
