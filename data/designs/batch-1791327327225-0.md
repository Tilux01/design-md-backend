# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Nocturnal Zen Garden, Dark Mode Atmospheric Landscape, Cinematic Minimalist UI.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High negative space ratio with deep immersive black canvas (`#000000`), contrasted against warm bioluminescent golden accents and rich moss green terrain.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered topographical depth using natural stone textures, organic moss elevations, and emissive lantern glow-spread drop-shadows with soft radial diffusion (`rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.25)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient warm particle simulation (fireflies), volumetric lantern glow effects, hyper-realistic moss micro-textures, cinematic vignette framing.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `4px 12px`, font-size: `0.75rem`, font-weight: `600`, tracking: `0.05em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.1)`, border: `1px solid rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-amber-400 inline-block animate-pulse"></span>`.
