# HUGINN Portfolio Website Design Specification

## 1. Global Styles & Layout
* **Layout Structure**: Full-screen immersive hero section with a horizontal accordion/slider layout displaying 4 distinct category panels simultaneously.
* **Aspect Ratio / Viewport**: 100% width, 100% height (Full-screen viewport layout, omitting presentation mockup frames).
* **Base Theme**: Dark mode aesthetic (Dark Charcoal/Off-black background tones).
* **Typography**:
  * **Primary Font Family**: Sans-serif, geometric, highly legible (e.g., Inter, Montserrat, or Helvetica Neue).
  * **Brand Logo Font**: Bold, uppercase sans-serif.
  * **Navigation Links**: Uppercase, lightweight sans-serif.
  * **Card Index Numbers**: Bold monospace or sans-serif (e.g., `01.`, `02.`, etc.).
  * **Card Titles**: Medium weight, capitalized.
  * **Card Categories (Labels)**: Uppercase, small tracking/letter-spacing, lower contrast (muted white/grey).

---

## 2. Header / Navigation Bar
* **Position**: Fixed/Absolute top overlay spanning the full width of the screen.
* **Left Section**:
  * **Logo**: 
    * Icon: Stylized bird/animal vector head silhouette in solid white.
    * Text: "HUGINN" in bold, uppercase tracking.
* **Center/Right Navigation Links**:
  * Items: `HOME`, `ABOUT`, `WORK`, `CONTACT`
  * Typography: Small, uppercase, white/light grey text with subtle hover states.
* **Utility Icons**:
  * Hamburger menu icon (three horizontal lines) positioned near the logo area.
  * Search icon (magnifying glass) positioned in the upper right corner.

---

## 3. Main Content: 4-Panel Grid / Accordion Slider
The main viewport is divided into 4 vertical image-based cards side-by-side. Each panel features a high-resolution background image with a dark gradient overlay for text legibility.

### Panel 01: "Beautiful Venice" (Active/Featured State)
* **Background Image**: Venice canal scene featuring gondolas, historic architecture, and water ripples.
* **Overlay Tint**: Subtle vertical dark gradient (darker at the bottom).
* **Content Overlay (Bottom-Left Alignment)**:
  * Index: `01.`
  * Title: **Beautiful Venice**
  * Sub-category: `TRAVEL`

### Panel 02: "Lovely Room"
* **Background Image**: Indoor interior shot of a room with a window, wooden bench, and circular coffee table lit by natural sunlight.
* **Content Overlay (Bottom-Left Alignment)**:
  * Index: `02.`
  * Title: **Lovely Room**
  * Sub-category: `INTERIOR`

### Panel 03: "Amazing Mountain"
* **Background Image**: Moody, overcast mountain landscape with layered hills and misty water/sea below.
* **Content Overlay (Bottom-Left Alignment)**:
  * Index: `03.`
  * Title: **Amazing Mountain**
  * Sub-category: `LANDSCAPES`

### Panel 04: "Street Portrait"
* **Background Image**: Portrait of a stylish man wearing a fedora hat, glasses, bow tie, and pocket watch necklace, standing against a dark slatted background.
* **Content Overlay (Bottom-Left Alignment)**:
  * Index: `04.`
  * Title: **Street Portrait**
  * Sub-category: `PORTRAITS`

---

## 4. Footer & Navigation Controls
* **Position**: Fixed at the bottom edge of the screen.
* **Left Section**:
  * Copyright text: `© All Rights Reserved 2017` (or current year).
* **Center Section**:
  * Interactive Pagination / Slider Progress Bar: A dark track line with an active glowing purple indicator segment indicating current slide position.
* **Right/Edge Controls**:
  * Carousel directional arrows (`PREV` / `NEXT` with subtle caret indicators) positioned at the outer edges of the slider view.
