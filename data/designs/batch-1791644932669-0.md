# Design Specification: Aer "Work Anywhere" Minimalist Editorial Landing Page

---

## 1. Overview & Design Concept

This document specifies the layout, visual hierarchy, typography, colors, and asset requirements to replicate the high-end, dark-mode, editorial e-commerce landing page for the brand **Aer**. 

The design combines minimalist utilitarian streetwear aesthetics with modern digital editorial layouts. The screen is rendered as a full-viewport application (`100vw` × `100vh`) with fixed vertical navigation, a prominent hero section featuring centered cinematic lifestyle photography, and a modular three-column content shelf anchored along the bottom.

## 5. Component Breakdown

### 5.1 Left Vertical Rail (Sidebar)
* **Width:** `72px` fixed width; full height (`100vh`).
* **Background:** `color-bg-sidebar` (`#1c1d20`).
* **Border:** Right border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
* **Top Element (Menu Trigger):**
  * Position: `top: 36px`, centered horizontally.
  * Icon: 3 horizontal bars:
    * Bar 1: `18px` wide, `2px` high.
    * Bar 2: `14px` wide, `2px` high (aligned left).
    * Bar 3: `10px` wide, `2px` high (aligned left).
    * Vertical gap: `4px`.
    * Color: `#ffffff`.
* **Bottom Element (Social Links):**
  * Position: `bottom: 40px`, centered horizontally.
  * Flex column running vertically upwards or downwards: `Tw`, `In`, `Fb`.
  * Text rotation: `-90deg` or stacked compact monogram text.
  * Gap between elements: `24px`.
  * Color: `color-text-muted` (`#5c5d62`), transitioning to white on hover.

---

### 5.2 Top Navigation Header
* **Height:** `80px`.
* **Position:** Absolute/fixed, top `0`, left `72px`, right `0`.
* **Padding:** `0 64px`.
* **Layout:** Flex row, `justify-content: space-between`, `align-items: center`.
* **Left:**
  * Wordmark: **"Aer"**
  * Weight: `800`, font size: `26px`, color `#ffffff`.
* **Right (Nav Links):**
  * Flex row with horizontal gap of `36px`.
  * Links: `Home`, `Products`, `About`, `Locations`, `Cart`.
  * Search Icon: Minimal outline magnifying glass (`16px × 16px`, stroke `1.5px`).
  * **Active Item Indicator ("Home"):**
    * Beneath the word "Home": a horizontal line `18px` long, `1.5px` thick, placed `6px` below text.
    * Line color: `color-accent-gold` (`#c6a87d`).

---

### 5.3 Hero Stage

#### A. Left Index & Collection Switcher
* **Position:** Absolute, top `38%`, left `136px`.
* **Index Element:**
  * Displays: `/ 01`
  * Right of `/ 01`: A subtle horizontal accent line `50px` long, `1px` thick, color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`.
* **Sub-Menu (Collections):**
  * Margin-top: `20px`.
  * Items:
    1. **Work Collection** (Active: `#ffffff`, bold/semibold, with an accent underline/highlight).
    2. **Active Collection** (Inactive: `#5c5d62`, regular).
    3. **Travel Collection** (Inactive: `#5c5d62`, regular).
  * Line spacing: `12px` between rows.

#### B. Main Hero Graphic (Centerpiece Asset)
* **Visual Subject:** A male figure seen directly from behind, wearing a black matte hooded jacket with the hood up, carrying a technical black commuter backpack.
* **Framing & Placement:**
  * Centered along the horizontal axis, slightly offset to the left of the headline text.
  * Base of the torso overlaps slightly behind or flush with the top edge of the bottom shelf.
  * Lighting: Moody, cinematic high-contrast directional edge light highlighting the technical fabric texture of the hood and the ballistic nylon contours of the backpack.
  * Image Type: Cutout/subject blended seamlessly into `#161719` background at the bottom and sides (soft dark vignette around edges).

#### C. Headline & Description Copy (Center-Right)
* **Position:** Located to the right of the hooded figure, vertically centered (`top: 38%`).
* **Headline:**
  ```text
  Work
  Anywhere
  ```
  * Style: 2 lines, `58px`, Bold (`700`), crisp white (`#ffffff`).
* **Paragraph Description:**
  * Margin-top: `28px`.
  * Max-width: `320px`.
  * Text:
    > "Introducing the Work Collection, a line of minimalist bags designed for a new generation specifically for the modern professional"
  * Style: `13px`, Regular (`400`), Line-height: `1.6`, color: `#9c9da1`.
  * Decorative detail: Subtle thin underline beneath selected words (`new generation specifically for the modern professional`) to reflect the editorial print aesthetic.

---

### 5.4 Bottom Content Shelf

Anchored across the bottom of the main display area (from `left: 72px` to `right: 0`, `bottom: 0`).
* **Height:** `180px`.
* **Background:** Multi-column layout divided by subtle border dividers (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────┬───────────────────────────┐
│ EVENTS                               │ IMAGE          │ NEWS                      │
│ Pop-up @ Fillmore  Pop-up @ Brooklyn │ THUMBNAIL      │ A fresh take on our...    │
│ SF - Sat, Aug 12   NYC - Sat, Aug 12 │ [Street View]  │ Read More                 │
│ • · ·                                │                │                           │
└──────────────────────────────────────┴────────────────┴───────────────────────────┘
```

#### Column 1: Events Module
* **Width:** `50%` of remaining viewport.
* **Padding:** `28px 40px`.
* **Header:** `EVENTS` (Uppercase, `11px`, letter-spacing `0.2em`, color `#ffffff`, margin-bottom `20px`).
* **Content:** 2-column inline sub-grid:
  * **Event 1:**
    * Venue: `Pop-up @ Steven Alan Fillmore` (`12px`, `#9c9da1`)
    * Date: `SF — Sat, August 12 / 5 - 8PM` (`12px`, Bold `#ffffff`, margin-top `4px`)
  * **Event 2:**
    * Venue: `Pop-up @ Steven Alan Brooklyn` (`12px`, `#9c9da1`)
    * Date: `NYC — Sat, August 12 / 5 - 8PM` (`12px`, Bold `#ffffff`, margin-top `4px`)
* **Pagination Dots:**
  * Positioned below Event 1.
  * Three horizontal dots (`5px` diameter each, `6px` spacing).
  * Dot 1: Active (`#c6a87d` gold or `#ffffff`).
  * Dots 2 & 3: Inactive (`#5c5d62`).

#### Column 2: Editorial Image Thumbnail
* **Width:** `140px` fixed width.
* **Height:** Full height of the bottom shelf (`180px`).
* **Asset Description:**
  * Full-bleed lifestyle urban photography.
  * Subject: A man in profile walking from left to right along a pale concrete urban wall, wearing a tan/khaki safari jacket and dark pants, carrying the black backpack.
  * Aspect Ratio: Tall vertical rectangle (~`3:4`).
  * Treatment: Natural daylight, crisp shadows, sharp contrast.

#### Column 3: News Module
* **Width:** Remaining flex space (`~35%`).
* **Padding:** `28px 40px`.
* **Header:** `NEWS` (Uppercase, `11px`, letter-spacing `0.2em`, color `#ffffff`, margin-bottom `16px`).
* **Copy:**
  > "A fresh take on our Active Collection, just in time for spring. Available in black."
  * Style: `12px`, Regular, line-height `1.5`, color `#9c9da1`.
* **Link:**
  * Label: `Read More`
  * Margin-top: `14px`.
  * Style: `12px`, Bold `#ffffff`, subtle underline on hover.

## 6. Micro-Interactions & States

1. **Top Nav Items:**
   * Default: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`.
   * Hover: `#ffffff` with a smooth 150ms opacity transition.
