# Design Specification: Joker Movie Landing Page

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Layout Type:** Full-screen immersive landing page (100vw, 100vh).
*   **Theme:** Gritty, dark, cinematic, and high-contrast.
*   **Background:** The background is composed of two primary character portraits meeting in the center, creating a split-screen effect.

## 2. Color Palette
*   **Primary Black:** `#050505` (Deep black for shadows and UI backgrounds).
*   **Primary White:** `#FFFFFF` (For main titles and navigation).
*   **Accent Red:** `#8B0000` (Deep blood red, used in the Joker's makeup and branding).
*   **Muted Grey:** `#A0A0A0` (For secondary text and UI borders).
*   **Star Gold/White:** `#FFFFFF` (Stars are pure white in this design).

## 3. Typography
### Main Title ("JOKER")
*   **Font:** A heavy, condensed, distressed Sans-Serif.
*   **Closest Match:** *Gothic 821 Condensed* or *Impact* (with a custom "grunge/distressed" texture overlay).
*   **Fallback:** `Arial Narrow, sans-serif`.
*   **Styling:** Uppercase, extra-bold, white color, slight letter spacing.

### Secondary Headers ("JOAQUIN PHOENIX", "PUT ON A HAPPY FACE")
*   **Font:** Clean, modern Geometric Sans-Serif.
*   **Closest Match:** *Montserrat (Medium)* or *Roboto*.
*   **Fallback:** `Helvetica, sans-serif`.
*   **Styling:** Uppercase, wide letter spacing (approx 4px), white color.

### Navigation & UI Text
*   **Font:** *Inter* or *System UI*.
*   **Styling:** Uppercase, very small font size (approx 10px - 12px), high letter spacing.

## 4. Imagery Details

### Background Image 1: The Joker (Left Side)
*   **Description:** A high-resolution, close-up portrait of Joaquin Phoenix in full Joker makeup. The face is painted white with blue diamond shapes over the eyes and a messy, wide red painted smile. His hair is dyed dark green and slicked back.
*   **Placement:** Aligned to the far left. The face is cropped so the right side of his face fades into the center black void.
*   **Background Status:** Backgroundless (cutout) or blended into a black gradient on the right edge.
*   **Rotation:** 0 degrees.

### Background Image 2: Arthur Fleck (Right Side)
*   **Description:** A high-resolution, close-up portrait of Joaquin Phoenix as Arthur Fleck (without makeup). He has a somber, intense expression, looking directly forward.
*   **Placement:** Aligned to the far right. The face is cropped so the left side of his face fades into the center black void.
*   **Background Status:** Backgroundless (cutout) or blended into a black gradient on the left edge.
*   **Rotation:** 0 degrees.

### Carousel Thumbnails (Bottom Center)
1.  **Image 01:** Arthur Fleck laughing in a dimly lit room.
2.  **Image 02 (Center/Active):** Arthur walking past a vintage green car on a city street. This image features a circular "Play" icon overlay in the center.
3.  **Image 03:** Arthur in his red suit, standing in a dark hallway.

## 5. UI Elements & Components

### Top Navigation Bar
*   **Structure:** Five elements spaced across the top.
    *   `ABOUT` (Left)
    *   `TRAILERS` (Left-Center)
    *   `DC LOGO` (Center - Circular white logo)
    *   `GALLERY` (Right-Center)
    *   `SHOP` (Right)
*   **Dividers:** Thin, vertical grey lines (1px) separate the sections.

### Central Title Block
*   **Top:** "JOAQUIN PHOENIX" (Small) | "OCT 4th" (Small, aligned right).
*   **Middle:** Large "JOKER" logo.
*   **Bottom:** "PUT ON A HAPPY FACE" (Spaced out).

### Bottom Carousel / Gallery
*   **Layout:** Three rectangular images with large, low-opacity grey numbers (`01`, `02`, `03`) behind them.
*   **Navigation:** A thin white left arrow (`←`) to the left of image 01 and a right arrow (`→`) to the right of image 03.
*   **Active State:** The center image (02) is slightly larger or highlighted with the play button.

### Review/Rating Blocks (Bottom Corners)
*   **Left Block:**
    *   Text: "FILM OF THE YEAR" (Quotes included).
    *   Stars: 5 solid white stars.
    *   Logo: Small Warner Bros (WB) shield logo in a circle.
*   **Right Block:**
    *   Text: "JOAQUIN PHOENIX IS ASTONISHING".
    *   Stars: 5 solid white stars.

## 6. Layout Measurements (Proportional)
*   **Header Height:** 10% of viewport height.
*   **Main Title Position:** Vertically centered, slightly above the midline.
*   **Carousel Position:** Anchored to the bottom, roughly 20% of the viewport height.
*   **Gutter/Margins:** 5% horizontal margin for the corner UI elements.

---
**Implementation Note:** To achieve the "distressed" look on the main Joker text, use a CSS `mask-image` with a noise texture or a high-quality SVG filter. The background images should use `background-size: cover` and `linear-gradient` overlays to ensure the text remains legible in the center.
