# Design Specification (`design.md`)

## 1. Overview & Color Palette
Minimalist, high-key e-commerce landing page with a stark, modern aesthetic.

* **Canvas Background:** `#F5F6F8` (Solid off-white/light gray)
* **Primary Text / Accents:** `#1A1A1A` (Near black)
* **Secondary Text:** `#73777D` (Neutral muted gray)
* **Active Indicator / Lines:** `#1A1A1A`
* **Button Background:** `#222222`
* **Button Text:** `#FFFFFF`

---

## 2. Typography
* **Primary Font:** `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, or `Arial`, sans-serif.
* **Hero Headline:** 
  * Font-weight: `800` (Extrabold)
  * Size: `3.5rem` (`56px`)
  * Line-height: `1.15`
  * Text: `"Beat & Sound"` / `"around the world"`
* **Hero Body Text:**
  * Font-weight: `400` (Regular)
  * Size: `0.875rem` (`14px`)
  * Line-height: `1.6`
  * Color: `#73777D`
* **Button Label:**
  * Font-weight: `700` (Bold)
  * Size: `0.8rem` (`13px`)
  * Letter-spacing: `1.5px` (All caps)
* **Footer / Metadata:**
  * Phone: `0.9rem` (`14px`), bold `#1A1A1A`
  * Address: `0.8rem` (`13px`), regular `#73777D`
* **Vertical Nav Links:**
  * Font-weight: `500` (Medium)
  * Size: `0.85rem` (`14px`)

---

## 3. Layout Structure

### Top Navigation Bar
* **Height:** `80px`, full width, flexbox (space-between).
* **Left:** 
  * Menu Icon: 3 horizontal bars (uneven/aligned left, ~20px width).
  * Brand Logo: Wordmark placeholder positioned `24px` right of the icon.
* **Right:** 
  * Social Icons: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram SVG icons (size: `16px`, gap: `20px`, color: `#1A1A1A`).

### Main Content Area (Two Columns)
* **Left Column (Text Content):**
  * Left padding: `8vw`, vertically centered.
  * Headline block followed by a `24px` gap.
  * 3-line descriptive copy:
    > "We provide better solutions to your clients.  
    > The best design for your business and for your life is here.  
    > Come to see it and fall in love."
  * Button: `SEE MORE`
    * Size: `150px` width, `46px` height.
    * Corners: `border-radius: 4px`.
    * Margin-top: `32px`.

* **Right Column (Product Display):**
  * Horizontally centered in the right half of the screen.

### Right Vertical Sidebar
* **Position:** Fixed along the right edge, vertically centered.
* **Items (Bottom-to-Top or Top-to-Bottom):**
  * `Home` (Active)
  * `Prices`
  * `Products`
  * `About Us`
* **Orientation:** `writing-mode: vertical-rl; transform: rotate(180deg);`
* **Spacing:** `32px` between links.
* **Active Indicator:** A solid `3px` wide vertical black line directly to the right of "Home".

### Footer
* **Position:** Absolute bottom-left, padding: `0 0 40px 8vw`.
* **Content:**
  * Left Accent: `2px` wide, `24px` tall black vertical stroke.
  * Margin-left: `16px`.
  * Text: `+ 01 800 25923857` followed by `100 Main St, New York NY 10002, USA` separated by `32px` horizontal space.

---

## 4. Visual Assets & Media

### Hero Product Image
* **Subject:** Matte black premium over-ear headphones (flat-lay perspective).
* **Format:** Backgroundless PNG (transparent).
* **Rotation/Angle:** Flat top-down view; headband centered at the top, earcups facing down and angled slightly inward.
* **Shadow:** Soft, diffused ambient drop shadow beneath the earcups and band (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`, `blur: 30px`, `y: 15px`).
* **Scale/Placement:** Occupies ~45% viewport width, placed centrally on the right side.
