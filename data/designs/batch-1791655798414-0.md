# Design Specification: Biorg Mobile App UI

## 1. Project Overview & Layout Architecture
- **Project Name:** Biorg
- **Year:** 2026
- **Theme:** Dark Mode, Modern Health/Supplement E-commerce & Tracker
- **Device Frames:** iOS status bar (9:41 time, signal, wifi, battery icons) with rounded notch/dynamic island area, bottom navigation bar with a floating active indicator.
- **Background Color:** Pitch Black (`#000000` or extremely dark `#0A0A0A`) with frosted glassmorphism cards (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
- **Primary Font Family:** Inter / SF Pro Display (Clean, grotesque sans-serif).
- **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
- **Text Styles:**
  - **Screen Headers (Shop, Checkout, Settings):** Bold, heavy weight (~700), size ~32px, tight tracking.
  - **Section Titles & Product Names:** Semi-bold (~600), size ~16px-18px, white (`#FFFFFF`).
  - **Descriptions & Secondary Text:** Regular (~400), size ~12px-14px, muted gray (`#8E8E93`).
  - **Prices & Highlights:** Bold (~700), white (`#FFFFFF`) or accent green (`#34C759`).
