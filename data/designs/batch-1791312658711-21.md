# Production-Grade Design Specification: Spatial Media Portal

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
* **Primary Mood:** Minimalist spatial computing interface, editorial, calm, refined, modern gallery aesthetic.
* **Brand Archetype:** High-end architectural tech, lifestyle curation, ambient computing OS.
* **Visual Density:** Low density, high negative space, breathable hierarchical layout optimized for spatial projection/AR display.
* **Whitespace Balance:** Generous whitespace (approx. 60–70% canvas negative space), emphasizing content card isolation and depth.
* **Theme Balance:** Light/Neutral mode dominated by soft, warm grays and off-white environments, accented by dynamic volumetric media cards. 
* **Depth Architecture:** Multi-layered elevation system using subtle drop shadows, semi-transparent frosted glass overlays, and z-index card layering.

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid

* **Container Max-Width:** Fluid layout bounded at `1440px` with dynamic horizontal margins.
* **Padding & Margin Scale:**
  * Outer Horizontal Padding: `64px` (Desktop)
  * Card Gap: `32px` (Flexbox/Grid track gap)
  * Card Internal Padding: `24px`
* **Section Sequence Breakdown:**
  1. **Top Utility Bar:** System status (Date, Weather, Room identifier, Navigation Links).
  2. **Main Carousel/Deck:** Horizontal scrollable/swipeable spatial cards containing 3D models or rich imagery.
  3. **Bottom Contextual Dock:** Current story identifier, associated collections, and anchor workstation interface.
* **Stacking Order (Z-Index Hierarchy):**
  * Background Wall/Canvas: `z-index: 0`
  * Cards / Carousel Deck: `z-index: 10`
  * Floating Modals / Active Tooltips: `z-index: 100`
  * Top Navigation Bar: `z-index: 1000`

## 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement

### A. Spatial Content Cards
* **Dimensions:** Fixed width `320px`, Height `480px`.
* **Border Radius:** `20px` (`var(--radius-lg)`).
* **Border Width:** `1px` solid `var(--border-subtle)`.
* **Drop Shadow Spec:** `0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`, with an ambient soft blur `0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
* **Internal Layout Stack (Top to Bottom):**
  1. Title (`24px`, Medium) & Subtitle (`14px`, Secondary text).
  2. Central 3D/Media Render Area (fills central mass with 3D projection or immersive imagery).
  3. Bottom-Left Author/Profile Avatar (`32px` diameter circular thumbnail with glowing gradient border).

### B. Navigation Bar & Header
* **Top Bar Layout:** Flex container, space-between alignment.
* **Left Group:** Date (`Friday, 10 aug`), Ambient Icon (Weather/Room sensor), Environment Label (`22°, living room`).
* **Right Group:** Links (`Stories`, `Schedule`, `Habitat`) paired with a spatial room icon.
* **Typography:** All uppercase tracking or muted lowercase styling (`13px`, medium weight).

### C. Workspace Anchor Dock (Bottom Left)
* **Icon Pill:** `40px` square container with gradient background (`var(--accent-gradient)`), rounded corners (`12px`).
* **Metadata Stack:** Title "Stories" (`18px`, Bold), Subtitle "Design, Living, Voyage" (`13px`, Muted).

## 6. Motion Mechanics, 3D & Mobile Gestures

* **Animation Triggers:**
  * Card Hover / Focus: Scale up (`transform: scale(1.03)`), elevation increase, shadow expansion.
  * Carousel Swipe / Navigation: Smooth horizontal translation with perspective skew.
  * 3D Asset Parallax: Subtle tilt based on pointer coordinates or spatial head-tracking.
* **Inferred Animation Libraries:** Framer Motion (React) or WebGL shaders for spatial depth rendering.
* **Transition Durations & Easing Physics:**
  * Standard State Transition: `300ms` cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) (Ease-out-expo).
  * 3D Tilt / Spring Physics: `stiffness: 120`, `damping: 14`.
* **Mobile & Spatial Gestures:**
  * Swipe Left/Right: Snap to next/previous spatial card in the deck.
  * Pinch-to-Expand: Open selected card into an immersive full-screen view.
  * Haptic Feedback: Light micro-haptic tick upon card snap alignment.
