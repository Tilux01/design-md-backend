# BMW M8 Competition Showcase — Production Design Specification

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
* **Primary Mood & Archetype:** High-performance luxury automotive brand showcase. Employs a dark, immersive "stealth luxury" aesthetic designed to highlight sleek vehicle contours, aggressive aerodynamic elements, and high-tech specs.
* **Visual Density & Whitespace:** Minimalist, cinematic layout featuring generous negative space, high contrast, and large editorial typography.
* **Theme Architecture:** 100% Dark Mode native (`#000000` / `#0B0B0C`). Matte charcoal and dark metallic gradients provide subtle surface depth without relying on heavy skeuomorphism.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

* **Primary & Accent:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Crimson Red (`#E50914` / `#FF2A2A` for sports accents and brake calipers).
* **Backgrounds:** Pure Black (`#000000`), Dark Charcoal Surface (`#121212`), Card Surface (`#1A1A1C`).
* **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle Sub-surface Gray (`#2C2C30`), Divider Line (`#3A3A3F`).
* **Text Colors:** Primary Heading (`#FFFFFF`), Body Muted (`#A1A1AA`), Secondary Subtitle (`#71717A`).

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy
* **Font Families:**
  * Headings & UI: `SF Pro Display`, `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif.
  * Monospace / Specs: `JetBrains Mono`, monospace.
* **Scale Breakdown:**
  * **Hero Title (BMW M8):** 48px / 56px line-height, Weight: 700, Tracking: `0.05em`.
  * **Section Heading (COMPETITION):** 64px / 72px line-height, Weight: 800, Tracking: `0.08em`.
  * **Body Copy:** 16px / 24px line-height, Weight: 400, Tracking: `normal`.
  * **Navigation / Micro-labels:** 12px / 16px line-height, Weight: 500, Tracking: `0.1em` (Uppercase).

---

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
* **Container:** Fluid max-width `1440px` with horizontal padding `40px` (`px-10`).
* **Spacing Scale:** 4px baseline grid (`space-1`: 4px, `space-2`: 8px, `space-4`: 16px, `space-8`: 32px, `space-16`: 64px).
* **Section Sequence:**
  1. Full-screen cinematic hero (Headlights teaser transitioning to centered front-facing vehicle).
  2. Editorial feature overview ("THE BMW M8 COMPETITION COUPÉ").
  3. Side profile showcase & technical specifications slider.
  4. Rear aesthetic breakdown (Carbon diffuser, M Sport exhaust).
* **Z-Index Stack:** Background Canvas (`z-0`), Vehicle Render (`z-10`), Navigation & Floating UI (`z-50`), Modals/Overlays (`z-100`).

---

## 5. AI Design Traits & Micro-Badges
* **High-Tracking Uppercase Labels:** Used for feature callouts and section headers (`letter-spacing: 0.12em`).
* **Scroll Indicators:** Minimalist vertical/horizontal progress bars with explicit text prompts (`SCROLL`).
* **Badges:** Pill-shaped micro-tags featuring 1px subtle borders (`border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`), zero background fill, and uppercase tracking.

## 8. Accessibility (a11y) & WCAG Contrast Audit
* **Text Contrast Ratios:**
  * White text (`#FFFFFF`) on Pure Black (`#000000`): **21:1 (Passes WCAG AAA)**.
  * Muted Gray (`#A1A1AA`) on Dark Surface (`#121212`): **7.2:1 (Passes WCAG AA)**.
* **Focus States:**
  * All interactive elements feature a clean `2px solid #E50914` outline with an offset of `2px` on `:focus-visible`.
