# Art Reference Sheet Layout

## Canvas & Viewport
- **Background**: Solid clean white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Layout Type**: Multi-pose reference sheet (Full-body figure on the left, zoomed-in head/torso portrait on the right).
- **Style**: Manga/Anime sketch guide using digital line art, construction lines, and pink joint markers.

---

## Left Component: Full-Body Figure Reference

### Position & Alignment
- **Placement**: Vertically aligned on the left side of the canvas.
- **Pose**: Standing, facing forward with arms held gently in front of the body at waist level, hands clasped. Legs straight and parallel.

### Proportions & Anatomy Lines
- **Head**: 
  - Oval shape with a vertical center-line and a horizontal eye-line dividing the face into upper and lower halves.
  - Ears indicated on the sides.
- **Torso & Limbs**:
  - Shoulders slope outward.
  - Waist is narrow and contoured.
  - Hips flare slightly.
- **Joint Markers (Pink/Red Circles)**:
  - Shoulders (bilateral)
  - Elbows (bilateral)
  - Wrists (bilateral)
  - Waist band line (horizontal pink guide across the torso)
  - Hip/pelvis line (curved pink guide across the hips)
  - Knees (bilateral)
  - Ankles (bilateral)

### Hands & Feet
- **Hands**: Simplified manga-style hands resting together near the pelvis.
- **Feet**: Planted flat, close together, pointing slightly forward/outward.

---

## Right Component: Portrait & Upper Torso Close-Up

### Position & Alignment
- **Placement**: Large focal crop on the right side, angled slightly downward.
- **Pose**: Head turned at a dynamic three-quarter angle, tilted down and to the left.

### Facial Construction Lines
- **Vertical Line**: Runs down the center of the face, curving with the perspective.
- **Horizontal Line**: Crosses through the center for eye alignment, extending back toward the ear.
- **Ear**: Detailed outline of a stylized anime ear visible on the right side.
- **Jaw/Chin**: Sharp, defined chin structure pointing downward.
- **Hair/Bang Strays**: Messy, sketchy ink lines overlapping the left side of the face.

### Torso & Shoulders
- **Neck**: Slender neck connecting the head to the collarbones.
- **Shoulders/Clavicle**: Sketchy, overlapping lines mapping the traps, collarbones, and shoulder muscle contours.
- **Chest**: Indication of bust line curving downward on the left.

---

## Decorative Elements & Doodles

- **Stars & Flowers**: Hand-drawn doodle icons scattered around the right portrait:
  - Small 5-point stars near the cheek, shoulder, and lower left.
  - Simple outline flower near the top center and upper right.
