# UI/UX Specification: Mobile Travel/Exploration App ("Lamiz")

## 1. Global Viewport & Layout Settings
* **Target Screen Size:** Standard Mobile (e.g., iPhone 14/15 Pro: `393px × 852px` or similar aspect ratio).
* **Base Grid/Layout:** Single-column vertical stack with horizontal scrolling components.
* **Global Padding:** `20px` horizontal padding for main content boundaries.
* **Color Palette:**
  * **Primary Dark (Text & Controls):** `#1F2421` (Deep Charcoal)
  * **Primary Light (Backgrounds):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
  * **Accent/Brand:** `#D9822B` (Warm Ochre/Orange)
  * **Secondary Muted Text:** `#8C9290` (Cool Gray)
  * **Card/UI Surfaces (Light Mode):** `#F4F6F5` (Soft Off-White/Gray)

---

## 2. Typography Specification
> **Primary Font Family:** *Inter* or *SF Pro Display* (Clean, geometric sans-serif).
> **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.`

### Typographic Hierarchy:
1. **App Title / Logo ("Lamiz"):**
   * **Font Size:** `28px`

   * **Color:** `#1F2421`
2. **Section Headings:**
   * **Font Size:** `18px`
   * **Font Weight:** `600` (Semi-bold)
   * **Color:** `#1F2421`
3. **Body / Card Labels:**
   * **Font Size:** `14px`
   * **Font Weight:** `500` (Medium)
   * **Color:** `#1F2421` (Primary) or `#8C9290` (Secondary metadata)
4. **Captions / Tiny Labels:**
   * **Font Size:** `11px`
   * **Font Weight:** `400` (Regular)
   * **Color:** `#8C9290`

## 3. Screen States & Component Breakdowns

This design showcases two distinct states or variations of the app (represented by the two phones side-by-side in the mock). We will build this as a modular, state-driven screen.

---

### VARIATION A: Dashboard / Explore Feed (Left View)

#### **A.1 Header Section**
* **Top Navigation:**
  * **Left Icon:** Back chevron or menu icon (stroke-width `2px`, color `#1F2421`).
  * **Center:** Logo text `"Lamiz"` centered horizontally.
  * **Right Icon:** Profile avatar or settings icon (circular, diameter `32px`).
* **Spacing:** `24px` margin below the header.

#### **A.2 Category / Filter Pills**
* **Layout:** Horizontal scrollable row.
* **Items:** Icon + Text pills (e.g., *All, Mountains, Beaches, Cities*).
* **Active Pill:** 
  * Background: `#1F2421` (Dark)
  * Text & Icon Color: `#FFFFFF`
  * Padding: `8px 16px`
  * Border Radius: `20px` (Full pill)
* **Inactive Pills:**
  * Background: `#F4F6F5`
  * Text & Icon Color: `#8C9290`

#### **A.3 Featured Cards Grid**
* **Layout:** 2-column grid with gap of `12px`.
* **Card Dimensions:** Width `~165px`, Height `~180px`.
* **Card Design:**
  * **Border Radius:** `16px`
  * **Internal Structure:** Edge-to-edge background image with subtle bottom-aligned text overlay or white container beneath.
  * **Image Content Description:** 
    * *Card 1 (Left):* Warm sunset/sunrise over a desert or canyon landscape with soft orange and pink hues. Backgroundless within the container (fills entire card area).
    * *Card 2 (Right):* Cool-toned mountain mist or foggy forest scene with deep teal and blue gradients.

#### **A.4 Interactive Toggle / Carousel Component**
* **Layout:** Horizontal control bar.
* **Elements:** A pill-shaped active switch (`#1F2421` background with white text label) followed by circular dot indicators (`#F4F6F5` background).

#### **A.5 Bottom Navigation Bar**
* **Layout:** Fixed floating dock at the bottom (`bottom: 20px`, width `calc(100% - 40px)`).
* **Background:** Solid `#FFFFFF` with a subtle elevation shadow (`0px 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`).
* **Border Radius:** `28px` (Pill shape).
* **Icons:** 4 evenly spaced minimalist outline icons representing Home, Search, Saved/Favorites, and Profile.
* **Active Indicator:** The center/selected icon features a warm golden accent ring or circular badge (`#D9822B`).

---

### VARIATION B: Immersive Detail / Hero View (Right View)

#### **B.1 Immersive Hero Background**
* **Layout:** Full-bleed background image covering the top 65% of the viewport.
* **Image Description:** A breathtaking panoramic photograph of a sunlit mountain range during golden hour (sunset/sunrise). Warm golden light filtering through mountain peaks, fading into soft atmospheric haze at the bottom. 
* **Overlays:** 
  * Top status bar elements (Time, Wi-Fi, Battery) in white.
  * Top navigation controls (Back arrow and bookmark icon) with semi-transparent dark circular backdrops (`rgba(0,0,0,0.2)` + backdrop blur).

#### **B.2 Bottom Sheet / Details Container**
* **Layout:** Overlapping white container anchored to the bottom of the viewport (`border-radius: 32px 32px 0 0`).
* **Padding:** `24px` top/sides.
* **Content inside Bottom Sheet:**
  * **Drag Handle:** Small centered horizontal pill (`width: 36px`, `height: 4px`, color `#D1D5D3`, `border-radius: 2px`).
  * **Thumbnail Row:** Two horizontal preview cards showing secondary locations/views related to the main destination.
    * *Thumbnail 1:* Warm cabin/landscape view.
    * *Thumbnail 2:* Coastal/water view.
  * **Action Button:** Full-width primary CTA button at the bottom of the sheet.
    * Background: `#1F2421` (Dark)
    * Text: `#FFFFFF` ("Book Now" or "Explore")
    * Border Radius: `24px`
    * Height: `52px`
