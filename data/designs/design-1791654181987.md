# Comprehensive UI/UX Design Specification & Replication Blueprint

This specification provides a pixel-precise blueprint for replicating the three-screen fitness mobile application design showcased in the provided media. The output focuses exclusively on the full-screen application layouts, treating them as distinct, production-ready interfaces.

---

## Global Design Tokens & System Foundation

### 1. Color Palette
*   **Background / Canvas Base:** Deep atmospheric dark indigo (`#0A0F1D` to `#12182B`) with a soft directional top-right radial blue glow (`#1E3A8A` at 30% opacity).
*   **Primary Accent / Action:** Bright Electric Cyan/Blue (`#2563EB` to `#3B82F6`), used for active states, progress indicators, and primary buttons.
*   **Surface / Glassmorphism Cards:** Semi-translucent dark slate panels (`#1E293B` or `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.65)`) paired with a frosted glass backdrop filter (`blur(16px)`), a subtle white top-border highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), and soft drop shadows (`0px 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`).
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   *Primary High Contrast:* Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
    *   *Secondary Muted:* Cool Silver/Gray (`#94A3B8`).
    *   *Tertiary Faded:* Slate Gray (`#64748B`).
*   **Interactive Toggles:** Vibrant Lime/Neon Green (`#10B981`) for active switches.

### 2. Typography System
*   **Font Family:** *Inter*, *SF Pro Display*, or *Plus Jakarta Sans* (Clean, modern geometric sans-serifs).
*   **Fallbacks:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Hierarchy Scale:**
    *   *Screen Headers / Titles:* 28px - 32px, Bold (Weight: 700), Line height: 1.2.
    *   *Card Headings / Section Titles:* 16px - 18px, Semi-Bold (Weight: 600).
    *   *Body / Descriptive Text:* 13px - 14px, Regular (Weight: 400), Line height: 1.4.
    *   *Captions / Metadata / Stats:* 11px - 12px, Medium (Weight: 500).

## Screen 1: Dashboard & Task Tracking (“Hi Dany, Welcome!”)

### Layout & Structure
*   **Container:** Full-height mobile canvas with an edge-to-edge layout.
*   **Background Asset:** A dynamic background image of a female fitness athlete in a athletic top and blue shorts, dynamically punching toward the left side.
    *   *Placement:* Absolute positioned, covering the upper-left to mid-right viewport, masked or blended into the dark navy background using a gradient fade.
    *   *Rotation/Pose:* Upper body turned right-to-left, left arm extended straight out with a white boxing wrap.

### Component Breakdown

1.  **Top Navigation / Header:**
    *   *Greeting:* "Hi Dany," (18px, Regular, `#94A3B8`) followed by "Welcome!" (28px, Bold, `#FFFFFF`) stacked vertically.
    *   *Profile / Home Icon:* Top right circular glassmorphism button containing a white home outline icon (`16px`).

2.  **Weekly Stats Bar (Glassmorphic Horizontal Pill):**
    *   *Background:* Translucent dark blur card spanning full width.
    *   *Content:* Four evenly spaced metric icons inside small circular badges (Activity rings, Clock, Flame, Weight/Medal) with a right-aligned "+" (Add New Task) button encased in a primary blue accent circle.

3.  **Active Task Section:**
    *   *Header:* "Active Task" (14px, Semi-Bold, `#FFFFFF`).
    *   *Card:* Large glassmorphic card dominating the lower-middle section.
        *   *Left Content:* "Running" title, followed by subtext "3.8 km Outdoor Walk".
        *   *Right Content Stack:* 
            *   Streak indicator badge ("🔥 60%").
            *   Circular SVG progress dial indicating "60%".
            *   Steps goal pill ("Steps Goal 5000/7000").

4.  **Previous Tasks Section:**
    *   *Header Row:* "Previous Tasks" on the left, "View All" link on the right (`#94A3B8`).
    *   *Card:* Horizontal summary bar indicating completion rate (`70%` circular badge) and text: "12 tasks completed / 12/20".

## Screen 3: Live Workout Tracker (“Running”)

### Layout & Structure
*   **Container:** Full-height mobile screen optimized for real-time activity monitoring.
*   **Background Asset:** Cinematic header image of a female runner in a black sports bra, captured mid-stride/pose with directional blue studio lighting.

### Component Breakdown

2.  **Header & Controls Bar:**
