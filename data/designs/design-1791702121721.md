# Design System & Implementation Specification (`design.md`)

**Project Name:** INSKIN — Hero Section Design  
**Visual Style:** Brutalist Luxury / Modern Minimalist / High-Contrast Monochrome  
**Target Canvas:** Full-Screen Browser Display (`100vw` × `100vh`)

---

## 1. Overview & Aesthetic Concept

The design merges stark high-contrast luxury cosmetics branding with natural, raw, industrial textures (volcanic rock, bare silhouettes of branches). It utilizes heavy condensed sans-serif display typography contrasted against high-fashion serif numbering, wrapped in a ultra-clean, floating UI interface.

* **Color Theme:** Strictly monochrome (Pure White, Deep Black, Muted Charcoal Greys).
* **Composition:** Center-focused vertical hierarchy with heavy bottom grounding (rocks) and framing side accents (branches).
* **Atmosphere:** Ethereal, moody, raw, tactile.

## 3. Typography System

### 3.1 Primary Headline Font (Display Condensed)
* **Visual Style:** Ultra-bold, compressed, uppercase geometric/grotesque sans-serif. Tall x-height, zero character spacing, high impact.
* **Exact/Ideal Font:** *Druk Condensed Super*, *Monument Extended UltraBold*, or *Bebas Neue (Bold)*.
* **Fallback Stack:** `"Druk Condensed", "Bebas Neue", "Impact", "Arial Narrow", sans-serif`
* **Properties:**
  * Size: `clamp(3.5rem, 7vw, 7.5rem)`
  * Line Height: `0.88`
  * Letter Spacing: `-0.02em`
  * Text Transform: `uppercase`
  * Font Weight: `900`

### 3.2 Accent Numbering Font (Editorial Serif)
* **Visual Style:** High-contrast luxury serif, italic/editorial curves.
* **Exact/Ideal Font:** *Bodoni 72 Italic*, *Didot*, or *Playfair Display (Italic)*.
* **Fallback Stack:** `"Bodoni MT", "Didot", "Playfair Display", "Georgia", serif`
* **Properties:**
  * Size: `clamp(2rem, 4vw, 4rem)`
  * Line Height: `1.0`
  * Font Style: `italic`
  * Font Weight: `400`

### 3.3 UI Navigation & Button Text
* **Visual Style:** Clean, geometric sans-serif for high legibility at small sizes.
* **Exact/Ideal Font:** *Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, or *SF Pro Text*.
* **Fallback Stack:** `"Inter", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
* **Properties:**
  * Size: `13px` / `0.8125rem`
  * Font Weight: `500` (Medium)
  * Letter Spacing: `0.01em`

### 3.4 Brand Logo (`[ INSKIN ]`) & CTA Code Text
* **Visual Style:** Technical, brutalist, light monospace.
* **Exact/Ideal Font:** *JetBrains Mono*, *Space Mono*, or *SF Mono*.
* **Fallback Stack:** `"JetBrains Mono", "Courier New", monospace`
* **Properties:**
  * Size: `12px` / `0.75rem`
  * Letter Spacing: `0.1em`
  * Font Weight: `600` (Semi-Bold)
  * Text Transform: `uppercase`

2. `z-index: 10` — Main Typography Block ("WHERE RAW...")
3. `z-index: 15` — Bottom Volcanic Rock Pedestal
4. `z-index: 20` — Foreground/Midground Tree Silhouette Branches (Left & Right)
5. `z-index: 30` — Glass Bottle Product Asset (sitting on the rock top)
6. `z-index: 40` — `[ SHOP COLLECTION ]` Button
7. `z-index: 100` — Top Navigation Header (Logo, Nav Bar, Cart)

## 6. Image & Visual Asset Specifications

### 6.1 Main Product Bottle (Fragrance / Skincare Container)
* **Asset Type:** Transparent PNG / High-res 3D Render with clean alpha channel.
* **Dimensions:** Width approx `180px - 220px` (scaled relative to screen height).
* **Position:** Dead center horizontally. Placed so its base rests firmly on the peak of the center rock pedestal.
* **Physical Description:**
  * Square glass bottle with rounded edges.
  * Frosted/matte off-white to pale silver liquid texture inside.
  * Center label: Minimalist square with a black geometric interlocking line-art monogram logo (`S` / `IS` brand emblem).
  * Cap: Cylindrical ribbed/smooth light silver metallic cap.
* **Lighting:** Overhead studio soft-box lighting. Gentle reflections on the glass shoulders. Subtle drop shadow casting onto the rock surface underneath.

### 6.2 Volcanic Rock Pedestal Base
* **Asset Type:** Transparent PNG asset spanning full width of the bottom screen.
* **Dimensions:** `100vw` width, height covering bottom `40%` of the viewport.
* **Position:** Attached to the bottom edge (`bottom: 0`, `left: 0`).
* **Physical Description:**
  * Dark charcoal and black sharp-edged volcanic rock structure.
  * Rough, porous, highly tactile stone surface with detailed light highlights along top ridges and deep black crevices in shadow.
  * Center peak is higher to act as a natural display pedestal for the bottle.
  * A subtle dark vignette/black gradient fades the lower third of the rocks into pure black `#000000` at the very bottom edge.

### 6.3 Left Silhouetted Tree Branch Asset
* **Asset Type:** PNG with pure transparency background.
* **Position:** Positioned along the left edge (`left: 0`, `top: 35%`).
* **Rotation:** Angled slightly upward towards the center (`rotate: 12deg`).
* **Physical Description:**
  * Completely stark black, leafless, gnarled tree/shrub branch.
  * Sharp silhouettes extending inward towards the product without obscuring the main text.
  * High dramatic contrast against the pure white backdrop.

### 6.4 Right Silhouetted Tree Branch Asset
* **Asset Type:** PNG with pure transparency background.
* **Position:** Positioned in the top right corner (`right: -2%`, `top: 22%`).
* **Rotation:** Sprawling downward and inward (`rotate: -5deg`).
* **Physical Description:**
  * Intricate, highly detailed bare winter branches framing the top-right quarter of the screen.
  * Pure black silhouette (`#000000`).

### 6.5 Background Canvas
* **Upper 65%:** Pure crisp White (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Lower 35%:** Occluded by the dark rock structure with soft ambient shadow transition.

## 7. Interactive Micro-Interactions & States

1. **Navigation Links:**
   * On hover: Inactive links transition opacity from `0.7` to `1.0` in `150ms ease-in-out`.
   * Cursor: `pointer`.
2. **Shopping Cart Button:**
   * On hover: Subtle scale increase `transform: scale(1.05)` with `200ms cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`.
   * Cursor: `pointer`.
3. **Primary CTA (`[ SHOP COLLECTION ]`):**
   * Default: Floating white semi-transparent pill.
   * On hover: Background transitions to solid black (`#000000`), text color transitions to pure white (`#FFFFFF`), border becomes `#000000`.
   * Scale on hover: `transform: scale(1.03) translateY(-2px)`.
   * Transition duration: `250ms ease`.
