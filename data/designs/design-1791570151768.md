# Comprehensive UI/UX Design Specification & Replication Blueprint

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Properties
- **Viewport:** 100% Full-Width (Responsive design scaling fluidly up to a 1440px+ max-width container baseline).
- **Background Color Palette:**
  - Primary Canvas Base: `#F5F5F0` (Warm off-white / light cream stone tone).
  - Surface Containers (Cards): `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for primary feature blocks).
  - Accent Green: `#A3E635` or `#84CC16` (Lime-chartreuse brand focal tone used for primary interactive highlights, badges, and focal shapes).
  - Header/Navigation Background: Transparent overlay sitting directly on top of the hero photographic imagery.
  - Text Primary: `#1C1917` (Stone 900 - dark charcoal black).
  - Text Secondary: `#57534E` (Stone 600 - muted gray).
- **Border Radius Standards:**
  - Outer Page Wrapper / Cards: `24px` to `32px` rounded corners.
  - Buttons / Badges: Full pill shape (`9999px`) or `12px` rounded rectangles.

---

## 2. Typography Specifications
*Note: If specific proprietary brand typefaces are unreachable, use the designated web-safe/Google Fonts fallbacks specified below.*

### Primary Headings (Display & H1-H3)
- **Font Family:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, or `Manrope` (Fallback: `sans-serif`).
- **Characteristics:** Geometric, highly legible, modern sans-serif with slight humanist touch.
- **Hero Title:** ~56px–64px, Font Weight: 600 (Semi-bold), Line Height: 1.1, Letter Spacing: Tight (`-0.02em`).
- **Section Headers:** ~36px–42px, Font Weight: 600, Line Height: 1.2.

### Body Text & Navigation
- **Font Family:** `Inter`, `Roboto`, or `system-ui` (Fallback: `sans-serif`).
- **Navigation Links:** 14px–15px, Font Weight: 500 (Medium).
- **Paragraphs / Descriptions:** 15px–16px, Font Weight: 400 (Regular), Line Height: 1.5.

---

## 4. Interaction & State Guidelines
- **Hover States on Buttons:** Buttons featuring lime-green (`#A3E635`) should scale up marginally (`transform: scale(1.02)`) with increased drop shadow intensity.
- **Icons:** All action arrows (`↗`) should smoothly translate diagonally up-right by `2px` on hover.
- **Transitions:** Smooth ease-in-out transitions (`200ms`) applied to all interactive elements, navigation dropdowns, and card surfaces.
