# Design Specification: "ANIMATE" Creative Academy Hero Landing Page

This document provides a comprehensive, pixel-accurate design and implementation blueprint to clone the full-screen interactive landing page.

---

## 1. Global Screen Layout & Viewport Configuration

- **Target Display Mode:** Full-viewport fluid layout (`100vw`, `100vh`, `min-height: 100vh`, `overflow: hidden`).
- **Aspect Ratio & Canvas:** Edge-to-edge full screen. No exterior presentation borders, drop-shadows, or framed device mockups.
- **Base Background:** Multi-stop dark gradient:
  - Deep Navy Base: `#0B0E14` (corners and edges).
  - Center/Glow Highlight: `#131A29` (subtle radial diffusion radiating behind the 3D asset at 45% X, 50% Y).
- **Structure Partitioning:**
  - **Main Canvas:** Occupies `calc(100vw - 80px)` from left edge.
  - **Right Sidebar Rail:** Fixed `80px` width from right edge spanning full height (`100vh`), separated by a subtle 1px border.

## 3. Typography & Font Hierarchy

### Font Families
- **Primary Display Font (Headline & Numbers):**
  - **Preferred:** `Oswald` (Bold 700 / Condensed) or `Bebas Neue`.
  - **Fallback:** `Impact, "Arial Narrow", sans-serif`.
  - **Characteristics:** Ultra-condensed, tall vertical proportion, minimal tracking for headline.
- **Secondary UI & Body Font:**
  - **Preferred:** `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Regular 400, Medium 500, Semi-Bold 600).
  - **Fallback:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.

## 4. Visual Asset Specification: Central 3D Asset

- **Format:** High-resolution transparent PNG / WebP (100% backgroundless cut-out).
- **Subject:** An organic, toroidal wreath (doughnut-ring cluster) made of tightly clustered spheres.
- **Physical Characteristics & Materials:**
  1. **Matte Navy Spheres (65% of volume):** Deep midnight blue, velvety matte rubber finish with crisp top-left rim reflections.
  2. **Metallic Teal Spheres (20% of volume):** Smooth satin cyan/petroleum-blue reflective chrome spheres interspersed through the upper cluster.
  3. **Crystalline Gold Flake Spheres (15% of volume):** Transparent glass/amber refractive spheres with internal glowing gold foil leaf, molten liquid gold filaments, and speckles.
- **Composition & Placement:**
  - **Position:** Vertically centered (`top: 50%; transform: translateY(-50%)`). Horizontally positioned with its center anchor at approximately `38vw` from the left screen boundary.
  - **Dimensions:** Width approx. `48vw` to `52vw`, keeping dynamic scaling bounded between `520px` and `720px`.
  - **Z-Index Layering (Depth Interlocking):**
    - The spheres on the left of the torus sit at `z-index: 10`.
    - The main text `"ANIMATE"` sits at `z-index: 5` (appearing tucked partially behind the foreground left-center spheres and extending out to the right).
