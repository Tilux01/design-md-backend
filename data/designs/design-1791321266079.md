# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist, Dark Mode Editorial / Cinematic Portfolio.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Highly spacious layout featuring generous margins, asymmetric visual balance, and clean typography with dramatic contrast between pure white surfaces, deep blacks, and monochromatic photography.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean flat white canvas (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle monochromatic borders (`#E5E7EB`), minimalist pill tag labels, and absolute-positioned glassmorphic floating callout cards (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` with backdrop-blur).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Minimalist floating glass cards, numbered slider pagination dots, portrait callout overlays, status indicator dots.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 12px`, font-size `10px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-black inline-block"></span>`.
