# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Glassmorphism & Immersive Digital Gaming Client UI.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-density interface balanced by dark translucent surface layers, subtle inner padding, and glowing ambient atmospheric lighting.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-tier elevation utilizing deep canvas backgrounds (`#0A0F1D`), semi-transparent glass cards (`rgba(18, 24, 38, 0.7)`), 1px subtle borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and deep atmospheric backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient background glow orbs, translucent glassmorphism panels, high-tracking uppercase pill badges with pulsing live indicators.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-400 inline-block"></span>`.
