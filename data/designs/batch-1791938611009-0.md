# Design Specification: Dignitas Player Profile & Portal

## 1. General Theme & Visual Language
*   **Theme:** Dark Mode / High-Contrast Esports Aesthetic.
*   **Primary Color Palette:**
    *   **Deep Black:** `#0A0A0A` (Main backgrounds).
    *   **Dignitas Yellow:** `#FFD700` or `#F5CF11` (Accents, primary buttons, graffiti).
    *   **Dark Gray:** `#1A1A1A` (Card backgrounds, secondary containers).
    *   **Medium Gray:** `#2D2D2D` (Borders, inactive states).
    *   **White:** `#FFFFFF` (Primary text).
    *   **Muted Text:** `#888888` (Labels and metadata).
*   **Corner Radius:** Subtle rounding on all cards and containers (approx. `8px` to `12px`).
*   **Effects:** Heavy use of drop shadows on cards and subtle glows on yellow elements.

## 2. Typography
The design uses a technical, condensed sans-serif font family common in gaming.
*   **Primary Font:** *Industry* or *Rajdhani*.
*   **Secondary Font:** *Roboto Condensed* or *Inter*.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `Arial Narrow, sans-serif`.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Player Name (Graffiti):** Custom hand-drawn script or "Street Art" style brush font.
    *   **Headings:** All-caps, bold, tracking (letter-spacing) set to `0.05em`.
    *   **Stats Numbers:** Large, bold, monospace-leaning sans-serif.

## 3. Global Navigation (Top Bar)
*   **Left:** Dignitas Logo (White) followed by a vertical separator and "LEAGUE OF LEGENDS - PROFILE" in small, all-caps muted gray text.
*   **Right:** 
    *   "LIVE NOW" text with a pulsing red dot indicator.
    *   A vertical three-line hamburger menu icon (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Background:** Transparent or `#0A0A0A` with a thin bottom border.

## 4. Vertical Utility Sidebar (Right Side)
A slim, dark bar (`#0D0D0D`) fixed to the right edge.
*   **Top:** A 3x3 grid icon (App launcher style).
*   **Middle:** Social icons (Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, Discord, Twitch, TikTok) in muted gray, turning yellow on hover.
*   **Bottom:** A controller icon and chevron arrows for page navigation.

## 5. Hero Section (Player Profile)
This is the centerpiece of the design.
*   **Background Graphic:** A large, bright yellow "Graffiti" splash that spells out "APHROMOO" in a messy, urban spray-paint style. It features drips at the bottom.
*   **Main Image (Aphromoo):** 
    *   **Description:** A high-resolution portrait of the player (Aphromoo) from the waist up. He is wearing a black and yellow camouflage Dignitas jersey.
    *   **Placement:** Centered, overlapping the graffiti text.
    *   **Transparency:** Backgroundless (PNG).
*   **Player Info (Left of Player):**
    *   "Aphromoo" in large white bold text.
    *   "ZAQUERI BLACK" in small yellow sub-text.
    *   Social icons (Twitter, Instagram, Twitch) and a US Flag icon.
*   **Stats Overlay (Bottom of Hero):**
    *   Three glassmorphism-style cards (semi-transparent dark gray with a blur effect).
    *   **Card 1:** "MATCHES PLAYED" / "59".
    *   **Card 2:** "GAMES PLAYED" / "71" (This card has a yellow glow/gradient overlay).
    *   **Card 3:** "AVG KDA" / "2.40".
    *   **Footer Stats:** A thin bar below the cards showing "KP% 62%", "VSPM% 2.40", etc., in small yellow and white text.

## 6. Media & Content Cards (Lower Sections)
The layout transitions into a grid of content cards.

### A. Article/Guide Card
*   **Image:** Backgroundless PNG of a player (e.g., Froggen) with a headset, positioned to the left.
*   **Content:** A dark box with a yellow border containing the title "How to Carry Your Team in League of Legends - A Guide with Froggen."
*   **Metadata:** Date, author avatar, and "TOP-LANE TERRORS" category tag.

### B. Video/Media Card
*   **Visual:** A large thumbnail with a play button overlay.
*   **Text:** "LCS APHROMOO" in large yellow/white outlined text.
*   **Footer:** Small icons for "Watch," "Share," and "Stats."

### C. Game Selection Grid (Bottom Left)
*   A 2x3 grid of square icons representing different games (CS:GO, Valorant, LoL, Smash Bros, etc.).
*   **Style:** Dark gray background with white minimalist game logos.

### D. Live Stream / News Feed (Bottom Right)
*   A vertical list or smaller grid showing "How to Carry..." guide again, alongside a "Make it with Creative Cloud" advertisement card (vibrant purple/blue gradient).

## 7. Image Asset Requirements
1.  **Player Portrait (Main):** High-res PNG, transparent background. Subject: Black male, glasses, Dignitas camo jersey, slight smile, looking at camera.
2.  **Graffiti Background:** Vector or high-res PNG. Bright yellow (`#F5CF11`). Style: "Dripping spray paint."
3.  **Article Portraits:** High-res PNG, transparent background. Subject: Players with headsets, side profile or 3/4 view.
4.  **Camo Pattern:** A subtle black/dark-gray digital camouflage pattern used as a texture on the player's jersey and occasionally in card backgrounds.
5.  **Brand Logos:** Dignitas "Owl/Alien" logo in white and yellow versions.

## 8. Layout Measurements (Desktop)
*   **Container Width:** 100% (Full screen).
*   **Sidebar Width:** 60px.
*   **Hero Height:** 70vh to 80vh.
*   **Card Gutter:** 20px.
*   **Typography Scale:**
    *   Hero Name: 48px - 64px.
    *   Card Titles: 18px - 22px.
    *   Stat Numbers: 36px - 42px.
    *   Body Text: 14px.
