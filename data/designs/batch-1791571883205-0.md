# Design Specification: Thinkeo App UI/UX

---

## 1. Global Styles & Design System

### A. Color Palette
*   **Primary Deep Purple (`#2A1B54` / `#31235C`):** Used for primary brand typography, main CTAs ("Let's Get Started"), and navigation headers.
*   **Background Off-White / Cream (`#F6F5FB` / `#F8F7FC`):** Soft, neutral canvas for mobile screens reducing eye strain.
*   **Accent Yellow (`#FFC837` to `#FFAC1C`):** Highlights stars, streak flames, and active badges.
*   **Soft Pastels (Card Backgrounds):**
    *   **Reading/Purple:** `#D7C7FE` (Light lilac)
    *   **Math/Green:** `#C2EDD3` (Mint green)
    *   **Science/Pink:** `#FAD2CD` (Dusty peach-pink)
    *   **Creativity/Yellow:** `#FDEB9E` (Warm cream-yellow)
*   **Interactive Green (`#34C759`):** Correct answer indicator, completion badges.

### B. Typography & Font Stack
*   **Primary Font Family:** **Quicksand** (Google Fonts), fallback to `Nunito`, `Comfortaa`, `Comic Sans MS`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Secondary/Body Font Family:** **Inter** or **Poppins**, fallback to `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Styling Characteristics:** Rounded geometric shapes, highly legible for early readers and children, friendly and approachable.

## 2. Screen 1: Onboarding Flow ("Big Dreams Start Here")

### Layout Structure
*   **Top Navigation:**
    *   Top-left: Subtle handwritten accent text: *"Learning that feels like play"* (Script font, angled slightly upward with yellow motion lines).
    *   Top-right: **"Skip"** text button in deep purple (`#31235C`).
*   **Main Header Section:**
    *   Title: **"Big Dreams Start Here"** (Massive, bold, extra-large headline in Deep Purple, line-broken with a decorative yellow pencil/scribble underline on "Dreams").
    *   Subtitle: *"A fun and personalized learning app that helps kids build skills, confidence and a brighter future."*
*   **Central Visual Asset:**
    *   **Illustration Description:** A warm, 3D-style animated illustration of a smiling young Black girl with dark afro-puffs, wearing a pink sweater, resting her chin on her hands with a golden crown hovering above her head. Floating around her are a glowing yellow star and handwritten notes reading *"Curious Minds Brighter Futures"* with a heart doodle.
*   **Bottom Action Area:**
    *   Pagination Dots: 4 small indicator dots (active dot is deep purple, inactive dots are gray/light purple).
    *   CTA Button: **"Let's Get Started →"** (Full-width rounded pill button, solid deep purple fill, white text with an arrow icon).
    *   Footer Link: *"Already have an account? **Sign in**"* centered at the very bottom.

## 3. Screen 2: Dashboard ("Hi, Amara!")

### Layout Structure
*   **Top Header:**
    *   Avatar: Circular profile picture of a smiling young girl with pigtails.
    *   Greeting: *"Hi, Amara! 👋"* with subtext *"Ready to learn something new?"*
    *   Action Icon: Notification bell icon in the top right.
*   **Streak Tracker Widget:**
    *   Header: *"You’re on a 5 day streak! 🔥"*
    *   Visual progress bar showing days of the week (Mon–Sun) with checkmarks on completed days and a star milestone on Sunday.
*   **Core Learning Modules (Grid 2x2):**
    *   **Card 1 (Reading):** Light purple background (`#D7C7FE`). Book icon, title *"Reading"*, subtitle *"Build your super reading skills"*, right-arrow action circle.
    *   **Card 2 (Math):** Mint green background (`#C2EDD3`). Math symbols icon (`+ - ÷`), title *"Math"*, subtitle *"Solve, play and grow"*, right-arrow action circle.
    *   **Card 3 (Science):** Pink/Peach background (`#FAD2CD`). Beaker icon, title *"Science"*, subtitle *"Explore the amazing world"*, right-arrow action circle.
    *   **Card 4 (Creativity):** Warm yellow background (`#FDEB9E`). Artist palette icon, title *"Creativity"*, subtitle *"Imagine, create and express"*, right-arrow action circle.
*   **Continue Learning Section:**
    *   Header: *"Continue Learning"* with a *"See all"* link.
    *   Card: Horizontal banner featuring a 3D baby lion ("Animals Around Us") with a green completion progress bar at 60% and an arrow icon.
*   **Badges Section:**
    *   Header: *"Badges"* with a *"See all"* link.
    *   Row of achievement badges: *First Lesson* (Star), *5 Day Streak* (Flame), *Math Star* (Medal), *Curious Mind* (Lightbulb).
*   **Bottom Navigation Bar:**
    *   Icons with labels: **Home** (active, purple), **Explore**, **Rewards**, **Profile**.

## 4. Screen 3: Interactive Lesson ("Let's Read!")

### Layout Structure
*   **Top Header & Progress:**
    *   Back arrow (`<`), centered title **"Reading"**.
    *   Progress bar split into 5 segments (3rd segment active/purple). Step counter: `3/5`.
*   **Question Card (Speech Bubble):**
    *   Header: *"Let's Read!"* with an audio speaker icon (`🔊`) for text-to-speech.
    *   Sentence Text: *"The **cat** sits on the **mat**. It is a sunny day."* (Key vocabulary words *cat* and *mat* are highlighted in soft green and yellow background pills).
*   **Central Visual Asset:**
    *   **Illustration Description:** A cute, 3D-rendered orange tabby kitten sitting happily on a purple yoga mat outdoors against a soft green park background with wooden fences and flowers.
*   **Question & Multiple Choice Options:**
    *   Prompt: *"What is the cat sitting on?"*
    *   Option Cards:
        1.  *"A bed"* (White card, clean border)
        2.  *"A mat"* (**Correct Choice:** Light green filled card with a green checkmark badge in the bottom-right corner)
        3.  *"A table"* (White card, clean border)
*   **Bottom Action Area:**
    *   CTA Button: **"Next →"** (Solid purple pill button spanning the bottom width).
