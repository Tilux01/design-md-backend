# Design Specification: Acquire Landing Page Clone

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

*   **View:** Full-screen viewport (`width: 100vw`, `height: 100vh` minimum, with scrolling enabled for footer content).
*   **Background Color:** Deep solid black (`#0A0A0A` or `#000000`).
*   **Grain Texture:** Subtle noise/grain overlay applied across the entire viewport to match the film-like texture in the source.
*   **Grid System:** Asymmetric layout.
    *   Top section: Hero image dominating the upper half, large typography and CTA button below it.
    *   Bottom section: 4-column footer link structure.
    *   Bottom bar: Copyright text aligned left.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Font Family
*   **Primary Family:** `Inter`, sans-serif (Clean, modern geometric sans-serif).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

### Typography Hierarchy

| Element | Font Size | Weight | Line Height | Color / Opacity | Letter Spacing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Headline (Part 1)** | `48px` / `3rem` | Light (300) | `1.1` | `#E0E0E0` (High emphasis) | `-0.02em` |
| **Headline (Part 2)** | `48px` / `3rem` | Semi-Bold (600) | `1.1` | `#FFFFFF` (Solid white) | `-0.02em` |
| **Footer Column Headers**| `10px` / `0.625rem`| Medium (500) | `1.5` | `#666666` (Muted gray) | `0.1em` (Uppercase) |
| **Footer Links** | `12px` / `0.75rem` | Regular (400) | `1.8` | `#CCCCCC` (Light gray) | `normal` |
| **Copyright Notice** | `10px` / `0.625rem`| Regular (400) | `1.5` | `#444444` (Dark gray) | `normal` |

## 3. Hero Section & Imagery

### Hero Image (Particle Figures)
*   **Asset Type:** Raster image / Transparent PNG or WebP.
*   **Description:** An abstract artistic composition rendered in glowing white particles/stardust against a black background. It depicts two human-like figures formed from dense clusters of dots, reaching out to shake hands or connect over an undulating, starry landscape made of particle waves.
*   **Background:** Backgroundless (Transparent).
*   **Placement:** Centered horizontally in the upper portion of the viewport, overlapping the top third to middle section.
*   **Rotation/Transform:** No rotation (`0deg`), natural upright orientation.

### Headline Content
*   **Text String:** 
    *   Line 1: `Provide your ` (Light, color `#8E8E93`) `customers` (Semi-bold, color `#FFFFFF`)
    *   Line 2: `the assistance`
    *   Line 3: `they need`
*   **Alignment:** Left-aligned, positioned in the middle-left area beneath the hero visual.

### Call to Action (CTA) Button ("Schedule a demo")
*   **Background Color:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white).
*   **Text Color:** `#000000` (Pure black).
*   **Font Size:** `13px`.
*   **Font Weight:** Medium (500).
*   **Padding:** `12px 24px`.
*   **Border Radius:** `2px` (Subtle, nearly sharp corners).
*   **Placement:** Positioned on the mid-right side of the screen, aligned horizontally with the middle of the headline block.
