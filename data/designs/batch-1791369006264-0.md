# Design Specification: STUDDS Motorcycle Helmet E-Commerce Website

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

### Color Palette
* **Primary / Accent Red:** `#E31B23` (Used for branding, key call-to-actions, and vertical separator accent stripe).
* **Primary Dark / Black:** `#111111` (Used for header text, footer background, primary body copy headers).
* **Primary White:** `#FFFFFF` (Used for card backgrounds, content boxes, background tones).
* **Off-White / Light Gray:** `#F4F4F4` or `#F7F7F7` (Used for page backgrounds, subtle section containers).
* **Muted Gray:** `#777777` / `#888888` (Used for secondary descriptions, footer metadata, navigation links).
* **Border Gray:** `#E0E0E0` (Used for subtle separation lines).

### Typography
* **Font Family:** Clean sans-serif system (e.g., *Helvetica Neue*, *Arial*, *Roboto*).
* **Headings:** Bold, uppercase sans-serif with tracked spacing.
* **Body Text:** Clean, readable sans-serif with ample line-height.

---

## 2. Header & Top Navigation Bar

* **Layout:** Fixed/Sticky top banner, full-width with contained content.
* **Background:** Light off-white (`#F2F1EC`).
* **Left Section:**
  * Navigation Links: `Home`, `Corporate`, `Products`, `Safety`.
  * Style: Uppercase, small, dark gray text with hover states.
* **Center Section:**
  * Brand Logo: `STUDDS` in bold uppercase white text inside a solid red rectangular badge (`#E31B23`).
* **Right Section:**
  * Navigation Links: `Media`, `Career`, `Contact Us`.
  * Search Icon: Magnifying glass symbol at the far right.

---

## 3. Hero Section (Top Banner)

* **Layout:** Asymmetric split banner with a striking central vertical red divider stripe running down through the page.
* **Background:** Clean gradient/off-white.
* **Left Column:**
  * Small Subtitle Tag: `"Check Out 2015 Fall Collection"` with a red icon accent.
  * Main Headline: `"CUB-07 DECOR"` in large, bold, black uppercase font.
  * CTA Button: Outline style button (`"know more"`).
  * Section Below Left:
    * Heading: `"Welcome to Studds Accessories Ltd."`
    * Body Copy: Company description text.
    * CTA Button: Outline style button (`"read more"`).
* **Center Focus (The Bike & Rider):**
  * A promotional image featuring a motorcycle model (e.g., rider leaning against a customized cruiser bike styled with rocks/scenery at the base).
  * Centered vertical red bar passing behind the feature items.
* **Right Column:**
  * Featured Helmet Card: `"KS-1 Metro"` with side directional arrows (`<` `>`) and product thumbnail view.
  * Section Below Right:
    * Heading: `"Share your design"`
    * Body Copy: Callout text inviting user graphic/sticker design submissions.
    * CTA Button: Outline style button (`"read more"`).

## 4. Product Categories Section

* **Container:** Off-white background section (`#EFECE6`) with subtle top/bottom borders.
* **Section Header:**
  * Small Tag: `"Product Categories"` in muted red uppercase text.
* **Main Product Showcase Carousel / Banner:**
  * Large Left/Right navigation arrows (`<` `>`).
  * **Title:** `"OPEN FACE HELMET"` in bold, heavy typography.
  * **Description:** Informational paragraph about Studds open face helmets and global presence.
  * **Product Display Area:** A wooden shelf platform showcasing:
    * An open-face helmet model with black/red trim.
    * A stylized helmet wrapped in a national flag design (e.g., French/Russian tricolor theme).
    * Matching riding goggles.
    * Riding gloves placed neatly on the left side of the shelf.

---

## 5. Interactive Media Strip (Video/Thumbnail Bar)

* **Layout:** Full-width dark media gallery divided into equal columns.
* **Background:** Dark charcoal/black (`#1E1E1E`).
* **Content:**
  * Series of 5 dark product/lifestyle thumbnails showing helmet manufacturing, riding gear, and action shots.
  * **Center Highlight Card:** Highlighted with a bright red border (`#E31B23`), featuring a `"WATCH VIDEO"` play button icon overlay.

## 6. Product Sub-Carousel & Filter Section

* **Background:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Navigation:** Left and right chevron arrows (`<` `>`) flanking a horizontal product row.

  * 5 thumbnail slots featuring different helmet models (e.g., *Motocross with Visor Plain*, *Ninja 30*, *DNK*).

  * Heading: `"Motocross With Visor Plain"`

  * **Column 4 (Products Links):** Links list (`Accessories`, `Motocross`, etc.).
  * **Column 5 (Media & Promo):**
