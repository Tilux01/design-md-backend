# UI/UX Design System Specification & Architectural Blueprint

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic

- **Primary Mood:** Modern, friendly, futuristic yet approachable, calm, and conversational AI companion.
- **Brand Archetype:** The "Creator & Sage" hybrid—empowering users through intuitive conversational intelligence, featuring clean pastel aesthetics that lower cognitive load.
- **Visual Density:** Spacious, breathable, and minimalist. Generous white/negative space paired with soft, floating cards to evoke tactile depth (neumorphism-lite combined with modern glassmorphism).
- **Theme Balance:** Exclusively Light Mode implementation featuring ethereal pastel gradients (Mint Green, Sky Cyan, Soft Lilac, and Warm Lemon) set against a crisp, clean canvas. 
- **Texture & Depth Architecture:** Multi-layered depth achieved through soft, diffused drop shadows (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgba(45, 120, 145, 0.08)`) and high-transparency backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`) over subtle radial gradient meshes.

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy

- **Recommended Font Families:** 
  - *Headings & UI:* `Inter`, `-apple-system`, `SF Pro Display`, sans-serif.
  - *Body Copy:* `Inter`, `SF Pro Text`, sans-serif.
  - *Monospace/Code:* `SF Mono`, `Fira Code`, monospace.

### Scale Breakdown
- **Screen Header / Title:** 20px | Weight: 600 (Semi-bold) | Line-height: 24px | Tracking: -0.02em

- **Body Copy:** 13px | Weight: 400 (Regular) | Line-height: 18px | Tracking: normal

- **Container Max-Width:** 393px (Standard iPhone 15 Pro viewport frame simulation).
- **Grid System:** Flexbox & CSS Grid, 12-column internal application gutters with fixed 16px lateral padding.
- **Spacing Scale (8pt Grid):** 
  - `xxs`: 4px
  - `xs`: 8px
  - `sm`: 12px
  - `md`: 16px
  - `lg`: 24px
  - `xl`: 32px
  - `xxl`: 48px

### Section Sequence Breakdown (Home View)
1. **Status Bar & Header:** Time (8:14), Dynamic Island, Battery/Wifi indicators; Navigation bar with Menu (hamburger), Title ("Chatbot"), and Notification Bell icon.
2. **Greeting Block:** Personalized greeting ("Hi Afzal John," / "Let's see what can I do for you ?").
3. **Featured Promotional Banner (Premium Plan):** Full-width card with gradient background, mascot illustration, and "Upgrade Plan" CTA.
4. **Primary Quick Action:** "Start New Chat" pill button with accessory icons (Camera, Gallery, Mic).
5. **Feature Grid (2-column):** "Generate AI Art" and "AI Chat" interactive cards.
6. **Secondary Grid (2-column):** "Recent Chats" and "Explore AI Chatbots".
7. **Fixed Bottom Navigation Bar:** Floating pill container housing Home, Chat, AI Assist toggle, and Settings.

## 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement

### Cards & Containers
- **Border Radius Token:** `24px` for main cards, `16px` for inner elements and buttons, `32px` for bottom navigation dock.
- **Border Width:** 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
- **Drop Shadows:** 
  - *Ambient Shadow:* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 90, 120, 0.08)`
  - *Elevation Shadow (Hover/Active):* `0 16px 32px -6px rgba(30, 90, 120, 0.15)`
- **Inner Padding:** Standard `16px` to `20px` internal breathing room.

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action Button ("Start New Chat"):**
  - Background: Solid `#00E5BC` to `#1DC1F5` horizontal gradient.
  - Border Radius: Full pill (`9999px`).
  - Height: `48px`.
  - Typography: 14px, Bold, `#0A1118`.
- **Ghost / Secondary Buttons:**
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`.
  - Backdrop Blur: `8px`.
- **Icon Buttons (Circular):**
  - Dimensions: `40px × 40px`.
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`.
  - Icon Color: `#1A2B3C`.

### Navigation Architecture
- **Bottom Nav Dock:** Floating pill anchored 24px above bottom safe area. Houses 4 primary destinations with active state highlighted by a glowing cyan/teal pill capsule background.

## 6. Motion Mechanics, 3D & Mobile Gestures

- **Animation Triggers:**
  - Page transitions: Slide-in with fade (Spring physics).
  - Voice assist activation: Radial pulse wave scaling from the central audio orb.
  - Keyboard invocation: Smooth bottom-up translation of input accessory bar and native keyboard layout.
- **Estimated Transition Durations & Easing:**
  - Micro-interactions (hover, press): `150ms` cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1).
  - Page & Modal Transitions: `350ms` spring physics (`damping: 20`, `stiffness: 300`).
  - Voice Orb Pulsing: Infinite loop, `2000ms` ease-in-out alternate.
- **Mobile Native Behaviors & Gestures:**
  - *Pull-to-refresh* on chat histories.
  - *Swipe-to-dismiss* for action sheets and media modals.
  - *Haptic feedback triggers* on primary button taps, voice recognition activation, and toggle switches.
