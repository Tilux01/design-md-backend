# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Modern Dark-Mode SaaS Enterprise Calendar & Productivity Suite with Neumorphic/Glassmorphic Soft Shadows.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-density functional layout balanced by deep obsidian surface layers (`#0f0f11`), generous padding (`16px` to `24px` gutters), and distinct visual grouping via card containerization.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered depth architecture:
  - **Base Canvas**: Dark charcoal grey `#18181b`
  - **Surface 1 (Cards / Containers)**: `#27272a` with subtle 1px light border separations (`#3f3f46`)
  - **Surface 2 (Modals / Popovers)**: `#ffffff` (light mode floating overlays) or elevated `#27272a` with deep ambient drop shadows (`0 16px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)`).
  - **Surface 3 (Floating Action Pills / Navigation)**: Rounded pill containers (`rounded-full`) utilizing `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase category tags, glowing status orbs, semi-transparent glass sheets, floating active pill selectors.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 10px`, font-size `11px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or `\2014`.
  - Calendar / Sparkle (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-blue-500 inline-block"></span>`.
