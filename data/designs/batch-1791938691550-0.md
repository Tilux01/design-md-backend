# Design Specification: Steam Modern Redesign

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Background:** Solid deep charcoal/black (`#0B0B0E`). The design should be edge-to-edge (full screen) with no external borders or window frames.
*   **Primary Accent Color:** Vivid Amber/Orange (`#FF8C00` or `#F26522`) used for active states, highlights, and buttons.
*   **Secondary Color:** Muted Gray (`#8E8E93`) for inactive text and icons.
*   **Corner Radius:** All major containers (cards, search bar, buttons) use a soft rounded corner of `16px`.
*   **Spacing:** Consistent `24px` padding between major sections.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Roboto`.
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:**
    *   Headers: Bold (700)
    *   Body: Regular (400) or Medium (500)
*   **Sizes:**
    *   Logo Text: `18px`
    *   Section Headers: `20px`
    *   Game Titles (Hero): `64px` (Stylized)
    *   Body Text: `14px`

## 3. Sidebar (Left Navigation)
*   **Width:** `240px`.
*   **Logo:** Steam icon (White) followed by "STEAM" text in all caps.
*   **Navigation Items:**
    *   **Store (Active):** Orange vertical indicator bar on the far left. Orange icon and orange text.
    *   **Library, Community, Friends:** Muted gray icons and text.
    *   **Bottom Section:** Settings and Help with muted gray icons.
*   **CTA Button (Bottom):** A dark, rounded rectangle with the text "Check the list and scope" and a small plus icon.

## 4. Top Bar (Header)
*   **Search Bar:** Centered horizontally. A wide, dark-gray pill-shaped input field (`#1C1C1E`). Contains a magnifying glass icon and placeholder text "Search games".
*   **User Profile (Right):**
    *   Notification Bell icon.
    *   Wallet/Balance icon.
    *   Circular Profile Picture (Avatar).
    *   Username "Jesse Wilson" with a small dropdown chevron.

## 5. Hero Section (Featured Game - Stray)
*   **Main Banner:** A large rectangular area featuring the game *Stray*.
    *   **Image Description:** A high-resolution, backgroundless (cut-out) orange tabby cat looking directly forward. The cat is positioned centrally, slightly overlapping the logo.
    *   **Logo:** The "STRAY" stylized wordmark in white, centered at the bottom of the cat image.
    *   **Platform Icons:** Small white icons for Steam/PC and controllers at the bottom left of the banner.
*   **Info Card (Right Side of Hero):**
    *   **Background:** A gradient card transitioning from dark gray to a glowing red/orange at the right edge.
    *   **Content:** Game title "Stray", a short paragraph of white descriptive text.
    *   **Button:** A large orange button labeled "Available now".
    *   **Volume Icon:** A small speaker icon to the right of the button.
    *   **Thumbnails:** Three small rectangular screenshots of gameplay positioned horizontally at the bottom of this info card.

## 6. Special Offers Section (Bottom Row)
*   **Header:** "Special offers" in white, bold text. "Learn more" link in small gray text on the far right.
*   **Game Cards:** Four vertical cards with a `2:3` aspect ratio.
    *   **Card 1 (Hogwarts Legacy):** Background image of the Hogwarts castle shrouded in blue/white mist. The logo "HOGWARTS LEGACY" is centered in gold/white text.
    *   **Card 2 (Call of Duty):** Background image of a soldier in tactical gear looking down. The "CALL OF DUTY: MODERN WARFARE II" logo is centered with green accents.
    *   **Card 3 (FIFA 23):** Background image of a football player (Kylian Mbappé) in a blue kit, mid-action against a white/gray circular graphic. "FIFA 23" logo at the top.
    *   **Card 4 (Mass Effect):** Background image of a planetary landscape with a character in the foreground. "MASS EFFECT LEGENDARY EDITION" logo at the top.

## 7. Visual Effects
*   **Glow:** The "Stray" info card has a subtle outer glow on the right side to create depth.
*   **Active State:** The active menu item (Store) has a subtle orange glow/drop shadow on the text.
*   **Overlays:** Game cards in the bottom row have a slight dark gradient overlay at the bottom to ensure the white logos remain legible.
