# Design Specification: Vinyl Record Music Player

## 1. Layout Overview
*   **Dimensions:** Full-screen mobile viewport (e.g., 393x852px for iPhone 14/15 Pro).
*   **Orientation:** Portrait.
*   **Padding:** 32px horizontal padding for text and controls.
*   **Background:** A linear gradient starting from a muted olive-gold at the top (`#8B7D55`) transitioning to a dark charcoal/brown at the bottom (`#2C261D`).

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** San Francisco (SF Pro Display) or Inter.
*   **Fallback Fonts:** Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Song Title:** 24px, Bold, Color: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
    *   **Artist Name:** 16px, Regular, Color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` (Translucent White).
    *   **Button Text ("PLAY"):** 10px, Bold, Uppercase, Color: `#FFFFFF`, Letter-spacing: 0.5px.

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. The Vinyl Record Player (Top Section)
This is a composite element centered horizontally, occupying the top 60% of the screen.

1.  **The Vinyl Disc:**
    *   **Shape:** Perfect circle.
    *   **Color:** Deep black (`#0A0A0A`) with subtle concentric circular ridges to simulate vinyl texture.
    *   **Shadow:** A soft, large drop shadow (`offset-y: 20px`, `blur: 40px`, `opacity: 0.4`).
2.  **The Tonearm (Stylus):**
    *   **Description:** A metallic silver/grey rod connected to a black rectangular headshell.
    *   **Placement:** The base starts at the top right of the record area. The arm extends diagonally across the right side of the record.
    *   **Rotation:** Approximately 20 degrees inward toward the center of the record.
3.  **The Center Label (Album Art):**
    *   **Shape:** Perfect circle nested inside the vinyl.
    *   **Image Content:** The official "Top Gun: Maverick" soundtrack cover. It features Tom Cruise in flight gear standing in front of a fighter jet with a bright, sun-drenched sky. The "TOP GUN MAVERICK" logo is prominent.
    *   **Rotation:** The image is rotated **180 degrees (upside down)** to simulate a spinning record that has stopped at a specific point.
    *   **Transparency:** The image is a full-color circle with no background (clipped to the circular center of the vinyl).

### B. Text Information (Middle Section)
*   **Alignment:** Left-aligned.
*   **Placement:** Positioned approximately 70% down the screen.
*   **Spacing:** 8px vertical gap between the Title and the Artist name.

### C. Playback Controls (Bottom Section)
*   **Container:** A horizontal row at the bottom of the screen, roughly 60px above the home indicator.
*   **Play Button:**
    *   **Shape:** Capsule/Pill shape.
    *   **Background:** Dark, semi-transparent grey (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`).
    *   **Content:** The word "PLAY" centered.
*   **Navigation Buttons (Previous/Next):**
    *   **Shape:** Smaller pill shapes.
    *   **Icons:** Standard "Skip Back" and "Skip Forward" icons (vertical bar with a triangle).
    *   **Color:** White icons on a semi-transparent dark background.
*   **Home Indicator:** A thin, horizontal white bar (`#FFFFFF`, 30% opacity) centered at the very bottom edge.

## 4. Image Assets & Details

### Image 1: Album Art Label
*   **Description:** A circular crop of the *Top Gun: Maverick* movie poster.
*   **Key Visuals:** A pilot (Tom Cruise) in the lower-left quadrant of the circle, a jet wing cutting diagonally, and the movie logo in the center-right.
*   **Background Status:** Backgroundless (circular mask).
*   **Placement:** Centered perfectly within the black vinyl disc.
*   **Rotation:** 180° (Upside down).

### Image 2: Tonearm/Stylus
*   **Description:** A high-fidelity render of a record player needle.
*   **Key Visuals:** A circular pivot base at the top, a brushed aluminum arm, and a matte black cartridge at the tip.
*   **Background Status:** Backgroundless (PNG with transparency).
*   **Placement:** Overlays the right edge of the vinyl disc.

## 5. UI Elements (Non-Image)
*   **Dynamic Island:** At the top center, there is a black pill-shaped cutout (`#000000`) representing the hardware camera notch.
*   **Interaction State:** The "PLAY" button appears active, while the skip buttons are slightly more muted, suggesting a minimalist aesthetic.
