# Helious Landing Page - Exact Design Specification

## 1. Global Page Structure & Layout
- **Viewport:** 100% width, 100% height (Full-screen, non-scrolling hero viewport).
- **Background Color:** `#0a0a0a` (Deep near-black) with an overlay noise/film grain texture covering the entire viewport.
- **Layout Paradigm:** Absolute/Flexbox layering. The main 3D landscape acts as the central background art, with structural navigation pinned to the top, huge branding typography layered over the bottom-left, descriptive body copy in the bottom-right, and a primary CTA button adjacent to the copy.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Brand Typography (Large Header & Navigation)
- **Font Family:** Geometric/Humanist Sans-Serif with high stroke contrast and rounded terminal details (closely matches typefaces like *Clash Display*, *General Sans*, or *Cabinet Grotesk*).
- **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Clash Display', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;`

### Secondary Body Typography (Bottom-Right Description)
- **Font Family:** Clean, legible neutral sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *SF Pro Text*).
- **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif;`

---

## 3. Component Breakdown & Specifications

### A. Top Navigation Bar
- **Position:** Fixed to top, centered horizontally, floating with a dark translucent rounded pill background (`rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.8)` with a subtle blur/border).
- **Items:** 
  - `About us`
  - `Community`
  - `Residencies`
  - `Courses`
  - `Questions`
- **Typography:** 
  - Font Size: `14px`
  - Font Weight: `400` / Regular
  - Color: `#f4f1ea` (Off-white/Cream)
  - Hover State: Smooth opacity transition to `0.7`.

---

### B. Centerpiece 3D Landscape Art (Background/Hero Image)
- **Asset Description:** A cinematic, stylized 3D render with a rich retro-film texture and warm golden-hour lighting. 
- **Scene Details:** 
  - A floating island made of earthy green grass, exposed brown dirt paths, and rocky outcrops.
  - A lone person sitting on the path, facing away, typing on a glowing laptop.
  - A small wooden cabin/shed to the left with warm light spilling out.
  - Two rustic wooden utility poles with power lines stretching across the upper-left sky.
  - Lush, voluminous golden-sunset clouds dominating the upper-right atmosphere.
- **Placement & Rotation:** 
  - **Position:** Centered-right offset, filling the core visual weight of the screen.
  - **Rotation:** Natural 3D isometric/perspective tilt (no flat 2D rotation applied via CSS; the image itself is pre-rendered at an isometric angle).
  - **Background Status:** The image fills its container natively; edges blend into the dark `#0a0a0a` void using a soft vignette/feather effect.

---

### C. Main Branding (`Helious*`)
- **Position:** Absolute positioned at the bottom-left (`left: 4vw`, `bottom: 4vh`).
- **Text Content:** `Heliou` + large bold `s` + superscript/inline asterisk `*`.
- **Styling:**
  - Font Size: `clamp(6rem, 12vw, 15rem)` (Massive, responsive, overlapping the lower boundary of the floating island).
  - Line Height: `0.85`
  - Font Weight: `600` (Semi-bold)
  - Color: `#f4f1ea` (Cream/Off-white)
  - Letter Spacing: `-0.03em`

---

### D. Descriptive Body Copy
- **Position:** Absolute positioned at the bottom-right (`right: 5vw`, `bottom: 15vh`, max-width: `320px`).
- **Text Content:** 
  > "Helious is a global collective of digital nomads, creatives, and independent makers who choose a different rhythm of life - one driven not by cities, deadlines, and noise, but by curiosity."
- **Styling:**
  - Font Size: `12px` to `13px`
  - Line Height: `1.5`
  - Font Weight: `400`
  - Color: `rgba(244, 241, 234, 0.8)` (Muted cream)

---

### E. Call-to-Action (CTA) Button (`Get started`)
- **Position:** Directly below the body copy at the bottom-right (`right: 5vw`, `bottom: 5vh`).
- **Styling:**
  - **Shape:** Rounded pill button (`border-radius: 50px`).
  - **Background Color:** `#f4f1ea` (Solid cream/off-white).
  - **Text Color:** `#0a0a0a` (Dark text for high contrast).
  - **Padding:** `12px 24px` (or proportional flex spacing).
  - **Font Size:** `14px`
  - **Font Weight:** `500`
  - **Internal Elements:** Text `"Get started"` followed by a circular icon containing a right-pointing arrow (`→`).
  - **Hover State:** Slight brightness increase or scale transform (`scale(1.02)`).
