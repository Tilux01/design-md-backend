## 1. Typography & Hierarchy

### 1.1 Font Families
* **Primary Display & Headings:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `General Sans` (Weights: `600 SemiBold`, `700 Bold`, `800 ExtraBold`).
  * *Fallback Stack:* `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `sans-serif`.
* **Body & UI Elements:** `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Weights: `400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 SemiBold`).
  * *Fallback Stack:* `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`.

### 1.2 Type Scale & Specs
* **Section Eyebrow Tag (`+ Who we are`, etc.):** 
  * Font Size: `11px` (`0.6875rem`)
  * Weight: `700 Bold`

  * Prefix Marker: Bold red `+` character (`#E52E2E`)
* **Hero Headline:**
  * Font Size: ` clamp(42px, 5.5vw, 76px)`
  * Weight: `700 Bold`
  * Line Height: `1.05`
  * Letter Spacing: `-0.035em`

* **Mission Headline ("We build search-first digital systems..."):**
  * Font Size: `clamp(28px, 3.8vw, 54px)`
  * Weight: `600 SemiBold`
  * Line Height: `1.15`

  * Split Color: Primary clause in `#0A0A0A`; Secondary clause (`leaders lead their industries.`) in `#A3A3A3`.
* **Hero Subheading / Feature Title:**
  * Font Size: `18px` (`1.125rem`)

  * Weight: `500 Medium`
  * Text Color: `#FFFFFF`

  * Font Size: `clamp(44px, 4.2vw, 68px)`

  * Label: `14px` (`0.875rem`), `700 Bold`, `#0A0A0A`

## 4. Comprehensive Imagery & Visual Asset Specifications

To replicate the visual presence accurately, use images adhering to the exact compositional and lighting parameters described below:

### 4.1 Hero Background Photograph
* **Visual Subject:** Candid cinematic shot of a design director or developer working intently in a dimly lit studio.
* **Composition:** Medium close-up, angled slightly from behind/side. The subject wears a light/white shirt that captures dynamic motion blur across their torso and arm as they interact with equipment or screens.
* **Lighting & Tone:** Deep moody shadows, low-key lighting, cool gray and charcoal tones with soft specular highlights along shoulder and hair contours. Soft, heavy directional lens blur toward the corners.

### 4.2 Gallery Image 1 (Red Monochrome Male Portrait)
* **Visual Subject:** Close-up headshot of a male with short dark hair and a trimmed beard/mustache.
* **Placement & Size:** First image card from the left; aspect ratio `3:4`, rounded corners (`16px`).
* **Lighting & Style:** High-contrast dramatic neon-magenta and crimson side-lighting. Direct, piercing gaze toward the camera. Deep black shadows dominate the right side of the face, casting a bold, artistic fashion-editorial mood.

### 4.3 Gallery Image 2 (Motion Blurred Warm Female Portrait)
* **Visual Subject:** Frontal portrait of a young woman with pale skin, blue/hazel eyes, and light brown/auburn hair.
* **Placement & Size:** Second card from the left; aspect ratio `3:4`, rounded corners (`16px`).
* **Lighting & Style:** Strong camera pan / shutter drag blur effect creating horizontal smearing around the temples, ears, and hair edges, while keeping the eyes, nose, and lips in sharp clarity. Warm incandescent amber backlight contrasted with neutral cool front fill.

### 4.4 Gallery Image 3 (Double Exposure Yellow Shirt Male Portrait)
* **Visual Subject:** Side/three-quarter profile of a bald man with a short beard wearing an unbuttoned mustard-yellow corduroy or linen shirt over a white crewneck tee.
* **Placement & Size:** Third card from the left; aspect ratio `3:4`, rounded corners (`16px`).
* **Lighting & Style:** Dynamic horizontal motion ghosting / double exposure trailing toward the right edge. Clean, high-key studio background (pale neutral gray-cyan), bright warm directional lighting hitting the collar and face.

### 4.5 Gallery Image 4 (Abstract Amber Silhouette Figure)
* **Visual Subject:** Dynamic full-body or half-body silhouette of a dancer or moving figure draped in flowing garments.
* **Placement & Size:** Fourth card from the left; aspect ratio `3:4`, rounded corners (`16px`).
* **Lighting & Style:** Saturated warm golden-amber and fire-orange backlighting. Extreme motion blur transforming limbs and clothing into ethereal energetic light trails. Deep brown/black shadows form the core silhouette.

### 4.6 Navigation Avatar Thumbnail
* **Visual Subject:** Tiny circular portrait (`22px × 22px`, `border-radius: 50%`) of a smiling woman with glasses and neutral background. Placed inside the "Book A Intro" pill button.

## 5. Micro-Interactions & Hover States

1. **Navigation Links:**
   * Transition: `color 0.2s ease`.
   * State: Shifts from `#E5E5E5` to `#FFFFFF` on hover with a subtle `1px` white dot indicator below.
2. **CTA Buttons:**
   * Primary Button ("View Projects"): Shifts from `#E52E2E` to `#FF4545`, scale `1.02` on active tap.
   * Secondary Button ("Reach Out"): Shifts from `#FFFFFF` to `#F0F0F0`, text remains solid `#0A0A0A`.
   * "Book A Intro" Header Pill: Subtle outline border highlight (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px rgba(255,255,255,0.3)`).
3. **Gallery Cards:**
   * Hover: Scale up subtly by `1.03` with smooth cubic-bezier transition (`transform: scale(1.03); transition: transform 0.4s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
4. **"About The Studio" Link:**
   * Arrow shifts right by `+4px` on hover (`transform: translateX(4px)`).
