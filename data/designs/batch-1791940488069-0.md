# Design Specification: Music Discovery & Player App

## 1. Global Styles
*   **Background:** A very light lavender-grey gradient or solid color (`#F4F7FF`).
*   **Primary Accent Color:** Vivid Blue (`#007BFF`) used for active states and primary buttons.
*   **Secondary Accent Colors:** Soft Pink (`#FF4D97`), Teal (`#4DFFEB`), and Soft Purple.
*   **Shadows:** Soft, high-spread "Glassmorphism" shadows. Use `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)` with a blur radius of 20px-40px.
*   **Corner Radii:** 
    *   Main Cards: 24px
    *   Buttons: 50% (Circular)
    *   Small UI Elements: 12px

## 2. Typography
*   **Headings (Serif):** 
    *   *Font:* `Playfair Display` or `DM Serif Display`.
    *   *Fallback:* `Georgia`, `serif`.
    *   *Usage:* "Discover.", "All Time Favourite.", "New Songs.", "Love me, like you!".
*   **UI/Body Text (Sans-Serif):** 
    *   *Font:* `Montserrat` or `Inter`.
    *   *Fallback:* `System-ui`, `sans-serif`.
    *   *Usage:* Navigation tabs, artist names, time stamps, ratings.

## 4. Screen B: Music Player (Right)

### Navigation
*   **Back Arrow:** Top-left, simple thin chevron.
*   **Profile Image:** Top-right, same as Screen A.

### Main Visual (Center Piece)
*   **Primary Image:** A large circle (approx 60% of screen width). 
    *   **Content:** A close-up portrait of a woman's face. The lighting is experimental: the left side of her face is in natural/cool tones, while the right side is bathed in a vibrant lime-green light.
    *   **Placement:** Centered horizontally, positioned in the upper half of the screen.
*   **Progress Ring:** A thin, light-grey circular track surrounding the primary image. 
    *   **Active Progress:** A teal-colored arc following the track.
    *   **Playhead:** A small white circle with a blue border indicating the current position.
*   **Audio Visualizer:** Located to the right of the main image. A vertical stack of horizontal teal bars of varying lengths, representing a waveform.

### Secondary Visual
*   **Image:** A smaller circle (approx 80px) overlapping the bottom-right edge of the main image area.
    *   **Content:** A profile-view (side view) of a woman with heavy pink makeup and pink studio lighting. Background is solid pink.
    *   **Placement:** Bottom right of the main visual, slightly overlapping the white background.

### Song Details & Controls
*   **Time Stamps:** "04:39" (Left, Grey) and "01:39" (Right, Grey) positioned around the progress ring.
*   **Title:** "Love me, like you!" (Centered, Serif, Bold, ~24px).
*   **Artist:** "by Enrique Iglesias." (Centered, Sans-serif, Grey, ~14px).
*   **Control Bar:**
    *   **Previous/Next:** Thin grey icons.
    *   **Play/Pause Button:** Large circular blue button (`#007BFF`) with a white "Pause" (two vertical bars) icon. It has a soft blue glow/shadow.
    *   **Rating:** Far right, a small outline star and "4.6" text.
