# Design Specification: Movieto Landing Page

## 1. General Overview
*   **Theme:** Dark, modern, cinematic streaming service.
*   **Canvas:** Full-screen (100vw, 100vh), edge-to-edge layout. No external borders or shadows.
*   **Primary Color Palette:**
    *   **Accent Pink:** `#FF007A` (Logo, Active Link, CTA Button)
    *   **Deep Navy/Background:** `#0F0F1E` (The base layer behind the posters)
    *   **Secondary Blue:** `#242A4A` (Secondary button, subtle overlays)
    *   **Text White:** `#FFFFFF` (Headings)
    *   **Text Grey:** `#B0B0B0` (Sub-headings and inactive nav)

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Montserrat** or **Inter**. A modern, geometric sans-serif with high legibility.
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `System-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:**
    *   **Bold (700):** Main Headline, Logo text.
    *   **Semi-Bold (600):** Navigation items, Button text.
    *   **Regular (400):** Sub-headline.

## 3. Layout Breakdown

### A. Navigation Bar (Header)
*   **Position:** Top, fixed or absolute, spanning 100% width.
*   **Padding:** `40px` horizontal, `30px` vertical.
*   **Logo (Left):**
    *   **Icon:** A stylized 'M' formed by two thick, rounded pink strokes.
    *   **Text:** "Movieto" in white, bold sans-serif, positioned directly under or to the right of the icon.
*   **Nav Links (Center):**
    *   Items: Home, Movies, Series, Category, Pricing, About.
    *   **Active State (Home):** Pink text (`#FF007A`) with a short, thick pink underline.
    *   **Inactive State:** White text with low opacity (~80%).
*   **Utility Icons (Right):**
    *   Search Icon (Magnifying glass).
    *   User Icon (Circle with a torso).
    *   Menu Icon (Three horizontal lines/Hamburger).
    *   *Style:* Thin white strokes, spaced roughly `20px` apart.

### B. Hero Section (Center-Left)
*   **Headline:** 
    *   "Unlimited movies, TV shows, and more"
    *   **Style:** White, Bold, `48px` to `64px` font size.
    *   **Alignment:** Left-aligned.
*   **Sub-headline:**
    *   "Find the latest and greatest movies and shows all available on Movieto."
    *   **Style:** Greyish-white, Regular, `18px` font size. `20px` margin-top from headline.
*   **Call to Action (CTA) Buttons:**
    *   **Button 1 (Get Started):** Solid Pink (`#FF007A`), white text, fully rounded corners (pill shape).
    *   **Button 2 (Show Plans):** Dark semi-transparent navy (`rgba(36, 42, 74, 0.6)`), white text, fully rounded corners.
    *   **Layout:** Horizontal row, `15px` gap.

### C. The Background Movie Grid
The background consists of a dense, overlapping grid of movie posters tilted at an angle.
*   **Global Transformation:** The entire grid is rotated approximately **-15 to -20 degrees** (counter-clockwise) to create a dynamic "falling" effect.
*   **Overlay:** A dark radial gradient overlay (Center: transparent, Edges: `#0F0F1E`) is applied over the posters to ensure text readability.
*   **Poster Details (Descriptions for Assets):**
    1.  **Top Left:** *Prey for the Devil* - Close up of a face with hands over eyes.
    2.  **Top Center:** *Smile* - The iconic creepy smiling face, red/dark tones.
    3.  **Top Right:** *Mr. Robot* - Red text logo on a dark background.
    4.  **Middle Left:** *Wednesday* - Jenna Ortega in a dark, moody setting.
    5.  **Middle Right:** *Dune* - Group shot of the cast in desert tones (beige/blue).
    6.  **Bottom Left:** *M3GAN* - Close up of the doll's face with a bow.
    7.  **Bottom Right:** *Aquaman* - Jason Momoa underwater with a trident.
    8.  **Bottom Center:** *John Wick* - Keanu Reeves in a suit, dark blue lighting.
*   **Image Properties:**
    *   **Backgroundless:** No, these are full rectangular posters.
    *   **Aspect Ratio:** Standard 2:3 vertical poster ratio.
    *   **Spacing:** Tight packing, roughly `10px` gap between posters.
    *   **Filter:** All background images should have a `brightness(0.4)` filter applied to prevent them from clashing with the white text.

## 4. Interaction & Effects
*   **Button Hover:** The "Get Started" button should have a slight scale-up (`scale(1.05)`) or a glow effect.
*   **Nav Hover:** Links should transition from white to pink.
*   **Background Parallax:** (Optional for clone) A very subtle movement of the poster grid when the mouse moves adds to the premium feel.

## 5. Technical Specifications for Implementation
*   **Container:** `display: flex` for the main layout.
*   **Background Grid:** Use `display: grid` with `grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fill, minmax(200px, 1fr))`. Apply `transform: rotate(-15deg) scale(1.2)` to the container to ensure no white gaps appear at the corners of the screen.
*   **Z-Index:** 
    *   Background Grid: `z-index: 1`
    *   Dark Overlay: `z-index: 2`
    *   Content (Text/Nav): `z-index: 3`
