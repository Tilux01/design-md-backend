# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Scenic Landscape Illustration / Fine Art Digital Painting Landscape.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High visual richness balanced by natural atmospheric gradients. Generous negative space dedicated to the dynamic sunset sky and tranquil turquoise water surface.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered depth through atmospheric perspective: Foreground alpine meadow and wildflowers (Elevation Level 3), midground lake and pine-studded shoreline (Elevation Level 2), background distant peaks and sunlit high-altitude ranges (Elevation Level 1 / Canvas).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase alpine badges, ambient golden hour glow orbs, organic glassmorphism overlays, sunset reflection highlights.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `0.75rem`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.15)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-amber-400 inline-block"></span>`.
