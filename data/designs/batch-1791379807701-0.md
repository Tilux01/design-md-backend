# UI/UX Specification & Design Document

## 1. Overview & Layout Strategy
This document outlines the complete specifications to recreate the provided digital showcase for the **Nike Air Max 2017 Running Shoes**. 

* **Viewport & Canvas:** Full-screen immersive layout (100vw, 100vh). The entire viewport serves as the stage, removing the rounded card container to let the design breathe edge-to-edge.
* **Background Architecture:** 
  * A deep, immersive radial and linear gradient spanning from dark teal (`#063b4c`) in the shadows to a luminous, vibrant turquoise (`#1ab3c9`, `#26c6da`) in the center and highlights.
  * A subtle, blurred macro photography or abstract background texture is layered underneath the gradient to give depth (simulating a futuristic glass HUD overlay).
* **Grid & Alignment:** A floating, asymmetrical layout where typography, navigation, and interactive widgets anchor off a central product hero shot.

## 3. Typography & Fallback Strategy

The UI uses modern, geometric, and functional sans-serif typefaces to achieve a futuristic athletic look.

### Primary Font Family: **"Montserrat"** (Google Fonts)
* **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`

#### Typography Hierarchy:
1. **Hero Title ("AIR MAX 2017"):**
   * **Font Weight:** 900 (Black / Ultra-Bold)
   * **Style:** Uppercase, tracked out.
   * **Background Watermark ("NIKE"):** Giant uppercase stencil/bold font (`font-weight: 900`, opacity: `0.15`, letter-spacing: `4px`).
2. **Section Header ("RUNNING-SHOES"):**
   * **Font Weight:** 700 (Bold)
   * **Transform:** Uppercase, rotated 90 degrees vertically (`writing-mode: vertical-rl` or standard horizontal stacked depending on precise breakpoint).
3. **Navigation & Metadata ("MEN", "WOMEN", "CASUAL", "SPORT"):**
   * **Font Weight:** 600 (Semi-Bold)
   * **Size:** 12px – 14px
   * **Letter Spacing:** Wide (`2px`)
4. **Interactive Action ("BUY NOW"):**
   * **Font Weight:** 700 (Bold)
   * **Size:** 16px
   * **Transform:** Uppercase, tracked out (`letter-spacing: 1.5px`)

## 5. Animations & Motion Design

To achieve the fluid, high-tech interface feel present in the design, implement the following keyframe animations:

1. **Entrance Float (Shoe Hero):**
   * *Animation:* `float 6s ease-in-out infinite`
   * *Keyframes:* translateY shifts between `0px` and `-15px`, combined with a subtle rotational tilt (`±1deg`).
2. **Glowing Pulse (CTA & Accents):**
   * *Animation:* `pulseGlow 3s infinite`
   * *Effect:* The outer stroke of the "BUY NOW" button gently pulses its box-shadow spread and opacity to draw the user's eye.
3. **Background Parallax:**
   * Subtle mouse-move parallax effect on the giant watermark typography (`NIKE`) moving inverse to cursor displacement.
4. **UI Fade-in on Load:**
   * Elements stagger-fade in from bottom-to-top with a cubic-bezier easing curve (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`) and a duration of `0.8s`.
