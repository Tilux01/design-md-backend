# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Fintech / Enterprise SaaS Glassmorphism (Sophisticated, high-tech, sleek financial ecosystem).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio in the hero framing; balanced density in the top navigation bar. Strong vertical centering focus with a 3D-oriented focal point.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered depth using semi-transparent dark gradients (`rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`), crisp thin borders with white/purple highlights, blurred backdrop filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), and radial ambient lighting glows (gold/yellow and purple accents).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Floating frosted glass pills (`backdrop-blur-md`), ambient warm-to-cool radial background gradients, high-end 3D metallic token assets, subtle geometric grid background matrix.
- **Pill Navbar Specification**: Padding: `6px 20px`, Font-size: `13px`, Font-weight: `500`, Tracking: `normal`, Border-radius: `9999px`, Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, Backdrop-filter: `blur(12px)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Dropdown Arrow (▾) -> Use `&#9662;` or CSS pseudo-element.
  - Brand Logo Diamond (◆) -> Use `&#9670;` or inline SVG path.
