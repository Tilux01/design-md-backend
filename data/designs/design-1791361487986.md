# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Futuristic Minimalist E-Commerce (Cyber-Industrial High-Fashion Editorial).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Extremely high whitespace ratio. The canvas relies on a clean, light polygonal/geometric background relief paired with stark obsidian-black structural framing and high-contrast typographic hierarchy.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered glassmorphic product specification cards with subtle inner border strokes (`1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.4)`), soft drop shadows (`0 12px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`), and floating interactive badges.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-contrast geometric chassis, backdrop-blur frosted glass overlays, subtle ambient drop shadows, precise typographic tracking on pill categories.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#0B0B0C` (inverse) or `rgba(255,255,255,0.7)` with `1px solid #E5E5EA`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Rotation/360 Icon (360°) -> Use inline SVG vector or `&#8635;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
