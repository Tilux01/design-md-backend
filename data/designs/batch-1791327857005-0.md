# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Golden Hour Cinematic Nature / Organic Editorial Landscape.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High ambient whitespace, immersive full-viewport photographic background with atmospheric warmth, shallow depth-of-field foreground framing.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Natural planar depth achieved via camera depth-of-field (bokeh blur on foreground flora), soft warm sunlit volumetric layers over rolling terrain.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Cinematic depth-of-field layering, high-tracking uppercase pill badges, golden hour volumetric lighting gradients, glassmorphism overlays.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `0.75rem`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(243, 186, 78, 0.15)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-amber-400 inline-block"></span>`.
