# Canon Lens Website Design Specification

## Global Styles
- **Background Color**: Dark charcoal/off-black (`#161616` to `#111111`)
- **Primary Text Color**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Secondary/Muted Text Color**: Light gray (`#A0A0A0`)
- **Accent/Highlight Color**: Warm beige/gold (`#C4A47C`)
- **Font Family**: Sans-serif, modern geometric (e.g., Inter, Roboto, or Helvetica)

---

## 1. Header & Navigation (Top)

### Layout
- **Position**: Fixed top, full width, generous padding (approx. 40px left/right).
- **Alignment**: Flexbox space-between (Logo left, Nav center/right, Search far right).

### Components
1. **Logo ("LONS")**:
   - **Text**: White, bold sans-serif.
   - **Icon**: Replaces the letter 'O' with a camera aperture/shutter symbol inside a circle.

2. **Navigation Links**:
   - **Items**: Home, Products, Gallery, About Us
   - **Font Style**: Regular weight, white text.
   - **Active State ("Home")**: Features a thin white underline indicator directly beneath the word.

3. **Search Icon**:
   - **Symbol**: Minimalist magnifying glass outline located on the far right.

---

## 2. Hero Section (Left Column)

### Layout
- **Position**: Left-aligned, vertically centered within the main content area.
- **Max Width**: Approx. 35% of the viewport.

### Content Elements
1. **Main Heading**:
   - **Text**: "Canon Lens"
   - **Font Size**: Large display heading (approx. 48px - 56px).
   - **Font Weight**: Semi-bold/Bold, pure white.

2. **Body Copy / Subtitle**:
   - **Text**: "For sharper shots, more detail and more zoom. feel the millions of colors in your eyes"
   - **Color**: Muted warm beige / light gray (`#C4A47C`).
   - **Line Height**: 1.5 for readability.

---

## 3. Product Display & Gallery (Center & Right)

### Layout
- **Composition**: Asymmetrical floating product cards and callout lines over a dark photographic backdrop.

### Elements

1. **Top Right Product (Camera with Lens)**:
   - **Visual**: A black DSLR body (Sony branding visible) viewed from a top-down/angled perspective, equipped with a Canon lens.
   - **Callout Annotation**:
     - **Line**: Thin white/gray connector line pointing from the lens barrel to the text block.
     - **Text**: 
       - Heading: "Canon Lens" (Bold, white)
       - Subtext: "EF 40mm\n1:2.8\nMade in Japan" (Small, gray)

2. **Bottom Right Product (Standalone Lens)**:
   - **Visual**: A standalone camera lens viewed from the front/top angle.
   - **Callout Annotation**:
     - **Line**: Thin white/gray angled connector line pointing from the lens element.
     - **Text**: 
       - Heading: "Canon Lens" (Bold, white)
       - Subtext: "EF 50mm\n1:1.4\nMade in Japan" (Small, gray)

3. **Bottom Left Carousel / Product Preview**:
   - **Visual**: A dark grey card housing a blue-tinted macro lens shot, flanked by partial adjacent cards suggesting a carousel.
   - **Text Details on Card**: "77 mm\n24mm\n1:1.4 L"
   - **Pagination Dots**: Three small dots centered below the card (Middle dot is white/active, side dots are gray/inactive).

---

## Spacing & Alignment
- **Margins**: Generous whitespace surrounding the content to create a high-end, minimalist luxury feel.
- **Z-Index Layering**: Background ambient shadow effects give depth to the floating product elements.
