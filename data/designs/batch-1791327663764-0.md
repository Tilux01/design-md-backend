# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist / High-End Architectural Interior Studio.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with strong typographic hierarchy, strict geometric alignment, and restrained, sophisticated color blocking.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, monolithic architectural surfaces utilizing subtle material contrasts (stone textures, wood grain, bouclé upholstery) over flat obsidian black backgrounds.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase typographic labels, monochromatic dark UI framing, architectural grid alignment, precise color hex annotations.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `4px 12px`, Font-size: `11px`, Font-weight: `500`, Tracking: `0.1em`, Border-radius: `9999px`, Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="inline-block w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-current"></span>`.

      ))}
