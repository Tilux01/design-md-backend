# Design Specification: Minimalist Automotive Media Interface

## 1. General Theme & Visual Language
*   **Style:** Minimalist Glassmorphism / Neumorphism hybrid.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Background:** Linear gradient from top-center `#C4C8CE` to bottom-center `#FFFFFF`.
    *   **Accent Color:** `#E67E22` (Soft Orange) used for the progress bar active state.
    *   **Text Color:** Primary `#1A1A1A` (Near Black), Secondary/Muted `#7A7A7A` (Medium Grey).
    *   **Surface:** Semi-transparent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`) with a backdrop-filter blur of `20px`.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Inter** or **SF Pro Display**.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Greeting ("Hi, Rosen"):** 14px, Medium weight, `#7A7A7A`.
    *   **Clock ("12:00"):** 14px, Semi-bold, `#1A1A1A`.
    *   **Song Title ("Die For You"):** 28px, Bold, `#1A1A1A`, Letter-spacing: `-0.5px`.
    *   **Artist Name ("Justin Bieber"):** 16px, Regular, `#7A7A7A`.
    *   **Timestamps:** 12px, Monospace or Medium weight Sans, `#1A1A1A`.

## 3. Layout Structure

### A. Top Status Bar
*   **Placement:** Fixed at the top, full width, padding: `20px 40px`.
*   **Left Section:** User profile icon (Circular, 32px diameter) followed by "Hi, Rosen".
*   **Center Section:** Digital clock ("12:00").
*   **Right Section:** Status icons (5G Signal, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Battery). Icons should be thin-stroke (approx 1.5px weight).

### B. Album Art Carousel (Center)
This is the focal point of the UI, using a 3D perspective layout.
*   **Active Item (Center):** 
    *   **Image:** A high-resolution portrait of Justin Bieber wearing a blue baseball cap and a dark green sweatshirt.
    *   **Dimensions:** 280px x 280px.
    *   **Rotation:** 0 degrees (flat).
    *   **Effect:** Subtle drop shadow (`0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`).
*   **Side Items (Left & Right):**
    *   **Rotation:** The images are rotated on the Y-axis (approx 45 degrees) facing the center.
    *   **Perspective:** `1000px`.
    *   **Opacity:** Gradually decreases from 0.8 to 0.2 as they move further from the center.
    *   **Images (Left to Right):** 
        1. Pink/White abstract ("transparent").
        2. Purple/Blue abstract with Chinese characters.
        3. White background with a group of people sitting on a green object.
        4. [Center - Justin Bieber].
        5. Blue/Purple "IF YOU" text with a hand graphic.
        6. Light blue sky with a person sitting on a ledge.
        7. White background, male portrait ("Beautiful").

### C. Playback Controls
*   **Progress Bar:** 
    *   Width: 60% of screen.
    *   Track: Light grey (`#E0E0E0`), 4px height, rounded.
    *   Progress: Orange (`#E67E22`), 4px height.
    *   Handle: No visible handle (clean look).
*   **Buttons:** 
    *   Layout: Flexbox, centered.
    *   Icons: Heart (Outline), Previous, Play/Pause (Double bar), Next, Shuffle.
    *   Stroke: 2px weight, `#1A1A1A`.
    *   Reflections: Each icon has a faint vertical reflection below it (50% height, 0.1 opacity).

### D. Bottom Navigation Bar
*   **Background:** A 3D "stage" or "road" effect. A trapezoidal white shape with a soft gradient to simulate a floor.
*   **Left Element:** 
    *   **Image:** A white luxury sedan (Cadillac Lyriq style), angled 45 degrees facing right. 
    *   **Details:** Backgroundless (PNG), high gloss, soft contact shadow on the "floor."
*   **Icon Strip:** 
    *   Centered on a semi-transparent glass pill-shape container.
    *   Icons: Car silhouette, Steering wheel, Fan/AC, Music Note (Active), Speaker/Volume, 3D Cube (App drawer).
    *   Icon Color: Dark grey/Black.

## 4. Image Descriptions for Assets

1.  **User Avatar:** A small circular crop of a person’s face (Rosen).
2.  **Main Album Art:** Justin Bieber, close-up portrait, blue cap, dark green crewneck, neutral grey studio background.
3.  **Car Asset:** A white modern electric SUV/Sedan. The image must be a transparent PNG. It is placed on the far left of the bottom navigation "shelf."
4.  **App Drawer Icon (Far Right):** A 3D isometric cube with a glowing orange/gold core, sitting on the right side of the bottom shelf.

## 5. Technical Implementation Notes
*   **Reflections:** Use `webkit-box-reflect: below 2px linear-gradient(transparent, rgba(255,255,255,0.1))` for the album art and playback icons.
*   **Transitions:** When switching songs, the carousel should use a `cubic-bezier` transition for the Y-axis rotation and scaling to ensure a smooth "flipping" motion.
*   **Blur:** The background behind the album carousel has a very faint, large-radius white glow to separate the foreground from the grey-gradient background.
