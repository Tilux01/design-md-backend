# Design Specification: MEWS Unfold 2025 Hero Page

A complete, full-screen technical design specification document for recreating the **MEWS Unfold 2025** event landing page hero section.

---

## 1. Overview & Visual Aesthetic

- **Theme**: Avant-garde, modern luxury event design blending organic 3D spatial art with clean Swiss/neo-grotesk typography.
- **Atmosphere**: Ethereal, soft daylight aesthetic using warm pastel gradients (lavender, blush pink, coral, peach) contrasted against bold, crisp white typography and glassmorphic UI elements.
- **Canvas Setup**: 
  - Full-screen viewport (`width: 100vw; height: 100vh; overflow: hidden;`).
  - Edge-to-edge layout without outer frames, borders, or presentation device mockups.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Gradients

The background is a smooth, diffuse radial/mesh gradient that feels bright, ambient, and multi-dimensional:
- **Top-Left (Muted Lavender/Lilac)**: `#CEACD5`

background: radial-gradient(circle at 75% 60%, #f7d5d6 0%, #ecd0dc 35%, #e4bdd3 65%, #ceacd5 100%);

## 4. Key Graphic Asset: 3D Unfolding Spherical Ribbon

### 4.1 Visual Description
- **Object**: A complex, multi-layered 3D parametric spherical vortex sculpted from spiraling ribbons/petals that appear to unfold outward from a central core.
- **Form & Curvature**: The left side displays tighter, upward-arching spiral folds, while the center and right reveal deep layered pockets that transition outward into delicate ribbon tails drooping organically toward the bottom right.
- **Coloration of the 3D Render**:
  - **Outer Left Ribbons**: Saturated fuchsia / vivid magenta (`#E42F7B` to `#EB5297`).
  - **Core Ribbons**: Radiant warm peach and soft coral pink (`#FA8775` to `#F67086`).
  - **Right Inner Petals**: Luminescent golden apricot and warm amber-orange (`#FB9B65` to `#FFAF7B`).
  - **Tips / Trailing Tails**: Pale pastel blush, slightly translucent frosted glass/nacre sheen.
- **Finish**: Smooth matte-satin finish with soft subsurface scattering (SSS) and subtle ambient occlusion within the crevices.

### 4.2 Placement, Dimensions & Layering
- **Format**: High-resolution backgroundless transparent PNG or WebP (32-bit RGBA with smooth antialiased alpha edges).
- **Dimensions**: Scaled to occupy approximately `55vw` to `60vw` (roughly `580px` to `680px` square bounding box on standard 1080p).
- **Positioning**: 
  - Centered horizontally (`left: 50%; transform: translateX(-50%);`).
  - Centered vertically with a slight upward bias (`top: 45%; transform: translate(-50%, -50%);`).
- **Layer Stacking (Z-Index)**:
  - **Layer 1 (Bottom)**: Background gradient canvas.
  - **Layer 2 (Middle)**: 3D Unfolding Sphere Graphic.
  - **Layer 3 (Top)**: Hero Typography ("Unfold") with crisp overlay.

## 6. Interaction & Motion Guidelines

1. **Hover on Navigation Capsule**:
   - Navigation links transition from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` to `#FFFFFF` on hover.
   - White CTA button triggers subtle scale `transform: scale(1.02);` and soft shadow lift.

2. **Hover on Bottom Center Pill**:
   - Background shifts from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.28)`.
   - Smooth 200ms ease-out transition.

3. **Ambient Page Dynamics (Optional Micro-animations)**:
   - **3D Sphere**: Slow, breathing floating rotation (`±2deg` rotation, `±6px` vertical float on an 8-second looping bezier).
   - **Background Mesh**: Subtle hue shift animation oscillating between magenta and warm champagne hues.
