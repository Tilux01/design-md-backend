# Design Specification: GoMart Mobile App Concept

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Color Palette
- **Primary Background (Scene):** Soft neutral gradient transitioning from light gray-blue to pale lavender (`#E8ECF2` to `#DCDFEB`).
- **App Canvas Background (Internal):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Primary Text Color:** Deep Charcoal / Off-Black (`#111111`).
- **Secondary Text Color:** Muted Gray (`#707070` or `#8A8A8A`).
- **Accent / Interactive Purple:** Vibrant Purple (`#7A5CFA`).
- **Dark Surface (Buttons/Nav):** Deep Near-Black (`#1A1A1A`).

### Typography (Font Families & Fallbacks)
- **Primary Heading & Body Font:** **Inter** (Fallback: `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`).
  - *Welcome Headings:* Bold (weight 700), clean geometric grotesque style.
  - *Body Text:* Regular (weight 400), highly legible at small sizes.

---

## 2. Screen 1: Welcome / Onboarding Screen

### Layout Structure
- **Container:** Standard iOS iPhone mock frame with rounded corners and a dynamic island notch at the top.
- **Top Section (Collage Grid):** 
  - A dynamic, asymmetric mosaic grid of product photography overlapping each other with rounded corners and subtle shadows.
  - **Fade Effect:** The bottom of the collage seamlessly fades from opaque images into the pure white background of the lower half.
- **Bottom Content Section:** Centered alignment.
  - **Heading:** "Welcome to GoMart" (Bold, ~28px, `#111111`).
  - **Subtext:** "Your go-to destination for smart, fast, and hassle-free shopping" (Regular, ~13px, Muted Gray, max-width constrained for 2-line wrap).
  - **Primary CTA Button:** 
    - Full-width pill button ("Next").
    - Background: Deep Near-Black (`#1A1A1A`).
    - Text: Pure White, Medium weight.
  - **iOS Home Indicator:** Thin horizontal pill bar at the absolute bottom.

### Image Assets (Collage Grid)
*Note: All images feature modern consumer electronics and lifestyle gadgets presented on clean, minimalist backgrounds.*
1. **Top-Left Image:** Top-down angle of a dark tech gadget surface.
2. **Top-Right Image:** Smartwatch with a dark teal/green perforated sport band, displaying a digital clock interface ("12:38", weather, notification icons).
3. **Center/Left Image:** Close-up of a gray electronic housing featuring two prominent glowing orange rotary knobs/dials.
4. **Bottom-Right Image:** Stylish sunglasses resting on a reflective/light surface.
- *Styling:* Arranged with slight rotations and overlaps, clipped inside the top container with a gradient fade mask at the bottom edge.

---

## 3. Screen 2: AI Assistant & Home Screen

### Layout Structure
- **Container:** Matching iPhone frame with dynamic island and status bar (Wi-Fi, 5G, Battery icons).
- **Header Section:**
  - **Left:** User avatar thumbnail (circular, featuring a smiling man's portrait) alongside greeting text: "Good Morning," (small, gray) and "Jhonathon Trot" (bold, dark).
  - **Right:** Shopping bag icon housed in a clean circular outline button.
- **Main Hero Section (Vertical Center):**
  - **Heading:** "Need anything?" (Large, bold, ~32px).
  - **Subtext:** "Our smart AI assistant helps you find exactly what you need—faster and easier than ever." (Centered, Muted Gray, ~13px).
- **AI Search Input Bar:**
  - Pill-shaped container with a subtle gray border/fill.
  - **Left Icon:** Camera outline icon (`Ask Anything...` placeholder text).
  - **Right Button:** Solid purple circular button containing a vertical audio waveform/mic icon (`#7A5CFA`).
- **Quick Suggestion Pills (Horizontal Scrollable Row):**
  - Rounded tags with light gray backgrounds and dark icons:
    1. 🎁 "Best mothers day gift"
    2. 👤 "Independent Day"
    3. 🏀 "Sports"
- **Bottom Navigation Bar:**
  - Floating pill-shaped bottom bar with a dark background (`#1A1A1A`).
  - **Icons (Left to Right):**
    1. Search / Magnifying Glass (Active state highlighted in white with a white circular background pill).
    2. Camera / Frame icon.
    3. Compass / Planet ring icon.
    4. Two-ticket / Pass icon.
  - **iOS Home Indicator:** Positioned cleanly below the navigation dock.

## 4. Presentation Branding Elements (External to Apps)
- **Top-Left Tag:** "Orbix Studio" inside a white pill badge with a subtle shadow.
- **Top-Right Tags:** "Mobile App" pill badge and "2025" year badge.
- **Footer Branding:** Centered bottom pill badge containing white text: "Hello@orbix.studio" set against a dark rounded container.
