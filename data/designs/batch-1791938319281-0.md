# Design Specification: STREAMVERSE Web Interface

## 1. General Theme & Layout
*   **Background Color:** Deep Charcoal/Black (`#0a0b0d`).
*   **Primary Accent Color:** Electric Blue (`#007bff`) used for buttons and active states.
*   **Text Color:** Primary: White (`#ffffff`); Secondary/Metadata: Light Grey (`#a0a0a0`).
*   **Layout Style:** Full-width, responsive vertical scroll. Content is organized in horizontal rows with consistent padding (approx. 60px on left/right).
*   **Border Radius:** Standardized at `8px` for all thumbnails and buttons.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Roboto` (Sans-serif).
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Heading Font:** `Montserrat` or `Poppins` (Bold/Semi-bold) for section titles.
*   **Specific Styles:**
    *   **Hero Title:** 48px - 64px, Uppercase, Extra Bold.
    *   **Section Titles:** 24px, Semi-bold.
    *   **Metadata (Year/Genre):** 12px, Regular.
    *   **Button Text:** 14px, Bold, Uppercase.

## 3. Header Navigation
*   **Position:** Fixed top, semi-transparent black background with a slight blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(10px)`).
*   **Logo (Left):** "STREAMVERSE" in white, bold, uppercase. To the left is a play-button icon (blue triangle inside a white circle outline).
*   **Nav Links (Center-Left):** "Home", "TV Shows", "Movies", "New & Popular", "My List". Font size 14px, white text.
*   **Search Bar (Right):** Pill-shaped, dark grey background. Contains a magnifying glass icon and placeholder text "Search movies, shows...".
*   **User Controls (Far Right):** 
    *   Bell icon (Notifications) with a small blue dot indicator.
    *   Profile Picture: Circular, featuring a man's face (Alex M.).
    *   Name: "Alex M." next to the avatar.

## 4. Hero Section (Featured Content)
*   **Background Image:** "The Astral Frontier". A high-resolution cinematic shot of a female astronaut in a detailed white/grey suit looking right. The background is a dark lunar landscape with a large planet/moon in the sky.
*   **Overlay:** A subtle dark gradient from left to bottom-left to ensure text readability.
*   **Text Content (Left Aligned):**
    *   **Tagline:** "PREMIUM STREAMING WEBSITE" in small, spaced-out blue text.
    *   **Title:** "THE ASTRAL FRONTIER" (Large, white, uppercase).
    *   **Metadata:** 4.5 stars (gold), "2021", "Genre".
    *   **Synopsis:** 3-line paragraph in light grey text.
*   **Buttons:**
    *   **Play Now:** Solid Blue (`#007bff`), white text, includes a small play icon.
    *   **+ My List:** Transparent with a white border, white text.
*   **Carousel Indicators:** Four small dots at the bottom center; the active dot is white, others are dark grey.

## 5. Content Rows
Each row consists of a Section Title followed by a horizontal grid of cards.

### A. Continue Watching
*   **Card Style:** Wide aspect ratio (approx 16:9).
*   **Progress Bar:** A thin blue line at the bottom of the image indicating how much has been watched.
*   **Images:**
    1.  **The Sentinel:** Three people standing in a dark, moody setting.
    2.  **Arcane:** Stylized blue-haired animated character (Jinx) looking forward.
    3.  **Foundation:** A man in futuristic armor standing in a desert-like landscape.
    4.  **Cyberpunk:** Yellow background with a silhouette of a character from behind.

### B. Trending Movies / New Release TV Shows
*   **Card Style:** Vertical poster aspect ratio (approx 2:3).
*   **Metadata Overlay:** Below the image: Title (White, Bold), Year, Genre, and Star Rating (Gold).
*   **Key Images to Describe:**
    *   **Eclipse:** A glowing orange solar eclipse against a black background.
    *   **Iron Heart:** A man in a high-tech red and black suit (superhero style).
    *   **Dune Part II:** Warm orange tones, silhouettes of people on a sand dune.
    *   **Nova:** A lone figure standing under a bright blue vertical light beam.
    *   **The Depths:** Underwater shot of a whale or large sea creature in deep blue water.
    *   **Stranger Things:** Red-hued poster with a group of kids and a monster silhouette.
    *   **Astra:** Silhouette of a person standing inside a glowing circular portal.

### C. Popular Genres
*   **Card Style:** Short, wide rectangles.
*   **Visuals:** Background images are blurred/darkened movie scenes with the genre name centered in bold white text.
*   **Genres:** Sci-Fi, Action, Drama, Fantasy (repeated).

### D. Award-Winning Films
*   **Card Style:** Vertical poster (2:3).
*   **Image Note:** These posters are identical to the "Trending" section but grouped under the "Award-Winning" category.

## 6. Footer
*   **Links (Left):** "Account", "Help Center", "Terms", "Privacy". Small, grey text.
*   **Social Icons (Right):** Facebook, X (Twitter), Instagram, YouTube. Small, circular grey icons.

## 7. Image Specifications
*   **Backgroundless Elements:** The astronaut in the Hero section is a "cut-out" style layered over the lunar background to create depth.
*   **Rotation/Placement:** All thumbnails are perfectly vertical/horizontal with no rotation.
*   **Shadows:** Each card has a subtle `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)` to separate it from the dark background.
*   **Hover State:** Cards should scale up slightly (`transform: scale(1.05)`) and increase brightness on hover.
