# Design Specification: Alpine E-Commerce Showcase

## 1. Typography & Hierarchy

### Primary Fonts
* **Brand Logo:** Extended geometric sans-serif (Recommended: *Microgramma Bold Extended* or *Eurostile Extended*).  
  *Fallback:* `Orbitron, "Arial Black", sans-serif` (letter-spacing: `0.35em`).
* **Headings & Body UI:** Clean grotesque sans-serif (Recommended: *Neue Haas Grotesk* or *Inter*).  
  *Fallback:* `"Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

### Type Scale
* **Logo:** `16px`, bold, uppercase, tracking `+0.4em`.
* **Hero Title (`/ Sunglasses`):** `20px`, semi-bold, tracking `+0.05em`.
* **Hero Body:** `12px`, line-height `1.6`, regular, `#555555`.
* **CTA Button:** `11px`, bold, uppercase, tracking `+0.15em`.
* **Filter Tabs:** `11px`, medium, uppercase, tracking `+0.15em`.
* **Product Titles:** `12px`, medium, `#111111`.
* **Product Prices:** `12px`, regular, `#111111`.
* **Product Tags:** `10px`, regular, `#888888`.
* **Footer Links:** `10px`, uppercase, tracking `+0.1em`, `#777777`.

---

## 2. Color Palette

* **Primary Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
* **Product Tile Background:** `#ECECEC` (Flat light gray)
* **Text Primary:** `#111111` (Deep black)
* **Text Secondary:** `#666666` (Mid-tone neutral gray)
* **Text Muted:** `#999999` (Light gray)
* **Border/Dividers:** `#D6D6D6` (Subtle boundary gray)

---

## 3. Page Layout & Components

### 3.1. Top Navigation Bar
* **Position:** Fixed or static top, full viewport width, height `72px`, padding `0 48px`.
* **Left:** Hamburger icon (`18px` width) spaced `24px` from `< Back` link (`11px`, regular).
* **Center:** `ALPINE` logotype.
* **Right:** `Cart (0)` and language switch `En ⌵` spaced `32px` apart.

---

### 3.2. Hero Showcase (Featured Product)
* **Dimensions:** Full width, padding `48px 96px 80px 96px`, two-column split (`40%` text, `60%` imagery).
* **Left Column:**
  * Title: `/ Sunglasses`
  * Body: Max-width `280px`, margin-top `20px`.
  * CTA Button: Outline rectangle (`140px × 38px`), border `1px solid #111111`, text `BUY 115 €`, margin-top `32px`.
  * Bottom Link: `⌵ Product details` placed at bottom-left corner.
* **Right Column:**
  * Featured image of matte-black sunglasses placed horizontally centered.
  * Pagination control placed bottom-right: `01 —————— 04 →`.

---

### 3.3. Filter Bar
* **Position:** Centered horizontal inline list, margin `64px 0 48px 0`.
* **Content:**
  * Active/selected items prefixed with `✕`: `✕ FASHION`, `✕ TRAVEL`, `✕ EVERY DAY`.
  * Inactive items: `MINIATURES` (rendered in `#999999`).
  * Spacing: `32px` between tags.

---

### 3.4. Product Catalog Grid
* **Grid Setup:** 3 columns × 3 rows, gap `24px`, container max-width `1100px`, centered.
* **Card Structure:**
  * **Image Canvas:** Rectangular container (`aspect-ratio: 1 / 1`), background `#ECECEC`, items vertically and horizontally centered with natural contact shadows.
  * **Metadata Row 1 (Directly beneath canvas):** Title aligned left, price aligned right (e.g., `Black leather Belt` / `95 €`).
  * **Metadata Row 2:** Sub-category aligned left, faint gray (e.g., `Fashion`).

---

### 3.5. Footer
* **Layout:** Single-row flexbox, full width, padding `64px 48px 48px 48px`, border-top `none`.
* **Left:** `En ⌵`
* **Center:** `CONTACT / MEDIA / LEGAL / COOKIES` (separated by slashes, `16px` gap).
* **Right:** Social links `Fb  Tw  Yt  In  Ln` (`16px` gap).
