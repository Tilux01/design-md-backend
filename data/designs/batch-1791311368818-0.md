# Design Specification: Reimagined History Website UI

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
* **Primary Mood & Archetype:** Minimalist luxury, high-end e-commerce editorial, contemplative, modernistic.
* **Visual Density:** Extremely low density with vast, intentional whitespace to draw maximum attention to the hero imagery and typography.
* **Theme Balance:** Pure light-mode interface (`#FFFFFF` canvas) anchored by a high-contrast black asymmetrical footer bar (`#000000`).
* **Texture & Depth:** Clean, flat vector design surfaces utilizing drop shadows solely for the main subject product imagery and subtle interactive hover states.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens
* **Primary (Brand / Focus):** `#000000` (Pure Black)
* **Surface Backgrounds:**
  * Canvas Base: `#FFFFFF` (White)
  * Secondary / Footer Surface: `#000000` (Pure Black)
* **Text Colors:**
  * Primary Headings: `#000000` (Pure Black)
  * Secondary / Muted Text: `#666666` (Neutral Gray)
  * Inverse Text (on dark surfaces): `#FFFFFF` (White)
* **Borders & Dividers:** `#E5E5E5` (Light Gray)

---

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy
* **Font Family:** Modern Grotesk Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter, Helvetica Neue, or similar clean neo-grotesk)
* **Scale Breakdown:**
  * **Brand Mark / Logo (`Si`):** 20px, Bold (700), Line-height: 1.2, Tracking: Normal
  * **H1 / Hero Headline ("Reimagined History."):** 48px–56px, Bold (700), Line-height: 1.1, Tracking: -0.03em
  * **Sub-headline / Category ("realreason."):** 14px, Medium (500), Line-height: 1.4, Tracking: +0.05em (Uppercase/Title case styled)
  * **Navigation Links:** 13px, Regular/Medium (400/500), Line-height: 1.5
  * **Body / Meta Text:** 12px, Regular (400), Line-height: 1.5

---

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
* **Container Max-Width:** 1440px fluid container with generous horizontal viewport padding (approx. 64px–80px desktop gutters).
* **Grid System:** 12-column asymmetric macro layout.
* **Section Sequence:**
  1. **Top Global Navigation Header:** Fixed top header containing brand logo, international country selectors, search, and hamburger menu.
  2. **Hero Core Section:** Split asymmetric layout featuring heavy typography to the left and a large product visualization (candle jar) to the right.
  3. **Bottom Multi-Action Footer Bar:** Dual-zone container merging user creator profile card, audio/progress scrub line, social integrations, carousel pagination dots, and navigation arrows.
* **Z-Index Hierarchy:**
  * Base Canvas: `z-index: 0`
  * Hero Imagery / Content: `z-index: 10`
  * Global Header / Bottom Docked Bar: `z-index: 100`

---

## 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement

### A. Global Navigation Header
* **Layout:** Flexbox space-between layout spanning the full width of the interface.
* **Left Cluster:** Brand logo (`Si`), followed by a horizontal list of region/country items (`Ghana`, `Canada`, `United States`, `United Arab Emirates`) spaced at `32px` gaps.
* **Right Cluster:** Search icon, Hamburger menu icon (`30px` gap).

### B. Hero Section & Product Display
* **Left Text Block:** Aligned on an inner left margin, featuring the micro-brand tag (`realreason.`), followed by the primary H1 block (`Reimagined History.`).
* **Right Product Display:** High-resolution product shot of a textured bark-wrapped candle in an amber glass jar, featuring soft drop-shadow physics (`0px 24px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`).

### C. Bottom Docked Footer Bar
* **Layout:** Asymmetric split container spanning the bottom viewport edge.
* **Left Sub-zone (Dark Surface `#000000`):** 
  * Features a circular avatar (Jackson Wagner), author/creator metadata, and an interactive audio/progress track line.
  * Right-aligned icons for Instagram (`instagram` icon) and Share (`share` icon).
* **Right Sub-zone (Light Surface `#FFFFFF`):**
  * Center: Carousel dot indicators (active dot is solid black, inactive are stroked outlines).
  * Right: Previous/Next circular navigation buttons (`<` / `>`) with `1px` solid borders, followed by `.more` text link.

---

## 6. Motion Mechanics & Interactions
* **Transitions:** Smooth cubic-bezier easing (`cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`) for hover states, pagination transitions, and modal expansions.
* **Hover States:** 
  * Navigation links shift color from muted gray (`#666666`) to solid black (`#000000`).
  * Circular pagination buttons invert background colors on hover (Black background with white icons).
* **Carousel Physics:** Horizontal sliding motion with subtle scale and fade transitions for the main hero product showcase.
