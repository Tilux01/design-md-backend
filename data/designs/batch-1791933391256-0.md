# Design Specification: Tailored Suit Landing Page

## 1. Global Setup & Color Palette

*   **Viewport**: Fullscreen (`100vw`, `100vh`), `overflow: hidden`.
*   **Background**: Deep black/charcoal radial gradient (`#18181D` center subtly fading to `#0A0A0C` at edges).
*   **Colors**:
    *   Primary Text: `#FFFFFF`
    *   Secondary/Muted Text: `#8B8B92`
    *   Border/Stroke Color: `#FFFFFF` (for outlined text)
    *   Accent: Subtle neutral grays (`#3A3A40`)

---

## 2. Typography

*   **Display / Hero Font**:
    *   Primary: *Monument Extended*, *Syne Bold*, or *Druk Wide* (Uppercase, heavy/wide sans-serif).
    *   Fallback: `Impact`, `Arial Black`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Navigation & Body Font**:
    *   Primary: *Inter*, *Montserrat*, or *Helvetica Neue*.
    *   Fallback: `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Watermark / Sub-brand Font**:
    *   Primary: *Montserrat Light* or *Futura PT* (Light weight, high letter spacing).
    *   Fallback: `sans-serif`.

---

## 3. Layout Structure

### A. Navigation Bar (Top)
*   **Placement**: Fixed at top, horizontal flexbox, `height: 80px`, `padding: 0 4%`.
*   **Left (Logo)**:
    *   Text: `SUITS.` (Font weight: 900, size: `18px`, tracked, white).
*   **Center Navigation**:
    *   Links: `OFFICE`, `SPECIALS`, `SALE`, `CUSTOMIZE`, `INSPIRATION`.
    *   Style: Uppercase, `11px`, letter-spacing: `1.5px`, color: `#D1D1D6`.
    *   Spacing: `gap: 28px`.
*   **Right Utilities**:
    *   Three minimalist line icons (`16px`, stroke: `1.5px`): User Profile, Search, Shopping Bag.
    *   Spacing: `gap: 20px`.

---

### B. Main Hero Content (Left)
*   **Placement**: Positioned left, vertically centered (`top: 45%`, `left: 8%`).
*   **Headline**:
    *   Line 1: `STEP UP` (Outlined/stroke text: `-webkit-text-stroke: 1.5px #FFFFFF`, fill: `transparent`, font-size: `clamp(40px, 5vw, 64px)`).
    *   Line 2: `IN YOUR` (Solid white, font-size: `clamp(40px, 5vw, 64px)`, font-weight: 900).
    *   Line 3: `STYLE` (Solid white, font-size: `clamp(40px, 5vw, 64px)`, font-weight: 900).
    *   Text properties: Extra wide, tight line height (`0.95`).

---

### C. Bottom Left Features
*   **Placement**: Bottom-left corner (`bottom: 8%`, `left: 8%`), horizontal layout with 2 columns (`gap: 48px`).
*   **Column 1**:
    *   Icon: Minimalist `×` (white, `12px`).
    *   Heading: `Choose your right suit` (`13px`, font-weight: 600, color: `#FFFFFF`).
    *   Subtext: `See our finest collections` (`11px`, color: `#8B8B92`, margin-top: `4px`).
*   **Column 2**:
    *   Icon: Minimalist `×` (white, `12px`).
    *   Heading: `Perfectly tailored suits` (`13px`, font-weight: 600, color: `#FFFFFF`).
    *   Subtext: `You set the tone in the office with style` (`11px`, color: `#8B8B92`, margin-top: `4px`).

---

### D. Hero Image (Center / Center-Right)

*   **Format**: Transparent PNG cutout (backgroundless) or seamlessly blended into the dark background.
*   **Description**: Half-body to three-quarter portrait of a confident man with a styled pompadour haircut and short beard, facing frontally with a direct gaze. He wears a dark navy/charcoal windowpane check tailored suit jacket, white dress shirt, and a navy dotted tie. Hands are at waist level buttoning the jacket; visible wrist watch and tattoo on the left hand.
*   **Placement**: Anchored horizontally at `50% - 65%` left and bottom-aligned (`bottom: 0`).
*   **Rotation**: `0deg` (upright).
*   **Sizing**: Height roughly `85vh` to `90vh`, scaling proportionally behind text or overlapping slightly with lower heading lines.

---

### E. Right Edge & Bottom-Right Details

*   **Vertical Contact Link**:
    *   Text: `CONTACT`
    *   Placement: Centered vertically against the far right edge (`right: 2%`, `top: 50%`).
    *   Rotation: `-90deg` (reading upwards), font size: `11px`, tracking: `2px`, color: `#D1D1D6`.
*   **Bottom-Right Watermark**:
    *   Placement: Bottom right (`bottom: 8%`, `right: 8%`).
    *   Content: `CARTERCODES` in thin, wide tracked uppercase (`14px`), followed by a small decorative 4-point star/cross icon (`❖`, `14px`, stroke only).
