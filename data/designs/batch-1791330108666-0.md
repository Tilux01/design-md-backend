# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: High-Tech Dark Mode Immersive Workspace / Web3 Infrastructure & Developer Platform.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Highly balanced with atmospheric depth; dark immersive canvas (`#060B0E`) punctuated by high-vibrancy accent highlights (crimson red and electric violet/cyan glowing cubes), maintaining spacious horizontal navigation and spacious hero padding.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered dark surfaces using subtle backdrop blur (`blur(16px)`), crisp 1px semi-transparent borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and ambient radial light gradients to simulate floating 3D depth.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges with glowing aura dots, glassmorphic navigation pill, asymmetric 3D lighting gradients, floating frosted glass containers.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(20, 28, 36, 0.8)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (`&mdash;`) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Sparkle / Star (`✦`) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (`●`) -> Use `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-red-500 inline-block animate-pulse"></span>`.
