# Design Specification: "LAST" Game Landing Page (Exact Clone)

This document outlines the exact design specifications for recreating the landing page provided in the image. The layout is designed to be fully responsive, spanning 100% width and height of the viewport, with zero decorative background framing.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

*   **View Mode:** Full-Screen (`width: 100vw; height: 100vh; overflow: hidden;`).
*   **Base Theme:** Dark / Cinematic. The background consists of a dark, moody landscape/forest overlaid with a stark, diagonal split.
*   **Diagonal Split Layout:**
    *   **Background Layer:** Dark cinematic forest graphic.
    *   **Foreground Diagonal Band:** A wide diagonal gray band spanning from the bottom-left toward the top-right.
        *   **Angle:** Approximately 30 to 35 degrees.
        *   **Color/Opacity:** Solid gray (`#8a8e91` or similar neutral cool gray) covering the upper right quadrant of the screen.
    *   **Z-Index Hierarchy:**
        1. Base dark background & "LAST" title.
        2. Central character art (spanning across the diagonal split).
        3. UI elements (Navigation, Text Box, Platform Icons, Footer links, Download CTA).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Font Family
*   **Display / Headings (Catalog, News, History, LAST):**
    *   **Font-family:** Modern geometric sans-serif or bold grunge display font. For a production-ready fallback stack:
        ```css
        font-family: 'Cinzel', 'Impact', 'Arial Black', sans-serif;
        ```
*   **Body Text (Paragraph in the chat box):**
    *   **Font-family:** Clean, highly legible sans-serif.
        ```css
        font-family: 'Inter', 'Roboto', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif;
        ```

---

## 3. Component Breakdown & Styling

### A. Top Navigation Bar
*   **Position:** Absolute / Fixed at the top (`top: 30px`, horizontal padding matching page margins).
*   **Left Element (Logo):**
    *   **Text:** `GAME`
    *   **Styling:** Custom logotype with intertwined lines. The 'M' and 'E' are stylized with horizontal cutouts.
    *   **Color:** White (`#ffffff`).
*   **Center Navigation Links:**
    *   **Items:** `Catalog`, `News`, `History`
    *   **Styling:** Clean sans-serif, regular weight, white text with smooth hover state (slight opacity drop or color shift to light gray).
*   **Right Element (Hamburger Menu):**
    *   **Icon:** Three horizontal parallel lines (burger menu).
    *   **Color:** White (`#ffffff`), cursor pointer.

### B. Central Character Art
*   **Asset:** A hooded warrior in dark ragged clothing wearing a conical straw hat (amigasa), holding a katana, surrounded by flying crows/birds.
*   **Positioning:** Centered vertically, overlapping both the dark background and the gray diagonal panel. 
*   **Visual Integration:** The character bridges the contrast between the dark lower-right and the gray upper-left.

### C. Promotional Text Box (Left)
*   **Position:** Absolute positioning on the mid-left area.
*   **Shape:** Rounded rectangle container with a semi-transparent dark background and a subtle light gray border.
    *   **Background:** `rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.6)` with backdrop blur.
    *   **Border:** Thin semi-transparent white/gray stroke (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).
    *   **Border Radius:** `12px`.
*   **Text Content:**
    *   *"Are you ready to show your strength and fight with powerful warriors to take the place of the supreme?"*
    *   **Font Size:** ~16px – 18px.
    *   **Color:** Off-white (`#f0f0f0`), line-height `1.5`.

### D. Title Graphic (Bottom Right)
*   **Text:** `LAST`
*   **Position:** Lower right quadrant, sitting over the dark background.
*   **Styling:** 
    *   Extra-large, bold display typography.
    *   **Texture:** The letters are filled with a grunge/stone or forest landscape texture blended into the text.
    *   **Color:** Dark metallic gray with textured highlights.

### E. Footer / Meta Elements

#### 1. Available Platforms (Bottom Left)
*   **Label:** "Available Platforms" (Small, uppercase, dark gray text).
*   **Badges:** Two rectangular buttons for Windows and PlayStation.
    *   **Windows Badge:** Dark gray background (`#333`), Windows 4-pane logo + "Windows" text. Rounded corners (`4px`).
    *   **PlayStation Badge:** Dark gray background (`#333`), PlayStation logo + "PlayStation" text. Rounded corners (`4px`).

#### 2. Social Media Links (Bottom-Left Corner)
*   **Icons:** Circular dark outline icons for Twitter/X (`𝕏`), a stylized `G` (Gaming forum/platform), and Facebook (`f`).
*   **Layout:** Horizontal flex row with small spacing gap.

#### 3. Central Download CTA Button
*   **Position:** Bottom center.
*   **Shape:** Pill-shaped or rounded rectangle with a double-line border effect (futuristic/cyber-action aesthetic).
*   **Text:** `DOWNLOAD`
    *   **Font Color:** Deep blood red (`#cc0000` or `#e60000`).
    *   **Font Weight:** Bold, tracked out (letter spacing).
*   **Border:** Thin dark gray/black border with rounded corners.

#### 4. Pagination / Next Action (Bottom Right)
*   **Text:** `Next >`
*   **Styling:** Sans-serif, small, white text with a hover state pointing to the next slide or section.

---

## 4. Animations & Interactions

*   **Hover States:**
    *   *Navigation links:* Fade to 70% opacity on hover.
    *   *Download button:* Subtle background fill transition or glow effect on hover.
    *   *Social icons:* Scale up slightly (`transform: scale(1.1)`) on hover.
*   **Page Load / Entrance Animation (Recommended for exact replica):**
    *   **Background Diagonal Band:** Slides in smoothly from the top-right corner to its resting position (`cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`).
    *   **Character Art:** Fade-in with a subtle upward translate (`translateY(20px)` to `0`).
    *   **Text Elements (LAST title & Quote box):** Staggered fade-in from left to right.
