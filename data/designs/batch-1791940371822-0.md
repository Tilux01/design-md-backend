This document provides a comprehensive design specification for the "Concerts" mobile application as shown in the provided media. This specification is intended to guide a developer or designer in creating an exact, full-screen clone of the interface.

# Design Specification: Concerts App Clone

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

### 1.1 Color Palette
*   **Primary Background (Light):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
*   **Primary Background (Dark/Detail View):** `#1C1C1E` (Deep Charcoal/Near Black)
*   **Accent Color (Red):** `#FF3B30` (Vibrant Red - used for location chips and active states)
*   **Secondary Background (Cards/Inputs):** `#F2F2F7` (Light Gray) / `#2C2C2E` (Dark Gray)
*   **Text Primary:** `#000000` (Light Mode) / `#FFFFFF` (Dark Mode)
*   **Text Secondary/Muted:** `#8E8E93` (Medium Gray)

### 1.2 Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `SF Pro Display` (or `Inter` as a web fallback)
*   **Secondary Font:** `SF Pro Text` (or `Roboto` as a web fallback)
*   **Fallback Stack:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
*   **Weights:** 
    *   Bold (700): Headers and Artist Names.
    *   Semibold (600): Section titles and Buttons.
    *   Regular (400): Body text and metadata.

### 1.3 Layout & Spacing
*   **Screen Padding:** 20px horizontal margin.
*   **Corner Radius:** 
    *   Large Cards: 24px
    *   Small Badges/Chips: 12px
    *   Buttons: 16px
    *   Circular Images: 50% (border-radius)

## 4. Image Descriptions

### 4.1 Lady Gaga (Main Portrait)
*   **Description:** A high-contrast, grayscale close-up portrait of Lady Gaga. She has voluminous, dark, wavy hair framing her face. Her expression is neutral/intense, looking directly at the camera.
*   **Background:** The image background is a solid, neutral gray, making it "backgroundless" in terms of environment.
*   **Placement:** 
    *   *Screen 1:* Rectangular crop, centered in the card. No rotation.
    *   *Screen 2:* Circular crop, centered in the top half of the screen. No rotation.

### 4.2 Tyler, The Creator (List Image)
*   **Description:** A grayscale, high-key (bright) circular photo. The artist is wearing a light-colored hat and looking slightly upward.
*   **Background:** Blown-out white/light gray background.
*   **Placement:** *Screen 1:* Circular frame on the left side of the list item. No rotation.

### 4.3 Secondary Artist (Partial Card)
*   **Description:** A grayscale image of an artist wearing a white garment or hood, looking to the side.
*   **Placement:** *Screen 1:* Rectangular crop, partially visible on the right edge. No rotation.

## 5. Technical Implementation Notes
*   **Status Bar:** Ensure the status bar icons (Time, Wi-Fi, Battery) flip between black (Screen 1) and white (Screen 2).
*   **Gradients:** Use a subtle black-to-transparent linear gradient at the bottom 30% of the featured cards on Screen 1 to ensure white text readability.
*   **Shadows:** Use very soft, large-radius shadows (`rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`) for the white cards on Screen 1 to create depth without harsh lines.
