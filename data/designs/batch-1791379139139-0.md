# Visual Design Specification & Implementation Blueprint

## 1. Overall Layout & Composition

*   **View Mode:** Fullscreen, edge-to-edge layout (`width: 100vw`, `height: 100vh`), bypassing any device browser framing or container padding.
*   **Grid Structure:** Absolute positioning overlay strategy over a centered subject hierarchy.
*   **Visual Hierarchy (Z-Index Layers):**
    *   `z-index: 0`: Background canvas (`#0A1124`).
    *   `z-index: 1`: Giant background typography ("Creativity" masking layer behind the subject).
    *   `z-index: 2`: Studio subject (Young man sitting on a metallic bar stool).
    *   `z-index: 3`: Interactive selection box (Cut, Copy, Share menu + highlighted quote text).
    *   `z-index: 4`: Bottom-right tertiary quote.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Lighting

*   **Background (Canvas):** Deep Midnight Navy (`#0A1124` to `#060A13` subtle vertical gradient).
*   **Subject Attire:**
    *   **Sweater:** Deep Indigo / Dark Navy Blue (`#141D32`).
    *   **Pants:** Light Stone / Off-White Chinos (`#D8D4CC`).
    *   **Footwear:** Crisp Pure White Leather Sneakers (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Typography & UI Elements:**
    *   **Main Background Word ("Creativity"):** White (`#FFFFFF`) with a heavy dark backdrop blur/glow effect.
    *   **Interactive Text & Highlight Box:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) text with a dark modal pill (`#111113`) featuring slight opacity and rounded corners.

---

## 3. Typography Specifications

### Primary Heading ("Creativity" - Background)
*   **Text:** `Creivity` (Note: The letter 'a' is dynamically obscured by the subject's head).
*   **Font Family:** Geometric Sans-Serif, Extra-Bold / Black Weight (e.g., *Montserrat Black*, *Futura Bold*, or *Helvetica Neue Black*).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Montserrat', 'Futura', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif;`
*   **Styling:** Uppercase/Mixed casing as rendered, massive scaling (`clamp(8rem, 20vw, 22rem)`), high contrast, positioned centrally behind the model.

### Mid-Ground Quote ("Be you, be creative, be an artist")
*   **Font Family:** Clean Modern Sans-Serif, Regular to Medium Weight (e.g., *Inter*, *Roboto*, or *SF Pro Text*).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif;`
*   **Size:** `1.15rem` to `1.35rem`.
*   **Color:** `#FFFFFF`.

### Bottom-Right Footer Quote ("Creativity is intelligence having fun")
*   **Font Family:** Same as the middle quote (*Inter* / *SF Pro Text*).
*   **Size:** `0.85rem`.
*   **Alignment:** Right-aligned or block-aligned to the bottom-right quadrant.

---

## 4. UI Elements & Interactive Components (OS-Style Text Selection)

*   **Selection Box (Tooltip Menu):**
    *   **Background:** Dark translucent pill (`rgba(17, 17, 19, 0.9)` with backdrop-filter blur).
    *   **Border Radius:** `6px`.
    *   **Padding:** `6px 12px`.
    *   **Options:** `Cut` | `Copy` | `Share` (separated by thin vertical divider lines).
    *   **Selection Handles:** Custom vertical handles with circular anchor points at both ends of the selected text string (`"Be you, be creative, be an artist"`).

---

## 5. Motion & Animation Blueprint (CSS / JS)

### Entry Animations (Load State)
1.  **Background & Typography (`.creativity-bg`):**
    *   *Effect:* Fade in with a slight scale-up from `0.95` to `1.0`.
    *   *Duration:* `1.2s` cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1).
2.  **Subject Reveal (`.hero-subject`):**
    *   *Effect:* Slide up from base with a smooth opacity fade (`translateY(20px)` to `translateY(0)`).
    *   *Duration:* `1.0s` delay `0.3s`.
3.  **UI Highlight & Context Menu (`.selection-ui`):**
    *   *Effect:* Pop-in scale animation springing from center.
    *   *Duration:* `0.4s` delay `1.2s`.

### Ambient / Subtle Hover Micro-Interactions
*   **Context Menu Items:** Subtle background color shift on hover (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
*   **Parallax Effect:** Subtle mouse-move tracking (optional) to create depth between the foreground model and the background typography.
