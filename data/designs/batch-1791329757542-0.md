# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: E-Commerce Promotional Minimalist / Playful Modern UI. Clean white backdrop accented by vibrant magenta-pink branding tokens, soft dimensional tags, and playful iconographic glyphs.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio focusing attention sharply on isolated icon symbols, crisp borders, and pill-shaped interactive surfaces.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layer 0 canvas backing, Layer 1 raised physical price tag cards with soft directional drop shadows (`0 8px 30px rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.15)`), and crisp 1px borders.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient glow orbs, glassmorphism card highlights, status indicators, floating promotional iconography.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `4px 12px`, Font-size: `0.75rem`, Font-weight: `600`, Tracking: `0.05em`, Border-radius: `9999px`, Background translucency: `rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.1)`, Border width: `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Percentage Symbol (%) -> Use `&percnt;` or native typography.
