# Design Specification: Real Estate UI System

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
*   **Primary Mood & Brand Archetype:** Luxury, futuristic, immersive, and high-end architectural elegance. Blends minimalist futuristic concepts with high-end real estate curation.
*   **Visual Density:** Medium-low density, prioritizing large atmospheric hero media, generous whitespace, and translucent frosted glassmorphism overlays.
*   **Theme Balance:** Light/Glassmorphic Hybrid over heavy photographic backgrounds. The UI uses frosted translucent panels (`backdrop-filter: blur()`) with soft white surfaces and deep dark purple/navy contrast accents (`#120D2C` logo/brand elements).
*   **Texture & Depth Architecture:** Multi-layered frosted glass system (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`) combined with subtle inner highlights and soft ambient drop shadows to separate floating cards from the background image.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens
*   **Primary Accent Colors:**
    *   `primary-base`: `#6C5CE7` (Deep Indigo/Purple used for primary brand icons)
    *   `primary-hover`: `#5849D6`
*   **Surface & Background Tokens:**
    *   `bg-canvas`: `#E5E5E5` (Underlying base aesthetic, though obscured by imagery)
    *   `surface-glass`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` (Main top bar, control panels)
    *   `surface-card-light`: `#FFFFFF` (Solid white containers like "Find The Perfect Place")
    *   `surface-card-glass`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` (Floating property details card with heavy blur)
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   `text-primary`: `#1A1A1A` (Primary headings, high-contrast labels)
    *   `text-secondary`: `#6B7280` (Muted labels, location specs)
    *   `text-inverse`: `#FFFFFF` (Hero titles and overlays)
*   **Border & Divider Colors:**
    *   `border-subtle`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`
    *   `border-card`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`

---

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy
*   **Font Families:**
    *   *Headings:* Modern geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Clash Display*, *Syne*, or *Plus Jakarta Sans*)
    *   *Body/UI:* Clean humanistic sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *SF Pro Display*)
*   **Scale Breakdown:**
    *   **Hero Display Title ("New Way Of Living"):**
        *   Weight: Bold (700)
        *   Line-height: 1.05
        *   Letter-spacing: -0.03em
    *   **Card Heading ("Lunar Oasis Villa"):**
        *   Size: 22px / 1.375rem
        *   Weight: Semi-bold (600)
        *   Color: `#1A1A1A`
    *   **Body Copy & Descriptions:**
        *   Size: 13px - 14px / 0.8125rem - 0.875rem
        *   Weight: Regular (400)
        *   Line-height: 1.5
        *   Color: `#4A5568`
    *   **Microcopy / Metadata (Location, Specs):**
        *   Size: 11px - 12px / 0.6875rem - 0.75rem
        *   Weight: Medium (500)

---

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
*   **Container Width:** Fluid inner container max-width approx. `1320px`, centered within a soft-edged rounded window frame (`border-radius: 36px`).
*   **Padding & Spacing Scale:**
    *   `space-xs`: `6px`
    *   `space-sm`: `12px`
    *   `space-md`: `20px`
    *   `space-lg`: `32px`
    *   `space-xl`: `48px`
*   **Section Sequence (Hero View):**
    1.  *Top Global Action & Filter Bar:* Buy/Rent toggle, Location, Property Type, Max Price filters.
    2.  *Left Vertical Icon Navigation:* Floating vertical dock containing navigation shortcuts (Home, Search, Grid, Messages, User Profile, Notifications, Settings, Theme toggles).
    3.  *Hero Typography & Value Proposition:* Massive centered display typography over high-end architectural render.
    4.  *Floating Feature Cards:* 
        *   Bottom-left: "Find The Perfect Place" solid white conversion card with property counter and avatar cluster.
        *   Bottom-right: "Lunar Oasis Villa" glassmorphic property detail card with specs, image actions, and quick-save buttons.

---

## 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement
*   **Floating Navigation Dock (Left):**
    *   Width: `56px`
    *   Border Radius: `28px` (Full capsule/pill)
    *   Background: Solid white / translucent white mix with vertical alignment of icon buttons.
    *   Active State: Deep indigo background (`#120D2C`) with white icon.
*   **Filter Top-Bar:**
    *   Border Radius: `32px` (Pill shape)
    *   Padding: `8px 16px`
    *   Backdrop Filter: `blur(20px)`
    *   Inputs: Chevron down indicators for dropdown selects (Arizona, Villa, $300,000).
*   **Action Buttons (Buy / Rent):**
    *   Pill-shaped toggle buttons with high-contrast text and diagonal arrow icons (`↗`).
*   **Property Cards (Glassmorphism):**
    *   Border Radius: `24px` to `32px`.
    *   Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px)` with a subtle inner border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`).
    *   Box Shadow: `0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1)`

---

## 6. Motion Mechanics, 3D & Mobile Gestures
*   **Transition Durations & Easing:**
    *   *Hover states & Micro-interactions:* `150ms ease-out`
    *   *Card expansions & Modal/Drawer slides:* `350ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (Spring physics)
*   **Interactive States:**
    *   *Buttons/Icons:* Scale up (`scale(1.05)`) on hover; subtle background luminance shift.
    *   *Cards:* Elevation lift with shadow diffusion increase on hover.
*   **Gesture Behaviors:**
    *   Horizontal swipe support for property gallery sliders.
    *   Pull-to-refresh and smooth edge-pan navigation for mobile viewports.
