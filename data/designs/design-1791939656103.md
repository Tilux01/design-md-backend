This document provides a comprehensive design specification to recreate the music application UI shown in the provided image. The design follows a modern, dark-themed aesthetic with vibrant yellow accents and a mix of sharp and organic curved shapes.

---

## 1. Global Styles

### Color Palette
*   **Primary Background (Dark):** `#1E1E26` (Deep charcoal/navy grey)
*   **Secondary Background (Light):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white)
*   **Accent Color (Yellow):** `#FFD700` to `#FFC107` (Vibrant golden yellow)
*   **Text (Primary):** `#FFFFFF` (White)
*   **Text (Secondary):** `#A0A0A0` (Medium grey)
*   **Text (Dark):** `#1E1E26` (For use on light backgrounds)
*   **Shadows:** Soft drop shadows using `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)` with high blur (20px-40px).

### Typography
*   **Primary Font:** A modern, geometric Sans-Serif. 
    *   *Recommended:* **Montserrat** or **Poppins**.
    *   *System Fallback:* `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:**
    *   Titles/Headers: Bold (700)
    *   Subtitles/Body: Medium (500)
    *   Metadata/Captions: Regular (400)

## 2. Screen 1: Discovery / Home

### Layout Structure
The screen is split into a dark top section and a white bottom section. The transition is a smooth, organic "S" curve that dips down on the left and rises on the right.

### Header
*   **Title:** "Music" (Left-aligned, Bold, White, ~24px).
*   **Search Bar:** Right-aligned. A pill-shaped container (`#2A2A35`). Contains a placeholder "Search..." in grey and a thin white magnifying glass icon on the right.

### Left Sidebar (Vertical Navigation)
*   **Orientation:** Text is rotated -90 degrees (reading bottom to top).
*   **Items:** "Recommend", "Album", "Style".
*   **Active State:** "Recommend" has a small horizontal dash prefix and is brighter white. Others are semi-transparent grey.
*   **Placement:** Vertically centered along the left edge of the dark section.

### Main Feature Card
*   **Shape:** Large rectangle with highly rounded corners (approx. 30px radius).
*   **Background:** A vibrant yellow gradient.
*   **Image Content:** A portrait of a man (Andy Grammer) looking forward. The image is heavily stylized with a yellow color wash/overlay. The bottom half of the image is obscured by thick, volumetric yellow smoke or clouds.
*   **Text Overlay:** 
    *   "Don't Give Up" (Large, White, Bold, ~22px).
    *   "Andy Grammer" (Smaller, White, Regular, ~14px).
*   **Play Icon:** A small white circular button with a yellow play triangle, positioned in the bottom left of the card.

### Collection Section
*   **Label:** "Collection" rotated -90 degrees, placed on the left side of the white section.
*   **List Items:**
    1.  **Item 1:** Thumbnail (Rounded corners). Image: A person in a black jacket looking at a yellow wall with art. Text: "Lost My Way" (Black, Bold), "David" (Grey, Regular).
    2.  **Item 2:** Thumbnail (Rounded corners). Image: A bright yellow wall with a bicycle leaning against it. Text: "A Werewolf Boy" (Black, Bold), "Carole" (Grey, Regular).

### Bottom Navigation Bar
*   **Background:** Pure white, slightly elevated with a soft shadow.
*   **Icons:**
    *   **Home:** A yellow circle containing a black record/disc icon.
    *   **Play (Center):** A large, floating yellow circle with a white play triangle. It sits slightly higher than the other icons.
    *   **Profile:** A simple grey outline of a person (head and shoulders).

## 3. Screen 2: Now Playing

### Background
*   **Color:** A vertical gradient from `#2A2A35` at the top to `#1E1E26` at the bottom.

### Header
*   **Icons:** Back arrow (Left), Lyrics/Text icon, Playlist icon, Share icon (Right). All icons are thin, white, and semi-transparent.

### Centerpiece: The Vinyl Record
*   **Visual:** A realistic 3D-styled black vinyl record.
*   **Label (Center Image):** A circular crop of the yellow "Andy Grammer" image from Screen 1.
*   **Label Text:** "ANDY GRAMMER" is written in a bold, sans-serif font, curved along the top-left inner edge of the yellow label.
*   **Shadow:** A deep, soft drop shadow behind the record to give it depth.

### Progress Indicator (Right Side)
*   **Orientation:** Vertical line on the far right.
*   **Style:** A thin, semi-transparent white line. A solid white circular "knob" indicates current progress.
*   **Timestamp:** "3:56" written vertically at the top of the line.

### Song Information
*   **Title:** "Don't Give Up" (Large, White, Bold, ~26px).
*   **Artist:** "- Andy Grammer -" (Medium, Grey, Regular, ~16px).

### Lyrics Display
*   **Placement:** Centered below the artist name.
*   **Active Line:** "I will fight I will fight for you" (White, Medium).
*   **Secondary Line:** "I always do until my heart is black and blue" (Grey, Regular).
*   **Faded Line:** "And I will stay I will stay with you" (Very faint grey, nearly transparent).

### Playback Controls
*   **Primary Play Button:** Located to the right of the song title. A glowing yellow circle with a white play triangle. It has an outer "glow" or soft yellow shadow.

## 4. Image Specifications

### Image A (Main Feature & Vinyl Label)
*   **Subject:** A young man with short, styled hair.
*   **Treatment:** Backgroundless or fully integrated into a yellow environment. The image must have a heavy yellow "Duotone" or "Color Overlay" effect.
*   **Atmosphere:** The bottom of the subject is blended into thick, yellowish-white smoke/fog.
*   **Rotation:** The image is upright in the card, but when used on the vinyl label, it is rotated approximately 45 degrees clockwise.

### Image B (Lost My Way Thumbnail)
*   **Subject:** Back view of a person with dark hair wearing a black jacket.
*   **Background:** A gallery wall with various colorful sketches/paintings.
*   **Placement:** Centered within a rounded square.

### Image C (Werewolf Boy Thumbnail)
*   **Subject:** A vintage-style bicycle.
*   **Background:** A solid, bright yellow wall with vertical panel lines.
*   **Placement:** Centered within a rounded square.

## 5. Technical Implementation Notes
*   **Corner Radii:** Use `30px` for large cards and `15px` for small thumbnails.
*   **Gradients:** Use subtle linear gradients for the dark backgrounds to prevent "banding" and add depth.
*   **Glassmorphism:** The search bar and some icons use a slight background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(10px)`) to feel integrated into the dark UI.
