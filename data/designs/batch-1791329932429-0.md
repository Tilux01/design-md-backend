# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Glassmorphism & Cybernetic Deep Tech SaaS.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High visual hierarchy centered around a bold hero focal point, expansive negative space at top and bottom margins, emphasizing futuristic 3D generative wave graphics and high-end typography.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered obsidian backdrop (`#050508`) with ambient pink/magenta and cyan/blue neon glowing orbs, subtle translucent glass pills with 1px soft borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), and deep blur backdrops (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Floating pill navigation with active border gradient highlights, gradient text styling (`background: linear-gradient(to right, #EC4899, #3B82F6)`), glowing volumetric 3D wave components, and atmospheric dark-mode gradients.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Horizontal padding `20px`, vertical padding `8px`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, backdrop-blur `12px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-pink-500 inline-block"></span>`.
