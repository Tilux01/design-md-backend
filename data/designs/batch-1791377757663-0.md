# Comprehensive UI/UX Design Specification: Pizza Landing Page

This document provides the precise structural, stylistic, and interactive specifications required to recreate the "Final Design" pizza landing page shown in the media. All decorative framing, drop shadows, and presentation canvases from the source image are excluded to ensure a full-screen, production-ready implementation.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Design Tokens

### Color Palette
*   **Background (Canvas):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
*   **Primary Accent / CTA:** `#E31B23` (Vibrant Pizza Red)
*   **Primary Text (Headings):** `#1A1A1A` (Near Black)
*   **Secondary Text (Body/Nav):** `#4A4A4A` (Dark Charcoal Gray)
*   **Subtle Text:** `#767676` (Medium Gray)

### Typography
*   **Primary Font Family:** **Montserrat**, fallback to `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Heading (H1):** 
    *   Size: `36px` to `42px` (responsive)
    *   Weight: `700` (Bold)
    *   Line Height: `1.2`
    *   Letter Spacing: `-0.02em`
*   **Body Copy:** 
    *   Size: `14px` to `16px`
    *   Weight: `400` (Regular)
    *   Line Height: `1.5`
*   **Navigation & UI Labels:** 
    *   Size: `13px` to `14px`
    *   Weight: `500` (Medium)

---

## 2. Layout & Structure

The layout is structured as a single-viewport, centered hero section with floating decorative ingredients, a central focal point, and an expansive pizza preview at the bottom edge.

### Grid & Spacing
*   **Max Content Width:** `1280px` centered within the viewport.
*   **Vertical Padding:** Generous whitespace framing the central text and hero imagery to create a light, premium aesthetic.

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation Header
*   **Positioning:** Fixed or sticky top bar spanning the full width of the screen.
*   **Left Element:** 
    *   Brand Logo: Text-based logo reading **"pizza."** in lowercase, bold typography, colored in `#E31B23`.
*   **Right Elements:** 
    *   Navigation Links: `Home`, `Menu`, `Contact` aligned horizontally with a gap of `24px`. Color: `#4A4A4A`, transitioning to `#E31B23` on hover.
    *   Iconography: Profile/User icon and Cart icon with a notification badge (positioned on the far right).

### B. Hero Section (Center Focus)
*   **Main Heading (H1):** 
    *   Text: *"Your Delicious Pizza Start Here!"*
    *   Alignment: Centered.
*   **Body Description:** 
    *   Text: *"Gather your friends and family and enjoy the best pizza in town. Freshly made and delivered hot!"*
    *   Max Width: `450px` centered below the H1. Color: `#4A4A4A`.
*   **Call to Action (CTA) Button:** 
    *   Text: *"View Our Menu"*
    *   Background Color: `#E31B23`
    *   Text Color: `#FFFFFF`
    *   Border Radius: `20px` (Pill/Capsule shape)
    *   Padding: `10px 24px`
    *   Hover State: Slight darkening (`#C8141C`) with a smooth CSS transition.

### C. Floating Ingredients (Visual Accents)
Scattered around the central text block are high-resolution photography assets of fresh pizza ingredients arranged asymmetrically:
*   **Left Side:** Fresh basil leaves, green olives, green chili peppers, and a fresh red cherry tomato.
*   **Right Side:** Whole red tomato, whole green olive, fresh basil leaf, and sliced button mushrooms.
*   *Note:* These elements float organically to frame the central typography without obstructing readability.

### D. Bottom Hero Graphic (Pizza Carousel Preview)
*   **Visual Asset:** A high-definition top-down cross-section/arch view of a freshly baked supreme pizza (topped with pepperoni, mushrooms, olives, cheese, and basil).
*   **Positioning:** Anchored centrally at the bottom boundary of the viewport, creating a "peeking" hero effect.
*   **Carousel Controls:** Minimalist circular arrow buttons (`<` and `>`) placed at the lower-left and lower-right sides of the pizza container to indicate slide functionality.

---

## 4. Animation & Interaction Specifications

To achieve a polished user experience, apply the following micro-interactions:

1.  **Entrance / Load Animation (Framer Motion / CSS Keyframes):**
    *   *Hero Text & CTA:* Fade in and slide up (`opacity: 0 -> 1`, `translateY: 20px -> 0`) with a duration of `0.6s` and an ease-out cubic-bezier curve.
    *   *Floating Ingredients:* Subtle continuous floating/parallax effect (gentle up-and-down oscillation using sine waves) to give the page depth.
    *   *Hero Pizza:* Slide up from the bottom (`translateY: 50px -> 0`) with a duration of `0.8s` upon initial page load.

2.  **Hover States:**
    *   **Nav Links:** Color transition from `#4A4A4A` to `#E31B23` over `0.2s`.
    *   **CTA Button:** Scale transform (`scale(1.03)`) and shadow elevation on hover.
    *   **Carousel Arrows:** Background color shifts slightly darker when hovered.
