# Design Specification: The Last of Us Media Interface

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Layout Style:** Split-screen asymmetrical grid.
*   **Left Panel (Hero):** Occupies approximately 65% of the viewport width. Fixed background with overlaying navigation and episode selection.
*   **Right Panel (Content):** Occupies approximately 35% of the viewport width. Scrollable container with a solid black background.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Background:** `#000000` (Pure Black).
    *   **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
    *   **Text Secondary:** `#A0A0A0` (Light Gray) for body descriptions.
    *   **Accent:** `#E5B32A` (Golden Yellow) for play icons and active indicators.
*   **Global Padding:** 40px internal padding for both sections.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Heading Font (Logo & Titles):** 
    *   *Choice:* **Bebas Neue** or **Impact**.
    *   *Characteristics:* All-caps, condensed, bold weight, tight letter spacing.
    *   *Fallback:* `Arial Narrow, sans-serif`.
*   **Secondary Heading Font:** 
    *   *Choice:* **Roboto Condensed** (Bold).
    *   *Characteristics:* All-caps, clean, high readability.
*   **Body Text Font:** 
    *   *Choice:* **Inter** or **Open Sans**.
    *   *Characteristics:* Regular weight, 14px - 16px, line height 1.6 for readability.
    *   *Fallback:* `sans-serif`.

---

## 3. Left Panel (Hero Section)

### A. Background Image (Main)
*   **Description:** A cinematic shot of Ellie sitting at the base of a large, moss-covered tree in a dense, overgrown forest. She is holding an acoustic guitar and looking down at her hands. In the background, a rusted, abandoned car is visible through the foliage.
*   **Placement:** Full-bleed within the left 65% container.
*   **Treatment:** A subtle dark gradient overlay at the bottom to ensure the "All episods" text is legible.

### B. Top Navigation Bar
*   **Logo:** "THE LAST OF US" stacked vertically in the top-left corner. Large, white, condensed font.
*   **Menu Links:** "ABOUT THE GAME", "CHAPTERS", "CHARACTERS", "UPDATES". 
    *   *Style:* All-caps, 12px, white. 
    *   *Indicator:* "ABOUT THE GAME" has a small horizontal white line underneath it.
*   **Utility Icons:** A shopping bag icon and a user profile icon located to the right of the text links.

### C. Bottom Carousel ("All episods")
*   **Title:** "All episods" (Note: maintain the original's spelling) in a bold, white, sans-serif font.
*   **Cards:** Three horizontal thumbnails.
    *   **Card 01:** Ellie in a dark forest. Label: "CHAPTER 01. ELLIE."
    *   **Card 02:** Joel’s face in shadow. Label: "CHAPTER 02. JOEL."
    *   **Card 03:** A character on horseback in a snowy landscape. Label: "CHAPTER 03. ROAD TRIP."
*   **Controls:** Small white arrow icons (`<` and `>`) stacked vertically to the right of the cards.

---

## 4. Right Panel (Content Section)

### A. Header
*   **Title:** "THE LAST OF US II" in large condensed font. Two vertical orange/gold bars follow the text.
*   **Menu Toggle:** "MENU" followed by a "hamburger" icon (three horizontal lines).

### B. Vertical Divider Text
*   **Text:** "PART 1" rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise.
*   **Placement:** Positioned on the border between the left and right panels, centered vertically.

### C. Content Blocks
*   **Block 1 (Intro):** Small paragraph describing Ellie’s journey for justice. White text, 14px.
*   **Block 2 (Feature):** 
    *   Heading: "A GROUND-BREAKING EXPERIENCE" (Condensed Bold).
    *   Body: Description of Jackson, Wyoming.
*   **Video Carousel:** 
    *   **Card 01:** Close-up of Ellie’s face looking distressed. 
    *   **Overlay:** A golden-yellow circular play button in the bottom left. 
    *   **Caption:** "ELLIE AND JOEL IN JACKSON, WYOMING" (All-caps, yellow/white).
    *   **Card 02 (Partial):** Joel’s face, partially cut off by the right edge of the screen.

### D. Footer Block (Partial)
*   **Heading:** "A COMPLEX AND EMOTIONAL STORY".
*   **Image:** A close-up of two characters (Ellie and Dina) in a warm, dimly lit setting.

---

## 5. Image Asset Requirements

1.  **Main Hero Image:** High-resolution, landscape orientation. No transparency.
2.  **Chapter Thumbnails:** 16:9 aspect ratio. High contrast, dark tones.
3.  **Video Cards:** 4:3 aspect ratio.
    *   *Ellie Card:* Backgroundless play icon (SVG) positioned at `bottom: 10px; left: 10px;`.
4.  **Icons:** 
    *   Shopping Bag, User, Play, and Arrows should be SVG format for crispness. 
    *   Play buttons must be Hex `#E5B32A`.

---

## 6. Interaction Details
*   **Hover States:** Menu links should increase in brightness or show the underline indicator.
*   **Carousel:** The "All episods" and video cards should support horizontal swiping/scrolling.
*   **Scroll:** The right panel scrolls independently of the left panel (which remains fixed).
