# UI Design Specification: Travel Booking Mobile Application

## 1. Global Styles & Design Tokens

### Color Palette
*   **Primary Dark / Text:** `#1A1A1A` (Deep charcoal used for primary headings, main price, and button text)
*   **Secondary Text / Subdued:** `#6E7681` (Muted gray for subtitles, locations, and descriptions)
*   **Background (App):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white base for the content containers)
*   **Background (Screen/Canvas):** `#F0F4F8` (Soft cool-toned light gray-blue canvas background)
*   **Accent Color:** `#FFFFFF` (White text on dark interactive elements)
*   **Button Dark:** `#1A1A1A` (Rounded rectangle "Book Now" CTA button)
*   **Counter / Interactive Buttons:** `#000000` with white icons (`-` and `+`)
*   **Avatar Stack Badge Background:** `#E1E8ED` (Light bluish-gray for the `+23` counter bubble)

### Typography & Fonts
*   **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `Roboto`, sans-serif
    *   *Fallback Strategy:* If `Inter` or `SF Pro Display` is unavailable, fall back to system-ui fonts (`-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, sans-serif).
*   **Typography Hierarchy:**
    *   **Location Label:** 12px, Regular, `#FFFFFF` (with a slight drop shadow for readability over image).
    *   **Heading (Ice Caves):** 20px, Bold (700), `#FFFFFF`.
    *   **Price (Header Card):** 18px, Semi-bold (600), `#FFFFFF`.
    *   **Section Header (About the place):** 14px, Semi-bold (600), `#1A1A1A`.
    *   **Body Text:** 12px, Regular, Line-height: 1.4, `#6E7681`.
    *   **Labels (Length / Persons):** 13px, Medium (500), `#1A1A1A`.
    *   **Values / Numbers:** 13px, Semi-bold (600), `#1A1A1A`.
    *   **Bottom Total Price:** 16px, Bold (700), `#1A1A1A`.
    *   **Bottom Subtext (Tickets):** 10px, Regular, `#6E7681`.
    *   **Button Text (Book Now):** 14px, Semi-bold (600), `#FFFFFF`.

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Screen Composition

*   **View Type:** Full-screen mobile viewport (aspect ratio approximately 9:19.5, centered on a split-tone gradient/solid background).
*   **Border Radius (Device Screen Frame):** `40px` rounded corners with subtle drop shadow (`0px 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`).
*   **Internal Padding:** 
    *   Horizontal: `20px`
    *   Vertical: `24px`

---

## 3. Component Breakdown (Detailed Specs)

### 3.1. Hero Image Card Component
*   **Dimensions:** Full width minus horizontal padding (`~312px` width assuming a `352px` card width), height: `~220px`.
*   **Border Radius:** `24px`.
*   **Content & Overlay:**
    *   **Image Description:** A dramatic, high-resolution landscape photograph of an ice cave interior in Iceland. Deep royal blue and turquoise translucent ice walls curve overhead, with a glowing bright blue exit or light source in the background where a solitary human figure in dark outdoor gear stands on rocky terrain. The image has a rich, cool color grading.
    *   **Placement & Rotation:** Cover fit, centered, no rotation (`0deg`), backgroundless (self-contained raster graphic).
    *   **Gradient Overlay:** A bottom-to-top linear gradient (`rgba(0,0,0,0.6)` to `transparent`) overlaid on the bottom half of the image to ensure high contrast for text.
    *   **Top-Left Icon:** Back arrow icon inside a semi-translucent circular badge (`32px` diameter, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` blur backdrop).
    *   **Bottom-Left Text Group:**
        *   Location icon (pin) + " Iceland"
        *   Title: "Ice Caves"
        *   Price: "$400"

---

### 3.2. "About the Place" Section
*   **Margin Top:** `16px`
*   **Heading:** "About the place" (Font size: 14px, weight: 600)
*   **Description Paragraph:** 
    *   *Text:* "Start an adventure with a 3-day journey in Iceland and explore the caves"
    *   *Styling:* 12px, color `#6E7681`, line-height: 1.5.

---

### 3.3. Details & Selectors (Length & Persons)
*   **Container:** Vertical stack with `12px` spacing between rows.
*   **Row 1 (Length):**
    *   Left: Icon (clock/calendar) + "Length" label (`13px`).
    *   Right: "14 Days" (`13px`, bold, `#1A1A1A`).
*   **Row 2 (Persons):**
    *   Left: Icon (user group) + "Persons" label (`13px`).
    *   Right: Stepper control component:
        *   Minus button (`-`): Dark circle, white icon, size `24x24px`.
        *   Value: `2` (Bold, `13px`).
        *   Plus button (`+`): Dark circle, white icon, size `24x24px`.

---

### 3.4. Participants / Avatar Stack
*   **Layout:** Horizontal overlapping row (negative margin `space: -8px` between avatars).
*   **Avatars:** 4 circular profile pictures showing different travelers (diameters: `28px`, border: `2px solid #FFFFFF`).
*   **Counter Badge:** A circular badge (`28px` diameter) matching the avatar size, featuring the text `+23` in `#1A1A1A` on an `#E1E8ED` background.

---

### 3.5. Bottom Action Bar (Footer)
*   **Position:** Fixed at the bottom of the card content area with top border separator or clear spacing.
*   **Left Side (Price Display):**
    *   Main Price: `$800` (Bold, 18px).
    *   Subtext: `(2 Tickets)` (Regular, 11px, `#6E7681`).
*   **Right Side (CTA Button):**
    *   **Text:** "Book Now"
    *   **Background:** `#1A1A1A` (Solid black/dark charcoal).
    *   **Text Color:** `#FFFFFF`
    *   **Dimensions:** Height `48px`, width `~140px`.
    *   **Border Radius:** `24px` (fully rounded pill shape).
    *   **Shadow:** Subtle elevation shadow (`0px 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.15)`).

---

## 4. Accessibility & Interactive States
*   **Buttons (Stepper & Book Now):**
    *   *Hover State:* Slight darkening or scaling (`transform: scale(1.02)`).
    *   *Active/Pressed State:* `transform: scale(0.98)`.
*   **Contrast Ratio:** All text elements meet WCAG AA standards against their respective background fills.
