# UI/UX Specification & Design System: VOXEN Headphone Landing Page

This document provides a comprehensive design specification and structural blueprint for recreating the VOXEN headphone landing page. The layout uses a futuristic, minimalist, high-tech aesthetic dominated by light gray tones, accented with vibrant orange lighting effects and translucent structural elements.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Color Palette
*   **Background (Light Mode):** `#F4F4F6` (Base canvas color), `#FFFFFF` (Card/Platform surfaces)
*   **Primary Text / Brand Black:** `#111111`
*   **Muted Text / Secondary:** `#666666`, `#888888`
*   **Accent Color (Vibrant Orange / Glow):** `#FF6600` / `#FF7A1A`
*   **Translucent Elements / UI Overlays:** White/Glassmorphic fills with backdrop blur (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` with `blur(10px)`) and subtle border strokes.

### Typography
*   **Primary Font Family:** Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `sans-serif`
*   **Scale & Hierarchy:**
    *   **Hero Background Watermark ("Feel Sound"):** ~180px–220px, Bold (700/800), extremely low opacity (~0.05) or cutout style masking.
    *   **Main Hero Header ("Next-level audio. Designed for you."):** 42px – 48px, Bold (700), Line height: 1.1, Color: `#111111`.
    *   **Navigation Links:** 13px, Medium (500), Uppercase tracking.
    *   **Price Display ("$399.99"):** 36px, Bold (700), Color: `#FF6600`.
    *   **Feature Callouts / Badges:** 11px–12px, Medium (500), Dark text on frosted glass pills.

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Components

### 2.1 Navigation Bar (`<header>`)
*   **Position:** Fixed or sticky top, spanning full width with generous padding (e.g., `40px` horizontal).
*   **Left Section:**
    *   Logo: Black rounded pill containing a white abstract geometric icon alongside the bold wordmark **VOXEN**.
*   **Center Section:**
    *   Links: `FEATURES`, `ABOUT DEVICE`, `SHIPPING`, `COLORS` (with an active orange notification badge reading `3`).
*   **Right Section:**
    *   Language Toggle: `EN` / `JP` pill selector.
    *   CTA Button: Black rounded pill button reading **CHOOSE YOUR STYLE**.

### 2.2 Hero Section & Central Product Showcase
*   **Background Typography:** Large, staggered typography reading "Feel" and "ound" positioned horizontally across the midsection behind the product rendering.
*   **Left Column (Text & Primary CTA):**
    *   **Headline:** "Next-level audio. Designed for you."
    *   **Primary Button:** Solid orange pill button (`#FF6600`), white text, reading `• PRE-ORDER NOW •`.
    *   **Bottom Left Card (Video / Interactive Element):**
        *   Compact card showing a preview thumbnail with a play icon button and the tag `Adaptive Fit`.
        *   Supporting caption text: *"Step into the future of sound with ultra-clear audio, bold design, and effortless comfort."*
*   **Center Column (3D Product Stage):**
    *   **Product:** Premium over-ear headphones featuring translucent clear acrylic earcups, exposed internal circuitry, and glowing orange LED rings.
    *   **Podium:** Minimalist light-gray 3D cubic platform anchoring the product.
    *   **Interactive Callout Tags (Floating Pills with pointer lines):**
        1.  `Soft and flexible embouchure` (pointing to headband cushion)
        2.  `Sturdy body` (pointing to structural hinge)
        3.  `Al sound helper` (pointing to earcup accent)
*   **Right Column (Secondary Info & Pricing):**
    *   **Sub-headline:** `FEEL EVERY BEAT.`
    *   **Body Copy:** *"Let the world disappear as you dive into immersive sound. Lightweight, elegant, powerful."*
    *   **Pricing Block:**
        *   Small label: `From`
        *   Price: `$399.99` (styled in vibrant orange)
        *   Tax notice: `Taxes free`
    *   **Editorial Image:** High-contrast portrait of a model wearing the VOXEN headphones.

### 2.3 Bottom Feature Bar & Footer Anchor
*   **Feature Pills Container:** A centered row of frosted glass pills detailing core specs:
    *   `Noise Canceling+`
    *   `Bluetooth 5.4`
    *   `Hi-res sound`
    *   `Long battery life`
    *   `Touch control`
*   **Bottom Right Action:**
    *   Black square/pill button with white text reading `LEARN MORE` accompanied by a downward arrow icon (`↓`).

---

## 3. Micro-Interactions & Animation Guidelines (For Developers)

1.  **Product Float Animation:** 
    *   Apply a gentle floating CSS keyframe animation (`translateY` offset of $\pm 8\text{px}$ over a 4-second duration, ease-in-out infinite) to the central headphone asset and its connected callout tags.
2.  **Glowing Pulse Effect:** 
    *   The orange rings inside the headphone earcups should feature a subtle opacity pulse animation (`box-shadow` spread and opacity fading between `0.6` and `1`).
3.  **Hover States:**
    *   Navigation links shift to a darker shade or underline on hover.
    *   Buttons scale up slightly (`scale(1.02)`) with smooth transitions (`transition: all 0.3s ease`).
    *   Feature pills elevate with a deeper backdrop blur and subtle shadow on mouse enter.
