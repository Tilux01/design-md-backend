# Design Specification: Street Ballers Interface

## 1. Overview & Layout
* **Layout Mode**: Fullscreen, edge-to-edge interface.
* **Base Background**: Pale bone/cream (`#EDEAE4`).
* **Grid**: Two-column layout with a top navigation bar.
  * **Left Column**: Floating event card overlay (approx. 380px–420px width).
  * **Right Column**: Hero typography and 3D textured graphic element.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Primary Background**: `#EDEAE4` (warm light gray)
* **Surface/Cards**: `#FFFFFF` / `#F8F7F4` with soft drop shadows
* **Accent / Olive Dark**: `#262522` (primary buttons, dark pill container)
* **Muted Olive Green**: `#9B987C` (active tab highlight)
* **Text Primary**: `#181716` (near-black)
* **Text Secondary**: `#7B7771` (neutral warm gray)
* **Text Inverted**: `#FFFFFF`

---

## 3. Typography
* **Hero Headline**:
  * *Font*: Anton, Impact, or Bebas Neue (Fallback: `Arial Black`, `sans-serif`)
  * *Weight*: 900 / Ultra-Bold Condensed
  * *Case*: UPPERCASE
  * *Tracking*: Tight (`-0.03em`)
* **Body / UI Labels**:
  * *Font*: Inter, SF Pro Display, or Helvetica Neue (Fallback: `system-ui`, `sans-serif`)
  * *Weights*: Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (700)
* **Signature/Tag Mark**:
  * *Font*: Street graffiti style / brush script SVG.

---

## 4. Header Navigation
* **Position**: Top, pinned with horizontal spacing.
* **Center Segmented Control**:
  * Capsule container (`#262522`, border-radius: 9999px, padding: 4px).
  * **Active Tab ("News")**: Olive-tan background (`#9B987C`), dark icon & text, rounded pill.
  * **Inactive Tab ("Map")**: Transparent background, white text and pin icon.
* **Right Control ("My profile")**:
  * Pill button (`#FFFFFF`), light border (`#E0DDD7`), dark icon and text.

---

## 5. Components

### Left Column: Event Card
* **Container**: High-radius card (`rounded-3xl`), white background, subtle elevation.
* **Media Header**:
  * High-contrast black-and-white image of a basketball player shooting outdoor at night under a hoop.
  * Overlay Tag: "Downtown" (semi-translucent dark pill).
  * Overlay Text: "Boooys night out" (Bold white) and "May 12, 9 PM" (subtext).
* **Card Body**:
  * **Host Row**: Circular portrait avatar + "Matthew Walker" (Host).
  * **Attendees Row**: 4 stacked overlapping avatar circles + `+3` counter bubble.
  * **Location**: Bold title "Shōten", subtitle "127 Sakura Ave, Sakyo-ku, Kyoto".
  * **Secondary Action**: Full-width pill button, "Check on map" (light gray, pin icon).
  * **Primary Action**: Full-width pill button, "Join game" (charcoal black `#262522`, white text).
  * **Footer Status**: "✓ Finished" (muted green/gray caption).

### Right Column: Hero Section
* **Sub-header**: "JOIN GAME" (letter-spaced, 12px, bold, secondary color).
* **Display Headline**:
  ```text
  FIND YOUR
  STREET
  BALLERS
  ```
* **Sub-badge**: "У68°" directly beneath the headline.
* **Graphic Asset (Basketball)**:
  * *Description*: 3D render of an off-white/cream textured leather basketball with dark gray recessed ribs. Backgroundless (transparent PNG).
  * *Placement*: Anchored bottom-center/left, partially masked behind the left event card, cropped at the bottom edge.
  * *Rotation*: Neutral frontal tilt, showing primary seam intersections.
* **Watermark / Tag**:
  * *Description*: Stylized, backgroundless black graffiti scribble tag ("Shafk").
  * *Placement*: Bottom-right corner.
