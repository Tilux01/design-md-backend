# UI/UX Specification: MODUS Real Estate Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Reset

### Colors
- **Primary Text / Brand:** `#111111` (Deep Charcoal Black)
- **Background Base (Full Screen):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White top section) to natural landscape photographic backdrop (bottom section).
- **Secondary Background/Overlay:** Subtle dark gradient overlays integrated via the mountain/sky photography.
- **Accent (UI Badges/Buttons):** 
  - Active Nav / Pill Button Fill: `#111111` with text `#FFFFFF`
  - Secondary Inactive Pill / Tag: White transluscent glassmorphism background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`) with `#111111` text.

### Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** `Montserrat`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, sans-serif
  - *Fallback Stack:* `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, "Open Sans", "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif`
- **Headings / Hero Brand Text ("MODUS"):**
  - Font Weight: 900 (Black / Heavy Ultra-Bold)
  - Text Transform: Uppercase
  - Letter Spacing: Tight (approx. `-0.03em`)
- **Body & Navigation Links:**
  - Font Weight: 400 (Regular) to 500 (Medium)
  - Text Transform: Capitalized / Normal

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Responsive Behavior

- **Display Type:** Full-screen CSS Grid / Flexbox layout (`width: 100vw; height: 100vh; overflow-x: hidden;`).
- **Container Max-Width:** `1440px` centered with fluid padding (`clamp(24px, 5vw, 80px)`).
- **Z-Index Layering System:**
  - `z-index: 0`: Background imagery (sky, mountains, modern house).
  - `z-index: 1`: Hero text (`MODUS` large typography split/layered behind the roofline).
  - `z-index: 2`: Interactive foreground elements (Navbar, Floating Tags, Bottom Footer labels).

---

## 3. Section-by-Section Breakdown

### A. Navigation Bar (`header`)
- **Position:** `fixed` or `sticky`, top `0`, `width: 100%`, high z-index.
- **Layout:** Flexbox, Space-Between (Left: Menu Links, Right: Contact / Messengers).
- **Left Group (Nav Items):**
  - Items: `Главная` (Home), `О нас` (About), `Проекты` (Projects), `Отзывы` (Reviews), `Контакты` (Contacts)
  - Style: Active item ("Главная") is housed inside a pill-shaped button background (`#111111`, border-radius `50px`, text color `#FFFFFF`). Non-active items are dark gray (`#333333`) transitioning to black on hover.
- **Right Group (Utility Contacts):**
  - Phone Icon + Number: `8 (812) 436-06-16` (Font-size: `14px`, Medium weight).
  - Social Icons: WhatsApp icon (green brand circle), Telegram icon (blue brand circle). Circular icon buttons (`36px` x `36px`).

---

### B. Hero Section (`section.hero`)

#### 1. Top Sub-headings (Micro-copy)
- Distributed across the top of the giant typography:
  - **Top-Left:** `Мечтай масштабно, живи стильно.` (Dream big, live in style.)
  - **Center-Top:** `Архитектурный шедевр.` (Architectural masterpiece.)
  - **Top-Right:** `Пространство для жизни и отдыха.` (Space for life and relaxation.)
- **Typography specs:** Font-size `12px`–`14px`, muted dark color, uppercase/normal tracking.

#### 2. Giant Typography ("MODUS")
- **Content:** `MODUS`
- **Styling:** Massive font scale (approx. `18vw` to fit viewport width cleanly).
- **Visual Integration:** The letters **D** and **U** intersect and cut behind the sharp architectural roofline of the modern villa photo, creating a layered 3D depth effect.
- **Color:** Solid `#111111` or dark charcoal matte finish.

---

### C. Featured Image / Visual Centerpiece (`div.hero-visual`)
- **Imagery:** High-end architectural photography of a contemporary single-story wooden/glass luxury villa set against a dramatic mountain twilight background.
- **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Warm interior golden lighting glowing through floor-to-ceiling glass walls, contrasting with cool blue hour mountain backdrop.
- **Foreground:** Rocky alpine terrain with natural ground cover at the base of the image.

---

### D. Bottom Overlays & Tags (`div.hero-footer-tags`)

#### 1. Filter / Category Pills (Bottom-Left)
- **Items:** 
  - `Коттеджи` (Cottages)
  - `Мини-дом` (Mini-house)
  - `Эко-вилла` (Eco-villa — *Active/Highlighted with white solid pill background*)
- **Styling:** Pill buttons (`border-radius: 30px`), padding `8px 20px`, glassmorphism effect over the terrain.

#### 2. Brand Watermark (Bottom-Right)
- **Content:** `MODUS` (Clean modern logotype in white, alongside a subtle geometric icon).
- **Position:** Absolute, pinned near the bottom-right corner of the architectural frame.

---

## 4. Interactive States & Animations

### Hover Effects
- **Navigation Links:** Smooth opacity shift (`opacity: 0.7` -> `1`) or underline micro-animation.
- **Social & Phone Icons:** Subtle scale-up transform (`transform: scale(1.08)`) with transition duration `0.25s ease`.
- **Category Pills:** Background brightness shift on hover.

### Entrance Animations (CSS Keyframes / Intersection Observer)
- **Page Load Sequence:**
  1. **Background & Imagery:** Fade-in (`opacity: 0` to `1`, duration `1.2s`).
  2. **Giant Typography ("MODUS"):** Slide-up from bottom with opacity fade (`translateY(40px)` to `translateY(0)`, duration `1.0s`, cubic-bezier easing).
  3. **Header & Navigation:** Slide-down from top (`translateY(-20px)` to `translateY(0)`, duration `0.8s`).
  4. **Micro-copy & Floating Badges:** Delayed fade-in (`delay: 0.5s`).
