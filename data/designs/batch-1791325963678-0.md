# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Glassmorphism & High-End Neomorphic Fintech SaaS.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-end financial dashboard density utilizing precise 8pt scaling, layered translucent glass cards (`backdrop-filter: blur`), floating mobile UI compositions, and heavy contrast with deep navy-to-black canvas gradients.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-tier depth hierarchy utilizing dark frosted glass (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`), subtle inner borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and soft ambient lighting orbs simulating deep 3D physical stacking.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges (`tracking-widest`), ambient radial blue background glows (`bg-gradient-to-tr from-cyan-500/20 to-blue-600/30`), frosted glassmorphism cards (`backdrop-blur-xl`), and crisp white monogram logos (`A` geometric glyph).
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG path.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-400 inline-block"></span>`.
