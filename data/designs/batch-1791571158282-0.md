# Design Specification: Be.run Health & Fitness Dashboard

This document provides a comprehensive design specification for replicating the "Be.run" health and fitness dashboard UI. It covers layout, typography, color palette, components, and responsive behaviors to ensure an exact visual and functional clone.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas

*   **View Type:** Full-screen web/desktop application dashboard (designed for a centered, padded card layout on a neutral canvas).
*   **Canvas Background Color:** `#EBE6DF` (Soft warm grayish-beige / oatmeal tone).
*   **Dashboard Container Background:** `#F8F6F2` (A slightly lighter, cream-white surface resting on the canvas).
*   **Border Radius (Dashboard Container):** `24px` to `32px` soft rounded corners with a subtle outer drop shadow.
*   **Layout Grid Structure:** 
    *   Fixed Left Navigation Bar (`~80px` width).
    *   Main Content Area featuring a top header and a 2-column asynchronous grid system for widgets.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Font Family
*   **Font Stack:** `"Inter", "SF Pro Display", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
*   **Fallback Strategy:** Sans-serif system fonts stack prioritizing modern, clean geometric grotesk typefaces.

### Typographic Hierarchy

| Element | Font Size | Font Weight | Line Height | Color / Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Main Greeting ("Hi, Amanda!")** | `24px` / `1.5rem` | Bold (`700`) | Normal | `#1A1A1A` |
| **Subtitle ("Let's take a look...")** | `13px` / `0.8125rem` | Regular (`400`) | Normal | `#666666` |
| **Widget Headers ("Your Workout...")** | `16px` / `1rem` | Semi-bold (`600`) | Normal | `#1A1A1A` |
| **Card / Sub-headings** | `14px` / `0.875rem` | Medium (`500`) | Normal | `#1A1A1A` |
| **Body / Meta Text** | `12px` / `0.75rem` | Regular (`400`) | Normal | `#777777` |
| **Numeric Metrics (Large)** | `18px` – `28px` | Bold (`700`) | Tight | `#1A1A1A` / Custom |

---

## 3. Color Palette

### Neutrals & Surfaces
*   `#F8F6F2`: Main Dashboard Background (Card surface).
*   `#EBE6DF`: Outer Canvas Background.
*   `#1E2229`: Dark Charcoal (Used for the Calendar Widget background and primary active icons).
*   `#2A2D35`: Secondary dark surface color (Calendar grid card background).
*   `#333333`: Primary Text & Icons.
*   `#8A8A8A`: Secondary Muted Text.

### Brand & Accent Colors
*   `#FFCC00` (Yellow/Gold): Primary Accent. Used for active indicators, calendar highlights, and the main calorie/metric blob.
*   `#FF5E57` (Coral/Red): Secondary Accent. Used for calories burned blob and streak progress indicators.
*   `#111111` (Near Black): Dark metric blob (Activity time).
*   `#181A20`: Navigation bar background (Deep charcoal-black pill).

---

## 4. Component Specifications

### 4.1. Left Navigation Sidebar
*   **Container:** A vertical pill-shaped floating sidebar (`#181A20`), positioned on the far left.
*   **Top Branding:** The "Be.run" logo featuring a minimalist stylized runner/tree icon in white, with "Be.run" text underneath in bold white.
*   **Navigation Icons:** 
    *   Vertical stack of outlined iconography (Home, Analytics, Calendar/Schedule, Notifications, Settings).
    *   **Active State:** The Home icon is highlighted inside a solid yellow circular pill (`#FFCC00`) with a dark icon.
    *   **Bottom Section:** Includes an action icon and a user profile avatar.
*   **User Avatar:** Circular cropped portrait image of a woman with short dark hair, wearing a blue athletic top.

### 4.2. Top Header Bar
*   **Left:** Greeting text ("Hi, Amanda!") stacked above the subtitle.
*   **Center-Right:** Search bar ("Search for health data")
    *   **Background:** White (`#FFFFFF`) with rounded pill corners (`30px`).
    *   **Icon:** Left-aligned magnifying glass search icon in gray.
    *   **Placeholder Text Color:** `#A0A0A0`, size `13px`.
*   **Right:** "Upgrade" button.
    *   **Background:** Dark charcoal/black (`#1E2229`).
    *   **Text:** White, bold, `13px`.
    *   **Shape:** Pill shape with generous horizontal padding.

---

### 4.3. Widget 1: "Your Workout Results for Today" (Hero Card)
*   **Card Background:** Warm muted taupe/khaki container (`#DED8CE` or semi-transparent gradient mesh).
*   **Top Right Icon:** Small dark square button with a yellow suitcase/bag icon (`#1E2229`).
*   **Data Visualization (Blob / Venn Diagram Graphic):**
    *   A frosted-glass style organic overlapping gradient shape container.
    *   **Black Circle:** Represents "Activity time" (`2.30 hours`), solid dark charcoal `#222222` with white text.
    *   **Red/Coral Blob:** Represents "Calories burned" (`850 kcal`), warm gradient with white text.
    *   **Yellow Blob:** Represents "Calories intake" (`1.875 kcal`), bright yellow/orange gradient with dark text.
*   **Legend (Bottom Left):**
    *   Three pill-shaped indicators matching the blob colors corresponding to: *Calories intake* (yellow), *Calories burned* (red), and *Activity time* (dark gray).

---

### 4.4. Widget 2: "Your Training Days" (Calendar Widget)
*   **Card Background:** Dark charcoal/black card (`#1C1E24`).
*   **Header:** 
    *   Title: "Your Training Days" in white text (`15px` bold).
    *   Dropdown: "June" with a dropdown chevron on the top right.
*   **Calendar Grid:**
    *   Week headers (`M T W T F S S`) in muted gray (`#666666`).
    *   Days numbered `1` through `30`.
    *   **Active/Highlighted Days:** 
        *   Day `1` and Day `5` are solid yellow circles (`#FFCC00`) with dark numbers.
        *   Completed past days feature subtle dark grey background fills with white text.
        *   Future/scheduled days feature subtle grey text.
*   **Footer Legend:**
    *   Small color-coded dots indicating: Current day (yellow ring/dot), Done (solid dot), Scheduled (grey dot).

---

### 4.5. Widget 3: "Steps for Today"
*   **Layout:** White card surface.
*   **Content:**
    *   Header: "Steps for Today" with subtitle "Keep your body toned".
    *   Button: "Change Goal" with a pencil/edit icon on a dark circular badge.
*   **Progress Indicator:** 
    *   Circular arc progress bar with a gradient stroke (Orange-to-Red).
    *   Center Text: "Goal" label above **"8.500"** in bold large typography.
    *   Target badge: A small floating pill reading `5.201` steps.

---

### 4.6. Widget 4: "My Habits"
*   **Header:** "My Habits" on the left; **"+ Add New"** button on the right (rounded black pill with a white plus icon).
*   **List Items:** Four horizontal habit tracking rows:
    1.  **Stretching** (Trainer: Alice McCain) — Progress: `9/12` sessions completed, represented by a row of red and grey pill segments (streak bar). Has a 3-dot menu icon on the far right.
    2.  **Yoga training** (Trainer: Jennifer Lubin) — Progress: `6/10` sessions completed.
    3.  **Massage** (Masseur: Johnson Cooper) — Progress: `4/8` sessions completed.
    4.  **Ab exercises** — Progress: `8/10` sessions completed.
*   **Avatars:** Each habit row features a small circular profile thumbnail of the respective trainer/masseur on the left of the title.

---

### 4.7. Widget 5: "Weight Loss Plan"
*   **Header:** "Weight Loss Plan" with completion status indicator on the right (**"68% Completed"**).
*   **Progress Visualization:**
    *   Horizontal gradient track bar from `58 kg` (left) to `50 kg` (right).
    *   Current weight pointer/bubble floating above the bar pointing to **`53.2 kg`**.
    *   Track includes small milestone marker ticks along the bar.

---

## 5. Image & Asset Descriptions

*   **Logo Icon ("Be.run"):** Minimalist vector icon combining abstract runner legs/stride motion lines with a leaf or geometric vertex.
*   **User Profile Avatar (Sidebar Bottom):** Professional headshot of a woman with dark hair tied back, wearing a blue athletic jersey, smiling gently.
*   **Trainer Avatars (My Habits rows):** 
    *   Row 1: Headshot of a smiling woman with blonde hair.
    *   Row 2: Headshot of a woman with dark shoulder-length hair.
    *   Row 3: Headshot of a man with short brown hair and stubble.
    *   Row 4: Generic silhouette/placeholder avatar icon inside a light beige circular badge.
