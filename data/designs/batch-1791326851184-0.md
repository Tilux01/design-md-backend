# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Glassmorphism / Clean Enterprise SaaS Operations Dashboard.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High information density balanced by structured card containerization, thin 1px borders, subtle dark surface gradients, and deep shadow elevation.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered surface hierarchy using dark translucent backgrounds (`rgba` values), backdrop blur (`blur-xl`), and crisp slate borders (`#1e293b`) with ambient status glows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient glow orbs, glassmorphic panels (`backdrop-blur-md`), live pulsing status indicators (`animate-pulse`).
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `px-3 py-1`, font-size `0.75rem`, font-weight `600`, tracking `wider`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.1)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block animate-pulse"></span>`.
