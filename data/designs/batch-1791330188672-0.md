# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Neutral Editorial / Minimalist Dark Neutral System.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-whitespace canvas layout with balanced paddings, centering focus entirely on subtle textural cues and unified grayscale tones.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Single-layer flat and neutral tonal layers with low-contrast borders instead of heavy drop shadows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Minimalist gray canvas, clear tone staging.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`
