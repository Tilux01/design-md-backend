# UI/UX Specification & Design Document

## 1. Global Styles & Design System

### 1.1 Typography
*   **Primary Typeface:** *Playfair Display* (or high-end editorial serif fallback: `Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif`). Used for all brand titles, feature section headings, and primary editorial callouts.
*   **Secondary Typeface:** *Inter* (or clean sans-serif fallback: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`). Used for navigation links, descriptive text paragraphs, metadata, buttons, and sub-headings.
*   **Font Weights Used:** 
    *   Regular (400) - Body copy, standard UI labels.
    *   Medium (500) - Navigation tabs, category tags.
    *   Bold (700) / Black (900) - Hero headline statements (`IT'S A MANIFESTO TO BE WORN!`).

### 1.2 Color Palette
*   **Background (Canvas):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) and subtle off-white variations `#F8F9FA` for product grid card containers.
*   **Primary Text & Branding:** `#1A1A1A` (Near Black / Charcoal).
*   **Accent Brand Color:** `#008B74` (Deep Editorial Teal / Green) — used for primary call-to-action buttons (`READ MORE`, `MAN`, `WOMAN`, `CHILD`), active state pills, and interactive highlights.
*   **Promotional Background Fills:** 
    *   `#FFD700` (Vibrant Yellow) for Man, Woman, Child collection highlights.
    *   `#0A0A14` (Midnight Dark Navy/Black) for the footer interactive galaxy section.

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Component Architecture

### 2.1 Header / Navigation Bar
*   **Layout:** Fixed top banner, centered navigation links with left-aligned brand logo and right-aligned utility actions.
*   **Left:** Brand logo wordmark (`MOTTO` in uppercase bold serif tracking).
*   **Center:** Navigation links (`Hoodie`, `Collection`, `Contacts`).
*   **Right:** Language selector (e.g., `EN`) and Cart icon link (`CART (0)`).

### 2.2 Hero Section
*   **Top Subheading Tag:** Small pill badge containing an icon and the word `HOODIE`.
*   **Headline:** Massive editorial serif statement: *"IT’S A MANIFESTO TO BE WORN!"* centered on the page with generous line-height and letter-spacing.
*   **Introductory Paragraph:** A 2-line brand mission statement explaining the philosophy of the garments.
*   **CTA Button:** Pill-shaped solid teal button (`#008B74`) reading `READ MORE` with white uppercase tracking text.
*   **Hero Carousel / Model Showcase:** 
    *   A horizontal scrolling sequence of 5 distinct cards showcasing models wearing different colored hoodies (Yellow, Navy, Purple, Green, Lime).
    *   Cards feature subtle rounded corners and clean white backgrounds floating above the primary canvas.

### 2.3 "Discover Collection" Section
*   **Section Heading:** Centered serif heading `Discover Collection`.
*   **Grid Layout:** 3-column asymmetric layout highlighting demographic targets: `MAN`, `WOMAN`, `CHILD`.
*   **Card Structure:**
    *   Solid vivid yellow background blocks (`#FFD700`).
    *   High-contrast full-bleed portrait photography of models.
    *   Hover/Active teal badge positioned at the bottom center of each card (`MAN`, `WOMAN`, `CHILD`).

### 2.4 "Featured Collection" Section
*   **Section Heading:** Centered serif heading `Featured Collection`.
*   **Filter Tabs:** A row of pill-shaped UI toggles (`Regular`, `Oversize`, etc.) above the product grid.
*   **Product Grid:** 
    *   Multi-row grid containing minimal product mockup cards against light grey/white backdrops.
    *   Each card features a product preview, product title (e.g., `NYC`, `Classic`), and subtle price / quick-add affordances.

### 2.5 Footer / Interactive Galaxy Section
*   **Background:** Deep dark navy/black (`#0A0A14`).
*   **Interactive Graphic:** Circular constellation/orbit layout with glowing profile avatars surrounding a central badge reading `ENJOY HOODIE`.
*   **Brand Statement & Signoff:** Centered editorial text block with a secondary brand wordmark (`MOTTO`), newsletter/search subscription input field, and bottom-aligned utility links.

---

## 3. Motion & Animation Specification

*   **Hero Model Carousel:** Infinite or touch-drag smooth horizontal translation with subtle scaling effect on the active center card (`transform: scale(1.05)`).
*   **CTA Button Hover States:** 
    *   *Transition:* `all 0.25s ease-in-out`
    *   *Hover:* Slight vertical translation (`translateY(-2px)`), increase shadow elevation (`box-shadow: 0px 8px 20px rgba(0, 139, 116, 0.25)`).
*   **Product Grid Hover:** Card background deepens slightly with a smooth zoom-in transition applied to the product mockup image (`scale(1.03)` over `0.3s`).
*   **Galaxy Footer Animation:** Continuous, subtle floating/pulsing keyframe animation on the orbit avatars to give a dynamic, living community feel.
