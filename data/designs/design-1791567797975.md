# UI Design & Implementation Specification: "Sarina" Wellness Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
* **Viewport:** Full-screen layout with a clean, light off-white/very light gray background (`#F0F3F6` or similar soft slate tint).
* **Grid System:** Multi-column layout featuring a navigation header, a primary hero text area on the left, a central featured hero image card, and a right-hand sidebar with secondary offerings and community teasers.
* **Containers:** Rounded card overlays use soft backdrop blurs (glassmorphism effect with subtle white borders and low-opacity fills).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
* **Primary Font Family:** Clean, geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, or *SF Pro Display*).
* **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
* **Headings:** Bold weights (700/800) for high-impact titles (e.g., "Mindfullness", "More Calm", "Explore Our Offerings").
* **Body Text:** Regular weight (400), highly legible at small scales, styled in dark slate/charcoal gray for optimal contrast against the light background.

---

## 3. Color Palette
* **Background:** Soft pale gray/off-white (`#F0F4F8`).
* **Primary Accent (Brand Green):** Deep emerald green (`#0F6D38` or `#12783D`) used for active buttons, branding text, and pill indicators.
* **Text Colors:** 
  * Primary Dark: Deep slate/near-black (`#1E293B`).
  * Secondary Muted: Medium gray (`#64748B`).
* **Card Overlays:** Semi-transparent white with soft drop shadows and subtle border outlines.

---

## 4. Component Breakdown & Detailed Specs

### A. Navigation Bar (`header`)
* **Logo:** Left-aligned brand identifier featuring a green lotus flower icon followed by the brand name "Sarina" in bold dark text.
* **Center Navigation:** Pill-shaped floating container holding menu items:
  * "Home" (Highlighted inside a solid green pill with white text).
  * "Product", "Service", "About" (Standard unselected items in dark/muted text).
* **Right Actions:** 
  * "Log in" text/button.
  * "Sign in" solid green pill button with white text.

### B. Hero Section (Left Column)
* **Tag:** Small pill badge reading `• meditation` with a green indicator dot.
* **Main Heading:** Multi-line typography block:
  * *"Mindfullness"* (Large, bold sans-serif).
  * *"— More Calm"* (Styled with an inline vertical separator and deep green text color).
  * *"Meaningful"* (Clean display typography).
* **Icon Row:** Three circular icon badges containing minimalist wellness icons (lotus, user meditation, mindfulness symbol).
* **Description Paragraph:** *"We offer a holistic approach to yoga and meditation, blending traditional practices with modern techniques to help you achieve harmony and inner peace."* preceded by a thin horizontal accent line.
* **Call-to-Action Buttons:**
  * Primary: Solid green button reading "Get started".
  * Secondary: White/light outlined pill button reading "Explore".

### C. Central Hero Image Card
* **Visual Element:** A high-quality photographic portrait of a person with eyes closed in a meditative yoga pose (lotus position) outdoors amidst lush green plants.
* **Floating Overlays:**
  * **Top Tags:** Small floating pills reading `• Meditation` and `• Mental Less`.
  * **Center Statistic Badge:** Semi-transparent glassmorphism card displaying `>240` in large bold numbers, followed by the subtext *"people improve their mentality here"*.
  * **Bottom Tags:** Two white pill badges reading `• Mental health` and `• Mindfulness`.

### D. Right Sidebar & Secondary Offerings
* **Section Header:** 
  * Title: "Explore Our Offerings" (Bold sans-serif).
  * Description: *"Whether you're a beginner or an experienced practitioner, our community is here to support your journey."*
* **Community Card:**
  * **Visual Element:** Photographic image of a person from behind sitting in a meditative pose facing a serene, misty nature landscape.
  * **Overlay:** Glassmorphism badge displaying `>240` with subtext *"Join our comunity now and strat with us"*.
  * **Tag:** Top-left badge reading `• Community`.
