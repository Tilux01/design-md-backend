# Design Specification (`design.md`)

## 1. Overview & Core Layout Strategy

This specification details the front-end layout, visual hierarchy, typography, color scheme, components, and media assets required to create an exact full-screen clone of the **Maryculter House** landing page.

*   **Viewport Target:** Full-bleed, edge-to-edge full screen (`100vw` × `100vh`).
*   **Frame Policy:** Completely borderless/frameless container. No outer margins, body padding, or browser-style frames.
*   **Overall Theme:** High-end luxury, historic, natural estate, dark moody overlay over dynamic background landscape media.
*   **Layout Engine:** CSS Flexbox (vertical distribution) combined with CSS Grid for the structured header top-bar.

## 3. Typography System

### 3.1 Primary Display Serif (Hero Headline)
*   **Font Family:** `"Cormorant Garamond"`, `"Playfair Display"`, `"Bodoni MT"`, `Georgia`, `serif` (Fallback: `serif`)
*   **Usage:** Central main header ("Hospitality since 1225")
*   **Properties:**
    *   `font-weight`: `300` or `400` (Light/Regular)
    *   `font-size`: ` clamp(2.75rem, 5vw, 5.5rem)`
    *   `letter-spacing`: `-0.01em`
    *   `line-height`: `1.1`
    *   `text-transform`: `none`

### 3.2 Secondary UI & Navigation Sans-Serif
*   **Font Family:** `"Montserrat"`, `"Jost"`, `"Futura"`, `"Helvetica Neue"`, `Arial`, `sans-serif` (Fallback: `sans-serif`)
*   **Usage:** Header navigation, top utility items, CTA labels, scroll text ("EXPLORE MARYCULTER HOUSE", "SCROLL TO DISCOVER")
*   **Properties (Navigation Links):**
    *   `font-weight`: `400` / `500`
    *   `font-size`: `0.725rem` (11px–12px)
    *   `letter-spacing`: `0.22em`
    *   `text-transform`: `uppercase`
*   **Properties (Sub-headline / Section Callouts):**
    *   `font-weight`: `400`
    *   `font-size`: `0.85rem` (13px–14px)
    *   `letter-spacing`: `0.28em`
    *   `text-transform`: `uppercase`

## 5. Detailed Component Specifications

### 5.1 Header Navigation Bar

The header is split into two stacked rows separated by ultra-thin subtle white borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).

#### Row 1: Utility & Brand Header
*   **Height:** `~60px`
*   **Layout:** 3-Column Grid (`1fr auto 1fr`)
*   **Left Cell:**
    *   Contains round vector outline icons for Instagram and Facebook.
    *   Icon size: `18px × 18px`, color: `#FFFFFF`, spacing: `16px` gap.
*   **Center Cell:**
    *   **Emblem:** Minimalist white line art crest featuring cross-knives/keys motif inside a shield shape (`~24px` high).
    *   **Brand Title:** "Maryculter House" stacked beneath the emblem.
    *   **Font:** Serif, uppercase, `font-size: 1.1rem`, `letter-spacing: 0.15em`.
*   **Right Cell:**
    *   Items: `GIFT VOUCHERS` and `BOOK NOW`.
    *   Style: Sans-serif uppercase, `font-size: 0.725rem`, `letter-spacing: 0.2em`. Separated by vertical grid lines or padded flex gaps (`24px`).

#### Row 2: Main Navigation Bar
*   **Height:** `~45px`
*   **Layout:** Horizontal Flexbox, centered items, equal spacing.
*   **Items (Left to Right):**
    `STAY`, `DINE`, `PACKAGES`, `EXPERIENCES`, `WEDDINGS`, `DISCOVER`, `FISHING`, `FESTIVE`, `MEET`, `OUR STORY`, `NEWS`, `CONTACT`, `COVID-19`
*   **Styling:**
    *   Color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`
    *   Font Size: `11px`
    *   Letter Spacing: `0.22em`
    *   Margin/Padding: `0 12px` per item.

---

### 5.2 Hero Core Section (Center Canvas)

Positioned strictly at the visual center of the hero screen using Flexbox column centering (`justify-content: center; align-items: center;`).

1.  **Main Headline:**
    *   Text: `"Hospitality since 1225"`
    *   Style: Serif font, white, centered, no drop shadow (or very subtle soft blur shadow `0 2px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.3)`).
2.  **Sub-headline Callout:**
    *   Text: `"EXPLORE MARYCULTER HOUSE"`
    *   Margin top: `24px`
    *   Style: Sans-serif, white, uppercase, widely spaced (`letter-spacing: 0.3em`).
3.  **Media Play Button Component:**
    *   Margin top: `32px`
    *   Shape: Circle (`border-radius: 50%`)
    *   Dimensions: `60px × 60px`
    *   Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`
    *   Background: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)` (Semi-transparent dark fill)
    *   Icon: Centered solid white right-facing triangle play arrow (`size: ~14px`).

---

### 5.3 Bottom Scroll Cue

Positioned at `bottom: 24px` from the screen base.

1.  **Label:**
    *   Text: `"SCROLL TO DISCOVER"`
    *   Style: Sans-serif, uppercase, `font-size: 10px`, `letter-spacing: 0.25em`, color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`.
2.  **Vertical Indicator Line:**
    *   Margin top: `12px`
    *   Dimensions: `width: 1px`, `height: 36px`
    *   Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`
    *   Animation (Optional): Slow downward fade/pulse pulse loop.

## 6. Imagery & Visual Media Assets Specification

### Background Media (Estate Aerial View)
*   **Type:** Full-screen background image or background HTML5 loop video element.
*   **Positioning:** `object-fit: cover`, `width: 100vw`, `height: 100vh`, fixed or relative absolute positioning behind contents (`z-index: -1`).
*   **Image Description:**
    *   **Composition:** High-angle drone/aerial twilight landscape photograph.
    *   **Features:** A wide, serene, reflective river (River Dee) sweeping diagonally through the center from top-center down toward the bottom left. On the right riverbank sits a traditional white historic Scottish hotel complex ("Maryculter House") with dark slated roof gables. Surrounding the river and house are dense, dark green trees and rolling forested countryside under a warm, soft sunset glow.
    *   **Tone/Mood:** Warm dusk lighting reflecting off the water surface, contrasted against deep, rich green foliage and dark muted shadows.
*   **Overlay Layer:**
    *   A continuous dark gradient layer (`background: linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(0,0,0,0.5) 0%, rgba(0,0,0,0.2) 50%, rgba(0,0,0,0.6) 100%)`) sits over the image to ensure high legibility for white text elements.

## 7. Responsive Behavior Guidelines

*   **Desktop (> 1024px):** Standard view as specified above with full horizontal menu.
*   **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Nav menu horizontally scrollable or collapsable into a mobile hamburger menu; font sizes for main title scale down to `3.2rem`.
*   **Mobile (< 767px):**
    *   Hide secondary nav list items; replace top left/right items with a single compact hamburger menu icon.
    *   Keep central Logo & Emblem.
    *   Main headline scales down dynamically (`clamp(2rem, 8vw, 3rem)`).
    *   Play button size shrinks slightly to `50px × 50px`.
