# Design Specification: "Thank You" Slide

## 1. Canvas & Palette

- **Dimensions:** 4:5 Aspect Ratio (1080 × 1350 px recommended) or 100vw / 100vh full screen.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Background: `#B8B9BA` (Neutral cool grey)
  - Text & Accents: `#0B0B0B` (Deep Charcoal / Black)
  - Organic Loop Highlight: `#F5EECD` (Pale warm cream / Butter yellow)

---

## 4. Components & Layering

### 1. Top Decorative Glyphs
- Three diamond-clover symbols (`❖ ❖ ❖` or 4-dot diamond cluster SVGs) horizontally centered.
- Gap: `12px` | Size: `10px` each | Color: `#0B0B0B`.

### 2. Center Hero Lockup
- **"THANK"**: Large display heading (~`120px` font size).
- **"YOU"**:
  - The "O" is replaced/underlaid by a tilted hand-drawn organic ring.
  - Ring dimensions: `~130px × 180px`, rotated ~`40deg` counter-clockwise.
  - Fill/Stroke: Soft cream stroke (`#F5EECD`), subtle interior shadow.
- **Side Label**: Aligned with the baseline/center of "U" on the right. Size: `~11px`, line-height: `1.2`.

### 3. Footer Section
- **Left Text**: 2-line layout, `~12px`, bold.
- **Right Icon**: Minimalist bookmark icon (`24px × 24px`), 2px stroke outline, no fill.

---

## 5. Animation (Optional Interaction)

1. **Page Load Entry:**
   - Background fade-in (`0.3s ease-out`).
   - "THANK YOU" scale + fade up: `translateY(16px)` to `0`, opacity `0` to `1` (`0.6s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
   - Cream loop: SVG path draw (`stroke-dashoffset`) or scale-in with a 0.2s delay.
2. **Bookmark Hover/Tap:**
   - Bookmark icon fills solid black on interaction: `transition: fill 0.2s ease`.
