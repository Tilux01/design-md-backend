# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Neo-Brutalist Minimalist Digital Studio Portfolio.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Extremely airy and expansive whitespace layout utilizing a structural background grid overlay, maintaining strong focal anchors on typographic scale and isolated modular cards.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Soft ambient drop shadows combined with subtle 1px border outlines on floating pill components and cards. Surfaces utilize flat warm-cream backgrounds (`#F5F4F0`) with raised interactive tiles featuring soft multi-layered depth (`0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Precision background grid lines (subtle intersecting grid lines spanning 100vw/100vh), monolithic condensed typography, floating tactile UI cards with heavy offset drop-shadows.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `8px 16px`, Font-size: `12px`, Font-weight: `700`, Tracking: `0.08em`, Border-radius: `9999px`, Background: `#1A1A1A`, Text color: `#FFFFFF`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Asterisk / Brand Star (*) -> Use `&#10033;` or `<span class="text-orange-600">*</span>`.
  - Arrow / Exploration (↘) -> Use `&#8600;` or SVG icon.
