# Design Specification: MachineFusion Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas

*   **View:** Full-screen layout (`width: 100vw; height: 100vh;` or `min-height: 100vh`).
*   **Background Color:** Soft, desaturated neutral gray (`#e5e8eb` or RGB: `229, 232, 235`), creating a modern, minimalist backdrop.
*   **Central Card Interface:** A rounded white card (`background-color: #ffffff`) container centered horizontally and vertically on the canvas. 
    *   **Border-Radius:** Large soft curves (approx. `24px` to `32px`).
    *   **Box Shadow:** Subtle, diffused shadow for depth against the gray background (`0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`).
    *   **Internal Padding:** Generous whitespace framing the content (`padding: 32px 48px`).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Font Family
*   **Primary:** `Inter`, sans-serif (Google Fonts)
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`

### Hierarchy & Styles

*   **Brand / Logo Text ("MachineFusion"):**
    *   **Font Weight:** Bold (`700`)
    *   **Size:** `16px` / `1rem`
    *   **Color:** `#111111`
*   **Navigation Pills / Tags:**
    *   **Font Weight:** Medium (`500`)
    *   **Size:** `12px` / `0.75rem`
    *   **Color:** `#333333`
*   **Hero Heading ("...naFusion / ...e th..."):**
    *   **Font Weight:** Bold (`700` / `800`)
    *   **Size:** `48px` to `64px` (Fluid scaling recommended)
    *   **Color:** `#1a1a1a`
*   **Body Copy ("Precision Engineering", "Bridging the gap between humanity..."):**
    *   **Font Weight:** Regular (`400`) for body, Semibold (`600`) for headers.
    *   **Size:** `13px` / `0.8125rem`
    *   **Color:** `#666666` (Muted dark gray for high readability).

---

## 3. UI Components & Structure

### A. Top Navigation Bar
*   **Position:** Absolute/Flex positioned at the top of the white container, spanning full width.
*   **Left Section:**
    *   **Logo Icon:** Minimalist abstract geometric mark (two interlocking curved strokes in black).
    *   **Brand Name:** "MachineFusion" set in clean sans-serif.
*   **Center Section:**
    *   **Interactive Menu Pill:** Pill-shaped toggle button (`background-color: #000000; color: #ffffff; border-radius: 999px; padding: 6px 16px;`). Features a circular indicator icon on the left.
    *   **Pill Tabs:** Secondary status indicators (`Cutting-edge R&D`, `Adaptive AI`) encased in soft grey rounded rectangles (`background-color: #f4f5f7; border-radius: 999px; padding: 6px 14px;`).
*   **Right Section:**
    *   **Action Button:** Circular icon button followed by a pill-shaped button ("Scalable Solutions") with subtle border styling.

---

### B. Hero Section (Core Imagery & Typography)

#### 1. Central Graphic (The Creation of Adam motif reimagined)
*   **Composition:** Two hands reaching toward each other from opposite sides, stopping just short of touching in the center.
*   **Left Hand (Human):**
    *   **Description:** A smooth, realistic human arm and hand reaching horizontally from the left. Skin tone is natural/warm-neutral.
    *   **Placement & Rotation:** Extends from the middle-left of the canvas, angled slightly downward.
    *   **Background:** Transparent/Backgroundless asset (`.png` or `.webp`).
*   **Right Hand (Robotic/AI):**
    *   **Description:** An intricate, metallic robotic hand/arm with visible joints, pistons, and paneling, mimicking the human gesture.
    *   **Color Palette:** Metallic silver, gunmetal grey, and subtle dark accents.
    *   **Placement & Rotation:** Extends from the middle-right, mirrored toward the human hand, angled downward.
    *   **Background:** Transparent/Backgroundless asset (`.png` or `.webp`).
*   **Lighting/Glow Effect:** A soft, ethereal white/cream radial glow centered in the gap between the two fingertips, suggesting energy transfer.

#### 2. Typography Placement
*   **Headline:** Positioned centrally, partially layered beneath the hand graphics to create depth and spatial dimension. Displays large, bold clipped lettering (`...naFusion`, `...e th...`).

---

### C. Bottom Section

*   **Bottom-Left:**
    *   **Micro-heading:** "Precision Engineering" (Small uppercase or semibold text).
    *   **Paragraph:** "Bridging the gap between humanity and robotics for a smarter, more connected future." (Max-width: ~280px, line-height: `1.4`).
*   **Divider:** A thin vertical divider line (`width: 1px; height: 24px; background-color: #d1d5db;`).
*   **Bottom-Right:**
    *   **Filter/Pill Group:** Three circular or softly rounded pill buttons labeled sequentially: `Algorithms`, `AI`, `Solutions` (`border: 1px solid #e5e7eb; background: #ffffff;`).

---

## 4. Responsive Behavior Guidelines
*   **Desktop (`>1024px`):** Maintain centered white card layout with generous whitespace margins.
*   **Tablet/Mobile (`<1024px`):** Collapse navigation into a hamburger menu; stack the hero text and imagery vertically; reduce padding of the main white container to `16px`.
