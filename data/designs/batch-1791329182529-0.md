# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Fintech SaaS / Glassmorphic Financial Dashboard.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-density operational layout balanced with generous card padding (`24px`), subtle border separators, and high-contrast numerical metrics.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered depth architecture featuring a deep neutral app background (`#F3F4F6`), elevated frosted-glass white cards (`#FFFFFF` with soft drop shadows and 1px muted borders), and interactive floating pill indicators.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient soft glow orbs, glassmorphism card surfaces, status indicator dots.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(250, 204, 21, 0.1)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
