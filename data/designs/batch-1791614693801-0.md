# Design Specification (`design.md`) — TestiQA Hero Landing Page

---

## 1. Overview & Canvas Structure

The design is a high-tech, futuristic QA & software testing agency landing page. It utilizes a **split-canvas composition**: an airy, ultra-light gray main interface on the left and a dark charcoal vertical console panel on the right. 

*   **Target Display:** Fullscreen Desktop Viewport (`100vw` × `100vh`, minimum recommended base: `1440px × 900px`).
*   **Viewport Layout:** Clean full-screen display without external presentation mockups, device bezels, or background showroom watermarks.
*   **Overall Theme:** Sci-Fi / High-Precision Tech Minimalist.

## 3. Typography & Font Specifications

### 3.1 Headline Display Font (Cyber/Futuristic Geometric)
*   **Primary Font:** `Conthrax`, `Nippo`, or `Orbitron` (Wide / Expanded Geometric Display)
*   **Fallback Stack:** `'Orbitron', 'Michroma', 'Eurostile Extended', 'Rajdhani', sans-serif`
*   **Usage:**
    *   **Main Hero Title:** 
        *   Weight: Bold / Black (`800`)
        *   Letter-spacing: `0.06em` (wide tracking)
        *   Line-height: `1.05` to `1.1`
        *   Text-transform: `uppercase`
        *   Size: `clamp(2.75rem, 4.5vw, 4.5rem)`
    *   **Giant Background Cutout Text ("TESTING" / "TE 16"):**
        *   Weight: Extra-bold (`900`)
        *   Size: `clamp(8rem, 14vw, 15rem)`
        *   Letter-spacing: `-0.02em`

### 3.2 Primary Interface Font (Modern UI Sans)
*   **Primary Font:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter`
*   **Fallback Stack:** `'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif`
*   **Usage:**
    *   **Logo Text ("TestiQA"):** Semi-Bold (`600`), `1.25rem` (`20px`)
    *   **Navigation Links:** Medium (`500`), `0.875rem` (`14px`), Letter-spacing: `0.01em`
    *   **Indicator ("01."):** Medium (`500`), `0.8125rem` (`13px`)
    *   **Primary CTA Button ("GET IN TOUCH"):** Bold (`700`), `0.8125rem` (`13px`), Letter-spacing: `0.08em`, Uppercase

### 3.3 Monospace Code Font
*   **Primary Font:** `JetBrains Mono` or `Fira Code`
*   **Fallback Stack:** `'Fira Code', 'SF Mono', Menlo, Consolas, monospace`
*   **Usage:** Code snippet widget in the dark panel (`0.65rem` / `10.5px`, line-height `1.4`).

## 6. Detailed 3D Centerpiece & Asset Specifications

The central visual asset is a **single multi-layered composition** (or a transparent PNG overlay cluster) that anchors the transition between the two background sections.

### 6.1 Main Floating 3D Core
*   **Asset Type:** Transparent, backgroundless 3D render (`PNG` or `WebP` with alpha channel).
*   **Dimensions:** ~`750px × 750px` relative to viewport.
*   **Placement:** Centered horizontally at `60%` from left edge, vertically centered.
*   **Component Breakdown:**
    1.  **Spherical Tech Shell:**
        *   Material: Matte satin white ceramic / polymer.
        *   Form: Top and bottom hemispherical caps with precision bevels and circular polar vent recesses.
    2.  **Exposed Labyrinth Core (Equatorial Band):**
        *   Material: High-detail carved volumetric 3D maze / labyrinth circuit pattern.
        *   Color: Chalk white with soft occlusion shadows giving extreme depth.
    3.  **Plexus Network Enclosure:**
        *   A delicate geometric wireframe dodecahedron/icosahedron cage surrounding the sphere.
        *   Connected with thin hairline platinum lines (`#A0A5AA`) and microscopic spherical vertex nodes.
    4.  **Floating Satellite Elements:**
        *   **Solid White Spheres:** 4–5 small ceramic white spheres of varying scale (`12px` to `48px`) orbiting the central orb with motion depth blur.
        *   **Glass Bubbles:** 3–4 transparent, highly refractive liquid glass bubbles catching rim light.
    5.  **Pedestal Base (Floor Anchor):**
        *   Position: Directly below the sphere (`bottom: 8%`).
        *   Appearance: Low-profile circular charging dish / stage. White matte beveled outer ring.
        *   Interior Recess: Illuminated amber/warm-orange glow basin (`#FF9E44`).
        *   Branding: Micro logo badge `"TestiQA"` printed in dark gray on the front edge.
        *   Resting Sphere: One solid matte white marble resting directly on the ground adjacent to the dish.

## 7. Responsive Behavior & Scaling Rules

*   **Desktop Standard (`1440px+`):** Full 1:1 fidelity with the reference.
*   **Laptop (`1024px – 1366px`):** Scale the 3D visual down by 15%; hero font down to `3rem`.
*   **Tablet / Mobile (Breakpoint `< 1024px`):**
    *   Convert from split horizontal layout to single stacked vertical column.
    *   The dark right panel drops below the main hero or transitions into a bottom sheet.
    *   3D visual centers above the main headline with reduced scale (`300px × 300px`).
    *   CTA button stacks full-width with identical inner proportions.
