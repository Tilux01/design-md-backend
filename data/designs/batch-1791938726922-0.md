# Design Specification: IGN Concept UI

## 1. General Page Styling
*   **Background:** A very subtle radial gradient starting from the top-left (#FFFFFF) to the bottom-right (#F2F4F7).
*   **Layout:** Full-screen, edge-to-edge fluid layout.
*   **Border Radius:** All main containers and cards use a consistent `24px` corner radius.
*   **Shadows:** Soft, diffused drop shadows. Example: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1) 0px 20px 40px -10px`.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Montserrat** or **Inter** (Geometric Sans-Serif).
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `System-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:** 
    *   Bold (700) for titles and active states.
    *   Medium (500) for navigation.
    *   Regular (400) for subtext.

## 3. Header Navigation
*   **Logo:** Located top-left. The "IGN" logo. "I" and "G" in black, "N" in red.
*   **Nav Items:**
    *   **Home:** Active state. Encased in a bright red (#FF003C) pill-shaped container with a subtle outer glow. Text color: White.
    *   **News, Videos, Reviews, Shows:** Dark grey (#333333) text, Medium weight, font size `14px`.
*   **Right Side Actions:**
    *   "Login" and "Sign up" in light grey (#888888).
    *   **Search Bar:** A white pill-shaped input with a very light border.
    *   **Profile Icon:** A circular black button with a white user icon, located at the far right.

## 4. Main Content Area
The layout is split into two primary sections: a large featured card (left) and a scrollable list of vertical cards (right).

### A. Featured Section (Left)
*   **Heading:** "Featured of the week" in a small, uppercase, light grey font.
*   **Main Card:**
    *   **Image:** A wide rectangular image (approx. 16:9 aspect ratio). 
    *   **Image Description:** A cinematic landscape from *The Outer Worlds*. It features a lone character in a sci-fi space suit standing on a rocky, yellow-tinted alien planet. The sky is a hazy orange/yellow. The character is positioned slightly off-center to the left.
    *   **Overlay Text:** "The Outer Worlds" in large white bold text. Below it, a short description in smaller white text.
    *   **Call to Action:** A bright blue (#0055FF) button labeled "Read more" with rounded corners, positioned at the bottom left of the image.
    *   **Controls:** A small dark grey translucent pill at the bottom right of the card containing white pagination arrows.

### B. Anticipated Games Section (Right)
*   **Heading:** "Top most anticipated games" in the same style as the left heading.
*   **Card Style:** Vertical cards with a high aspect ratio (approx. 2:3). Rounded corners (`20px`).
*   **Individual Cards:**
    1.  **The Last of Us Part II:** 
        *   **Image:** Close-up portrait of Ellie. Her face is centered, looking slightly to the right with a serious, intense expression. Lighting is moody and dark.
        *   **Text:** White title at the bottom.
    2.  **Cyberpunk 2077:** 
        *   **Image:** Close-up portrait of the male protagonist (V). He is looking directly at the camera. Warm, orange-toned lighting hitting one side of his face.
        *   **Text:** White title at the bottom.
    3.  **Half-Life: Alyx:** 
        *   **Image:** A character in a gas mask and tactical gear. Dark, industrial background. The character is centered and slightly hunched.
        *   **Text:** White title at the bottom.
    4.  **Red Card (Partial):** A peek of a fourth card on the far right, showing a solid red background with a silhouette (likely *Red Dead Redemption*).

## 5. Footer / Bottom Navigation
*   **Left Side:** Three small dots (Pagination). The first dot is a dark grey square, the others are light grey circles.
*   **Center:** A single, thin diamond-shaped icon (`<>` style) acting as a separator or scroll indicator.
*   **Right Side:** A small, bright blue (#0055FF) square button with rounded corners containing a white number "1" or a specific icon.

## 6. Color Palette
*   **Primary Red:** `#FF003C` (Used for active nav and logo accents).
*   **Primary Blue:** `#0055FF` (Used for buttons and accents).
*   **Text Dark:** `#1A1A1B` (Titles).
*   **Text Muted:** `#757575` (Secondary text).
*   **Card Background:** Dark grey/black overlay on images to ensure text readability.

## 7. Interaction Details
*   **Hover States:** Cards should lift slightly (translate -5px) and the shadow should deepen.
*   **Transitions:** All transitions (hover, active states) should be `0.3s ease-in-out`.
