# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: High-Tech Genomic Science & Dark Mode Glassmorphism.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Generous negative space surrounding left-aligned typographic callouts and a balanced asymmetrical 3D hero asset on the right.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Deep space background with layered glassmorphic video callout containers, subtle backdrop-blur, and emissive neon-blue/amber glowing elements.
