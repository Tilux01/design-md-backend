# UI Design Specification: NEWW(AI)VE TECHWEAR App

This document provides a comprehensive, pixel-level specification to recreate the **NEWW(AI)VE TECHWEAR** mobile application interface shown in the design system. It details layout structures, precise color codes, typography pairings, interactive states, and recommended motion design.

---

## 1. Global Design Tokens

### 1.1 Color Palette
*   **Backgrounds:**
    *   App Base Background: `#16141F` (Deep muted dark navy/charcoal)
    *   Card Surface Background: `#201D2B` (Elevated dark purple-grey tint)
    *   Card Overlay/Blur Surface: `rgba(32, 29, 43, 0.65)` with Backdrop Blur `20px`
*   **Brand Gradients:**
    *   Primary Neon Pink-to-Purple Gradient: Linear `90deg`, `#D936F8` (Left) to `#8C24FF` (Right)
    *   Interactive State Gradient: Linear `90deg`, `#B235F8` to `#671FE3`
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   Primary Text: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
    *   Secondary Text / Labels: `#9E9AAB` (Muted lavender-gray)
    *   Tertiary Text: `#6C677C` (Darker structural gray)
*   **Borders & Dividers:**
    *   Glass Stroke/Outline: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`
    *   Input Field Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

### 1.2 Typography
*   **Brand / Logo Font:** *Futura Bold* or *Space Grotesk* (Geometric Sans-Serif, uppercase, tracked out with customized brackets around AI: `(AI)`).
    *   *Fallback:* `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
*   **Headings & UI Labels:** *Inter* / *SF Pro Display* (Medium/Semibold weights).
    *   *Fallback:* `Arial, sans-serif`
*   **Pricing & Numerical Data:** *Inter* (Bold/Black weight).

## 3. Interactive States & Micro-Interactions

*   **Buttons & Pills:** 
    *   *Hover/Press:* Scale down slightly (`scale(0.97)`), brightness increases by 10%.
*   **Glassmorphism Panels:** 
    *   Utilize CSS `backdrop-filter: blur(20px);` combined with semi-transparent white borders (`border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`) to achieve the signature translucent futuristic aesthetic.
*   **Image Gradients:**
    *   Dark vignettes applied to all product cards (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(22,20,31,0) 50%, rgba(22,20,31,0.85) 100%)`) to ensure legibility of overlaid white typography.

---

## 4. Animation & Transition Guidelines

1.  **Screen Transitions (Page Navigation):**
    *   Use a smooth **Shared Element Transition** when tapping product cards from Screen 2 to Screen 3 (the product image scales up seamlessly into the detail view).
    *   Slide-in from right for sub-screens (`cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`, duration: `350ms`).
2.  **Micro-Interactions:**
    *   *Size Selection:* Smooth pill background color morph (Duration: `200ms`).
    *   *Button Press:* Spring-physics scale animation (`tension: 300, friction: 20`).
    *   *Background Parallax:* Subtle gyroscope-driven parallax effect on the hero product images in Screen 3 to enhance the futuristic AR-app feel.
