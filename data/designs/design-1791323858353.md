# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist, Modern Portfolio Showcase, High-End Brand Strategy & Creative Direction.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Generous whitespace centered around a fluid, curved 3D interactive carousel showcase with minimal typography and high-contrast orange accent markers.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean flat white canvas (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle rounded cards featuring curved 3D perspective transforms, soft multi-layered drop shadows (`0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`), and floating pill badges.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase subtitle labels (`Behind the Designs`), 3D curved perspective cards simulating spatial rotation, orange arrow-action pills, step indexing (`#01 Strategy & Planning`).
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `13px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.04em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, shadow `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Arrow icon (→) -> Use `&rarr;` or SVG chevron icon component.
  - Step index (#) -> Render literal string `&#35;01`.
