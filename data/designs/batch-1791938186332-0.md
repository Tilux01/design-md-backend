# Design Specification: Marvel Spider-Man Landing Page

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Layout Type:** Full-screen hero landing page (Single Page).
*   **Background:** A radial gradient. The center is a vibrant crimson red (`#b71c1c`), fading out to a dark, deep burgundy/black (`#2b0505`) at the corners.
*   **Overall Style:** Modern, clean, gaming-focused with high-contrast white elements against a red backdrop.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **"Montserrat"** or **"Gotham"** (Bold/Black weight for titles, Regular for body).
*   **Secondary Font (Logo/Accents):** **"Spiderman-Font"** (specifically for the "Spider-Man" logo text) or a heavy sans-serif like **"Inter"** or **"Roboto"**.
*   **Fallbacks:** `System-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.

## 3. Header (Navigation Bar)
*   **Placement:** Top of the screen, fixed or absolute, spanning 100% width with horizontal padding (approx 5%).
*   **Left Section:** 
    *   **PlayStation Logo:** White vector icon (PS logo).
*   **Center Section:** 
    *   **Nav Links:** "Games", "News", "Shop", "Services".
    *   **Style:** White text, uppercase or title case, small font size (approx 12-14px). Each link has a small downward chevron icon next to it.
*   **Right Section:**
    *   **Login Button:** Pill-shaped white button with dark text.
    *   **Search Icon:** White magnifying glass icon to the right of the button.

## 4. Hero Content (Center-Left)
*   **Game Title:** 
    *   "MARVEL" (Small, white, bold, above the main title).
    *   "SPIDER-MAN" (Large, white, bold, uppercase).
*   **Description:** A short paragraph of white text below the title. Font size approx 14px, line height 1.5. Max-width approx 300px.
*   **CTA Button:** 
    *   **Text:** "PRE-ORDER NOW" (Uppercase, bold).
    *   **Style:** Pill-shaped, solid white background, black text. Subtle drop shadow.
*   **Social Icons:** Bottom-left corner. Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter icons in white.

## 5. Visual Assets (The Hero)
*   **Background Graphic (Graffiti Spider):**
    *   **Description:** A large, white spray-paint style spider emblem. It looks "dripping" and hand-drawn.
    *   **Placement:** Centered behind the main character.
    *   **Transparency:** Backgroundless (PNG/SVG).
*   **Main Character (Miles Morales):**
    *   **Description:** Miles Morales in his black and red suit. He is in a dynamic "leap" or "crouch" pose, facing slightly right but looking towards the viewer. One hand is reaching forward (foreshortened), and the other is back.
    *   **Placement:** Centered on the screen, layered on top of the graffiti spider.
    *   **Rotation/Angle:** Tilted slightly counter-clockwise to enhance the feeling of motion.
    *   **Image Type:** High-resolution PNG, strictly backgroundless (transparent).

## 6. Sidebar / Review Section (Right)
*   **Game Reviews:**
    *   **IGN:** A white circle outline containing the score "9.9". To the right, the text "IGN" and a sub-caption "Probably the best game of the year".
    *   **GameRadar:** A white circle outline containing the score "9.7". To the right, the text "GameRadar" and a sub-caption "PlayStation's Masterpiece".
*   **Watch Trailer Component:**
    *   **Placement:** Bottom-right.
    *   **Thumbnail:** A rectangular image showing an action scene from the game.
    *   **Overlay:** A semi-transparent dark overlay with a white "Play" button icon centered.
    *   **Effect:** The thumbnail has rounded corners and a significant "floating" drop shadow to give it depth.
    *   **Label:** "WATCH TRAILER" text in white, bold, uppercase, placed above the thumbnail.

## 7. Spacing & Grid
*   **Container:** Use a standard 12-column grid or a Flexbox layout with `justify-content: space-between`.
*   **Padding:** Standardized 60px to 80px padding on the left and right sides of the main container.
*   **Vertical Alignment:** The main content (Title, Hero, Reviews) is vertically centered on the viewport.

## 8. Interactive Elements (Hover States)
*   **Nav Links:** Should turn slightly opaque or show a small underline on hover.
*   **Buttons:** The "Pre-Order Now" button should have a slight scale-up effect (1.05x) or a subtle color shift to light grey on hover.
*   **Trailer Thumbnail:** Should brighten or the play button should grow slightly when hovered.
