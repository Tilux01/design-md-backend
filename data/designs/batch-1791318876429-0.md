# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial E-Commerce Dark Mode with high-contrast Neon Accents.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High content-to-padding density with structured card layouts, rounded container blocks, and asymmetric floating badge overlaps.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered dark charcoal to obsidian background surfaces with subtle inner shadows, frosted glass cards (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), and vivid chartreuse/neon-green branding highlights.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Lime green highlight badges, price tag micro-pills, floating isometric product compositions, rounded glass cards.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `700`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.15)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-lime-500 inline-block"></span>`.
