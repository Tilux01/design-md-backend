# Design Specification: Pontus Wellgraf Interview Landing Screen

## 1. Overview & Layout Architecture
* **Canvas:** Full viewport (`100vw` × `100vh`), split into an asymmetric two-column grid.
* **Left Column (58% width):** Clean white content area with geometric overlays, primary typography, and UI controls.
* **Right Column (42% width):** Full-bleed photographic portrait with ambient sky background.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Base Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
* **Primary Text & Dark Accents:** `#111111` (Deep Black)
* **Muted Heading Tone:** `#7E848C` (Mid Slate Grey)
* **Subtle UI & Numbering:** `#A4B3BF` (Cool Muted Grey)
* **Translucent Overlays:** `rgba(215, 225, 232, 0.5)`
* **Right Panel Sky Tone:** `#BDD1DE` (Pale Sky Blue)

---

## 3. Typography
* **Primary Font Family:** *Neue Haas Grotesk*, *Inter*, or *Helvetica Neue*
* **Fallback Fonts:** `Arial`, `sans-serif`
* **Hierarchy:**
  * **Brand Mark (Top-Left):** 11px, Bold, Uppercase, Tracking: `0.2em`, `#111111`.
  * **Counter ("01"):** 36px, Semi-Bold, `#9AA8B3`.
  * **Main Title:** 48px–56px, Extra-Bold, Leading: `0.95`, Uppercase:
    * `"INTERVIEW WITH"`: `#7E848C`
    * `"PONTUS WELLGRAF"`: `#111111`
  * **CTA Button Text:** 11px, Bold, Uppercase, Tracking: `0.25em`, `#FFFFFF`.
  * **Body Caption:** 12px, Regular, Leading: `1.4`, Sentence Case, `#111111`.
  * **Metadata ("INTERVIEW", "YOUTUBE"):** 10px, Bold, Uppercase, Tracking: `0.2em`.
  * **Vertical Year Indicator ("2 0 2 0"):** 10px, Bold, Tracking: `0.4em`, Rotated 90° clockwise.

---

## 4. Components & Positioning

### Left Content Zone
* **Author Tag:** Located at `top: 8%`, `left: 6%` ("MINH PHAM").
* **Translucent Geometric Blocks:**
  * Top box: ~160px × 160px square at `top: 0`, `left: 40%`, filled with `rgba(215, 225, 232, 0.45)`.
  * Nested counter `"01"` anchored at top-right inside this box.
  * Secondary overlapping rectangle extends downward into the center divide.
* **Hero Headline:** Left-aligned at `left: 6%`, vertically centered.
* **CTA Button:**
  * Placement: Directly below hero headline with a 28px gap.
  * Size: 110px × 36px solid black rectangle (`#111111`).
  * Label: "START" centered in white.
* **Footer Metadata:**
  * "INTERVIEW" at `bottom: 8%`, `left: 6%`.
  * Three-line text "How self-promoting / can change your / career" positioned at `bottom: 8%`, `left: 48%`.

### Graphic Dividing Anchor
* **Center Pivot Icon:** Placed on the vertical seam between panels (`left: 58%`, `top: 58%`).
  * Outer circle: 56px diameter, 1px solid `#111111` outline, transparent fill.
  * Inner center dot: 6px solid `#111111` circle centered inside.

### Right Content Zone & Media
* **Portrait Media Description:**
  * **Content:** Photographic portrait of a young Caucasian male with slicked-back blonde hair, subtle smile, angled slightly toward the left. Wears a dark textured waffle-knit crewneck sweater.
  * **Type:** Integrated background photo (not cut out/not backgroundless); seamlessly blends into a pale natural sky backdrop (`#BDD1DE`).
  * **Placement:** Spans `left: 58%` to `100%` width and full height (`100vh`), object-fit: cover, centered on face.
* **Logo Badge:**
  * Solid black square (~64px × 64px) at `top: 8%`, `right: 6%`.
  * Graphic: White line-art icon composed of a square base and a stylized overlapping 'W' chevron.
* **Vertical Label:** "2 0 2 0" vertically aligned at `right: 4%`, `top: 50%`.
* **Platform Tag:** "YOUTUBE" at `bottom: 8%`, `right: 6%`, styled in white/light grey against the dark sweater.
