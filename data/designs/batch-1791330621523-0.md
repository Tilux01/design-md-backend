# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Modern SaaS / Biotech Editorial Minimalist.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace focus with asymmetric hero framing. Generous padding around container boundaries (`space-16` / `space-12`) and tight internal component rhythm (`space-3` to `space-6`), ensuring clarity and scannability.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Surface 1 white base cards, floating translucent glassmorphism interface panels with cyan-to-purple gradient highlights, floating action pill badges with soft perimeter drop shadows, and absolute geometric accent hexagons.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Floating isometric glass projection screens, DNA helix vector representations, glowing cyan interactive nodes, high-tracking uppercase navigation links, geometric floating ambient hexagon tokens.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.08)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-violet-600 inline-block"></span>`.
