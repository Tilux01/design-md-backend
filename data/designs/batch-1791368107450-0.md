# Creative Direction & Layout Specification

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Properties
* **Aspect Ratio:** Vertical (approx. 9:16 mobile-optimized poster layout)
* **Bleed/Margins:** Full-bleed edge-to-edge composition, zero margins.
* **Focal Point:** The intersection of the holding hand, the pale pink flower, and the sunset resting on the distant horizon line (rule of thirds centered vertically/horizontally).

---

## 2. Visual Hierarchy & Composition Layers (Back to Front)

### Layer 1: Atmospheric Sky (Background)
* **Vertical Distribution:** Occupies the upper 45% of the frame.
* **Color Palette (Top to Bottom Gradient):**
  * Top Zenith: Dusty muted purple-blue (`#4A4A63` to `#3B3B4F`)
  * Transition Zone: Soft peach-orange (`#D48C6B`)
  * Sunset Core: Vibrant warm orange (`#FF6B00` to `#FF8C00`)
* **Cloud Structure:** Horizontal, wispy, low-density stratus clouds spanning the middle of the sky layer, catching the warm orange highlights from the setting sun.

### Layer 2: Ocean & Horizon (Midground)
* **Horizon Line:** Positioned precisely at the 45% mark from the top.
* **Sun Placement:** A glowing, intense golden-white disk (`#FFF5E6`) sitting directly on the ocean horizon line, slightly obscured by a distant low island silhouette.
* **Water Surface:** 
  * Deep navy/grey rolling waves further back (`#1C252B`).
  * Smooth, glassy wet sand and shallow wash in the foreground acting as a mirror.
  * **Reflection:** High-intensity vertical reflection of the orange sunset bleeding across the center of the water surface toward the viewer.
  * **Foam Line:** A clean, soft white foam line curving organically across the lower-middle portion of the beach (`#F2F5F8`).

### Layer 3: Subject & Foreground (Foreground)
* **Subject Object:** A single, five-petaled pale pink cherry blossom (sakura-like) flower.
  * **Petal Color:** Semi-translucent delicate blush pink (`#EED9DC`), backlit by the sun so the edges glow and the inner red stamen structure is visible as a dark, intricate silhouette.
* **Hand Element:**
  * **Position:** Entering from the lower-left corner.
  * **Grip:** Gently pinching the base/stem of the flower between thumb and index finger, with remaining fingers curled softly underneath.
  * **Skin Tone:** Warm neutral, naturally shadowed by the sunset light source (silhouetted warm mid-tones).
  * **Lighting:** Rim-lit along the edges by the bright ambient glow of the sunset and reflection off the water.

---

## 3. Lighting, Color Grading & Atmosphere
* **Light Source:** Direct backlighting from the setting sun.
* **Contrast:** Soft high-dynamic-range (HDR) look—shadows on the hand and flower center are deep and rich, while the highlights on the water and sky are radiant and saturated.
* **Color Temperature:** Warm, golden-hour twilight balance leaning heavily into oranges, magentas, and soft purples.

---

## 4. Typography (Optional / Overlay Guide)
* *If text is applied:*
  * **Font Family:** Modern humanist sans-serif or clean geometric serif (e.g., *Cinzel* or *Montserrat*).
  * **Color:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or soft cream (`#F8F4E8`) with a subtle 30% drop shadow to maintain legibility against the bright sky/water backdrop.
  * **Placement:** Positioned in the upper negative space (dusty purple sky area) to keep the focal point on the flower and sunset unobstructed.
