# VR BOXXX - Website Design Specification

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
- **Theme:** Dark Mode, futuristic, immersive.
- **Background Color:** Deep charcoal/black (`#0B0B0E`) with vibrant magenta and electric blue ambient lighting/gradients.
- **Font Family:** Modern sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Montserrat, or Roboto).
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Primary Neon / Accent:** Hot Magenta (`#FF007A`, `#FF2A85`)
  - **Secondary Accent:** Electric Blue (`#2A9D8F`, `#3A86FF`)
  - **Text Primary:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - **Text Secondary / Muted:** Light Gray (`#A0A0A5`)
  - **Surface / Card Background:** Translucent dark glassmorphism with subtle borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).

---

## 2. Layout & Structure

### A. Navigation Bar (Header)
- **Position:** Fixed / Sticky top, full width with generous horizontal padding.
- **Logo (Left):**
  - Text: **VR BOXXX** (stacked, bold white).
  - Icon: Minimalist geometric VR headset/cube outline in magenta.
- **Navigation Links (Center):**
  - `SERVICES`, `SOLUTIONS`, `GLASSES`, `COMPANY` (Uppercase, small, tracking/letter-spacing wide, muted gray color transitioning to white on hover).
- **Action Button (Right):**
  - Text: `EXPLORE` with a right-pointing triangle icon (`▶`).
  - Styling: Rounded pill shape, dark semi-transparent fill with a subtle white/magenta border.

### B. Hero Section
- **Background Watermark Text:**
  - Giant, faint, semi-transparent text reading **`VR BOXXX`** spanning horizontally across the middle background.
- **Hero Centerpiece (Main Character):**
  - A high-detail 3D render of an **alpaca** wearing glowing pink/magenta VR goggles.
  - Positioned centrally, looking forward.
- **Left Interactive Element:**
  - Circular badge / button reading **`LEARN MORE`** (centered text inside a dark glassmorphic circle with a subtle gradient border).

### C. Featured Products (Bottom Grid)
- **Layout:** Two side-by-side dark glassmorphism cards positioned over the lower portion of the hero area.
- **Card 1: `BC 2 LIGHT`**
  - **Visual:** 3D render of a colorful bear wearing neon-pink VR goggles.
  - **Badge:** Pill badge reading `BC 2 LIGHT`.
  - **Description Text:** *"This headset offers sharp visuals and a comfortable fit, perfect for gaming, movies, and virtual tours."*
  - **Button:** `LEARN MORE` with a plus/arrow icon inside a circular button.
- **Card 2: `BR 3 LIGHT`**
  - **Visual:** 3D render of a futuristic cat/leopard wearing neon-pink VR goggles.
  - **Badge:** Pill badge reading `BR 3 LIGHT`.
  - **Description Text:** *"Featuring advanced motion tracking, ultra-high-definition graphics and a wide field of view."*
  - **Button:** `LEARN MORE` with a plus/arrow icon inside a circular button.

### D. Ambient Visual Effects (Edges)
- **Left & Right Edges:**
  - Abstract fiber-optic / hair-like particle streams flowing vertically down the left and right borders.
  - Colors: Blends of electric blue, deep purple, and hot magenta.
