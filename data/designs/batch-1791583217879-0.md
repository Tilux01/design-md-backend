# UI/UX Specification: "UN:LEASH" Energy Drink Landing Page

---

## 1. Global Layout & Structural Overview

* **View Mode:** Full-Screen / Edge-to-Edge Responsive. (Discard outer device mockups, drop shadows, and presentation frames visible in the concept image).
* **Grid System:** 12-column fluid grid with centralized alignment for the main hero content, featuring floating absolute elements along concentric polar grids.
* **Aspect Ratio:** Optimized for 16:9 desktop viewports (1920px × 1080px base).

---

## 3. Typography & Font Stack

* **Primary Heading Font:** *Montserrat* or *Syncopate* (Bold, Extra-Bold, Uppercase).
* **Secondary/Body Font:** *Inter* or *Roboto* (Regular, Medium).
* **Fallback Stack:** `sans-serif`, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont.

### Type Hierarchy:
1. **Logo ("UN:LEASH"):**
   * Font Size: `22px`
   * Weight: Bold (700)
   * Styling: Uppercase, split-color or bold colon punctuation.
2. **Navigation Links ("HOME", "ABOUT", "DRINKS", "CONTACT"):**
   * Font Size: `12px`
   * Weight: Medium (500)
   * Letter Spacing: `2px`
3. **Hero Subtitle ("POWER INITIATED"):**
   * Font Size: `10px`
   * Weight: Semi-Bold (600)
   * Color: Muted Cyan / Light Grey (`#8c929d`)
   * Letter Spacing: `3px`
4. **Hero Main Headline ("UN:LEASH YOUR ENERGY"):**
   * Font Size: `56px` – `64px`
   * Line Height: `1.1`
   * Weight: Heavy / Black (900)
   * Styling: Stacked layout, stark white color.
5. **Card Heading ("HEALTH"):**
   * Font Size: `14px`
   * Weight: Bold (700)
   * Letter Spacing: `1.5px`
6. **Card Body Copy:**
   * Font Size: `11px`
   * Line Height: `1.5`
   * Color: `#8c929d`

## 4. Component Breakdown & Spatial Positioning

### A. Background & Texture Layer
* **Base Fill:** Solid `#0a0c10`.
* **Subtle Patterns:** A very fine dot-matrix/carbon-fiber texture overlay centered around the middle canvas (opacity: `0.15`).
* **Concentric Technical Circles:** Multiple large, thin vector circles centered slightly right of the viewport midpoint, featuring dashed stroke styles, coordinate crosshairs (`+`), and tracking rings in faint cyan and grey (`rgba(255,255,255,0.04)`).

---

### B. Header / Navigation Bar (`position: absolute; top: 0;`)
* **Layout:** Flexbox, spaced evenly between brand logo, central navigation links, and empty right balancer.
* **Left:** Brand logo text `"UN:LEASH"` in pure white, uppercase.
* **Center Links:** 
  * `"HOME"` (Active state: underlined with a 2px cyan bar `#00ffcc`).
  * `"ABOUT"`, `"DRINKS"`, `"CONTACT"` (Inactive state: muted grey `#8c929d`, hover transitions to white).
* **Spacing:** `padding: 40px 60px;` absolute layout matching the inner container margins.

---

### C. Hero Section (Left Column)
* **Position:** Absolute / Flex alignment, anchored to the middle-left (`left: 8%; top: 45%`).
* **Content Stack:**
  1. Micro-label: `"POWER INITIATED"`
  2. Main Heading: 
     ```
     UN:LEASH
     YOUR ENERGY
     ```
  3. Interactive Marker 1: Positioned below the main text block featuring a glowing cyan plus sign (`+`) and the label `"POWER"` aligned to the left.

---

### D. Central Hero Asset (The Energy Can)
* **Image Description:** A hyper-realistic, metallic, brushed-aluminum beverage can rendered at a dynamic 3D tilt angle (approx. 35-degree rotation along the z-axis and y-axis). The top pull-tab lid is visible. A glowing, vibrant cyan/neon-green energy aura bleeds through the middle-lower body of the can, fading smoothly into the metallic reflections.
* **Asset Properties:** 
  * Backgroundless (Transparent PNG/WebP or direct 3D WebGL canvas).
  * Layer positioning: Centered slightly right of the screen midpoint (`left: 52%; top: 50%`).
* **Surrounding Orbital Rings:** 3 distinct, glowing elliptical wireframe rings wrap diagonally around the can, tilting on an axis to give a sense of rotation, depth, and kinetic energy. Stroke color: Bright Cyan (`#00ffcc`), with glowing soft-light blur filters.

---

### E. Sidebar Modules & Information Cards (Right Column)

#### 1. "HEALTH" Information Box
* **Position:** Absolute positioning near top-right (`right: 12%; top: 26%`).
* **Container:** Semi-transparent dark card (`background: rgba(18, 22, 28, 0.7);`, subtle border, backdrop blur `blur(10px)`).
* **Content:**
  * Title: `"HEALTH"` (Bold white, uppercase).
  * Description Text: *"Guarana features large leaves and clusters of flowers, and is best known for the seeds from its fruit, which are about the size of a coffee bean."*

#### 2. Secondary Technical Markers
* **Energy Marker:** Positioned below the health box (`right: 25%; top: 60%`), featuring a glowing cyan plus sign (`+`) and the bold label `"ENERGY"`.
* **Target Reticle Node:** A small concentric target circle with a node point located at `right: 32%; top: 28%`.

---

### F. Footer Elements
* **Left Footer:** Copyright text `"© 2023 UN:LEASH INC. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED."` in faint micro-font (`9px`, `#3a414e`).
* **Right Footer:** 
  * Social Media Icons (Minimalist vector outlines for **Facebook**, **Twitter/X**, and **Instagram**) stacked vertically on the far right edge (`right: 5%; top: 45%`).
  * Micro branding string `"UN:LEASH"` in bottom-right corner.

---

## 5. Interaction & State Guidelines (CSS/JS Notes)
* **Hover States on Nav:** Smooth transition of color from `#8c929d` to `#ffffff` with a `0.3s ease` duration.
* **Can & Rings Animation:** Subtle CSS keyframe float/levitate effect combined with a slow rotation pulse on the elliptical wireframe rings to simulate active energy radiation.
