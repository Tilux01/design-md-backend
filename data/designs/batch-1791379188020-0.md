# Design & Implementation Spec (`design.md`)

### 1. Overview & Layout Strategy
* **Viewport Coverage:** 100% width and height (`100vw` / `100vh`), removing any outer bounding box or framed card container.
* **Core Subject:** A central frosted glass card (`backdrop-filter: blur`) that floats above a deep, abstract gradient background.
* **Grid/Alignment:** Flexbox-centered container that scales gracefully across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.

### 2. Color Palette & Background
* **Base Background:** Deep, moody dark red-to-black radial gradient to mimic the ambient lighting circles in the source.
* **Glass Card Surface:** Semi-transparent dark neutral with white borders and multi-layered shadows to give it physical depth (skeuomorphic glassmorphism).
* **Colors:**
  * Background Dark: `#0d0203`
  * Background Red Glow: `#b80614`
  * Glass Fill: `rgba(28, 26, 27, 0.45)`
  * Glass Border: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`
  * Glass Shadow: `0 30px 60px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`

### 3. Typography & Fonts
* **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, fallback to `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
* **Hierarchy:**
  * **Top-Left Badge ("Glass morphic effect"):** 13px, Regular/Medium, tracking/letter-spacing tight.
  * **Top-Right CTA ("Let's create"):** 13px, Regular, muted opacity.
  * **Main Heading ("Hello, I'm a Designer."):** 
    * Size: Fluid/Responsive (`clamp(3rem, 6vw, 5.5rem)`)
    * Weight: `700` (Bold)
    * Line-height: `1.05` (Tight stacking for dramatic impact)
  * **Footer handle ("@_Taiwo_taiwo"):** 12px, Monospace or Sans-Serif, low opacity.

### 4. Micro-Interactions & Animation (Optional Polish)
* **Hover State:** The central glass card elevates slightly on hover (`translateY(-5px)`) with a subtle intensification of the backdrop glow.
* **Entry Animation:** Fade-in and upward slide (`keyframes fadeInSlide`) on load to give a high-end web feel.
