# Design Specification: "Wonder" Oceanic Hero Section

## 1. Global Settings

### 1.1 Canvas & Background
*   **Viewport:** Full screen (100vw, 100vh). No scrolling required for this specific view (hidden overflow).
*   **Base Background Image:**
    *   **Type:** High-resolution photograph.
    *   **Description:** A deep, underwater ocean scene. The bottom half is dominated by rugged, dark, textured rocks/coral reefs. The top half transitions into deep, murky oceanic blue water. There are faint silhouettes of tiny fish swimming in the distance.
    *   **Sizing:** `cover` (fills the entire screen, maintaining aspect ratio), `center center` positioning.
*   **Overlay/Gradient:**
    *   To ensure text readability, there must be a dark gradient overlay applied over the background image.
    *   It should be darkest on the left side (behind the text) and slightly more transparent on the right side (where the main fish is).
    *   *Suggested CSS gradient:* `linear-gradient(to right, rgba(4, 15, 25, 0.9) 0%, rgba(4, 15, 25, 0.5) 50%, rgba(4, 15, 25, 0.2) 100%)`.

### 1.2 Global Color Palette
*   **Primary Text:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
*   **Secondary Text (Paragraph):** Light Gray/Off-White (`#D1D5DB` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`)
*   **Accent Color (Nav line, active dot, fish glow):** Bright Orange-Red (`#FF4500` - exact shade matches the glowing fin on the fish).

### 1.3 Typography System
All fonts must be clean, modern sans-serifs. 
*   **Primary Display Font (Main Title "FISH", Logo "ASD"):** 
    *   *Primary:* `Montserrat` (Weights: Black/900 for Title/Logo).
    *   *Fallback:* `Arial Black`, `Impact`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Secondary Font (Navigation, Paragraph, Subtitles):**
    *   *Primary:* `Poppins` or `Inter` (Weights: Regular/400 for paragraph, Light/300 for Subtitles, Medium/500 for Navigation).
    *   *Fallback:* `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
