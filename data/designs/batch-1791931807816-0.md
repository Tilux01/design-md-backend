# Design Specification: Nike Hero Landing Page

## 1. Global Setup & Canvas
* **Layout**: Fullscreen viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), fixed overflow.
* **Canvas Background**: Vertically split two-tone background:
  * **Left Half (50%)**: Deep charcoal (`#181A20`).
  * **Right Half (50%)**: Muted dark slate (`#22262E`).
  * **Center Glow**: Radial gradient behind the shoe (`rgba(255, 94, 34, 0.15)` fading to transparent at 400px).
  * **Background Watermark**: Large, faded uppercase typography `"JUST DO IT"` running vertically on the right half (`color: #2D323B; opacity: 0.35; font-size: 14vw; font-weight: 900`).

---

## 2. Typography
* **Primary Sans-Serif**: `Futura`, `'Helvetica Neue'`, `Arial, sans-serif`.
* **Headings**:
  * Style: All-caps, bold (`font-weight: 800`), tracking `1px`.
  * Title Size: `3.5rem` (`NIKE AIR` on line 1, `MAX` on line 2).
* **Navigation & Micro-copy**:
  * Style: Medium (`font-weight: 500`), size `0.85rem`, tracking `0.5px`.
  * Colors: White (`#FFFFFF`) for active, slate gray (`#7B8290`) for default.

---

## 3. Color Palette
* **Accent Orange**: `#FF5E2B` (buttons, accent badges, active sliders).
* **Dark Base 1**: `#181A20` (left panel).
* **Dark Base 2**: `#22262E` (right panel).
* **Text / Primary**: `#FFFFFF`.
* **Text / Muted**: `#7B8290`.
* **Card Surface**: `rgba(34, 38, 46, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.

---

## 4. Header Navigation
* **Container**: `position: absolute; top: 0; left: 0; width: 100%; height: 80px; padding: 0 4rem; display: flex; align-items: center; justify-content: space-between; z-index: 10;`.
* **Left**: White Nike Swoosh vector icon (width: `50px`).
* **Center**: Horizontal menu (`gap: 2.5rem`):
  * Links: `HOME`, `MEN`, `WOMEN`, `KIDS`.
* **Right**:
  * Circular orange accent notification/cart dot (`10px`).
  * Circular grey profile/utility icon pill (`36px x 36px`, dark charcoal background).

---

## 5. Main Content Area

### Left Column
* **Headline**: Positioned at `top: 30%`, `left: 4rem`.
  * Text: `NIKE AIR`<br>`MAX` (white, high contrast).
* **Left Floating Glass Card**:
  * Positioned lower-left (`top: 58%`, `left: 4rem`).
  * Size: `220px x 90px`, semi-transparent frosted card with rounded borders (`8px`).
  * Content: Subheading and two lines of muted skeleton/preview text.

### Center / Hero Image
* **Image Asset Requirements**:
  * Transparent background PNG cutout.
  * Subject: High-top basketball sneaker (Air Jordan/Air Max hybrid aesthetic) in white, black, and vibrant orange leather panels, with an orange Nike Swoosh, padded collar, and orange laces.
  * Orientation: Side profile facing left, tilted downward at approximately `-20deg` rotation.
* **Placement**:
  * Centered across the split background boundary (`top: 50%`, `left: 50%`, `transform: translate(-50%, -50%) rotate(-20deg)`).
  * Layered drop shadow underneath: `filter: drop-shadow(0 35px 25px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65))`.

### Right Column
* **Right Floating Glass Card**:
  * Positioned at `top: 60%`, `right: 4rem`.
  * Size: `240px x 90px`, semi-transparent frosted panel with micro-details and a small circular navigation arrow/action button on the far right.

---

## 6. Footer & Controls
* **Container**: Fixed to bottom, `height: 90px; padding: 0 4rem; display: flex; align-items: center; justify-content: space-between; z-index: 10;`.
* **Bottom Left (Slider / Pagination)**:
  * Orange active pill indicator (`width: 48px; height: 6px; border-radius: 3px;`).
  * Inactive track indicator (`width: 24px; height: 6px; background: #353B45; border-radius: 3px;`).
* **Bottom Center/Right (Actions)**:
  * Secondary Text Link: `"MORE"` (muted gray, uppercase, `margin-right: 2rem`).
  * Primary CTA: `"GET IN ON"` / `"BUY NOW"` button.
    * Style: Solid orange (`#FF5E2B`), rectangular with slight border radius (`4px`), white bold text, `padding: 0.9rem 2.2rem`.
