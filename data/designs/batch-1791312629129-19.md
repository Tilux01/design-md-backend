# Production-Grade Design Specification

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
- **Primary Mood & Archetype:** High-end digital editorial, luxury fashion publication, minimalist avant-garde, and contemporary artistic portfolio.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Balance:** High whitespace utilization with asymmetric layouts. The layout relies heavily on expansive negative space, deliberate content offsets, and a split-screen composition that balances high-contrast photographic imagery with pristine, minimalist UI panels.
- **Theme Balance:** Hybrid editorial light/dark composition. Dark mode photographic content is anchored against an ultra-clean off-white/light gray canvas (`#F3F3F5`). Deep charcoal/black typography creates striking contrast.

## 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

### Primary & Accent Colors
- **Primary Deep (Charcoal):** `#111111` (Used for primary typography, icons, and major interactive states)
- **Primary Hover/Active:** `#333333`
- **Accent Pink (Gradient/Blob):** `#F7C8DA` transitioning into `#E2D1F9` (Soft pastel atmospheric gradient blobs)
- **Accent Dark (Badge):** `#181818` (Special Issue hexagonal badge fill)

### Base & Surface Backgrounds
- **Application Background:** `#F3F3F5` (Off-white canvas)
- **Image Section Backdrop:** Monochrome editorial treatment (High-contrast grayscale photo filter)
- **Surface Card / Badge Background:** `#181818` (Hexagonal stamp)
- **Backdrop Blur:** Atmospheric layering for abstract background graphics (`backdrop-filter: blur(40px)`)

### Border & Divider Colors
- **Subtle Divider:** `#E5E5EA`
- **Dark Mode Border/Line:** `#111111` (Used for line indicators and pagination tracks)

### Text Colors
- **Primary Text:** `#111111`
- **Muted Text / Metadata:** `#6E6E73` (Used for social links, category indices, and navigation sub-links)
- **Inverse Text:** `#FFFFFF` (Used inside dark badges and primary interactive buttons)

- **Headings & Editorial Titles:** Didot / Bodoni / High-Contrast Modern Serif (e.g., *Playfair Display* or *Bodoni Moda*)

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid

- **Container Structure:** Fluid grid spanning 100% viewport width with a central asymmetric breakpoint dividing the editorial imagery (left 60%) and the editorial typography/UI panel (right 40%).
- **Padding & Margin Scale:** 
  - Outer viewport padding: `40px` horizontal, `40px` vertical.
  - Component internal gaps: `16px`, `24px`, `32px`.
- **Section Sequence Breakdown (Top to Bottom / Left to Right):**
  1. **Top Navigation Bar:** Left-aligned brand logo/icon (`⚿`), Center-left navigation items (*Editorial*, *Categories*, *Prêt-à-porter*, *Brands*), Right-aligned search/menu triggers.
  2. **Hero Feature Area (Left):** Immersive portrait photography featuring structural contrast, slide counter (`0` with active underline indicator).
  3. **Editorial Typography Block (Right):** Subtitle prefix ("T H E   L I T T L E"), massive serif display title ("VANITY"), abstract gradient accent badge ("SPECIAL ISSUE"), and interactive discovery link ("FIND YOURS").
  4. **Bottom Control Deck:** Carousel pagination controls (`<`, `>`), active index counter (`* 01`), and social footer links (`TW`, `FB`, `INS`).
- **Stacking Order (Z-Index Hierarchy):**
  - Background Canvas / Gradient Blobs: `z-index: 0`
  - Editorial Photography / Base Panels: `z-index: 1`
  - Typography & Interactive Elements: `z-index: 10`
  - Floating Badges & Navigation Overlay: `z-index: 100`

## 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement

### Hero Section Alignment & Ratios
- **Split Ratio:** 60% visual viewport (monochrome editorial image) / 40% negative space & typographic layout panel.
- **Image Container:** Full-bleed edge alignment on the left, blending into the off-white background via precise drop-shadow framing (`box-shadow: 0 20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.15)`).

### Cards & Containers
- **Border-Radius Tokens:** 
  - Standard UI containers/buttons: `0px` (Sharp architectural editorial aesthetic).
  - Special Issue Badge: Hexagonal polygon clip-path (`polygon(25% 0%, 75% 0%, 100% 50%, 75% 100%, 25% 100%, 0% 50%)`).
- **Inner Padding:** Consistent `24px` internal bounding box for utility modules.

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Arrow Link ("FIND YOURS"):** Inline text accompanied by a directional thin vector arrow (`→`). Hover state triggers a horizontal translation (`transform: translateX(6px)`).
- **Pagination Controls (`<`, `>`):** Minimalist glyph buttons with subtle hover background fills (`background-color: rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`).

---

## 6. Motion Mechanics, 3D & Mobile Gestures

- **Animation Triggers:** 
  - Page load stagger animations for typography (`opacity: 0 -> 1`, `translateY: 20px -> 0`).
  - Hover state shifts on navigation links and interactive directional arrows.
- **Transition Durations & Easing:**
  - Micro-interactions (links, arrows): `200ms cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)` (Swift ease-out).
  - Page/Slide transitions: `600ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (Smooth editorial deceleration).
- **Mobile Responsive Behavior:**
  - Collapse split-screen layout into a stacked vertical progression (Image header on top, typographic metadata below).
  - Hamburger menu activation transforms top navigation into a full-screen immersive overlay.
