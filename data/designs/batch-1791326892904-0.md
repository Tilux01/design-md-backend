# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Neo-Brutalist E-Commerce / Bold Editorial Coffee Brand. High-contrast typography, playful high-energy stickers and badges paired with clean, structured grid alignment.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Asymmetrical balance with dense hero sections, generous padding in editorial blocks, and rigid 8pt grid structured cards. Focuses heavily on high-impact typography and saturated product packaging highlights.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered papercut feel. Sharp solid black borders (2px solid #000000), bright primary fills (red `#E63946`, yellow `#FFD166`, green `#52B788`), and flat card surfaces with high contrast ink typography.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Neo-Brutalist badge overlays, high-contrast black borders with hard offset shadows, floating asymmetric stickers ("PEAK FRESH!", "NEW!"), stamp-like badges with dotted borders.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `700`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#FFD166`, border `2px solid #000000`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2.5 h-2.5 rounded-full bg-[#52B788] inline-block border border-black"></span>`.
