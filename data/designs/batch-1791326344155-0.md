# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise SaaS / Defense Aerospace Dashboard with Glassmorphism and futuristic simulation aesthetics.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High structural discipline; structured 12-column dashboard layout with cards utilizing generous inner padding (`16px` to `24px`) balanced against dense metric trackers.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered glass cards with light semi-translucent white backgrounds, subtle border highlights (`rgba(255,255,255,0.6)`), and soft drop shadows (`0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, live status indicator dots with ambient glow, glassmorphism cards (`backdrop-blur-md`), subtle micro-interactions on hover.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.1)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (&mdash;) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
