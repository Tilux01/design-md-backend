# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist meets Glassmorphism SaaS — clean, sophisticated, and focused on clarity and mental organization.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with generous padding and centralized hero framing. Elements breathe inside an uncluttered layout, emphasizing typographic contrast and subtle floating component clusters.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Soft multi-layered depth utilizing semi-translucent frosted glass (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), delicate light borders (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`), and subtle ambient background glow gradients.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking lowercase/uppercase pill badges, ambient radial blur gradients, floating asymmetrical card layouts, editorial serif headers paired with clean sans-serif UI copy.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `6px 16px`, font-size: `12px`, font-weight: `500`, tracking: `0.05em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background translucency: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`, border width: `1px solid #E5E5E5`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
