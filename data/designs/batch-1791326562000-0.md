# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Futuristic Minimalist AI Web App / 3D Spatial Interface. Clean, tech-forward, high-precision laboratory look.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with spacious layouts, sharp geometric alignments, and balanced negative space surrounding the central 3D wireframe canvas.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, translucent light-gray surfaces with crisp solid borders (`1px solid #d1d5db`), subtle drop shadows, and pill-shaped floating toolbars (`backdrop-blur-md`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase navigation headers, wireframe vertex point targets (&#10022;), technical HUD crosshairs (`+`), radial completion ring loaders (`32%`), profile avatars with status indicators.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `6px 14px`, font-size: `11px`, font-weight: `600`, tracking: `0.08em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`, border width: `1px solid #d1d5db`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Crosshair HUD Mark (+) -> Use `&#43;` or SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
