# Design Specification: Turkish Airlines Flight Training Center Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Color Palette
- **Primary Red:** `#E81932` (Turkish Airlines Red)
- **Deep Navy / Background Dark:** `#111625`
- **Footer Navy:** `#0D121F`
- **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Muted Text / Slate:** `#7A8B9E`
- **Body Gray:** `#6B7280`

### Typography
- **Primary Font:** Gotham or Montserrat (Fallback: `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`)
  - Headline styles: Bold (700/800), Uppercase, tight tracking.
- **Secondary / Body Font:** Inter or Open Sans (Fallback: `sans-serif`)
  - Regular (400), Medium (500) for subheads and descriptions.

---

## 2. Navigation & Header

- **Layout:** Full-width flex container, transparent background over the hero cloud gradient.
- **Left:** Official Turkish Airlines logo emblem with text: `TURKISH AIRLINES` (bold red) and `FLIGHT TRAINING CENTER` (subtext navy).
- **Right:**
  - Navigation menu: `ABOUT US`, `SIMULATORS`, `TRAINING`, `SCHEDULE`, `CONTACT` (Caps, regular weight, dark navy text).
  - Search trigger: White circle containing a navy magnifying-glass icon.

---

## 3. Hero Section

### Left Content
- **Main Heading:** "ARE YOU READY FOR TAKE-OFF?" (`ARE YOU READY FOR` in dark navy, `TAKE-OFF?` in primary red).
- **Subtext:** 2-line gray descriptor.
- **Buttons:**
  - `LEARN MORE`: Solid red pill button, white text.
  - `E-REGISTRATION`: Transparent pill button, red outline, red text.

### Right Visual & Background
- **Watermark:** Faint large gray text `01 PILOTS` layered behind the aircraft.
- **Aircraft Asset:** Turkish Airlines commercial airliner angled forward, descending right-to-left through dynamic, realistic white clouds. (Asset is clipped/blended seamlessly with white space).
- **Slider Pagination:** Vertical right-edge indicator with active red dots.

---

## 4. Simulator Showcase Carousel

- **Layout:** 3-card horizontal carousel.
- **Left Card ("Cabin Trainings"):**
  - Narrow preview with a solid red vertical tab on the left reading `CABIN TRAININGS` (rotated 90°).
  - Image: Flight attendant trainees seated in a simulator cabin.
- **Center Focus Card ("Full Flight Simulators"):**
  - Main display showing a full-motion Turkish Airlines flight simulator pod on hydraulic legs inside a hangar.
  - Overlaid right-aligned red box containing:
    - Title: `FULL FLIGHT SIMULATORS` (White, bold, uppercase).
    - Description: White subtext.
    - Button: `ALL SIMULATORS` (White outline pill button).
- **Right Card ("Cockpit Trainings"):**
  - Narrow preview with a solid red vertical tab reading `COCKPIT TRAININGS` (rotated 90°).
  - Image: Cockpit interior with instrument panels and pilots.

---

## 5. Statistics Section

- **Container:** Dark Navy (`#111625`).
- **Divider:** Bottom edge uses a rugged, ripped-paper/grunge brush mask revealing the white section below.
- **Grid:** 4-column layout:
  1. **Stat 1:** Watermark `1994` | Red line icon of server/simulator racks | "Increasing number of flight simulators... **IN 1994.**"
  2. **Stat 2:** Watermark `15.900` | Red line icon of airport control tower & runway | "Turkish Airlines FTC... **15.600 square meters**"

  4. **Stat 4:** Watermark `21` | Red line icon of a flight simulator pod | "Which 8.420 square meters... **21 SIMULATORS**"
