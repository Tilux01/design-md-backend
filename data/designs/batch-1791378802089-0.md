# UI Design Specification: Earbeats EvoBuds M5 Landing Page

This document outlines the exact design specifications, typography, layout, color palette, and micro-interactions required to build a pixel-perfect, full-screen responsive clone of the provided Earbeats EvoBuds M5 landing page.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

- **View:** Full-screen immersive layout (`width: 100vw; height: 100vh; overflow: hidden;`).
- **Background:** Clean solid white (`#FFFFFF`) canvas overlaid with a soft, ethereal radial gradient/glow at the center in soft pastel violet and sky blue (`rgba(180, 200, 255, 0.4)` transitioning to transparent).
- **Grid System:** Absolute positioning combined with Flexbox for header and footer alignment.
- **Aspect Ratio:** Optimized for widescreen viewports (1440px+ width).

---

## 2. Navigation Header

### Positioning
- **Placement:** Absolute top, spanning horizontally with generous padding (`top: 40px; left: 60px; right: 60px;`).
- **Layout:** Flexbox, `justify-content: space-between; align-items: center;`.

### Left Element: Brand Logo
- **Mark:** Minimalist sound wave / signal bars icon followed by wordmark.
- **Text:** "earbeats" in lowercase, tracked tightly.

### Right Element: Navigation Links
- **Items:** `Earbuds`, `Headphones`, `Accessories`, `Software`, `Support`.
- **Active State:** `Earbuds` features a vertical indicator line (pill/notch) above or slicing through the text to denote the current active page.
- **Spacing:** `gap: 36px;` between menu items.

---

## 3. Hero Section

### Left Content Area (Typography & Call to Action)
- **Sub-brand/Partner tag:** `logi` (small, muted gray).
- **Product Title:** `EvoBuds M5®`
  - *Typography Styling:* Bold, clean sans-serif. Large scale.
- **CTA Link:** `EXPLORE PRODUCT ↗`
  - *Style:* Uppercase, small font size, accompanied by a diagonal top-right arrow icon (`↗`). Hover state triggers a subtle color shift or arrow translation.
- **Ghost/Background Visual:** Faint, semi-transparent wireframe or depth-of-field render of earbuds positioned behind the left text block to create spatial depth.

### Central Product Showcase (3D Hero Object)
- **Object:** Two futuristic purple/magenta wireless earbuds floating in a dynamic isometric/perspective angle.
- **Left Earbud:** Facing outward, showing a circular touch-surface with embossed `+` and `-` volume/control markers, wrapped in a magenta/orange accent band.
- **Right Earbud:** Angled to show the inner silicone tip (glowing orange/yellow inner core) and glossy purple housing resting against a white/silver charging stem.
- **Navigation Arrow:** A small circular button with a right arrow (`→`) positioned directly beneath the right earbud for carousel interaction.

### Right Background Elements
- **Carousel Indicator:** `02 / 15` positioned cleanly in the bottom right corner.
- **Blurred Depth Effect:** Ghosted background renders of previous/next products drifting out of focus in the background to simulate a 3D carousel carousel depth effect.

---

## 4. Footer

- **Placement:** Absolute bottom-left (`bottom: 40px; left: 60px;`).
- **Content:** `Copyright © Earbeats | 2020`
- **Typography:** Extra small, muted cool-gray (`#999999`).

---

---

## 7. Animations & Interactions

1. **Float / Levitation Animation:**
   - The central 3D earbuds should feature a continuous, subtle vertical floating keyframe animation (`transform: translateY(0px)` to `translateY(-10px)` over 4 seconds, ease-in-out infinite).
2. **Parallax Depth:**
   - Mouse move listener (`mousemove`) shifting the background glow and ghosted background elements slightly opposite to cursor direction to create a 3D glass-pane feel.
3. **Hover States:**
   - **Nav Links:** Smooth color transition to black with a slight scale up (`transform: scale(1.02)`).
   - **Explore Link:** Arrow icon (`↗`) translates diagonally by `+3px` on hover.
   - **Carousel Arrow Button:** Scales up by `1.1` with a soft drop shadow on hover.
