# Design Specification: AI Assistant Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Canvas
* **Layout:** Fullscreen single-viewport landing page (100vw, 100vh, `overflow: hidden`).
* **Background:** Solid Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Theme:** High-contrast editorial monochrome with frosted glass accents.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Primary Background:** `#FFFFFF`
* **Text / Primary Elements:** `#000000` (deep black) and `#111111`
* **Muted Text / Rules:** `#737373` / `#CCCCCC`
* **Floating Navbar Background:** Linear gradient from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` to `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`.
* **CTA Button:** `#FFFFFF` text on pure white pill container with dark text `#000000`.

---

## 4. Components & Layout

### 4.1 Floating Navigation Bar
* **Position:** Fixed top center (`top: 24px`, `left: 50%`, `transform: translateX(-50%)`, `z-index: 50`).
* **Shape:** Pill (`border-radius: 9999px`).
* **Dimensions:** Height `48px`, Auto width, `padding: 4px 6px 4px 18px`.
* **Visuals:** Dark frosted translucent background with subtle border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).
* **Contents (Flexbox row, gap: 24px, vertical center):**
  1. Plus Icon (`+` or cross icon, white, size `14px`).
  2. Links: `Product`, `How it works`, `Features`, `Mission`, `Company`.
  3. Action Button: Pill shape, background `#FFFFFF`, color `#000000`, padding `8px 16px`, text: `Get Early Access`.

### 4.2 Main Hero Copy
* **Position:** Absolute, left `6vw`, top `20vh` (`z-index: 20`).
* **Max Width:** `480px`.

### 4.3 Secondary Annotation (Bottom Right)
* **Position:** Absolute, bottom `12vh`, right `8vw` (`z-index: 20`).
* **Layout:** Flex row, vertically centered, `gap: 16px`.
* **Elements:**
  1. Divider line: `width: 60px`, `height: 1px`, `background: #B0B0B0`.
  2. Paragraph text block (2 lines).

---

## 5. Visual Asset: Hero Model

### Placement & Dimensions
* **Position:** Absolute center/right (`right: 15%`, `bottom: 0`, `height: 95vh`).
* **Z-Index:** 10 (sits beneath text and navbar).

### Asset Description
* **Style:** Monochromatic, high-contrast silhouette portrait with selective lighting.
* **Subject:** Profile of a person facing right, holding a smartphone at bottom right.
* **Key Details:**
  * Wearing bold, thick, clear transparent round eyeglasses catch light reflections.
  * Wearing a translucent, frosted milky-white high-collar coat.
  * Deep black silhouette skin tone with subtle edge-light defining jawline and skull.
  * Foreground/torso has a heavy soft blur/mist effect bleeding cleanly into `#FFFFFF`.
* **Background Format:** Transparent PNG/WebP or an image naturally fading to `#FFFFFF` on all edges with no visible bounding box.
