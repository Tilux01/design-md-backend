# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist & High-Performance Athletic Dark Mode.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace focus, immersive full-screen media containers, centered editorial typography layouts with generous horizontal and vertical padding.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered matte black surfaces (`#0a0a0a` to `#141414`), subtle hairline borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), high-contrast white typography, and sharp lime/yellow accent highlights (`#ccff00`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-contrast typography, asymmetrical editorial image reveals, subtle underline markers on active metrics, dark mode glassmorphism containers.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-[#ccff00] inline-block"></span>`.
