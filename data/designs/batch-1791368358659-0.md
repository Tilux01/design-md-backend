# Panerai Submersible Automatic Web Page Design Specification

This document outlines the exact design specifications for a full-screen, responsive web page clone of the Panerai Submersible Automatic promotional layout, based on the provided visual reference.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

### Color Palette
*   **Primary Background (Dark):** `#0A0A0A` (Deep almost-black for the main showcase area)
*   **Secondary Background (Accent Panel):** `#6B6D4E` or `#787B56` (Muted olive/khaki green used for the right-side product/slider panel)
*   **Header / Navigation Background:** `#111111` (Slightly lighter than the main background for contrast)
*   **Primary Text Color:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for main headings)
*   **Secondary Text / Subdued Elements:** `#A0A0A0` (Light gray for descriptions, body text, and inactive navigation items)
*   **Interactive / Accent Lines:** `#FFFFFF` with varying opacity (e.g., `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` for borders and dividers)

### Typography
*   **Brand Font Family:** Modern sans-serif (Geometric, uppercase-heavy, high legibility similar to *Helvetica Neue*, *Montserrat*, or *Inter*).
*   **Logo:** Bold, tracked-out uppercase letters (`PANERAI`).
*   **Headings (H1):** 
    *   **Text:** `SUBMERSIBLE AUTOMATIC`
    *   **Font Weight:** Bold (`700`)
    *   **Text Transform:** Uppercase
    *   **Letter Spacing:** Wide tracking
*   **Subheadings / Model Name:**
    *   **Text:** `42MM CARBOTECH™`
    *   **Font Weight:** Medium (`500`)
    *   **Text Transform:** Uppercase

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Components

### A. Top Navigation Bar (Header)
*   **Position:** Fixed or sticky at the very top of the viewport. Full width.
*   **Background Color:** `#111111`
*   **Height:** Compact (approx. 60px – 70px)
*   **Left Section:**
    *   Brand Logo: `PANERAI` (White, bold, left-aligned with standard padding).
*   **Center Section (Navigation Links):**
    *   Horizontal menu items spaced evenly:
        *   `COLLECTIONS` (Active state indicated by a subtle white underline)
        *   `ACCESSORIES`
        *   `KNOW HOW`
        *   `SERVICES`
        *   `BOUTIQUES`
        *   `ABOUT PANERAI`
    *   *Typography:* Small uppercase sans-serif, muted gray (`#A0A0A0`), turning white on hover.
*   **Right Section (Utility Icons):**
    *   Icons included (left to right): Location/Store locator pin, Wishlist (Heart), User Account (Profile).
    *   Color: White (`#FFFFFF`).

---

### B. Main Hero Section (Dark Canvas)
*   **Dimensions:** Full width, occupying the remaining viewport height below the header.
*   **Background:** Radial or subtle gradient from `#141414` in the center to `#080808` at the edges, featuring soft ambient lighting on the left where the watch is positioned.

#### 1. Left Area: Featured Product Imagery
*   **Product Shot:** High-resolution studio photograph of the Panerai Submersible watch angled dynamically.
    *   *Details:* Black dial, luminous markers, textured olive-green rubber strap, and a dark forged carbon (Carbotech) bezel.
    *   *Shadows/Effects:* Realistic soft drop shadow beneath the watch blending into the dark background.
*   **Floating Utility Controls:**
    *   Share icon (top-left of the image area).
    *   Carousel pagination dots at the bottom-left (`• • • • •`) indicating multiple slides or views.

#### 2. Center-Right Area: Typography & Call-to-Action (CTA)
*   **Main Title Block:**
    *   `SUBMERSIBLE` (Large display font, white)
    *   `AUTOMATIC` (Large display font, white)
    *   `42MM CARBOTECH™` (Smaller subtitle, uppercase, wide letter spacing)
*   **Interactive Video Component ("Official Campaign"):**
    *   Horizontal layout featuring a circular play button icon (`▶`) inside a dark translucent badge.
    *   Label: `Official Campaign` (White, bold) followed by smaller subtext `WATCH VIDEO` (Gray).
*   **Product Metadata:**
    *   `PAM01661 SPECIAL EDITION` (Small, uppercase)
*   **Disclaimer Text:**
    *   Small legal/marketing disclaimer paragraph in muted gray:
        > *"Please note that on-line stock shots, photographs and that colors and sizes may not exactly correspond to actual products."*
*   **Bottom Navigation / Link:**
    *   `DISCOVER LUMINOR` with a directional arrow icon (`➔`).

#### 3. Bottom-Right Navigation Controls
*   **Carousel / Page Switcher:**
    *   Left/Right chevron arrows (`<` and `>`) enclosed in dark circular buttons to cycle through featured watches or campaigns.

---

### C. Right-Side Accent Panel (Olive Green)
*   **Position:** Vertical panel spanning the right edge of the screen.
*   **Background Color:** `#6B6D4E` (Muted olive green)
*   **Content:**
    *   Partial preview of the next slide's watch (another Panerai model visible on the edge).
    *   Left-pointing navigation chevron (`<`) near the edge to indicate scrollability.
    *   Bottom floating action button (dark circular icon with a white symbol).

---

## 3. Responsiveness & Interaction Guidelines
*   **Full Screen:** The layout must stretch to 100% width and 100% height (100vw / 100vh) without visible browser frames or borders.
*   **Hover States:** 
    *   Navigation links transition from gray to bright white.
    *   Play buttons and arrows feature subtle scale or opacity changes on hover.
*   **Transitions:** Smooth slide animations when interacting with the left/right carousel chevrons.
