# Design Specification (`design.md`)

## 1. Project Overview & Visual Identity

* **Brand / Project Name:** `noomo♡time` — *"The Story: Connecting Every Heart with Love"*
* **Aesthetic Theme:** Ethereal Neo-Romanticism / Surreal Dreamscape. Blends ultra-refined high-fashion Didone typography, gilded 18th-century Rococo baroque ornament, tactile floating 3D elements (rose petals, crystallised heart), and a soft, fog-draped white dreamscape.
* **Canvas Dimensions:** Full-screen responsive viewport (`100vw` × `100vh`), borderless, edge-to-edge layout with fixed UI HUD overlays and a 3D WebGL / layered compositional canvas.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Material Specifications

### Core Colors
* **Background Atmosphere (Sky/Fog):** `#F6F3F5` (High-key luminous pearl-white with a soft radial blush gradient towards center-bottom `#EDE4E8`).
* **Primary Typographic Tone (Dark Aubergine/Charcoal):** `#181124` (Used for "STORY"). Deep velvety purple-black with high contrast.
* **Accent Script / Rose Tint (Coral Crimson):** `#E15A68` to `#D84153` (Used for the handwritten *"The"* and scattered rose petals).
* **Secondary Text / Subtitles:** `#423C46` (Neutral muted slate for *"CONNECTING EVERY HEART WITH LOVE"* and navigation items).
* **CTA Button Background:** `#120E19` (Near-black obsidian with 90% opacity).
* **CTA Button Text:** `#F4EFF6` (Soft off-white pearl).

### Materials & Specular Accents
* **Rococo Mirror Frame:** Polished antique gold leaf (`#DFBA73` highlights, `#B88B42` mid-tones, `#6A4A1C` shadow creases) layered over an ivory porcelain/plaster base (`#F8F6F2`).
* **Mirror Interior Glass:** Iridescent pastel gradient (Prismatic blend of soft cyan `#C8E6F5`, lilac `#DEC3F0`, and rose quartz `#F8D2D9`) beneath a soft specular reflection map.
* **Floating Heart Object:** Translucent tumbled quartz/calcite stone heart infused with internal gold-foil flakes and ambient subsurface scattering (`#FFF5DF`).

## 3. Typography & Exact Font Fallbacks

### 1. Hero Serif Title (`"S T [Mirror] R Y"`)
* **Primary Typeface:** High-contrast Didone/Modern Display Serif (e.g., *Playfair Display 900*, *Ogg Roman*, *Bodoni Poster*, or *Didot LT Pro*).
* **Styling:** 
  * Font Weight: Ultra / Heavy (`800`–`900`).
  * Optical Size: Display.
  * Contrast: Extreme hairline serifs with thick, vertical geometric stems.
  * Letter Spacing: Wide, proportional kerning (`~0.12em` to accommodate the mirror).
* **Fallback Stack:**
  ```css
  font-family: "Bodoni MT", "Didot", "Playfair Display", "Cinzel", "Baskerville", serif;
  ```

### 2. Accent Cursive Overlay (`"The"`)
* **Primary Typeface:** Monoline Elegant Script / Signature Calligraphy (e.g., *Brittany Signature*, *Creattion*, *La Luxes Script*, or *Amsterdam Four*).
* **Styling:**
  * Color: Coral-Crimson (`#E15A68`).
  * Stroke Width: Fine hairline monoline (`1.5px` equivalent visual weight).
  * Flow: Interlocking with the upper terminal of the capital `"S"`.
* **Fallback Stack:**
  ```css
  font-family: "Great Vibes", "Alex Brush", "Brush Script MT", cursive;
  ```

### 3. Navigation & Audio Status
* **Primary Typeface:** Minimalist Neo-Grotesk / Geometric Clean Sans (e.g., *Neue Haas Grotesk Text 55*, *Söhne*, *Inter Tight*, or *Barlow*).
* **Styling:**
  * Case: Uppercase (`text-transform: uppercase`).
  * Font Weight: Medium (`500`).
  * Letter Spacing: Expanded tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.15em` / `2.4px`).
  * Font Size: `11px` to `12px` (desktop standard).
* **Fallback Stack:**
  ```css
  font-family: "Inter", "Helvetica Neue", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Arial, sans-serif;
  ```

### 4. Hero Subtitle (`"CONNECTING EVERY HEART WITH LOVE"`)
* **Primary Typeface:** Condensed Grotesque / Editorial Clean Sans (e.g., *Franklin Gothic Condensed*, *Barlow Semi Condensed*, or *Neue Haas Grotesk Condensed*).
* **Styling:**
  * Case: Uppercase.
  * Font Weight: Regular / Book (`400`).
  * Letter Spacing: Generous letter-spacing (`0.2em` / `3.2px`).
  * Font Size: `13px`–`14px`.
* **Fallback Stack:**
  ```css
  font-family: "Barlow Semi Condensed", "Helvetica Neue Condensed", "Arial Narrow", sans-serif;
  ```

### 5. Call-to-Action (`"BEGIN JOURNEY"`)
* **Primary Typeface:** Modern Monospace or Sharp Technical Sans (e.g., *Space Mono*, *GT America Mono*, or *Druk Wide*).
* **Styling:**
  * Case: Uppercase.
  * Font Weight: Medium (`500`).
  * Letter Spacing: `0.22em`.
  * Font Size: `10px`–`11px`.

## 5. Visual Asset Descriptions (Imagery & 3D Render Objects)

### Asset A: The Gilded Rococo Mirror (Centerpiece "O")
* **Format:** Transparent background PNG (or interactive WebGL glTF 3D model with PBR shaders).
* **Dimensions / Aspect Ratio:** Tall vertical oval; approx. `1:1.35` ratio.
* **Frame Detailing:**
  * Inner frame: Smooth, glossy pearl-white / off-white beveled oval rim.
  * Outer crest (Top): Elaborate sunburst / scallop shell motif flanked by curling acanthus leaves and miniature rose blooms cast in antique gold.
  * Outer base (Bottom): Fan-shaped palmette / shell apron bracket with outward curling gold scrolls.
  * Sides: Symmetrical scrolled brackets with intertwined floral vines hugging the oval contour.
* **Interior Mirror Cavity:**
  * Semi-reflective, translucent glass portal revealing an ethereal holographic soft pastel sky (hues of gentle pink, baby blue, and lavender).
  * **Floating Object Inside Mirror:** A floating, faceted/rough-tumbled raw gemstone heart. The heart is rendered in pale golden quartz/crystallized rock salt texture with metallic gold inclusions across its surface. It hovers weightlessly in the center with a gentle warm ambient glow.
* **Placement & Rotation:**
  * Placed precisely between the stem of the `"T"` and the leg of the `"R"`.
  * Rotation: Pure vertical (`0deg` tilt), face forward with a slight front-facing perspective tilt (`perspective: 1000px; rotateX: 2deg`).

### Asset B: Floating Rose Petals
* **Format:** Cutout transparent assets (approx. 12–16 distinct petals) or dynamic GPU particle system.
* **Visual Characteristics:**
  * Crimson/scarlet fresh rose petals (`#C41E3A` to `#E63946`), naturally curved with light-catching edges.
  * Lighting: Backlit with translucent edge glow (subsurface scattering).
  * Depth of Field:
    * *Far-field:* Small, out-of-focus, low opacity (50%).
    * *Mid-field (Letter plane):* Crisp, distinct orientation (some drifting sideways, some top-down).
    * *Foreground:* Larger, slightly blurred petals creating atmospheric parallax depth.
* **Distribution:**
  * Clustered softly along the upper arch above the mirror.
  * Drifting across the left vertical stem of the `"S"`.
  * Suspended between the right of the mirror and the letter `"R"`.
  * Sparsely drifting across the bottom terrain.

### Asset C: Terrain & Floral Groundscape
* **Format:** Environment baseplate graphic or WebGL plane.
* **Visual Characteristics:**
  * Undulating, dreamlike landscape resembling soft, velvety white snowdrifts or thick billowy cumulus cloud floor.
  * Embedded with miniature botanical beds: Delicate, leafless white frosted twigs bearing tiny, vibrant scarlet-red blossom buds that echo the falling rose petals.
  * Depth treatment: Ground fades seamlessly into the luminous pearlescent white background fog using an atmospheric depth gradient.

### Asset D: Stepping Plinth / Altar Steps
* **Format:** Embedded in the ground plane directly below the center mirror.
* **Visual Characteristics:**
  * Faint, low-rise alabaster/rose-quartz rectangular steps or pedestal platform leading the eye inward toward the base of the mirror.
  * Shrouded in soft ground-level mist and volumetric light falloff.

## 6. Motion, Interaction & Behavior

### 1. Mouse Parallax & Depth
* The background terrain, the typography, the floating mirror, and the rose petals move on distinct parallax planes relative to cursor position (`mousemove`):
  * **Foreground Petals:** Large displacement (`factor: 0.05`).
  * **Mirror & Typography:** Medium displacement (`factor: 0.015`).
  * **Background Fog & Horizon:** Minimal displacement (`factor: 0.005`).

### 2. Ambient Floating Animations
* **The Gilded Mirror:** Gentle continuous floating bob (`keyframes: translate3d(0, -6px, 0)` to `translate3d(0, 6px, 0)` over 4.5s ease-in-out alternate).
* **The Gemstone Heart (Inside):** Continuous slow 3D rotation (`rotateY: 0deg -> 360deg` over 20s linear) with a subtle oscillating heartbeat scale pulse (`1.0 -> 1.04 -> 1.0`).
* **Rose Petals:** Continuous gentle drift downwards and sideways with slow tumbling axial rotations.

### 3. Interactive States
* **Header Links:** Underline expand transition on hover from center outwards (`0.3s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), color shifts from `#423C46` to `#181124`.
* **CTA Button (`BEGIN JOURNEY`):**
  * Default: Obsidian fill `#120E19`, crisp white text.
  * Hover: Subtle elevation, slight scale-up (`scale(1.03)`), background color shifts to deep garnet/crimson tone (`#280F16`), inner letter-spacing expands slightly to `0.26em`.
  * Cursor: Custom pointer with delicate circular ring cursor.
