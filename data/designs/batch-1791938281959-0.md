# Design Specification: Modern YouTube Clone (`design.md`)

## 1. Global Styles
*   **Background:** The application should occupy the full viewport. The primary background color is a deep matte black/dark grey (`#0f0f0f`).
*   **Border Radius:** A consistent radius of `16px` to `24px` is used for all major containers (video player, thumbnails, search bar, and buttons).
*   **Theme:** Dark Mode.
*   **Spacing:** A standard gutter of `16px` or `20px` between elements.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Roboto`, `Inter`, or `YouTube Sans`.
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `Segoe UI`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:**
    *   **Bold (700):** Video Title, Section Headers ("Shorts").
    *   **Medium (500):** Button labels, Channel name.
    *   **Regular (400):** Metadata (views, time), Search placeholder.
*   **Sizes:**
    *   Video Title: `24px`
    *   Channel Name: `16px`
    *   Metadata/Subscribers: `14px`
    *   Sidebar Titles: `14px`

## 3. Color Palette
*   **Primary Background:** `#0f0f0f`
*   **Surface/Card Background:** `#1e1e1e` (with subtle `0.8` opacity if using glassmorphism).
*   **Search Bar/Button Fill:** `#2a2a2a`
*   **Accent Red:** `#FF0000` (YouTube Logo & Progress Bar).
*   **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (100% opacity).
*   **Text Secondary:** `#AAAAAA` (Metadata and placeholders).
*   **Border/Divider:** `#333333`

## 4. Layout Components

    *   **Search Bar:** A pill-shaped container (`height: 40px`).
    *   **Icon (Left):** Magnifying glass in light grey.
    *   **Placeholder:** "Search videos..." in `#AAAAAA`.
    *   **Microphone:** A circular button on the right side of the search bar with a white mic icon.
*   **Right Section:**
    *   **Create Button:** Pill-shaped, grey background, white text "+ Create".

## 5. Image Assets & Details
1.  **Main Video Image:** High-resolution, cinematic, dark blue/teal color grading. Harry Potter is centered, slightly rotated toward the viewer.
2.  **Channel Icon:** Backgroundless gold "HP" initials in a stylized serif font.
3.  **User Avatar:** High-quality circular crop, no border.
4.  **Shorts Thumbnails:** These are backgroundless-style portraits layered over vibrant, text-heavy backgrounds. They should be perfectly vertical with no rotation.

## 6. Interactive States
*   **Hover:** Buttons (Subscribe, Like, Search) should lighten by 10% on hover.
*   **Active:** The "All" chip is highlighted in white; others remain dark.
*   **Transitions:** Smooth `0.2s` ease-in-out for all hover states.
