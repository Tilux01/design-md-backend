# Design Specification: Bake & Taste Hero Slider

## 1. Global Styles
*   **Background:** The entire viewport should have a radial gradient background. 
    *   **Center:** `#2b1d16` (Dark Chocolate Brown)
    *   **Edges:** `#0a0a0a` (Near Black)
    *   **Texture:** A subtle "smoky" or "dust" overlay with low opacity (approx. 10%) to give depth to the dark space.
*   **Text Color:** Primary text is `#FFFFFF`. Secondary/Body text is `#A0A0A0` (Light Grey).
*   **Container:** Full-width, full-height (`100vw`, `100vh`), overflow hidden.

## 2. Typography
*   **Logo Font:** 
    *   *Primary:* "Playfair Display" (Bold) or "Cormorant Garamond".
    *   *Fallback:* Serif.
    *   *Style:* White, uppercase, letter-spacing: 1px.
*   **Main Headline ("CHOCOLATE DELIGHT!"):**
    *   *Primary:* "Archivo Black" or "Montserrat" (Black 900).
    *   *Fallback:* Sans-serif.
    *   *Style:* Uppercase, **Italic**, font-size: approx 80px, line-height: 0.9, letter-spacing: -2px.
*   **Sub-headline ("TREAT YO SELF"):**
    *   *Primary:* "Montserrat" (Medium 500).
    *   *Style:* Uppercase, font-size: 14px, letter-spacing: 3px, color: `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Body Text:**
    *   *Primary:* "Roboto" or "Open Sans".
    *   *Style:* Font-size: 12px, line-height: 1.6, color: `#A0A0A0`, max-width: 250px.
*   **UI Elements (Buttons/Nav):**
    *   *Primary:* "Montserrat" (Semi-bold).
    *   *Style:* Uppercase, font-size: 12px.

## 3. Header Section
*   **Placement:** Fixed or absolute at the top. Padding: `40px 60px`.
*   **Left Side:** Logo text "Bake & Taste". Note the "&" symbol is slightly stylized/thinner.
*   **Right Side:** 
    *   Link: "ALL PRODUCTS" (Uppercase).
    *   Icon: Hamburger menu (two horizontal lines of unequal length, top longer than bottom).

## 4. Hero Content (Main Layout)
The layout uses a split-grid approach.

### Left Column (Text & CTA)
*   **Vertical Alignment:** Centered.
*   **Elements:** 
    1.  "TREAT YO SELF" (Top).
    2.  "CHOCOLATE DELIGHT!" (Middle).
    3.  **Button:** "SHOP NOW".
        *   *Shape:* Pill-shaped (rounded-full).
        *   *Border:* 1px solid `#555555`.
        *   *Padding:* 12px 35px.
        *   *Hover effect:* White background, black text.

### Center-Right (Main Image)
*   **Image Description:** A high-resolution, **backgroundless (PNG)** chocolate milkshake served in a vintage glass mason jar mug with a handle.
    *   **Top:** A tall peak of whipped cream.
    *   **Details:** Dark chocolate syrup dripping down the sides of the glass.
    *   **Floating Elements:** 3 to 4 individual squares of milk chocolate floating around the top of the mug.
    *   **Placement:** The mug is centered vertically but offset slightly to the right of the screen center.
    *   **Rotation:** The mug is straight, but the floating chocolate squares are tilted at various angles (approx 15° to 45°) to simulate falling.
    *   **Shadow:** A soft, dark contact shadow directly beneath the base of the mug to ground it.

### Right Column (Details)
*   **Slider Indicator:** "01 / 04" placed above the body text. The "01" is white, the "/ 04" is grey.
*   **Description Text:** A paragraph of Lorem Ipsum justified to the left, positioned to the right of the mug.

## 5. Footer & Controls
*   **Bottom Left:** 
    *   Pagination controls: `<` (Left Arrow), four dots (the first dot is white/active, others are grey), and `>` (Right Arrow).
*   **Bottom Right:**
    *   Label: "FOLLOW US" (Small, uppercase).
    *   Icons: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube (Small, white, spaced 15px apart).

## 6. Image Asset Requirements
1.  **Main Mug:** `chocolate_shake.png` - Transparent background, high contrast, warm lighting.
2.  **Chocolate Chunks:** `chocolate_chunk.png` - Transparent background, varying sizes.
3.  **Background Texture:** `smoke_overlay.jpg` - Subtle, dark, used with `mix-blend-mode: screen` or `opacity: 0.1`.

## 7. Spacing & Measurements (Desktop)
*   **Side Margins:** 60px.
*   **Headline to Sub-headline:** 10px.
*   **Headline to Button:** 40px.
*   **Mug Width:** Approximately 35% of the viewport width.
