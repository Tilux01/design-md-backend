# Visual Design Specification: E-Commerce Shoe Store Homepage

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Color Palette
- **Primary / Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Secondary / Surface:** `#F8F8F8` (Off-White / Light Gray for subtle product cards or backgrounds)
- **Text Main:** `#1A1A1A` (Near Black for headings and primary labels)
- **Text Muted / Secondary:** `#666666` (Medium Gray for descriptions and subtitles)
- **Border / Divider:** `#E5E5E5` (Light Gray for structural lines and image containers)

### Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** `Montserrat`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- **Fallback Stack:** `sans-serif`
- **Scale & Weights:**
  - **Hero Heading (Brand Heritage overlay):** 28px - 36px, Weight: `600` (Semi-Bold), Uppercase, Letter-spacing: `2px`
  - **Section Titles (e.g., "FEATURED COLLECTION / HAND PATINA"):** 14px - 16px, Weight: `500` (Medium), Uppercase, Letter-spacing: `1.5px`
  - **Product Titles:** 11px - 12px, Weight: `500` (Medium), Uppercase
  - **Product Prices:** 11px - 12px, Weight: `400` (Regular), Muted color (`#666666`)
  - **Button Text ("SHOP ALL"):** 10px - 11px, Weight: `600` (Semi-Bold), Uppercase, Letter-spacing: `1px`

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Components

### 2.1 Hero Banner Section
- **Dimensions:** Full-width container (`100%` width), height proportional to screen view (approx. `500px` to `600px`).
- **Background Image:** High-resolution lifestyle photography showing a man sitting on stone steps wearing tailored trousers and dress shoes.
- **Image Treatment:** Dark horizontal overlay gradient to ensure high text legibility over the photography.
- **Content Placement (Centered/Bottom-Centered):**
  - **Heading:** `"BRAND HERITAGE"` (White, uppercase, bold).
  - **Call to Action (CTA) Button:** 
    - Outline or solid minimalist rectangular button with the label `"SHOP ALL"`.
    - Padding: `8px 24px`.
    - Border: `1px solid #FFFFFF`.
    - Background: Transparent or semi-transparent white hover effect.

### 2.2 Featured Products Grid Section
- **Container:** Centered layout with max-width (approx. `1200px`), generous top and bottom padding (`60px` vertical).
- **Sub-header:** 
  - Eyebrow text: `"FEATURED COLLECTION"` (Muted gray, small uppercase).
  - Main section title: `"HAND PATINA"` (Bold, centered, dark text).
- **Grid Configuration:**
  - **Desktop:** 4 columns × 2 rows (Total 8 product items visible).
  - **Mobile:** 2 columns × 4 rows.
  - **Gap:** `24px` horizontal and vertical spacing.

### 2.3 Product Card Component
- **Image Container:**
  - Aspect ratio: `4:3` or square (`1:1`).
  - Background: Light gray/off-white (`#F8F8F8`) product tile.
  - Image: Clean, studio-lit shoe photograph isolated on a plain background (facing left or angled).
- **Product Details (Below Image, Left-Aligned):**
  - **Product Name:** E.g., `"KIDSLANE DERBYAN BROWN"` or `"BLACK CHELSEA BOOT W/KID"`. (Font size: `11px`, Uppercase, Dark text).
  - **Price:** E.g., `"FROM $599.00 AUD"`. (Font size: `11px`, Muted gray text).

---

## 3. Responsive Breakpoints

- **Desktop (`>= 1024px`):** Full 4-column product grid, prominent hero banner with large typography.
- **Tablet (`768px - 1023px`):** 3-column product grid, scaled padding.
- **Mobile (`< 768px`):** 2-column product grid, stacked vertical elements, touch-optimized spacing and button sizes.

---

## 4. Animation & Interactions (CSS / JS)

- **Hover Effects on Product Cards:**
  - Smooth scale/zoom on product image (`transform: scale(1.03)`, duration: `0.3s ease`).
  - Subtle shadow enhancement on the card container.
- **Button Micro-interactions:**
  - `"SHOP ALL"` button background inverts on hover (White background with dark text `#1A1A1A`).
- **Scroll Animations:**
  - Fade-in-up animation (`opacity: 0` to `1`, `translateY(20px)` to `0`) applied to the product grid items as they enter the viewport.
