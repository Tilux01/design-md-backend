# Comprehensive UI/UX Design Specification & Replication Blueprint

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Architecture
- **Viewport Structure:** Full-screen implementation (`width: 100vw; height: 100vh; overflow: hidden; position: relative;`).
- **Background Color:** Deep charcoal/obsidian black (`#111111` or `#131313`), providing a high-contrast dark-mode aesthetic.
- **Grid & Positioning System:** Absolute positioning layout with responsive flex containers for top-bar navigation and footer elements. Content is layered using clear z-index hierarchies (Background typography at `z-index: 1`, Main imagery at `z-index: 2`, Foreground UI/Text at `z-index: 3`).

---

## 2. Typography & Font Stack
The design relies on a bold, geometric, grotesque sans-serif pairing emphasizing high-weight header impact and clean, legible tracking for metadata.

### Font Families
- **Primary Headers & Display Text:** 
  - *Primary:* "Bebas Neue", "Anton", or "Syne" (Extra Bold).
  - *Fallback Stack:* `Impact, sans-serif`.
- **UI Elements, Metadata, & Body Copy:** 
  - *Primary:* "Inter", "Plus Jakarta Sans", or "Space Grotesk".
  - *Fallback Stack:* `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

### Typography Specifications
| Element | Font Family | Size | Weight | Tracking (Letter Spacing) | Color |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top-Bar Status** | Inter | 11px | Regular (400) | `0.05em` | White (`#FFFFFF`) / Muted Gray (`#888888`) |
| **"CREATIVE OFFICE"** | Inter / Space Grotesk | 10px | Bold (700) | `0.15em` | Orange Accent (`#FF4500`) |
| **"IDEAS WITH EDGE"** | Bebas Neue / Anton | 56px–72px | Heavy / Black | `0.02em` | Bright Orange (`#FF4500`) |
| **"BUILT TO ENDURE"** | Bebas Neue / Anton | 56px–72px | Heavy / Black | `0.02em` | Light Gray / Off-White (`#E5E5E5`) |
| **Watermark Text ("ANTA")**| Anton / Impact | 280px+ | Black (900) | `0.05em` | Dark Charcoal / Layered Background (`#1C1C1C`) |
| **Body Paragraph** | Inter | 14px | Regular (400) | `0.01em` | Light Silver (`#CCCCCC`) |
| **"SELECTED PARTNERS"** | Inter | 10px | Medium (500) | `0.1em` | Muted Gray (`#777777`) |
| **Location Subtext** | Inter | 10px | Medium (500) | `0.12em` | Muted Gray (`#999999`) |

- **Positioning:** Fixed top bar, spanning horizontally across the viewport with structural padding (`top: 30px; left: 40px; right: 40px;`).

  - **Left Pill Segment ("AVAILABLE FOR SELECT PROJECTS"):** 
    - Features a small blinking/solid orange status indicator dot.
    - Text: `AVAILABLE FOR SELECT PROJECTS` (Uppercase, small, tracked out). Separated from the next section by a thin vertical divider line (`rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`).
  - **Middle Pill Segment ("SPRING 2026"):** 
    - Text: `SPRING 2026` in muted gray/white.
  - **Right Action Button ("START A PROJECT ↗"):**

## 5. Visual Asset Specification (Main Imagery)
- **Asset Description:** A striking, high-contrast photographic portrait of a young person viewed in profile (three-quarter view facing left). 
  - **Subject:** A person with dark, wet-styled or textured hair swept forward, sharp jawline, and defined cheekbones. Wearing a dark mock-neck or turtleneck top.
  - **Lighting & Color Grading:** Dramatic, cinematic side-lighting with a vivid warm orange/red rim-light accent sweeping up from the bottom-left, casting a fiery glow along the jaw, neck, and lower facial contours against a pitch-black background.
- **Background State:** The image naturally blends into the dark UI background (the source image file itself is framed with a dark backdrop, styled seamlessly into the `#111111` canvas).
- **Placement & Dimensions:** 
  - Positioned on the right side of the screen (`right: 0; top: 100px; bottom: 0; width: 55vw; height: 100vh; object-fit: cover;`).
  - No rotation; cropped dynamically to anchor the subject’s profile on the right edge.
- **Side Watermark ("IMAGE NOT INCLUDED"):**
  - Vertically oriented text running along the absolute right margin edge (`writing-mode: vertical-rl; transform: rotate(180deg); font-size: 10px; letter-spacing: 0.2em; color: rgba(255,255,255,0.3);`).
