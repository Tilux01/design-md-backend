# Design Specification: Coca-Cola Dark Theme Landing Page

Full-screen desktop landing page for a modern, dark-themed Coca-Cola product showcase.

---

## 1. Color Palette

*   **Primary Background:** `#080808` (Deep Matte Black)
*   **Accent Red:** `#E61A27` (Coca-Cola Red)
*   **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
*   **Text Secondary:** `#737373` (Muted Gray)
*   **Dividers / Subtle Accents:** `#1F1F1F` to `#330005` (Dark Red-Black Gradient)

---

## 2. Typography

*   **Logo & Headings:** Modern geometric sans-serif
    *   *Primary:* `Montserrat`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif
    *   *Weights:* 400 (Regular), 700 (Bold)
*   **Script Title (Background):** 
    *   *Primary:* `Loki Cola` or official `Coca-Cola Caret` script
    *   *Fallback:* `Brush Script MT`, cursive
*   **Body Text:** 
    *   *Primary:* `Inter`, `Roboto`, sans-serif
    *   *Weights:* 300 (Light), 400 (Regular)
*   **Character Casing & Spacing:** Navigation links, badges, and footer text use `uppercase` with wide tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.15em`).

---

## 3. Layout Structure

*   **Container:** `100vw` × `100vh`, fixed viewport, no scroll (`overflow: hidden`).
*   **Grid:** 12-column layout with 48px side margins.

### Navigation (Top Bar)
*   **Height:** ~80px, flex layout, vertically centered.
*   **Left:** Brand mark `CL.` (Bold, 20px, white).
*   **Center-Right:** Menu list (`HOME`, `OUR PRODUCTS`, `HERITAGE`, `ABOUT`, `CONTACT`) in 11px uppercase gray, spaced 32px apart. Active/Hover state: White.
*   **Far Right:** Social icons (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter) in muted white/gray, 14px size, 16px gap.

### Left Sidebar (Slider Navigation & Legal)
*   **Indicator (Vertical Center):**
    *   Vertical line with navigation dots.
    *   Active dot: Surrounded by a dual circular outline.
    *   Label: `01 / 05` next to the active dot (11px, white, monospace/sans-serif).
*   **Bottom Left:** Two-line copyright notice (`© 2019 / ALL RIGHT ARE RESERVED`), 9px, uppercase, `#555555`.

### Main Stage (Center Display)
*   **Red Accent Line:** Thin horizontal line crossing the viewport horizontally behind the foreground elements.
*   **Background Typography:** Large Coca-Cola script logo (`#E61A27`, roughly 45vw wide), centered horizontally, positioned across the upper-middle canvas.
*   **Foreground Product Asset:**
    *   *Description:* Matte-black Coca-Cola Zero glass bottle with black cap and white/gray vertical typography (`Coca-Cola zero`).
    *   *Format:* Transparent PNG / WebP (backgroundless).
    *   *Placement:* Centered, directly in front of the red script text, extending from bottom-center to upper-middle.
    *   *Rotation:* 0° (perfectly vertical).

### Content Block (Middle Right)
*   **Alignment:** Left-aligned, positioned to the right of the bottle.
*   **Badge/Title:**
    *   Solid red rectangle badge containing `COCA` in white bold sans-serif.
    *   Adjacent plain text `COLA` in thin white sans-serif.
*   **Description:** Short paragraph (`Coca-Cola, or Coke, is a carbonated soft drink manufactured by The Coca-Cola Company.`), 12px, line-height 1.6, color `#8E8E8E`, max-width 260px.
*   **CTA Link:** `READ MORE >>>` (10px, bold, `#E61A27`, tracking 0.1em).

### Bottom Right (Slider Controls)
*   **Arrows:** Two square buttons (`<` and `>`), dark gray background (`#1F1F1F`), light gray icons.
*   **Placement:** Fixed bottom right corner, aligned with side margins.
