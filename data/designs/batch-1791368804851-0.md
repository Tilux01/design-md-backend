# UI Design Specification: Devialet E-commerce Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Foundations

### Color Palette
- **Primary Background (Black):** `#0a0a0a` (Deep matte black with subtle gradient overlay)
- **Primary Text & Highlights (White):** `#ffffff`
- **Secondary Text (Muted Gray):** `#888888`
- **Subtle Surface/Border Gray:** `#1f1f1f`
- **Accent/Interactive Glow:** Subtle white vignettes and radial gradients (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`)

### Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** Inter, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif
- **Heading Styles:**
  - **Hero Heading ("DEVIALET"):** `font-size: 110px`, `font-weight: 800`, `letter-spacing: 0.05em`, `text-transform: uppercase`, `color: #ffffff`
  - **Section Headings ("ABOUT US", "DEVIALET COLLECTION"):** `font-size: 28px`, `font-weight: 700`, `letter-spacing: 0.1em`, `text-transform: uppercase`
- **Body & Subtext Styles:**
  - **Hero Subtext / Tagline:** `font-size: 12px`, `font-weight: 400`, `color: #aaaaaa`, `line-height: 1.5`
  - **About Paragraphs:** `font-size: 13px`, `font-weight: 400`, `color: #999999`, `line-height: 1.6`

---

## 2. Component Breakdown & Layout

### A. Navigation Header
- **Layout:** Flexbox, full width, fixed/sticky to top.
- **Left Element:** Brand Logo/Name `"DEVIALET"` (`font-size: 16px`, `font-weight: 700`, `letter-spacing: 0.2em`).
- **Center Links:** `"HOME"`, `"ABOUT"`, `"COLLECTION"` (`font-size: 11px`, `font-weight: 500`, `letter-spacing: 0.15em`, `color: #cccccc`).
- **Right Element:** Call-to-action button `"BUY"` (Pill-shaped, transparent background, thin white border, `font-size: 11px`, `padding: 8px 20px`, `border-radius: 20px`).

---

### B. Hero Section
- **Background:** Dark abstract fluid/smoke textures in grayscale with radial lighting from the center.
- **Background Typography:** Giant `"DEVIALET"` watermark text positioned centrally behind the product render.
- **Left Floating Text Block:**
  - `"Elevate the Sound."` (`font-size: 12px`, `font-weight: 500`)
  - `"Get Your Sonic Fix: Where Luxury Meets Acoustical Euphoria with Devialet."` (`font-size: 11px`, `color: #777777`, `max-width: 200px`)
- **Centerpiece:** 3D Product render of the Devialet speaker resting on a dark cylindrical pedestal.
- **Right Floating Elements:** Vertical stack of social media icons (Instagram, Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn) in white outline style.
- **Bottom Center:** Minimalist downward chevron scroll indicator inside a thin rounded pill outline.

---

### C. About Us Section
- **Section Heading Group:**
  - `"ABOUT US"` (`font-size: 24px`, `font-weight: 700`, `text-align: center`)
  - `"Sound in its purest state."` (`font-size: 12px`, `color: #666666`, `text-align: center`)
- **Two-Column Text Grid:**
  - Balanced columns detailing Devialet's heritage as a French audio company, the Phantom speaker, and luxury design awards.
- **Gallery Grid (3 Grayscale Images):**
  - Three equal-width containers displaying atmospheric black-and-white photography of high-end audio installations and listening rooms.
  - Subtle dark rounded borders (`border-radius: 8px`, `border: 1px solid #1a1a1a`).

---

### D. Product Collection Carousel Section
- **Section Heading:** `"DEVIALET COLLECTION"` (Bottom left).
- **Carousel Layout:** Horizontal alignment with a featured central item and faded side previews.
  - **Left Preview Item:** Devialet portable/wearable speaker asset within a circular dark gradient card (`background: radial-gradient(#151515, #080808)`).
  - **Center Featured Item:** Large prominent Devialet speaker render on a subtle stage floor reflection.
  - **Right Preview Item:** Devialet wireless earbuds charging case within a circular dark gradient card.
- **Navigation Controls:** 
  - Left/Right chevrons (`<`, `>`) flanking the carousel items with accompanying labels (`"Left"`, `"Right"`).
- **Carousel Pagination:**
  - Horizontal progress indicator line with step numbers: `01` — `02 03 04 05`
- **Bottom Right Footer Social Links:** `TW` / `IN` / `FB` text links.

---

## 3. Responsive & Interactive Guidelines
- **Hover States:** Links and buttons subtly brighten to pure white (`#ffffff`).
- **Spacing:** Generous vertical padding (`100px+`) between major sections to maintain a high-end, uncluttered luxury aesthetic.
- **Color Treatment:** Strictly monochrome/grayscale palette across all imagery, typography, and UI elements.
