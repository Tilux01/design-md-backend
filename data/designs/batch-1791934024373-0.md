# Design Specification: Sukhart Portfolio Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Layout
* **Layout Style:** Fullscreen, single-viewport dark portfolio landing page.
* **Canvas Size:** 100vw × 100vh, `overflow: hidden`, zero margins/padding.
* **Background Color:** `#000000` (Pure Black).
* **Grid / Alignment:** Two-column split layout (Text on left, typographic portrait on right).

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Background:** `#000000`
* **Primary Text:** `#FFFFFF`
* **Muted / Divider:** `#333333` to `#4D4D4D`
* **Sub-element Highlight / Underline:** `#7A7A7A`

---

## 3. Typography & Fallbacks
* **Primary Display Font (Headings):**
  * *Font:* **Monument Extended (Black)**, **Druk Wide Bold**, or **Syne (Extra Bold)**.
  * *Fallbacks:* `'Arial Black'`, `Impact`, `sans-serif`.
* **Body / Navigation Font:**
  * *Font:* **Inter**, **Helvetica Neue**, or **Arial**.
  * *Fallbacks:* `sans-serif`.

---

## 4. Header / Navigation
* **Placement:** Fixed at top, full width, padded `30px 50px`.
* **Divider:** Continuous `1px solid #333333` running beneath the navigation items.
* **Brand/Name (Left):**
  * Text: `Grigory Sukhaterin`
  * Specs: Regular weight, 14px, `#FFFFFF`.
* **Nav Links (Right):**
  * Items: `Projects`, `About`, `Contacts`
  * Specs: 14px, regular weight, `#FFFFFF`, `gap: 30px`.

---

## 5. Main Content (Left Column)
Positioned on the left side (`left: 50px`), vertically distributed:

### A. Main Headline
* **Text:**
  * Line 1: `HELLO!`
  * Line 2: `I'M SUKHART`
* **Specs:** Upper case, ~96px–110px font size, line-height 0.95, letter-spacing -1px, font-weight 900.

### B. Role Tag
* **Placement:** Below headline with `margin-top: 80px`.
* **Text:** `UX/UI DESIGNER`
* **Specs:** Upper case, ~24px font size, bold extended font.
* **Decoration:** A subtle light gray/translucent horizontal underline bar (`#7A7A7A`, ~3px thick) directly beneath the text baseline extending slightly past the text edge.

### C. Bio Paragraph
* **Placement:** Positioned lower-left (`margin-top: 100px`, max-width `280px`).
* **Text:**
  > I'm a UX/UI designer, available for work, new projects, collaborations, and freelance opportunities. I also have tree years of experience as a graphic designer, creating digital and print design
* **Specs:** 12px font size, regular weight, line-height 1.5, `#FFFFFF`.

---

## 6. Visual Asset: Typographic Face Portrait (Right Column)

* **Asset Type:** Transparent PNG or SVG silhouette portrait (backgroundless, blends directly into `#000000`).
* **Description:**
  * A human face/bust rendered purely through varied-density typography and calligram text (Latin/Lorem Ipsum text such as *dolor*, *consectetur*, *laboris*).
  * Shading, facial contours, eyes, nose, and lips are defined by the scale, weight, and white/gray brightness levels of the letters against the dark background.
  * Bottom/neck text trails off upside down and fades into the darkness.
* **Placement:**
  * Horizontally: Center-right (`right: 8%` to `12%`).
  * Vertically: Centered (`top: 52%`, `transform: translateY(-50%)`).
  * Scale: Height roughly `70vh` to `75vh`.
  * Rotation: `0deg` (straight-on frontal view).
