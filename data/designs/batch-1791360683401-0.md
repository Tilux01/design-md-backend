# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Luxury Minimalist with Technical High-Horology Precision & Immersive Dark-Mode E-commerce.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-end luxury editorial balance. Ample negative space combined with immersive asymmetric split-screen visual storytelling.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-layered absolute and flex surfaces featuring absolute black canvases (`#0b0b0b`), deep charcoal card backgrounds, and muted olive-drab tactical accent blocks (`#6b7353`). Soft diffuse shadows and precise 1px subtle borders.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase luxury navigation items, circular interactive video play buttons with glowing hover states, subtle product spec watermarks, precise technical spec badges.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `11px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#141414`, border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Arrow symbols (‹ / › / ➔) -> Use entities `&#8249;`, `&#8250;`, `&#10132;` or clean SVG icons.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-emerald-400 inline-block"></span>`.
