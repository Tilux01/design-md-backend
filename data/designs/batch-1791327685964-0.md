# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Futuristic Cyber-Noir Minimalist & AI-Tech Editorial.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-impact editorial whitespace juxtaposed with dense geometric wireframe visual assets. Optimized for immersive dark-mode digital experiences.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered obsidian panels utilizing stark high-contrast pure white typography, subtle glassmorphic alpha overlays (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`), and sharp 1px borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Geometric grid matrices, 3D wireframe heads, floating particle atomic structures, high-tracking uppercase branding.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `6px 16px`, font-size: `12px`, font-weight: `600`, tracking: `0.1em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`, border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-blue-500 inline-block"></span>`.
