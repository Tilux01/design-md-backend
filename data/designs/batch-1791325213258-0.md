# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist Portfolio / Creative Showcase
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio with heavy emphasis on central typographic hierarchy and dynamic horizontal card galleries.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, clean background with subtle border-free image cards and rounded perspective container framing.

---

# 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

## Color Token Definitions
- **Primary Brand Color & Variants**: Default `#FF6A00` (Vibrant Amber/Orange), Hover `#E05D00`, Active `#C25000`, Subdued `rgba(255, 106, 0, 0.1)`.
- **Secondary & Accent Colors**: Dark Charcoal `#111827`, Muted Gray `#6B7280`.
- **Background & Surface Architecture**: Canvas background `#FFFFFF`, Surface 1 (Cards) `#F3F4F6`, Surface 2 (Pills) `#FFFFFF`.
- **Border & Divider Scale**: Base border `#E5E7EB`.
- **Text Color Hierarchy**: Primary text `#111827`, Secondary/Muted text `#6B7280`, Accent text `#FF6A00`.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-contrast brand pill button with arrow icon, curved card perspective carousel layout, step indicators (`#01`, `#02`, `#03`, `#04`).
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, border-radius `9999px`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**: Arrow right icon -> Use SVG or `&rarr;`.
