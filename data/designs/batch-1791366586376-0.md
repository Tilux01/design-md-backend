# Design Specification: AIAIAI TMA-2 Website Concept

## 1. Global Styles & Variables

*   **Color Palette:**
    *   Primary Background (Dark Section): `#212121` (Dark Charcoal / Off-Black)
    *   Secondary Background (Light Section): `#ECECEC` (Light Gray)
    *   Accent Color (Red): `#F40600` (Vibrant Red)
    *   Text Primary (Light): `#FFFFFF` (White)
    *   Text Secondary (Light): `#B0B0B0` (Muted Gray)
    *   Text Primary (Dark): `#1A1A1A` (Near Black)
    *   Text Secondary (Dark): `#777777` (Medium Gray)
    *   Surface / Container: `#333333` (Slightly lighter dark gray for UI blocks)

*   **Typography:**
    *   Font Family: Sans-serif (Geometric/Grotesque, e.g., Inter, Montserrat, or Helvetica Neue)
    *   Header 1 ("CATCH THE BEAT"): Bold, Uppercase, ~56px, Line-height: 1.1
    *   Section Numbers ("02"): Bold, ~72px
    *   Navigation Items: Medium, Uppercase, ~13px, Letter-spacing: 0.5px
    *   Body Text: Regular, ~13px, Line-height: 1.5, Color: `#CCCCCC`

*   **Layout Structure:**
    *   Full-width viewport (100vw × 100vh).
    *   Split-screen layout style (asymmetrical vertical/horizontal interplay).
    *   Dark background dominates the right/top side; light background anchors the bottom-left.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation Bar

*   **Position:** Fixed top, padding: `24px 48px`, full width.
*   **Elements (Left to Right):**
    *   **Brand Logo:** "AIAIAI" (Bold, uppercase, tracking: wide, color: dark gray/black `#1A1A1A` on the left side, adapting or remaining dark across the split).
    *   **Navigation Links:**
        *   `Headphones` (Active/Highlighted or standard)
        *   `Discovery`
        *   `Configuration`
        *   `Artist`
    *   **Icons (Right side):**
        *   User/Account Icon (Outline)
        *   Search Icon (Outline)

---

## 3. Hero Section / Main Content Area

### Left Side (Light Panel)
*   **Background:** `#ECECEC`
*   **Video Thumbnail Component:**
    *   Position: Absolute/Relative offset within the light area.
    *   Dimensions: ~160px × 100px.
    *   Content: Image of headphones on a dark surface with a vertical red accent bar on the right edge containing the text vertical: `PLAY VIDEO`.
*   **Date Stamp:**
    *   Text: `13 November 2017` (Vertical line separator or styled horizontally/vertically as shown: "13 November 2017").
*   **Carousel / Navigation Dots:**
    *   Two circular buttons at the bottom left: Red background (`#F40600`) with white left/right arrows (`←` `→`).

### Right Side (Dark Panel)
*   **Background:** `#212121`
*   **Slide Number:**
    *   Text: `02`
    *   Styling: Large bold font, framed by a thin red circular outline offset to the top right of the number.
*   **Main Headline:**
    *   Text: `CATCH THE BEAT`
    *   Styling: Heavy weight, stacked lines, white color.
*   **Body Copy:**
    *   Text: `AIAIAI's TMA-2 combines sleek design with immaculate sound performance. They allow users to create their unique sense of listening. Idealy configurated and dynamic headphone for the passionate audiophile...`
    *   Styling: Regular weight, muted gray (`#CCCCCC`), max-width constrained to align with the text block.
*   **Call to Action (CTA) Button:**
    *   Text: `READ MORE`
    *   Styling: Solid red background (`#F40600`), white text, bold, uppercase, padding: `10px 24px`, sharp rectangular corners.

---

## 4. Floating / Overlay Elements

*   **Product Imagery (Headphones):**
    *   A physical, modular over-ear headphone unit (black matte finish) rendered centrally, bridging the light and dark sections.
    *   Interactive hotspot indicators: Small white circles with semi-transparent pulsing outer rings pointing to specific parts of the headphone (e.g., headband adjustment, ear cushion).
*   **Pagination Indicators (Right Edge):**
    *   Vertical stack of three dots (top two inactive/gray outlines, middle one active/solid white).
*   **Footer / Social Links (Bottom Right):**
    *   Text links aligned horizontally: `FB` | `TW` | `IN` separated by subtle spacing, paired with a horizontal white line extending to the edge.

---

## 5. Responsive Behavior & Interactions
*   **Hover States:**
    *   Nav links underline or slightly brighten.
    *   CTA button (`READ MORE`) darkens slightly on hover.
    *   Hotspot markers expand on hover to show tooltips.
