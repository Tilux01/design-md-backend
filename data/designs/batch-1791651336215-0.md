# Design Specification: "THE FUTURE" Landing Page (Hybri)

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport
- **Mode:** Full-screen viewport, edge-to-edge layout (ignoring any device mockup framing/shadows present in the presentation image).
- **Aspect Ratio & Scaling:** Responsive web layout optimized for wide desktop viewports (1440px+).
- **Background Color:** Deep dark navy/black gradient background (`#0B0E14` fading into darker charcoal).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### **Primary Display Font (Brand & Headings)**
- **Font Family:** `Syncopate`, `Montserrat`, or `Orbitron` (Fallback: `sans-serif`). Geometric, ultra-wide sans-serif style.
- **Usage:** 
  - Brand Logo: "HYBRI" (White, uppercase, spaced tracking).
  - Main Heading: "THE FUTURE" ("THE" is smaller, tracking-spaced; "FUTURE" is large, bold display text in light cream/off-white color).

### **Body & Navigation Font**
- **Font Family:** `Inter`, `Roboto`, or `Helvetica Neue` (Fallback: `sans-serif`). Clean, modern sans-serif.
- **Usage:** 
  - Navigation links (HOME, ABOUT, SERVICE, WORKS, CONTACT)
  - Paragraph descriptions
  - Button text ("Let's go")
  - Footer cards ("OUR MISSION", "OUR VISION")

---

## 3. Navigation Bar (Header)
- **Positioning:** Absolute/Fixed top, spanning horizontally with generous padding.
- **Left Element:** 
  - Brand Logo text: **HYBRI** in clean, bold white uppercase letters with letter-spacing.
- **Center Elements (Navigation Links):**
  - Items: `HOME`, `ABOUT`, `SERVUCE` (Note: Typography on design reads "SERVUCE" / "SERVICE"), `WORKS`, `CONTACT`.
  - Color: White / Light Gray (`#A0A5B0`), active/hover state in pure white.
- **Right Element:**
  - Icon: Grid/Menu icon composed of 3x3 small white dots (`grid-dots` icon).

---

## 4. Hero Section Content (Left Column)

- **Subheading / Pre-header:** 
  - Text: `THE` (Small tracking-spaced white uppercase text).
- **Main Heading:**
  - Text: `FUTURE`
  - Styling: Large geometric font, light cream/champagne color (`#EFEAD8`), sharp edges.
- **Body Paragraph:**
  - Text: *"Our major focus is to make people control the world as they wish to any direction of their choice. People who do so are strong."*
  - Styling: Muted gray text (`#8E939E`), regular weight, line-height 1.6, constrained max-width (~450px).
  - Accent: A vertical thin white/light line is positioned immediately to the left of the paragraph text.
- **Call to Action (CTA) Button:**
  - Text: `Let's go`
  - Styling: Dark/transparent background with a thin white border (`1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.3)`), subtle rounded corners or sharp box, padding ~12px 32px, white text.

---

## 5. Hero Imagery & Composition (Right Area)

- **Main Subject:**
  - **Description:** A cinematic portrait of a young person with dark tied-back hair and wire-rimmed glasses, wearing a dark textured/tweed jacket over a black shirt. They are holding a glowing horizontal neon-orange/amber light bar horizontally across their hands and chest.
  - **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Dramatic cyber-punk cinematic lighting. The amber light casts a strong warm glow onto their hands, face, and clothing, contrasting with the cool deep blue/purple ambient tones of the background.
  - **Background Integration:** The image blends seamlessly into the dark background on the right side.
  - **Placement & Rotation:** Positioned on the mid-right side of the viewport, vertically centered, filling approximately 50% of the right container width. No rotation applied to the subject itself; the light bar is held completely horizontal.

---

## 6. Bottom Cards Section (Our Mission & Our Vision)

- **Container Placement:** Positioned at the bottom right of the hero section, overlapping the lower portion of the visual space.
- **Card Styling:** Semi-transparent dark glassmorphism cards with a subtle dark backdrop (`rgba(15, 20, 30, 0.7)`), thin border strokes, and dark gradient overlays.
- **Card 1: OUR MISSION**
  - Header: `OUR MISSION` (Small, uppercase, white, bold).
  - Description: *"Making the world united to its fullest with technology"* (Muted gray font).
- **Card 2: OUR VISION**
  - Header: `OUR VISION` (Small, uppercase, white, bold).
  - Description: *"Our mission is to make the world use its resources to the fullest"* (Muted gray font).

---

## 7. Interactive States & Micro-interactions
- **Hover on Nav Links:** Color transition from muted gray (`#A0A5B0`) to pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Hover on CTA Button ("Let's go"):** Background fill transitions from transparent to solid white, text color transitions from white to deep black (`#0B0E14`).
- **Hover on Mission/Vision Cards:** Slight increase in border brightness or backdrop opacity.
