# Design Specification: Alex Morgan Portfolio

## 1. Typography & Colors

### Fonts
- **Primary Font:** Inter, SF Pro Display, or Helvetica Neue.
- **Fallbacks:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif`.
- **Weights:** Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (700).

### Color Palette
- **Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Card Background:** `#F8F9FA` (Soft Off-White/Light Gray)
- **Primary Text:** `#111111` (Deep Charcoal/Black)
- **Secondary Text:** `#666666` (Neutral Gray)
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#E5E7EB` (Subtle Light Gray)
- **Accent/Button Fill:** `#111111` (Black), `#FFFFFF` (White)

---

## 2. Layout & Spacing
- **Container:** Full width, centered max-width `1280px` with `48px` horizontal padding.
- **Section Gap:** `64px` to `80px` vertical spacing between sections.

---

## 3. Header / Navigation
- **Height:** `80px`, flex layout, space-between, vertically aligned.
- **Brand Name:** "Alex Morgan" (Bold, `18px`, `#111111`).
- **Nav Links:** Centered horizontally.
  - Links: "Home", "Projects", "About", "Contact" (`15px`, medium).
  - Active State ("Home"): Underlined with a `2px` solid black indicator placed `6px` below.
- **Right CTA:** "Let's Talk"
  - Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`).
  - Solid `#111111` background, `#FFFFFF` text (`14px`, medium), padding: `10px 22px`.

---

## 4. Hero Section
- **Grid Layout:** 2 columns (50% / 50% split, `48px` gap, vertically centered).

### Left Column (Content)
- **Eyebrow:** "Hello, I'm Alex Morgan" (`16px`, regular, `#111111`, margin-bottom `16px`).
- **Headline:** "I design digital experiences that make an impact." (`52px`, bold, line-height `1.15`, letter-spacing `-0.02em`, margin-bottom `20px`).
- **Body Copy:** "I'm a digital product designer based in San Francisco, specializing in user experience and interface design." (`16px`, regular, `#666666`, max-width `460px`, margin-bottom `32px`).
- **CTA Group:** Horizontal flex with `12px` gap.
  - Primary: "View My Work →" (Black pill button, white text, padding `12px 24px`, includes right arrow).
  - Secondary: "About Me" (White pill button, 1px `#E5E7EB` border, black text, padding `12px 24px`).

### Right Column (Hero Media)
- **Dimensions:** Aspect ratio ~4:3, rounded corners (`20px`), overflow hidden.

---

## 5. Featured Projects Section

### Section Header
- Flex layout, space-between, bottom margin `24px`.
- **Title:** "Featured Projects" (`20px`, bold, `#111111`).
- **View All Link:** "View All Projects →" (`14px`, medium, `#111111`).

### Card Grid
- **Columns:** 4-column responsive grid (`20px` gap).
- **Card Structure:** 
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`.
  - Border: 1px solid `#F0F0F0`.
  - Border Radius: `16px`.
  - Internal Padding: `12px` (top/sides), `16px` (bottom).

#### Card Elements
1. **Media Container:** Aspect ratio ~4:3, rounded inner corners (`12px`), background `#F3F4F6`, overflow hidden.
2. **Text Area:** Margin top `12px`.
   - **Title Row:** Bold title + category (e.g., "Furni — E-commerce") at `14px`, `#111111`.
   - **Description:** 2 lines of copy (`12px`, `#666666`, line-height `1.4`).
3. **Action Button:** Circular arrow button (`32px` diameter, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, centered black `→` icon) aligned bottom-right.

---

## 6. Image & Asset Descriptions

### Hero Portrait
- **Subject:** Young adult male with glasses, curly brown hair, light beard, wearing a black crew-neck t-shirt.
- **Composition:** Chest-up profile, facing slightly left toward the copy against a smooth, solid light gray background.
- **Rotation/Frame:** 0° rotation, rectangular container with `20px` border radius.

### Project 1: Furni — E-commerce
- **Subject:** Open silver laptop showing an e-commerce website for modern furniture. Potted plant beside it on a neutral desk.
- **Orientation:** Front 3/4 angled view.

### Project 2: FinTrack — Fintech
- **Subject:** Black smartphone displaying a dark-mode personal finance app interface with balance figures and line charts.
- **Orientation:** Centered, upright, zero rotation, light gray studio backdrop.

### Project 3: Wander — Travel
- **Subject:** Tablet device laid at a slight tilt showing a travel application with a scenic lake/mountain card layout.
- **Orientation:** Angled ~10° clockwise on a clean tabletop.

### Project 4: FitLife — Health
- **Subject:** Black smartphone displaying a dark-themed health/fitness UI with progress rings and daily step counts.
- **Orientation:** Centered, slight perspective tilt, light gray backdrop.
