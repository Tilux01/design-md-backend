# Design Specification: VERA (Hero Section)

## 1. Overview & Layout Strategy
* **Target:** Full-screen Hero Section.
* **Layout Structure:** 
  * **Header/Navigation Bar:** Fixed or absolute position at the top with minimalist branding and menu items.
  * **Hero Content:** Centered or vertically stacked typography overlayed dynamically with a prominent visual element.
* **Composition:** 
  * Background image of lush green aloe vera leaves.
  * Large, bold serif typography (`NOURISH` or similar) layered *behind* or seamlessly interacting with the central aloe vera plant imagery.
  * Minimalist brand name (`VERA`) positioned centrally above the main heading.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Aesthetics
* **Primary Background / Accent:** Light, airy sky blue (`#E3F0F7` or `#D4E9F2`) representing a clean, natural, and fresh skin-care/wellness aesthetic.
* **Typography Color:** Deep charcoal / off-black (`#1A1A1A` or `#222222`) to provide stark contrast against the light blue and vibrant green.
* **Natural Elements:** Vibrant botanical greens (`#2E7D32`, `#388E3C`) found in the central aloe vera stalks.

---

## 3. Typography & Fallbacks

The design relies heavily on high-fashion, editorial-style, high-contrast serif typography.

### A. Navigation & Small Text (Sans-Serif)
* **Elements:** "Home", "Shop", "About Us", "Blog", Profile/Cart icons.
* **Font Family:** `Montserrat`, `Helvetica Neue`, or `Inter`.
* **CSS Font Stack:** 
  ```css
  font-family: 'Montserrat', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif;
  ```
* **Weight/Size:** Light to Regular (300–400), `12px` to `14px`, uppercase letter spacing (`letter-spacing: 0.05em`).

### B. Brand Title & Hero Heading (Editorial Serif)
* **Elements:** "VERA" (top center) and the main background title spanning the screen width (e.g., "NOURISH").
* **Font Family Target:** *Didot*, *Bodoni MT*, *Playfair Display*, or *Cinzel*.
* **CSS Font Stack (with Fallbacks):**
  ```css
  font-family: 'Didot', 'Bodoni MT', 'Playfair Display', 'Times New Roman', serif;
  ```
* **Styling Properties:**
  * **Weight:** Regular to Bold (400–700).
  * **Style:** High contrast between thick and thin strokes (Didone style).
  * **Text Transform:** Uppercase.
  * **Letter Spacing:** Wide tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.1em`).
  * **Blend Mode / Layering:** Depending on exact implementation, the text sits in a lower z-index layer or utilizes `mix-blend-mode` to give the illusion of weaving behind the foreground aloe leaves.

---

## 4. Visual Elements & Assets
* **Background / Central Imagery:** High-resolution studio photograph of stacked/growing Aloe Vera stalks shot against a clean light-blue studio backdrop.
* **Layering Technique (Parallax / Depth):**
  * Layer 1 (Back): Background color (`#EBEFF2`).
  * Layer 2: Main serif typography (`NOURISH`).
  * Layer 3 (Foreground): Cropped/transparent cutout of the central aloe vera leaves overlapping the typography to create physical depth.

---

## 5. Animations & Interactive States (Implied/Recommended)
To match modern luxury e-commerce interactions:
* **Hover States:** Links in the navigation bar should feature a subtle fade or underline transition (`opacity: 0.7` transitioning to `1`).
* **Page Load Animation:** 
  * **Fade-In:** The background and typography fade in smoothly over 0.8s (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
  * **Subtle Parallax:** As the user moves the mouse, the foreground aloe image shifts slightly faster than the background typography to create a 3D depth effect.
