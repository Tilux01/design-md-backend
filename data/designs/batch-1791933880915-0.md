# Design Specification (`design.md`)

## 1. Project Overview & Aesthetic Direction
* **Genre**: High-end Editorial / Audio Lifestyle Landing Page (Dark Mode).
* **Tone**: Minimalist, avant-garde, cinematic, technical luxury.
* **Canvas Dimensions**: Full viewport (`100vw` × `100vh`), borderless, responsive hero container. All external borders, bezels, and mock frames from the presentation mockup are omitted.
* **Core Composition**: Asymmetrical layout featuring a centered-right studio portrait with editorial micro-copy, geometric line elements, and a high-contrast serif headline on the left.

## 3. Typography & Fallback Stacks

### 3.1. Primary Headline Font (Serif)
* **Target Font**: `Playfair Display`, `Cormorant Garamond`, or `Ogg`

* **Attributes**:
  * Style: Regular / Book (Weight: 400)
  * Size: `clamp(2.75rem, 4.5vw, 4.25rem)` (~52px–64px)
  * Line Height: `1.08`
  * Letter Spacing: `-0.02em`

* **Target Font**: `Baskerville`, `Playfair Display`, or custom geometric modern serif

## 4. Visual Asset Specification: Central Portrait

### 4.1. Subject & Composition
* **Model**: Young woman with fair skin, natural makeup, and a tapered, slicked-back dirty-blonde/light-brown pixie crop.
* **Pose / Angle**: Three-quarter profile, head tilted slightly downward, gaze lowered toward the bottom-right.
* **Apparel**: Jet-black crewneck t-shirt/sweater (`#0A0A0A`) seamlessly merging into the dark background.
* **Accessory**: A single vertical silver/metallic capsule pin (clip/mic) clipped to the left collar area (viewer's lower-right). Size: ~4px wide × 18px tall.
* **Lighting**: Directional soft rim light from the left/front illuminating the facial contour, nose bridge, ear, and cheekbone, with deep shadow roll-off on the right side.

### 4.2. Background & Placement
* **Isolation / Background**: The subject is either cut out with clean alpha transparency or shot on a seamless pure black backdrop (`#080808`–`#0C0C0C`) with feathered soft edges at the chest and silhouette.
* **Positioning**:
  * Anchor: Centered horizontally at `X: 54%`, with the base of the torso anchored at the bottom edge (`Y: 100%`).
  * Scale: Bust crop taking up approximately `78%` of the viewport height.

### 4.3. Digital Art Treatment (Glitch / Slit-Scan Slice)
* **Forehead Horizontal Displacement**:
  * A horizontal rectangular slice/band (~12% of viewport height) across the upper forehead and hair (from eyebrows upward).
  * Treatment: Horizontal motion blur/chromatic smear extending rightward.
  * Overlay: A crisp horizontal white hairline (`1.5px` height, `#FFFFFF`) running across the upper forehead from `X: 48%` to `X: 86%`.
