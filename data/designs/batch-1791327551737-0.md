# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist, High-End Luxury Fashion Branding System.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio, meticulously structured grid layout with stark high-contrast typography, prioritizing structural typography showcases, material macro-photography, and clean architectural product staging.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Monolithic flat dark mode surfaces (`#000000`) layered with ultra-thin hairline dividers (`rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`), structured card containers, and pure tone isolation (Carbon, Porcelain, Stone, Rouge, Chrome).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Clean architectural grids, high-contrast monochrome framing, razor-sharp editorial typography hierarchy, color swatch metadata overlays.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Not applicable in this minimalist editorial theme; replaced with clean hairline-bordered category labels and uppercase tracked descriptors.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Status Indicator -> Use `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-[#F6F3EE] inline-block"></span>`.

# 6. Exhaustive Component-by-Component Architectural Specs

- **Layout & Positioning**: `flex`, `row`, `justify-between`, `align-items: flex-end`, `sticky top-0`, `z-index: 50`.
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Padding `24px 32px`, Gap `24px`.

- **Typography**: 
  - Family: `'Syne', sans-serif`
  - Title "LOOM": Font Size `5.5rem` (88px), Weight `700`, Tracking `-0.03em`.
  - Sub-letters "a g R": Font Size `5.5rem` (88px), Weight `400` / `600`, Tracking `0.05em`.

- **Layout & Positioning**: `grid`, `grid-cols-5`, `w-full`.
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Padding `16px 32px`, Gap `1px` (using borders).
- **Borders, Radii & Shadows**: Border-top & bottom `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`, vertical dividers `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`.
- **Typography**: 
  - Weight Titles: `0.875rem` (14px), Weight `600`, Color `#FFFFFF`.
  - Alphabet strings (`abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz`): `0.75rem` (12px), Weight `400`, Color `#9A948C`.

## 3. Campaign Imagery Grid (Hero / Material / Object)
- **Layout & Positioning**: `grid`, `grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-3`, `w-full`.
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Gap `16px`, Padding `32px`.
- **Borders, Radii & Shadows**: Border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`, Surface `#0A0A0A`.
- **Typography**: Category Tags ("SPACE / HERO", "MATERIAL", "OBJECT / DETAIL"): `0.75rem` (12px), Weight `500`, Tracking `0.1em`, Text-Transform `uppercase`, Color `#F6F3EE`.

- **Box Model & Spacing**: Gap `0px` (flush cards), Padding `0 32px 32px 32px`.
