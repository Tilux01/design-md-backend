# Design Specification Document (`design.md`)
**Project:** GoPro Hero 8 Black Landing Page Concept  
**Target Screen:** Full Screen (`100vw` × `100vh`), Responsive Desktop First (Aspect Ratio 16:9 base focus)

---

## 1. Color Palette & Lighting

### Color Tokens
*   **Background Canvas:** `#0D0E10` (Ultra-dark matte charcoal/black)
*   **Radial Glow Highlight:** `#2A2D34` to `#0D0E10` (Subtle center ambient glow behind camera)
*   **Primary Text / White Accents:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
*   **Primary Neon Glow:** `#FFFFFF` with `drop-shadow(0px 0px 35px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75))`
*   **Muted Text / Secondary Labels:** `#7A7E85` (Medium cool gray)
*   **Disabled / Background UI Text:** `#22252A` (Dark gray for large faint background text)
*   **Border Strokes / Cards:** `#1A1C20` (Subtle dark card containers)

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Headings (Display Font)
*   **Font Family:** `Orbitron`, `Eurostile Extended`, `Nexa Bold`, or `Syncopate`
*   **Fallback Stack:** `'Bank Gothic', 'Arial Black', sans-serif`
*   **Character Style:** Wide, geometric, futuristic, uppercase.

#### Typography Hierarchy:
1.  **Main Title ("HERO", "BLACK"):**
    *   Font Size: `4rem` – `5.5rem` (64px - 88px)
    *   Weight: `800` (Extra Bold)
    *   Letter Spacing: `0.15em` (Wide)
    *   Color: `#FFFFFF`
2.  **Giant Background Numeral ("8"):**
    *   Font Size: `22rem` – `26rem` (350px - 420px)
    *   Weight: `900` (Ultra Black)
    *   Color: `#FFFFFF`
    *   Visual Effect: Intense outer drop-shadow glow + light masking.
3.  **Section Sub-headers ("MOD IT OUT", "BUY NOW"):**
    *   Font Size: `0.85rem` – `1rem` (136px - 16px)
    *   Weight: `700` (Bold)
    *   Letter Spacing: `0.25em` (Extremely tracked out)
    *   Color: `#FFFFFF`
4.  **Body Copy ("With unshakable HyperSmooth...", "Add lights, mics..."):**
    *   Font Family: `Inter`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif
    *   Font Size: `0.85rem` (13.5px)
    *   Weight: `400` (Regular)
    *   Line Height: `1.6`
    *   Color: `#7A7E85`
5.  **Navigation Links ("OVERVIEW", "TECH SPECS", "REVIEWS"):**
    *   Font Size: `0.75rem` (12px)
    *   Weight: `600` (Semi-Bold)
    *   Letter Spacing: `0.2em`
    *   Color: `#FFFFFF` (Hover: `#7A7E85`)
6.  **Feature Icon Labels ("Touch Screen", "Boost + HyperSmooth", etc.):**
    *   Font Size: `0.7rem` (11px)
    *   Weight: `500` (Medium)
    *   Color: `#7A7E85`

## 4. Visual Elements & Image Asset Specifications

### Asset 1: Main Center Product Asset (GoPro Hero 8 + Splashes)
*   **Type:** High-resolution PNG with alpha transparency (`backgroundless`).
*   **Subject:** 
    *   GoPro Hero 8 Black camera placed centrally.
    *   **Orientation/Rotation:** Rotated approximately `-22°` on the Z-axis (counter-clockwise tilt), pitched forward toward the viewer.
    *   **Action FX (Integrated in image):**
        *   **Water Splash:** A dynamic, high-speed fluid water burst wrapping from the bottom right edge of the camera, throwing droplets upwards.
        *   **Dirt/Mud Particles:** Dark brown dirt and mud splatter bursting across the front left side of the lens and body, extending onto the giant background numeral "8".
*   **Placement:** Absolute center (`top: 50%`, `left: 50%`, `transform: translate(-50%, -45%)`).
*   **Z-Index:** Layered *above* the giant numeral "8" and flanking text, but behind interactive overlays.

### Asset 2: Mod Light Video Preview Thumbnail
*   **Type:** PNG / JPG Image inside a widget frame.
*   **Subject:** GoPro Light Mod accessory glowing brightly with white LED matrix light in a pitch-black setting.
*   **Placement:** Bottom-right corner container (`width: 220px`, `height: 125px`).

## 5. Detailed Component Breakdown

### A. Top Navigation Bar
*   **Position:** Absolute top (`top: 0`, `left: 0`, `right: 0`), padding `40px 60px`.
*   **Left Element:** White GoPro official logo (SVG Vector, height ~`20px`).
*   **Right Element:** Flex row (`gap: 32px`) containing uppercase links:
    *   `OVERVIEW`
    *   `TECH SPECS`
    *   `REVIEWS`

---

### B. Center Hero Section
*   **Background Layer:** Giant text element `"8"`
    *   Position: Centered vertically and horizontally.
    *   Style: Solid white with a thick outer halo/glow.
*   **Foreground Text Layer:**
    *   `"HERO"` placed to the immediate left of the numeral "8" (`margin-right: 20px`).
    *   `"BLACK"` placed to the immediate right of the numeral "8" (`margin-left: 20px`).
*   **Product Layer:** GoPro Camera image asset floating in front, breaking the text boundaries to create depth.

---

*   **CTA Link:**
    *   Text: `"BUY NOW"`

### D. Right Sidebar Section (Mod Feature & Media Widget)
*   **Position Top Right:** `top: 42%`, `right: 60px`, `width: 240px`, text align right.

## 6. Depth & Layering (Z-Index Hierarchy)

1.  `z-index: 0` – Background (`#0D0E10` + Radial ambient dark gradient)
2.  `z-index: 1` – Faint UI background text (`PREV`, `NEXT`)
3.  `z-index: 2` – Giant glowing numeral `"8"`
4.  `z-index: 3` – Main titles `"HERO"` and `"BLACK"`
5.  `z-index: 4` – GoPro Hero 8 Main Image (Camera + Water/Dirt Splash Asset)
6.  `z-index: 5` – Foreground Text Copy, Feature Icons, Video Player Widget
7.  `z-index: 6` – Top Navigation Header & Social Footer Icons
