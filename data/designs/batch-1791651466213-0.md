# Design Specification: "PLEY" Futuristic Landing Page

This document provides a comprehensive design specification to create an exact, full-screen clone of the provided UI design. 

---

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

*   **View:** Full-screen responsive viewport (100vw, 100vh).
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Background:** Deep charcoal black (`#0B0B0C`) with subtle dark gradients and vignette effects.
    *   **Accent Color:** Electric Blue (`#0044FF` or `rgb(0, 68, 255)`).
    *   **Text Color - Primary:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`).
    *   **Text Color - Secondary:** Muted Gray (`#8E8E93`).
    *   **UI Borders/Lines:** Thin, translucent white/gray strokes (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
*   **Grid System:** Asymmetric layout featuring a left-aligned text hero area and a right-aligned focal visual subject.

---

## 2. Typography

*   **Primary Heading / Logo Font:** Sans-serif, ultra-modern, geometric display font (e.g., *Orbitron*, *Rajdhani*, or *Syncopate*). Fallback: `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.
*   **Body & Navigation Font:** Clean, legible modernist sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Roboto*, or *Helvetica*). Fallback: `sans-serif`.

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation Bar (Header)
*   **Position:** Fixed at the top, spanning full width with padding (`~30px` top/sides).
*   **Left Element:** 
    *   **Logo:** Text logo reading **"PLEY"** in bold, wide-tracked futuristic geometric lettering. Color: White.
*   **Center Elements (Links):**
    *   Items: `HOME`, `ABOUT`, `WORKS`, `SERVICES`, `CONTACTS`.
    *   Style: Uppercase, small font size (`~12px`), tracked out, semi-transparent gray (`#8E8E93`), shifting to pure white on hover.
*   **Right Elements:**
    *   **Language Switcher / Region:** Text reading **"ENG"** with a small dropdown chevron.
    *   **Menu/Hamburger Icon:** Three horizontal stacked lines representing an off-canvas trigger.

---

### B. Hero Section (Left Column)
*   **Vertical Subheading Box:**
    *   A small vertical rectangle highlighted with an **Electric Blue** left border block.
    *   Text inside: **"HERE AND NOW"** (stacked layout, uppercase, tracking applied).
*   **Main Headline:**
    *   Text: **"FUTURE"**
    *   Font Style: Extra-large, wide geometric display font, ultra-bold, white.
*   **Paragraph Description:**
    *   Text: *"People write more about the future, sometimes as a travel then today, and we wrote in this direction, it was a vision of action."*
    *   Font Style: Regular weight, small size (`~13px`), muted gray color (`#A0A0A5`), constrained to a narrow text width (`~320px`).
*   **Call-to-Action (CTA) Button:**
    *   Text: **"LETS GO"**
    *   Style: Outlined button with a thin white/gray border, sharp corners (no border-radius), uppercase text, transparent background with subtle hover states.

---

### C. Focal Visual Element (Right Column)
*   **Main Image Subject:**
    *   **Description:** A hyper-detailed, dark metallic futuristic android/cyborg head and upper torso profile shot. The figure has an intricate, segmented exoskeleton structure made of matte black and gunmetal carbon-fiber materials. 
    *   **Key Feature:** Wears striking cybernetic goggles featuring two large circular lenses that glow with a vibrant, neon cyan/blue gradient reflection.
    *   **Background Status:** Backgroundless (transparent or perfectly blended into the dark `#0B0B0C` ambient background).
    *   **Placement & Rotation:** Positioned on the mid-to-right side of the viewport, angled slightly in a three-quarter profile facing toward the left/center of the screen.
*   **Annotation Callouts (HUD elements):**
    *   Thin, hairline leader lines extending from parts of the cyborg head to text labels on the far right.
    *   Labels: 
        *   `ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE`
        *   `NO EMOTION`
    *   Style: Monospace or ultra-small technical sans-serif font, uppercase, muted gray.

---

### D. Footer / Bottom UI Elements
*   **Right-Side Indicators:**
    *   A vertical stack of small pagination/section dots on the far-right edge (one active dot highlighted).
*   **Feature Cards (Bottom Right):**
    *   Two compact, side-by-side dark frosted-glass cards positioned underneath the main visual.
    *   **Card 1:** Title **"TECHNOLOGY"** with a brief sub-description underneath.
    *   **Card 2:** Title **"INNOVATION"** with a brief sub-description underneath.
    *   Style: Dark translucent background (`rgba(255,255,255,0.03)`), thin border strokes, subtle padding.
