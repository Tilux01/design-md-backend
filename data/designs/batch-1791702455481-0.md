## 6. Responsive Rules & Scaling Strategy

1. **Fluid Typography:** Use `clamp()` for the giant background text so it scales dynamically across standard monitors and widescreen displays:
   `font-size: clamp(100px, 22vw, 360px);`
2. **Subject Image Scaling:** Ensure `max-height: 85vh` and `width: auto` to prevent aspect ratio distortion on narrower viewport widths.
3. **Mobile Fallback Structure:** On screens `< 768px`:
   * Hide center header navigation links (`Home`, `About`, `Contact`).
   * Reduce main display font size.
   * Shift subtext blocks to stack vertically under the hero section.
