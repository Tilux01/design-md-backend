# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist Health & Beauty Tech (Clean, scientific skincare meets high-end digital lifestyle).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with structured container grids, balanced asymmetry (heavy typographic focal point left, centered high-res portrait hero, contextual data stats right).
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Ultra-clean minimalist light surface with subtle translucent glassmorphic pills (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), crisp 1px neutral borders (`#E2E8F0`), and clean flat containment.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Bounding-box facial detection UI overlays, high-tracking uppercase micro-pills, user avatar stacking pill, high-contrast crisp typography.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#F1F5F9`, border width `1px` (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> `&mdash;`
  - App Store / Apple Icon -> SVG vector component.
  - Status Indicator -> `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
