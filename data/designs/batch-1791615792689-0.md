# Design System Specification (`design.md`)
**Project:** Nike Air Max 270 Landing Page (E-Commerce Visual Experience)  
**Viewport:** Fullscreen Responsive Desktop (Target base: 1440px × 900px, 100vh)

---

## 1. Color Palette

### Base Colors
* **Page Background (`--bg-main`):** `#FFFFFF` (Solid White)
* **Primary Text (`--text-main`):** `#111111` (Deep Black / Charcoal)
* **Secondary Text (`--text-muted`):** `#A0A0A5` (Neutral Medium Light Grey)
* **Header Links (`--nav-text`):** `#222222` (Dark Grey)

### Visual Accent Colors
* **Dark Hero Card Background (`--card-dark-bg`):** `#23272A` to `#181A1B` (Dark Slate/Charcoal Gradient or Solid)
* **Bubble Accent Pink (`--pink-accent`):** `#FFA2AC` (Glossy translucent bubblegum pink)
* **Thumbnail Box Background (`--thumb-bg`):** `#FFB2B9` (Soft pastel coral/pink)

---

## 2. Typography & Fallbacks

### Primary Font Family
* **Font:** `SF Pro Display` or `Helvetica Neue` or `Inter`
* **Fallback Chain:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Display", "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`

### Hierarchy Specifications

1. **Brand Hero Title:**
   * **Text:** `"Men's Air"` / `"Max 270"` / `"Shoes"`
   * **Line 1 & 2:** `3rem` (48px), Weight: `700` (Bold), Color: `#111111`, Line-Height: `1.1`
   * **Line 3 ("Shoes"):** `3rem` (48px), Weight: `400` (Regular), Color: `#A0A0A5`, Line-Height: `1.1`

2. **Navigation Items:**
   * **Font Size:** `0.85rem` (13.6px)
   * **Font Weight:** `500` (Medium)
   * **Letter Spacing:** `0.02em`
   * **Color:** `#222222`

3. **Sub-labels / Card Typography:**
   * **Card Watermark ("AIR270"):** `0.75rem` (12px), Weight: `600`, Mono-spaced / All Caps, Color: `#666666`

---

## 3. Header & Navigation Specs

* **Layout:** Flexbox, `align-items: center`, `justify-content: space-between`, horizontal padding `60px`.
* **Elements:**

  * **Center:** Navigation menu inline list with `gap: 28px`:
    * `Men`
    * `Women`
    * `Boys`
    * `Girls`
    * `Customize`
    * `Nike +`
  * **Right:** Action links with `gap: 24px`:
    * `Cart (0)`
    * `Login` (Underlined with a small black active indicator bar beneath it)
    * **Search Icon:** Magnifying glass SVG (`width: 16px`, stroke `#111111`).

---

## 4. Grid & Main Layout Structure

* **Main Container:** Full height (`calc(100vh - 80px)`), relative positioning, overflow hidden.
* **Layout Split:**
  * **Left Column (Text & Thumbnail):** Occupies ~30% width, inset `padding-left: 100px`, `padding-top: 60px`.
  * **Right/Center Composition:** Occupies ~70% width. A multi-layered visual stack containing text, 3D assets, product images, and typography graphics.

---

## 5. Detailed Component Breakdown & Asset Placement

### A. Left Content Column
1. **Title Block:**
   * **Position:** Top-left of hero container.
   * **Content:**
     ```
     Men's Air
     Max 270
     Shoes
     ```

2. **Bottom-Left Thumbnail Card:**
   * **Position:** Fixed to bottom-left area (`bottom: 80px`, `left: 100px`).
   * **Dimensions:** `width: 180px`, `height: 180px`, `border-radius: 4px`.
   * **Content:** Square aspect-ratio inset image.
   * **Image Asset Description:** Soft pink/coral background (`#FFB2B9`). Shows two dark-skinned hands holding/shaping a spherical white opaque object (symbolizing air/bubble volume).
   * **Background:** Solid matte, no transparency inside the square frame.

---

### B. Center-Right Hero Composition (Layer Stack)

The visual focal point uses absolute layering (`z-index`) to create depth.

#### Layer 1 (z-index: 10) — 3D "air" Inflatable Text
* **Asset Description:** A large, 3D-rendered inflatable balloon spelling `"air"` in lowercase stylized bubble letters. Highly reflective surface with soft pink base tone and sharp white specular highlights. Semi-translucent appearance.
* **Type:** Backgroundless PNG asset.
* **Placement:**
  * Spans across the center-left behind the shoe.
  * **Scale:** ~650px wide.
  * **Position:** Centered vertically, overlapping from the left edge of the hero composition toward the center.

#### Layer 2 (z-index: 20) — Dark Graphic Card
* **Asset/Container:** Dark rectangular card element.
* **Dimensions:** `width: 300px`, `height: 380px`, `border-radius: 8px`.
* **Background Color:** `#1C1E20` (Dark Charcoal).
* **Position:** `top: 15%`, `right: 28%`.
* **Internal Graphic Content:**
  * Top bar: Three small light gray dot indicators (`...`) on left; `"AIR270"` text in light gray on right.
  * Subtle `270` watermark branding printed on the card canvas.

#### Layer 3 (z-index: 30) — Primary Product Image (Nike Air Max 270)
* **Asset Description:** High-resolution studio photograph of a Nike Air Max 270 sneaker.
  * **Colorway:** Triple Black engineered mesh upper with a transparent glossy/pink-tinted Air Max heel unit.
  * **Format:** Backgroundless PNG with clean cutout edges.
* **Rotation/Angle:** Rotated **-32 degrees** counter-clockwise (pointing diagonally down toward the bottom-left).
* **Placement & Overlay:**
  * Overlays both the **Dark Graphic Card** (Layer 2) and the **3D "air" Balloon** (Layer 1).
  * **Scale:** Large display, approximately `720px` in width across the diagonal.
  * **Center Alignment:** Shoe heel sits over the dark card's right side; toe box extends out over the white background toward the left.

#### Layer 4 (z-index: 35) — Floating Balloon Dog (Top Right Detail)
* **Asset Description:** Small 3D-rendered glossy pink balloon dog miniature.
* **Type:** Backgroundless PNG asset.
* **Position:** `top: 12%`, `right: 15%`.
* **Scale:** Approximately `90px` wide.
* **Rotation:** Tilted slightly upward to the right (~15 degrees).

#### Layer 5 (z-index: 25) — Stacked Bubble Typography Logos (Bottom Right)
* **Asset Description:** Three vertically stacked variations of the `"AIR 270"` logo in chrome/glossy 3D outline bubble lettering.
* **Type:** Vector/PNG Graphic (Backgroundless).
* **Placement:** Positioned directly below the bottom edge of the Dark Graphic Card (Layer 2), aligned toward the bottom-right quadrant (`bottom: 12%`, `right: 32%`).
* **Scale:** Width ~160px.
* **Stack Layout:**
  1. Top: Chrome-outlined bubble text.
  2. Middle: Filled metallic bubble text.
  3. Bottom: Soft outlined variant.

## 7. Interactive & Dynamic States

* **Nav Items:** Hover state changes text color from `#222222` to `#FF7575` with a smooth `0.2s ease` transition.
* **Login Indicator:** A `2px` thick black bar centered directly under the "Login" text.
* **Shoe Focal Effect:** Subtle floating animation (`translateY(-8px)` over `3s` infinite ease-in-out curve) can be applied to Layer 3 (Shoe) and Layer 4 (Balloon Dog) to bring life to the layout.
