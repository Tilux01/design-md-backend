# Design Specification: Matte Black Coke Landing Page

## 1. Color Palette

*   **Primary Background**: `#0D0D0E` with a subtle radial gradient (`#1C1C1E` center-right highlight behind the can, fading to `#0B0B0C`).
*   **Surface / Panels**: Translucent dark grey (`#1F1F22`, ~85% opacity / `rgba(31, 31, 34, 0.85)`).
*   **Accent Graphic**: Pure White `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Typography**:
    *   Headings & Primary Text: `#FFFFFF`
    *   Secondary/Body Text: `#8E8E93` (muted grey)
    *   Watermark/Credits: `#636366`

---

## 2. Typography

*   **Brand Logo Font**: Custom Coca-Cola script font.
    *   *Fallback*: `Loki Cola`, `Brush Script MT`, cursive.
*   **Primary Headings**: Modern geometric sans-serif (Bold / Heavy), ~56px.
    *   *Primary*: `Poppins`, `Montserrat`, or `Inter` (700/800 weight).
    *   *Fallback*: `Helvetica Neue`, Arial, sans-serif.
*   **Body & Captions**: Clean neutral sans-serif (Regular / Medium), 12px–16px.
    *   *Primary*: `Inter`, `Roboto`, or `Segoe UI` (400/500 weight).
    *   *Fallback*: Arial, sans-serif.

---

## 3. Layout & Structure

*   **Layout**: Full-screen viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), non-scrollable, relative positioning.
*   **Grid/Split**:
    *   Left section (~45%): Brand texts, primary headline, rating card, designer attribution.
    *   Right section (~55%): Hero product visual, geometric ring, carousel arrow, bottom info overlay.

---

## 4. Components & Placement

### 4.1. Navigation Bar
*   **Position**: Fixed/absolute top, `padding: 32px 48px`, full width.
*   **Left**: Coca-Cola script wordmark in muted silver-grey (`#A1A1A6`), height ~28px.
*   **Right**: Hamburger menu icon (three thin horizontal lines), `#FFFFFF`, size ~24px.

### 4.2. Hero Headline
*   **Position**: Left column, vertically centered (`top: 38%`, `left: 8%`).
*   **Text Hierarchy**:
    *   `Matte`: Bold, white, 64px, line-height 1.05.
    *   `Black Coke`: Bold, white, 64px, line-height 1.05.
    *   `Hello Mostazir!`: Medium, white, 20px, margin-top 16px.

### 4.3. Product Display (Center/Right)
*   **Geometric Ring**:
    *   White stroke (~14px thick), hollow circle, diameter ~320px.
    *   Positioned behind the upper section of the can (`top: 18%`, `left: 58%`).
*   **Hero Can Asset**:
    *   Layered over the white ring with realistic drop shadows.
    *   Position: Centered horizontally at ~58% width, `height: ~65vh`.

### 4.4. Left Info Card ("Black Version")
*   **Position**: `left: 8%`, `top: 54%`, dimensions ~220px × 150px.
*   **Styling**: Flat dark grey container (`rgba(36, 36, 38, 0.9)`), sharp corners (0–4px radius).
*   **Content**:
    *   Title: "Black Version" (14px, Bold, `#FFFFFF`).
    *   Description: 2 lines of micro-copy/lorem ipsum (10px, `#8E8E93`).
    *   Rating: 3 small white 5-pointed stars arranged horizontally (margin-top 12px).

### 4.5. Right Info Card
*   **Position**: Bottom-right corner (`bottom: 0`, `right: 0`), pinned to the viewport boundary.
*   **Styling**: Semi-transparent dark strip (`rgba(22, 22, 24, 0.95)`), padding `24px 36px`.
*   **Content**:
    *   Left side: "Black Version" (14px, Bold, `#FFFFFF`) over 2 lines of micro-copy (10px, `#8E8E93`).
    *   Right side: Share icon (`<` style fork icon with nodes), white, 18px.

### 4.6. Carousel Navigation Arrow
*   **Position**: Right edge, vertically centered (`right: 6%`, `top: 50%`).
*   **Graphic**: Slender right arrow (`→`), `#8E8E93`, hover state `#FFFFFF`.

### 4.7. Footer / Attribution
*   **Position**: Bottom-left corner (`bottom: 32px`, `left: 8%`).
*   **Content**:
    *   "Designed By" label (10px, `#8E8E93`).
    *   Dark pill badge (`#2C2C2E`, rounded full) containing "FOX Graphix" text (11px, white bold).

---

## 5. Asset Specifications

*   **Can Image**:
    *   *Type*: Transparent PNG (backgroundless).
    *   *Description*: 330ml aluminum can in ultra-matte jet black finish. Surface covered in realistic condensation water droplets. Glossy black Coca-Cola vertical script embossed across the body. Lighting hits the right rim, creating a clean white edge highlight. No tilt (0° rotation, standing upright).
*   **Icons**:
    *   Hamburger menu (SVG, 3 strokes, white).
    *   Right pagination arrow (SVG, thin stroke, grey).
    *   Share node icon (SVG, 3 connected circles, white).
    *   Three solid 5-point stars (SVG, white, ~10px each).
