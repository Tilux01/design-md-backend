# Comprehensive Design Specification: "Martha Lives" Editorial Treatment

---

## 1. Global Page Setup & Layout Structure

- **Viewport Size:** Full-screen responsive (Desktop-first scaling, designed at a high-resolution canvas ratio of approx. 1080px × 1920px vertical scroll format).
- **Layout Model:** Single-column vertical scroll composed of **three distinct immersive horizontal sections**.
- **Global Background Color:** Deep Obsidian Black (`#121212` or pure `#000000`).
- **Global Text Color:** Off-White/Parchment (`#EAEAEA` to `#FFFFFF`).
- **Global Constraints:** Zero margins or padding on the viewport edges; content bleeds edge-to-edge. No outer frames or simulated browser chrome.

---

## 2. Typography Strategy & Font Mapping

### Primary Display / Title Font (Gothic Blackletter / Medieval Style)
*Used for: Main titles ("Martha Lives"), prominent headers, and high-impact thematic words.*
- **Target Font:** *Old English Text MT*, *Friz Quadrata*, or *UnifrakturMaguntia* (Google Fonts equivalent).
- **Fallback Stack:** `UnifrakturMaguntia, "Old English Text MT", "Cinzel Decorative", serif, fantasy`.

### Secondary Body & Accent Font (Clean Serif / Modern Editorial)
*Used for: Narrative paragraphs, metadata, awards, captions, and side-rotated text.*
- **Target Font:** *Cormorant Garamond* or *Playfair Display* (Italicized for editorial grace).
- **Fallback Stack:** `"Cormorant Garamond", Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif`.

### Tertiary Sans-Serif Font (Minimalist UI / Labels)
*Used for: Director credits, agency metadata, website links, and small functional tags.*
- **Target Font:** *Inter* or *Montserrat* (All uppercase, wide letter-spacing).
- **Fallback Stack:** `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.

---

## 3. Section-by-Section Detailed Breakdown

---

### SECTION 1: Hero Header (The Forest Scene)

#### Layout & Dimensions
- **Height:** Approx. 35% of total viewport height.
- **Background Layer:** Full-bleed image.

#### Image 1 (Hero Background)
- **Visual Description:** A haunting, high-contrast monochrome (desaturated blue-gray tone) photograph of a sparse, misty forest. Tall, bare, skeletal trees rise vertically. The ground is covered in dense, dark undergrowth and swirling textured debris or dark water ripples. In the absolute vertical center stands a lone female figure wearing a vintage, long-sleeved nightgown/dress, staring blankly forward with long dark hair obscuring part of her face. Her arms are slightly raised, holding a long thin branch or wand.
- **Processing:** Heavy vignette, graded with cool slate shadows and stark highlights.

#### Overlay Typography
1. **Top-Left Micro-Metadata:**
   - **Text:** `DIRECTOR’S INTERPRETATION BY` / `DIRECTOR’S NAME`
   - **Font:** Tertiary Sans-Serif, 9px, uppercase, tracked out (`letter-spacing: 2px`). Color: `#B0B0B0`.
2. **Top-Right Micro-Metadata:**
   - **Text:** `AGENCY NAME` / `PRODUCTION NAME`
   - **Font:** Tertiary Sans-Serif, 9px, uppercase, tracked out. Positioned on the upper right. Color: `#B0B0B0`.
3. **Central Hero Title ("Martha Lives"):**
   - **Text:** `Martha lives`
   - **Font:** Primary Display Font (Gothic Blackletter), large scale (approx. 72px–96px).
   - **Style:** Sentence case with stylized historic glyphs (the 'M' and 'l' have dramatic flourishes).
   - **Color:** Crisp off-white (`#F5F5F5`) with a soft drop shadow/glow to separate it from the complex forest background.
   - **Position:** Horizontally centered, placed dynamically across the lower third of the hero image boundary.

### SECTION 3: Visual Collage & Moodboard (The Climax)

#### Layout & Dimensions
- **Height:** Approx. 35% of total viewport height.
- **Background:** Solid deep black blending into a multi-layered photographic collage overlay.

#### Collage Elements & Exact Composition
1. **Left Element (The Rose):**
   - **Visual Description:** A hyper-detailed, dramatic close-up of a pale cream/white garden rose with soft shadow gradients. The petals are soft and velvety, fading into deep atmospheric shadows on the left edge.
   - **Placement:** Anchored to the bottom-left corner, overlapping the central portrait.
2. **Center Element (The Pierced Face Portrait):**
   - **Visual Description:** A large, ultra-close-up monochrome crop of a human face focusing intensely on the eye and bridge of the nose. The texture is gritty and film-grained.
   - **Overlay Graphic:** A minimalist four-point star (✦) centered below the eye with the micro-text: `TRANSPORTING THE AUDIENCE TO ANOTHER UNIVERSE` set in tiny uppercase sans-serif letters.
   - **Masking:** The portrait is sharply angled/cropped diagonally, slicing through the visual space.
3. **Right Element (The Woodland Figure / Ghillie Suit):**
   - **Visual Description:** A full-body or three-quarter shot of a figure completely camouflaged in a wild, organic ghillie suit made of dense forest foliage, pine branches, and ferns. The figure is crouched or standing in a misty landscape, holding textured natural elements.
   - **Placement:** Overlaid on the right side, cutting across the diagonal geometry of the collage.
4. **Vertical Marginalia (Left Edge):**
   - **Text:** `TREATMENT TITLE HERE`
   - **Font:** Tertiary Sans-Serif, 8px, rotated 90 degrees vertically upwards, running along the extreme left border of the section. Color: `#666666`.

## 4. Interactive & Responsive Behavior Notes

- **Scroll Performance:** All images must use modern compressed formats (WebP/AVIF) to ensure instant rendering without layout shift.
- **Typography Scaling:** Use `clamp()` for the primary display headers to ensure "Martha Lives" scales gracefully down on tablet and mobile viewports without breaking the gothic letterforms.
- **Accessibility:** Ensure all text overlays meet minimum contrast ratios against their respective photographic backgrounds via subtle dark gradient overlays (`rgba(0,0,0,0.4)` to `rgba(0,0,0,0.8)`).
