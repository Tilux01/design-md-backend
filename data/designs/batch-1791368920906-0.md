# Design Specification: Shopio E-Commerce Website

## 1. Global Styles & Variables

*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Background (Body):** `#F7F7F8` (Off-white / Light Gray)
    *   **Background (Cards/Banners):** Warm beige/taupe gradient tones (`#EAE3DB` to `#D6CBC1`), white (`#FFFFFF`) for navigation and content containers.
    *   **Primary Text:** `#1A1A1A` (Near Black / Dark Charcoal)
    *   **Secondary Text / Muted:** `#6B6B6B` (Medium Gray)
    *   **Accent (Buttons):** `#1A1A1A` (Dark Charcoal/Black) with white text `#FFFFFF`.
    *   **Highlights/Borders:** `#E5E5E5` (Light neutral border)

*   **Typography:**
    *   **Font Family:** Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
    *   **Logo:** Bold, modern sans-serif (with a small green/yellow accent dot on the 'o').

---

## 2. Top Utility Bar

*   **Layout:** Full-width flex container, border-bottom `#EAEAEA`.
*   **Background:** `#FFFFFF`
*   **Padding:** `8px 40px`
*   **Font Size:** `12px`
*   **Color:** `#4A4A4A`
*   **Content:**
    *   **Left:** "Free shipping on all orders over $50" | "Easy returns"
    *   **Right:** "Help & Support" | "Track Order" | "USD" (with dropdown chevron) | "EN" (with dropdown chevron)

---

## 3. Main Navigation Header

*   **Layout:** Full-width flex container, sticky positioning.
*   **Background:** `#FFFFFF`
*   **Padding:** `16px 40px`
*   **Content Sections:**
    1.  **Logo:** "Shopio." (`24px`, Bold, black text with a green dot on the end).
    2.  **Navigation Links:** Center aligned.
        *   Home (Active state: Underlined, `#1A1A1A`)
        *   Shop
        *   Categories (with dropdown chevron)
        *   Deals
        *   New Arrivals
        *   Pages (with dropdown chevron)
    3.  **Action Icons:** Right aligned (Search icon, User account icon, Wishlist heart icon with counter badge, Cart bag icon with counter badge). Icon size: `20px`, spacing: `20px`.

---

## 4. Hero Section

*   **Layout:** Full-width banner container with rounded corners (`16px`), soft shadow, and soft gradient warm beige background.
*   **Padding:** `60px 80px`
*   **Grid Structure:** 2-Column Split (Left: Text & CTAs, Right: Imagery).
*   **Left Content:**
    *   **Badge:** "NEW COLLECTION 2024" (`11px`, Uppercase, tracking wide, background: `#FFFFFF`, padding: `6px 12px`, rounded).
    *   **Headline:** "Elevate Your Everyday Style" (`48px` to `56px`, Serif/Sans-serif bold, line-height tight).
    *   **Subheadline:** "Discover premium quality products crafted for comfort, style and performance." (`16px`, `#555555`, max-width: `450px`).
    *   **CTA Buttons:**
        *   Primary: "Shop Now" (Black background `#1A1A1A`, white text, rounded `8px`, padding `14px 28px`).
        *   Secondary: "View Collection" (Transparent with dark border or solid light beige background, dark text, rounded `8px`, padding `14px 28px`).
*   **Right Content:**
    *   High-end editorial lifestyle photography of a male and female model wearing neutral-toned smart-casual apparel against a minimalist beige interior.
*   **Feature Bar (Bottom of Hero):**
    *   Horizontal layout divided into 3 distinct trust points:
        1.  🚚 **Free Shipping** — On orders over $50
        2.  🔄 **Easy Returns** — 30 days return policy
        3.  🔒 **Secure Payment** — 100% secure checkout

---

## 5. Category Circles Section

*   **Layout:** Horizontal row of 6 circular category navigation cards centered on the page.
*   **Gap:** `32px`
*   **Card Structure:**
    *   Circular image container (`100px` diameter, soft shadow, subtle white border ring).
    *   Category Title below image (`14px`, Semibold, `#1A1A1A`).
    *   Item count below title (`12px`, `#888888`, e.g., "120+ Items").
*   **Categories:**
    1.  Men
    2.  Women
    3.  Shoes
    4.  Bags
    5.  Watches
    6.  Accessories

---

## 6. Promotional Banners Section (Mid-Page)

*   **Layout:** 2-Column Grid of promotional cards.
*   **Gap:** `24px`
*   **Banner 1 (Left - Summer Sale):**
    *   **Background:** Warm gradient beige with subtle vertical wood-slat pattern.
    *   **Content:**
        *   "SUMMER SALE" (Small uppercase badge)
        *   "Up to 50% Off" (`32px` Bold)
        *   "On selected items" (`14px`)
        *   "Shop Now" (Button)
    *   **Visual:** Elegant leather handbag placed centrally.
*   **Banner 2 (Right - New Arrivals):**
    *   **Background:** Warm beige gradient with tropical leaf shadow overlay.
    *   **Content:**
        *   "NEW ARRIVALS" (Small uppercase badge)
        *   "Discover The Latest Trends" (`32px` Bold)
        *   "Explore Now" (Button)
    *   **Visual:** Designer sunglasses resting on a minimalist surface next to decorative plants.

---

## 7. Trending Products Section

*   **Layout:** Grid layout with section header.
*   **Header:**
    *   Left: "Trending Products" (`22px`, Bold)
    *   Right: "View All →" (Interactive link, `#1A1A1A`, hover underlined)
*   **Product Grid:** Multi-column product card layout featuring item image, discount/status badges (-20%, New), title, price, and quick-add actions.
