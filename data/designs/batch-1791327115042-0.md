# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Neo-Pointillism / Digital Mosaic Fine Art Editorial.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High visual density via uniform rasterized stippling, completely filling the viewport canvas with balanced chromatic gradients, dark silhouetted foregrounds, and vibrant sunset warmth.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Single-plane flat digital matrix painting with layered silhouette depth achieved entirely through high-contrast color values, transitioning from deep indigo shadows to warm amber highlights.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Raster dot-matrix grid alignment, pointillism color blending, ambient twilight horizon, high-contrast silhouette separation.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(230, 129, 35, 0.15)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-amber-400 inline-block"></span>`.
