# Design Specification: Salesforce Invoices Dashboard (Exact Clone)

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport
* **Layout Structure:** Full-screen viewport design featuring a complex multi-pane SaaS dashboard interface. 
* **Background Canvas:** Light grayish-blue background (`#EAEFF4` or similar soft neutral tone) across the main canvas. The primary structural components (containers, panels, widgets) use frosted glassmorphism effects (`rgba(255, 255, 255, ...)` with subtle backdrop blur or opaque white/dark panels).
* **Color Palette:**
  * **Primary Brand:** Salesforce Cyan/Blue (`#0176D3` or similar deep corporate blue used in top nav).
  * **Accent / Highlight:** Bright Lime/Yellow-Green (`#D3FF00` or `#C6F432`) used for progress bars, active states, tags, and "Pay out now" buttons.
  * **Dark Panel:** Deep charcoal/black (`#1E2229`) used for the main data table container ("Unpaid Invoices").
  * **Text Colors:** Primary dark text (`#1A1A1A`), Muted gray labels (`#7A8B9E` or `#8C9BA5`).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
* **Primary Font Family:** Salesforce Sans (fallback stack: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`).
* **Font Hierarchy:**
  * **Page Title ("Invoices"):** Bold, ~32px, clean sans-serif.
  * **Metric Values ($31,211.00):** Bold/Medium, ~24px-28px.
  * **Section Headers & Tab Labels:** Semi-bold, ~14px-16px.
  * **Metadata & Table Text (Invoice numbers, status):** Regular/Medium, ~12px-13px.

---

## 3. Header Section
* **Top Navigation Bar:** Dark rounded pill container (`#111622`) spanning the top center.
  * **Left Side:** Salesforce star logo icon followed by text link "salesforce".
  * **Center Nav Items:** "Estimates", "Invoices" (highlighted with yellow pill background), "Payments", "Recurring Invoices", "Checkouts".
  * **Right Side Icons:** Grid, document, payout, settings, profile avatar.
* **Page Header:**
  * Left-facing back arrow icon inside a circular button.
  * Title: **"Invoices"** in large bold text.
  * Right Action Bar: Filter icon and primary button: **"+ Create an invoice"**.

---

## 4. Metrics & Overview Cards
Four primary white/translucent metric cards laid out horizontally:

1. **Overdue:**
   * Label: `Overdue` (gray, small)
   * Value: `$31,211.00` (large bold)
   * Mini timeline chart showing months (Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec) with a striped yellow progress bar across November and December. Below the timeline are circular avatars of contacts associated with the invoices.
2. **Due within next month:**
   * Label: `Due within next month`
   * Value: `$172,560.00`
3. **Average time to get paid:**
   * Label: `Average time to get paid`
   * Value: `12 days`
4. **Available for Instant Payout:**
   * Label: `Available for Instant Payout`
   * Value: `$214,390.00` with an "Expects" badge.
   * Payment method breakdown pills (`#443`, `#177210` highlighted yellow, `#711221`) corresponding to Visa, Stripe, and PayPal icons.
   * Action Button: **"Pay out now"** (dark rounded button).

---

## 5. Active Filters Bar
* Container with pill-shaped filter controls:
  * `Active filters` badge with count `2`.
  * Dropdown: `All customers` (with chevron).
  * Dropdown: `All statuses` (with chevron).
  * Date range pickers: `November 2023` and `December 2023` (each with calendar icons).
  * Search input: `Enter invoice #` with a search magnifying glass icon on the right.

---

## 6. Main Content Area: Unpaid Invoices (Dark Theme Panel)
A large dark-themed panel (`#1E2229`) split into a **left list view** and a **right detail view**.

### Left Column: Invoice List
* Section header: **"Unpaid Invoices"**
* Sub-tabs: `All Invoices`, `Draft (3)`, `Unpaid (5)` (highlighted with yellow pill).
* **List Items (Rows):**
  * Each row includes:
    * User avatar image.
    * Invoice number (e.g., `# 404-002`) and relative due time (`In 2 days`).
    * Status tag: `Unsent` (gray pill) or `Viewed` (dark outline pill).
    * Amount value (e.g., `$80,770.00`).
  * Selected row (`# 427-012`) is highlighted with a lighter gray/blue background and accented border.

### Right Column: Detailed Invoice View
A frosted/light slate-blue panel displaying the selected invoice breakdown (`# 427-012`):
* **Top Header:**
  * Invoice ID: `# 427-012` with `Unsent` status badge.
  * Company Info: **BlueRock** logo.
  * Customer Info: Maria Jones card with avatar and title (`CEO, BlueRock Pvt Ltd`).
* **Line Items / Development Blocks:**
  * Three horizontal milestone cards with amounts and progress graphs:
    1. `$10,630.80` — Concept Development
    2. `$31,892.40` — CRM Development
    3. `$10,630.80` — CRM Integration
* **Footer Summary Bar:**
  * Sub Total: `$53,154.00`
  * Total: `$53,154.00`
  * Balance Due: `$53,154.00`
  * Action controls: PDF/Print icons and a bright yellow **"Pay out now"** button.

---

## 7. Image Assets Description
* **Avatars:** Circular professional headshots of various clients/team members used across the metrics timeline, list view rows, and the customer profile card (Maria Jones).
* **Logo Icons:** 
  * Salesforce star/sparkle icon in the top header.
  * BlueRock company logo (stylized diamond/geometric shape) in the invoice detail view.
* **Payment Icons:** Small brand logos representing Visa, Stripe, and PayPal within the payout widget.
