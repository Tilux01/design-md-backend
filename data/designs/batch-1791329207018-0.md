# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise SaaS & Financial Analytics Dashboard.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High structural density with neatly organized metric cards, sidebar navigation, and soft internal padding. Balanced whitespace focused on clear visual grouping and rapid data scanning.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, clean surface hierarchy featuring solid light neutral backgrounds, crisp border lines, and subtle elevation tokens for modal overlays and elevated cards.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, minimalist dashboard cards, soft financial status indicators, clean tabular alignments.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `2px 8px`, font-size `11px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(56, 161, 105, 0.1)`, border width `0px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
