# Design Specification: Alexander Krivitskiy Portfolio (Work Section)

## 1. Layout & Structure
- **Viewport:** Full-screen immersive layout (100% width, 100% height).
- **Background Color:** Deep charcoal/black (`#111111` or `#0e0e0e`).
- **Layout Architecture:**
  - **Top Navigation Bar:** Fixed horizontal layout containing the creator's name on the far left, core navigation links in the upper center, and a "Contact" link on the far right.
  - **Hero Section:** Large, bold centered heading reading "Work" positioned vertically between the navigation and the projects section.
  - **Section Divider:** A thin white/light-gray horizontal rule spanning the width of the content area, separating the header from the "Recent Projects" grid.
  - **Recent Projects Section:** Left-aligned subsection title "Recent Projects" followed by a 2-column image and text grid.
  - **Project Grid:** Two distinct black-and-white portrait images placed side-by-side with equal spacing, each accompanied by a 2-line subtitle (Project Name + Category).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
- **Font Family:** Clean, geometric sans-serif (such as *Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*, or *SF Pro Display*).
- **Fallback Stack:** `sans-serif, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial`
- **Text Elements:**
  - **Logo / Brand Name ("Alexander Krivitskiy"):**
    - Font Size: Small (~14px)
    - Font Weight: Regular/Medium
    - Color: White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - **Navigation Links ("Work", "About", "Shop", "Contact"):**
    - Font Size: Small (~14px)
    - Font Weight: Regular
    - Color: White (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle active state indicator (a small horizontal underline beneath "Work").
  - **Hero Title ("Work"):**
    - Font Size: Extra Large (~72px - 96px, bold display size)
    - Font Weight: Bold (`700`)
    - Color: Light Gray / Off-White (`#E5E5E5`)
  - **Section Header ("Recent Projects"):**
    - Font Size: Medium (~18px - 20px)
    - Font Weight: Medium (`500`)
    - Color: White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - **Project Titles & Categories ("Dreams / Portraits", "Aftermath / Portraits"):**
    - Font Size: Small (~13px - 14px)
    - Line-Height: 1.4
    - Font Weight: Regular
    - Color: White (`#FFFFFF`)

---

## 3. Imagery & Visual Assets
- **Image 1 (Left Project - "Dreams"):**
  - **Description:** A high-contrast, moody black-and-white close-up portrait of a person. Their face is partially obscured by dynamic, wind-blown strands of hair falling across the frame. The focus is intimate and dramatic, highlighting shadows and texture in the hair and facial structure.
  - **Placement:** Left side of the 2-column grid.
  - **Background:** Contained within the image boundary (not backgroundless).
- **Image 2 (Right Project - "Aftermath"):**
  - **Description:** A moody black-and-white close-up profile portrait. The subject has their hand near their mouth/chin, eyes closed, with loose strands of hair across the face. A long, elegant jeweled/crystal drop earring is clearly visible on the right earlobe, adding a touch of texture and reflectivity.
  - **Placement:** Right side of the 2-column grid, directly adjacent to the first image.
  - **Background:** Contained within the image boundary (not backgroundless).

---

## 4. Spacing & Alignment
- **Margins & Padding:** Generous whitespace and padding surrounding all elements to maintain a clean, high-end editorial aesthetic.
- **Grid Alignment:** The project titles align precisely with the left edge of their respective images.
- **Dividers:** Clean, subtle horizontal lines separating structural blocks.
