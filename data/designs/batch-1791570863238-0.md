# UI/UX Design Specification: Nextgen E-Commerce Product Detail Page

---

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Fonts
- **Primary Font Family**: Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif
- **Fallback Stack**: `sans-serif`
- **Font Weights Used**: 
  - Regular (`400`)
  - Medium (`500`)
  - Semi-bold (`600`)
  - Bold (`700`)

### Color Palette
- **Primary / Dark (Text & Buttons)**: `#111111` (Nearly Black)
- **Secondary (Muted text / Borders)**: `#767676` or `#8A8A8A`
- **Background (Global Page)**: `#F7F7F7` (Light Off-white)
- **Container / Card Background**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Accent / Highlight (Star Ratings, Tags)**: `#FFC107` (Gold/Yellow), `#E25252` or soft pastel red (`#FDF2F2` for discount badges)
- **Borders & Dividers**: `#E5E7EB` (Light Gray)

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Grid

- **Layout Type**: Containerized centralized layout maxing out around `1440px` width with generous horizontal padding (`80px` outer margins).
- **Grid System**: 
  - Header: Flexbox space-between layout.
  - Main Product Section: Two-column grid (Left: Product Gallery / Right: Product Details & Actions), split roughly 50/50.
  - Ratings & Reviews: Two-column asymmetrical layout (Left: Overall score & breakdown / Right: Individual reviews carousel/cards).
  - "You might also like": 4-column product card grid.
  - Footer: 5-column layout (Brand info, Company, Help, FAQ, Resources) plus bottom copyright/payment bar.

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation Header
- **Top Bar Elements**:
  - Left: Hamburger menu icon + "Categories" dropdown + "New Product" dropdown.
  - Center: Search bar with magnifying glass icon and placeholder text `"Search"`, followed by quick links: `Men`, `Women`, `Children`, `Brands`.
  - Center Logo: Circular badge with monogram "N" followed by typography `Nextgen`.
  - Right: `About`, `FAQs`, and a shopping bag icon with an item badge count (`0`).
- **Sub-navigation Breadcrumb**: 
  - Left aligned: `Home > Product details` (gray text, active link styled darker).

---

### B. Main Product Section (Top)

#### Left Column: Product Gallery
- **Main Image Display**:
  - Large vertical aspect ratio card with rounded corners (`border-radius: 16px`).
  - Contains a high-resolution photograph of a male model wearing a maroon loose-fit hoodie.
  - Image pagination indicators (horizontal dashes at the top center of the main image container, with the first dash active/white).
- **Thumbnail Row**:
  - Three smaller thumbnail cards positioned below the main image for switching angles/views.
  - Selected thumbnail has a soft border/focus ring.

#### Right Column: Product Details
- **Category Tag**: Small pill tag reading `Man Fashion` (uppercase, tracked out, light gray background).
- **Product Title**: `Loose Fit Hoodie` (Heading level 1, bold, `~28px`).
- **Price**: `$24.99` (Bold, prominent).
- **Urgency/Stock Banner**: Light container with an info icon and text: `Order in 02:30:25 to get next day delivery`.
- **Size Selector**:
  - Label: `Select Size`
  - Horizontal list of pill buttons: `S` (selected/dark background with white text), `M`, `L`, `XL`, `XXL`.
- **Action Buttons**:
  - Primary button: `Add to Cart` (full width, dark background `#111111`, white text, rounded corners).
  - Wishlist button: Circular icon button with a heart outline (`🤍`).
- **Accordion 1: Description & Fit** (Expanded by default):
  - Description text: *"Loose-fit sweatshirt hoodie in medium weight cotton-blend fabric with a generous, but not oversized silhouette. Jersey-lined, drawstring hood, dropped shoulders, long sleeves, and a kangaroo pocket. Wide ribbing at cuffs and hem. Soft, brushed inside."*
- **Accordion 2: Shipping** (Collapsed/Summary view):
  - Details included: Discount (`Disc 50%`), Package (`Regular Package`), Delivery Time (`3-4 Working Days`), Estimation Arrive (`10 - 12 October 2024`).

---

### C. Rating & Reviews Section
- **Header**: `Rating & Reviews` (Heading level 2).
- **Left Metric Block**:
  - Giant score display: `4,5` / `5`
  - Total reviews subtext: `(50 New Reviews)`
  - Rating distribution breakdown bars (5-star down to 1-star with progress lines).
- **Right Review Cards**:
  - Reviewer profile: Name (`Alex Mathio`), 5 yellow star ratings, date (`13 Oct 2024`).
  - Review body text: `"Nextgen’s dedication to sustainability and ethical practices resonates strongly with today’s consumers, positioning the brand as a responsible choice in how fashion world."`
  - Reviewer avatar thumbnail.
  - Carousel navigation arrow on the far right.

### E. Footer
- **Columns**:
  1. **Brand Column**: Logo (`N Nextgen`), short mission statement, social media icons (Facebook, Instagram, Dribbble, GitHub).
  2. **Company Column**: Links for `About`, `Features`, `Works`, `Career`.
  3. **Help Column**: Links for `Customer Support`, `Delivery Details`, `Terms & Conditions`, `Privacy Policy`.

---

## 4. Imagery & Visual Assets Guidelines

- **Product Photography Style**: Clean studio lighting, neutral grey/off-white backdrops, high-contrast streetwear styling, professional fashion editorial look with young adult models.
- **UI Icons**: Minimalist, thin-stroke line icons for search, shopping bag, user actions, delivery trucks, discount tags, and social media handles.
