<!-- design.md -->

- **Design Philosophy:** Dark, minimalist, and sleek "Glassmorphism" aesthetic. The composition emphasizes depth, lighting interaction, and high-end texture contrast using frosted glass overlaying organic, polished dark wood.
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Background / Canvas:** `#121212` (Deep matte black/charcoal)
  - **Glass Card Fill:** `rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.65)` (Semi-transparent dark charcoal with a frosted blur effect)
  - **Glass Border / Highlights:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` (Subtle rim lighting for 3D depth)
  - **Primary Text:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white)
  - **Secondary Accent Text:** `#C4B5A5` (Muted warm taupe/gold)
  - **Wood Spheres:** Rich dark brown gradient with subtle grey-brown undertones (`#332B25` to `#1A1512`) showing realistic wood grain patterns.

- **Typography:**
  - **Font Family:** Inter / SF Pro Display (Clean, modern sans-serif)
  - **Header ("Glass Morphism"):**
    - Size: `32px`
    - Weight: Bold (`700`)
    - Color: `#FFFFFF`
    - Line-height: `1.2`
  - **Brand / Subtext ("mockup."):**
    - Size: `22px`
    - Weight: Regular / Medium (`400`–`500`)
    - Color: `#C4B5A5`
    - Letter spacing: `-0.5px`
  - **Badges ("4K", "PSD"):**
    - Font size: `10px`
    - Weight: Bold (`700`)
    - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`
    - Border-radius: `3px`
    - Padding: `2px 4px`
  - **Footer Caption ("Isolated Objects & Editable Colors"):**
    - Size: `11px`
    - Weight: Regular (`400`)
    - Color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`

- **Layout & Structure:**
  - **Canvas:** Full viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), centered flex layout.
  - **Background Elements (Spheres):**
    - Three 3D dark wood spheres positioned behind the glass card to demonstrate refraction and distortion effects.
    - Sphere 1 (Bottom Left): Large scale, overlapping the bottom-left corner of the card.
    - Sphere 2 (Top Center): Partially visible, peeking over the top edge of the card.
    - Sphere 3 (Right): Medium scale, protruding from the right edge.
  - **Foreground Element (Glass Card):**
    - Position: Absolute center, overlapping the wood spheres.
    - Dimensions: `~420px` width × `~300px` height (scaled proportionally).
    - Border Radius: `24px` (Smooth, rounded modern corners).
    - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px)` (Frosted glass refraction).
    - Drop Shadow: Deep atmospheric shadow `0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

- **UI Components / Details:**
  - **Navigation / Indicators:** Three vertical dots on the right side of the card (`8px` diameter each, vertical spacing `6px`). The middle/bottom dot is highlighted in bright white, others are dimmed.
  - **Badges Section:** Placed near the bottom-left, featuring inline "4K" and "PSD" boxes followed by two lines of descriptive text.
