# Design Specification: "Botany" Hero Section

## 1. Global Settings

### 1.1. Color Palette
The design uses a high-contrast, minimalist color palette driven heavily by the imagery.
*   **Background Top (Sky/Fog):** `#F4F4F4` (Light Gray/Off-White)
*   **Background Bottom (Solid):** `#0E0E10` (Deep Charcoal/Almost Black)
*   **Primary Text:** `#FFFFFF` (White)
*   **Navigation Text (Top):** `#111111` (Nearly Black)
*   **Subdued/Secondary Text:** `#A0A0A0` (Medium Light Gray) - *Used for small captions.*
*   **Card Background:** `#1C1C1E` (Dark Gray/Soft Black) - *Blends into the bottom background.*
*   **Borders/Lines:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)` (Semi-transparent white)

### 1.2. Typography
The design relies on clean, modern, geometric sans-serif fonts to contrast with the organic imagery. 

*   **Primary Font (Headings & Big Text):** Looks like **PP Neue Montreal**, **Inter**, or **Helvetica Now Display**. It features tight tracking and perfectly circular counters (inside the 'o').
    *   *Fallback Stack:* `'Inter', 'Helvetica Neue', 'Helvetica', 'Arial', sans-serif`
*   **Secondary Font (Body & UI Elements):** A legible geometric sans-serif, slightly wider. 
    *   *Fallback Stack:* `'Circular Std', 'Roobert', 'San Francisco', '-apple-system', sans-serif`

**Typographic Hierarchy:**
1.  **Massive Background Display:** Lowercase, ultra-large (approx. `28vw` or `350px`), tight letter spacing (`-0.05em`), regular weight.
2.  **Foreground Heading:** Mixed case, large (approx. `80px`), tight line height (`0.9`), medium weight.
3.  **UI/Nav Text:** Small (approx. `12px` to `14px`), normal tracking, medium weight.
4.  **Captions:** Very small (approx. `10px` to `12px`), regular weight, gray color.

## 2. Image Assets (Descriptions & Placement)

This design relies entirely on complex image layering to create a 3D depth effect. No CSS drawing is required for the imagery.

### Image 1: Main Organic Wood & Moss (The "Sandwich" Asset)
*   **Description:** A highly detailed, photorealistic 3D render or macro photograph of a thick piece of gnarled, twisted driftwood covered in vibrant green moss patches. The lighting comes from the top left, creating deep shadows in the wood's crevices.
*   **Transparency/Background:** To achieve the text intersection effect (where the wood goes *over* the text "botany"), this image **must have a transparent background (PNG or WEBP)**. The sky/light gray area in the original design is a separate CSS background layer behind the text. 
*   **Placement & Rotation:** The wood structure spans diagonally. It enters from the middle-left edge, bows slightly upwards towards the center, and exits towards the bottom-right corner. It takes up approximately 80% of the viewport width.
*   **Layering Rule:** This image sits at `z-index: 2` (above the giant background text, but below the bottom UI elements).

### Image 2: Secondary Moss Crop (For the Bottom Right Card)
*   **Description:** A close-up crop of the same or similar mossy driftwood. It focuses on a vertical twist of the wood with heavy moss on the left edge of the wood piece.
*   **Transparency/Background:** Must be backgroundless/transparent on its left and bottom edges so it blends seamlessly into the dark background of the card.
*   **Placement & Rotation:** Placed inside the bottom-right "Selected Work" card. It is anchored to the right side of the card, slightly bleeding off the right edge.

## 3. Layout & Structure

The layout is a full-screen `100vh` hero section divided into three depth layers (Z-axis) and three vertical sections (Y-axis).

### 3.1. Z-Index Layering (Back to Front)
*   **Layer 0 (Background):** A CSS gradient or split solid background. Top 60% is `#F4F4F4`, bottom 40% fades into or sharply transitions to `#0E0E10`.
*   **Layer 1 (Background Text):** The giant word "botany".
*   **Layer 2 (Main Image):** The transparent driftwood image (Image 1).
*   **Layer 3 (UI & Foreground):** Navigation, floating captions, bottom text, buttons, and the bottom-right card.

### 3.2. Top Section: Header Navigation
*   **Positioning:** Top center of the viewport, `absolute` or `fixed`, with generous top padding (approx `40px`).
*   **Layout:** Flexbox, horizontal row, evenly spaced (`gap: 40px`).
*   **Items (Left to Right):**
    1.  Text: "Home" (Color: `#111111`)
    2.  Text: "Ecosystem" (Color: `#111111`)
    3.  **Logo Icon:** A small, solid black geometric shape. It resembles two abstract, thick 'U' shapes or puzzle
