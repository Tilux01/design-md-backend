# UI/UX Design Specification & Replication Guide

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Color Palette
*   **Primary Background (`--bg-primary`):** `#0A0A0A` (Deep dark, almost pure black)
*   **Container Background (`--bg-container`):** `#0F0F0F` (Slightly lighter than primary, used for the main card/wrapper)
*   **Text Primary (`--text-main`):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
*   **Text Secondary (`--text-muted`):** `#CCCCCC` (Light Gray, used for paragraph descriptions)
*   **Accent Color (`--accent-green`):** `#A3FF00` (Neon Lime Green used for action buttons, arrows, and focus frames)
*   **Border/Divider Color (`--border-color`):** `#222222` (Subtle dark gray for separators)

### Typography
*   **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif
*   **Fallback Font:** `sans-serif`
*   **Font Weights Used:** Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (700)

#### Type Hierarchy
*   **Section Header Prefix `(srvs)`:**
    *   Size: `12px` / Weight: `400` / Color: `--text-muted` / Tracking: Wide
*   **Main Title ("WHAT WE CAN DO"):**
    *   Size: `48px` to `64px` (Responsive) / Weight: `700` / Line-height: `1.1` / Color: `--text-main`
*   **Service Index Numbers ("01", "02", ...):**
    *   Size: `24px` / Weight: `500` / Color: `--text-main`
*   **Service Titles ("MARKETING STRATEGY", etc.):**
    *   Size: `24px` / Weight: `700` / Tracking: `0.05em` / Color: `--text-main`
*   **Body Copy / Descriptions:**
    *   Size: `14px` / Weight: `400` / Line-height: `1.6` / Color: `--text-muted`
*   **Button Text ("LEAVE A REQUEST"):**
    *   Size: `12px` / Weight: `700` / Color: `--accent-green` (or dark on hover)

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Containers

*   **View Mode:** Full-screen responsive container (`width: 100vw`, `min-height: 100vh`) with a centered, padded card interface (`max-width: 1200px`).
*   **Outer Page:** Deep black background with a subtle ambient starfield/noise texture.
*   **Main Wrapper Box:** Contained inside a thin, dark-gray bounding stroke (`border: 1px solid #222222`) with generous internal padding (`40px` to `60px`).

---

## 3. Section-by-Section Component Breakdown

### Header Section
*   **Top Sub-header:** Centered small text reading `[ SERVICES ]` at the very top of the layout frame.
*   **Main Heading Block:**
    *   Positioned on the right side of the upper section.
    *   Small label: `(srvs)` positioned just above the main heading.
    *   Heading text: **"WHAT WE CAN DO"** split across two lines, aligned to the right.

---

### Accordion / Services List Component

The layout is divided into **4 distinct horizontal rows** separated by thin borders (`--border-color`). 

#### Row 1: Active / Expanded State (MARKETING STRATEGY)
*   **Left Column:** 
    *   Index number: `01`
    *   Small tracking text underneath: `Complete solution from 0 to 100%` (Size: `11px`, Color: `--text-muted`).
*   **Middle Column:** 
    *   Title: **MARKETING STRATEGY**
    *   Call-to-Action Element: A button styled with a bright neon green border and black fill reading **LEAVE A REQUEST**, framed by four corner square accents.
*   **Right Column (Description & Action):**
    *   Paragraph 1: *"We will build an effective strategy for your business from the ground up, taking into account market realities and user needs."*
    *   Paragraph 2: *"Determine the competitive advantages of your product or service."*
    *   Paragraph 3: *"Develop a plan for quality retention of old customers and attracting new ones for your business."*
    *   Top-Right Icon: A diagonal arrow pointing up-right (`↗`) colored in `--accent-green`.

#### Rows 2, 3, 4: Collapsed / Inactive States
*   **Row 02:** `GOOGLE ADS` (with right-aligned neon green arrow `↗`)
*   **Row 03:** `TARGETING` (with right-aligned neon green arrow `↗`)
*   **Row 04:** `SMM PROMOTION` (with right-aligned neon green arrow `↗`)

---

## 4. Interactive States & Micro-Interactions

*   **Hover on Service Rows:** 
    *   Background subtly highlights or brightens.
    *   The diagonal arrow icon (`↗`) scales up slightly or shifts position toward the top-right.
*   **Button Interaction ("LEAVE A REQUEST"):**
    *   Background: Solid `--accent-green` (`#A3FF00`) on hover, transparent with neon border in default state.
    *   Text color shifts to dark (`#0A0A0A`) on hover for high contrast.
