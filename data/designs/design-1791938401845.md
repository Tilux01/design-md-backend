# Design Specification: Cyberpunk Games Store UI

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Theme:** Futuristic Cyberpunk / Dark Mode.
*   **Background:** Solid deep charcoal/black (`#050505`).
*   **Canvas:** Full-screen edge-to-edge layout.
*   **Grid System:** Two-column layout. 
    *   **Left Column (Main Content):** 65% width.
    *   **Right Column (Cart Sidebar):** 35% width.
*   **Corner Radii:** Heavy use of rounded corners (approx. `40px` for main containers) and organic, wavy shapes for internal elements.

---

## 2. Color Palette
*   **Primary Background:** `#050505` (Pure Black/Dark Grey).
*   **Accent Lime Green:** `#C1FF00` (Used for "Shop" button, "Ninja" text, and Cart Item 1).
*   **Accent Blue:** `#1E5BFF` (Used for Cart Item 2).
*   **Dark Grey Surface:** `#121212` (Used for the Cart background and Genre widget).
*   **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (White).
*   **Text Secondary:** `#A0A0A0` (Medium Grey for descriptions).
*   **Text Tertiary:** `#555555` (Dark Grey for metadata).

---

## 3. Typography
*   **Primary Heading Font:** *Orbitron* or *Rajdhani* (Futuristic Sans-serif).
    *   *Fallback:* `Impact`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Body/UI Font:** *Inter* or *Roboto*.
    *   *Fallback:* `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Main Title:** Bold, All-caps, `48px`.
    *   **Subheadings:** Semi-bold, `18px`.
    *   **Body Text:** Regular, `14px`, line-height `1.5`.
    *   **Cart Price:** Bold, `20px`.

---

## 4. Header & Navigation
*   **Logo (Top Left):** "CYBERPUNK GAMES" in a stacked, futuristic font. "CYBERPUNK" in white, "GAMES" in lime green.
*   **Nav Bar (Center Top):** A pill-shaped dark container.
    *   **Shop Button:** Lime green background, black text, includes a small shopping bag icon.
    *   **Links:** "Community", "Library", "News" in white text with generous spacing.
*   **User Info (Top Right):** 
    *   Currency display: `$2000.015` with a small wallet icon.
    *   Flag icon: UK/Union Jack circular icon.

---

## 5. Main Hero Section (Left)
### Image 1: Main Character
*   **Description:** A cyberpunk male character with spiky white/silver hair, wearing a high-tech tactical mask/respirator and a dark puffer-style jacket with a red patch on the shoulder.
*   **Placement:** Anchored to the bottom-left corner.
*   **Properties:** Backgroundless (PNG). The character is slightly rotated 5 degrees counter-clockwise to give a dynamic feel. The bottom of the image is cut off by a diagonal "hazard stripe" pattern (white/grey).

### Game Info (Center-Left)
*   **Title:** "ULTIMATE **NINJA** WARS". "NINJA" is highlighted in lime green.
*   **Description:** Small block of grey text describing the strategy game.
*   **Thumbnails:** Three circular avatars below the description showing different character classes.

### Genre Widget (Bottom Center)
*   **Shape:** A rounded square container (`#121212`).
*   **Content:** A white "Puzzle Piece" icon with the word "Strategy" underneath in grey.

---

## 6. Cart Sidebar (Right)
*   **Container:** A tall, rounded-corner dark grey panel (`#121212`).
*   **Header:** "CART" (Left aligned) and "3 games selected" (Right aligned, small grey text).
*   **Cart Items:** Three distinct cards with "wavy/liquid" top and bottom borders.
    1.  **Top Card (Lime Green):** "ANBU WARS", Black text. Includes a stylized ninja leaf icon.
    2.  **Middle Card (Bright Blue):** "ATTACK OF THE TITANS", White text. Includes a winged crest icon.
    3.  **Bottom Card (Dark Grey):** "NARUTO", White text. Includes a Sharingan/Eye icon.
*   **Item Details:** Each card contains "Release: 2024" and "Creator: [Name]" in very small text.
*   **Action Icons:** Each card has two circular buttons on the right: a red "Trash" icon and a white "Bookmark" icon.

### Footer (Bottom of Cart)
*   **Total Price:** "Total Price: $1 250" in bold white.
*   **Checkout Button:** A circular lime green button with a black shopping cart icon, positioned to the right of the price.

---

## 7. Visual Effects & Details
*   **Glow:** Subtle outer glow on the Lime Green elements.
*   **Shadows:** Soft drop shadows on the Cart cards to create depth against the sidebar background.
*   **Masking:** The wavy separators in the cart should have a smooth SVG path to ensure the "liquid" look is perfect.
