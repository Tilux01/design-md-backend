# Design & Implementation Specification: COFFEE VIBES UI

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas (`design.md`)

* **Viewport:** Fullscreen responsive container (`100vw` width, `100vh` height).
* **Background Style:** 
  * **Color:** Warm peach / pastel cream (`#FCE3D2` or CSS gradient equivalent).
  * **Pattern:** Subtle, organic topographic contour lines overlay (vector SVG pattern or repeating smooth SVG path overlay) in a slightly darker terracotta/brown tint (`#E8CCA8` / opacity: 0.6).
* **Header Branding (Top Center):**
  * **Logo Text:** "Cafe & Restaurant" (italic serif, small size) stacked above "COFFEE VIBES +" (bold, geometric slab-serif or sans-serif, uppercase, tracking spaced).
  * **Graphic:** Stylized steam lines rising out of a coffee cup icon on the right side of the logotype.

---

## 2. Typography & Fallbacks

| Element | Target Font | Fallback Stack | Weight / Style |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brand Title ("COFFEE VIBES")** | `Montserrat` / `League Spartan` | `Arial Black`, `Impact`, sans-serif | Extra Bold (800), Uppercase |
| **Subtitle ("Cafe & Restaurant")** | `Playfair Display` / `Georgia` | `Times New Roman`, serif | Italic (400) |
| **Card Headers ("Cofee", "ink", "B...")** | `Montserrat` / `Poppins` | `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif | Bold (700), Rounded Terminals |

---

## 3. Carousel Component Structure

The interface acts as a **Horizontal Card Carousel / Slider** featuring rounded cards floating on a soft shadow plane.

### Card Specifications:
* **Shape:** Rounded rectangle (Border-radius: `24px` to `32px`).
* **Background Color:** Solid rich medium brown (`#8C5339` or `#794429`).
* **Shadows:** Deep multi-layered drop shadow to create a 3D levitating effect over the topographic background.
* **Layout Per Card:**
  * **Top Text:** Bold white text centered at the top of the card (e.g., *"Cofee"*).
  * **Center Content:** High-resolution cutout PNG image of the food/drink item.
    * *Active Card (Center):* Fully scaled (`scale: 1`), crisp, centered.
    * *Inactive Cards (Left/Right):* Partially clipped/hidden off-screen to indicate swipeability.

---

## 4. Interactive Elements & Animation Specs

1. **Finger Gesture / Cursor Pointer:**
   * An animated human hand pointer graphic enters from the bottom right to indicate interactivity (swipe/drag gesture).
2. **Carousel Swipe / Scroll Animation:**
   * **Transition:** Smooth cubic-bezier slide (`cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`), duration `0.4s` to `0.6s`.
   * **Scale Effect:** The active card scales smoothly up to 100% while adjacent cards scale down slightly (`scale: 0.85`) and reduce opacity (`opacity: 0.6`) for depth-of-field focus.

---
