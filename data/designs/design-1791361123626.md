# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist E-Commerce (Clean, high-end sportswear showcase combining brutalist structural framing with soft neumorphic/glassmorphic surface elements).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio (approx. 65% negative space) focusing complete visual attention on the hero product render. Padding density is generous (`space-8` to `space-12`), establishing an airy, premium catalog feel.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered depth model utilizing soft ambient drop shadows (`box-shadow: 0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`), crisp 1px borders (`#E5E7EB`), and subtle inset dish shadows for circular product staging pedestals.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Minimalist monochrome palette, high-contrast typography, floating neumorphic containers, concentric circular drop shadows, subtle status checkmarks.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `11px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#000000` (inverse) or `#F3F4F6` (subdued), border `none`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> `&mdash;`
  - Star (★ / ☆) -> `&#9733;` / `&#9734;`
  - Up/Down Chevrons -> `&#9650;` / `&#9660;` or SVG icons.
  - Checkmark (✓) -> `&#10003;`
