# 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
- **Primary Mood:** Cinematic, immersive, sophisticated, and tranquil.
- **Brand Archetype:** High-end travel curation / premium geographic exploration platform.
- **Visual Density:** Airy and spacious with balanced information density anchored at the lower viewport edges.
- **Whitespace Balance:** Heavy reliance on full-bleed photographic background media coupled with intentional framing margins.
- **Theme Balance:** 100% Dark Mode. Utilizes deep slate/charcoal backgrounds overlaid with a full-bleed dark-toned photography backdrop paired with translucent frosted-glass (backdrop-filter) panels and vibrant cyan/teal accent triggers.

# 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
- **Container Max-Width:** `1280px` centered with fluid responsive padding.
- **Padding & Margin Scale:** 
  - Outer viewport padding: `40px` horizontal, `32px` vertical.
  - Component gap scale: `8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `48px`, `64px`.
- **Section Sequence Breakdown:**
  1. *Top Header:* Brand logotype (left), Navigation links (center-right), Hamburger/Menu icon (right).
  2. *Hero Left Content:* Main title "ENDLESS DISCOVERY", Video trigger ("WATCH MOVIE" with play icon pill), Action CTA button.
  3. *Hero Center/Background:* Full-bleed immersive cultural heritage photography with centered architectural landmark.
  4. *Hero Right Content:* Contextual info card ("JAPAN" description snippet), Vertical pagination slider indicator (`01` to `04`).
  5. *Footer Bar / Lower Content:* Social links (Instagram, YouTube) on bottom-left, Key metrics grid (`2,000 Temples`, `6,850 Islands`, `362 Rivers`) anchored at the bottom-center.
- **Stacking Order & z-index:**
  - Background Media: `z-index: 0`
  - Overlays / Vignette: `z-index: 1`
  - UI Content / Cards / Navigation: `z-index: 10`
  - Floating Modals / Overlays: `z-index: 100`

# 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement
- **Hero Layout:** Asymmetric split. Heavy focal weight on central architecture, text anchored to the mid-left quadrant, metrics aligned across the bottom baseline.
- **Cards & Containers (Info Card):**
  - Border Radius: `4px`
  - Border Width: `1px` solid `var(--color-border-glass)`
  - Backdrop Blur: `blur(12px)`
  - Inner Padding: `20px`
  - Background: `rgba(15, 18, 25, 0.70)`
- **Buttons & Action Triggers:**
  - *Primary CTA Pill:* 
    - Background: `#00D2FF`
    - Text: `#0B0E14`
    - Border-radius: `2px`
    - Padding: `10px 24px`
    - Font-weight: `600`
  - *Video Trigger ("WATCH MOVIE"):* 
    - Circular play icon container (`36px` diameter) with semi-transparent dark backdrop and white border stroke, paired with adjacent uppercase tracking label.
- **Navigation Components:**
  - Inline horizontal list, uppercase text, generous tracking (`0.1em`), low opacity transition on hover (`1.0` active/hover vs `0.6` default).

# 6. Motion Mechanics & Interactions
- **Animation Triggers:** Page load staggered fade-in, hover scaling on cards, active state shift on the vertical pagination indicator.
- **Transition Durations & Easing:**
  - Standard Micro-interactions: `200ms cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` (Ease-out)
  - Page Transitions / Hero Parallax: `600ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (Custom smooth deceleration)
- **Pagination Mechanics:** Vertical slider rail with active indicator pill shifting dynamically between steps `01` through `04`.
