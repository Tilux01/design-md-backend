# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist High-Fashion / Luxury Digital Magazine.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with stark asymmetrical split-screen composition. Generous padding around navigation links, editorial category headers, and typographic focal points.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, paper-like surface architecture divided into a dual-pane asymmetric split. Left pane features high-contrast monochromatic imagery with subtle photographic grain; right pane utilizes a clean, ultra-light alabaster canvas (`#F3F3F5`) with overlapping geometric subtle pastel blur orbs and crisp typographic hierarchy.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking editorial uppercase typography, minimalist asymmetric layout grids, pastel aesthetic gradient orbs, ultra-thin precision borders.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `6px 16px`, font-size: `11px`, font-weight: `700`, tracking: `0.2em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `#0A0A0A` (solid dark variant) or `#FFFFFF` (glass variant), border width: `1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> `&mdash;`
  - Star / Special Badge (★) -> `&#9733;`
  - Number Indicator (01) -> `<span className="font-mono">&ast;01</span>`
