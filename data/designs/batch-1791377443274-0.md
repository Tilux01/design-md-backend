# Comprehensive Design Specification: Pitch Deck Presentation

---

## 1. Global Design System & Layout Architecture

### 1.1 Canvas & Grid
- **Master Canvas Dimensions:** Designed as a horizontal 16:9 widescreen multi-slide presentation layout (rendered here as a cohesive architectural storyboard grid).
- **Global Margins & Padding:** 
  - Outer margins: `40px` (top, bottom, left, right perimeter containment).
  - Internal slide padding: Generous whitespace margins of `48px` horizontally and `40px` vertically.
- **Layout Paradigm:** Modern minimalist editorial layout characterized by an asymmetrical split-screen structure, heavy use of whitespace, strict typographical hierarchy, and structural border framing.

### 1.2 Color Palette
- **Primary Background:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) / Off-White Canvas.
- **Primary Text / Headings:** Deep Charcoal / Obsidian Black (`#111111`).
- **Body Copy / Subheadings:** Medium Neutral Gray (`#666666`).
- **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle Thin Line Gray (`#E0E0E0`).
- **Accent / Highlight:** Soft Muted Tan / Warm Cream (`#F5F5F3`) and occasional deep earth-tones picked up dynamically from native photography.

### 1.3 Typography System
- **Primary Font Family:** Clean, geometric neo-grotesque sans-serif (such as **Inter**, **Helvetica Neue**, or **Arial** as a reliable web-safe fallback).
- **Secondary / Accent Font:** Monospace or tracking-spaced uppercase tracker for micro-headers and meta metadata (such as **SF Mono**, **Roboto Mono**, or **Courier New**).
- **Typographic Scale:**
  - **Slide Titles / Macro Headers:** 
    - Font Size: `36px` to `48px`
    - Weight: Bold (`700`)
    - Letter Spacing: Tight (`-0.02em`)
    - Line Height: `1.1`
  - **Section Sub-headers / Feature Labels:** 
    - Font Size: `16px` to `20px`
    - Weight: Semi-Bold / Medium (`600`)
    - Letter Spacing: Normal (`0`)
    - Line Height: `1.3`
  - **Body Copy:** 
    - Font Size: `11px` to `13px`
    - Weight: Regular (`400`)
    - Line Height: `1.5`
    - Color: `#666666`

---

- **Slide Transitions:**
