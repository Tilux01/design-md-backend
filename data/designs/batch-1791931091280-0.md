# Design Specification: MIYASATO Product Slider

## 1. Overview & Layout Strategy
A full-screen, high-end dark luxury eCommerce hero slider centered around a 3D sneaker floating over bold background typography. 

- **Canvas**: Full viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), fixed overflow.
- **Background**: Subtle radial gradient:
  - Center: `#481838` (deep royal plum)
  - Edges/Corners: `#1d0717` (near-black violet)

---

## 2. Color Palette
| Role | Hex / RGBA | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Canvas Radial Center** | `#481838` | Viewport center glow |
| **Canvas Base/Edges** | `#1d0717` | Edge vignette & depth |
| **Text Primary** | `#FFFFFF` | Brand titles, price, headings, active labels |
| **Text Muted** | `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.55)` | Subtitles, ratings, body copy |
| **Hairline UI** | `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` | Slider navigation rings, underlines |

---

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Display Font**: Heavy geometric sans-serif (`Montserrat Black`, `Syne 800`, or `Clash Display 700`; Fallback: `Arial Black, sans-serif`).
  - Used for: Central text `MIYASATO`.
  - Properties: `font-size: clamp(4rem, 12vw, 11rem)`, uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.18em`.
- **Secondary Monospace/Tech Font**: Clean technical mono (`JetBrains Mono`, `Space Mono`; Fallback: `Courier New, monospace`).
  - Used for: Navigation links, metadata, description, price, CTA.
  - Properties: Uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.15em` to `0.25em`, light-to-medium weight.

---

## 4. Assets & Visual Elements

### 4.1. Central Product Image
- **Description**: High-top futuristic sneaker/boot rendered in matte lilac, plum, and grey-white materials. Features an ankle strap with buckle, textured tread, and a glowing neon white LED bar embedded into the lower lateral heel.
- **Format**: High-resolution cutout PNG (transparent background).
- **Positioning**: Absolute center (`top: 50%`, `left: 50%`, `transform: translate(-50%, -55%) rotate(-35deg)`).
- **Layering**: Layered directly over the central display text with a soft diffuse drop shadow (`box-shadow: 0 25px 50px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`).

---

## 5. UI Structure & Components

### 5.1. Top Navigation Bar
- **Container**: `position: absolute; top: 0; left: 0; width: 100%; height: 80px; padding: 0 48px; display: flex; align-items: center; justify-content: space-between; z-index: 20;`
- **Left**: `FLOAT` (Bold sans, `14px`, `font-weight: 900`, `letter-spacing: 0.2em`).
- **Center-Left**: `WOOCOMMERCE PRODUCT SLIDER` (Mono, `10px`, muted white, `letter-spacing: 0.15em`).
- **Center-Right**: Social Icons (Facebook, Instagram, Dribbble) in `13px` hairline white, spaced `16px` apart.
- **Right**: `CART (0)` (Mono, `11px`, `letter-spacing: 0.15em`).

### 5.2. Hero Display Area
- **Central Text**: `MIYASATO` centered horizontally and vertically (`z-index: 1`).
- **Shoe Asset**: Floating over the `Y`, `A`, and `S` characters (`z-index: 2`).

### 5.3. Side Slider Navigation
- **Left Button**: 
  - Position: `left: -40px`, `top: 50%`, `transform: translateY(-50%)`.
  - Style: Circular hairline border (`120px` diameter, `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).
  - Icon: Thin left arrow `←` (`font-size: 20px`, white).
- **Right Button**: 
  - Position: `right: -40px`, `top: 50%`, `transform: translateY(-50%)`.
  - Style: Identical `120px` hairline circular border.
  - Icon: Thin right arrow `→`.

### 5.4. Bottom Content Row
- **Container**: `position: absolute; bottom: 48px; width: 100%; padding: 0 80px; display: flex; justify-content: space-between; align-items: flex-end; z-index: 20;`
- **Left Column**:
  1. **Price**: `$849` (`font-size: 24px`, bold sans, white, `margin-bottom: 8px`).
  2. **Rating**: 5 stars (`★★★★☆`, 4 filled, 1 outline; `10px`, white/muted).
  3. **Description**: `A WooCommerce featured products slider that ticks all the boxes.` (`11px`, mono, muted white, max-width `260px`, line-height `1.5`).
- **Right Column**:
  - **CTA Button**: `BUY NOW` (Mono, `13px`, bold, `letter-spacing: 0.2em`, white).
  - Underline: Solid bottom border (`border-bottom: 2px solid #FFFFFF`, padding bottom `4px`).
