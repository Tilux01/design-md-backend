# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Scientific Minimalist & Bio-Tech Data Visualization.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-contrast central focal point surrounded by deep canvas negative space, optimized for mathematical clarity and minimal distraction.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat atmospheric dark canvas with high-luminance nodes, floating on a single unified depth layer.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase status badges, bioluminescent dual-strand node glows, precise connecting bridge rods, micro particle accents.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `11px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(138, 43, 226, 0.15)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(138, 43, 226, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
