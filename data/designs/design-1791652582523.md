# UI Design Specification: Alliance Unisex Grey-Toned Thermos Bottle App

This document provides a comprehensive, pixel-level specification for replicating the provided mobile product detail page. The design follows a modern **Neumorphic / Soft UI** aesthetic with a clean white/light-grey palette, subtle drop shadows, and high-end product presentation.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Screen Setup

*   **Platform:** Mobile App (iOS / Android adaptive)
*   **Target Viewport Width:** 393px (Standard modern smartphone width)
*   **Target Viewport Height:** 852px (Full-screen viewport, no outer device frame/mockup background)
*   **Background Color:** `#EAEFF6` (Soft, cool off-white with subtle gradients simulating studio lighting).
*   **Border Radius (Outer Screen):** `44px` (if rendered inside a container) or full screen (`0px` edges).

---

## 2. Typography System

*   **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `system-ui`, sans-serif.
*   **Fallback Font:** `Arial`, `Helvetica`, sans-serif.

## 3. Component-by-Component Specifications

### A. Header Navigation Bar
*   **Position:** Sticky top (`Top: 16px`, `Left: 24px`, `Right: 24px`).
*   **Height:** `44px`.
*   **Left Element (Back Button):**
    *   Container: Circular button (`40px × 40px`).
    *   Background: `#FFFFFF` with soft shadow (`0px 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`).
    *   Icon: Left arrow (`←`), Color: `#1E232B`.
*   **Right Element (Favorite Button):**
    *   Container: Circular button (`40px × 40px`).
    *   Background: `#FFFFFF` with soft shadow (`0px 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`).
    *   Icon: Outline heart (`♡`), Color: `#1E232B`.

---

### B. Product Hero Section & Visuals
*   **Position:** Centered below the header (`Top: 70px`).
*   **Height:** Approximately `360px`.

#### 1. Main Product Image
*   **Asset Description:** A high-resolution, studio-lit product render of the **Alliance Unisex Grey-Toned Thermos Bottle (500ml)**.
*   **State:** Backgroundless (transparent PNG/WebP).
*   **Rotation / Angle:** Angled at approximately 15 degrees diagonally from top-right to bottom-left, creating dynamic movement.
*   **Visual Details:** 
    *   Brushed metallic silver cap/lid with an integrated metal loop handle.
    *   Matte grey/white cylindrical body.
    *   Branding: The word "Alliance" printed vertically down the lower body in dark charcoal/black typography.
    *   Lighting: Soft edge highlights on the left side, smooth gradient shading on the right to emphasize cylinder curvature.

#### 2. Dynamic Floating Graphics (Overlay)
*   **Graphic Element:** A thin, glowing cyan/blue orbital ring/wave looping dynamically around the middle of the thermos bottle to emphasize sleekness and design.
*   **Color:** Gradient from transparent to `#00C2FF` to `#0072FF`.

#### 3. Feature Badges (Left Side)
*   **Position:** Absolute positioned on the left (`Left: 24px`, `Top: 190px`).
*   **Item 1 (BPA Free):**
    *   Icon: Minimalist double leaf graphic (black/dark grey).
    *   Label: "BPA free" (`10px`, Regular, `#1E232B`).
*   **Item 2 (100% Leak proof):**
    *   Icon: Water droplet graphic (black/dark grey).
    *   Label: "100%\nLeak proof" (`10px`, Regular, `#1E232B`, line-height `12px`).

#### 4. Color Swatches / Carousel Indicators (Right Side)
*   **Position:** Absolute positioned on the right (`Right: 24px`, `Top: 360px`).
*   **Layout:** Vertical stack.
*   **Items:**
    1.  Light Grey Dot (`8px` diameter, active state indicator with subtle outline ring).
    2.  Dark Charcoal/Black Dot (`8px` diameter).
    3.  Burnt Orange Dot (`8px` diameter).
    4.  Vibrant Purple Dot (`8px` diameter).

### C. Product Details & Information Card
*   **Position:** Overlaps the bottom of the hero section (`Top: 450px`).
*   **Background Container:** Solid `#FFFFFF` card with rounded top corners (`Border Radius: 32px 32px 0 0`) and a soft upward shadow (`0px -10px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`).
*   **Padding:** `28px` horizontal, `24px` vertical.

#### 1. Ratings Bar
*   **Layout:** Flex row, vertically centered.
*   **Pill Container:** Soft background `#F4F6F9`, padding `4px 12px`, border radius `20px`.
*   **Content:**
    *   Score: `4.5`
    *   Star Icon: Solid gold star (`#FFC107`).
    *   Separator: Vertical divider line (`|`, color `#D1D5DB`).
    *   Text: "Ratings >" (`#717680`).

#### 2. Product Title
*   **Text:** `Alliance unisex grey-toned thermos bottle – 500ml`
*   **Styling:** Bold, dark grey/black (`#1E232B`), size `20px`, line-height `26px`.
*   **Spacing:** `12px` margin top from the rating pill.

#### 3. Category / Best Seller Badge
*   **Position:** Below the title (`8px` spacing).
*   **Badge Style:** Pill shape, background `#3B82F6` (vibrant blue), text color `#FFFFFF`, padding `4px 10px`, border radius `12px`.
*   **Text:** `Best Seller` (`11px` Semi-Bold).

#### 4. Description Text
*   **Text:** `Find your inner peace with Alliance Bottle. You can carry it with you everywhere ` + **`read more`**
*   **Styling:** Regular, `#717680`, size `13px`, line-height `20px`.
*   **Interactive Link:** The words "read more" are styled in darker accent text (`#1E232B` or `#3B82F6`) to indicate interactivity.

*   **Position:** Fixed at bottom (`Bottom: 24px`, horizontal margins `24px`).
