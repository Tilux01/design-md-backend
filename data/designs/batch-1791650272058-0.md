# Design Specification: IGNITEX "Design & Strategy" Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
- **Viewport:** Full-screen immersive layout (100vw, 100vh). No containment frames or boxed borders in the final view.
- **Background:** Dark theme. A rich charcoal/near-black base (`#0f0f0f` to `#141414`) overlaid with a subtle, blurred photographic background.
- **Background Image Description:** A dark, moody, abstract macro or industrial shot featuring spherical metallic elements (resembling metallic knobs, ball bearings, or ergonomic styling objects) with crisp specular highlights and deep shadows. The image is softly blurred using a depth-of-field effect, creating a sophisticated backdrop without distracting from the typography. It is placed centrally-right and spans the mid-to-lower section of the viewport.
- **Grid Structure:** Fluid grid with a strict top navigation bar, a massive hero typographic block, and a two-column bottom layout.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
*Note: Since exact proprietary fonts cannot be guaranteed, fallback stacks are provided for web-safe matching.*

### Primary Display Font (Headings)
- **Used For:** "Design & Strategy"
- **Style:** Heavy, geometric, modern sans-serif with high visual weight. The ampersand ("&") is rendered in an elegant, flowing script/cursive style to contrast with the bold geometric letters.
- **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`

### Secondary UI/Body Font
- **Used For:** Navigation links, location badge, tagline, and list items.
- **Style:** Clean, minimalist, modern neo-grotesque sans-serif. Lowercase and uppercase pairings with tight tracking.
- **Fallback Stack:** `Inter, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, sans-serif`

---

## 3. Header & Navigation Bar
- **Position:** Fixed at the top, spanning edge-to-edge with generous horizontal padding.
- **Left Element:** 
  - Brand Logo: **IGNITEX**™ rendered in bold uppercase sans-serif with a trademark symbol.
- **Center Element:**
  - Location Badge: `Based in: Los Angeles, USA` in muted grey text.
- **Right Elements:**
  - Navigation Links: `.Home`, `About us`, `Projects (10+)`, `Contact`
  - Menu Icon: Minimalist hamburger icon consisting of three parallel horizontal lines stacked vertically.
- **Colors & States:** White text for active elements, muted grey (`#888888`) for secondary text. Hover states transition smoothly to pure white.

---

## 4. Hero Section ("Design & Strategy")
- **Layout:** Positioned centrally-left with massive typographic scale.
- **Line 1:** 
  - Text: `Design`
  - Font Weight: Extra Bold / Black
  - Color: Pure white (`#ffffff`) with high contrast.
- **Line 2 (Overlapping/Interwoven):**
  - Ampersand (`&`): Large, elegant, cursive/script ampersand in a semi-transparent light grey, gracefully intertwined with the word "Strategy".
  - Text: `Strategy`
  - Font Weight: Extra Bold / Black
  - Color: Crisp white with subtle lighting falloff.

---

## 5. Footer / Bottom Content Area
Split into two distinct columns at the bottom of the viewport:

### Bottom Left Column
- **Call-to-Action / Value Proposition:**
  - Icon: A small `+` plus sign symbol.
  - Text: `We turn bold ideas into standout brands and sleek websites. Let's`
  - Styling: Small, highly readable sans-serif font in light grey, optimized for readability over the dark background.

### Bottom Right Column
- **Numbered Service List (Right-aligned):**
  - `(01) Brand identity design`
  - `(02) Framer development`
  - `(03) UI/UX strategy`
- **Styling:** Numbers `(01)`, `(02)`, `(03)` are styled in a slightly lighter or accented tone, followed by clean white uppercase/title-case typography for the service offerings.
