# Design Specification: Creamy Landing Page

## 1. Color Palette

*   **Primary Background:** Medium Sage/Mint Green (`#4E9B6C` to `#3B8256` subtle radial gradient).
*   **Bottom Wave Accent:** Warm Cream/Vanilla (`#FAF0D7`).
*   **Text Primary:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Text Secondary / Body:** Off-white (`#E2E8F0`) on green, Dark Slate (`#2D3748`) on cream.
*   **UI Accents:** Dark Forest Green (`#1E3A27`), White pill overlays (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).

---

## 2. Typography

*   **Primary Display / Headings:** 
    *   *Font:* `Fredoka`, `Poppins Bold`, or `Recoleta` (Rounded, playful display).
    *   *Fallback:* `system-ui, "Arial Rounded MT Bold", sans-serif`.
*   **Body & Navigation:** 
    *   *Font:* `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
    *   *Fallback:* `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`.

---

## 3. Global Layout & Structure

*   **Viewport:** 100vw, 100vh (full screen, overflow hidden).
*   **Composition:** Fixed top navigation, asymmetric two-column hero section, sweeping organic cream-colored wave at the bottom.

---

## 4. Components & Layout Breakdown

### A. Navigation Bar
*   **Placement:** Fixed/absolute top, full-width with horizontal padding (`px-12 py-6`).
*   **Left:** Brand wordmark `"Creamy"` in bold, organic rounded script/display font (white).
*   **Center:** Floating pill navigation container:
    *   Background: Semi-transparent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), backdrop-blur.
    *   Links: `HOME` (active: dark pill background `#1E3A27`, white text), `MENU`, `ABOUT`, `CONTACT` (dark text, medium weight).
*   **Right Utilities:**
    *   Search icon (white line icon).
    *   Shopping bag icon with an orange/red badge showing counter `"2"`.

### B. Hero Content (Left Column)
*   **Heading:** `"Taste Joy in\nEvery Bite"`
    *   White, display font, size: `~4.5rem`, line-height: `1.1`, bold.
*   **Subtext:** Small paragraph (`~0.85rem`), white opacity (`0.8`), max-width `320px`.
*   **CTA Buttons:**
    *   `ORDER NOW`: Solid white pill button, dark text (`#1E3A27`), bold.
    *   `SEE MENU ITEMS`: Translucent dark green pill, thin border, white text.

### C. Product Showcase (Right Column & Center)
*   **Weight Selector (Top Right):**
    *   Three circular buttons aligned horizontally: `"50g"`, `"100g"`, `"500g"`.
    *   Border: thin white line. Active item (`500g`): filled semi-transparent white.
*   **Product Imagery (Layered Perspective):**
    *   *Primary Item (Center):* Backgroundless pint of "N!CK'S Mint Chokladchip". Upright, lid off, oversized sculpted scoop of mint chip ice cream overflowing the top. Positioned centrally, breaking downward into the cream section.
    *   *Secondary Item (Mid-Right):* Backgroundless pint of "N!CK'S Strawberry" (red tub with strawberry ice cream scoop), scaled down to ~70% size, layered slightly behind the main pint.
    *   *Tertiary Item (Far-Right):* Backgroundless pint of "N!CK'S Vanilla/Caramel" tub, scaled down to ~40% size, placed toward the far-right edge.

### D. Bottom Wave & Footer Controls
*   **Wave Graphic:**
    *   Organic, asymmetrical SVG curve in solid cream (`#FAF0D7`) spanning the entire bottom 25–30% of the screen.
*   **Left (Social Proof):**
    *   Three overlapping circular customer avatar images.
    *   Adjacent text: **"10K+ Reviews"** (bold dark text), subtext *"Customers are satisfied"*.
*   **Center (Slider Controls):**
    *   Two circular outline buttons with arrows (`←` and `→`) for carousel navigation, centered beneath the main pint.
*   **Right (Social Icons):**
    *   Three small dark icons (Facebook, Instagram, Web) horizontally aligned.
