```markdown
# Design Specification: Ford "Rediscover the Classics" Interactive Landing Page

---

## 1. Global Styles & Design Tokens

### Color Palette
- **Background / Base:** `#0a0a0a` (Deep Charcoal / Near Black)
- **Primary Text (White):** `#ffffff` (Pure White, used for primary headings)
- **Secondary Text (Muted Gray):** `#8c8c8c` (Used for subheadings and body copy)
- **Accent / Highlight:** `#d4a373` (Warm Amber/Gold, used for subtle highlights and headlights)
- **UI Element Borders:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` (Subtle white overlays)

### Typography
- **Font Family:** Sans-serif, modern geometric style (e.g., *Helvetica Neue*, *Arial*, or *Inter*).
- **Hierarchy:**
  - **Main Heading ("REDISCOVER the CLASSICS"):** 
    - Size: `48px` - `64px` (Fluid scaling)
    - Weight: `300` ("REDISCOVER") / `700` ("CLASSICS")
    - Tracking (Letter spacing): `2px`
  - **Navigation & Secondary Links:**
    - Size: `11px` - `12px`
    - Weight: `400`
    - Tracking: `1.5px` (Uppercase)
  - **Body / Descriptive Text:**
    - Size: `13px`
    - Weight: `300`
    - Line Height: `1.5`
    - Color: `#8c8c8c`

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Grid

- **Layout Type:** Full-Screen Immersive Viewport (`100vw` × `100vh`), fixed container without scrolling.
- **Z-Index Layers:**
  1. **Background Layer:** Dark ambient garage environment with overhead lighting.
  2. **Hero Image / Car Object:** Centered car covered with a draped white sheet, partially revealing the front-right headlight.
  3. **Interactive Overlay:** Circular drag slider mechanism.
  4. **Typography & UI Layer:** Absolute positioned header, side text, and footer elements.

---

## 3. Component Specifications

### A. Header & Navigation
- **Position:** Absolute, top (`top: 32px`, `left: 48px`, `right: 48px`).
- **Layout:** Flexbox, space-between alignment.
- **Left Element:** 
  - Ford Logo (Classic oval or stylized typography icon).
  - Subtext beside logo: `"It's the classic performance car that only gets better."` (Font size: `11px`, Color: `#8c8c8c`).
- **Right Elements:**
  - Links: `BUILD & PRICE`, `CURRENT OFFERS`.
  - Icon: Hamburger menu icon (three horizontal lines).
  - Hover States: Opacity transition from `0.7` to `1.0`.

---

### B. Hero Content (Left Column)
- **Position:** Absolute, middle-left (`top: 32%`, `left: 48px`).
- **Content:**
  - Pre-header / Action prompt: `"REDISCOVER"` (Uppercase, Light weight).
  - Main Heading: `"the CLASSICS"` (Uppercase, Bold weight, positioned directly beneath).

---

### C. Centerpiece: The Car Reveal
- **Position:** Absolute, centered horizontally and vertically (`transform: translate(-50%, -50%)`).
- **Visuals:**
  - **Background Environment:** Concrete garage floor with realistic dust/smoke particles and atmospheric lighting.
  - **Lighting:** Geometric overhead LED light bars creating harsh specular highlights on the floor and vehicle curves.
  - **Vehicle:** Classic Ford Mustang silhouette covered by a rumpled white fabric sheet. The front right quarter is uncovered, showing the grille, amber headlight glowing warmly, and classic bumper detail.
- **Interactive Drag Mechanism:**
  - A large subtle wireframe semi-circle arc overlaid on the center.
  - **Drag Handle:** A glowing white circular point on the right side of the arc with the instructional label: `"DRAG TO REVEAL"`.

---

### D. Descriptive Text & Footer
- **Bottom-Right Paragraph:**
  - **Position:** Absolute (`bottom: 80px`, `right: 48px`, max-width: `260px`).
  - **Content:** `"It's a look that has spanned generations and continues to evolve — a timeless masterpiece."`
  - **Styling:** Small, muted text (`12px`, `#8c8c8c`).
- **Bottom-Left Footer:**
  - **Position:** Absolute (`bottom: 48px`, `left: 48px`).
  - **Content:** `"SHARE"` (Uppercase, small tracker text).

---

## 4. Interactive States & Animations

- **Drag to Reveal Interaction:**
  - Dragging the handle along the semi-circular arc triggers a CSS clip-path or JavaScript canvas wipe that pulls the fabric sheet back from right to left, transitioning the image from the "covered" state to the fully exposed classic car.
- **Hover Transitions:**
  - All navigation links and interactive UI items feature smooth `0.3s ease` opacity and color transitions.
```
