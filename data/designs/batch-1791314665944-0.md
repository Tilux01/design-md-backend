# 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic

- **Primary Mood & Brand Archetype:** Futuristic, immersive, high-tech AI agent dashboard. It bridges sleek cyber-aesthetic with highly functional productivity tools.
- **Visual Density:** High density, organized into clear modular cards and grid widgets without feeling cramped, utilizing deep dark space and atmospheric glows to segment information.
- **Whitespace Balance:** Tight internal padding within cards (12px–16px) juxtaposed with generous macro-spacing between major grid columns and sections (20px–24px).
- **Theme Balance:** Pure dark-mode first design system (`#050814` base) paired with vibrant neon-cyan and electric-violet glow accents (`#00F0FF`, `#7000FF`) to indicate live intelligence states, active connections, and primary actions.

# 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid

- **Container Max-Width:** Fluid scaling up to `1440px` max-width, optimized for tablet/desktop dashboards or expansive mobile viewports.
- **Spacing Scale:** 4px base multiplier (`4px`, `8px`, `12px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`).
- **Layout Grid (3-Column Layout):**
  - **Left Column:** AI Assistant intro, Quick Actions, Smart Shortcuts, Connected Devices. Width: ~28%
  - **Center Column:** Interactive AI Hologram / Voice Interaction, Chat Feed, Voice Waveform HUD. Width: ~44%
  - **Right Column:** Weather Widget, AI Insights, Today's Schedule, Trending Articles. Width: ~28%
- **Stacking Order (Z-Index):**
  - Base UI Grid: `z-0`
  - Floating Cards & Panels: `z-10`
  - Sticky Bottom Navigation / Audio HUD: `z-40`
  - Modals & Overlays: `z-50`

# 7. Accessibility (a11y) & WCAG Contrast Audit

- **Contrast Ratios:**
  - `#FFFFFF` (Primary Text) on `#0D1326` (Surface) = **15.4:1** (Passes WCAG AAA)
  - `#94A3B8` (Muted Text) on `#0D1326` (Surface) = **5.8:1** (Passes WCAG AA)
  - `#00F0FF` (Accent Cyan) on `#050814` (Base) = **12.1:1** (Passes WCAG AAA)
- **Keyboard Focus Ring Specifications:**
