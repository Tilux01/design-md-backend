# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Futuristic SaaS, Ambient Glassmorphism, Editorial Minimalist.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio focused on immersive hero branding, centered navigation, and glowing atmospheric lighting effects.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Deep obsidian base canvas (`#020202`) paired with frosted glass navigation overlays, subtle gridline overlays, and vibrant royal blue luminous glow orbs.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient background blue glow arches, glowing horizontal navigation pill, high-contrast monospace branding logo ("ZYNC"), subtle geometric background wireframe grid.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-blue-500 inline-block"></span>`.
