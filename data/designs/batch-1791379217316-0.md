# UI/UX Specification: Starbucks "Strawberry" Promotional Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Layout Strategy
This specification details a full-screen, responsive clone of the Starbucks promotional landing page shown in the media. 

*   **Layout Type:** Asymmetric split-screen hero section.
*   **Left Side (Typography & Call to Action):** Heavy promotional typography ("STRAWBERRY"), subheadline, and product cards carousel/grid.
*   **Right Side (Hero Visual):** Soft pastel abstract pink backdrop featuring a prominent hero product image (Strawberry Frappuccino with whipped cream and red drizzle) angled diagonally.
*   **Responsiveness:** Fluid scaling down to mobile viewports where the hero image stacks above the product cards.

---

## 2. Color Palette
All color values are exact approximations of the source image branding.

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code / RGBA | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Brand** | Starbucks Green | `#006241` | Buttons, iconography, Starbucks Siren logo |
| **Primary Text** | Deep Crimson / Black | `#111111` | Main headings, navigation items, product descriptions |
| **Accent Text/Title** | Vibrant Strawberry Red | `#D32F2F` | Hero header "STRAWBERRY" |
| **Background (Main)** | Soft Pale Pink | `#FCE4EC` / `#F8D7DA` | Page background gradients, abstract blobs |
| **Surface (Cards)** | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Product card containers |
| **Secondary Text** | Muted Gray | `#757575` | Prices, promotional subtitles, navigation links |

---

## 3. Typography & Font Stack
The design relies on strong editorial serif typography for the header and clean sans-serif for UI elements.

### Font Families
1.  **Display Serif (Headlines):** 
    *   *Primary:* `Playfair Display`, `Merriweather`, or high-contrast editorial serif.
    *   *Fallback:* `Georgia`, `Times New Roman`, `serif`.
2.  **Sans-Serif (Body, Buttons, Navigation):** 
    *   *Primary:* `SoDo Sans` (Starbucks proprietary) -> Substitute with `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, or `Inter`.
    *   *Fallback:* `sans-serif`.

### Typography Hierarchy
*   **Navigation Links:** 14px, Weight: 500 (Medium), Color: `#111111`
*   **Hero Main Title ("STRAWBERRY"):** ~72px–96px (responsive), Weight: 800 (Extra Bold), Tracking (letter-spacing): Tight (`-0.03em`), Color: `#D32F2F`.
*   **Section Heading ("Fuel Your Passion"):** 28px, Weight: 700 (Bold), Color: `#111111`.
*   **Body Copy:** 14px, Line-height: 1.5, Weight: 400 (Regular), Color: `#555555`.
*   **Product Price:** 16px, Weight: 700 (Bold), Color: `#111111`.

---

## 4. Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation Bar (Header)
*   **Position:** Sticky / Fixed top.
*   **Height:** ~80px.
*   **Left Group:** Starbucks Siren Logo + Nav links (*Menu*, *Rewards*, *Gift Cards*, *Store Locator*).
*   **Right Group:** Authentication Action Buttons:
    *   *Sign In:* Transparent background, pill border or clean text.
    *   *Join Now:* Solid Starbucks Green (`#006241`) pill-shaped button with white text.

### B. Hero Section
*   **Background:** Multi-layered gradient/abstract SVG blobs in varying shades of pink (`#FCE4EC`, `#F8D7DA`).
*   **Text Block (Left):**
    *   `H1`: "STRAWBERRY" (styled with uppercase, heavy serif).
    *   `H2`: "Fuel Your Passion"
    *   `Paragraph`: *"Experience the perfect blend of premium coffee and exceptional moments. Every cup tells a story."*
    *   `CTA Buttons`: "Order Now" (Solid Green button) and "Find Store" (Outlined pill button).
*   **Hero Image (Right):**
    *   High-resolution photo of a clear cup filled with pink blended beverage, topped with whipped cream and strawberry drizzle, tilted at approximately a **25-degree angle**.
    *   Drop-shadow added to give depth against the abstract background.

### C. Product Carousel / Grid Section
A horizontal card layout displaying featured drinks.

*   **Card Specifications:**
    *   **Background:** White (`#FFFFFF`).
    *   **Border-Radius:** 12px.
    *   **Padding:** 16px.
    *   **Shadow:** Subtle elevation shadow (`0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`).
*   **Card Contents:**
    *   **Image:** Square or rounded container with a product photograph (e.g., Caramel Macchiato in a glass/mug).
    *   **Title:** "Caramel Macchiato" (14px, Bold).
    *   **Description:** Short ingredient snippet (12px, Muted gray, truncated).
    *   **Footer:** 
        *   Price: `$4.95` (Left-aligned).
        *   Action Button: Circular green button with a white plus icon (`+`) inside (Right-aligned).

---

## 5. Animations & Interactions (Motion Design)
To replicate a modern high-end web experience (like Starbucks' promotional pages):

1.  **Page Load Entrance (Framer Motion / CSS Keyframes):**
    *   *Hero Text:* Fade in and slide up from 20px down with a stagger effect (`staggerChildren: 0.1s`).
    *   *Hero Image:* Scale up slightly from `0.95` to `1.0` with a smooth ease-out cubic-bezier curve (`0.25, 1, 0.5, 1`).
2.  **Hover States:**
    *   **Buttons:** Slight scaling (`transform: scale(1.02)`) and background color darken (e.g., `#004d33` for green buttons).
    *   **Product Cards:** Subtle elevation lift (`transform: translateY(-4px)` with increased box-shadow).
    *   **Nav Links:** Color transition to Starbucks Green on hover.
3.  **Carousel Interaction:**
    *   Smooth horizontal scroll behavior (`overflow-x: auto` with smooth scrolling enabled).
