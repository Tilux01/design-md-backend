# Design Specification: Sarina Mindfulness Web Application

## 1. Global Styles & Layout Structure

### 1.1 Viewport & Container
- **Layout Type:** Full-screen responsive layout (no outer decorative frame/mockup borders).
- **Background Color:** Soft, desaturated cool-gray/off-white (`#EDF1F5` to `#F4F6F9`).
- **Layout Grid:** 12-column structural grid layout with generous whitespace padding on the left and right margins.

### 1.2 Typography System
- **Primary Font Family:** Clean, geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, or *SF Pro Display*).
- **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
- **Text Hierarchy:**
  - **Brand Logo Text:** `16px`, Medium weight (`500`), Dark charcoal.
  - **Navigation Links:** `14px`, Regular weight (`400`), Dark charcoal.
  - **Headings (Hero - "Mindfullness More Calm Meaningful"):** `44px` to `56px`, Bold weight (`700` / `800`), dark green-black (`#11221F`), tight line height (`1.1`).
  - **Body Copy:** `13px` / `14px`, Regular weight (`400`), Muted gray-slate (`#5A6572`), line-height `1.5`.
  - **Buttons & CTAs:** `14px`, Medium weight (`500`), White or Dark.

---

## 2. Header & Navigation Component

- **Logo Section:** 
  - Lotus flower icon in deep green (`#0F6E50`) inside a clean circular container, paired with the text "Sarina".
- **Floating Pill Navigation Bar:**
  - Centered horizontally inside a rounded pill-shaped container (`border-radius: 50px`) with a subtle white/translucent glassmorphism background and soft drop shadow.
  - Links: **Home** (active state styled with a solid green pill background `#0F6E50` and white text), **Product**, **Service**, **About**.
- **User Actions:**
  - Right-aligned buttons: **Log in** (text button) and **Sign in** (solid dark green pill button `#0F6E50` with white text).

---

## 3. Hero Section (Left Column)

- **Micro-Tag / Pill:**
  - Small pill badge: `● meditation` with dark text and a subtle translucent backdrop.
- **Main Heading Text Group:**
  - Multi-line prominent headline:
    - *"Mindfullness"*
    - Vertical separator bar paired with a lotus icon leading into:
    - *"More Calm"*
    - *"Meaningful"*
- **Feature Icons Row:**
  - Three circular icon tokens arranged horizontally featuring minimal line art inside soft white/green circular backgrounds.
- **Sub-headline / Description:**
  - Short descriptive paragraph: *"We offer a holistic approach to yoga and meditation, blending traditional practices with modern techniques to help you achieve harmony and inner peace."*
- **Call-to-Action (CTA) Buttons:**
  - Primary Button: **Get started** (Solid dark green `#0F6E50`, rounded corners, white text).
  - Secondary Button: **Explore** (White/light outline style with dark text).

---

## 4. Central Visual Component (Main Hero Image Card)

- **Container:**
  - Large rounded rectangle card (`border-radius: 24px`) framing the primary visual.
- **Visual Imagery Description:**
  - A crisp, photographic portrait of a person with dark hair in a top-knot bun, wearing a casual white t-shirt and light gray pants, meditating outdoors in a lotus pose (padmasana) with hands resting in a mudra position. Lush, out-of-focus green foliage/plants fill the background.
- **Floating Glassmorphic Overlays:**
  - **Top-left tag:** Semi-transparent frosted glass pill reading `● Meditation`.
  - **Top-right tag:** Semi-transparent frosted glass pill reading `● Mental Less`.
  - **Center-bottom stat card:** Frosted glass overlay containing large bold white typography: `>240` and subtitle text `people improve their mentality here`.
  - **Bottom-right pill filters:** Two floating pill tags reading `● Mental health` and `● Mindfulness`.
  - **Pagination dots:** Two small white indicator dots at the bottom-left corner of the image card.

---

## 5. Right Sidebar (Offerings & Community Cards)

### 5.1 "Explore Our Offerings" Section
- **Heading:** `Explore Our Offerings` (Bold sans-serif, `24px`).
- **Paragraph:** *"Whether you're a beginner or an experienced practitioner, our community is here to support your journey."*

### 5.2 Community Image Card

- **Floating Glassmorphic Overlays:**
  - **Top-left tag:** `● Community` badge.
  - **Bottom overlay box:** Frosted glass panel featuring bold white text `>240` and subtext `Join our comunity now and strat with Us`.
  - **Pagination dots:** Two small white indicator dots at the bottom-left corner of the card.
