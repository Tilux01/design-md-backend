# Design Specification: Dark-Themed Music App UI

## 1. Global Theme & Foundations

### 1.1 Color Palette
* **Base Background**: `#101216` to `#16191E` (Dark charcoal with subtle vertical gradient).
* **Card Surface**: `#1D2128` with soft inner border highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
* **Primary Accent**: `#4FE3C3` (Vibrant Mint / Cyan).
* **Secondary Accent**: `#FF6B50` (Subtle warm glow / highlight).
* **Text Primary**: `#FFFFFF` (Solid White).
* **Text Muted**: `#7E8793` (Cool gray).
* **Icon Inactive**: `#606975`.

### 1.2 Typography
* **Primary Font**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `SF Pro Display`.
* **Fallback Stack**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
* **Scale**:
  * Large Title: 28px, Bold (Weight 700).
  * Section Title: 18px, Semi-Bold (Weight 600).
  * Body: 13px, Regular (Weight 400).
  * Caption/Metadata: 11px, Regular (Weight 400).

---

## 2. Screen 1: Onboarding / Splash

### Visual Layout (Full-Screen)
* **Background**: Solid deep black/dark charcoal gradient (`#101216`).

### Center Artwork
* **Image Asset**: Matte-black over-ear headphones.
  * **Format**: Backgroundless PNG/3D render.
  * **Placement**: Centered horizontally and vertically in the upper 60% of the viewport.
  * **Rotation**: `0deg` (Neutral front-facing view).
* **Glow/Waves**: Horizontal neon soundwave visualizer effect directly behind the headphones, glowing with faint teal and amber tints at the peaks and troughs.

### Bottom Content
* **Heading**: `"Playme."`
  * Text style: 28px, Bold, `#FFFFFF`.
  * Position: Left-aligned, `32px` horizontal padding.
* **Description**: Multi-line copy (~12px, `#7E8793`, line-height `1.5`):
  * *"We're the second most popular place to listen to podcasts in the world - and growing fast."*
* **Indicator**: Bottom drag/home indicator line (Width `120px`, Height `4px`, `#2D323B`, centered).

---

## 3. Screen 2: Discovery / Home

### Header
* **Height**: `56px`, horizontal padding `20px`.
* **Left**: Menu icon (4-dot grid, `#FFFFFF`).
* **Center**: Dropdown pill (`"Podcasts"`, 13px bold, trailing down-chevron).
* **Right**: Search icon (Magnifying glass, `#FFFFFF`).

### Category Switcher
* **Tabs**: `"Beauty"`, `"Music"`, `"Design"`.
* **Active State (`"Music"`)**: 16px, Bold, `#FFFFFF`, centered.
* **Inactive States**: 14px, Regular, `#7E8793`.

### Featured Carousel
* **Center Card**:
  * Dimensions: `240px × 260px`, corner radius `24px`.
  * Artwork: Wide-angle concert stage view with teal/cyan ambient lights and crowd silhouettes.
  * Bottom floating bar: Rounded dark pill container (`#1D2128/90` with blur).
    * Left: Title `"Live On"` (12px, White) and artist label (10px, Gray).
    * Right: Circular play button (`#4FE3C3`, 28px diameter, dark play arrow).
* **Side Cards**: Partially visible adjacent carousel cards (`16px` peek on left and right) with dark overlay masking.

### Genre Section
* **Title**: `"Genre"` (16px, Semi-Bold, `#FFFFFF`, left-aligned).
* **Grid**: 3 horizontal cards (`100px × 100px`, corner radius `18px`):
  1. **Dance**: Ocean/cyan abstract liquid imagery.
  2. **Techno**: Silhouette of a DJ framed by an intense orange/red stage light.
  3. **Rock**: Live stage silhouette with muted warm lighting.
* **Labels**: Centered at card bottom in 11px Semi-Bold white text.

### Floating Navigation Bar
* **Container**: Suspended pill dock (`Height: 60px`, `Radius: 30px`, Background `#1A1D23`, inner border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`).
* **Icons (4)**:
  * Home (Active: Solid rounded pill background `#FFFFFF`, dark icon).
  * Favorites (Outline heart, `#606975`).
  * Discover (Compass/Disc icon, `#606975`).
  * Profile (Outline user avatar, `#606975`).

---

## 4. Screen 3: Now Playing

### Top Navigation Bar
* **Left**: Down-chevron / back button (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Center**: Label `"Playing from..."` with small circular indicator (11px, `#7E8793`).
* **Right**: More options icon (Vertical 3-dots, `#FFFFFF`).

### Main Artwork
* **Container**: Centered square/rounded card (`Width: 85%`, `Border-Radius: 28px`, `Aspect-Ratio: 1:1`).
* **Image**: High-energy concert hall with warm red/orange ceiling truss spotlights focused on a central stage with crowd silhouettes. No tilt/rotation.

### Track Details
* **Title**: `"Space Station"` (20px, Bold, `#FFFFFF`).
* **Artist**: Artist name (13px, Regular, `#7E8793`).
* **Favorite**: Heart icon on the far right, colored `#4FE3C3`.

### Playback Scrubber
* **Track**: Thin bar (`3px`, Background `#262A32`).
* **Progress**: Filled portion in `#4FE3C3` up to circular drag handle (`10px` diameter).
* **Timestamps**: Left current time and right remaining time in 10px `#7E8793`.

### Playback Controls
* **Primary Play/Pause Button**:
  * Shape: Circle (`56px` diameter).
  * Color: Solid `#4FE3C3`.
  * Icon: Dark Pause bars (`#101216`).
* **Secondary Controls**:
  * Previous / Next: White skip chevrons (`#FFFFFF`, `20px`).
  * Left/Right Utilities: Shuffle and Repeat icons (`#606975`, `16px`).
