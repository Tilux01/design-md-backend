# Design Specification: 3D Animated Character Concept

## 1. Visual Overview & Layout
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (Vertical Format)
- **Background:** Pure solid white (`#FFFFFF`), full screen, minimalist.
- **Composition:** Centered portrait layout consisting of two primary visual blocks:
  1. **Header Text ("PROMPT")** positioned in the upper center.
  2. **Main Subject (3D Avatar)** centered horizontally, taking up the middle-to-lower third of the frame.
- **Watermark (Top Left):** "KSV.DESIGN" in a small, clean sans-serif font.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Header Text ("PROMPT")
- **Font Family:** Geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., *Helvetica Neue*, *Futura*, *Inter*, or *Montserrat*).
- **Weight:** Bold to Extra-Bold (700–800).
- **Case:** Uppercase.
- **Letter Spacing (Tracking):** Wide, generous kerning characteristic of modern editorial and UI design.
- **Color:** Deep black (`#000000`).
- **Fallback Font Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

### Watermark ("KSV.DESIGN")
- **Font Family:** Clean Sans-Serif.
- **Weight:** Medium (500).
- **Case:** Uppercase.
- **Color:** Dark charcoal/black (`#111111`).

### Footer Prompt Text (Bottom)
- **Font Family:** Clean Sans-Serif (matching the header/watermark style).
- **Weight:** Regular (400).
- **Case:** Sentence case / Paragraph format.
- **Color:** Dark grey/black.
- **Alignment:** Centered beneath the avatar.

---

## 3. Main Subject: 3D Character Avatar

### Aesthetic & Quality
- **Style:** Pixar-quality digital 3D art, smooth matte-clay textures, minimalist character design, clean subsurface scattering on skin.
- **Key Proforma Feature (Stylistic Exaggeration):** "No necks" design philosophy—the head flows directly and seamlessly into the upper torso/shoulders, creating a unified pillar-like upper body silhouette.

### Facial Features & Expression
- **Expression:** Extremely joyful, serene, closed-eye smiling expression.
- **Eyes:** Simple curved, closed happy arcs (`^ ^`).
- **Eyebrows:** Rounded, thick brown stylized brows positioned high above the eyes.
- **Nose:** Large, prominent, rounded spherical 3D nose.
- **Mouth:** Wide, thin, upturned curved smile spanning the lower face.
- **Head Shape:** Vertically elongated, pill-shaped head with smooth, soft features and blushed pink cheeks.
- **Ears:** Simple, rounded cartoon ears positioned low on the head.

### Hair
- **Style:** Volumetric, neatly styled, textured brown hair with defined comb/strand lines and slight upward volume on top.
- **Color:** Warm medium brown with subtle highlights.

### Clothing
- **Garment:** Simple white crew-neck t-shirt or textured sweatshirt with a tight collar.
- **Color:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle fabric micro-texture to catch the studio lighting.

---

## 4. Lighting & Color Palette

- **Lighting Style:** Soft studio 3-point lighting. Even, shadowless front fill with subtle rim lighting to separate the character from the pure white background.
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Background:** `#FFFFFF` (White)
  - **Skin Tone:** Warm peach/beige (`#F4D0B6` range with subsurface warmth)
  - **Hair:** Rich brown (`#8D5B3C`)
  - **Clothing:** White (`#FFFFFF`)

---

## 5. Animation Specifications (If Animated)

If rendered as a motion graphic or short looping video:
- **Character Idle Motion:** Subtle, organic breathing animation—slight vertical scaling of the torso and gentle bouncing/sway of the voluminous hair.
- **Micro-expressions:** The smile subtly widens and contracts in a rhythmic, comforting loop.
- **Text Animation:** The header "PROMPT" remains statically locked in place, while the avatar has a soft, floating center-weighted stabilization.
