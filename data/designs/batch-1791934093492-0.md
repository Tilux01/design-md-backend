# Design Specification: "Visual Poetry" Portfolio Landing Page

## 1. Overview
A minimalist, editorial portfolio landing page designed for full-viewport desktop displays (`100vw` × `100vh`). It uses a split-screen hero layout pairing oversized typography with an asymmetrical rounded media card.

---

## 2. Color Palette

| Token | Hex Value | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Canvas Background** | `#F5F4F0` | Main application background (warm off-white) |
| **Accent Mustard** | `#F9B338` | Feature card and active badge background |
| **Primary Black** | `#111111` | Primary headings, icons, CTA buttons |
| **Secondary Gray** | `#6B6B6B` | Body text, stat subheadings |
| **Card White** | `#FFFFFF` | Signature script, globe icon line art |
| **Border Gray** | `#D4D3CE` | Outlines on social badges |

---

## 3. Typography

### Primary Display (`visual poetry`)
* **Font Family**: *Clash Display*, *Syne*, or *Cabinet Grotesk* (Fallback: `Arial Black`, `Impact`, `sans-serif`)
* **Weight**: 800 (Extrabold)
* **Transform**: Lowercase
* **Letter Spacing**: `-0.05em`
* **Line Height**: `0.88`

### UI & Body Sans
* **Font Family**: *Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, or *Neue Haas Grotesk* (Fallback: `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`)
* **Weights**: 400 (Regular), 600 (Semi-bold), 700 (Bold)

### Decorative Signature
* **Font Family**: *Brittany Signature* or custom monoline vector script (Fallback: `cursive`)
* **Color**: `#FFFFFF` at 85% opacity.

---

## 4. Grid & Layout Structure

* **Root**: Full-screen grid (`100vw`, `100vh`), `overflow: hidden`, padding: `32px 48px`.
* **Top Navigation Bar**: Horizontal flexbox (`justify-between`), height `60px`.
* **Hero Content Area**: 2-column flex/grid (`1fr 1fr`), gap: `48px`, taking up remaining vertical space.

---

## 5. Header Component

* **Left (Logo)**: Black geometric glyph (solid circle, half circle, chevron).
* **Center (Nav Menu)**: Horizontal list (`gap: 32px`). Text size `13px`, medium weight.
  * Links: `Home`, `About`, `Portfolio`, `Exhibitions`, `Contact`.
* **Right (Event Badge)**:
  * Pill container: Yellow dot (`+`), text `"Sala Canal, 22 Nov 23"`, followed by a 24×24px thumbnail preview of an artwork.

---

## 6. Left Column: Editorial & Stats

### Heading & Subtitle Group
* **Main Title**: Two-line text block:
  ```text
  visual
  poetry
  ```
  * Font size: `~7.5vw` (~110px).
* **Mission Subtext**: Located to the right/below the title break.
  * Copy: *"Welcome to a visual journey that transcends time and space. Discover the artistry of moments captured in motion"*
  * Max-width: `260px`, text size: `12px`, color: `#6B6B6B`, line-height: `1.4`.

### Social Row
* Horizontal cluster of 4 circular stroke pills (`height: 32px`, `width: 32px`, `border: 1px solid #111111`):
  * Labels: `yt`, `ig`, `fb`, `x` (11px, regular).

### Key Performance Indicators (Bottom)
Two-column metric block aligned at the bottom:
* **Stat 1**:
  * Heading: `+250k` (Bold sans, ~32px, tracking tight).
  * Label: *"Videos that reaching a wide audience and give lasting impression"* (11px, muted).
* **Stat 2**:
  * Heading: `+800k` (Bold sans, ~32px, tracking tight).
  * Label: *"Hours watched, engaging storytelling that captivates viewers"* (11px, muted).

---

## 7. Right Column: Media Card & Controls

### Main Media Container
* **Geometry**: Rounded rectangle (`border-radius: 48px`).
* **Background**: Solid `#F9B338`.
* **Drop Shadow**: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(249, 179, 56, 0.35)`.
* **Top-Right Corner**: 40px circular black badge featuring a white wireframe globe icon.
* **Top-Left/Center**: White monoline handwritten signature overlay.

### Main Image Asset
* **Description**: Young Caucasian male with short textured brown hair in profile facing left. Wearing a black fitted turtleneck and holding a vintage Super-8 / 8mm film camera up to his eye with both hands. A camera strap hangs down his shoulder.
* **Format**: Cutout with transparent background (or integrated seamlessly on the `#F9B338` background).
* **Placement**: Anchored to the bottom-right inside edge of the yellow card, scaling slightly over the bottom border.

### Floating Floating Overlay Sidebar (Left Card Edge)
Vertically stacked controls overlapping the left border of the yellow card:
1. **Top Thumbnail**: Small rounded pill previewing hands holding a camera.
2. **Middle Thumbnail**: Small circular avatar previewing an artist portrait.
3. **Bottom CTA**: 48px solid black circle containing a white 45° arrow icon pointing top-right (`↗`).
