    *   Font-weight: Medium (`500`)
    *   Text-transform: Uppercase

    *   Font-size: `18px` - `22px`

## 5. Animations & Interactions

To achieve the fluid, high-end feel of the reference design, implement the following CSS/JS animations:

1.  **Floating Ingredients Animation (`@keyframes float`):**
    *   The mochi pieces and edamame floating above the central cup should continuously bob up and down asynchronously using CSS keyframes.
    *   *Duration:* `3s - 4s`, `ease-in-out infinite`.

2.  **Glow Pulse Effect:**
    *   The radial mint-white glow behind the central cup should subtly pulse in opacity (`0.2` to `0.35`) to give a magical/seasonal emphasis.

3.  **Carousel Slide Transition:**
    *   When clicking the left/right arrows, trigger a smooth horizontal translate and scale transition (`transition: all 0.5s cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`):
        *   Active center cup scales down, moves to the side, and dims.
        *   Incoming side cup scales up to center, brightens, and gains the glow effect.

4.  **Hover States:**
    *   Navbar links: Subtle brightness shift to pure white.
    *   Buy button: Background fill transitions from transparent to solid white with dark green text on hover.
    *   Arrows: Opacity increases from `0.6` to `1.0` with a slight scale-up (`1.05x`).
