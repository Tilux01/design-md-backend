# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Ultra-Minimalist Monochromatic Dark Mode / Generative 3D Engineering Abstract.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High negative space ratio with absolute focus centered on the parametric 3D structure; zero distracting outer clutter.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Deep abyssal black background `#0A0A0A` layered with subtle linear metallic gradients and volumetric high-contrast lighting simulating chrome-finished physical elements.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Parametric repeating cylindrical arrays, volumetric metallic lighting gradients, pitch-black negative space framing.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `4px 12px`, Font-size: `11px`, Font-weight: `600`, Tracking: `0.08em`, Border-radius: `9999px`, Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;`.
