# Design Specification: Avant-Garde Editorial Fashion Landing Page (`lab.`)

---

## 1. Overview & Creative Direction

A high-fashion, avant-garde streetwear/techwear editorial landing page blending **Swiss International Typographic Style** with **minimalist neo-brutalist interaction design**. 

- **Canvas Mode**: Fullscreen viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), borderless edge-to-edge, fixed/clipped layout (no default browser scrollbars).
- **Core Theme**: Utilitarian, sterile laboratory aesthetic, contrast between neutral clay/monochrome tones and high-chroma safety orange (`#FF4D24`).

## 4. Visual Assets & Imagery Requirements

### Asset 1: Hero Foreground Subject (Black Latex Glove Hand)
- **Type**: Cutout transparent PNG / WebP (Zero background).
- **Subject**: A human hand wearing a seamless, snug, matte/semi-gloss black medical or chemical latex glove.
- **Pose & Placement**:
  - Pointed vertically upward from the wrist at bottom-center.
  - Index finger extended upright and slightly curved.
  - Middle, ring, and little fingers curled in toward the palm.
  - Thumb positioned outward towards the left.
- **Lighting & Special Details**: 
  - Neutral cool studio rim-lighting from the left/top.
  - Crucial visual accent: A distinct **crimson/red reflected ambient light bounce** casting on the underside of the inner knuckles/curled fingers.
- **Dimensions & Origin**: Width `~340px - 380px`, anchored firmly to the bottom margin (`bottom: 0px`, `left: 46% - 50%`, horizontally centered or shifted slightly left of center).

### Asset 2: Background 3D Clay Spheres
- **Type**: Soft-shaded, matte white/chalk 3D geometric balls with realistic ambient occlusion and diffuse shadows matching background light from top-left.
- **Pair A (Upper-Left Composition)**:
  - Base large sphere: `~360px` diameter, centered at `top: 15%`, `left: 22%`.
  - Nested smaller sphere: `~120px` diameter, overlapping its top-left perimeter (`top: 18%`, `left: 17%`).
- **Pair B (Lower-Right Composition)**:
  - Base medium sphere: `~220px` diameter, centered at `bottom: 12%`, `right: 12%`.
  - Nested smaller sphere: `~100px` diameter, resting to its left side (`bottom: 18%`, `right: 23%`).

### Asset 3: Exhibition Thumbnail Card
- **Type**: Composite inline image card (`width: 140px`, `height: 58px`).
- **Left section**: Square crop of a techwear model wearing a tactical ninja balaclava, hooded cowl, with visible eyes looking forward.
- **Right section**: Solid safety orange box containing a pure white minimal play icon (filled triangle in a circle).

## 8. Micro-Interactions & Hover States

1. **Navigation Links (`PROJECTS`, `SHOP`, `NEW ARRIVALS`)**:
   - `transition: color 0.2s ease;`
   - Hover state: `#FF4D24`.
2. **Exhibition Card**:
   - Hover on Play button: scale up `1.08`, cursor `pointer`.
3. **Card `EXPLORE >` Button**:
   - Background transitions from `#0D0D0D` to `#262626` with arrow translating `2px` right on hover.
4. **Bottom Right CTA (`↓`)**:
   - Background transitions to `#E53D15`; arrow moves down `3px` on hover via `transform: translateY(3px); transition: transform 0.2s ease;`.
5. **Pagination Dots**:
   - Cursor `pointer`. Inactive dots expand to `5px` and turn `#0D0D0D` on hover.
