# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean 3D Scientific Minimalist / Modern SaaS.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio focusing attention on centered 3D sculptural elements against absolute minimalist white canvas.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Monochromatic flat-to-volumetric depth rendered via realistic soft-lighting gradients and ambient occlusion shadows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient soft lighting, clean minimal padding, structural geometry.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(255,255,255,0.8)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`
