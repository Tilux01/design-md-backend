# UI Design Specification & Layout Blueprint

This specification details a pixel-perfect clone of the provided mobile application screens. The layout represents a modern, dark-themed UI (likely for a bar, nightlife event, beverage menu, or cocktail guide app). 

*Note: As per instructions, device frames, drop shadows behind the phones, and surrounding mockup backgrounds are omitted. The layout is rendered full-screen.*

---

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

### Color Palette
* **Primary Background:** Deep Navy Blue/Black (`#0B1320` or `#0e1626`)
* **Surface/Card Dark:** Darker Charcoal/Blue (`#141E30` to `#101824`)
* **Primary Accent (Glow/Highlight):** Golden Yellow / Amber (`#F5A623` / `#FFC107`)
* **Secondary Accent (Vibrant):** Crimson Red (`#D32F2F`)
* **Text Primary:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
* **Text Secondary:** Muted Slate/Gray (`#8A99AD`)

### Typography & Fonts
* **Primary Heading Font:** A high-contrast, modern geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., *Montserrat*, *Poppins*, or *Helvetica Neue* for fallback). Bold, tracked-out for titles.
* **Body Font:** Clean, readable Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter* or *Roboto*).
* **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Montserrat', 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif;`

---

## 2. Screen 1: Detail / Product View (Left Mockup)

### Layout Structure
* **Container:** Vertical scrolling layout or fixed flex column with vertical padding.
* **Header / Top Navigation:**
  * Left: Back arrow icon (white, thin stroke).
  * Right: Share or Bookmark icon.
* **Hero Image Section:**
  * **Image Description:** A vibrant, close-up photograph of a tall cocktail/beer glass filled with a golden liquid and topped with a generous, frothy white foam head. A lime wedge or garnish rests on the rim. 
  * **Properties:** Backgroundless product cutout style (or seamlessly blended into the dark background). Positioned in the upper-right quadrant of the screen content area, tilted slightly with a dynamic angle (approx. 15-degree clockwise rotation).
* **Text Content Block:**
  * **Title:** Bold, white, medium-large heading. Text implies a beverage name (e.g., "The Juice" or similar stylized title).
  * **Description Paragraph:** Multi-line body copy in muted gray (`#8A99AD`), describing ingredients, flavor profiles, or background information about the drink.
* **Sub-Section / Carousel ("SIMILAR"):**
  * **Section Header:** Small uppercase tracking text ("SIMILAR") in white/gray.
  * **Card Row:** A horizontal scrollable row containing 3 distinct product/drink cards.
    * **Card Style:** Dark rounded rectangles with photographic previews.
    * **Card 1 (Left):** Dark blue hue, silhouette of a dark bottle/glass.
    * **Card 2 (Middle):** Warm amber/orange lighting, showing a glass or bottle with glowing liquid contents.
    * **Card 3 (Right):** Deep red/crimson lighting, showing a cocktail with red ingredients (berries/cranberry).

---

## 3. Screen 2: Event / Menu / Hub View (Right Mockup)

### Layout Structure
* **Container:** Centered vertical stack layout with heavy ambient visual styling.
* **Background Texture:** A dark, moody mosaic or geometric abstract pattern composed of muted gray/charcoal polygons/shapes layered over the primary navy background.
* **Top Navigation:**
  * Left: Menu hamburger icon.
  * Right: Notification bell or profile icon.
* **Central Hero / Logo Mark:**
  * **Image Description:** A stylized, minimalist line-art icon centered near the top-middle of the screen (resembling an archway, building facade, or cocktail glass outline) rendered in a glowing white/silver gradient stroke.
* **Typography Block:**
  * **Sub-header / Event Type:** Small uppercase text (e.g., "LIVE" or category label).
  * **Main Title:** Bold, multi-line title stacked centrally (e.g., "EVENING NIGHT CLUB" or similar venue/event branding). High contrast, crisp white typography.
  * **Details/Metadata:** Secondary text indicating time, location, or pricing details.
* **Call-to-Action (CTA) Button:**
  * **Shape:** Pill-shaped or rounded rectangle button with an outlined border (white stroke) and transparent or semi-transparent dark fill.
  * **Text:** Centered uppercase action text (e.g., "BOOK NOW" or "VIEW MENU").

---

## 4. Implementation Guidelines for Exact Match

1. **Images:** Replace placeholders with high-resolution, professionally shot product photography matching the lighting (warm golden highlights and deep red accents against dark moody tones). Ensure images have soft drop shadows or transparent edges to blend into the `#0B1320` background.
2. **Spacing & Padding:** Maintain generous whitespace around content cards and consistent margins (default 16px–24px horizontal padding).
3. **Effects:** Apply subtle CSS box-shadows and backdrop filters (`blur`) to cards to give depth against the patterned background on Screen 2.
