# Design Specification: Astral Media Interface

## 1. General Theme & Layout
*   **Background:** The entire application uses a deep charcoal/black background (`#0D0D0D`). The main visual interest is provided by the high-contrast movie imagery.
*   **Layout Style:** A full-screen, immersive horizontal dashboard. 
*   **Grid:** A split-screen approach where the left 60% is dedicated to the "Hero" movie, and the right 40% contains a scrollable carousel of upcoming titles.
*   **Padding/Margins:** Global container padding of `40px` on all sides.

## 2. Typography
The design uses a clean, geometric Sans-Serif typeface.
*   **Primary Font:** `Montserrat` or `Inter`.
*   **Secondary/Fallback Font:** `System-ui`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Logo ("Astral"):** Bold, 24px, White.
    *   **Hero Number ("02"):** Extra Light/Thin, 120px, White.
    *   **Hero Title ("Kong: Skull Island"):** Bold, 48px, White, Line-height 1.1.
    *   **Section Labels ("Today", "Tomorrow"):** Medium, 18px, White.
    *   **Metadata (Genre, Times):** Regular, 14px, Light Gray (`#A0A0A0`).
    *   **Button Text:** Bold, 14px, Uppercase/Sentence case.

## 3. Top Navigation Bar
*   **Logo (Left):** "Astral" text with a stylized "A" consisting of three slanted lines.
*   **Filter (Left-Center):** A pill-shaped button labeled "All movies" with a small clock icon. Border: `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.3)`.
*   **Location (Right-Center):** Text "Zhytomyr, Global" with a downward chevron.
*   **User Profile (Right):**
    *   Circular avatar (approx. 32px).
    *   User name "Ivan" in small text.
    *   Search Icon (Magnifying glass).
    *   Hamburger Menu Icon (Three horizontal lines of varying lengths).

## 4. Hero Section (Left Side)
*   **Background Image:** 
    *   **Description:** A hyper-detailed, extreme close-up of a giant gorilla's (Kong) face. The focus is on the orange-glowing eye and the texture of the dark fur. In the upper right of this section, a military Huey helicopter is flying toward the right, slightly tilted.
    *   **Background Status:** The image should be full-bleed but masked/faded into the black background on the right and bottom edges to ensure text legibility.
*   **Content Stack:**
    *   **Timeline Indicator:** A vertical thin white line starting from a dot next to the word "Today".
    *   **Rating:** Four solid yellow stars and one hollow star.
    *   **Metadata:** "Genre: Action, Adventure, Fantasy" followed by small boxed badges for "IMAX 3D" and "PG-13".
    *   **Session Schedule:** 
        *   Label: "Session schedule:" in small caps.
        *   Time Slots: Pill-shaped outlines (`1px solid #444`) containing times (15:20, 17:30, 19:40, 20:50, 23:15).
    *   **Action Buttons:**
        *   **Book a Session:** Solid Golden Yellow (`#F2C94C`) background, black text, includes a small ticket icon.
        *   **More:** Transparent background, white border, includes a "Play" triangle icon.

## 5. Movie Carousel (Right Side)
*   **Timeline Indicator:** A vertical thin white line labeled "Tomorrow".
*   **Navigation Arrows:** Two circular buttons (Left/Right) located at the bottom center of the screen to scroll the carousel. The "Right" arrow has a progress ring around it.
*   **Movie Cards:**
    1.  **Card 04 ("Fury"):** 
        *   *Image:* Two men in long coats (Fantastic Beasts style) standing in a misty blue/grey environment.
        *   *Label:* Small "04" at the top, "fury" in lowercase at the bottom.
    2.  **Card 05 ("Passenger"):** 
        *   *Image:* Two profiles of actors (Chris Pratt and Jennifer Lawrence) facing each other closely. Blue/teal lighting.
        *   *Label:* Small "05" at the top, "Passenger" at the bottom.
    3.  **Card 01 ("Eadly Labyrinth"):** 
        *   *Image:* Close up of a woman's face (Zoe Kravitz style) with puzzle-piece cutouts missing from her skin, revealing a dark void.
        *   *Label:* Small "01" at the top, "Eadly Labyrinth" at the bottom.
    4.  **Card 02 (Partial):** 
        *   *Image:* A fiery orange explosion/landscape.
        *   *Label:* Small "02" at the top.

## 6. Detailed Image Asset Descriptions
*   **Hero Image (Kong):** High-resolution, dark fur textures, warm orange lighting in the eye. The helicopter is a separate element or part of the composition, placed at the top right of the Kong face, rotated about 15 degrees nose-down.
*   **Poster 1 (Fury):** Vertical portrait, cool color temperature, foggy/mystical atmosphere.
*   **Poster 2 (Passenger):** Vertical portrait, high contrast, cinematic teal and orange color grading.
*   **Poster 3 (Eadly Labyrinth):** Vertical portrait, surrealist/horror vibe. The face is centered, looking directly at the viewer.
*   **Note on Backgrounds:** All poster images are rectangular with slightly rounded corners (approx. 4px) and no external borders.

## 7. Interaction States
*   **Time Slots:** Hover state should fill the pill with white and change text to black.
*   **Carousel Cards:** The active card (centered) should be slightly larger or have a higher brightness than the inactive cards.
*   **Buttons:** The yellow "Book" button should have a slight drop shadow or glow effect.
