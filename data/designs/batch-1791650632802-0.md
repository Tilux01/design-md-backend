# UI/UX Specification: "OUN" AI Product Image Generator Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
- **View:** Full-screen viewport experience (`width: 100vw`, `height: 100vh`).
- **Background:** Deep obsidian black (`#0A0A0A` or `rgb(10, 10, 10)`) framing a central glassmorphism/dark UI canvas.
- **Atmospheric Layer:** The UI container floats amidst hyper-realistic, dark volcanic rock formations with sharp edges and subtle warm rim lighting (amber/orange gradients on the rock contours). 

---

## 2. Typography Strategy & Fallbacks
- **Primary Typeface:** Modern, geometric, futuristic sans-serif (e.g., *Syne*, *Space Grotesk*, or *Inter* with extended letter-spacing).
- **Secondary/Body Typeface:** Clean minimalist sans-serif (*Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*).
- **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Space Grotesk', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;`

---

## 3. Header / Navigation Bar
- **Positioning:** Fixed at the top inner boundary of the dark container (`padding: 24px 40px`).
- **Left Element:** Minimalist geometric circular logo mark (nested rings/target style icon).
- **Center Elements (Pill Navigation):**
  - Pill-shaped container with a subtle dark glass border (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, `border-radius: 999px`, `padding: 4px`).
  - Active Tab: **"CREATE"** (Solid white pill background, black bold text).
  - Inactive Tab: **"EXPLORE"** (Transparent background, muted gray text).
- **Right Element:** Text label `"SMART EDITING TOOLS"` in small, uppercase, tracked-out sans-serif font (approx. 10px, tracking: 0.15em, color: `#888`).

---

## 4. Hero Section & Core Elements

### A. Central Typography & Branding (`OUN`)
- **Main Wordmark:** Large, bold, stylized geometric lettering spelling **"OUN"** integrated with image masks and negative space.
  - **Letter "O":** An octagonal/rounded geometric frame filled with negative space, featuring a copyright symbol `©` tucked into the bottom-right corner.
  - **Letter "U":** A sharp, angular U-shape cutout masking background content.
  - **Letter "N":** Integrated geometric structure overlapping interactive image cards.
- **Subtitle / Tagline (Bottom Right of Wordmark):**
  - Text: *"Instantly Generate Stunning Product Images with AI"*
  - Font Size: 14px / Line-height: 1.4
  - Color: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a secondary version tag `"Version 1"` aligned underneath in muted gray (`#666666`).

### B. Floating Image Cards & Product Previews
The interface features layered, floating interactive cards intersecting the typography:
1. **Primary Product Card (Top Left):**
   - **Shape:** Rounded squircle with smooth corners (`border-radius: 24px`).
   - **Content Description:** A high-end cosmetic jar (white cylindrical cream container with metallic cap) shown floating at a dynamic 45-degree angle. The jar is set against a dramatic backdrop of dark, glowing volcanic rock and fiery amber light streaks.
   - **Background State:** Backgroundless/integrated directly into the scene lighting.
2. **Secondary Preview Cards (Center/Right):**
   - Smaller, overlapping image cards embedded within the letterforms and interface flow.
   - **Content Description:** Abstract luxury perfume bottle and glowing amber cosmetic packaging scenes rendered with cinematic studio lighting against dark, rocky environments.

### C. Floating Micro-Labels & Feature Pointers
- **"Instant Image Creation"** (Left margin, aligned vertically with subtle dashed connector lines).
- **"Bring product concepts..."** (Mid-left, pointing toward image containers).
- **"Brand & Marketing Tools"** (Top right, positioned above CTA).

### D. Primary Call-to-Action (CTA)
- **Position:** Upper right, below the "Brand & Marketing Tools" label.
- **Button Style:** Pill-shaped button (`background: #FFFFFF`, `color: #000000`, `border-radius: 999px`, `padding: 10px 20px`, `font-weight: 600`).
- **Label & Icon:** `"GET STARTED WITH OUN AI"` followed by a right-pointing arrow icon (`→`).

---

## 5. Footer / Bottom Toolbar
- **Position:** Bottom-left inside the main UI container.
- **Left Element:** Circular badge/icon containing a star or sparkle symbol next to the label **"CREATIVITY"**.
- **Pill Filters:** A series of horizontal pill tags with thin borders (`border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`, `border-radius: 999px`, `padding: 6px 14px`, `font-size: 11px`, `color: #CCCCCC`):
  - Tag 1: `✦ AI-Powered Perfection`
  - Tag 2: `QUALITY`
  - Tag 3: `Endless Customization`

---

## 6. Lighting, Depth & FX Specifications
- **Shadows & Elevation:** Deep drop shadows (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`) applied to floating cards to create realistic separation from the background.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Dominant: Obsidian Black (`#0A0A0A`)
  - Accent: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Highlights: Warm Amber/Orange (`#FF8C00` to `#FF4500` gradients simulating volcanic glow)
  - Neutral Grays: `#888888`, `#333333`
