# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist E-Commerce (Clean, Monochrome, High-End Product Focus).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with generous padding around cards and typography sections, establishing clear visual hierarchy.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, minimalist card architecture relying on very subtle light gray surface differences (`#F7F7F7` against `#FAFAFA`) with rounded corner containment (`16px`/`24px`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-contrast dark pill buttons, generous card padding, monochrome grayscale palettes with exact product isolation.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#111111`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Arrow (→) -> Use `&rarr;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
