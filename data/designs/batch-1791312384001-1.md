# Design Specification: Nike Minimalist Editorial UI

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
* **Primary Mood:** High-fashion, avant-garde, ultra-minimalist, and cinematic.
* **Brand Archetype:** Premium, bold, disruptive lifestyle brand (Nike editorial concept).
* **Visual Density:** Extremely low density with asymmetric weight distribution; high reliance on negative space and editorial typography.
* **Whitespace Balance:** Over 65% of the primary viewport consists of intentional whitespace, focusing attention on typographic asymmetry and human portraiture.
* **Theme Balance:** Hybrid split layout. The left side is a pristine light canvas (`#FFFFFF`), while the right-hand panel utilizes dark structural containers (`#22252A` and vibrant accent `#FF453A`).

---

## 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

### Core Colors
* **Primary Brand / Action:** `#FF453A` (Vibrant Coral Red)
* **Primary Brand Variant (Hover):** `#FF5F56`
* **Primary Brand Variant (Active):** `#E0382E`
* **Background Light (Left Canvas):** `#FFFFFF`
* **Surface Dark (Right Sidebar Top):** `#22252A`
* **Surface Accent (Right Sidebar Bottom):** `#FF453A`
* **Base Backdrop (App Wrapper):** `#EAF0F4` (Soft gray-blue exterior presentation canvas)

### Text & Neutral Tokens
* **Text Primary (Dark on Light):** `#1A1A1A`
* **Text Secondary / Muted (Dark):** `#8E95A0`
* **Text Inverse (Light on Dark):** `#FFFFFF`
* **Border / Divider:** Transparent / Edge-to-edge split layout (No hard borders; separation achieved via color blocks and photographic contrast).

---

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy
* **Font Family (Headings & UI):** Inter / Helvetica Neue (Clean grotesque sans-serif)
* **Font Family (Editorial Numbers/Spacing):** Custom geometric sans with wide tracking.

| Level | Font Size | Weight | Line Height | Letter Spacing (Tracking) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hero Brand Wordmark** | `56px` | 300 (Light) | `1.1` | `0.4em` (Wide spaced grid: `N I` / `K E`) |
| **Navigation Items** | `10px` | 500 (Medium) | `1.0` | `0.3em` (`MENS`, `WOMENS`) |
| **Social / Footer Links**| `9px` | 400 (Regular) | `1.0` | `0.25em` (`Fb`, `In`, `Tw` - rotated vertical layout) |

---

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
* **Container Max-Width:** `1024px` fixed aspect ratio desktop viewport presentation.
* **Grid System:** 12-Column asymmetric split-screen layout.
  * Left White Canvas: Columns 1–6 (`50%` width)
  * Center Imagery Focus: Columns 5–9 (Overlapping hero portrait)
  * Right Action Sidebar: Columns 10–12 (`25%` width split into upper dark search module and lower red social module).
* **Spacing Scale:**
  * Outer Padding (Viewport margin): `64px` symmetric padding from outer container edge.
  * Internal Navigation Margin: `48px` top, `64px` left.
  * Brand Wordmark Position: Absolute / Flex bottom-left aligned (`64px` from bottom edge, `64px` from left edge).

---

## 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement

### Brand Header & Navigation
* **Nike Swoosh:** Placed at top-left (`x: 64px`, `y: 64px`). Color: `#1A1A1A`. Dimensions: `~24px × 10px`.
* **Nav Links (`MENS`, `WOMENS`):** Centered horizontally within the left white panel, uppercase, tracking-expanded, medium weight in dark slate (`#4A4D52`).

### Hero Section & Typography
* **The "NIKE" Wordmark:** Arranged in a striking 2x2 grid layout occupying the bottom-left quadrant:
  ```text
  N   I
  K   E
  ```
  * Inter-letter gap: `32px` horizontal, `24px` vertical.

### Interactive Elements & Action Triggers
* **Video Play Trigger (Floating Action Button):**
  * **Shape:** Perfect Circle (`border-radius: 50%`).
  * **Size:** `48px × 48px`.
  * **Background:** `#FF453A` with a soft, diffused radial drop shadow (`0px 12px 24px rgba(255, 69, 58, 0.4)`).
  * **Icon:** White play triangle centered inside (`14px`).
  * **Placement:** Intersecting the boundary between the white canvas and the portrait image (`x: 476px`, `y: 50%` vertical alignment offset).

### Right Action Sidebar
* **Top Module (Dark Surface - `#22252A`):**
  * Contains Search Icon (`🔍`) centered horizontally and vertically (`16px`, color: `#FFFFFF`).
* **Bottom Module (Red Surface - `#FF453A`):**
  * Contains vertical text labels (`Fb`, `In`, `Tw`) rotated or stacked vertically, bottom-aligned in crisp white (`#FFFFFF`).

---

## 6. Motion Mechanics & Interaction Spec
* **Hover State (Play Button):** 
  * Scale transform: `scale(1.08)` with a smooth cubic-bezier curve.
  * Box-shadow expansion: Increases blur radius to `32px` with elevated opacity (`0.6`).
* **Hover State (Nav Links):**
  * Color transition from `#4A4D52` to `#1A1A1A` with a `150ms` ease-out duration.
* **Page Transition / Entrance Animation:**
  * Staggered fade-in: Left white canvas slides in from left (`X: -20px` to `0`), image scales up softly from `98%` to `100%` with a `600ms` duration using `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (Apple/Vercel-style fluid ease).
