# Design Specification: DENTYTECH Hero Section

## 1. Overview & Viewport Setup
* **Canvas**: Fullscreen viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), `overflow: hidden`.
* **Background**: Solid white (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Layout Model**: Flexbox column header/footer with absolute/relative centered hero staging.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Primary Background**: `#FFFFFF`
* **Text / Primary Dark**: `#0A0A0A`
* **Accent Orange**: `#FF5528` (Sign Up button, logo icon, bullet accent)
* **Subtle Layer Blues**: `#EAF2F8`, `#DCE9F5`, `#CEE2F5` (stacked card silhouettes)
* **Borders / Outlines**: `#E5E5E5` (light badges), `#0A0A0A` (buttons, icons)
* **Secondary Text**: `#555555`

---

## 3. Typography
* **Hero Headline Font ("OUR A   TAGES")**:
  * *Primary*: `Clash Display`, `Syne`, or `Cabinet Grotesk` (Light/Regular, 300–400 weight).
  * *Fallback*: `Century Gothic`, `Futura`, `sans-serif`.
  * *Styling*: Geometric, high circularity on 'O', wide letter-spacing (`0.05em`), uppercase, font-size ~`11vw`.
* **UI & Body Text ("DENTYTECH", "Menu", "Log In", etc.)**:
  * *Primary*: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`.
  * *Fallback*: `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `sans-serif`.
  * *Weights*: 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium).

---

## 4. Header Navigation
* **Container**: Fixed/Absolute top, `width: 100%`, `padding: 28px 48px`, flex row, `align-items: center`, `justify-content: space-between`.
* **Left – Menu Pill**:
  * Capsule button (`border: 1px solid #0A0A0A`, height `40px`, border-radius `9999px`).
  * Left segment: "Menu" text.
  * Right segment: Vertical divider line followed by a 2-line hamburger icon inside a circular enclosure.
* **Center – Logo**:
  * Icon: Coral-orange circle (`#FF5528`, ~`24px`) with a minimalist white line-art tooth inside.
  * Brand Name: `DENTYTECH`, all caps, font-size `13px`, medium weight, letter-spacing `0.08em`.
* **Right – Auth Controls**:
  * Container: Pill container with thin border (`#E5E5E5`).
  * "Log In": Flat text button, dark text.
  * "Sign Up": Pill button, background `#FF5528`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded-full.

---

## 5. Hero Central Composition

### 5.1. Headline Text ("OUR ADVANTAGES")
* Layered directly behind the central image card.
* **Left Block**: "OUR A" positioned horizontally to the left of the image center.
* **Right Block**: "TAGES" positioned horizontally to the right of the image center.
* Both vertically centered to intersect the card.

### 5.2. Layered Graphic Cards (Background Stack)
* 3–4 stacked rounded rectangles behind the main photo:
  * Dimensions: ~`360px × 460px`, `border-radius: 40px`.
  * Colors: Soft pastel blues (`#EBF3FA`, `#DCEDFC`, `#C9E2F7`).
  * Rotation: Tilted counter-clockwise between `-8deg` and `-14deg` with slight horizontal leftward offsets.

### 5.3. Main Image Card (Foreground)
* **Dimensions**: Width ~`340px`, Height ~`440px`, `border-radius: 36px`, `overflow: hidden`, `z-index: 2`.
* **Image Description**:
  * Portrait of a smiling Caucasian brunette woman with bangs, eyes closed in joy, hands raised near her cheeks/chin.
  * Wearing a plain white T-shirt.
  * Background: Studio neutral light-grey (not transparent, fully contained within the rounded rect).
  * Rotation: `0deg` (straight vertical).

### 5.4. Floating Pill Badge
* **Position**: Overlapping bottom edge of the photo card, centered horizontally, `z-index: 3`.
* **Style**: White background, light border (`#E5E5E5`), shadow (`0 4px 14px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`), border-radius `9999px`, padding `8px 20px`.
* **Content**:
  * Icon: Thin floral/gear star outline icon.
  * Label: "Advanced Technology" (font-size `13px`, medium weight).

---

## 6. Footer Section
* **Container**: Fixed/Absolute bottom, `width: 100%`, `padding: 24px 48px`, flex row, `align-items: flex-end`, `justify-content: space-between`.
* **Bottom-Left**:
  * Orange accent dot: `#FF5528`, ~`5px` circle.
  * Text: "Your Smile, Our Passion" (`12px`, color `#0A0A0A`).
* **Bottom-Center**:
  * Semi-circle/arch wireframe button touching the bottom viewport line:
    * Shape: `width: 70px`, `height: 35px`, border: `1px solid #0A0A0A`, `border-radius: 70px 70px 0 0`, `border-bottom: none`.
    * Content: Centered downward arrow (`↓`).
* **Bottom-Right**:
  * Top: Row of 3 line-art social icons (Instagram camera outline, YouTube play triangle inside rounded square, Facebook 'f' inside rounded square).
  * Bottom: Text "Best Start Up of 2023" (`12px`, `#0A0A0A`).
