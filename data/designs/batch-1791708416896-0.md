# Design Specification: GrowthRevolution Homepage Clone

This document provides a precise, pixel-accurate design blueprint for cloning the provided web page interface. 

---

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Settings

*   **View:** Full-screen viewport (`width: 100vw`, `height: 100vh`, or flex container spanning 100% of the screen width).
*   **Background Color:** Soft off-white / light gray (`#F0F0F0` or `#EFEFEF`).
*   **Main Container:** Centered white/light-grey card/section with smooth, rounded corners (approx. `border-radius: 24px`), generous internal padding, and subtle depth.
*   **Grid System:** Asymmetric 2-column structural layout below the hero banner (Left: Title & CTA; Right: Descriptive text and interactive cards).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Font Family
*   **Primary Choice:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** or **Inter** (Clean, modern sans-serif with geometric neutrality).
*   **Fallback Font Stack:** `sans-serif`, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto.

### Text Styles & Hierarchy

| Element | Font Weight | Font Size | Line Height | Color / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Top Nav Brand ("GrowthRevolution")** | Medium / Semi-Bold (`500/600`) | 14px – 16px | Normal | Dark charcoal (`#111111`) with a small 4-petal flower icon. |
| **Meta Labels ("Project of the Year", "From 2016")** | Regular (`400`) | 12px – 13px | Tight | Muted gray (`#666666`), uppercase tracking or sentence case. |
| **Main Heading ("Pioneering Success")** | Bold / Heavy (`700`) | 56px – 72px | Tight (`1.05`) | Deep dark charcoal (`#111111`), tight letter-spacing. |
| **Button Text ("Join The Revolution")** | Medium (`500`) | 13px – 14px | Normal | White (`#FFFFFF`) inside a pill-shaped dark button. |
| **Body Text ("At GrowthRevolution...")** | Regular (`400`) | 14px – 15px | 1.4 | Dark gray (`#333333`). |
| **Metric Value ("2k+")** | Bold (`700`) | 28px – 32px | Tight | Deep charcoal (`#111111`). |
| **Metric Label ("Of happy clients...")** | Regular (`400`) | 12px – 13px | Tight | Dark gray (`#444444`). |
| **Curved Marquee Text ("Igniting Business Growth...")** | Medium (`500`) | 12px – 13px | Normal | Dark charcoal, tracked along an SVG curved path. |

---

## 3. Detailed Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation & Top Bar
*   **Logo Area:** Centered or left-aligned group containing a small 4-petal dark flower icon followed by the text "GrowthRevolution".
*   **Hero Meta Row:** 
    *   Left: `"Project of the Year"`
    *   Center: `"From 2016"`
    *   Right: Hamburger / Menu icon (`=`) positioned on the far right.

### B. Hero Banner Component
*   **Container:** Large full-width banner with rounded corners (`border-radius: 16px`) and overflow hidden.
*   **Background Photography:** A black-and-white (monochrome) lifestyle image depicting people in the water/sea (surf/swimming context), viewed from a low angle. 
*   **Curved Text Overlays (SVG Paths):**
    *   Two arches of text following curved vector paths overlaying the image:
        *   *Left Curve:* Reads `"Igniting Business Growth, Leading the Revolutio..."` following a gentle upward/downward arc bounded by a semi-transparent light purple/lavender pill background shape (`rgba(200, 190, 230, 0.4)`).
        *   *Right Curve:* Reads `"igniting Business Growth..."` wrapped along the top-right corner.

### C. Left Column: Main Call-to-Action
*   **Pill Button:** `"Join The Revolution"`
    *   Background: Dark charcoal / Black (`#111111`).
    *   Text color: White (`#FFFFFF`).
    *   Shape: Fully rounded pill (`border-radius: 50px`).
*   **Hero Typography:**
    *   Two-line stacked heading:
        *   Line 1: `Pioneering`
        *   Line 2: `Success`
    *   Extremely large display font size with tight vertical tracking.

### D. Center/Right Elements: Supporting Cards
*   **Descriptive Paragraph:** 
    *   *"At GrowthRevolution, we're not just another business consulting firm; we're catalysts for change, architects of innovation, and drivers of growth."*
*   **Decorative Flower Icon:** A solid black 4-petal abstract flower / star symbol (`✦` style) positioned between the main title and the right-side cards.
*   **Image Card (Left of bottom right stack):**
    *   Monochrome receipt/document snippet graphic showing financial data or invoice details, angled or vertically oriented.
*   **Avatar & Metric Block (Right side):**
    *   **Row of Circular Avatars:** Overlapping monochrome headshots of 4 team members / clients in small circular frames.
    *   **Metric Box (Light Purple Background):**
        *   Background: Soft pastel lavender/purple (`#DCD6EE`).
        *   Contains the large bold text `"2k+"` accompanied by a globe/network icon.
        *   Subtext below: `"Of happy clients and businesses"`.
        *   **Action Link ("Explore more"):** Includes a diagonal arrow icon (`↗`) inside a small circular button.

---

## 4. Images & Asset Specifications

1.  **Hero Background Image:**
    *   *Description:* Monochrome, high-contrast, wide-angle cinematic shot of individuals in the ocean/sea. 
    *   *Treatment:* Grayscale filter, high clarity, moody atmosphere.
2.  **Document/Receipt Graphic:**
    *   *Description:* Vertical grayscale snippet of financial charts, tax/VAT details, and printed data columns. Backgroundless / clean cut-out aesthetic.
3.  **Client/Team Avatars:**
    *   *Description:* 4 circular monochrome portraits of diverse professionals looking directly at the camera.
4.  **Icons:**
    *   *Flower/Star Logo Icon:* Solid black 4-petal geometric flower.
    *   *Arrow Icon:* Minimalist thin-stroke diagonal arrow pointing top-right (`↗`).
