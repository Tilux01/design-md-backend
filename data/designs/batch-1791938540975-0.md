# Design Specification: Loop.tv Interface Clone

## 1. Global Styles & Environment
*   **Background:** The entire application sits on a full-screen background image.
    *   **Description:** A high-contrast, dark-toned photo of a large crowd at a concert, facing a stage. 
    *   **Processing:** Apply a heavy Gaussian blur (approx. 20px) and a dark blue/purple tint overlay (`rgba(10, 12, 31, 0.8)`) to ensure text legibility.
*   **Layout:** Full-screen, non-scrolling (fixed viewport) or long-scroll depending on implementation. The screenshot represents the "Home" dashboard view.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** A geometric, rounded sans-serif. 
    *   **Recommended:** *Comfortaa* or *Quicksand* (Google Fonts).
    *   **Fallback:** `system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif`.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Logo:** Bold, lowercase, white.
    *   **Nav Links:** Medium weight, lowercase, white.
    *   **Section Headers:** Bold, lowercase, white, size approx 24px.
    *   **Metadata:** Regular weight, white, size approx 14px-16px.

## 3. Color Palette
*   **Primary White:** `#FFFFFF` (Text, Icons, Borders).
*   **Active State Background:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` (Used for the "home" pill).
*   **Nav Bar Background:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` with a backdrop-filter blur.
*   **Shadows:** Soft black glows around cards to create depth against the dark background.

## 4. Component Breakdown

### A. Header Navigation
*   **Logo (Left):** "loop.tv" text. The "loop" is bold; the ".tv" is slightly thinner.
*   **Nav Pill (Center):** 
    *   A horizontal container with a pill shape (fully rounded corners).
    *   Background: Semi-transparent dark grey/blue.
    *   **Items:** "home", "signage", "rewards", "search", and a [Gear/Settings Icon].
    *   **Active State:** The "home" item has a lighter grey rounded-rectangle background.
*   **Menu Toggle (Right):**
    *   Text: "toggle menu" (lowercase).
    *   Icon: A circle containing three horizontal lines (hamburger menu).

### B. Main Carousel (Featured Content)
This section consists of three cards with a focus on the center.
*   **Center Card (Active):**
    *   **Border:** 4px solid white, rounded corners (approx 30px radius).
    *   **Image:** A music video still featuring a man (Blake Shelton) and a woman (Gwen Stefani) sitting in a diner booth, drinking from cups. Warm, cinematic lighting.
    *   **Overlay (Bottom Left):**
        *   **Thumbnail:** Small square image of Blake Shelton.
        *   **Text Block:** 
            *   "WARNER RECORDS" (Small, uppercase, greyish-white).
            *   "Nobody But You" (Large, bold, white).
            *   "Blake Shelton - (Duet with Gwen Stefani)" (Medium, white).
    *   **Overlay (Bottom Right):** "loop.tv" logo in white.
*   **Side Cards (Inactive):**
    *   **Left Card:** Partially visible. Image of a band in a recording studio (Bruno Mars style). No border.
    *   **Right Card:** Partially visible. Image of a large outdoor concert stage at sunset with scaffolding visible. No border.

### C. "Trending Channels" Section
*   **Header:** "trending channels" text, left-aligned, lowercase.
*   **Grid:** A horizontal row of cards with rounded corners (approx 15px radius).
*   **Image Descriptions (Left to Right):**
    1.  **Pool Table:** A close-up, high-angle shot of a yellow pool table with a rack of billiard balls.
    2.  **Flamingo:** A pink plastic lawn flamingo standing in front of a grey fence and green shrubbery.
    3.  **Stage Light:** A black and white, low-angle shot of a single spotlight shining down on a microphone stand on a stage.
    4.  **Concert Heart:** A silhouette of hands forming a heart shape against a bright, orange-lit concert crowd.

## 5. Image Asset Specifications
*   **Backgroundless Elements:** None. All images are rectangular crops within containers.
*   **Aspect Ratios:**
    *   Main Carousel: 16:9.
    *   Trending Channels: 16:9.
*   **Rotations:** All elements are at 0-degree rotation. The layout relies on a clean, horizontal grid.

## 6. Detailed Styling Notes
*   **Transparency:** Use `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)` on the navigation pill and any overlays to give a "glassmorphism" effect common in Smart TV interfaces.
*   **Spacing:** 
    *   Main card is significantly larger than side cards to create a focal point.
    *   The gap between the header and the carousel is roughly 40px.
    *   The gap between the carousel and the "trending channels" label is roughly 60px.
*   **Transitions:** If implemented as a functional UI, moving between nav items or cards should use a smooth ease-in-out transition (approx 300ms).
