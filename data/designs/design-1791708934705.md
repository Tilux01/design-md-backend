# Aura Store - Design Specification

## Overview
A minimalist, brutalist-inspired e-commerce landing page with a monochromatic color palette, heavy grain texture, and futuristic typography. The design emphasizes streetwear aesthetics with high-contrast bold typography layered over a central editorial photograph.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas

*   **View:** Full-screen responsive viewport (100vw × 100vh), removing any mockup framing or rounded border containers.
*   **Background Color:** Off-white / cream (`#F7F5F0` or `#FAF8F5`).
*   **Texture:** Subtle high-frequency film grain overlay across the entire canvas to give it a tactile, grunge/editorial feel.
*   **Borders & Lines:** Thin 1px solid dark strokes (`#111111`) used for minimal geometric framing (e.g., around the secondary product card on the bottom right).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Display Font (Headers / Navigation / Accents)
*   **Font Family:** Geometric/futuristic sans-serif with rounded terminals and wide tracking (e.g., **"Space Mono"**, **"Chakra Petch"**, or **"Syne"**).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `monospace`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Usage:** Navigation links (`main`, `shop`, `custom`, `about`, `contact`), account ID, brand logo, and call-to-action buttons.

### Body / Subtitle Font
*   **Font Family:** Clean, technical sans-serif (e.g., **"Inter"**, **"JetBrains Mono"**, or **"Helvetica Neue"**).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `sans-serif`.
*   **Usage:** Paragraph descriptions and manifesto text.

---

## 3. Header / Navigation Bar

Positioned absolute at the top of the viewport with generous padding (`24px` to `40px` from top and sides).

*   **Left Element (Brand Logo):**
    *   Text: `"Aura"` (bold, stacked above `"store"`).
    *   Icon: A cluster of 4 small 4-pointed stars/crosses (`✦ ✦ ✦ ✦`) positioned immediately to the right of the brand name.
*   **Center Navigation Links:**
    *   Items: `main`, `shop`, `custom`, `about`, `contact`.
    *   Styling: Lowercase, wide letter-spacing, muted gray-to-black color (`#444444`). Active/hover state transitions to pure black (`#000000`).
*   **Right Element (Account ID):**
    *   Text: `"accaunt id - 003.201"` (Note: Retain the intentional misspelling `"accaunt"` as seen in the source).
    *   Styling: Monospace, small font size, uppercase/lowercase mixed as displayed.

---

## 4. Hero Section & Central Model Image

### Central Hero Image (Editorial Cutout)
*   **Subject:** A full-body studio portrait of a model wearing head-to-toe black technical streetwear.
    *   Head/Face: Completely covered by a dark, textured mesh/fabric balaclava mask.
    *   Torso: Layered dark charcoal/black distressed zip-up jacket over a grey hoodie.
    *   Legs: Black cargo pants with ribbed knee panels and multiple tactical straps/pockets.
*   **Background Status:** **Backgroundless / Transparent cutout** seamlessly integrated into the off-white canvas.
*   **Position & Scale:** Vertically centered, spanning from the bottom edge up to the middle of the navigation bar.

### Big Typography Behind/Beside the Model
*   **Text:** `"Aura"` (left of the model) and `"store"` (right of the model).
*   **Styling:** 
    *   Extremely large, heavy, bold sans-serif display font.
    *   Text features a heavy noise/stipple texture inside the letterforms.
    *   Positioned directly behind or framing the torso of the model.

---

## 5. Left Column Content (Brand Manifesto)

Positioned in the lower-left quadrant of the screen.

*   **Recycling / Eco Icon:** 
    *   A black recycling/eco symbol (Japanese/Universal recycling mark inside a square outline).
*   **Main Headline:**
    *   Text: `"Clothes without excess. Only style."`
    *   Styling: Bold, heavy black sans-serif, prominent font size.
*   **Body Paragraph:**
    *   Text: `"Modern silhouettes, natural fabrics, and honest design. For those who choose simplicity and quality."`
    *   Styling: Regular weight, technical sans-serif, muted dark gray tone, optimized for readability with a max-width constraint (~320px).

---

## 6. Right Column Content (Product Card & CTA)

Positioned in the lower-right quadrant of the screen inside a minimal card container.

*   **Product Card Container:**
    *   Thin rounded border (`1px solid #111111`, border-radius: `12px`).
    *   Padded internal layout.
*   **Product Image Inside Card:**
    *   Subject: A black puffer jacket featuring a striking vibrant pink/magenta anatomical skeleton graphic across the chest and back.
    *   Background Status: **Backgroundless cutout** on a neutral light grey surface inside the thumbnail frame.
*   **Card Footer Text:**
    *   Text: `"2025"` centered underneath the product image.
*   **Call-to-Action Button ("New Collection"):**
    *   Positioned directly below the product card.
    *   Pill-shaped outline button (`1px solid #111111`, border-radius: `50px`, transparent background).
    *   Text: `"new collection ↗"` (includes an angled top-right arrow icon).
    *   Hover effect: Inverts colors (solid black background with white text).
