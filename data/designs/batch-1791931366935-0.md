# UI/UX Specification Document: "N.JEY - FUTURE" Landing Page

---

## 1. Overview & Canvas Setup

* **Platform:** Full-screen desktop web application / responsive web experience.
* **Canvas Dimensions:** Full viewport (`100vw` × `100vh`), edge-to-edge layout, strictly omitting any external device mockup shells, browser chromes, or background workspace mats.
* **Aesthetic:** Dark cyberpunk, minimalist sci-fi, premium futuristic luxury tech aesthetic.
* **Theme & Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian dark mode with high-contrast monochrome elements, subtle blue backplate accents, volumetric reflections, and fine technical HUD (Heads-Up Display) wirelines.

## 3. Typography System

### 3.1 Display & Brand Fonts
* **Primary Futuristic Display (Logo & "FUTURE" Title):**
  * **Target Font:** *Syncopate* (Bold / ExtraBold), *Orbitron* (Bold), or *Michroma*.
  * **Stylized Glpyh Modification:** The word **"FUTURE"** utilizes horizontal stencil-style segmented cuts:
    * The letter **E** features detached horizontal bars.
    * The letter **F** has a sharp geometric top bar with a disconnected crossbar.
  * **Fallback Stack:** `"Michroma", "Syncopate", "Orbitron", "Eurostile", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`.

### 3.2 UI, Navigation & Body Fonts
* **Secondary Font (Body Text, Menu, Kicker, HUD Labels):**
  * **Target Font:** *Space Grotesk*, *Rajdhani*, or *Inter* (Medium / Regular).
  * **Fallback Stack:** `"Space Grotesk", "Inter", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`.

## 4. Layout & Grid Architecture

* **Vertical Architecture Grid:**
  * The full screen is divided into 5 equal vertical columns separated by ultra-fine `1px` lines at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
  * Grid lines extend seamlessly from `top: 0` to `bottom: 0`.
* **Left Margin Rail:** Width: `60px` – reserved for pagination indicators and vertical guide marks.
* **Right Margin Rail:** Width: `60px` – reserved for social link vertical stack.
* **Z-Index Layering:**
  1. `z-index: 0` – Background color & vertical architectural grid lines.
  2. `z-index: 10` – 3D Cyborg visual asset (anchored right/center).
  3. `z-index: 15` – HUD leader lines, callout text, and bottom glass cards.
  4. `z-index: 20` – Main hero text, electric blue box accent, CTA button.
  5. `z-index: 30` – Top fixed header and side rails.

## 5. Visual Asset Specifications (3D Cyborg Model)

* **Asset Type:** Transparent cut-out (Alpha channel PNG or WebP) 3D bust rendering.
* **Subject Description:**
  * Highly detailed, sleek sci-fi cyborg / android bust (head, neck, collarbone, and upper chest).
  * Smooth, glossy obsidian-black carbon-fiber and polished polycarbonate armor plates.
  * Headpiece: Streamlined helmet with a forward-slanted, high-gloss smoked visor. Visor features a subtle, glowing internal edge/circuitry trace.
  * Neck & Torso: Articulated ergonomic musculature composed of synthetic carbon-weave fibers, chrome joints, and glossy black lacquered plates.
* **Pose & Camera Orientation:**
  * **Placement:** Anchored on the right half of the canvas (occupying `50%` to `90%` of screen width, centered vertically).
  * **Rotation & Angle:** Three-quarter profile, facing towards the left (profile angled at ~45 degrees leftward and tilted slightly downward at ~5 degrees).
  * **Cropping:** Bottom of the bust fades smoothly or is clipped behind the bottom glass cards; top of the helmet sits cleanly below the top navigation line.
* **Lighting & Shading:**
  * Studio rim lighting along the back of the helmet and neck contours.
  * Crisp specular highlights bouncing off the curved glossy visor and shoulder plates.
  * Absolute shadow detail blending organically into `#08090C`.

## 7. Interactive States & Micro-Interactions

* **Cursor:** Custom futuristic crosshair or minimal circular pointer (`border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.4)` with center dot).
* **Links & Navigation Items:** Smooth transition to `color: #FFFFFF` and subtle text-shadow glow (`0 0 8px rgba(255,255,255,0.6)`).
* **Parallax Effect:**
  * 3D Cyborg asset responds to mouse coordinates with subtle 3D rotational tilt (`rotateY: ±3deg`, `rotateX: ±2deg`).
  * Background vertical grid lines and HUD annotations carry subtle depth differential (layer offset of `5px` to `12px`).
* **CTA Button Hover:**
  * Border color shifts from muted gray to bright cobalt `#1A47FF`.
  * Text tracking expands slightly (`letter-spacing: 0.3em`).
