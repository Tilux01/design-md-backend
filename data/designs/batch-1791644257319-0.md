# Design Specification: "The Three Graces" Editorial Exhibition Page

---

## 1. Overview & Aesthetic Mood

- **Concept**: A minimalist, high-end, dark-mode museum / art gallery digital editorial experience dedicated to Antonio Canova’s Neoclassical sculpture *The Three Graces*.
- **Visual Style**: Chiaroscuro aesthetic (extreme contrast between light and dark), Renaissance/Neoclassical fine art fusion with modern minimalist typography.
- **Tone**: Elegant, quiet, monumental, theatrical, timeless.
- **Viewport Target**: Fullscreen (`100vw` × `100vh`), non-scrolling single-screen hero composition (or fixed initial viewport on responsive devices).

## 3. Typography & Hierarchy

### 3.1 Font Families & Fallback Stacks

1. **Title Serif (High-Contrast Neoclassical Didone)**
   - **Primary Font**: `Didot`, `Bodoni MT`, `Cormorant Garamond`, or `Playfair Display`
   - **Fallback Stack**: `Didot, "Bodoni MT", "Cinzel", "Cormorant Garamond", "Times New Roman", serif`
   - **Characteristics**: Extreme contrast between thick stems and razor-thin hairlines; refined neoclassical look.

2. **Cursive / Classical Italic Serif (Title Prefix)**
   - **Primary Font**: `Cormorant Garamond (Italic)`, `Playfair Display (Italic)`, or `Baskerville (Italic)`
   - **Fallback Stack**: `"Cormorant Garamond", "Playfair Display", "Baskerville", Georgia, serif`
   - **Style**: Genuine calligraphic italic, low-to-medium stroke weight.

3. **Sans-Serif (Navigation & Body Text)**
   - **Primary Font**: `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, or `Arial`
   - **Fallback Stack**: `"Inter", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`
   - **Style**: Clean, geometric neo-grotesque, ultra-readable at small scales.

### 5.2 Central Artwork (The Sculpture)

#### Detailed Visual Description
- **Subject**: Antonio Canova's marble masterwork *The Three Graces* (*Euphrosyne, Aglaea, and Thalia*).
- **Format**: Monochromatic (Black & White), high-contrast chiaroscuro photography.
- **Lighting**: Dramatic single-source side-key lighting from the upper right, casting strong, luminous white specular highlights across the shoulders, faces, and entwined hands of the figures while letting the deep contours and left flank fall into total pitch-black shadow.
- **Composition & Pose**:
  - Three nude mythological female figures embrace one another.
  - The rightmost figure is captured in near profile/three-quarter back view, leaning tenderly inward with her arm reaching around her companion.
  - The central figure stands slightly taller, her head turned delicately in half-profile.
  - The left figure is submerged in deeper shadow, creating depth and intimacy.
- **Background Treatment**: **Completely backgroundless/seamless black fade.** The image has no hard rectangular edges; its perimeter dissolves softly via an alpha radial gradient mask (`radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 45%, black 40%, transparent 75%)`) seamlessly melting into the `#000000` page background.
- **Scale & Alignment**:
  - Width: Approx. `48vw` (max `620px`).
  - Height: Approx. `55vh` (max `520px`).
  - Horizontal Alignment: Dead center (`50%`).
  - Vertical Offset: Centered vertically in the upper two-thirds of the screen, with the lower torso of the statues gracefully disappearing behind the title typography.

---

### 5.3 Floating Circular Accent (Orb)
- **Visual Description**: A crisp, small, solid moon-like spherical circle.
- **Size**: Diameter `18px` × `18px`.
- **Color**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` with a very soft subtle outer drop-shadow (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`).
- **Positioning**:
  - Located to the right side of the sculpture group.
  - Aligned roughly with the eye/ear level of the right-most Grace figure.
  - Offset approximately `180px` to `200px` to the right of the screen's horizontal center line, and `38vh` from the top of the viewport.
- **Function**: Visual anchor / interactive balance point indicating exhibition spatial or focal exploration.

### 5.5 Historical Body Caption
- **Text Content**:
  > *"Antonio Canova's statue The Three Graces is a Neoclassical sculpture, in marble, of the mythological three charites, daughters of Zeus - identified on some engravings of the statue as, from left to right, Euphrosyne, Aglaea and Thalia - who were said to represent youth/beauty (Thalia), mirth (Euphrosyne), and elegance"*
- **Layout & Constraints**:
  - Max Width: `660px` to `700px`.
  - Alignment: Center-aligned (`text-align: center`).
  - Margin: Centered horizontally (`margin: 0 auto`).
  - Margin Bottom: `36px` to `44px` padding from the viewport bottom edge.
  - Line-height: `1.75`.
  - Word balance: `text-wrap: balance`.

## 7. Interactive & Animation States (Recommended)

1. **Navigation Links**:
   - Idle: Color `#888888`, transition: `color 0.3s ease`.
   - Hover: Color `#FFFFFF`.
   - Active: Indicator line has smooth sliding transform (`transition: transform 0.4s cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`).

2. **Sculpture Presence**:
   - Subtle Ken-Burns or parallax effect: Very slow breathing scale on load (`scale(1.0)` to `scale(1.03)` over 10s).

3. **Floating Orb**:
   - Pulse animation: Opacity oscillating gently between `0.85` and `1.0`, glow radius softly expanding between `8px` and `16px`.
   - Cursor: `pointer`.
