# Design Specification: Luscious Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Layout
* **Viewport**: Full-screen desktop landing page (`100vw`, `100vh`, `overflow: hidden`).
* **Layout**: Two-column asymmetric hero layout over a full-bleed textured background.
* **Background**: Off-white plaster/marble texture (`#F7F7F6`) covering the entire screen.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Primary Accent (Orange)**: `#E64A19` (CTA background, Login border & text)
* **Text Dark (Headings & Logo)**: `#1A1A1A`
* **Text Muted (Body copy)**: `#757575`
* **Text Nav**: `#333333`
* **Surface White**: `#FFFFFF`

---

## 3. Typography
* **Display / Headings (Serif)**:
  * Primary: *Playfair Display* (Weight: 700 / Bold)
  * Fallbacks: *Didot*, *Bodoni MT*, *Georgia*, serif
* **Sub-display ("Life is so endlessly delicious.")**:
  * Primary: *Playfair Display* (Weight: 600 / Semi-Bold)
  * Fallbacks: *Georgia*, serif
* **Body & Navigation (Sans-Serif)**:
  * Primary: *Inter* or *DM Sans* (Weight: 400 for body, 500 for navigation)
  * Fallbacks: *system-ui*, *-apple-system*, *Helvetica Neue*, sans-serif

---

## 4. Components & Content

### 4.1 Navigation Bar
* **Positioning**: Absolute or fixed at top (`top: 0`, `left: 0`, `width: 100%`, `padding: 32px 64px`).
* **Left**:
  * Logo text: `luscious` (lowercase, Bold Serif, `24px`, `#1A1A1A`).
* **Right**:
  * Menu links: `Dumplings`, `Recipes`, `Food Menu`, `Order Now` (Sans-serif, `14px`, `#333333`, horizontal spacing: `32px`).
  * "Login" Button: Pill-shaped (`border: 2px solid #E64A19`, `border-radius: 999px`, `color: #E64A19`, `padding: 8px 24px`, `font-size: 13px`).

### 4.2 Hero Left Content
* **Positioning**: Center-left (`left: 8%`, `top: 45%`, `transform: translateY(-50%)`, max-width `460px`).
* **Heading 1**: 
  * "Take a taste" / "Come join us." (`font-size: 52px`, `line-height: 1.1`, bold serif, `#1A1A1A`).
* **Sub-heading**:
  * "Life is so endlessly delicious." (`font-size: 24px`, serif, `margin-top: 16px`).
* **Body Text**:
  * "Dumpling is a broad classification for a dish that consists of pieces of dough made from a variety of starch sources wrapped around a filling, or of dough with no filling." (`font-size: 12px`, `line-height: 1.5`, color: `#757575`, `margin: 16px 0 28px 0`).
* **Primary Button**:
  * Label: "Explore Now"
  * Style: Pill-shaped (`background: #E64A19`, `color: #FFFFFF`, `padding: 14px 36px`, `border-radius: 999px`, `font-size: 14px`, subtle drop shadow).

---

## 5. Imagery & Asset Specifications

All decorative assets are isolated PNGs with transparent backgrounds and natural drop shadows.

### Top-Left Spice Spoons
* **Description**: Four to five wooden spoons filled with powdered spices (turmeric, paprika, curry, chili flakes) fanning inward.
* **Placement**: Anchored to top-left corner (`top: 0`, `left: 0`).
* **Rotation**: ~-25 degrees pointing diagonally towards the center.
* **Details**: Small scattered red chili flakes and black peppercorns directly underneath.

### Top-Right Fresh Ingredients
* **Description**: Fresh ripe red cherry tomatoes with green stems, red onion slices, and cilantro sprigs.
* **Placement**: Anchored to the top-right edge (`top: 8%`, `right: 0`).
* **Rotation**: Natural orientation, cropped slightly by the right window edge.

### Floating Circular Element
* **Description**: Plain white, soft-embossed circular UI disk with subtle diffused drop shadow.
* **Placement**: `top: 18%`, `right: 22%`, size: `54px × 54px`.

### Center-Right Main Dish (Dumplings)
* **Description**: Round, shallow wooden platter holding 4-5 steamed white Asian dumplings garnished with black sesame seeds. A pair of light bamboo chopsticks lies diagonally across the platter.
* **Placement**: Occupying the right half of the screen (`right: 5%`, `bottom: 8%`, width: ~`550px`).
* **Rotation**: Platter is flat; chopsticks angle diagonally at ~-40 degrees.

### Dipping Sauce Bowl
* **Description**: Small, deep white ceramic ramekin filled with red dipping sauce, garnished with fresh green basil leaves.
* **Placement**: Overlapping the lower-left edge of the dumpling plate (`left: -15%` relative to the wooden plate).
* **Details**: Loose black peppercorns scattered directly beneath and to the left of the bowl.
