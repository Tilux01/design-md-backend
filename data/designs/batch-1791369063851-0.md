# Design Specification: E-Commerce Homepage

## 1. Global Styles & Layout

*   **Layout Structure:** Full-screen, edge-to-edge responsiveness (no outer padding on the main viewport, inner containers centered with max-width restrictions).
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Background (Body):** `#FFFFFF` (White)
    *   **Text Primary:** `#1A1A1A` (Dark Charcoal / Almost Black)
    *   **Text Secondary / Subdued:** `#666666` (Medium Gray)
    *   **Accent / Highlights:** `#E5E5E5` (Light Gray for borders/dividers)
    *   **Promotional Banners Backgrounds:** Soft beige/neutral gradients matching the imagery.
    *   **Discount Badge Red:** `#C93B3B` (Muted Crimson)
    *   **New Badge Green:** `#2E7D32` (Forest Green)
*   **Typography:**
    *   **Font Family:** Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif.
    *   **Base Size:** 14px / 16px body.

---

## 2. Category Circles Navigation Section

*   **Container:** Horizontal flexbox/grid centered at the top, allowing scroll on smaller screens.
*   **Items (6 categories):** Men, Women, Shoes, Bags, Watches, Accessories.
*   **Component Structure (per item):**
    *   Circular image container (`border-radius: 50%`, light neutral background/shadow).
    *   Category Title: Bold, 14px, `color: #1A1A1A`.
    *   Item Count subtitle: Regular, 12px, `color: #666666` (e.g., "120+ Items").

---

## 3. Promotional Banners Section

*   **Layout:** Two-column grid layout (Side-by-side equal width banners).
*   **Left Banner (Summer Sale):**
    *   **Background:** Warm beige gradient with palm leaf background art watermark.
    *   **Sub-heading:** "SUMMER SALE" (Small, uppercase, tracked out).
    *   **Main Headline:** "Up to 50% Off" (Large, bold serif or sans-serif display).
    *   **Description:** "On selected items" (12px-14px).
    *   **CTA Button:** Dark pill/rounded rectangle button (`background: #1A1A1A`, `color: #FFFFFF`, text: "Shop Now").
*   **Right Banner (New Arrivals):**
    *   **Background:** Warm neutral gradient with sunglasses featured on the right side and palm leaf shadows.
    *   **Sub-heading:** "NEW ARRIVALS" (Small, uppercase).
    *   **Main Headline:** "Discover The Latest Trends" (Large bold display).
    *   **CTA Button:** Dark pill/rounded rectangle button (`background: #1A1A1A`, `color: #FFFFFF`, text: "Explore Now").

---

## 4. Trending Products Section

*   **Header Bar:**
    *   **Left:** "Trending Products" (Heading, bold, 20px+).
    *   **Right:** "View All →" link (`color: #666666` or `#1A1A1A`, hover underlined).
*   **Product Grid:** Horizontal scrollable or 5-column grid containing product cards.
*   **Product Card Structure:**
    *   **Background:** White card with soft border/shadow (`border: 1px solid #EAEAEA`, `border-radius: 8px`).
    *   **Badges (Absolute positioned):**
        *   Discount badge (e.g., `-20%` in top-left, red background).
        *   New badge (e.g., `New` in top-left, green text/bg).
    *   **Image Area:** Centered product photography on neutral podium/background.
    *   **Product Details:**
        *   **Title:** Regular/Medium weight, 14px (`color: #1A1A1A`).
        *   **Price:** Current price bold (`$49.99`), old price crossed out (`$60.00` in light gray).
        *   **Rating:** Star icons (5 yellow stars) followed by review count in parentheses (e.g., `(128)`).

---

## 5. Trust / Value Proposition Bar

*   **Background:** Very light beige/neutral bar (`background: #F7F5F0`).
*   **Layout:** 4 equal columns distributed horizontally.
*   **Items:**
    1.  **Icon + Free Shipping:** "Free Shipping" (Bold), "On orders over $50" (Subdued).
    2.  **Icon + 24/7 Support:** "24/7 Support" (Bold), "We are here to help" (Subdued).
    3.  **Icon + Secure Payment:** "Secure Payment" (Bold), "100% secure payment" (Subdued).
    4.  **Icon + Money Back:** "Money Back" (Bold), "30 days guarantee" (Subdued).

---

## 6. Brand Logos Footer Section

*   **Header:** "Trusted by 10,000+ Customers" centered with subtle horizontal divider lines on both sides.
*   **Layout:** Horizontal flexbox evenly spacing brand logos.
*   **Brands Displayed:**
    *   **NIKE** (Bold, uppercase sans-serif)
    *   **ZARA** (Tracked out elegant serif)
    *   **adidas** (Lowercase geometric)
    *   **FOSSIL** (Bold block letters)
    *   **PUMA** (Bold athletic typography)
    *   **CASIO** (Classic bold lettermark)
