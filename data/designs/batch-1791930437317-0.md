# Design Specification: MachinaFusion Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Layout Structure
* **Viewport**: Fullscreen (`100vw`, `100vh`), no scroll, relative positioning.
* **Layout Grid**: 3-tier vertical stack (Header, Central Hero Visual/Type, Footer) with edge-to-edge alignment and standard horizontal margins (`40px` to `60px`).
* **Base Theme**: Minimalist, clean tech aesthetic. White background (`#FFFFFF`) with high-contrast monochrome UI elements and soft central ambient lighting.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Primary Background**: `#FFFFFF`
* **Text / Core Elements**: `#111111` (Near Black)
* **Secondary Text / Borders**: `#757575` (Muted Gray), `#E5E5E5` (Light Border Gray)
* **Pill Container Fills**: `#F1F1F1`
* **Ambient Glow**: Radial gradient from `#F7EEDB` (soft beige/yellow) blending into `#FFFFFF` at `0%` opacity.

---

## 3. Typography
* **Primary Font Family**: `Inter`, `Geist Sans`, or `SF Pro Display`
* **Fallback Stack**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
* **Hierarchy**:
  * **Hero Title**: Bold (700), ~`72px`–`84px`, tight letter-spacing (`-0.03em`), color: `#111111`.
  * **Hero Subtext Layer**: Semi-bold (600), ~`56px`–`64px`, layered behind foreground elements.
  * **Nav / Buttons**: Medium (500), `13px`–`14px`.
  * **Footer Heading/Label**: Regular (400), `11px`, uppercase, tracking (`+0.05em`), `#757575`.
  * **Footer Description**: Regular (400), `14px`, line-height `1.4`, `#111111`.

---

## 4. Components & Layout

### 4.1 Header (Top Navigation)
* **Container**: `display: flex`, `justify-content: space-between`, `align-items: center`, `padding: 24px 40px`.
* **Left Section (Flex, gap: `16px`)**:
  * **Brand Logo**: Minimalist icon (three angled geometric bars) followed by text `MachinaFusion` (Font-weight: 600, `15px`).
  * **Menu Pill**: Black capsule (`#111111`), white text (`13px`), left white circular dot indicator.
  * **Nav Links Capsule**: Light gray pill (`#F1F1F1`), containing text links: `Cutting-edge R&D` and `Adaptive AI` (gap: `16px`, color: `#111111`, font-size: `13px`).
* **Right Section**:
  * **CTA Capsule**: Light gray pill (`#F1F1F1`), left dark circular icon with geometric glyph, followed by text `Scalable Solutions` (`13px`, `#111111`).

---

### 4.2 Hero Section (Center Composition)
* **Positioning**: Center of viewport, stacked via `z-index`.
* **Background Light Layer**:
  * Centered blurred radial gradient (approx. `450px × 450px`, blur: `80px`, warm cream/beige `#FCEFD8` to transparent).
* **Typography Layer** (`z-index: 2`):
  * Centered text block:
    * Line 1: `MachinaFusion`
    * Line 2: `[Intelli]gence that fe[els]` (semi-obscured by hands).
* **Visual Imagery Layer** ("Creation of Adam" pose):
  * **Human Hand Asset** (`z-index: 3`):
    * **Description**: Photorealistic left human arm and hand reaching horizontally inward towards the center-right.
    * **Format**: Backgroundless PNG.
    * **Placement**: Fixed to left edge (`left: 0`, `top: 48%`, `transform: translateY(-50%)`).
    * **Rotation**: `0deg` to slightly angled upwards (`~5deg`).
  * **Robotic Hand Asset** (`z-index: 3`):
    * **Description**: Futuristic mechanical/cyborg right arm and hand with white composite plating and exposed joint linkages, reaching horizontally inward towards center-left.
    * **Format**: Backgroundless PNG.
    * **Placement**: Fixed to right edge (`right: 0`, `top: 50%`, `transform: translateY(-50%)`).
    * **Rotation**: Slightly angled downwards (`~-5deg`).
    * **Interaction**: Index fingers extend toward each other near the center with a slight gap.

---

### 4.3 Footer Section
* **Container**: `position: absolute`, `bottom: 32px`, `left: 40px`, `right: 40px`, `display: flex`, `justify-content: space-between`, `align-items: flex-end`.
* **Left Column**:
  * Sub-label: `Precision Engineering` (`11px`, muted gray).
  * Description: `Bridging the gap between humanity\nand robotics for a smarter, more\nconnected future` (Max-width: `280px`).
* **Center**:
  * Minimal vertical scroll line divider (`1px` width, `24px` height, color: `#D1D1D1`).
* **Right Column**:
  * Horizontal tag group (`gap: 8px`).
  * Three pill badges: `Algorithms`, `AI`, `Robotics`.
  * Styling: Transparent background, `1px solid #D1D1D1`, rounded pill radius (`999px`), padding: `6px 14px`, font-size: `11px`, color: `#555555`.
