## 1. Global Design Tokens & Typography Foundation

### 1.1 Color Palette
The design uses an organic, modern, and minimalist aesthetic grounded in nature-inspired greens, warm cream/off-white surfaces, and deep charcoal text elements.

*   **Primary Backgrounds:**
    *   `Screen Canvas / Base:` `#F5F3EE` (Warm, creamy off-white)
    *   `Card / Surface White:` `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for elevated containers/cards)
*   **Primary Brand & Interactive Colors:**
    *   `Forest Green (Primary Button / Active State):` `#2E4631`
    *   `Dark Charcoal (Primary Typography):` `#1C1C1C`
    *   `Muted Gray Text (Secondary Descriptions):` `#686868`
    *   `Light Neutral Gray (Input Backgrounds / Subtle Borders):` `#EBE8E0`
*   **Status & Accent Colors:**
    *   `Rating Star Gold:` `#E5A93B`
    *   `Delete Icon / Alert Red (Subtle):` `#A94442` (with soft backgrounds)

### 1.2 Typography System & Fallback Strategy
The interface relies heavily on a high-contrast pairing of an **Editorial Serif** for headings and clean, highly legible **Geometric Sans-Serif** for UI elements, prices, and body copy.

*   **Primary Serif Font (Headings / Brand Names):**
    *   *Target:* `Playfair Display` or `Cormorant Garamond`
    *   *Fallback Stack:* `Georgia, Cambria, "Times New Roman", Times, serif`
*   **Primary Sans-Serif Font (Body, Navigation, Buttons, Prices):**
    *   *Target:* `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans`
    *   *Fallback Stack:* `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`

## 3. Design Asset & Implementation Notes for Developers

*   **Images:** All plant illustrations must be exported as **transparent PNGs (backgroundless)** to seamlessly blend into the `#F5F3EE` canvas and container backgrounds.
*   **Shadows / Elevation:** Use extremely soft, diffused shadows to maintain the modern minimalist aesthetic (`box-shadow: 0px 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`).
*   **Border Radiuses:** Maintain consistent rounding across cards (`16px` to `24px`) and buttons (`28px` for full pills, `12px` for standard inputs/containers).
