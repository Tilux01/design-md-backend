# Design Specification: ENJOVIE Streaming Interface (Attack on Titan)

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Background Color:** Deep Charcoal/Black (`#0a0a0a`).
*   **Primary Accent:** Vibrant Red (`#e50914`) used for the "Watch Now" button and logo mark.
*   **Secondary Accent:** Golden Yellow (`#f5c518`) used for the IMDb rating and star icon.
*   **Border Radius:** 
    *   Buttons: `20px` (Pill-shaped).
    *   Thumbnails: `8px`.
    *   Main Container: `24px` (if not full screen).
*   **Layout:** Full-screen responsive grid with a heavy emphasis on the hero background image.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Montserrat** or **Inter**.
*   **Secondary Font:** **Roboto** or **Open Sans**.
*   **Fallbacks:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`.

### Text Styles:
*   **Logo:** Montserrat Bold, `22px`, Uppercase, White.
*   **Nav Links:** Inter Medium, `14px`, White (Opacity 80%). Active link ("Animes") has a small red underline.
*   **Season Tag:** Inter Bold, `10px`, White, contained in a semi-transparent dark pill (`rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`).
*   **Main Title:** Montserrat Black (Extra Bold), `48px`, Uppercase, White, Letter spacing: `-1px`.
*   **Description:** Inter Regular, `16px`, White (Opacity 90%), Line-height: `1.5`.
*   **IMDb Score:** Montserrat Bold, `32px`, White. "Rating" label: Inter Bold, `14px`, White.

## 3. Navigation Bar (Top)
*   **Position:** Fixed top, `0` padding top/bottom `40px`, padding left/right `60px`.
*   **Logo (Left):** A solid red circle icon followed by the text "ENJOVIE".
*   **Menu (Center):** Horizontal list: Home, Movies, TV Shows, Animes, My List.
    *   *State:* "Animes" has a `2px` red border-bottom, `5px` below the text.
*   **Utilities (Right):** 
    *   Search Icon (Magnifying glass, white).
    *   Notification Icon (Bell, white).
    *   **Profile Image:** Circular crop, `40px` diameter. Image shows a young man with dark hair against a blue background.

## 4. Hero Section & Background
*   **Main Background Image:** 
    *   **Description:** A high-resolution, dramatic anime illustration. In the center-top, a colossal Titan with an open mouth and glowing eyes reaches forward. In the foreground (bottom-center), a character in a tan military trench coat (Falco/Reiner style) stands with their back to the viewer, looking toward the Titan. The setting is a destroyed European-style city at dusk/night.
    *   **Placement:** `background-size: cover; background-position: center top;`.
    *   **Overlays:** 
        1.  A dark radial gradient from the bottom-left (`rgba(0,0,0,0.9)`) fading toward the top-right to ensure text readability.
        2.  A subtle "noise" or "grain" texture overlay to match the cinematic feel.
*   **Watermark:** Large, low-opacity (5%) "TITAN" text behind the main content area, rotated 0 degrees but spanning the width.

## 5. Content Elements (Left Side)
*   **Season Badge:** Located above the title. Pill shape, dark grey background. Text: "4 Season".
*   **Title:** "ATTACK ON TITAN".
*   **Description Text:** Block of text roughly 4 lines long. 
*   **Rating Group:** 
    *   **Star:** Large 5-pointed yellow star icon to the left of the text.
    *   **IMDb Badge:** Small yellow rectangle with black "IMDb" text.
    *   **Score:** "9.0/10" in large white bold font.
*   **Button Row:**
    *   **Watch Now:** Red background, white text. Includes a "Play" triangle icon on the left.
    *   **Add to List:** Dark grey/semi-transparent background (`rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`). Includes a "+" icon on the left.

## 6. Sidebar Thumbnails (Right Side)
A vertical stack of three preview images, positioned on the right third of the screen.
*   **Thumbnail 1 (Top):** 
    *   **Image:** Silhouette of a character (Eren) standing in front of a burning city/sunset.
    *   **Icon:** Centered white "Play" button with a thin circular border.
*   **Thumbnail 2 (Middle):** 
    *   **Image:** Action shot of characters using ODM gear against a Titan's neck.
    *   **Icon:** Centered white "Play" button with a thin circular border.
*   **Thumbnail 3 (Bottom):** 
    *   **Image:** Close up of Levi Ackerman in mid-air combat.
    *   **Icon:** Centered white "Play" button with a thin circular border.

## 7. Image Asset Details
*   **Background Image:** Must be a **backgroundless** PNG for the character in the foreground if you want to apply parallax, otherwise a flat high-res JPEG. The character is standing straight, no rotation.
*   **Thumbnail Images:** Standard `16:9` aspect ratio. No rotation. 
*   **Profile Picture:** `1:1` aspect ratio, centered headshot.

## 8. Layout Spacing (Desktop)
*   **Container Padding:** `80px` from the left edge.
*   **Title to Description Gap:** `20px`.
*   **Description to Rating Gap:** `30px`.
*   **Rating to Buttons Gap:** `40px`.
*   **Right Sidebar Margin:** `60px` from the right edge.
*   **Thumbnail Vertical Gap:** `15px`.
