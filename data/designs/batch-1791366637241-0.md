# Luscious Dumplings - Full-Screen Web Design Specification

## Global Layout & Foundations
- **Layout Type:** Full-Screen Hero Section (Single-page view, 100vw × 100vh)
- **Background Texture:** Off-white mottled marble/stone texture (`#f7f7f7` base with subtle grey/white mineral veining)
- **Grid Structure:** Asymmetrical two-column layout
  - **Left Column:** Branding, navigation, primary typography, descriptive paragraph, and primary call-to-action (CTA).
  - **Right Column:** Floating culinary photography elements (spoons, bowl of dumplings, sauce, garnishes) layered over the stone background.

---

## 1. Navigation Header
- **Position:** Fixed top, spanning full width with padding (`padding: 24px 60px`).
- **Logo:**
  - **Text:** `luscious`
  - **Font Family:** Serif, lowercase, bold (`font-weight: 700`, `letter-spacing: -0.5px`)
  - **Color:** Dark charcoal (`#1a1a1a`)
- **Nav Links:**
  - **Items:** `Dumplings`, `Recipes`, `Food Menu`, `Order Now`
  - **Font Family:** Sans-serif, medium (`font-weight: 500`, `font-size: 14px`)
  - **Color:** Dark charcoal (`#2c2c2c`)
  - **Hover State:** Subtle color darken or underline.
- **Login Button:**
  - **Text:** `Login`
  - **Shape:** Pill button (fully rounded corners, border-radius: 50px)
  - **Border:** Thin burnt orange outline (`1px solid #ff5500`)
  - **Color:** Burnt orange (`#ff5500`)
  - **Font:** Sans-serif, medium (`font-size: 14px`, `font-weight: 600`)

---

  - **Text:**

## 3. Visual Assets & Floating Elements (Right Column & Accents)

### A. Top-Left Spice Spoons
- **Description:** A diagonal cluster of vintage wooden and copper spoons filled with spices (turmeric, curry powder, chili flakes, peppercorns).
- **Position:** Absolute positioned near the top-left margin, extending off-screen.
- **Accents:** Scattered loose peppercorns falling naturally around the spoons.

### B. Main Dumpling Platter
- **Description:** A round wooden bowl filled with four freshly steamed dumplings garnished with black sesame seeds.
- **Position:** Center-right placement.
- **Chopsticks:** Two light bamboo chopsticks resting diagonally across the wooden bowl from top-left to bottom-right.
- **Shadow:** Soft directional drop shadow beneath the bowl to give depth against the marble texture.

### C. Dipping Sauce Bowl
- **Description:** A small white ceramic bowl filled with vibrant red chili/tomato dipping sauce, topped with fresh green basil leaves.
- **Position:** Bottom-left of the main wooden bowl, overlapping slightly.
- **Accents:** Scattered loose peppercorns near the base of the bowl.

### D. Top-Right Fresh Produce Accents
- **Description:** Fresh red tomatoes on the vine, green basil leaves, and sliced red onions.
- **Position:** Top-right corner of the viewport, slightly cropped by the edge.

---

## 4. Typography & Color Palette Summary

### Color Palette
- **Background:** `#f5f5f5` (Mottled White/Grey Marble)
- **Primary Text:** `#111111` (Near Black)
- **Secondary Text:** `#666666` (Neutral Grey)
- **Brand Accent (Orange):** `#ff5500` (Vibrant Burnt Orange)

### Typography Stack
- **Headings:** High-contrast Serif (`Playfair Display`, `Georgia`, serif)
- **Body / Navigation:** Clean Sans-serif (`Inter`, `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, sans-serif)
