# Design Specification: Sevora Portfolio Template

## 1. Overview & Layout
* **Layout Structure:** Single-page container, max width `1280px`, centered, padding `0 24px` on desktop.
* **Background:** Light off-white / subtle cream (`#F9F9FB`).
* **Visual Rhythm:** Generous vertical spacing, minimal borders, subtle elevation effects.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Background:** `#F9F9FB`
* **Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF`
* **Card Border:** `#EEEEEE`
* **Primary Text:** `#111111`
* **Secondary Text:** `#666666`
* **Accent / Dark UI:** `#111111`
* **Button Text:** `#FFFFFF`

---

## 3. Typography & Fallbacks

### Primary Serif (Hero Title)
* **Font:** *Newsreader*, *Playfair Display*, or *Instrument Serif*
* **Fallback:** `Baskerville, Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif`
* **Properties:** Font weight 400, optical size default, line height ~1.15.

### Primary Sans-Serif (Body, UI, Stats)
* **Font:** *Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, or *Geist*
* **Fallback:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
* **Scale:**
  * Nav Links: `14px`, 500 weight.
  * Body Copy: `16px`, 400 weight, line height 1.6.
  * Stat Numbers: `20px`–`22px`, 700 weight.
  * Stat Labels: `14px`, 600 weight.
  * Stat Details: `13px`, 400 weight.

---

## 4. Navigation Bar
* **Positioning:** Full-width flex row, space-between, aligned center, height `80px`.
* **Brand:** Black stylized geometric "S" logo with "Sevora" text in bold sans-serif.
* **Menu Links:** Center-aligned horizontal list: `Home`, `About`, `Projects`, `Article`, `Contact`. Color: `#111111`, font size `14px`, gap `28px`.
* **Action Button:** Right-aligned pill button (`Remix Template`), background `#111111`, text `#FFFFFF`, padding `10px 20px`, border radius `100px`.

---

## 5. Hero Section

### Grid
* Two-column split layout (`55%` text / `45%` media) aligned center with an `80px` gap.

### Left Content
1. **Pill Tag:**
   * Text: "About me"
   * Styling: Capsule shape, border `1px solid #E5E5E5`, padding `4px 14px`, font size `12px`, text `#555555`.
2. **Main Headline:**
   * Text: "Good design starts with clear thinking."
   * Style: Serif, `52px`–`56px`, tight letter spacing, black (`#111111`).
3. **Bio Description:**
   * Text: *"I'm an independent digital designer creating refined identities, websites, and product experiences shaped by strategy, clarity, and thoughtful execution."*
   * Style: Sans-serif, `16px`, `#666666`, max-width `460px`.
4. **Signature:**
   * Script signature ("Geni" or stylized initials) in dark ink (`#111111`), placed ~`24px` below the description. Implemented via inline SVG.

### Right Content (Image Container)
* **Shape:** Tall vertical rectangle, rounded corners (`border-radius: 28px`), overflow hidden.
* **Image Description:**
  * Studio editorial portrait of a man with dark, wavy hair and a trimmed beard, looking slightly left.
  * Attire: Black turtleneck under a sharp black blazer.
  * Foreground: Low table on the lower-left featuring a matte black ceramic coffee mug resting on two minimalist design books (e.g., Kinfolk, Aesop).
  * Background: Solid, warm neutral beige studio wall (not transparent).
  * Orientation: Upright, eye-level framing, warm natural side-lighting.

---

## 6. Metrics & Status Row

### Grid
* 3-column equal grid beneath hero (`grid-template-columns: repeat(3, 1fr)`), gap `20px`, margin-top `48px`.

### Card Style
* Background: `#FFFFFF`
* Border: `1px solid #EFEFEF`
* Border Radius: `18px`
* Padding: `24px`
* Layout: Flex column with an icon row on top and descriptive text below.

### Card Details

1. **Card 1 (Experience):**
   * **Icon:** 4-point star outline inside a soft white elevated square badge (drop shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`).
   * **Header:** `8+` (Bold) | `Years of experience`
   * **Body:** "Working with brands and teams around the world since 2016."

2. **Card 2 (Clients):**
   * **Icon:** Two user/team outline silhouettes in an elevated square badge.
   * **Header:** `24+` (Bold) | `Happy clients`
   * **Body:** "Trusted by startups, agencies, and established brands."

3. **Card 3 (Availability):**
   * **Icon:** Angled paper airplane / direct message icon in an elevated square badge.
   * **Header:** `Available` (Bold) | `For new project`
   * **Body:** "Currently available for branding, web, and digital projects."
