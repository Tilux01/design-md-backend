# Design System & UI/UX Specification: Logitech G Product Landing Page

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
* **Primary Mood:** High-tech, performance-driven, stealth/cyberpunk minimalism. Designed for competitive gamers and hardware enthusiasts.
* **Brand Archetype:** Authoritative, cutting-edge, precision-engineered.
* **Visual Density:** Medium-high in hero and showcase areas, well-balanced with generous vertical whitespace (padding-block: `120px` to `160px`) between feature modules.
* **Theme Balance:** Pure Dark Mode implementation. Utilizes deep carbon-black gradients (`#0B0C0E` to `#141619`) paired with subtle slate-gray card containers (`#101215`) to create layered depth. Accents are kept minimal, leaning on clean high-contrast white text and subtle cool-gray secondary labels.

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid

* **Container Max-Width:** `1200px` (Centered with auto margins).
* **Grid System:** 12-column fluid grid with `24px` gutter width.
* **Vertical Spacing Scale:**
  * `xs`: `8px`
  * `sm`: `16px`
  * `md`: `32px`
  * `lg`: `64px`
  * `xl`: `120px` (Section padding)
  * `xxl`: `160px` (Hero vertical padding)

### Section Sequence Breakdown
1. **Global Header / Navigation:** Fixed top bar with brand logo, core links (`Home`, `About`, `Product`, `Specifications`), and social/action anchors.
2. **Hero Section:** Split layout (Text left, 3D Product Isometry right) introducing the flagship product.
3. **Overview Showcase ("MAKE YOUR PLAY"):** High-impact central imagery with secondary feature copy block and primary CTA.
4. **Interactive Multi-View Carousel ("SEE MORE DETALIS"):** Horizontal multi-angle product thumbnail carousel with dot indicators.
5. **Feature Breakdown Matrix (Timeline Connection):** Alternating specs paired with visual asset cards connected by a vertical neon-cyan trace line.
6. **Specifications Data Sheet:** Three-column structured breakdown (Physical, Technical, Other Features) with clean grid lines.
7. **Global Footer:** Duplicate core navigation, copyright, and legal links.

## 6. Motion Mechanics, 3D & Mobile Gestures

* **Animation Triggers:**
  * **Hero Entry:** Staggered fade-up (`opacity: 0 -> 1`, `translateY: 24px -> 0`) across title, description, and CTA buttons on page mount (`duration: 0.8s`, easing: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
  * **Timeline Reveal:** As the user scrolls down the feature matrix, the vertical neon-cyan stroke scales downward using `transform: scaleY()` anchored at the top, illuminating icons and text blocks sequentially via Intersection Observer.
  * **Product 3D Rotation:** Parallax mouse-tracking or scroll-tied rotation on hero product renders, simulating multi-axis gimbal rotation.
* **Interactive States:**
  * Hover scale on feature cards: `transform: translateY(-4px)` with box-shadow elevation intensification (`duration: 0.3s ease`).
  * Carousel pagination dots scale smoothly (`width: 6px` to `24px` active state transition).
