# Design System Specification

## 1. Colors

### Brand & Primary
- **Midnight Blue (Dark Backgrounds/Headers):** `#09101E`
- **Deep Navy (Dark Cards/Nav):** `#0F1E36`
- **Primary Accent Blue (Buttons, Active States, Key Accents):** `#1E40AF` (Tailwind `blue-800` equivalent) / Gradient highlights on primary cards: `#1E3A8A` to `#3B82F6`

### Neutrals
- **App Background (Light Mode):** `#F3F4F6` (Tailwind `gray-100`)
- **Card Background (White):** `#FFFFFF`
- **Card Background (Subtle Blue Tint - Health Overview):** `#EFF6FF` (Tailwind `blue-50`)
- **Text Dark (Primary Headings):** `#0F172A` (Tailwind `slate-900`)
- **Text Muted (Subtitles, Secondary Details):** `#64748B` (Tailwind `slate-500`)
- **Border / Divider:** `#E2E8F0` (Tailwind `slate-200`)

### Status & Accents
- **Heart Rate / Red Accent:** `#EF4444` (Tailwind `red-500`)
- **Blood Pressure / Blue Accent:** `#3B82F6` (Tailwind `blue-500`)
- **Oxygen Level / Cyan Accent:** `#06B6D4` (Tailwind `cyan-500`)

---

## 2. Typography

- **Font Family:** Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif

### Scale & Weights
- **Screen Title (Large - e.g., "Find The Right Treatment"):** 
  - Size: `24px` / `1.5rem`
  - Weight: Bold (`700`)
- **Heading 1 (User Greeting, Section Titles):** 
  - Size: `20px` / `1.25rem`
  - Weight: Bold (`700`)
- **Heading 2 (Card Titles):** 
  - Size: `16px` / `1rem`
  - Weight: Semi-Bold (`600`)
- **Body / Standard Text:** 
  - Size: `14px` / `0.875rem`
  - Weight: Regular (`400`) / Medium (`500`)
- **Caption / Metadata:** 
  - Size: `12px` / `0.75rem`
  - Weight: Regular (`400`) / Medium (`500`)

---

## 3. Layout & Structure

- **Device Frame / Screen Width:** Optimized for standard mobile viewport (`390px` to `430px` width)
- **Border Radius:**
  - **Screen / Device Corners:** `40px`
  - **Containers & Large Cards:** `24px`
  - **Buttons & Icon Containers:** `16px` to `9999px` (Full Pill)
- **Spacing / Padding:**
  - Standard Screen Margin: `20px`
  - Internal Card Padding: `16px` to `20px`
  - Gap between flex items: `12px` to `16px`

---

## 4. Component Library

### A. Navigation Bars
- **Bottom Navigation Bar:**
  - Background: White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle top border or elevation shadow.
  - Items: Home, Calendar/Appointments, Chat/Messages, Profile.
  - Active Item: Deep Navy/Midnight Blue tint with pill background.
  - Inactive Item: Muted Gray (`#94A3B8`).

### B. Appointment Card (Screen 1)
- **Background:** Dark gradient or solid Midnight Blue (`#0F1E36` to `#1E3A8A`).
- **Content:**
  - Label: "Your Next Appointment" (Muted light blue text, `12px`).
  - Time/Date: "08" (Large bold `28px`), "May 2025, Thursday", "10:30 AM".
  - Graphic: 3D Stethoscope illustration aligned to the right.
- **Border Radius:** `24px`.

### C. Health Overview Grid (Screen 1)
- **Container:** Light blue-tinted card (`#EFF6FF`).
- **Header:** "Health Overview" (Bold `14px`) with dropdown selector "This Week".
- **Metrics (3 columns):**
  - **Heart Rate:** Value `72` bpm with Red Heart icon.
  - **Blood Pressure:** Value `120/80` mmHg with Blue Gauge icon.
  - **Oxygen Level:** Value `98` % with Cyan Drop icon.

### D. Quick Access Icons (Screen 1)
- **Layout:** Horizontal row of 4 circular icon buttons.
- **Items:** Find Doctor, Symptoms Checker, Medicines, Lab Tests.
- **Style:** Circular light blue/white container (`#F8FAFC`) with dark icon and small label underneath (`12px`).

### E. Search Bar (Screen 2)
- **Background:** White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Shape:** Pill shape (`9999px` radius).
- **Elements:** Search magnifying glass icon on the left, placeholder text `"Search treatments, doctors..."` (`14px`, muted text), and filter/sliders icon button on the right inside a distinct circle.

### F. Service / Treatment Cards (Screen 2 & 3)
- **Large Hero Card (Advanced Imaging):**
  - Background image with dark gradient overlay at the bottom.
  - Title: "Advanced Imaging" (White, Bold `16px`).
  - Subtitle: "MRI, CT, X-Ray" (Light gray, `12px`).
  - Action: Circular white arrow button on the bottom right.
- **Split Cards (Vaccination & Surgical Procedures):**
  - Side-by-side or stacked grid layout with image thumbnails and clear textual labels + arrow indicators.

### G. Action / CTA Buttons (Bottom Sticky)
- **Style:** Dark solid pill button (`#09101E` or `#0F1E36`).
- **Content:** Left-aligned text (e.g., "Book Consultation", "From $150") and a circular white arrow button aligned to the far right.
