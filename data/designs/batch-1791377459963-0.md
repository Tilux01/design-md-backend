# DESIGN.MD: "Elder Adaptogenic Sparkling Water" Brand System

## 1. Overview & Creative Direction
* **Brand Name:** elder (stylized lowercase with a period, e.g., `elder.`)
* **Product Type:** Adaptogenic Sparkling Water (No Added Sugar, Caffeine Free, Natural Flavours).
* **Aesthetic:** Bold, playful, neo-brutalist pop art. It combines retro 1960s/70s poster typography with modern, whimsical, high-contrast flat-black cartoon/line-art illustrations.
* **Canvas Ratio:** Vertical (9:16 mobile-first / Full-screen ready).

---

## 2. Color Palette

Each can utilizes a distinct pastel background color paired with an ultra-high-contrast primary ink color for maximum shelf-pop.

### Universal Colors
* **Ink / Illustration Black:** `#0D0D0D` (Rich black used for all primary illustrations, linework, and secondary branding).
* **Base White/Cream:** `#F4F2EE` (Subtle off-white accents where applicable).

### Flavor-Specific Palettes

#### **Variant 1: Lime Watermelon (Active Recovery)**
* **Background (Can Body):** Mint/Lime Green (`#A3E06C`)
* **Primary Title Text:** Bright Neon Coral-Red (`#FF2A36`)
* **Secondary Text/Badges:** Dark Black (`#0D0D0D`)

#### **Variant 2: Peach Hibiscus (Balanced Focus)**
* **Background (Can Body):** Muted Peach / Warm Beige (`#F4D1BA`)
* **Primary Title Text:** Hot Magenta / Bubblegum Pink (`#FF007A`)
* **Secondary Text/Badges:** Dark Black (`#0D0D0D`)

#### **Variant 3: Blueberry (Balanced)**
* **Background (Can Body):** Soft Lavender / Periwinkle (`#B8B4E5`)
* **Primary Title Text:** Electric Yellow (`#FAFF00`)
* **Secondary Text/Badges:** Dark Black (`#0D0D0D`)

## 3. Typography & Font Specifications

The typography is a defining feature of the brand, leaning heavily on heavy, condensed, grotesque sans-serifs that evoke vintage packaging and posters.

### Primary Brandmark (`elder.`)
* **Role:** Top-left brand logo.
* **Style:** Rounded, friendly script/sans hybrid, lowercase with a period.
* **Font Match / Fallback:** 
  * *Primary Recommendation:* **Cooper Soft** or **Sniglet** (bolded).
  * *Web Safe Fallback:* `'Comic Sans MS', 'Arial Rounded MT Bold', sans-serif`.

### Primary Flavor Headings (e.g., `LIME WATERMELON`, `PEACH HIBISCUS`)
* **Role:** Main hero text on the upper half of the can.
* **Style:** Ultra-bold, ultra-condensed sans-serif (all caps), tracked tightly (negative letter-spacing), often styled with a slight blocky perspective or heavy ink-trap feel.
* **Font Match / Fallback:** 
  * *Primary Recommendation:* **Anton**, **Bebas Neue Pro (Extra Bold Condensed)**, or **Impact**.
  * *Web Safe Fallback:* `Impact, 'Arial Black', sans-serif`.

### Secondary Text (e.g., `ADAPTOGENIC SPARKLING WATER FOR...`)
* **Role:** Product category descriptor and functional callouts.
* **Style:** Clean, highly-legible geometric or neo-grotesque sans-serif (all caps), medium weight.
* **Font Match / Fallback:** 
  * *Primary Recommendation:* **Inter (Bold)** or **Futura Condensed**.
  * *Web Safe Fallback:* `Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

## 4. Layout & Composition Anatomy

The can layout is vertically stacked into three distinct visual tiers:

### **Tier 1: Header (Top 20%)**
* **Logo:** Placed top-center or top-left, set in the background-contrasting ink color.
* **Main Title:** Stacked 2-line bold headline (`[FLAVOR 1]` over `[FLAVOR 2]`). Aligned left with a slight geometric shear/slant.

### **Tier 2: The Character Illustration (Middle 50%)**
* **Style:** 2D flat vector art with a heavy black fill (`#0D0D0D`), accented by white negative-space cutouts and minimal linework.
* **Composition:** Surreal, whimsical characters interacting with the product identity:
  * *Lime Watermelon:* A figure with a mushroom/cone head reaching upward dynamically.
  * *Peach Hibiscus:* A character with a flat square/box head riding a skateboard.
* **Callout Badges:** Accompanied by jagged comic-book style starburst badges (e.g., *“WITH CORDYCEPS”*, *“WITH BACOPA MONNIERI”*). The badge uses solid black fill with white reversed text.

### **Tier 3: Footer / Functional Specs (Bottom 30%)**
* **Descriptor:** `"ADAPTOGENIC SPARKLING WATER FOR"` set in small, tracked-out caps.
* **Sub-Brand / Product Benefit:** Large bold outlined or filled sub-headline (e.g., `ACTIVE RECOVERY`, `BALANCED FOCUS`) in a contrasting accent color.
* **Pill/Metatags:** A clean horizontal inline list separated by bullet points or spacing: `NO ADDED SUGAR  •  CAFFEINE FREE  •  NATURAL FLAVOURS`.

---

## 5. Lighting & Texture (Digital Rendering Notes)
If rendering this as a 3D asset or high-fidelity UI graphic:
* **Surface Finish:** Semi-matte satin aluminum can texture. Avoid high-gloss chrome reflections to maintain the graphic illustration style.
* **Condensation/Water Droplets:** Overlay hyper-realistic micro-water droplets across the can surface to simulate an ice-cold refrigerator environment. Droplets should refract the background colors and cast subtle soft shadows.

---

## 6. Animation Guidelines (If Applicable)

If this design is brought to life in motion (e.g., UI banner, video ad, or interactive web scroll):
1. **Can Entrance:** Cans slide into frame horizontally with a snappy, elastic physics easing (cubic-bezier `0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1`).
2. **Droplet Shimmer:** A subtle light sweep moves diagonally across the surface, causing the water droplets to catch a bright specular highlight.
3. **Typography Kinetic Effect:** On hover or active state, the main flavor titles (`LIME WATERMELON`) can undergo a slight rubber-band scale or jitter animation to mimic vintage pop-art print misregistration.
