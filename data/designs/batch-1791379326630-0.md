# Design Specification: Creative Studio Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Layout Strategy
- **Layout Type:** Full-width hero section, single-page landing page aesthetic.
- **Orientation:** Horizontal, desktop-optimized (approx. 1440px standard width reference).
- **Structure:** 
  - **Header:** Sticky navigation bar containing logo, central menu links (Work, Services, About, Contact), and a prominent CTA button ("Lets Connect").
  - **Hero Section:** 
    - Left column: Headline typography, supporting body copy, and a pill-shaped "Read More" button.
    - Floating badges (Net Worth: 15M+, Success Rate: 96%) positioned bottom-left.
  - **Center:** High-impact portrait photography of a model with dramatic lighting.
  - **Background Typography:** Giant oversized text ("CREATIVE") layered behind the subject with gradient opacity/blend modes.
  - **Right Column:** Studio branding ("CREATIVE STUDIO"), descriptive subcopy, and a glassmorphism testimonial/partner card ("Your Partner in Business Growth" with client avatars).

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary Background:** Deep crimson/wine red gradient shifting into warm amber/burnt orange (`#8B1E1E` transitioning to `#3A0808` / warm golden hour tones for the second variation).
- **Accent / Highlight:** Vivid orange-red (`#C41E3A`) used for buttons and active states.
- **Text Primary (White):** `#FFFFFF` used for headings, large branding, and primary UI labels.
- **Text Secondary (Muted):** Light cream/soft gray (`#E0D5D5`) used for body copy and secondary descriptions.
- **UI Element Backgrounds (Glassmorphism):** Translucent white/dark overlays with frosted glass effects (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `0.15`).

---

## 3. Typography & Font Stack

### Headings & Large Display Text
- **Style:** Geometric, bold, all-caps sans-serif with distinct character spacing.
- **Primary Font:** **Syne** or **Clash Display** (fallback: `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`).
- **Letter Spacing:** Wide tracking for large display headings (`letter-spacing: -0.02em` for bold headers, `letter-spacing: 0.05em` for small uppercase labels like nav items).

### Body Copy & Navigation
- **Style:** Clean, highly legible contemporary sans-serif.
- **Primary Font:** **Inter** or **Plus Jakarta Sans** (fallback: `Arial, sans-serif`).
- **Font Weights Used:** 
  - Regular (400) for body descriptions.
  - Medium (500) for navigation links and button labels.
  - Bold / Extra Bold (700/800) for metrics, headings, and primary studio titles.

---

## 4. UI Components & Elements

### A. Navigation Bar
- **Logo:** Minimalist abstract geometric icon on the far left.
- **Links:** Centered, lowercase navigation items (`Work`, `services`, `About`, `Contact`) with subtle hover states.
- **CTA Button:** Pill-shaped, solid deep red background (`#A31D1D`) with white text ("Lets Connect").

### B. Hero Typography & Copy
- **Main Headline:** *"GIVE YOUR BRAND A SYSTEM IT CAN ACTUALLY GROW INTO"* (Left-aligned, clean line breaks).
- **Subcopy:** *"From considered Foundations to Components your team can trust. And from first principles to production-ready detail."*
- **Call to Action:** Red pill button labeled `"Read More"`.

### C. Giant Background Typography
- **Text:** `"CREATIVE"` displayed across the horizontal axis behind the portrait.
- **Styling:** Outlined text stroke combined with subtle linear gradients fading from solid white/cream to transparent.

### D. Floating Cards & Badges
- **Metrics Badge (Bottom-Left):** Frosted glass container featuring:
  - `Net Worth: 15M+`
  - `Success Rate: 96%`
- **Partner/Testimonial Card (Bottom-Right):** Frosted glass panel with the heading *"Your Partner in Business Growth"*, placeholder lorem ipsum text, and stacked client profile avatars with a `+5000 Happy Clients` indicator.

---

## 5. Animation & Interaction Specifications (For Development)

- **Entry Animations (Scroll/Load):**
  - **Fade-in Up:** Hero text and navigation fade in sequentially with a staggered delay (`0.2s` intervals).
  - **Parallax Effect:** The background giant typography (`CREATIVE`) moves at a slower scroll speed relative to the foreground portrait and UI cards.
  - **Image Reveal:** The central portrait scales subtly from `1.05` to `1.0` upon initial page load (`ease-out`, duration: `1.2s`).
- **Hover States:**
  - **Buttons ("Read More", "Lets Connect"):** Scale up slightly (`transform: scale(1.03)`) with a brightness/luminosity increase.
  - **Nav Links:** Smooth color transition to pure white or underline accent.
  - **Floating Cards:** Subtle elevation/lift effect (`translateY(-4px)`) with enhanced drop shadows on hover.
