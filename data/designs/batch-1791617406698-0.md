# Design Specification: Neuromarketing Dashboard / Interactive Showcase

---

## 1. Executive Design Overview

* **Art Direction**: Futuristic Neuromorphic Minimalist / Tech-Human Centric.
* **Canvas Target**: Fullscreen Desktop Viewport (`100vw` × `100vh`, base reference: `1920px × 1080px` and `1440px × 900px`).
* **Visual Characteristics**:
  * Light-gray layered backdrop featuring subtle low-contrast polygon faceting.
  * Organic topographic contour lines paired with geometric skewed accent cards.
  * High-contrast neon gradients (Electric Orange, Deep Violet, Neon Cyan/Mint) against neutral silvers and dark charcoal text.
  * Weightless, zero-gravity floating elements (3D product renders and isolated model).

---

### 5.4 The Tri-Card Product Row (Bottom)

All three cards follow a synchronized **two-layer stacked architecture**:

1. **Backdrop Layer (Slanted Metric Polygonal Tag)**:
   * Positioned behind the white card, offset to the right and top.
   * Shape: Skewed parallelogram using CSS transform (`transform: skewY(-14deg); border-radius: 18px;`) or clip-path.
   * Width: `130px`, Height: `280px`.
   * Houses the numerical ID at its top right (`710`, `601`, `410`) in white font.
2. **Foreground Layer (White Elevated Card)**:
   * Dimensions: `width: 220px; height: 260px; border-radius: 20px;`
   * Background: Solid pure white `#FFFFFF`.
   * Shadow: `0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
   * Padding: `24px`.
   * Contains the category icon, category title, and product name.
3. **Pop-Out Product Asset**:
   * Isolated 3D image protruding outside the top-left boundary of the white card.

## 6. Background Vector Geometry & Topographic Detailing

### 6.1 Faceted Background Polygons
* The canvas background is not a flat gray; it consists of large-scale, low-contrast polygonal facets:
  * A diagonal wedge cutting from top-left (`#ECEEF2`) down toward center (`#DFE1E5`).
  * A darker triangular shadow facet running from center toward bottom-left (`#D5D8DE`).

### 6.2 Topographic Contour Network
* **Stroke Styling**: Vector lines, `1.0px` to `1.2px` stroke weight.
* **Stroke Color**: `rgba(40, 48, 64, 0.14)`.
* **Flow Pattern**:
  * Concentric, smoothly undulating elevation curves originating from top-right.
  * Curves wrap around the upper-right hemisphere, contouring around the sleeping figure and converging gently near the center canvas.
  * Small circular data nodes (`4px` diameter solid dots) scattered along major contour junctions.
