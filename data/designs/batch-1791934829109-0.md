# Design Specification: Creative Studio Hero Section

## 1. Overall Layout & Themes
* **Screen Mode**: Fullscreen viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), `overflow: hidden`.
* **Themes**:
  * **Crimson Theme**: Radial gradient transitioning from central/left bright glow (`#E03E26`) to deep maroon (`#6E151E`) down to dark vignette edges (`#240509`).
  * **Amber Theme**: Radial gradient transitioning from bright amber glow (`#F38F31`) to rich burnt orange (`#A84E18`) and dark bronze base (`#381706`).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
* **Primary Display Font**: Modern Geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., *Clash Display*, *Syne*, or *Cabinet Grotesk*).
  * *Fallback*: `Arial Black, Impact, sans-serif`.
* **Body / UI Font**: Neutral Clean Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*).
  * *Fallback*: `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.
* **Font Weights**:
  * Giant Backdrop Title: Bold (700–800)
  * Headings: Semi-bold to Bold (600–700)
  * Body Text: Regular (400)
  * Numbers/Stats: Medium to Semi-bold (500–600)

---

## 3. Navigation Bar
* **Position**: Fixed/absolute at top, full width, padded (`px-12 py-6`).
* **Components**:
  * **Left (Logo)**: Abstract seashell/concentric arch icon in solid white.
  * **Center (Links)**: Horizontal list (`Work`, `services`, `About`, `Contact`). Font size: `14px`, regular weight, color: `#FFFFFFD9`, gap: `32px`.
  * **Right (CTA)**: Pill button (`Lets Connect`).
    * Background: Dark translucent crimson/burgundy (`rgba(90, 10, 20, 0.75)`).
    * Text: White, `13px`, medium weight.
    * Padding: `8px 20px`, full border-radius (`9999px`).

---

## 4. Central Visual Element (Subject)
* **Subject**: Isolated cutout (transparent PNG background) of a young athletic male with dark curly hair, wearing a black ribbed tank top, facing slightly right.
* **Lighting**: Dual-tone studio rim lighting (warm golden/red side-light matching theme glow, dark shadowed edges).
* **Placement**: Centered horizontally, anchored to the bottom edge. Head extends into the upper third of the canvas, layered in front of the giant backdrop typography.

---

## 5. Background Typography
* **Text**: `CREATIVE`
* **Styling**: All-caps, extreme scale (~`22vw`), bold geometric sans-serif, letter-spacing: `0.05em`.
* **Layering**: Positioned vertically mid-canvas, positioned *behind* the subject model so letters are partially obscured by the torso and head.
* **Color**: Semi-transparent white gradient (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)` fading to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).

---
