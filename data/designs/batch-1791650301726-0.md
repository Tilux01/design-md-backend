# Design Specification: Nulo Pet Food Landing Page Clone

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
- **View:** Full-screen responsive viewport (Desktop-first approach, min-width: 1200px).
- **Background Color:** Deep charcoal/black (`#111217` or CSS `radial-gradient(circle at center, #1b1d24 0%, #0d0e12 100%)`).
- **Outer Frame/Presentation:** The mockup red backdrop and rounded drop shadows are presentation-only. The actual webpage design fills 100% of the screen width and height.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation Bar
- **Position:** Fixed or absolute, pinned to the top with generous padding (`top: 30px`, horizontal padding matching content container).
- **Logo:**
  - **Text/Image:** "nulo" lowercase with a small red heart dotting or integrated into the brand mark. 
  - **Color:** White (`#ffffff`), with a vibrant red heart (`#e52521`).
  - **Position:** Top-left.
- **Navigation Links:**
  - **Items:** `For dogs`, `For cats`, `Our story`
  - **Font Family:** Sans-serif (Inter, Roboto, or system-ui).
  - **Font Size:** `14px`
  - **Font Weight:** Regular (`400`) to Medium (`500`).
  - **Color:** White (`#ffffff`), opacity ~85%.
  - **Position:** Top-right, horizontally aligned with standard spacing (`gap: 32px`).

---

## 3. Hero Section
### Typography
- **Subheadline:** 
  - **Text:** "Feed me with"
  - **Font Family:** Modern Sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Helvetica Neue, Arial).
  - **Font Size:** `32px` - `38px`
  - **Font Weight:** Medium (`500`)
  - **Color:** White (`#ffffff`)
- **Main Headline (The "LOVE" typographic focal point):**
  - **Text:** "love" (Stylized vertically/overlapping with the letter **'l'** stretching tall on the left).
  - **Design Detail:** The letter **'l'** is an oversized vertical white bar with a vertical gradient fade. The remaining letters (**o**, **v**, **e**) overlap seamlessly behind the cat photography.
  - **Color:** White-to-transparent gradient with soft drop-shadowing/glow.

### Call to Action (CTA) Button
- **Text:** "shop pet food"
- **Shape:** Pill/Capsule button with rounded corners (`border-radius: 50px`).
- **Background Color:** Vibrant Pet-Food Red (`#e52521`).
- **Font Size:** `12px` - `13px`, Uppercase or lowercase with tracking.
- **Font Weight:** Bold (`600`).
- **Position:** Placed cleanly over the left side of the cat's body.

---

## 4. Featured Imagery (Cat & Food Bowl)
- **Main Cat Image:**
  - **Subject:** A fluffy grey-and-brown tabby cat with striking amber/yellow eyes, resting in a comfortable loaf/lying position facing toward the right side.
  - **Background Status:** **Backgroundless** (isolated transparent PNG/WebP cutout seamlessly blended onto the dark charcoal canvas with a soft ambient shadow beneath it).
  - **Position & Scale:** Centered-right, overlapping the lower half of the "love" typography block.
- **Pet Food Bowl:**
  - **Subject:** A sleek metallic silver pet bowl filled with dark kibble.
  - **Position:** Placed right in front of the cat's front paws on the lower right.
  - **Background Status:** **Backgroundless** cutout with realistic metallic lighting and ground contact shadow.

---

## 5. Bottom Interactive Cards (3-Column Grid)
- **Layout:** 3 distinct vertical content cards spanning the bottom section of the hero viewport.
- **Card Background:** Semi-transparent dark surface (`rgba(25, 27, 35, 0.6)`) with subtle border separation or backdrop blur.

### Card 1: Shop dog food
- **Title:** "Shop dog food" (White, Bold, `16px`)
- **Subheadings/Links:**
  - **Category:** LIFESTYLE
  - **Options:** Puppy | Adult | Senior
  - **Category:** SPECIALTY NEEDS

### Card 2: Shop cat food
- **Title:** "Shop cat food" (White, Bold, `16px`)
- **Subheadings/Links:**
  - **Category:** LIFESTYLE
  - **Options:** Kitten | Adult | Senior
  - **Category:** SPECIALTY NEEDS

### Card 3: Community
- **Title:** "Community" (Accent red-tinted or white, Bold, `16px`)
- **Subheadings/Links:**
  - **Category:** THE COMPANY WE KEEP
  - **Body text:** "Dogs and cats are more than just our pets. They're our..." (Muted grey text, regular weight, `12px`).

---

## 6. Typography & Fallback Guidelines
- **Primary Font:** `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`.
- **Fallback Stack:** If `Inter` or custom web fonts fail to load, the browser will seamlessly fall back to system sans-serif fonts while maintaining identical font weights and sizing rules.
