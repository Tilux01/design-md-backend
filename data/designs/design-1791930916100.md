# Design Specification: Scott Foil 30 Landing Page

## 1. Color Palette

*   **Background Primary:** `#181f26` (Deep Charcoal Slate)
*   **Background Facets / Angled Shapes:** `#131920` (Dark Navy-Black) and `#222c37` (Muted Slate)
*   **Accent / Brand Red:** `#e44d3a` (Vibrant Coral Red)
*   **Text Primary:** `#ffffff` (Pure White)
*   **Text Muted:** `#8e9aa8` (Cool Grey)
*   **Active Indicator / Hotspots:** `#e44d3a` with white icons (`#ffffff`)

---

## 2. Typography

*   **Primary Font:** `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, or `Arial`, sans-serif
*   **Logo/Sport Display Font:** `Rajdhani` or `Eurostile`, sans-serif (bold, geometric, slightly italicized)
*   **Hierarchy:**
    *   **Hero Title (Scott Foil):** 52px, Semi-Bold (600), Line-height 1.1, Color `#fff`.
    *   **Hero Subtitle (30 Bike):** 52px, Light/Regular (300), Line-height 1.1, Color `#fff`.
    *   **Body Description:** 14px, Regular (400), Line-height 1.6, Color `#8e9aa8`, Max-width 360px.
    *   **Navigation Links:** 11px, Bold (700), Uppercase, Letter-spacing 1.5px, Color `#8e9aa8` (Active: `#e44d3a`).
    *   **Button Text:** 11px, Bold (700), Uppercase, Letter-spacing 1px, Color `#fff`.
    *   **Feature Card Title:** 11px, Bold (700), Uppercase, Letter-spacing 1px, Color `#fff`.
    *   **Feature Card Details:** 10px, Regular (400), Line-height 1.4, Color `#707d8d`.

## 6. Central Visual: Bike & Interactive Hotspots

### Bike Asset
*   **Asset Type:** High-resolution cutout PNG with a fully transparent background.
*   **Subject:** Scott Foil road bike, matte slate-grey frame, deep-dish aero wheels, red accent line on inner top-tube and wheel rims, drop handlebars facing left.
*   **Placement:** Centered horizontally toward the right half (X: 35% to 90%), vertically centered (Y: 20% to 85%).
*   **Rotation:** 0 degrees flat side-profile, perspective angled slightly 5° toward the viewer.

### Hotspot Pins (5 Positions)
Circular badges (diameter 20px), coral red (`#e44d3a`) with a centered white plus symbol (`+`, 10px, bold).
1.  **Cockpit/Stem:** Junction of handlebar stem and headtube.
2.  **Saddle/Seatpost:** Underneath the rear saddle rail.
3.  **Bottom Bracket/Crankset:** Center of the front chainring.
4.  **Rear Derailleur:** Center of the rear cassette/hanger.
5.  **Front Wheel:** Bottom lip of the front rim/tire contact point.
