# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Ultra-Minimalist Noir / Modernist Tech Dark Mode.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Extremely sparse and intentional whitespace. Focus is directed toward central content with generous container padding and generous outer margins to emphasize high-end digital editorial clarity.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered obsidian architecture utilizing deep background layers (`#000000` / `#0A0A0A`), subtle absolute-positioned glowing ambient orbs, thin border delineations (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and soft radial backdrop blurs (`backdrop-blur: 24px`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges with pulsing status dots, ambient blurred glow orbs (`blur-3xl`), border gradients, and dark glassmorphic surfaces.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `0.75rem`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.1)`, border width `1px` (`rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.3)`).
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
