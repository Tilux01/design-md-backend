# Design Specification: "Nat8." Botanical Editorial Landing Page

---

## 1. Overview & Architectural Vision
This document outlines the design and front-end specification for cloning the minimalist, high-end editorial botanical landing page. 

The design is rendered as a **fullscreen viewport application** ($100\text{vw} \times 100\text{vh}$), removing external showcase framing, device bezels, or social media review overlays. The interface combines ultra-clean Swiss international typographic principles with organic photorealism and skeuomorphic depth through layered botanical cutouts, frosted glass effects, and soft ambient studio lighting.

* **Background Gradient**:
  ```css

  ```
* **Floor Horizon**: A subtle light falloff horizontally across the bottom 25% of the screen where cast shadows settle.

## 4. Layout & Grid Architecture

* **Container Setup**: $100\text{vw} \times 100\text{vh}$, `overflow: hidden`, `position: relative`.
* **Outer Margins / Safe Area**:
  * Desktop ($>1280\text{px}$): `padding: 3rem 4rem` (48px 64px).
  * High-density displays ($1920\text{px}$): `padding: 4rem 5rem` (64px 80px).
* **Grid Partitioning**:
  * **Top Row**: Flexbox, full width, space-between alignment, pinned at the top.
  * **Center Stage**: Absolute centering for hero text and visual composition.
  * **Bottom Row**: Flexbox, full width, space-between alignment, pinned at the bottom.

### 5.2 Center Hero Stage (Composition & Depth Layering)

The center stage uses a multi-layered sandwich arrangement (`Z-Index`) to create physical depth between typography and botanical specimens.

#### A. Typography Layer: The Wordmark "Plant"
* **Position**: Centered horizontally, vertically situated slightly above true center ($\approx 44\%$ from viewport top).
* **Styling**:
  * Solid Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  * Optical size: Giant display scale (`~16vw`).
* **Depth & Frosted Treatment**:
  * The vertical stem of the capital letter **"P"** features an integrated frosted glass/blur effect on its lower edge. As it approaches the ground, the opacity drops to $40\%$ with a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), softening the boundary against the shadow of the pinecone.

#### B. Circular Artisan Emblem / Watermark
* **Position**: Floated just above the upper-right shoulder of the letter **"t"** (roughly offset $X: +8\%$, $Y: -15\%$ from the top right of the word "Plant").
* **Dimensions**: $96\text{px} \times 96\text{px}$ circular badge.
* **Content**:
  * Outer Circular Path Text (SVG `<textPath>`): `"SERGIO ARTEAGA · DISEÑADOR GRÁFICO ·"` uppercase, micro-grotesk font, $8\text{px}$ size.
  * Inner Core: Delicate cursive script initials / signature monogram `"S"` or `"A"`, angled at $-15^\circ$.
* **Color**: Faint grey-taupe `--watermark-tint`, blending softly with the warm background.

### 5.3 Photographic & Natural Assets (Cutouts)

All photographic elements are **backgroundless PNG assets with crisp alpha channels** and realistic pre-rendered or CSS-rendered contact and ambient shadows.

#### 1. Dried Conifer / Pine Branch (Centerpiece)
* **Visual Description**: A sprawling, desiccated pine/cedar twig with brittle, dense needles. Color tones range from golden dry straw (`#AF9469`) to deep wood bark brown (`#3E2F20`).
* **Placement**:
  * Spans across the center foreground, extending horizontally beneath the letters **"l"**, **"a"**, **"n"**, and overlapping into the base of **"t"**.
  * Sits directly on the floor plane.
* **Z-Index Layer**: Placed **in front** of the wordmark (`z-index: 20`).
* **Shadow**:
  * Soft diffuse contact shadow on the ground: `filter: drop-shadow(0 12px 14px rgba(45, 38, 30, 0.22))`.
  * Tight contact occlusion shadow at touchpoints: `rgba(20, 15, 10, 0.35)`.

#### 2. Open Pinecone (Left Anchor)
* **Visual Description**: Natural woody cone, fully opened, positioned horizontally/tilted slightly upward at $15^\circ$. Texture is rich in dark umber, charcoal, and warm wood tones.
* **Placement**:
  * Positioned at the bottom-left of the letter **"P"**, slightly overlapping the lower-left stem.
  * X-Position: Aligned with the left baseline of "Plant".
* **Z-Index Layer**: Middle ground (`z-index: 15`). It sits partially behind the letter "P"'s frosted zone and slightly casts an ambient shadow towards the bottom center.

#### 3. Smooth River Stones / Pebbles (Right Anchor)
* **Visual Description**: A tranquil Japanese stone arrangement:
  * **Primary Stone**: Large, rounded, matte grey-taupe river stone with smooth, weathered surface texture.
  * **Secondary Stone**: Medium oval stone resting tilted against the primary stone.
  * **Tertiary Foreground Pebble**: Small, flat, smooth oval pebble lying flat to the right.
* **Placement**:
  * Situated around the base and lower right of the letter **"t"**.
  * The tail of the letter **"t"** clips subtly *behind* the foreground stone and *in front* of the large background stone, interleaving text and 3D matter.
* **Z-Index Layer**: Interleaved:
  * Large stone: `z-index: 5` (behind "Plant").
  * Medium & flat pebble: `z-index: 25` (in front of the foot of "t").

## 6. Depth & Z-Index Matrix

To achieve the 3D optical illusion, the stacking order must be strictly managed:

| Layer Level | Element | CSS `z-index` | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Layer 0** | Studio Cyclorama Background | `0` | Base radiant gradient floor |
| **Layer 1** | Background Stone (Large river rock) | `5` | Casts shadow onto floor |
| **Layer 2** | Hero Typography (`Plant`) | `10` | Massive pure white wordmark |
| **Layer 3** | Pinecone cutout | `15` | Sits near "P" base |
| **Layer 4** | Dried Cedar/Pine Branch cutout | `20` | Crosses foreground of "l", "a", "n", "t" |
| **Layer 5** | Foreground Flat Pebbles | `25` | Overlaps front of "t" |
| **Layer 6** | Circular Designer Watermark Badge | `30` | Top-right of hero |
| **Layer 7** | Header & Footer UI Overlays | `50` | Navigation, controls, indices |

## 7. Responsive Breakpoint Strategy

While designed primarily as an expansive desktop widescreen art exhibition piece, viewport adaptations are as follows:

* **Ultra-wide ($>1600\text{px}$)**:
  * Restrict hero content maximum scale while expanding canvas margins proportionally.
  * Hero wordmark locked to a maximum font size of `260px`.
* **Standard Desktop / Laptop ($1024\text{px}$ – $1440\text{px}$)**:
  * Hero wordmark scales with `15vw`.
  * Padding: `2.5rem 3.5rem`.
* **Tablet Landscape ($768\text{px}$ – $1023\text{px}$)**:
  * Reduce top navigation links gap from `36px` to `20px`.
  * Reduce branch and stone scale to $85\%$ to avoid clipping the header/footer.
* **Mobile / Tablet Portrait ($<768\text{px}$)**:
  * Hide secondary nav ("Support the Garden", "Volunteer", "Contact") behind a minimal hamburger icon.
  * Align footer controls into two stacked rows: category title above, pagination and pill button below.
