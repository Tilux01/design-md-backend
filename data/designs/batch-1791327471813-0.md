# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Editorial Neo-Brutalist & Cyberpunk Aesthetic.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-contrast, dense informational display with spacious container padding balanced by sharp accent blocks.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered frosted glass panels (`backdrop-blur-md`), stark borders, and vibrant neon pink/magenta volumetric glows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient neon glow orbs, backdrop blur frosted glass, custom mono asset stamps.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `700`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(255, 46, 136, 0.1)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(255, 46, 136, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-[#FF2E88] inline-block animate-pulse"></span>`.
