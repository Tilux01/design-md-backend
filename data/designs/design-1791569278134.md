# Design Specification: Solvanta Solar Energy Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
* **Viewport:** Full-screen responsive layout optimized for desktop viewports.
* **Canvas Background:** Solid dark charcoal/black background (`#1a1a1a` or similar) framing the core white/off-white application card.
* **Main Container:** Centered white content card with rounded corners (`border-radius: ~24px`), housing the entire application interface.
* **Color Palette:**
  * **Primary/Brand:** Solar Yellow (`#FFB800` to `#FFD13B` gradients).
  * **Dark Neutrals:** Charcoal black (`#111111`) for primary text and headings.
  * **Light Neutrals:** Off-white/cream (`#F9F6F0`) for secondary background cards (bottom left and right modules).
  * **Muted Text:** Medium gray (`#6b7280`) for body copy and captions.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
* **Primary Font Family:** Clean, geometric sans-serif (e.g., **Inter**, **Plus Jakarta Sans**, or **SF Pro Display**).
* **Fallback Stack:** `sans-serif`, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont.
* **Typographic Scale:**
  * **Hero Background Text ("SOLVANTA"):** Extra-bold, large-scale display text (`font-weight: 800`), treated with a subtle vertical gradient fading from solid gray to transparent white against the upper background.
  * **Navigation Links:** Medium weight (`font-weight: 500`), approx. `14px`, dark neutral color.
  * **Main Section Headers ("POWER TODAY, PROTECT TOMORROW"):** Bold, all-caps sans-serif (`font-weight: 700`), high contrast.
  * **Body Copy:** Regular weight (`font-weight: 400`), approx. `14px`, optimized for readability.

---

## 3. Section-by-Section Breakdown

### A. Header & Navigation
* **Logo Area:**
  * Icon: A stylized sun symbol overlapping four dark blue/black solar panels.
  * Wordmark: "SOLVANTA" in bold tracking-spaced sans-serif typography.
* **Navigation Links:**
  * Centered list containing: `Solutions`, `Services`, `Projects`, `About`, `Pricing`.
* **Action CTA:**
  * Pill-shaped dark button on the top right: `Get a Quote →` with a white text and arrow indicator.

### B. Hero Section
* **Hero Visual:**
  * **Main Image:** A photograph of a modern residential home equipped with rooftop solar panels, surrounded by lush trees under a warm, golden-hour sun flare.
  * **Floating Badge (Top Right of House):** A small rounded card displaying a photo thumbnail with the text `7K+ Systems Installed`.
  * **Interactive Slider Line:** A horizontal divider line featuring a centered circular drag handle positioned over the image.
* **Left Floating Callout Card:**
  * White rounded card layered over the left side of the imagery.
  * **Heading:** "CLEAN ENERGY, BUILT TO LAST"
  * **Subtext:** "Smart solar systems for homes and businesses."
  * **Button:** Dark pill button `Explore Solar →`.
* **Right Floating Data Widgets:**
  * **Live Production Card:** Dark frosted-glass widget displaying a signal/bar chart icon, live output metric (`24.8 kW Generating Now`), and a small graphic of a solar inverter unit.
  * **Today's Energy Card:** Vibrant yellow gradient card displaying total output (`142 kWh`) with a dynamic sparkline chart indicating clean energy generated.

### C. Bottom Left Module (Eco Solutions)
* **Background:** Cream/off-white rounded container.
* **Icons:** Leaf icon (eco-friendly) and glowing sun icon.
* **Description:** "Solvanta delivers efficient solar solutions engineered for lasting performance."
* **Link:** `EXPLORE SOLUTIONS` with a right arrow.

### D. Central Banner ("POWER TODAY, PROTECT TOMORROW")
* **Placement:** Overlaid centrally across the lower middle section of the hero image.
* **Text:** Bold white typography with a dark semi-transparent backdrop pill or gradient shade underneath to ensure high legibility against the photo background.
* **CTA Button:** Centered prominent yellow pill button reading `START SAVING →`.

### E. Bottom Right Module (Client Reviews)
* **Background:** Cream/off-white rounded container matching the bottom-left module.
* **Header:** `CLIENT REVIEWS`
* **Rating:** 5 solid gold star icons.
* **Review Quote:** *"Professional service, clean installation and excellent results."*
* **Author Info:** "James Carter — Homeowner"
* **Score Indicator:** `4.9 / 5` displayed in the bottom right corner.

---

## 4. Imagery & Asset Guidelines
* **Hero Imagery:** Use high-resolution architectural photography of modern residential homes featuring clean, well-aligned rooftop solar arrays during golden hour lighting.
* **UI Graphics & Badges:** Maintain smooth drop shadows (`box-shadow`), semi-transparent backdrop filters (`backdrop-filter: blur`), and rounded corner radiuses (`12px` to `20px`) for all floating cards to create depth and layering.
