# Fjällräven Keb Eco Jacket Product Page

- **Canvas Size:** 1200px × 800px (Desktop view, scales to full screen)
- **Design Style:** Neumorphic / Glassmorphism UI card overlaying a photographic nature background.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Background Base / Forest: `#111` blended with foggy mountain forest photography.
  - Card Background (Left/Right blend): Semi-transparent dark olive-gray gradients ranging from `rgba(40, 42, 38, 0.85)` to `rgba(75, 72, 60, 0.7)`.
  - Accent / Text Gold/Sand: `#E5C085` (used for titles, active states, buttons, sliders).
  - Text Muted: `#C5BFA8` (used for secondary labels and unselected sizes).
  - Dark UI Elements: `#2B2B28` (color swatch blocks).
- **Typography:**
  - Font Family: Clean, modern sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Montserrat, or Roboto).
  - Product Brand: 12px, uppercase, tracking/letter-spacing wide, `#C5BFA8`.
  - Product Title ("KEB ECO JACKET"): 36px–42px, bold, uppercase, stacked in two lines, `#E5C085`.
  - UI Labels ("COLOR", "SIZE", "SLEEVE LENGTH", "CHEST"): 10px–11px, uppercase, letter-spacing wide, `#C5BFA8`.
  - Measurements ("66 CM", "102 CM"): 11px, `#E5C085`.
  - Button Text ("UPDATE"): 13px, bold, uppercase, dark charcoal `#2E2A22`.

## Layout Structure

### 1. Full-Screen Background
- **Layer:** Bottom-most background image.
- **Content:** Atmospheric, misty, high-altitude pine/larch forest in autumn colors (muted greens, browns, and golden highlights).
- **Overlay:** Soft radial and linear gradient vignette to darken edges and focus attention on the central card element.

### 2. Central UI Card Container
- **Position:** Centered horizontally and vertically (`max-width: 900px`, `height: 580px`, `border-radius: 20px`).
- **Background Effect:** Frosted glass effect (backdrop-filter: blur) with a subtle multi-stop linear gradient transitioning from olive-taupe on the left to deeper charcoal-brown on the right.
- **Shadows:** Soft, deep drop-shadow (`0 20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)`) to create depth against the forest background.

---

## Component Breakdown

### Left Section: Product Display & Branding
- **Brand Header:**
  - Fjällräven logo icon (small stylized arctic fox silhouette) followed by text `"Fjällräven"` positioned at the top-left of the left pane.
- **Product Title:**
  - `"KEB ECO JACKET"` in large, bold, warm sand/gold tone, aligned to the left with generous line spacing.
- **Product Visual:**
  - High-resolution product shot of the olive-green "Keb Eco Jacket" with hood up, centered in the left-hand column.
  - Drop shadow underneath the jacket giving it a floating, tactile appearance.
  - Abstract organic wave/curve graphic silhouette softly rendered in the background behind the jacket.

---

### Right Section: Product Customizer Panel
- **Layout:** Vertical stack with comfortable spacing between control groups.

#### A. Color Selector
- **Label:** `"COLOR"` (top-aligned small text).
- **Swatch Track:** Three horizontal selection indicators / color blocks representing available shades (e.g., light stone, olive green, dark charcoal).
- **Active State:** The middle olive-green swatch features an active selection border/marker.

#### B. Size Selector
- **Label:** `"SIZE"`
- **Options:** Circular pills for `[ S ]` `[ M ]` `[ L ]` `[ XL ]`
- **Active State:** Size `[ M ]` is highlighted with a distinct circular gold outline and matching text color, while others use a simpler muted border.

#### C. Sleeve Length Slider
- **Label:** `"SLEEVE LENGTH"` (left-aligned) alongside dynamic value `"66 CM"` (right-aligned).
- **Track:** Thin horizontal line with a thumb handle positioned at approximately 60% of the bar, matching the gold accent tone.

#### D. Chest Slider
- **Label:** `"CHEST"` (left-aligned) alongside dynamic value `"102 CM"` (right-aligned).
- **Track:** Thin horizontal line with a thumb handle positioned at approximately 40% of the bar.

#### E. Action Button ("UPDATE")
- **Shape:** Pill/rounded rectangle button spanning the lower portion of the right control panel.
- **Styling:** Solid soft-gold fill (`#E5C085`) with subtle inner/outer shadow for a tactile, clickable appearance.
- **Text:** Centered `"UPDATE"` in dark, bold uppercase lettering.
