# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Modern SaaS Dashboard & Logistics Glassmorphism. Clean, lightweight card-based UI with ambient multi-layered depth and subtle frosted-glass overlays.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio with precise 8pt scaling. Focus is strictly centered on actionable delivery states, metadata tags, and clean card segmentation.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-tier depth system. Background canvas uses a soft radial gradient mesh (light icy blue to pale violet-blue). Surface cards utilize crisp white/translucent layers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) paired with ultra-soft, wide-spread drop shadows and clean 1px solid structural borders.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges (`LABEL CREATED`), interactive stepper dots with active glow, translucent frosted backgrounds, blurred background flanking preview cards (`blur(4px)`).
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.02em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#F1F5F9`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="inline-block w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500" />`
