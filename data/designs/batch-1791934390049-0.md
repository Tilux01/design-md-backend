# Axona Landing Page Design Specification (`design.md`)

## 1. Overview & Layout
A full-screen, single-viewport neurological health landing page featuring a dark teal aesthetic, layered typography, floating contextual tags, and an ethereal central portrait.

- **Viewport**: 100vw, 100vh (overflow hidden or auto).
- **Layout Model**: Flexbox/CSS Grid with layered absolute positioning for floating elements and background trails.

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Background Gradient**: Radial gradient centered at `50% 40%`.
  - Core Glow: `#1a5b6e`
  - Midtones: `#0f3a47`
  - Base / Edges: `#09222c` to `#05151c`
- **Text Primary**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Text Secondary / Muted**: `#B8D3DC`
- **Glass / Frosted UI**:
  - Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`
  - Backdrop Blur: `12px`
- **Button Surfaces**:
  - White Button: `#FFFFFF` (Text: `#09222c`)
  - Hover: `#E6F0F2`

---

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Font**: *Plus Jakarta Sans* or *Satoshi*
- **Alternative / Fallback**: *Inter*, `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `sans-serif`
- **Hierarchies**:
  - **Hero Title**: `font-size: clamp(2.5rem, 5vw, 4.5rem)`, `font-weight: 400`, `letter-spacing: -0.03em`, `line-height: 1.1`.
  - **Subheadline**: `font-size: 0.95rem`, `font-weight: 300`, `color: #B8D3DC`, `max-width: 420px`.
  - **Navigation Links**: `font-size: 0.85rem`, `font-weight: 400`, `color: #FFFFFF`.
  - **Tag Text**: `font-size: 0.75rem`, `font-weight: 400`, `color: #E0EFF2`.
  - **Buttons**: `font-size: 0.85rem`, `font-weight: 500`.

---

## 4. Visual Assets & Imagery

### Central Subject Image
- **Description**: Front-facing portrait of a Black male with a buzz cut and serious, neutral expression, wearing a dark mock-neck collar.
- **Type**: Backgroundless PNG/WebP with soft feathering around the lower torso.
- **Visual Effect**: Long exposure/shutter-drag echo. 
  - Central instance: 100% opacity, sharp focus.
  - Symmetrical duplicates: 2–3 transparent copies (`opacity: 0.1` to `0.25`) fading horizontally to both the left and right sides to visualize mental dissociation/fog.
- **Placement**: Centered horizontally (`left: 50%`, `transform: translateX(-50%)`), positioned vertically behind the primary headline and above the bottom fold.

### Brand Glyph
- Symmetrical, geometric vertical spinal/neural emblem.
- Displayed twice: left of "Axona" in the header, and centered directly above the headline "Reclaim your".

---

## 5. Components & Structure

```
├── Header (fixed/absolute, top: 0, w: 100%, z-index: 50)
│   ├── Left: Logo (Glyph + "Axona")
│   ├── Center: Pill Navigation Bar
│   └── Right: CTA Pill ("Book a Consultation →")
├── Hero Stage (relative, h: 100vh, flex-center)
│   ├── Background Subject + Trailing Silhouettes
│   ├── Floating Pill Badges (absolute)
│   │   ├── "Depression" (top-left of subject)
│   │   ├── "Brain Rot" (mid-left of chest)
│   │   └── "Anxiety" (mid-right of chest)
│   └── Foreground Content (bottom-center aligned)
│       ├── Centered Brand Glyph
│       ├── Headline: "Reclaim your <br/> Mental Health"
│       ├── Subheadline
│       └── Pill CTA Button: "Explore Neurology"
```

### Component Details
1. **Navigation Pill**:
   - Container: Flex row, gap `1.5rem`, padding `0.5rem 1.25rem`, border-radius `9999px`, frosted glass styling.
   - Links: Neurology, Brain Health, Diagnostics, About.

2. **Floating Pill Badges**:
   - Styling: Rounded capsules (`border-radius: 9999px`), frosted glass surface (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), subtle glow.
   - Positioning:
     - `Depression`: `top: 36%`, `left: 33%`
     - `Brain Rot`: `top: 46%`, `left: 38%`
     - `Anxiety`: `top: 43%`, `right: 34%`

3. **CTAs**:
   - Both buttons use full pill shape (`rounded-full`), white background, dark teal text, subtle hover lift (`translateY(-1px)`).
