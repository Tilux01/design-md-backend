# Design Specification: Selecta Landing Page Clone

This document provides the exact specifications to build a pixel-perfect, full-screen clone of the provided Selecta landing page design. All wireframe containers and layout grid lines shown in the reference image should be omitted in the final implementation.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Fonts
*   **Primary Typeface:** `Inter`, sans-serif (Fallback: `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, sans-serif).
*   **Headings:** Bold to Extra-Bold weights (700/800), tight letter-spacing.
*   **Body:** Regular to Medium weights (400/500), neutral dark gray (`#111111` or `#1a1a1a`).

### Color Palette
*   **Background:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
*   **Primary Text:** Near Black (`#111111`)
*   **Secondary Text (Muted):** Cool Gray (`#666666` or `#888888`)
*   **Button Dark:** Near Black (`#111111`) with White text (`#FFFFFF`)
*   **Button Outline / Borders:** Light Gray (`#E0E0E0`)
*   **Card Backgrounds / Borders:** White background with subtle 1px light gray borders (`#EAEAEA`).

---

## 2. Layout & Structure

*   **Width:** Full-screen responsive layout constrained to a maximum content width of approximately `1200px` centered horizontally with generous padding.
*   **Vertical Flow:** Structured into clear vertical sections:
    1.  Navigation Bar
    2.  Hero Section
    3.  Social Proof / Client Logo Banner
    4.  Benefits Section Header
    5.  Three-Column Feature Cards Grid

---

## 3. Section-by-Section Specifications

### A. Navigation Bar (`<header>`)
*   **Layout:** Flexbox, space-between alignment, sticky or fixed to the top with a pure white background.
*   **Left (Logo):** 
    *   Selecta Logo icon (black abstract curved shape) followed by the brand name **Selecta** in bold sans-serif.
*   **Center (Links):** 
    *   Nav links with subtle hover states: `Product`, `Customers`, `Pricing`, `Blog`, `Resources`. Font size: `14px`, Color: `#444444`.
*   **Right (Action):** 
    *   `Login` text button or bordered pill button with subtle rounded corners (`border: 1px solid #E0E0E0`, padding: `8px 16px`, font-size: `14px`).

---

### B. Hero Section
*   **Tag Badge:** 
    *   Centered pill badge: black background box containing the text **NEW** in white, followed by space and **WORKFLOW AUTOMATION** in dark text, enclosed inside a light gray bordered container with rounded corners (`border-radius: 4px`, uppercase, font-size: `11px`, letter-spacing: `0.5px`).
*   **Main Headings:**
    *   Line 1: **Security & Approvals for** (Font size: ~`52px`, weight: `800`, color: `#111111`).
    *   Line 2: **AI-Driven Workflows** (Font size: ~`52px`, weight: `800`, color: `#A0A0A0` / light gray gradient/solid tone).
*   **Subtitle Paragraph:**
    *   Centered text: *"Centralize approvals, enforce security policies, and keep AI systems compliant across every operation."* (Font size: `16px`, color: `#666666`, max-width: `550px`).
*   **CTA Buttons:**
    *   Primary Button: **Book free Demo** (Solid black background `#111111`, white text, rounded corners, padding `12px 24px`, font-weight: `600`).
    *   Secondary Button: **Try it now** (White background, black text, light gray border `#CCCCCC`, rounded corners, padding `12px 24px`, font-weight: `600`).
*   **Hero Central Image:**
    *   **Description:** A high-contrast, black-and-white dithered/halftone dot-matrix illustration of a curved, sweeping architectural arch or wave structure viewed from a low angle. 
    *   **Properties:** Backgroundless (transparent PNG/SVG), centered horizontally below the CTA buttons, maintaining an aspect ratio that spans roughly 60% of the container width.

---

### C. Social Proof (Trusted by industry leaders)
*   **Heading:** Centered small uppercase label: *"Trusted by industry leaders"* (Font size: `12px`, color: `#888888`, letter-spacing: `1px`, margin-bottom: `24px`).
*   **Logos Row:** 
    *   Evenly spaced horizontal flex container displaying partner/client logos in dark monochrome:
        1.  **Juicebox** (with icon)
        2.  **valley** (with asterisk/star icon)
        3.  **Hadrius** (with geometric polygon icon)
        4.  **Sameday.** (bold dot typography)
        5.  **BuySellAds**
        6.  **intangible**
    *   Logos should have low opacity (`opacity: 0.7`) and scale uniformly.

---

### D. Benefits Section Header
*   **Tag Badge:** Small pill with a square bullet icon and the text **BENEFITS** (`font-size: 11px`, uppercase, tracking `1px`).
*   **Heading:** 
    *   **Join modern teams** (bold black) **managing AI operations with confidence** (muted gray). 
    *   Font size: ~`36px`, weight: `700`.

---

### E. Three-Column Feature Cards Grid
*   **Layout:** CSS Grid with 3 equal columns, separated by thin borders (`1px solid #EAEAEA`).
*   **Card Structure:** Each card contains:
    1.  **Category Tag:** Small uppercase pill at the top-left (`WORKFLOW AUTOMATION`, `SECURITY & GOVERNANCE`, `VISIBILITY & CONTROL`).
    2.  **Illustration:** Centered backgroundless image specific to each card:
        *   *Card 1 (Left):* A dithered/halftone 3D isometric cube/satellite module rendered in black and white with subtle perspective rotation.
        *   *Card 2 (Middle):* A dithered/halftone abstract sphere composed of layered horizontal circular slices/rings.
        *   *Card 3 (Right):* A dithered/halftone 3D cross-shaped mechanical node or satellite connector floating centrally.
    3.  **Card Title:** Bold heading (`font-size: 18px`, weight: `700`, color: `#111111`):
        *   *Card 1:* Centralized approvals for AI workflows
        *   *Card 2:* Built-in security and policy enforcement
        *   *Card 3:* Real-time oversight across operations
    4.  **Card Description:** Body text (`font-size: 14px`, color: `#666666`, line-height: `1.5`):
        *   *Card 1:* Submit, track, and approve requests across teams with clear ownership and full auditability.
        *   *Card 2:* Apply consistent security controls and approval rules across all AI-powered processes.
        *   *Card 3:* Monitor activity, surface risks, and maintain control as AI systems evolve and scale.

---

## 4. Implementation Checklist for Developer
1.  **Omit all layout grid overlays, bounding boxes, and alignment crosses** present in the source wireframe. Ensure a clean, production-ready UI.
2.  Import `Inter` via Google Fonts.
3.  Ensure exact spacing consistency (use generous vertical whitespace/padding between sections, roughly `80px` to `120px` intervals).
4.  Export all dithered illustrations as transparent background vector SVGs or high-resolution transparent PNGs to maintain the exact dot-matrix monochrome aesthetic.
