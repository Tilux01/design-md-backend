# Design Specification: Light Bulb Landing Page

This document outlines the exact design specifications for a dark-themed, minimalist product landing page centered around a light bulb theme.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

### Color Palette
*   **Background (Primary Dark):** `#1e1e1e` (approx. dark charcoal/gray)
*   **Accent Color (Orange):** `#ff5a2c` (used for numbers, buttons, icons, and highlights)
*   **Primary Text (White):** `#ffffff`
*   **Secondary Text (Muted Gray):** `#b0b0b0`
*   **Subtle Text / Vertical Label (Dark Gray):** `#4a4a4a`
*   **Footer Background:** `#181818` (slightly darker than the main stage)

### Typography
*   **Font Family:** Modern Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter, Roboto, or Helvetica Neue)
*   **Main Title ("Be The Light."):** 
    *   Size: Large display (approx. 36px - 44px)
    *   Weight: Light / Thin (`300`)
    *   Color: White (`#ffffff`)
*   **Section Number ("01"):** 
    *   Size: Extra large display (approx. 48px)
    *   Weight: Bold (`700`)
    *   Color: Accent Orange (`#ff5a2c`)
*   **Body Copy:** 
    *   Size: Small (approx. 11px - 13px)
    *   Weight: Regular (`400`)
    *   Line Height: Generous (approx. 1.5)
    *   Color: Muted Gray (`#b0b0b0`)
*   **Navigation & Footer Links:** 
    *   Size: Small uppercase/lowercase (approx. 12px)
    *   Weight: Medium (`500`)

---

## 2. Layout Structure (Full Screen)

The viewport is divided into three main visual zones:
1.  **Header (Top Bar):** Logo on the left, Menu button on the right.
2.  **Main Content Area (Center Stage):** 
    *   Left: Product Image (Vintage Edison-style bulb).
    *   Center: Vertical background text ("Light Bulb.").
    *   Right: Information block (Step number, title, horizontal rule, descriptive text).
3.  **Footer (Bottom Bar):** Social links (Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest) and a scroll/pagination button.

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### Header
*   **Logo (Top Left):** 
    *   Custom monogram icon (intertwined stylized letters inside a rounded rectangle/circle).
    *   Color: White (`#ffffff`).
*   **Menu (Top Right):** 
    *   Four small square dots arranged in a 2x2 grid, followed by the text **"Menu"**.
    *   Color: White (`#ffffff`).

### Main Content Area

*   **Left Column (Image Showcase):**
    *   **Visual:** High-resolution photograph of an incandescent Edison bulb against a moody, neutral gray background.
    *   **Focus:** Detailed view of the glowing orange filament.
    *   **Framing:** Occupies roughly the left 45% of the viewport width.

*   **Center Column (Vertical Watermark):**
    *   **Text:** `Light Bulb.`
    *   **Orientation:** Vertical (rotated 90 degrees or stacked letters reading upwards/downwards).
    *   **Styling:** Low-opacity, blending subtly into the dark background (`#4a4a4a`).

*   **Right Column (Information Block):**
    *   **Number:** `01` (Orange, Bold).
    *   **Separator Line:** A thin horizontal divider line beneath the number (`#3a3a3a`).
    *   **Heading:** `Be The Light.` (White, Light weight).
    *   **Paragraph:** 
        > *"The filament is protected from oxidation with a glass or fused quartz bulb that is filled with inert gas or a vacuum. In a halogen lamp, filament evaporation is slowed by a chemical process that redeposits metal vapor onto the filament, thereby extending its life."*
    *   **Alignment:** Left-aligned text block.

### Footer Bar
*   **Left Side:** 
    *   An orange envelope/mail icon accompanied by minimal padding.
*   **Center:** 
    *   Social media links, each preceded by a small right-facing triangle (▶) bullet:
        *   `▶ facebook`
        *   `▶ instagram`
        *   `▶ pinterest`
    *   Color: White/Light gray labels with orange bullets.
*   **Right Side:** 
    *   A solid orange square button containing a white downward-pointing arrow icon (`↓`).
    *   Serves as a "Next Slide" or "Scroll Down" action trigger.
