# Design Specification: Team Section

## 1. Color Palette

*   **Background**: `#F6F6F6` (Neutral off-white)
*   **Grid Lines**: `#E5E5E5` (Subtle dotted/dashed border)
*   **Primary Text**: `#111111` (Deep black)
*   **Secondary Text / Subheadings**: `#555555` / `#777777` (Muted gray)
*   **Meta Labels**: `#888888` (Light neutral)
*   **Watermark**: `#EBEBEB` (Very faint gray)
*   **Pill Button Background**: `#FFFFFF` with `#E5E5E5` border

---

## 2. Typography

*   **Primary Display & Body Font**: *Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, or *Neue Haas Grotesk*
    *   *Fallback*: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
*   **Monospace Tag Font**: *JetBrains Mono* or *Space Mono*
    *   *Fallback*: `SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, monospace`
*   **Hierarchy**:
    *   **Section Tag** (`+ TEAM`): Monospace, 11px, Uppercase, Regular, `#888888`.
    *   **Main Title** (`Small team. Big standards.`): Display Sans, 48px–56px, Tight tracking (-0.03em), Leading 1.05. "Small team." at Semi-Bold (`#555555`), "Big standards." at Extra-Bold/Black (`#111111`).
    *   **Subtext**: Sans, 14px–15px, Regular, Line-height 1.4, `#555555`.
    *   **Member Names**: Sans, 13px, Semi-Bold, `#111111`.
    *   **Member Roles**: Sans, 11px, Regular, `#777777`.
    *   **Bottom Tag** (`Built by specialists`): Monospace, 10px, Regular, `#888888`.
    *   **Footer Statement**: Sans, 16px, Regular (`Behind every result is `) + Bold (`a team that cares.`).

---

## 3. Layout & Structure

*   **Container**: Full-width viewport layout (`min-height: 100vh`, `padding: 40px 60px`), dynamic vertical dashed lines dividing columns.
*   **Header Row**: Flexbox (space-between). Left holds the section tag and stacked title; right holds a constrained paragraph (max-width: 280px).
*   **Team Grid**: 4 equal-width columns (`gap: 16px`).
*   **Footer Row**: Multi-column base containing a large watermark background, left-aligned statement, and right-aligned CTA block.

---

## 4. Components & Content

### A. Team Cards (4-Column Layout)
Each card consists of an image container followed by a metadata row.

*   **Card Container**: Border radius `6px`, overflow hidden.
*   **Image Specs**: Aspect ratio `4:5`, grayscale filter, high contrast.
*   **Meta Row**:
    *   Left: Name (top), Title (bottom).
    *   Right: Social action icons (X icon, LinkedIn square badge) sized `14px`, gray stroke.

### C. Footer & Background Details
*   **Watermark**: Huge typography ("KUDOS") spanning horizontally behind the bottom section, opacity `0.06`, non-interactive (`pointer-events: none`).
*   **Bottom Statement**: Left-aligned, vertically aligned with CTA.
*   **CTA Button**:
    *   *Label*: "More about us" with a vertical 3-dots icon on the far right.
