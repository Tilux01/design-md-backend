# Design Specification: E-Commerce Landing Page (Winter Collection)

## Global Styles & Variables

*   **Color Palette:**
    *   Background: `#F4F4F4` (Light grey, almost white)
    *   Dark/Header Background: `#1A1A1A` (Near black, top bar)
    *   Primary Text (Dark): `#111111`
    *   Muted Text (Grey): `#666666` / `#888888`
    *   Accent/White: `#FFFFFF`
*   **Typography:**
    *   Primary Font: Inter / System UI, Sans-Serif (Geometric, modern)
    *   Headings: Bold, uppercase tracking for small subtitles; clean sans-serif for main titles.
    *   Code/Details: Monospace style used for numbers like `<100>`, `<300>`, and indicators.
*   **Layout Structure:**
    *   Full viewport width (`100vw`, `100vh`) with a light grey background base.
    *   Grid-like, magazine/editorial design layout with absolute/flex positioning elements, crosshairs, UI arrows, and minimal borders.

---

## Component Breakdown

### 1. Top Navigation Bar (Dark Header)
*   **Position:** Fixed top, full width. Height approx. 60px. Background: `#1A1A1A`.
*   **Left Section:**
    *   Navigation Links (Uppercase, thin border containers): `CATALOG`, `ABOUT US`, `CONTACT US`.
*   **Right Section:**
    *   Icons: Wishlist (Heart icon), Cart (Shopping bag/cart icon), User Account (Profile icon). White color.

### 2. Header / Meta Information Area (Under Top Bar)
*   **Left Side (`<100>`):**
    *   Text: `<100>` followed by `"New collections of winter jackets"` (Small, muted grey font, uppercase/monospaced style).
*   **Right Side (`<300>`):**
    *   Text: `<300>` followed by `"stylishy dressed people with our things"`
    *   Sub-text: `"Quality that Withstands / Time and Temperature"`
*   **Graphic elements:** Small image preview thumbnail with arrows (`[ < > ]`) on the top-left area of the content.

### 3. Hero Section (Center Stage)
*   **Central Image:**
    *   A high-fashion dynamic cut-out photograph of a model jumping/floating, wearing a beige puffer jacket, patterned face covering, and layered oversized patchwork denim pants with light sneakers.
    *   Drop shadow beneath the floating model to create depth.

### 4. Left Sidebar Elements
*   **Vertical SHOP Thumbnail:**
    *   A small square photo block with vertical text: `S H O P` alongside a small preview image.
*   **Circular Action Button:**
    *   Black circle with a white diagonal arrow pointing up-right (`↗`).
*   **Pagination Dots:**
    *   Vertical stack of 4 small dots (top one black, others light grey).
*   **Callout Indicator Line:**
    *   A thin black line extending from a square bracket icon (`[▼]`) to text: `Go to catalog now` with a small dot anchor.
*   **Barcode & Typography Block:**
    *   Barcode graphic.
    *   Main Heading: **"Your Ultimate Defense Against the Coldest Chill"** (Bold, sans-serif, size ~32px).
    *   Body text: `***When winter's icy grip tightens____there's no room for compromise on warmth and protection.////`
    *   CTA Button: Black pill/rounded rectangle with white text: `Shop now ///` and a right arrow.

### 5. Right Sidebar Elements
*   **Weather/Temp Metric Box:**
    *   A bordered minimalist container with text: `"Find out how cold our jackets can withstand"` and a bracket icon with an asterisk/snowflake symbol inside (`[ * ]`).
*   **Size Selector:**
    *   Vertical column of circular radio/size buttons: `S`, `M`, `L`, `XL` (with `L` highlighted/active).
    *   Vertical alignment line ending with the year `2016`.

### 6. Bottom Right Carousel / Slider Component
*   **Navigation Controls:**
    *   `>>>` and `<<<` slider controls.
*   **Feature List (Bullet points with icons):**
    *   `① Unyielding Protection,`
    *   `℗ Uncompromising`
    *   `☺ Comfort`
*   **Mini Gallery Carousel:**
    *   Horizontal scrolling track containing 3 grayscale/desaturated editorial fashion thumbnails showing models in winter wear.
    *   Heart icon on the top-right of the active thumbnail.
    *   Pagination / scroll arrows (`>>`) on the right side.
*   **Brand Logo/Stamp:**
    *   Stylized overlapping circular monogram logo (`CC`) at the bottom left of the carousel module.

---

## Responsive & Interactive Notes
*   **Hover States:** Buttons (like `Shop now` and the circular arrow) should invert colors or scale slightly on hover.
*   **Grid Accents:** Technical UI crosses (`+` or corner brackets `[ ]`) are scattered across the layout to give it an architectural, technical fashion-magazine aesthetic.
