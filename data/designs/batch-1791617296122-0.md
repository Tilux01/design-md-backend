# Design System & Implementation Specification: Aniwall Landing Hero

This document provides the exact visual, structural, and asset specifications required to recreate the design as an edge-to-edge, full-screen responsive web page.

---

## 1. Global Page Architecture

* **Viewport Bounds**: `100vw` × `100vh` (Single-screen above-the-fold layout, `overflow: hidden` on initial load).
* **Base Background**: Clean, studio-lit off-white/pearl-gray backdrop with soft, diffuse lighting.
  * **Background Color**: `#F8F9FB`
  * **Subtle Lighting Gradient**:
    ```css
    background: radial-gradient(
      circle at 60% 40%,
      #FFFFFF 0%,
      #F8F9FB 60%,
      #EEF0F5 100%
    );
    ```
* **Shadow Effects**: Soft, blurred ambient occlusion shadows cast beneath the central 3D ribbon toward the bottom-left and center floor plane (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06)`).

## 5. 3D Focal Artwork Specification

### 5.1 Asset Characteristics
* **Format**: High-resolution transparent background PNG or WebP with alpha channel.
* **Subject**: A dynamic, serpentine 3D helical wave composed of hundreds of cascading, semi-translucent glass/acrylic rectangular rectangular slabs (slats/dominoes).
* **Material & Shading**:
  * **Material Type**: Transparent holographic/iridescent acrylic with Fresnel reflection and chromatic dispersion.
  * **Color Spectrum**: Prismatic shifts between cyan (`#06B6D4`), electric blue (`#3B82F6`), soft lilac/magenta (`#D946EF`), and pearlescent frost-white edges.
  * **Refraction**: High edge highlights, clear glossy bevels, interior light scattering.

### 5.2 Geometry & Path Trajectory
* **Origin**: Originates from the upper-left off-screen margin (behind and extending past the navbar).
* **Path Arc**:
  * Forms a sweeping S-curve descending from top-left toward center.
  * Twists gently along its spine in 3D space, showing the top, side, and beveled faces of each rectangular unit.
  * Sweeps dynamically between the right tagline and the lower-left headline.
  * Continues descending toward the bottom-right corner, tapering off-screen.
* **Orientation / Tile Rotation**: Each slab is rotated progressively by approximately `2°` to `3°` along the path, creating a domino-topple / accordion fan effect.

### 5.3 Placement & Layering
* **Position**: `absolute`, covering the canvas (`width: 100%`, `height: 100%`, `object-fit: cover` or placed via an explicit canvas/container).
* **Container Alignment**:
  * Width: `~105%` of screen width.
  * Placed with pivot center aligned diagonally from top-left (`-5%`, `-5%`) through center to bottom-right (`100%`, `90%`).
* **Z-Index**: `10` (Behind textual content and interactive header, above canvas background).
* **Drop Shadow**: Soft, elongated ambient ground shadows directly projected onto the base background floor plane (`filter: drop-shadow(0 40px 60px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08))`).
