# Design System & Implementation Specification: "ТОЧКА ОПОРЫ"

## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Vibe
* **Genre:** Mental Health / Psychological Support / Therapy Platform.
* **Aesthetic:** Editorial, High-End Minimalist, Neo-Brutalist Grid Structure with Soft Humanistic Touches.
* **Mood:** Calm, Grounded, Safe, Modern, Empathetic yet Authoritative.
* **Viewport Base:** Designed for full-screen edge-to-edge desktop layouts (1920x1080 baseline) with a responsive fluid container grid.

## 3. Typography & Font Stacks

### Primary Font Families
1. **Display & Impact Headings (Condensed Uppercase):**
   * **Primary:** `Oswald`, `Druk Wide`, or `Bebas Neue`
   * **Fallbacks:** `"Arial Narrow", "Helvetica Neue Condensed", sans-serif-condensed, sans-serif`
   * **Characteristics:** Uppercase, tight letter-spacing, heavy weight (Bold / Extra Bold / Heavy).

2. **Hero Main Title Display ("ТОЧКА ОПОРЫ"):**
   * **Primary:** `Outfit`, `Montserrat`, or `Futura` (Bold, geometric sans-serif)
   * **Fallbacks:** `Arial, sans-serif`
   * **Letter Spacing:** Slightly expanded for "ОПОРЫ", standard tight for "ТОЧКА".

3. **Body & Navigation Text:**
   * **Primary:** `Inter`, `Roboto`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`
   * **Fallbacks:** `"Segoe UI", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif`
   * **Weights:** Regular (400), Medium (500).

## 8. Micro-Interactions & Hover States
1. **Nav Items (`МИССИЯ .`, etc.):** Slight opacity shift from `1.0` to `0.6` on hover.
2. **Action Buttons (`[ ЭТАПЫ ]`, `[ МАТЕРИАЛЫ ]`):** Soft scale transform `scale(1.03)` with faint drop shadow on hover.
3. **Card Gallery Images:** Subtle zoom-in effect on the image (`scale(1.05)`) within a hidden overflow image wrapper upon hovering individual grid cards.
