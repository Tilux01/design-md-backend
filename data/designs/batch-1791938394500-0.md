# Design Specification: Cyberpunk Interface Clone

## 1. General Layout & Composition
*   **Canvas:** Full-screen (16:9 aspect ratio).
*   **Grid System:** A modular grid with a heavy emphasis on the corners and a central "Hero" area.
*   **Background:** Deep charcoal/black (#0A0A0A) with a subtle digital grain or "noise" texture.
*   **Overlays:** A faint scanline effect (horizontal lines with 20% opacity) and occasional red glitch artifacts on the far edges.

## 2. Typography & Fonts
To achieve the "exact" look, use the following font hierarchy:

1.  **Primary Title Font (The "RUINER/HONOR" text):** 
    *   **Type:** Custom distressed brush/glitch font.
    *   **Recommended:** *Cyberpunk Waifus*, *Dead Kansas*, or *Urban Jungle*.
    *   **Fallback:** *Permanent Marker* (heavily italicized and tracked out).
    *   **Styling:** White color, slight outer glow (Cyan or White), 15-degree italic tilt.

2.  **Brand Logo (Top Left):** 
    *   **Font:** Official *Cyberpunk 2077* font.
    *   **Fallback:** *Reflex* or *Michroma*.

3.  **UI/Functional Text (Sidebars/Counters):**
    *   **Font:** *Rajdhani* (Bold/Medium) or *Orbitron*.
    *   **Fallback:** *Roboto Mono* or *Helvetica Condensed*.
    *   **Styling:** All caps, high letter spacing (tracking: 0.1em to 0.2em).

4.  **Body/Credit Text:**
    *   **Font:** *Arial* or *Inter* (Regular).
    *   **Size:** Very small (8pt - 10pt).
    *   **Opacity:** 60-70% white.

## 3. UI Component Details

### A. The Left Sidebar (Navigation)
*   **Position:** Top-left, below the main logo.
*   **Content:** A vertical stack of "WATCH" and "STEP" repeated.
*   **Visuals:** Each "WATCH" is contained in a solid white rectangular button with black text. "STEP" is ghost text (outline only or low opacity). A vertical dotted line runs down the left edge.

### B. The Header/Footer Branding
*   **Top Left:** "Cyberpunk" logo in white.
*   **Bottom Left:** "CD PROJEKT RED" in small, tracked-out sans-serif caps.
*   **Top Right:** A "Hamburger" menu icon consisting of three horizontal lines of varying lengths (top and bottom medium, middle long).
*   **Bottom Right:** A digital timer (e.g., `07 30 56`). The numbers are large, white, and separated by wide spaces.

### C. The "Point of No Return" Label
*   **Position:** Just above the main hero text.
*   **Styling:** Small yellow or white text. A thin horizontal line extends from the left of the text.

## 4. Hero Image Variations (Assets)
All hero images are **backgroundless (PNG)** or masked to blend into the dark background, featuring high-contrast lighting and "rim lighting" (glowing edges).

### Variation 1: "RUINER"
*   **Image Description:** A futuristic soldier in a white/light-grey tactical suit and a full-face black visor helmet. The character is angled toward the right, holding a high-tech rifle.
*   **Placement:** Centered, slightly shifted right.
*   **Rotation:** 0 degrees, but the character's pose creates a diagonal line from bottom-left to top-right.
*   **Key Detail:** A bright cyan/white light flare originates from the chest/shoulder area.

### Variation 2: "HONOR"
*   **Image Description:** A close-up of a heavy futuristic turret or vehicle-mounted machine gun. The word "POLICE" is visible on the side of the metallic casing.
*   **Placement:** Mid-ground, filling the left and center.
*   **Rotation:** Tilted 10 degrees downward toward the right.
*   **Key Detail:** Blue and green ambient lighting reflecting off the chrome metal.

### Variation 3: "POWR"
*   **Image Description:** A close-up of a "Night City Police Department" (NCPD) badge/patch on a dark tactical vest.
*   **Placement:** Right-side heavy.
*   **Rotation:** Tilted 15 degrees clockwise.
*   **Key Detail:** High texture detail on the fabric and a small "15.5" digital readout overlaying the badge.

### Variation 4: "FLAN"
*   **Image Description:** A close-up of a robotic/cyborg head with a red metallic finish. It features a single glowing cyan "eye" (camera lens) in the center.
*   **Placement:** Centered-left.
*   **Rotation:** Facing directly forward.
*   **Key Detail:** Glowing red vents on the "jaw" area and four small "TRYU" buttons at the bottom.

## 5. Interactive Elements (Small Widgets)
*   **Video Thumbnails:** Small rectangular boxes (approx 150px wide) placed near the bottom center. They contain a play icon (triangle) and a string of gibberish code (e.g., `FOR EKD3F0 DF`).
*   **Metadata Blocks:** Small blocks of text describing "Venom: The Deadly Guardian" or production credits. These are placed in the lower-left quadrant, usually aligned with the "CD PROJEKT RED" footer.

## 6. Color Palette (Hex Codes)
*   **Primary Black:** `#0A0A0A`
*   **Primary White:** `#FFFFFF`
*   **Accent Cyan:** `#00FFFF` (Used for glows and UI highlights)
*   **Accent Red:** `#FF003C` (Used for glitch effects and the "FLAN" helmet)
*   **Accent Yellow:** `#FCEE0A` (Used for the "Point of No Return" text)

## 7. Execution Notes
*   **Blending:** Use "Screen" or "Linear Dodge" blending modes for all light flares and glow effects.
*   **Masking:** The hero images should fade into black at the waist/bottom to ensure the footer text remains legible.
*   **Depth:** The main title text (e.g., "RUINER") should be placed *behind* the foremost part of the character/object but *in front* of the main body to create a 3D parallax effect.
