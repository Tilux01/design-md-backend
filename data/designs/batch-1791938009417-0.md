# Design Specification: CS:GO Modern UI Concept

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Background:** A deep, dark charcoal/navy gradient. 
    *   *Primary Background:* `#0F1219`
    *   *Secondary Surface (Cards/Panels):* `#1A1D26` with a slight border-radius of `8px`.
*   **Accent Color:** Vibrant Sky Blue (`#4D88FF`) used for active states, buttons, and toggles.
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   *Primary:* `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
    *   *Secondary/Muted:* `#8E9297` (Cool Gray)
*   **Overall Aesthetic:** Clean, flat design with subtle drop shadows and high-contrast typography.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Stratum 2` (The official CS:GO font).
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `Inter`, `Roboto`, `Segoe UI`, sans-serif.
*   **Weights:** 
    *   Bold (700) for headers and map names.
    *   Medium (500) for navigation and buttons.
    *   Regular (400) for wait times and subtext.
*   **Transform:** Most navigation and titles are `text-transform: uppercase`.

## 3. Layout Structure

### A. Top Navigation Bar
*   **Placement:** Fixed to the top, spanning 100% width.
*   **Left Section:** 
    *   **CS:GO Logo:** White monochrome logo.
*   **Center Section (Main Menu):** 
    *   Items: `PLAY`, `INVENTORY`, `WATCH`, `EVIDENCE`, `SETTINGS`.
    *   `PLAY` is highlighted in Blue (`#4D88FF`).
    *   `INVENTORY` has a blue circular badge with the number `2`.
*   **Right Section (User Profile):**
    *   **Avatar:** Circular image of a player (PashaBiceps).
    *   **User Info:** Stacked text. Top: `PASHABICEPS` (Bold, White). Bottom: `VIRTUS.PRO` (Smaller, Muted Gray).
    *   **Rank Icon:** A rectangular gold/blue badge (Global Elite rank icon).
    *   **Friends:** Text `FRIENDS` followed by a blue badge `32`. A small chevron `>` points right.

### B. Sub-Navigation (Game Modes)
*   **Placement:** Below the Top Bar, left-aligned.
*   **Items:** `COMPETITIVE`, `CASUAL`, `DEATHMATCH`, `WAR GAMES`, `WINGMAN`.
*   **Active State:** `COMPETITIVE` is white with a thick blue underline. Other items are muted gray.

### C. Main Content: Map Selection Grid
*   **Layout:** 4x2 Grid of Map Cards.
*   **Card Design:**
    *   **Background:** A high-quality, desaturated/darkened screenshot of the map.
    *   **Overlay:** A subtle dark gradient from the bottom to ensure text readability.
    *   **Top-Left Icon:** A circular blue checkmark icon (indicates selection).
    *   **Center-Left Logo:** The specific map's official logo (e.g., the Mirage "A" logo, Inferno's shield).
    *   **Bottom Text:** 
        *   Map Name (e.g., `MIRAGE`): Large, Bold, White, Uppercase.
        *   Wait Time (e.g., `WAIT TIME: 1:32`): Small, Muted Gray, Uppercase.
*   **Selection State:** Selected maps have a bright blue circular checkmark. Unselected maps (bottom row) have a muted gray checkmark and the image is significantly darkened/desaturated.

### D. Right Sidebar (Matchmaking Settings)
*   **Placement:** Right side, vertically aligned with the map grid.
*   **Lobby Toggle:** `SOLO` vs `LOBBY (0)`. `SOLO` is active with a blue underline.
*   **Settings Toggles:**
    *   `PRIME ACCOUNT STATUS`: Label on left, Blue toggle switch (ON) on right.
    *   `FRIENDS/NEARBY PLAYERS`: Label on left, Gray toggle switch (OFF) on right.
*   **Selected Maps List:**
    *   Header: `SELECTED MAPS` (Muted Gray, Small).
    *   List: `MIRAGE, CACHE, OVERPASS, TRAIN` (White, Bold, Uppercase).
*   **Action Button:**
    *   **Text:** `FIND THE MATCH`
    *   **Style:** Large, full-width rectangular button.
    *   **Color:** Solid Blue (`#4D88FF`) with white bold text. Subtle outer glow/drop shadow.

## 4. Image Descriptions

1.  **Background Environment:** A blurred, cinematic shot of a Counter-Terrorist or Terrorist character holding a rifle. The image is backgroundless (blends into the dark UI theme) and occupies the full screen behind the interface panels.
2.  **Map Thumbnails:**
    *   **Mirage:** View of A-site looking toward Palace/Stairs. Warm, sandy tones.
    *   **Inferno:** View of Banana/B-site entrance. Stone textures, overcast lighting.
    *   **Cache:** View of Mid/Checkers area. Industrial, green/gray tones, red shipping container.
    *   **Overpass:** View of the "Monster" tunnel entrance with graffiti. Concrete textures.
3.  **Map Logos:** Small, transparent PNGs placed above the map names.
    *   *Mirage:* Red/White "A" site icon.
    *   *Inferno:* Blue/Red shield icon.
    *   *Cache:* Red star/industrial icon.
    *   *Overpass:* Yellow/Black bridge icon.
4.  **User Avatar:** A high-resolution headshot of a person, cropped into a perfect circle with a thin gray border.

## 5. Interaction Details
*   **Hover States:** Map cards should slightly brighten or scale (1.02x) when hovered.
*   **Transitions:** Smooth 0.2s ease-in-out for toggle switches and navigation underlines.
*   **Scroll:** The main map grid should be scrollable if more maps are added, while the sidebar and top nav remain fixed.
