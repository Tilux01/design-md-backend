# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Modern Enterprise SaaS / High-End AI Agency & Strategy Dashboard, combining clean deep blue canvas tones with floating glassmorphism cards and editorial typography.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio in hero sections, balanced with dense interactive card clusters and floating card stacks. Generous padding (`40px` to `64px`) framing the viewport.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered depth using semi-transparent frosted glass (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), subtle 1px border highlights (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), and soft ambient glow orbs behind interactive cards.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient blue glow orbs, frosted glassmorphism cards with overlapping 3D tilt effects, status indicator dots.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `6px 16px`, font-size: `12px`, font-weight: `600`, tracking: `0.05em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-400 inline-block"></span>`
