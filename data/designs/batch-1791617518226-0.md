# Design System & Implementation Specification (`design.md`)

This document provides the exact design specifications to recreate the full-screen NFT/Digital Art landing page layout with pixel-level precision.

---

## 1. Global Page Architecture & Canvas

- **Target Display:** Full-screen responsive viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`, `min-height: 100vh`).
- **Canvas Base Color:** Light gray/off-white neutral matte finish.
  - Background: `#ECECEC` (or ultra-subtle radial gradient: center `#F5F5F5` to edge `#EAEAEA`).
- **Layout Paradigm:** Modern high-key minimalist editorial layout with absolute/flex positioning, anchored around a high-detail 3D transparent central asset.
- **Content Padding:**
  - Desktop Viewport Margin: Top/Bottom `40px` (or `4vh`), Left/Right `60px` (or `4.5vw`).
  - Maximum Container Width: `100%` fluid up to `1920px`.

---

## 2. Typography System

### Font Families
- **Primary Display Font (Headings):**
  - *Recommended:* **Clash Display** (Bold / 700) or **Plus Jakarta Sans** (ExtraBold / 800).
  - *Fallbacks:* Syne, Montserrat (700/800), system sans-serif (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`).
- **Secondary Body & UI Font:**
  - *Recommended:* **Inter**, **General Sans**, or **Plus Jakarta Sans** (Regular / 400 & Medium / 500).
  - *Fallbacks:* Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif.

### Type Scale & Hierarchy
1. **Logo (`IMMER`):**
   - Font Size: `18px` (`1.125rem`), Weight: `700` (Bold), Letter-spacing: `0.18em`, Text-transform: `uppercase`.
2. **Main Hero Headline:**
   - Font Size: `clamp(48px, 5.5vw, 76px)`, Weight: `800` (ExtraBold), Line-height: `1.08`, Letter-spacing: `-0.03em`.
3. **Hero Paragraph Description:**
   - Font Size: `12px` (`0.75rem`), Weight: `400` (Regular), Line-height: `1.6`, Color: `#5F5F5F`.
4. **Navigation Links:**
   - Font Size: `13px` (`0.8125rem`), Weight: `500` (Medium), Color: `#1A1A1A`.
5. **Right Side Meta List:**
   - Active Item (`• Silhouette`): `13px`, Weight: `700` (Bold), Color: `#1A1A1A`.
   - Inactive Items: `13px`, Weight: `500` (Medium), Color: `#8C8C8C`.
6. **Footer Information:**
   - Section Title (`Digital Art`, `Start Watching`): `14px`, Weight: `700` (Bold), Color: `#1A1A1A`.
   - Section Subtitle (`What you should know`): `12px`, Weight: `400`, Color: `#333333`.
   - Descriptive Copy: `10px`–`11px`, Line-height: `1.5`, Color: `#666666`.
   - Video Subtitle: `10px`–`11px`, Line-height: `1.4`, Color: `#666666`.

### 4.1 Header / Top Navigation Bar
- **Dimensions & Placement:** Spans `100%` width; flexbox layout with `justify-content: space-between; align-items: center;`.
- **Left Element (Logo):**
  - Content: `IMMER`
  - Style: Wrapped in a crisp, non-rounded border (`1.5px solid #111111`), padding `4px 10px`, tight letter-spacing.
- **Center Element (Nav Links):**
  - Links: `Home`, `Collections`, `Creators`, `About`
  - Gap: `36px` to `48px` between items.
  - Interaction: Default cursor pointer, subtle hover opacity shift (`0.65`).
- **Right Element (Action Button):**
  - Content: `Contact us`
  - Style: Outlined pill/slight rounded rectangle (`border: 1px solid #111111; border-radius: 2px; padding: 7px 18px;`).
  - Text: `12px`, Medium weight.

---

### 4.2 Left Hero Section
- **Positioning:** Aligned to the left margin, vertically centered/stacked in the left 40% of the screen.
- **Headline Structure:**
  ```text
  Discover
  & Collect
  Nfts ⤻
  ```
  - *Capitalization note:* "Discover" (Titlecase), "& Collect" (Titlecase), "Nfts" (Titlecase with lowercase 'fts').
  - **Doodle Arrow Asset:** Immediately following "Nfts" is a hand-drawn, minimalist stroke vector arrow. It features a loose clockwise spiral loop at the lower left and arches upward-right with an open chevron arrowhead (`stroke: #111111; stroke-width: 1.8px; fill: none;`).
- **Hero Body Copy:**
  - Copy: `Join our digital are community and have access to a large number of the beautiful art .` *(Note original grammar spacing).*
  - Max-width: `260px` to `300px`.
  - Margin: `24px 0 36px 0`.
- **CTA Button ("Join Now" Circular Motif):**
  - Concept: Text intersecting an open circular wireframe line.
  - Circle: Diameter `64px`, `border: 1.2px solid #111111`, `border-radius: 50%`.
  - Text: `Join Now` positioned horizontally, centered or slightly offset to the left edge of the circle, creating an overlapping modern graphic look.

---

### 4.3 Center 3D Hero Artwork (Asset Description & Placement)

- **Asset Specification:**
  - **Type:** Ultra-high-resolution isolated transparent PNG or WebP image (no background or card cutouts).
  - **Subject:** Surreal, futuristic deconstructed human facial sculpture/bust.
  - **Material & Texture:**
    - Primary surface is ultra-smooth matte white porcelain/ceramic with soft ambient occlusion shadows.
    - Floating modular organic droplets, spheres, and pill-shaped liquid forms detaching from the forehead, cheeks, and collar.
    - Deep geometric/fluid crevices revealing an inner holographic, iridescent liquid flow with vibrant gradient sheens of cyan, electric blue, violet-magenta, and molten orange/gold.
    - Glossy caustics and glass-like wet reflections along the borders where iridescent liquid meets white porcelain.
  - **Pose & Angle:**
    - Face is rotated ~30 degrees toward the right, chin elevated, looking upward and into the upper-right corner.
    - The neck and lower throat dissolve into floating smooth pebble-like porcelain forms.
  - **Position & Dimensions:**
    - CSS Placement: Centered in the canvas horizontally (`left: 52%`, `transform: translateX(-50%)`) or relative middle column in a 3-column grid.
    - Height: Approx. `70vh` to `78vh`.
    - Z-index: Layered behind nav items, but visually dominating the canvas depth.

- **Positioning:** Aligned towards the right edge, vertically centered (`top: 48%`).

- **Items:**

- **Positioning:** Fixed or flex-aligned at the lower viewport perimeter (`bottom: 40px`).
- **Width:** `100%`, flex row with space distributed between Left and Right info modules.

#### Left Module (Digital Art Explainer):

  - Line 2: `What you should know` (`font-weight: 400; font-size: 11px; color: #333;`)

  - `width: 1px`, `height: 38px`, `background: #B0B0B0`, `margin: 0 18px`.
- **Right Column Copy:**
  - Copy: `To join our close community of digital art designers and NFTs lovers the process is simple, click on the play button to see the full experience`
  - Max-width: `240px`–`270px`.
  - Font size: `9.5px`–`10.5px`, line-height: `1.45`, color: `#666666`.

#### Right Module (Start Watching CTA):
- **Flex Layout:** Horizontal group containing an interactive circular play button and two lines of descriptive copy.
- **Play Button Component:**
  - Outer Ring: Diameter `38px`–`42px`, `border: 1px solid #111111`, `border-radius: 50%`, flexbox centered.
  - Inner Circle: Diameter `24px`–`26px`, `background: #111111`, `border-radius: 50%`, flexbox centered.
  - Icon: Solid white equilateral play triangle pointing right (`width: 8px; height: 9px;`).
- **Text Column (Margin-left: `14px`):**
