# Design Specification: Ali Hassan Portfolio

## 1. Color Palette

* **Primary Dark / Text:** `#111111` (Deep Charcoal/Black)
* **Body / Secondary Text:** `#555555` (Muted Grey)
* **Background Light:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
* **Background Off-White / Contrast:** `#F9F9FB` (Subtle Cool Grey)
* **Accent Primary:** `#5E4B8B` / `#6C5CE7` (Medium Slate Purple)
* **Accent Pastel (CTA Banner & Hero Glow):** `#EAE8FA` (Soft Lavender)
* **Border / Divider:** `#E5E5EA` (Light Grey)

---

## 2. Typography

* **Headings (Display Serif):**
  * *Font:* `Playfair Display`, `Cormorant Garamond`, fallback `Georgia, serif`
  * *Style:* High-contrast modern serif with italics for emotional emphasis (e.g., *"A Lasting Impression."*).
* **Body & UI (Sans-Serif):**
  * *Font:* `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, fallback `Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif`
  * *Style:* Clean geometric/neo-grotesque sans-serif. Uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`1px` to `2px`) for labels and tags.
* **Signature Accent:**
  * *Font:* `Creattion`, `Brittany Signature`, fallback `cursive`

---

## 3. Global Layout & Structure

* **Max Width:** `1280px` centered with `24px` gutter on mobile, `64px` on desktop.
* **Base Grid:** 12-column flexible grid system.

---

## 4. Components & Sections

### 4.1 Navigation Bar
* **Position:** Fixed or sticky top, transparent/white background with bottom border `#F0F0F0`.
* **Left:** Brand lockup with abstract geometric `A/` monogram alongside stacked text "ALI HASSAN / GRAPHIC DESIGNER".
* **Center:** Horizontal nav list (`HOME`, `ABOUT`, `WORK`, `SERVICES`, `BLOG`, `CONTACT`) in uppercase 12px sans-serif. Active item has a bottom bar.
* **Right:** Button "LET'S TALK →", solid black fill, white text, 4px rounded corners.

### 4.2 Hero Section
* **Grid:** 2-column layout (55% text / 45% visual).
* **Left Column:**
  * Eyebrow: `HI, I'M ALI HASSAN` in purple uppercase sans-serif.
  * H1: "Designing Brands / That Leave / *A Lasting Impression.*"
  * Decorative horizontal rule: 40px width, `#5E4B8B`.
  * Subtitle text: 16px muted grey.
  * Button Group:
    * Primary: Black solid button `VIEW MY WORK →`.
    * Secondary: Ghost button with black border `ABOUT ME`.
  * Scroll Indicator: "SCROLL DOWN ↓" small vertical layout.
* **Right Column:**
  * Circular ambient backdrop in `#EAE8FA` with subtle dot-matrix pattern overlay.
  * Top-right circular stamp: "ALI HASSAN GRAPHIC DESIGNER" curved around a script signature.
  * **Hero Image:**
    * *Description:* Portrait cutout of a young man with dark wavy hair, beard, and clear-framed glasses, dressed in a black crew-neck sweater, facing slightly left.
    * *Treatment:* Backgroundless / transparent PNG, positioned over the purple circular shape.

### 4.3 Services Strip
* **Layout:** 4-column horizontal card grid bordered with thin `#E5E5EA` dividers.
* **Card Content:**
  * Purple line-art icon (Diamond for Brand Identity, Document for Print, Screen for Digital, Box for Packaging).
  * 13px bold uppercase title.
  * 12px muted description (max 2 lines).

### 4.4 Featured Work
* **Header:** Left eyebrow `FEATURED WORK` + H2 `Selected Projects`; right-aligned text button `VIEW ALL PROJECTS →`.
* **Grid:** 4-column equal-width portfolio gallery.
* **Card Structure:** 1:1 or 4:5 image container, followed by bold uppercase title and muted service category below.
* **Project Images:**
  1. *Noiré Parfum:* Front-facing luxury black square perfume bottle with ribbed cap beside its textured black box.
  2. *Avenue Studio:* Top-down corporate stationery mockup on white surface (letterheads, business cards, black folders).
  3. *The Taste:* Angled view of an elegant black textured restaurant menu card beside cutlery and styling props.
  4. *Lush Organics:* Isometric view of a minimalist white cosmetic packaging box featuring delicate botanical line art.

### 4.5 About & Statistics
* **Layout:** 3-column asymmetric grid (4 cols text / 4 cols image / 4 cols stats).
* **Left Sub-column:**
  * Eyebrow `ABOUT ME`, H2 quote: "Design is not just what it looks like, it's how it works."
  * Descriptive paragraph, handwritten signature graphic, and outline button `DOWNLOAD CV [icon]`.
* **Center Sub-column (Image):**
  * *Description:* Interior desk vignette featuring a vertical black framed print with "GOOD DESIGN IS GOOD BUSINESS.", a white vase with fluffy dried pampas grass, and a black mug containing pens and brushes on a white table.
  * *Treatment:* Rectangular photo with neutral white wall backdrop.
* **Right Sub-column (Stats):**
  * 4 vertically stacked rows.
  * Icon (outline purple/black) + Large Number (`5+`, `150+`, `100+`, `20+`) in serif/bold + uppercase label.

### 4.6 Call To Action Banner
* **Background:** Solid full-width or boxed `#EAE8FA` banner with rounded corners (8px).
* **Layout:** Flex row, space-between, vertically aligned.
* **Left:** Tagline `HAVE A PROJECT IN MIND?` followed by serif heading `Let's Create Something Amazing Together.`
* **Right:** Black button `LET'S WORK TOGETHER →`.

### 4.7 Footer
* **Layout:** 5-column layout on clean white background with top divider line.
* **Columns:**
  1. *Brand:* Logo monogram + name lockup.
  2. *Quick Links:* Vertical list of site links.
  3. *Services:* Vertical list of provided services.
  4. *Contact:* Email, phone, location with small line icons.
  5. *Follow Me:* Inline social icon links (Behance, Instagram, Dribbble, LinkedIn).
* **Copyright Row:** Centered `© 2024 Ali Hassan. All Rights Reserved.` with short top rule.
