# IVL Collective - Website Clone Design System & Specifications

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Color Palette
- **Primary Text / Headers:** Black (`#000000`)
- **Backgrounds:** 
  - Main Pages: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Accent Overlays/Sections: Deep Olive/Khaki (`#4B4B42` or similar editorial tone, dark muted gray/brown)
- **Announcement Bar:** Black background with White text (`#000000`)

### Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** Serif (Editorial style, e.g., Playfair Display or similar high-fashion serif) for headings and callouts.
- **Secondary Font Family:** Sans-serif (Clean, geometric, e.g., Montserrat, Inter, or Arial) for navigation, prices, and body copy.

---

## 2. Header & Navigation

- **Announcement Bar:**
  - Background: Black
  - Text: Uppercase, tracking/letter-spacing wide, white, font-size ~11px.
  - Copy: `DUE TO HIGH VOLUME OF ORDERS, SHIPPING MAY TAKE UP TO 5-7 BUSINESS DAYS`
- **Main Nav Bar:**
  - Layout: Flexbox, centered logo with links flanking.
  - Left Links: `NEW ARRIVALS`, `SHOP`, `ABOUT`, `STUDIO IVL`, `BLOG`
  - Logo: Centered, bold serif uppercase `IVL`
  - Right Links: `ACCOUNT`, `CART`
  - Spacing: Generous padding top/bottom (~20px).

---

## 3. Hero Section (Top Banner)

- **Layout:** Two-column split grid (50% text/editorial frame, 50% lifestyle imagery).
- **Left Column:**
  - Background: Off-white / Pure White
  - Heading (Serif, Italicized balance): 
    - `Crafted to` (Normal)
    - `seamlessly` (Italic)
    - `blend fashion` (Normal)
    - `with function.` (Italic)
  - CTA Button:
    - Text: `SHOP NEW ARRIVALS`
    - Style: Solid black background, white uppercase text, small padding, sharp borders.
- **Right Column:**
    - Full-bleed lifestyle photography of a model crouching in activewear (puffer jacket, leggings).

---

## 4. Marquee / Ticker Bar

- **Style:** Thin solid black bar running horizontally.
- **Content:** Repeating text `FREE SHIPPING ON ALL DOMESTIC ORDERS OVER $150.` separated by spacing.

---

## 5. "This Season’s Best Sellers" Section

- **Header:**
  - Title: `This Season’s Best Sellers` (Serif, left-aligned)
  - Action: `VIEW ALL BEST SELLERS` (Small, sans-serif, uppercase, right-aligned)
- **Product Carousel / Grid:**
  - 5-column horizontal product layout.
  - Each item contains:
    - Product Image (Clean backdrop / ghost mannequin / flat lay).
    - Product Title: Uppercase sans-serif (e.g., `PUFFER JACKET`, `CONTRAST LEGGING`).
    - Price: Sans-serif (e.g., `$218`, `$98`).

---

## 6. Full-Width Editorial Banner ("Style. Technical. Innovative.")

- **Layout:** Full-width container, dark editorial background image with model sitting wearing headphones and activewear.
- **Overlay Content:**
  - Left-aligned text box over dark background:
    - Heading (Serif): `Style. Technical. Innovative.`
    - CTA: `SHOP ACTIVEWEAR` (Uppercase, underlined or clean text link).

---

## 7. Scrolling Marquee / Ticker Bar 2

- **Style:** Thin solid black bar.
- **Content:** Repeating text `LOOK BETTER, WEAR COMFORTABLE ACTIVE WEAR.`

---

## 8. "Shop The Look" Section

- **Layout:** Two-column split layout.
- **Left Column (Flat Lay):**
  - Artistic flat-lay photograph showcasing a complete activewear outfit (jacket, sports bra, leggings, water bottle) arranged neatly on a textured surface.
- **Right Column:**
  - Section Title: `Shop The Look.` (Large serif)
  - Sub-Product Cards (3 columns):
    - Thumbnail image of individual items worn in the look.
    - Product name + Price.
    - `ADD TO CART` button (Black background, white uppercase text, compact).

---

## 9. Brand Values / "What We Stand For" Section

- **Layout:** Two-column split layout.
- **Left Column:**
  - Subtitle: `WE'RE MORE THAN AN ACTIVEWEAR BRAND` (Small, uppercase, letter-spaced)
  - Heading: `What We Stand For.` (Serif)
  - Body Copy: Standard paragraphs of brand mission lorem ipsum text.
  - CTA: `READ MORE` (Black solid button).
- **Right Column:**
  - Editorial photography featuring two models sitting together outdoors/stairs, laughing and wearing matching casual/active tops.

---

## 10. Global UI / Functional Rules
- **Buttons:** Sharp corners (0px border-radius), solid black fill, crisp white uppercase text. Hover states invert colors or adjust opacity slightly.
- **Images:** High-contrast, premium fitness-editorial aesthetic, mixing studio shots with natural outdoor lighting.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Minimalist use of thin 1px solid black or dark gray dividers where structural breaks occur.
