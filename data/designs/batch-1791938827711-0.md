# Design Specification: Netflix TV Interface Clone

## 1. Global Layout & Theme
*   **Background:** Solid Pitch Black (`#000000`).
*   **Viewport:** Full screen, 16:9 aspect ratio.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   Primary Red: `#E50914` (Netflix Red)
    *   Text Primary: `#FFFFFF` (White)
    *   Text Secondary: `#A3A3A3` (Grey)
    *   Overlay: Linear gradient from bottom-to-top and left-to-right (Black to Transparent) to ensure text legibility over hero images.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Netflix Sans`.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `Inter`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:**
    *   Light (300): Metadata and description.
    *   Regular (400): Sidebar labels and sub-headers.
    *   Medium (500): Titles in cards.
    *   Bold (700): Hero title and section headers.

## 3. Sidebar Navigation (Left)
*   **Width:** Approximately 5% of screen width.
*   **Background:** Transparent (icons float over the background).
*   **Alignment:** Vertically centered icons.
*   **Active State:** A vertical red bar (`#E50914`) 4px wide to the immediate left of the active icon.
*   **Icons (Top to Bottom):**
    1.  **Search:** Magnifying glass.
    2.  **Home (Active):** House icon.
    3.  **TV Shows:** Rectangular screen icon.
    4.  **Movies:** Film clapperboard icon.
    5.  **My List:** Plus (+) icon.
    6.  **Profile:** Square icon with rounded corners (User Avatar).

## 4. Hero Section (Top 60% of Screen)
*   **Background Image:** Full-width image (See *Asset 1*).
*   **Content Alignment:** Left-aligned, positioned roughly 10% from the left edge and 15% from the top.
*   **Elements:**
    *   **N-Film Logo:** Small Red "N" icon followed by the word "FILM" in spaced-out, uppercase white text.
    *   **Main Title:** "VJERAN TOMIC" (Small, uppercase) above "THE SPIDER-MAN OF PARIS" (Large, bold, uppercase).
    *   **Metadata Row:** 
        *   "New" (Green text: `#46D369`).
        *   "2023", "16+", "1h 26m" (White text).
        *   **Badges:** "Dolby Vision" and "5.1" enclosed in thin white rectangular borders.
    *   **Description:** 3-4 lines of text. Max-width: 40% of screen. Font size: Medium-small. Color: Light grey.

## 5. Content Rows (Bottom 40% of Screen)
*   **Section Header:** "Only on Netflix" (White, Bold, Font-size: 1.5rem).
*   **Card Container:** Horizontal flex row with 20px spacing between cards.
*   **Card Dimensions:** Vertical rectangles (approx. 2:3 aspect ratio).
*   **Card Styles:**
    *   **"Recently Added" Badge:** A solid red rectangle (`#E50914`) at the bottom of the card with white centered text.
*   **Secondary Header:** "Continue watching for Rasmus" (Partially visible at the very bottom).

## 6. Asset Descriptions (Images)

### Asset 1: Hero Background
*   **Description:** A high-angle, cinematic shot of a man (the burglar) dressed in a dark hoodie and dark trousers. He is captured mid-motion, scaling a stone ledge of a classical Parisian building. The Eiffel Tower is visible in the background against a hazy, orange-tinted sunset sky.
*   **Placement:** Fills the top 70% of the screen, fading into black at the bottom and left.
*   **Backgroundless:** No.

## 7. Interaction Details
*   **Focus State:** When a card is selected, it should scale up slightly (1.1x) and gain a thick white border.
*   **Scrolling:** The horizontal row should move left/right with a smooth transition (ease-in-out).
