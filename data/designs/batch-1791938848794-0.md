# Design Specification: 11Screen Dashboard

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

*   **Background Color:** `#F4F5FA` (A very light, cool-toned lavender-grey).
*   **Primary Accent Color:** `#7B61FF` (Soft violet used for active states, buttons, and notification dots).
*   **Surface Color:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for the main container and sidebar).
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   **Primary:** `#1A1A1A` (Near black for headings).
    *   **Secondary:** `#7E7E8E` (Medium grey for labels and inactive links).
    *   **Tertiary:** `#B0B0BC` (Light grey for metadata and placeholders).
*   **Border Radius:** 
    *   Main Container: `0px` (Full screen).
    *   Large Cards/Hero: `24px`.
    *   Small Cards/Buttons: `12px`.
*   **Spacing Unit:** `8px` (All margins and paddings should be multiples of 8).

## 2. Typography

*   **Primary Font:** `Inter`, `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `Oxygen`, `Ubuntu`, `Cantarell`, `Open Sans`, `Helvetica Neue`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Font Weights:** Regular (400), Medium (500), Semi-Bold (600), Bold (700).
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Logo:** 20px, Bold.
    *   **Section Headings:** 18px, Semi-Bold.
    *   **Card Titles:** 14px, Semi-Bold.
    *   **Body/Metadata:** 12px, Medium.

---

## 3. Layout Structure

The interface is a three-column layout:
1.  **Left Sidebar (15% width):** Navigation and branding.
2.  **Main Content (60% width):** Hero section and movie grids.
3.  **Right Sidebar (25% width):** Search, Profile, "Continue Watching," and "Friends Activity."

---

## 4. Left Sidebar (Navigation)

*   **Logo:** Top left, text "11Screen" in `#1A1A1A`.
*   **Navigation List:** Vertical list of items with icons.
    *   **Home (Active):** Background `#EEECFF`, Text/Icon color `#7B61FF`. Small vertical pill on the far left.
    *   **Channels, Favorites, Community, Playlist, History:** Default state (Secondary text color, no background).
    *   **Notification Dots:** Small `#7B61FF` circles next to "Community" and "Playlist".
*   **Bottom Section:** Separated by flexible space.
    *   **Settings, Log out:** Standard list items with icons.

---

## 5. Main Content Area

### Top Navigation & Search
*   **Tabs:** "Movies" (Active: Pill shape, violet background, white text), "Series", "Anime", "TV Shows" (Inactive: Grey text).
*   **Search Bar:** Located at the top right of the main area. Rounded capsule shape, light grey border, search icon on the right.
*   **User Profile:** To the right of the search bar. Circular avatar image with a small notification bell icon to its left.

### Hero Section (Featured)
*   **Card:** Large horizontal card with a background image.
*   **Image Description:** A cinematic shot from *Dune: Part Two*. The scene features two figures (Paul Atreides and Chani) standing in a vast, orange-hued desert landscape during sunset. The lighting is warm and dramatic. The image is full-bleed within the card.
*   **Content Overlay:**
    *   **Title:** "Dune Part Two" in large white bold text.
    *   **Description:** 2-3 lines of white text describing the film.
    *   **Buttons:** 
        *   "Watch" (Violet background, white text, play icon).
        *   "Add to list" (Blurred glass effect background or transparent with white border).
    *   **Social Proof:** Small overlapping circular avatars of friends who have watched it, located at the bottom right of the hero card.

### Movie Grids ("Trending now" & "Coming soon")
*   **Heading:** Text with a small right-facing chevron.
*   **Card Style:** Vertical aspect ratio (~2:3). Rounded corners.
*   **Images:**
    *   **Oppenheimer:** Close-up of Cillian Murphy with an orange/fire background.
    *   **The Witcher:** Geralt of Rivia in profile, cool blue/grey tones.
    *   **The Batman:** Moody, red-tinted shot of Robert Pattinson's Batman.
    *   **Wonka:** Vibrant, colorful shot of Timothée Chalamet.
*   **Card Details:** Title at the bottom in white text over a subtle dark gradient. A small white "HD" or "4K" tag in the top left corner of each card.

---

## 6. Right Sidebar (Activity)

### Continue Watching
*   **List Items:** Horizontal layout.
    *   **Thumbnail:** Small rounded rectangle with a play button icon centered and a thin progress bar at the bottom.
    *   **Text:** Title (e.g., "Barbie") in Semi-Bold, Subtext (e.g., "1h 20m left") in Tertiary color.
*   **Items:** Barbie, The Hunger Games, Ant-Man.

### Friends Activity
*   **List Items:**
    *   **Avatar:** Small circular profile picture.
    *   **Text:** Friend's Name (Semi-Bold) followed by "is watching [Movie Name]" (Regular/Secondary color).
*   **Items:**
    *   **James Gouse:** Watching "Stranger Things".
    *   **Adela Barber:** Watching "The Witcher".
    *   **Sam Dawson:** Watching "The Batman".
    *   **Jonathan Ray:** Watching "Wonka".

---

## 7. Images & Assets Details

*   **Backgrounds:** All movie thumbnails should have a slight dark gradient overlay at the bottom to ensure text legibility.
*   **Icons:** Use a consistent thin-stroke icon set (like Lucide or Phosphor Icons).
*   **Image Placement:**
    *   Hero image is centered and scaled to cover the card.
    *   Grid images are centered and scaled to cover.
    *   Avatars are perfectly circular.
*   **Rotation/Perspective:** The entire UI is presented flat (0-degree rotation). The outer "floating" effect in the reference image (the purple cloth background) should be ignored for the "full screen" implementation. The dashboard itself should take up 100vw and 100vh.
