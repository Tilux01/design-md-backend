# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist & Modern Dark/Light Portfolio UI.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with generous container padding (48px–64px vertical gaps), clean typography grouping, and asymmetric negative space layout.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat card layout on minimalist backdrop, utilizing subtle borders (`#E5E7EB` / `#2D2D2D`), clean grid layouts, and minimalist pill-shaped interactive triggers.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, minimalist monochrome palette, hover state expansion lists, subtle status indicators.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#F3F4F6`, border `1px solid #E5E7EB`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
