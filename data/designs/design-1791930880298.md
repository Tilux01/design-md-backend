# Design Specification: Dark Minimalist Portfolio

## 1. Overview
A sleek, dark-themed personal portfolio landing page featuring neon-green accents, vertical navigation, and a central hooded portrait framed by a glowing geometric ring. Designed for full-screen desktop viewing (100vw × 100vh).

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Background Black:** `#000000` (Pure Black) or `#050505`
* **Accent Neon Green:** `#00FF66` (Primary vibrant green for highlights, logo, ring, and mask accents)
* **Text Primary (White):** `#FFFFFF` / `#E5E5E5`
* **Text Secondary (Muted Gray):** `#888888`
* **Icon Fill (Light Gray):** `#D1D5DB`

---

## 3. Typography
* **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Poppins`
* **Fallback Fonts:** `Cabinet Grotesk`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`
* **Hierarchy:**
  * **Brand Mark ("Olympus / MONS"):**
    * "Olympus": Sans-serif, Regular, ~14px, Color: `#00FF66`
    * "MONS": Sans-serif, Bold, Uppercase, ~18px, Letter-spacing: +1.5px, Color: `#00FF66`
  * **User Identity ("Jane Doe"):** Sans-serif, Medium, ~15px, Color: `#E5E5E5`
  * **Hero Subtext ("Graphics Designer"):** Sans-serif, Regular, ~14px, Color: `#888888`
  * **Location Tag ("BAY AREA"):** Sans-serif, Semi-Bold, Uppercase, ~16px, Letter-spacing: +1px, Color: `#00FF66`
  * **Vertical Nav Links:** Sans-serif, Regular, ~14px, Color: `#D1D5DB`

---

## 4. Layout & Grid
* **Container:** 100vw × 100vh, `overflow: hidden`, `position: relative`.
* **Padding:** Fixed edge spacing of ~40px to 48px from all viewport borders.

---

## 5. UI Components

### Top Navigation Bar
* **Left:** 
  * Audio/Equalizer icon consisting of 5 vertical white bars of varying heights (center tallest, edges shortest).
  * Text label: "Jane Doe" placed directly to the right of the icon with 12px gap.
* **Right:** 
  * Stacked two-line logo aligned to the right edge: "Olympus" stacked over "MONS".

### Center Hero Section
* **Neon Ring:**
  * Centered hollow circular ring behind/around the subject.
  * Diameter: ~450px – 500px.
  * Border thickness: ~14px solid `#00FF66`.
  * Style: Crisp edges with subtle neon luminescence.
* **Central Subject:**
  * Overlaps the bottom-half and interior of the green ring.
  * Centered horizontally, anchored to the bottom third of the screen.

### Right-Side Navigation
* **Alignment:** Centered vertically along the right viewport edge (~48px inward).
* **Orientation:** Vertical text orientation (`writing-mode: vertical-rl` with text rotated 90 degrees clockwise).
* **Items (Top-to-Bottom order):** `Home`, `About`, `Contact`.
* **Spacing:** 40px gap between links.

### Bottom Section
* **Bottom-Left:**
  * Stacked text group anchored ~48px from the bottom/left edges.
  * Line 1: "Graphics Designer" (`#888888`).
  * Line 2: "BAY AREA" (`#00FF66`).
* **Bottom-Right:**
  * Horizontal social icon group anchored ~48px from bottom edge, aligned to the left of the right edge navigation axis.
  * Icons (left to right): Dribbble, Twitter, Instagram, Facebook.
  * Icon dimensions: ~18px × 18px each, 20px gap, color `#D1D5DB`.

---

## 6. Asset & Image Details

### Central Cutout Image
* **Format:** Transparent PNG (strictly backgroundless).
* **Subject:** Front-facing waist-up shot of a person wearing a dark grey/black hooded sweatshirt with the hood up.
* **Mask Detail:** A dark LED "Purge"-style face mask featuring glowing neon-green/cyan stitched "X" marks over the eyes and vertical glowing stitch lines along the mouth seam.
* **Placement & Layering:**
  * Z-index: Positioned above the green ring's bottom border so the shoulders and body occlude the lower arc of the circle.
  * Head positioned directly inside the center circle of the green ring.
  * Zero rotation (perfectly upright, facing forward).
