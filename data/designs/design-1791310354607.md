# Design Specification: Fresh & Reddy Brand Website

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
- **Primary Mood & Archetype:** Premium, modern, artisanal corporate, and bold. It conveys an uncompromising, high-end office beverage service brand with a rebellious edge ("make bad office coffee a thing of the past").
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Balance:** Spacious and deliberate. Generous horizontal padding and breathing room around components give the central 3D product imagery maximum visual weight.
- **Theme Balance:** Pure dark-mode experience (`#1a1a1a` to `#242424` tonal range) featuring dark-textured physical product mockups (black/red paper cups) contrasted with ultra-crisp white typography.

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy
- **Font Families:**
  - *Headings & Display:* Geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., *Futura Condensed*, *Inter Black*, or *Montserrat Black*).
  - *Body & UI:* Clean Modern Grotesk Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*).
- **Type Scale Breakdown:**

| Level | Font Size | Font Weight | Line Height | Letter Spacing (Tracking) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Display Hero** | `56px / 3.5rem` | 900 (Black) | `1.05` | `-0.03em` (Tight) |
| **Section Title / Tagline** | `14px / 0.875rem` | 600 (SemiBold) | `1.2` | `+0.05em` |
| **Body Copy** | `12px / 0.75rem` | 400 (Regular) | `1.5` | `normal` |
| **Navigation Links** | `11px / 0.6875rem` | 500 (Medium) | `1.0` | `+0.08em` |
| **Footer / Meta** | `10px / 0.625rem` | 400 (Regular) | `1.0` | `+0.1em` |

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
- **Container Max-Width:** `1280px` centered with fluid horizontal padding (`64px` desktop min).
- **Spacing Scale (8px Grid System):**
  - `4px` (`0.25rem`) – Micro spacing (icon-to-text gap)
  - `8px` (`0.5rem`) – Tight element padding
  - `16px` (`1rem`) – Standard component padding
  - `24px` (`1.5rem`) – Component gap
  - `48px` (`3rem`) – Section block spacing
- **Section Sequence (Top to Bottom):**
  1. *Top Bar Navigation:* Brand logo top-left, primary links top-right.
  2. *Hero Main Body:* Left-aligned large typography, call-to-action buttons, descriptive subcopy; Right-aligned 3D floating coffee cup composition.
  3. *Pagination Dots:* Absolute-positioned right edge for slide/section state.
  4. *Footer Bar:* Category taxonomy list left, scroll/mouse indicator center, social links right.
- **Z-Index Hierarchy:**
  - Base Content: `z-index: 1`
  - 3D Floating Elements: `z-index: 10`
  - Fixed Navigation Bar: `z-index: 100`
  - Modals / Overlays: `z-index: 1000`

## 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement

### A. Navigation Bar
- **Position:** Fixed top, full width with interior padding (`32px` horizontal, `24px` vertical).
- **Elements:**
  - *Left:* Minimalist logo icon (`FRESH & REDDY` stacked badge).
  - *Right:* Horizontal menu items (`HOME`, `ABOUT`, `COFFEE TASTING`, `SERVICES`, `EQUIPMENT`, `CULTURE`, `CONTACT US`). Spacing between items: `32px`. Uppercase, tracking `+0.08em`.

### B. Hero Action Buttons
- **Primary Button ("BOOK A FREE TRIAL"):**
  - Shape: Rounded capsule / Pill (`border-radius: 9999px`).
  - Background: Solid White (`#ffffff`).
  - Text: Dark Charcoal (`#1a1a1a`), font-weight: 600.
  - Padding: `12px 28px`.
- **Secondary Button ("OUR SERVICES"):**
  - Shape: Rounded capsule / Pill (`border-radius: 9999px`).
  - Background: Transparent (`rgba(0,0,0,0)`).
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`.
  - Text: White (`#ffffff`), font-weight: 600.
  - Padding: `12px 28px`.
  - Hover State: Border transitions to solid white `#ffffff`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.

### C. Carousel Pagination Indicators
- **Layout:** Vertical stack positioned on the far right edge of the canvas.
- **Item Specs:** 5 dots. Inactive dots are small white circles (`6px` diameter, opacity `0.4`). Active dot is expanded vertically or highlighted in Brand Red (`#e53935`). Gap: `12px`.

### D. Footer Bar
- **Left Taxonomy:** `COFFEE  •  TEA  •  WATER  •  SNACKS  •  DELIVERED` (Separated by middle dots, tracking `+0.1em`).
- **Center:** Mouse scroll icon indicator (vertical pill outline with internal scrolling wheel dot).
- **Right:** Social icons (`f`, `instagram camera icon`) and directional arrows (`←`, `→`) enclosed in subtle circular ghost buttons (`border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`).

---

## 6. Motion Mechanics, 3D & Mobile Gestures
- **Animation Triggers:** 
  - Page Load: Staggered fade-in and slide-up for hero typography (`duration: 0.8s`, `easing: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
  - 3D Floating Cups: Continuous subtle floating loop (sinusoidal bobbing effect on the Z-axis paired with slight rotational sway).
- **Hover Transitions:** 
  - Buttons and interactive links use `transition: all 0.25s ease-in-out`.
- **Mobile Gestures:** 
  - Swipe left/right for carousel card switching. 
  - Pull-down to refresh micro-interactions.
