# Visual Design Specification: Elder Adaptogenic Sparkling Water Cans

## 1. Overall Layout & Composition
* **Format:** Product photography featuring four distinct beverage cans displayed horizontally in a refrigerator door rack.
* **Camera Angle:** Straight-on, eye-level close-up shot.
* **Lighting:** Studio lighting from the front, highlighting water condensation droplets on all metallic surfaces.
* **Composition:** 
  * A horizontal white refrigerator shelf rack bisects the frame.
  * Cans are placed vertically behind/resting on the shelf.
  * The green can (Lime Watermelon) leans diagonally to the left.
  * The peach can (Peach Hibiscus) stands upright in the center-right.
  * The purple can (Blueberry...) is partially visible on the far right.
  * An orange-yellow can (Passion Flower / Mood) is partially visible on the far left.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Typography

### Can 1: Lime Watermelon ("ACTIVE RECOVERY")
* **Background Color:** Bright lime green (`#98E842`) with subtle vertical ribbing/shading.
* **Brand Logo ("elder."):** Red (`#E51A23`), lowercase, stylized sans-serif with a registered trademark symbol (`™`).
* **Primary Flavor Text ("LIME WATERMELON"):** 
  * Font Style: Bold, condensed geometric sans-serif, heavy distressed/textured fill.
  * Color: Bright red (`#E51A23`).
  * Layout: Stacked in two lines ("LIME" over "WATERMELON").
* **Illustration Style:** 
  * Abstract black-and-white graphic of a character wearing a large mushroom/wizard hat running dynamically.
  * Accompanying badges: Black starburst badge reading "WITH CORDYCEPS" in white text.
* **Sub-branding / Footer Text:**
  * Subtitle ("ADAPTOGENIC SPARKLING WATER FOR"): Red, uppercase sans-serif.
  * Secondary Title ("ACTIVE RECOVERY"): Bold, red sans-serif.
  * Bottom Badges (Black text inside rounded white pill shapes or plain text): "NO ADDED SUGAR", "CAFFEINE FREE", "NATURAL FLAVOURS".

---

### Can 2: Peach Hibiscus ("BALANCED FOCUS")
* **Background Color:** Soft muted peach / pastel orange (`#F2C7AD`) with subtle metallic sheen and vertical ribbing.
* **Brand Logo ("elder."):** Bright pink/magenta (`#E61A8D`), lowercase, stylized sans-serif with `™`.
* **Primary Flavor Text ("PEACH HIBISCUS"):**
  * Font Style: Bold, condensed geometric sans-serif.
  * Color: Bright pink/magenta (`#E61A8D`).
  * Layout: Stacked ("PEACH" over "HIBISCUS").
* **Illustration Style:**
  * Abstract black graphic of a character riding a skateboard, holding a square object.
  * Accompanying badges: Black starburst badge reading "WITH BACOPA MONNIERI" in white text.
* **Sub-branding / Footer Text:**
  * Subtitle ("ADAPTOGENIC SPARKLING WATER FOR"): Dark charcoal gray, uppercase sans-serif.
  * Secondary Title ("BALANCED FOCUS"): Bold pink/magenta sans-serif.
  * Bottom Text: "NO ADDED SUGAR", "CAFFEINE FREE", "NATURAL FLAVOURS".

---

### Can 3: Blueberry (Partially Visible)
* **Background Color:** Soft lavender purple (`#AFAFE9`).
* **Brand Logo ("elder."):** Yellow-gold.
* **Primary Flavor Text ("BLUEBERRY"):**
  * Color: Bright yellow (`#F9F838`).
  * Font Style: Bold, condensed sans-serif.
* **Illustration Style:** Abstract black geometric graphic.
* **Footer Text:** Yellow and white text reading "ADAPTOGENIC SPARKL...", "BALANCED...".

---

### Can 4: Orange / Passion Flower (Partially Visible)
* **Background Color:** Vibrant orange-yellow (`#F5B914`).
* **Primary Flavor Text / Subtitle:** "SPARKLING WATER FOR...", "MOOD".
* **Illustration Style:** Black graphic with a starburst badge reading "WITH PASSION FLOWER".

---

## 3. Surface Textures & Finish
* **Material:** Matte-finish aluminum beverage cans with horizontal ridged textures molded into the body.
* **Effects:** Realistic condensation water droplets scattered densely across the entire surface of the cans, catching studio highlights.
