# 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
- **Primary Mood & Archetype:** E-commerce dashboard/marketplace with high-density inventory management, promotional banners, and category filtering. Clean, utility-focused, and functional.
- **Visual Density:** High information density. UI components are tightly packed to display maximum product listings, metrics, and navigation categories simultaneously.
- **Whitespace Balance:** Minimalist padding around major content containers (`12px` to `16px`) with tighter internal spacing (`4px` to `8px`) inside card surfaces.
- **Theme Balance:** Predominantly light mode (`#FFFFFF` base canvas, `#F8F9FA` surfaces) punctuated by high-saturation purple brand accents (`#7C3AED` range) and vibrant product imagery.

---

# 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens
- **Primary Brand Color:** Deep Violet (`#6366F1` to `#7C3AED`)
- **Secondary & Accent:** Coral/Red (`#EF4444` for sale badges, `#10B981` for success states)
- **Base Background:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Surface Backgrounds:** Card surfaces (`#FAFAFC`), Sidebar/Navigation (`#F4F5F7`), Modal/Elevated (`#FFFFFF` with shadow)
- **Border & Divider Colors:** Light Gray (`#E5E7EB`)
- **Text Colors:** Primary (`#111827`), Muted (`#6B7280`), Inverse (`#FFFFFF`), Accent (`#7C3AED`)

# 3. Typography System & Hierarchy
- **Font Families:** System sans-serif stack (`Inter`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`).
- **Scale Breakdown:**
  - **Headings (H1):** `font-bold text-[18px] leading-[24px] tracking-tight`
  - **Headings (H2):** `font-semibold text-[14px] leading-[20px]`
  - **Body Copy:** `font-normal text-[12px] leading-[16px]`
  - **Captions / Meta:** `font-medium text-[10px] leading-[14px] tracking-wide text-gray-500`

---

# 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
- **Container Max-Width:** Fluid full-width application shell with fixed sidebar navigation (`220px` width) and multi-column grid containers.
- **Spacing Scale:** `4px` (xs), `8px` (sm), `12px` (md), `16px` (lg), `24px` (xl).
- **Section Sequence Breakdown:**
  1. Left fixed vertical navigation sidebar.
  2. Top promotional header/banner carousel.
  3. Category quick-access icon row.
  4. Multi-item product/card grid layout sections.
  5. Right-side checkout/cart/summary drawer.

---

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Special Glyphs
- **Pill Badges:** Used for promotional callouts (`padding: 2px 8px`, `font-size: 10px`, `font-weight: 600`, `letter-spacing: 0.05em`, `border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Symbols & Glyphs:** Utilizes status indicators (● `&bull;`) and promotional sparkles (✦ `&#10022;`).

# 7. Accessibility (a11y) & WCAG Contrast Audit
- **Text Contrast:** Primary dark text (`#111827`) on light surface (`#FAFAFC`) exceeds **14:1 contrast ratio** (passes WCAG AAA). Muted gray text (`#6B7280`) on white passes **4.6:1** (passes WCAG AA).
- **Focus Rings:** Accessible custom outline applied on keyboard navigation:
```css
*:focus-visible {
  outline: 2px solid #7C3AED;
  outline-offset: 2px;
}
```
