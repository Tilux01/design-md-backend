# Design Specification: IGM.GG Game Store Interface

## 1. General Styles
*   **Background Color:** `#050505` (Pure black/near-black).
*   **Primary Accent Color:** `#E82B2B` (Vibrant Red).
*   **Secondary Accent Color:** `#222222` (Dark Grey for buttons/inputs).
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   Primary: `#FFFFFF` (White)
    *   Secondary: `#A0A0A0` (Muted Grey for descriptions)
*   **Border Radius:** 
    *   Buttons/Badges: `20px` (Pill-shaped)
    *   Game Cards: `12px`
    *   Main Container: `0px` (Full-screen requirement)

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Montserrat` (Bold/Semi-bold for headings).
*   **Secondary Font:** `Roboto` or `System-UI` (Regular for descriptions).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Sizes:**
    *   Logo: `24px` (Italic, Extra Bold)
    *   Hero Title: `36px` (Bold)
    *   Price: `28px` (Bold)
    *   Body Text: `14px` (Regular, Line-height: 1.5)

## 3. Layout Structure

### A. Header (Top Navigation)
*   **Left Section:** 
    *   **Catalog Button:** Red background (`#E82B2B`), white text, "Grid" icon on the left.
    *   **Media Button:** Dark grey background (`#1A1A1A`), white text, "Play/Monitor" icon on the left.
*   **Center Section:** 
    *   **Logo:** "IGM.GG". The "IGM" is red, ".GG" is white. Font is heavy italicized sans-serif.
*   **Right Section:** 
    *   **Wishlist Icon:** Outline heart with a red notification bubble "13".
    *   **Cart Icon:** Outline cart with a red notification bubble "5".
    *   **Search Bar:** Capsule-shaped, dark grey (`#1A1A1A`). Contains a magnifying glass icon and the text "Search" in muted grey.
    *   **Profile:** Circular avatar with a glowing purple/pink border.

### B. Game Carousel (Upper Third)
*   **Container:** Horizontal flex row with a gap of `15px`.
*   **Items:** 10 visible game posters (vertical rectangles with rounded corners).
*   **Active State:** The second item (Modern Warfare III) has a `2px` solid red border.
*   **Navigation:** Two small circular buttons (Left/Right arrows) positioned at the bottom right of the carousel area.
*   **Poster Order (Left to Right):**
    1.  *Fallout 4* (Partial)
    2.  *Call of Duty: MWIII* (Selected)
    3.  *God of War Ragnarok*
    4.  *Cyberpunk 2077*
    5.  *Alan Wake II*
    6.  *Total War: Warhammer III*
    7.  *Baldur's Gate 3*
    8.  *Civilization VI*
    9.  *Crusader Kings III*
    10. *Diablo IV* (Partial)

### C. Hero Content (Lower Left)
*   **Badges:** 
    *   "Sale 52:24:03": Red background, white text, flame icon.
    *   "New Release": Dark grey background, white text, checkmark icon.
*   **Title:** "Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III" (White, Bold).
*   **Description:** "In the direct sequel to Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II, Captain Price and Task Force 141 face their greatest threat yet." (Muted grey, max-width: 500px).
*   **Pricing:**
    *   Original Price: `$120` (Grey, strikethrough).
    *   Discount: `-34%` (Red text, no background).
    *   Current Price: `$69.99` (White, large font).
*   **Action Buttons:**
    *   **Купить (Buy):** Red background, white text, shopping cart icon.
    *   **Подробнее (Details):** Dark grey background, white text.
    *   **Wishlist:** Circular dark grey button with a white heart icon.

## 4. Image Assets & Visual Effects

### Background Hero Image
*   **Description:** A high-resolution close-up of the character "Ghost" from Modern Warfare. He is wearing a tactical headset and his signature skull-patterned ballistic mask.
*   **Placement:** The image is anchored to the right side of the screen, occupying approximately 60% of the width.
*   **Rotation:** 0 degrees, but the character is angled slightly toward the viewer.
*   **Styling:** 
    *   **Backgroundless:** The character should be a cutout or blended into the black background using a gradient.
    *   **Lighting:** Strong red/orange ambient lighting coming from the bottom right, casting deep shadows on the left side of the mask.
    *   **Masking:** A heavy linear gradient transition from the left (Pure Black, 100% opacity) to the right (Transparent, 0% opacity) to ensure the text on the left remains legible.

### Game Posters (Carousel)
*   **Format:** Vertical 2:3 aspect ratio.
*   **Styling:** High-contrast box art. Each image should have a slight `brightness(0.8)` filter unless hovered or selected.

## 5. Technical Implementation Notes
*   **Overlay:** Use a subtle vignette effect around the edges of the entire screen to focus attention on the center.
*   **Transitions:** The red border on the carousel should have a `0.3s ease-in-out` transition when selection changes.
*   **Language:** Note that the primary buttons ("Купить", "Подробнее") are in Russian, while the rest of the interface is in English. This must be preserved for an "exact clone."
