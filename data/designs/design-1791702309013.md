# Design Specification (`design.md`) — Velora Interior Design Studio Website

This document provides the exact implementation specifications required to construct a pixel-perfect, full-screen, responsive clone of the **Velora Interior Design Studio** web design layout. 

*Note: All external frames, background wrappers, and presentation device mockups have been removed. This specification covers the full-viewport web layout directly.*

### Typography & Font Stacks

To achieve the modern, high-end editorial Italian aesthetic, the following primary fonts and fallbacks are defined:

1. **Brand / Display Title Font (`--font-display`)**
   * **Primary:** *Clash Display* or *Syne* (Bold / Semibold, geometric custom sans with clean, distinct curves).
   * **Fallback:** `'Plus Jakarta Sans', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif`
   * **Character:** Ultra-large, sleek, balanced geometric proportions.

2. **Editorial Serif Accent Font (`--font-editorial`)**
   * **Primary:** *Instrument Serif* or *Cormorant Garamond* (Italic).
   * **Fallback:** `'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif`
   * **Usage:** Applied selectively to emphasis phrases (e.g., *"interior design studio"*, *"space"*, *"lifestyle"*, *"vision"*).

3. **Body & Navigation Font (`--font-body`)**
   * **Primary:** *Inter* or *SF Pro Display* (Light, Regular, Medium).
   * **Fallback:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif`
   * **Usage:** Navigation, body paragraphs, metrics numbers, badges, and labels.

## 3. Micro-Interactions & Transitions

1. **Navigation Hover Effects:**
   * Menu text links transition from `rgba(255,255,255,0.7)` to `#FFFFFF` on hover with a smooth `0.2s ease`.
2. **Button Interactions:**
   * `Book a Consultation →`: Scales up slightly (`transform: scale(1.02)`), background transforms to soft silver off-white on hover.
   * `Learn Service →`: Fill background with `#111111` and transition text/arrow to `#FFFFFF` on hover.
3. **Floating Badge Effect:**
   * Floating pills (`• Milan Inspired`, etc.) have a subtle vertical subtle floating animation (`translateY(-4px)` over `4s ease-in-out infinite alternate`).
