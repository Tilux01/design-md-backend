# Design Specification: "CineStream" UI Clone

## 1. Global Styles & Environment
*   **Canvas:** Full-screen (1920x1080 viewport), edge-to-edge. No external browser chrome or margins.
*   **Background Color:** Deep Charcoal/Black (`#0B0B0B`).
*   **Text Color:** Primary: White (`#FFFFFF`), Secondary/Metadata: Light Grey (`#B3B3B3`).
*   **Accent Color:** Action Red (`#E50914`).
*   **Font Family:** 
    *   **Primary:** `Montserrat` (Sans-Serif).
    *   **Secondary/Body:** `Inter` or `Roboto`.
    *   **Fallback:** `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Spacing Unit:** 8px base grid.

---

## 2. Header (Navigation Bar)
*   **Height:** 80px.
*   **Background:** Transparent to semi-opaque black gradient (`rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`).
*   **Left Section:** 
    *   **Hamburger Menu:** Three horizontal white lines (24px width), 2px thickness.
*   **Center Section (Navigation Links):** 
    *   **Items:** Home, Browse, Movies, TV Shows, Watchlist, Genres.
    *   **Styling:** Uppercase, 14px, Font-weight 600, Letter-spacing 1px.
    *   **Active State:** "Home" has a white underline (2px thick, 30px wide) centered 10px below the text.
*   **Right Section:**
    *   **Search Icon:** Magnifying glass icon in white.
    *   **User Profile:** Circular avatar (36px). Image: A stylized yellow/gold illustrated face on a dark background. A small downward chevron (arrow) sits to the right of the avatar.

---

## 3. Hero Section (Feature Spotlight)
*   **Height:** 60% of viewport height.
*   **Background Image:** 
    *   **Description:** A cinematic wide shot from the movie *Inception*. It features five characters (four men in suits, one woman in a brown jacket) standing on a wide, empty city street with modern buildings and a bus in the background. The lighting is cool-toned (blues and greys).
    *   **Placement:** Centered, background-size: cover.
    *   **Overlay:** A subtle linear gradient from the bottom (solid black) to the top (transparent) to ensure text readability.
*   **Content Block (Left Aligned):**
    *   **Title:** "Inception". Font: Montserrat Bold, 48px.
    *   **Rating:** Five stars. Four stars are bright gold (`#FFD700`), the fifth is a dark grey outline.
    *   **Review Count:** "(85,657 reviews)" in 12px light grey text next to stars.
    *   **Description:** Three lines of text. "A thief who steals corporate secrets through the use of dream-sharing technology is given the inverse task of planting an idea into the mind of a CEO." 16px, line-height 1.5.
    *   **Buttons:**
        1.  **Watch Now:** Solid Red (`#E50914`), rounded corners (4px), white text, 14px Bold.
        2.  **Add to Watchlist:** Ghost button (transparent with 1px white border), white text, 14px Bold.
*   **Carousel Indicators:** 
    *   **Placement:** Bottom center of the hero section.
    *   **Style:** Four small dots. The second dot is white (active), others are semi-transparent grey.

---

---

## 5. Image Specifications
*   **Backgroundless Elements:** None. All movie posters and hero images are full-bleed rectangular assets.
*   **Rotation:** All elements are 0-degree rotation (perfectly vertical/horizontal).
*   **Shadows:** Each movie poster has a subtle `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`.
*   **Border Radius:** All movie posters have a slight 4px corner radius.

---

## 6. Interaction Details
*   **Hover State:** When a user hovers over a poster (as seen with *Blade Runner 2049*), the poster should scale by 10%, a 40% black overlay should appear, and a white Play button icon (30% opacity background circle) should fade into the center.
*   **Scroll:** Horizontal overflow. The rows should allow for side-scrolling (hidden scrollbar).
