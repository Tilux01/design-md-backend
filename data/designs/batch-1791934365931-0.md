# Design Specification: AI Style Gallery

## 1. Global Setup & Theming
- **Canvas:** Full-screen responsive viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Primary Accent:** Orange (`#F05023`).
- **Text Colors:** Primary `#111827`, Secondary/Body `#4B5563`, Light `#FFFFFF`.

### Typography
- **Primary Sans-Serif:** Inter, `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`.
- **Display/Headings (Fallback):** Syne or Plus Jakarta Sans.
- **Sizes & Weights:**
  - Header Nav Items: `14px`, Medium (`500`).
  - Button Text: `14px`, Semi-bold (`600`).
  - Subheading/Body: `14px`, Regular (`400`).
  - Active Card Label: `20px` to `24px`, Medium (`500`).

---

## 2. Navigation Bar
- **Position:** Top, full width, padded horizontally (`padding: 20px 48px`).
- **Logo (Center):**
  - Circular icon (`32px × 32px`).
  - Orange radiant sunburst icon inside a dark circle outline (`#F05023` / `#1E293B`).
- **Right Navigation Menu:**
  - Horizontal list with `gap: 24px`, aligned vertically center.
  - Links: `Pricing`, `Inspo`, `What's New`, `Log in`.
  - Button: "Get started"
    - Background: `#F05023`
    - Text: `#FFFFFF`
    - Shape: Full pill (`border-radius: 9999px`)
    - Padding: `8px 18px`

---

## 3. Hero Subheading
- **Placement:** Positioned above the gallery, aligned left with gallery edge (`margin-left: 48px`, `margin-top: 40px`).
- **Content:** "Generate images in any one of our 60 preset styles — or create your own."
- **Styling:** Gray `#4B5563`, max-width `360px`, line-height `1.4`.

---

## 4. Accordion Gallery Component
- **Layout:** Flex container, horizontal row, `gap: 8px`, centered horizontally.
- **Total Dimensions:** Width ~`90vw`, height ~`420px`.
- **Card Radius:** All cards have uniform `border-radius: 8px`, `overflow: hidden`.
- **Interaction Model:** Horizontal accordion. Hovering or clicking an item expands it while compressing others.

---
