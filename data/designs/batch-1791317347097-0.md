# Design System & Technical Specification

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
*   **Primary Mood & Archetype:** High-end, immersive, modern hardware & audio engineering boutique. The aesthetic combines sleek minimalism with deep, tactile dark-mode surfacing.
*   **Visual Density:** Balanced and breathable. Generous whitespace around typographic focal points creates a strong sense of acoustic resonance and premium clarity.
*   **Whitespace & Balance:** Fluid container padding with central hero anchoring. Typography scales gracefully with high-impact, center-aligned statement headings.
*   **Theme Balance:** Monochromatic dark mode (`#0B0C10` to `#121318`) with subtle atmospheric gradients and high-contrast stark white typography (`#FFFFFF`).

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
*   **Max Container Width:** `1280px` (centered with auto margins).
*   **Grid System:** 12-column responsive flex/grid layout.
*   **Spacing Scale:** 
    *   `2xs`: `4px`, `xs`: `8px`, `sm`: `12px`, `md`: `16px`, `lg`: `24px`, `xl`: `32px`, `2xl`: `64px`, `3xl`: `96px`.
*   **Section Sequence:**
    1. Fixed/Floating Top Navigation Bar (Logo, Links, Action Button)
    2. Hero Section (Immersive 3D/Texture background, Centered Title, Subtitle, CTA Button)
    3. Brand Logos / Partner Bar (Monochrome grayscale asset row)

## 8. Accessibility (a11y) & WCAG Contrast Audit
*   **Text Contrast Ratios:**
    *   White (`#FFFFFF`) on Dark Background (`#0A0A0C`): **21:1** (Passes WCAG AAA)
    *   Muted Text (`#9BA1AE`) on Dark Background (`#0A0A0C`): **7.2:1** (Passes WCAG AAA)
*   **Focus Ring Specification:**
    ```css
    :focus-visible {
      outline: 2px solid #FFFFFF;
      outline-offset: 2px;
    }
    ```
