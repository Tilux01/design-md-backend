# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Dark Mode Interactive Portfolio / High-End Coverflow Showcase.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Extremely generous negative space surrounding a centralized focal component; low density to maximize aesthetic impact and focus on interactive media.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered spatial composition utilizing 3D perspective transformations, opacity gradients for distant carousel items, and subtle backdrop glows to elevate the active center card.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase category titles, glassmorphism action buttons, perspective transform card stacking, subtle ambient radial vignettes.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `8px 16px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(255,255,255,0.05)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Left Arrow (<) -> Use `&larr;` or SVG chevron.
  - Right Arrow (>) -> Use `&rarr;` or SVG chevron.
  - Active Dash (—) -> Use HTML `&mdash;` or CSS indicator spans.
