# Design Specification: Jems Kemerun Portfolio Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Layout
* **Layout Mode**: Fullscreen single-page hero section (`100vw`, `100vh`, `overflow: hidden`).
* **Grid**: 12-column layout with faint vertical guide lines (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`) spanning full height.
* **Background**: Very light cool gray/blue (`#F1F3F7`) with faint, soft ambient blur circles.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Accent Red**: `#E61C3D` (active links, name highlight, CTA button)
* **Text Dark (Primary)**: `#242833` (headings, body text)
* **Text Muted (Secondary)**: `#6D7380` (nav inactive, subtitle)
* **Text Disabled / Grid**: `#9AA0AE` (social icons, lines)
* **Background Light**: `#F1F3F7`
* **Brush Splatter Tone**: `#D7DFEB`

---

## 3. Typography & Fallbacks
* **Primary Font**: `Poppins`, `Montserrat`, or `Inter`
* **Fallback Stack**: `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
* **Scale**:
  * **Nav Links**: 11px, font-weight 600, letter-spacing 1.5px, uppercase.
  * **Pre-title ("HELLO")**: 12px, font-weight 700, letter-spacing 2px, uppercase.
  * **Hero Title ("I’m Jems Kemerun")**: 48px – 56px, font-weight 800, line-height 1.15.
  * **Body Text**: 13px, font-weight 400, line-height 1.6, color `#5A606E`.
  * **Button Text**: 11px, font-weight 700, letter-spacing 1px, uppercase.
  * **Scroll Text**: 10px, font-weight 600, letter-spacing 2px, uppercase.

---

## 4. Header / Navbar
* **Position**: Absolute top, full width, padding: `32px 64px`.
* **Left**: Brand logo mark (dark gray solid rectangle or stylized typographic logo).
* **Center**: Horizontal menu links (`HOME`, `ABOUT`, `SERVICE`, `PORTFOLIO`, `RESUME`, `BLOG`, `CONTACT`). 
  * `HOME` colored in Accent Red; remaining links in dark gray/muted.
* **Right**: Language selector (`FRA`, `FER`, `ENG`). `ENG` highlighted in Accent Red.

---

## 5. Hero Content (Left Column)
* **Alignment**: Left-aligned, vertically centered, left padding: `64px`.
* **Elements**:
  1. **Tag**: Horizontal dash (`—`) followed by `HELLO`.
  2. **Headline**: `I’m <span style="color: #E61C3D;">Jems</span> Kemerun`.
  3. **Description**: Max-width ~420px. Text:
     > *"This is Jems Kemerun visual designer, Photographer, Web Developer and Creative Director ocated in United States, looking for working around the globe."*
  4. **CTA Button**:
     * Text: `DOWNLOAD CV`
     * Background: `#E61C3D`, text: `#FFFFFF`.
     * Dimensions: ~140px width, 44px height, rectangular with subtle 2px border radius.
  5. **Social Icons**:
     * Positioned bottom-left (`left: 64px; bottom: 32px`).
     * Set: Instagram, Dribbble, Tumblr, Twitter, Vimeo.
     * Style: Line-art icons, 16px, muted gray `#9AA0AE`.

---

## 6. Visual & Imagery (Right Column)

### Graphic Element (Splatter)
* **Type**: Pastel light-slate watercolor/dry-brush stroke.
* **Placement**: Centered behind the subject's torso and shoulders.
* **Angle**: Slanted diagonally (~35° upwards from bottom-left to top-right).
* **Color**: `#D7DFEB`.

### Subject Image
* **Format**: Backgroundless transparent PNG, high-contrast monochrome (black & white).
* **Subject**: Adult male with full beard, black-rimmed glasses, dark fedora hat, scarf wrapped around neck, and black long-sleeve knit shirt with arms crossed.
* **Expression**: Direct gaze with a subtle smile.
* **Alignment**: Anchored directly to the bottom edge; right offset ~15% from the screen edge.
* **Scale**: Head reaches near top nav bar; torso cropped at waist level.

---

## 7. Peripheral Elements
* **Scroll Indicator**:
  * Position: Fixed along bottom-right edge (`right: 48px; bottom: 80px`).
  * Rotation: 90° clockwise (`writing-mode: vertical-rl`).
  * Content: `SCROLL DOWN —→`.
* **Grid Lines**: 5–6 equidistant vertical hairline borders across the viewport in `#E4E7ED`.
