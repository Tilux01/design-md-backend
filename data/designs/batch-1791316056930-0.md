# Comprehensive Design & Frontend Specification

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
* **Primary Mood:** Modern, clean, wellness-focused e-commerce with an organic, luxury editorial feel.
* **Brand Archetype:** High-end holistic health and supplement brand (Wellina).
* **Visual Density:** Clean, generous whitespace combined with crisp photographic imagery and subtle dark UI anchors.
* **Theme Balance:** Implements a dual-context design paradigm—an immersive, deep forest-green dark mode navigation bar & hero header section contrasted with a pristine, light, minimalist content body (off-white surfaces, light gray product cards).
* **Texture & Depth:** Smooth matte surfaces, rounded card shells (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`), pill badges with soft backgrounds, and minimal drop shadows on cards (`shadow-sm` on hover states).

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy
* **Headings:** Serif font family (e.g., *Playfair Display*, *Newsreader*, or *Instrument Serif*) conveying high-end editorial authority.
* **Body & Navigation:** Clean Geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, or *SF Pro Display*).

| Level | Font Size | Line Height | Weight | Letter-Spacing (Tracking) | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hero Display** | `4rem` / `64px` | `1.05` | `400` / Regular | `-0.03em` | Main hero marketing headers |
| **H1 (Section)** | `2.75rem` / `44px` | `1.1` | `400` / Regular | `-0.02em` | "Find your supplement", "Science-backed..." |
| **H3 (Card Title)**| `1.125rem` / `18px`| `1.3` | `500` / Medium | `normal` | Product names (Daily Synbiotic) |
| **Body / Price** | `1rem` / `16px` | `1.5` | `400` / Regular | `normal` | Prices, descriptions |
| **Caption / Nav** | `0.875rem` / `14px`| `1.4` | `500` / Medium | `0.05em` | Navigation links, micro-labels |
| **Micro-Badge** | `0.75rem` / `12px` | `1.2` | `600` / Semi-bold | `0.08em` (Uppercase) | "Sale", "Best Sellers", category tags |

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
* **Container Max-Width:** `1440px` centered with fluid horizontal padding (`clamp(1.5rem, 5vw, 4rem)`).
* **Grid Specifications:** 
  * Product Carousel / Grid: 4 columns on desktop (`grid-cols-4`), flexible horizontal scrolling or pagination on mobile.
  * Hero Section: Split layout (Left: Editorial lifestyle imagery & typography, Right: Botanical macro photography).
* **Z-Index Stacking Order:**
  * Base content: `z-0`
  * Sticky Navigation: `z-50`
  * Floating Modals / Dropdowns: `z-100`

---

## 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Special Glyphs
* **High-Tracking Uppercase Labels:** Used for contextual sub-headings (e.g., `FORMULATIONS` with `letter-spacing: 0.1em`).
* **Pill Badges (Rounded Pills):**
  * **Sale Badge:** Background `#C83E2D`, text `#FFFFFF`, padding `4px 10px`, rounded-full.
  * **Best Seller Badge:** Background `#4A6B52`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded-full.
  * **Action Pill Button ("Shop Now"):** Background `#D3E1B8`, text `#0E2319`, inline icon with circular wrapper, hover scale effect.
* **Symbol Safety:** 
  * Arrow right/left navigation icons (`&rarr;`, `&larr;` or SVG paths).
  * Cart count indicator badge (`1` inside a solid black/dark circle).

## 8. Accessibility (a11y) & WCAG Contrast Audit

* **Contrast Ratios:**
  * `#FFFFFF` text on `#0E2319` (Hero/Nav background): **14.2:1** (Passes WCAG AAA)
  * `#1A1A1A` text on `#F4F4F2` (Card surface): **13.5:1** (Passes WCAG AAA)
  * `#666666` secondary text on `#F4F4F2`: **5.7:1** (Passes WCAG AA for normal text)
  * `#D3E1B8` accent background with `#0E2319` text: **7.1:1** (Passes WCAG AAA)

* **Focus States & Keyboard Navigation:**
  ```css
  button:focus-visible, 
  a:focus-visible {
    outline: 2px solid #0E2319;
    outline-offset: 3px;
  }
  ```
