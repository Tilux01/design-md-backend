# Design Specification: Loop.tv Interface Clone

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Background:** A deep, dark navy-to-black gradient.
    *   *Primary Background:* `#05070A`
    *   *Overlay:* A subtle radial gradient emanating from the top center to provide depth.
    *   *Bottom Background:* A silhouette of a concert crowd, semi-transparent (approx. 20% opacity), layered behind the content but above the base background color.
*   **Typography:**
    *   **Primary Font:** A modern, geometric sans-serif with rounded terminals. 
    *   **Recommendation:** *Lexend* or *Montserrat*.
    *   **Fallback:** `system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`.
    *   **Case:** Almost all UI text (labels, navigation, headings) is **lowercase**.
*   **Border Radius:** 
    *   Main Hero Card: `40px`
    *   Secondary Cards: `24px`
    *   Navigation Pill: `50px`

---

## 2. Header Section
The header is a single row with three distinct parts, spaced using `justify-content: space-between`.

### 2.1 Logo (Left)
*   **Content:** "loop.tv™"
*   **Style:** Bold, white, lowercase. The "loop" text is slightly heavier than the ".tv".
*   **Placement:** Top-left, with a padding of `40px`.

### 2.2 Navigation Bar (Center)
*   **Container:** A translucent pill-shaped bar.
    *   *Background:* `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` (Glassmorphism effect).
    *   *Blur:* `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)`.
*   **Items:** "home", "signage", "rewards", "search", and a [Settings Gear Icon].
*   **Active State ("home"):** A secondary pill inside the bar with a lighter background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).
*   **Spacing:** `24px` between text items.

### 2.3 Toggle Menu (Right)
*   **Content:** Text "toggle menu" followed by a circular icon containing three horizontal lines (hamburger menu).
*   **Style:** White text, lowercase. Icon is a white circle with thin dark lines.
*   **Placement:** Top-right, aligned with the logo.

---

## 3. Hero Carousel Section
A horizontal layout featuring one prominent center card and two partially visible side cards.

### 3.1 Center Focused Card
*   **Border:** `4px solid white`.
*   **Dimensions:** Approximately `60%` of the screen width.
*   **Content Overlay (Bottom Left):**
    *   **Artist Thumbnail:** A small square image of Blake Shelton.
    *   **Label:** "WARNER RECORDS" (Small, grey, uppercase).
    *   **Title:** "Nobody But You" (Large, white, bold).
    *   **Subtext:** "Blake Shelton - (Duet with Gwen Stefani)" (Medium, white).
*   **Content Overlay (Bottom Right):**
    *   "loop.tv" logo in white, semi-transparent.
*   **Main Image Description:** A cinematic shot inside a diner. A man (Blake Shelton) with short brown hair and a tan jacket sits on the left, and a woman (Gwen Stefani) with blonde hair and a denim jacket sits on the right. They are looking at each other across a table, drinking from milkshakes with straws. The lighting is warm and cinematic.

### 3.2 Side Cards (Left & Right)
*   **Style:** No border, slightly dimmed/darkened to show they are not in focus.
*   **Left Image Description:** A music studio setting. A man in a yellow shirt and brown jacket sits at a keyboard. Another man in a patterned shirt and hat stands behind him. The room has acoustic foam panels.
*   **Right Image Description:** An outdoor concert stage at sunset. Massive scaffolding and speaker stacks are visible against an orange and blue sky. A large crowd is visible at the bottom of the frame.

## 4. "Trending Channels" Section
Located below the hero section.

### 4.1 Heading
*   **Text:** "trending channels"
*   **Style:** Bold, white, lowercase, size approx `32px`.
*   **Margin:** Left-aligned with the start of the Hero Carousel.

### 4.2 Channel Grid
A horizontal row of cards with rounded corners (`24px`).

1.  **Card 1 (Pool):** A close-up, high-angle shot of a yellow pool table. A wooden triangle is filled with colorful billiard balls.
2.  **Card 2 (Flamingo):** A bright pink plastic lawn flamingo standing in a green yard. In the background, there is a grey fence and a soft, hazy sky. The image is centered and upright.
3.  **Card 3 (Microphone):** A black and white, moody shot of a microphone stand on a stage. High-contrast lighting with a bright spotlight coming from above, creating a misty/smoky effect.
4.  **Card 4 (Heart Hands):** A silhouette of a person's hands forming a heart shape in front of a bright, warm-lit concert stage. The background is blurry with golden and orange stage lights.

## 5. Image Asset Requirements

*   **Background Image:** `crowd_silhouette.png`. Must be **backgroundless** (transparent PNG) or a very dark silhouette that blends into `#05070A`. No rotation. Placed at the very bottom of the viewport, spanning the full width.
*   **Hero Center:** `diner_music_video.jpg`. High resolution, 16:9 aspect ratio.
*   **Hero Left:** `studio_session.jpg`. 16:9 aspect ratio.
*   **Hero Right:** `concert_sunset.jpg`. 16:9 aspect ratio.
*   **Channel Thumbnails:** All should be 16:9 aspect ratio, cropped to fit the rounded containers.
*   **Icons:** 
    *   `settings_gear.svg`: White, stroke-based.
    *   `menu_burger.svg`: White circle with three horizontal lines.

---

## 6. Spacing & Layout Logic (CSS)
*   **Container Padding:** `40px` horizontal, `20px` vertical.
*   **Hero Gap:** `30px` between the center card and side cards.
*   **Trending Gap:** `20px` between channel cards.
*   **Flexbox/Grid:** Use `display: flex` for the header and hero section. Use `display: grid` or `flex` for the trending channels row to allow for horizontal overflow/scrolling.
