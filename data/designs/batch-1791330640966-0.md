# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise SaaS / Genomics & Biotech Minimalist.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio, asymmetric split layout with generous card padding and clear visual grouping.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean flat cards with subtle 1px border separation and soft ambient drop-shadows on elevated statistics widgets.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Rounded floating pill navigation, pill-shaped CTA buttons, clean statistical data widgets with minimal bar charts, avatar stack indicators.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `14px`, font-weight `500`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#f8f9fc`, border `1px solid #e2e8f0`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
