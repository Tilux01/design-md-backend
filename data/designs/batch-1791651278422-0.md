# Design Specification: AtomAI Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport
- **Viewport:** Full-screen (`width: 100vw`, `height: 100vh`, `overflow-x: hidden`).
- **Base Background:** Deep, pitch black (`#000000`).
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary Background: `#000000` (Pure Black)
  - Secondary/Container BG: Dark gradients merging into absolute black
  - Text Primary: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with silver gradients
  - Text Secondary: Muted Gray (`#999999`)
  - Accent/Buttons: White pill buttons (`#FFFFFF`) with dark text (`#000000`) and dark outlined semi-transparent buttons (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Typography Engine:** Sans-serif system using modern geometric fonts.
  - Primary Font: **Inter** or **Plus Jakarta Sans**.
  - Fallback: `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation Bar
- **Positioning:** Fixed or sticky at the top, full width with horizontal padding (`max-width: 1400px`, centered).
- **Height:** ~80px.
- **Left Element (Logo):**
  - **Type:** Brand lockup.
  - **Icon:** Circular vector icon featuring a stylized 'A' with a target/atom orbit ring (monochrome silver/white).
  - **Text:** "AtomAI" in bold, clean white sans-serif typography.
- **Center/Right Elements (Nav Links):**
  - Links: `Company`, `Careers`, `Pricing`, `Blog`, `Change Log`, `Contact Us`.
  - **Font Styling:** Regular weight, small text (~14px), color `#A0A0A0`, transitioning to `#FFFFFF` on hover.
- **Far Right Element (Action Button):**
  - **Type:** Pill-shaped button.
  - **Text:** "Get Template".
  - **Styling:** Dark background with a subtle white border (`1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`), white text, rounded corners (`border-radius: 9999px`), padding `8px 16px`.

---

## 3. Hero Section

### A. Background Ambient Effects
- **Atmospheric Glow:** A sweeping, organic, monochromatic white/silver light wave that arches across the upper-middle background (creating a sleek "cybernetic wave" or curved horizon line effect). It fades smoothly into the pure black background via heavy radial/linear gradients.

### B. Badge Element
- **Positioning:** Above the main headline, left-aligned.
- **Styling:** Pill-shaped container (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, `border-radius: 9999px`, `padding: 6px 14px`).
- **Content:** 
  - A small solid white dot acting as a status indicator.
  - Text: "Atom AI-Automation Partner" in small, muted-white font (~12px).

### C. Main Headline (H1)
- **Content:** 
  ```
  Scale Business
  with Automation
  ```
- **Typography:**
  - Font Size: ~64px to 72px (responsive scale).
  - Line Height: Tight (1.05 to 1.1).
  - Font Weight: Bold / Extra-Bold (~700/800).
  - Color Effect: Smooth vertical gradient transitioning from pure white (`#FFFFFF`) at the top to a muted metallic silver (`#777777`) toward the bottom.

### D. Subtitle / Description Paragraph
- **Content:** 
  > Manage all your data and workflows in one place. Automate processes, gain insights, and optimize your strategy with AI-powered intelligence.
- **Typography:**
  - Font Size: ~16px.
  - Line Height: 1.5.
  - Color: `#9A9A9A` (Muted gray).
  - Max Width: ~520px.

### E. Call-to-Action (CTA) Buttons Group
- **Layout:** Flex row, gap: 12px, left-aligned beneath the subtitle.
- **Primary Button ("Get Started Now"):**
  - Background: Solid White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Text Color: `#000000` (Black).
  - Font Weight: Medium (~500).
  - Icon: Right-pointing arrow icon (`↗`) placed before or after text.
  - Border Radius: `9999px` (Pill).
  - Padding: `12px 24px`.
- **Secondary Button ("View Services"):**
  - Background: Semi-transparent dark (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
  - Text Color: `#E0E0E0`.
  - Icon: User/Profile + menu lines icon (`⍾≡`) on the left.
  - Border Radius: `9999px` (Pill).
  - Padding: `12px 24px`.

---

## 4. Social Proof / Partner Logos Section
- **Positioning:** Placed near the bottom of the viewport, centered horizontally.
- **Layout:** Flex row with equal spacing (`justify-content: space-between` or evenly distributed), muted styling.
- **Brand Items (Monochrome/Gray logos):**
  1. **Asterisk:** Stylized asterisk/starburst icon combined with the word "Asterisk" in clean bold lowercase/title sans-serif.
  2. **Cooks:** Custom typography featuring a stylized connected 'C' monogram followed by "ooks".
  3. **Opal:** Circular line-art opal/gem icon followed by the word "Opal".
  4. **Dune:** Abstract layered-dune ring icon followed by the word "Dune".
  5. **Oasis:** Clean sans-serif wordmark "Oasis".
- **Visual Treatment for Logos:** All logos are rendered in a uniform, low-opacity, muted gray tone (`#666666`) to blend seamlessly into the dark interface without distracting from the primary hero content.
