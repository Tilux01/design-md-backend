# UI/UX Specification: BIORG Project (2026 Concept)

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport
- **Screen Architecture:** A dual-theme responsive landing page split into a top light-themed section and a bottom dark-themed section.
- **Full Screen Implementation:** Designed to stretch edge-to-edge (100vw x 100vh) without containing mock-up framing borders.
- **Grid Structure:** CSS Grid/Flexbox hybrid system utilizing symmetric alignment around a central product anchor point.

---

## 2. Typography System
- **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `-apple-system`, `sans-serif` (Clean, geometric, grotesque sans-serif).
- **Fallback Font:** `Arial`, `Helvetica Neue`, `sans-serif`.
- **Text Styles Hierarchy:**
  - **Project Branding (`PROJECT: BIORG`, `YEAR: 2026`):** Extra-small uppercase monospaced/sans-serif (`10px`, Letter spacing: `0.05em`, Color: `#1a1a1a`).
  - **Navigation Links (`FORMULA`, `PERFORMANCE`, `RESEARCH`, `APP`):** Small uppercase (`10px`, Letter spacing: `0.1em`, Color: `#1a1a1a`).
  - **Hero Header (`Engineered For Strength, Focus And Longevity`):** Bold, heavy-weight header (`42px`, Line height: `1.1`, Split-color/weight emphasis on "For Strength, Focus And Longevity").
  - **Section Subheaders (`Science-Backed Nutrition For Modern Men`):** Centered bold white typography (`32px`, balanced contrast on black background).
  - **Body Copy:** Regular weight (`12px`, Line height: `1.4`, muted gray for readability).

---

## 3. Section-by-Section Breakdown

### A. Top Section (Light Theme Background: `#f3f3f3`)

#### **Header / Navigation Bar**
- **Left:** Minimalist logo mark featuring a stylized lowercase script letter `b` enclosed inside an abstract rounded rectangle/capsule outline.
- **Center:** Navigation anchors (`FORMULA`, `PERFORMANCE`, `RESEARCH`, `APP`).
- **Right (Top-Right Panel):** 
  - Search bar input field with placeholder text `Search`, paired with a magnifying glass icon.
  - Interactive icon set: Chat bubble (`💬`), Profile (`👤`), and Shopping Bag (`🛍️`).

#### **Left Hero Panel**
- **Social Proof / Links:** Vertical stack of minimal outline icons for Instagram (`📷`) and Facebook (`📘`).
- **Typography Block:** 
  - Heading: *“Engineered **For Strength, Focus And Longevity**”* (The word "Engineered" is heavy dark weight; sub-phrase is styled in a lighter muted tone).
- **Call-to-Action (CTA):** 
  - Pill-shaped dark button (`#111111`) containing text `Shop Collection` paired with an enclosed circular right-arrow button (`→`).

#### **Center Product Feature**
- **Central Element:** A premium dark green glass jar mockup (`Multivitamins Daily Formula`).
  - **Description:** Dark translucent forest-green jar with a ribbed black screw-on cap. Centrally displays the minimalist logo mark and product typography (`Multivitamins / Daily Formula`) in crisp white.
  - **Background Status:** Backgroundless product asset centered dynamically across the light/dark transition fold.

#### **Right Side Panel (Product Selector / Cart context)**
- **Thumbnail Stack:**
  - Thumbnail 1: Green jar product variant labeled `Gut Health` with an active `+` add button underneath.
  - Thumbnail 2: Green jar product variant labeled `Joint Support` with an active `+` add button underneath.
- **Product Information Card:**
  - Small header: `Precision Nutrition`
  - Body text: *"Premium supplements developed to support energy, focus, cardiovascular health, and long-term performance for modern men."*

---

### B. Bottom Section (Dark Theme Background: `#0a0a0a`)

#### **Section Header**
- Centered layout featuring the brand logo mark (`b`) above the bold white heading: **"Science-Backed Nutrition For Modern Men"**.
- Subtext centered below: *"Premium formulas developed to support energy, focus, recovery, and long-term well-being through every stage of life."*

#### **Metrics Grid (Data Display)**
- **Left Column:**
  - `1M+` (Label: `Men Supported`)
  - `98%` (Label: `Customer Satisfaction`)
- **Right Column:**
  - `15+` (Label: `Targeted Formulas`)
  - `50+` (Label: `Premium Ingredients`)

#### **Visual Content & Imagery Assets**
- **Central Circular Trio Gallery:** Three circular image callouts arranged horizontally:
  - *Left Circle:* Professional portrait of a smiling man in a tailored dark suit against a studio backdrop.
  - *Center Circle:* High-resolution macro image of a vibrant green cannabis/hemp leaf on a pure white background.
  - *Right Circle:* Casual lifestyle portrait of a man smiling while holding a branded supplement bottle.
- **Botanical Footer Element:**
  - **Description:** A realistic, highly detailed dried woody root/branch styled with vibrant green leaves sprouting along its length. 
  - **State:** Backgroundless, organically curved horizontally across the lower center of the dark section, creating a natural framing effect.
