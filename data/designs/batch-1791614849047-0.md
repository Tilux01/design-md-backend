# DESIGN SYSTEM SPECIFICATION: "LITUP" LIGHTING SHOWCASE

---

## 1. Overview & Viewport Strategy

This design specification details a full-screen, immersive luxury e-commerce interface for designer lighting fixtures. 

* **Viewport Display Mode:** Full-screen edge-to-edge web application (`100vw` × `100vh`).
* **Frame Removal:** Omit any outer desktop mockups, floating rounded card boundaries, and external background drop shadows shown in the reference presentation. The layout fills the browser viewport directly.
* **Layout Split:**
  * **Main Canvas (Left & Center):** 76% width (`flex: 0 0 76%` or `calc(100vw - 320px)`). Background color: Sky Blue / Cyan.
  * **Control Panel (Right):** 24% width (`flex: 0 0 24%` or `min-width: 300px`). Background color: Warm Sunshine Yellow.
  * **Height:** Fixed or fluid `100vh`, non-scrolling desktop experience with horizontal carousel pagination.

## 3. Typography System

### 3.1. Backdrop Display Script
* **Text Content:** `"litup"` (Stylized lower-case continuous brush script)
* **Font Family:** `Pacifico`, `Yellowtail`, `Grand Hotel`, or `Allura`
* **Fallback Stack:** `"Brush Script MT"`, `cursive`, `sans-serif`
* **Font Weight:** Bold / Heavy (400–700 depending on script family)
* **Font Size:** Responsive `16vw` (approx. `180px` to `240px` on desktop)
* **Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
* **Layering:** Positioned directly behind the 3D Hero Lamp and above the cyan canvas (`z-index: 2`).
* **Special Treatment:** Text sits at a slight upward diagonal angle (-3° rotation), smoothly curving across the horizontal center.

### 3.2. Interface & Product Typography
* **Font Family:** `Outfit`, `Poppins`, `Montserrat`, or `Inter`
* **Fallback Stack:** `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`
* **Hierarchy:**
  * **Product Title ("Suprematic"):**
    * Size: `13px` / `0.8125rem`
    * Weight: `600` (Semi-Bold)
    * Letter-spacing: `0.04em`
    * Line Height: `1.2`
    * Color: `#FFFFFF`
  * **Product Category ("Lighting"):**
    * Size: `11px` / `0.6875rem`
    * Weight: `400` (Regular)
    * Letter-spacing: `0.02em`
    * Line Height: `1.2`
    * Color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`

## 5. Detailed Component Specifications

### 5.1. Left Vertical Navigation Rail
* **Placement:** Fixed to the far left inside the Cyan section, top-to-bottom spanning.
* **Width:** `80px`
* **Padding:** `32px 0`
* **Alignment:** Centered vertical flexbox (`align-items: center; justify-content: space-between`).
* **Items (Top to Bottom):**
  1. **Category Icon 1 (Storage/Cabinet):** Line art icon, outline white (`20px`).
  2. **Active Icon (Pendant Lamp):** 
     * Container: Rounded squircle (`44px × 44px`), background `#6E7CF4`, border-radius `14px`.
     * Icon: Hanging bell/ceiling lamp silhouette in pure white.
  3. **Category Icon 2 (Windows/Blinds):** Line art icon, outline white (`20px`).
  4. **Category Icon 3 (Desk Lamp):** Line art adjustable task lamp, outline white (`20px`).
  5. **Category Icon 4 (Chair/Furniture):** Minimalist chair line icon, outline white (`20px`).
  6. **Bottom Action (Scroll Down):** Downward arrow indicator (`18px`), pure white (`#FFFFFF`).

### 5.2. Top Right Main Stage Utilities
* **Placement:** Positioned in the upper-right corner of the Cyan canvas section (`top: 32px; right: 40px`).
* **Elements:**
  * **Bag Icon:** Shopping tote bag silhouette in solid yellow (`#F4B828`), `20px` height.
  * **Wishlist Icon:** Solid heart icon in soft light cyan (`#86DEFD`), `18px` height.
  * **Spacing:** `24px` horizontal gap between icons.

### 5.3. Hero Product Display (Centerpiece)
* **Position:** Hanging vertically from the top viewport edge down into the upper-middle canvas (`top: 0; left: 34%`).
* **Suspension:** Single ultra-thin vertical cord (`1px` width, color: `#FFFFFF`, opacity `0.85`).
* **Detailed Asset Description:**
  * **Visual Nature:** High-resolution, fully backgroundless (transparent PNG/WebP), photorealistic 3D rendering.
  * **Outer Dome Shell:** Cracked white porcelain/eggshell surface with visible organic fissure lines and textured, matte stone fractures.
  * **Interior & Bottom:** The hollow interior casts a radiant amber-gold ambient glow.
  * **Hanging Core:** Multiple translucent organic botanical discs resembling stylized lotus leaves or lily pads hanging from thin internal brass pins. Discs alternate between warm translucent golden amber and deep forest green with wavy, scalloped leaf margins.
  * **Rotation & Angle:** Sits facing forward with a very subtle 5° right tilt, exposing the concave lower cavity and internal cascading discs.
  * **Z-Index:** Layered in front of the cursive script (`z-index: 10`).

### 5.4. Bottom Horizontal Product Carousel (Cyan Section)
* **Position:** Bottom zone of cyan canvas (`bottom: 40px; left: 100px; right: 40px`).
* **Left Arrow Button:**
  * Dimensions: `36px × 36px` circle.
  * Background: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
  * Icon: Left chevron (`<`), thin stroke, dark gray/indigo (`#2C3E50`), centered.
  * Dropshadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
* **Product Items Layout:** 3 items arranged horizontally with equal spacing (`gap: 48px`).
* **Product Item Structure:**
  1. **Suspension Wire:** `1px` vertical line extending from item top (`16px` height, `#2C3E50` or `#FFFFFF`).
  2. **Product Image:** Height `90px` to `110px`, transparent background, horizontally centered.
  3. **Separator Line:** Horizontal hairline rule (`32px` wide, `1.5px` height, color `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`), placed `12px` below lamp.
  4. **Title & Category:** Two-line text block centered below separator.
* **Product Descriptions (Left to Right):**
  * **Item 1 (Dark Glass Pendant):**
    * *Visual:* Backgroundless transparent asset. Spherical glass pendant featuring an opaque glossy smoked dark-violet upper cap with an illuminated, glowing clear glass lower bowl revealing an internal filament bulb.
    * *Label:* Line 1: `Suprematic`, Line 2: `Lighting`.
  * **Item 2 (Frosted Bi-Color Sphere):**
    * *Visual:* Backgroundless transparent asset. Spherical pendant lamp with a matte frosted white upper half and an opaque cool-gray lower hemisphere base ring.
    * *Label:* Line 1: `Suprematic`, Line 2: `Lighting`.
  * **Item 3 (Matte Pure White Orb):**
    * *Visual:* Backgroundless transparent asset. Minimalist spherical globe light made of solid matte white opal glass with soft diffuse lighting reflection on the upper left rim.
    * *Label:* Line 1: `Suprematic`, Line 2: `Lighting`.

## 7. Elevation, Shadows, and Materials

* **Right Panel Divider:** Clean vertical split between `#58BCE4` and `#F4B828`. No border or separator line; pure color contrast defines the edge.
* **Squircle Tile Shadows (Yellow Panel):**
  * White tiles: `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04);`
  * Yellow tonal tiles: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 2px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4), 0 3px 8px rgba(180, 120, 10, 0.18);`
* **Circular Pagination Buttons (`<` and `>`):**
  * `box-shadow: 0 6px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12);`
* **Translucency / Glassmorphism:** Not used on panels. Surface finishes are flat, saturated, and matte, creating a graphic, high-contrast poster-like modern interior aesthetic.
