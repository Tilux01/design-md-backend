# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Cyberpunk Editorial Dark Mode / High-End E-Commerce Showcase.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High contrast, expansive negative space balanced with bold typography and vibrant neon/magenta accents.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered dark translucent surfaces (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with subtle inset shadows, gradient borders, and soft magenta drop-shadow glows (`0 10px 40px rgba(255, 20, 147, 0.25)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient neon magenta glow, glassmorphism containers, dark technical grid lines.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `700`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(255, 20, 147, 0.1)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(255, 20, 147, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-pink-500 inline-block"></span>`.
