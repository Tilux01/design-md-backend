# Design Specification: Squid Game Landing Page Clone

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Viewport:** Full-screen (100vw, 100vh), no margins or external frames.
*   **Theme:** Dark, cinematic, and high-contrast.
*   **Background:** 
    *   **Base:** A horizontal linear gradient. Left side: Deep Magenta/Plum (`#4a0e2a`). Right side: Dark Charcoal/Teal-Black (`#0f1717`).
    *   **Background Image:** A low-opacity, desaturated image of two main characters (Seong Gi-hun and Cho Sang-woo) looking upwards. They should be positioned behind the text but in front of the base gradient.
    *   **Overlay:** A subtle vignette effect to darken the edges, especially the bottom and right.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Title ("SQUID GAME"):** 
    *   **Font:** Custom stylized slab-serif with a "distressed" or "grunge" texture.
    *   **Color:** White with a slight inner glow.
    *   **Styling:** The "Q" in Squid contains a pink circle. The "A" in Game is replaced with a solid pink triangle. The letters have a slight "glitch" offset.
    *   **Fallback:** `Impact`, `Arial Black`, sans-serif.
*   **Navigation & UI Text:** 
    *   **Font:** Clean, modern Sans-Serif (e.g., **Montserrat** or **Poppins**).
    *   **Weight:** Medium (500) for Nav; Light (300) for description.
    *   **Letter Spacing:** Increased (approx 2px) for uppercase links.
    *   **Fallback:** `Inter`, `Helvetica`, sans-serif.

## 3. Imagery Assets
### Image A: The Guard (Foreground)
*   **Description:** A high-resolution cutout of a Squid Game guard wearing a bright red/pink hooded jumpsuit. The guard is wearing a black mesh mask with a white **Circle** symbol.
*   **Pose:** Profile view, facing the left side of the screen.
*   **Placement:** Right-hand side, vertically centered. The bottom of the torso should be cut off by the bottom of the viewport.
*   **Properties:** Backgroundless (PNG), sharp edges, high contrast. No rotation.

### Image B: The Background Characters
*   **Description:** Desaturated, dark-toned photo of the two lead actors.
*   **Placement:** Centered-left, behind the main title.
*   **Properties:** 20-30% opacity, blended into the background gradient using "Multiply" or "Overlay" blend modes.

## 4. UI Elements & Components

### Top Navigation Bar
*   **Left:** Three geometric icons (Triangle, Circle, Square) in white.
*   **Center:** Text links: `ABOUT`, `GAMES`, `ACTORS`, `HISTORY`. All caps, white, small font size.
*   **Right:** A "Hamburger" menu icon (three horizontal lines) with the word `MENU` next to it in white.

### Main Content Area (Left-Center)
*   **Title:** Large "SQUID GAME" logo as described in Typography.
*   **Icon:** A small white outline icon of the "Squid Game" floor diagram (a circle, triangle, and square connected) located just above the description.
*   **Description:** 
    *   **Text:** "Hundreds of cash-strapped players accept a strange invitation to compete in children's games. Inside, a tempting prize awaits—with deadly high stakes."
    *   **Style:** White, left-aligned, max-width of approx 400px, font-size approx 14px.

### Bottom Controls
*   **Play Button (Bottom Left):** A circular pink button (`#e91e63`) with a white "Play" triangle icon. Next to it, the text "PLAY TRAILER" in white uppercase.
*   **Episode Navigation (Bottom Center):** 
    *   Three items: `01 RED LIGHT, GREEN LIGHT`, `02 THE MAN WITH THE UMBRELLA`, `03 STICK TO THE TEAM`.
    *   Style: Numbers are smaller and placed above the titles. White text, uppercase.
*   **Social Icons (Bottom Right):** Small white icons for Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook.

### Vertical Pagination (Far Right)
*   **Description:** Three small vertical dots. The middle dot is larger/active.
*   **Placement:** Vertically centered on the far right edge.

## 5. Color Palette (Hex Codes)
*   **Primary Pink:** `#FF1F6D` (Used for the Play button and logo accents).
*   **Deep Purple/Background Left:** `#2E0B1A`.
*   **Dark Teal/Background Right:** `#0A1212`.
*   **Text White:** `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Muted Text:** `#B3B3B3` (Used for secondary labels).

## 6. Layout Logic (CSS/Structure)
*   **Container:** `display: flex` or `grid`.
*   **Z-Index Layers:**
    1.  Base Gradient (Bottom)
    2.  Background Character Image
    3.  Main Title and Text Content
    4.  Foreground Guard Image (Top layer, overlapping the right side of the text slightly)
    5.  Navigation and UI overlays.
