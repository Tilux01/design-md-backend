# Design Specification: Soundwalker Web Experience

---

## 1. Overview & Visual Aesthetic

- **Product / Concept:** Soundwalker — avant-garde audio exploration & streaming platform.
- **Design Philosophy:** Minimalist Ethereal Neo-Brutalism with Y2K 3D elements.
- **Core Canvas:** Full-screen edge-to-edge layout (`100vw` × `100vh`, non-scrolling desktop experience).
- **Dominant Visual Tone:** Cool lavender, mist periwinkle, deep indigo typography, and iridescent 3D organic tactile geometry.

---

## 2. Typography & Font Specifications

### Primary Display Font (Headlines, Brand, Card Titles)
- **Visual Style:** Ultra-condensed, tall geometric display sans-serif with sharp angles, tight tracking, and heavy vertical presence.
- **Closest Commercial Fonts:** *Druk Condensed Super*, *Monument Extended Ultra-Condensed*, *Tungsten Bold*.
- **Google Fonts Equivalent:** [Bebas Neue](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bebas+Neue) or [Oswald](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Oswald) (weight: 700, condensed).
- **CSS Declaration & Fallbacks:**
  ```css
  font-family: 'Druk Condensed', 'Bebas Neue', 'Oswald', -apple-system, sans-serif;
  text-transform: uppercase;
  letter-spacing: 0.04em;
  font-stretch: 75%;
  ```

### Secondary / Metadata Font (Navigation, Subtitles, Tiny Meta)
- **Visual Style:** Technical, clean grotesque/monospaced-hybrid sans-serif with open apertures and neutral contrast.
- **Recommended Free / Google Fonts:** [Space Grotesk](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk) or [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter).
- **CSS Declaration & Fallbacks:**
  ```css
  font-family: 'Space Grotesk', 'Inter', system-ui, sans-serif;
  letter-spacing: 0.08em;
  text-transform: uppercase;
  ```

### Giant Background Watermark Font
- Same as Primary Display Font (`Bebas Neue` / `Druk Condensed`), scaled up to approximately `24vw`, rendered with near-zero opacity (`fill: transparent; stroke: rgba(180, 182, 220, 0.22)` or fill `rgba(215, 218, 245, 0.35)`).

## 5. Detailed Component Specifications

### 5.1. Top Navigation Bar
- **Dimensions:** Full width, height `80px`, horizontal padding `48px` (`padding: 0 48px`).
- **Layout:** Flexbox, `align-items: center`, `justify-content: space-between`.
- **Left Column:**
  - **Brand Logo:** "SOUNDWALKER"
    - Font: Primary Display Font, Bold, Size: `22px`, Color: `--text-primary`.
  - **Subline:** "LIMITED EDITION / SOUND LAB" directly beneath brand in `8px` Secondary Font, Color: `--text-secondary`.
- **Right Column:**
  - **Log In Link:** Secondary Font, Size: `11px`, Weight: `600`, tracking `0.1em`, Color: `--text-primary`.
  - **Action Button:**
    - Text: "EXPLORE SOUND" / "BECOME MEMBER"
    - Style: Rectangular pill with slight border-radius (`4px` to `6px`).
    - Background: `--accent-pill` (`#5856D6`).
    - Font: Size `10px`, Weight `700`, Color: `#FFFFFF`, tracking `0.06em`.
    - Padding: `8px 20px`.

### 5.2. Hero Section (Mid-Canvas)
- **Layout:** Two-column split across horizontal axis with vertical center alignment.
- **Background Watermark Element:**
  - Giant text: `SOUNDWALKER`
  - Position: Absolute, vertically centered (`top: 40%`, `transform: translateY(-50%)`), left aligned (`left: 4%`).
  - Size: Responsive `18vw` to `22vw`.
  - Color: Very subtle periwinkle outline/fill, blending into the background with zero interference to foreground legibility.

#### Left Column (Hero Content)
- **Positioning:** Left offset `48px`, width approx. `42%`.
- **Title:** "CONNECT ON SOUNDWALKER"
  - Font: Primary Display, Size: `clamp(36px, 4vw, 56px)`, Tracking: `0.02em`, Color: `--text-primary`.
- **Divider Line:**
  - Height: `1px`, width: `180px` to `240px`, background: `--divider-lines`, margin: `16px 0 12px 0`.
- **Meta Subtext:**
  - Text: "128.00 BPM / D-FLAT MAJOR / SYSTEM ARCHIVE"
  - Font: Secondary Font, Size: `10px`, Color: `--text-secondary`, line-height `1.5`.

#### Right Column (3D Sculptural Asset)
- **Asset Positioning:** Absolute/relative anchoring occupying right `50%` of viewport width, vertically centered, slightly bleeding into the top navigation margin.
- **Detailed Asset Description:**
  - **Subject:** Highly complex, parametric, accordion-pleated / wavy 3D abstract form resembling ruffled acoustic acoustic fabric, organic marine coral, or a sculpted sound-wave explosion.
  - **Color & Lighting:** Cool periwinkle and saturated lavender (`#8478E3` core highlights, transitioning into deep royal violet `#4C449D` in crevices and ambient shadows). Soft specular highlights on crests, clay/velveteen matte surface finish.
  - **Alpha / Transparency:** Fully transparent background (cutout PNG or 3D WebGL element). Smooth anti-aliased contour with ambient light falling on the left side facing the typography.
  - **Rotation / Perspective:** Angled diagonally, leaning approximately 15° forward and slightly rotated clockwise.

## 6. Motion & Interactive Behaviors

- **Background Organic 3D Form:**
  - Micro-floating animation (`translateY`: oscillation of ±8px over 6 seconds with ease-in-out timing).
  - Subtle mouse-tracking parallax response (`rotateX` / `rotateY` within a tight 3° clamp).
- **Track Cards Hover State:**
  - Thumbnails scale up slightly (`transform: scale(1.04)`, `transition: transform 0.3s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
  - Vertical accent markers expand slightly in height or switch color to `--accent-pill`.
- **Primary Pill Button:**
  - Hover state shifts background color from `#5856D6` to `#4644BA` with ease.
