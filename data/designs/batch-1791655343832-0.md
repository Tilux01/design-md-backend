# Comprehensive UI/UX & Design Specification: "Game App & Gaming Store" Landing Page

## 1. Global & Page-Level Layout Architecture

- **Orientation & Viewport:** Full-screen desktop presentation view (100vw, 100vh), intended for a dark-themed promotional layout.
- **Background Layer:** A dramatic, seamless radial gradient.
  - **Color Progression:** Transitions dynamically from a deep charcoal/slate grey (`#2C353D`) at the center/light source areas to an obsidian black-blue (`#13181E`) at the extreme corners.
  - **Texture/Effect:** A soft, diffused overhead spotlight effect casting a subtle top-left white atmospheric bloom over the primary header typography and the front-left floating mobile mockup.
- **Content Organization:** 
  - **Header Section:** Centered at the top, featuring bold white title copy and descriptive subcopy.
  - **Floating UI Showcase Layer:** A dynamic, non-linear 3D isometric scattering of overlapping mobile app mockups (screens) showcasing various parts of the gaming application (Skins Store, User Profile, Payment Methods, Skin Lists).

---

## 2. Typography Specifications

*Fallback Stack for all typography:* `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`

### A. Main Section Heading
- **Text:** "Game App & Gaming Store" (Split across two lines)
- **Font Family:** Modern Sans-Serif Geometric (e.g., *Inter*, *SF Pro Display*, *Poppins*)
- **Font Weight:** Bold (`700`)
- **Font Size:** `48px` (desktop scale)
- **Line Height:** `1.15`
- **Letter Spacing:** `-0.02em` (Tight, modern tracking)
- **Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Alignment:** Center-aligned relative to the viewport container.

### B. Mobile Interface Typography
- **App Screen Titles / Headers ("Skins List", "Select Your Payment Method"):**
  - **Weight:** Semi-Bold (`600`)
  - **Size:** `16px` – `18px`
  - **Color:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Card Subtitles / Skin Names ("Aeronaut", "Flapjackie", "Cobalt", "Insight", "Rogue Agent"):**
  - **Weight:** Semi-Bold (`600`)
  - **Size:** `14px` – `16px`
  - **Color:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Metadata / Body Labels (Release Date, Price, Type, Availability):**
  - **Weight:** Regular (`400`)
  - **Size:** `10px` – `12px`
  - **Color:** `#8B95A5` (Muted cool grey)
- **Action Buttons ("CHOOSE SKIN"):**
  - **Weight:** Bold (`700`)
  - **Size:** `11px`
  - **Letter Spacing:** `0.05em` (Uppercase)
  - **Color:** `#0B1218` (Dark text contrasting against vibrant cyan background)

## 4. Component Layouts & Component-Specific Styling

### A. Mobile App Mockup Containers (Common Structure)
- **Shape:** Rounded rectangle (iOS-style corner radius approx. `36px` to `40px`).
- **Border/Stroke:** Thin, elegant inner/outer border (`1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`).
- **Shadows:** Deep, multi-layered drop shadows simulating realistic depth and floating elevation off the dark background (`box-shadow: 0 30px 60px rgba(0,0,0,0.6), 0 10px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)`).
- **Notch/Dynamic Island:** Minimalist top speaker/notch indicator line centered at the top bezel.
- **Bottom Navigation Bar:** Fixed bottom bar featuring minimal monochrome iconography (Home, Grid, Compass, Profile) with an active pill indicator.
