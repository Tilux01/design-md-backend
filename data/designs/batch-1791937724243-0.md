# Design Specification: Ghost of Tsushima Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Environment
*   **Canvas:** Full-screen viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`).
*   **Overflow:** Hidden (no scrollbars).
*   **Background:** 
    *   **Base Layer:** A cinematic, high-contrast landscape image of Tsushima. The sky is a desaturated, overcast white/grey. The ground consists of tall, white pampas grass.
    *   **Atmospheric Layer:** Overlay of floating, blurred red maple leaves (momiji) scattered across the screen to create depth.
    *   **Gradient Overlay:** A subtle radial gradient from the center (lighter) to the edges (darker) to focus attention on the character.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Roboto` or `Inter` (Sans-serif).
*   **Logo Font:** Custom stylized serif for "GHOST" and "TSUSHIMA". Fallback: `Trajan Pro` or `Cinzel`.
*   **Heading Font:** `Montserrat` or `Poppins` (Medium weight).
*   **Body Font:** `Open Sans` or `Roboto` (Light weight, 300-400).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

## 3. Layout Sections

### A. Header (Top Navigation)
*   **Placement:** Fixed at the top, `100%` width, height approx `60px`.
*   **Left:** PS4 Logo (White).
*   **Center:** Navigation Links (`Ghost Of Tsushima`, `Editions`, `Gameplay Showcase`, `Images`, `Videos`, `Wallpapers`, `About Developer`, `Check Out`).
    *   *Style:* Uppercase, font-size `12px`, letter-spacing `1px`, color: `#FFFFFF` (low opacity for inactive, full white for active).
    *   *Active Indicator:* A thin red horizontal line under "Ghost Of Tsushima".
*   **Right:** `Login` (with icon) and `Search` (with magnifying glass icon).

### B. Hero Character (Jin Sakai)
*   **Image Description:** A high-resolution, backgroundless PNG of the protagonist, Jin Sakai. He is positioned slightly right of center. He is wearing dark samurai armor, a headband, and has a large bow strapped to his back. He is looking down at a mask held in his hands.
*   **Placement:** Z-index should be above the background but below the UI text. The image should be cut off at the waist by the bottom UI elements.

### C. Left Feature Panel (Manga Collage)
*   **Placement:** Left side, vertically centered.
*   **Content:** A vertical rectangular grid of black-and-white manga-style illustrations showing various combat scenes.
*   **Accent:** Scattered red maple leaves are layered *over* the black and white art to provide a pop of color.
*   **Navigation:** Small white circular buttons with `<` and `>` arrows on the left and right edges of the panel.

### D. Central Information Block
*   **Title:** "JIN SAKAI" (Large, white, sans-serif, approx `48px`).
*   **Body Text:** A paragraph describing Jin’s journey. 
    *   *Style:* White text, `14px`, line-height `1.6`, width approx `450px`.
*   **Placement:** Positioned to the left of the character's torso, slightly overlapping the character's arm.

### E. Main Logo & Call to Action (Bottom Left)
*   **Logo:** "GHOST OF TSUSHIMA" in white. The 'O' in Ghost contains the red Sakai clan crest.
*   **Buttons:**
    1.  **PRE-ORDER NOW:** Solid red button (`#C8102E`), rounded corners, white bold text.
    2.  **WATCH TRAILER:** Dark grey semi-transparent button, rounded corners, white text.
*   **Social Icons:** Small white icons (Instagram, Twitter, Facebook, YouTube) at the very bottom left corner.

### F. Release Date & Badges (Middle Right)
*   **Countdown Box:** A white rectangular box with a slight drop shadow.
    *   *Header:* "Release Date" in black.
    *   *Timer:* "2 : 23 : 10 : 45" (Days, Hours, Minutes, Seconds).
*   **Platform Badges:** 
    *   "Only On PlayStation" logo.
    *   "PS4 Pro Enhanced" text.
    *   "Sucker Punch" logo (Square black/white icon).
    *   "ESRB Mature 17+" rating badge.

### G. Bottom Feature Cards
*   **Structure:** Three horizontal cards with rounded corners.
*   **Card 1 (The Rise of the Ghost):** Image of Jin standing in a golden forest.
*   **Card 2 (Mud, Blood and Steel):** Image of a duel at sunset. Features a red "Active" banner at the top and a `+` icon in the center.
*   **Card 3 (Tsushima, The Island):** Image of a rider on horseback in a white pampas field.
*   **Placement:** Bottom right, aligned horizontally.

## 4. Color Palette
*   **Primary Red:** `#C8102E` (Buttons, active links, maple leaves).
*   **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF` (Headings, countdown box).
*   **Deep Black:** `#000000` (Shadows, UI backgrounds).
*   **Text Grey:** `#CCCCCC` (Secondary navigation, body text).

## 5. Asset Requirements
1.  **Background:** `tsushima_landscape.jpg` (High-res, 4K).
2.  **Character:** `jin_sakai_hero.png` (Transparent background, high-res).
3.  **Manga Collage:** `manga_panels.png` (Black and white).
4.  **Maple Leaves:** `red_leaf.png` (Multiple variations, transparent).
5.  **Logos:** `ps4_logo.svg`, `sakai_crest.svg`, `sucker_punch_logo.svg`, `esrb_mature.svg`.
6.  **Card Thumbnails:** Three `16:9` aspect ratio gameplay screenshots.
