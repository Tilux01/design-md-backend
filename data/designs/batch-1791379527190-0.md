# Design Specification: Dual-Tone Color Showcase

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
- **Viewport Structure**: The design consists of a vertical stack of two distinct, full-bleed (or large framed) container sections displayed sequentially. Based on the prompt's request for a "full screen" experience, the layout should utilize a CSS Grid or Flexbox container that splits the screen or creates two full-width viewport sections.
- **Outer Backdrop**: The surrounding background of the presentation frame is a very dark charcoal/near-black (`#11161a` or similar dark neutral) to make the color swatches pop.
- **Spacing/Gap**: There is a distinct vertical gap between the two cards, mirroring the rounded-rectangle layout shown in the source.

---

## 2. Section 1: "Masterpiece Red"

### Color & Background
- **Background Color (HEX)**: `#5A2132` (A deep, muted burgundy/wine red).
- **Border Radius**: Rounded corners (`border-radius: 16px` to `24px`) on desktop viewports.

### Typography
- **Main Title**: *"Masterpiece Red"*
  - **Font Family**: Elegant serif, editorial style (similar to *Playfair Display*, *Bodoni Moda*, or *Cinzel*).
  - **Fallback Stack**: `Georgia, Cambria, "Times New Roman", Times, serif`
  - **Font Style**: Italic
  - **Color**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Subtitle/HEX Badge**: *"HEX CODE: #5A2132"*
  - **Font Family**: Sans-serif, clean geometric (similar to *Inter*, *Montserrat*, or *Helvetica*).
  - **Fallback Stack**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
  - **Text Transformation**: Uppercase
  - **Border/Pill Container**: Enclosed in a thin, rounded border outline pill matching the background color or styled as a semi-transparent white border (`border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`).
  - **Color**: White (`#FFFFFF`)

---

## 3. Section 2: "Dirty White"

### Color & Background
- **Background Color (HEX)**: `#EFE9E9` (A warm, soft off-white/pale grayish-pink).
- **Border Radius**: Matches Section 1 (`border-radius: 16px` to `24px`).

### Typography
- **Main Title**: *"Dirty White"*
  - **Font Family**: Matches Section 1 (*Playfair Display* / editorial serif).
  - **Fallback Stack**: `Georgia, Cambria, "Times New Roman", Times, serif`
  - **Font Style**: Italic
  - **Color**: Deep Burgundy/Dark Red matching the brand tone (`#5A2132`), creating high contrast against the pale background.
- **Subtitle/HEX Badge**: *"HEX CODE: #EFE9E9"*
  - **Font Family**: Matches Section 1 sans-serif.
  - **Fallback Stack**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
  - **Text Transformation**: Uppercase
  - **Border/Pill Container**: Enclosed in a thin, rounded border outline pill matching the text color (`border: 1px solid #5A2132` or similar opacity).
  - **Color**: Matching the dark title (`#5A2132`).

---

## 4. Footer / Branding Watermark
- **Logo/Text**: Small geometric/stenciled logomark at the bottom center of the lower card reading *"DGTL DSNR"* (or similar stylized branding).
- **Font Style**: Monospaced, geometric, or custom graphic badge.
- **Color**: Matches the primary text color of the card it sits upon (`#5A2132`).

---

## 5. Animation & Interaction (Optional / Recommended)
- **Entrance Animation**: Subtle fade-in and upward slide (`translateY`) using CSS keyframes or Intersection Observer (`opacity: 0` to `1`, `transform: translateY(20px)` to `0`).
- **Hover Effects**: Gentle scale up (`transform: scale(1.01)`) or subtle elevation shadow shift when hovering over either color block to add a modern, interactive feel.
