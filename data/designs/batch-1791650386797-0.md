# Design Specification: Atyeo – Idea Generation Company Landing Page

## 1. Global & Layout Settings

*   **View Mode:** Full-screen layout (no decorative mockup frames or device borders).
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Background (Dark/Charcoal):** `#1E2024` (or deep dark gray `rgb(30, 32, 36)`) extending smoothly throughout the viewport.
    *   **Text Color (Primary White):** `#FFFFFF`
    *   **Text Color (Muted/Secondary):** `#A0A5B0` (used for menu links, subtitles, and descriptions).
    *   **Glow/Highlight Effect:** Soft, radial warm-to-white light emission directly surrounding the lit bulb (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` center fading out to transparent dark gray).
*   **Typography (Font Families):**
    *   **Headings / Logo / Navigation / Main Hero Text:** Primary font **Montserrat** (Weights: 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 700 Bold). *Fallback:* `sans-serif`.
    *   **Body Text / Captions:** Primary font **Open Sans** or **Roboto** (Weights: 300 Light, 400 Regular). *Fallback:* `sans-serif`.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation Bar

*   **Position:** Fixed or absolute at the top of the viewport, spanning 100% width with generous horizontal padding (approx. 5% from left and right edges).
*   **Logo (Left):**
    *   Text/Graphic mark: Stylized letter **"A"** with a horizontal line/slash cutting across the upper stem.
    *   Font style: Bold sans-serif.
    *   Color: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Navigation Links (Center-Right):**
    *   Items: `ABOUT`, `FEATURES`, `CONTACT`.
    *   Font: Montserrat, Medium (`500`), Uppercase, letter-spacing: `1.5px`, font size: `12px`.
    *   Color: `#FFFFFF` (or light gray `#E0E0E0`).
*   **Action Icons (Far Right):**
    *   **Hamburger Menu Icon:** Three horizontal parallel lines. Color: White.
    *   **Search Icon:** Magnifying glass symbol. Color: White.
    *   Spacing between items: approx. `24px` gap.

---

## 3. Hero Section

*   **Vertical Centerpiece – Hanging Lightbulb:**
    *   **Cord:** A thin, vertical black/dark gray line (`#111111`) originating from the absolute top center of the viewport, dropping down into the upper-middle section of the screen.
    *   **Lamp Fixture/Base:** Small black cylindrical screw cap (`#1A1A1A`) at the base of the bulb socket.
    *   **Lightbulb Image/Asset:**
        *   *Description:* A classic vintage/modern incandescent pear-shaped glass bulb emitting an intense, focused, bright white glow from the filament area.
        *   *Background:* Backgroundless (transparent PNG/SVG element).
        *   *Lighting Effect:* A realistic radial gradient glow overlaying the dark background directly behind and around the bulb, casting soft light downward onto the central typography.
*   **Slider Navigation Arrows:**
    *   Left Arrow (`←`) positioned vertically centered on the left third of the hero area. Color: Muted gray (`#777C85`), transition to white on hover.
    *   Right Arrow (`→`) positioned symmetrically on the right third of the hero area.
*   **Hero Typography:**
    *   **Faint Watermark Text (Behind Main Title):** Large faint letters reading `A T Y E O` displayed horizontally in wide tracking with very low opacity (`opacity: 0.08`), positioned directly beneath the hanging lightbulb.
    *   **Main Title:** `A T Y E O`
        *   Font: Montserrat, Bold (`700`), extremely wide letter spacing (tracking approx. `12px` to `16px`).
        *   Size: Large display text (approx. `42px` to `56px`).
        *   Color: `#FFFFFF`.
    *   **Subtitle:** `I D E A   G E N E R A T I O N   C O M P A N Y`
        *   Font: Montserrat, Regular (`400`), uppercase, very wide letter-spacing (`6px`).
        *   Size: `11px` to `13px`.
        *   Color: `#A0A5B0` (Muted gray).

---

## 4. Bottom Grid / Feature Cards Section

*   **Layout:** A 3-column horizontal grid spanning the lower portion of the screen, with subtle semi-transparent dark background panels dividing the sections.
*   **Card Styling:**
    *   Background: Semi-transparent dark slate (`rgba(25, 27, 31, 0.6)` or slightly lighter than the main background `#22252A`).
    *   Borders: Thin, subtle borders separating the three columns (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
    *   Padding: Generous vertical and horizontal internal padding (`32px` top/bottom).
*   **Card 1 (Left):**
    *   **Heading:** `NUMEROLOGY`
        *   Font: Montserrat, Bold (`700`), uppercase, tracking `2px`, size `13px`. Color: `#C0C5D0`.
    *   **Body Text:** `New chapter of the idea has been given by the company, to be discussed in detail in direct meet`
        *   Font: Open Sans, Light/Regular (`300`), size `12px`, line-height `1.6`. Color: `#8A8F9A`.
*   **Card 2 (Center - Active/Highlighted Variant):**
    *   Background: Slightly lighter or more opaque dark card (`rgba(32, 35, 40, 0.85)`).
    *   **Heading:** `THE IDEOLOGY`
        *   Font: Montserrat, Bold (`700`), uppercase, tracking `2px`, size `13px`. Color: `#FFFFFF`.
    *   **Body Text:** `New chapter of the idea has been given by the company, to be discussed in detail in direct meet`
        *   Font: Open Sans, Regular (`400`), size `12px`, line-height `1.6`. Color: `#A0A5B0`.
*   **Card 3 (Right):**
    *   **Heading:** `METHODOLOGY`
        *   Font: Montserrat, Bold (`700`), uppercase, tracking `2px`, size `13px`. Color: `#C0C5D0`.
    *   **Body Text:** `New chapter of the idea has been given by the company, to be discussed in detail in direct meet`
        *   Font: Open Sans, Light/Regular (`300`), size `12px`, line-height `1.6`. Color: `#8A8F9A`.

---

## 5. Responsive Behavior Notes
*   **Desktop (>1024px):** Exact 3-column layout as specified, fixed vertical proportions centered around the hanging lightbulb.
*   **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Reduce horizontal padding, scale down main typography (`A T Y E O`), stack or condense bottom grid into a 2-column or scrollable format.
*   **Mobile (<768px):** Hide or simplify secondary background text, stack bottom cards vertically into single rows, simplify top navigation into a hidden drawer triggered by the hamburger menu icon.
