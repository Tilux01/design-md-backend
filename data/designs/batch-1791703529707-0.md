# Design Specification: IMA—HOME Hero Landing Page

---

## 1. Overview & Visual Direction

This design is a high-fashion, minimalist digital agency landing page. It pairs a stark, pitch-black backdrop (`#0A0A0A`) with hyper-scaled display typography, a centered cinematic video window, subtle navigational micro-details, and playful asymmetrical geometric accents (such as a floating white orb).

The composition relies on severe contrast, strict symmetry anchoring the central frame, and deliberate horizontal tension caused by edge-to-edge typography.

## 3. Typography & Hierarchy

* **Recommended Web Fonts**: **TWK Lausanne**, **Neue Haas Grotesk Display**, or **Söhne** (Medium / Regular).
* **System / Open-Source Fallbacks**:

  font-family: "TWK Lausanne", "Neue Haas Grotesk", "Inter", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;

### 4.1 Header Bar
* **Position**: Fixed or absolute to viewport top.
* **Height**: `72px` to `80px`.
* **Padding**: Left and right margins of `48px` (responsive scale to `24px` on tablet).
* **Alignment**: Flexbox `space-between`, vertical alignment `center`.
* **Components**:
  * **Brand Mark**: `IMA—HOME` on the left.
  * **Navigation Cluster**: Horizontal flex list on the right with `32px` column gap between links:
    * `Work`
    * `Agency`
    * `Careers` (appended with an inline `4px` indicator dot in `#FF3B30` offset `4px` top-right)
    * `Thoughts`
    * `Contact`

## 5. Main Centerpiece & Media Container

### 5.1 Central Media Frame
* **Dimensions**: 
  * Width: `42vw` to `48vw` (approx. `600px` to `700px` on standard desktop).
  * Aspect Ratio: `16:9` or `16:10`.
* **Positioning**: Perfectly centered horizontally and vertically within the viewport (`top: 50%; left: 50%; transform: translate(-50%, -50%);`).
* **Visual Styling**:
  * Clean straight edges (no corner radius: `border-radius: 0px`).
  * No external border or outer drop shadow.
* **Content Description**:
  * A looping, muted video or high-definition still plate.
  * **Subject**: A young man with short dark brown hair and a light beard, dressed in a muted sand/cream turtleneck sweater.
  * **Action/Pose**: Both hands raised near ears (adjusting headphones or gesture).
  * **Lighting & Background**: Deep cobalt/indigo blue solid background. Moody cinematic directional lighting across the face and shoulders. Warm skin tones contrasting against the cool blue void.
  * **Tone**: Contemporary fashion editorial, artistic and quiet.

### 5.2 Micro Caption
* **Position**: Positioned directly beneath the central video frame, centered horizontally.
* **Spacing**: `16px` to `20px` margin below the bottom edge of the video.
* **Text**: `© IMA-HOME 2022`
* **Color**: `#FFFFFF` at `80%` opacity.

## 6. Giant Display Headline Layer (`Hello — G'day`)

The signature element of the design is the giant typographic statement layered horizontally across the exact center line of the screen.

### 6.1 Placement & Structure
* **Z-Index**: Positioned *above* the video layer so text elements overlay the video frame seamlessly.
* **Horizontal Structure**:
  1. **Left Word**: `Hello`
     * Placed flush to the screen edge or slightly cut off by the left margin (starts at `-1vw` to `0` offset).
  2. **Central Divider Line**:
     * A sharp, horizontal line (`border-top: 2px solid #FFFFFF` or `3px`) bridging the gap between `Hello` and `G'day`.
     * Passes directly through the middle of the video.
     * Length: spans approximately `12vw` to `16vw` depending on container width.
  3. **Right Word**: `G'day`
     * Ends flush with or slightly extending past the right screen border.

### 6.2 Floating Accent Dot
* **Shape**: Perfect geometric circle (solid fill `#FFFFFF`).
* **Diameter**: `clamp(28px, 2.5vw, 42px)`.
* **Placement**:
  * Positioned in the upper right quadrant.
  * Vertically sits around `36%` from the top of the viewport.
  * Horizontally aligned above the apostrophe (`'`) / `d` area of `G'day`.
  * Creates an intentional visual counterweight to the horizontal line.

---

## 7. Interactive Behaviors & Polish

1. **Hover States**:
   * Navigation links shift smoothly from `#FFFFFF` to `#8E8E8E` on mouse-over (`transition: color 250ms ease`).
2. **Video Behavior**:
   * Autoplays inline, muted, continuously looping (`autoplay loop muted playsinline`).
3. **Cursor Interaction**:
   * Hovering over the central video displays an inverted circular custom cursor or play/reel interaction indicator.
4. **Text Layering Contrast**:
   * When typography traverses across bright portions of the video, it remains readable due to the low-key, dark-blue grading of the media asset.
