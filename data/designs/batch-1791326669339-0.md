# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Fintech Mobile App UI, Neumorphic / Glassmorphic Hybrid, High Contrast Dark Card on Off-White Canvas.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace density with soft pill containers, floating action bars, and distinct modular card sections grouped logically with 16px to 24px gaps.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered surface architecture using subtle drop shadows (`0 8px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`), crisp 1px neutral borders (`#E2E8F0`), and dark primary floating balance card with high contrast.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-contrast dark cards embedded inside light minimalist canvas, pill-shaped action buttons, rounded progress indicators, clean tabular analytics bars.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 10px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.02em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#DCFCE7` (success), text `#16A34A`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Plus Sign (+) -> Use `&#43;` or inline SVG
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`
