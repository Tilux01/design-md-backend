# Design Specification: Interactive Apple AirPods Max Showcase Card

---

## 1. Overview & Layout Concept

This design is a dynamic, split-panel horizontal interactive showcase for the Apple AirPods Max headphones. 

- **Presentation Canvas:** The full-screen viewport hosts a horizontally divided container or carousel-style multi-column showcase.
- **Card Structure:** A wide, horizontally split container (with prominent rounded corners: ~`32px` to `40px` radius) divided into **4 distinct vertical color columns**.
- **Interactive Logic / Proportions:**
  - **Active / Focused Column (Leftmost - Sky Blue):** Takes up approximately **40% to 45%** of the total card width. It serves as the primary detail section featuring product title, large typography, main product image, pricing, brief description, and a Call-to-Action (CTA) button.
  - **Inactive / Alternate Color Columns (3 Rightmost columns):** Distribute the remaining ~`55% to 60%` width equally (approx. `18% to 20%` each). These columns act as interactive selectors (accordion/color-picker style) displaying alternate product colorways (Pink, Green, Space Gray). Hovering or clicking any column expands it to become the active detail column.

## 2. Color Palette & Column Breakdown

### Column 1: Sky Blue (Active Showcase)
- **Background Color:** Medium muted steel blue / slate blue: `#8CA0B8` (or soft gradient from `#95A9C0` to `#8296AE`).
- **Accent Overlay Glow:** Subtle radial glow behind the headphone cups (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).
- **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (pure white).
- **Text Secondary / Watermark:** Semi-transparent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`).

### Column 2: Pink / Coral
- **Background Color:** Soft warm coral pink: `#FFA59E` (or linear gradient from `#FFAF9B` at the top to `#F89690` at the bottom).
- **Lighting Effect:** Faint soft white light highlight centered vertically around the floating headphone position.

### Column 3: Green / Pistachio
- **Background Color:** Muted sage / matcha green: `#AABCA5` (or linear gradient from `#B2C4AC` to `#9FB09A`).
- **Lighting Effect:** Faint diffuse light directly behind the center of the headphones.

### Column 4: Purple / Space Gray (Light Violet Base)
- **Background Color:** Soft lavender / periwinkle purple: `#B4B0D5` (or linear gradient from `#BCB8DC` to `#A8A4CC`).
- **Lighting Effect:** Centered ambient glow behind the dark headphones.

1. **Large Background Watermark ("APPLE"):**

   - **Font Weight:** Heavy / Black (`800` or `900`).
   - **Font Size:** Approx. `96px` – `110px`.

   - **Text Transform:** UPPERCASE.

2. **Main Overlay Title ("APPLE"):**

   - **Font Weight:** Black (`900`).
   - **Font Size:** Approx. `96px` – `110px`.
   - **Color:** Solid pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
   - **Tracking:** Matching the watermark (`4px` to `6px`).

3. **Product Price:**
   - **Text:** `$239.00`
   - **Font Weight:** Bold (`700`).
   - **Font Size:** Approx. `20px` – `22px`.

4. **Product Description Copy:**
   - **Text:** *"Experience the modernised design of the apple Controller, featuring sculpted surfaces and"* (Note: Mockup text contains this specific copy).
   - **Font Weight:** Regular (`400`) / Medium (`500`).

   - **Line Height:** `1.4`.

## 6. Container Dimensions, Borders & Shadows

- **Showcase Card Dimensions:**
  - **Aspect Ratio:** Approximately `16:9` or `1.8:1`.
  - **Desktop Width:** `900px` – `1050px` (or dynamic `85vw` - `90vw` maxing at `1100px`).
  - **Height:** `520px` – `580px`.
- **Border Radius:** `36px` uniform on all outer corners. Card content clips at boundary (`overflow: hidden`).
- **Dividers:** Clean vertical division between columns; no borders or lines—transitions are purely defined by color contrast.
- **Card Shadow:** Smooth, elevated depth shadow:
  ```css
  box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.18),
              0 8px 16px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08);
  ```

---

## 7. Interactive & Animation States (Accordion Carousel)

- **Hover / Click Transition:**
  - Clicking any of the 3 collapsed columns smoothly expands its flex-basis/width from `~18%` to `~42%`, while collapsing the currently active column down to `~18%`.
  - **Transition Timing:** `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` with a duration of `600ms`.
  - **Scale Animation:** The newly activated headphone image smoothly scales up (`transform: scale(1.45)`), while the collapsed ones scale down to `scale(1)`.
  - **Content Fade:** The text overlay (`APPLE`), pricing, description, and "Buy Now" button fade in (`opacity: 1`, `translateY(0)`) on the active column and fade out (`opacity: 0`, `translateY(10px)`) on inactive columns with a `300ms` ease.
