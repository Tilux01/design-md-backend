# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Modern FinTech / Healthcare Mobile Application, Clean Glassmorphism with Neumorphic Soft Shadows, Editorial Minimalist.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace focus with rounded card containers, floating navigation pills, generous internal padding, and distinct visual hierarchy separating background maps from active modal cards.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered depth architecture consisting of a light map background (Surface 0), elevated white frosted cards with soft diffused drop shadows (Surface 1), and high-contrast dark floating elements (Surface 2).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient blur glow orbs, translucent glassmorphism surfaces, subtle boundary borders, contextual location map pins.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `0.75rem`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.1)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
