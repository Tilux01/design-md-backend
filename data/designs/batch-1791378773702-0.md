# Design Specification: Kove Brand Website (Landing Page)

This document outlines the exact design specifications for creating a pixel-perfect, responsive clone of the provided Kove brand website landing page. All device frames, shadows, and outer borders shown in the original presentation are omitted; the implementation should fill the full screen viewport.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport

*   **View:** Full-screen responsive web layout (`width: 100vw; height: 100vh; overflow: hidden;`).
*   **Grid Structure:** Asymmetrical two-column split screen:
    *   **Left Column (Brand Identity Panel):** Takes up approximately **50% to 55%** of the horizontal viewport. Features a clean, solid background, centered typography, navigation links, and copyright text.
    *   **Right Column (Visual Collage / Media Grid):** Takes up the remaining horizontal space. Arranged as a vertical masonry/grid layout containing multiple photographic elements separated by clean white/light-grey gutter lines.

---

## 2. Color Palette

*   **Primary Background (Left Panel):** Pure / Off-White (`#FFFFFF` or `#FAFAFA`)
*   **Typography & Branding:** Deep Charcoal / Rich Black (`#0A0A0A`)
*   **Grid Gutters / Dividers:** Dark Charcoal / Black (`#000000` or `#111111`) creating sharp separations between photographic cards.
*   **Images:** Natural photography spanning moody blues, earthy greens, soft overcast skies, and neutral tones.

---

## 3. Typography & Font Stack

### Brand Logo & Headings
*   **Font Family Primary:** A high-contrast, modern serif typeface with elegant, exaggerated curved terminals (resembling typefaces like *GT Alpina*, *Roslindale*, *SangBleu*, or *Delyth*).
*   **Fallback Font Stack:** `Georgia`, `Cambria`, `"Times New Roman"`, `serif`.
*   **Logo Specifications:**
    *   **Text:** "Kove" (with a registered trademark symbol `®` subscript/superscript aligned to the top right of the 'e').
    *   **Styling:** Extra-large display size, lowercase-heavy with a capitalized 'K', distinct italicized or curved flourish on the entry stroke of the 'K'.

### Navigation & Micro-Copy
*   **Font Family:** Clean grotesque or neo-grotesque sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*, *Neue Haas Grotesk*).
*   **Fallback Font Stack:** `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Navigation Links (Top Right of Left Panel):** Small, uppercase, tracked-out letterspacing (`letter-spacing: 0.1em`). Items: `PROJECTS`, `ABOUT`, `CONTACT`.
*   **Footer / Metadata (Bottom of Left Panel):** 
    *   Bottom-left: Copyright/Location info (e.g., *© 2023 ALL RIGHTS RESERVED*).
    *   Bottom-right: Status indicator or secondary meta (e.g., *SCROLL DOWN* or interactive indicator).

---

## 4. Section-by-Section Breakdown

### A. Left Panel (Brand Hero Section)
*   **Alignment:** Absolute center vertically and horizontally for the main logo mark. Flexbox layout (`display: flex; flex-direction: column; justify-content: space-between;`).
*   **Padding:** Generous internal padding (approx. `40px` to `60px` on all sides).
*   **Header Navigation:** Positioned at the top right, items spaced evenly with subtle hover states (opacity change from `1` to `0.6`).
*   **Centerpiece:** The oversized "Kove®" wordmark.
*   **Footer Area:** Small, muted sans-serif text anchored to the bottom edge.

### B. Right Panel (Dynamic Image Collage)
*   **Layout:** Multi-row vertical grid system displaying lifestyle and landscape photography.
*   **Images Breakdown (Top to Bottom):**
    1.  **Top Left Sub-grid:** Landscape shot featuring a wide open green field under an overcast, moody sky.
    2.  **Top Right Sub-grid:** Detail shot (e.g., silhouette/figure on a rugged terrain/roof).
    3.  **Middle Sub-grid:** Central feature portrait of a person standing in a misty, ethereal outdoor landscape (beach/desert/salt flat).
    4.  **Right-Middle Sub-grid:** Interior/POV vehicle shot looking out toward a body of water with a steering wheel in frame.
    5.  **Bottom Sub-grid:** Soft atmospheric gradient/horizon view paired with coastal/water photography.
*   **Borders:** Each image tile is wrapped in a clean, thin border or separated by a sharp grid gap (`gap: 4px` to `8px` with a dark background showing through).

---

## 5. Animation & Interaction Specifications

*   **Page Load / Entrance Animation:**
    *   **Fade In:** The entire interface fades in smoothly over `0.8s` using an `ease-out` cubic-bezier curve (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
    *   **Staggered Reveal:** The right-hand image collage items slide upward slightly (`translateY(20px)` to `0`) with a staggered delay (`0.1s` per image block).
*   **Hover Effects:**
    *   **Images:** Subtle zoom-in effect (`transform: scale(1.03)` with `transition: transform 0.6s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`) when hovering over any individual photo tile in the right column.
    *   **Nav Links:** Smooth opacity transition on hover.
*   **Scrolling Behavior:**
    *   The right-hand image collage acts as a smooth parallax or vertical scroll container while the left-hand branding panel remains fixed (`position: sticky; top: 0;`) during desktop viewports.

---

## 6. Responsive Breakpoints

*   **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** Side-by-side split screen (Left: Fixed Brand Panel, Right: Scrollable Image Grid).
*   **Tablet & Mobile (`<= 1024px`):** Stacked layout. The left brand panel appears first, followed seamlessly by the image collage stacked vertically underneath.
