# Design Specification: "Jacket Masters" Puffer Jacket E-Commerce Landing Page

This document provides the exact design specifications for replicating the provided landing page, optimized for a full-screen implementation (omitting the outer mockup frame/shadows).

---

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Color Palette
*   **Primary Background (Gradient):**
    *   `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF7E1D 0%, #D45B00 50%, #8C2F00 100%)`
    *   *Fallback solid color:* `#E66A00`
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   Primary Headings & Key Prices: `#FFFFFF` (White)
    *   Body Text & Subtitles: `#F0D5C2` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` (Soft Warm Off-White)
    *   Strikethrough Price: `#C28B6B` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`
*   **UI Elements / Buttons:**
    *   Pill Navigation / Accent Background: Semi-transparent dark orange/brown overlay `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)` with a subtle inner border or backdrop blur (`blur(10px)`).
    *   Selected Size Button: Solid White (`#FFFFFF`) with dark text (`#000000`).
    *   Unselected Size Buttons: Transparent with white border/text, or semi-transparent dark fill (`rgba(0,0,0,0.3)`).

### Typography
*   **Font Family:** Modern Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *SF Pro Display*, *Poppins*, or *Helvetica Neue*).
*   **Fallbacks:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
*   **Hierarchy:**
    *   **Logo Text:** Bold, uppercase, tracking/letter-spacing applied (~1px).
    *   **Hero Heading ("Stand out Without trying"):** Extra-bold (`700`), large scale (~48px - 64px depending on view), tight line-height (~1.1).
    *   **Body Copy:** Regular (`400`), size ~14px–16px, line-height ~1.6.
    *   **Price:** Bold, large display font (~32px for current, ~20px strikethrough for old price).

---

## 2. Layout & Structure (Full-Screen)

### Header / Navigation
*   **Position:** Fixed or absolute, top-center floating pill.
*   **Logo:** Top-left corner (`JM JACKET MASTERS` with an orange square icon containing "JM").
*   **Center Nav Pill:** 
    *   Links: `PUFFER JACKET` (active/highlighted), `ALL PRODUCTS`, `ABOUT US`, `CONTACT`.
    *   Style: Rounded pill container (`border-radius: 50px`), semi-transparent dark background.
*   **Top Right Icons:** Shopping cart icon and Wishlist (heart) icon.

### Hero Section (Left Column)
*   **Carousel Navigation:** Two small arrow buttons (`<` `>`) enclosed in circular semi-transparent containers above the main title.
*   **Headline:** "Stand out Without trying" (Two lines).
*   **Description Paragraph:** 
    > *"It’s not just about staying warm. It’s about stepping outside and instantly feeling confident, comfortable, and completely yourself. Designed to elevate even the simplest outfit, this jacket wraps you in lightweight warmth."*
*   **CTA Button ("Get the look >"):**
    *   Pill shape (`border-radius: 50px`).
    *   Background: White (`#FFFFFF`).
    *   Text color: Dark charcoal/black (`#1A1A1A`).
    *   Padding: ~12px 24px.

### Product Showcase (Center & Right Column)
*   **Main Product Image:** 
    *   High-resolution 3D render/photograph of the Glossy Orange Puffer Jacket, centered prominently.
    *   Subtle drop shadow underneath the jacket on the ground plane to ground the 3D element.
*   **Tagline underneath jacket:** *"Confidence, wrapped in warmth"* (Centered, italicized or lighter font weight).
*   **Pricing (Top Right):**
    *   Current Price: `$149` (Large, bold white).
    *   Original Price: `~$199` (Smaller, strikethrough).
*   **Size Selector:**
    *   Label: *"Choose your size:"*
    *   Options: **36**, **38**, **40** (Circular button pills; size `36` shown as selected with white background).
*   **Thumbnail Preview (Bottom Right):**
    *   Small thumbnail of an alternate colorway (e.g., dark metallic/black jacket) with a subtle border/selection state.

### Footer / Social Links (Bottom Left)
*   Icons for social channels: Instagram, Facebook, Globe/Web, Behance.
*   Color: White icons with low opacity (~0.7).

---

## 3. Animations & Interactions

1.  **Page Load / Entrance Animations:**
    *   **Staggered Fade-in:** The logo, header pill, and social links fade in first.
    *   **Hero Text Slide:** The headline and paragraph slide in from the left with a subtle fade (`transform: translateX(-20px)` to `0`).
    *   **Jacket Float / Scale:** The central puffer jacket scales up smoothly from 95% to 100% with a slight float effect (infinite CSS floating animation: `translateY(0px)` -> `translateY(-10px)` -> `translateY(0px)` over 4 seconds).
2.  **Hover States:**
    *   **Nav Links:** Smooth color transition to bright orange or full white opacity.
    *   **Buttons ("Get the look"):** Slight scale up (`scale(1.05)`) and enhanced box-shadow.
    *   **Size Options:** Background transitions smoothly when clicked/hovered.
    *   **Carousel Arrows:** Background darkens slightly on hover.
3.  **Dynamic Elements:**
    *   Clicking size options (36, 38, 40) toggles the active white background state.
    *   Clicking the thumbnail on the bottom right triggers a product image swap with a smooth cross-fade animation.
