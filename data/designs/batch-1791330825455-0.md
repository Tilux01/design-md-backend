# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: High-Tech Genomic Science & Dark Mode Glassmorphism.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Spacious full-viewport presentation with immersive gradient depth, high focus on central 3D molecular structures.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered depth through radial background gradients (`linear-gradient` from electric cyan to deep ultraviolet), floating translucent 3D models with natural spatial shadows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient cyan/violet radial glows, glassmorphism surface layers, subtle biological iconography.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.1)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-cyan-400 inline-block"></span>`.
