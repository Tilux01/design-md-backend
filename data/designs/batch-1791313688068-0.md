# 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
- **Primary Mood & Archetype:** High-end health and fitness tracking dashboard optimized for dark environments. Sleek, biometric, modern, and focused on clarity and habit formation.
- **Visual Density:** Compact card-based layout featuring rich data visualization, progress rings, and multi-state metric displays balanced with generous inner card padding.
- **Whitespace Balance:** Tight vertical stacking of frosted-glass metric modules separated by deep ambient space against a dark, cosmic abstract backdrop.
- **Theme Balance:** Pure dark-mode first design utilizing deep navy-black backgrounds (`#0a0a12`), translucent glass surfaces with subtle borders, and vivid accent markers (electric blue/violet for navigation, crisp orange/amber for calorie burn indicators).

# 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
- **Container:** Mobile device viewport (Fixed width 390px / 414px reference, full bleed).
- **Padding & Margin Scale:** 
  - Outer screen horizontal padding: `20px`
  - Vertical stack gap between cards: `16px`
  - Internal card padding: `20px`
- **Section Sequence Breakdown (Top to Bottom):**
  1. Status Bar / Device System Clock & Icons
  2. Header Bar (`RiseUp` branding and `DAY / WEEKLY` pill toggle switch)
  3. Primary Metric Card 1: Sleep Tracker (`Sleep at least 7 hours today`)
  4. Secondary Metric Card 2: Calorie Burn Tracker (`Burn at least 600 kcal today`)
  5. Bottom Navigation Bar (Floating pill dock with 5 navigational tabs)
- **Stacking Order (Z-Index):**
  - Background Layer: `z-0`
  - Cards & Content Layer: `z-10`
  - Floating Bottom Navigation: `z-50`

# 7. Accessibility (a11y) & WCAG Contrast Audit

- **Contrast Ratios:**
  - Primary white text (`#FFFFFF`) on Dark Glass Background (`#0A0B10`): **16.5:1** (Passes WCAG AAA)
  - Secondary text (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.55)`): **5.4:1** (Passes WCAG AA for normal text)
  - Active toggle buttons / Indicators against dark cards: Exceeds **7:1** threshold.
- **Keyboard Focus Ring Specs:**
  ```css
  button:focus-visible, a:focus-visible {
    outline: 2px solid #8E82FF;
    outline-offset: 2px;
  }
  ```
