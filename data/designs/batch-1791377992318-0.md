# UI/UX Specification: Immersive AR Virtual Try-On & E-Commerce Interface

## 1. Global Layout & Aesthetic Overview
- **Design Style:** Spatial computing frosted-glass UI (Neumorphism / Glassmorphism), optimized for AR/VR environments (e.g., Apple Vision Pro).
- **Background Layer:** A warm, neutral, photorealistic interior living room setting featuring a textured area rug, wooden flooring, modern leather armchairs, and lush indoor greenery. 
- **Application Window:** Floating translucent glassmorphic panel with high blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`), rounded corners, and soft white specular borders.
- **Color Palette:**
  - `Primary Glass`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` with `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`
  - `Text Primary`: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - `Text Muted`: Soft Gray (`#A0A0A0`)
  - `Accent / Interactive Elements`: Dark charcoal pills (`#2A2A2A`), white active pills.

---

## 2. Typography System
*Note: Due to the spatial nature of the UI, a clean, highly legible geometric sans-serif font family is utilized across all elements.*

- **Primary Font Family:** `SF Pro Display`, `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif.
- **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.`
- **Type Hierarchy:**
  - **Category Tabs (Top Navigation):** 
    - Font Size: `13px` | Weight: `500 (Medium)` | Tracking: `0.5px` | Color: `#FFFFFF` / Muted Gray for inactive.
  - **Main Headings (Product Titles):** 
    - Font Size: `14px` | Weight: `600 (Semi-Bold)` | Tracking: `0.2px` | Color: `#FFFFFF`.
  - **Prices & Highlights:** 
    - Font Size: `14px` / `16px` | Weight: `700 (Bold)` | Color: `#FFFFFF`.
  - **Body / Metadata (Size/Color indicators):** 
    - Font Size: `12px` | Weight: `400 (Regular)` | Color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`.

---

## 3. Structural Components & UI Elements

### A. Top Segmentation Navigation (Category Level)
- **Position:** Centered horizontally at the top outer edge of the main container.
- **Style:** Pill-shaped segmented control (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Items:** `Full body` | **`Top Body` (Active)** | `Head` | `Pants` | `Foot`
- **Active State:** Solid white background with dark gray text (`#1A1A1A`).
- **Inactive State:** Transparent background with white text (`#FFFFFF`).

### B. In-App Header Bar
- **Left Side:**
  - **VirtualFit Toggle:** Pill button featuring a white gear/circle icon + text label `"VirtualFit"` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).
  - **Navigation/View Icons:** User silhouette, grid view, and list view icons grouped in compact circular glass buttons.
- **Right Side:**
  - **Add to cart:** Pill button with a shopping cart icon (`"🛒 Add to cart"`).
  - **Checkout:** Pill button with a checkmark icon (`"⊙ Checkout"`).

### C. Left Vertical Toolbar (Sidebar Menu)
- **Position:** Fixed vertically on the left exterior margin of the screen.
- **Style:** Vertical pill container with frosted glass finish.
- **Icons Included (Top to Bottom):**
  1. T-shirt / Apparel icon (Active state: solid white background with dark icon).
  2. Cart / Bag icon.
  3. Layout / Dashboard icon.
  4. User Profile icon.

### D. Central Model & Carousel Stage
- **Model Display:** Centered realistic male model wearing the active item (Plain white crew-neck T-shirt and dark pants).
- **Interactive Garment Carousel (Horizontal Scroll):**
  - **Left Item:** *Basic Sweater* — Off-white knitted crewneck sweater displayed on a floating invisible hanger, accompanied by title `"Basic Sweater"` and price `"$105"`.
  - **Right Item:** *Basic Shirt* — White button-down collared shirt displayed on a floating hanger, accompanied by title `"Basic Shirt"` and price `"$105"`.
  - **Depth Effect:** Non-active items fade slightly into the background with reduced opacity and scale to create a 3D carousel depth perspective.

### E. Floating Active Product Details Card
- **Position:** Bottom center, overlapping the lower torso of the model.
- **Background:** Dark translucent glass (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)` with heavy backdrop blur).
- **Content Structure:**
  - **Header Line:** `"Basic-t-shirt"` (Left) | `"$105"` (Right)
  - **Interactive Selection Pills:**
    - Size Selector: `"Size M"` (Dropdown pill)
    - Color Selector: `"Color White"` (Dropdown pill with a white color swatch indicator)
  - **Action Button:** `"Edit"` pill button on the bottom right.

---

## 4. Animations & Micro-Interactions

- **Carousel Transition (Swipe / Drag):** 
  - *Effect:* Smooth 3D physics-based carousel rotation. When rotating items, the center model morphs or cross-fades seamlessly into the newly selected outfit (`transition: all 0.4s cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`).
- **Hover & Active States:** 
  - *Pills & Buttons:* Subtle scaling (`transform: scale(1.03)`) and background opacity shift (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)` to `0.35`) on hover.
- **Spatial Floating Effect:** 
  - *Ambient Animation:* Floating garments (sweater, button-down) maintain a continuous, subtle vertical bobbing animation (`translateY` offset of ±4px over a 3-second infinite loop) to simulate zero-gravity spatial AR placement.
