# Design Specification: IRIS_AI Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Reset

- **Background Color:** `#e5e5e5` (Light gray gradient background outside the main container, soft glow at the bottom center).
- **Primary Text Color:** `#1a1a1a` (Dark charcoal, almost black).
- **Secondary Text Color:** `#666666` (Muted gray for subtitles/descriptions).
- **Font Family:** Sans-serif (Geometric, modern style like Inter or Helvetica).
- **Layout:** Full-screen viewport, centered content box with subtle rounded corners (`border-radius: 20px`), soft outer drop-shadow, and inner padding.

---

## 2. Header / Navigation

- **Positioning:** Absolute / Flexbox spread across the top of the interface.
- **Left Element:**
  - **Logo Text:** `(IRIS_AI)`
  - **Font Weight:** Bold / Medium.
  - **Version Tag:** `| v01b |` placed just below the logo in small, muted text.
- **Right Menu Items:**
  - Links: `home`, `plans`, `services`, `contact us`.
  - **Style:** Small, lowercase text accompanied by subtle diagonal arrow icons (`↗`).
  - **Layout:** Vertical stack aligned to the right (or horizontal depending on responsiveness, currently shown as a right-aligned vertical list with generous line-height).

---

## 3. Hero Section

### Left Content (Typography & Call to Action)
- **Main Heading:**
  - `"WHERE AI MERGES"` (Bold, clean uppercase typography).
  - `"with"` (Italicized, regular weight).
  - `"// YOUR"` (Prefixed with double slash).
  - `"VISION"` (Bold uppercase).
- **Bottom-Left Controls:**
  - A subtle share/connect icon button (`<`).

### Center Artwork (Abstract 3D Visual)
- **Description:** A bundle of dark, fiber-optic-like strands curving downwards, emitting warm, glowing yellow/orange dots of light at their tips.
- **Position:** Centered prominently within the layout, blending softly into the light gray backdrop.

### Right Content (Secondary Details)
- **Section Identifier:** `( A )`
- **Pagination / Counter:** `[ 001 / 004 ]`
- **Body Text:** 
  - *"IrisAI is where your boldest ideas meet their full potential. We give you the tools not just to see the future."*
  - **Font Size:** Small, highly legible, muted gray.
- **Primary CTA Button:**
  - **Text:** `GET STARTED`
  - **Style:** Pill-shaped outline button with a thin border and centered text.

---

## 4. Footer / Bottom UI Elements

- **Scroll Indicator:** A centered downward-pointing arrow (`↓`) positioned near the bottom center of the interface.
- **Glow Effect:** A warm golden/orange radial glow emanating from underneath the bottom-center of the interface box.
