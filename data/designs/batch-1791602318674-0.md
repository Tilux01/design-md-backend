# Comprehensive Design Specification: Modern Timber Frame Construction Landing Page (`carcas house`)

This document provides a pixel-accurate, implementation-ready design specification for recreating the modern timber frame home construction landing page.

---

## 1. Design Overview & Visual Identity

The interface follows a clean, Scandinavian-inspired architectural aesthetic combining minimal Swiss typography, high-contrast dark charcoal cards, generous negative space, soft neutral background tones, and tactile pill-shaped interactive elements.

* **Target Canvas / Viewport**: Desktop first (`1440px` base canvas, max-content container `1280px` centered, responsive down to `375px`).
* **Design Mood**: Architectural, dependable, modern, sustainable, premium yet accessible.
* **Surface Strategy**: Multi-layered card architecture with large border radiuses (`20px` to `28px`) sitting over an ultra-light warm gray canvas.

## 6. Media & Image Asset Guidelines

### 6.1 Hero Image (Right Side of Hero)
* **Type**: High-resolution architectural photography.
* **Content**: Contemporary two-story timber frame residence with vertical warm-toned wooden siding, black architectural trim, floor-to-ceiling panoramic glass windows showing illuminated warm interior rooms, an integrated open balcony on the second level, surrounded by green fir/pine trees and green lawn under soft daylight.
* **Aspect Ratio**: `4:5` vertical portrait.
* **Styling**: `border-radius: 28px`, `object-fit: cover`.

### 6.2 Structural Roof Frame 3D Model (Section 2 - Left)
* **Type**: Isolated architectural render with **100% transparent PNG background** (zero background artifacts).
* **Content**: An open timber frame roof truss structure featuring clean light pine/spruce framing joists, rafters, ridge board, and wall stud base framing.
* **Angle**: 3/4 perspective axonometric view, tilted downward approximately 20 degrees, rotated to show depth and construction density.
* **Lighting**: Soft studio clay lighting, subtle contact drop-shadow under the bottom edge.

### 6.3 Feature Inset Icons (Section 2 - 3 Dark Cards)
* **Type**: Square rounded mini-renders (`68px × 68px`, `border-radius: 12px`, white background).
  1. *Frame Joint*: Technical 3D close-up of structural cross-laminated timber studs and metal corner bracket tie-down.
  2. *Insulation Sandwich*: Cutaway section showing multi-layer barrier: gypsum, vapor barrier, rockwool/fiber batt insulation, windproof membrane, outer siding.
  3. *Foundation Connection*: Clean foundation sill plate anchor bolt mounting into concrete foundation without gap.

### 6.4 Catalog Images (Section 3 - 4 Cards)
* **Type**: Architectural exterior photographs of completed houses:
  * **House 1 (77m²)**: Single-story Scandinavian wood cabin with large front patio and glass facade in a forest setting.
  * **House 2 (94m²)**: Two-story modern barnhouse with dark standing seam metal roof and warm vertical timber planks.
  * **House 3 (65m²)**: Minimalist cubic modular timber house with dark espresso stained wood cladding and corner windows.
  * **House 4 (60m²)**: Compact A-frame or modern gabled tiny-house villa with lit deck lights at dusk.

## 7. Interactive Behaviors & States

1. **CTA Pill Buttons (`#1B1E22`)**:
   * *Default*: Background `#1B1E22`, color `#FFFFFF`.
   * *Hover*: Background `#2E3339`, subtle transform `translateY(-1px)`.
   * *Active*: Transform `translateY(0px)`, background `#111315`.

2. **Catalog Image Cards**:
   * *Hover*: Zoom image slightly (`transform: scale(1.03)`, transition `0.4s ease`), overlay price tag raises `2px`.

3. **Payment Cards (Diagonal Arrow Buttons `↗`)**:
   * *Hover*: The small round button rotates `45deg` pointing directly right `→`, with slight scale increase (`1.1`).

4. **Carousel Navigation Controls (`‹` / `›`)**:
   * *Hover*: Border color transitions from `#E1E4E8` to `#111315`, background changes to `#F4F5F7`.
