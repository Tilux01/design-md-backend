# UI/UX Design Specification: "Crispy Chicken" Food Delivery Mobile App

This document provides a comprehensive, pixel-accurate design and implementation blueprint for the mobile app screen depicted in the reference image. The design embodies a warm, minimalist "Neumorphic-Lite" aesthetic with high-contrast food photography and energetic orange/red accent colors.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas

*   **View Type:** Full-screen mobile view (Designed for standard iOS/Android viewports, ~390px–414px width).
*   **Background Color (App Canvas):** `#FDF8F2` (Warm off-white / very light peach cream).
*   **Border Radius (if previewed on container):** Curved device corners (~40px) with subtle drop shadows.
*   **Grid & Alignment:** 16px standard horizontal padding (margins). Consistent 12–16px gaps between cards and component blocks.

---

## 2. Typography System

The app utilizes a clean, modern sans-serif typeface family (e.g., **Inter**, **SF Pro Display**, or **Plus Jakarta Sans**). 

*   **Primary Font Family:** `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
*   **Fallback Strategy:** System default sans-serif stack ensuring clean readability across iOS, Android, and web platforms if custom fonts fail to load.

### Type Hierarchy:
1.  **Header Greeting ("Hi, Chicken Lover!"):**
    *   *Size:* 14px / 0.875rem
    *   *Weight:* Regular (400)
    *   *Color:* `#6B635B` (Muted warm gray)
2.  **App Main Tagline ("Crispy. Juicy. Always Delicious."):**
    *   *Size:* 22px / 1.375rem
    *   *Weight:* Bold (700)
    *   *Color:* `#1A1612` (Near black, warm undertone)
3.  **Section Headers ("Popular Combos", etc.):**
    *   *Size:* 18px / 1.125rem
    *   *Weight:* Semi-Bold (600) / Bold (700)
    *   *Color:* `#1A1612`
4.  **Card Titles ("8 Pcs Chicken Bucket"):**
    *   *Size:* 13px / 0.8125rem
    *   *Weight:* Bold (700)
    *   *Color:* `#1A1612`
5.  **Body / Sub-descriptions ("2 Dips + 2 Fries + 2 Drinks"):**
    *   *Size:* 11px / 0.6875rem
    *   *Weight:* Regular (400)
    *   *Color:* `#8C827A`
6.  **Prices ($24.99):**
    *   *Size:* 14px / 0.875rem
    *   *Weight:* Bold (700)
    *   *Color:* `#1A1612`

## 4. Component-by-Component Breakdown

### A. Top Navigation Bar
*   **Hamburger Menu Icon:** Left-aligned. Simple 3-line icon (`#1A1612`), housed inside a circular white container (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle shadow.
*   **User Greeting & Tagline:** Center-left aligned stack. Includes a small emoji (👋) next to the greeting.
*   **Action Icons (Right):**
    *   *Notification Bell:* White circular button with a small orange notification badge and counter ("3").
    *   *Shopping Bag:* White circular button with an orange badge and counter ("2").

### B. Search Bar
*   **Container:** Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 50px`), pure white (`#FFFFFF`), with a subtle inner shadow or soft drop shadow.
*   **Left Element:** Search magnifying glass icon (`#7A7067`).
*   **Placeholder Text:** *"Search for your favorite chicken..."* (Color: `#A89F95`, Size: 13px).
*   **Right Element:** Filter/Sliders icon inside a small circular wrapper.

### C. Hero Banner Carousel ("Crave the Crispy Perfection!")
*   **Background:** Deep rich burgundy-to-black gradient (`#5A0C08` to `#2A0402`), featuring a subtle glowing radial red accent behind the chicken bucket.
*   **Badge:** Pill-shaped tag at the top left reading **"HOT & CRISPY"** in white text over a dark red/black background.
*   **Typography:** 
    *   Headline: *“Crave the Crispy Perfection!”* (White, Bold, ~24px, 2-line wrap).
    *   Subtitle: *“Hand-breaded. Freshly cooked. Every time.”* (Light warm gray, ~12px).
*   **Imagery:** High-definition cutout of a classic red-and-white striped chicken bucket filled with fried chicken, side fries, dipping sauce, and a cola glass.
*   **CTA Button:** Pill-shaped solid orange button (`#FF4500`) with text *"Order Now"* and a right-pointing arrow icon in a white circle.
*   **Pagination Dots:** 5 dots at the bottom center of the banner (1 active white pill, 4 inactive translucent dots).

### D. Horizontal Category Selector
*   **Layout:** Horizontal scrollable row containing 6 category items:
    1.  *All Items* (Active state: Soft peach circular background `#FCEFE6`, highlighted thumbnail).
    2.  *Buckets*
    3.  *Burgers*
    4.  *Tenders*
    5.  *Sides*
    6.  *Drinks*
*   **Card Style:** Each category has a circular thumbnail image with a soft light background and a compact label underneath (11px, Bold).

### E. Section: Popular Combos
*   **Header:** *"Popular Combos"* on the left, *"View All ->"* in orange on the right.
*   **Product Cards (Horizontal Scroll / Grid):**
    *   *Card Background:* Pure white (`#FFFFFF`), border-radius: 16px, subtle drop shadow.
    *   *Top Badges:* Red badge ("BESTSELLER" / "POPULAR" / "SAVE 15%") positioned on the top-left; outline bookmark/heart icon on the top-right.
    *   *Image:* Centered cutout of the combo meal (e.g., bucket + drink + fries).
    *   *Title:* 2-line bold product name.
    *   *Subtitle:* Ingredients/Compositions (e.g., `2 Dips + 2 Fries + 2 Drinks`).
    *   *Rating:* 5 yellow stars (`#FFB800`) followed by numeric rating and review count (e.g., `4.8 (245)`).
    *   *Footer:* Price on the left (`$24.99`) and a floating circular orange **Plus (`+`) button** on the bottom-right for quick adding.

### F. Promo Banner Card ("Spicy Deals")
*   **Container:** Wide horizontal banner with a gradient soft peach/beige background (`#F9EFE6` to `#F3E5D8`), rounded corners (~16px).
*   **Content:**
    *   Tag: *"Spicy Deals 🔥"*
    *   Headline: *"Up to 30% OFF"*
    *   Subtitle: *"On selected combos"*
    *   Button: *"Order Now"* with an arrow inside a pill button.
*   **Visuals:** Large zinger burger with visible spicy sauce dripping, accompanied by a small bowl of chili sauce and side items on the right side of the card. A floating circular badge reads **"30% OFF"** in bold white text over a deep red circular background.

### G. Bottom Navigation Bar
*   **Container:** Floating pill bar at the absolute bottom, elevated over the background with a clean white surface and soft shadow.
*   **Items (Left to Right):**
    1.  **Home:** Orange house icon with active indicator label *"Home"* (`#FF4500`).
    2.  **Menu:** Grid icon with label *"Menu"* (`#8C827A`).
    3.  **Orders (Center):** Elevated prominent circular orange button (`#FF4500`) housing a document/receipt icon. This serves as the primary navigation anchor.
    4.  **Offers:** Tag/Ticket icon with label *"Offers"*.
    5.  **Profile:** User outline icon with label *"Profile"*.

## 5. Animation & Interaction Specifications

*   **Banner Carousel:** Auto-scrolls every 4 seconds with a smooth horizontal slide transition. Manual swipe enabled with spring physics.
*   **Category Selection:** Tapping any category smoothly centers the item with a micro-scale animation (1.05x zoom on active state).
*   **Quick Add (`+` buttons):** Tapping the plus button triggers a ripple effect and a floating particle animation towards the cart icon in the top right.
*   **Bottom Bar Navigation:** Smooth tab transition with color morphing (inactive gray `#8C827A` transitions to active primary orange `#FF4500`).
