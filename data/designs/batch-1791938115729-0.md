This design document provides a comprehensive specification for recreating the **Delfi TV Tizen App** interface. The goal is a pixel-perfect, full-screen implementation.

---

# Design Specification: Delfi TV Interface

## 1. Global Styles & Environment
*   **Canvas Size:** 1920px x 1080px (Standard 16:9 TV Resolution).
*   **Background:** A deep radial or linear gradient. 
    *   **Direction:** Top-left to bottom-right.
    *   **Colors:** Start at `#0a0e1a` (Deep Midnight Blue) transitioning to `#1a3a8a` (Royal Blue) in the center-right, and back to a darker shade at the edges.
*   **Overall Aesthetic:** Dark mode, glassmorphism influences, high contrast for readability at a distance.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Inter** or **Roboto**.
*   **Fallback Fonts:** System-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif.
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   Primary: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
    *   Secondary/Subtitle: `#A0A0A0` (Light Grey, approx 60% opacity).
*   **Font Weights:**
    *   Logo/Time: Bold (700).
    *   Card Titles: Semi-Bold (600).
    *   Subtitles/Date: Regular (400).

## 3. Layout Sections

### A. Header (Top Left)
*   **Logo Asset:** A square icon with rounded corners (approx. 8px radius). Background color is a vibrant blue `#2563EB`. Inside is a white capital letter "D" and a small graphic of two hands shaking/holding. This image should be **backgroundless (PNG)** if placed on a custom container.
*   **App Name:** Text "Delfi TV" placed to the right of the logo.
    *   **Font Size:** 32px.
    *   **Spacing:** 15px gap between logo and text.

### B. Vertical Navigation Sidebar (Left)
*   **Width:** 120px.
*   **Position:** Centered vertically on the left edge.
*   **Icons:** Five circular action buttons.
    1.  **Search:** Magnifying glass.
    2.  **Home (Active):** A house silhouette inside a blue glowing circle.
    3.  **Live TV:** A classic TV set icon.
    4.  **Favorites:** A heart icon.
    5.  **Settings:** A cog/gear icon.
*   **Active State:** The "Home" icon is encased in a blue circular highlight (`#2563EB`) with a soft outer glow. Other icons are white with 50% opacity.

### C. Main Content (Center Cards)
Three vertical rectangular cards with rounded corners (20px radius).

1.  **Card 1 (Active/Focused):**
    *   **Background:** A bright blue gradient (`#2563EB` to `#1E40AF`).
    *   **Top Badge:** A small rounded rectangle labeled "Tiešraide" (Live) in white text on a slightly darker blue background.
    *   **Bottom Glow:** A vibrant blue horizontal neon-style glow effect at the very bottom of the card.
    *   **Content:** A TV icon, Title "Delfi TV - Tiešraide", Subtitle "Tagad studijā".
2.  **Card 2 (Inactive):**
    *   **Background:** Semi-transparent dark grey/blue (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
    *   **Content:** Newspaper icon, Title "Delfi TV - Ziņas", Subtitle "Pēdējie jaunumi".
3.  **Card 3 (Inactive):**
    *   **Background:** Same as Card 2.
    *   **Content:** Radio/Podcast icon, Title "Delfi TV - Podcasti", Subtitle "Tagad studijā".

**Card Dimensions:** Approx 280px width x 450px height.
**Spacing:** 20px gap between cards.

### D. Information Widget (Bottom Right)
*   **Weather Component:** 
    *   **Icon:** A white line-art icon of a cloud with raindrops. **Backgroundless PNG.**
    *   **Temperature:** "13°C" in 36px white text.
*   **Clock:**
    *   **Time:** "12:51" in large, bold 120px white text.
*   **Date:**
    *   **Text:** "Otrdien, 28 Februārī".
    *   **Style:** 24px, white, centered under the time.

## 4. Iconography Details
All icons should be **White, Line-art style, and Backgroundless (PNG or SVG)**.
*   **Search:** Simple thin-stroke circle with a diagonal handle at 45 degrees.
*   **Home:** Solid house shape with a pointed roof.
*   **TV Icon:** Rectangle with two small antennas on top.
*   **Heart:** Standard symmetrical heart shape.
*   **Settings:** 8-tooth gear/cogwheel.
*   **News Icon:** A rectangle representing a folded newspaper with horizontal lines.
*   **Podcast Icon:** A rectangle representing a radio with a circular dial and speaker grille.

## 5. Spacing & Alignment
*   **Sidebar Padding:** 40px from the left edge.
*   **Info Widget Padding:** 80px from the right edge, 100px from the bottom edge.
*   **Card Alignment:** The cards are centered vertically within the main viewport, slightly offset to the left to balance the large clock on the right.
*   **Vertical Spacing in Cards:** Icons and text are bottom-aligned within the cards, leaving the top 60% of the card as empty visual space (or space for thumbnails).
