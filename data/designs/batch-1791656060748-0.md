## 1. Global Foundations & System Architecture

### 1.1 Color Palette
The application utilizes a clean, high-contrast, modern editorial style with a stark primary accent to drive calls to action (CTA).

*   **Primary Brand / Interactive Red:** 
    *   `#FF3B30` (Apple iOS Red / Vibrant Crimson)
    *   *Usage:* Primary CTAs, active selection states, checkmarks, badge counts, notifications, and interactive toggles.
*   **Surface / Background:** 
    *   Light Mode: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) for cards and modals; `#F2F2F7` (iOS Grouped Background Light Grey) for app background canvases.
*   **Text Hierarchy:**
    *   Primary Headings: `#000000` (Pure Black) or `#1C1C1E` (iOS Label Primary).
    *   Secondary Body/Labels: `#8E8E93` (iOS Label Secondary).
    *   Tertiary Metadata / Timestamp: `#AEAEB2` (iOS Label Tertiary).
*   **Dividers & Borders:**
    *   `#E5E5EA` (iOS Separator Light).

### 1.2 Typography System
The typography follows a clean, geometric, humanist sans-serif structure optimized for readability in editorial and news formats.

*   **Primary Typeface:** **SF Pro Display** / **SF Pro Text** (System Native for iOS).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `Inter`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, sans-serif.
*   **Type Scale:**
    *   *Screen Title (e.g., "Create an account", "Choose your topics"):* 28px–32px, Bold (Weight: 700), Line height: 1.2.
    *   *Section Header / Card Title:* 16px–18px, Semi-Bold (Weight: 600).
    *   *Body Text / Input Text:* 14px–15px, Regular (Weight: 400).
    *   *Metadata / Badges / Captions:* 11px–12px, Regular/Medium (Weight: 400–500).

## 3. Asset & Image Direction (No-Code Descriptions)

Every image asset utilized across these screens follows strict editorial art direction:
*   **Architectural Photos:** Crisp daylight architectural photography featuring historical European stone buildings with tiled roofs and clear blue sky backgrounds.
*   **Sports Photography:** Dynamic, motion-blurred sports shots capturing movement (running, soccer) with high contrast.
*   **Portraits:** Clean, professional headshots with neutral studio lighting and shallow depth-of-field background blur (bokeh).
*   **Empty States & Icons:** Monochromatic iconography styled using SF Symbols standards (stroke weight: regular, 1.5pt).
