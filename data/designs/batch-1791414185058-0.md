# Leafora - Plant E-Commerce App Design Specification

## 1. Global Design System & Foundations

### 1.1. Color Palette
The design utilizes a sophisticated, nature-inspired dark forest green aesthetic with glowing glassmorphism accents.

*   **Primary Dark Base:** `#0F1C16` (Deep forest background)
*   **Surface Cards / Glass:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`
*   **Primary Accent / CTA:** `#A8C99B` (Sage green) to `#8DAF7E` (Muted olive green)
*   **Primary CTA Text / Dark Text:** `#0A130F` (Deep dark green for high contrast on light buttons)
*   **Text Primary (Light):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for main headings)
*   **Text Secondary (Muted):** `#A3B8B0` (Soft sage-gray for subtext and descriptions)
*   **Borders / Dividers:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` / subtle inner glows

### 1.2. Typography
*   **Primary Font Family (Headings):** *Cormorant Garamond* or *Playfair Display* (Editorial, high-end serif style).
*   **Secondary Font Family (Body/UI):** *Inter* or *SF Pro Display* (Clean sans-serif for high legibility at small sizes).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif;` for headers, and `font-family: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif;` for body text.

## 3. Interactive States & Animations (Motion Specs)

1.  **Page Transitions:** Smooth slide-in from right (`cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`, duration `350ms`) when navigating from Home to Product Detail or Cart.
2.  **Glassmorphism Hover / Tap:** 
    *   Buttons scale down slightly to `0.97` on active tap (`transform: scale(0.97)`).
    *   Glass cards exhibit an enhanced specular highlight pulse when tapped.
3.  **Cart Stepper Animation:** Instant numeric counter update with a subtle scale spring animation on the quantity text (`1` -> `2`).
4.  **Wishlist Heart Toggle:** Heart icon transforms from outline (`♡`) to filled red/white heart (`♥`) with a quick bounce keyframe animation (`scale(1.2)` -> `scale(1.0)`).
