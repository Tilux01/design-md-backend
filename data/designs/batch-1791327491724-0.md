# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Industrial Luxury / Precision Engineering Minimalism.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-end hardware presentation focused on tactile minimalism, emphasizing material texture, machined metal tolerances, and mechanical articulation.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered brushed-aluminum planes interacting through clean micro-shadows, directional highlights, and mechanical fasteners (hex-socket precision screws).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Brushed metal grain lines, mechanical fasteners, precise chamfered edges, shadow depth mapping.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `0.75rem`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#16181C`, border `1px solid #4A4F56`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Hex Fastener (⚙) -> Use `&#9881;` or SVG component.
  - Status Indicator (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
