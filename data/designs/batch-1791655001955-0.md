- **Target Device:** iPhone 14 Pro / 15 Pro (Dynamic Island layout).
- **Dimensions:** 393px × 852px (Standard iOS viewport).
- **Global Constraints:** 
  - Omit device mockups (frames, bezels, buttons, shadows).

  - Screen edge padding: `16px` left and right.

  - **Background (Screen 1 - Light):** `#F3F3F5` (Off-white / Light gray).
