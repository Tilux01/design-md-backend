# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Futuristic Minimalist Glassmorphism SaaS / Web3 platform. Clean, ethereal, and high-tech with soft lavender/periwinkle lighting and 3D fluid sculptures.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio focusing heavily on asymmetric hero layouts, bold typography on the left, and immersive 3D abstract imagery on the right.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean flat grid background overlaid with glossy, extruded 3D tracks, soft drop shadows (`0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`), and floating golden currency assets.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient lavender depth lighting, subtle particle specs across background grid, smooth 3D extruded metallic tracks, glossy floating circular assets.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `13px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.04em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2DCEF`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
