# Design Specification: Stodio® Agency Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Color Palette

### Colors
- **Primary Background (Dark):** `#0a0a0a` / `#000000`
- **Secondary Background (Light):** `#ffffff`
- **Accent Red:** `#e12d2d` (Tags, buttons, indicators)
- **Text Dark:** `#111111`
- **Text Light / Muted:** `#737373` / `#a3a3a3`
- **Text White:** `#ffffff`
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#e5e5e5` (light mode), `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` (dark mode), dashed lines in `#d4d4d4` and `#8b1e1e`

---

## 2. Typography

- **Primary Typeface:** `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, or `Söhne`
- **Fallback Font Stack:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
- **Monospace (Tags):** `JetBrains Mono`, `Roboto Mono`, or monospace fallback

### Hierarchy
- **Hero Title (H1):** `4.5rem` (72px) | Font-weight: `700` | Line-height: `1.05` | Tracking: `-0.03em`
- **Section Heading (H2):** `2.75rem` (44px) | Font-weight: `600` | Line-height: `1.15` | Tracking: `-0.02em`
- **Stats Numerals:** `3.5rem` (56px) | Font-weight: `700` | Line-height: `1.0`
- **Body & Subtitles:** `1rem` – `1.125rem` (16–18px) | Font-weight: `400`–`500` | Line-height: `1.4`–`1.5`
- **Labels / Meta / Tags:** `0.75rem` (12px) | Uppercase | Font-weight: `600` | Tracking: `0.05em`

---

## 3. Component & Layout Breakdown

### 3.1. Navigation Bar
- **Position:** Absolute / Sticky top, transparent overlay over hero.
- **Left:** Stodio® wordmark with red dot-matrix icon.
- **Center:** Nav links (`Home`, `Studio`, `Work [25]`, `Careers`, `Blog`, `Contact`) with a red pill notification tag over "Work".
- **Right CTA:** Pill button (`border-radius: 9999px`) with avatar thumbnail, text `"Book A Intro ↗"`, white fill, dark text.

### 3.2. Hero Section
- **Background:** High-contrast, motion-blurred black & white photo with dark overlay gradient.
- **Top Badge:** `+ Next-Gen Design Agency` in monospace red/white.
- **Heading:** Left-aligned, white H1 spanning 60% container width.
- **Bottom Left:** Tag list (`+ Define`, `+ Design`, `+ Development`) spaced horizontally.
- **Bottom Right:** 
  - Text block: *"Branding / Mobile & Web App Design for Startups and Giants"*.
  - Action buttons: Red filled pill (`View Projects ↗`) and white pill (`Reach Out ↗`).

### 3.3. Client Logo Ticker
- **Container:** Full-width light section (`#ffffff`), single-row flex layout.
- **Left Label:** Asterisk icon + `"WE'VE DONE 500+ ENTERPRISE AND BUSINESS CONSULTING."` in bold 11px uppercase.
- **Logos:** Monochromatic SVGs (Red Bull, JD, XBOX, HubSpot, etc.) with infinite horizontal scroll marquee.

### 3.4. Introduction Section ("Who We Are")
- **Layout:** White background, two-column split.
- **Left Column:** Tag `[+] Who we are` followed by H2: *"We build search-first digital systems to help category leaders lead their industries."* 
  - Dual-tone text: First clause `#111111`, concluding words (`leaders lead their industries.`) in `#a3a3a3`.
- **Right Column:** Bottom-aligned anchor button `→ ABOUT THE STUDIO` in uppercase bold tracking.

### 3.5. Gallery Showcase
- **Layout:** 4-column dynamic grid with rounded corners (`border-radius: 16px`).
- **Aspect Ratio:** `3:4` vertical portrait cards.
- **Content:** Motion-blurred, hyper-saturated portraits (ambient red, diffused radial light, sepia motion streak, warm amber).
- **Behavior:** Subtle zoom/parallax on hover.

### 3.6. Metrics Grid
- **Container:** 4 equal columns spaced evenly with dashed top/bottom borders.
- **Columns:**
  1. `1%` | **Expert-Vetted** | Micro-copy text.
  2. `30+` | **Clients served** | Micro-copy text.
  3. `100%` | **Success Score** | Micro-copy text.
  4. `8+` | **Years of expertise** | Micro-copy text.
- **Separators:** Horizontal dashed line (`border-bottom: 1px dashed #d4d4d4`) under each number.

### 3.7. Services Section
- **Theme:** Transitions to solid black (`#0a0a0a`).
- **Tag:** `[+] Services` in red.
- **Header:** `"Our [Icon Badge] Services"` with right-aligned muted description text.
- **Content:** Red dashed accent divider followed by accordion/list preview card items.

---

## 4. Animations & Micro-Interactions

- **Navigation CTA:** Hover scales button (`scale(1.03)`) with inset shadow shift.
- **Logo Marquee:** Continuous CSS keyframe translation:
  ```css
  @keyframes ticker {
    0% { transform: translateX(0); }
    100% { transform: translateX(-50%); }
  }
  ```
- **Text Reveal:** Split-type slide-up animation (`opacity: 0; transform: translateY(20px)` to `opacity: 1; transform: translateY(0)`) on scroll.
- **Gallery Cards:** Overflow hidden; images scale `1.05` on hover with a `0.4s ease-out` transition.
