This is a detailed design specification for a high-fidelity clone of the "Primland Residences" landing page.

# Design Specification: Primland Residences Landing Page

## 1. General Layout & Atmosphere
*   **Viewport:** Full-screen (100vw, 100vh), no margins or external borders.
*   **Composition:** Centered hero content with a top-bar navigation and a bottom utility link.
*   **Atmosphere:** Luxury, serene, and adventurous. The design uses high-contrast white text against a dark, lush green organic background.

---

## 2. Background Layer
*   **Primary Image:** A high-resolution, top-down aerial (satellite-style) photograph of a dense, mountainous forest.
    *   **Visual Details:** The forest is vibrant green with deep shadows in the valleys. A winding, dark river snakes through the terrain from top to bottom.
    *   **Clouds/Mist:** Layered over the terrain are soft, semi-transparent white clouds and mist. These should appear to have varying depths—some tucked into valleys, others floating above the trees.
    *   **Placement:** The image should be `background-size: cover` and `background-position: center`.
*   **Overlays:**
    *   **Vignette:** A subtle radial gradient overlay (darker towards the edges, clearer in the center) to ensure text legibility.
    *   **Mist Layer:** If implemented as a separate asset, these are backgroundless (PNG/WebP) wisps of white smoke/clouds concentrated in the bottom-left and center-right areas.

---

## 3. Typography & Fonts
*   **Primary Serif (Headings):** A sophisticated, high-contrast serif font.
    *   **Recommended:** *Cormorant Garamond* or *Cinzel*.
    *   **Fallback:** *Times New Roman, Georgia, serif*.
*   **Secondary Sans-Serif (UI/Subtitles):** A clean, wide-tracked geometric sans-serif.
    *   **Recommended:** *Montserrat* (Light/Regular) or *Futura*.
    *   **Fallback:** *Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif*.

---

## 4. Header Section (Top Bar)
*   **Placement:** Fixed or absolute at the top, spanning the full width with roughly `50px` padding on left and right.
*   **Left Element (Icon):** A small, minimalist logo icon. It resembles a stylized, thin-stroked "W" or a wave pattern. Color: Off-white.
*   **Center Element (Main Logo):**
    *   **Icon:** A small, symmetrical crest/fleur-de-lis style icon at the top.
    *   **Text:** "Primland" in a medium-weight serif. Below it, in a much smaller, wide-spaced sans-serif: "RESIDENCES".
*   **Right Element (Action):** The word "INQUIRE" in all-caps sans-serif. Font size: ~12px. Letter spacing: ~2px.

---

## 5. Hero Content (Center)
*   **Alignment:** Vertically and horizontally centered.
*   **Element 1: The Spark:** A tiny, 4-pointed gold/white star icon centered above the text.
*   **Element 2: Top Label:** "IN THE HEART OF" in all-caps sans-serif. Small font size (~10-12px), high letter spacing.
*   **Element 3: Main Heading:** "THE BLUE RIDGE MOUNTAINS".
    *   **Style:** All-caps serif. Large font size (~60px-80px).
    *   **Layout:** Split into two lines: "THE BLUE RIDGE" and "MOUNTAINS".
*   **Element 4: Tagline:** "*Journey to the ultimate adventure paradise.*"
    *   **Style:** Serif, italicized, sentence case. Lower opacity (~0.8) or slightly grey-white.
*   **Element 5: Call to Action (Button):**
    *   **Text:** "EXPLORE THE MAP" (All-caps sans-serif).
    *   **Style:** Rectangular, solid white background, dark charcoal text.
    *   **Dimensions:** Wide padding (approx. 15px top/bottom, 40px left/right).

---

## 6. Footer Section (Bottom)
*   **Placement:** Centered at the very bottom, approx. `40px` from the edge.
*   **Element:** "START WITHOUT AUDIO".
    *   **Style:** All-caps sans-serif, very small font size.
    *   **Decoration:** A very thin, 1px white underline that spans the width of the text.

---

## 7. Interaction & Assets
*   **Cursor:** The design implies a custom or standard pointer.
*   **Image Assets:**
    1.  **Main Terrain:** Opaque JPG/WebP.
    2.  **Cloud Overlays:** Backgroundless (transparent) PNGs. These should be placed at `z-index` higher than the terrain but lower than the text. No rotation is visible, but they should be scaled to look like natural fog.
    3.  **Logos:** Vector (SVG) format for the Primland crest and the corner icon to maintain sharpness.

## 8. Color Palette
*   **Text:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) and `#F5F5F5` (Off-white).
*   **Button Text:** `#1A1A1A` (Dark Charcoal).
*   **Accent (Star):** `#E5D5B0` (Soft Gold).
*   **Background:** Natural greens, deep forest shadows, and river-water blues/blacks.
