# UI Design Specification: Cannabis Lab Concept & Event Promotion

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Color Palette
- **Primary Background:** Warm beige/peach (#F3EBE3, #EFE3D5)
- **Glassmorphism Base (Light):** Translucent white with backdrop blur (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` to `0.7`)
- **Glassmorphism Base (Dark/Card):** Deep charcoal/black (`#161616` / `rgba(22, 22, 22, 0.9)`)
- **Accent Orange:** Vibrant gradient orange/amber (`#FF7F11` to `#FF9F1C`)
- **Text Color (Dark):** Near-black (`#111111`)
- **Text Color (Muted):** Muted gray (`#666666`)
- **Text Color (Light):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`)

### Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** Clean, geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*, or *SF Pro Display*).
- **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
- **Headings (e.g., "Log in", "Thu 24th", "New in"):**
  - Font Weight: Bold (700) / Light-to-Medium depending on context.
  - "Thu 24th": Extra-large display weight (300/Light for "Thu", 600/Semi-bold for "24th").
- **Body & Labels:** Regular (400), size range 12px–14px.
- **Buttons:** Medium (500), uppercase tracking where applicable (e.g., "Sign up", "Discover", "Join in").

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Composition

The design is a full-screen desktop/tablet composition showcasing two overlapping mobile/card UI components set against a surreal 3D-rendered studio background (sand, stones, abstract glass domes, cactus, and gradient spheres).

### Background Elements (3D Environment)
- **Base Surface:** Warm sand texture spanning the entire viewport (`#EFE3D5`).
- **Foreground Objects (Bottom/Sides):**
  - Organic white and gradient-orange spheres resting on the sand.
  - Scattered light-gray natural rocks and pebbles casting soft shadows.
  - Abstract transparent glass dome in the upper center background.
  - Stylized cactus on the upper-right edge.
  - Stylized pineapple/botanical asset on the lower-left edge.

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### Component A: "Log in" Card (Left Floating Panel)
* **Dimensions & Style:** Rounded rectangular card with heavy glassmorphism effect (frosted translucent background, subtle border, drop shadow).
* **Header Area:**
  - Top-left: Brand name "Cannabis Lab" (Small, dark gray, uppercase/normal tracking).
  - Top-right: "Sign up" action link (Muted gray/dark).
- **Main Section:**
  - Title: "Log in" (Large, bold, dark).
  - Social Auth Button: Pill-shaped button with Facebook icon and "Facebook" text (`#FFFFFF` background, dark text).
- **Form Fields:**
  - Input 1: `@ e-mail address` (Rounded pill input, white translucent background, left mail icon).
  - Input 2: `⚷ password` (Rounded pill input, white translucent background, left lock icon). Inside the right edge of this field is an "I forgot" pill button.
- **Legal / Disclaimer Footer:**
  - Small text: *"For use by adults only (18 years of age and older). Keep out of reach of children and pets. In case of accidental ingestion contact our hotline."* ("hotline." is underlined).
  - Sub-footer text: *"Please consume responsibly!"* centered at the bottom.
- **Action Button:**
  - Circular/Pill black button with a right-arrow `>` icon positioned bottom-right of the card.

---

### Component B: "New in C.Lab Joints" Card (Bottom-Left Floating Panel)
* **Dimensions & Style:** Solid dark charcoal/black rounded card (`#161616`).
* **Content:**
  - Header: "New in" (Muted white, medium weight).
  - Subheader: "C.Lab Joints" (Large white, bold).
- **Action:**
  - Bottom-right aligned link/button: "Discover" (White text with underline or clean link styling).

---

### Component C: "Thu 24th - Grand Opening" Card (Right Floating Panel)
* **Dimensions & Style:** Tall mobile-app-style card split vertically or featuring overlapping frosted panels.
* **Left Half (Frosted Overlay):**
  - Large date display: "Thu" (Light, medium size) stacked above "24th" (Large display weight).
  - Event details: 
    - Time: "18 PM"
    - Address: "Kerkstraat 12B, Amsterdam"
  - Bottom logo mark: Minimalist starburst/sunburst icon above "C.Lab".
* **Right Half (Clean White Background):**
  - Top-right header: "Grand opening" / "New store" (Right-aligned, small, muted gray).
  - Center graphic element: A striking solid orange circle overlapping the central split.
- **Action Footer:**
  - Bottom-right pill button: "Join in >" (Solid black background, white text, arrow icon).

---

## 4. Interactive States & Behaviors (Specification)
- **Glassmorphism:** All translucent panels must utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and semi-transparent white/black fills (`rgba`).
- **Shadows:** Soft, diffused drop shadows (`box-shadow: 0 20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`) to create depth between the UI cards and the 3D background.
- **Hover/Active States:** Buttons with arrows (`>`) should feature subtle translation on hover (moving 4px to the right). Links should subtly shift opacity.
