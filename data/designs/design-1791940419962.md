# Design Specification: LetsFly Landing Page

## 1. General Theme & Layout
*   **Overall Aesthetic:** Minimalist, luxury, airy, and high-end.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Background:** `#E8EAEB` (a very light, cool-toned grey).
    *   **Primary Text:** `#1A1A1A` (near black).
    *   **Secondary Text:** `#666666` (medium grey for sub-headers).
    *   **Accent (Buttons):** `#000000` (pure black) and `#FFFFFF` (pure white).
*   **Layout Style:** Single-page scroll, centered content, full-screen width (100vw).
*   **Canvas:** Edge-to-edge (no borders or frames).

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Inter** or **Montserrat** (Geometric Sans-Serif).
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `System-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Text Details:**
    *   **Logo ("LetsFly"):** Sans-serif, Semi-bold, ~20px, letter-spacing: -0.5px.
    *   **Nav Links:** Sans-serif, Regular, 12px, Uppercase/Sentence case, color: `#666666`.
    *   **Eyebrow Text ("PERSONAL JETS"):** Sans-serif, Bold, 10px, Uppercase, letter-spacing: 2px, color: `#666666`.
    *   **Main Headline ("Luxury. Affordable."):** 
        *   Size: ~90px to 110px.
        *   Weight: Bold.
        *   Leading (Line Height): 0.9.
        *   Letter Spacing: -2px.
    *   **Tagline ("We believe loyalty..."):** Sans-serif, Regular, 18px, color: `#1A1A1A`.
    *   **Secondary Section Header ("One simple fly rate."):** Sans-serif, Bold, 48px.
    *   **Secondary Sub-header ("A discount that..."):** Sans-serif, Bold, 42px, color: `#999999` (faded effect).

## 3. Header & Navigation
*   **Position:** Fixed or Absolute at the top.
*   **Padding:** 40px top, 60px left/right.
*   **Logo:** Placed top-left.
*   **Navigation Menu:** Placed top-right.
    *   Items: Intro, About, Costs, Features, FAQ.
    *   Spacing: 25px between items.

## 4. Hero Section
*   **Alignment:** All text elements are horizontally centered.
*   **Vertical Spacing:**
    *   Top margin to "PERSONAL JETS": 120px.
    *   "PERSONAL JETS" to Headline: 15px.
    *   Headline to Tagline: 20px.
    *   Tagline to Buttons: 40px.
*   **Buttons:**
    *   **"Learn more":** Pill-shaped, transparent background, 1px light-grey border, black text, 14px font size, padding: 12px 30px.
    *   **"Let's Fly":** Pill-shaped, solid black background, white text, 14px font size, padding: 12px 30px.
    *   **Interaction:** Buttons sit side-by-side with a 15px gap.

## 5. Imagery & Visual Assets

### A. The Background Clouds
*   **Description:** Soft, voluminous, 3D-rendered cumulus clouds. They are not flat images; they have depth and soft shadows.
*   **Placement:** 
    *   One large cluster behind the main headline, centered.
    *   Smaller, wispy clouds in the top left and top right corners.
    *   A thick "cloud floor" at the bottom of the hero section that the jet appears to be emerging from.
*   **Transparency:** The clouds should blend seamlessly into the `#E8EAEB` background.

### B. The Private Jet (Primary Asset)
*   **Format:** High-resolution PNG (Backgroundless/Transparent).
*   **Description:** A sleek, matte black/dark charcoal private jet. 
*   **Orientation/Rotation:** 
    *   The jet is angled upwards at approximately **15 degrees**.
    *   It is flying from the left toward the right.
    *   The nose of the jet is positioned slightly below the horizontal center of the screen.
*   **Visual Effects:**
    *   **Engine Glow:** A bright orange/yellow "heat glow" coming from the rear engine turbine.
    *   **Motion Blur:** The wings and the rear tail section have a slight horizontal motion blur to suggest high speed.
    *   **Shadows:** Soft contact shadows where the jet meets the clouds below it.
    *   **Reflections:** Subtle white highlights on the cockpit windows and the top ridge of the fuselage.

## 6. Secondary Content Section
*   **Placement:** Directly below the jet, separated by a significant amount of white space (approx. 200px).
*   **Content:** 
    *   "One simple fly rate." (Black).
    *   "A discount that gets better and better." (Medium grey/gradient fade).
*   **Alignment:** Center-aligned.

## 7. Technical Implementation Notes
*   **Z-Index Hierarchy:** 
    1.  Background Color (Bottom)
    2.  Background Clouds
    3.  Main Jet Image
    4.  Text Content / Buttons
    5.  Navigation / Logo (Top)
*   **Responsiveness:** On mobile, the headline font size should scale down to ~40px, and the jet image should be centered and scaled to fit the viewport width.
*   **Transitions:** The jet should ideally have a subtle floating animation (up and down 5px) to simulate flight.
