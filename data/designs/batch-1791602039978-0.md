# Design Specification (`design.md`)

## 1. Design Overview & Aesthetic Direction

*   **Theme:** Ultra-clean, modern, minimalist SaaS landing page juxtaposed over a serene, cinematic high-country nature visual.
*   **Aesthetic Style:** Floating translucent glassmorphic components, high contrast dark typography over soft natural sky background, soft depth-of-field foreground elements.
*   **Canvas Layout:** Full-screen responsive viewport (`100vw` × `100vh` minimum hero height), edge-to-edge full bleed visual. No frame, outer browser border, or device mockups.

---

## 2. Visual & Image Asset Specification

### 2.1 Background Landscape Asset Description
*   **Asset Type:** Full-bleed background hero photograph / hyper-realistic 3D render.
*   **Composition & Framing:**
    *   **Foreground (Bottom 25% of viewport):** Out-of-focus (shallow depth-of-field / bokeh effect) meadow filled with dense wildflowers (vibrant orange poppies, purple/lavender buds, small white field flowers). Soft green grass stems blurring upwards into the frame.
    *   **Midground (Center to Left):** Rolling green hills and alpine valley receiving warm golden-hour side lighting from the right. On the left midground, a small rustic alpine barn/cabin with a sloped wooden roof and grey-painted siding sits nestled on a grassy slope. Scattered pine and deciduous trees dot the valley floor.
    *   **Background (Left to Right):** Majestic, sharp limestone mountain peaks rising steeply on the left side, sweeping down into a haze-filled valley center, fading into low rolling mountains on the right.
    *   **Sky (Top Center):** Soft, warm pastel gradient shifting from pale golden-peach near the horizon to clear, light muted sky-blue at the top. The top-center sky area provides natural high-contrast negative space for the main dark headline text.
*   **Positioning:** `background-size: cover; background-position: center 60%;`
*   **Color Tone & Lighting:** Warm golden hour sunlight coming from the right-horizon, creating long soft shadows across the valley and highlighting grass edges.

## 7. Interactive States & Micro-animations

1.  **Navigation Hover States:**
    *   Nav links transition smoothly from `#4B5563` to `#000000` over `200ms ease`.
2.  **Primary CTA Button Hover:**
    *   `transform: translateY(-2px)` elevation shift.
    *   Shadow expands to `0px 16px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`.
3.  **Top Announcement Badge Hover:**
    *   Cursor: Pointer.
    *   Background shifts from `rgba(255,255,255,0.7)` to `rgba(255,255,255,0.9)`.
4.  **Scroll Indicator Animation:**
    *   Subtle vertical bounce animation (`translateY(0px)` to `translateY(4px)`) over a `2s` infinite loop.

## 8. Responsive Adaptations (Breakpoints)

### Desktop (`>= 1024px`)
*   Full visual feature layout as described.
*   Main Headline size: `64px`.

### Tablet (`768px - 1023px`)
*   Main Headline size: `48px`.
*   Navigation items spacing shrinks to `16px`.
*   Hero container top margin adjusts to `140px`.

### Mobile (`< 768px`)
*   **Navigation:** Nav links (`Home`, `Usecases`, etc.) hide into a hamburger menu; only Logo and "Login" button remain in the floating capsule.
*   **Headline Size:** Scales down to `36px` - `40px`.
*   **Subheadline Size:** Scales down to `16px`.
*   **CTA Layout:** Converts from horizontal flex row to vertical stacked column with `width: 100%` full-width buttons.
