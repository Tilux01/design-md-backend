# Design Specification: AI Agent Landing Page Hero

## 1. Global Setup & Canvas
* **Background:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Layout:** Centered single-column, max content width ~1120px, responsive fluid margins.
* **Canvas Boundary:** Full viewport width, edge-to-edge.

---

## 2. Typography
* **Primary Sans-Serif (Headings, UI, Body):** 
  * Font: Inter, SF Pro Display, or Helvetica Neue. Fallback: `system-ui`, `sans-serif`.
* **Eyebrow Serif (Tagline):** 
  * Font: New York, Garamond, or Georgia. Fallback: `serif`.
* **Scale & Hierarchy:**
  * **Eyebrow:** 11px, Uppercase, Medium weight, `letter-spacing: 0.15em`, Color: `#1A1A1A`.
  * **Main Title:** 56px–64px, Bold (700), `line-height: 1.05`, `letter-spacing: -0.03em`, Color: `#0A0A0A`.
  * **Subtitle:** 14px–15px, Regular (400), `line-height: 1.5`, Color: `#666666`, Max-width: 580px.
  * **CTA Text:** 13px, Medium (500), Color: `#FFFFFF`.

---

## 3. Top Hero Content (Centered)
* **Eyebrow Text:** `"AN AI AGENT THAT THINKS AHEAD, ACTS FAST, AND ADAPTS TO YOU."`
* **Heading:** `"Your Intelligent"` <br> `"Partner, On Demand"`
* **Subheading:** `"Streamline tasks, make smarter decisions, and stay one step ahead. Our AI agent works quietly in the background—so you can focus on what matters most."`
* **CTA Button:**
  * Shape: Pill (`border-radius: 9999px`).
  * Fill: Solid black (`#000000`).
  * Dimensions: ~130px width, 40px height.
  * Content: `"Try It Free"` followed by a circular icon with a rightward arrow (`→`).

---

## 4. Media Showcase Card

### Container
* **Dimensions:** Width: 100%, Max-width: 980px, Aspect Ratio: ~16:11.
* **Border Radius:** 28px.
* **Position:** Centered below the CTA with 48px top spacing.

### Embedded Image
* **Type:** Full bleed photographic image (not backgroundless).
* **Description:** Low-angle surreal portrait of a figure wearing a white cap and puffer jacket, completely overgrown with blooming white chamomile/daisy flowers across their face and body. The figure stands in a warm, blurred field of orange poppies under a clear, gradient pale blue sky.
* **Position & Scale:** Centered, `object-fit: cover`.

### Text Overlays (White, `#FFFFFF`)
* **Top-Left Metric:**
  * Primary: `"98%"` (Bold, 44px, sans-serif).
  * Label: `"Get More Done,"` <br> `"Effortlessly"` (Regular, 13px, opacity 0.9).
  * Offset: 36px from top/left borders.
* **Bottom-Right Card:**
  * Primary: `"Advanced"` <br> `"Integrations"` (Bold, 22px, sans-serif).
  * Label: `"Connect with your"` <br> `"favorite tools to create a"` <br> `"seamless workflow."` (Regular, 12px, opacity 0.85).
  * Offset: 40px from right, 50px from bottom.

---

## 5. Bottom Integration Bar (Dock)
* **Shape:** White capsule dock (`#FFFFFF`), `border-radius: 9999px`.
* **Placement:** Centered horizontally, embedded along the bottom edge of the hero card.
* **Content:** Horizontal row of 4 partner logos with black monochrome icons and text (12px, Medium):
  1. `Supa Blox` (Hollow circle icon)
  2. `Hype Blox` (Hourglass/geometric icon)
  3. `Ultra Blox` (Overlapping circles icon)
  4. `Frame Blox` (Split circle icon)
* **Padding:** 10px 24px; Item gap: 28px.
