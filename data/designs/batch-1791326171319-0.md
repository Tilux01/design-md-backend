# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Industrial High-Tech Editorial (Robotics & Engineering Showcase Minimalist).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with structured grid alignments, technical crosshairs, and asymmetric typographic layouts.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, minimal surface architecture relying on crisp 1px structural borders, precision orange accent markers (#FF5000), and stark monochrome contrast.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Precision engineering crosshair brackets (`┌`, `┐`, `└`, `┘`), technical coordinate markers (`48.2745° N, 22.5973° E`), high-tracking uppercase section numbering (`01 / GENESIS`).
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(255, 80, 0, 0.08)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
