# Comprehensive Design Specification & System Architecture

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic

- **Primary Mood & Archetype:** High-octane futuristic, cyberpunk-lite, ultra-modern energy drink marketing landing page. Sleek, high-contrast, technical, and immersive.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Balance:** Medium-low density centered around a dramatic hero product visualization. Generous use of surrounding dark spatial void and radial geometric guides to direct the viewer’s eye toward the central 3D element.
- **Theme Balance:** 100% Dark Mode. The interface relies on deep carbon-textured gradients, subtle radial glow spotlights, and high-luminance cyan accents (`#00F2FE`) to create focal depth.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

### Core Palette
- **Primary Brand Accent (Cyan Glow):** `#00F2FE` (RGB: 0, 242, 254)
- **Primary Accent Hover/Active:** `#4FACFE` (RGB: 79, 172, 254)

### Backgrounds & Surfaces
- **App/Base Background:** `#0B0D12` (Deep Obsidian Carbon Base)
- **Primary Surface / Card Background:** `#12161F` with `rgba(18, 22, 31, 0.85)` and backdrop blur `blur(12px)`
- **Overlay / Modal Backdrop:** `rgba(5, 7, 10, 0.75)`

### Borders & Dividers
- **Subtle Structural Rings/Borders:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Active Interactive Rings/Trackers:** `rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.35)`

### Text Colors
- **Primary Headings & Key Labels:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Body Copy / Descriptive Text:** `#94A3B8` (Slate Muted Gray)
- **Micro-Copy / Subtitles / Badges:** `#64748B` (Darker Slate)
- **Inverse / Contrast Text:** `#0B0D12` (Obsidian Dark)

---

  - *Headings:* `"Inter", "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif` (Geometric, bold, highly tracked)

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid

- **Container Constraints:** Max width `1280px`, centered fluid layout with automatic responsive side gutters (`min(4vw, 48px)`).
- **Grid Structure:** 12-column structural grid overlaid with absolute-positioned radial tracking vectors and concentric perspective rings.
- **Spacing Scale (Tokens):**
  - `xxs`: `4px`
  - `xs`: `8px`
  - `sm`: `16px`
  - `md`: `24px`
  - `lg`: `48px`
  - `xl`: `80px`
- **Section Sequence (Hero Viewport):**
  1. *Header Navigation Bar:* Brand logotype left-aligned, centered navigational links.
  2. *Hero Core:* Left-hand typography stack ("POWER INITIATED", "UN:LEASH YOUR ENERGY"), Central 3D floating can with orbiting vector halos, Right-hand floating info card ("HEALTH"), Lower-right metric label ("ENERGY").
  3. *Footer Infrastructure:* Copyright left, social link icons right.
- **Z-Index Hierarchy:**
  - Base Background / Carbon Grid: `z-index: 0`
  - Concentric Vector Rings & Orbit Paths: `z-index: 10`
  - Central 3D Product (Can): `z-index: 20`
  - UI Cards, Typography & Interactive Overlays: `z-index: 30`
  - Modal / Popups: `z-index: 100`

---

## 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement

### Navigation Component
- **Layout:** Flexbox, space-between alignment across the top horizontal axis.
- **Brand Logo ("UN:LEASH"):** Bold typography featuring a colon separator in accent cyan (`UN:LEASH`).
- **Nav Links ("HOME", "ABOUT", "DRINKS", "CONTACT"):** Uppercase, spaced tracking, active indicator represented as a small 4px cyan dot underneath the active link ("HOME").

### Hero Section Layout
- **Left Column:** Absolute positioning (`left: 8%`, `top: 40%`) containing the contextual label, main title, and secondary "POWER" anchor tag marked with a cyan plus symbol (`+`).
- **Center Piece:** 3D rendered aluminum can wrapped in subtle green/cyan atmospheric rim lighting, tilted at a ~15-degree angle, pierced through multiple elliptical 3D wireframe rings.
- **Right Card ("HEALTH"):**
  - Dimensions: Width `210px`, Auto height.
  - Border Radius: `4px` (Sharp modern edges).
  - Background: Semi-transparent dark card with heavy shadow profile `0 16px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`.
  - Content: Title "HEALTH" followed by descriptive product background copy.

### Action Triggers & Markers
- **Crosshair / Node Anchors:** Geometric cross (`+`) and circle markers positioned along orbital SVG paths to indicate interactive touchpoints (`POWER` at bottom-left, `ENERGY` at bottom-right).

---

## 6. Motion Mechanics, 3D & Mobile Gestures

- **Animation Triggers:** 
  - Page load entry sequence (staggered fade-in of rings and typography).
  - Mouse/Gyroscope parallax on the central 3D can and orbital vector rings.
- **Inferred Animation Libraries:** Three.js / WebGL for the central 3D can asset; Framer Motion or GSAP for UI layout transitions and floating orbital loops.
- **Transition Physics & Timing:**
  - *Orbital Rotation Loops:* Continuous smooth linear rotation, duration `20s` infinite.
  - *Hover Micro-Interactions:* `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (Spring-like deceleration), duration `300ms`.
- **Mobile Native Behaviors:**
  - Gyroscopic tilt control mapping device orientation directly to the 3D can rotation angles (`rotateX`, `rotateY`).
  - Pinch-to-zoom disabled on canvas; smooth vertical swipe inertia for page scroll transitions.
