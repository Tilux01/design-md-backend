# Design Specification: Monochrome Hero

## 1. Canvas & Layout Overview
* **Dimensions:** Fullscreen viewport (`100vw` × `100vh`).
* **Background:** Deep neutral black (`#0a0a0a` to `#000000`).
* **Layout Grid:** 
  * Header: Fixed top edge with inset padding (`40px 48px`).
  * Body: Centered vertical alignment with positioned editorial copy on the mid-left and central giant title.
  * Footer: Fixed bottom edge with inset padding (`40px 48px`).

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Background Black:** `#000000`
* **Text Primary (White):** `#FFFFFF`
* **Text Secondary (Subtle Grey):** `#8E8E93`
* **Title Overlay:** Semi-transparent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)` with subtle horizontal linear gradient fading at edges).
* **Borders / Accents:** `#333333` and `#FFFFFF`

---

## 3. Typography

### Primary Display (Hero Title)
* **Font Family:** *Monument Extended*, *Syne*, or *Druk Wide Bold*
* **Fallback:** `Impact, Arial Black, sans-serif`
* **Styles:** Uppercase, extra-bold/heavy weight, high horizontal tracking (`0.15em`).

### Secondary / Body Text
* **Font Family:** *Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*
* **Fallback:** `Arial, sans-serif`
* **Styles:** 
  * Navigation/Labels: Uppercase, weight `600`, tracking `0.1em`, font size `11px - 13px`.
  * Editorial Copy: Sentence case, weight `400`, line-height `1.5`, font size `12px - 13px`.
  * Pagination: Monospace or tabular numerals, font size `11px`.

---

## 4. Imagery

### Central Subject Image
* **Type:** Integrated photographic background (not transparent).
* **Subject:** Monochrome, high-contrast portrait of a male bust (chest to lower half of face; eyes cropped above the top frame). 
* **Details:** Matte-black skin texture with contrasting thick white liquid/paint dripping vertically down the nose and cheeks.
* **Placement & Fit:** Centered horizontally, anchored from top-to-center; `object-fit: cover`.
* **Vignette:** Blends softly into the `#000000` background at the bottom and sides via linear/radial gradient overlays.

---

## 5. UI Components & Placement

### Top Navigation
* **Left:** 
  * Label: `MOOD`
  * Style: Bold sans-serif, white, uppercase.
* **Right:** 
  * Element 1: `Share •` (Secondary grey, text link).
  * Element 2: Grid menu icon (6 tiny square dots in a 2×3 array), white.

### Middle Section
* **Left Editorial Text Block:**
  * Position: Vertically centered, aligned left (`x: 48px`).
  * Content: 
    > "Black is the darkest color, the result of the absence or complete absorption of visible light."
  * Max Width: `220px`.
  * Color: Light muted grey (`#9E9E9E`).
* **Center Giant Headline:**
  * Content: `MONOCHROME`
  * Position: Horizontally centered, placed in the lower-middle third behind/across the lower neck.
  * Style: Large, ultra-expanded sans-serif, semi-translucent (`opacity: 0.5`).

### Bottom Navigation / Footer
* **Left:** 
  * Content: `Explore  →`
  * Style: Small white body font with thin rightward arrow icon.
* **Center:**
  * Content: `001 / 006`
  * Style: Secondary grey, monospaced tracking, perfectly centered horizontally.
* **Right Controls:**
  * Content: `NEXT   Out of the blue` (Text: "NEXT" in bold white; "Out of the blue" in muted grey).
  * Control Button: Circular icon button (`~36px` diameter) with a partial white border ring and an inset right chevron (`>`).
