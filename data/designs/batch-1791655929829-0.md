# Design Specification: BIORG — Science-Backed Nutrition

## 1. Global Styles & Design Tokens

### Color Palette
*   **Primary Background (Dark/App):** `#0a0a0a` (Deep Off-Black)
*   **Secondary Surface / Card Background:** `#121212` to `#161616` (Subtle dark grays)
*   **Primary Text (Light):** `#ffffff` (Pure White)
*   **Secondary Text (Muted):** `#a3a3a3` (Neutral Gray)
*   **Brand Accents:** 
    *   Deep Forest/Olive Green (Bottle & Leaf Tones): `#1b261c`
    *   Interactive Pill Background: `#ffffff` with Black Typography
    *   Border Strokes: `#262626` (Thin, subtle separation lines)

### Typography
*   **Primary Heading Font:** *Clash Display* or *Syne* (Bold, geometric sans-serif with distinct character terminals and tight tracking).
    *   *Fallback Stack:* `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Body / UI Font:** *Inter* or *General Sans* (Clean, highly legible grotesque sans-serif).
    *   *Fallback Stack:* `"Inter", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.
