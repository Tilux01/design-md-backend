# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Ethereal Glassmorphic Wellness Dashboard & OS Interface. Clean, ambient, atmospheric, health-tech luxury.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Spacious and balanced layout with high whitespace ratio, centering around a focal 3D interactive object and symmetrical widget distribution.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-layered translucent glass panels utilizing high backdrop-blur (`blur(16px)` to `blur(24px)`), soft inset/outset glow borders, and subtle radial warm-to-cool gradients mimicking dawn/sunset clouds.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, glowing perimeter borders on interactive 3D elements, floating translucent glass containers, soft atmospheric depth gradients.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-400 inline-block"></span>`.
