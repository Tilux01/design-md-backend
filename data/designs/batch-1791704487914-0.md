# Design Specification: Mugen Studio Website Clone

## 1. Global Styles & Theming

### Color Palette
- **Background (Primary):** Pure Black (`#000000` or `#050505`)
- **Card Background / Secondary Surface:** Dark Gray (`#121212`)
- **Text (Primary / Headings):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Text (Secondary / Body):** Light Gray (`#A0A0A0` to `#C0C0C0`)
- **Accent / Highlights:** White glowing contrasts, semi-transparent white borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`)

### Typography
- **Headings (Display, Hero, Section Titles):**
  - *Font Family:* Inter, Helvetica Neue, sans-serif (Geometric Sans-Serif style)
  - *Fallback:* Arial, sans-serif
  - *Style:* Bold / Extra-Bold, tight letter-spacing (`letter-spacing: -0.03em`), uppercase for main hero text.
- **Body Copy:**
  - *Font Family:* Inter, sans-serif
  - *Fallback:* Roboto, Arial, sans-serif
  - *Style:* Regular, smooth antialiasing, high readability against dark backgrounds.

---

## 2. Layout Structure (Full-Screen Viewport)

### Layout Grid
- **Container Max-Width:** `1280px` centered with responsive horizontal padding (`24px` to `48px`).
- **Layout Paradigm:** Single-column vertical stacking with modular card components, asymmetric grid layouts for case studies, and floating glassmorphism UI widgets.

---

## 3. Section-by-Section Breakdown

### SECTION 1: Hero Section
#### Background
- **Visuals:** Dark, moody abstract motion background featuring blurred circular metallic or lighting elements, angled streaks/light leaks in grayscale with a high-contrast industrial feel.
- **Placement:** Absolute positioning behind the hero content, filling the top viewport area (`height: ~100vh`).

#### Navigation Bar (Header)
- **Top Left:** Logo ("MUGEN" / brand icon mark).
- **Top Right:** Navigation links / menu icon aligned horizontally with generous spacing.

#### Hero Main Content
- **Typography:**
  - Massive display text spanning two lines:
    - Line 1: **"MUGEN"** (Left-aligned, extra-bold display font, white).
    - Line 2: **"STUDIO"** (Offset to the right below Mugen, massive scale).
- **Floating UI Components (Overlays):**
  - **Profile Card (Left-Center):** 
    - *Content:* Circular avatar of a smiling Asian woman (Carly Park) against a dark backdrop, with text labels underneath.
    - *Styling:* Glassmorphism container (`background: rgba(20,20,20,0.7)`, backdrop blur, thin white border, rounded corners).
  - **Copy Block (Right):**
    - *Content:* Short introductory paragraph describing the studio's philosophy ("We're redefining design... no project management theatre...").
    - *Styling:* Small, readable sans-serif text aligned to a structured column.

#### Client Logo Ticker
- **Placement:** Directly below the main hero fold.
- **Content:** A horizontal row of monochromatic white/gray client/partner brand logos (e.g., *Stripe, Coinbase, Webflow, etc.*) evenly spaced across the container width.

---

### SECTION 2: Value Proposition / Manifesto
#### Content Structure
- **Heading/Subheadline:** 
  - *"Traditional agencies perfected the art of the pitch. We perfected the art of the work. When you need design that moves at the speed of your ambition, you need a different kind of studio."*
- **Typography Styling:**
  - Large-scale editorial typography.
  - Highlight words (e.g., *"art of the pitch"*, *"speed of your ambition"*) styled with higher font weight or lighter contrast to guide the reader's eye.

#### Founder's Note / Testimonial Module
- **Layout:** Asymmetric two-column or card-based layout set on the dark background.
- **Left Component (Profile):**
  - *Image Description:* Professional headshot of a man (Alex West, Founder) with dark hair and stubble, wearing a black collared shirt, set against a solid dark gray backdrop. The image is cropped in a vertical rectangle with rounded corners.
  - *Label:* "Alex West, Founder / CD" displayed below the image.
- **Right Component (Quote):**
  - *Content:* A styled blockquote discussing the flaws of traditional agencies (*"After 15 years in traditional agencies, I saw the same problems repeatedly..."*).
  - *Styling:* Includes quotation marks, structured paragraph spacing, and smaller secondary citation text at the bottom.

---

### SECTION 3: Case Studies Section
#### Header
- **Title:** "Case studies" (Clean, bold, medium-large heading).
- **Sub-label / Year:** "2023 - 2024" displayed discretely below or beside the title.

#### Grid / Gallery Layout
- **Layout:** 3-column horizontal grid of project cards with fixed aspect ratios.
- **Card Content & Images:**
  - *Card 1:* Cinematic thumbnail showing a figure standing in an atmospheric, neon-lit industrial corridor.
  - *Card 2:* Close-up abstract visual of a textured surface or sculpture under dramatic lighting.
  - *Card 3:* Stylized metallic or reflective 3D object rendering on a neutral dark background.
- **Image Specifications:** 
  - All images are backgroundless-integrated (seamlessly blended into dark cards), high-contrast photographic/3D renders with subtle color grading (warm lighting accents against cool dark tones).
  - Cards feature subtle hover states (slight zoom or brightness shift).

---

## 4. Interaction & Responsiveness Notes
- **Transitions:** Smooth CSS transitions on buttons, interactive cards, and navigation items (`ease-in-out`, `0.3s`).
- **Responsive Behavior:** 
  - On mobile/tablet viewports, multi-column grids stack vertically.
  - Floating UI profile cards snap into inline stacking order below the hero text to prevent overlap on smaller screens.
