# Design Specification: Strawberry Shake Product View

## 1. Overview & Layout
* **Screen Mode:** Full-screen mobile view (390px × 844px base reference, flexible height).
* **Grid & Padding:** Base 16px screen padding; rounded corners (`border-radius: 24px` for cards).
* **Overall Background:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) to soft Off-White (`#F9FAFB`).

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Primary / Accent Pink:** `#FF85A1` (Card background & accents)
* **Soft Pink Tint:** `#FFE5EC` (Card gradient fill)
* **Strawberry Red:** `#E63946` / `#D90429` (Fruit assets)
* **Mint / Leaf Green:** `#40916C` (Leaves & pill badge)
* **Badge Background:** `#D8F3DC` (Light mint green)
* **Text Primary:** `#1A1A1A` (Dark charcoal/black)
* **Text Secondary:** `#6C757D` (Neutral muted)
* **Surface / White:** `#FFFFFF`

---

## 3. Typography
* **Primary Font:** San Francisco (`SF Pro Display`, `SF Pro Text`)
* **Fallbacks:** `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, `sans-serif`
* **Hierarchy:**
  * **Badge Text:** 12px, Semi-Bold (600), Uppercase/Caps, Tracking: `0.5px`.
  * **Title / Header:** 24px–28px, Bold (700), Line-Height: 1.2.
  * **Body / Subtitle:** 14px–16px, Regular (400), Line-Height: 1.4.

---

## 4. UI Components & Structure

### A. Status Bar
* iOS-native style: Time left, Battery/Wi-Fi right. Color: Dark `#000000`.

### B. Header / Category Badge
* **Element:** Pill-shaped badge at the top-left of the card.
* **Style:** Background `#D8F3DC`, Text `#2D6A4F`, padding `6px 12px`, radius `999px`.

### C. Hero Visual Card
* **Container:** 
  * Centered card, aspect ratio ~3:4, rounded corners (`28px`).
  * Background: Subtle vertical linear gradient from `#FFB3C6` to `#FF85A1`.
* **Central Graphic:**
  * Glass of pink strawberry milkshake with whipped cream/milk splash.
  * Dynamically positioned floating whole strawberries, sliced strawberries, and green mint leaves bursting upwards and outwards.
* **Depth & Shadow:** Soft directional drop-shadow (`box-shadow: 0 16px 32px rgba(255, 133, 161, 0.25)`).

### D. Bottom Details / CTA Section
* **Product Details:** Title, short description, pricing/rating placed below the visual card.
* **Action Button:** Full-width rounded button or bottom navigation bar (`height: 52px`, `border-radius: 16px`).

---

## 5. Micro-Interactions & Animations
* **Card Entrance:** Fade-in with scale up (`scale: 0.95` to `1.0`, duration `400ms`, easing `ease-out`).
* **Floating Particles (Strawberries/Leaves):** Continuous subtle vertical float (looping keyframe `translateY(-6px)` to `translateY(6px)`, duration `3s–4s`, `ease-in-out`).
* **Badge Press / CTA Press:** Scale down (`scale: 0.97`) on tap.
