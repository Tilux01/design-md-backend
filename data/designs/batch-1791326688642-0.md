# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Glassmorphism & High-Performance Fitness Dashboard.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High information density balanced by deep dark volumetric gradients, rounded card enclosures, and soft multi-layered shadows.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-layered card system utilizing semi-transparent dark surfaces (`rgba(20, 20, 24, 0.75)`), subtle inner strokes (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).

# 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

## Color Token Definitions
- **Primary Brand Color & Variants**: Lime Green (`#C6FF00`), Hover (`#D4FF33`), Active (`#B2E600`), Subdued (`rgba(198, 255, 0, 0.15)`).
- **Secondary & Accent Colors**: Coral/Orange (`#FF5C00`), Cyan (`#00E5FF`), Soft White (`#F4F4F5`), Muted Gray (`#71717A`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient lime-green progress rings, high-contrast dark glass containers, status indicator dots, micro-avatars with overlapping badge counts.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(198, 255, 0, 0.15)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-[#C6FF00] inline-block"></span>`.
