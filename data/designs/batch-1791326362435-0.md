# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise SaaS, Dark/Light Hybrid Control Center, Modern Spatial Dashboard.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High information density balanced by generous card padding (16px–24px), subtle separation lines, and clear visual grouping.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered surface architecture featuring a clean off-white app background (`#F8FAFC`), crisp white surface cards (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px solid borders (`#E2E8F0`), and soft floating glass pills with backdrop blur.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, subtle glowing status rings, glassmorphism panels, isometric 3D interactive floor plan visualization.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
