# Design Specification: AI Fitting Room Interface ("RUSH")

## 1. Overall Layout & Canvas
- **Mode**: Full-screen view. The entire UI is contained within a centered rounded modal card overlaying a blurred urban background, but for a pure screen implementation, treat the main card as a full viewport container with a light gray/off-white background (`#f0f0f0` or `rgb(240, 240, 240)`).
- **Border Radius**: The main card container has a smooth rounded corner radius (approx. `24px`).
- **Typography Font Family**: Modern sans-serif stack (e.g., *Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*, *Arial*, sans-serif fallback). Clean, geometric, highly legible at small scales.

---

## 2. Header Section
- **Top-Left**: 
  - Text: **"AI Fitting room"** (Bold, dark gray/black, ~18px).
  - Below/Left aligned with it: A back button with a left chevron icon and text **"< Back"** (~14px, medium gray).
- **Center**:
  - Brand Logo: **"RUSH"** in ultra-bold, uppercase display sans-serif (heavy weight, tracked slightly tight, ~24px).
- **Top-Right**:
  - Action items:
    - **Bag (2)**: Shopping bag icon + text enclosed in a pill-shaped button with a subtle border (`#e0e0e0`) and padding.
    - **User Profile Icon**: Minimalist outline profile icon.

---

## 3. Left Panel: Interactive 3D Model & Dressing Room View
- **Model Display Area**:
  - Full-length photo of a female model facing forward, wearing a white crop top, brown baggy cargo/denim pants, and an oversized brown faux-leather jacket.
  - Image characteristics: High-contrast fashion photography style, neutral lighting, cut out cleanly without background.
- **Interactive Rotation/View Elements**:
  - Two curved arrows curving left and right around the lower half of the model's body, indicating 360-degree rotation functionality.
  - **Zoom Controls**: Floating vertical group on the far-left containing circular icons with `+` (Zoom In) and `-` (Zoom Out) symbols.
  - **Macro-Texture Zoom Circle**: A circular magnifying lens floating in the upper-right section of the model area, displaying an extreme close-up of the brown leather jacket's vertical seam and texture.
- **Bottom Carousel ("On a hanger")**:
  - A translucent white floating frosted-glass container (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, backdrop-blur).
  - Contains horizontally scrollable garment thumbnails:
    1. Oversized brown leather jacket.
    2. Gray/brown trousers.
    3. Striped top/dress.
    4. Small red clutch bag.
    5. Brown wide-leg jeans.
    6. White graphic hoodie.
    7. Tan work boots.
  - Navigation arrows (`<` and `>`) at the edges of the carousel.
  - Search/filter icon (`🔍`) on the right side of the container.

---

- **Background**: Solid white rounded container.

  - **Hips (sm)**: Value input pill `86`
  - **Height (sm)**: Value input pill `168`
- **Confirmation Button**: 
  - Full-width pill button.
  - Background color: Bright yellow (`#F9D342` or similar vibrant gold).
  - Text: **"Confirm"** (Dark, semi-bold).

### B. "You are wearing" Card
- **Background**: Solid white rounded container.
- **Header**: "You are wearing" (Bold, ~14px).

#### Item 1: Oversize faux leather jacket

## 5. Color Palette Reference
- **Primary Yellow (CTA)**: `#F9D342`
- **Background Main**: `#EBEBEB` (or system light gray)

- **Text Primary**: `#111111` (Near black)
- **Text Secondary / Borders**: `#CCCCCC` to `#E0E0E0`
