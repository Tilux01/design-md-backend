This is a comprehensive `design.md` specification for the **SoundGroove** music streaming application. This document is designed to guide a developer or UI designer to create an exact, pixel-perfect clone of the provided interface.

---

# SoundGroove UI Design Specification

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Theme:** Dark Mode.
*   **Primary Background:** `#0D0D0D` (Pure black/near-black).
*   **Accent Color (Primary):** `#D1A3FF` (Soft Lavender/Pastel Purple).
*   **Secondary Accent:** `#FFD700` (Gold/Yellow - used for highlights in cards).
*   **Surface/Card Color:** `#1A1A1A` (Dark Gray).
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   Primary: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
    *   Secondary: `#A0A0A0` (Muted Gray).
    *   Accent Text: `#000000` (Black - used on lavender buttons).
*   **Border Radius:** 
    *   Standard Cards: `24px`
    *   Buttons/Chips: `50px` (Pill shape)
    *   Small Thumbnails: `12px`

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` (or `SF Pro Display`).
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `Oxygen`, `Ubuntu`, `Cantarell`, `"Open Sans"`, `"Helvetica Neue"`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:**
    *   Bold: 700 (Headings, App Name).
    *   Medium: 500 (Subtitles, Button text).
    *   Regular: 400 (Lyrics, Metadata).

## 3. Screen 1: Home Screen

### Header
*   **Profile Image:** Top left. Circular, `40x40px`.
*   **Greeting:** "Good Morning!" (Secondary text, 12px) above "Eleanor Fitzgerald" (Primary text, 14px, Bold).
*   **Action Icons:** Top right. Notification Bell and Heart icon. 
    *   *Notification Badge:* Lavender circle with white text "12" positioned at the top-right of the bell icon.

### Hero Section
*   **Headline:** "SoundGroove Your Music, Anytime". Font size `32px`, Bold, White. Left-aligned.
*   **Filter Chips:** Horizontal scrollable list. 
    *   "All" (Active): Lavender background, black text.
    *   "New Release", "Trending", "Top Sc": Dark gray background, white text.

### Curated & Trending Section
*   **Section Header:** "Curated & Trending" (Left) | "See All" (Right, Lavender).
*   **Feature Card:** Large Lavender rectangle (`#D1A3FF`).
    *   **Content:** "Discover Weekly" (Bold, Black). "The Original slow instrumental best playlists" (Regular, Black).
    *   **Controls:** Row containing a Play button (Black circle, White icon), Heart, Share, and Triple-dot menu.
    *   **Visual Element:** A vertical gold/yellow stripe (`#FFD700`) on the far right edge of the card, slightly rounded.

### Top Daily Playlists
*   **List Item:**
    *   **Thumbnail:** `50x50px` square with `12px` border radius.
    *   **Text:** Title (White, 14px) above Artist "By Budiarti • 8 Songs" (Muted Gray, 12px).
    *   **Action:** Play icon (White) on the far right.

### Floating Navigation Bar
*   **Position:** Bottom center, floating `20px` from the bottom.
*   **Style:** Dark gray semi-transparent blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(10px)`), pill-shaped.
*   **Icons:** Home (Active, Lavender circle), Search, Library, Profile.

## 4. Screen 2: My Music (Library)

### Header
*   **Back Button:** Left-aligned arrow in a dark circle.
*   **Title:** "My Music" (Centered, Bold, 18px).
*   **Action Icons:** Same as Home (Bell with badge, Heart).

### Song List
*   **Layout:** Vertical scroll.
*   **Item Structure:**
    *   **Image:** `55x55px` square thumbnail.
    *   **Details:** Title (e.g., "Shape of You") in White, Subtitle ("By Budiarti • 24 Songs") in Muted Gray.
    *   **Trailing:** Small white Play icon.
*   **Floating Mini-Player (Bottom):**
    *   A pill-shaped bar containing a small thumbnail, a Play button, and a "Playlist" icon inside a Lavender circle.

## 5. Screen 3: Now Playing

### Header
*   **Back Button:** Left.
*   **Title:** "Now Playing" (Centered).
*   **Action Icons:** Bell and Heart.

### Main Visual (Album Art)
*   **Shape:** Perfect Circle.
*   **Border:** Thin lavender glow/border.
*   **Image Description:** A high-resolution portrait of a Black woman with reddish-brown micro-braids/dreads. She is looking directly at the camera with a serious expression. The lighting is dramatic, with a pink/purple neon backlight creating a halo effect around her head. The background of the photo is dark.
*   **Placement:** Centered in the top half of the screen. No rotation.

### Track Info
*   **Title:** "Blinding Lights" (White, 22px, Bold).
*   **Artist:** "The Weeknd" (Muted Gray, 16px).

### Lyrics
*   **Style:** Centered, medium gray text.
*   **Content:** "Whispers in the midnight breeze, / Carrying dreams across the seas, / I close my eyes let go and drift away,".

### Playback Controls
*   **Progress Bar:** Lavender line. The "thumb" (current position) is a small white circle.
    *   **Timestamps:** "45:25" (Left) and "58:45" (Right) in small gray text.
*   **Control Row:**
    *   Shuffle icon (Left).
    *   Previous Track icon.
    *   **Play/Pause:** Large Lavender circle (`60x60px`) with a black Pause icon.
    *   Next Track icon.
    *   Repeat/Queue icon (Right).

## 6. Image Assets Requirements

1.  **Profile Avatar:** Backgroundless PNG of a stylized 3D avatar or high-res headshot.
2.  **Playlist Thumbnails:** 
    *   *Blinding Lights:* Green-tinted close-up of a face.
    *   *Easy On Me:* Blue-tinted portrait.
    *   *As It Was:* Red/Warm-tinted portrait.
    *   *Shape of You:* Teal-tinted portrait.
3.  **Now Playing Art:** 
    *   **Description:** A backgroundless-style portrait (subject is isolated or background is deep black). A woman with red-tinted hair, wearing a dark turtleneck. The image must have a circular crop.
    *   **Rotation:** 0 degrees (Straight).
    *   **Placement:** Exactly centered horizontally; top edge starts roughly 15% down from the top of the screen.

## 7. Interaction Notes
*   **Transitions:** Smooth fade-in for screen changes.
*   **Button States:** Lavender buttons should have a slight glow effect (`box-shadow`) when active.
*   **Scrolling:** The filter chips scroll horizontally; the main content scrolls vertically. The Bottom Nav and Mini-Player remain fixed/floating.
