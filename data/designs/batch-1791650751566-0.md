# Comprehensive Design Specification: AI Product Imagery Landing Page

This document provides the exact technical and visual blueprint to recreate the landing page displayed on the laptop screen in the provided media. The layout is designed to be **full-screen (edge-to-edge)**, stripping away the laptop frame, bezel, and background rock textures to deliver a pure, immersive digital interface.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Theme

*   **View Mode:** Fullscreen, responsive viewport (`width: 100vw`, `height: 100vh`).
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Background (Canvas):** `#050505` (Deep monochromatic black).
    *   **Primary Text & UI Elements:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
    *   **Secondary Text & Accents:** `#888888` to `#AAAAAA` (Muted Cool Gray).
    *   **Gradients/Highlights:** Subtle dark charcoal gradients behind UI cards (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`).
*   **Grid Structure:** Asymmetrical, modern editorial grid with generous whitespace and absolute-positioned hero typography.

---

## 2. Typography Specification

The typography blends clean modern geometric sans-serifs for UI elements with unique, stylized display typography for the hero branding.

### Primary Font Stack (UI & Body Text)
*   **Font Family:** `Inter`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Fallbacks:** System default sans-serif.

### Hero Display Font (Brand Logo "own")
*   **Font Family:** Custom geometric, ribbon-like stylized vector font (or rendered as SVG paths).
*   **Fallback:** `Montserrat`, `Futura`, or `Helvetica Bold` with custom letter-spacing and border/stroke styling if vector paths are unavailable.

---

## 3. Detailed Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation Header
*   **Position:** Fixed top, spanning full width with padding (`top: 24px`, `left/right: 48px`).
*   **Left Element:**
    *   **Icon/Control:** Minimalist gear/settings icon (circular, thin outline).
*   **Center Element (Pill Navigation Switch):**
    *   **Container:** Rounded pill background (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, backdrop-blur).
    *   **Buttons:** Two tabs: **CREATE** (active, solid white pill with dark text) and **EXPLORE** (inactive, transparent text).
*   **Right Element:**
    *   **Text Link:** "SMART EDITING TOOLS" (uppercase, tracking-wide, font-size: `11px`, color: `#A0A0A0`).

---

### B. Hero Section (Left-Center)

#### 1. Secondary Labels
*   **"Instant Image Creation":**
    *   Positioned on the middle-left.
    *   Font size: `12px`, uppercase, color: `#999999`, line-height: `1.4`.

#### 2. Central Brand Typography ("own")
*   **Composition:** The letters **"o"**, **"w"**, and **"n"** are formed using a folded, ribbon-like structure with dynamic lighting, cutout loops, and overlapping dimensions.
    *   **Letter "o":** A continuous hexagonal/rounded geometric loop resembling a folded white metallic or paper ribbon with an inner dark shadow. A small copyright symbol (`©`) sits at the bottom-right of the loop.
    *   **Letter "w":** Composed of intersecting angular ribbon folds with sharp negative space.
    *   **Letter "n":** An open arch resembling an inverted U-shape ribbon fold, featuring high-contrast highlights along its top-right curve.
*   **Visual Style:** Monochromatic white/silver with sharp edge highlights and soft ambient occlusion shadows cast onto the background.

---

### C. Interactive Product Card (Overlaying the Hero)

*   **Position:** Placed diagonally, overlapping the top-left portion of the "own" typography.
*   **Container:** Rounded rectangular card with a dark semi-transparent glassmorphism background and subtle white border.
*   **Image Content Inside Card:**
    *   **Subject:** A luxury white cosmetic jar (cylindrical cream container with a cap) floating dynamically at an angle.
    *   **Background / Environment:** A dramatic dark rocky/volcanic landscape with warm golden-orange lighting/lava glow reflecting off the ground.
    *   **Image Properties:** Backgroundless container frame, high-contrast, professional commercial product photography style.

---

### D. Right-Side Feature Modules

#### 1. Top Right Tag & CTA
*   **Label:** "Brand & Product" (small muted text).
*   **Button ("GET STARTED WITH OUR AI >"):**
    *   **Style:** Pill-shaped button, white background, black bold text.
    *   **Icon:** Right chevron arrow (`>`) inside the pill.

#### 2. Bottom Right Value Proposition
*   **Heading:** "Instantly Generate" (Medium weight, white, font-size: `16px`).
*   **Subheading:** "Stunning Product Images" (Bold, white, font-size: `20px`).
*   **Visual Anchor:** A tiny circular thumbnail preview showing a dark bottle product floating against a blue-toned abstract background, positioned inline below the text.

---

### E. Footer / Bottom Left Element

*   **"CREATIVITY" Button:**
    *   Positioned at the bottom-left corner.
    *   Style: Pill-shaped outline button with a small spark/star icon (`✦`) followed by the uppercase text "CREATIVITY".

---

## 4. Implementation Checklist for Developers

1.  **Canvas Setup:** Set root background to `#050505` with zero margins.
2.  **Typography & Icons:** Load `Inter` via Google Fonts. Ensure SVG icons are used for the gear, sparks, and chevrons.
3.  **Hero Logo ("own"):** Implement as either a high-resolution transparent SVG composition or WebP image asset to preserve the exact ribbon-folding geometry and lighting details shown in the reference.
4.  **Floating Product Card:** Use CSS `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` with subtle box-shadows to achieve the frosted glass UI effect.
