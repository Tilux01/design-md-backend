# Design System Specification: Editorial Minimalist Portfolio

## 1. Global Setup & Dimensions
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (`1920x1080px`), Full-screen edge-to-edge.
- **Base Grid:** 12-column fluid grid, 24px gutters, 64px outer margins.
- **Background:** Primary `#FFFFFF`, secondary neutral panels `#EEEEEE`.

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Accent Red:** `#FA3E19` (High-saturation vermilion).
- **Ink Black:** `#121212` (Primary headlines and body).
- **Neutral Light:** `#F3F3F3` (Card fills and structural blocks).
- **Hairline Gray:** `#D8D8D8` (1px dividers and coordinate lines).
- **Muted Text:** `#707070` (Captions, dates, metadata).

---

## 3. Typography Hierarchy

### Primary Headline (Editorial Serif)
- **Family:** `Playfair Display`, `Bodoni Moda`, or `Didot`
- **Fallback:** `Baskerville`, `Georgia`, serif
- **Weight:** 700 / Bold, uppercase tracking: `+0.05em`

### Accent Script (Calligraphic / Signature)
- **Family:** `Pinyon Script`, `La Luxes Script`, or `Great Vibes`
- **Fallback:** `Brush Script MT`, cursive
- **Weight:** 400 / Regular

### Body & Structural UI (Modern Grotesk)
- **Family:** `Inter`, `Neue Haas Grotesk`, or `Helvetica Neue`
- **Fallback:** `Arial`, sans-serif
- **Scale:**
  - Lead Titles: `48px–72px` | Semibold
  - Section Tags: `12px–14px` | Uppercase, tracked `+0.15em`
  - Body Text: `14px–16px` | Line-height `1.6`
  - Project Data / Numbers: `36px–64px` | Medium

---

## 4. Distinctive Visual Motifs
- **Color Badges:** Solid `#FA3E19` circular discs (`h: 36px, w: 36px`) layered beneath or intersecting section headings (e.g., "MISSION", "INDEX", "SERVICES").
- **Large Geometric Cutout:** Bold, abstract vermilion letterform/block anchoring cover and closing slides.
- **Wireframe Accents:** Large, thin hairline circular arcs (`1px stroke, #FA3E19`) clipping into borders.
- **Imagery:** Strictly desaturated, high-contrast black-and-white photography for portraits and architecture.
- **Structural Lines:** 1px vertical and horizontal grid lines traversing slide canvases.

## 6. Animation & Interaction Directives
- **Transitions:** Horizontal slide/wipe (`600ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
- **Image Entrances:** Subtle scale reduction (`scale(1.05)` to `scale(1.0)`) via high-speed ease-out.
- **Typography Reveals:** Staggered vertical mask slide (`translateY(100%)` to `translateY(0%)`).
- **Graphic Elements:** Red badge markers expand from `scale(0)` to `scale(1)` at `400ms`.
