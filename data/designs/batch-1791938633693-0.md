# Design Specification: "Origin" Audiobook Interface

## 1. Global Styles
*   **Background:** Solid deep charcoal/near-black (`#0F1115`). The design should occupy the full viewport (100vw, 100vh) without any external borders or frames.
*   **Primary Accent Color:** Muted Gold/Champagne (`#C5A377`).
*   **Text Color:** 
    *   Primary: Off-white (`#E0E0E0`)
    *   Secondary: Muted Grey (`#888888`)
*   **Layout:** A fixed-width left sidebar (approx. 80px) and a fluid main content area.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Serif Font (Titles):** *Playfair Display* or *Lora*. 
    *   *Fallback:* Times New Roman, serif.
*   **Secondary Sans-Serif Font (UI/Body):** *Inter* or *Montserrat*.
    *   *Fallback:* Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.

---

## 3. Sidebar (Left Navigation)
*   **Width:** 80px.
*   **Background:** Slightly darker than the main area (`#0A0C0E`).
*   **Items (Top to Bottom):**
    1.  **Logo:** A stylized white outline of a strawberry or fruit-like icon. Placed at the top with 40px padding.
    2.  **Search Icon:** Muted grey magnifying glass.
    3.  **Home Icon (Active):** A white house icon. To the left of the icon is a vertical gold bar (4px wide, 30px tall) with rounded edges, indicating the active state.
    4.  **Library Icon:** Muted grey stack of books/rectangles.
    5.  **Settings Icon:** Muted grey gear icon.
    6.  **Profile Icon (Bottom):** Muted grey user silhouette icon, positioned at the very bottom of the sidebar.

---

## 4. Main Content Area (Top Section)
*   **Padding:** 60px top, 80px left, 60px right.

### A. Text Metadata
*   **Main Title:** "Origin"
    *   *Font:* Serif, 48px, Semi-bold.
    *   *Color:* Off-white.
*   **Author/Narrator Line:** 
    *   "by **Dan Brown** | Narrated by **Paul Michael**"
    *   *Font:* Sans-serif, 14px.
    *   *Color:* Muted grey, with names in slightly brighter white.
*   **Stats Line:**
    *   4.5 Stars (Gold stars) | 13 hrs 24 mins.
    *   *Font:* Sans-serif, 12px, Muted grey.
*   **Description:**
    *   A paragraph of roughly 4-5 lines of justified/left-aligned text.
    *   *Font:* Sans-serif, 13px, Line-height 1.6.
    *   *Color:* Muted grey.

### B. Action Buttons
*   **"LISTEN NOW" Button:**
    *   *Background:* Solid Gold/Champagne (`#C5A377`).
    *   *Text:* Black, All-caps, Bold, 12px.
    *   *Icon:* Small black "Play" triangle to the left of the text.
    *   *Shape:* Rectangular with very slight (2px) corner radius.
*   **"ADD TO WISHLIST" Button:**
    *   *Background:* Transparent with a thin 1px muted grey border.
    *   *Text:* White, All-caps, 12px.
    *   *Icon:* Small white "Plus" sign to the left of the text.
    *   *Shape:* Rectangular.

### C. Hero Image (Featured Book)
*   **Placement:** Right-hand side, vertically centered with the text block.
*   **Image Description:** The book cover for "Origin" by Dan Brown. It features a deep blue background with a white/silver architectural spiral (Guggenheim-style) in the center. The text "DAN BROWN" is at the top in a serif gold font, and "ORIGIN" is at the bottom.
*   **Styling:** 
    *   The image is a vertical rectangle.
    *   **Shadow:** A soft, large "Glow" shadow behind the book, using a dark navy blue tint to make it pop from the black background.
    *   **Rotation:** 0 degrees (perfectly vertical).
    *   **Background:** The image itself has no transparency; it is a full rectangular book cover.

---

## 5. Bottom Section ("Most Similar Books")
*   **Heading:** "MOST SIMILAR BOOKS"
    *   *Style:* Sans-serif, 12px, All-caps, Letter-spacing 2px.
    *   *Color:* Muted grey.
    *   *Margin:* 40px above the book row.

### Book Carousel/Grid
*   **Layout:** A horizontal row of 5 book covers.
*   **Cover 1:** *Daniel Silva - The Other Woman*. Red background with a silhouette of a man.
*   **Cover 2:** *Stephen King - The Outsider*. Dark, monochromatic cover with a silhouette of a man standing in water.
*   **Cover 3:** *Betrayal*. Blue/Black cover with a bright light/figure in the center.
*   **Cover 4:** *James Rollins - The Seventh Plague*. Dark red/brown cover with a central circular emblem.
*   **Cover 5:** *Steve Berry - The Lost Order*. Dark cover with a central golden/lighted emblem.
*   **Styling for all:** 
    *   Uniform height and width.
    *   No rotation.
    *   Backgroundless (the covers are solid rectangles).
    *   Slight 10px gap between each cover.

---

## 6. Visual Effects
*   **Gradients:** There is a very subtle radial gradient on the main background, where the center behind the "Origin" text is slightly lighter than the edges, creating a "spotlight" effect.
*   **Transitions:** The active state on the sidebar (the gold bar) should appear sharp and clean.
