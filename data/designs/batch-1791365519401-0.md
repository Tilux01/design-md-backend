```markdown
# Design Specification: Traverse Audio Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Reset
- **Background Color:** Dark charcoal gradient ranging from `#1e1e1e` (top-left) to `#121212` (bottom-right).
- **Primary Text Color:** Pure white (`#ffffff`) for main headings.
- **Secondary Text Color:** Muted grey (`#8c8c8c` / `#a0a0a0`) for body copy and navigation.
- **Accent Color:** Soft ice blue (`#7289da` or `#6ba4ff`) used for active states, slider progress, and indicator line.
- **Font Family:** Modern sans-serif (e.g., Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif).

---

## 2. Layout Structure
- **Viewport:** Full-screen layout (`100vw` width, `100vh` height), centered content with generous padding.
- **Grid Layout:** 
  - **Left Section (Content):** Takes up ~45% of the width. Aligned to the left with vertical rhythm.
  - **Center Section (Hero Visual):** Showcases the product image with large background typography (`Travers...`).
  - **Right Sidebar:** Fixed-width vertical strip (`~10%` width) for social links and menu toggle.

---

## 3. Header / Navigation
- **Logo (Top-Left):**
  - Text: `Traverse.`
  - Font Weight: Bold, clean sans-serif.
  - Color: White (`#ffffff`).
- **Main Menu (Top-Center):**
  - Links: `Home`, `About`, `Resources`, `Content`.
  - Color: Light grey (`#cccccc`), hover state transitions to white.
  - Active Indicator: Small horizontal line under `Home`.
- **Search Icon (Top-Right of nav):**
  - Minimalist magnifying glass icon.
- **Sidebar Menu Toggle (Far Right):**
  - Hamburger icon (`☰`) at the top right corner of the sidebar panel.

---

## 4. Hero Content (Left Column)
- **Tag (Top):**
  - Text: `Traverse Audio`
  - Style: Small, boxed or cleanly padded label with subtle background border.
- **Main Heading:**
  - Text: `Unmatched Sound Quality.`
  - Font Size: Extra large (`~48px` to `64px`), bold.
  - Line-height: Tight, stacked on two lines.
- **Body Paragraph:**
  - Text: *"A handpicked collection of the highest quality, industry standard, professional audio equipment at realistic, affordable prices."*
  - Color: Muted grey (`#a0a0a0`).
  - Max Width: `~350px`.
- **Call to Action (CTA):**
  - Button text: `Check the shop`
  - Style: Outlined box button with a subtle white border, transparent background, white text.

---

## 5. Visual Centerpiece (Hero Visual)
- **Background Watermark Typography:**
  - Text: `Travers...`
  - Font Size: Massive (`~180px`+), semi-transparent dark grey (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`), positioned behind the product.
- **Product Image:**
  - Item: Cylindrical smart speaker / audio device with a metallic mesh grille and smooth matte black top/bottom caps.
  - Position: Centered-right, casting a soft dark shadow underneath.

---

## 6. Footer & Controls (Bottom-Right of Main Area)
- **Pagination Indicator:**
  - Text: `01` (highlighted in white with a blue underline track) `| 06` (muted grey).
  - Progress bar beneath the numbers showing progression.
- **Slider Navigation Arrows:**
  - Left arrow (`←`) and Right arrow (`→`) housed in square outlined boxes next to each other.

---

## 7. Right Sidebar Panel
- **Background:** Slightly darker or separated shade of dark grey from the main canvas.
- **Social Media Links (Vertical Stack):**
  - Text rotated or vertically aligned reading downwards:
    - `Twitter`
    - `Facebook`
    - `Instagram`
  - Color: Muted grey (`#8c8c8c`).
```
