# UI Design & Implementation Specification: "Furniture" E-Commerce Website

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
- **Viewport:** Full-width (100% viewport width), responsive desktop layout scaling down cleanly.
- **Background Color:** Off-white/light gray canvas background (`#f8f8fa`) with white cards and containers.
- **Container Structure:** Centered content container spanning max-width ~1200px with generous vertical padding between sections.

---

## 2. Typography & Font System
- **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, or system-ui sans-serif fallback.
- **Fallbacks:** `sans-serif`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`.
- **Text Hierarchy:**
  - **Top Banner:** 12px, Regular, White.
  - **Nav Links:** 14px, Medium, Dark Gray/Black (`#111111`).
  - **Hero Heading (H1):** 44px–48px, Bold (`font-weight: 700`), Dark Slate (`#111827`), tight line-height (~1.1).
  - **Section Titles (H2):** 28px–32px, Bold, Dark Slate.
  - **Body / Descriptions:** 14px–16px, Regular, Muted Gray (`#4b5563`), relaxed line-height (~1.5).
  - **Product Titles:** 15px, Semi-bold (`font-weight: 600`), Dark Slate.
  - **Badges / Tags:** 11px–12px, Bold, uppercase or capitalized, white text on colored fills (e.g., green `#0d5c2e`).

---

## 3. Color Palette
- **Primary Brand / Forest Green:** `#0d5c2e` (Used in top banner, primary buttons, category active tabs, badge backgrounds).
- **Secondary / Accent Green (Light):** `#10b981` (Used for sale/discount badges like "10% off" / "50% off").
- **Accent Yellow/Gold:** `#f59e0b` (Used for rating stars, flash sale countdown timers, slider navigation arrows).
- **Dark Slate / Text Primary:** `#111827` (Main headings and product names).
- **Muted Gray / Text Secondary:** `#6b7280` (Body text, subheadings, placeholder links).
- **Background White:** `#ffffff` (Card containers, navbar background).
- **Canvas Gray:** `#f8f8fa` (Page background).

---

## 4. Detailed Section-by-Section Breakdown

### A. Top Notification Bar
- **Background:** Dark Forest Green (`#0d5c2e`).
- **Layout:** Flexbox, space-between.
- **Left Content:** "Call Us: +123-456-789" (White text, small icon).
- **Center Content:** "Sign up and GET 25% OFF for your first order. **Sign up now**" (Underlined CTA link).
- **Right Content:** Social/currency/language dots or icons.

### B. Main Navigation Header
- **Background:** Solid White (`#ffffff`).
- **Layout:** Flexbox row with sticky positioning (`position: sticky; top: 0; z-index: 1000;`).
- **Left:** Brand logo ("Furniture" with a green house/chair icon).
- **Center Links:** Home, Shop, Categories, About Us, Contact Us, Blog (Hover effect: subtle color shift to green).
- **Right Icons:** Search (magnifying glass), Wishlist (heart), Cart (shopping bag with item count dot), User Profile.

### C. Hero Section
- **Layout:** Two-column grid (Left: Text & CTAs, Right: Featured Image Slider).
- **Left Column:**
  - Sub-heading: Small green microphone/store icon + "The Best Online Furniture Store" (Uppercase/Semi-bold, 13px).
  - Heading: "Explore Our Modern Furniture Collection" (Large 48px, bold).
  - Description: Standard two-line placeholder text in muted gray.
  - Buttons: Primary dark green pill button ("Shop Now →") + Outline secondary button ("View All Products").
  - Social Proof / Ratings: Avatar stack of 4 users + yellow star rating ("4.9 Ratings+ Trusted by 50k+ Customers").
- **Right Column (Interactive Carousel / Slider):**
  - Card displays a modern living room interior photo with interactive hotspots (price tags like `$1,500`).
  - Navigation controls: Left/Right circular yellow arrow buttons at the bottom.
  - Indicator dots or pagination.

### D. Value Proposition Bar (Icons Row)
- **Layout:** 3-column flex container with light background card styling.
- **Items:**
  1. **Free Shipping:** Truck icon + "Free Shipping" / "Free shipping for order above $180".
  2. **Flexible Payment:** Secure card icon + "Flexible Payment" / "Multiple secure payment options".
  3. **24x7 Support:** Support headset icon + "24×7 Support" / "We support online all days."

### E. Category Showcase Grid
- **Layout:** Asymmetrical 3-column grid layout.
  - **Column 1 (Chairs):**
    - Header: "1500+ Items" (small orange badge), "Chairs" (H3).
    - List links: Gaming Chair, Lounge Chair, Folding Chair, Dining Chair, Office Chair, Armchair, Bar Stool, Club Chair.
    - Large product showcase image of a grey upholstered bar stool with wooden legs.
  - **Column 2 (Sofa):**
    - Header: "750+ Items", "Sofa" (H3).
    - List links: Reception Sofa, Sectional Sofa, Armless Sofa, Curved Sofa.
    - Product showcase image of a white tufted armchair.
  - **Column 3 (Lighting):**
    - Header: "450+ Items", "Lighting" (H3).
    - List links: Table Lights, Floor Lights, Ceiling Lights, Wall Lights.
    - Product showcase image of a copper dome pendant lamp.

### F. Product Collections Tabs Section
- **Header:** Small tag `— Our Products —` followed by center-aligned H2: "Our Products Collections".
- **Tab Navigation:** Pill-shaped filter buttons:
  - `All Products`
  - `Latest Products` (Active state: Dark Forest Green background with white text)
  - `Best Sellers`
  - `Featured Products`

### G. Product Cards Grid
- **Layout:** 4-column responsive grid of product cards.
- **Card Elements:**
  - **Badge:** Top-left discount badge (e.g., `50% off` or `10% off` in vibrant green background).
  - **Action Icons:** Top-right vertical stack (Wishlist Heart, Quick View Eye, Compare arrows).
  - **Product Image:** Centered high-resolution transparent PNG or clean cutout product photo.
  - **Flash Sale / Countdown Timer (on selected cards):** Yellow background pill with black text (`05 Days : 12 Hours : 30 Mins : 25 Sec`).
  - **Category Sub-text:** e.g., "Chair".
  - **Product Title:** e.g., "Wooden Sofa Chair", "Circular Sofa Chair", "Wooden Nightstand".
  - **Rating & Reviews:** Star icon (`★ 4.9` / `★ 5.0` / `★ 4.8`) in gold.

---

## 5. Animation & Interaction Specifications
- **Hover Transitions:** All buttons, links, and product cards must feature smooth CSS transitions (`transition: all 0.3s ease-in-out;`).
- **Product Cards:** Subtle lift / elevation shadow on hover (`transform: translateY(-4px); box-shadow: 0 12px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.08);`).
- **Buttons:** Background color darkens slightly on hover; active states depress by 1px.
- **Image Carousels & Sliders:** Smooth horizontal slide animation with easing (`cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`), duration 400ms.
- **Countdown Timers:** Real-time ticking digits (visual placeholder format specified above).
