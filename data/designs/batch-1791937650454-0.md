# Design Specification: HBO "Game of Thrones" Landing Page

## 1. Layout & Global Styles
*   **Dimensions:** Full-screen (100vw, 100vh). No margins or external padding on the body.
*   **Background:** A single, high-resolution cinematic image covering the entire viewport (`background-size: cover`).
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Text:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
    *   **Accent 1 (Cyan):** `#00BCD4` (Used for "watch trailer" and the glow effect).
    *   **Accent 2 (Orange):** `#FF5722` (Used for navigation arrows and specific highlights).
    *   **Overlays:** A subtle linear gradient from the bottom (black, 40% opacity) to the top (transparent) to ensure legibility of bottom navigation.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Gilroy** (Bold, Medium, and Light weights).
*   **Secondary/Fallback Font:** **Inter**, **Montserrat**, or **system-ui, sans-serif**.
*   **Specific Styles:**
    *   **Logo (HBO):** Heavy Bold, uppercase, tight letter spacing.
    *   **Nav Links:** Medium weight, 12px - 14px, uppercase, letter spacing: 1px.
    *   **Main Title ("Game of Thrones"):** Bold, 72px - 84px, line-height: 1.1.
    *   **Body Text:** Light/Regular, 16px, line-height: 1.5, opacity: 0.8.
    *   **Vertical Text:** Regular, 12px, uppercase, rotated -90 degrees.

---

## 3. Header Navigation
*   **Placement:** Top edge, 40px padding from top, 60px padding from sides.
*   **Left Section:**
    *   **HBO Logo:** White text.
    *   **Links:** "SERIES", "MOVIES", "KIDS" (Horizontal list, 30px gap).
*   **Right Section:**
    *   **Search Icon:** Thin-stroke magnifying glass.
    *   **"GET HBO":** Text link, uppercase.
    *   **"SIGN IN" Button:** White background, black text, bold, uppercase, 12px padding (top/bottom) by 30px padding (left/right). Sharp corners (no border-radius).

---

## 4. Main Content (Hero Section)
*   **Vertical Sidebar (Left):**
    *   **Text:** "SUNDAY AT 9 AM". 
    *   **Placement:** Centered vertically on the left edge, rotated -90 degrees.
    *   **Icon:** A small clock icon placed above the text.
*   **Main Text Block:**
    *   **Placement:** Left-aligned, roughly 40% from the top of the screen.
    *   **Title:** "Game of Thrones" (Two lines).
    *   **Description:** "Winter is here, and so is the Great War. First episode is out now."
    *   **Call to Action:** "watch trailer" in Cyan. Next to it, a Cyan circular play button icon (thin stroke).

---

## 5. Bottom Navigation & Carousel
*   **Placement:** Bottom 15% of the screen.
*   **Left Component ("Barry"):**
    *   **Image:** Circular crop of Bill Hader (Barry).
    *   **Indicator:** A small orange circle with a right-pointing white arrow overlapping the image at the 3 o'clock position.
    *   **Text:** "Barry" (Bold) and "Sundays at 10 PM" (Light) stacked vertically.
*   **Center Controls ("Prev/Next"):**
    *   **Structure:** Two horizontal lines with arrows.
    *   **Top Line:** A left-pointing arrow in a white circle, followed by a thin white line. The word "PREV" sits small above the line.
    *   **Bottom Line:** A thin white line followed by a right-pointing arrow in a white circle. The word "NEXT" sits small above the line.
*   **Right Component ("Last Week"):**
    *   **Image:** Circular crop of John Oliver.
    *   **Indicator:** A small orange circle with a left-pointing white arrow overlapping the image at the 9 o'clock position.
    *   **Text:** "Last Week" (Bold) and "Sundays at 10 PM" (Light) stacked vertically.

---

## 6. Image Asset Descriptions

### A. Main Hero Background (Daenerys)
*   **Subject:** A close-up portrait of a woman (Daenerys Targaryen) with white/platinum hair.
*   **Lighting/Effects:** 
    *   The left half of her face and body is washed in a **Cyan/Blue light leak** or glow effect.
    *   The right half is in natural, slightly desaturated tones.
    *   Her skin appears textured with dirt or ash.
    *   Her lips have a small dab of bright orange/red paint in the center.
*   **Placement:** Centered, filling the frame.

### B. The Dragon (Overlay)
*   **Description:** A small, realistic baby dragon with dark scales and glowing orange eyes.
*   **Format:** **Backgroundless PNG**.
*   **Placement:** Perched on the character's right shoulder (viewer's left).
*   **Rotation:** Angled slightly toward the center of the screen.
*   **Special Effect:** The dragon should be partially obscured by the Cyan light blur coming from the left side of the screen to integrate it into the scene.

### C. Avatar 1 (Barry)
*   **Description:** Backgroundless circular headshot of a man with dark hair and a serious expression, wearing a dark suit.
*   **Border:** None, but sits within the layout grid near the bottom-left.

### D. Avatar 2 (Last Week Tonight)
*   **Description:** Backgroundless circular headshot of a man with glasses (John Oliver), smiling slightly.
*   **Border:** None, sits near the bottom-right.

---

## 7. Interaction Notes (For Implementation)
*   **Hover States:** Nav links and "watch trailer" should increase in brightness.
*   **Button:** The "SIGN IN" button should have a subtle drop shadow or slight scale-up on hover.
*   **Carousel:** The "PREV" and "NEXT" arrows should be the primary interactive elements for switching the background content.
