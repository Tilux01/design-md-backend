# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Neo-Brutalist Financial SaaS / High-Contrast Neon Dark Mode Dashboard.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High density with structured, compartmentalized grid cards; balanced with dark backgrounds and intense acid-green visual focal points.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered dark charcoal and near-black surfaces (`#121212` to `#1E1E1E`) paired with vivid acid-lime (`#CCFF00`) accent containers and subtle 1px internal borders.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-contrast acid-lime monochrome focus blocks, rounded card enclosures, minimal dark-mode surface separation, financial data visualization waveforms.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#CCFF001A`, border width `1px` `#CCFF0033`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
