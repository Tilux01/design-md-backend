# Design Specification: Gaming Launcher UI

## 1. General Layout & Environment
*   **Screen Mode:** Full-screen (1920x1080 aspect ratio).
*   **Theme:** Dark Mode / Cinematic Glassmorphism.
*   **Background:** A full-bleed, high-resolution image of the currently selected game's environment. 
    *   **Effect:** The background should have a subtle **Gaussian Blur (approx. 10-15px)** and a **Dark Overlay (Black at 40-50% opacity)** to ensure text legibility.
    *   **Current Image:** An interior shot of a rustic wooden building (likely a saloon or cabin) with warm lighting coming from windows.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Inter** or **Roboto**.
*   **Fallback Sequence:** `Inter, "Segoe UI", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Game Title:** Bold, 32pt - 36pt, White.
    *   **Body Text:** Regular, 14pt - 16pt, Off-white/Light Gray (#CCCCCC), Line-height: 1.5.
    *   **Navigation:** Medium, 14pt, White (Active) / Semi-transparent White (Inactive).
    *   **Button Text:** Bold, 12pt, Uppercase.

## 3. Top Navigation Bar
*   **Position:** Fixed at the top, spanning the full width with horizontal padding (approx. 60px).
*   **Left Section (User Profile):**
    *   **Icon:** Small circular avatar (32x32px).
    *   **Text:** Username ("codewithari" or similar) with a small downward chevron icon to the right.
*   **Center Section (Menu):**
    *   **Items:** Home, My Games, Store, Library, Settings.
    *   **Active State ("My Games"):** Encapsulated in a pill-shaped "Glass" container. 
        *   **Style:** Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, Backdrop-filter: `blur(10px)`, Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
*   **Right Section (Status):**
    *   **Clock:** Digital time (e.g., 10:24 AM) in a clean, thin weight.

## 4. Active Game Details (Left Overlay)
*   **Position:** Mid-left, vertically centered or slightly lower.
*   **Title:** "The Last Of Us Part II" (Large, White).
*   **Description:** A paragraph of text (approx. 3-4 lines). 
    *   *Content:* "The years after their dangerous journey across the post-pandemic United States, Ellie and Joel have settled down in Jackson... [truncated]".
*   **Action Buttons:**
    *   **Button 1 (Play Now):** Solid Light Gray/White background, Dark text. Rounded corners (4px).
    *   **Button 2 (More Info):** Transparent background, White border (1px), White text. Rounded corners (4px).

## 5. Game Carousel (The Tiles)
*   **Position:** A horizontal row positioned in the lower-middle section of the screen.
*   **Tile Styling:**
    *   **Dimensions:** Vertical rectangles (approx. 2:3 aspect ratio).
    *   **Corners:** Rounded (12px - 16px).
    *   **Active Tile (The Last of Us Part II):** Slightly larger than the others (scale 1.1x) with a subtle outer glow or high-contrast border.
*   **Tile Image Descriptions (Left to Right):**
    1.  **Far Cry 5:** A group of characters standing in front of a rural landscape with a flag.
    2.  **The Last of Us Part II (Selected):** A dark, moody shot of a character (Ellie) in a rainy, wooded environment.
    3.  **Red Dead Redemption 2:** High-contrast red background with a silhouette of a cowboy (Arthur Morgan) holding a revolver.
    4.  **PUBG:** A character in iconic gear (white shirt, tie, helmet) walking away from an explosion.
    5.  **Valorant:** A character (Jett) in a stylized, high-contrast blue and purple composition.
    6.  **Call of Duty: Warzone:** A gritty, military-themed shot featuring soldiers and a vehicle in a dusty environment.

## 6. Footer / Navigation Indicators
*   **Position:** Bottom center.
*   **Elements:** Three small icons/dots.
    *   **Center Icon:** A white pill-shaped indicator or "Home" icon.
    *   **Side Icons:** Small, semi-transparent dots or icons representing "Back" and "Overview" functionality.

## 7. Visual Effects & Transitions
*   **Glassmorphism:** All UI panels (except the main text) should utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and `background: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`.
*   **Transitions:** When switching games in the carousel, the background image should cross-fade smoothly (0.5s duration).
*   **Shadows:** Each game tile should have a soft drop shadow: `0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`.

---
**Note on Assets:** All game images should be high-resolution 400x600px (approx) for the tiles and 1920x1080px for the background. Images should be cropped to fit the aspect ratio of the tiles without stretching.
