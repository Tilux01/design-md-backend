# Visual Design Specification

## Overview
A moody, cinematic composition of US 100-dollar bills seemingly floating or falling against a pitch-black background. The lighting is dramatic, highlighting the texture and denomination numbers of the currency.

---

## 1. Canvas & Layout
* **Aspect Ratio:** Vertical (approx. 9:16)
* **Background Color:** Absolute Black (`#000000`)
* **Composition:** Scattered arrangement of multiple 100-dollar bills floating at various angles, creating a sense of depth through overlapping and varying focus levels.

---

## 2. Typography & Text Elements
* **Currency Denominations:** 
  * Large "100" numbers in the standard US Treasury font/styling (bold, copper/gold tone).
* **Federal Reserve Text:** Standard microprinting and lettering visible on the bills ("FEDERAL RESERVE NOTE", "IN GOD WE TRUST", "THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA").

---

## 3. Color Palette
* **Dominant (Background):** `#000000` (Pitch Black)
* **Currency Base:** `#687C58` (Muted Olive Green)
* **Denomination Numbers:** `#C89B5C` to `#E6C58B` (Metallic Gold/Copper)
* **Highlights:** Bright white rim lighting (`#FFFFFF`) on edges facing light sources.

---

## 4. Lighting & Effects
* **Lighting Style:** Dramatic directional lighting from the top-right, casting deep shadows and highlighting the edges and folds of the bills.
* **Depth of Field:** Shallow focus effect. Bills closer to the viewer or in the center are sharp, while background bills are softly blurred.
* **Texture:** Crisp paper texture visible on the currency, emphasizing the crispness of the bills.
