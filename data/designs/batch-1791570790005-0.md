# Design Specification: Cuong Nguyen Portfolio Landing Page

## 1. Overall Layout & Structure
- **Canvas/Viewport**: Full-screen layout with an off-white/light gray background.
- **Grid & Alignment**: The layout uses an editorial, layered composition with a prominent vertical red block anchoring the center. Plus signs (`+`) act as coordinate markers in the corners and intersections, giving it a technical, blueprint-like feel.
- **Hero Section**:
  - Top-left: "WELCOME TO MY" text stack.
  - Top-right: A circular target/eye icon.
  - Center: A bold, stylized, multi-layered typographic headline "PORTFOLIO" intersected by a vertical red rectangle (`#E74C3C`).
  - Bottom: Author name "CUONG NGUYEN" on the left, and title "MULTIMEDIA DESIGNER" on the right.
- **Interactive/Floating Cards Section**:
  - Below the hero text, an overlapping fan of cards/posters is scattered across the bottom half of the screen.
  - Cards include: a dark abstract blue fluid/nebula art card, a vibrant red card with a black abstract clover/eye logo, a portrait photo card of the designer wearing sunglasses, a sleek white automotive design card ("Polestar 1:1"), a circular dial element, and a dark ornate casino/royale-themed card.

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Background**: Light Gray / Off-White (`#EBEBEB` or `#EAEAEA`)
- **Primary Red**: Vibrant Red / Vermilion (`#E63946` or `#E74C3C`)
- **Primary Dark/Black**: Deep Charcoal / Rich Black (`#111111` or `#1A1A1A`)
- **Text & Accents**: Pure Black (`#000000`), White (`#FFFFFF`), with semi-transparent gray borders and shadows.

---

## 3. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Headline Font ("PORTFOLIO")
- **Style**: Bold, heavy sans-serif with a mix of filled, outlined, and offset ghost text styles.
- **Suggested Font**: **Helvetica Neue (Black)**, **Montserrat (Black)**, or **Inter (Black)**.
- **Fallback**: `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.
- **Properties**: Uppercase, extremely tight letter-spacing (tracking), layered opacity effects (some letters are filled black, some are white outlines over the red block, and some are translucent gray).

### Secondary UI Font ("WELCOME TO MY", "CUONG NGUYEN", "MULTIMEDIA DESIGNER")
- **Style**: Clean, geometric sans-serif.
- **Suggested Font**: **Space Grotesk**, **JetBrains Mono**, or **Montserrat (Medium/Bold)**.
- **Fallback**: `monospace, sans-serif`.
- **Properties**: Uppercase, wide tracking for designer titles, clean micro-typography.

---

## 4. Section-by-Section Breakdown

### A. Header / Hero Area
- **Top Left**: 
  - Text: "WELCOME TO" (smaller, bold sans-serif) stacked above "MY" (larger, bold sans-serif).
- **Top Right**: 
  - Icon: A crisp vector icon depicting an eye inside a target circle, enclosed in a rounded square boundary.
- **Central Red Block**: 
  - A vertical rectangular block spanning the upper-middle section, colored in bright red.
- **Headline ("PORTFOLIO")**: 
  - Overlaid across the horizontal center, breaking through the red block.
  - Features overlapping visual treatments: drop shadows, black solid fills on the left, white outlines inside the red bar, and translucent outlined ghosting to the right.
- **Footer Metadata**:
  - Left: "CUONG NGUYEN"
  - Right: "MULTIMEDIA DESIGNER"
  - Both accompanied by subtle crosshair `+` symbols on their outer flanks.

### B. Floating Cards Grid (Bottom Half)
The bottom portion features a dynamic, overlapping collage of multimedia project cards with realistic drop shadows and rounded corners:
1. **Dark Nebula Card (Far Left)**: A dark rectangular frame containing a vibrant, glowing blue cosmic/nebula explosion.
2. **Red Brand Identity Card**: Solid red background featuring a stylized black graphic logo (resembling an abstract four-leaf clover or geometric eye symbol).
3. **Designer Portrait Card**: A vertical white card featuring a black-and-white (or high-contrast) portrait of a young man wearing sunglasses, with small metadata text ("DVS X2X4") in the top right corner.
4. **Automotive Concept Card ("Polestar 1:1")**: A white-themed futuristic car design card featuring a sleek white sports car render, bold "1:1" typography, and minimal blueprint-style technical lines.
5. **Circular Dial Element**: A subtle, circular geometric element at the bottom center with dashed concentric rings and the word "ME" pointing upward with a triangular indicator, surrounded by repeating "PORTFOLIO" text along the arc.
6. **Casino Royale Card (Bottom Right)**: A dark-themed card with ornate baroque/rococo filigree framing at the top, a silhouette of a person in a tuxedo/dress, and subtle branding ("CASINO ROYALE").

---

## 5. UI/UX Interaction Notes
- **Shadows & Depth**: Soft, diffused multi-layered drop shadows (`box-shadow: 0px 20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.15)`) give the floating cards a tactile, physical feel.
- **Z-Index Layering**: The cards overlap cleanly from back to front, creating spatial depth beneath the static typography of the hero section.
