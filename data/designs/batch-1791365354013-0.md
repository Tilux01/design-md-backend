# Screen Specifications & Design System

## Global Styles & Tokens

### Color Palette
- **Primary / Accent:** `#FFB72B` (Warm Yellow/Orange used for highlights, main CTA buttons, and active states)
- **Background (App):** `#F7F7F7` (Light off-white screen background)
- **Background (Card Containers):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for cards and modal wrappers)
- **Text Primary:** `#1A1A1A` (Dark charcoal for primary headings and important text)
- **Text Secondary / Muted:** `#7A7A7A` (Medium gray for subtitles, metadata, and inactive labels)
- **Border / Divider:** `#E5E5E5` (Light gray for subtle separators)

### Typography
- **Font Family:** System Sans-Serif (`Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `-apple-system`)
- **Font Weights:** Regular (`400`), Medium (`500`), Semi-Bold (`600`), Bold (`700`)
