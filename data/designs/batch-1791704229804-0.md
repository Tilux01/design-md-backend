# Design System Specification (`design.md`)
**Project:** Luxury Editorial Presentation Template System (“Vision. Art. Identity.”)  
**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 Widescreen (1920 × 1080 px per slide, scalable to full-screen display)  
**Style Archetype:** Dark Minimalist / Editorial Brutalism / Warm Chiaroscuro Luxury  

---

## 1. Design Overview & Atmosphere
The template embodies an ultra-refined, high-fashion editorial aesthetic built on dramatic contrast:
* **Lighting Concept:** Low-key chiaroscuro lighting, deep carbon blacks, and smooth, diffuse radial gradients in amber, rust, and burnt orange.
* **Compositional Balance:** Heavy asymmetry balanced with micro-grid typography, massive bold titles anchored at the bottom, and ample negative space.
* **Mood:** Sophisticated, mysterious, authoritative, and cinematic.

---

## 2. Typography System

### Font Families
1. **Primary Display & Headings:**
   * **Font:** *PP Neue Montreal* or *Neue Haas Grotesk Display Pro* (Weight: Medium / Semi-Bold)
   * **Alternative / System Fallbacks:** *Inter Display*, *Helvetica Neue*, *Arial*, sans-serif
   * **Attributes:** Tight letter-spacing (`-0.025em` to `-0.035em`), natural tracking, lowercase/title-case hybrid layout.
2. **Body & Micro-Labels:**
   * **Font:** *PP Neue Montreal* (Weight: Book / Regular) or *Inter* (Weight: Regular / Light)
   * **Alternative / System Fallbacks:** *Helvetica*, *system-ui*, sans-serif
   * **Attributes:** Clean, neutral, high legibility even at 10px–12px.
3. **Data & Numerical Callouts:**
   * **Font:** *PP Neue Montreal Mono* or *Space Mono* (Fallback: *Courier New*, monospace)
   * **Attributes:** Tabular figures for numbers (`01`, `02`, `08`, `98%`).

### Hierarchy & Scale (Based on 1920 × 1080 px Canvas)

| Level | Size | Weight | Tracking | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hero Display Large** | 88px – 104px | Medium / Semi-Bold | `-0.03em` | Bottom statement titles (`Your visual identity.`, `Vision. Art. Identity.`) |
| **Section Title** | 42px – 48px | Medium | `-0.02em` | Slide headers (`Studio in numbers`, `The Art That Pushes the Limits.`) |
| **Sub-Header / Metric Large** | 36px – 40px | Regular / Medium | `-0.01em` | Numerical stats (`08`, `50+`, `12`, `15`, `98%`) |

| **Body Paragraphs** | 13px – 15px | Light / Regular | `+0.01em` | Explanatory 2–3 line columns |
| **Micro Labels / UI Badges** | 10px – 12px | Regular / Medium | `+0.04em` | Navigation tags (`design profile`, `contacts 00`, `unique concepts`) |

## 3. Color Palette & Lighting

```
Primary Void Black:     #080808 (Deepest background floor)
Charcoal Slate:         #111111 to #161616 (Subtle container cards)
Radiant Burnt Orange:   #E25B18 (Primary spotlight source)
Warm Rust / Amber:      #A83D0D (Secondary ambient glow)
Smoked Sienna:          #541F09 (Atmospheric shadow gradient)
Pure Display White:     #FFFFFF (100% white for hero titles)
Muted Text Gray:        #A09E9B (60% contrast for secondary body copy)
Faint Boundary Gray:    rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12) (Hairline borders & pills)
```

### Ambient Gradient Engine
* **The "Amber Core" Effect:**  
  Radial glow configured at `radial-gradient(ellipse 65% 55% at 50% 45%, #E25B18 0%, #A83D0D 35%, #351305 70%, #080808 100%)`.
* **Atmospheric Vignette:**  
  Every slide has a subtle 360-degree linear/radial dark edge vignette to focus gaze inward.

## 4. Visual Imagery & Photography Specifications

### Image 1: Female Fashion Editorial Silhouette (Profile / 3/4 Turn)
* **Subject:** Black female model with sculpted features, natural high-shine skin, hair pulled back into a sleek, sculptural high updo / chignon bun, wearing delicate metallic hoop earrings and a minimal dark strap top.
* **Pose & Gaze:** Three-quarter rear-to-front silhouette, chin slightly raised, head turned back glancing past the camera into the distance.
* **Lighting Style:** True chiaroscuro rim lighting. The front of the face and torso fall into soft, velvety shadow; warm amber or silver-white light catches the perimeter of the face, neck, jawline, and collarbone.
* **Background Integration:** Seamlessly integrated into the canvas. No hard box edges; the dark shadows of the model’s torso and lower edge dissolve smoothly into `#080808` via alpha mask or deep feathering.

* **Placement & Environment:** Sits inside an illuminated dark square frame atop a faint obsidian pedestal. Top-down, focused spotlight creates high-sheen specular highlights on the spiral ridge, surrounded by subtle smoky atmosphere.
* **Compass Guides:** Subtle hairline crosshairs with small directional labels (`Process`, `Impact`, `Focus`, `Energy`) radiating outward around the frame.

### Image 3: Minimalist Wood Chair Silhouette
* **Subject:** An architectural, vintage-inspired wooden dining chair with angled legs and a curved backrest.
* **Lighting & Tone:** Dipped in high-grain warm orange/rust shadow, standing as an evocative graphic object in the lower-right foreground.

## 6. Component & UI Element Details

### 1. Pill Badges / Nav Chips
* **Border Radius:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) or `8px` capsule depending on width.
* **Border:** `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
* **Background:** `rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.6)` with backdrop blur (`12px`).
* **Padding:** `8px 18px`.
* **Typography:** Uppercase/lowercase mono pairs (`about 02`).

### 2. Divider Lines & Hairlines
* Fine lines separating sections utilize `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or thin amber tint `rgba(226, 91, 24, 0.18)`—never solid pure white rules.

### 3. Pacing & Rhythm Rule
* **Text Density:** Maximum 3 lines per paragraph block. High line-height (`1.5` to `1.6`) ensures effortless skimming.
* **Alignment Rule:** Micro text is strictly aligned to grid edges (margins: `60px` left/right, `50px` top/bottom).
* **Display Titles:** Anchored at the absolute bottom margin to create a grounded, monolithic base for each composition.
