# Design Specification: Success Digital Landing Page

A high-converting, monochrome e-commerce agency hero section featuring high-contrast typography and a focal portrait.

---

## 1. Color Palette

- **Background:** Primary off-white/light gray (`#F2F2F5`) with subtle top-to-bottom white gradient (`#FFFFFF` to `#EBEBED`).
- **Primary Text / Solids:** Deep Black (`#0A0A0A`).
- **Secondary Text / Muted:** Neutral Gray (`#666666`).
- **Border / Lines:** Light Gray (`#E0E0E3`).
- **Overlays / Pills:** Semi-transparent light gray (`rgba(230, 230, 235, 0.75)`) with backdrop blur.

---

## 2. Typography

- **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, or `Montserrat`.
  - *Fallback:* `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.
- **Logo Serif Font:** `Cinzel` or `Playfair Display` (for the monogram).
  - *Fallback:* `Georgia, serif`.

### Scale & Hierarchy
- **Main Heading:** 56px–64px | Bold (700–800) | Line-height: 1.05 | Tracking: -0.02em.
- **Subheadline:** 16px–18px | Medium (500) | Line-height: 1.4 | Color: `#333333`.
- **Navigation Links:** 14px | Medium (500) / Active: Semi-Bold (600).
- **Body / Feature Descriptions:** 13px–14px | Regular (400) | Line-height: 1.35 | Color: `#666666`.
- **Feature Titles:** 14px–15px | Semi-Bold (600) | Color: `#0A0A0A`.
- **Badges & Meta:** 12px–13px | Medium (500).

---

## 3. Layout Architecture

A full-screen, 2-column grid layout with fixed padding (48px horizontal, 36px vertical).

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [Logo]        [Nav: Home, Services, About Us, Results]                |
|                                                                       |
| [Main Headline]                     [Floating Badge: Strategy]        |
| [Brush Stroke Highlight]                                        01    |
| [Subheadline]                       [Joker Portrait]             (o)  |
| [CTA Button]                                                     .    |
| [Feature List (3 rows)]             [Badge: Results Speak]       .    |
|                                                                  05   |
| [IG Social Pill]                    [Watermark]  [Circular Logo Seal] |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 4. Components & Content

### 4.1 Header
- **Logo (Top-Left):** Interlocking serif monogram "SD" above uppercase subtext "SUCCESS DIGITAL" (Tracking: +0.2em, 9px).
- **Navigation (Center-Left):** Horizontal flex list.
  - Links: `Home` (Active, bold), `Services`, `About Us`, `Results`.
  - Margin/Gap: 24px between links.

### 4.2 Left Content Column (Width: ~48%)
- **Headline Stack:**
  - "We Turn Chaos Into"
  - Highlighted Container: Solid black rough brush-stroke rectangle containing white bold text: "Conversions.".
- **Sub-caption:** "E-Commerce Strategies That Bring Clarity, Customers & Consistent Sales."
- **Primary CTA:** Rounded pill button (`#0A0A0A`), white text:
  - Icon: Outlined Rocket / Sparkle.
  - Label: "Grow Smarter. Sell More."
  - Padding: 12px 24px, radius: 999px.
- **Value Proposition List (Vertical Stack, gap: 16px):**
  - Items with 36px circular black badge containing a white outlined icon:
    1. **Shopping Cart Icon:** Title: "E-Commerce Strategy" | Text: "Data-driven plans that convert visitors into loyal buyers."
    2. **Growth Chart Icon:** Title: "Store Optimization" | Text: "High-converting stores built for performance and scale."
    3. **Bullseye/Target Icon:** Title: "Marketing That Works" | Text: "From email to ads – we bring traffic that actually buys."
- **Footer Tag (Bottom-Left):**
  - Pill container (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`), border: 1px solid `#E0E0E0`, radius: 999px.
  - Content: Instagram camera icon + `@succesdigital001 | Let's Build Your Success Story.`

### 4.3 Right Visual Column (Width: ~52%)
- **Background Watermark:** Subtle, high-scale (opacity: 0.04) "SD" monogram centered in upper-right quadrant.
- **Main Portrait Asset:**
  - **Subject:** Black and white photographic portrait of the Joaquin Phoenix Joker in clown face paint and tailored suit.
  - **Pose:** Direct head-and-shoulders front view, slight smirk, intense gaze.
  - **Treatment:** Background fully removed/blended into the page canvas. Natural contrast, deep shadows on suit lapels.
  - **Placement:** Anchored to the center-right, running off the bottom edge.
- **Floating Overlays:**
  - **Badge 1 (Upper Right):** Translucent capsule (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) with black indicator dot + "Strategy". Positioned at shoulder height.
  - **Badge 2 (Mid-Right, Overlapping Suit):** Translucent capsule with black indicator dot + "Results That Speak For Us".
  - **Circular Seal (Bottom-Right):** Solid white 70px circular badge with 1px border containing the black "SD SUCCESS DIGITAL" emblem.

### 4.4 Pagination Tracker (Far Right Margin)
- Fixed vertical alignment centered along right edge.
- **Top:** Text "01" (12px, gray).
- **Middle:** Vertical dot track:
  - Selected state: Target ring (outer circle + centered dot).
  - Unselected states: 3 solid dots (diameter: 4px, color: `#A0A0A5`).
- **Bottom:** Text "05" (12px, gray).
