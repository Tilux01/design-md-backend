# UI/UX Specification: SpaceX Concept Landing Page

This document provides a comprehensive blueprint to recreate the provided SpaceX concept landing page. All dimensions, colors, typography, and content descriptions are specified to ensure an exact visual clone.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport

- **Layout Type:** Full-screen responsive hero section.
- **Background Color:** Deep space black (`#0a0a0c` to `#000000` radial/linear gradients).
- **Outer Frame Note:** The rounded outer container visible in the source image is purely a presentation mockup frame. **The implementation must span 100% width and 100% height of the viewport (Full Screen).**

---

## 2. Typography

### Primary Font Families
- **Logo & Navigation:** Geometric, high-track sans-serif (e.g., `Orbitron`, `Share Tech Mono`, or `Montserrat` with heavy letter-spacing).
- **Headings & Body Text:** Clean, modern geometric sans-serif (e.g., `Rajdhani`, `Inter`, or `Roboto`).

### Font Fallback Stack
```css
font-family: 'Orbitron', 'Rajdhani', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;
```

## 3. Header & Navigation Bar

Positioned absolute/sticky at the top with generous padding.

- **Logo:** 
  - Text-based or SVG vector: **"SPACEX"** with a distinct stylized, sweeping 'X'.
  - Position: Left-aligned (`calc(5% + 20px)` from the left edge).
- **Navigation Links:**
  - Items: `HOME`, `GALLERIES`, `HISTORY`, `STARSHIP`, `MISSION`, `ABOUT`
  - Position: Right-aligned, spaced evenly using flexbox gap (`~32px` apart).
  - Uppercase styling with active state indicator (none explicitly glowing in this view, standard white text).

---

## 4. Hero Content Section (Left Column)

Aligned vertically center-left, occupying approximately 45% of the screen width.

### Heading
- **Text:** `TAKING HUMANS TO SPACE`
- **Styling:** Uppercase, high contrast white text.

### Paragraph / Description
- **Text:** 
  > In 2020, SpaceX returned America's ability to fly NASA astronauts to and from the International Space Station on American vehicles for the first time since 2011. In addition to flying astronauts to space for NASA, SpaceX's Dragon spacecraft can also carry commercial astronauts to Earth orbit, the ISS or beyond.
- **Styling:** Muted gray text, line-height `1.6`, maximum width restricted to `~420px` to maintain a clean reading column.

### Interactive CTA Button ("Read more")
- **Container:** White pill/rounded-rectangle button.
- **Border-radius:** `4px` (slight rounding) or fully rounded.
- **Text:** `Read more` in solid black.
- **Icon / Separator:** 
  - A vertical divider line `|` followed by a small interactive dot/icon cluster `::` or arrow symbol on the right side inside the button.
- **Padding:** `10px 20px`.

---

## 5. Hero Visual Asset (Right Column / Background)

### Astronaut Subject Image
- **Description:** A detailed, high-contrast monochrome (black and white) photograph shot from behind an astronaut wearing a futuristic SpaceX-style pressure suit and sleek reflective helmet. The visor catches a subtle highlight/reflection at the top arch. The backpack life-support system and shoulder straps are clearly visible, with a small American flag patch on the left shoulder.
- **Treatment / Background:** **Backgroundless / Transparent Cutout** or deeply blended into the dark background via a vignette effect. 
- **Placement:** Positioned on the mid-right side of the viewport, partially overlapping the center-right space.
- **Rotation / Angle:** Straight-on rear view, no dramatic tilt, perfectly level.

### Ambient Particle Overlay (Background Effect)
- **Description:** Bokeh-style glowing white/blue star dust particles scattered across the top-left and bottom-right corners of the entire viewport, creating a depth-of-field space atmosphere.
- **Implementation:** Layered canvas or CSS pseudo-elements with radial gradients and low opacity (`opacity: 0.4` to `0.7`).

---

## 6. Micro-Interactions & UI States

- **Hover on Nav Items:** Smooth color transition from `#ffffff` to `#cccccc`.
- **Hover on "Read more" Button:** Slight background brightness shift (e.g., solid white to off-white `#f0f0f0` with a subtle box-shadow).
- **Carousel / Pagination Indicators:** A subtle horizontal line indicator at the bottom-center of the astronaut image (`—` style pagination dash indicating slide 1 of an active slider).
