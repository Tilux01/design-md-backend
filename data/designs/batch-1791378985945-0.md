# UI/UX Specification & Design System: Travel Landing Page ("Machu Picchu - Peru")

This document outlines the exact design specifications for building a pixel-perfect, responsive clone of the provided travel landing page. The design features a dark-mode travel booking aesthetic with a central coverflow/carousel card layout, dynamic typography, and subtle lighting effects.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Design Tokens

### Color Palette
*   **Background (Primary):** `#111113` (Deep Charcoal/Black used for the main page background)
*   **Background (Surface/Navbar):** `#161619` with slight transparency (`rgba(22, 22, 25, 0.9)`)
*   **Text (Primary):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
*   **Text (Secondary/Muted):** `#A0A0A5` (Cool Light Gray)
*   **Accent Color (CTA / Highlights):** `#FF6B00` to `#FF8C00` (Vibrant Sunset Orange gradient)
*   **Card Overlay Gradients:** Linear gradient from `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1)` (top) to `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)` (bottom) to ensure high text contrast.

### Typography System
*   **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `Poppins`, or `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Sans-serif, clean geometric curves).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;`

#### Hierarchy
1.  **Hero Heading (Active Card):**
    *   *Text:* "MACHU PICCHU - PERU"
    *   *Weight:* 700 (Bold) / Extra Bold
    *   *Size:* `32px` to `42px` (fluid/responsive)
    *   *Letter Spacing:* `1.5px` (Wide tracking)
    *   *Transform:* Uppercase
2.  **Hero Subtext / Tagline:**
    *   *Text:* "Adventure is never far away"
    *   *Weight:* 400 (Regular)
    *   *Size:* `14px`
    *   *Color:* Muted white/light gray
3.  **Navigation Links:**
    *   *Weight:* 500 (Medium)
    *   *Size:* `14px`
    *   *Color:* Primary white for active, muted gray for inactive.
4.  **Buttons / CTA:**
    *   *Weight:* 600 (Semi-bold)
    *   *Size:* `14px`
    *   *Transform:* Uppercase or Sentence case (depending on state)

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Components

### A. Navigation Bar (`<header>`)
*   **Position:** Fixed or Sticky at the top (`z-index: 100`).
*   **Layout:** Flexbox with `space-between` alignment.
*   **Left Section (Logo):**
    *   Brand name/Logo: Stylized text ("Voyage" or similar) in bold white font.
*   **Center Section (Nav Links):**
    *   Items: `Destinations`, `Tour Types`, `Tour Packages`, `Special Offers`.
    *   Active indicator: A thin horizontal orange accent line beneath the active link (`Destinations`).
*   **Right Section (Actions):**
    *   Search icon (`🔍`).
    *   Primary CTA Button: Rounded pill shape (`border-radius: 50px`), filled with the vibrant orange gradient (`#FF6B00`), featuring white text (e.g., "Book Now" or user profile icon).

---

### B. Background Visuals
*   **Backdrop Effect:** A deeply blurred (`blur(40px)` or greater) atmospheric travel landscape image spanning the full viewport, darkened by a heavy dark overlay (`rgba(17, 17, 19, 0.85)`).

---

### C. Hero Section: Carousel / Coverflow Card Stack
The core interactive component is a 3D-perspective or translated carousel showing 5 cards simultaneously with depth scaling.

*   **Layout:** Centered horizontal flex container with hidden overflow.
*   **Card Dimensions (Center/Active):**
    *   *Width:* ~280px - 320px
    *   *Height:* ~420px - 480px
    *   *Border Radius:* `16px` to `24px`
    *   *Box Shadow:* Deep drop shadow with soft glow (`0 20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`).
*   **Card States & Positioning (Left to Right):**
    1.  **Far Left Card:** Scaled down (~80%), lower opacity (`0.4`), heavily translated left and rotated slightly backward.
    2.  **Mid Left Card:** Scaled (~90%), medium opacity (`0.7`), closer to center.
    3.  **Center (Active) Card:** 100% scale, full opacity (`1`), featuring full text typography overlay ("MACHU PICCHU - PERU").
    4.  **Mid Right Card:** Scaled (~90%), medium opacity (`0.7`).
    5.  **Far Right Card:** Scaled down (~80%), lower opacity (`0.4`).

---

### D. Carousel Pagination / Indicator
*   Located directly beneath the active card stack.
*   Consists of a subtle dot pagination system:
    *   Active dot: White pill/oval shape.
    *   Inactive dots: Small muted gray circular dots.

---

## 3. Animations & Micro-Interactions

### Carousel Transition (Hover / Swipe / Click)
*   **Transition Curve:** `cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)` (Smooth ease-out).
*   **Duration:** `0.6s`.
*   *Behavior:* Clicking adjacent cards or using navigation arrows smoothly rotates the stack, scaling the newly selected card to the center while bringing its associated title and subtitle into focus with a fade-in animation (`opacity: 0 -> 1`, `translateY(10px -> 0)`).

### Hover Effects
*   **Navigation Links:** Smooth color transition to bright white or slight upward shift.
*   **CTA Button:** Subtle scale increase (`transform: scale(1.05)`) with an intensified drop shadow.
*   **Cards:** Slight elevation (`translateY(-8px)`) when hovered.
