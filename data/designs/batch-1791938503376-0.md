# Design Specification: BBC Earth - Africa Landing Page

## 1. Layout Overview
*   **Format:** Full-screen (100vw, 100vh), no-scroll landing page.
*   **Structure:** A layered composition.
    *   **Layer 0 (Base):** Background landscape (Sky and Ground).
    *   **Layer 1 (Mid):** Main Title Text ("AFRICA").
    *   **Layer 2 (Foreground):** Elephant subjects (cutouts) overlapping the title.
    *   **Layer 3 (UI):** Navigation, Logo, Socials, and Footer controls.

## 2. Color Palette
*   **Primary Background:** Muted earthy tones.
    *   **Sky:** Deep slate blue and charcoal grey (#4A5568 to #2D3748).
    *   **Ground:** Burnt orange, sienna, and dried grass gold (#A0522D, #8B4513).
*   **Typography:** 
    *   **Main Titles:** Pure White (#FFFFFF).
    *   **Secondary Text:** Off-white/Light Grey (#E2E8F0) with roughly 80% opacity.
*   **Accents:** Thin white borders for buttons.

## 3. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **BBC Reith Sans** (Official BBC font).
*   **Fallback Fonts:** 
    *   *Heading:* **Montserrat** (Bold/Extra Bold) or **Inter**.
    *   *Body/Nav:* **Open Sans** or **Roboto**.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Logo:** "BBC" in blocks, "earth" in lowercase light-weight sans-serif.
    *   **Sub-headline ("SEVEN WORLDS..."):** All-caps, tracking (letter-spacing) set to approximately `0.5em`.
    *   **Main Title ("AFRICA"):** Extra Bold, All-caps, massive scale (approx `15vw`).
    *   **Nav/Footer Text:** Uppercase, `12px` to `14px`, medium weight.

## 4. Header (Top Navigation)
*   **Placement:** Fixed top, padding approx `40px` from top, `60px` from sides.
*   **Left Group:** 
    *   Hamburger Menu Icon (three horizontal lines).
    *   Links: "ABOUT", "WHERE TO WATCH". Spacing between links: `30px`.
*   **Center:** **BBC earth** logo. Perfectly centered horizontally.
*   **Right Group:** Social Icons (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube). Small, white, high-contrast icons spaced `20px` apart.

## 5. Main Content (Hero Section)
*   **Headline Group:** Centered vertically and horizontally.
    *   **Top Text:** "SEVEN WORLDS ONE PLANET" (Small, wide tracking).
    *   **Main Text:** "AFRICA". Note: The "A" and "A" at the ends should be slightly clipped or tucked behind the elephant assets to create a 3D depth effect.
*   **Visual Assets (Elephants):**
    *   **Image 1 (Left):** A large adult African elephant walking right. High detail on skin texture. **Placement:** Overlapping the left side of the letter "A" in AFRICA.
    *   **Image 2 (Center-Left):** A medium-sized elephant following the first. **Placement:** Positioned between the "F" and "R".
    *   **Image 3 (Center-Right):** A small baby elephant (calf). **Placement:** Positioned in front of the "I" and "C".
    *   **Image 4 (Right):** A large adult elephant walking right. **Placement:** Overlapping the final "A" in AFRICA.
    *   **Asset Requirement:** All four elephants must be **backgroundless (transparent PNGs)**. They should have a slight "grounding" shadow at their feet to blend into the background savanna image.

## 6. Footer (Bottom Controls)
*   **Placement:** Bottom-aligned, padding approx `60px` from bottom and sides.
*   **Left Component (Description):** 
    *   Max-width: `300px`.
    *   Text: "Home to the greatest wildlife gatherings on Earth, but even in this land of plenty, wildlife faces huge challenges."
    *   Font-size: `14px`, line-height: `1.6`.
*   **Center Component (CTA):** 
    *   Button: "WATCH TRAILER".
    *   Style: Transparent background, `1px` solid white border, rectangular shape.
    *   Padding: `12px 40px`.
*   **Right Component (Carousel Nav):**
    *   Text: "← AFRICA   AUSTRALIA →"
    *   "AFRICA" is bold/active; "AUSTRALIA" is lower opacity.
    *   Small arrows flanking the text.

## 7. Image Asset Descriptions (for Sourcing/Creation)
1.  **Background Image:** A high-resolution landscape of an African savanna. The bottom 40% is dry, golden-orange grass. The top 60% is a dramatic, dark, overcast sky with heavy cumulus clouds. No visible sun.
2.  **Elephant Asset 1 (Far Left):** Profile view of an adult elephant, trunk slightly curved, tusks visible. Walking right. No background.
3.  **Elephant Asset 2 (Middle Left):** Slightly angled profile of an adult elephant. Walking right. No background.
4.  **Elephant Asset 3 (Calf):** Small baby elephant, mid-stride, trunk down. No background.
5.  **Elephant Asset 4 (Far Right):** Profile view of an adult elephant, walking right, head slightly lowered. No background.

## 8. Technical Effects
*   **Parallax (Optional but recommended):** The elephants should move at a slightly different speed than the background to enhance the 3D depth.
*   **Text Masking:** The "AFRICA" text should feel integrated into the environment, sitting "behind" the elephants but "in front" of the horizon line.
