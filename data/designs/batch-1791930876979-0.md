# Design Specification: EXEO Gaming Controllers Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Theme
*   **Viewport**: Full-screen desktop (1440px+ recommended, 100vh layout).
*   **Color Palette**:
    *   Background Base: `#0F1012` to `#16171A` (subtle dark matte gradient).
    *   Card/Dock Background: `#1C1D21` (translucent surface with subtle border `#2A2C32`).
    *   Text Primary: `#FFFFFF`
    *   Text Secondary / Muted: `#80838D`
    *   Accent Color (Active controls): Coral/Salmon Pink (`#FA9287`)
    *   Hardware LED Accents: Cyan (`#4CC9F0`), Magenta (`#D946EF`), Red-Orange (`#FF5722`).
*   **Typography**:
    *   Primary: Geometric Sans-Serif (`Euclid Circular A`, `Inter`, or `Montserrat`).
    *   Fallbacks: `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.

---

## 2. Header (Top Navigation)
*   **Container**: Absolute, top 0, full width, padded (`40px 60px`).
*   **Left Section**:
    *   **Logo**: "EXEO" in bold geometric caps (18px, letter-spacing +2px) with a small circle accent under the first 'E'.
    *   **Nav Links**: "Home", "Products", "About" (13px, weight 500, muted color `#80838D`, horizontal spacing 32px).
*   **Right Section**:
    *   **Menu Icon**: Minimalist hamburger icon (three stacked lines, right-aligned).

---

## 3. Hero Content (Left Side)
*   **Placement**: Vertically centered, left: `60px`, max-width: `420px`.
*   **Step Indicator**: `(01)` surrounded by a fine circular stroke, placed above the headline.
*   **Main Headline**:
    *   Text: `GAMING`<br>`CONTROLLERS`
    *   Style: Bold/Black weight (900), ~56px font size, uppercase, tight line-height (1.05), `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Description**:
    *   Text: *"Exeo modular gaming controllers are a range of motion sensing controllers that allow the gamers to make their own customized [experience]."*
    *   Style: 13px, line-height 1.6, muted `#80838D`.
*   **CTA Link**:
    *   Text: `Learn more >`
    *   Style: 14px, weight 600, `#FFFFFF`, margin-top 32px.

---

## 4. Product Imagery (Hero Showcase)
*   **Type**: 3D photorealistic render of modular gaming hardware.
*   **Asset Breakdown**:
    *   **Main Stick Controller ("Brutus")**: Cylindrical matte-black baton angled downward at ~45°, featuring neon blue and purple illuminated LED slit strips.
    *   **Secondary Grips ("Velox")**: Angled modular attachments resting in the background.
    *   **Base Docks ("Terra")**: Two puck-shaped circular docking bases resting on the bottom-right plane, displaying glowing red and white edge lights with "TERRA" printed on the rim.
*   **Format**: Transparent PNG or WebP layer positioned behind the bottom dock and floating dynamically across the center-right viewport.

---

## 5. Bottom Navigation Dock
*   **Container**: Fixed at bottom, height ~90px, width 100%, background `#18191D` with a subtle top border line `#25272D`.
*   **Components (Left to Right)**:
    1.  **Preview Thumbnail**:
        *   Miniature dark card showing a modular base with a circular "Play/Focus" icon overlay.
    2.  **Navigation Arrows**:
        *   Two square buttons (Left/Right).
        *   Left button: Dark gray (`#222429`) with `<` icon.
        *   Right button: Active accent `#FA9287` with `>` icon.
    3.  **Module Category List**:
        *   Section Label: `MODULES` (10px, uppercase, muted).
        *   Horizontal item list (13px, `#FFFFFF`):
            *   `Brutus (The big brother)`
            *   `Velox (The agile one)`
            *   `Terra (The ground hugger)`
    4.  **Language Selector**:
        *   Right-aligned: `En ▾` (12px, muted text).
