# Design Specification: Samsung Galaxy Watch Landing Page Clone

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport
- **Screen State:** Full-screen immersive web layout (omitting the device frame shown in the preview mockup).
- **Background Structure:** A three-way split overlapping circular/intersecting gradient aesthetic layered horizontally:
  - **Left Section (Peach/Rose Beige):** `rgb(222, 187, 163)` approx.
  - **Center Section (Light Gray/Silver):** `rgb(205, 207, 209)` approx.
  - **Right Section (Dark Charcoal/Graphite):** `rgb(68, 73, 76)` approx.
- **Aspect Ratio & Sizing:** Responsive fluid container spanning `100vw` width and `100vh` height.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
- **Primary Brand Font Family:** `SamsungOne`, `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif.
- **Fallback Font Stack:** `font-family: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;`

### Typography Hierarchy:
1. **Brand Logo ("SAMSUNG"):**
   - Font Weight: Bold (`700`)
   - Text Transform: Uppercase
   - Letter Spacing: Tight (`1px`)
2. **Navigation Menu Items ("MOBILE", "TVS & HOME THEATER", "COMPUTING", "APPLIANCES & SMART HOME"):**
   - Font Size: `11px`
   - Font Weight: Medium (`500`)
   - Letter Spacing: `0.5px`
3. **Main Hero Title ("GALAXY WATCH"):**
   - Font Size: `28px` to `36px` (responsive)
   - Font Weight: Extra Bold (`800`)
   - Text Transform: Uppercase
   - Alignment: Centered at the bottom-center of the main stage.
4. **Subtitle ("NEW GENERATION"):**
   - Font Size: `10px`
   - Font Weight: Semi-Bold (`600`)
   - Letter Spacing: `2px`
   - Text Transform: Uppercase
5. **Pagination / Page Number ("036"):**
   - Font Size: `14px`
   - Font Weight: Regular (`400`)
   - Position: Bottom-left.

---

## 3. Header & Navigation Component
- **Position:** Absolute / Fixed at the top (`top: 30px`, `left: 50px`, `right: 50px`).
- **Layout:** Flexbox layout with space-between.
- **Left Group:**
  - Samsung Logo SVG/Text.
  - Hamburger Menu Icon (three horizontal lines).
- **Center Group (Nav Links):**
  - Horizontal list of navigation categories: `MOBILE`, `TVS & HOME THEATER`, `COMPUTING`, `APPLIANCES & SMART HOME`.
  - Color: Dark charcoal/black. Active state: underline or bold styling.
- **Right Group:**
  - Utility icons: User profile icon and Search magnifying glass icon (`🔍`).

---

## 4. Imagery & Watch Assets (Backgroundless PNGs)
The layout features three distinct Samsung Galaxy Watch product renders layered over the intersecting color blocks. All images are **backgroundless transparent PNGs** with subtle drop shadows for depth.

### A. Left Watch (Rose Gold Variant)
- **Description:** Samsung Galaxy Watch in Rose Gold / Pink Gold finish with a matching silicone/leather textured strap. The watch face displays an analog minimalist gold-toned dial.
- **Placement:** Positioned on the left-center peach section, angled slightly toward the right.
- **Rotation:** Tilted roughly `15 degrees` clockwise.

### B. Center Watch (Silver/Black Sport Variant - Hero Element)
- **Description:** Samsung Galaxy Watch with a silver metallic bezel, black ribbed sport band, and a highly detailed digital/analog hybrid chronograph face featuring orange accent hands and multiple complication sub-dials.
- **Placement:** Centered prominently on the page, overlapping the transition between the peach and light gray sections.
- **Scale:** Larger than the side watches to indicate focal hierarchy.
- **Rotation:** Straight/Upright (`0 degrees`).

### C. Right Watch (All-Black Stealth Variant)
- **Description:** Samsung Galaxy Watch in matte black finish with a black rugged strap and a dark analog watch face with subtle yellow/gold markers and hands.
- **Placement:** Positioned on the right dark charcoal section.
- **Rotation:** Tilted roughly `-15 degrees` counter-clockwise, angled inward toward the center watch.

---

## 5. UI Controls & Interactive Elements

- **Left Pagination / Navigation Arrow:**
  - A circular button containing a left-facing chevron (`<`) accompanied by a thin horizontal guide line extending to the right.
  - Position: Center-left of the viewport.
- **Right Pagination / Navigation Arrow:**
  - A circular button containing a right-facing chevron (`>`) preceded by a thin horizontal guide line extending to the left.
  - Position: Center-right of the viewport.
- **Bottom-Right Indicators:**
  - Tiny pagination dots or thumbnail letters (`fb`, `tw`, `in` representing social media shortcuts or carousel dots) aligned neatly at the bottom-right corner.

---
