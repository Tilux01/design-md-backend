# Design Specification: Mills' Memorial Day Sale - 20% OFF Everything

## 1. Global Styles & Variables

*   **Primary Background Color:** `#E2E2FC` (Light lavender/periwinkle tint)
*   **Primary Text & Accent Color (Outline/Dark):** `#514892` (Deep muted purple)
*   **Secondary Text Color (Solid):** `#867BB5` (Medium lavender-purple)
*   **Banner Background Color:** `#6458A5` (Rich purple)
*   **Banner Text Color:** `#FFFFFF` (White)
*   **Primary Font Family:** Clean, geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Poppins*, *Montserrat*, or *Inter*).
*   **Secondary/Brand Font (on product):** Rounded, lowercase sans-serif (e.g., *Quicksand* or custom lowercase styling).

---

## 2. Layout & Structure

*   **Canvas:** Full screen (100vw x 100vh), vertical layout.
*   **Structure:**
    1.  **Top Ticker Banner** (Fixed at top)
    2.  **Hero Content Area:**
        *   "20%" Outline Heading
        *   Layered Product Mockup with Swatch Background
        *   "OFF" Outline Heading
        *   "everything" Solid Subheading
        *   Call-to-Action / Tagline sentence
    3.  **Bottom Ticker Banner** (Fixed at bottom)

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. Ticker Banners (Top & Bottom)
*   **Background:** `#6458A5`
*   **Padding:** `12px 0`
*   **Text Style:** Uppercase, tracking/letter-spacing wide, bold sans-serif, white (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Content Pattern (Repeating):** `[SALE] [whale/icon] [MILLS' MEMORIAL DAY SALE]`
*   **Icon:** Small white outline whale/creature silhouette.

---

### B. Hero Section

#### 1. "20%" Header (Outline Style)
*   **Text:** `20%`
*   **Font Size:** Very large (Display size, occupying ~30% width).
*   **Style:** Transparent fill with a solid `#514892` outline stroke (approx. `2px` to `3px` width).
*   **Position:** Centered horizontally, upper-middle area.

#### 2. Product Showcase (Center Layer)
*   **Background Swatch Element:**
    *   **Shape:** Soft, organic, pill-shaped cream/light-peach smear (`#F7E5DE` or similar soft warm flesh tone).
    *   **Position:** Placed diagonally behind the product tube.
*   **Product Tube (`florence by mills - DREAMY DEW MOISTURIZER`):**
    *   **Container/Tube Color:** Soft pastel lilac (`#CFC0E8`).
    *   **Orientation:** Angled diagonally from bottom-left to top-right (approx. 20-degree tilt).
    *   **Drop Shadow:** Soft, diffused dark purple/gray shadow cast to the bottom-left to create depth against the background.
    *   **Branding & Text on Tube:**
        *   Brand logo vertically oriented reading `florence` (in lowercase, custom rounded sans-serif, white).
        *   Sub-brand text: `by mills™` (small, white).
        *   Product Name: `DREAMY DEW MOISTURIZER` (stacked, white, bold uppercase).
        *   Volume: `50ml | 1.7 fl. oz. liq. e` (small, white).
    *   **Cap:** Light purple flip-top cap.

---

#### 3. "OFF" Header (Outline Style)
*   **Text:** `OFF`
*   **Font Size:** Large display size, matching the visual weight of "20%".
*   **Style:** Transparent fill with solid `#514892` outline stroke.
*   **Position:** Centered horizontally, overlapping slightly with the lower portion of the product tube.

---

#### 4. "everything" Subheading
*   **Text:** `everything`
*   **Font Style:** Lowercase, medium weight sans-serif.
*   **Color:** Solid `#867BB5` (Medium muted purple).
*   **Position:** Centered directly below the "OFF" text.

---

#### 5. Tagline
*   **Text:** `swap your spring products out for summer!`
*   **Font Style:** Lowercase, regular weight sans-serif.
*   **Color:** Solid `#514892`.
*   **Position:** Centered at the bottom, just above the lower banner.

---

## 4. Responsive & Layering Guidelines

*   **Z-Index Hierarchy (from back to front):**
    1.  Base Background (`#E2E2FC`)
    2.  Outline Typography (`20%` and `OFF`)
    3.  Cream/Peach Smear asset
    4.  Product Tube asset (with drop shadow)
    5.  Solid Typography (`everything` and tagline)
    6.  Top and Bottom Ticker Banners
