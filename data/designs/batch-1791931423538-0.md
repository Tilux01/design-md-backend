# Design Specification: Nike Air Max Plus Landing Page

This document defines the exact layout, visual styling, typography, colors, and asset requirements needed to build a pixel-perfect, full-screen reproduction of the Nike Air Max Plus product display page.

---

## 1. Viewport & Canvas Layout

* **Display Type:** Full-screen responsive viewport (`100vw` × `100vh`), fixed/overflow-hidden on desktop.
* **Canvas Background:** Deep obsidian / charcoal gradient with a localized warm radial glow:
  * **Base Fill:** `#0D0D0F`
  * **Radial Ambient Glow:** Positioned behind the shoe in the upper-right quadrant:
    * `radial-gradient(circle at 70% 45%, #38160B 0%, #1F0D07 35%, #0D0D0F 75%)`
* **Layout Structure:**
  * **Header:** Sticky/fixed top navigation bar spanning full width (`height: ~80px`).
  * **Main Content Area:** Two-column split layout (`flex` or CSS Grid, `1fr 1.2fr` split).
    * **Left Column:** Product titles, descriptive body copy, price, and CTA.
    * **Right Column:** Parametric vector wave graphic, product sneaker asset, and dynamic lighting.
  * **Footer Action:** Anchored near bottom-center/bottom-right of the viewport.

## 4. Component Breakdown & Specifications

### 4.1. Header Navigation Bar
* **Container:** Full width, horizontal flexbox, `padding: 32px 64px`.
* **Left Item:** Nike Swoosh icon in solid white (`#FFFFFF`), pointing right.
* **Center Items:** Horizontal list of navigation items:
  * `MEN`, `WOMEN`, `KIDS`, `CUSTOMIZE`, `COLLECTION`
  * `gap: 36px`
* **Right Item:** Minimalist magnifying glass search icon (thin stroke line, `18px × 18px`, white stroke `#FFFFFF`).

---

### 4.2. Left Column: Product Info & Actions
* **Position:** Aligned to the left margin (`padding-left: 80px`), vertically centered within the hero area.
* **Text Stack:** 
  * "Nike"
  * "Air Max Plus"
  * "MAKE THE GROUND SHAKE"
  * Description paragraph:
    > *"Let your attitude have the edge in your Nike Air Max Plus, a Tuned Air experience that offers premium stability and unbelievable cushioning. Featuring the OG's wavy design lines, TPU accents and airy mesh on the upper, it celebrates defiant style."*
* **Purchase Action Row:**
  * Horizontal layout (`display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 32px;`).
  * **Price Display:** `$249,99` (standard European comma decimal separator).
  * **Button Component:**
    * **Text:** `PRE-ORDER NOW`
    * **Padding:** `12px 28px`
    * **Border Radius:** `6px`
    * **Border:** None
    * **Background:** `linear-gradient(90deg, #D96B3D 0%, #A74623 100%)`
    * **Shadow:** `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(217, 107, 61, 0.25)`

---

### 4.3. Right Column: Imagery & Visual Atmosphere

#### Visual Asset 1: Background Parametric Wave Graphic
* **Description:** A mathematical 3D generative wireframe ribbon consisting of dozens of fine, continuous lines twisting in a Mobius/elliptical loop.
* **Colors:** Fluorescent orange (`#FF5B22`) fading toward golden-amber (`#E8A238`) at the edges, fading to near-transparent black in the shadows.
* **Placement:**
  * Center-right of the screen, layered **behind** the sneaker (`z-index: 1`).
  * Scale: ~`650px` width, tilted slightly counter-clockwise.

#### Visual Asset 2: Nike Air Max Plus Sneaker
* **Asset Type:** Cutout high-resolution raster image with an **alpha transparent background** (PNG/WebP).
* **Subject:** Lateral (outer profile) view of the classic **Nike Air Max Plus "Sunset" / "Tiger"** colorway.
  * Upper mesh features a vertical color gradient transitioning from fiery mandarin orange to dark graphite/black.
  * Glossy black curved TPU rib cage fingers spanning from sole to eyelets.
  * Glossy black toe cap and mudguard.
  * Visible Tuned Air units with orange internal pillars embedded in a chunky black midsole.
  * Midfoot shank arch plate showing orange-to-black fade.
  * Small vibrant orange Nike Swoosh emblem with a white/silver outline on the lateral quarter panel.
* **Placement & Orientation:**
  * Layered **in front** of the parametric wave (`z-index: 2`).
  * **Rotation/Tilt:** Tilted diagonally downward toward the front toe: rotated approximately `-18deg` to `-20deg` counter-clockwise.
  * **Toe Position:** Points toward the lower-left center.
  * **Heel Position:** Elevated toward the upper-right quadrant.
  * **Scale:** Dominates the right half of the canvas (`width: ~680px - 720px`).
* **Lighting/Shadow Effect:**
  * Soft drop shadow cast downward beneath the shoe to separate it cleanly from the background glow and wire lines (`filter: drop-shadow(0 25px 35px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7))`).

---

### 4.4. Floating Footer Action: "Watch Preview"
* **Position:** Fixed / absolute placement at the bottom center-right of the viewport (`bottom: 40px`, horizontal position aligned roughly beneath the sneaker's front outsole).
* **Layout:** Inline flex row (`display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 12px; cursor: pointer;`).
* **Icon:**
  * Circular badge (`width: 30px; height: 30px; border: 1.5px solid #FFFFFF; border-radius: 50%`).
  * Inward solid/outlined play triangle pointing right (`▷`), vertically and horizontally centered.
* **Label:**
  * Two stacked lines:
    ```
    WATCH
    PREVIEW
    ```
  * Style: All-caps, small sans-serif, bold, white, tracking `1.2px`.

## 6. Micro-Interactions & Hover States

* **Nav Links:** Color transition from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` to pure `#FFFFFF` with an underlying subtle glow on `:hover`.
* **Pre-Order Button:**
  * Smooth scale transition: `transform: translateY(-2px)` on hover.
  * Elevation increase: `box-shadow: 0 12px 30px rgba(217, 107, 61, 0.45)`.
* **Watch Preview Action:**
  * Circular play icon gains an orange accent border (`#E16235`) and an orange play icon fill on `:hover`.
* **Shoe Float (Optional Ambient Animation):**
  * Subtle vertical floating translation (`translateY(-8px)` to `translateY(0px)` across a 4-second ease-in-out infinite loop).
