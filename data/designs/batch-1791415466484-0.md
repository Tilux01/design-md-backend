# Design Specification: Mindful Journal App

## 1. Typography

* **Primary Font:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Urbanist`
* **Fallback Fonts:** `Inter`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `sans-serif`
* **Hierarchy:**
  * **Hero Metric:** 48px, Bold (700), tracking -1px (e.g., `420`)
  * **H1 / Greeting:** 24px, Bold (700) (e.g., `Hi, Jose Maria`)
  * **H2 / Section Titles:** 18px, Semi-Bold (600) (e.g., `My Journal`, `Quick Journal`, `Emotions`)
  * **Card Titles:** 15px, Bold (700)
  * **Body Text:** 13px–14px, Regular (400) / Medium (500)
  * **Captions & Tags:** 11px–12px, Medium (500)
  * **Calendar Days / Dates:** 12px, Regular (400) / Semi-Bold (600)

---

## 2. Color Palette

### Primary & Accent
* **Primary Brand Yellow:** `#FBB731` (CTA buttons, active date, floating action button)
* **Background Canvas:** `#F8F8F8` (Off-white / light neutral)
* **Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white)

### Emotion Chart Colors
* **Happy (Yellow):** `#FBB731`
* **Sad (Rust/Auburn):** `#7A3E33`
* **Calm (Olive Green):** `#8EA43B`
* **Anxious (Slate Brown):** `#6F685E`
* **Pill Track Background:** `#F1F1F1`

### Pastel Card Tones
* **Peach Card:** `#FEE4D9`
* **Lavender Card:** `#E9E7FD`
* **Beige Card:** `#DCD3C7`

### Text & Neutrals
* **Primary Text:** `#1A1A1A`
* **Secondary / Muted Text:** `#8F8F8F`
* **Border / Dividers:** `#EAEAEA`

---

## 3. Global Layout & Components

* **Base Viewport:** 390px × 844px (Mobile full screen, no mockups/bezels)
* **Horizontal Page Padding:** `20px`
* **Border Radii:**
  * Cards & Modals: `24px`
  * Buttons & Progress Pills: `9999px` (Full round)
  * Inner Badges: `12px`

---

## 4. Screen 1: Home Dashboard

### Header
* **Greeting:** "Hi, Jose Maria" (Left-aligned).
* **Avatar:** 40px circle, right-aligned, displaying user profile image with an active border.

### Horizontal Calendar Strip
* **Structure:** 7-day row (Mon–Sun).
* **Inactive Day:** Day initials in `#8F8F8F`, date number in a 36px circular clear container.
* **Active Day ("Thu 10"):** Filled circle `#FBB731`, white text (`#FFFFFF`).

### "My Journal" Section
* **Header:** Title + "See all" link (`#8F8F8F`).
* **Hero Carousel Card:**
  * Background: `#FCE681` to `#F6BC47` vertical gradient.
  * Content: Title ("Let's start your day"), subtitle, flat illustration of smiling sun rising over green hills and pine trees.
  * Adjacent Card: Partially visible beige container (`#DCD3C7`) with vertically rotated label ("Evening").

### "Quick Journal" Section
* **Header:** Title + "See all" link.
* **Cards (Horizontal Scroll):**
  * **Card 1 (Peach `#FEE4D9`):** Title "Pause & reflect 🌱", prompt text, tags `Today` (white pill) and `Personal` (white pill).
  * **Card 2 (Lavender `#E9E7FD`):** Title "Set Intentions 😊", prompt text, tags `Today` and `Family`.

### Bottom Navigation Bar
* **Container:** Floating white dock with subtle drop shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`).
* **Items:** 
  1. `Home` (Dark active capsule)
  2. `Explore` (Compass icon)
  3. `Add (+)` (Raised 48px circular yellow button `#FBB731`)
  4. `Journey` (Book icon)
  5. `Profile` (User icon)

---

## 5. Screen 2: Journal Insights & Emotion Tracking

### Top App Bar
* **Back Control:** Rounded button with `<` chevron (Left).
* **Title:** "My Journal" (Centered).
* **Action:** Rounded `...` button (Right).

### Stat Display
* **Counter:** `420` (Centered display typography).
* **Subtext:** "Celebrate what made you smile today." (Centered, `#8F8F8F`).

### Emotion Breakdown Card
* **Surface:** White card, `24px` border radius, padding `20px`.
* **Title:** "Emotions", Sub: "Here are four core emotions for your journal".
* **Vertical Bar Chart:**
  * 4 vertical pill tracks: width `52px`, height `180px`, background `#F1F1F1`.
  * **Fills (Bottom-up):**
    * Happy: 48% (`#FBB731`)
    * Sad: 33% (`#7A3E33`)
    * Calm: 27% (`#8EA43B`)
    * Anxious: 40% (`#6F685E`)
  * Percentage label embedded inside top of each colored fill (White text, 12px, bold).
  * Emotion labels beneath each track (`Happy`, `Sad`, `Calm`, `Anxious`).

### Bottom Sticky CTA
* **Button:** Full width, height `54px`, radius `9999px`.
* **Style:** Background `#FBB731`, text "Create a New Journal" (Medium weight, `#1A1A1A`).

---

## 6. Motion & Interaction

* **Chart Reveal:** Staggered spring animations for vertical bars (`duration: 0.6s`, `easeOutCubic`) filling from bottom (height `0%` to target `%`).
* **Carousels:** CSS scroll-snap on "My Journal" and "Quick Journal" cards with smooth deceleration.
* **FAB Interaction:** Tap trigger on `+` expands a radial sub-menu or opens the journal editor sheet (`spring: damping 20, stiffness 250`).
