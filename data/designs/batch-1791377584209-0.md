## 3. Detailed Component Breakdown

### A. Full-Body Pose Mannequin (Left)
* **Proportions:** Approximately 7.5 to 8 heads tall, stylized shōjo/anime proportion.
* **Head Construction:** Standard circle with a vertical center-line and a horizontal eye line intersecting just below the halfway point. Simple triangular jaw/chin.
* **Torso & Waist:** Slender shoulders, tapered waist marked with horizontal pink boundary indicators.
* **Limbs & Joints:** 
  * Arms hang naturally downward, forearms angled inward toward the lower abdomen/pelvis.
  * Hands are delicately clasped together in front of the lower waist/thigh area.
  * Legs are straight, standing close together with minimal negative space between the ankles.
* **Pink Structural Markers:** Perfect circles placed at the major articulation points (shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips/waist, knees, ankles) to serve as a rigging or drawing reference.

### B. Close-Up Head & Shoulder Angle (Right)
* **Composition:** Positioned diagonally in the foreground to emphasize dynamic three-quarter perspective.
* **Head Angle:** Tilted slightly downward and turned, showing clear directional construction lines (curved vertical center-line sweeping across the cheek/jaw, and a sloping horizontal eye-line leading toward the ear).
* **Ears & Neck:** Simplified outer ear anatomy with inner curve markings; slender neck connecting smoothly into curved collarbones and sloping shoulders.
* **Shading / Sketch Lines:** Rough, overlapping construction strokes (sketchiness) to convey a dynamic, hand-drawn digital pencil feel (e.g., Clip Studio Paint / Procreate pencil brush).

### C. Decorative Elements (Doodles)
* **Stars:** Multiple five-pointed outlined stars (`stroke-width: ~2px`, crisp black outlines with transparent interiors). Positioned dynamically near the close-up face and lower torso.
* **Flowers:** Stylized 5-petaled cloud-like organic flower doodles outlined in simple black ink strokes.
