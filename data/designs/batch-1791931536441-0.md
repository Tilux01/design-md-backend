# Design Specification: Smart Mirror Health Dashboard

A full-screen, high-contrast, minimalist smart mirror UI showing health metrics and user overview.

---

## 1. Canvas & Layout Setup
* **Screen Dimensions:** Fullscreen (100vw, 100vh), default 1920x1080 (16:9).
* **Background:** Smooth radial gradient from pure white `#FFFFFF` at the top-center to soft light gray `#D8DBE0` at the edges.
* **Layout Paradigm:** Absolute positioning around a centered circular focal point.

---

## 2. Typography
* **Primary Font:** `Inter`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `SF Pro Display`
* **Fallback Font:** `sans-serif`
* **Font Specs:**
  * **Top Bar:** 18px, Regular (400), Color: `#1F242D`
  * **Greeting ("Hello, Josh"):** 44px, Semi-Bold (600), Color: `#16191F`
  * **Timestamp ("18 minutes ago"):** 16px, Regular (400), Color: `#6B7280`
  * **Menu Labels:** 14px, Medium (500), Color: `#16191F`

---

## 3. Color Palette
* **Background:** `#FFFFFF` to `#D8DBE0`
* **Text Dark:** `#16191F`
* **Text Muted:** `#6B7280`
* **Dark Central Circle:** `#1E1E1E`
* **Pill - Summary:** `#3559E0` (Cobalt Blue)
* **Pill - Performance:** `#9A26E0` (Purple)
* **Pills - Battery / Rest:** `#1A1C20` (Matte Black)
* **Pills - Right Column:** Frosted glass, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` and border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`

---

## 4. Header Bar
* **Position:** Fixed top, `top: 48px`, `left: 64px`, `right: 64px`.
* **Left Item:** "Good morning  11 dec" (two spaces between words).
* **Right Items:** Horizontal flex container (`gap: 24px`):
  * Running figure icon (18px) + "11 10" (Time)
  * "22°" (Temperature)

---

## 5. Central Subject & Backdrop
* **Dark Circle Backdrop:**
  * Diameter: 520px.
  * Position: Centered horizontally (`left: 50%`, `transform: translateX(-50%)`), `top: 18%`.
  * Fill: `#1E1E1E`.
* **User Image Description:**
  * Cutout image (transparent background, no rotation).
  * Depicts a young Black man, centered bust view, short textured fade haircut, focused forward gaze.
  * Wearing a light-brown/camel tailored short-sleeve tunic with vertical pleat detailing down the left chest.
  * Layered directly over the dark circle, torso anchored at the bottom edge.

---
