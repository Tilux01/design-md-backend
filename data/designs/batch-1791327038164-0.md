# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Glassmorphism & Immersive Music Producer Interface.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-density dashboard layout with floating container panels, translucent glass layers (`backdrop-filter`), balanced with generous padding and rhythmic 8pt spatial intervals.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-tier depth hierarchy utilizing dark amber/espresso surfaces, subtle 1px luminous borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), radial gradient background glows, and floating player controls with drop-shadows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges (`TEXT-XS TRACKING-WIDEST`), ambient radial glow orbs behind central players, glassmorphic backdrop filters, live audio waveform visualizers.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.1)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-amber-500 inline-block"></span>`.
