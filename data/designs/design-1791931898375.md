# Design Specification: Nocs x Beats Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Palette
* **Canvas:** Fullscreen layout, no outer mock borders.
* **Colors:**
  * Background: `#DFE1E3` (Soft light grey)
  * Primary Text & Buttons: `#111111` (Deep black)
  * Secondary / Muted Text: `#8E9093`
  * Graphic Highlights: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white)
  * Accent Dot: `#E04B4B` (Small red cart indicator)

---

## 2. Typography
* **Primary Font:** Circular, GT Walsheim, or Inter (Medium to Bold).
* **Fallback Stack:** `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
* **Text Styles:**
  * **Logo (`nocs.`):** 20px, Bold, tracking tight.
  * **Heading (`Feel The Difference`):** 14px, Semi-Bold, `#111111`.
  * **Body Description:** 11px, Regular, `#8E9093`, max-width 200px.
  * **Product Details (`Beats Pro / $399.95`):** 12px, Medium/Bold, uppercase-leaning numbers.
  * **CTA (`BUY`):** 12px, Bold, uppercase, tracking wide (`0.15em`), `#FFFFFF`.
  * **Background Typo (`Beats`):** ~280px–320px, Heavy sans-serif, transparent fill, 1.5px black outline (`stroke: #111111`).

---

## 3. Structural Layout & Layers

### Layer 1: Background Elements
* **Right Shape:** Large, abstract white figure-8 / dual-circle graphic (`#FFFFFF`) covering the right 40% of the viewport.
* **Large Outlined Text:** Centered-right wordmark "Beats" spanning horizontally behind the main product image. Fully stroked, non-filled.

### Layer 2: Main Content Grid
* **Header (Top Bar):** Fixed/absolute `h-20`, spanning full width with 48px horizontal padding.
  * **Left:** `nocs.` brandmark.
  * **Center-Left:** Thin stroke search icon (magnifying glass).
  * **Right:** "Cart / 0" text with a small red dot indicator.
  * **Far Right:** 44px circular black button with two centered white horizontal lines (hamburger menu).

* **Left Panel:**
  * Positioned vertically centered, offset left (~8% from left edge).
  * Copy: "Feel The Difference" followed by "Made with an aluminum gunmetal frame and high-quality leather".
  * Video Thumbnail: 160×90px card below the text.
    * Left edge: White square overlay with a small black right-pointing play triangle.
    * Right portion: Thumbnail photo (description below).

* **Center Hero Product:**
  * Centered directly on screen, layered on top of the "Beats" outlined text.
  * Interactive Markers: Three subtle circular hotspots (thin white outline, center white dot) located on:
    1. Top headband inner edge.
    2. Lower-left outer rim of the earcup.
    3. Bottom-right base of the earcup.

* **Footer Controls (Bottom Bar):**
  * Spanning full width along the bottom edge, 48px side padding.
  * **Bottom-Left:** Two 40px circular black action buttons with white left/right arrows (`<`, `>`).
  * **Bottom-Center-Left:** Subtext `• Black •`.
  * **Bottom-Center:** Text label `Beats Pro / $399.95`.
  * **Bottom-Right:** 160×64px solid black rectangular button anchored to the bottom edge containing centered text `BUY`.

---

## 4. Asset Descriptions

### Main Product Image
* **Format:** Backgroundless transparent PNG.
* **Subject:** Matte black Beats Pro headphone.
* **Orientation & Placement:** 
  * Ear cup facing directly forward (flat orthographic angle), headband extending vertically straight up.
  * Centered horizontally, vertically aligned in the lower-middle half.
  * Soft, diffused drop-shadow directly behind it onto the background.

### Video Card Thumbnail
* **Format:** JPG / Rectangular image with soft rounded corners.
* **Subject:** Profile view of a person wearing black Beats Pro headphones in a music studio, with audio production equipment (keyboard/mixer) softly blurred in the background.
