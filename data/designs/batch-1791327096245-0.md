# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist / High-End Fashion Brand Direction.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with structured grid alignments, bold typographic statements, and muted monochromatic framing designed to highlight texture and photography.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat architectural layout with clean 1px solid structural borders and subtle material isolation zones. No heavy drop shadows; depth is implied through tonal shifts in surface grays and blacks.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase typographic specifiers, editorial split-panel layouts, precise color token hex callouts, clean monochromatic framing.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `4px 12px`, Font-size: `11px`, Font-weight: `500`, Tracking: `0.1em`, Border-radius: `0px` (Sharp architectural bounds), Background: `transparent`, Border: `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Color Hash (#) -> Rendered directly in text specs.
