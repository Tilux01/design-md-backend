# Design System & Implementation Guide: Sevora Portfolio

## 1. Overview & Layout Strategy
A minimalist, monochrome-dominant personal portfolio featuring high-contrast typography, subtle glassmorphism, and structured grid alignment. The design uses an outer viewport with a textured diagonal-hatch background and a central elevated content card.

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Background Pattern Base**: `#F4F4F6` (pattern: 45° subtle repeating lines `#E5E5EB`)
- **Main Hero Card Surface**: `#FFFFFF` to `#F1F2F6` (subtle vertical gradient)
- **Primary Text**: `#111111`
- **Secondary/Muted Text**: `#7A7D85`
- **Card Glass Surface**: `rgba(40, 44, 52, 0.55)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`
- **Border Highlight**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`
- **Accent/Inverted Surface**: `#111111` (White text `#FFFFFF`)

---

## 3. Typography
- **Headings (Display Serif)**: 
  - Primary: *Instrument Serif*, *Playfair Display*, or *Newsreader*
  - Fallback: `Georgia, serif`
  - Style: Semi-bold / Regular, tight tracking (`-0.02em`)
- **Body & UI (Geometric Sans-Serif)**:
  - Primary: *Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, or *SF Pro Text*
  - Fallback: `system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`
  - Weight: 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (Semi-bold)

---

## 4. Component Breakdown

### 4.1 Global Navigation
- **Placement**: Fixed/Sticky top, full-width container (`max-width: 1280px`).
- **Logo**: Interlocking dual-curve icon + bold text **"Sevora"** (Left).
- **Navigation Links**: Centered row; links: *Home*, *About*, *Projects*, *Article*, *Contact* (Font size: `14px`, Color: `#111111`, Gap: `32px`).
- **CTA**: Rounded pill button **"Start a project"** (Black `#111111`, white text, padding: `10px 24px`, radius: `9999px`).

### 4.2 Hero Section (Main Container)
- **Card**: Centered, rounded rectangle (`border-radius: 36px`), light shadow (`0 20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`).
- **Left Column**:
  - **Headline**: 
    - Line 1: *"Design Better"* in solid `#111111` (Display Serif, ~`56px`).
    - Line 2: *"Faster Smarter"* in muted `#8E929C` (Display Serif, ~`56px`).
  - **Subheadline**: *"I design refined brands, websites, and interfaces for ambitious founders and creative teams."* (`16px`, color `#686C75`, max-width `400px`).
  - **Button Group**:
    - Primary: *"View projects"* (Black pill, drop shadow).
    - Secondary: *"Get in touch"* (White/transparent pill, `1px solid #E1E3E8`, dark text).
  - **Metrics Row**: 3-column stats list:
    - `120+` / Projects completed
    - `8yr` / Experience
    - `40+` / Happy clients
    - Metric numbers in bold display serif (`32px`); labels below in muted sans-serif (`13px`).

- **Right Column (Visuals & Overlay)**:
  - **Portrait Image**:
    - **Subject**: Medium close-up of a man with wavy dark hair, light beard, wearing a black turtleneck and blazer.
    - **Treatment**: High-contrast black and white, crisp cutout (isolated background/transparent or seamlessly matched to card background).
    - **Placement**: Anchored bottom-right of the card; top of head naturally breaks slightly near the top curve.
  - **Floating Contact Card (Glassmorphism)**:
    - **Position**: Overlays lower torso/chest of the portrait, bottom-right aligned.
    - **Styling**: `border-radius: 20px`, dark translucent gradient, `1px` subtle light border, blur effect.
    - **Content**: 
      - Tag: *"Select project"* (Small, muted grey).
      - Title: *"Available for projects"* (Semi-bold, white).
      - Body: *"Share a few details, and I'll get back with a clear direction."* (Muted grey, `13px`).
      - Action: Squircle button (`border-radius: 12px`, white background, black `↗` diagonal arrow icon).

### 4.3 Client Logos Banner
- **Placement**: Directly below the hero container, centered row.
- **Logos**: Grayscale/monochrome vectors with consistent height (`~20px`) and `40px` gap:
  - *Codify*, *Flowboard*, *Agentify*, *TodoFusion*, *Identify*, *Nexus AI*, *Landify*.
