# Design Specification: REDSUN Landing Page Clone

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Color Palette
- **Background Color (Primary Dark):** `#0c0d12` (Very dark slate/black)
- **Primary Text:** `#ffffff` (Pure white)
- **Secondary Text:** `#9ca3af` (Muted gray)
- **Brand/Accent Color (Orange):** `#ff5722` or `#ff6b00` (Vibrant orange for buttons, tags, and glowing elements)
- **Accent Gradient:** Transition from dark grey/charcoal (`#1a1a1a`) through bright orange (`#ff5722`) to a warm white glow.

### Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif
- **Fallback Stack:** `sans-serif`
- **Headings (H1):** 
  - Size: `48px` to `56px` (Desktop)
  - Weight: Bold (`700`)
  - Line-height: `1.1`
- **Body / Subtitle Text:** 
  - Size: `14px` to `16px`
  - Weight: Regular (`400`)
  - Line-height: `1.5`
- **Navigation & Buttons:** 
  - Size: `13px` to `14px`
  - Weight: Medium (`500`)

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Full-Screen Behavior
- **Layout Type:** Full-width viewport (`width: 100vw`, `height: 100vh`), centered single-page application landing style.
- **Max Content Container:** `max-width: 1200px`, horizontally centered with standard padding (`20px` to `40px` left/right).
- **Background:** Solid `#0c0d12` with atmospheric radial gradients behind central elements mimicking a subtle warm orange glow.

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. Header / Navigation Bar
- **Position:** Fixed or sticky top navigation.
- **Left Element:** 
  - **Logo:** Text "REDSUN" in bold uppercase sans-serif with a bright orange circular dot/icon integrated into the end of the text.
- **Center Navigation Links:**
  - Items: `Home`, `Features`, `About`, `Pricing`, `Blog`, `Contact`, `Cart (0)`
  - Color: `#9ca3af` (Hover state: `#ffffff`)
- **Right Element:**
  - **CTA Button:** Pill-shaped button with solid orange background (`#ff6b00`), white text ("Book a Demo →"), subtle padding (`8px 16px`), and rounded corners (`border-radius: 50px`).

### B. Hero Section
- **Announcement Badge:**
  - Pill-shaped badge centered above the main heading.
  - Background: Dark translucent surface with a glowing orange pill tag on the left reading **"Whats New"** and white text following: **"Ease Update v0.1 >"**.
- **Main Heading (H1):**
  - Text: *"Intelligent Solutions Powered by AI."*
  - Alignment: Centered.
- **Sub-headline / Paragraph:**
  - Text: *"Gain clarity and harness the power of your data with RedSun. Our intuitive dashboard provides real-time analytics."*
  - Alignment: Centered, max-width constrained (`~500px`).
- **Hero CTA Buttons:**
  - **Button 1 (Secondary):** Dark background, white text, border stroke, text: *"Read More →"*, rounded corners (`50px`).
  - **Button 2 (Primary):** Solid orange background (`#ff6b00`), white text, text: *"Book a Demo →"*, rounded corners (`50px`).

### C. Central Visual Graphic (Dashboard Preview / Graphic)
- **Description:** A stylized, futuristic semi-circular curved horizon/ring graphic representing a glowing planet or futuristic dashboard UI element.
- **Image/Graphic Details:**
  - **Type:** Abstract glowing orbital arc / crescent ring.
  - **Characteristics:** Backgroundless/transparent surrounding elements, prominent fiery orange gradient stroke along the top curve blending into metallic dark greys (`#2a2a2a`) at the bottom edges with soft ambient bloom/glow effects underneath.
  - **Placement:** Centered horizontally directly beneath the hero CTA buttons, overlapping the bottom viewport edge.
- **Embedded Dashboard UI Card (Overlay):**
  - **Container:** Rounded dark glassmorphism card (`background: rgba(18, 19, 26, 0.9)`, border stroke: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, `border-radius: 12px`).
  - **Top Bar (Mock Window Header):** 
    - Left: "REDSUN" logo + orange dot.
    - Center/Navigation Icons: `Analytics`, `Products`, `Customers`, `Campaigns`, `Settings` (each accompanied by a tiny minimalist line icon).
    - Right: Notification bell icon and user avatar circle.

---

## 4. Interaction & States
- **Hover States:** 
  - Navigation links transition smoothly from muted gray (`#9ca3af`) to pure white (`#ffffff`).
  - Buttons feature slight brightness enhancement or shadow elevation on hover.
- **Responsiveness:** Flexbox/Grid-based layout designed to scale fluidly from mobile to ultra-wide desktop displays without fixed bounding frames.
