# Design Specification: Modern Furniture Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

### Color Palette
*   **Primary / Brand Orange:** `#F26522` (Used for CTA buttons, sale tags, and highlights)
*   **Dark Neutral / Charcoal:** `#1F2421` (Used for primary text and dark chair elements)
*   **Background (Body):** `#F8F9FA` (Clean, light off-white)
*   **Card / Component Background:** `#FFFFFF`
*   **Muted Text / Gray:** `#6C757D` (Used for secondary labels and subtitles)
*   **Border Color:** `#E9ECEF`

### Typography
*   **Font Family:** Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
*   **Headings:** Bold, Charcoal (`#1F2421`)
*   **Body Text:** Regular / Medium, Muted Gray or Charcoal

---

## 2. Layout Structure (Top to Bottom)

### A. Navigation Bar (Header)
*   **Layout:** Flexbox, space-between, full-width with horizontal padding (`24px` on mobile, `64px` on desktop).
*   **Left:** Brand Logo (Bold minimalist typography or graphic).
*   **Center:** Navigation Links (e.g., *Home, Living Room, Chairs, Decor, Contact*). Hidden on smaller mobile screens, visible on desktop.
*   **Right:** Icons (Search, Cart with item badge, User Profile).

### B. Hero Section
*   **Layout:** Two-column grid (Desktop) / Stacked (Mobile).
*   **Background:** Split or clean white card with subtle backdrop styling.
*   **Left Column (Featured Dark Chair):**
    *   Large high-contrast product image of the dark modern ergonomic chair.
    *   Floating price tag or highlight badge in orange (`#F26522`).
*   **Right Column (Lifestyle Image & Text):**
    *   Warm orange-toned interior background image featuring an orange armchair, plants, and wall decor.
    *   **Heading:** Bold lifestyle marketing copy (e.g., *"Elevate Your Living Space"*).
    *   **CTA Button:** Solid Orange (`#F26522`), rounded corners (`8px`), white text (*"Shop Collection"*).

### C. Category & Filter Section
*   **Layout:** Horizontal scrolling list or flex row of filter tabs.
*   **Items:** *"All", "Armchairs", "Sofas", "Decor", "Lighting"*.
*   **Active State:** Underlined or filled pill in Brand Orange (`#F26522`).

### D. Product Grid (Best Sellers / Featured Items)
*   **Layout:** CSS Grid (`repeat(auto-fit, minmax(180px, 1fr))`).
*   **Card Design:**
    *   White background, subtle box-shadow on hover.
    *   Product image thumbnail centered.
    *   Product title (Medium weight, small text).
    *   Price (Bold, charcoal with discount percentage in red/orange if applicable).
*   **Products Displayed in Grid:**
    1.  Dark Minimalist Vase / Decor
    2.  Orange Accent Chair ($249)
    3.  Modern Lounge Chair ($480)
    4.  Side Table / Accessory (with Sale badge)

### E. Promotional Banner / Secondary Grid
*   **Layout:** Two-column split featuring curated lifestyle imagery and close-up product angles (matching the bottom reference thumbnails).
