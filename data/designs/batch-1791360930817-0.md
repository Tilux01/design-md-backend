# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Architectural Dark Mode with High-End Minimalist Typography and Glassmorphism Overlays.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Spacious and breathable. Employs large viewport typography balanced against an immersive full-screen architectural photography backdrop.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered depth through subtle backdrop blurs (`blur(12px)`), dark translucent containers (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`), fine hairline borders, and glowing interactive coordinate points.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill navigation items, interactive blueprint coordinate hotspots, frosted glass panels, ambient architectural depth.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `8px 20px`, font-size `13px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, backdrop-filter `blur(12px)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Arrow (→) -> Use `&rarr;` or inline SVG chevron.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-white inline-block"></span>`.
