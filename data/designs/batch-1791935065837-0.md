# Design Specification: Game of Thrones Cast Explorer UI

Full-screen desktop UI specification cloned from the provided media, excluding external presentation margins/bezels.

---

## 1. Color Palette

*   **Background Base**: `#4A585F` (Cool slate gray)
*   **Overlay Tint**: `rgba(28, 38, 43, 0.45)` (Frosted translucent dark teal-gray)
*   **Accent/Text Pure**: `#FFFFFF` (100% white)
*   **Muted Text/Lines**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`
*   **Borders & Dividers**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)` (1px solid)

---

## 2. Typography

*   **Primary Display / Serif (Titles, Headings, Logo)**:
    *   *Font*: **Cinzel** or **Trajan Pro**
    *   *Fallbacks*: `Cormorant Garamond`, `Georgia`, `serif`
    *   *Usage*: "GOT" logo, "EMILIA CLARKE", "Cast", pagination counters.
*   **Character Role (Italic Serif)**:
    *   *Font*: **Cormorant Garamond Italic**
    *   *Fallbacks*: `Baskerville Italic`, `Times New Roman`, `serif`
    *   *Usage*: "Daenerys Targaryen"
*   **UI Controls & Meta (Sans-Serif)**:
    *   *Font*: **Montserrat** or **Inter**
    *   *Fallbacks*: `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`
    *   *Usage*: "Select Season", language switchers, track numbers.

---

## 3. Image Assets

### Background Hero Image
*   **Description**: Close-up portrait of Emilia Clarke with wavy, highlighted brunette hair, looking directly at the camera with neutral lighting and a cool blue-gray background. Non-transparent (full background included).
*   **Placement**: Fullscreen absolute background (`width: 100vw; height: 100vh; object-fit: cover; object-position: center 20%`).
*   **Rotation**: `0deg`.

## 4. Layout & Components

### 4.1 Inset Frame
*   A 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`) inset `24px` from all viewport edges, containing the entire UI structure.

### 4.2 Header Bar
*   **Height**: `56px`; aligned with top inset border.
*   **Left**:
    *   Serif text: "GOT" (20px, bold).
    *   Language switch: `•• En ••` (11px, muted white).
*   **Center**:
    *   Title: "Cast" flanked by thin horizontal lines with an ornate diamond-shaped heraldic crest pinned at top center.
*   **Right**:
    *   Search icon (magnifying glass) with decorative dots `•• Q ••`.
    *   Audio visualizer: 4 vertical animated EQ bars (`#FFFFFF`).

### 4.3 Sub-Header & Hero Content
*   **Dropdown (Top Left)**: "Select Season ⌵" (12px, sans-serif, muted).
*   **Character Display (Left-aligned, vertical center)**:
    *   Sub-label: *Daenerys Targaryen* (16px, italic serif).
    *   Main name: **EMILIA CLARKE** (44px, all-caps, tracked serif, margin-top: `4px`).
*   **Index Counter (Right-aligned, above carousel line)**:
    *   Text: `01 / 64` (12px, bold serif).

### 4.4 Thumbnails Carousel
*   **Container**: Positioned along bottom third; horizontal flex row with backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
*   **Divider**: 1px horizontal line across the screen separating hero area from the carousel.
*   **Navigation**:
    *   Left arrow (`‹`) and right arrow (`›`) vertically centered beside carousel cards.
*   **Cards**: 7 visible cards, width: `~120px`, height: `~160px`, gap: `16px`. Active card has a `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.7)` stroke.

### 4.5 Footer Bar
*   **Height**: `48px`; aligned to bottom inset border.
*   **Left**: Speaker icon with a small dotted level indicator.
*   **Center**: Pagination bar formatted as `••• 01 ────` (tracking playback or cast index).
*   **Right**: Audio scrub bar and the white vector **HBO** logo.
