# 404 Error Page Design Specification

## 1. Overview & Layout
- **Fullscreen Display:** The design spans the full width and height of the viewport.
- **Background Color:** Deep solid black (`#000000`).
- **Layout Type:** Asymmetric split composition. 
  - **Left Side:** High-contrast monochromatic photographic subject.
  - **Right Side / Center:** Floating UI elements, typography, and scattered graphical elements.

---

## 2. Typography & Font Families
- **Logo ("B&Y"):** 
  - Style: Serif, uppercase, tracked wide.
  - Subtitle: "YOUR • GOAL • TRAINER" in a tiny sans-serif uppercase font.
- **Main Error Heading ("404"):**
  - Font: Ultra-bold sans-serif.
  - Color: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle layered shadow/depth effect.
- **Button ("BACK HOME"):**
  - Font: Clean, uppercase sans-serif.
  - Styling: Outlined box with thin white borders, crisp tracking.
- **Vertical Subtext ("PAGE NOT FOUND"):**
  - Orientation: Rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise (vertical text aligned vertically).
  - Font: Thin, minimalist uppercase sans-serif.
- **Navigation/Auxiliary Labels ("BACK", "HOME"):**
  - Tiny lowercase/uppercase sans-serif flanking horizontal lines.

---

## 3. Color Palette
- **Primary Background:** `#000000` (Pure Black)
- **Primary Text & UI:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Accent Elements (Rose Petals):** Varied shades of vibrant magenta, crimson, and deep ruby red (`#D7385A`, `#9A1F36`, `#FF5E7E`) with soft gradient lighting.
- **Monotone Imagery:** Grayscale / black-and-white cinematic contrast for the background portrait.

---

## 4. UI Components & Detailed Elements

### A. Header Section
- **Top Left:**
  - Hamburger menu icon (three horizontal lines).
- **Top Center:**
  - Brand Logo: "B&Y" with the tagline below it.
- **Top Right:**
  - Search icon (magnifying glass).
  - User profile icon.
  - Shopping bag/cart icon.

### B. Center / Hero Section
- **Portrait Subject (Left-Center):**
  - A dramatic, cinematic black-and-white photograph of a veiled figure wearing an ornate, dark, thorny crown.
  - The figure holds a dark flower (peony/rose) near their face in veiled, gloved hands.
- **Floating Numbers ("404"):**
  - Large, layered white typography positioned centrally over the interface.
  - Overlaps the right edge of the photographic portrait.
- **Call to Action ("BACK HOME"):**
  - Positioned above the "404" text within a clean rectangular outline box.
- **Vertical Label:**
  - "PAGE NOT FOUND" written vertically along the right edge of the middle "0" in "404".
- **Scattered Rose Petals:**
  - Dozens of semi-transparent, dynamic red rose petals floating across the upper-right and central portion of the composition, creating depth and motion.

### C. Footer / Edge Navigation
- **Far Left (Bottom):**
  - Home icon.
  - Sliders/Equalizer settings icon.
- **Bottom Center:**
  - Three tiny pagination dots (active dot is white, inactive are dimmed).
- **Far Right (Bottom):**
  - Social media icons (Twitter/X, Facebook, Instagram).
- **Side Guides:**
  - Horizontal line with "BACK" label on the mid-left edge.
  - Horizontal line with "HOME" label on the mid-right edge.

---

## 5. Visual Effects & Atmosphere
- **Depth of Field:** High contrast lighting with a spotlight effect centered on the floral elements and petals.
- **Texture:** Matte black backgrounds paired with crisp vector strokes and photographic grain.
