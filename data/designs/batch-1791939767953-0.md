# Design Specification: Neumorphic Music Player

## 1. Global Theme & Style
*   **Design Paradigm:** Neumorphism (Soft UI). This relies on light and dark shadows to create depth, making elements appear to be extruded from or recessed into the background.
*   **Background Color:** `#E0E9F1` (A very soft, desaturated light blue).
*   **Shadow Logic:**
    *   **Light Shadow (Top-Left):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`
    *   **Dark Shadow (Bottom-Right):** `rgba(163, 177, 198, 0.5)`
    *   **Standard Elevation:** `box-shadow: 6px 6px 12px rgba(163, 177, 198, 0.5), -6px -6px 12px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8);`
    *   **Pressed/Recessed State:** `box-shadow: inset 4px 4px 8px rgba(163, 177, 198, 0.5), inset -4px -4px 8px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8);`

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Montserrat** (Bold for headings, Regular/Medium for body).
*   **Secondary Font:** **Poppins** or **Inter**.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   **Primary (Titles):** `#1A253F` (Deep navy blue).
    *   **Secondary (Subtitles/Labels):** `#7E8A9A` (Muted blue-gray).
    *   **Accent:** `#FF4D4D` (Soft red, used for the heart icon and progress indicators).

## 3. Screen 1: The Player View

### Layout
*   **Container:** Full-screen, no margins, background color `#E0E9F1`.
*   **Header Section:**
    *   **Title:** "DANCE MONKEY" (All caps, Bold, ~24px). Positioned top-left with significant padding.
    *   **Subtitle:** "Tones and I" (Regular, ~14px, muted color). Placed directly below the title.

### Components
1.  **Vertical Control Bar (Left Side):**
    *   **Shape:** A tall, slim pill-shaped container with a Neumorphic "extruded" effect.
    *   **Icons:** Five icons arranged vertically:
        *   Shuffle/Repeat (Top)
        *   Previous Track
        *   Play/Pause (Center)
        *   Next Track
        *   Heart Icon (Bottom - solid red color `#FF4D4D`).
    *   **Placement:** Floating on the left side, vertically centered.

2.  **The Record Player (Right Side):**
    *   **Shape:** A massive circular element partially cropped off the right edge of the screen.
    *   **Structure:**
        *   **Outer Ring:** A thick Neumorphic ring with a thin red progress arc along the top-left edge.
        *   **Inner Disc:** A deep navy/black circular area representing the vinyl.
        *   **Center Label:** A small circular image of the artist (Tones and I) in the very center.
    *   **Visual Detail:** The disc should have a subtle "recessed" shadow within the outer ring.

3.  **Bottom Navigation/Action:**
    *   **Text:** "See you later" (Small, centered at the bottom).
    *   **Button:** A horizontal pill-shaped button at the very bottom center, slightly wider than the text above it.

## 4. Screen 2: Discovery View

### Layout
*   **Container:** Full-screen, background color `#E0E9F1`.
*   **Sections:** Divided into three distinct vertical sections: "Recently played", "Popular Playlists", and "Artists".

### Components
1.  **Section Headers:**
    *   Left side: Section title (e.g., "Recently played") in bold navy.
    *   Right side: "See all" text in small, muted gray.

2.  **Recently Played Row:**
    *   Three circular buttons, each containing a profile or album image.
    *   **Image 1:** A close-up portrait of a woman with warm lighting.
    *   **Image 2:** A dark, moody silhouette or deep red abstract image.
    *   **Image 3:** A black and white portrait of a man.
    *   **Note:** Images should be perfectly circular and slightly recessed into the buttons.

3.  **Popular Playlists Cards:**
    *   Two vertical rectangular cards with heavily rounded corners.
    *   **Card 1 (Dance Pop):** 
        *   **Image:** A high-contrast, backgroundless (transparent) image of a dancer in a white outfit, mid-motion.
        *   **Text:** "Dance Pop" (Bold) and "120 Songs" (Small).
    *   **Card 2 (Club Music):**
        *   **Image:** A backgroundless, dark-toned image of a DJ or person with headphones, stylized with blue/purple highlights.
        *   **Text:** "Club Music" (Bold) and "85 Songs" (Small).

4.  **Artists List:**
    *   Two horizontal pill-shaped list items.
    *   **Content:** A circular avatar on the far left, followed by two lines of text (Artist Name and Genre/Sub-info).
    *   **Image 1:** A man with a blue cap.
    *   **Image 2:** A woman with blonde hair.
    *   **Styling:** These pills should have the standard "extruded" Neumorphic shadow.

## 5. Image Asset Descriptions

For the developer/designer, use the following descriptions for placeholders:

*   **Artist Center Disc (Screen 1):** A circular crop of the "Dance Monkey" album art featuring a stylized, colorful illustration of the artist.
*   **Dancer Image (Screen 2 - Card 1):** A high-resolution, backgroundless PNG of a female contemporary dancer in a dynamic pose, wearing light-colored clothing.

*   **Avatars:** Small, high-quality headshots with transparent backgrounds or clean, solid-color backgrounds to fit within the circular Neumorphic recesses.

## 6. Spacing and Geometry
*   **Border Radius:** 
    *   Cards/Pills: `30px` to `40px` for a very soft look.
    *   Main Record: `50%` (Circle).
*   **Padding:** Consistent `24px` padding from the screen edges for all text elements.
*   **Gaps:** `20px` vertical gap between sections on Screen 2.
