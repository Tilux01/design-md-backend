# Design Specification: Interactive 3D Floor Plan Dashboard

## 1. Executive Summary & Canvas Spec
- **Target Screen Mode:** Fullscreen, edge-to-edge application (100vw, 100vh), zero outer margin or browser frame.
- **Visual Style:** Dark-mode luxury architectural dashboard combining real-time 3D web rendering with frosted glassmorphism UI overlays.
- **Theme:** Deep Midnight Navy Background (`#111728` to `#161F33`).

---

## 2. Color Palette & Aesthetics

### Backgrounds & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#111728` (Solid dark navy)
- **Glass Panel Surface:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`
- **Glass Panel Border:** `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`
- **Active UI Item Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Inactive UI Tab Background:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Callout Tag Background:** `rgba(20, 26, 38, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`

### Typography & Accent Colors
- **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Text Muted / Secondary:** `#8A96A8`
- **Text Active/Dark (on White Buttons):** `#111728`
- **Accent Light / Daylight Icon:** `#E2E8F0`
- **Leader Lines:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` (Dashed)

---

## 3. Typography & Font Stack

### Font Family
- **Primary:** `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, or `SF Pro Display`
- **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`

### Type Scale
- **Brand Logo Text:** `22px` | Semi-Bold (`600`) | Tracking: `-0.02em`
- **Section Titles ("Utilities"):** `14px` | Regular (`400`)
- **Nav Tabs / Buttons:** `12px` | Medium (`500`)
- **Data Callout Titles:** `10px` | Semi-Bold (`600`) | Uppercase | Tracking: `0.08em`
- **Data Callout Values:** `13px` | Regular (`400`)
- **Counter Labels:** `12px` | Semi-Bold (`600`)

## 6. 3D Scene Asset & Architectural Details

### Viewport Configuration
- **Camera Perspective:** Isometric/Orthographic angle (35° tilt down, 45° horizontal rotation).
- **Cutaway Style:** Clean sliced outer perimeter walls showing inner layout without roof blocking light.

### Room Layout & Visual Descriptions (3D Assets)
1. **Master Bedroom (Bottom Left):**
   - Dark wooden accent headboard wall.
   - King-size bed with light-gray duvet, dark pillows, and two warm lit nightstand lamps.
2. **Main Terrace (Bottom Center/Front):**
   - Natural wood/concrete decking.
   - Two modern dark gray outdoor armchair loungers facing a small white round coffee table with green potted plants nearby.
3. **Living Room (Center):**
   - L-shaped off-white sectional fabric couch with decorative yellow and orange accent cushions.
   - Dark wood rectangular coffee table resting on a dark charcoal area rug.
4. **Dining Area (Middle Center-Back):**
   - Dark wooden dining table with 6 dark chairs.
   - Large framed abstract modern art hanging on the back wall.
   - Warm hanging pendant light above the table.
5. **Kitchen (Top Left):**
   - Modern white upper cabinets, dark base cabinets, stainless steel stove, and tall black side-by-side refrigerator.
6. **Secondary Bedroom & Bathroom (Right):**
   - Glass shower partition, modern white floating sink, wall-mounted toilet.
   - Compact second bedroom with light wood bed frame.

### Lighting & Atmosphere
- **Ambient Lighting:** Soft neutral interior fill light with localized warm points (2700K color temp) coming from lamps and kitchen spots.
- **Directional Sunlight:** Cool morning daylight (5000K color temp) cast from the left side, creating realistic long shadows across the floorboards.
