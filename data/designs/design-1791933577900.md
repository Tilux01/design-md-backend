# Design Specification: Media Streaming Dashboard

## 1. Overall Layout & Architecture
* **Viewport**: Full screen (`100vw` × `100vh`), overflow hidden.
* **Theme**: Dark mode with an immersive background hero image and gradient overlays for contrast.
* **Layout Structure**: 
  * Fixed top navigation bar.
  * Central-left aligned hero metadata and call-to-action (CTA) controls.
  * Bottom-anchored horizontal media carousel ("My List").

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Background Overlays**:
  * Bottom vignette: `linear-gradient(to top, #000000 0%, rgba(0,0,0,0.85) 25%, transparent 60%)`
  * Left vignette: `linear-gradient(to right, rgba(0,0,0,0.7) 0%, rgba(0,0,0,0.3) 40%, transparent 80%)`
* **Text**:
  * Primary: `#FFFFFF`
  * Secondary / Muted: `#A0A5A8`
  * Success / Match: `#00E676` (bright green)
* **Accents & Controls**:
  * Primary CTA Button: `#7A1AF0` to `#8F00FF` (vibrant purple)
  * Secondary CTA Button: Transparent background, `#FFFFFF` border (1.5px solid)

---

## 3. Typography
* **Primary Font Family**: `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, Arial, sans-serif.
* **Styles & Scales**:
  * **Navigation Links**: 12px, font-weight: 600, letter-spacing: 1.2px, uppercase.
  * **Hero Title**: 48px – 56px, font-weight: 800, line-height: 1.05, uppercase.
  * **Metadata**: 13px, font-weight: 500, letter-spacing: 0.5px.
  * **Section Heading ("MY LIST")**: 16px, font-weight: 700, letter-spacing: 1px, uppercase.
  * **Button Labels**: 13px, font-weight: 700, letter-spacing: 0.8px, uppercase.

---

## 4. Components & Layout Specifications

### Top Navigation Bar
* **Container**: Full width, `display: flex`, `justify-content: space-between`, `align-items: center`, padding: `32px 48px`.
* **Left Menu**:
  * Flex row, gap: `32px`.
  * Items: `DASHBOARD`, `MOVIES`, `SERIES`, `KIDS`.
  * Style: White text, subtle opacity transition on hover (`opacity: 0.7` to `1.0`).
* **Right Controls**:
  * Flex row, gap: `20px`, `align-items: center`.
  * Search: Minimal circular magnifying glass icon (stroke: `#FFFFFF`, 18px).
  * Profile Avatar: 32px diameter circle, 1px border (`rgba(255,255,255,0.4)`).

### Hero Section
* **Placement**: Positioned absolutely or flex-aligned to the upper-left, padding-left: `48px`, top offset: `18%`.
* **Title**:
  * Text: `RAYA AND THE`<br>`LAST DRAGON`
  * Max-width: 480px.
* **Metadata Row**:
  * Flex row, gap: `12px`, `align-items: center`, margin-top: `16px`.
  * Content: `A Disney Original Film` (Muted), `98% Match` (Accent Green), `2021` (Muted).
* **CTA Buttons**:
  * Flex row, gap: `16px`, `align-items: center`, margin-top: `24px`.
  * **Play Button**: Pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`), padding: `10px 36px`, background: `#8F00FF`, label: `PLAY` (`#FFFFFF`).
  * **Add Button**: Circular (`border-radius: 50%`), width/height: `42px`, border: `1.5px solid #FFFFFF`, center-aligned `+` icon (`#FFFFFF`, 20px).

### "My List" Carousel Section
* **Placement**: Bottom-aligned, anchored `32px` from screen bottom, padding-left: `48px`.
* **Header**: `MY LIST`, white, bold, margin-bottom: `16px`.
* **Card Strip**:
  * Horizontal row, `display: flex`, gap: `16px`, overflow-x: hidden.
  * Card dimensions: Width: `140px`, aspect ratio: `2:3`, `border-radius: 6px`, object-fit: `cover`.
  * Elevation: Subtle drop shadow (`0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`).

## 5. Visual Assets & Imagery Descriptions

### Hero Background Image
* **Description**: Raya from *Raya and the Last Dragon* standing in a rainy, sun-dappled jungle. She is centered to slightly right, facing forward with a focused expression, wearing a pointed woven conical straw hat and a wrapped red cape over a brown leather tunic. Rain streaks and warm sunlight filter through the dark green foliage.

* **Placement & Size**: `position: absolute; inset: 0; width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover; object-position: center 20%;`.
* **Rotation**: None (`0deg`).
