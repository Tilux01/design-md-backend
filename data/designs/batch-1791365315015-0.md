# Design Specification: SSENSE E-Commerce Concept

## 1. Global Styles & Reset
- **Color Palette:**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
  - Primary Text / Headers: `#000000` (Pure Black)
  - Secondary / Navigation / Inactive Text: `#666666` (Medium Gray)
  - Light Text / Outlines: `#CCCCCC` to `#E5E5E5` (Light Gray)
- **Typography:**
  - Font Family: Sans-serif geometric (e.g., *Helvetica Neue*, *Arial*, or *Inter*)
  - Headings/Brand: Bold, uppercase, tracked-out/condensed styling.
- **Layout & Sizing:**
  - Full-screen viewport (`100vw` width, `100vh` height).
  - Centered hero product focus.
  - Generous whitespace.

---

## 2. Header & Navigation

### Top Bar (Fixed / Absolute Positioned)
- **Alignment:** Horizontal row, evenly spaced.
- **Left Links:** 
  - `MEN`, `WOMAN` (Font size: ~12px, uppercase, medium weight).
- **Center Logo:**
  - `SSENSE` (Font size: ~20px, bold, highly tracked/letter-spaced).
- **Right Links & Icons:**
  - `WISHLIST`, `BLOG`
  - Icons: Search magnifying glass, Shopping bag (with item count indicator dot), Language selector (`EN`).

---

## 3. Left Sidebar (Category Navigation)

- **Search Bar:**
  - Icon: Magnifying glass
  - Placeholder: `RECHERCHER` (Uppercase, light gray)
- **Navigation Menu List:**
  - `ACCESSORIES` (Inactive, light gray)
  - `BAG` (Inactive, light gray)
  - `CLOTHING` (Inactive, light gray)
  - `SHOES` (Active, bold black)
    - Sub-item: `Sneakers` (Smaller text below SHOES, medium gray, underlined or active state)

---

## 4. Main Content Area (Hero Section)

### Product Carousel / 3D Display
- **Layout:** Three sneakers displayed horizontally in a 3D perspective carousel format.
- **Center (Active Product):**
  - High-definition, detailed sneaker (**Gucci Crystals Flashtrek Sneakers**).
  - Multi-colored (red, gold, brown) with jeweled/crystal encrusted straps.
  - Angled vertically floating.
- **Left & Right (Inactive Products):**
  - Blurred / out-of-focus preview states of adjacent sneakers in the carousel.

---

## 5. Product Details & Call to Action

- **Brand Name:**
  - `GUCCI` (Centered below the sneaker, large bold uppercase display font, e.g., ~28px-32px).
- **Product Title & Price:**
  - `Crystals Flashtrek Sneakers | 1590$` (Centered below brand name, regular weight, ~13px, gray).
- **Button:**
  - Text: `SEE MORE`
  - Style: Rounded pill-shaped button, light gray background, subtle drop shadow, dark gray uppercase text.

---

## 6. Right Utility Controls

- **View Switcher:**
  - Grid view / List view toggle icons located on the middle-right edge of the viewport.
