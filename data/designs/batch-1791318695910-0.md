# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Luxury Minimalist with Dark Mode Industrialism (High-end Swiss watchmaker aesthetic).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Extremely balanced, generous negative space surrounding centered product showcases, high contrast hero sections with asymmetric split-color backgrounds (Deep Obsidian / Military Olive).
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat matte obsidian layers paired with olive-drab sectional split panels; minimalist crisp borders, subtle hover lighting shifts, and clean typography layouts.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase editorial typography, minimalist outline media triggers, asymmetric color panels, dark obsidian backgrounds contrasted with olive-drab sections.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(255,255,255,0.05)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Arrow / Navigation (→) -> Use `&rarr;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-surface-olive inline-block"></span>`.
