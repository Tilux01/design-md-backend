# Design Specification: Coffee Culture Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Layout Structure
* **Mode:** Dark-mode, split-screen hero layout.
* **Dimensions:** Fullscreen (100vw, 100vh), overflow hidden.
* **Canvas Split:** 
  * Left side (50% width): Base color `#1E2227`.
  * Right side (50% width): Base color `#262A30`.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Background Left:** `#1E2227`
* **Background Right:** `#262A30`
* **Card Overlays:** `rgba(25, 28, 33, 0.75)` with subtle backdrop blur.
* **Primary Accent:** `#FF6B1A` (Vibrant warm orange).
* **Text Primary:** `#F5EFEB` (Off-white / Warm cream).
* **Text Secondary:** `#7D848C` (Muted gray).
* **Text Watermark:** `#2D323A` (Low-contrast background text).

---

## 3. Typography
* **Primary Display Font:** *Poppins* (Weights: 700, 600)
  * *Fallbacks:* *Plus Jakarta Sans*, *Montserrat*, sans-serif.
* **Body Font:** *Inter* (Weights: 400, 500)
  * *Fallbacks:* *system-ui*, -apple-system, sans-serif.
* **Hierarchy:**
  * **Hero Title ("Coffee / Culture"):** ~64px, weight 700, line-height 1.1, color `#F5EFEB`.
  * **Background Watermark ("Coffee Culture"):** ~120px, weight 700, opacity 0.4, color `#2D323A`.
  * **Card Headings:** ~18px, weight 600, color `#FFFFFF`.
  * **Card Body Text:** ~12px, weight 400, line-height 1.5, color `#7D848C`.

---

## 4. Header Navigation
* **Top Left (Padding: 40px):**
  * Minimalist coffee cup glyph with two rising steam lines in `#FF6B1A`. Size: 28px × 28px.
* **Top Right (Padding: 40px):**
  * Hamburger menu icon (three equal-width horizontal white lines). Size: 24px wide.

---

## 5. Visual Assets & Centerpiece

### Central Product Image
* **Description:** Matte black disposable coffee tumbler with a tight-fitting black lid, photographed straight-on at eye-level on a flat surface with soft bottom contact shadows.
* **Format:** Backgroundless PNG (transparent).
* **Placement:** Dead center of the viewport (horizontal center aligns with the split boundary; vertical base aligns along the bottom quarter).
* **Layering:** Sits above the background text/accent ring, but slightly below foreground text boxes.

### Graphical Elements
1. **Orange Ring Accent:**
   * **Shape:** Thick 270° circular open arc.
   * **Color:** `#FF6B1A`
   * **Placement:** Centered behind the upper-right shoulder and lid rim of the black cup.
2. **Background Watermark:**
   * Two stacked words ("Coffee" / "Culture") positioned on the right half behind the cup.

---

## 6. Content Modules & Cards

### Main Headline (Left)
* **Text:** "Coffee\nCulture"
* **Position:** Left column, vertically positioned above the lower text box (top ~30%, left ~12%).

### Bottom Left Overlay Card
* **Container:** 360px wide, dark translucent surface (`rgba(25, 28, 33, 0.75)`), 20px padding.
* **Position:** Fixed at bottom left (`bottom: 80px; left: 60px;`).
* **Title:** "Black Lifestyle Lovers"
* **Body:** Excerpt text about Latin literature origins (~3 lines).
* **Slider Indicator:** Positioned directly below the card:
  * Bold orange horizontal bar (`#FF6B1A`), followed by a thin white line terminating in a small white circular dot.

### Bottom Right Overlay Card
* **Container:** 340px wide, identical dark translucent treatment.
* **Position:** Fixed at bottom right (`bottom: 80px; right: 60px;`).
* **Content:**
  * Left sub-block: Title "The Design" with short descriptive body text.
  * Right sub-block: Circular play button (thin white/gray border, `#FF6B1A` solid triangle play icon).
* **Pagination Bar:** Thin horizontal white rule anchored near the bottom-right corner.

### Edge Navigation
* **Right Arrow (`→`):** Thin white arrow centered vertically against the right viewport edge (`right: 40px`).
