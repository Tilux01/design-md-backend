# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist, Dark Mode Glassmorphism, Developer Tool SaaS.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio in the primary canvas area, contrasted with a dense, highly structured inspection control panel (Motion Lab) featuring compact padding and tight control groupings.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered surface architecture featuring a warm cream/off-white canvas base (`#F7F6F2`), flat neutral container cards with subtle hairline borders, and a floating Dark Mode control panel (`#111215`) with subtle drop-shadow elevation and backdrop blur.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Floating interactive inspector panels, live-state toggle matrices, high-contrast monochrome UI surfaces with electric blue live status indicators.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `4px 10px`, font-size: `11px`, font-weight: `600`, tracking: `0.08em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `#1B1D22` (active) / `#FFFFFF` (inactive toggle).
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (`—`) -> Use `&mdash;` in markup or CSS `content: "\2014"`.
  - Status Dot (`●`) -> Use `<span className="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-blue-500 inline-block"></span>`.
