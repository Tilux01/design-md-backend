# Design Specification: Cyberpunk Games Dashboard

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Background:** Solid deep matte black (`#050505`). The UI should be edge-to-edge (full screen) with no external margins or frames.
*   **Primary Accent Color:** Neon Lime Green (`#B6FF00`).
*   **Secondary Accent Color:** Electric Cobalt Blue (`#2B59FF`).
*   **Surface Colors:** 
    *   Dark Grey Containers: `#121214` with a subtle 1px border of `#1A1A1C`.
    *   Glassmorphism: Use `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)` on navigation elements.
*   **Border Radius:** Highly rounded corners throughout. Standard radius: `24px`. Large container radius: `40px`.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Orbitron` (Google Fonts) for headings and logo to capture the futuristic feel.
*   **Secondary Font:** `Inter` or `Rajdhani` for body text and UI elements.
*   **Fallbacks:** `System-UI`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   Headings: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
    *   Body: Light Grey (`#A0A0A0`).
    *   Accents: Neon Lime Green (`#B6FF00`).

---

## 3. Layout Structure

The layout is divided into two primary sections: the **Main Hero Area (70% width)** and the **Cart Sidebar (30% width)**.

### A. Top Navigation Bar
*   **Logo (Top Left):** Text "CYBERPUNK GAMES" in all caps. "CYBERPUNK" is white, "GAMES" is Neon Green. Font: Orbitron, Bold.
*   **Search Bar:** A pill-shaped button/input. Background is Neon Green. Contains a magnifying glass icon and the word "Search".
*   **Nav Links:** "Community", "Library", "News". Small font size (12px), uppercase, light grey color.
*   **User Profile (Top Right):** A circular avatar icon next to a currency/points display (e.g., "150000").

### B. Main Hero Section (Left)
*   **Hero Character Image:**
    *   **Description:** A high-detail digital painting of a cyberpunk ninja. The character has spiky white/silver hair, a high-tech metallic face mask/respirator covering the nose and mouth, and a dark tactical jacket with a red glowing inner collar.
    *   **Placement:** Positioned on the far left, vertically centered. The character is cropped from the waist up.
    *   **Background Status:** Backgroundless (Transparent PNG).
    *   **Rotation/Orientation:** Facing slightly to the right, leaning forward.
*   **Main Title:** "ULTIMATE **NINJA** WARS". "NINJA" is highlighted in Neon Green. Font: Orbitron, Extra Bold, large scale.
*   **Description Text:** A block of justified light grey text describing the game.
*   **Preview Thumbnails:** Three perfect circles arranged horizontally below the description. Each contains a different cinematic gameplay screenshot.
*   **Genre Badge (Bottom):** A rounded dark rectangle containing a stylized "S" logo and the text "Game Genre".

### C. Cart Sidebar (Right)
*   **Container:** A tall, dark rounded rectangle (`#0D0D0F`).
*   **Header:** The word "CART" in white, bold, uppercase.
*   **Cart Items:** Three distinct cards with a unique "wavy/organic" top and bottom edge.
    1.  **Top Card (Neon Green):** Background is `#B6FF00`. Text is black. Contains game title "ULTIMATE NINJA WARS", a small icon, and a "remove" (x) button.
    2.  **Middle Card (Electric Blue):** Background is `#2B59FF`. Text is white. Contains "ATTACK ON THE TITANS - LIMITED EDITION".
    3.  **Bottom Card (Dark):** Background is `#1A1A1C`. Text is white. Contains "NARUTO CYBERPUNK".
*   **Footer Section:**
    *   **Total Price:** "Total Price: $1,200" in white.
    *   **Checkout Button:** A small, bright Neon Green square/circle button with a black arrow or "check" icon, located at the bottom right of the cart.

## 4. Visual Effects & Details
*   **Shadows:** Use soft, large outer glows (bloom) for the Neon Green elements to simulate light emission.
*   **Wavy Shapes:** The cart items are not standard rectangles; they use a "liquid" or "wave" SVG path for the top and bottom borders to create a stacked, organic look.
*   **Iconography:** All icons (Search, Cart, User, Genre) should be thin-line vector icons (Linear style).
*   **Image Overlap:** The Hero Character should slightly overlap the background elements to create a sense of depth (3D layering).
