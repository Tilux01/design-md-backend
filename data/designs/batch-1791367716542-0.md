# Design Specification: Matcha E-Commerce Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Reset
- **Base Font Family**: System sans-serif (Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif)
- **Background Color**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Text Color**: `#1A1A1A` (Near Black for high contrast)
- **Secondary Text Color**: `#666666` (Muted Gray for subtitles/metadata)
- **Accent Color**: `#556B2F` (Dark Olive Green, characteristic of matcha branding)
- **Layout Model**: Full-width viewport (`100vw`), minimum height `100vh`, modern CSS Grid/Flexbox.

---

## 2. Header & Navigation (`<header>`)
- **Layout**: Fixed or sticky full-width flex container.
- **Padding**: `20px 40px` (or responsive equivalent).
- **Left Section**: Brand logo/wordmark (minimalist, bold, lowercase or uppercase tracking).
- **Center Section**: Navigation links (e.g., *Shop*, *About*, *Journal*, *Contact*).
  - Font Size: `14px`
  - Text Transform: Uppercase or regular with letter-spacing.
  - Color: `#333333`
- **Right Section**: Utility icons (Search, Cart count indicator, User account).

---

## 3. Hero / Intro Section (`<section class="hero">`)
- **Layout**: Centered text block with ample negative space.
- **Top Elements**: A subtle carousel/indicator featuring miniature product previews or thumbnail images (horizontal row of 3 small circular/rounded cards).
- **Heading/Subtext**: 
  - Tagline or main hook statement in the center.
  - Font Size: `24px` to `32px` (Medium weight, elegant line height).
  - Max Width: `700px` (Centered horizontally).
- **Call-to-Action (CTA)**: 
  - A pill-shaped or rounded solid button (e.g., `#000000` background with `#FFFFFF` text).
  - Padding: `12px 28px`.
  - Border-radius: `30px`.

---

## 4. Product / Content Grid (`<section class="product-grid">`)
- **Layout**: 3-column CSS Grid with equal gap spacing (`24px` to `32px`).
- **Container Max-Width**: `1200px`, centered with auto margins.
- **Padding**: `40px 20px`.

### Card 1: Whisk & Accessories (`.card-1`)
- **Image**: High-resolution studio shot of matcha whisk (chasen) and bowl on a light surface. Aspect ratio: `4:5`. Border-radius: `8px`.
- **Content Below Image**:
  - Title: Left-aligned product name.
  - Price/Action: Right-aligned price tag.

### Card 2: Ceremonial Grade Matcha (`.card-2`)
- **Image**: Dark-toned mood shot of someone preparing matcha or minimalist packaging. Aspect ratio: `4:5`. Border-radius: `8px`.
- **Content Below Image**:
  - Title: Left-aligned product name.
  - Price/Action: Right-aligned price tag.

### Card 3: Ready-to-Drink / Bowl Set (`.card-3`)
- **Image**: Vibrant green matcha bowl held in hands. Aspect ratio: `4:5`. Border-radius: `8px`.
- **Content Below Image**:
  - Title & Variant options (e.g., sizes, grades).
  - Price: Clean typography, right-aligned.

---

## 5. Typography Specs Summary
- **H1 / Hero Title**: `32px`, Line-height: `1.3`, Weight: `400`
- **Card Titles**: `16px`, Line-height: `1.4`, Weight: `500`
- **Body / Nav**: `14px`, Line-height: `1.5`, Weight: `400`
- **Prices / Meta**: `14px`, Weight: `600`
