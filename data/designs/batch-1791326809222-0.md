# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Glassmorphism, Ambient Audio/Soundscape Interface ("Orbit" audio design system).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High visual breathing room with prominent focal content areas. Floating card modules layered against a rich obsidian background with deep violet/indigo atmospheric lighting.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-layered glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), frosted dark surfaces with semi-transparent borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and soft ambient radial glows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges with subtle border glows, ambient radial gradients, interactive audio frequency waveforms, frosted glass containers.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-violet-400 inline-block"></span>`.
