# Design System & Specification: ORBIT Luxury Space Travel

This document serves as the exact visual, structural, and technical design specification for recreating the full-screen ORBIT space travel landing page.

---

## 1. Canvas & Global Page Foundation

* **Viewport Strategy**: Full-screen layout (`width: 100vw; min-height: 100vh; overflow-x: hidden;`).
* **Background Color**: Ivory / Warm Off-White (`#F7F5F0`).
* **Frame / Margins**: The design fills the viewport without any simulated device frames, drop-shadow enclosures, or outer preview gradients.
* **Layout Container**:
  * Max-width: `1440px` centered, or dynamic full-width layout with horizontal padding: `clamp(24px, 4vw, 56px)`.
  * Grid System: 12-column baseline grid with `24px` gutter.

## 3. Typography Specification

### Primary Fonts & Fallback Hierarchy
1. **Display & Headings**:
   * **Preferred**: `Syne` (Weights: 700 Bold, 800 ExtraBold) or `Monument Extended` / `Clash Display`.
   * **Fallbacks**: `Arial Black`, `Impact`, `Helvetica Neue Condensed`, `sans-serif`.
   * **Styling**: Uppercase, tight tracking (`letter-spacing: -0.02em` to `-0.04em`), rigid vertical geometry.

2. **Body & Interface Text**:
   * **Preferred**: `Inter` or `PP Neue Montreal` (Weights: 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 600 SemiBold).
   * **Fallbacks**: `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `sans-serif`.
   * **Styling**: Line-height: `1.5` to `1.6`, clean grotesque legibility.

3. **Data / Coordinates / Timers**:
   * **Preferred**: `JetBrains Mono` or `Space Mono` (Weights: 400 Regular, 600 SemiBold).
   * **Fallbacks**: `SF Mono`, `Menlo`, `Courier New`, `monospace`.
   * **Styling**: Uppercase, tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

### 6.4. Hero Astronaut Visual Asset
* **Asset Specifications**:
  * **Format**: High-resolution PNG with strict alpha channel transparency (**Backgroundless**).
  * **Subject**: Full Apollo/Artemis Extravehicular Mobility Unit (EMU) space suit.
  * **Details**: Highly detailed fabric folds, gold/black reflective bubble visor reflecting subtle studio light, heavy chest-mounted Remote Control Module (RCM) with valves and switches, bulky Portable Life Support System (PLSS) backpack on the rear. Greyscale American flag shoulder patch visible on the left arm.
  * **Tone / Finish**: Monochrome/high-contrast greyscale with soft natural shadows. Zero blue spill or halo on cutout edges.
  * **Placement**:
    * Pinned absolutely to the right-central area of the hero container (`right: 12%`, `bottom: 0`).
    * **Layering**: `z-index: 2` (Layered explicitly **in front of** the giant "INFINITY" text, occluding the lower halves of "I", "T", and "Y").
    * **Crop**: Bottom of the astronaut terminates cleanly at the process section divider bar.

### 6.5. Scroll Indicator (Far Right)
* **Position**: Fixed/absolute along the far right margin.
* **Elements**:
  1. Micro image preview at top (`24px × 36px` greyscale space silhouette thumbnail).
  2. Vertical text: `SCROLL TO DISCOVER` (`writing-mode: vertical-rl; transform: rotate(180deg); font-size: 10px; font-weight: 600; letter-spacing: 0.12em; color: #63666A;`).
  3. Vertical hairline line running downward.

## 10. Responsive Behavior & Breakpoints

* **Desktop Wide (`≥ 1280px`)**: Full multi-column layout matches the screenshot exactly.
* **Tablet / Small Desktop (`1024px – 1279px`)**:
  * Reduce "INFINITY" font size to `11vw`.
  * Astronaut image scales proportionally, retaining its z-index overlap.
  * 3-column bottom layout shifts to 2 columns (Left mission + center visual side-by-side, feature list wraps below).
* **Mobile Viewport (`< 768px`)**:
  * Process bar switches from 4-column horizontal to a vertical stacked list.
  * Center Earth-Moon banner spans 100% width.
  * Polaroid and mission text stack vertically.
  * Nav bar collapses into hamburger menu; "Book Journey" button remains in header.
