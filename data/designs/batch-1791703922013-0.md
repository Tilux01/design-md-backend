# Design Specification: MakeLine™ "Future Ready" Agency Hero Section

This document defines the exact visual design, layout architecture, typography, color system, and asset specifications to build a 1:1 full-screen viewport clone of the **MakeLine™** agency hero section.

---

## 1. Visual Style & Aesthetic Philosophy

* **Art Direction**: High-fashion editorial minimalism combined with modern digital brutalism and motion-blur photography.
* **Canvas Constraints**: Full-screen desktop landing view (`100vw` × `100vh`, non-scrolling single viewport fold on standard 1440px–1920px screens).
* **Compositional Balance**: 
  * Heavy dark typography anchoring the upper third on a pale light gray background.
  * Cinematic motion-blurred crowd in the midground creating dynamism.
  * A tack-sharp, static female figure in the center foreground anchoring human connection.
  * Massive, semi-transparent/white typography anchoring the bottom edge.

## 4. Visual Imagery & Asset Specifications

### A. Central Photography Composition
The photographic element sits across the center and lower half of the screen.

1. **Layer 1: Motion-Blurred Crowd (Midground)**
   * **Subject**: A group of corporate/business pedestrians walking horizontally past the camera.
   * **Visual Effect**: Heavy horizontal shutter-drag / directional motion blur (simulating ~1/4s shutter exposure). Dark tailored clothing (blazers, coats) in deep charcoal, sage-tinted shadows, and muted olive-gray ambient light.
   * **Placement**: Spans 100% width, horizontally centered, fading smoothly into the pale `#E8EBEC` background at the top edge via a subtle vertical gradient mask.

2. **Layer 2: Female Subject (Foreground Cutout)**
   * **Status**: Clean backgroundless cutout (or perfectly focused depth-of-field isolation) positioned directly over the blurred crowd.
   * **Pose & Framing**: Medium bust/torso portrait of a young woman with a sharp brunette bob haircut, looking directly forward into the lens with a calm, confident, neutral expression.
   * **Attire**: Crisp, contemporary off-white/cream loose-fit linen button-down blouse with rolled cuffs and subtle front pocket details.
   * **Sharpness**: 100% crisp focus, zero motion blur, contrasting sharply against the streaking figures around her.
   * **Horizontal Position**: Centered horizontally (`left: 50%; transform: translateX(-50%);`), anchoring the lower-middle vertical section.

---

### B. Graphic Vector Overlays
1. **Celestial Orbit Vector Line**:
   * A delicate, thin (`1px` to `1.5px`) white semi-elliptical arc (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`) sweeping diagonally around the woman’s torso from the lower left behind her arm and looping towards the right shoulder.
2. **Sparkle / 4-Point Star**:
   * A crisp, 4-pointed sparkle icon positioned near the upper right of the woman’s silhouette (`x: ~58%`, `y: ~48%`).
   * Color: Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) with a faint luminous halo.
3. **Agency Award Crest**:
   * Laurel wreath circular badge with center text: `"AWARD WINNING BEST AGENCY 2025"`.
   * Line weight: `1.2px` stroke, rendered in pure white.
   * Accompanied immediately to its right by 3-line uppercase copy:
     ```text
     AWARDED BY THE
     WORLD'S LEADING
     EXPERTS
     ```

## 7. Responsive Breakpoint Logic

* **Desktop Wide (`≥ 1440px`)**: Full exact 1:1 layout as specified above. Viewport locked to `100vh`.
* **Tablet Landscape (`1024px – 1439px`)**:
  * Scale typography proportionally using `clamp()`.
  * Maintain relative alignment of the Clutch badge and circular CTA.
* **Tablet Portrait / Mobile (`< 1024px`)**:
  * Header collapses into a hamburger navigation menu while retaining the `"TALK TO US ↗"` button.
  * Headline `"FUTURE READY"` stacks naturally without horizontal offsets.
  * Supporting paragraph moves beneath the main headline.
  * Giant bottom banner `"BRANDING UI/UX DESIGN"` transitions to an infinite horizontal marquee ticker across the lower screen edge.
