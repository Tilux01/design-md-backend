# Design Specification: "Sonya & Jerome Wine Tours" Hero Section

This document defines the complete visual, structural, and asset specifications to reproduce an exact full-screen clone of the luxury wine tour landing page hero section.

---

## 1. Overview & Visual Aesthetic

- **Design Tone:** High-end editorial, luxury lifestyle, Italian/French vineyard aesthetic.
- **Composition Style:** Layered 3D montage with typographic interlacing. Foreground product elements break through a framed architectural window containing scenic landscape imagery, woven between giant stylized serif display lettering.
- **Screen Sizing:** Full viewport hero (`100vw` × `100vh`), borderless, responsive scaling with centered focal lockup.

## 3. Typography System

### Primary Display Serif (Logo, Main Headline, Framing Words)
* **Font Style:** High-contrast neoclassical/modern serif with organic, calligraphic swashes and flared terminals.
* **Recommended Web Fonts:**
  * Primary: **Cormorant Garamond** (Google Fonts — weights: 300, 400, 500)
  * Alternative/Commercial: **Ogg**, **Canela**, or **Playfair Display**
  * System Fallbacks: `Didot`, `Bodoni MT`, `Baskerville`, `serif`

### Secondary UI & Navigation (Nav links, CTA Button)
* **Font Style:** Clean, high-legibility transitional serif or elegant modern sans-serif with delicate stroke weight.
* **Recommended Web Fonts:**
  * Primary: **Cinzel** / **Montserrat** (Light 300) / **Cormorant Infant**
  * Fallbacks: `Optima`, `Georgia`, `sans-serif`

### Typography Hierarchy

1. **Brand Logo Title (`Sonya & Jerome`):**
   * Font: *Cormorant Garamond* (Regular / 400)
   * Size: `24px` – `28px`
   * Letter Spacing: `-0.01em`
   * Color: `#1C1B1A`

2. **Brand Logo Subtitle (`WINE TOURS`):**
   * Font: *Cinzel* or *Cormorant Garamond* (Uppercase / Medium 500)
   * Size: `8px` – `9px`
   * Letter Spacing: `+0.25em` (wide tracking)
   * Color: `#75726D`

3. **Navigation Links (`Home`, `About us`, etc.):**
   * Font: *Cormorant Garamond* or *Georgia* (Regular / 400)
   * Size: `13px` – `14px`
   * Letter Spacing: `+0.02em`
   * Color: `#1C1B1A`

4. **Headline Framing Text Left (`MEMORABLE`):**
   * Font: *Cormorant Garamond* (Regular / 400, Uppercase)
   * Size: `28px` – `36px`
   * Letter Spacing: `+0.18em`
   * Color: `#1C1B1A`

5. **Headline Framing Text Right (`JOURNEYS`):**
   * Font: *Cormorant Garamond* (Regular / 400, Uppercase)
   * Size: `28px` – `36px`
   * Letter Spacing: `+0.18em`
   * Color: `#1C1B1A`

6. **Giant Center Display Text (`WINE`):**
   * Font: *Cormorant Garamond* (Italic / Swash variant for `W`, Bold/Regular for `I`, `N`, `E`)
   * Size: `clamp(140px, 22vw, 320px)`
   * Weight: 400 – 500
   * Color: `#F3BA45`
   * Letter Spacing: `-0.02em`

7. **Floating CTA Text (`CHECK TOURS`):**
   * Font: *Cormorant Garamond* (Uppercase / 500)
   * Size: `10px` – `11px`
   * Line Height: `1.2`
   * Letter Spacing: `+0.12em`
   * Color: `#1C1B1A`

## 4. Layout Structure & Layering (Z-Index Architecture)

The composition relies on exact z-index stacking to create visual depth:

```
[Layer 0] Viewport Background (#FAF9F5)
  └─ [Layer 1] Top Navigation Bar
  └─ [Layer 2] Scenic Architectural Frame (Vineyard Image + Double Gold Outline)
  └─ [Layer 3] Giant Gold Typography ("W I N E") + Subtitles ("MEMORABLE", "JOURNEYS")
  └─ [Layer 4] Transparent Foreground Asset (Wine Bottle & Glass Cutout)
  └─ [Layer 5] Floating CTA Circular Badge ("CHECK TOURS ↗")
```

---

## 5. Detailed Component Breakdown

### 5.1 Top Navigation Bar
* **Position:** Fixed or absolute, top `0`, left `0`, width `100%`.
* **Padding:** `32px 5%` (horizontal margin alignment).
* **Display:** Flexbox, `justify-content: space-between`, `align-items: center`.
* **Left Item (Logo):**
  * Vertical flex column, centered or left-aligned.
  * Line 1: `Sonya & Jerome`
  * Line 2: `WINE TOURS`
* **Right Item (Nav Items):**
  * Horizontal list with `gap: 36px` – `44px`.
  * Items: `Home`, `About us`, `Tours`, `Wineries`, `Blog`, `Contact us`.

---

### 5.2 Scenic Architectural Center Frame
* **Position:** Absolute center horizontally and vertically aligned.
* **Dimensions:**
  * Width: ~`28vw` (approx. `380px` – `420px` at desktop).
  * Height: ~`70vh` (approx. `580px` – `660px`).
* **Frame Geometry (Chamfered Top Corners):**
  * The top corners are angled at 45 degrees (hexagonal arch silhouette).
  * CSS representation: 
    ```css
    clip-path: polygon(
      15% 0%, 85% 0%, 
      100% 8%, 100% 100%, 
      0% 100%, 0% 8%
    );
    ```
* **Borders / Stroke:**
  * Outer and nested inner frame stroke line in metallic gold `#E2B35B`.
  * Border thickness: `1.5px` outer, with a `6px` spacing inset before the image edge.
* **Image Description (Within Frame):**
  * **Subject:** Panoramic Tuscan or Piedmont vineyard landscape.
  * **Lighting/Mood:** Late afternoon / warm golden hour sunset.
  * **Details:** Foreground rolling terraces with vibrant green vine rows, an Italian rustic farmhouse roof partially visible on the middle left, gentle misty valleys, and soft distant layered mountain ridges beneath a warm, cloudy amber sky.
  * **Fitting:** `object-fit: cover`, positioned to show the rolling hills directly through the transparent body of the wine bottle.

---

### 5.3 Typography Placement & Intertwining

* **"MEMORABLE":**
  * Positioned above the left diagonal stroke of the giant letter **"W"**.
  * Aligned roughly at `top: 28%`, `left: 12%`.
* **"JOURNEYS":**
  * Positioned directly beneath the base of the giant letter **"E"**.
  * Aligned roughly at `bottom: 22%`, `right: 14%`.
* **"WINE":**
  * Centered across the screen width.
  * **"W":** Extends significantly to the left of the scenic frame. Elegant swooping left terminal.
  * **"I":** Sits behind or directly aligned with the center bottle neck.
  * **"N":** Spans across the center, partially visible through the glass bottle.
  * **"E":** Anchors the right side, extending outside the right boundary of the scenic frame.

---

### 5.4 Foreground Focal Asset: Transparent Bottle & Wine Glass

* **Asset Type:** Background-less, transparent PNG / WebP cut-out.
* **Position:** Vertically centered, perfectly aligned along the vertical axis of the scenic frame.
* **Z-Index:** Layer 4 (renders directly *over* the scenic frame and the *WINE* typography).
* **Visual Characteristics:**
  1. **Top Section:**
     * Tall, slender clear-glass wine bottle neck with a dark brown/black foil capsule/cork at the top.
     * The top of the bottle extends **above** the top line of the scenic frame, creating a 3D pop-out effect.
  2. **Middle Section:**
     * Crystal-clear elongated silhouette tapering down into a sleek tulip-shaped wine glass.
     * Realistic refraction and highlights along the glass edges, allowing the vineyard hills and golden typography behind it to remain partially visible through the glass body.
  3. **Bottom Section:**
     * The glass contains a measure of pale white or amber wine resting in the bowl.
     * Below the bowl, an elongated stem and a base rest near the bottom edge of the frame.
* **Rotation / Transform:** `0deg` (straight vertical orientation).

---

### 5.5 Floating CTA Badge ("CHECK TOURS ↗")

* **Position:**
  * Overlapping the bottom-left boundary of the scenic frame and the right stroke of the giant letter **"W"**.
  * Relative offset: `bottom: 26%`, `left: calc(50% - 190px)`.
* **Geometry & Styling:**
  * Shape: Perfect circle (`width: 96px`, `height: 96px`).
  * Background: Solid pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
  * Border: None.
  * Box Shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
  * Display: Flexbox column, `justify-content: center`, `align-items: center`.
* **Contents:**
  * **Icon:** Diagonal arrow (`↗`) pointing top-right; clean stroke, dark charcoal `#1C1B1A`, `16px` height.
  * **Text:**
    * Line 1: `CHECK`
    * Line 2: `TOURS`
    * Text alignment: Centered.

---

## 6. Interaction & Micro-Animations

* **Navigation Links:** Subtle color fade to `#E2B35B` on hover with a `250ms` cubic-bezier transition.
* **CTA Badge ("CHECK TOURS"):** 
  * Scales up slightly on hover (`scale(1.06)`).
  * Arrow tilts slightly further top-right (`translate(2px, -2px)`).
  * Smooth transition duration: `0.3s ease-out`.
* **Parallax Motion (Optional Enhancement):**
  * Mouse movement causes subtle differential movement: Foreground bottle moves by `±6px`, vineyard frame background moves by `±2px` in the opposite direction.
