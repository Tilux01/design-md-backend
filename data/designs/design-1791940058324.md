# Design Specification: Glassmorphic Automotive Media Interface

## 1. General Theme & Layout
*   **Visual Style:** Glassmorphism / Neumorphism. High use of background blurs, soft drop shadows, and semi-transparent white overlays.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Background:** A soft linear gradient from top-left `#C5D9ED` to bottom-right `#A3C1DA`.
    *   **Glass Panels:** White (`#FFFFFF`) with opacity ranging from 20% to 40% and a `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.
    *   **Accent Color:** Vibrant Blue (`#3B82F6`) for active states and progress bars.
    *   **Text Color:** Dark Grey/Black (`#1A1A1A`) for high contrast; Medium Grey (`#666666`) for secondary info and inactive lyrics.
*   **Layout Structure:** A fixed-width left sidebar (approx. 80px) and a main content area that occupies the remaining width.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `SF Pro Display` (San Francisco).
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `Oxygen`, `Ubuntu`, `Cantarell`, `Open Sans`, `Helvetica Neue`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Greeting:** 18px, Semi-bold.
    *   **Clock:** 16px, Medium.
    *   **Song Title (Main):** 28px, Bold.
    *   **Artist Name (Main):** 18px, Medium.
    *   **Lyrics (Active):** 20px, Bold.
    *   **Lyrics (Inactive):** 18px, Regular, 50% opacity.
    *   **Card Titles:** 14px, Semi-bold.

## 3. Sidebar (Navigation)
*   **Position:** Left-aligned, full height.
*   **Background:** Solid dark grey/black (`#121212`).
*   **Elements (Top to Bottom):**
    1.  **User Profile:** Circular image (1:1), 40px diameter. Image: A person wearing a green hoodie against a neutral background.
    2.  **Car Icon:** Outlined sedan icon.
    3.  **Steering Wheel Icon:** Outlined circular wheel icon.
    4.  **Navigation Icon:** Outlined arrowhead/compass icon.
    5.  **Temperature:** Text "25°", white, 16px.
    6.  **Music Icon:** Outlined musical note.
    7.  **Apps Icon:** 2x2 grid of squares.
    8.  **Phone Icon:** Outlined telephone handset.
*   **Spacing:** Icons are vertically centered with even distribution (approx. 60px gap).

## 4. Top Status Bar
*   **Greeting (Left):** A circular gradient icon (Pink/Blue/Orange) followed by the text `" Hi, Rosen "`.
*   **Clock (Center):** `12:00` centered horizontally.
*   **Status Icons (Right):**
    *   **Signal:** Four vertical bars of increasing height.
    *   **Wi-Fi:** Standard three-arc signal icon.
    *   **Bluetooth:** Standard stylized 'B' icon.
    *   **Battery:** Horizontal battery outline with a small green fill at the end.

## 5. Main Player Widget (Center-Left)
*   **Container:** A large rounded rectangle (border-radius: 32px) with a frosted glass effect.
*   **Album Art Circle:** 
    *   **Image:** Portrait of Ariana Grande (from the "Sweetener" era). She is looking down, hand to face, with a cloudy/misty background. 
    *   **Shape:** Perfectly circular, centered in the left half of the widget.
    *   **Border:** A thick, soft-white inner glow/border.
*   **Progress Ring:** 
    *   A thin blue arc (`#3B82F6`) tracing the outer edge of the album art from the 12 o'clock position to roughly the 4 o'clock position.
    *   Ends with a small blue circular handle/knob.
*   **Controls:**
    *   **Shuffle (Left):** Small circular button, crossed arrows icon.
    *   **Heart (Right):** Small circular button, outlined heart icon.
    *   **Back (Bottom-Left):** Circular button, skip-back icon.
    *   **Play/Pause (Bottom-Center):** Large Blue circle (`#3B82F6`) with white "Pause" (two vertical bars) icon.
    *   **Next (Bottom-Right):** Circular button, skip-forward icon.

## 6. Lyrics Section (Center-Right)
*   **Header:** "Same old love" (Bold) above "Selena Gomez" (Medium).
*   **Lyrics Text:** Centered vertically and horizontally within the right pane.
*   **Active Line:** "all the money and the fame baby hear me when I say" - Bold, dark text.
*   **Inactive Lines:** Faded text above and below the active line.
*   **Background Detail:** A very faint, large-scale ghosted image of the artist (Selena Gomez) is visible behind the lyrics text, set to roughly 5% opacity.

## 7. Bottom Carousel (Recent/Suggested)
Four horizontal cards with rounded corners (24px).
1.  **Card 1 (Active):** "Same old love - Selena Gomez". Features a vinyl record icon. Includes a small blue visualizer icon in the bottom right corner.
2.  **Card 2:** "Please don't go - Brown Boy". Image: A person with colorful, stylized hair/headwear against a light background. Includes a small grey play button.
3.  **Card 3:** "Let somebody go - Adele". Image: A woman (Adele) in a red outfit looking to the side. Includes a small grey play button.
4.  **Card 4:** "Baby - Justin Bieber". Image: A stylized, high-contrast portrait of a man with colorful lighting. Includes a small grey play button.

## 8. Image Specifications
*   **Backgroundless Images:** None. All album art and profile images are contained within their respective circular or rectangular frames with their original backgrounds intact.
*   **Rotation:** All images are at 0-degree rotation (standard upright).
*   **Shadows:** All containers (cards and main widget) utilize a `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`.
