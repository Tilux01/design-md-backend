# Design Specification: OMusic Dashboard

## 1. General Theme & Environment
*   **Background:** Deep Matte Black (`#0B0B0B`).
*   **Overall Aesthetic:** Dark Mode, Glassmorphism, and Vibrant Gradients.
*   **Layout:** Three-column layout (Sidebar, Main Content, Right Panel).
*   **Corner Radius:** 
    *   Main Container: `0px` (Full screen).
    *   Cards/Buttons: `20px` to `30px`.
    *   Small Icons/Avatars: `8px` to `12px` or Circular.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `SF Pro Display`.
*   **Secondary Font:** `Poppins` (for headings).
*   **Fallbacks:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `Oxygen`, `Ubuntu`, `Cantarell`, `Open Sans`, `Helvetica Neue`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:**
    *   Headings: `700` (Bold).
    *   Body: `400` (Regular).
    *   Metadata: `300` (Light).

## 3. Color Palette
*   **Accent 1 (Purple Glow):** `rgba(128, 0, 128, 0.2)` (Used for background glows).
*   **Accent 2 (Pink/Blue Gradient):** Linear Gradient `to right, #4A56E2, #EF5DA8`.
*   **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (White).
*   **Text Secondary:** `#A0A0A0` (Muted Gray).
*   **Active Indicator:** `#1DB954` (Spotify Green - seen on Sidebar "Home").
*   **Glass Effect:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.

---

## 4. Sidebar (Left Column)
*   **Width:** ~200px - 240px.
*   **Background:** Subtle gradient from dark purple (top-left) to black.
*   **Logo:** "OMusic" text with a white musical note icon to the left.
*   **Search Bar:** Rounded pill shape, dark gray background, magnifying glass icon, placeholder "Search...".
*   **Navigation Groups:**
    *   **Menu:** Home (Active - green vertical bar on left), Discover, Radio, Podcast.
    *   **Library:** Albums, Song, Artist.
    *   **Playlist:** List of "abdscs" entries. Each entry has a music note icon on the left and a trash bin icon on the right (visible on hover).

---

## 5. Main Content (Center Column)
*   **Header:**
    *   Navigation: Small circular buttons for Back `<` and Forward `>`.
*   **Hero Section ("Trending"):**
    *   **Title:** "Top Song Of The Week" in large, bold white text.
    *   **Card Background:** A vibrant, blurred gradient mesh featuring deep blue on the left, transitioning into a bright magenta/pink on the right.
    *   **Buttons:** 
        *   "Play": White pill-shaped button, black text, black play icon.
        *   "View Playlist": Transparent pill-shaped button with a thin white border.
*   **Global Top 50 Section:**
    *   **Table Headers:** `#`, `Name Song`, `Artist`, `Time`, `Like`. Text is muted gray.
    *   **Table Rows:**
        *   **Row 1:** Sammy Simorangkir - "Tak Mampu Pergi".
        *   **Row 2:** Sammy Simorangkir - "Kaulah Segalanya".
        *   **Row 3:** Kerispatih - "Lagu Rindu".
    *   **Row Elements:**
        *   Square album art with `8px` rounded corners.
        *   Heart icon (outlined white) and "1k" text.
        *   Vertical ellipsis `⋮` for options.

## 7. Detailed Image Descriptions

1.  **Sidebar Logo Icon:** A simple, white, vector-style musical note (eighth note).
2.  **Hero Card Gradient:** A non-geometric mesh gradient. **Placement:** Spans the width of the center column. **Colors:** Start `#3F51B5`, Mid `#9C27B0`, End `#E91E63`.
3.  **Album Art (Table):**
    *   **Tak Mampu Pergi:** A man in a blue suit against a dark background.
    *   **Kaulah Segalanya:** A woman in a red dress in a warm-lit room.
    *   **Lagu Rindu:** A glowing orange/fire-like abstract shape on a black background.
4.  **Artist Avatars:**
    *   **Sammy:** Man in a suit, headshot.
    *   **Rossa:** Woman with long dark hair, glamorous pose.
    *   **Dewa 19:** Group photo of a band.
    *   **Juicy Luicy:** Group photo, casual attire.
5.  **Mini Player Main Image:** High-resolution portrait of a man with dark hair and rectangular glasses, looking slightly to the side. The image should be cropped to a wide rectangle with rounded corners. Background of this specific image is a dark, warm studio setting.

---

## 8. Interactive States
*   **Hover (Sidebar/Table):** Background should change to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
*   **Active (Sidebar):** Vertical green line (`#1DB954`) `3px` wide on the far left of the menu item.
*   **Buttons:** Scale down slightly (`transform: scale(0.98)`) on click.
