# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Immersive Dark Mode Scientific Particle Simulator / Generative Art Studio.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Highly balanced, atmospheric composition centered on a three-dimensional particle simulation inside a cylindrical containment glass chamber. Clean, uncluttered UI floating subtly over high-contrast space backgrounds.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Deep void canvas (`#02040A`), translucent glassmorphic floating elements (`rgba(10, 14, 28, 0.6)`), subtle backdrop blurs (`blur(16px)`), and crisp inner borders with glowing particle light emitters.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient particle glow orbs, glassmorphic floating toolbars, interactive physics triggers, live coordinate/particle counters.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `4px 12px`, font-size: `11px`, font-weight: `600`, tracking: `0.15em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background translucency: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`, border width: `1px` solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-blue-400 inline-block animate-pulse"></span>`.
