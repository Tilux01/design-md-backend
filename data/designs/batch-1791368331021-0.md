# Design Specification: Devialet E-commerce / Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Foundations

### Color Palette
- **Background (Light):** `#f0f2f5` (soft off-white/light gray canvas)
- **Primary Text & Brand Headers:** `#1a1a1a` (near-black)
- **Secondary Text / Subtitles:** `#4f5660` (medium gray)
- **Accent / Metallic Details:** Warm copper/rose gold (`#c29b7c` range) for speaker trim and carousel indicators.
- **Pure White:** `#ffffff` for UI containers (e.g., carousel active state, buttons, product cases).

### Typography
- **Font Family:** Sans-serif (Clean modern geometric/neo-grotesque like Inter, Montserrat, or Helvetica Neue).
- **Brand Logo Header:** Uppercase, wide letter-spacing (tracking), bold weight.
- **Hero Title (`DEVIALET`):** Massive display size, uppercase, heavy bold weight (`700`/`800`).
- **Body Text:** Regular weight, optimized line-height for readability (approx. 1.5).

---

## 2. Header & Navigation (Global)

- **Layout:** Fixed or sticky top bar, edge-to-edge with generous horizontal padding.
- **Left Element:** Brand logotype `DEVIALET` in bold uppercase.
- **Center Navigation Links:**
  - `HOME`
  - `ABOUT`
  - `COLLECTION`
- **Right Element:** Pill-shaped `BUY` button with a subtle border and rounded corners.

---

## 3. Hero Section

- **Background:** Gradient/wave-form abstract light grey mesh background, giving a clean, premium studio feel.
- **Main Typography:**
  - Giant background/hero title: `DEVIALET`
  - Sub-tagline: `"Elevate the Sound."`
- **Product Visual:** 
  - Centered floating high-end spherical speaker (Devialet Phantom style) resting on a minimalist cylindrical podium pedestal.
  - Features metallic copper/rose gold side panels and a high-gloss white acoustic orb.
- **Left Callout Text:** 
  - `"Get Your Sonic Fix: Where Luxury Meets Acoustical Euphoria with Devialet."`
  - Accompanied by a down-arrow scroll pill button.
- **Right Social Icons:** Vertical/horizontal alignment of social links (`Instagram`, `Twitter/X`, `Facebook`, `LinkedIn`).

---

## 4. "About Us" Section

- **Section Header:** 
  - Heading: `ABOUT US`
  - Sub-heading: `Sound in its purest state.`
- **Content Columns (Two-Column Text):**
  - **Column 1:** *"Devialet is a French audio company renowned for delivering unmatched sound quality through innovative products like the Phantom Speaker. Founded in 2007, Devialet blends advanced technology and elegant design to create an exceptional audio experience."*
  - **Column 2:** *"Devialet is renowned for its luxurious products and captivating aesthetics, earning awards for the unique fusion of superior audio quality and appealing design. As a leader in the luxury audio industry, the brand continues to combine top-tier audio technology with beautiful design."*
- **Image Gallery (Three Columns):**
  - Three distinct monochrome/desaturated lifestyle photography shots displaying high-end interior setups, boutique display cases, and modern living rooms featuring Devialet sound systems. Rounded container corners with subtle drop shadows.

---

## 5. Product Carousel / "Devialet Collection" Section

- **Section Heading:** 
  - Title: `DEVIALET COLLECTION`
  - Positioned on the left side with a pagination tracker below (`01` — `02 03 04 05` with an active indicator line).
- **Carousel Layout:**
  - **Center (Active Item):** Large, prominent display of the flagship white-and-copper spherical speaker.
  - **Left (Previous Item):** Angled thumbnail preview of a specialized compact earbud/pod case labeled with a `< Left` navigation arrow.
  - **Right (Next Item):** Preview of wireless charging/earbud case labeled with a `Right >` navigation arrow.
- **Footer Social/Brand Links:** Bottom right alignment (`TW`, `IN`, `FB`).

---

## 6. Layout & Responsiveness Guidelines

- **Container Max-Width:** 1200px centered content with full-width background washes (`#f0f2f5`).
- **Spacing:** Generous whitespace (padding/margins of `60px` to `120px` between major sections) to maintain the high-end luxury minimalist aesthetic.
- **Borders & Shadows:** Ultra-subtle drop shadows (`box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`) used primarily on floating product pods and imagery containers.
