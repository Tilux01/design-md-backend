# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist, Modern Monolithic Branding, Clean Luxury.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio with precise, centered focal alignment. Designed to maximize visual impact through high contrast and stark minimalism.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, single-layer canvas architecture utilizing negative space and negative cutouts (negative-space typography/monogramming) rather than traditional drop-shadow depth cards.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Precision geometry, negative space integration, absolute central alignment, minimalist vector contouring.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `0.75rem`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.05)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
