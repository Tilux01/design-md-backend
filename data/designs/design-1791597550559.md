# Design Specification: "Mr L Production" Travel Agency Landing Page

## 1. Global Specifications

*   **Layout:** Full-screen web application landing page. Responsive design is implied, but the layout described is for desktop (1440px+ width).
*   **Theme:** Luxury, immersive, glassmorphism, high-contrast typography.
*   **Global Background:**
    *   **Image Description:** A breathtaking, full-screen, high-resolution photograph of a luxurious coastal Mediterranean town at sunset. In the foreground, an infinity pool reflects the sky, bordered by lounge chairs and warm lanterns. To the right, terraced white buildings with warm lighting are built into a cliff. The background features calm ocean water with a small yacht, distant rugged mountains, and a sky transitioning from warm golden/orange hues at the horizon to soft blue at the top.
    *   **Positioning:** `center center`, `background-size: cover`.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Dark:** `#1A2B33` (Used for primary buttons, dark UI elements, and main text).
    *   **Accent Gold/Bronze:** `#9B744A` (Used for specific headline words and star ratings).
    *   **Surface White:** `#FFFFFF` (Used for text on dark backgrounds and icon backgrounds).
    *   **Glassmorphism Surface:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` (Approximate: Translucent white with a background blur backdrop-filter).
    *   **Text Grey (Subtitles):** `#5E6D77` (Approximate dark slate grey).
    *   **Border Color:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)` (Thin, subtle white borders on glass elements).
*   **Typography:**
    *   **Primary Serif (Headings):** *Playfair Display* (Fallback: *Georgia*, *Times New Roman*, serif). Used for elegant, large headers.
    *   **Primary Sans-Serif (UI & Body):** *Inter*, *Roboto*, or *Helvetica Neue* (Fallback: sans-serif). Used for navigation, buttons, descriptions, and small text. Clean and highly legible.

## 2. Header / Navigation Bar

**Placement:** Fixed or absolute at the top of the screen. Full width with internal padding.

*   **Left: Logo Area**
    *   **Icon:** A stylized monogram "NP" where the left stem of the 'N' is bold and connects fluidly to the 'P'. Color: Primary Dark.
    *   **Text:** "MR L PRODUCTION". Font: Sans-serif, uppercase, heavy letter-spacing (tracking), small font size (approx 10px-12px), color: Primary Dark.
*   **Center: Navigation Links**
    *   **Container:** A pill-shaped (fully rounded corners) container with a very subtle glassmorphism effect (low opacity white + slight blur) and a delicate white border.
    *   **Links:** "Home", "Destinations", "Tours", "Experiences", "About", "Contact". Font: Sans-serif, approx 14px.
    *   **Active State ("Home"):** Contained within its own dark pill shape (`#1A2B33`), text color is White.
    *   **Inactive State:** Text color is Primary Dark.
*   **Right: Actions**
    *   **Search Button:** A perfect circle with a solid white background. Contains a minimalist dark magnifying glass icon.
    *   **CTA Button:** Pill-shaped. Background: Primary Dark. Text: "Book a Journey" (White, sans-serif, approx 14px). Inside the pill on the far right is a circular white button containing a small, dark, right-pointing arrow icon (`→`).
