# Design Specification: Rice Cream Product Page

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Background:** The entire viewport should be a subtle radial gradient starting from `#FFFFFF` (center) to a very light grey `#F2F4F7` (edges).
*   **Main Container:** A centered, large white card with extremely rounded corners (`border-radius: 40px`).
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Purple:** `#5D4E9E` (Used for titles, active buttons, and the hero circle).
    *   **Light Lavender/Blue:** `#E8F5FF` (Used for the left-side accent background).
    *   **Text Grey:** `#333333` (Titles), `#888888` (Body text).
    *   **Accent Orange:** `#FFD8B1` (Used for small indicator dots).
*   **Shadows:** Soft, diffused drop shadows (`box-shadow: 0 20px 50px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`).

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** 'Poppins' or 'Montserrat'.
*   **Fallback Fonts:** 'Segoe UI', 'Roboto', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Main Title:** 32px, Bold, `#5D4E9E`.
    *   **Body Text:** 12px, Regular, Line-height 1.6, `#888888`.
    *   **Price:** 18px, Semi-bold, `#333333`.
    *   **Tab Labels:** 13px, Medium.

## 3. Header Navigation
*   **Left Section:** "Ladies" text with a small downward chevron icon. Font weight: Bold.
*   **Center Section:** A search bar with a pill-shape (`border-radius: 20px`). Inside, the word "Search" is left-aligned, and a magnifying glass icon is right-aligned.
*   **Right Section:** 
    *   Back/Forward arrows (simple chevrons).
    *   Shopping bag icon with a small badge "7".
    *   A "grid" menu icon (four dots).

## 4. Left Hero Section (Product Display)
*   **Background Accent:** A light blue/lavender vertical strip (`#E8F5FF`) on the far left with a rounded right edge.
*   **Hero Shape:** A perfect circle of solid Primary Purple (`#5D4E9E`) centered behind the product.
*   **Product Image:** 
    *   **Description:** A matte white glass bottle with a silver/metallic screw-top cap. The label says "Rice" in a serif font with a small heart icon above it.
    *   **Status:** Backgroundless (PNG).
    *   **Placement:** Centered over the purple circle, slightly overlapping the bottom edge of the white card container.
*   **Carousel Indicators:** Below the bottle, three dots. The middle dot is the Primary Purple (active), the others are light grey.

## 5. Center Content Section
*   **Product Info:** Title "Rice Cream" followed by a two-line product description.
*   **Price Row:** "$19,00" in bold. Next to it, a small pill-shaped button with a dashed border saying "Add discount code".
*   **Tab Switcher:** A pill-shaped container with three options: "Detail", "Ingredients", and "Direction". 
    *   "Detail" is the active tab, styled as a solid Primary Purple pill with white text.
*   **Stats/Progress Bars:**
    *   **Natural Extracts (66%):** A horizontal bar. The filled portion is Primary Purple. To the left is a small circular thumbnail of a green sprout in soil (backgroundless).
    *   **Rice Ingredients:** A horizontal bar with a thin line and a small orange slider thumb (`#FFD8B1`).

## 6. Right Sidebar (Action Bar)
*   **Container:** A vertical light-grey translucent strip on the far right.
*   **Icons:**
    *   **Save:** A bookmark icon inside a white rounded square.
    *   **Intro:** A play/info icon inside a white rounded square.
    *   **Purchase:** A large Primary Purple square button with a white "+" sign and the word "Purchase" in small text below it.
    *   **Rating:** A circular progress ring at the bottom showing "6.0".

## 7. Decorative Elements (Floating Assets)
*   **Leaves:**
    *   One bright green leaf floating on the far left, mid-height, rotated 45 degrees counter-clockwise.
    *   Two leaves at the bottom center, overlapping slightly, laying flat.
    *   One small leaf fragment near the bottom right.
*   **Flower:**
    *   **Description:** A high-quality, realistic white gardenia or rose bloom.
    *   **Status:** Backgroundless (PNG).
    *   **Placement:** Bottom right corner, sitting on the "floor" outside the main card.
*   **Floating Clock:** A small, faint circular clock icon floating near the top-left of the product bottle.

## 8. Implementation Notes
*   **Transitions:** All buttons should have a `0.3s ease` hover effect (slight scale up or opacity change).
*   **Spacing:** Use a consistent 24px padding for internal elements.
*   **Alignment:** The "Rice Cream" title must align vertically with the top of the purple circle in the hero section.
