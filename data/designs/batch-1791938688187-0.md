This document provides a comprehensive design specification for a high-fidelity clone of the provided spatial UI streaming interface. The design follows a **Glassmorphic** aesthetic, optimized for an immersive, full-screen AR/VR environment.

---

# Design Specification: Spatial Media Interface

## 1. Global Styles & Environment
*   **Background:** The UI should be rendered against a transparent or deeply blurred background. If implemented in a browser, use a `backdrop-filter: blur(40px)`.
*   **Core Aesthetic:** Frosted glass effect. High translucency, white inner borders (1px) to simulate light catching the edges of glass.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Accent:** Netflix Red (`#E50914`).
    *   **Glass Base:** White with low opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
    *   **Text:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for primary; Light Grey (`#B3B3B3`) for secondary.
    *   **Shadows:** Soft, expansive drop shadows to create depth (`box-shadow: 0 20px 50px rgba(0,0,0,0.3)`).

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Inter** or **SF Pro Display**.
*   **Fallback Sequence:** `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:**
    *   Titles: Bold (700)
    *   Body/Metadata: Regular (400) or Medium (500)
*   **Styling:** Letter spacing should be slightly tight (-0.02em) for a modern cinematic look.

## 3. Layout Components

### A. Left Navigation Sidebar
*   **Shape:** Vertical pill-shaped glass container.
*   **Placement:** Fixed to the left, vertically centered.
*   **Elements (Top to Bottom):**
    1.  **Netflix Logo:** The iconic "N" in Netflix Red.
    2.  **Home Icon:** Outline house shape (Active state: white glow).
    3.  **Search Icon:** Magnifying glass.
    4.  **TV Icon:** Screen silhouette.
    5.  **Movie Icon:** Film clapperboard.
    6.  **Trending Icon:** Upward zig-zag arrow.
    7.  **Library Icon:** Four small squares/grid.
    8.  **Profile Icon:** Circular silhouette.
*   **Interaction:** A subtle red vertical indicator bar (pill shape) appears to the left of the active icon.

### B. Hero Section (Featured Content)
*   **Content:** "Extraction 2"
*   **Main Image:** 
    *   **Description:** A high-resolution, backgroundless (cutout) image of Chris Hemsworth as Tyler Rake. He is positioned on the right side of the hero area, facing slightly left. He is wearing tactical gear, looking intense, with a gritty, dusty texture.
    *   **Placement:** The image should bleed off the right edge of the hero container.
    *   **Rotation:** 0 degrees, but the character's body is angled at a 3/4 view.
*   **Text Block (Left Aligned):**
    *   **Title:** "Extraction 2" (Large, Bold, White).
    *   **Description:** 2-3 lines of grey subtext describing the plot.
    *   **Buttons:** Two glass-pill buttons ("Watch" and "Info").
    *   **Metadata:** "Time: 2h 2m" and a "Rating" row featuring 4.5 gold stars.
*   **Call to Action:** A floating red "Watch Now" button with a Play icon, positioned at the bottom right of the hero glass panel.

### C. Content Grid ("Popular on Netflix")
*   **Header:** "Popular on Netflix" in white, medium weight, positioned above the row.
*   **Cards:** A horizontal row of 5-6 cards.
*   **Card Anatomy:**
    *   **Image:** A vertical movie poster (backgroundless/masked to the card).
    *   **Overlay:** A dark gradient at the bottom for text legibility.
    *   **Text:** Movie title at the bottom left.
    *   **Rating:** Small star icons below the title.
*   **Card 6 (Far Right):** Tilted slightly (approx. 5-10 degrees Y-axis rotation) to simulate a 3D carousel effect in spatial space.

### D. Top Right Utility
*   **Search Button:** A circular glass button containing a magnifying glass icon, floating independently in the top right corner.

---

## 4. Visual Effects & Details
*   **Lighting:** The "Glass" panels should have a subtle diagonal linear gradient from top-left (brighter) to bottom-right (darker) to simulate a light source.
*   **Borders:** All containers must have a `1px` solid border with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` to define the "cut" of the glass.
*   **Corner Radii:** 
    *   Main Panels: `40px`
    *   Sidebar: `50px`
    *   Content Cards: `20px`
*   **Image Treatments:** The hero image of the actor should have a soft "fade to transparent" at the waist/bottom to blend into the glass panel seamlessly.
