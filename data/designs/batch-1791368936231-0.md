# Design Specification: "Harmony" Electric Scooter E-Commerce Website

## 1. Global Styles & Foundations

### Color Palette
*   **Primary / Backgrounds:**
    *   `#F4F4F6` (Very light cool gray/off-white) – Main canvas background.
    *   `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) – Card backgrounds, header containers, inner containers.
*   **Text & Typography Colors:**
    *   `#111111` (Near Black / Dark Charcoal) – Primary headings, major brand text (`h r m o n y.`).
    *   `#555555` to `#777777` (Medium Gray) – Secondary labels, metadata, body descriptions, specs.
    *   `#333333` (Dark Gray) – Buttons, icons, accent markers.
*   **Brand Accents (Product Colorways):**
    *   Grey (Neutral Silver/Grey): `#A0A6AA`
    *   Blue (Slate Blue): `#8297B0`
    *   Green (Sage/Mint Green): `#8AA79D`

### Typography
*   **Font Family:** Clean, modern geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, or *SF Pro Display*).
*   **Headings (`h r m o n y.`):**
    *   Extra-large hero typography, extra bold weight (`700`), lowercase style with special spacing/interlacing (the 'o' in harmony is replaced by a product/feature dial graphic).
*   **Body & UI Text:**
    *   Weights: Regular (`400`), Medium (`500`), Semi-bold (`600`).
    *   Clean, highly legible sans-serif for specifications and navigational menus.

---

## 2. Header & Navigation (Global)

*   **Layout:** Fixed top header bar, centered horizontally inside a pill-shaped container or floating cleanly over the top margin.
*   **Left Element:** Circular icon button (Cart/Menu toggle, displaying a counter `0`).
*   **Center Navigation Links:**
    *   `HOME` (Active state indicated by a small dark dot below).

    *   `FEATURES`
