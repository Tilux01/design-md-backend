# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Luxurious Dark Mode Digital Art / High-End Generative Motion Graphics Portfolio.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High visual density built via structured dot-matrix grids, focal subject isolation, and atmospheric gold gradient background lighting.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered micro-dot raster arrays combined with deep cinematic drop-shadows and subtle amber-to-gold backlighting.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-density dot grid matrices, atmospheric rear illumination orbs, dark glassmorphic containers, high-contrast silhouette masking.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 197, 66, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-[#F5C542] inline-block"></span>`.
