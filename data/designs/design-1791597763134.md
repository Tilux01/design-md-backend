# Design Specification: SugarCRM Case Management Dashboard

## 1. Global Design Language & Theme
*   **Style:** A blend of Glassmorphism and Neumorphism (Soft UI). The interface relies on soft shadows, frosted glass translucency, and floating elements over a gradient background.
*   **Global Background:** A soft, ambient, full-screen radial gradient. It transitions from a very pale, cool grey-blue at the top left (`#F0F4F8` approx.) to a slightly deeper, muted slate-blue at the bottom (`#DCE4EC` approx.).
*   **Corners:** Extensive use of heavy border-radius. Almost all containers, buttons, and cards use rounded corners (`border-radius: 24px` to fully rounded pills/circles).

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, or `Roboto`.
*   **Fallback Sequence:** `'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Characteristics:** Clean, highly legible sans-serif.
*   **Weights:**
    *   *Regular (400):* Used for secondary text, dates, sub-labels.
    *   *Medium (500):* Used for navigation links, table headers, standard card text.
    *   *Semi-Bold (600) / Bold (700):* Used for page titles ("Customer Journeys"), section headers ("New Case Managment"), active states, and highlighted workflow steps.
*   **Colors:**
    *   Primary Text: Dark Slate / Almost Black (`#111827`).
    *   Secondary Text: Muted Grey (`#6B7280`).
    *   Inverted Text: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) on black elements.

## 3. Layout Structure
The screen is divided into three main zones:
1.  **Top Navigation Bar:** Horizontal, full width.
2.  **Left Sidebar (Action Menu):** Vertical, floating pill on the far left edge.
3.  **Main Content Area:** Takes up the remaining space, containing a header, a large central workflow canvas, and a two-column bottom widget area.
