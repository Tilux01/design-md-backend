# Design Specification: E-Commerce Website (Wînk)

## Global Styles & Variables

*   **Color Palette:**
    *   `Background (Page):` `#F5F6F0` (Off-white/light sage gradient background wrapper)
    *   `Background (Main UI):` `#FFFFFF` (Pure white container card)
    *   `Primary Text / Headings:` `#000000` (Deep black)
    *   `Secondary Text / Muted:` `#555555` / `#777777` (Medium gray)
    *   `Accent / Hero Text:` `#FFFFFF` (White text over imagery)
    *   `Button Background (Primary):` `#FFFFFF` (White with dark text)
    *   `Search Bar / Filter Pills Background:` `#F3F4F6` (Light gray)
*   **Typography:**
    *   `Font Family:` Clean Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter, SF Pro Display, Helvetica)
    *   `Hero Heading ("CloudeyStyles."):` ~42px, Bold, Tight line-height
    *   `Section Title ("Fresh Fashion at Modern Vibes"):` ~32px, Bold, Centered
    *   `Body Text:` ~14px, Regular, Relaxed line-height (~1.5)
*   **Border Radius:**
    *   `Main App Container:` 16px - 24px rounded corners
    *   `Buttons / Pills:` Fully rounded (pill shape, `9999px`)
    *   `Image Cards:` 12px - 16px rounded corners

---

## Layout Structure

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| TOP BAR / HEADER (Logo, Nav Links, Icons)                      |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| SUB-NAV / FILTERS (Dropdowns, Category Pills, Search Bar)        |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| HERO CAROUSEL SECTION (Dark Image Banner, CTA, Navigation Arrows)|
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| INTRO SECTION ("Fresh Fashion at Modern Vibes" + Description)   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| CONTENT GRID (Left: Image Card + Text | Right: Promo Box Card)  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## Component Specifications

### 1. Header (`<header>`)
*   **Layout:** Flexbox, space-between, fixed top padding, horizontal alignment.
*   **Left Elements:** 
    *   Hamburger menu icon (3 horizontal lines).
*   **Center Elements:** 
    *   Brand Logo: `"Wînk"` (Custom typography, bold with stylized circumflex on the 'î').
*   **Right Elements:** 
    *   Text Links: `"Blogs"`, `"FAQs"`
    *   Action Icons: Notification bell, Shopping cart (with badge indicator), User profile avatar.

---

### 2. Sub-Navigation / Filter Bar
*   **Layout:** Horizontal scrollable/flex container with light grey pills (`#F3F4F6`).
*   **Left Side:**
    *   Dropdown: `"Clothing"` with dropdown chevron.
    *   Pills: `"New Arrivals"`, `"Sale"`.
    *   Search Input: `"Search..."` with a magnifying glass search icon on the right.
*   **Right Side (Categories):**
    *   Pills: `"Men"`, `"Women"`, `"Children"`, `"Brand"`, etc.

---

### 3. Hero Section
*   **Container:** Full-width dark background image banner with an overlaid gradient/dark tint for high contrast readability. Rounded corners (`16px`).
*   **Content (Left Aligned):**
    *   *Heading:* `"CloudeyStyles."` (Large, white, bold).
    *   *Subtext:* `"Discover our wide ranging and timeless lifestyle products. Pick your favourite stuff that matches your personal taste, style and suits your style."`
    *   *CTA Button:* `"Start shopping"` with an arrow-up-right icon inside a circular button.
    *   *Sub-label (Centered bottom of banner):* `"Top Collection"`.
*   **Navigation Controls (Top Right of Banner):**
    *   Circular left (`<`) and right (`>`) arrow buttons.

---

### 4. Section Intro: "Fresh Fashion at Modern Vibes"
*   **Layout:** Centered text block.
*   **Heading:** `"Fresh Fashion at Modern Vibes"` (Large, bold, dark).
*   **Paragraph:** `"Our collection is constantly updated with the latest styles, ensuring you're always on point. Shop now and let your fashion sense shine with the newest arrivals at Wink."` (Muted gray, constrained width).

---

### 5. Content Grid (Two Columns)

#### **Left Column Card:**
*   **Image:** A clothing rack displaying a variety of coats, jackets, shirts, and handbags.
*   **Text Block (Below Image):**
    *   Heading: `"Discover the unlimitless"` (Italicized serif or elegant sans style).

#### **Right Column Card:**
*   **Container:** Light gray/off-white background card (`#F0F1EC`).
*   **Text Block:**
    *   Heading: `"Hold on, new product is coming!"` (Italicized, medium-large).
    *   Subtext: `"-Bringing you a new era of simple clothes."`
*   **Nested Card (Bottom):**
    *   Rounded white container overlapping the bottom.
    *   Displays preview imagery and text: `"2024"`, `"New Arrival"`.
