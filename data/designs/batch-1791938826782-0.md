# Design Specification: Shōgun Media Landing Page

## 1. Layout Overview
*   **Format:** Full-screen (100vw, 100vh), overflow hidden.
*   **Structure:** A two-column layout.
    *   **Left Column (Visuals):** Occupies approx. 45% of the width. Contains the character, the red brushstroke, and the atmospheric mist.
    *   **Right Column (Content):** Occupies approx. 55% of the width. Contains branding, title, metadata, description, and call-to-action buttons.
*   **Alignment:** The right-side content is vertically centered and left-aligned within its container.

## 2. Background & Atmosphere
*   **Base Layer:** A deep, dark teal/cyan gradient. 
    *   *Top:* `#0a2b2e`
    *   *Bottom:* `#020d0e`
*   **Texture Layer:** A subtle, grainy "fog" or "smoke" overlay to give depth to the battlefield atmosphere.
*   **Bottom Shadow:** A heavy black vignette/gradient at the very bottom to ground the character and hide the feet, blending into the UI.

## 3. Visual Assets (Images)

### A. The Samurai (Main Character)
*   **Description:** A high-resolution portrait of a man in traditional Japanese Samurai armor (O-yoroi) with a gold-trimmed kabuto (helmet). He is facing right in a profile view, holding a katana.
*   **Properties:** Backgroundless PNG.
*   **Placement:** Positioned on the left side, anchored to the bottom.
*   **Effect:** The bottom of the image should have a "dissolve" or "smoke" mask to blend seamlessly into the dark ground.
*   **Rotation:** 0 degrees (vertical).

### B. The Red Brushstroke
*   **Description:** A large, thick, textured red paint stroke resembling traditional Japanese calligraphy (Sumie style).
*   **Properties:** Backgroundless PNG.
*   **Placement:** Layered *behind* the Samurai. It starts from the top left and curves down behind his back.
*   **Color:** Vibrant Crimson Red (`#e60000`).

### C. Japanese Kanji
*   **Description:** The characters "将軍" (Shōgun) written in red vertical calligraphy.
*   **Placement:** Positioned immediately to the left of the English "SHŌGUN" title.

### D. Branding Logo (Top Right)
*   **Description:** "Night Owls" logo consisting of a white owl head icon inside a red circle, with "Night Owls" text in white sans-serif.
*   **Placement:** Absolute top-right corner with approx. 40px padding.

## 4. Typography & Text Styles

### A. Title: "SHŌGUN"
*   **Font:** A custom, elegant Serif font with high contrast and sharp serifs. 
    *   *Primary:* 'Cinzel' or 'Spectral'.
    *   *Fallback:* 'Times New Roman', serif.
*   **Styling:** All-caps, white color, wide letter spacing (approx 0.2em). The "Ō" features a distinct macron (horizontal bar) above it.
*   **Size:** Large (approx 80px - 100px).

### B. Sub-header: "SEASONS 01"
*   **Font:** Bold Sans-Serif (e.g., 'Inter' or 'Montserrat').
*   **Color:** Bright Red (`#ff0000`).
*   **Size:** 18px, uppercase, tracked out.

### C. Metadata Line
*   **Content:** "10 EPISODES • WAR | DRAMA | POLITICS • 2024"
*   **Font:** Medium Sans-Serif.
*   **Color:** White.
*   **Decoration:** A horizontal red line (3px height, 150px width) sits directly above this text.

### D. Synopsis Paragraph
*   **Font:** Clean Sans-Serif (e.g., 'Roboto' or 'Open Sans').
*   **Color:** White with 80% opacity for readability.
*   **Size:** 16px.
*   **Alignment:** Left-aligned, max-width of 500px to ensure line length is comfortable.

## 5. Interactive Elements (Buttons)

### A. "WATCH NOW" Button
*   **Background:** Solid Red (`#ff0000`).
*   **Text:** White, Bold Sans-Serif.
*   **Icon:** A white "Play" triangle to the left of the text.
*   **Shape:** Rectangular with very slight rounding (2px).
*   **Padding:** 15px 40px.

### B. "IMDb" Button
*   **Background:** Transparent with a 2px White border.
*   **Text:** "8.6 IMDb" in White.
*   **Icon:** A small yellow star icon (`#ffcc00`) to the left of the text.
*   **Shape:** Rectangular matching the "Watch Now" button.
*   **Padding:** 15px 40px.

## 6. CSS Specifics
*   **Container:** `display: flex; align-items: center; justify-content: space-between;`
*   **Character Layering:** 
    *   `z-index: 1`: Red Brushstroke
    *   `z-index: 2`: Samurai Image
    *   `z-index: 3`: Text Content
*   **Text Shadow:** A subtle black drop shadow on the "SHŌGUN" title to ensure it pops against the teal background.
