# Design Specification: Creamy Minimalist E-Commerce / Content Carousel

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Strategy
*   **Target Viewport:** Mobile Full Screen (`100vw` × `100vh`).
*   **Layout Structure:** A vertical content flow consisting of a top navigation/header zone, a central hero image area, detailed typographic hierarchy, and a persistent sticky Call-to-Action (CTA) button at the bottom.
*   **Corner Radii:** Remove all interior card border-radius properties. Content must stretch flush to the screen edges.

## 2. Color Palette
*   **Primary Background (Canvas):** Warm Cream / Off-White (`#F7F4EE` or `rgb(247, 244, 238)`).
*   **Primary Typography (Text):** Deep Espresso Brown (`#3A2E2B`).
*   **Secondary Typography / Muted Text:** Soft Taupe (`#8C7C75`).
*   **Accent / CTA Button Background:** Terracotta / Muted Rose (`#D98871` or `#C87A64`).
*   **CTA Button Text:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or very light cream (`#FCFBF9`).

## 3. Typography & Font Stack
*   **Primary Font Family:** Serif typeface with editorial, high-contrast characteristics (similar to *Playfair Display*, *Cormorant Garamond*, or *Bodoni*).
*   **Secondary/Body Font Family:** Clean, elegant Sans-Serif for subtitles and body copy (similar to *Montserrat*, *Lato*, or *Inter*).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif;` for headers; `font-family: 'Montserrat', sans-serif;` for body.

### Typographic Hierarchy (Mobile Scale):
1.  **Category / Subheader (Top):**
    *   *Font:* Sans-serif, all-caps, letter-spaced.
    *   *Size:* `11px` to `12px`.
    *   *Weight:* Medium (`500`).
    *   *Color:* Soft Taupe (`#8C7C75`).
    *   *Letter Spacing:* `0.15em`.
2.  **Main Title (Hero Heading):**
    *   *Font:* Serif, italicized variants preferred for artistic flair.
    *   *Size:* `24px` to `28px`.
    *   *Weight:* Regular/Normal (`400`).
    *   *Color:* Espresso Brown (`#3A2E2B`).
    *   *Line Height:* `1.2`.
3.  **Body / Descriptive Text:**
    *   *Font:* Sans-serif.
    *   *Size:* `13px` to `14px`.
    *   *Weight:* Light (`300`) to Regular (`400`).
    *   *Color:* Espresso Brown (`#3A2E2B`), slightly muted.
    *   *Line Height:* `1.5`.

## 4. Component Breakdown & Image Placement

### A. Header / Top Navigation
*   **Position:** Absolute top, centered.
*   **Content:** Small category indicator text (e.g., *“SKINCARE”*, *“RITUALS”*, or *“GLOW”*).
*   **Spacing:** `40px` top padding.

### B. Central Imagery (Visual Focus)
*   **Format:** Transparent PNGs (backgroundless) or precisely masked product photography matching the warm color grading of the background.
*   **Placement:** Centered horizontally, vertically positioned in the upper-middle third of the viewport.
*   **Visual Style:** Soft drop shadows (`rgba(58, 46, 43, 0.08)`) underneath the products to give a subtle floating effect.
*   **Specific Image Descriptions based on grid context:**
    *   *Image 1 (Skincare Bottle/Jar):* A minimalist, low-profile amber or dark ceramic cosmetic jar with a dark lid, resting on a subtle circular pedestal shadow. No rotation (0deg).
    *   *Image 2 (Botanical/Hands):* An artistic, line-art or stylized photographic illustration of hands interacting with natural elements, rendered in soft brown tones.
    *   *Image 3 (Apparel/Textile):* Folded soft loungewear or aesthetic minimalist garments in warm terracotta/brown tones.

### C. Body Copy Section
*   **Alignment:** Center-aligned.
*   **Spacing:** Sits directly below the central image with `24px` gap.
*   **Content width:** Constrained to `80%` of the screen width to maintain comfortable reading lines.

### D. Bottom Call-to-Action (CTA) Button
*   **Position:** Fixed or sticky at the bottom of the screen with generous bottom padding (`32px` from screen edge).
*   **Dimensions:** Full-width minus horizontal margins (e.g., `width: calc(100% - 48px)`, centered). Height: `48px` to `52px`.
*   **Styling:** 
    *   Background: Terracotta (`#D98871`).
    *   Border Radius: Fully rounded pill shape (`26px`) or softly rounded (`8px` depending on card style). *Recommendation: Soft pill shape for modern aesthetic.*
    *   Text: Uppercase, bold sans-serif, letter-spaced (`0.1em`), white text (e.g., *"SHOP THE COLLECTION"* or *"DISCOVER MORE"*).

## 5. Responsive Behavior & Spacing Rules
*   **Vertical Centering:** Use CSS Flexbox (`display: flex; flex-direction: column; justify-content: space-between; align-items: center;`) to distribute space evenly between the top header, middle image/text cluster, and bottom button.
*   **Padding:** Uniform horizontal screen padding of `24px`.
