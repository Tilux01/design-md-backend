# Design System

## Colors

### Brand & Primary Colors
*   **Green (Background Top / Deep):** `#488b60` (approximate deep sage/forest green)
*   **Green (Main Hero):** `#3e8254` (dominant canvas green)
*   **Cream / Soft Yellow (Background Bottom):** `#faebd7` (antique white / light cream)
*   **Mint Ice Cream Brand Green (NICK'S):** `#1f7040`

### Neutral Colors
*   **White:** `#ffffff` (text, navigation pill background, buttons)
*   **Dark Neutral / Text:** `#222222` (headings, body copy)
*   **Muted Text:** `#666666` (subheadings, footer copyright)

---

## Typography

### Font Family
*   **Primary / Sans-Serif:** Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif

### Scale & Hierarchy

#### Hero Heading (H1)
*   **Font Size:** `48px` - `56px`
*   **Font Weight:** `700` (Bold)
*   **Line Height:** `1.1`
*   **Color:** `#ffffff`

#### Body Copy
*   **Font Size:** `11px` - `13px`
*   **Font Weight:** `400` (Regular)
*   **Line Height:** `1.5`
*   **Color:** `#e0e0e0` / `#ffffff` (with high opacity)

#### Navigation Items
*   **Font Size:** `12px`
*   **Font Weight:** `500` (Medium)
*   **Color:** `#111111` (inside active pill) / `#ffffff`

---

## Layout & Spacing

*   **Page Structure:** Full-screen layout with a centered, rounded container (`border-radius: 24px` to `32px`, shadow elevation) floating on a solid sage green backdrop (`#5bb277`).
*   **Grid System:** 12-column layout base, split into left-hand hero text content and right-hand product carousel showcase.
*   **Container Max-Width:** `1200px` (inner web preview window).

---

## Components

### 1. Navigation Bar (Header)
*   **Logo:** Text logo `"Creamy"` in bold white, plus abstract icon.
*   **Nav Pill:** Centered floating pill with rounded corners (`border-radius: 50px`), white background, containing links: `HOME`, `MENU`, `ABOUT`, `CONTACT`.
*   **Active Link:** Black pill background with white text (`HOME`).
*   **Action Icons:** User profile icon and Shopping bag badge (with red notification dot).

### 2. Buttons
*   **Primary Button (ORDER NOW):**
    *   **Background:** `#111111` (Black)
    *   **Text:** `#ffffff` (White), Uppercase, `11px` Bold
    *   **Padding:** `10px 20px`
    *   **Border Radius:** `20px` (Pill shape)
*   **Secondary Button (SEE MENU ITEMS):**
    *   **Background:** Transparent
    *   **Border:** `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`
    *   **Text:** `#ffffff`, Uppercase, `11px`
    *   **Padding:** `10px 20px`
    *   **Border Radius:** `20px`

### 3. Product Showcase (Carousel)
*   **Active Item:** Large central tub (`NICK'S Mint Chokladchip`) featuring a 3D scoop of ice cream on top, 270 calories badge, and flavor graphics.
*   **Inactive Items:** Smaller tubs staggered to the right (perspective scale down).
*   **Portion Selectors:** Small circular selector pills (`50g`, `100g`, `500g`) floating top-right of the product stage.
*   **Carousel Navigation:** Left and right circular arrow buttons (`←`, `→`) centered below the main product tub.

### 4. Social Proof / Reviews Widget
*   **Avatars:** Stacked circular overlapping thumbnails of reviewers.
*   **Rating Text:** `"10K+ Reviews"` (Bold), followed by `"Customers are satisfied"` (Muted).

### 5. Footer
*   **Copyright:** `"© 2025 All rights reserved"` (bottom left, muted white).
*   **Social/Extra Links:** Minimal icon set bottom right.
*   **Watermark:** `"@uiuxzaid"` floating pill top-right and bottom-right outside the main window.
