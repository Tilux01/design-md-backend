# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Futuristic SaaS & AI Platform (Glassmorphism + Cyber-Neural Aesthetics).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio in the hero framing, centering attention on the high-impact central headline and dynamic neural orb element.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered canvas with a rich deep navy/black radial gradient background, semi-transparent frosted glass navbar, and floating high-contrast pill action buttons with subtle drop glows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges (`#1OT AI AGENT PLATFORM`), ambient radial glowing backgrounds, subtle glassmorphic backdrop filters, futuristic wireframe visualizers.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`, border width `1px` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-blue-500 inline-block"></span>`.
