# Design Specification: Fitness Platform Interface Clone

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

*   **View Mode:** Full-Screen viewport (`100vw` width, `100vh` height).
*   **Root Container Background:** A cinematic, soft gradient or photographic full-screen background featuring a dynamic action shot of an athlete in mid-motion wearing a white tank top, dark shorts, white sneakers, and futuristic sunglasses. The background should be subtly blurred/desaturated relative to the central UI glass container.
*   **Central Dashboard Frame:** The UI is housed in a floating, rounded "glassmorphism" card container centered on the screen.
    *   **Border Radius:** Large rounded corners (approx. `28px` to `32px`).
    *   **Backdrop Filter:** Frosted glass effect (`blur(20px)`) with a dark, semi-transparent charcoal background (`rgba(22, 24, 25, 0.85)`).
    *   **Border:** Thin, subtle inner border with light opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).

---

## 2. Typography & Font System

The interface uses a clean, modern, geometric sans-serif typeface (reminiscent of **Inter**, **SF Pro Display**, or **Plus Jakarta Sans**).

*   **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Type Scale:**
    *   **Hero / Headline (Cream Card):** `32px` / `36px` line-height, Weight: Semi-Bold (`600`), Color: `#161819` (Dark charcoal).
    *   **Navigation & Tabs:** `13px`, Weight: Medium (`500`), Color: `#A0A0A0` (Inactive), `#FFFFFF` (Active/Hover).
    *   **Body Copy:** `13px` / `18px` line-height, Weight: Regular (`400`), Color: `#5A5D5E` (Muted dark gray on cream background).
    *   **Small Labels / Metrics:** `11px`, Weight: Medium (`500`), Uppercase/Sentence case.
    *   **Footer Tagline ("Train smart..."):** `16px` / `24px`, Weight: Regular (`400`), Color: `#E0E0E0`, aligned bottom-right.

---

## 3. Top Navigation Bar

Located at the very top of the central dashboard container.

*   **Left Section (Nav Links):** Horizontal flex layout with generous spacing (`24px` gap).
    *   Items: `A-News`, `Training`, `Programs`, `Nutrition`, `Shop`, `Docs`.
    *   *Active State:* `A-News` is highlighted in solid white with a subtle pill indicator background.
*   **Middle Section (Search Bar):**
    *   **Shape:** Pill-shaped input field (`border-radius: 999px`).
    *   **Background:** Dark translucent gray (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
    *   **Icon:** Magnifying glass search icon on the left.
    *   **Placeholder Text:** `Search workouts` in muted gray (`#888888`).
*   **Right Section (Actions):**
    *   **Create Post Button:** Circular plus icon button with a soft warm cream/yellow background (`#E6E2C3`) paired with text `Create post`, text color `#161819`.

---

## 4. Content Grid Layout

The main dashboard body is divided into a sophisticated asymmetrical grid system:

### Column 1: Left Stack (Stacked Cards)

*   **Top Card (Article Preview):**
    *   **Background Image:** Full-bleed photographic image of an athlete running/training outdoors with warm sun flare.
    *   **Overlay:** Dark gradient overlay from bottom to top to ensure text legibility.
    *   **Content:** Title text overlay reading *"How to Build Strength Without Burning Out"*. Secondary small metadata text below. Top-right corner features a circular navigational arrow icon (`↗`).
*   **Bottom Card ("Recommended" Card):**
    *   **Background Color:** Soft warm pastel cream/yellow (`#F5F4D3`).
    *   **Padding:** Generous internal padding (`24px`).
    *   **Header:** Small uppercase label `"Recommended"` with a triple-dot menu icon (`•••`) aligned to the right.
    *   **Headline:** `"The Community Lift-Off showcased an inspiring lineup of everyday athletes"`.
    *   **Body Text:** `"From first-time lifters to seasoned pros, the Lift-Off proved that form, focus, and friends beat fancy gear. Coaches broke down cues for squats, presses, and pulls and we left with a simple plan you can start tonight."`
    *   **Interactive Element:** Pill-shaped outline button reading `"Read more"` with a dark border and arrow icon.
    *   **Footer inside card:** Horizontal rule divider with links: `"The truth about supplements"` on the left and `"Next"` with an arrow icon on the right.

### Column 2: Middle Stack (Metrics & Duration)

*   **Top Metric Card:**
    *   **Background:** Solid dark charcoal (`#1E2022`).
    *   **Content:** Large number `"30"` centered, with the word `"minutes"` underneath in muted text.
*   **Bottom Icon Card:**
    *   **Background:** Solid dark charcoal (`#1E2022`).
    *   **Content:** Centered minimalist lime-green water bottle/recovery icon with the word `"recovery"` underneath.

### Column 3: Right Stack (Hero Feature & Product Showcase)

*   **Background:** Full-height container featuring an action photography of an athlete captured mid-stride/jump in a white tank top and black shorts against an overcast sky.
*   **Top Action Buttons:**
    *   **"Add to cart" Pill Button:** Soft light green background (`#D2F8A4`) with dark text (`#161819`).
    *   **Plus Icon Button:** Circular button with a lime-green accent and black plus sign (`+`).
*   **Bottom-Right Floating Product Card ("Discover new product"):**
    *   **Shape:** Dark rounded container (Floating card over the hero image).
    *   **Image:** Product shot of a sleek black fitness shaker bottle with a red/orange accent cap (**Backgroundless asset** / isolated on dark background, slight diagonal rotation).
    *   **Text:** `"Discover new product"`.
    *   **Button:** Pill-shaped button reading `"Shop now"`.

1.  **Hero Athlete (Background & Column 3):** Cinematic sports photography, high contrast, cool color grading with desaturated blue/gray tones.
2.  **Article Preview Image:** Warm-toned outdoor action shot with dramatic natural lighting.
