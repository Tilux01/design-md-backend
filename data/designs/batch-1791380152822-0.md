# design.md: Fruite - Strawberry Beer Website Clone

This document serves as the complete technical blueprint for creating an exact, full-screen clone of the "Fruite - Strawberry Beer" landing page shown in the image.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport

*   **Fullscreen & Edge-to-Edge:** The design occupies the entire viewport (`width: 100vw; height: 100vh; overflow: hidden;`). No desktop frames, bezels, or browser window chrome are included.
*   **Base Background:** A vibrant, radial-gradient pink/red backdrop.
    *   *Center/Focal Point:* Bright, saturated magenta-pink (`#ff1d58` or similar).
    *   *Edges:* Slightly deeper crimson/magenta (`#d10034`).
*   **Grid System:** Centered flexbox layout. The primary product can (`.can-container`) acts as the absolute center point, with large typography (`STRA` and `ERRY`) flanking it horizontally.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Fonts
*   **Brand Logo (`Fruite`):** 
    *   *Style:* Cursive, playful script font (resembling a brush script or lobster-style font).
    *   *Fallback:* `cursive`, `Brush Script MT`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Navigation & Header (`Home`, `Shop`, `Contact`, `Choose Your Favorite Beer`):**
    *   *Style:* Clean, modern geometric sans-serif (light weight).
    *   *Fallback:* `'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif`.
*   **Hero Typography (`STRA` / `ERRY`):**
    *   *Style:* Ultra-bold, massive sans-serif or bold geometric display font (all caps, heavy tracking/kerning to accommodate the central can).
    *   *Fallback:* `Impact`, `'Arial Black'`, `sans-serif`.

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation Bar (Top Header)
*   **Logo (Left):** 
    *   Text: "Fruite"
    *   Color: Pure White (`#ffffff`)
    *   Styling: Italicized script.
*   **Navigation Links (Center/Left-Center):**
    *   Items: `Home` | `Shop` | `Contact`
    *   Color: White with high transparency or muted grey (`rgba(255,255,255,0.8)`).
    *   Hover State: Full opacity white (`#ffffff`).
*   **Call-to-Action (Top Right):**
    *   Text: "Choose Your Favorite Beer" + Beer glass/mug icon (`🍺`).
    *   Styling: Subtle pill/badge styling or plain text link with icon, highlighted in white.

---

### B. Hero Section (Center Stage)

#### 1. Background Typography (`STRA` & `ERRY`)
*   **Text:** `STRA` (Left of the can) and `ERRY` (Right of the can).
*   **Font Properties:**
    *   Color: Pure White (`#ffffff`).
    *   Opacity: Near solid or slight translucency to blend with the background lighting effects.
    *   Size: Extremely large viewport-scaled font (`clamp(8rem, 15vw, 16rem)`).
    *   Z-Index: Placed *behind* the central can.

#### 2. Central Product Can (`.beer-can`)
*   **Position:** Exactly centered (`position: absolute; left: 50%; top: 50%; transform: translate(-50%, -50%);`).
*   **Visual Elements:**
    *   Can Body: Deep red/magenta metallic finish with subtle vertical lighting gradients to simulate a cylindrical metal can.
    *   Label: Oval/rounded rectangular central emblem in white/cream with the text `"STRAWBERRY BEER"` curved along the top edge, and a detailed strawberry graphic in the center.
    *   Z-Index: Placed *above* the background text (`STRA` / `ERRY`).

---

### C. Floating Decorative Elements (Strawberries)

Scattered across the screen are realistic, high-resolution sliced and whole strawberries with green leafy tops. 

*   **Top Left:** Single whole strawberry angled diagonally with a green stem pointing up-left.
*   **Top Right:** Whole strawberry facing forward/right.
*   **Bottom Left:** Whole strawberry resting near the bottom-left edge.
*   **Bottom Right (Cluster):** A dynamic cluster featuring a whole strawberry, a sliced strawberry showing internal white/red seed geometry, and lush green leaves.
*   **Shadows/Depth:** Each strawberry has a subtle drop shadow to lift it off the flat background, creating a 3D layered effect.

---

## 4. Animations & Micro-Interactions (CSS / JS)

To match the live website experience:

1.  **Floating / Parallax Animation:**
    *   The strawberries should have a gentle, infinite floating keyframe animation (subtle `translateY` oscillation and slight rotation) to mimic floating in liquid or air.
2.  **Can Hover Effect:**
    *   Hovering over the central can triggers a slight scale-up (`scale(1.05)`) and enhanced drop shadow.
3.  **Page Load Entrance:**
    *   *Fade-in:* Background fades from dark to bright pink.
    *   *Slide-in:* `STRA` slides in from the left, `ERRY` slides in from the right, and the central can scales up from 0 to 1 with a smooth cubic-bezier easing curve.
