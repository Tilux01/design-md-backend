# Design Specification: DVSY Brand Website Clone

## 1. Global & Layout Specifications

### 1.1 Screen & Canvas
- **Layout Type:** Full-screen responsive layout (no bounding container frames; edge-to-edge).
- **Background Color:** Deep charcoal/near-black (`#111111` or `#131313`).
- **Accent Color:** Warm coral/orange (`#FF6B4A` or `#FF5B36`) used for primary call-to-actions, badges, and key highlight elements.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Backgrounds: `#111111` (Primary Dark), `#1A1A1A` (Card/Container Dark)
  - Text Primary: `#FFFFFF` (White)
  - Text Secondary: `#A0A0A0` (Muted Grey)
  - Accents: `#FF5B36` (Coral Orange)
  - Borders/Lines: Subtle semi-transparent white/grey (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`)

---

## 2. Typography & Font Stack

### 2.1 Font Families
- **Primary Headers & Display Text:** Modern geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Syne*, *Space Grotesk*, or fallback to *Helvetica Neue*, *Arial*, sans-serif).
- **Body Text:** Clean, highly legible sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Roboto*, or fallback to *Arial*, sans-serif).

### 2.2 Typography Scale
- **Main Hero Headline ("DESIGN & FREEDOM"):** 
  - Size: ~72px–88px (Responsive clamp)
  - Weight: Bold (700)
  - Tracking/Letter-spacing: Tight
  - Line-height: 1.05
- **Section Headers ("WHERE FASHION MEETS FREEDOM", "OUR ADVANTAGES"):** 
  - Size: ~36px–48px
  - Weight: Bold (700)
- **Subheaders & Card Titles ("INDEPENDENCY", "UNIQUITY", "QUALITY", "SUSTAINABILITY"):** 
  - Size: ~16px–18px
  - Weight: Semi-bold (600)
  - Tracking: Wide letter-spacing
- **Body & Description Text:** 
  - Size: ~14px–15px
  - Weight: Regular (400)
  - Line-height: 1.5

---

## 3. Section-by-Section Breakdown

### 3.1 Navigation Bar (Header)
- **Logo:** "DVSY" in crisp white, high-contrast, bold uppercase geometric font.
- **Nav Links:** DESIGNERS, COLLABS, EVENTS, BLOG, CARD.
  - Color: `#FFFFFF`
  - Font Size: ~12px–13px (Uppercase)
  - Hover State: Slight opacity drop or accent color shift.
- **CTA Button ("GET IN TOUCH"):**
  - Background: `#FF5B36` (Coral Orange)
  - Text: White, Bold, Uppercase, ~12px
  - Shape: Rounded-pill or subtle rounded rectangle with generous horizontal padding.

---

### 3.2 Hero Section
- **Visual Composition:** 
  - Left side: Large typography ("DESIGN & FREEDOM") overlapping/adjacent to a high-fashion editorial portrait of a model wearing a tailored red suit and sunglasses against a vertical slatted background.
  - Subtext: *"Explore Independent Style by Embracing Uniqueness with Our Exclusive Designer Apparel"*
- **Interactive Element:** 
  - "↓ LEARN MORE" pill button featuring a downward arrow icon inside a circular outline.

---

### 3.3 Feature Cards Grid (4-Column)
- **Cards:** INDEPENDENCY, UNIQUITY, QUALITY, SUSTAINABILITY.
- **Container Styling:**
  - Background: `#1A1A1A`
  - Border: Subtle thin border (`1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`)
  - Padding: Generous internal spacing.
- **Card Content:**
  - Title: Uppercase, bold, tracked out.
  - Description: Short, concise summary text in muted grey (`#A0A0A0`).
  - Action Link: Circular arrow button (↗ icon pointing top-right) with label *"LEARN MORE"*.

---

### 3.4 Statistics Counter Row
- **Metrics Displayed:**
  - `150+` DESIGNERS
  - `500+` CLIENTS
  - `20K+` MASTERPIECES
  - `50+` EVENTS
- **Styling:**
  - Numbers: Extra-large font weight, clean white display.
  - Labels: Small uppercase muted text positioned below the numbers.

---

### 3.5 "About" / Editorial Split Section
- **Left Column:**
  - Small tracker label: `ABOUT`
  - Headline: *"WHERE FASHION MEETS FREEDOM"*
  - Two-column descriptive text block detailing brand philosophy, independent designers, and customer individuality.
- **Right Column:**
  - Editorial photograph showing a model holding a luxury handbag walking down modern architectural stairs.

---

### 3.6 "Our Advantages" Section
- **Header:** *"OUR ADVANTAGES"* with matching typography style.
- **Layout Grid (Asymmetric Editorial Grid):**
  - Combines lifestyle photography (full-body shot of model in cream apparel) with structured content cards.
- **Advantage Cards:**
  - **INDEPENDENT DESIGNERS**
  - **EXCLUSIVE & UNIQUITY**
  - **HIGH QUALITY**
  - **ECO-FRIENDLY**
  - Each card includes descriptive copy and an interactive `↗ EXPLORE` action link.
- **Secondary Image:**
  - Tailor's workroom display featuring multiple draped mannequin forms showing garment construction.

---

## 4. Animation & Interactive States (Interactions Spec)

- **Hover Effects on Cards:** 
  - Subtle elevation/lift or border-color transition to highlight interactivity.
- **Buttons & Links:** 
  - Scale or color brightness transition on hover (`transition: all 0.3s ease`).
- **Scroll Animations (Recommended):** 
  - Fade-in and slide-up (`translate-y` animation) for images and cards as the user scrolls down the viewport.
