# Design Specification: Pepsi Zero Sugar Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Canvas
- **Theme**: Premium dark mode, high-contrast product landing page.
- **Canvas**: Fullscreen viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), overflow hidden.
- **Base Background**: `#141414` (solid dark charcoal) with a faint radial gradient spotlight centered behind the product can (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`).

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary Background**: `#141414`
- **Card/Container Surface**: `#1E1E1E` (semi-opaque dark grey)
- **Active Accent**: `#C8102E` (Pepsi crimson red)
- **Primary Text**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Muted Text**: `#7A7A7A`
- **Border / Lines**: `#2A2A2A`

---

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Font**: `Montserrat` or `Gilroy` (Geometric Sans-Serif).
- **Fallback**: `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `Arial`, sans-serif.
- **Text Styles**:
  - **Hero Title**: Bold, 48px, line-height 1.1, white.
  - **Body / Subtitles**: Medium, 12px–14px, muted grey (`#7A7A7A`).
  - **Navigation Links**: Medium, 11px, uppercase, letter-spacing 1px.
  - **Metrics & Numbers**: Semi-bold, 20px–24px, white.
  - **Microcopy / Legal**: Regular, 9px–10px, muted grey.

---

## 4. Layout & Components

### 4.1 Header (Top Bar)
- **Height**: 80px, horizontal padding 48px.
- **Logo (Left)**: Minimal Pepsi globe glyph (red/white) + bold uppercase "PEPSI" wordmark.
- **Navigation (Center)**:
  - Links: `PRODUCTS`, `WHAT'S NEW`, `NEWSLETTER`, `CONTACT US`.
  - Active item indicator: Small red dot (`#C8102E`) placed directly left of `PRODUCTS`.
- **Action (Right)**:
  - Pill button: "BUY PRODUCTS", transparent fill, 1px white border, uppercase 11px font.

### 4.2 Left Vertical Toolbar
- **Position**: Absolute left, vertically centered.
- **Width**: 60px.
- **Items**: Stacked social icons (Instagram, YouTube, Twitter, Info) in `#7A7A7A`, hover to `#FFFFFF`.

### 4.3 Left Column: Product Details & Controls
- **Width**: ~50% screen width; left margin 100px.
- **Back Navigation**: Left chevron arrow (`←`) positioned above the title.
- **Heading**: "Pepsi Zero Sugar".
- **Ingredients Paragraph**: 12px muted grey text detailing beverage ingredients.
- **Nutrition Facts Matrix**:
  - Horizontal strip of 5 metric tiles.
  - **Tile 1 (Active)**: Filled `#C8102E`, text: `Amount Per Serving` / `0 cl` / `0%`.
  - **Tiles 2–5**: Dark grey fill `#202020`, borders `#2C2C2C`:
    - Total Fat: `0g` / `0%`
    - Sodium: `40mg` / `2%`
    - Total Carb: `0g` / `0%`
    - Protein: `0g` / `0%`
  - Footnote: Small 9px disclaimer directly beneath tiles.
- **Primary CTA**: Pill button "BUY NOW" with a subtle arrow icon.
- **Volume Selector (Bottom Left)**:
  - Row of size silhouettes (can/bottle outlines: 0.15, 0.33, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2 L).
  - Selected size (`0.33`): Inverted white rectangular badge behind the label.

### 4.4 Right Column: Product Presentation
- **Flavor Switcher (Top Right)**: Text "WILD CHERRY →" in muted white/grey.
- **Hero Image (Can)**:
  - Rendered at ~60vh height, centered vertically in the right half.
  - Soft horizontal contact shadow cast on the floor.
- **Serving Detail Card (Bottom Right)**:
  - Attached to bottom-right corner.
  - Dimensions: ~150px wide, 90px tall.
  - Background: `#101010` with a solid red bottom edge (`#C8102E`, 8px height).
  - Text: `330 ml` (22px bold), `Serving Size / 1 serving per container` (10px muted).

---

## 5. Image Asset Specification

### Product Can
- **Asset Type**: High-resolution PNG with transparent background.
- **Subject**: Sleek 330ml aluminum can of Pepsi Max / Zero Sugar.
- **Coloring**: Matte black lower half with bold red top segment, separated by a white stylized ribbon curve.
- **Details**: Realistic condensation droplets across the surface, metallic top rim, gloss reflections on the right curvature.
- **Placement**: Centered vertically in the right quadrant, standing upright at a slight front-facing angle.
