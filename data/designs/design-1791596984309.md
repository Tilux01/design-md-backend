# Design Specification: "Mr L Production" Architecture Landing Page

## 1. Global Architecture & Theming

### 1.1. Layout Philosophy
The UI is a single-page, full-bleed hero layout. It utilizes a heavy "Glassmorphism" aesthetic, overlaying frosted, translucent UI panels on top of a stunning, high-resolution background image. The primary shape motif is the "pill" (fully rounded rectangles) and perfect circles.

### 1.2. Global Background Image
*   **Description:** A high-quality, photorealistic architectural render or photograph. It depicts a serene landscape at sunrise or sunset. In the distant background are majestic, slightly snow-capped, rugged mountains. Below the mountains is a calm, expansive lake reflecting the soft, warm light. On the right side, occupying the mid-to-foreground, is a highly modern, two-story circular luxury home built into a rocky terrain. The home features extensive floor-to-ceiling glass windows, showing a warmly lit interior that contrasts with the cooler tones of the landscape. Outside the lower level, there is a patio with sun loungers near the water.
*   **Properties:** Full-bleed (`width: 100vw; height: 100vh`), `object-fit: cover`, positioned to ensure the circular house on the right is fully visible without being obscured by the main text.
*   **Backgroundless:** No. This is a solid, full-scene photograph.

### 1.3. Color Palette
*   **Primary Dark (Buttons, Main Text):** `#1A1A1A` (Almost black/dark charcoal)
*   **Primary Light (Text on dark backgrounds):** `#FFFFFF`
*   **Glass Surface Base (Light):** `rgba(245, 240, 235, 0.65)` (A very warm, off-white translucent base).
*   **Glass Surface Base (Dark/Card 3):** `rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.85)` to a solid `#222222`.
*   **Text Secondary/Muted:** `#555555` or `rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.6)`
*   **Glassmorphism Effect:** All glass panels require a background blur (e.g., `backdrop-filter: blur(16px); -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(16px);`). They also feature a very subtle, 1px solid white/light-grey inner border to define their edges (`border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3);`).

### 1.4. Typography
The design relies on a contrast between a highly legible geometric sans-serif for UI elements and a sophisticated serif for the main headline.

*   **Primary UI Font (Sans-Serif):** `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, sans-serif. 
    *   *Usage:* Navigation, buttons, body copy, small labels.
    *   *Characteristics:* Clean, geometric, highly legible at small sizes.
*   **Headline Font (Serif):** `Playfair Display`, `Lora`, `Georgia`, serif.
    *   *Usage:* Exclusively for the large "Spaces That Inspire" text.
    *   *Characteristics:* High contrast between thick and thin strokes, elegant, editorial feel.
