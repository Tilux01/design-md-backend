# Design System & Implementation Blueprint

---

## 1. Global Styles & Design Tokens

### Colors
- **Light Background (`--bg-light`):** `#EFEFF1` (Very light cool gray/off-white)
- **Dark Background (`--bg-dark`):** `#161622` (Deep navy/charcoal)
- **Primary Accent / Brand (`--accent-purple`):** `#8C62A8` (Muted violet)
- **Text Light (`--text-light`):** `#111111` (Near black)
- **Text Dark (`--text-dark`):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white)
- **Text Muted (`--text-muted`):** `#888890` (Cool gray for secondary labels)
- **Border / Line (`--border-line`):** `#444455` (Subtle dark stroke for markers)

### Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif
- **Scale:**
  - **Logo / Nav:** 13px, Medium (500), Uppercase
  - **Hero Letters (P-O-R-C-H-E):** 48px – 64px, Bold (700), Uppercase, Letter-spacing: `0.1em`
  - **Sub-labels (Diesel, Watches, Tough, Modern):** 11px, Medium, Uppercase, Letter-spacing: `0.15em`
  - **Action Button ("Explore More"):** 11px, Semi-bold, Uppercase

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Containers

- **Full-Screen Split Layout:** 100vw width, 100vh height, zero margins, fixed viewport.
- **Background Split:** Left side takes `50%` (`--bg-light`), right side takes `50%` (`--bg-dark`).
- **Central Focus:** A large purple overlapping graphic ("S" or curvy ribbon shape) centered horizontally and vertically, bridging the two halves.
- **Grid / Alignment System:**
  - Absolute positioning for floating utility elements (Content drawer, Print button, Navigation headers).
  - Flexbox for footer items and carousel controls.

---

## 3. Component Specifications

### A. Header Navigation
- **Left Side:**
  - Brand Logo text: `"Diesel"` (Font-weight: 700, 14px)
  - Links: `"Store"`, `"Shop"`, `"Collection"` (Color: `--text-muted`, hover to `--text-light`)
- **Right Side:**
  - Links: `"Cart"`, `"Login/Registration"`, `"Profile"` (Color: `--text-muted`, hover to `--text-dark`)

### B. Vertical Side Elements
- **Left Vertical Tag:**
  - Box container with thin border, vertical text `"| Content"`, rotated or column-flex, aligned left-center.
- **Right Vertical Tag:**
  - Box container with thin border, vertical text `"PRINT"`, aligned right-center.

### C. Hero Central Section ("PORCHE" + Watch + Ribbon)
- **Lettering Placement:**
  - `P O R` positioned on the left light background.
  - `C H E` positioned on the right dark background.
- **Central Ribbon / Shape:**
  - Smooth vector path filled with `--accent-purple` (`#8C62A8`).
  - Drop shadow creating a 3D depth effect over the background split.
- **Product Image (Watch):**
  - High-res product shot of a metallic chronograph watch centered directly on top of the purple ribbon shape.
  - Subtle drop shadow underneath the watch case.
- **Axis Labels & Markers:**
  - Thin horizontal rules (`--border-line`) pointing to the center axis.
  - Left labels: `"Diesel"` (top), `"Tough"` (bottom).
  - Right labels: `"Watches"` (top), `"Modern"` (bottom).
  - Center tags: `"Special Edition"` (top-center), `"Rid..."` (bottom-center).

### D. Call to Action Button ("Explore More")
- **Container:** White filled pill/rounded rectangle box positioned at the bottom curve of the purple ribbon.
- **Text:** `"Explore More"` in dark uppercase font.

### E. Footer Components
- **Bottom Left:**
  - `"World wide Shipping & Support"` | `"Customer Care"`
  - Carousel navigation control box containing left/right arrow icons (`<` `>`).
- **Bottom Right:**
  - Social links: `"Facebook"`, `"Instagram"`, `"Twitter"` in muted uppercase text.

---
