# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Glassmorphism & Modern SaaS Dashboard System.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-density UI component kit utilizing layered depth cards, distinct surface elevations, and precise grid alignment on a near-black canvas.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-tier dark surface hierarchy (`#121212` to `#1E1E1E`) paired with subtle backdrop blur (`blur-xl`), ultra-thin inner borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and high-contrast vibrant accents (Vibrant Orange `#FF6B00` and Soft Yellow `#FFE17D`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges (`+3.17 (+0.74%)`), ambient orange and yellow glowing accents, translucent dark glass cards (`bg-opacity-80`), and precise border highlights.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 10px`, font-size `11px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
