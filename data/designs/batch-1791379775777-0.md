# Design Specification: Cannabis Lab Concept Interface

## 1. Global Styles & Design Tokens

### Color Palette
*   **Background (Canvas):** Soft warm beige gradient `#F5EBE1` to `#EADCCF`
*   **Glassmorphic Surfaces:**
    *   Primary Card Background: Semi-transparent white overlay `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)` with a backdrop blur (`blur(20px)`).
    *   Borders: Subtle white-to-transparent gradient borders to simulate physical glass depth.
*   **Typography Colors:**
    *   Primary Text / Headers: Deep charcoal/black `#121212`
    *   Secondary Text / Labels: Muted warm gray `#6E6A66`
    *   Accents: Vivid sunset orange `#FF7A00` to `#FF5500` (used in gradients and geometric focal elements)
*   **UI Elements:**
    *   Dark Pill Button Background: `#111111`
    *   Input Fields: Soft cream/translucent white `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`

### Typography
*   **Primary Font Family:** *Inter*, *SF Pro Display*, or clean geometric sans-serif alternatives.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
*   **Scale:**
    *   **Main Dates / Hero Titles:** 36px - 48px, Bold / Medium weight.
    *   **Section Headers (Log in, New in):** 24px - 28px, Semi-bold.
    *   **Body & Input Text:** 14px - 16px, Regular.
    *   **Labels & Microcopy:** 10px - 12px, Regular/Medium.

---

## 2. Layout & Composition
*   **Grid System:** Asymmetric layout featuring floating glassmorphic panels layered over a 3D-rendered abstract editorial background (smooth spheres, textured rocks, cacti, and glass domes).
*   **Responsive Viewport:** Designed for a high-end desktop/tablet showcase display (minimum width: 1200px recommended for exact spatial positioning).

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. Authentication Card (Left Panel)
*   **Dimensions & Structure:** Rounded rectangular glass card (`border-radius: 24px`).
*   **Header:**
    *   Top Left: "Cannabis Lab" (12px, uppercase/spaced, subtle gray).
    *   Top Right: "Sign up" toggle link.
*   **Main Title:** "Log in" (28px, bold, `#121212`).
*   **Social Auth Button:**
    *   Pill shape (`border-radius: 50px`), bordered.
    *   Contains Facebook icon + "Facebook" label.
*   **Input Fields:**
    *   Email Field: Icon (`@`), placeholder "e-mail address", rounded pill container.
    *   Password Field: Icon (key/lock symbol), placeholder "password", right-aligned "I forgot" button inside or adjacent.
*   **Footer Microcopy:**
    *   Disclaimer text (10px): *"For use by adults only (18 years of age and older)..."* with an underline on "hotline."
    *   Action Button: Black circular pill button with a right-pointing arrow (`>`).
    *   Safety Tagline: *"Please consume responsibly!"* centered at the bottom.

### B. "New In" Promotional Card (Bottom-Left)
*   **Structure:** Solid dark charcoal/black floating banner (`border-radius: 20px`, background `#111111`, text color white).
*   **Content:**
    *   Title: "New in" (24px).
    *   Subtitle: "C.Lab Joints" (18px, warm white/gray).
    *   Action: "Discover" text button anchored to the bottom-right of the card.

### C. Event / Grand Opening Mobile Card (Right Panel)
*   **Structure:** Tall vertical card resembling a smartphone frame interface (`border-radius: 28px`, pure white background on the right half, warm gradient blur on the left half).
*   **Header Section:**
    *   Top-Left Split Typography: "Thu" (large, bold) / "24th" (stacked underneath).
    *   Top-Right: "Grand opening" / "New store" (right-aligned, subtle metadata).
*   **Visual Graphic:**
    *   A vibrant orange/amber glowing circle overlapping the central division of the card, with a soft radial blur effect.
*   **Event Details:**
    *   Time: "18 PM"
    *   Address: "Kerkstraat 12B, Amsterdam"
*   **Footer:**
    *   Logo/Mark: Minimalist starburst/abstract sun icon + "C.Lab".
    *   CTA Button: Black pill button with "Join in" and a right arrow (`>`).

---

## 4. Micro-Interactions & Animations (CSS / Framer Motion)

*   **Glassmorphism Hover State:**
    *   `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` transitions to `blur(25px)` on card hover with a subtle scale up (`transform: scale(1.01)`).
*   **Buttons:**
    *   Black pill buttons scale slightly (`scale(1.05)`) and darken or glow softly when hovered.
*   **Background Elements:**
    *   Subtle floating animation (keyframe float up/down by 5–10px) on the 3D-style spheres and glass domes to create depth.
