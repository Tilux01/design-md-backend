# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Organic Botanical Editorial & Clean Nature-Tech SaaS. Combines earthy terracotta and forest green tones with organic textures (botanical leaf overlays, smooth gradients, and subtle shadows).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Generous spacing with a high-end editorial feel. Balanced padding across modular cards, structured grids, and spacious container layouts.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-tier layered cards with soft inner shadows, subtle multi-stop linear gradients, rounded corners (`border-radius: 16px` to `24px`), and delicate contrasting translucent borders.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Botanical editorial aesthetic, high-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient organic glow, frosted glassmorphism cards, micro-iconography.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `0.75rem`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(181, 132, 99, 0.15)`, border width `1px` (`rgba(181, 132, 99, 0.3)`).
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
