```markdown
# Design Specification: Men's Modern Apparel E-Commerce Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
*   **Viewport Handling:** Full-width responsive layout (`width: 100%`, `max-width: 1440px` centered container for inner content).
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   `--background-primary`: Off-white / Cream `#F4F1EA` (Main page background)
    *   `--background-secondary`: Subtle warm gray `#EAE5DE` (Hero background texture, card backings)
    *   `--text-primary`: Deep charcoal `#1A1A1A` (Primary headings, high-contrast text)
    *   `--text-secondary`: Muted stone gray `#666666` (Subheadings, descriptions, navigation)
    *   `--accent-gold`: Warm bronze `#C5A880` (Promotional badges, price highlights)
    *   `--border-color`: Light neutral border `#E0DCD4`
*   **Typography Stack:**
    *   *Primary Serif (Headings/Hero):* `Playfair Display`, Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif
    *   *Secondary Sans-Serif (Nav, Buttons, Body, Prices):* `Inter`, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif

---

## 2. Navigation Header
*   **Layout:** Fixed or sticky top bar, 100% width, transparent background blending into the hero section, utilizing dark typography.
*   **Logo:** 
    *   Text: "MEN’S"
    *   Style: Serif, tracked out (`letter-spacing: 2px`), uppercase, bold (~24px).
*   **Navigation Links (Center/Left):**
    *   Items: `Home`, `Shop`, `Clothing`, `Footwear`, `Accessories`, `Lookbook`, `Sale`
    *   Style: Sans-serif (~14px, medium weight), color `#1A1A1A` with subtle hover transitions (underline or fade to `--text-secondary`).
*   **Action Icons (Right):**
    *   Search icon (magnifying glass)
    *   Wishlist icon (heart outline)
    *   User account icon (profile outline)
    *   Shopping bag icon (tote bag outline with counter badge potential)
    *   Spacing: ~24px gap between icons.

---

## 3. Hero Section (`Define Your Style`)
*   **Layout:** Asymmetric split banner spanning full width with a rich textural warm-gray backdrop.
*   **Left Column (Typography & CTA):**
    *   *Main Heading:* "Define Your Style" (Serif, ~52px–64px, line-height: 1.1, color `#1A1A1A`).
    *   *Subheading:* "Modern Menswear for Every Occasion" (Sans-serif, ~16px, color `#666666`, margin-top: 16px).
    *   *Buttons:*
        *   Primary Button ("Shop Now"): Solid fill (`#1A1A1A` or cream depending on contrast, shown as rounded pill button with dark text/light background or solid cream with dark border, padding: 12px 28px, border-radius: 30px).
        *   Secondary Button ("New Arrivals"): Transparent background, thin border, matching pill shape.
*   **Right Column (Visual):**
    *   High-fashion editorial photography of a male model seated against a textured wall, wearing a beige wool blazer, dark knit top, and luxury wristwatch.
    *   Integration: Soft gradient drop-off blending the photography naturally into the background texture.

---

## 4. "Essentials" Section
*   **Section Title:** 
    *   Text: "ESSENTIALS"
    *   Style: Uppercase, tracking +2px, small sans-serif (~13px), centered or left-aligned with flanking subtle horizontal divider lines.
*   **Grid Layout:** 
    *   5-column grid of category cards.
    *   Gap: ~16px–20px.
*   **Card Design:**
    *   *Image:* Full-bleed vertical model photography showcasing specific outfit types.
    *   *Border Radius:* Rounded corners (~12px).
    *   *Label Bar:* Bottom overlay or inset label containing the category name centered inside a pill-shaped container or clean white surface base.
    *   *Categories:*
        1. Casual Wear
        2. Formal Wear
        3. Street Style
        4. Outerwear
        5. Accessories

---

## 5. Promotional Banner ("Autumn Collection")
*   **Layout:** Full-width horizontal promotional block with split visual framing.
*   **Left Side (Copy):**
    *   *Heading:* "Autumn Collection – Up to 30% OFF" (Serif, ~32px).
    *   *CTA Button:* "SHOP COLLECTION" (Outlined or solid pill button with uppercase tracking).
*   **Right Side (Visual):**
    *   Editorial image showing a model in layered autumn outerwear, cropped dynamically to fit the banner container.

---

## 6. "Trending Now" Section
*   **Section Title:** 
    *   Text: "TRENDING NOW"
    *   Style: Matching the "ESSENTIALS" header style (uppercase, tracking, subtle horizontal rules).
*   **Product Grid:**
    *   5-column product card grid.
*   **Product Card Specifications:**
    *   *Image Container:* Clean minimal background, high-res product/editorial shot. Badges (e.g., "Sale", "New") positioned absolute top-left.
    *   *Product Title:* Sans-serif, ~14px, regular weight (`#1A1A1A`), e.g., "Beige Bomber Jacket", "Smart Casual Blazer".
    *   *Price & Rating Row:*
        *   Price: Bold sans-serif (~14px), e.g., "$149", "$199", "$79". Discounted items display old price struck through in lighter gray.
        *   Rating: 5-star rating system rendered using gold star icons (`★★★★★`) next to review counts (e.g., `(358)`).

---

## 7. Interactive & Animation Specifications (CSS / JS)
*   **Hover States:**
    *   *Navigation Links:* Smooth opacity transition (`opacity: 0.7` on hover).
    *   *Product/Category Cards:* Subtle scale-up zoom on the background/model image (`transform: scale(1.03)` with `transition: transform 0.4s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
    *   *Buttons:* Background color inversion or elevation shadow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 10px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`).
*   **Scroll Animations (Fade-In):**
    *   Sections (`Essentials`, `Autumn Collection`, `Trending Now`) animate upward with a fading opacity (`opacity: 0` to `1`, `translateY(20px)` to `0`) upon entering the viewport using Intersection Observer.
```
