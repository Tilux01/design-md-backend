# `design.md` — BRAIX Brain-Computer Interface Landing Page

This document provides exact, pixel-level implementation specifications to recreate the full-screen BRAIX neurotechnology landing page interface.

---

## 1. Canvas & Viewport Architecture

*   **Target Viewport**: Full-screen single viewport (`100vw` × `100vh`, non-scrolling desktop experience, recommended base target: `1440px × 900px` to `1920px × 1080px`).
*   **Base Surface Background**:
    *   Multi-stage subtle atmospheric gradient:
        *   Top-left / Top-center: `#E2ECF7` (soft periwinkle mist)
        *   Center: `#D0E4FA` (ice blue glow)
        *   Bottom / Bottom-right: `#A8CEF6` (vibrant soft sky cyan)
    *   CSS implementation:
        ```css
        background: radial-gradient(
          circle at 65% 60%,
          rgba(168, 206, 246, 0.95) 0%,
          rgba(208, 228, 250, 0.8) 45%,
          rgba(226, 236, 247, 1) 100%
        );
        ```

### 5.3. Central Visual Anchor (Portrait & AR Hardware)

#### Visual Asset Description
*   **Subject**: Clean, profile-to-three-quarter view of a young East Asian man looking upwards and to the right with focused aspiration.
*   **Headwear / Device**: Sleek, futuristic enterprise AR headset / smart glasses.
    *   *Chassis*: Matte black/charcoal brow bridge containing miniaturized forward-facing camera/depth sensors.
    *   *Optics*: Angular, transparent glass prisms/lenses projecting faint internal HUD holographic reflections (tinted pale cyan/blue) with gold/silver fine wire perimeter rims.
    *   *Temples*: Smooth, streamlined dark gray mechanical arm running along the side of the head past the ear.
*   **Lighting & Color**:
    *   Warm, realistic skin tones with natural rim lighting along the nose, ear, and cheek.
    *   Deep natural dark brown/black textured hair styled back.
*   **Framing & Transparency**:
    *   The subject has a completely backgroundless cut-out upper contour.
    *   **Bottom Edge**: Does not cut off abruptly; it is smoothly gradient-masked into the cyan/periwinkle atmospheric background from the mid-neck down (`mask-image: linear-gradient(to bottom, black 65%, transparent 100%)`).
*   **Z-Index & Placement**:
    *   Horizontal position: Anchored centrally, offset slightly right (`left: 32%` to `right: 28%`).
    *   Vertical position: Base aligns with the bottom viewport edge, top of head reaches approximately `100px` below top viewport edge.
    *   Z-layering: Sits behind the right metric cards, sits behind the "MIND" text.

## 6. Micro-Interactions & Transitions

1.  **Nav Items**: Subtle opacity shift from `1.0` to `0.65` on `:hover` with `transition: opacity 180ms ease`.
2.  **Explore CTA Inner Circle**:
    *   Hover: Scale up from `1.0` to `1.05` (`transform: scale(1.05)`).
    *   Outer Ring: Subtle pulse animation (faint opacity oscillations between `0.4` and `0.8`).
3.  **Right Metric Cards**:
    *   Subtle float-in animation on initial render: `translateY(16px)` to `translateY(0)` with `opacity: 0` to `1`.
    *   Hover: Micro elevation shift `translateY(-3px)` with increased shadow blur.

---

## 7. Edge Cases & Viewport Adaptability

*   **Ultra-Wide (>1920px)**: The left text, center portrait, and right card column are clamped into a max-width stage container of `1720px` centered horizontally to prevent excessive layout stretching.
*   **Short Vertical Viewports (<800px)**: Right card stack gaps scale down from `16px` to `10px`, card interior paddings reduce by `20%` to avoid overflowing the vertical screen bounds.
