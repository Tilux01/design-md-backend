## 2. Typography & Font Specifications

### Primary Fonts & Fallbacks
1. **Wide Futuristic Display Title (English):**
   * **Target:** *Monument Extended (Ultra Bold)*, *Syne (Extra Bold)*, or *Bank Gothic*
   * **Fallback:** `'Arial Black', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif` with `transform: scaleX(1.2)` or expanded letter-spacing.
2. **Tall Stretched Display (English Header):**
   * **Target:** *Druk Condensed Super*, *Compacta*, or *Impact*
   * **Fallback:** `'Impact', 'Arial Narrow', sans-serif` with `font-stretch: ultra-condensed`.
3. **Clean Interface & Metadata (English):**
   * **Target:** *Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*, or *Space Grotesk*
   * **Fallback:** `'Arial', sans-serif`
4. **Primary CJK (Chinese Typography):**
   * **Target:** *PingFang SC*, *Noto Sans SC*, *Heiti SC*
   * **Fallback:** `'Microsoft YaHei', sans-serif`
