# Design Specification: "Library" Mobile App Interface

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
- **Platform:** iOS (designed for iPhone 14/15 Pro form factor with Dynamic Island and home indicator).
- **Aspect Ratio:** Vertical mobile screen (approx. 19.5:9 ratio).
- **Background Color:** Light neutral off-white (`#F2F2F2` or similar soft grey).
- **Layout Type:** Single-column scrollable interface featuring large editorial typography, circular visual media cards, and horizontal carousel sections.
- **Frames/Mockup Devices:** The physical phone bezel and shadow shown in the source image are excluded; the layout spans the full viewport width and height.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
*Note: This design heavily relies on modern Swiss/Neo-Grotesk style typography typical of high-end editorial iOS applications.*

- **Primary Font Family:** `SF Pro Display`, `SF Pro Text`, or system-default Apple sans-serif stack.
- **Fallback Stack:** `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, sans-serif.

### Text Hierarchy:
1. **Main Header ("Library"):**
   - **Font Weight:** Bold (700)
   - **Size:** Large display (approx. 36px - 40px)
   - **Color:** Deep black (`#000000`)
   - **Letter-spacing:** Tightly tracked (negative letter-spacing).

2. **Subtitle ("A curated collection..."):**
   - **Font Weight:** Regular (400)
   - **Size:** Small body (approx. 12px - 14px)
   - **Color:** Muted medium grey (`#7A7A7A`)

3. **Card Title ("Silent Echoes"):**
   - **Font Weight:** Semi-bold / Bold (600/700)
   - **Size:** Medium-Large heading (approx. 24px - 28px)
   - **Color:** Deep black (`#000000`)

4. **Card Subtext ("Discover something new every day"):**
   - **Font Weight:** Regular / Medium (400/500)
   - **Size:** Small (approx. 12px)
   - **Color:** Dark grey (`#333333`)

5. **Section Header ("Featured"):**
   - **Font Weight:** Regular / Medium (500)
   - **Size:** Extra-small uppercase/sentence case (approx. 11px - 12px)
   - **Color:** Muted grey (`#666666`)

6. **Carousel Titles ("Life Stories", "Personal Moments"):**
   - **Font Weight:** Bold (700)
   - **Size:** Medium heading (approx. 20px - 24px)
   - **Color:** Deep black (`#000000`)

---

## 3. UI Components & Elements

### A. Status Bar
- **Time:** "9:41" (Bold sans-serif, black).
- **Icons:** Standard iOS status icons (Cellular signal, WiFi, Battery indicator) aligned to the top right.
- **Notch/Island:** Centered black Dynamic Island pill at the top center.

### B. Top Navigation / Action Bar
- **Filter/Settings Button:** 
  - Located on the top-right area below the status bar.
  - **Shape:** Circular pill/button with light grey background (`#E4E4E4`).
  - **Icon:** Sliders / filter icon (black, minimal stroke).

### C. Hero Section ("Silent Echoes")
- **Container:** A rounded rectangular card spanning the width with generous padding.
- **Background / Accents:** 
  - Integrated streaming platform badges (Apple logo and Spotify logo inside small overlapping circular white pills positioned on the upper right of the card).
- **Visual Elements (Images):**
  1. **Left Image (Orange Poppy Flower):**
     - **Shape:** Perfect circle.
     - **Content:** Vibrant macro photograph of a bright orange poppy flower against a solid, warm gradient-orange background. The stem enters from the lower edge.
     - **Placement:** Positioned towards the bottom-left of the hero block.
     - **Rotation:** No explicit rotation; standard upright botanical crop.
  2. **Middle Image (White Vinyl / Abstract Disc):**
     - **Shape:** Perfect circle.
     - **Content:** Minimalist white vinyl record or abstract white circular badge featuring a centered solid black square icon with a diagonal expand/external-link arrow (`↗`).
     - **Placement:** Overlapping to the right of the orange flower circle.
  3. **Right Edge Image (Dark Red/Shadowed Scene):**
     - **Shape:** Partially visible circular edge on the right boundary.
     - **Content:** Moody, dark photographic texture with deep red and black tones.

### D. Sub-Card / Banner ("Discover something...")
- **Container:** Clean white background card with rounded corners (`border-radius: 16px`).
- **Content:** Left-aligned text "Discover something new every day" with a small navigation arrow (`↗`) aligned to the bottom-right corner of the inner card.

### E. Carousel Section ("Featured")
- **Header:** "Featured" label positioned above the card row.
- **Cards:** Horizontal scrolling cards with white/light backgrounds.
  - **Card 1 ("Life Stories"):** Bold typography, generous white space, clean padding.
  - **Card 2 ("Personal Moments"):** Adjacent card partially cut off on the right edge to indicate horizontal scrollability.

### F. Home Indicator Bar
- **Shape:** Thin horizontal capsule bar centered at the very bottom of the screen.
- **Color:** Solid black (`#000000`).

---

## 4. Summary of Images to Prepare
1. **Orange Poppy Circular Graphic:** High-resolution macro photography of an orange poppy on solid orange tones, cropped strictly to a circle.
2. **White Minimal Disc Graphic:** White circular asset with a centered black square and external link icon.
3. **Dark Ambient Edge Graphic:** Dark red/black textural photographic asset cropped to a circle for the right overflow.
