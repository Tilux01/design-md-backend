# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist, Architectural Portfolio & Design System Showcase.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High structural discipline with generous framing padding. Designed with strict typographic hierarchy, neutral obsidian canvas grounds, and precise geometric grid alignment.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat-surface chromatic zoning with high-contrast tonal layering, utilizing ultra-subtle borders (`1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`) and organic photography framing instead of heavy box-shadows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill labels, geometric column splitting, precise structural dividers, architectural photography framing.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255,255,255,0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
