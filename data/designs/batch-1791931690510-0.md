# Design Specification: Trevour – Nightfall Landing Page

## 1. Global Setup & Color Palette

*   **Layout:** Fullscreen viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), overflow hidden, desktop-first.
*   **Background:** Solid dark charcoal `#0C0D0E` with a subtle radial gradient centered behind the candle to enhance depth.
*   **Palette:**
    *   Primary Text: `#FFFFFF`
    *   Secondary/Body Text: `#7C7E83`
    *   Spec Labels: `#4A4C52`
    *   Border/Lines: `#26282E`
    *   Button Fill: `#FFFFFF`
    *   Button Text: `#0C0D0E`

---

## 2. Typography

*   **Primary Display (Product Title):**
    *   Font: *Cormorant Garamond* (Light / 300) or *Ogg*.
    *   Fallback: `Didot, "Bodoni MT", serif`.
*   **Secondary UI & Body (Navigation, Specs, Body, Buttons):**
    *   Font: *Montserrat* or *Inter* (Regular / 400).
    *   Fallback: `"Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`.

## 4. Visual Imagery & Composition

### Pedestal (Base Platform)
*   **Type:** 3D rendered matte concrete/stone cylindrical podium.
*   **Placement:** Anchored to bottom right; spans from mid-screen horizontal center across to the right edge, bottom offset partially cropped by frame.
*   **Lighting:** Diffuse top-left directional key light creating soft shadows across the top surface.

### Hero Product (Floating Candle)
*   **Type:** Matte black ceramic/stone cylinder candle filled with smooth black wax and a single centered cotton wick.
*   **Asset Type:** Isolated foreground PNG with transparent background.
*   **Placement & Orientation:**
    *   Positioned directly above the pedestal.
    *   Rotation: Tilted approximately `-40°` to `-45°` along the Z-axis (tilted backward and rightward), exposing the top inner rim and wax pool.
*   **Shadow Effect:** Soft, diffused drop shadow projected directly beneath it onto the top plane of the concrete pedestal.
