# Comprehensive Design Specification (`design.md`)

---

## 1. Design Overview & Aesthetic Direction

* **Design Style**: Futuristic Neo-Minimalism / High-Tech Glassmorphism / Cyber-Editorial.
* **Canvas Dimensions**: Full-width fluid responsive design (Standard desktop reference viewport: `1440px` to `1920px` width, content container max-width: `1280px`).
* **Visual Atmosphere**: Clean, clinical off-white canvas overlaid with faint wireframe grids, luminous lime/neon chartreuse radial gradients, translucent frosted-glass cards, and sharp industrial typography.

## 3. Typography & Font Stack

### Font Families
1. **Display & Primary Headers**:
   * *Primary*: `Clash Display`, `Syne`, or `Space Grotesk` (Weight: 700 / 800 Bold to Extrabold).
   * *Fallback*: `Arial Black`, `Impact`, `sans-serif`.
   * *Style*: High-contrast geometric uppercase letterforms with tight tracking.

2. **Body & Interface Elements**:
   * *Primary*: `Inter`, `Geist Sans`, or `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Weight: 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 600 SemiBold).
   * *Fallback*: `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `sans-serif`.

3. **Technical Meta / Monospace Labels**:
   * *Primary*: `JetBrains Mono`, `Space Mono`, or `Geist Mono` (Weight: 400 Regular, 500 Medium).
   * *Fallback*: `Consolas`, `Monaco`, `monospace`.
   * *Style*: Uppercase, letter-spacing: `0.08em` to `0.15em`.

## 8. Micro-Interactions & Animation Specs

1. **Neon Pulsing Elements**:
   * The active status dots (Header, Freelance badge, Footer) have a continuous subtle `pulse` animation:
     `box-shadow: 0 0 0 0 rgba(168, 255, 0, 0.7)` expanding to `10px` blur at `0%` opacity over `2s` infinite loops.
2. **Card Hover Elevations**:
   * Hovering on service cards and portfolio items initiates a subtle `-2px` vertical translation with transition timing `300ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
   * Card border transitions from translucent white to a soft green neon glow (`rgba(168, 255, 0, 0.5)`).
3. **Buttons & Arrows**:
   * Arrows (`→`, `↗`) translate `3px` diagonally or horizontally on card hover.
