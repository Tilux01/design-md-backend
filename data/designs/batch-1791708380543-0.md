# Design Specification: Far From Noise — Modern Living Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Layout Structure
- **Viewport:** 100% width, full screen, responsive vertical scroll layout.
- **Background Color:** Light off-white / soft gray (`#f0f0f0` or `#e8e8e8`).
- **Typography Engine:** 
  - Primary Display / Headers: Heavy sans-serif, geometric, tight tracking (Fallback: `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial Black`, sans-serif).
  - Body Text: Clean, minimalist sans-serif (Fallback: `Inter`, `Helvetica`, sans-serif).
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary text & brand elements: Deep forest green/black (`#1a1f18`) and stark white (`#ffffff`).
  - Muted secondary text: Dark gray (`#6b6b6b`).

---

### A. Hero Section (Top Full-Width Banner)

  - Positioned centrally, full coverage behind text.
- **Top Navigation Bar:**
  - Left: Brand logo icon (White, minimalist geometric interlocking letters "FF").
  - Right: Navigation links aligned horizontally in small uppercase sans-serif: `Home`, `About`, `Catalog`, `Contacts`.

  - **Main Heading (Left overlay):**

  - **Sub-tagline (Bottom Left):**
