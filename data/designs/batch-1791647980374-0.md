# Design System & Technical Specification: TurboTweak Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Aesthetic Direction
* **Theme:** High-end, dark-mode luxury automotive tuning platform ("Turbotweak").
* **Canvas Size:** Full viewport height/width (`100vw`, `100vh` or min-height `100vh`), edge-to-edge layout with zero outer container margins/borders.
* **Overall Vibe:** Sleek, minimalist, high contrast, metallic accents, moody studio spotlighting.

---

## 2. Color Palette

### Base & Backgrounds
* **Primary Background:** `#080809` (Ultra-deep matte black)
* **Floor Studio Gradient:** Linear gradient from `#080809` at `0%` down to `#1C1D21` at `75%`, fading back to `#050505` at `100%` (creates the reflective studio floor beneath the car).
* **Card/Surface Background:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` with a subtle blur `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
* **Card Border:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (1px solid).

### Typography & Accents
* **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
* **Text Secondary:** `#9A9A9E` (Muted Studio Gray)
* **Text Muted / Background Graphic:** `#1E1E22` (Dark Charcoal for the large outline background graphic)
* **Pointer Line:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)` thin vector line.

---

## 5. Main Hero Layout (3-Column Grid)

* **Layout Structure:** Full height (`100vh`), CSS Grid with 12 columns or 3 flexible flex sections with padding `120px 48px 40px 48px`.

---

### Left Column (Primary Pitch & CTA)
* **Width:** ~30% of canvas width (`max-width: 420px`).
* **Vertical Alignment:** Center-left vertically aligned.

1. **Main Heading (H1):**
   * **Content:** "Elevate Your Drive to Extraordinary Heights"
   * **Layout:** Word wrapped into 4 lines:
     * Line 1: `Elevate Your`
     * Line 2: `Drive to`
     * Line 3: `Extraordinary`
     * Line 4: `Heights`
2. **Subtitle Paragraph:**
   * **Content:** "Crafting Power, Performance, and Style for the Ultimate Driving Experience"
   * **Margin-Top:** `24px`.
   * **Max Width:** `320px`.
3. **Primary CTA Button ("Get started"):**
   * **Margin-Top:** `40px`.
   * **Shape:** Fully rounded pill / capsule (`border-radius: 9999px`).
   * **Background:** Solid Pure White `#FFFFFF`.
   * **Text:** "Get started" (Dark `#080809`, size `14px`, medium weight).
   * **Dimensions:** Height `48px`, Width `160px`. Hover effect: subtle glow or opacity `0.9`.

---

### Center Column (Hero Vehicle Visual & Background Graphic)
* **Width:** ~45% of canvas width.
* **Positioning:** Relative, visual focal point.

1. **Background Graphic (Huge Outline Typography/Shape):**
   * **Description:** Large stylized vector stroke of "S2" or double rounded track shape.
   * **Color:** Stroke only (`2px` width), color `#2A2A30`, fill `none`.
   * **Placement:** Positioned high behind the car roof/windshield, centered horizontally.
   * **Size:** ~`500px` wide.

2. **Main Vehicle Asset (Porsche 918 Spyder):**
   * **Asset Type:** Backgroundless high-resolution PNG cutout / 3D render.
   * **Subject:** Dark Metallic Charcoal/Black Porsche 918 Spyder.
   * **Angle & Perspective:** Straight front-facing angle (`0°` rotation), eye-level slightly low to show aggressive stance.
   * **Lighting:** Studio top-down spotlighting highlighting the hood curves, windshield glare, aggressively lit quad-LED headlamps, and lower intake splitters.
   * **Positioning:** Centered, bottom aligned flush with the floor reflection horizontal plane. Size: ~`650px` to `850px` width depending on screen resolution.

3. **Ground Shadow & Floor Reflection:**
   * **Shadow:** Radial dark gradient blurred heavily (`filter: blur(20px)`) directly beneath the tires and chassis.
   * **Reflection:** Mirror-image floor reflection with `opacity: 0.25`, vertically flipped, fading out downward with a linear gradient mask.

---

* **Width:** ~25% of canvas width (`max-width: 360px`).
* **Positioning:** Stacked vertically, shifted toward the right edge.

* **Secondary CTA Button ("View more"):**

2. **Right Content Area:**
