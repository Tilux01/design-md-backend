# E-Commerce Product Showcase & Landing Page Design Specification

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
* **Primary Mood & Brand Archetype:** Luxury, modern streetwear, immersive product showcase. High-end lifestyle brand archetype emphasizing minimal effort with maximum aesthetic impact ("Stand out without trying").
* **Visual Density & Whitespace:** Generous whitespace, asymmetric product framing, balanced horizontal hero split with clear visual hierarchy.
* **Theme Balance:** Immersive dark-mode/warm monochrome palette centered entirely around rich autumnal gradients (tangerine orange, burnt sienna, deep espresso browns). Matte gradients, realistic drop shadows, and subtle glassmorphic pill navigation.

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy
* **Font Families:** 
  * Headings & Display: `Outfit`, `Inter`, sans-serif
  * Body: `Inter`, sans-serif
* **Scale Breakdown:**
  * **Display Title (H1):** `font-weight: 750`, `font-size: 3.5rem` (56px), `line-height: 1.05`, `letter-spacing: -0.03em`
  * **Section Subtitle / Tagline:** `font-weight: 400`, `font-size: 0.95rem` (15px), `line-height: 1.5`, `letter-spacing: 0`
  * **Nav Links / Pill Text:** `font-weight: 500`, `font-size: 0.75rem` (12px), `letter-spacing: 0.08em`, `text-transform: uppercase`
  * **Pricing (Current):** `font-weight: 700`, `font-size: 2.25rem` (36px), `letter-spacing: -0.02em`
  * **Pricing (Strike):** `font-weight: 500`, `font-size: 1.5rem` (24px), `text-decoration: line-through`
  * **Body Copy:** `font-weight: 400`, `font-size: 0.875rem` (14px), `line-height: 1.6`, `color: var(--color-text-muted)`

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
* **Container Max-Width:** `1280px` (centered with auto margins, fluid padding `24px` to `64px`).
* **Section Sequence:**
  1. **Top Header Bar:** Brand Logo (left), Glassmorphic Nav Pill (center), Action Icons — Search/Cart/Wishlist (right).
  2. **Hero Core Section:**
     * Left Column (w-5/12): Carousel Controls, Headline, Description, "Get the look" CTA button, Social links.
     * Center Column (w-4/12): 3D Hero Product (Glossy Puffer Jacket) with realistic ground shadow.
     * Right Column (w-3/12): Pricing Block, Size Selector (36, 38, 40), Thumbnail Switcher.
  3. **Footer Micro-Copy:** "Confidence, wrapped in warmth" center-aligned baseline.

---

## 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Special Glyphs
* **Glassmorphic Navigation Pill:**
  * Padding: `8px 24px`
  * Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`
  * Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`
  * Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px)`
  * Border Radius: `9999px`
* **Interactive State Indicators (Size Selector):**
  * Unselected: Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`, text color `#FFFFFF`.
  * Selected: Solid `#FFFFFF`, text color `#1F0E08`, font-weight `600`.
* **Symbol Safety Guide:** Use clean HTML entities (`&rarr;`, `&bull;`, `&hearts;`) or SVG wrappers for arrows and icons to ensure cross-platform consistency.

## 8. Accessibility (a11y) & WCAG Contrast Audit
* **Text Contrast:** 
  * White text (`#FFFFFF`) on dark warm brown background (`#3D1C0F`) — **Contrast Ratio: 12.4:1 (Passes WCAG AAA)**
  * Muted tan text (`#D2B4A7`) on dark background (`#3D1C0F`) — **Contrast Ratio: 5.8:1 (Passes WCAG AA)**
* **Focus States:** All interactive buttons and size selectors include visible focus rings:
  ```css
  button:focus-visible {
    outline: 2px solid #FF6A00;
    outline-offset: 3px;
  }
  ```
