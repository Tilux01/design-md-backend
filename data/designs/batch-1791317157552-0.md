# Production Design Specification: Stringout Brand System

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic

* **Primary Mood & Brand Archetype:** Cyber-brutalist, editorial, high-contrast, technical. Blends retro digital pixelation with modern high-fashion typography.
* **Visual Density:** High contrast, striking negative space balanced by dense, stepped pixel geometry.
* **Theme Balance:** Dark mode dominant with vibrant neon-to-pastel chromatic transitions (hot pink, electric cyan, violet-orange gradients). Flat structural layouts with stepped, rasterized polygon cutouts.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

* **Primary Colors:** Hot Pink (`#FF2EA6`), Electric Cyan (`#00A3FF`), Neon Orange (`#FF5E36`)
* **Secondary & Accent:** Deep Violet (`#9D4EDD`), Pitch Black (`#0B0B0C`), White (`#FFFFFF`)
* **Surface Backgrounds:** Canvas Black (`#000000`), Dark Gray Surface (`#141416`), Card Dark (`#1C1C1E`)
* **Borders & Dividers:** High-contrast White/Black raster edges, subtle dark neutral dividers (`#2C2C2E`)
* **Text Colors:** Primary White (`#F4F4F5`), Muted Gray (`#9E9EA6`), Inverse Black (`#0B0B0C`), Accent Neon (`#FF2EA6`)

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy

* **Headings / Brand Display:** `Space Grotesk` / Custom Pixel Display Font (700 Bold, Uppercase/Sentence Case)
* **Body Copy:** `Space Grotesk` / `JetBrains Mono` (400 Regular, crisp anti-aliasing disabled or crisp-edges for digital look)

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid

* **Container Max-Width:** Fluid responsive max `1440px` with centered alignment.
* **Padding & Margin Scale:** 8pt modular grid system (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`).
* **Z-Index Hierarchy:** 
  - Background Canvas: `z-0`
  - Stepped Graphic Elements: `z-10`
  - Content & Typography: `z-20`
  - Floating Overlays/Modals: `z-50`

---

## 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Special Glyphs

* **Visual Hallmarks:** Stepped pixel geometry intersecting dark text containers, high-chroma gradients transitioning from hot pink to orange/cyan, minimalist branding typography.
* **Glyph Representation:** Use explicit HTML/Unicode entities for clean rendering:
  - Sparkle: `&#10022;` (`✦`)
  - Dash: `&mdash;` (`—`)
  - Status Indicator: `&#9679;` (`●`)

## 8. Accessibility (a11y) & WCAG Contrast Audit

* **Text Contrast Ratios:** 
  - White text (`#F4F4F5`) on Black background (`#000000`): **21:1 (Passes WCAG AAA)**
  - Neon Pink (`#FF2EA6`) on Black background (`#000000`): **4.6:1 (Passes WCAG AA)**
* **Keyboard Focus Rings:**
  ```css
  :focus-visible {
    outline: 2px solid #FF2EA6;
    outline-offset: 4px;
  }
  ```
