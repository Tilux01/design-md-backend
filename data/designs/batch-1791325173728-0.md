# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist, Spatial Glassmorphic Interface, Modern Smart Home Dashboard.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with generous padding and centered visual focus, allowing cards to command attention against a soft neutral backdrop.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean, flat cards with subtle drop shadows (`0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`), light rounded corners, and minimal borders on a light canvas background.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient soft shadows, subtle glass cards, minimalist date/weather telemetry, high-end editorial typography.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
