## 5. Implementation Notes & Directives
1. **No External Frames:** Render directly on the native browser viewport without any mockup device frames or mock wrapper bounding boxes.
2. **Gradients & Glows:** Apply CSS `box-shadow` with expansive blur radii (`blur: 40px` to `80px`) using warm amber/orange tones to replicate the ambient atmospheric lighting behind the hero section and central UI nodes.
3. **Icons & Logos:** All node icons must be treated as clean, white, backgroundless vector assets (`SVG`) centered precisely within their respective glassmorphic containers.
