# 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
- **Primary Mood & Vibe:** Cinematic, ultra-minimalist, deep atmospheric dark mode designed to evoke high-end automotive luxury, precision engineering, and elite digital craftsmanship ("Boutique Automotive House").
- **Visual Density:** Highly calibrated sparse density. Relies heavily on expansive negative space, thin hairline dividers, and precise micro-typography to direct focus.
- **Whitespace Balance:** Generous macro-padding combined with tight component-level padding creates an intentional tension between cinematic openness and precision data presentation.
- **Theme Architecture:** Deep immersive pitch black (#000000) base coupled with multi-layered surface elevations using subtle alpha channels, glassmorphic blur layers, and monochromatic neutral greys.
