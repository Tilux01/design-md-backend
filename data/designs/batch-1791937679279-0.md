# Design Specification: Red Dead Redemption 2 Official Site Clone

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Background Color:** `#000000` (Pure Black).
*   **Primary Accent Color:** `#E51921` (Vibrant Red).
*   **Secondary Accent Color:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
*   **Text Color (Body):** `#CCCCCC` (Light Grey for readability).
*   **Scroll Behavior:** Smooth scrolling. The design is a full-width, edge-to-edge layout with no visible outer frames or padding on the main container.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Heading Font:** `Chalet-LondonNineteenSixty` or `Impact`.
    *   *Fallback:* `Arial Black`, `sans-serif`.
    *   *Usage:* "RED DEAD REDEMPTION II", Section Headers.
*   **Secondary Heading/Serif Font:** `HPLHS Old Style` or `Clarendon`.
    *   *Fallback:* `Georgia`, `serif`.
    *   *Usage:* "LOYALTY. HONOR. REDEMPTION."
*   **Body Font:** `Helvetica Neue` or `Roboto`.
    *   *Fallback:* `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
    *   *Usage:* Paragraph text, navigation links, and footer links.

## 3. Section Breakdown

### A. Header & Navigation
*   **Top Bar:** Slim black bar. Left side: Rockstar Games Logo. Right side: "ROCKSTAR GAMES", "SOCIAL CLUB", "SUPPORT".
*   **Main Nav:** 
    *   Left: "RDR II" logo (Red).
    *   Center: Links (Home, Story, Gameplay, Online, Media, News) in white, uppercase, small font size.
    *   Right: Search icon, User Login icon, Cart (0) icon.

### B. Hero Section (Top)
*   **Background Image:** A cinematic shot of a cowboy (Arthur Morgan) on a horse, viewed from behind, looking toward a massive, low-hanging blood-red moon. The landscape is silhouettes of mountains.
*   **Text Overlay (Left Aligned):**
    *   "ROCKSTAR GAMES PRESENTS" (Small, Red).
    *   "RED DEAD REDEMPTION II" (Large, White/Red stacked logo).
    *   "AMERICA, 1899. THE END OF THE WILD WEST HAS BEGUN." (White, sans-serif).
*   **Buttons:** 
    *   "BUY NOW" (Solid Red button, white text).
    *   "WATCH TRAILER" (Transparent with white border, play icon).

### C. Feature Grid (4 Columns)
Four equal-width boxes with a subtle dark-grey top border.
1.  **Living World:** Icon: Cowboy Hat. Text: Description of the ecosystem.
2.  **Rich Story:** Icon: Revolver. Text: Description of the Van der Linde gang.
3.  **Dynamic Gameplay:** Icon: Campfire. Text: Description of interactions.
4.  **Red Dead Online:** Icon: Sheriff Star. Text: Description of the multiplayer mode.
*   *Hover Effect:* Red underline or glow on the icon.

### D. Story Spotlight (Middle)
*   **Background:** A deep red textured background with subtle, large-scale faded stars (reminiscent of the US flag).
*   **Main Image (Right):** A high-resolution, backgroundless profile shot of Arthur Morgan looking to the left. He is wearing a leather hat, a dark jacket, and holding a rifle over his shoulder. The lighting is dramatic (chiaroscuro), highlighting the textures of his beard and clothing.
*   **Text (Left):** 
    *   "THE STORY" (Small, Red).
    *   "LOYALTY. HONOR. REDEMPTION." (Large, White, Serif, Bold).
    *   Paragraph text explaining the plot in light grey.
    *   "EXPLORE THE STORY" (Red button).

### E. Icon Navigation Bar
A horizontal strip of 5 icons with labels below them, separated by thin vertical lines.
*   Icons: Map (Open World), Gun (Combat), Horse (Animals), Satchel (Activities), Group (Online).
*   Style: Red icons, White text, all uppercase.

### F. Latest Trailers & Media
*   **Header:** "LATEST TRAILERS & MEDIA" (White, Left). "VIEW ALL" (Red, Right).
*   **Grid:** 4-column thumbnail gallery.
*   **Thumbnails:** 16:9 aspect ratio. Each has a play button overlay in the center.
    *   Image 1: Arthur in a red-tinted forest.
    *   Image 2: Three riders in black and white.
    *   Image 3: A steam train in a sepia-toned landscape.
    *   Image 4: A lone rider against a sunset.

### G. Footer
*   **Left:** Large "RED DEAD REDEMPTION II" logo + ESRB "M" Rating logo.
*   **Center Links:** Four columns of links (Game, Support, Community, Newsletter).
*   **Newsletter Box:** Input field with "Enter your email" and a Red "Sign Up" arrow button.
*   **Social Icons:** Small white icons (Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Twitch) in the bottom left.
*   **Bottom Right:** Rockstar Games "R" logo.

## 5. Technical Requirements for "Perfect Clone"
*   **Container:** `width: 100vw; overflow-x: hidden;`
*   **Red Gradient:** Use a radial gradient for the Story section: `radial-gradient(circle, #8b0000 0%, #000000 100%)`.
*   **Transitions:** All buttons should have a `0.3s ease-in-out` transition on hover (opacity or color shift).
*   **Responsiveness:** On screens smaller than 1024px, the 4-column grids should stack into 2 columns. On mobile, 1 column.
