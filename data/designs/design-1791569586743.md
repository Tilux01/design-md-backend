# UI Design & Implementation Specification: Leafora E-Commerce Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
- **View:** Full-screen responsive desktop view (target min-width: 1440px).
- **Background Color:** Soft, warm off-white cream (`#F8F5EE` or `#F4F0E6`).
- **Container Margins:** Generous whitespace with a central layout aligned to a 12-column grid system.
- **Outer Shell:** Remove any drop-shadowed device frames or mock-up cards present in the preview image; the design must stretch fluidly to fill the browser viewport.

---

## 2. Typography & Font Stack
The design relies on a pairing of a high-end editorial serif display typeface and a clean, legible sans-serif for UI elements.

### Heading Font (Serif)
- **Primary Font:** *Playfair Display*, *Bodoni Moda*, or *Merriweather*.
- **Fallback Stack:** `Georgia, Cambria, "Times New Roman", Times, serif`
- **Usage:** Main hero header ("Greener Spaces Happier You").
- **Styling:** Italic styling applied to specific words ("Happier You"), heavy weights (Bold/700), high line-height to allow for overlapping flourishes.

### Body & UI Font (Sans-Serif)
- **Primary Font:** *Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, or *Outfit*.
- **Fallback Stack:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
- **Usage:** Navigation links, paragraph descriptions, button texts, footer feature icons.
- **Styling:** Clean geometry, tracking adjusted for readability (slightly open letter-spacing on uppercase category tags).

### Handwritten/Script Font (Accents)
- **Primary Font:** *Caveat*, *Reenie Beanie*, or *Homemade Apple*.
- **Fallback Stack:** `cursive`
- **Usage:** Organic annotations ("Good Vibes Grow Here", "Plants make life better").

---

- **Primary Dark (Text & Buttons):** Deep Forest Green (`#1E3A2F` / `#162E25`)
- **Background Cream:** Warm Paper (`#F7F3E9`)
