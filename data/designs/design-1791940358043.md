# Design Specification: AeroStream IFE Dashboard

## 1. General Layout & Background
*   **Orientation:** Landscape (16:9 or 16:10 aspect ratio).
*   **Background:** A soft, multi-stop linear gradient flowing from the top-left to the bottom-right.
    *   **Color 1 (Top-Left):** `#B6D8F2` (Soft Sky Blue)
    *   **Color 2 (Center):** `#E1F5FE` (Pale Azure)
    *   **Color 3 (Bottom-Right):** `#F0FDF4` (Mint White)
*   **Overall Aesthetic:** Clean, minimalist, modern, and airy. High use of whitespace and rounded corners (20px - 30px radius).

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** A modern, geometric Sans-Serif. 
    *   **Recommended:** *Montserrat*, *Poppins*, or *Inter*.
    *   **Fallback:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Headings ("Welcome on board"):** 
    *   Weight: Bold (700).
    *   Color: `#0A2540` (Deep Navy).
    *   Size: Large (~48pt to 60pt).
*   **Flight Codes (SFO, NYC):** 
    *   Weight: Bold (700).
    *   Color: `#0A2540`.
    *   Size: Medium (~24pt).
*   **Secondary Text/Labels:** 
    *   Weight: Regular (400).
    *   Color: `#4F5B76` (Muted Blue-Grey).
*   **Temperature (62°):** 
    *   Weight: Semi-Bold (600).
    *   Color: `#0A2540`.

## 3. Sidebar (Left Navigation)
*   **Container:** A vertical white bar (`#FFFFFF`) with a width of approximately 8% of the screen. The right edge has a large border-radius (~40px) creating a "tab" look.
*   **Icons (Top to Bottom):**
    1.  **Airplane:** Red (`#E63946`), indicates active/home state.
    2.  **Headphones:** Dark Grey, for music/audio.
    3.  **Play Button:** Dark Grey, for movies/video.
    4.  **Pac-Man/Ghost:** Dark Grey, for games.
    5.  **Shopping Bag:** Dark Grey, for duty-free/food ordering.
    6.  **Grid/Menu:** Dark Grey, for settings/apps.
*   **Floating Action:** At the bottom-left, a circular white button with a dark grey **Camera icon**, likely for a tail-cam view.

## 4. Top Utility Bar
*   **Left Side:**
    *   **Logo:** A circular swirl icon (White/Blue gradient).
    *   **Power Icon:** Outlined circle with a dash.
    *   **Brightness Icon:** Sun symbol.
    *   **Volume Icon:** Speaker with sound waves.
*   **Right Side:**
    *   **Assist Button:** A pill-shaped button.
    *   **Background:** `#3B5998` (Medium Blue).
    *   **Content:** A white bell icon followed by the text "ASSIST" in uppercase, white, bold font.

## 5. Central Hero Image (Airplane)
*   **Description:** A high-fidelity, top-down (plan view) image of a white commercial airliner (twin-engine, long-haul jet).
*   **Visual Details:** 
    *   **Fuselage:** White with subtle grey shading for depth.
    *   **Tail Fin:** Features an orange and yellow gradient accent.
    *   **Atmosphere:** The plane is surrounded by soft, wispy white clouds. Faint vapor trails extend from the wingtips.
*   **Placement:** Centered horizontally, but shifted slightly to the left.
*   **Rotation:** The nose of the plane is rotated approximately **15° clockwise**, pointing toward the top-right corner.
*   **Transparency:** The image should be backgroundless (PNG/WebP) to blend seamlessly into the UI gradient.

## 6. Content Cards (Right Side)
### A. Flight Status Card
*   **Background:** Solid White (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle drop shadow and 24px corner radius.
*   **Top Row:** "San Francisco (SFO)" on the left, "New York City (NYC)" on the right.
*   **Progress Bar:** 
    *   A thin light-grey horizontal line.
    *   A small red airplane icon (`#E63946`) positioned roughly 60% along the line.
*   **Bottom Row:** 
    *   Departure: "09:50 AM".
    *   Arrival: "1:30 PM".
    *   **Status Badge:** A green pill (`#2ECC71`) in the center with white text "2 hours Remaining".

### B. Weather Card
*   **Background:** Solid White (`#FFFFFF`) with 24px corner radius.
*   **Left Content:** Large "62°" text.
*   **Right Content:** A decorative graphic showing a bright yellow sun partially tucked behind a fluffy white cloud, set against a small blue sky background contained within the card's right half.

## 7. Color Palette Summary
*   **Primary Navy:** `#0A2540` (Text)
*   **Accent Red:** `#E63946` (Active icons, flight path)
*   **Accent Green:** `#2ECC71` (Time remaining badge)
*   **Accent Blue:** `#3B5998` (Assist button)
*   **Soft Grey:** `#F2F4F7` (Dividers/Progress lines)
*   **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF` (Cards and Sidebar)
