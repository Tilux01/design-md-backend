# Design Specification: Findora Real Estate App

## Overview
This document outlines the UI/UX design specifications for the "Findora" real estate mobile application. It covers two primary screens: a **Welcome / Onboarding Screen** and a **Home / Dashboard Screen**. The design follows a modern, clean aesthetic utilizing a monochromatic color scheme (black, white, and shades of gray) with high-contrast text and clean card layouts.

---

## Global Design Tokens & Typography

### Color Palette
*   **Primary Black:** `#181818` (Used for headers, buttons, primary brand text)
*   **Secondary Dark Gray:** `#4A4A4A` (Used for body text, subtitles, inactive states)
*   **Light Gray Background:** `#F5F5F5` / `#F0F0F0` (Used for search bars, screen background, unselected filter chips)
*   **White:** `#FFFFFF` (Used for cards background, button text, foreground containers)
*   **Accent Blue:** `#2F80ED` (Used for bookmark checkmarks/badges)
*   **Rating Star Yellow:** `#F2C94C` (Used for review stars)

### Typography
*   **Primary Font Family:** Inter (Fallback: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`)
*   **Weights Used:** Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600), Bold (700)

---

## Screen 1: Welcome / Onboarding Screen

### Layout Structure
*   **Full-screen vertical layout** divided into two main sections:
    1.  **Top Visual Area:** A scenic photographic hero image.
    2.  **Bottom Content Area:** A white curved/overlapped container holding branding, welcome text, and call-to-action buttons.

### Components & Elements

1.  **Hero Image (Top Area):**
    *   **Description:** A modern architectural house with dark wood/metal vertical siding, a steeply pitched gable roof, and a chimney. The exterior is landscaped with green bushes and pink floral patches under a partly cloudy blue sky.
    *   **Placement:** Fills the upper half of the screen, curving downward at the bottom edge to overlap with the white content container.
    *   **Logo Overlay:**
        *   Centered near the lower middle of the image.
        *   Contains a circular white badge (`#FFFFFF`) with a minimalist icon of a house combined with sparkles/sun rays.
        *   Text below the icon: "FINDORA" in bold uppercase sans-serif, and "Dream House" in a smaller lighter font beneath it.

2.  **Welcome Content Area (Bottom White Container):**
    *   **Heading:**
        *   Text: "WELCOME"
        *   Font: Bold, ~24px, `#181818`, centered.
    *   **Subtext:**
        *   Text: "Find your next space, feel at home\nWhere comfort meets convenience"
        *   Font: Regular, ~14px, `#7A7A7A`, centered, multi-line.

3.  **Action Buttons:**
    *   **Login Button (Primary):**
        *   Background: `#181818` (Solid Black)
        *   Text Color: `#FFFFFF` (White)
        *   Border Radius: Fully rounded pill shape (~25px height/radius).
        *   Text: "Login" (Medium weight).
    *   **Sign Up Button (Secondary):**
        *   Background: Transparent with a 1px solid `#CCCCCC` border.
        *   Text Color: `#181818` (Black)
        *   Border Radius: Fully rounded pill shape.
        *   Text: "Sign Up" (Medium weight).

---

## Screen 2: Home / Dashboard Screen

### Layout Structure
*   **Full-screen layout** featuring a standard iOS status bar at the top, a top navigation/header section, a search bar, horizontal filter tabs, a section header, and a vertically scrolling grid/list of rental house cards. A fixed bottom navigation bar anchors the screen.

### Components & Elements

1.  **Top Navigation Bar:**
    *   **Location Dropdown (Left):**
        *   Pill-shaped badge with a location pin icon and text: "Kochi, Kerala".
        *   Background: `#181818`, Text: White.
    *   **Notification Icon (Right):**
        *   Bell icon inside a soft gray circular button with a blue notification dot.
    *   **Profile Avatar (Right):**
        *   Circular 3D avatar/illustration of a person with glasses and a blue hoodie.

2.  **Header Titles:**
    *   **Main Title:** "Discover" (Light/Regular weight, ~22px)
    *   **Subtitle:** "Your New House" (Bold weight, ~22px)

3.  **Search & Filter Section:**
    *   **Search Bar:**
        *   Background: `#EFEFEF` (Light gray), rounded corners (~12px radius).
        *   Left Icon: Magnifying glass.
        *   Placeholder Text: "Search Place..." (`#8E8E93`).
        *   Right Icon: Filter/sliders icon.

4.  **Filter Tabs (Horizontal Scroll):**
    *   Options: `[Rental House]`, `[Apartment]`, `[Houses]`, `[Rooms]`
    *   **Active Tab ("Rental House"):** Black background (`#181818`), White text.
    *   **Inactive Tabs:** Light gray background (`#EFEFEF`), Dark gray text (`#4A4A4A`).

5.  **Section Header:**
    *   Text: "Nearby Rental House's" (Semi-bold, ~16px).

6.  **Rental Property Cards (Grid / Scrollable List):**
    *   **Card Structure:** Rounded rectangle container (`12px` radius), white background with subtle drop shadow. Image covers the top 75% of the card; text and details sit at the bottom.
    *   **Image Overlays:**
        *   *Top-Left:* Blue circular badge with a white checkmark icon.
        *   *Top-Right:* Semi-transparent dark pill displaying pricing (e.g., "10.000/ Month") with white text.
        *   *Bottom-Left (on image):* Rating badge featuring a yellow star and numeric score (e.g., "4.5").
    *   **Card Details:**
        *   **Title:** Name of the property (e.g., "Georgie House", "Max House", "Luna Haven", "The Serene Nest"). Semi-bold, ~14px.
        *   **Location:** Pin icon followed by the neighborhood/city (e.g., "Kochi, Kerala"). Regular, ~12px, muted gray color.

7.  **Bottom Navigation Bar:**
    *   Fixed to the bottom of the screen with a white background and subtle top border.
    *   Contains 4 primary navigation items with icons and labels:
        1.  **Home:** Blue active icon and label.
        2.  **Locations:** Outline icon, gray label.
        3.  **Chat:** Outline icon, gray label.
        4.  **Settings:** Outline gear/dial icon, gray label.
    *   *Note:* The card content from the scrollable list slightly peeks out behind the translucent bottom navigation bar.
