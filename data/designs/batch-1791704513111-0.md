# Design Specification: Oakley Product Showcase (Slide 05)

A comprehensive design documentation file (`design.md`) engineered for a pixel-perfect, 1:1 screen reproduction of the Oakley 2025 Brand Kit Product Showcase slide.

---

## 1. Global Concept & Visual Identity

* **Theme / Aesthetic:** High-performance tactical minimalism, industrial sport engineering, HUD/technical schematic accents, premium dark-mode presentation.
* **Canvas Dimensions:** Full-screen viewport (`100vw` × `100vh`), optimized for a standard 16:9 ratio baseline (`1920px × 1080px`) with no outer bezel, margins, or surrounding device mockups.
* **Base Background:** Pitch solid black (`#000000`).
* **Grid Architecture:** 4-column balanced card deck framed between a technical status-bar header and a military/tactical footer.

### Layout Sections Breakdown

1. **Header Zone (`height: 100px; display: flex; justify-content: space-between; align-items: flex-start;`)**
   * **Left Group:**
     * Reticle mark positioned at `top: 0, left: 0` with a dotted vertical guide line extending down `48px`.
     * `05` index number positioned adjacent to the vertical guide line.
     * Stacked headline container:
       * `PRODUCT SHOWCASE`
       * `ENGINEERED TO PERFORM.`
       * `DESIGNED TO STAND APART.`
   * **Right Group:**
     * `OAKLEY BRAND KIT   |   2025` text string aligned horizontally with top coordinate.
     * Reticle mark centered on the top-right corner.

2. **Main Product Grid (`height: calc(100vh - 220px); min-height: 580px;`)**
   * Grid display: `display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(4, 1fr); gap: 16px;`
   * All cards share identical structural paddings: `padding: 32px 28px 24px 28px;`
   * Every card is wrapped in a 1px solid stroke (`border: 1px solid #1D1D1D;`).

3. **Footer Zone (`height: 40px; display: flex; justify-content: space-between; align-items: center; margin-top: 24px;`)**
   * Left: `BUILT FOR THE ONES WHO DON'T STOP.`
   * Right: Dotted horizontal line rule (`border-bottom: 1px dotted rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4); width: 140px;`) terminating into a right-aligned crosshair icon.

### Card 01 — SUTRO LITE

#### 1. Background & Border
* **Background:** Solid dark charcoal `#080808`.
* **Border:** `1px solid #1C1C1C`.

#### 2. Product Image
* **Subject:** Oakley Sutro Lite sports performance eyewear.
* **Frame Style:** Semi-rimless nylon frame, matte black finish with sculpted geometric temples and ribbed earstem textures.
* **Lens:** Cylindrical wide shield lens with high-wrap coverage. Lens tint is **PRIZM Road** (rich fiery gradient mirroring with hot magenta, deep red, and neon orange/amber refractions).
* **Isolation / Background:** Completely backgroundless (transparent PNG / WebP cutout).
* **Placement & Angle:** Positioned in the upper 55% of the card. Scaled to fill ~85% of card width. Perspective is 3/4 angled view, front-left corner facing viewer, tilted slightly downward.
* **Shadow:** Very subtle, soft ambient occlusion contact shadow beneath the lowest rim edge (`rgba(0,0,0,0.8)`).

#### 3. Text & Copy Block
* **Title:** `SUTRO LITE`
* **Subtitle:** `PRIZM™ ROAD`
* **Divider:** `1px solid #222222; margin: 16px 0;`
* **Features List:**
  * `Expanded field of view.`
  * `Ventilated, lightweight design.`
  * `All day comfort.`
  * *(Line-height: 1.6; color: `#8E8E93`)*

#### 4. Brand Mark
* **Asset:** Oakley Ellipse Logo.
* **Styling:** Horizontal elongated oval ring icon, crisp white stroke/fill (`#FFFFFF`). Centered horizontally at the bottom of the card (`height: 14px; width: auto;`).
