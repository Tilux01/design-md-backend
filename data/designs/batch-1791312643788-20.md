# Design Specification: X-LAB High-End Editorial & E-Commerce Web Application

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
* **Primary Mood:** Avant-garde, industrial, brutalist-luxury, technical, and high-fashion streetwear aesthetic. 
* **Brand Archetype:** Subcultural designer label, performance-meets-luxury technical outerwear (similar to ACRONYM, Kiko Kostadinov, or Off-White).
* **Visual Density:** Intentional high contrast between dense typographic grid columns and generous minimalist negative space.
* **Theme Balance:** Clean, editorial **light mode base** (`#EDEDED`) with stark, saturated **industrial orange accents** (`#FF451B`) and obsidian black contrast elements (`#111111`).
* **Depth & Surface Architecture:** Zero heavy drop-shadows or skeuomorphism. Depth is achieved via stark planar overlapping, strict structural gridlines, and high-contrast color zoning (light grey canvas vs. saturated red horizontal display band).

---

## 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

### Core Colors
* **Primary Accent (Vivid Technical Orange):** `#FF451B` *(Hover: `#E03B13`, Active: `#C4310E`)*
* **Obsidian Black (Text & Primary Contrast):** `#111111`
* **Canvas Light Gray (Base Background):** `#EDEDED`
* **Pure White (Surface Elements):** `#FFFFFF`

### Supporting & Neutral Tokens
* **Muted Gray (Secondary Metadata/Labels):** `#888888`
* **Border / Divider Stroke:** `#D0D0D0`
* **Dark Charcoal (Overlay/Masks):** `#1A1A1A`

---

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy

### Font Families
* **Headings & Display:** *Helvetica Neue / Inter / Akzidenz-Grotesk* (Sans-serif, neutral grotesque with aggressive tracking variations).
* **Metadata & Technical Labels:** *JetBrains Mono / Space Mono* (Monospace for product codes, tracking numbers, and technical specifications).

### Typographic Scale
* **Display / Hero Brand Title (`.lab-title`):**
  * Font-size: `84px` / `5.25rem`
  * Font-weight: `700` (Bold)
  * Line-height: `0.9`
  * Letter-spacing: `-0.04em`
  * Color: `#111111`
* **Section Heading 1 (`h1`):**
  * Font-size: `24px` / `1.5rem`
  * Font-weight: `600`
  * Line-height: `1.1`
  * Letter-spacing: `-0.02em`
* **Body / Editorial Metadata (`.meta-text`):**
  * Font-size: `9px` / `0.5625rem`
  * Font-weight: `500` (Medium)
  * Line-height: `1.4`
  * Letter-spacing: `0.08em` (Uppercase tracked out)
  * Color: `#111111` / `#666666`
* **Navigation Items (`.nav-link`):**
  * Font-size: `10px` / `0.625rem`
  * Font-weight: `500`
  * Letter-spacing: `0.05em`
  * Uppercase styling.

---

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid

* **Container Model:** Fixed-aspect ratio editorial landscape viewport canvas (designed for desktop cinematic presentation).
* **Grid System:** 12-column rigid architectural grid with asymmetric focal weighting.
* **Spacing Scale (8pt grid derivative):**
  * `xxs`: `4px`
  * `xs`: `8px`
  * `sm`: `16px`
  * `md`: `24px`
  * `lg`: `48px`
  * `xl`: `80px`
* **Z-Index Hierarchy:**
  * Base Canvas: `z-index: 0`
  * Background Color Blocks (Orange Banner): `z-index: 10`
  * Typography & Metadata Layers: `z-index: 20`
  * Interactive Elements & Media Imagery: `z-index: 30`
  * Navigation Bar & Floating Controls: `z-index: 50`

---

## 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement

### Top Navigation Bar
* **Position:** Absolute / Fixed top (`top: 32px`, `left: 48px`, `right: 48px`).
* **Layout:** Flexbox horizontal distribution (Space-between).
* **Left cluster:** Plus action trigger (`+1`), followed by category links (`PROJECTS`).
* **Right cluster:** Season identifier (`AW19`), Store switch (`SHOP`), Collection filter (`NEW ARRIVALS ▾`), and Ellipsis settings menu (`...`).

### Hero Section & Content Structure
* **Brutalist Indexing Number (`001`):** 
  * Positioned vertically aligned on the bottom-left. 
  * Features a heavy, custom-designed geometric technical stencil font.
* **Horizontal Technical Banner:**
  * A full-width horizontal vector stripe in `#FF451B` spanning the lower-middle viewport.
  * Intersects precisely behind the lower torso of the hero subject.
* **Primary Call-to-Action (CTA) Arrow Button:**
  * Square container: `40px × 40px`
  * Background Color: `#111111`
  * Icon: White right-pointing arrow (`→`) or down-right directional arrow.
  * Placement: Nested inside the bottom-left edge of the orange banner block.
* **Editorial Model Cutout:**
  * High-contrast technical outerwear portrait (bucket hat, tactical mask, multi-pocket red jacket).
  * Bleeds off the right viewport margin with precise vector clipping.

---

## 6. Motion Mechanics, 3D & Mobile Gestures

* **Animation Triggers:**
  * **Page Entry:** Staggered sliding frames where metadata blocks slide downward from `transform: translateY(-20px)` while the orange banner scales horizontally from `scaleX(0)` to `scaleX(1)`.
  * **Hover States:** Links feature a rapid tracking expansion (letter spacing increases by `0.02em` on hover with a `150ms` cubic-bezier transition).
* **Easing Physics:** 
  * `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (High-end Apple-style fluid deceleration for spatial layout transitions).
* **Transition Durations:**
  * Micro-interactions (Buttons/Links): `150ms` to `200ms`
  * Layout structural shifts: `400ms`
