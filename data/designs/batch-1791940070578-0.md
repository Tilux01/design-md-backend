# Design Specification: "Rock With Me" Music App

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Background:** A soft, linear gradient starting from a pale peach/pink at the top (`#FDE2E2`) to a near-white or very light pink at the bottom (`#FFF9F9`).
*   **Primary Text Color:** Dark Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`) for titles and active text.
*   **Secondary Text Color:** Muted Grey-Pink (`#8E8E8E`) for subtitles, labels, and timestamps.
*   **Accent Color:** Muted Coral/Salmon (`#E8A0A0`) used for the play button background and active progress bars.
*   **Border Radius:** 
    *   Album Art: `12px`
    *   Play Button: `50%` (Circle)
    *   List Thumbnails: `6px`

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Montserrat** or **Poppins** (Geometric Sans-Serif).
*   **Fallback Fonts:** System Sans-Serif, Helvetica, Arial.
*   **Weights & Sizes:**
    *   **Album Label:** Uppercase, Bold, 10pt, Letter spacing: `1px`.
    *   **Album Title (Header):** Bold, 14pt.
    *   **Track Title (Large):** Bold, 24pt.
    *   **Artist Name:** Regular, 14pt.
    *   **List Item Title:** Semi-bold, 14pt.
    *   **Timestamps/Numbers:** Regular, 10pt.
    *   **Nav Labels:** Regular, 9pt.

## 3. Screen A: Now Playing (Left)

### Header
*   **Back Arrow:** Thin line icon, left-aligned.
*   **Center Text:** "ALBUM" (Top, small) and "Rock With Me" (Bottom, bold).
*   **Status Bar:** Standard iOS-style icons (Time 9:41, Signal, Wi-Fi, Battery) in dark grey.

### Main Content
*   **Album Art Image:** 
    *   **Description:** A square image with a solid soft-pink background. In the center, a pair of white over-ear headphones is placed symmetrically. The headphone cord drops straight down from the center.
    *   **Placement:** Centered horizontally, large margins on left/right (approx. 40px).
*   **Track Info:**
    *   "On My Mind" (Centered, Bold).
    *   "Ellie Goulding" (Centered, Regular, Muted Grey).
*   **Progress Bar:**
    *   A thin horizontal line. The "played" portion is the accent coral color; the "remaining" portion is a very light grey.
    *   **Handle:** A small coral circle at the 1:02 mark.
    *   **Timestamps:** "1:02" (Left) and "3:37" (Right) placed just above the bar.

### Controls
*   **Primary Row:** 
    *   **Previous/Next:** Thin line chevron/bar icons.
    *   **Play Button:** A large coral-colored circle with a white "Play" triangle icon centered inside. It has a soft outer glow/shadow of the same color.
*   **Secondary Row:** 
    *   Four icons spaced evenly: Shuffle, Repeat, Playlist, and Share. All thin-line icons in dark charcoal.

### Footer
*   **Up Next Section:** "Up Next" (Bold) and "Life Without You" (Muted). An "up" chevron icon on the far right.
*   **Bottom Navigation:** Four icons (Home/Discover, Music Note, Search, User Profile) with text labels underneath. The "Discover" icon is the active state (darker).

## 4. Screen B: Playlist View (Right)

### Header & Mini-Player
*   The top section mirrors the "Now Playing" header but transitions into a track summary.
*   **Mini-Controls:** "On My Mind" and "Ellie Goulding" are left-aligned. To the right, smaller Previous, Play, and Next icons are grouped.

### Track List
Each row consists of:
1.  **Index Number:** (e.g., 04, 05) - Muted grey, left-aligned.
2.  **Thumbnail Image:** 
    *   **Description:** Small square images with rounded corners. Each features abstract, high-contrast artistic photography (e.g., a sunset, a forest, a dark silhouette).
    *   **Background:** These images are full-bleed within their small squares (no transparency).
3.  **Track Title:** (e.g., "New York Soul II") - Bold, black.
4.  **Action Icons:** A heart (outline) and a "three-dot" more icon, right-aligned.

## 5. Image Assets Detailed Descriptions

1.  **Main Album Art (Headphones):**
    *   **Content:** Minimalist studio shot. 
    *   **Subject:** White matte headphones, viewed from the front.
    *   **Background:** Solid pastel pink (`#F4B4B4`).
    *   **Rotation:** 0 degrees (perfectly vertical).
    *   **Transparency:** None (Full square).

2.  **Playlist Thumbnails:**
    *   **NY Soul II:** Dark orange/purple sunset over a city skyline.
    *   **Fashion:** Abstract teal and dark brown textures.
    *   **Maybe IDK:** Red and dark grey abstract painting style.
    *   **Woke The F**k Up:** Dark, moody forest or underwater scene (mostly black/dark green).
    *   **Overwhelming:** Bright teal/blue sky with clouds.
    *   **Weight of the World:** Dark grey/black textured stone or metal.
    *   **The Good In Me:** Green foliage/forest canopy shot from below.

## 6. Iconography Style
*   **Stroke Weight:** 1.5px to 2px.
*   **Style:** Linear/Outline (not filled), except for the Play button icon.
*   **Corner Radius:** Slightly rounded ends on icon lines for a "soft" feel.
