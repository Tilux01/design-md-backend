# Design Specification: "Into the Wild" Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Layout Structure
* **Scope**: Fullscreen desktop landing page (`100vw`, `100vh`), overflow hidden.
* **Layout**: Multi-layer stacked layout:
  * **Base Layer**: Fullscreen background photograph.
  * **Overlay Layer**: Radial/linear gradient scrim on the left half for text legibility.
  * **Content Layer**: Flex/grid container hosting header, hero content, controls, and social links.

---

## 2. Typography
* **Primary Font**: `Poppins`, `Montserrat`, or `Inter` (sans-serif).
* **Fallback Stack**: `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.

| Element | Weight | Size | Letter Spacing | Color |

| **Nav Links** | 500 (Medium) | 14px / 0.875rem | Normal | `#FFFFFF` |

---

## 3. Color Palette
* **Background Dark Scrim**: `rgba(7, 18, 26, 0.85)` fading horizontally to `rgba(7, 18, 26, 0)` at 60% viewport width.
* **Text Primary**: `#FFFFFF`
* **Text Secondary**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`
* **Surface White**: `#FFFFFF`
* **Border Color**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`

---

## 4. Visual Assets & Imagery
### Background Image
* **Description**: High-resolution, top-down aerial photograph of a tropical coast. Deep navy/black water on the left, transitioning to turquoise shallow reefs, white sandbars, and a dense, hook-shaped dark green forest canopy in the center and right.
* **Format**: Full cover (`background-size: cover`, `background-position: center right`), non-transparent, 0° rotation.
* **Treatment**: Soft dark gradient mask applied to the left 50% to ensure high contrast for typography.

---

## 5. Component Specifications

### 5.1 Navigation Bar
* **Position**: Top, full width (`padding: 32px 64px`), flexbox layout.
* **Left**: "Home" link.
* **Right**: Horizontal list: "Download app", "Search", "Sign in", "Register" with `32px` gap.

### 5.2 Hero Content (Left-Aligned)
* **Position**: Absolute/flex positioned `15%` from left, vertically centered.
* **Max Width**: `420px`.
* **Hierarchy**:
  1. `INTO THE` (subtitle)
  2. `WILD` (primary display heading, tight line-height)
  3. Description block: Left-bordered with a thin `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.4)` line, `12px` left padding.
* **Buttons Container**: Horizontal flex row, `16px` gap, `24px` top margin:
  * **Primary**: White pill button (`padding: 10px 24px`, `border-radius: 4px`, solid `#FFFFFF`, text `#0B131A`).
  * **Secondary**: Outline ghost button (`padding: 10px 24px`, `border-radius: 4px`, `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.8)`, text `#FFFFFF`).

### 5.3 Audio / Media Controller
* **Position**: Below CTA buttons (`margin-top: 48px`).
* **Elements**:
  * Circular play button: `28px` diameter, translucent white fill, centered white play triangle icon.
  * Timeline bar: `180px` width, `2px` height, horizontal rule with `30%` active white segment and `70%` faint translucent track.

### 5.4 Social Links (Bottom-Left)
* **Position**: Fixed bottom-left corner (`bottom: 32px`, `left: 48px`).
* **Direction**: Vertical column, `16px` gap.
* **Icons**: Small (`16px`), monochrome white: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram.

### 5.5 Carousel Pagination (Bottom-Right)
* **Position**: Fixed bottom-right corner (`bottom: 40px`, `right: 64px`).
* **Layout**: Horizontal row of 3 circular dots (`6px` diameter, `8px` gap).
* **States**: First dot `#FFFFFF` (active); remaining dots `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`.
