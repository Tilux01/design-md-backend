# UI/UX Specification: TaskLab Dashboard

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

### Viewport & Background
*   **Viewport Size:** Desktop-first (1440px wide minimum, fluid scaling).
*   **Background:** High-contrast, desaturated landscape photography of a desert dune with a heavy grain/frosted glass overlay (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`).
*   **Theme Mode:** Dark/Glassmorphic hybrid style utilizing translucent white panels, deep charcoal cards (`#141414`), and vibrant lime-green accents (`#CCFF00`).

### Grid & Spacing
*   **Layout Structure:** Three-column functional layout.
    *   *Column 1 (Left):* Fixed Navigation Sidebar + Floating Chat Widget (`width: 240px`).
    *   *Column 2 (Center):* Main Content Hub / Analytics Workspace (`flex: 1`, max-width: 780px).
    *   *Column 3 (Right):* Productivity Metrics, History Calendars, and Time Tracking (`width: 320px`).
*   **Spacing Scale:** 4px baseline grid (8px, 12px, 16px, 24px, 32px padding/margins).
*   **Border Radius:** Highly rounded containers (`border-radius: 24px` for major cards, `16px` for nested blocks, `50px` for pills/buttons).

---

## 2. Typography System

### Primary Font Family
*   **Font Family:** `Inter`, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.
*   **Fallback:** System sans-serif stack.

### Type Hierarchy
*   **App Logo / Brand:** 
    *   Size: 18px / Weight: Bold (700) / Tracking: Tight (-0.02em).
*   **Page Title ("Your Team Members"):** 
    *   Size: 26px / Weight: Bold (700) / Color: `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Section Headers (Tasks, Core Team, Team Insights):** 
    *   Size: 16px to 18px / Weight: Semibold (600) / Color: `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Body & Descriptions:** 
    *   Size: 13px / Weight: Regular (400) / Color: `#8A8A8A` to `#CCCCCC`.
*   **Microcopy / Metadata (Dates, Stats):** 
    *   Size: 11px to 12px / Weight: Medium (500).
*   **Large Numeric Display (Timers, Hours):** 
    *   Size: 32px (Tasks total) to 36px (Time Tracker) / Weight: Bold (700).

---

## 3. Component Specifications

### 3.1. Left Navigation Sidebar
*   **Background:** Translucent white glass panel (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).
*   **Brand Header:** TaskLab logo with a square black icon containing a stylized intersecting loop (`#000000`).
*   **Nav Items:** Vertical list with icon + text label.
    *   *Active State ("Team"):* Solid black background pill (`#000000`), white text and icon.
    *   *Inactive States:* Dark gray text (`#444444`), simple line icons (Home, Projects, Meetings, Schedules, Analytics, Calendar, Settings).
*   **Chat Widget (Bottom Left):**
    *   Floating card integrated into the lower sidebar.
    *   Header: "Chat" with a green notification badge (`46`).
    *   Subtext: "Lukas is typing..." with animated dots.
    *   Message Bubbles: Mixed sender avatars (circular photos), audio waveform players (`1:10`, `1:56`), text snippets ("Great job, Guys, keep it up!").
    *   Input Bar: Text field with attachment icon and voice note icon.

### 3.2. Center Workspace (Main Dashboard)

#### Header Bar
*   **Title:** "Your Team Members" (Subtitle: "Manage all your projects with team members").
*   **Top Right Utilities:** 
    *   Status widget showing date (`12:45PM, 18 Apr, Tue`), weather (`23°C Cloudy`), and team warning pill ("You can add board for your team").

#### Top Row Cards
1.  **Tasks Card:**
    *   Background: White card (`#FFFFFF`) with dark text.
    *   Header: "Tasks" icon + title, featuring an open-link circular arrow icon.
    *   Metric: "34,5 h" (Large bold font).
    *   Legend: Green dot ("In Work") and light gray/blue dot ("Completed").
2.  **Core Team Card:**
    *   Background: Lime-yellow solid fill (`#D1F853`).
    *   Header: Green user profile icon + "Core Team" / "Product Members".
    *   Subtext: "32 Members".
    *   Stacked Avatars: Four overlapping circular profile photos of diverse team members, plus a "+29" overflow counter pill.
3.  **Add New Board Card:**
    *   Style: Dashed border outline container with a glowing lime-green circular "+" button in the center.

#### Analytics Bar Chart
*   **Background:** Solid dark charcoal (`#0F0F0F`).
*   **Visuals:** Vertical white rounded bar chart representing days/months (Jan through Jul), overlaid with an organic, glowing lime-green smooth trend line passing across the data points.
*   **Productivity Change Cards (Right of Chart):**
    *   Top card: "Productivity this week" with a red negative pill `"-7%"`.
    *   Bottom card: "Productivity this month" with a green positive pill `"+13%"`.

#### Schedule & Process Matrix
*   **Schedule Header:** Date navigator ("April 2023") with left/right arrows, showing a weekly date track (M3 to F14) with active indicator on "W5".
*   **Task List ("15 Upcoming Tasks"):** Nested cards detailing operational workflows (e.g., "UX Research", "Prototype", "UI Kit", "Moodboard") mapped across a dotted matrix timeline grid. Each task item includes completion percentages and assigned team member avatars.
*   **Process Widget:** Lime-yellow container (`#D1F853`) categorizing tasks into pills: "In Test", "Reviewed", "Complete" with status dots.

---

### 3.3. Right Column (Insights, History & Tracking)

#### Top Status Bar
*   **Controls:** Toggle switch (dark theme active).
*   **Meeting Alert:** Notification banner showing meeting time (`12:45PM Starts Next Meeting`, countdown timer `-02:31:12`) with a user avatar thumbnail.

#### Team Insights Card
*   **Header:** "Team Insights" with a lightning bolt icon and "View all >" link.
*   **Audio/Activity Waveform:** Dense vertical frequency spectrum graph in white and gray, annotated with tiny user avatars below active peaks.
*   **Controls:** Two user profile thumbnails coupled with a solid black play button circle.

#### History & Calendar Section
*   **Header:** "History" / "Working Days" with active selector pills ("Yours", "Lukas", "Janny", "Andrey", "Tiffany").
*   **Calendar Grid:** Monthly view for April 2023.
    *   Days of the week initials (M, T, W, T, F, S, S).
    *   Days `1` to `30` in a grid. Selected active working days are highlighted with solid black circular backgrounds (`#000000`) and white text, while unselected days use faint gray outlines or solid lime backgrounds for designated dates.
*   **Navigation:** Bottom row features left/right arrows and the label "April 2023".

#### Time Tracker Module
*   **Background:** Deep black container (`#0A0A0A`).
*   **Header:** "Time Tracker" with a clock icon and settings button.
*   **Display:** Large digital timer digits: `07:40:15` rendered in bold white monospace-style typography.
*   **Controls:** Pause/Play dual circular buttons (`||` and `[ ]`) in solid white/black accents.

---

## 4. Image Assets & Visual Placeholders

*   **Avatars:** Circular profile photos scattered across chat bubbles, team cards, and schedule rows. 
    *   *Description:* High-resolution professional portraits of diverse individuals (men and women of various ethnicities) shot against neutral or studio backdrops, cropped tightly to circular bounds with subtle white borders.
*   **Background Scene:** 
    *   *Description:* Grayscale landscape photograph depicting rolling sand dunes under an overcast, textured sky, heavily processed with high contrast and a dense frosted-glass blur effect.
