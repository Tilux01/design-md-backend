# Design Specification: Supercell Games Dashboard

## 1. General Layout & Theme
- **Background:** Solid white (`#FFFFFF`). The design should be full-screen (100vw, 100vh) with no external borders or frames.
- **Canvas:** A centered container with a maximum width of 1440px, though the elements should scale responsively.
- **Padding:** Global horizontal padding of `5%`.
- **Primary Aesthetic:** Clean, minimalist UI with high-contrast 3D character renders that "pop" out of their containers.

## 2. Typography
- **Primary Font:** A bold, geometric sans-serif. 
    - *Recommended:* **Inter** (Bold/Extra Bold) or **Montserrat**.
    - *Fallback:* `System-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
- **Logo Font:** Blocky, heavy, stacked sans-serif (Custom Supercell branding).
- **Text Colors:**
    - Headings/Titles: Pure Black (`#000000`).
    - Subtitles/Metadata: Medium Grey (`#888888`) or white when on dark backgrounds.
    - Navigation: Dark Grey (`#333333`).

## 3. Header Section
- **Logo (Left):** "SUPER CELL" text stacked vertically. Black, heavy weight, uppercase.
- **Navigation (Left-Center):** The word "Games" in a medium weight, placed next to the logo.
- **Search Bar (Center):**
    - **Shape:** Pill-shaped (fully rounded corners).
    - **Color:** Very light grey background (`#F2F2F2`).
    - **Content:** A small magnifying glass icon on the left, followed by the placeholder text "Search games..." in a light grey.
- **Menu (Right):**
    - **Text:** "Menu" in medium weight.
    - **Icon:** A "bento menu" icon (4 small squares arranged in a 2x2 grid) placed to the right of the text.

## 4. Main Content: Game Carousel
The center of the screen features a horizontal row of cards. Each card consists of a colored background rectangle and a character image that overflows the top.

### Card Shared Properties
- **Shape:** Rounded rectangles (border-radius: `24px`).
- **Dimensions:** Aspect ratio approximately 4:5.
- **Shadow:** Soft, diffused drop shadow below the card.
- **Text Layout:** Bottom-aligned inside the card. Title in Bold White, Subtitle in smaller, lighter White.

### Individual Card Details

#### Card 1: Brawl Stars
- **Background Color:** Vibrant Red (`#D62D2D`).
- **Image Description:** 
    - **Characters:** Nita (a girl in a bear hat) riding on top of Bruce (a large red spirit bear).
    - **Placement:** The bear's body fills the card; the girl and the bear's head extend significantly above the top edge of the card.
    - **Rotation:** Facing slightly right (approx. 10 degrees).
    - **Background:** Transparent (backgroundless PNG).
- **Text:** "Brawl Stars" (Title), "Supercell · Action" (Subtitle).

#### Card 2: Clash Royale
- **Background Color:** Bright Blue (`#2D7DD6`).
- **Image Description:** 
    - **Character:** The Prince riding a pony. He holds a long blue-and-white striped lance.
    - **Placement:** The pony is centered; the lance extends diagonally toward the top-left corner, breaking the card boundary.
    - **Rotation:** Facing forward-right.
    - **Background:** Transparent (backgroundless PNG).
- **Text:** "Clash Royale" (Title), "Supercell · Strategy" (Subtitle).

#### Card 3: Clash of Clans
- **Background Color:** Dark Charcoal/Brown (`#2B2522`).
- **Image Description:** 
    - **Character:** A Barbarian with yellow hair and a mustache, swinging a sword.
    - **Placement:** The character is leaning forward; the sword and his right arm extend past the left and top edges. Small brown "dust" or "debris" particles surround him.
    - **Rotation:** Dynamic action pose, leaning left.
    - **Background:** Transparent (backgroundless PNG).
- **Text:** "Clash of Clans" (Title), "Supercell · Strategy" (Subtitle).

#### Card 4: Boom Beach (Partially Visible)
- **Background Color:** Muted Purple (`#6C5B7B`).
- **Image Description:** 
    - **Character:** Dr. T (a villain in a lab coat).
    - **Placement:** Only the right half of the character is visible as the carousel cuts off.
    - **Background:** Transparent (backgroundless PNG).
- **Text:** "Boom Beach" (Title).

## 5. Footer Section
- **Social Links (Bottom Left):**
    - "Facebook" and "Youtube" listed horizontally with generous spacing.
    - Font: Medium weight, Dark Grey (`#555555`).
- **Pagination Controls (Bottom Right):**
    - **Prev:** A left-facing chevron `<` followed by the word "Prev".
    - **Next:** The word "Next" followed by a right-facing chevron `>`.
    - **Style:** Black text, clickable/hover state indicated by slight opacity change.

## 6. Technical Implementation Notes
- **Overflow:** The container for the cards must have `overflow: visible` to allow the character heads and weapons to pop out of the colored boxes.
- **Spacing:** Use `justify-content: space-between` for the header and footer to push elements to the edges.
- **Transitions:** If implemented as a functional site, use a smooth `ease-in-out` transition for the carousel sliding effect.
