# Design Specification: Premium SaaS Glassmorphism Landing Page

## 1. Overview
This design is a highly elegant, premium, minimalist landing page utilizing a refined "Glassmorphism" aesthetic layered over a soft, 3D-rendered beige and rose-gold environment. 

**Important Layout Note:** Per the requirements, this design is intended to be **full-screen**. The entire 3D background scene should stretch to the edges of the viewport (100vw, 100vh). The UI elements (Navigation, Grid, Center Card, and Floating Assets) sit absolutely positioned or centered on top of this background.

## 2. Typography
The typography is clean, rounded, and geometric. It conveys a modern tech/SaaS feel but remains approachable.

*   **Primary Font:** `Poppins` or `Gilroy` (A rounded, geometric sans-serif).
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `'Montserrat', 'Helvetica Neue', Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
*   **Weights Used:**
    *   **Bold (600/700):** Used for the Logo/Brand name ("Premium Saas/i2B") and the Main Hero Title ("Progria Nova").
    *   **Regular (400):** Used for Navigation links, Subtitle text, and Button text.

## 3. Color Palette
The color palette relies heavily on soft creams, warm beiges, and rose-gold/copper accents, combined with transparent whites for the glass effects.

*   **Dark Text/Brand Color:** `#333333` to `#4A4543` (A very dark, warm charcoal/brown).
*   **Primary White Text:** `#FFFFFF` (Used on the glass cards).
*   **Subtitle Text:** `#E0E0E0` (Slightly dimmed white).
*   **Nav Link Text:** `#7A7571` (A medium-light warm gray).
*   **Button Glow Accent:** `#F2C4A2` (A soft, warm peach/orange glow).
*   **Glass Background Base:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` (Semi-transparent white).
*   **Glass Border Base:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` (Used for the top and left edges to create a 3D bevel effect).

## 4. Background (Full Screen)
The background is a cohesive 3D scene that spans the entire viewport. 
*   **Base Layer:** A high-quality image of a soft, studio-lit 3D environment.
*   **Scene Description:** A smooth, highly reflective beige floor transitioning into a seamless beige backdrop. The lighting is soft and diffused, coming from the top left, casting soft shadows to the right. 
*   **Static Background 3D Elements (Part of the background image):**
    *   *Bottom Left (Extreme Foreground):* A massive, heavily blurred peach-colored sphere.
    *   *Mid-Left:* A smooth beige dome shape with a glowing arched light wrapping around its base.
    *   *Mid-Right:* A cluster featuring a textured, speckled stone arch, a smooth peach sphere resting next to it, a metallic rose-gold hoop, and soft green plant leaves slightly out of focus in the depth.

## 5. Background Grid Overlay
Between the 3D background and the UI elements is a subtle, technical grid.
*   **Style:** Faint, semi-transparent white lines (`rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`).
*   **Detail:** At every intersection of the grid lines, there is a tiny, solid white dot (`1px` or `2px` wide).
*   **Placement:** The grid occupies the central area of the screen, framing the main content. It does not touch the absolute edges of the screen.

## 6. Top Navigation Bar
A horizontal glassmorphism bar resting near the top of the viewport.

*   **Container Style:** 
    *   Frosted glass effect (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
    *   Background color: `rgba(245, 240, 235, 0.4)` (slightly warmer than pure white glass).
    *   Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`.
    *   Border Radius: Completely rounded edges (pill shape, approx `24px` radius).
    *   Padding: Generous left and right padding.
*   **Left Element (Brand):** Text "Premium Saas/i2B". Color: Dark Text. Font Weight: Bold.
*   **Center Elements (Links):** Three textual links horizontally spaced: "Ultraoe", "Sürudile", "Sigdiev". Color: Nav Link Text. Font Weight: Regular. Size: Small (`~14px`).
*   **Right Elements (Icons & Button):**
    *   *Icons:* Three minimalist line-art icons spaced evenly: A magnifying glass (search), an equals sign (hamburger menu alternative), and a circle with a dot inside (user/profile). Color: Nav Link Text.
    *   *Button:* A solid white pill-shaped button. 
    *   *Button Text:* "max 5 ins". Color: Dark Text. Font Weight: Bold.

## 7. Main Hero Card (Center)
The focal point of the UI, floating directly in the center of the screen.

*   **Container Style:**
    *   Large rectangle with heavily rounded corners (`~30px` radius).
    *   Frosted glass effect (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
    *   Background color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
    *   Borders: Subtle white border on top and left (`rgba(255,255,255,0.4)`) to simulate light catching the glass edge. No border on bottom/right.
    *   Shadow: Soft, diffuse drop shadow (`rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`) to separate it from the floating elements behind it.
*   **Content (Centered vertically and horizontally):**
    *   **Title:** "Progria Nova". Color: Pure White. Font Weight: Bold. Size: Large (`~48px`).
    *   **Subtitle:** "Slort hium,o higahises - elmsreeclecnogrado clialty 1170pm". Color: Subtitle Text. Font Weight: Regular. Size: Small (`~14px`). Margins: Generous top and bottom spacing.
    *   **Call to Action (CTA) Button:**
        *   Shape: Pill-shaped (fully rounded).
        *   Background: Very light beige/white.
        *   Text: "Ccta hera". Color: Dark Text. Font weight: Bold. Size: Small.
        *   **Crucial Detail (Glow):** The button has a distinct, intense, soft outer glow in a warm peach/orange color (`box-shadow: 0px 0px 20px 5px rgba(242, 196, 162, 0.6)`).

## 8. Floating 3D Assets (Backgroundless Images)
Surrounding the central Hero Card are 5 distinct 3D objects. **These should be implemented as individual, backgroundless PNG/WebP images** positioned via CSS (absolute positioning with `z-index` and `transform` properties).

**Asset 1: The Small Sphere**
*   **Image Description:** A perfectly smooth, matte sphere in a warm peach/flesh tone. Softly lit from the top left.
*   **Placement:** Top left of the main Hero Card.
*   **Z-Index:** Behind the left floating glass card (Asset 2), but in front of the grid.
*   **Rotation/Scale:** Standard scale, no rotation needed.

**Asset 2: Top-Left Glass Card**
*   **Image Description:** A rectangular piece of frosted glass with rounded corners. Inside the glass is an embedded design: a curved, swooping line made of solid white and a metallic rose-gold trim. 
*   **Placement:** Left side, partially tucked behind the main central Hero Card.
*   **Z-Index:** Behind the main Hero Card.
*   **Rotation/Transform:** Tilted diagonally. Rotated roughly 35 degrees clockwise on the Z-axis, and angled slightly backward in 3D space (perspective/rotateX).

**Asset 3: Top-Right Glass Card**
*   **Image Description:** Similar to Asset 2. A frosted glass rectangle with rounded corners, featuring a sweeping white and metallic rose-gold curve embedded within it.
*   **Placement:** Top right, hovering above and slightly behind the main central Hero Card.
*   **Z-Index:** Behind the main Hero Card.
*   **Rotation/Transform:** Tilted diagonally. Rotated roughly -25 degrees (counter-clockwise) on the Z-axis.

**Asset 4: Bottom-Left Terrazzo Card**
*   **Image Description:** A solid, opaque rectangular card with rounded corners. The surface texture is a speckled beige terrazzo pattern (dark brown and gray dots on a beige base). A metallic rose-gold curved line cuts across it.
*   **Placement:** Bottom left, emerging from behind the main Hero Card.
*   **Z-Index:** Behind the main Hero Card.
*   **Rotation/Transform:** Highly angled. It appears to be laying almost flat but tilted towards the viewer. Rotated roughly 45 degrees clockwise on the Z-axis, with significant X-axis rotation giving it a skewed, flat perspective.

**Asset 5: Bottom-Right Detailed Glass Card**
*   **Image Description:** The largest floating card. A frosted glass rectangle with rounded corners. The embedded design features a large sweeping white curve bordered by a thick metallic rose-gold band. The rose-gold band has a textured, stippled/speckled pattern on it. There are also fine, etched parallel lines on the bottom left corner of the glass.
*   **Placement:** Bottom right, overlapping the bottom right corner of the main Hero Card.
*   **Z-Index:** **In front** of the main Hero Card.
*   **Rotation/Transform:** Tilted diagonally. Rotated roughly -30 degrees (counter-clockwise) on the Z-axis.
