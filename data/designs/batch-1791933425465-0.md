# Design System & Implementation Spec: Aura Store

## 1. Overview & Layout Concept
* **Type:** Fullscreen brutalist/techwear e-commerce landing hero.
* **Layout:** Full-viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), centered single-screen layout with an asymmetrical grid. Outer window bezels/borders are omitted for full-bleed display.
* **Base Background:** Off-white to pale warm grey (`#F1F1F0`) with a subtle monochromatic film grain overlay.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Background:** `#F1F1F0`
* **Primary Text / Solids:** `#111111` (Near-black)
* **Secondary / Muted Text:** `#737373`
* **Card Border / Outlines:** `#111111` (1.5px solid)
* **Accent (in graphic):** Neon Crimson / Flesh Red (`#FF3B5C` on the jacket print)

---

## 3. Typography & Fallbacks

| Role | Primary Font | Alternative / Fallbacks | Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hero & Headings** | *Clash Display* (Bold) | *Syne*, *Impact*, *Helvetica Neue (Heavy)*, sans-serif | Tight tracking, high contrast, textured fill. |
| **Interface / Tech Text** | *Eurostile Extended* | *Space Grotesk*, *Orbitron*, *Courier New*, sans-serif | Wide horizontal stance, geometric/retro-futuristic. |
| **Body Description** | *Microgramma / Eurostile* | *Rajdhani*, *Space Grotesk*, sans-serif | Wide lowercase/small-caps feel, extended tracking. |

---

## 4. Header Section
* **Dimensions:** Edge-to-edge, `padding: 24px 40px`.
* **Left:** 
  * Stacked text: `Aura` (top), `store` (bottom) in bold display sans.
  * Right of the text: A cluster of four 4-pointed sparkle stars.
* **Center (Nav Items):**
  * Horizontal row: `main` (active: `#111`), `shop`, `custom`, `about`, `contact` (inactive: `#737373`).
  * Font: Tech/Extended sans, lowercase, `12px`.
* **Right:**
  * Label: `account id - 003.201` (Tech font, lowercase, `12px`).

---

## 5. Central Hero Section

### A. Large Watermark / Text
* Content: **"Aura"** (left aligned) and **"store"** (right aligned).
* Styling: Font-size approx. `14vw`, bold, black with internal stippled/grain noise.
* Position: Centered vertically, layered behind the central model cutout.

### B. Center Cutout Model
* **Image Description:** Standalone full-shot cutout of a fashion model with transparent background (PNG/WebP). The model's head is completely wrapped in a black textured veil/fabric. Wears heavy layered oversized techwear/streetwear: faded charcoal/black distressed zip work jacket, dark hoodie, and black ribbed knee tactical cargo pants.
* **Positioning:** Exact center (`transform: translate(-50%, 0)`), scaled to cover ~85% of viewport height from lower chest/knees down to the bottom border. 0° rotation.

---

## 6. Bottom Left Column
* **Position:** Absolute, pinned bottom-left (`left: 40px`, `bottom: 40px`), `max-width: 320px`.
* **Elements:**
  1. **Icon:** Japanese recycling plastic mark (square with rounded corners enclosing `プラ`).
  2. **Subheading:** "Clothes without excess. Only style." (Heavy sans-serif, `24px`, tight line-height).
  3. **Body Paragraph:** "Modern silhouettes, natural fabrics, and honest design. For those who choose simplicity and quality." (Extended tech font, `11px`, line-height `1.5`, `#111`).

---

## 7. Bottom Right Floating Preview
* **Position:** Absolute, pinned bottom-right (`right: 40px`, `bottom: 40px`), flex column, gap `12px`, alignment `center`.

### A. Product Thumbnail Card
* **Dimensions:** `140px × 180px`, rounded corners (`16px`), border: `1px solid #111111`.
* **Content:**
  * **Product Image:** Backgroundless cutout of a black cropped leather puffer jacket featuring a bright pink/crimson anatomical skeleton graphic across the chest and torso. Centered, 0° rotation.
  * **Label:** `2025` positioned directly below the jacket in extended tech font (`12px`).

### B. Call to Action Button
* **Shape:** Pill / fully-rounded container (`border-radius: 9999px`), `border: 1px solid #111111`.
* **Padding:** `6px 18px`.
* **Content:** `new collection ↗` (Extended tech font, lowercase, `11px`).
