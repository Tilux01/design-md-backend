# Comprehensive UI/UX Design Specification & Replication Guide

This document outlines the complete structural, visual, and functional blueprint for replicating the provided Mercedes-Benz C 63 S Coupe web interface. The design will be implemented as a fully responsive, edge-to-edge full-screen layout, stripping away any browser window chrome or mockup bounding boxes present in the source image.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Typography Architecture

### Layout System
* **Viewport:** Full-screen (`100vw` width, minimum `100vh` height), zero external padding.
* **Canvas Background:** Pure clean white (`#FFFFFF`) throughout the primary body, punctuated by soft, frosted-glass structural cards (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` to `rgba(245, 245, 247, 0.8)` with backdrop filters).
* **Grid Structure:** A fluid, asymmetrical 12-column macro-grid with distinct modular floating layout panels.

### Typography Hierarchy & Fallback Stack
The design relies on a clean, modern, ultra-legible geometric sans-serif aesthetic resembling Apple's San Francisco or Inter design systems.

1. **Primary Font Family:** `'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
2. **Font Weights Used:** Light (300), Regular (400), Medium (500), Semi-Bold (600).

| Element | Size | Weight | Line Height | Color / Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Main Heading** (`Mercedes-Benz`) | `32px` (`2rem`) | Semi-Bold (600) | `1.2` | `#111111` (Dark Charcoal) |
| **Subheading** (`AMG C 63 S Coupe - 2023`) | `14px` (`0.875rem`) | Regular (400) | `1.4` | `#6E6E73` (Muted Grey) |
| **Navigation & Links** | `13px` (`0.8125rem`) | Medium (500) | `1.0` | `#1D1D1F` active, `#86868B` inactive |
| **Feature Callout Text** | `11px` (`0.6875rem`) | Regular (400) | `1.3` | `#424245` |
| **Card Data Metrics** (`503 hp`) | `36px` (`2.25rem`) | Light / Regular | `1.0` | `#111111` |

---

## 2. Header & Navigation Structure

Positioned fixed at the top edge of the viewport with a subtle backdrop blur.

### Top Bar Components
* **Left Branding Area:** 
  * Text block: `"Car."` in bold dark weight, followed by a compact stacked secondary badge reading `"Cars Sell / Center"` in micro-typography (`9px`, upper-case).
* **Center Navigation Pills:**
  * A horizontal floating segmented control bar with a pill-shaped dark active state indicator.
  * Links: `Models` (Active: black pill background `#000000`, white text `#FFFFFF`), `Services`, `Experience`, `Shop`, `Purchase`, `Configurate`.
* **Right Utility Actions:**
  * Icons represented as minimalist line-art symbols (Search/Magnifying glass, User profile, Global settings/Hamburger menu).

## 3. Hero Section & Interactive Car Showcase

The central canvas features the hero vehicle display layered over a subtle, giant background watermark logo.

### Background Elements
* **Watermark Graphic:** An oversized, ultra-low-opacity (`opacity: 0.04`) outline of the iconic Mercedes-Benz three-pointed star centered directly behind the vehicle profile.

### Primary Vehicle Image Specifications
* **Asset Type:** High-resolution side-profile studio shot of a matte-gold/bronze Mercedes-Benz AMG C 63 S Coupe.
* **Format:** Backgroundless PNG/WebP cutout.
* **Placement & Rotation:** Centered horizontally within the main stage area, rotated at `0deg` (level horizon), showing clear ground shadow gradients underneath the tires (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)` feathered blur).

### Interactive Hotspots (Callout Bubbles)
Positioned dynamically across the body of the car using absolute coordinate percentages relative to the vehicle container:
1. **Warranty Tooltip (`top: 32%, left: 30%`):**
   * Floating rounded card with a connecting pointer line pointing to the rear quarter panel.
   * Text: *"A variety of warranty options are available for your new or Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) Mercedes-Benz."*
2. **AMG Night Pack (`top: 48%, left: 24%`):** Small circular interaction node.
3. **AMG Body Styl (`top: 55%, left: 45%`):** Small circular interaction node.
4. **Panorama Roof (`top: 44%, left: 60%`):** Small circular interaction node.
5. **Pure Perform (`top: 60%, left: 68%`):** Small circular interaction node.
