# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Industrial Luxury Hardware Product Visualizer / Precision Audio Architecture.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-contrast minimalist staging. Tight macro framing emphasizes tactile engineering, metallic textures, and precision tolerances.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered mechanical articulation. The upper headband housing rests atop a brushed-aluminum slider fork, which connects via a micro-hinge and barrel pivot directly to the brushed radial ear-cup housing.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Macro industrial product styling, brushed anisotropic highlights, machined hex-screw detailing, high-precision geometry.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(197, 203, 211, 0.1)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-blue-500 inline-block"></span>`.
