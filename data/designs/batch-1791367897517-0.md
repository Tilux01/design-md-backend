# Design Specification: E-Shop Media Brand Identity

This document outlines the precise specifications for recreating the provided "E-Shop" logo and brand mark as a high-fidelity, full-screen vector asset, stripped of any background framing or mockups.

---

## 1. Global Specifications

*   **Format:** Scalable Vector Graphic (SVG) / Full-Screen Responsive
*   **Background:** Transparent (No background container, mockup surfaces, or outer padding)
*   **Color Palette Profile:** RGB
*   **Composition Style:** Modern, e-commerce oriented, isometric/perspective line-art combined with stylized typography.

---

## 2. Color Palette

Use these exact hex codes for color matching across vector paths and text layers:

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Accent** | Vibrant Red / Coral | `#FF3333` | Shopping bag outline, handle contours |
| **Secondary Accent** | Electric Orange | `#FF6600` | The letter **"S"**, gradient transitions on the bag |
| **Primary Brand Blue**| Cyan / Light Blue | `#00BFFF` | Letters **"HOP"**, integration line |
| **Neutral Subtext** | Deep Charcoal / Black | `#1A1A1A` | Secondary text ("SOCIAL MEDIA" or similar sub-branding) |

---

## 3. Typography Specifications

The wordmark uses a custom, highly stylized geometric sans-serif font featuring sharp angles and integrated styling.

### Primary Wordmark: "SHOP"
*   **Letter S:**
    *   **Color:** Electric Orange (`#FF6600`)
    *   **Style:** Angular, blocky terminals matching the shopping bag's handle drop angle.
*   **Letters H-O-P:**
    *   **Color:** Cyan/Light Blue (`#00BFFF`)
    *   **Style:** Connected or tightly kerned sans-serif characters with rounded outer curves and sharp interior corners.

### Secondary Subtext (If applicable):
*   **Text:** `SOCIAL MEDIA` (or equivalent sub-tagline)
*   **Font Family:** Clean Geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., Montserrat, Helvetica Bold, or Gotham)
*   **Case:** Uppercase
*   **Placement:** Positioned directly beneath the baseline of the main "SHOP" wordmark.

---

## 4. Graphic Iconography: The Shopping Bag

The left-hand icon represents a stylized shopping bag viewed from a dynamic isometric perspective.

*   **Structure:**
    *   Outlined using a dynamic stroke weight that tapers slightly from top to bottom.
    *   **Handle:** Loops outward and downward, integrating seamlessly into the top curve of the orange letter **"S"**.
    *   **Perspective:** Slanted forward to suggest motion and modern e-commerce speed.
*   **Stroke Properties:**
    *   **Stroke Color:** Vibrant Red (`#FF3333`) transitioning subtly into orange at stress points.
    *   **Corner Radius:** Sharp miters at the base, slight smoothing on the bag folds.

---

## 5. Layout & Alignment

1.  **Composition:** Horizontal lockup consisting of the **Isometric Shopping Bag** on the left, immediately followed by the **Wordmark ("SHOP")** nested inside and extending to the right.
2.  **Full-Screen Rendering:** 
    *   Center the entire composite logo within the viewport.
    *   Ensure zero margins/padding outside the bounding box of the graphic elements when exporting to vector formats.
