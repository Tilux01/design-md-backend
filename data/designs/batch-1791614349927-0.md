# Design Specification: Valentine’s Interactive Envelope Screen

This document provides the pixel-level visual and architectural design specification for creating a 1:1 full-screen mobile web experience based on the provided media, omitting the external mobile frame and outer social media caption.

---

## 1. Global Viewport & Layout Architecture

* **Target Device Layout:** Full-bleed, edge-to-edge mobile viewport (`100vw`, `100vh` / `100dvh`).
* **Safe Area Handling:** 
  * `padding-top: env(safe-area-inset-top, 24px)`
  * `padding-bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 24px)`
* **Content Alignment:** Centered vertically and horizontally along the primary axis, with structured vertical separation between the headline text (top section) and the interactive envelope (center section).
* **Overflow:** `hidden` (no horizontal or vertical scrolling allowed).

## 3. Typography Specification

### Primary Headline: `"Will You Be My \n Valentine?"`
* **Text Structure:** Two lines, centered:
  * Line 1: `Will You Be My`
  * Line 2: `Valentine?`
* **Primary Font:** **"Dancing Script"** (Google Fonts) or **"Great Vibes"** (Google Fonts)
* **Fallback Stack:** 
  ```css
  font-family: 'Dancing Script', 'Great Vibes', 'Brush Script MT', 'Snell Roundhand', cursive, sans-serif;
  ```
* **Font Weight:** `600` (Semi-Bold)
* **Font Size:** `clamp(28px, 7vw, 36px)`
* **Line Height:** `1.25`
* **Letter Spacing:** `0.5px`
* **Text Shadow:** `0 2px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 20px rgba(255, 180, 180, 0.15)`
* **Color:** Warm Ivory / Pearlescent Off-White (`#F7EBE8`)

---

### Call-to-Action Annotation: `"click here"`
* **Text Structure:** Single line, all lowercase: `click here`
* **Primary Font:** **"Caveat"** or **"Reenie Beanie"** (Handwritten style)
* **Fallback Stack:** 
  ```css
  font-family: 'Caveat', 'Comic Sans MS', 'Bradley Hand', cursive;
  ```
* **Font Weight:** `400` (Regular)
* **Font Size:** `13px` – `14px`
* **Color:** Semi-translucent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`)
* **Text Shadow:** `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`

---

## 4. Background & Ambient Atmosphere

The background is composed of three layered visual effects:

1. **Base Radial Gradient:**
   * Center: `rgba(82, 6, 10, 1)` at `50% 45%`
   * Edges: `rgba(44, 2, 5, 1)` at `100%`
2. **Textured Paper / Film Grain:**
   * A full-bleed overlay with low-opacity noise/grain texture (`opacity: 0.12`, blend mode `soft-light` or `overlay`) giving the screen a velvet or heavy cardstock feel.
3. **Floating Specular Dust / Stardust:**
   * Approximately 20–30 soft, glowing circular specks scattered across the canvas.
   * Sizes range from `1px` to `3.5px`.
   * Opacity varies between `0.2` and `0.75`.
   * Some specks have a slight gaussian blur (`0.5px` to `1.5px`) to simulate camera depth-of-field.

## 5. Visual Asset Specifications

### Asset 1: The Valentine Envelope
* **Format:** Transparent PNG or WebP (backgroundless cut-out).
* **Visual Description:** 
  * A traditional horizontal rectangular invitation envelope made of matte, textured deep-crimson cardstock.
  * Features a pointed, downward-facing triangular top flap.
  * Natural highlights on the fold creases define the top, bottom, and side flaps without harsh black borders.
* **Dimensions:** 
  * Width: ~`280px` to `310px` (approximately `75%` to `80%` of mobile screen width).
  * Aspect Ratio: ~`1.45 : 1` (Standard landscape envelope proportion).
* **Orientation / Rotation:**
  * Rotated **`-5.5 degrees` counter-clockwise** (slanted slightly downward on the left and upward on the right).
* **Drop Shadow:**
  * Multi-layered soft ambient shadow beneath the envelope:
    `box-shadow / drop-shadow: 0 15px 35px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.55), 0 5px 15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`

---

### Asset 2: The Monogram Wax Seal
* **Format:** Transparent PNG or WebP (backgroundless cut-out) layered directly over the tip of the envelope’s triangular flap.
* **Visual Description:**
  * A circular, slightly organic/irregular melted wax seal stamp.
  * Finish: Translucent metallic pearl / antique silver-white with soft highlights on the raised outer rim.
  * Center Stamp: An intricately debossed cursive monogram (featuring flowing calligraphic script letters, resembling an interlocking `"M"` or `"A"`).
* **Dimensions:**
  * Diameter: ~`68px` to `74px` (proportional to the envelope flap).
* **Position:**
  * Centered directly over the downward tip of the envelope’s front flap.
* **Interactive Guide Indicator:**
  * A subtle circular halo/ring (`border: 1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`) framing the seal, pulsing faintly to invite a tap.

---

### Asset 3: Handwritten Arrow & Annotation
* **Format:** Transparent vector overlay (SVG).
* **Visual Description:**
  * A delicate, fine-line hand-drawn white arrow.
  * Originates underneath/to the left of the `"click here"` text and curls in a smooth arc upward and leftward, pointing its arrowhead directly at the right rim of the wax seal.
* **Stroke Width:** `1.2px` – `1.5px`.
* **Color:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`.
* **Position:**
  * Located immediately to the lower-right side of the wax seal, resting entirely within the boundaries of the envelope body.

## 7. Interactive Micro-Interactions

1. **Wax Seal Idle State:**
   * The guide ring around the seal subtly pulses in opacity (`0.3` to `0.7`) every 2 seconds.
2. **Tap / Click State:**
   * Active state scale: `transform: scale(0.95)` on tap down.
   * On click: Initiates the envelope unsealing / letter opening animation sequence.
