# Blvck Tumbler Landing Page Design Specification

This document outlines the exact design specifications for the "Blvck Tumbler" landing page, translated into an implementation-ready blueprint.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Variables

### Color Palette
- **Background (Primary):** `#1a1a1c` (Deep Charcoal Black)
- **Background (Secondary Panels):** `#222225` (Dark Gray, semi-transparent for content boxes)
- **Text (Primary/Headings):** `#f4ece1` (Warm Off-White / Cream)
- **Text (Secondary/Body):** `#8c8c94` (Muted Cool Gray)
- **Accent (Orange):** `#ff5a1f` (Vibrant Neon/Safety Orange)
- **Accent (Muted Orange):** `#d14814` (For subtle glows/gradients)

### Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** Inter, sans-serif (Clean, modern sans-serif)
- **Heading Weight:** Bold (`700`)
- **Body Weight:** Regular (`400`)

---

## 2. Layout & Structure

- **Viewport:** Full-screen responsive design (100vw × 100vh) with a centered, balanced layout.
- **Grid Layout:** 
  - **Left Section:** Product branding, title, and "Black Lifestyle Lovers" descriptor block.
  - **Center Section:** High-impact 3D product render (Black Tumbler with straw) and floating orange geometric ring accent.
  - **Right Section:** "The design" descriptor block, interactive play button, and background watermark typography ("BLVCK TUMBLER").

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation Bar (Top)
- **Left:** 
  - Logo/Brand text: `+ GRIDS.` in white/orange accent.
- **Right:** 
  - Hamburger Menu Icon (`☰`) in muted gray.

### B. Left Content Area
- **Main Heading:**
  - Text: `Blvck` (Line 1) / `Tumbler` (Line 2)
  - Color: `#f4ece1`
  - Font Size: `48px` - `56px`
  - Line Height: `1.1`
- **Description Box ("Black Lifestyle Lovers"):**
  - Background: Semi-transparent dark panel (`rgba(34, 34, 37, 0.6)`) with subtle backdrop blur.
  - Title: "Black Lifestyle Lovers." (White, Bold)
  - Body Text: Short micro-copy about enjoying favorite beverages out of the tumbler. (Muted gray, `12px`, line-height `1.5`)
- **Bottom CTA:**
  - Text: `SCROLL DOWN` with an active orange progress/indicator bar.

### C. Center Product Stage
- **Product Image:**
  - Matte black insulated tumbler featuring a matching black straw.
  - Embossed/Subtle text on tumbler: `ALL BLACK EVERYTHING`.
- **Graphic Accent:**
  - A vibrant orange (`#ff5a1f`) floating outline circle positioned slightly behind the top-right of the tumbler lid.
- **Background Watermark:**
  - Large, semi-transparent typographic overlay behind the product reading `BLVCK TUMBLER`.

### D. Right Content Area
- **Description Box ("The design"):**
  - Background: Semi-transparent dark panel matching the left box.
  - Title: "The design." (White, Bold)
  - Body Text: Micro-copy explaining the unique features of the tumbler and video link prompt. (Muted gray, `12px`)
- **Interactive Elements:**
  - **Play Button:** Circular button with a dark fill and a thin orange border containing a play triangle icon (`▶`).
  - **Pagination/Navigation Arrow:** Right-facing arrow (`→`) indicating horizontal scrolling or carousel interaction.
  - **Footer Credit:** `CREDIT` label fixed to the bottom right.

---

## 4. Interactive States & Micro-interactions
- **Hover on Buttons/Icons:** Subtle scale-up (1.05x) and color intensity shift on the orange accent elements.
- **Scroll Indicator:** Smooth scroll trigger bar animating along the bottom left.
