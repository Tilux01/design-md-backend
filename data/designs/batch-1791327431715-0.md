# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Golden Hour Editorial Nature Scenery / Cinematic Warmth.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High atmospheric density, organic full-viewport composition, floating foreground depth-of-field blur with expansive rolling midground hills and expansive twilight sky.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Natural photographic depth separation using optical Gaussian blur for foreground blooms (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` equivalent), midground rolling terrain contour lighting, and soft ambient horizon gradients.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Cinematic depth-of-field blurs, warm golden hour sun shafts, high-tracking uppercase nature identifiers, organic bokeh simulation.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(244, 162, 97, 0.15)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-amber-400 inline-block"></span>`.
