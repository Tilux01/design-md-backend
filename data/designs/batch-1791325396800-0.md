# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist, Modern Design Agency & SaaS Portfolio.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with asymmetric, curated card stacking. The design relies on clean structural separation and generous padding to emphasize high-end branding.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered paper structure featuring subtle off-white/cream canvas backgrounds, stark dark navy primary cards (`#1E1B4B`), and soft floating glass pills.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Floating contextual action pill, minimal brand badge icons, precise monochrome dark cards with stark white editorial headers.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `6px 16px`, font-size: `12px`, font-weight: `600`, tracking: `0.05em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, border width: `1px` solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-400 inline-block"></span>`
