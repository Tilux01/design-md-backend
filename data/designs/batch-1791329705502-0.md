# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Modern 3D Isometric SaaS Landing Page, clean tech-startup aesthetic combining minimal typography with isometric 3D illustrations.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio in the hero area, maintaining a clean left-aligned content hierarchy balanced by the right-hand isometric store visual.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean flat white canvas (`#ECEEF3`) with pill inputs resting on surface 1 containers, utilizing subtle drop shadows and soft ambient lighting to ground 3D elements.

- **AI Visual Traits**: Floating 3D isometric scene, high-rounded pill inputs, clean monochromatic surfaces with saturated CTA buttons.
