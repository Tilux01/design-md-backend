# Design Specification: Squadro Quad Bike Product Page

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport
- **Viewport:** 100vw × 100vh, full-screen immersive design.
- **Background:** Dark charcoal radial gradient (`#181a21` center fading to `#0d0e12` edges) with subtle lighting effects.
- **Overall Structure:** Fixed dashboard frame layout featuring a top navigation bar, central hero section (typographic watermarks, large model title, and product visual), category filters on the left, pagination on the right, and a bottom specifications ticker bar.

---

## 2. Typography & Font Stack
- **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif.
- **Secondary/Display Font:** Bold, geometric sans-serif for numbers and headers (e.g., `Montserrat`, `Oswald`).
- **Font Hierarchy:**
  - **Brand Logo:** `SQUADRO` – Bold, uppercase, tracked out, white text inside an orange-amber rectangular badge (`#e67300`).
  - **Nav Links:** 12px, uppercase, medium weight, tracked out, light gray (`#8a8d98`).
  - **Hero Watermark ("02 BIKES"):** ~120px height, bold, ultra-spaced, faint translucent dark-gray (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`).
  - **Quad Bike Type Header:** 12px, uppercase, light gray (`#8a8d98`).
  - **Model Title ("ATV RENEGAD XXC 1000"):** ~36px, heavy bold, uppercase, pure white.
  - **Spec Labels (FROM, ENGINE, SUSPENSION, etc.):** 10px, uppercase, colored orange-amber (`#e67300`) for headers and white for values.

---

## 3. Header Section (Top Bar)
- **Position:** Absolute, top 0, spanning full width (`max-width: 1200px` centered container with horizontal padding).
- **Left Element:** 
  - Squadro Brand Logo: Orange box with white bold text `SQUADRO`.
- **Center Navigation:** 
  - Links: `BIKES`, `EQUIPMENT`, `TUTORIALS`, `CONTACTS`, `MAIN`.
  - Active State: `BIKES` has a thin horizontal underline bar in orange (`#e67300`).
- **Right Elements:** 
  - Shopping cart icon (white, clean vector line style).
  - Hamburger menu icon (three horizontal lines, white).

---

## 4. Left Sidebar (Quad Bike Type Filters)
- **Position:** Absolute, left side.
- **Content:**
  - Label: `Quad bike type` (small, gray, with up/down indicator arrows).
  - List of Categories:
    - `FREESTYLE` (Active, highlighted in pure white with bold weight).
    - `ALL-TERRAIN` (Muted gray `#6c707d`).
    - `HIGH-SPEED` (Muted gray).
    - `MOUNTAIN` (Muted gray).

---

## 5. Center Hero Section
- **Watermark Text:** Large background typography reading `02 BIKES` layered behind the vehicle and title.
- **Sub-tagline:** `FREESTYLE QUAD BIKE` in orange-amber small caps.
- **Main Heading:** 
  - `ATV RENEGAD`
  - `XXC 1000`
- **Product Visual (The Quad Bike):**
  - **Image Description:** High-end studio shot of a high-performance sports quad bike (ATV) in vibrant yellow and black trim. Features aggressive front bumper styling, specialized heavy-duty off-road knobby tires with bright yellow beadlock wheel rims, exposed front suspension springs (orange), and a sleek black carbon-textured chassis.
  - **Image Characteristics:** Backgroundless PNG/transparent cutout asset.
  - **Placement & Rotation:** Positioned on the right-center of the screen, angled dynamically in a three-quarter front view facing slightly left towards the typography, creating visual depth.

---

## 6. Right Sidebar (Pagination / Carousel Indicators)
- **Position:** Absolute, right edge.
- **Content:** Vertical numeric index list:
  - `01`
  - `02` (Active indicator, highlighted with an adjacent vertical tracking line and brighter white text).
  - `03`
  - `04`
  - `05`

---

## 7. Bottom Specifications Bar
- **Position:** Absolute, bottom edge, spanning the full width inside a darker semi-transparent container bar (`rgba(10, 11, 15, 0.85)` with top border stroke).
- **Columns (Grid Layout):**
  1. **FROM** -> `$8,349`
  2. **ENGINE** -> `570 / 850 / 1000R`
  3. **SUSPENSION** -> `DOUBLE A-ARM`
  4. **SHOCKS** -> `FOX PODIUM 1.5`
  5. **CLEARANCE** -> `26.7 CM`
  6. **Call to Action Button:** 
     - Text: `VIEW PACKAGE` with right arrow icon (`▶`).
     - Styling: Solid vibrant orange background (`#ff7700`), white bold text, rounded rectangular shape.
     - Scroll/Navigation arrows on the far right (`▲` and `▼`).

---

## 8. Color Palette Reference
- **Primary Orange:** `#ff7700` / `#e67300`
- **Background Dark:** `#12141a`
- **Card/Bar Background:** `#1a1d26`
- **Text Primary:** `#ffffff`
- **Text Secondary/Muted:** `#7c808f`
- **Border/Divider Lines:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
