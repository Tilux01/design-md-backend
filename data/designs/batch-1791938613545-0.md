# Design Specification: Dignitas Player Profile (Aphromoo)

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Background Color:** Deep Matte Black (`#0a0a0a`).
*   **Primary Accent Color:** Cyber Yellow (`#fee800`).
*   **Secondary Accent:** Dark Charcoal/Grey (`#1a1a1a`) for card backgrounds.
*   **Text Colors:** 
    *   Primary: White (`#ffffff`)
    *   Secondary: Muted Grey (`#888888`)
    *   Highlight: Cyber Yellow (`#fee800`)
*   **Layout:** Full-screen, edge-to-edge (no margins on the main container).

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Heading Font:** A heavy, condensed geometric sans-serif. 
    *   *Recommended:* **Industry Black** or **Barlow Condensed (ExtraBold)**.
    *   *Fallback:* `Impact, "Arial Narrow", sans-serif`.
*   **Body/UI Font:** A clean, modern sans-serif with high readability.
    *   *Recommended:* **Inter** or **Roboto**.
    *   *Fallback:* `system-ui, sans-serif`.
*   **Stylized Background Text:** Custom graffiti/brush script.
    *   *Note:* This is treated as a graphic element, not live text.

## 3. Navigation Bars

### 3.1 Top Navigation Bar
*   **Position:** Fixed to top, 100% width, height approx 60px.
*   **Left:** Dignitas Logo (White owl head icon + "DIGNITAS" in bold sans-serif).
*   **Center:** Breadcrumbs in small, uppercase white text: `LEAGUE OF LEGENDS > PROFILE`.
*   **Right:** "LIVE NOW" indicator. A white pill-shaped button with a small red glowing dot to the right.
*   **Far Right:** A "hamburger" menu icon (three horizontal lines).

### 3.2 Right Sidebar (Social/Utility)
*   **Position:** Fixed to right edge, vertical orientation.
*   **Background:** Semi-transparent dark grey overlay.
*   **Icons:** Vertical stack of social icons (Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, Twitch, Discord, TikTok).
*   **Bottom Element:** A square yellow button with a black controller icon, indicating "Gaming/Stats" mode.

## 4. Hero Section (Player Focus)

### 4.1 Background Graffiti
*   **Description:** Large, aggressive brush-stroke text reading "APHROMOO" in Cyber Yellow.
*   **Placement:** Centered behind the player image. The paint has a "dripping" effect on the right side.
*   **Style:** Distressed, street-art aesthetic.

### 4.2 Player Image
*   **Subject:** Aphromoo (Zaqqueri Black).
*   **Description:** A high-resolution photo of the player from the waist up. He is wearing a black and grey camouflage Dignitas jersey with a yellow collar and a yellow Dignitas owl logo on the chest. He is wearing black-rimmed glasses and looking slightly to the right with a neutral expression.
*   **Properties:** Backgroundless (PNG), placed centrally. The image is layered *in front* of the yellow graffiti but *behind* the foreground stat cards.

### 4.3 Player Info (Left Side)
*   **Name:** "Aphromoo" in large white condensed font.
*   **Real Name:** "ZAQUERI BLACK" in small yellow uppercase text below the name.
*   **Socials:** Small white icons for Twitter, Instagram, and Twitch.
*   **Nationality:** Small US Flag icon.

## 5. Statistics Section
Three horizontal cards positioned across the player's midsection.

### 5.1 Main Stat Cards
*   **Style:** Dark grey (`#1a1a1a`) rounded rectangles (approx 15px border-radius) with a subtle inner glow.
*   **Card 1 (Matches):** Label "MATCHES PLAYED" (Yellow, small). Value "59" (White, large).
*   **Card 2 (Games):** Label "GAMES PLAYED" (Yellow, small). Value "71" (White, large). *Note: This card has a slight yellow blur/glow effect over it in the mockup.*
*   **Card 3 (KDA):** Label "AVG KDA" (Yellow, small). Value "2.40" (White, large).

### 5.2 Sub-Stats Bar
*   **Position:** Directly below the cards, a thin dark bar.
*   **Content:** Small white text showing `KP% 62%`, `VSPM% 2.40`, `AVG DMG% 6.6%`, and `GOLD% 12.9%`. Labels are in yellow, values in white.

## 6. Content Grid (Lower Sections)

### 6.1 Feature Article Card (Left)
*   **Image:** Backgroundless cutout of a player (Froggen) wearing a headset, looking left.
*   **Content:** A dark translucent box containing the headline "How to Carry Your Team in League of Legends - A Guide with Froggen."
*   **Text:** White headline, yellow sub-header. Small paragraph of grey body text below.

### 6.2 Media/Video Gallery (Right)
*   **Main Video:** A large thumbnail showing a player in a darkened room with a yellow "LCS APHROMOO" overlay.
*   **Overlay Text:** "AKAADIAN" visible in a hollowed-out font style behind the main text.
*   **Controls:** Small yellow play button and progress indicators at the bottom.

### 6.3 Lower Dashboard (Footer Area)
*   **Game Selector:** Icons for various games (LoL, CS:GO, Smash Bros, etc.) in a horizontal row on the far left.
*   **Live Stream/News Feed:** A grid of smaller cards.
    *   **Card 1:** CS:GO action shot with a "Counter-Strike" logo.
    *   **Card 2:** A "Match Win" card showing team logos (Dignitas vs Fnatic) with a yellow "WIN" highlight.
    *   **Card 3:** A "Make it with Creative Cloud" advertisement with a vibrant purple/blue gradient.

## 7. Visual Effects
*   **Depth:** Use of Z-index is crucial. Background (Black) -> Graffiti (Yellow) -> Player (PNG) -> UI Cards (Foreground).
*   **Glows:** The yellow elements (especially the "Games Played" card) should have a soft `box-shadow` or `drop-shadow` in yellow to simulate a neon glow.
*   **Transitions:** Hover states on cards should slightly increase brightness or scale (1.02x).
