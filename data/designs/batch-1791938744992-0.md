# Design Specification: Netflix "Daredevil" Concept UI

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Background:** The entire application should be full-screen with a base color of `#050505` (near black). A subtle radial gradient should be applied from the center-right (behind the hero character) using a deep purple/indigo tone (`#1a0b2e`) to create depth.
*   **Primary Color:** Netflix Red (`#E50914`) used for the logo, active navigation, and primary buttons.
*   **Text Color:** Primary text is Pure White (`#FFFFFF`). Secondary text/metadata is Light Gray (`#A3A3A3`).
*   **Spacing:** Use a standard 12-column grid system with a horizontal margin of `60px` on both sides.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Geist Sans`.
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Headings:** Bold weight (700-800).
*   **Body:** Regular weight (400) with a line height of `1.6`.

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation Bar (Header)
*   **Position:** Fixed at the top, transparent background, height `80px`.
*   **Logo:** The Netflix "N" icon in red, positioned at the far left.
*   **Menu Links:** "Movies", "TV shows", "Categories".
    *   "TV shows" is active (Color: `#E50914`).
    *   "Categories" has a small downward chevron icon to its right.
    *   Font size: `14px`, Medium weight.
*   **Search Bar:** 
    *   A thin horizontal line (`1px`, `#333`) with a magnifying glass icon on the left.
    *   Placeholder text: "e.g. Stranger things" in `#666`.
*   **User Profile (Right):**
    *   Name: "Lucas Green" (White, `14px`).
    *   Avatar: Circular image (approx `32px`) showing a male face.
    *   Logout Icon: A thin-line power symbol icon to the right of the avatar.

### B. Hero Content (Left Side)
*   **Back Link:** `< back to all TV shows` in `#A3A3A3`, font size `12px`, uppercase or small caps.
*   **Title:** "Daredevil"
    *   Font: Extra Bold, `72px` to `84px`.
    *   Color: White.
*   **Metadata Row:**
    *   `16+`: White text inside a thin white rounded-rectangle border.
    *   `2016 | 2 seasons | 51 minutes`: Separated by thin vertical pipes (`|`). Font size `16px`, Bold.
*   **Description:**
    *   Two-line paragraph. Font size `16px`, Color `#CCCCCC`.
    *   Content: "Blinded as a young boy, Matt Murdock fights injustice by day as a lawyer and by night as the Super Hero Daredevil in Hell's Kitchen, New York City."
*   **Starring:** 
    *   Label "Starring:" in `#A3A3A3`.
    *   Names "Charlie Cox, Deborah Ann Woll, Elden Henson" in White.
*   **Action Buttons:**
    *   **Button 1 (Primary):** "Go to season 1". Background: `#E50914`, Text: White, Border-radius: `25px`, Padding: `12px 30px`.
    *   **Button 2 (Secondary):** "Watch trailer". Background: Transparent, Border: `2px solid #FFFFFF`, Text: White, Border-radius: `25px`, Padding: `12px 30px`. Includes a small "Play" triangle icon inside a circle to the left of the text.

### C. Hero Image (Right Side)
*   **Description:** A high-resolution, backgroundless (transparent PNG) cutout of the character Daredevil.
*   **Pose:** The character is viewed from the back/side, looking over his right shoulder toward the viewer. He is wearing his dark red/maroon tactical suit.
*   **Placement:** Positioned on the right half of the screen. The bottom of the character is cut off by the thumbnail gallery.
*   **Atmospheric Effects:** 
    *   A subtle rain overlay (thin white diagonal streaks) should be applied over the image.
    *   Behind the character, include a silhouette of a New York City water tower and distant building outlines to provide context.

### D. Thumbnail Gallery (Bottom)
*   **Container:** A horizontal row of 4 rectangular thumbnails at the bottom of the viewport.
*   **Thumbnail 1:** Dark scene, includes a "zoom/search" icon overlay in the center.
*   **Thumbnail 2:** A close-up of Daredevil in a red-lit room.
*   **Thumbnail 3:** A bar scene with two men talking.
*   **Thumbnail 4:** A dark scene with two figures.
*   **Progress Bar:** A thin white line running across the bottom of the thumbnails. A thicker white segment indicates the current scroll position (currently under the first thumbnail).

## 4. Image Asset Details

1.  **Hero Image:** 
    *   **Subject:** Daredevil (Matt Murdock) in suit.
    *   **Lighting:** Low key, rim lighting on the shoulders and helmet.
    *   **Rotation:** 0 degrees, but the character's body is angled roughly 45 degrees away from the camera.
2.  **Background Elements:** 
    *   **Water Tower:** Dark silhouette, placed behind the character's left arm.
    *   **Cityscape:** Very dark, low-contrast buildings in the far background.
3.  **Icons:**
    *   Search: Feather-style thin magnifying glass.
    *   Play: Solid white triangle.
    *   Power: Standard thin-line circular power button.

## 5. Technical Implementation Notes
*   **Layering:** The Hero Image (Daredevil) should have a higher `z-index` than the background gradients but a lower `z-index` than the Navigation Bar and Hero Text.
*   **Responsiveness:** On smaller screens, the Hero Image should scale down or move to the background, while the text content remains legible and centered.
*   **Hover States:** Navigation links should turn Red on hover. The "Watch trailer" button should have a slight white semi-transparent fill on hover.
