# Design Specification (`design.md`)

## 1. Overview & Canvas Setup

This document outlines the complete design specification for cloning the **PICTO — AR Productivity Projector** landing page. 

* **Display Mode**: Full-screen, edge-to-edge web canvas (100vw × 100vh), without external hardware bezels, mockups, or monitor frames.
* **Aesthetic**: Futuristic, tactile industrial minimalism with an iridescent cyberpunk/pastel synthwave lighting scheme.
* **Aspect Ratio / Target Resolution**: 16:9 base canvas (`1920 × 1080` desktop standard), responsive down to tablet/mobile.

## 4. Layout & Grid System

The viewport uses a fixed full-screen flex/grid layout with absolute-positioned peripheral rails:

* **Canvas Margins**: `48px` horizontal margin, `40px` vertical margin.
* **Left Rail Width**: Fixed `60px` reserved along the left edge for rotated social channels.
* **Content Split**:
  * **Left Side (40% width)**: Text content, kicker, headline, description, specs, and bottom index marker.
  * **Right Side (60% width)**: 3D hardware visual asset and bottom-right interactive slider.

### 5.4 Central 3D Product Visual Asset

* **Asset Type**: High-resolution, backgroundless transparent PNG / WebP.
* **Subject**: Picto AR Projector hardware prototype.
* **Perspective & Orientation**:
  * 3/4 perspective angle viewed from front-right.
  * The main cylindrical projector body sits horizontally with a slight downward pitch towards the front lenses.
* **Visual Details & Form**:
  * **Front Face**: Rounded pill shape finished in vibrant satin magenta/electric orchid. Contains two large circular dual lenses.
  * **Lenses**: Recessed camera/projector glass with deep circular ribbed heat-sink turbines that reflect iridescent cyan and navy blue tones.
  * **Face Center**: A vertical pill-shaped groove separating the lenses with a status LED pinhole.
  * **Main Body Hood**: Extends back towards the left; aerodynamic, organic matte anodized aluminum shifting smoothly from metallic periwinkle/lilac to pink.
  * **Base / Mount**: Below the main cylinder, a streamlined mechanical fin/clamp paddle extends downward and backward at a ~35° angle. Features fine tactile micro-ridges in a rich violet/magenta tone.
  * **Vents**: Horizontal cooling gill slits visible along the lower metallic chassis.
* **Placement & Dimensions**:
  * Bounds: Occupies roughly `55%` of the viewport width.
  * Anchor: Centered vertically (`top: 48%`, `right: 8%`).
  * Shadow: Soft ambient occlusion underneath the lower paddle clamp (`box-shadow` or baked drop shadow with blur `40px`, opacity `25%`, tint deep indigo `#2D124D`).
