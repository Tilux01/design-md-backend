# Design Specification: "The Ultimate Collections" Fashion Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Document Structure
- **View:** Full-screen responsive viewport (ignoring the inner frame mockup from the preview).
- **Background Color:** Light stone/off-white (`#F3F3F3` or subtle radial gradient from white to very soft grey `#EAEAEA` to mimic the textured aesthetic).
- **Global Constraints:** Centered alignment, maximum content width of `1280px` for the inner container, generous whitespace padding.

---

## 2. Typography & Font Stack
The design employs a mix of modern geometric sans-serifs and a clean, ultra-bold black letterform for emphasis.

- **Primary Brand Font / Heading 1 (The Ultimate / COLLECTIONS):**
  - **Family:** `Montserrat`, `Inter`, sans-serif.
  - **Fallback:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`.
  - **Weights Used:** Light/Regular (300/400) for "The Ultimate", Heavy/Black (900) for "COLLECTIONS".
  - **Letter-spacing:** Tight tracking for "COLLECTIONS" (`letter-spacing: -0.03em`), standard tracking for "The Ultimate".

- **Navigation & Body Text Font:**
  - **Family:** `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif.
  - **Fallback:** `system-ui`, `sans-serif`.
  - **Weights Used:** Regular (400), Medium (500).

---

## 3. Navigation Bar (Header)
- **Position:** Fixed or sticky at the top, padding `24px 48px`.
- **Layout:** Flexbox, space-between alignment.
- **Left Element (Logo):**
  - Text: **`Fashion.`**
  - Style: Bold serif or heavy sans-serif (`Playfair Display` or high-contrast sans), 18px size.
- **Center Element (Nav Links):**
  - Items: `Home`, `Product`, `About Us`, `Contact`.
  - Style: 14px, Medium weight, dark charcoal (`#111111`), with a subtle hover opacity shift (`0.7`).
- **Right Element (Icons):**
  - User profile icon (circle outline) and Shopping bag icon with a red notification badge (`1` item). Size: 18px stroke icons.

---

## 4. Hero Section
- **Title Block:**
  - **Line 1:** "The Ultimate" (Font size: ~32px, Regular weight, centered).
  - **Line 2:** "COLLECTIONS" (Font size: ~64px-72px, Black 900 weight, uppercase, centered, stark black `#000000`).
- **Spacing:** Margin-bottom beneath headers before image cards: `40px`.

---

## 5. Interactive Cards Carousel / Fan Grid
- **Layout:** Horizontal overlapping card stack/grid with a cascading visual rhythm (slightly fanned out).
- **Card Styling:**
  - **Border Radius:** `16px` to `20px` rounded corners.
  - **Shadows:** Deep, soft directional drop shadows (`0px 12px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)`) to give a physical card/polaroid feel.
  - **Aspect Ratio:** Vertical portrait cards (~3:4 ratio).
- **Card Contents:**
  - Each card contains high-fashion editorial imagery (models posing against dynamic backdrops: yellow, orange, blue, and neutral studios).
  - **Top Tag inside each card:** A small pill badge reading **`Buy Now`** with a tiny arrow icon (white background, dark text, rounded).

- **Floating Badges / Callouts (Around the cards):**
  - **Left Pill (Blue):** A speech-bubble style pill saying *"Get upto 20% OFF"*, colored in vibrant turquoise/blue (`#2CA5E0`).
  - **Right Pill (Orange):** A speech-bubble style pill saying *"Best Hoodies"*, colored in warm amber/orange (`#E89A3E`).

---

## 6. Footer Call-to-Action (CTA) Area
- **Sub-headline Text:** 
  - *"We Do Not Divide Our Collections To Seasons We Create New Models Every Week, And We In A Few Items"*
  - **Style:** 12px width-constrained centered text, muted dark gray (`#555555`), line-height `1.5`.
- **Primary Button ("Explore More"):**
  - **Background:** Solid black (`#000000`).
  - **Text Color:** White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - **Border Radius:** Fully rounded pill style (`9999px`).
  - **Padding:** `12px 28px`.
  - **Font:** 13px, Medium.
  - **Hover State:** Slight scale up (`scale(1.03)`) or background color transition to dark gray (`#222222`).

---

## 7. Micro-Interactions & Animations (Motion Design)
Since this design originates from a motion/UI animation showcase (`@uiuxzaid`), the implementation requires specific transitions:

1. **Page Load Entrance (Staggered Fade-Up):**
   - Nav bar slides down from `-20px` with `opacity: 0` to `1` over `0.6s` cubic-bezier.
   - Hero header text fades up with a slight blur-to-focus filter (`filter: blur(4px)` to `blur(0)`).
2. **Card Fan-Out Animation:**
   - On load, the 6 portrait cards fly in from the center, rotating slightly into their respective fixed angles (e.g., Card 1 rotates `-6deg`, Card 3 is straight `0deg`, Card 6 rotates `6deg`).
3. **Hover States:**
   - **Cards:** Hovering over any card elevates it (`translateY(-8px) scale(1.02)`) and brings it to the highest `z-index`.
   - **Explore More Button:** Smooth shadow expansion on hover.
   - **Floating Badges:** Subtle floating/bouncing keyframe animation (`keyframes float { 0%, 100% { transform: translateY(0); } 50% { transform: translateY(-6px); } }`) looping every 3 seconds.
