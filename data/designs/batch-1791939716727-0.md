This `design.md` provides a comprehensive specification for recreating the music player interface shown in the provided media. The design is a dark-themed, high-contrast mobile UI focused on circular elements and vibrant accent colors.

# Design Specification: Neon-Dark Music Player

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Background Color:** Deep Charcoal / Midnight Blue (`#121418`).
*   **Primary Accent:** Vibrant Magenta/Pink (`#E91E63`).
*   **Secondary Accent:** Electric Cyan (`#00F2FF`).
*   **Text Primary:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Text Secondary:** Muted Grey (`#8E8E93`).
*   **Corner Radius:** 20px for menus and containers; 50% (circular) for avatars and album art.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **San Francisco** (iOS) or **Inter**.
*   **Fallback Fonts:** System-ui, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Header Title:** 12px, Uppercase, Bold, Letter-spacing: 1.5px.
    *   **Artist Name:** 14px, Regular, White.
    *   **Song Title:** 18px, Bold, Cyan (`#00F2FF`).
    *   **Album Name:** 12px, Regular, Muted Grey.
    *   **Lyrics:** 14px, Semi-bold, Line-height: 1.6. Active line is White; inactive lines are Muted Grey at 40% opacity.
    *   **Menu Items:** 13px, Regular, White.

## 3. Layout Breakdown

### 3.1 Top Navigation Bar
*   **Height:** 60px.
*   **Back Button:** Left-aligned, thin chevron icon (`<`), White.
*   **Title:** "NOW PLAYING", centered, uppercase.
*   **Action Icons:** Right-aligned. A Search icon (Magnifying glass) followed by a More icon (Vertical three dots).

### 3.2 The "Vinyl" Player Section
*   **Central Element:** A perfectly circular album art container.
*   **Album Art Image:** 
    *   **Description:** High-resolution square image cropped into a circle.
    *   **Placement:** Centered horizontally, top margin of 40px from the nav bar.
    *   **Visual Detail:** The image should have a subtle inner shadow to give it depth within the ring.
*   **Progress Ring:**
    *   **Outer Ring:** A thin, dark grey circular track (`#2A2D34`) surrounding the album art.
    *   **Active Track:** A Magenta (`#E91E63`) stroke that follows the outer ring.
    *   **Thumb/Indicator:** A solid Magenta circle (approx. 12px diameter) at the current progress point.
    *   **Time Stamps:** "0:43" (Left) and "-2:32" (Right) placed horizontally aligned with the center of the circle, outside the ring.

### 3.3 Playback Controls
*   **Shuffle/Repeat:** 
    *   Shuffle icon on the left, Repeat icon on the right, both in Magenta. 
    *   Positioned symmetrically below the circular player.
*   **Main Controls:**
    *   **Previous/Next:** Thin line-art icons (Skip back/forward), White.
    *   **Play/Pause:** Large, prominent vertical bars (Pause) or Triangle (Play), White.
    *   **Spacing:** Centered horizontally with even distribution.

### 3.4 Content Toggles (Bottom Half)
The bottom section alternates between two views:

#### View A: Queue (Up Next/Previous)
*   **Section Headers:** "UP NEXT" and "PREVIOUS" in small, grey, uppercase text.
*   **List Items:**
    *   **Thumbnail:** Small circular version of the album art (32px).
    *   **Text:** Song title (White) above Artist & Duration (Grey).
    *   **Action:** A small "Play" chevron on the far right.
    *   **Separator:** A very thin, dark grey horizontal line between items.

#### View B: Lyrics (Lyrics-enabled)
*   **Overlay:** Text starts below the Song/Artist info.
*   **Alignment:** Center-aligned.
*   **Visual Effect:** A vertical gradient fade at the top and bottom of the lyrics block to suggest scrolling.

## 4. Context Menu (Overflow)
*   **Background:** Solid Dark Grey (`#1A1C22`) with a slight drop shadow.
*   **Placement:** Anchored to the top-right "More" icon.
*   **Items:** Each item consists of a small White icon on the left and text on the right.
*   **Options:** Add to Playlist, Add to Favorites, Find/Hide Lyrics, Use as Ringtone, Share, Settings.

## 5. Image Specifications
*   **Album Art (Taylor Swift/Ariana Grande):** 
    *   **Background:** The images used in the mockup feature artists against neutral or stylized backgrounds. 
    *   **Transparency:** The images themselves are not backgroundless; they are standard square photography cropped into a circle by the UI container.
    *   **Rotation:** In a live environment, the circular album art should rotate slowly (33 RPM style) while music is playing.
*   **Icons:** All icons (Search, More, Play, Shuffle) should be SVG-based line icons with a consistent stroke weight (approx 1.5pt to 2pt).

## 6. Interaction States
*   **Active Progress:** The Magenta ring fills clockwise.
*   **Button Press:** Icons should reduce opacity to 70% when touched.
*   **Lyrics Scrolling:** The text should auto-scroll, keeping the "active" line centered vertically in the lyrics block.
