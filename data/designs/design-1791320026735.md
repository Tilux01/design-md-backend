# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist, Cinematic Dark Mode Portfolio.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace balanced with strong typographic hierarchy, asymmetrical composition, and split-layout framing.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean flat white/light-gray main surface (#F1F3F5) contrasting sharply with high-impact charcoal/black bottom navigation blocks (#0A0A0A) and sketch illustrations.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Vertical tracking typography for category labels, minimalist numbering counters (`01 / 100`), high-contrast sketch-to-flat juxtaposition, side-action pagination ticks.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Arrow right (>) -> Use `&rarr;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-black inline-block"></span>`.
