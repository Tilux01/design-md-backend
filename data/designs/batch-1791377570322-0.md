# Visual Design Specification: "Snoopy at the Sea"

## 1. Overview & Composition
* **Concept:** A grainy, low-fidelity, handheld snapshot featuring a Snoopy plush toy in the foreground against a moody, turbulent ocean and a dusky sky.
* **Aspect Ratio:** Near square (approx. 1:1.05 based on provided bounds).
* **Composition:** Center-weighted portrait. The Snoopy plush occupies the lower-center frame, its head dominating the lower-middle half of the image. The horizon line of the sea sits roughly at the upper-third boundary.
* **Style / Medium:** Photography. Emulates a vintage or digital low-megapixel snapshot taken at twilight or dusk with high sensor noise (grain).

---

## 2. Color Palette
The color scheme is dominated by cool, desaturated blue tones, stark black, and off-white.

| Color Name | Hex Code (Approx.) | Usage / Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Twilight Blue** | `#1C3B57` | Deepest shadows in the sea and distant horizon. |
| **Ocean Slate** | `#3A6080` | Midtones of the churning water. |
| **Seafoam White** | `#C5D7E5` | Foam, wave crests, and scattered highlights. |
| **Plush Cream** | `#EBEBEB` | The off-white fabric of the Snoopy plush. |
| **Jet Black** | `#111111` | Snoopy’s nose, ears, eyes, and deep shadow areas. |

---

## 3. Subject: Snoopy Plush
* **Placement:** Positioned centrally, facing directly forward.
* **Anatomy/Features:**
  * **Head/Snout:** Rounded, bulbous white fabric with slight motion blur.
  * **Nose:** Oval, jet-black velvet/fabric nose positioned near the lower center of the head.
  * **Eyes:** Simple, small black dot eyes, slightly elongated horizontally.
  * **Ears:** Floppy black beagle ears.
    * **Left Ear (Viewer's Left):** Flips upward playfully, caught in the wind.
    * **Right Ear (Viewer's Right):** Slanted downwards and out to the side.
* **Texture & Lighting:** Soft, fuzzy plush texture. Directional ambient light comes from the front-left, casting soft shadows on the right side of the face and neck.

---

## 4. Background: Ocean & Sky
* **Foreground/Midground (Sea):** 
  * Turbulent, choppy waves with white foam crests spreading horizontally across the frame.
  * Strong motion blur suggesting windy weather conditions or movement during capture.
* **Background (Sky):** 
  * A flat, gradient-free expanse of deep twilight blue representing a heavy, overcast or dusk sky. No visible clouds or celestial bodies.

---

## 5. Camera & Digital Effects (Post-Processing)
* **Grain/Noise:** Heavy digital ISO noise layered uniformly across the entire image to emulate a late-90s/early-2000s digital camera or cheap smartphone sensor.
* **Focus:** Shallow depth of field with soft focus (slight blur) on the Snoopy plush; the sea in the background has a slightly different focal plane blur.
* **Color Grading:** Cool-toned temperature shift leaning heavily into blues and cyans.

---

## 6. Typography & Text Elements
* **Presence:** **None.** There are no overlays, watermarks, titles, or subtitles in the source media.
* **Fallback Rule (If text were required):** 
  * *Primary Font:* **Courier New** or **VCR OSD Mono** to match the low-fi, nostalgic aesthetic.
  * *Fallback Stack:* `Courier New`, `Courier`, `Lucida Console`, `monospace`.
  * *Style:* White, small, centered or lower-third, with a soft drop shadow for legibility against the blue background.

---

## 7. Animation Specification (Optional Motion State)
If this static image were to be converted into a dynamic/animated sequence:
* **Ocean Animation:** Continuous loop of horizontal wave movement, foam shifting left-to-right, and gentle rolling waves.
* **Wind / Plush Animation:** Subtle, organic swaying of the black ears (especially the upturned left ear) reacting to a simulated breeze.
* **Camera Animation:** A very subtle, slow "handheld" drift (pan/zoom micro-movements using Perlin noise) to mimic natural camera shake.
