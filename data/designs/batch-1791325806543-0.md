# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Cyber-Spatial Particle Simulation / High-Tech Data Visualization Interface.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High technical density with a balanced focal center, utilizing deep dark space and glowing spatial elements to frame 3D interactive particle meshes.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered glassmorphic panels and floating control pills with subtle backdrop blurs, crisp 1px borders with inner glow states, and zero heavy drop-shadows.

# 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase HUD labels, glowing coordinate grid lines, neon lighting rings, particle dispersal effects, monospace metadata readouts.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`, border width `1px` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-white inline-block"></span>`
