# Comprehensive UI/UX Design Specification: "Styra" Multiview E-Commerce & Brand Experience

---

## 1. Global Styles & Design Tokens

### 1.1 Typography System
*   **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, sans-serif
*   **Fallback Stack:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
*   **Scale & Hierarchy:**
    *   **Hero / Display Titles (e.g., "HOW IT STARTED.", "DESIGNED WITH PURPOSE", "404 YOU'RE OFF TRACK."):**
        *   Font Weight: 700 (Bold) to 900 (Black)
        *   Text Transform: Uppercase
        *   Tracking / Letter-spacing: Tight (`-0.03em` to `-0.05em`)
    *   **Subheadings / Section Headers (e.g., "PRESENT", "A GROWING MOVEMENT"):**
        *   Font Weight: 600 (Semi-Bold) / 700 (Bold)
        *   Text Transform: Uppercase
    *   **Body Text & Descriptions:**
        *   Font Weight: 400 (Regular)
        *   Line Height: `1.5` to `1.6`
    *   **Navigation & UI Labels:**
        *   Font Weight: 500 (Medium)
        *   Text Transform: Uppercase (primarily) or Sentence Case for descriptive actions.
        *   Font Size: `12px` to `14px`

### 1.2 Color Palette
*   **Monochromatic Base:**
    *   `Pure Black` (#000000): Primary background for dark mode sections, high-contrast text.
    *   `Off-Black / Charcoal` (#0A0A0A, #111111): Surfaces, card containers.
    *   `Off-White / Light Gray` (#F4F4F4, #FAFAFA): Primary background for light mode editorial sections.
    *   `Pure White` (#FFFFFF): High-contrast text on dark backgrounds, card containers.
*   **Accent Color:**
    *   `Electric Blue` (#0022FF / #0038FF): Interactive buttons, badges, active states, navigational icons, interactive "+" hotspots.
*   **Muted Text / Borders:**
    *   `Gray` (#7A7A7A, #8E8E93): Secondary labels, timeline nodes, inactive states, borders.

---

## 2. Component Specifications

### 2.1 Global Header / Navigation Bar
*   **Layout:** Fixed or sticky horizontal bar spanning full width. Split into left branding, central navigational links (or top announcement bar integration), and right-side utility icons/language selectors.
*   **Announcement Bar (Dark Mode View):**
    *   Background: `#000000` with subtle border-bottom.
    *   Content: Left-aligned status indicators ("LIGHT MODE [Toggle]"), Center: "SHOP OUR FALL SALE: 30% OFF", Right: Language ("ENGLISH v"), Currency ("USD $ v").
*   **Main Navigation Bar:**
    *   Background: `#000000` (Dark) / `#FAFAFA` (Light).
    *   **Branding:** "styra" logo set in lowercase, bold italicized serif or geometric sans with stylized typography (`styra` in bright blue or contrasting monochrome).
    *   **Menu Links:** `SHOP`, `SALE`, `COLLECTIONS`, `EXPLORE` (Uppercase, medium weight, tracked out).
    *   **Utility Icons (Right):**
        *   Wishlist / Notifications badge (Red badge with count "2").
        *   Search magnifying glass.
        *   User profile icon.
        *   Shopping bag / cart icon (Badge with count "2").

---

## 3. Section-by-Section Layout & Content Analysis

---

### SECTION 1: Brand History / Timeline View ("Our Journey")
*   **Theme:** Dark Mode (`#000000` background).
*   **Layout Structure:** Asymmetric split. Left side houses bold conceptual typography; right side features an interactive vertical timeline overlaying a textural background image.

#### 3.1.1 Background & Imagery
*   **Background Asset:** Macro/close-up photographic image of a textured black sneaker knit/mesh material showing interwoven synthetic threads and eyelets. Deep, high-contrast shadows with dramatic directional lighting highlighting the weave structure.
*   **Overlay Effect:** Dark linear gradient overlay (from 80% opacity black to 40% opacity) ensuring text legibility.

#### 3.1.2 Content & Typography (Left Column)
*   **Eyebrow:** `• OUR JOURNEY` (Uppercase, small, gray/white).
*   **Main Headline:**
    *   `HOW IT STARTED.`
    *   `WHY IT MATTERS.`
    *   *Styling:* Huge display font size, bold weight, left-aligned, tight line-height.

#### 3.1.3 Interactive Timeline (Right Column)
*   **Structure:** A central vertical thin white/gray line running from top to bottom.
*   **Timeline Nodes:** Circular hollow points with solid borders representing years:
    *   `2019` (Top)
    *   `2020` (Middle-top)
    *   `2021` (Middle)
    *   `PRESENT` (Bottom, highlighted with a solid blue dot indicator).
*   **Bottom Right Callout:**
    *   **Heading:** `PRESENT` (Extra-large display text, bold, uppercase).
    *   **Subheading:** `A GROWING MOVEMENT OF WEARERS REDEFINING THEIR STRIDE.` (Medium weight, uppercase).

---

### SECTION 2: Product Showcase / Editorial View ("Designed With Purpose")
*   **Theme:** Light Mode (`#FAFAFA` / `#FFFFFF` background).
*   **Layout Structure:** Full-width editorial layout dominated by massive overlapping typography and a central hero product display.

#### 2.2.1 Hero Typography & Composition
*   **Headline (Split across the canvas):**
    *   `DESIGNED WITH` (Top left, massive bold uppercase font).
    *   `PURPOSE` (Second line left, massive bold uppercase font).
    *   `N WITH` / `ENTION` (Framing the product on the right: `DESIGN WITH INTENTION` offset visually).
*   **Central Hero Product Image:**
    *   **Subject:** High-performance chunky lifestyle sneaker in cool gray, cream, and black colorway. Features multi-layered paneling, dynamic ridged sculpted midsole, black heel counter, and rope laces.
    *   **Placement:** Positioned centrally, angled in a dynamic three-quarters profile facing right. Backgroundless (isolated on transparent/white background).
    *   **Interactive Hotspot:** A circular white button with a bold blue plus (`+`) icon positioned precisely over the midfoot/lace cage of the sneaker.

#### 2.2.2 Product Description Block (Bottom Left)
*   **Body Copy:** *"From the daily commute to the spontaneous detour, our shoes are built to carry you forward with clean design, intentional materials, and comfort that lasts all day. We're not chasing trends. We're designing for the way you actually live."*
*   **CTA Button:** `• KNOW MORE` (Solid electric blue background, white uppercase text, rounded/pill-shaped border).

#### 2.2.3 Floating Product Card (Bottom Right)
*   **Container:** Dark card (`#111111` background with subtle white border), floating over the light background.
*   **Thumbnail:** Thumbnail image of the gray sneaker (`ATHLEISURE` collection).
*   **Details:**
    *   Category label: `ATHLEISURE` (Small uppercase text).
    *   Product Name: `Worn Up` (Bold white text).
    *   Action indicator: Arrow icon (`↗`) in the top right corner.

---

### SECTION 3: Error / State View ("404 You're Off Track")
*   **Theme:** Dark Mode (`#000000` background with technical/system-status design accents).
*   **Layout Structure:** Technical diagnostic dashboard aesthetic combining editorial typography, terminal logs, and motion-blurred background photography.

#### 3.3.1 Background & Imagery
*   **Background Asset:** Dynamic, horizontal motion-blurred photographic image capturing a runner in motion or a futuristic speed trail effect, blending dark charcoal, silver, and warm amber/orange light streaks.
*   **Texture/Effects:** Subtle horizontal scanlines or grid lines overlaying the background to evoke a technical terminal/diagnostic screen.

#### 3.3.2 Terminal / System Log Box (Top Left)
*   **Container:** Semi-transparent dark box with a thin border.
*   **Content (Monospace Font):**
    ```text
    > SYSTEM ERROR: 404
    > SUBJECT DEVIATED FROM ROUTE
    > ENVIRONMENT UNMAPPED
    ```
*   **Header Tags:** `// SIGNAL LOST` (Left) | `RECALIBRATING ROUTE` (Right).

#### 3.3.3 Main Error Typography
*   **Heading:** `404 YOU’RE OFF TRACK.`
    *   `404`: Huge display numerals in light gray.
    *   `YOU'RE OFF TRACK.`: Heavy bold white uppercase typography.
*   **Subtext:** *"THE PAGE COULDN'T KEEP UP WITH YOUR STRIDE. TIME TO REROUTE."* (Letter-spaced, medium weight).

#### 3.3.4 Status Footer Bar
*   **Content:**
    *   `STATUS: OUT OF RANGE` (Left)
    *   `// SYSTEMS REBOOTING...` (Right)
    *   Separated by a clean thin horizontal rule line.

## 4. Responsive & Interactive Behavior Notes
*   **Hover States:**
    *   Navigation items show subtle color shifts or underline indicators.
    *   Buttons (`KNOW MORE`) invert or slightly scale on hover (`transform: scale(1.02)`).
    *   Interactive Hotspot (`+`) pulses gently to draw user attention to product details.
*   **Transitions:** Smooth cubic-bezier transitions (`0.3s ease`) for modals, tooltips, and interactive states.
