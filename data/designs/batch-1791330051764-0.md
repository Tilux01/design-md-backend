# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Ultra-modern Web3 / AI SaaS Dark Mode. High-tech, sleek, cryptographic, and performance-oriented.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Generous vertical whitespace framing a centralized high-impact hero block. Symmetrical horizontal padding anchored by edge-to-edge ambient neon light streaks.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Pitch-black canvas background (`#030303`) layered with deep carbon-black gradient containers (`#0A0A0C`), subtle 1px border highlights, and vibrant neon chromatic curves (cyan, electric blue, magenta, crimson).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Glowing chromatic wave graphics, pill-shaped floating badges with glowing status orbs, high-contrast monochrome dark surfaces, glassmorphic navbar.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `6px 16px`, Font-size: `12px`, Font-weight: `500`, Tracking: `0.05em`, Border-radius: `9999px`, Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`, Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) $\rightarrow$ Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) $\rightarrow$ `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) $\rightarrow$ `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-cyan-400 inline-block"></span>`.

