# Design Specification: Stylist A.I. Shopping Interface

## 1. Overall Aesthetic & Theme
*   **Style:** Glassmorphism / Soft Minimalist.
*   **Background:** A full-screen, soft-focus radial gradient. Colors transition smoothly between Pastel Blue (`#A8DADC`), Muted Purple (`#BDB2FF`), and Soft Pink (`#FFC6FF`).
*   **Main Container:** A centered frosted-glass card (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`) with a white border (1px, opacity 0.2) and a border-radius of `40px`.
*   **Shadows:** Soft, diffused drop shadows on all floating elements to create depth.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Poppins` (Sans-serif).
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:** 
    *   Regular (400) for descriptions and secondary text.
    *   Medium (500) for labels and navigation.
    *   Semi-Bold (600) for product titles and prices.
*   **Color:** Primary text is Deep Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`). Secondary text is Muted Grey (`#757575`).

## 3. Layout Structure

### A. Navigation Sidebar (Left)
*   **Width:** ~80px.
*   **Background:** Transparent, separated by a thin vertical line (1px, light grey, 0.1 opacity).
*   **Icons:**
    *   **Top:** 9-dot grid icon (Menu).
    *   **Center Group:** Lightning bolt, T-shirt (Active state: white icon inside a black circle), Socks, Heart, Gear/Settings.
    *   **Bottom:** User Profile Image (Circular, 40px diameter, featuring a woman’s face).
*   **Spacing:** Icons are vertically distributed with `flex-direction: column` and `justify-content: space-between`.

### B. Header (Top)
*   **Breadcrumbs:** "Shop > **Stylist A.I.**" (Left aligned).
*   **Search Bar:** Centered-right. A subtle pill-shaped input with a magnifying glass icon and placeholder text "Search something...".
*   **Action Icons:** 
    *   Logout/Exit icon.
    *   Shopping Bag icon: Black circle with a white bag icon and a small notification badge "2" in the top right corner.

### C. Featured Product Section (Center-Left)
*   **Visual Effect:** Three stacked cards. The back two cards are slightly offset to the right and top, creating a "deck" look.
*   **Main Card:** White background, `30px` border-radius.
*   **Product Image:** 
    *   **Description:** A woman wearing a light blue satin button-down shirt dress, a matching blue patterned headscarf, and white-rimmed sunglasses. 
    *   **Placement:** The image is **backgroundless (transparent)**, centered in the top half of the card. The woman is facing forward, hands touching the scarf.
*   **Interactive Elements:**
    *   **Floating Heart:** A black circular button in the top right corner of the card with a white heart icon.
    *   **Color Pickers:** Three vertical circles on the left side (Light Blue, Black, White). The Blue one has a selection ring.
*   **Product Info:**
    *   **Title:** "Satin Shirt Dress" (Large, Bold).
    *   **Price:** "$ 59" (Right-aligned with title).
    *   **Description:** Small grey text: "Midi design. Flared design. Flowy fabric. Shirt-style. Satin finish."
*   **Footer Actions:**
    *   **Size Dropdown:** A small grey box labeled "SIZE: S" with a downward chevron.
    *   **Add to Cart:** A wide, black rectangular button with rounded corners (`10px`) and white text.

### D. Recommendations Sidebar (Right)
*   **Title:** "YOU MAY ALSO LIKE:" (All caps, small, grey).
*   **Product Items (List of 3):**
    *   **Structure:** Image thumbnail (left), Text info (center), Price & Heart (right).
    *   **Item 1:** "Night Business Dress" ($230). Image: Woman in a black blazer-style dress. Subtext: "Pleated Skirt". Heart: Filled black.
    *   **Item 2:** "White Winter Belt Dress" ($215). Image: Woman in a white turtleneck knit dress with a brown belt. Subtext: "Adjustable Belt". Heart: Outline.
    *   **Item 3:** "Red Dress With Flounces" ($205). Image: Woman in a bright red short-sleeved dress with a wavy neckline. Subtext: "Classic Collar". Heart: Outline.
*   **Thumbnail Styling:** Images are contained in soft-colored boxes (beige/off-white) with `15px` border-radius. Images are backgroundless.

### E. Promo Banner (Bottom-Right)
*   **Background:** A vibrant horizontal gradient (Blue to Purple to Pink).
*   **3D Element:** A gold-textured icosahedron (geometric gem) floating on the left side of the banner.
*   **Text:** "INVITE FRIEND AND GET PROMO: **FS2022**".
*   **Badge:** A large white "-20%" text overlay on the right side with low opacity.

## 4. Color Palette (Hex Codes)
*   **Primary Black:** `#000000`
*   **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF`
*   **Active Blue:** `#7FB3D5`
*   **Text Grey:** `#8E8E93`
*   **Card Background:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` (Glass effect)
*   **Banner Gradient:** `linear-gradient(90deg, #74ebd5 0%, #acb6e5 100%)`

## 5. Spacing & Dimensions
*   **Main Container Padding:** `40px`.
*   **Gap between Main Card and Right Sidebar:** `50px`.
*   **Border Radius (Standard):** `20px` for small cards, `40px` for main containers.
*   **Icon Size:** `24px` for standard navigation icons.
