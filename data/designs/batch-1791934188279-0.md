# Design Specification: Brainka Landing Page

## 1. Global Setup & Layout
* **Viewport**: Fullscreen, responsive desktop layout.
* **Background Color**: Solid white (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Layout Flow**: Single-column vertical flex, centered alignment.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Background**: `#FFFFFF`
* **Primary Text / Buttons**: `#0D0D0D` (Near Black)
* **Secondary Text**: `#2D2D2D`
* **Nav Pill Background**: `#E4E4E6` (Subtle warm gray)
* **Button Text**: `#FFFFFF`
* **Icon Drop Shadows**: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)` with `blur: 16px`, `y: 8px`

---

## 3. Typography

### Primary Display Serif (Headline: "Meet Brainka,")
* **Family**: *Recoleta*, *Ogg*, or *Playfair Display*
* **Fallback Stack**: `"Playfair Display", "Georgia", "Times New Roman", serif`
* **Weight**: 700 (Bold)
* **Size**: `72px` – `80px`
* **Letter Spacing**: `-0.02em`
* **Line Height**: `1.1`

### UI & Body Sans-Serif (Nav, Subheading, Button)
* **Family**: *Inter* or *SF Pro Display*
* **Fallback Stack**: `"-apple-system", "BlinkMacSystemFont", "Inter", "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif`
* **Weights**:
  * Subheading: 400 (Regular), `18px`, color `#2D2D2D`
  * Nav links: 500 (Medium), `14px`, color `#1A1A1A`
  * CTA Button: 500 (Medium), `15px`, color `#FFFFFF`

---

## 4. UI Components

### Floating Navigation Pill
* **Placement**: Top-center, `margin-top: 32px`.
* **Dimensions**: Height `40px`, horizontal padding `16px`.
* **Border Radius**: `9999px` (Fully rounded capsule).
* **Background**: `#E4E4E6`.
* **Contents** (Horizontal flex, `gap: 16px`, centered):
  1. **Icon**: 4-node diamond cluster symbol (black, `14px × 14px`).
  2. **Links**: "About", "Pricing", "News".

### Hero Copy & CTA
* **Headline**: "Meet Brainka," (`margin-top: 60px`, `margin-bottom: 12px`).
* **Subheading**: "and discover connections you didn't know existed." (`margin-bottom: 24px`).
* **Download Button**:
  * **Background**: `#0D0D0D`.
  * **Border Radius**: `10px`.
  * **Padding**: `10px 22px`.
  * **Content**: Apple glyph logo (``) followed by "Download" (`gap: 8px`).

---

## 5. Central Graphic Composition

### Classical Bust Asset
* **Description**: Monochrome, stipple-engraved classical male bust sliced flat at forehead level to expose a dark open hollow top. Neck dissolves into dot stipples at the base.
* **Format**: Backgroundless (transparent PNG).
* **Placement**: Centered horizontally directly beneath the CTA button (`margin-top: 40px`).
* **Width**: Approx. `480px` – `520px`.

### Floating App Icons (Left-to-Right Arch)

1. **Dropbox Icon**:
