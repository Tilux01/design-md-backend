# Design Specification: Tesla Roadster & Car-By Multi-Section UI/UX Concept

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport
- **Canvas / Viewport**: Full-screen responsive desktop view (optimized for `1440px` wide grid structure).
- **Core Theme**: High-end automotive dark mode aesthetic. Employs deep obsidian blacks (`#0A0A0A`, `#121212`), glossy metallic charcoal gray panels, and stark crimson-red brand accents (`#CC0000`, `#E31937`).
- **Background Layer**: Subtle textured carbon fiber or dark matte gradient backing. The primary main interface is framed cleanly as a layered web application.

---

## 2. Typography & Font System
*Note: If custom fonts cannot be loaded locally or via CDN, standard modern geometric sans-serif fallback stacks are assigned.*

- **Primary Display Font (Headings, Branding, Stats)**:
  - *Font Family*: `Orbitron`, `Tesla (Custom)`, or `Eurostile Extended`.
  - *Fallback Stack*: `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
  - *Characteristics*: Geometric, ultra-wide kerning, futuristic angular geometry, uppercase dominant.
- **Secondary Body Font (UI Labels, Pricing, Specs)**:
  - *Font Family*: `Inter`, `Montserrat`, or `Roboto`.
  - *Fallback Stack*: `system-ui, sans-serif`.
  - *Characteristics*: Highly legible at small sizes, clean weight contrasts (Light, Regular, Medium, Bold).

---

## 3. Section-by-Section UI Breakdown

### Section A: Main Header & Tesla Roadster Hero Viewport
- **Navigation Bar (`<header>`)**:
  - **Left**: Tesla Logo (`<svg>` in pure white) followed by top-level text links: `LOGIN`, `ACCOUNT`, `MENU`, `SEARCH`.
  - **Right**: Language selector indicator (`EN v`) and a toggle hamburger menu icon.
- **Hero Title (`<h1>`)**:
  - Massive, centered, glowing bevel typography spelling **"ROADSTER"** in bright crimson red with a 3D inset silver/white depth outline.
- **Hero Vehicle Display**:
  - Top-down or high-angle dramatic photography of a bright red Tesla Roadster resting on a dark metallic circular platform.
- **Radial Performance Dial & Icons (`01` to `05`)**:
  - **Ring Arc**: A glowing circular instrument panel framing the car with markers.
  - **Stat Callouts**:
    - `01`: `TRI-MOTOR AWD SYSTEM`
    - `02`: `620 MI DRIVING RANGE`
    - `03`: `0-60 MPH IN 1.9 SEC`
    - `04`: `250+ MPH TOP SPEED`
- **Feature Split Callout**:
  - **Left Box (`BUILT FOR PERFORMANCE`)**: Dark brushed metallic preview card showing interior steering wheel telemetry. Caption: *"Tesla Roadster is engineered with cutting-edge technology to deliver..."*
  - **Right Box (`SMART • INTUITIVE • FUTURISTIC`)**: Complementary secondary text box highlighting the futuristic driving dynamics.

---

### Section B: Brand Selector Grid (`SELECT YOUR CAR BY`)
- **Section Header**: Left-aligned red line marker followed by `"SELECT YOUR CAR BY"` and a pill button toggle for `"BRANDS"`.
- **Brand Logo Matrix**:
  - A clean 2-row grid showcasing monochrome metallic logo badges against matte dark card panels:
    - Row 1: Mercedes-Benz, Bentley, Jaguar, Audi.
    - Row 2: Aston Martin, BMW, Lamborghini, Porsche.

---

### Section C: Car Catalog Grid (Primary Showcase: `CAR BY`)
- **Layout**: 3-column grid of glossy dark UI cards showcasing luxury/supercars.
- **Card Structure**:
  - **Image Thumbnail**: High-resolution centered studio photo of the vehicle (e.g., Mercedes-Benz S-Class, Bentley Continental, Jaguar F-Type R, Audi R8 V10, Aston Martin DB12 S, BMW M8 Competition).
  - **Model Information**:
    - Title: Bold uppercase (e.g., `MERCEDES-BENZ S-CLASS 2025`).
    - Specs: Priceing tiers (`Price: Starting from around $125,000`), Body Type (`Type: Luxury Sedan`), Engine specs (`Engine: Twin-Turbo V8`).
  - **CTA Button**: A dark pill-shaped button with a thin border reading `EXPLORE PRODUCT`.

---

### Section D: Secondary Embedded Modules (Floating Layout Mockups)
1. **Top-Right Mini Catalog Card (`CAR BY`)**:
   - Compact variation of the main car catalog pinned inside a floating dark container. Features tab headers (`Latest`, `Featured`, `POPULAR`).
2. **Bottom-Right Interactive Newsletter & Footer Component (`STAY IN TOUCH`)**:
   - **Newsletter Box**: Features an input field (`Enter your email address`) paired with a crimson circular submit arrow button (`→`). Next to it sits a preview thumbnail of a luxury coupe with a play/video overlay icon.
   - **Footer Links**: Clean columns for `QUICK LINKS` (Home, Inventory, Brands, Luxury Cars, Sports Cars, Contact Us), `CONTACT` details (Address, Phone, Email), and a `NEWSLETTER` sign-up prompt with social icon glyphs at the base.

---

## 4. Micro-Interactions & Animations (UX Specification)
- **Hover States on Catalog Cards**:
  - `transform: translateY(-4px);`
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 30px rgba(204, 0, 0, 0.15);` (Subtle crimson environmental glow).
- **CTA Button Interactivity**:
  - Background shifts from transparent/dark matte to solid `#CC0000` with smooth transition (`all 0.3s cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`).
- **Radial Dial Pulse**:
  - The telemetry arcs surrounding the Roadster feature a subtle opacity breathing animation (`keyframes pulse-glow`).
