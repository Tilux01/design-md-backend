# Design Specification: Omega Speedmaster Moonwatch Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Layout
* **Layout Style**: Fullscreen dark-mode luxury showcase.
* **Canvas Dimensions**: 1920 × 1080 (16:9 responsive).
* **Grid**: 12-column layout with fixed vertical sidebars, centered hero product, and a bottom specification bar.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Base Background**: Deep Charcoal / Space Black (`#0F1115` to `#16191E`)
* **Primary Brand Red**: Crimson Red (`#E10628`)
* **Text Primary**: White (`#FFFFFF`)
* **Text Muted**: Medium Slate Gray (`#8E929A`)
* **Watermark / Backdrop Typography**: Dark Muted Charcoal (`#22262C`)
* **Floating Panel Fill**: Semi-transparent black (`rgba(18, 21, 26, 0.7)`) with backdrop blur (`12px`)

---

## 3. Typography
* **Primary Font**: `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif (Fallbacks: `Arial`, `system-ui`)
* **Styles**:
  * **Brand Mark / Large Titles**: Bold, 48px–64px (`Moonwatch`)
  * **Section Overline**: Semi-bold, 12px, tracking: +0.25em (`SPEEDMASTER`)
  * **Nav Links & Specs**: Regular to Medium, 13px–15px
  * **Body Text**: Regular, 12px–13px, line-height: 1.6
  * **Giant Background Text**: Extra-Bold, ~180px–220px (`dark side`)

---

## 4. UI Components & Layout Structure

### 4.1 Header & Brand Badge
* **Omega Badge**: Flush top-left red square (`width: 64px`, `height: 64px`, background: `#E10628`) containing the white Omega Greek symbol (`Ω`), centered.
* **Top Navigation Bar**: Positioned top-right (`top: 32px`, `right: 64px`).
  * Links: `Collection`, `Heritage`, `Apollo`, `Production`.
  * Style: 13px, white/muted gray text, flex layout with `gap: 32px`. Active indicator on `Collection`.

### 4.2 Left Sidebar (Language Switcher)
* **Position**: Bottom-left corner, vertical alignment.
* **Content**: `EN`, `DE`, `PL` stacked vertically or rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise.
* **Style**: 11px uppercase, active language highlighted in red (`#E10628`).

### 4.3 Hero Content (Left Side)
* **Coordinates**: `left: 10%`, vertically centered.
* **Overline**: `SPEEDMASTER` (letter-spaced, gray).
* **Heading**: `Moonwatch` (Large, white).
* **Description**: Multi-line copy describing the Apollo 8 heritage and black ceramic Co-Axial build (`max-width: 320px`, muted gray).
* **CTA Button**:
  * Label: `READ MORE →`
  * Style: Solid red pill button (`background: #E10628`), white uppercase text (11px, bold), horizontal padding: 24px, vertical padding: 10px.

### 4.4 Centerpiece & Background Visuals
* **Background Texture**: Subtle starfield with a faint galactic gradient in the upper right.
* **Moon Asset**:
  * *Description*: High-detail grayscale 3D sphere of the lunar surface. Left-to-right lighting with strong crescent highlight on the left edge.
  * *Placement*: Centered slightly to the left behind the watch; cropped lower boundary.
* **Backdrop Watermark**:
  * Large dark gray lettering reading `"dark side"`.
  * Layered behind the watch and partially overlapping the moon.
* **Main Product (Watch Asset)**:
  * *Description*: Omega Speedmaster "Dark Side of the Moon" all-black ceramic chronograph with black Cordura/nylon fabric strap.
  * *Format*: Cutout PNG with transparent background.
  * *Placement*: Exactly centered vertically and horizontally, 0-degree rotation (perfect vertical alignment), cast soft drop-shadow onto background elements.

### 4.5 Right Column Content
* **Price Tag**:
  * Positioned mid-right (`top: ~55%`, `right: 12%`).
  * Label: `Price` (muted, 12px).
  * Value: `9900$` (bold white, 28px).
* **Next Piece Tile**:
  * Positioned lower-right (`right: 10%`, `bottom: 12%`).
  * Red square tile (`#E10628`) with soft radial red glow behind it.
  * Text: `Next Piece` (white, bold) and pagination `01/04` (semi-transparent white).

### 4.6 Bottom Specifications Bar
* **Position**: Centered horizontally at the bottom (`bottom: 32px`, `width: ~65%`).
* **Container**: Dark translucent capsule with subtle border (`border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
* **Grid**: 4 equidistant spec columns featuring fine-line vector icons:
  1. **Sapphire** (`Glass material`) — Ring icon.
  2. **Ceramic** (`Case material`) — Watch case outline icon.
  3. **5 bar** (`Water resistance`) — Mesh/grill icon.
  4. **Caliber 9300** (`Automatic movement`) — Rotor/movement icon.
* **Typography**: Title in bold white (14px), subtitle in muted gray (11px).

---

## 5. Layering Order (Z-Index)
1. Space background / star texture (`z-index: 0`)
2. Moon graphic (`z-index: 1`)
3. `"dark side"` backdrop text (`z-index: 2`)
4. Omega watch PNG (`z-index: 3`)
5. Floating Specs bar, Hero text, and UI Controls (`z-index: 10`)
