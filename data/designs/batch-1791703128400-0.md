### 1.2 Typography System

The design uses a high-contrast editorial pairing: a crisp geometric/neo-grotesque sans-serif combined with an elegant, high-contrast transitional serif italic.

#### Primary Sans-Serif Font
* **Font Family:** `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, or `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
* **Weights:** `400` (Regular), `500` (Medium), `600` (Semi-Bold), `700` (Bold)
* **Usage:** Navigation, main headlines, standard titles, body paragraphs, and UI labels.

#### Accent Editorial Serif Font
* **Font Family:** `Instrument Serif`, `Playfair Display`, `Baskerville`, or `Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif`
* **Style:** *Italic*
* **Weight:** `400` (Regular Italic)
* **Usage:** Eyebrow accents (*"Your Day, in Perfect Rhythm."*), contrasting heading phrases (*"With Less"*, *"Stress"*).

## 2. Layout & Grid Specifications

* **Canvas Width:** Full viewport width (`100vw`), maximum content container constrained to `1200px`.
* **Margins:** Adaptive horizontal margins:
  * Desktop (≥ 1200px): `80px` outer margins.
  * Tablet (768px – 1199px): `40px` outer margins.
  * Mobile (< 768px): `20px` outer margins.
* **Vertical Spacing:** Generous breathing room with sections separated by `100px` to `140px` margins.

## 3. Section-by-Section Component Specifications

### 3.1 Navigation Bar
* **Positioning:** Fixed or sticky at `top: 0`, full width, `z-index: 50`.
* **Container:** Height `72px`, flex layout with `justify-content: space-between; align-items: center;`.
* **Left Element (Logo):** Text "Enblox" in pure black (`#0D0D0D`), sans-serif bold, 18px.
* **Center Element (Nav Links):** Horizontal menu spaced with `32px` gap.
  * Links: `Home`, `Services`, `Features`, `Blog`, `Pricing`.
  * Style: Color `#4A4A4A`, font-size `13px`, font-weight `400`. Hover state shifts to `#0D0D0D`.
* **Right Element (CTA Button):**
  * Pill container: `border: 1px solid #1A1A1A; border-radius: 9999px; padding: 10px 22px;`.
  * Text: `TRY IT FOR FREE →` (bold uppercase, 11px, letter-spacing `0.05em`).
  * Hover transition: Background transitions to `#0D0D0D`, text transitions to `#FFFFFF`.

* **Description:** A realistic, high-resolution photographic image of a person's hand (medium-deep brown skin tone) holding a modern smartphone (resembling an iPhone Pro model with flat metallic polished edges and thin minimal bezels).

* **Hand Grip:** The hand grips the phone from the left edge and base; fingers naturally curl around the back and side frame.
* **Device Screen Display:** The smartphone display is illuminated with a glossy, abstract, high-contrast wallpaper matching the background palette: fluid swirls of electric blue, fuchsia, purple, and sunset orange.
* **Position & Rotation:**
  * Centered horizontally.
  * Upright orientation with a subtle tilt: tilted approximately `12°` to `15°` to the right (clockwise) or perspective tilted.
* **Bottom Edge Blend:** The wrist/forearm fades cleanly into the white background using a smooth linear gradient alpha mask (`mask-image: linear-gradient(to bottom, black 70%, transparent 100%)`).
* **Z-Index:** Positioned in front of the blurred gradient aura and behind any overlapping overlays.

## 4. Responsive Breakpoint Guidelines

### Desktop (`>= 1024px`)
* Standard multi-column grid layouts for feature blocks (3 columns) and header splits (2 columns).
* Full typography scale (`64px` hero title, `56px` section headline).

### Tablet (`768px – 1023px`)
* Hero Headline scales down to `48px`.
* Section editorial headline scales down to `42px`.
* Upper feature row stacks vertically: Headline on top, descriptive paragraph below with a `24px` margin.
* 3-column feature grid collapses into a 2-column or 3-row stacked format.

### Mobile (`< 768px`)
* Navigation bar: Hide central text links, keep logo and "TRY IT FOR FREE" compact button.
* Hero Headline: `36px` to `40px`.
* Hand mockup: Scale to `85%` width, maintain central focus.
* Feature Grid: 1 column layout, each block spaced `32px` vertically.
* Editorial Heading: `32px` - `36px` with proper line breaks preserving italic contrast.
