# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Luxury Minimalist Dark Mode. Blends high-end fragrance branding with immersive, cinematic gallery interactions.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Extremely airy and spacious. Generous vertical and horizontal padding around typography and image panels, allowing visual focus to remain entirely on the immersive product cards and atmospheric imagery.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Monolithic dark backdrop (`#0B0B0B`) with subtle, curving image panels displaying soft ambient shadows (`0 12px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`). Interactive elements feature clean white text and minimalist spacing.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Cinematic editorial layout, atmospheric shadow grading, serif-italic font blending, curved gallery perspective.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `0.75rem`, font-weight `400`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `transparent`, border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-white inline-block"></span>`.
