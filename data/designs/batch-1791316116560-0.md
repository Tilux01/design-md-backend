# NovaTrend E-Commerce Design System Specification

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
* **Primary Mood:** Modern, premium, clean, high-conversion e-commerce lifestyle brand.
* **Brand Archetype:** Contemporary Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) marketplace balancing high-fashion minimalism with utilitarian warmth.
* **Visual Density:** Medium-dense grid layouts with generous internal card padding and whitespace buffers.
* **Theme Balance:** Pure Light Mode design system utilizing ultra-clean backgrounds (`#F8F9FA`), stark high-contrast typography, and floating warm accent gradient pools (Coral/Orange gradients).

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
* **Container Max-Width:** `1280px` centered with auto margins.
* **Grid System:** 12-column flexible grid system with standard gap spacing of `24px` (`gap-6`).
* **Section Sequence:**
  1. Top Promotional Banner (`#1A1A1A` background, white text)
  2. Primary Navigation & Utility Bar
  3. Hero Banner with Floating Micro-Product Cards
  4. Trust Badges Bar (Free Shipping, Secure Payments, Easy Returns, 24/7 Support)
  5. Shop by Categories Grid (Circular category icons)
  6. New Arrivals Product Grid
  7. Best Sellers List & Featured Cards
  8. Flash Sale Banner & Summer 2025 Promo Tiles
  9. Footer Guarantee Strip

## 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Special Glyphs
* **Pill Callout Badges:** 
  * Padding: `4px 10px`
  * Font-Size: `11px`
  * Letter-Spacing: `0.05em` (Uppercase)

* **Symbols & Glyphs:**
  * Star Rating: `&#9733;` (`★`)
  * Arrow Right: `&rarr;` (`→`)
  * Bullet Indicator: `&bull;` (`●`)
  * Sparkle/Highlight: `&#10022;` (`✦`)

## 8. Accessibility (a11y) & WCAG Contrast Audit
* **Primary Text (`#111827`) on White Background (`#FFFFFF`):** Contrast ratio **16.1:1** (Passes WCAG AAA).
* **Muted Text (`#6B7280`) on White Background (`#FFFFFF`):** Contrast ratio **4.6:1** (Passes WCAG AA).
* **Accent Orange (`#FF5722`) Button with White Text (`#FFFFFF`):** Contrast ratio **3.1:1** (Passes WCAG AA for UI components and large text).
* **Focus State Indicator:**
```css
*:focus-visible {
  outline: 2px solid #FF5722;
  outline-offset: 2px;
}
```
