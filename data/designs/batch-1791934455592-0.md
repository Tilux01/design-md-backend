# Design Specification: Smith Legal Group Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

### Color Palette
*   **Background Primary:** `#F3F4F6` (light warm gray background with a soft gradient to `#FFFFFF`).
*   **Text Primary:** `#111827` (almost black for primary headings and prominent UI text).
*   **Text Secondary:** `#6B7280` (neutral gray for subtitles, captions, and secondary links).
*   **Accent Red:** `#E11D48` to `#DC2626` (vibrant crimson used for primary CTAs and logo accent).
*   **Card Background:** `#FFFFFF` (pure white with soft drop shadows and slight border opacity).
*   **Dark Neutral:** `#0F172A` (used for login pill button and social bar dock).

### Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, or `Helvetica Neue`.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:**
    *   Headings: `700` (Bold) / `800` (Extra Bold), uppercase letter spacing slightly condensed.
    *   Body text: `400` (Regular) / `500` (Medium).
    *   Labels & Buttons: `600` (Semi-Bold).

---

## 2. Layout Structure

*   **Viewport:** Fullscreen (100vw, 100vh), flex or grid-based layout with a fixed, unscrollable hero format.
*   **Padding:** Desktop padding of `40px 64px`.

---

## 3. Navigation Bar (Top)

*   **Layout:** 3-column flex container, horizontally centered alignment.
    *   **Left Section:** Navigation links horizontally arranged with 24px gap:
        *   `Home` (active/semi-bold)
        *   `About`
        *   `Services` (with chevron-down icon)
        *   `English` (prefixed with globe icon `🌐`, chevron-down icon)
    *   **Center Section (Logo):**
        *   Icon: Stylized red geometric starburst/flower icon.
        *   Text: "Smith Legal Group" in bold dark text.
    *   **Right Section:**
        *   Pill-shaped button: Dark fill (`#0F172A`), white text "Login / Register", fully rounded corners (`border-radius: 9999px`).

---

## 4. Hero Content (Left Column)

*   **Headline:**
    *   Text: `FIRST LEGAL` / `GUIDANCE YOU` / `SHOULD TRUST`
    *   Style: All caps, large scale (~56px–64px), bold (`800`), dark neutral `#111827`.
*   **Subheadline:**
    *   Text: "Helping individuals and businesses move forward with trusted legal strategies and proven results."
    *   Style: 16px, regular (`400`), muted gray `#6B7280`, max-width ~420px.
*   **Action Buttons:**
    *   **Primary CTA:** Pill button, red gradient (`#EF4444` to `#DC2626`), white text: "Get Started →".
    *   **Secondary CTA:** Pill button, transparent/white fill, subtle border (`#E5E7EB`), text: "Learn More".

---

## 5. Imagery & Central Visuals

### Central Subject: Lady Justice Statue
*   **Description:** Metallic, reflective chrome-silver 3D render of Lady Justice wearing draped robes, holding the scales of justice aloft in her right hand.
*   **Asset Details:**
    *   **Format:** Backgroundless transparent PNG.
    *   **Placement:** Centered horizontally, vertically anchored to bottom/mid-right.
    *   **Scale:** Extends from top navigation level down to the base of the viewport.
    *   **Blend Effect:** Soft bottom fade gradient into the page background.

---

## 6. Floating Cards & Widgets

### Top-Right Card: "Closure Statistics"
*   **Position:** Top right, overlapping behind the statue’s shoulder.
*   **Style:** White rounded card (`border-radius: 20px`), subtle shadow, light gray border.
*   **Content:**
    *   Header: "Closure Statistics" (chart icon prefix) and a pill dropdown: `Yearly ⌵`.
    *   Chart: Smooth splined area line chart (pink/red stroke with faint red gradient fill).
    *   Data Callout: Tooltip pin highlighted at the peak: "34 Cases" with a circular marker.
    *   X-Axis Labels: "Jan", "Feb", "Mar", "Apr", "May", "Jun", "Jul".

### Bottom-Left Card: Mission & Lawyer Highlight
*   **Position:** Bottom-left quadrant, below the main CTA buttons.
*   **Style:** Minimal floating card, light backdrop.
*   **Components:**
    *   **Image:** Square avatar with rounded corners (`12px`) showing a professional male lawyer in a dark suit and tie (neutral gray studio background).
    *   **Text Block:**
        *   Small top-right link indicator: Diagonal arrow icon `↗`.
        *   Title: "YOUR RIGHTS IS OUR MISSION" (All-caps, bold, 14px).
        *   Subtext: "Focused on You, Grounded in Law" (muted gray, 12px).
        *   Link: "MORE DETAILS" (underline/link style, red/dark text).

### Bottom-Center: Social Media Bar
*   **Position:** Bottom center-left, below the scales of justice.
*   **Style:** Floating dark pill dock (`#0F172A`), rounded-full, compact padding.
*   **Icons:** Instagram, Facebook, and X (Twitter) icons in muted white/gray.

### Bottom-Right Widget: Client Metric
*   **Position:** Bottom right corner.
*   **Content:**
    *   Metric: "52K" (Large, bold, ~36px), subtitle "Satisfied Clients" (12px, gray).
    *   Avatar Stack: Three overlapping circular profile photos of clients (young adults smiling, diverse).
    *   Action Button: White circular button with an outward diagonal arrow `↗` attached alongside the avatar stack.
