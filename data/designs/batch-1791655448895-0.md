# UI/UX Specification: "Mr.Bobrovsky" Profile App Screen

This document provides a precise, pixel-level specification for replicating the provided mobile app UI screen. All mock-up framing (such as the iPhone device mockup, outer drop shadows, and external gradient background) are **omitted** per instructions. The target is a pure, full-bleed mobile application canvas.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Typography Architecture

### 1.1 Canvas & Grid
* **Aspect Ratio / Viewport:** Designed for standard modern mobile viewports (e.g., iPhone 14/15 Pro: `393px` × `852px`).
* **Layout Structure:** Vertical flex/stack layout with internal padding.
* **Horizontal Padding:** Consistent `16px` margin on the left and right edges.
* **Background Color:** Deep, near-black obsidian (#111111 or #0e0e0e). The interface relies heavily on dark-mode card containers with subtle elevation gradients.

### 1.2 Typography & Fallbacks
The interface utilizes a clean, geometric sans-serif typeface (similar to **SF Pro**, **Inter**, or **Plus Jakarta Sans**).
* **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
* **Font Weights Used:** Regular (400), Medium (500), Semi-Bold (600), Bold (700).

## 2. Component-by-Component Breakdown

### 2.1 Status Bar & Navigation Header
* **Status Bar:**
  * **Time:** `9:41` (Bold, ~15px, White `#FFFFFF`).
  * **Right Icons:** Cellular signal bars, Wi-Fi icon, and Battery level indicator (all in solid white `#FFFFFF`).
* **Top Navigation Bar:**
  * **Left Action:** Pill-shaped "Close" button with an "X" icon.
    * *Background:* Semi-transparent dark gray (`#222222`).
    * *Border Radius:* Full pill (`20px`).
    * *Text/Icon:* White (`#FFFFFF`), ~13px medium.
  * **Right Action Group:**
    * Dropdown selector button featuring a down-chevron and a three-dot menu icon, styled identically to the Close button (pill-shaped, dark gray background).

### 2.2 Header Controls & Balance Display
* **Left Side:**
  * **Settings Icon (Gear):** Solid white glyph, ~20px.
  * **Notifications Icon (Bell):** Solid white glyph with a small bright lime-green dot indicator (`#B6FF00`) positioned at the top-right of the bell.
* **Right Side (Currency / Wallet Pill):**
  * **Container:** Pill-shaped dark background (`#1A1A1A`) with a soft internal padding.
  * **Currency Icon:** Green circular icon with a white currency symbol (e.g., dollar or coin indicator).
  * **Balance Text:** `20,710.00` (Bold, White `#FFFFFF`, ~15px).
  * **Add Button:** Bright lime-green (`#B6FF00`) circular button containing a bold black plus (`+`) icon.

### 2.3 User Identity Section
* **Avatar:**
  * **Description:** A 3D-rendered character head wearing a light-gray balaclava/hood mask with rounded eye holes revealing a stylized face. The avatar has a vibrant, dashed/dotted lime-green circular border (`#B6FF00`) offset slightly from the image.
  * **Size:** ~80px diameter, centered horizontally.
* **Username:**
  * **Main Name:** `Mr.Bobrovsky`
  * **Styling:** Bold, White (`#FFFFFF`), ~22px.
* **Handle:**
  * **Text:** `@mr.bobrovsky`
  * **Styling:** Regular, Muted Gray (`#888888`), ~13px. Margin-top: `2px`.

### 2.4 User Stats Bar (Tri-metric Row)
A horizontal card containing three distinct statistic columns separated by subtle vertical divider lines.
* **Container:** Rounded rectangle (`border-radius: 16px`), dark charcoal background (`#181818`), padding vertical `14px`.
* **Columns (Left to Right):**
  1. **Win/Lose:** 
     * Icon: Lime-green ring/pie chart icon (`#B6FF00`).
     * Value: `70%` (Bold, White, ~16px).
     * Label: `Win/Lose` (Muted gray, ~11px).
  2. **Level:** 
     * Icon: Checkmark-in-badge icon (`#B6FF00`).
     * Value: `8` (Bold, White, ~16px).
     * Label: `Level` (Muted gray, ~11px).
  3. **Days:** 
     * Icon: Calendar icon (`#B6FF00`).
     * Value: `76` (Bold, White, ~16px).
     * Label: `Days` (Muted gray, ~11px).

### 2.5 Horizontal Calendar / Daily Streak Bar
A scrolling or grid-based weekly tracker displaying days of the week.
* **Layout:** 7 equal columns representing days (Monday to Sunday).
* **Day Cells:**
  * **Inactive Days (Mon-Wed, Fri-Sun):** Dark rounded pill background (`#1C1C1C`). Text color for day abbreviation (e.g., `Mon`) in gray (`#777777`), date number (e.g., `19`) in white (`#FFFFFF`). A small status dot sits above the text (green for completed, gray for pending).
  * **Active/Selected Day (Thursday, 22):** 
     * Highlighted with a distinct vibrant lime-green border (`#B6FF00`).
     * Background slightly lighter charcoal (`#222222`).
     * Top indicator features a three-dot menu icon instead of a single dot.
     * Text: `Thu` (Gray/White), `22` (White, bold).

### 2.6 Interactive Widgets Grid (Middle Section)
Two side-by-side cards (or stacked depending on responsive breakpoints, here structured as a 2-column or asymmetrical grid):

#### Left Card: Friends Widget
* **Background:** Dark charcoal card (`#181818`), rounded corners (`16px`).
* **Visual Elements:** Overlapping circular avatars of friends (stacked horizontally).
* **Text:** 
  * Title: `Friends` (Bold, White, ~15px).
  * Subtext: `14 online` (Lime-green, ~12px).

#### Right Card: "Keep it up!" Trophy Widget
* **Background:** Dark charcoal card with a rich radial gradient transition on the right side blending into dark olive/lime tones.
* **Text Content:**
  * Header: `Keep it up!` (Bold, White, ~15px).
  * Subtext: `35 days in a row you are here!` (Muted gray, ~12px, wrapped across two lines).
  * Indicator Pips: A row of small dots below the text (3 gray dots, 1 bright lime dot, 2 dark dots).
* **Image Asset (Trophy):**
  * **Description:** A glossy, 3D-rendered shiny lime-green trophy cup angled slightly toward the viewer, emerging dynamically from the right edge of the card.
  * **Background:** Backgroundless (transparent PNG/model).
  * **Placement:** Positioned on the right side of the card, slightly overflowing the right boundary to create depth.

### 2.7 Banner Widget: "Complete new tasks"
* **Container:** Full-width horizontal banner card (`border-radius: 16px`), featuring a striking dark-to-lime gradient background on the left side.
* **Text Content:**
  * Title: `Complete new tasks` (Bold, White, ~16px).
  * Subtext: `Get a bonus on your winnings with the teams in your collection` (Muted gray, ~12px, two-line layout).
* **Image Asset (Badges/Tokens):**
  * **Description:** Glossy, metallic/glassmorphic 3D rounded tokens or shields featuring dark abstract paw-print or claw-mark iconography, tinted in lime-green and deep charcoal. 
  * **Background:** Backgroundless.
  * **Placement:** Arranged in a staggered cluster on the left side of the banner, with one large primary token in the foreground and blurred/smaller tokens layered in the background for a depth-of-field effect.

### 2.8 Bottom Navigation Bar
* **Container:** Fixed or pinned to the bottom of the canvas, spanning full width. Background matches the deep obsidian theme (`#111111`) with a subtle top border or separation.
* **Items (Left to Right):**
  1. **Main:** Grid icon (4 small squares) + Label `Main` (Gray).
  2. **News:** Globe/Network icon + Label `News` (Gray).
  3. **Shop:** Storefront icon + Label `Shop` (Gray).
  4. **Profile (Active):** User profile silhouette icon + Label `Profile`. 
     * *Active State:* Highlighted entirely in vibrant lime-green (`#B6FF00`), with a small horizontal indicator bar centered just above the icon.
