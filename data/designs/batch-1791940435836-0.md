# Design Specification: "Aura Music" Desktop Interface

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
The application uses a three-column layout with a persistent bottom playback bar. The design is "Full Screen," meaning the background fills the entire viewport.

*   **Overall Background:** A deep charcoal/black gradient. `#121212` at the bottom, transitioning slightly lighter to `#1A1A1A` at the top.
*   **Border Radius:** Standardized at `12px` for large cards and `8px` for smaller elements.
*   **Padding:** Consistent `24px` gutter between major sections.

## 2. Typography
The interface uses a modern, clean Sans-Serif typeface.
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Roboto`.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:** 
    *   Regular (400) for secondary text and durations.
    *   Medium (500) for navigation and track titles.
    *   Bold (700) for section headers and hero text.

## 3. Color Palette
*   **Backgrounds:**
    *   Sidebar/Main: `#121212`
    *   Right Sidebar (Now Playing): `#181818` (slightly lighter for depth)
*   **Accents:**
    *   Primary Action (Hero): Forest Green `#3B593F`
    *   Hero Graphic: Salmon Pink `#F28C8C`
    *   Progress Bar: Sky Blue `#3498DB`
    *   Text (Primary): `#FFFFFF`
    *   Text (Secondary/Muted): `#A0A0A0`

## 4. Component Breakdown

### A. Left Sidebar (Navigation)
*   **Width:** 200px.
*   **Top Section:** 
    *   App Icon (Top left).
    *   Menu items: Home, Radio, Podcasts, Books. (Icon + Text, 14px, Muted color).
*   **Middle Section ("My Music"):**
    *   Heading: "My Music" (Uppercase, 12px, Bold, Muted).
    *   Items: Tracks, Albums, Artists, Playlists, Files.
*   **Bottom Section:** 
    *   Settings, Logout.
*   **Active State:** The active item should have a white text color and a small vertical indicator bar on the far left.

### B. Header & Search
*   **Search Bar:** Located at the top of the main content. A simple magnifying glass icon followed by "Search" placeholder text. Borderless, slightly darker than the background.
*   **User Profile:** Top right. Displays user name "Tasha R." and a circular profile picture (32x32px).

### C. Main Content Area
#### 1. Hero Section ("Main")
*   **Container:** A large horizontal card with rounded corners (`20px`).
*   **Left Side:** A dark green (`#3B593F`) background containing:
    *   Artist Name: "Billie Eilish" (White, 16px).
    *   Track Title: "What Was I Made For?" (White, 32px, Bold).
    *   Button: "Listen Now" (Pill-shaped, White background, Black text, small play icon).
*   **Right Side:** 
    *   **Background Graphic:** A large, multi-pointed starburst/explosion shape in Salmon Pink (`#F28C8C`). It is rotated approximately 15 degrees clockwise.
    *   **Artist Image:** A portrait of Billie Eilish.
        *   *Description:* The image is **backgroundless (PNG)**. She has blonde hair, looking slightly upward with a neutral expression, wearing a dark blue collared shirt.
        *   *Placement:* Centered over the pink starburst, cut off at the waist by the bottom of the hero card.

#### 2. "Playlists for you" Section
*   **Grid:** 4-5 columns.
*   **Card Style:** Vertical cards with a large square image and text below.
*   **Image 1 (The Weeknd style):** Backgroundless photo of a man with an afro and glasses against an orange/yellow gradient background.
*   **Image 2 (Pop):** A man in a white sweatshirt against a blue/grey gradient background.
*   **Image 3 (Ariana Grande style):** Backgroundless photo of a woman with a high ponytail against a purple gradient background.

#### 3. "You may also like" Section
*   Horizontal scrolling list of smaller square album covers with titles and artists underneath.

### D. Right Sidebar ("Now Playing")
*   **Width:** 280px.
*   **Header:** "Now Playing" with a "View all" link.
*   **Track List:**
    *   **Active Track (Top):** Highlighted with a darker grey background. 
        *   *Image:* Red/Black abstract art.
        *   *Text:* "Cruel" - Jackson Wang.
        *   *Duration:* 3:24.
*   **Queue Items:** A vertical list of tracks. Each row contains a small square thumbnail (40x40px), Title (White), Artist (Muted), and Duration (Muted) on the far right.

### E. Bottom Playback Bar
*   **Height:** 80px.
*   **Left Section:** Current track info (Thumbnail, "Cruel", "Jackson Wang").
*   **Center Section:**
    *   **Progress Bar:** A thin line spanning the width of the center. The "played" portion is Sky Blue (`#3498DB`), the "remaining" portion is dark grey. A small white circle acts as the playhead.
    *   **Controls:** Previous, Play/Pause (Large white circle with black icon), Next, Repeat, Shuffle.
*   **Right Section:** Volume slider, Queue icon, and Fullscreen icon.

## 5. Image Asset Requirements

1.  **Billie Eilish Hero:** PNG, transparent background. Subject: Blonde female, blue shirt, looking up.
2.  **Pink Starburst:** SVG or PNG. 12-point star, Salmon Pink (`#F28C8C`).
3.  **Playlist Covers:** 
    *   *Artist 1:* Male, glasses, orange background.
    *   *Artist 2:* Male, white shirt, blue background.
    *   *Artist 3:* Female, ponytail, purple background.
4.  **Album Thumbnails:** Various 1:1 ratio square images for the queue and "also like" sections.

---

## 6. Interactions & Hover States
*   **Cards:** Should subtly scale up (1.02x) on hover.
*   **Buttons:** "Listen Now" should have a slight drop shadow on hover.
*   **List Items:** Rows in the "Now Playing" list should change background color to `#252525` on hover.
*   **Progress Bar:** The blue playhead should enlarge slightly when the mouse hovers over the seek bar.
