# Design Specification: BBC Earth - Africa Landing Page

## 1. General Layout & Structure
*   **Viewport:** Full-screen (100vw, 100vh), no margins or external padding.
*   **Background:** A composite of a landscape background and a layered foreground (elephants).
*   **Layout Engine:** Use `Flexbox` for the header and footer, and `Relative/Absolute` positioning for the central hero text to allow for the "text-behind-subject" layering effect.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Gill Sans MT` (The standard BBC branding font).
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `Montserrat`, `Trebuchet MS`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Text Styles:**
    *   **Nav Links:** Uppercase, 12px-14px, letter-spacing: 2px, font-weight: 600.
    *   **Sub-headline ("SEVEN WORLDS..."):** Uppercase, 18px-20px, letter-spacing: 8px, font-weight: 700.
    *   **Main Title ("AFRICA"):** Uppercase, 180px-220px (responsive), font-weight: 900, color: #FFFFFF.
    *   **Body Text (Bottom Left):** Sentence case, 12px-14px, line-height: 1.5, max-width: 300px.
    *   **Pagination ("AFRICA AUSTRALIA"):** Uppercase, 14px, font-weight: 700.

## 3. Color Palette
*   **Primary White:** `#FFFFFF` (Text and Logo).
*   **Sky Tones:** Muted blue-grey `#4A5D6A` transitioning to a dark storm-cloud grey `#2C3338`.
*   **Earth Tones:** Burnt orange `#A86B4C`, dried grass gold `#D4A373`, and deep shadow brown `#2B1B12`.
*   **Overlay:** A subtle dark-to-transparent linear gradient from the top (20% opacity) and bottom (40% opacity) to ensure text legibility.

## 4. Component Breakdown

### A. Header (Top Navigation)
*   **Placement:** Fixed at top, 40px padding from top, 60px padding from sides.
*   **Left Group:** 
    *   Hamburger Icon (three horizontal lines).
    *   Links: "ABOUT", "WHERE TO WATCH" (spaced 30px apart).
*   **Center Group:** 
    *   **BBC Earth Logo:** The "BBC" in three separate blocks followed by "earth" in a thinner, lowercase sans-serif font.
*   **Right Group:** 
    *   Social Icons: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube (spaced 20px apart, white, approx 16px size).

### B. Hero Section (The "Sandwich" Layering)
To achieve the exact look, the layers must be stacked in this order (bottom to top):
1.  **Background Image:** The landscape (sky and distant plains).
2.  **Text Layer:** "SEVEN WORLDS ONE PLANET" and "AFRICA".
3.  **Foreground Image:** The elephants (cut out/backgroundless).

**Text Positioning:**
*   Centered horizontally and vertically.
*   "AFRICA" should be positioned so the legs of the elephants partially overlap the bottom of the letters, creating depth.

### C. Footer (Bottom Controls)
*   **Placement:** Fixed at bottom, 60px padding from bottom and sides.
*   **Left:** A small paragraph of descriptive text regarding wildlife gatherings.
*   **Center:** **"WATCH TRAILER" Button.**
    *   Style: Ghost button (transparent background).
    *   Border: 1px solid white.
    *   Padding: 12px 40px.
    *   Text: Uppercase, 12px, letter-spacing: 2px.
*   **Right:** Pagination.
    *   Left arrow (`←`), "AFRICA" (Active state: Bold), "AUSTRALIA" (Inactive state: Lower opacity), Right arrow (`→`).

## 5. Image Descriptions

### Image 1: The Background (Landscape)
*   **Description:** A wide-angle shot of a savanna. The top 60% of the image is a dramatic, heavy sky filled with dark, blue-grey storm clouds. The bottom 40% is a flat, vast plain of dried, golden-orange grass. The horizon line is slightly below the vertical center.
*   **Visual Properties:** High contrast, warm color grading in the grass, cool tones in the sky.

### Image 2: The Elephant Family (Foreground/Cutout)
*   **Description:** A group of four African elephants walking from left to right in a single-file line.
    *   **Elephant 1 (Left):** Large adult, trunk slightly raised, tusks visible.
    *   **Elephant 2 (Middle-Left):** Large adult, head down, walking forward.
    *   **Elephant 3 (Middle-Right):** A small baby elephant, tucked between the two adults.
    *   **Elephant 4 (Right):** A large adult bringing up the rear.
*   **Requirements:** This image must be **backgroundless (transparent PNG)**. 
*   **Placement:** Positioned along the bottom third of the screen. The feet should align with the bottom edge of the "AFRICA" text.
*   **Rotation:** 0 degrees (straight horizontal alignment).

## 6. Interaction Notes
*   **Hover State:** Navigation links and social icons should shift from 100% opacity to 70% opacity.
*   **Button Hover:** The "WATCH TRAILER" button should fill with white, and the text should turn black.
*   **Parallax (Optional):** A slight parallax effect where the background moves slower than the elephant foreground during mouse movement would enhance the 3D effect of the text layering.
