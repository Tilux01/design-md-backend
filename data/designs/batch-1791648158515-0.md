### 1.2 Typography Hierarchy

#### Font Families & Fallbacks
* **Display / Headline Font:**
  * **Primary:** `Space Grotesk` or `Syne` (Weight: 600 SemiBold to 700 Bold)
  * **Fallback Stack:** `'Cabinet Grotesk'`, `'Inter'`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `'Segoe UI'`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`
  * **Characteristics:** Monospaced-influenced grotesque sans-serif, tall x-height, geometric, stark uppercase presence.
* **Body & UI Font:**
  * **Primary:** `Inter` (Weight: 400 Regular, 500 Medium)
  * **Fallback Stack:** `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `'Segoe UI'`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`
  * **Characteristics:** Highly legible grotesque, neutral aperture, optical kerning.

## 2. 3D Visual Asset Specifications

All 3D assets are **backgroundless transparent PNGs / WebP images** with ultra-high-resolution alpha channels, depicting optical glass with chromatic dispersion.

### Asset 1: Hero Translucent Prismatic Cube
* **Visual Description:** A soft-beveled, rounded 3D rectangular cuboid / pill-like prism crafted from hyper-reflective, semi-transparent smoky glass. Internally, it displays luminous caustics and rainbow chromatic aberration (amber, fiery gold, emerald cyan, and magenta). Edges catch intense crisp white specular highlights.
* **Placement & Layering:**
  * **Position:** Hero section, upper-right to center.
  * **Scale:** Width ~`460px – 520px`, Height ~`460px – 520px`.
  * **Rotation:** Axonometric/isometric isometric tilt (~15° counter-clockwise tilt on the Z-axis, ~25° tilt on the X/Y axes).
  * **Layer Depth (`z-index`):** Placed behind the words `"THE SOCIAL"` and `"WEB"` (Text: `z-index: 2`, Asset: `z-index: 1`), allowing the bold white typography to remain legible while creating realistic dimensional overlap.

### Asset 2: About Section Mini Prismatic Capsule
* **Visual Description:** A smaller, pill-shaped or rounded cuboid made of the same obsidian tinted glass with an internal liquid gold/amber refraction and sharp specular rim lighting.
* **Placement & Layering:**
  * **Position:** Far right of the About Section, directly adjacent to the `"3 offices"` statistic block.
  * **Scale:** Width ~`120px – 140px`, Height ~`120px – 140px`.
  * **Rotation:** Angled slightly toward the left viewport edge (~20° pitch).
  * **Format:** Backgroundless transparent PNG.

### Asset 3: E-Commerce Card Prismatic Torus / Hollow Rounded Pebble
* **Visual Description:** A dark chrome/smoked glass organic ring (torus/hollow squircle) with luminous rainbow dispersion along its inner curved rim and deep ambient occlusion in the core cavity.
* **Placement & Layering:**
  * **Position:** Bottom-right quadrant inside Card `04 (E-COMMERCE)`.
  * **Scale:** Width ~`180px – 200px`, Height ~`180px – 200px`.
  * **Overflow:** Contained within or subtly bleeding toward the lower edge of the card container (`overflow: hidden`).
  * **Format:** Backgroundless transparent PNG.
