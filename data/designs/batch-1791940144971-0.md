# Design Specification: "Dreamscape" Music Player UI

## 1. General Theme & Style
*   **Design Language:** Glassmorphism (Frosted Glass).
*   **Background:** Full-screen immersive gradient/image.
*   **Visual Effects:** High background blur (backdrop-filter), subtle white inner borders (strokes), and soft drop shadows to create depth.
*   **Layout:** Vertical stack, centered alignment.

## 2. Color Palette
*   **Primary Background:** A vibrant sunset/twilight gradient.
    *   Top: `#4A4E91` (Deep Purple)
    *   Middle: `#A65D9B` (Magenta/Pink)
    *   Bottom: `#E89C87` (Soft Orange/Peach)
*   **Glass Panels:** White with low opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` to `0.25`).
*   **Text/Icons:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Accent (Heart Icon):** Soft Coral/Red (`#FF5E62`).
*   **Progress Fill:** Solid White (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Progress Track:** Translucent White (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`).

## 3. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Inter** or **SF Pro Display** (San Serif).
*   **Fallbacks:** System-ui, -apple-system, Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Header ("Now Playing"):** Medium weight, 16px, letter-spacing: 0.5px.
    *   **Song Title ("Dreamscape"):** Bold weight, 24px, margin-bottom: 4px.
    *   **Artist Name ("Aurora Lights"):** Regular weight, 16px, opacity: 0.8.
    *   **Time Stamps (1:24 / 3:56):** Regular weight, 12px, opacity: 0.7.

## 4. Layout Breakdown

### A. Background Layer
*   **Description:** A full-screen, high-resolution image of a coastal sunset. The image is heavily blurred (approx. 40px - 60px blur radius) to ensure the UI elements remain legible.
*   **Placement:** `object-fit: cover`, `width: 100vw`, `height: 100vh`.

### B. Main Glass Container
*   **Shape:** Rounded Rectangle (Border-radius: 40px).
*   **Effect:** `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
*   **Border:** 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`.
*   **Padding:** 30px top/bottom, 20px left/right.

### C. Header Section
*   **Left:** Chevron-down icon (v-shape), 20px size.
*   **Center:** "Now Playing" text.
*   **Right:** Horizontal ellipsis icon (three dots), 20px size.

### D. Album Art Component
*   **Image Description:** A square image with 25px rounded corners. It depicts a silhouette of a person with curly hair, seen from behind, sitting on a rocky cliff. They are looking out over a calm sea toward a distant coastline illuminated by city lights under a purple and orange twilight sky.
*   **Placement:** Centered, width approx. 85% of the container.
*   **Interaction Element:** A small circular glass button in the bottom-right corner of the image containing a solid red heart icon.

### E. Song Information
*   **Structure:** Stacked vertically, centered text.
*   **Spacing:** 30px below the album art.

### F. Playback Controls
*   **Progress Bar:** 
    *   A horizontal line with a circular "thumb" (handle).
    *   The thumb is located at the 1:24 mark (approx 35% progress).
    *   Time labels are placed at the far left and right ends immediately below the bar.
*   **Main Buttons:**
    *   **Shuffle:** Far left, thin lines.
    *   **Previous:** Standard skip-back icon.
    *   **Play/Pause:** A large circular glass button (approx 70px diameter) with a white border and two vertical bars (pause icon) in the center.
    *   **Next:** Standard skip-forward icon.
    *   **Repeat:** Far right, circular arrow icon.

### G. Bottom Toolbar (Pill Bar)
*   **Shape:** Capsule/Pill shape, 50px height.
*   **Background:** Darker glass `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
*   **Icons (Left to Right):**
    1.  Playlist (three lines with a musical note).
    2.  Equalizer (three vertical bars of varying heights).
    3.  Waveform (audio frequency visualizer icon).
    4.  Settings (Gear/Cogwheel icon).

### H. Volume Slider
*   **Placement:** Bottom-most element.
*   **Icons:** Low volume speaker (left), High volume speaker (right).
*   **Slider:** Thin white line with a circular thumb at approx 60% volume.

## 5. Image Specifications

1.  **Main Background Image:**
    *   **Content:** Coastal sunset landscape.
    *   **Properties:** Background-less (full bleed), no rotation.
    *   **Treatment:** Gaussian blur applied.

2.  **Album Cover Image:**
    *   **Content:** Person sitting on a cliff overlooking a purple-hued bay.
    *   **Properties:** Square aspect ratio, no rotation, high contrast.
    *   **Placement:** Centered in the upper half of the glass card.

3.  **Icons:**
    *   All icons should be SVG format for crispness.
    *   Stroke weight: 1.5px to 2px.
    *   Color: `#FFFFFF`.

## 6. Technical Implementation Notes
*   **Shadows:** Use `box-shadow: 0 8px 32px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.37)` on the main glass container.
*   **Inner Glow:** Use a subtle `inset` box shadow on the glass container to simulate the edge of the glass catching light.
*   **Responsiveness:** The layout is designed for a 9:19.5 aspect ratio (modern smartphone). Elements should scale proportionally, maintaining the side margins.
