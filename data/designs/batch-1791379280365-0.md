# UI Design & Implementation Specification: Plantac Product Page (Ruby Rubber Tree)

This document serves as the complete technical blueprint for reproducing the provided e-commerce product page interface. It details layout geometry, typography, color systems, component states, and responsive behavior for pixel-perfect implementation.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Specifications

*   **View Type:** Full-Screen Desktop View (1440px standard reference width).
*   **Background:** 
    *   **Upper Section (Wall):** Soft Sage Green (`#B2C9B5` or `rgb(178, 201, 181)`).
    *   **Lower Section (Surface):** Pale Warm Sand/Beige (`#E3D5C6` or `rgb(227, 213, 198)`).
    *   *Note:* The horizon line where the wall meets the surface sits approximately at **75%** of the viewport height.

---

## 2. Color Palette System

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Text / Brand** | Deep Charcoal | `#1A231C` | Logo, Headings, Product Title, Price |
| **Secondary Text** | Muted Slate Green | `#4A574D` | Subtitles, Descriptions, Metadata |
| **Background (Wall)** | Sage Green | `#B2C9B5` | Upper page canvas |

| **Rating Stars** | Dark Olive | `#2F3E32` | Star rating fill |

---

## 3. Typography System

The design employs a sophisticated contrast between an elegant, high-contrast serif font for headings and a clean, highly legible sans-serif for UI elements and body text.

### Headings (Brand & Product Name)
*   **Primary Font Family:** `Playfair Display`, `Cormorant Garamond`, or fallback to generic `serif`.
*   **Weights Used:** Regular (400), Italic.
*   **Implementation Rule:** Use italic styling for botanical sub-names (`Ficus elastica ruby`).

### Body & UI Elements (Navigation, Buttons, Metadata)
*   **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, or fallback to system-ui, `-apple-system`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Weights Used:** Light (300), Regular (400), Medium (500).

---

## 4. Component Breakdown & Spatial Hierarchy

### A. Navigation Header
*   **Position:** Fixed top, padding roughly `32px` horizontal, `24px` vertical.
*   **Left Element:** Brand Logo — `Plantac` (Serif, Bold, ~20px).
*   **Center Navigation Links:** 
    *   Items: `SHOP NOW`, `COLLECTIONS`, `WORKSHOP`
    *   Font: Sans-serif, uppercase, tracking (letter-spacing): `1.5px`, size: `12px`.
*   **Right Elements:** 
    *   Link: `ACCOUNT`
    *   Icon: Minimalist Shopping Bag/Cart outline with counter badge.

### B. Left Column: Product Imagery
*   **Product:** Ruby Rubber Tree in a two-tone ceramic pot resting on a minimalist wire plant stand.
*   **Shadows:** Soft directional drop-shadow falling towards the bottom-right on both the wall and the floor surface to create depth.
*   **Sub-elements:**
    *   `BACK` navigation link with a small left arrow at the top-left of the image zone.
    *   Floating 3D-rotation icon on the bottom-left of the plant container (indicates interactive 360-degree view feature).

### C. Right Column: Product Details
*   **Hierarchy:**
    1.  **Rating:** 5-star rating icon followed by review count (`786`) in small muted text.
    2.  **Product Title:** "Ruby Rubber Tree" (`Playfair Display`, ~42px, Dark Charcoal).
    3.  **Botanical Subtitle:** "Ficus elastica ruby" (`Playfair Display Italic`, ~16px, Muted Green).
    4.  **Price:** `£86` (Large serif display, aligned to the upper right of the text block).
    5.  **Description Paragraph:** 
        *   Text: *"Dramatic darling will be a staple in your home for years to come. The leaves of this plant have a unique, painted effect in coral pink and yellow."*
        *   Font: Sans-serif, 14px, line-height 1.6, color: Secondary Text.

### D. Purchase Configuration Card
A floating semi-opaque/cream card (`#F4EFE6`) housing transaction controls:

1.  **Purchase Options (Radio/Tab Selectors):**
    *   **Option 1:** *One time purchase* — `£86.00` (Active state, bordered in terracotta/dark).
    *   **Option 2:** *Subscribe and save* — `£78.00` (Inactive state).
2.  **Quantity Selector:**
    *   Stepper control: `[-]` `[ 2 ]` `[+]`
3.  **Call to Action (CTA):**
    *   Button: `Add to cart` with price indicator (`£86.00`).
    *   Background: Terracotta (`#D47A5C`), Text: White, Border-radius: Pill-shaped (`9999px`) or slightly rounded (`8px`).
4.  **Trust Badge:**
    *   Text: *"90-Day confirmed money back guaranteed"* with a small checkmark icon below the purchase options.

## 5. Animations & Interactive States (Micro-Interactions)

*   **Hover on Navigation Links:** Subtle color shift from secondary text to primary dark green (`#1A231C`).
*   **Quantity Stepper (`+` / `-`):** 
    *   Cursor changes to pointer.
    *   Background flash on click (`scale(0.95)` active state).
*   **Purchase Option Cards:** 
    *   Smooth border transition when switching between *One time purchase* and *Subscribe and save*.
*   **Add to Cart Button:** 
    *   Hover state: Darken background by 10% (`#BF6A4E`).
    *   Click state: Triggers a slide-down animation or drawer for the shopping cart interface.
