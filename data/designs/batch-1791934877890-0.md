# Design Specification: Michael Carter Portfolio

## 1. Canvas & Layout
* **Viewport:** Full-width desktop responsive (1440px target artboard).
* **Base Background:** Clean pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Container Width:** Max 1200px, centered horizontally with `padding: 0 40px`.

---

## 2. Typography
* **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
* **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.
* **Weights & Hierarchy:**
  * **Hero Headline:** Semi-Bold (600), ~36px, line-height 1.2, letter-spacing -0.02em.
  * **Section Titles:** Medium (500), ~24px.
  * **Body Text:** Regular (400), ~13px–14px, line-height 1.5, color: `#6B7280`.
  * **Pills & Meta:** Medium (500), ~12px.

---

## 3. Color Palette
* **Primary Dark:** `#111111` (Headings, primary buttons, dark testimonial card).
* **Text Muted:** `#6B7280` (Paragraphs, subheaders).
* **Borders/Lines:** `#E5E7EB` (Subtle outlines, light pill borders).
* **Card Neutral:** `#F9FAFB` (Subtle metric/preview cards).
* **Accent Green:** `#10B981` (Availability status dot).
* **Accent Purple:** `#8B5CF6` (Checkmark badge).

---

## 4. Components & Structure

### Header (Navbar)
* **Height:** ~80px, flexbox space-between, vertically aligned.
* **Left:** "Michael Carter" (15px, font-medium, `#111111`).
* **Center:** Navigation list: "Work", "About", "Contact" (14px, muted, 24px gap).
* **Right:** Button "Use For Free" (Pill shape, `#111111` background, white text, 12px font-medium, padding: `8px 18px`).

---

### Hero Section
* **Grid Layout:** 3-part layout featuring a centered backgroundless cutout portrait with flanking text elements.
* **Center Visual:** 
  * Large portrait centered horizontally, top-aligned.
  * Bottom edge has a linear gradient mask fading cleanly to white (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Bottom Left Block:**
  * **Status:** Green status circle (`6px`, `#10B981`) followed by text "2 projects left in April" (12px, `#6B7280`).
  * **Main Title:** "Carter is solving problems through strategic design and compelling visuals" (Max-width: 420px).
* **Bottom Right Block:**
  * **Description:** Right-aligned box containing 3 lines of summary text (13px, `#6B7280`, max-width: 320px).
  * **CTA:** "Email Me" (Black pill button, white text, 12px, padding: `8px 20px`, top margin: 16px).

---

### Featured Works
* **Header Bar:** "Featured works" (24px, left) and "All Works →" (right pill button with subtle grey background and outline).
* **Grid:** 2 columns × 2 rows, gap: 24px.
* **Card Specs:** Aspect ratio ~16:11, border-radius `24px`, relative positioning with overlay content.
* **Overlay Detail (Bottom-left of each card):**
  * Project Title: White, 18px, font-medium.
  * Tag Pills: Translucent blurred background (`rgba(0,0,0,0.3)` or `rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`), border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.3)`, white text, 11px.

---

### Proof & Testimonials Section
* **Grid:** 3 equal columns, gap: 24px, align-items: start.
* **Card 1 (Awards):**
  * Background: `#FFFFFF`, border: `1px solid #E5E7EB`, radius: `20px`, padding: `24px`.
  * Top: Monogram "w." + Gold ribbon icon.
  * Text: "Awwwards Nominee" + short caption.
  * Bottom: Mini UI frame mockup showing a dark-themed site design.
* **Card 2 (Testimonial - Center Elevation):**
  * Background: `#111111`, radius: `20px`, padding: `28px`, deep shadow: `0 20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.2)`.
  * Top: Quote marks icon (`”` stylized, muted).
  * Body: White text, 14px, quote praising Carter's design expertise.
  * Bottom: Circular mini-avatar + "Samantha / Founder at NexaTech" (11px, muted).
* **Card 3 (Metrics):**
  * Background: `#F9FAFB`, radius: `20px`, min-height: 240px, centered content.
  * Badge: Floating white pill with drop shadow: "20+ projects completed" followed by a purple circular checkmark badge.
