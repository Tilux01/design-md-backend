# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Cinematic Dark Mode Glassmorphism / Entertainment Streaming Hub.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-focus central card carousel with deep atmospheric backdrops, dark radial vignettes, and edge-fading horizontal overflow layouts designed for immersive media browsing.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-layered depth using translucent glass cards (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), distinct focus z-index escalation for active cards, subtle ambient red backlighting, and crisp 1px stroked borders on selected items.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient radial red backdrop glow, high-fidelity card scaling for active states, blurred glassmorphism overlays, provider badge branding (HBO, Prime, Netflix), star-rated review tags.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding 4px 10px, font-size 12px, font-weight 600, tracking 0.05em, border-radius 9999px, background rgba(0,0,0,0.5), border 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.15).
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Star Rating (★) -> Use `&#9733;` or inline SVG star icon.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.

# 6. Exhaustive Component-by-Component Architectural Specs

## 1. Ambient Background Container
- **Layout & Positioning**: `absolute inset-0`, `z-0`, `flex items-center justify-center`, overflow hidden.
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Full viewport coverage `100vw` / `100vh`.
- **Borders, Radii & Shadows**: None. Radial gradient background with deep crimson red glow `#450a0a` fading to `#050505`.
- **Typography & Font Stack**: N/A.

## 2. Media Carousel Viewport
- **Layout & Positioning**: `relative z-10`, `flex items-center justify-center`, `gap-6`, `w-full`, `max-w-7xl`, `h-screen`.
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Horizontal padding `32px`, vertical padding `64px`.
- **Borders, Radii & Shadows**: None.
- **Typography & Font Stack**: Inter, sans-serif.

## 3. Inactive Media Card Component (e.g., Inception, Interstellar)
- **Layout & Positioning**: `relative`, `flex flex-col justify-end`, `w-[260px]`, `h-[420px]`, `shrink-0`, scale `0.85`, opacity `0.4`, transition transform/opacity.
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Padding `20px`, gap `8px`.
- **Borders, Radii & Shadows**: Border 1px `rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`, border-radius `20px`, backdrop-filter `blur(12px)`.
- **Typography & Font Stack**: Inter, font-size `14px`, weight `600`, color `#FFFFFF`.

## 4. Active Hero Media Card Component ("The Last of Us")
- **Layout & Positioning**: `relative`, `flex flex-col justify-end`, `w-[360px]`, `h-[540px]`, `shrink-0`, `z-20`, scale `1.0`, opacity `1.0`.
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Inner padding `24px`, gap `12px`.
- **Borders, Radii & Shadows**: Border 1px `rgba(255,255,255,0.3)`, border-radius `24px`, shadow `0 25px 50px rgba(0,0,0,0.9)`, backdrop-filter `blur(20px)`.
- **Detailed Typography & Font Stack**:
  - Provider Badge: 10px, uppercase, tracking `0.05em`, weight 700.
  - Rating Badge: 20px, weight 700, color `#FFFFFF` with gold star `&#9733;`.
  - Title: Font size `28px`, weight `700`, line-height `1.1`, tracking `-0.02em`, color `#FFFFFF`.
  - Metadata (2020-2023 1 Season): Font size `13px`, weight `500`, color `#9CA3AF`.
