# Design Specification: Neumorphic Minimalist Music Player

## 1. Global Styles

### 1.1 Color Palette
*   **Background (Primary):** `#F8F9FB` (A very light, cool-toned off-white).
*   **Surface/Card Color:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for elevated elements).
*   **Text (Primary):** `#1A1A1A` (Near black for headers and titles).
*   **Text (Secondary):** `#9E9E9E` (Medium gray for subtitles, timestamps, and labels).
*   **Accent Color:** `#2D50A0` (Deep blue used for active tabs, play buttons, and progress bars).
*   **Shadows:** 
    *   *Soft Elevation:* `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)` blur: 20px, offset-y: 10px.
    *   *Neumorphic Inner:* `inset 2px 2px 5px rgba(0,0,0,0.05), inset -5px -5px 10px rgba(255,255,255,0.5)`.

### 1.2 Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Poppins` or `Inter`.
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica Neue`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:**
    *   **Bold (700):** Screen titles ("Discover", "Player", "Musics").
    *   **Semi-Bold (600):** Song titles, "Welcome !", section headers.
    *   **Regular (400):** Artist names, search placeholder, timestamps.

### 1.3 Iconography
*   **Style:** Thin-stroke line icons (approx. 1.5px to 2px stroke width).
*   **Library Suggestion:** Feather Icons or Lucide Icons.

## 2. Screen 1: Discover (Home)

### 2.1 Header
*   **Layout:** Flexbox row with `justify-content: space-between`.
*   **Left:** Hamburger menu icon (three horizontal lines).
*   **Center:** "Discover" (Font-size: 18px, Weight: 700).
*   **Right:** Vertical ellipsis (three dots) icon.

### 2.2 Welcome Section
*   **Text:** "Welcome !" (Font-size: 24px, Weight: 600, Margin-top: 30px).

### 2.3 Search Bar
*   **Container:** Height: 50px, Background: `#FFFFFF`, Border-radius: 15px, Box-shadow: Soft Elevation.
*   **Content:** Placeholder "Search music, album..." in Secondary Gray. Search magnifying glass icon on the far right.

### 2.4 Music Trending Section
*   **Header:** "Music Trending" (Left) and "Show more" (Right, Secondary Gray, 12px).
*   **Cards:** Horizontal scrollable row. 
    *   **Card Dimensions:** Width: 160px, Aspect Ratio: 1:1.
    *   **Styling:** 20px border-radius.
    *   **Content Overlay:** A semi-transparent dark gradient at the bottom. 
    *   **Overlay Text:** Song title and Artist in white (10px). A small white play icon button on the right.
    *   **Image 1:** A night sky with a lone pine tree and stars.
    *   **Image 2:** A basketball hoop against a dark blue twilight sky.

### 2.5 Category Tabs
*   **Labels:** "Recently", "Popular", "Similiar".
*   **Active State:** "Recently" is bolded with a small blue dot or underline (implied).

### 2.6 Vertical Song List
*   **Item Layout:** Thumbnail (Left), Text Column (Center), Triple-dot menu (Right).
*   **Thumbnails:** 50px x 50px, Border-radius: 12px.
*   **Image Descriptions:**
    *   *Homura:* A tall lighthouse at dusk.
    *   *Homura (Duplicate):* A winding road through snowy mountains.

### 2.7 Floating Mini-Player
*   **Position:** Fixed at bottom, spanning 90% width.
*   **Styling:** White background, 25px border-radius, heavy soft shadow.
*   **Content:** 
    *   Small circular thumbnail (Image: A person sitting on a beach).
    *   Text: "At my worst - Lisa - kimetsu no yaiba".
    *   Control: Circular blue play button on the far right.

## 3. Screen 2: Player (Now Playing)

### 3.1 Header
*   **Layout:** Back arrow (Left), "Player" (Center), Vertical ellipsis (Right).

### 3.2 Album Art
*   **Container:** Large square with extremely rounded corners (approx 40px radius).
*   **Shadow:** Large, soft drop shadow.
*   **Image Description:** A high-resolution, close-up shot of a pine forest heavily covered in white snow. The lighting is bright, cold, and daylight-balanced. No background/transparency; the image fills the rounded square completely.

### 3.3 Song Info
*   **Title:** "On the ground" (Font-size: 22px, Weight: 700).
*   **Artist:** "Rose - Blackpink first album" (Secondary Gray, 14px).
*   **Interaction:** Heart icon (outline) on the far right of the title.

### 3.4 Progress Bar
*   **Track:** Light gray line.
*   **Progress:** Blue line (`#2D50A0`) with a small circular handle.
*   **Timestamps:** "1:20" (Left) and "3:09" (Right) in Secondary Gray.

### 3.5 Controls
*   **Row 1:** Shuffle icon, Previous icon, Play/Pause toggle, Next icon, Playlist icon.
*   **Play Button:** Large dark blue circle (`#2D50A0`) with white "Pause" bars (two vertical lines).

### 4.1 Header & Tabs
*   **Title:** "Music list" (Large, Bold).
*   **Tab Bar:** "Songs", "Artists", "Album", "Playlist".

### 4.2 Detailed List

    2.  *Wherever you will go:* A person sitting on a sandy beach/dune.
    3.  *Stay:* A wooden boardwalk leading into a foggy landscape.
    4.  *Kil this love:* A splash of blue liquid or ice against a black background.

### 4.3 Bottom Navigation Bar
*   **Container:** White background, top-border radius 30px, shadow.
*   **Icons:** Home, Music (Active), Heart, Notification bell.
*   **Active State:** The "Music" icon is blue with a small blue dot underneath.

## 5. Image Asset Requirements

All images should be **high-resolution** and **object-centered**.

1.  **Snowy Forest (Main Player):** Full-bleed, no transparency, landscape-oriented but cropped to a square.
2.  **Lighthouse (Homura):** Vertical orientation, dark blue/purple sky, centered lighthouse.
3.  **Beach (At my worst):** Warm tones, person sitting, centered.
4.  **Mountain Road (Homura):** Cold tones, asphalt road leading into mountains.
5.  **Teal Forest (Dear God):** Moody, dark, centered light source.
6.  **Red Moon (Homura):** Deep orange/red circle against a dark background.

---

## 6. Implementation Notes
*   **Spacing:** Use a consistent 20px padding for all screen edges.
*   **Transitions:** When moving between Screen 1 and Screen 2, the album art should ideally use a "Hero" transition (expanding from the small thumbnail to the large player square).
*   **Blur:** The background behind the screens in the mockup is a soft gradient with white bokeh circles; for the app itself, keep the background a solid `#F8F9FB`.
