# Design Specification Document (`design.md`)

## 1. Executive Overview & Visual Aesthetic
* **Theme**: Modern Dark Luxury / Editorial Classical Fine Art.
* **Mood**: Stoic, cinematic, high-contrast, prestigious, and minimalist.
* **Canvas Dimensions**: Full-screen desktop landing page (`100vw` × `100vh`, `overflow: hidden`). Device mockups and outer borders are stripped away; the canvas extends seamlessly to all edges.
* **Layering Concept (Depth Stacking)**:
  1. Base Dark Canvas (`z-index: 0`)
  2. Vertical Architectural Grid Lines (`z-index: 1`)
  3. Luminous Glowing Ring / Halo Graphic (`z-index: 2`)
  4. Classical Bust Cutout Asset (`z-index: 3`)
  5. Hero Headline Typography & Excerpt Copy (`z-index: 4`)
  6. Fixed Navigation & UI Overlays (Header, Sidebar Dots) (`z-index: 10`)

---

## 3. Typography & Hierarchy

### 3.1 Font Families & Fallbacks
1. **Primary Editorial Serif (Display Headings & Logo)**:
   * **Target Fonts**: *Cormorant Garamond*, *Ogg*, *Didot*, or *Playfair Display*.
   * **Font Fallback Chain**: `"Cormorant Garamond", "Didot", "Bodoni MT", "Cinzel", "Times New Roman", serif;`
   * **Characteristics**: High contrast between thick and thin strokes, elegant serifs, classical Roman proportions.

2. **Secondary Clean Sans-Serif (Navigation, Body, Indicators)**:
   * **Target Fonts**: *Inter*, *Neue Haas Grotesk*, or *Montserrat*.
   * **Font Fallback Chain**: `"Inter", "Helvetica Neue", "Arial", sans-serif;`
   * **Characteristics**: Neutral, highly legible at micro-sizes, wide letter-spacing when uppercase.

---

### 3.2 Type Scale & Styling

* **Brand Logo ("LOURVE")**:
  * Family: Primary Serif
  * Size: `18px` – `20px`
  * Weight: `500` (Medium)
  * Transform: `uppercase`
  * Letter Spacing: `0.22em` (`tracking-widest`)
  * Color: `#FFFFFF`

* **Top Navigation Links (`HOME`, `COLLECTION`, `ARTISTS`, etc.)**:
  * Family: Secondary Sans-Serif
  * Size: `10px` – `11px`
  * Weight: `500`
  * Transform: `uppercase`
  * Letter Spacing: `0.18em`
  * Color: Inactive `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60)`, Active (`HOME`) `#FFFFFF`

* **Hero Headline ("Museum of Ancient Art")**:
  * Family: Primary Serif
  * Size: `72px` – `88px` (responsive `clamp(3.5rem, 6vw, 5.5rem)`)
  * Weight: `400` (Regular)
  * Line Height: `0.95` – `1.0` (tight editorial line spacing)
  * Letter Spacing: `-0.01em`
  * Text Alignment: Left-aligned, staggered layout:
    * Line 1: `Museum`
    * Line 2: `of Ancient`
    * Line 3: `Art` (slightly indented or aligned beneath the second word)

* **Hero Body Paragraph (Metadata / Blurb)**:
  * Family: Secondary Sans-Serif
  * Size: `10px` – `11px`
  * Weight: `400`
  * Line Height: `1.6`
  * Letter Spacing: `0.02em`
  * Color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.40)`
  * Container Width: `180px` – `210px`

## 4. Background Architectural Grid System
* The layout is segmented by **7 to 8 faint vertical hairline divider lines** spanning `100vh`.
* **Implementation**:
  * A full-screen flex/grid container positioned absolute at `top: 0, left: 0, width: 100%, height: 100%`, `pointer-events: none`.
  * Columns: 6 to 7 evenly distributed columns or aligned to key interface milestones (Left sidebar margin, Nav boundaries, Center boundary, Right margin).
  * Border style: `border-right: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.

## 5. Structural Layout & Component Specifications

### 5.1 Top Navigation Bar
* **Position**: Fixed/Absolute, `top: 0`, `left: 0`, `width: 100%`, `height: 80px`.
* **Padding**: Horizontal `48px` – `64px`.
* **Alignment**: Flex container, `align-items: center`, `justify-content: space-between`.
* **Left Item**:
  * Logo text: `LOURVE`.
* **Center / Nav Items**:
  * Grouped horizontally with `gap: 40px` – `48px`.
  * Links: `HOME`, `COLLECTION`, `ARTISTS`, `EVENTS`, `VISIT`.
  * **Active Item Indicator (`HOME`)**:
    * A horizontal royal blue bar placed directly under the text.
    * Dimensions: Width `18px`, Height `2px`, Margin-top `6px`.
    * Color: `#3D5AFE`.
* **Right Item**:
  * 9-Dot App Menu / Grid Icon:
    * 3 × 3 matrix of miniature circles.
    * Circle diameter: `3px` each.
    * Spacing between dots: `3px` – `4px`.
    * Color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`.

---

### 5.2 Left Vertical Pagination Rail
* **Position**: Absolute, pinned to the left edge at `left: 48px` – `64px`.
* **Vertical Alignment**: Centered vertically (`top: 50%`, `transform: translateY(-50%)`).
* **Components (Stacked Vertically)**:
  1. **Active Bar**: A vertical royal blue line.
     * Width: `2px`
     * Height: `24px` – `28px`
     * Background Color: `#3D5AFE`
     * Margin Bottom: `10px`
  2. **Dot Indicators**: 3 circular dots vertically arranged.

     * Spacing: `8px` gap between each dot.
     * Color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`.

---

### 5.3 Hero Composition (Centerpiece)

The center of the viewport contains an intersecting composition of three primary elements:

#### Element A: Luminous Halo Ring
* **Shape**: Perfect geometric circle with an open/hollow center (border only).
* **Dimensions**: Diameter `400px` – `460px`.
* **Border Stroke**: `8px` – `10px` solid `#FFFFFF`.
* **Glow**: Subtle outer drop shadow / bloom (`filter: drop-shadow(0 0 16px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35))`).
* **Positioning**:
  * Centered relative to the viewport or slightly offset upper-center.
  * Sits *behind* the statue bust so the statue's head and neck naturally break out of the circle's top and foreground.
* **Z-Index**: `2`.

#### Element B: Classical Sculpture Asset
* **Asset Description**:
  * A classical antique Greco-Roman female bust / Aphrodite / Venus de Milo sculpture.
  * Material finish: Dark burnished bronze, patinated dark marble, or dark cast iron.
  * Lighting: Dramatic chiaroscuro / rim-lit from the upper-left, illuminating the edge of the hair, cheekbone, bridge of the nose, and the left shoulder, while the front torso fades into deep shadow.
  * Asset Format: **Backgroundless cutout (transparent PNG or WebP)**.
* **Placement & Transform**:
  * Placed directly over the circular ring.
  * Head and shoulders break the front plane of the halo ring.
  * Bottom of the bust extends down and softly terminates or cuts off towards the lower third of the canvas.
* **Z-Index**: `3`.

#### Element C: Hero Typography ("Museum of Ancient Art")
* **Layout**: Stacked across 3 lines:
  ```text
  Museum
  of Ancient
  Art
  ```
* **Positioning**:
  * Anchored horizontally over the lower-left chest area of the bust and crossing outside the left edge of the halo.
  * The white serif letters directly contrast against the dark body of the statue and the dark canvas.
* **Z-Index**: `4` (Renders *in front* of the sculpture).

#### Element D: Secondary Information / Excerpt Paragraph
* **Text**: Standard typographic excerpt / Latin placeholder text describing the collection or exhibition.
* **Positioning**:
  * Placed to the lower right of the sculpture and headline, aligned to the right side of the word "Art".
  * Starts below the horizontal midpoint of the canvas.
* **Width**: Max width `200px`.
* **Alignment**: Left-aligned, compact, low-contrast text.
* **Z-Index**: `4`.
