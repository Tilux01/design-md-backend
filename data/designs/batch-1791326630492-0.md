# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Modern Dark-Mode Glassmorphism E-commerce & Logistics Dashboard with refined floating widget layers.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High card density with heavy reliance on overlapping cards, floating pills, rounded badges, and subtle internal padding (16px to 24px) creating a multi-layered isometric or flat modular workspace UI.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-tier depth system. Base background layer in dark slate-black (`#0B0E14`), primary container surfaces in translucent dark glass (`#161B22` / `#1F2633`) with soft inner borders (`#2D3748`) and heavy backdrop blurs (`blur(16px)`). Floating accents and status badges utilize high-contrast glow gradients.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges with dot status indicators, multi-stop glowing gradients, dark glassmorphism backdrops, card overlays with drop-shadow elevations.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
