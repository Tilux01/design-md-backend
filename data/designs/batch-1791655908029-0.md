# Design Specification: Biorg Website Clone

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

*   **View:** Full-screen responsive layout (displaying the core webpage sections as shown in the design).
*   **Background Hierarchy:**
    *   Upper section (Hero/Cards): Light off-white/very light gray canvas (`#F4F4F4` or similar neutral).
    *   Lower section (Footer/CTA): Deep pitch black (`#000000`).
*   **Border Radius:** Soft curved layout cards with rounded corners (approx. `24px` to `32px` radius) for the upper split container sections.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

*   **Primary Typeface:** Clean, modern Sans-Serif (Geometric/Grotesk style, e.g., *Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*, or *SF Pro Display*).
*   **Fallback Font Stack:** `sans-serif`, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont.
*   **Weights Used:**
    *   Light / Regular (Body text, metadata, navigational labels)
    *   Medium / Semibold (Subheadings, buttons, links)
    *   Bold / Heavy (Hero headlines, phone numbers, contact strings)

---

## 3. Section-by-Section Breakdown

### A. Upper Content Area (Light Theme)

#### 1. Left Product Card
*   **Container:** Light gray/white rounded card with subtle padding.
*   **Header Micro-Copy (Top Left):**
    *   Text: "Science-Backed Formulas"
    *   Style: Tiny uppercase/sentence-case, muted gray.
*   **Central Image (Product Mockup):**
    *   **Description:** A sleek, matte black, low-profile cylindrical jar/container with a screw-on lid. The label features subtle white branding showing a stylized lowercase logo "b", followed by secondary product text reading "Joint Care" and "Joint Support".
    *   **Background:** Backgroundless (transparent PNG/WebP product cutout).
    *   **Placement:** Centered horizontally and vertically within the left card container. No rotation (0 degrees).

#### 2. Right Action/Promise Card
*   **Container:** Matching light gray/white rounded card layout.
*   **Header Micro-Copy (Top Left):**
    *   Text: "30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee"
    *   Style: Tiny uppercase/sentence-case, muted gray.
*   **Headline:**
    *   Text: "Ready To Elevate Your Daily Routine?"
    *   Style: Bold, large-scale typography ("Elevate Your Daily" emphasized with alternating weights/tones).
*   **Body Copy:**
    *   Text: "From premium ingredients to thoughtfully crafted formulas, every detail is designed to support your long-term health, performance, and well-being."
    *   Style: Regular weight, muted dark gray, constrained max-width.
*   **CTA Button ("Start Your Journey"):**
    *   Style: Pill-shaped, dark border/fill button containing the text "Start Your Journey" paired with a right-pointing arrow icon (`→`) inside a circular wrapper.

---

### B. Lower Content Area (Dark Theme / Pitch Black)

#### 1. "Still Have Questions?" Section
*   **Branding Element:** Small minimalist lowercase "b" logomark centered at the top.
*   **Headline:**
    *   Text: "Still Have Questions? Let's Talk."
    *   Style: Bold white heading, centered alignment.
*   **Body Copy:**
    *   Text: "Our team is here to help you choose the right products, understand our formulas, and get the most out of your wellness journey."
    *   Style: Muted light gray text, center aligned, max-width constrained.
*   **CTA Button ("Contact Our Team"):**
    *   Style: Pill-shaped white button with black text reading "Contact Our Team", featuring a trailing arrow icon inside a circular container on the right edge.

#### 2. Navigation & Sitemap Footer Row
*   **Brand Icon:** Minimalist lowercase "b" logo aligned to the left edge.
*   **Navigation Links (Columns):**
    *   Column 1: `ABOUT US`, `PRODUCTS`
    *   Column 2: `BENEFITS`, `MANUFACTURING`
    *   Column 3: `MOBILE APP`, `REVIEWS`
    *   Column 4: `PRIVACY POLICY`, `TERMS & CONDITIONS`
    *   Style: Small uppercase sans-serif links, muted gray transitioning to white on hover.
*   **Search Input:**
    *   Style: Rounded pill-shaped search input field with placeholder text "Search" aligned to the right side of the navigation row.

#### 3. Large Typography Contact Callout
*   **Phone Number:**
    *   Text: `+1 (800) 555-2847`
    *   Style: Massive scale, bold white typography, center-aligned.
*   **Email Address:**
    *   Text: `hello@biorg.com`
    *   Style: Massive scale, bold white typography, center-aligned, positioned directly beneath the phone number.

#### 4. Background Landscape Image (Footer Base)
*   **Description:** A dramatic, moody photograph of rolling hills or terrain covered in lush, mossy green vegetation/grass under ambient lighting, creating an organic, natural aesthetic texture.
*   **Placement:** Positioned at the absolute bottom of the dark container, spanning full width, serving as the atmospheric footer background beneath the contact details.
*   **Rotation:** 0 degrees (horizontal landscape orientation).

#### 5. Copyright Bar
*   **Text:** `© 2026 BIORG. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED.`
*   **Style:** Tiny centered uppercase text at the very bottom edge overlaid on top of the moss landscape graphic.
