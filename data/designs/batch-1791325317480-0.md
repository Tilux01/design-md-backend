# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Neo-Brutalist Editorial & Technical Laboratory, combining asymmetrical typography, stark contrast blocks, technical metadata callouts, and high-fashion streetwear aesthetics.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Asymmetrical balance with high structural whitespace density, relying on precise alignments, grid borders, and vertical typographic stacking to direct focus.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, hard-edged compartmentalization with solid color blocking (`#e6e6e6` background, `#f15123` high-impact orange brand block, `#111111` dark UI accents) rather than drop-shadow card layers.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Technical coordinate tracking, upright technical annotations, high-contrast monochrome paired with safety orange, vertical running text metadata.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `4px 12px`, font-size: `10px`, font-weight: `500`, tracking: `0.1em`, border-radius: `0px` (Sharp/Industrial) or `9999px`, background: `#111111`, text: `#ffffff`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Arrow (→) -> Use `&#8594;` or SVG arrow.
  - Bullet (●) -> Use `&#9679;`.
