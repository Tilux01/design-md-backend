# Design Specification: Movieto Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles
*   **Background Color:** A deep, dark navy-to-purple radial gradient. 
    *   *Primary Hex:* `#161625` (Dark background)
    *   *Gradient:* Radial gradient starting from the top right (`#1D1D35`) to the bottom left (`#0F0F1A`).
*   **Overall Layout:** Full-screen (100vw, 100vh), overflow hidden to maintain the "app" feel.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Accent:** `#FF007A` (Vibrant Pink) - Used for logo, active links, and primary buttons.
    *   **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
    *   **Text Secondary:** `#B0B0C0` (Muted Grey/Blue).
    *   **Secondary Button:** `#252545` (Dark Navy Blue).

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Montserrat`.
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `sans-serif`, `Arial`.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Hero Title:** 64px, Bold (700 weight), Line-height: 1.1.
    *   **Hero Subtext:** 18px, Regular (400 weight), Line-height: 1.6.
    *   **Nav Links:** 14px, Medium (500 weight).
    *   **Button Text:** 16px, Bold (600 weight).

## 3. Header / Navigation Bar
*   **Position:** Top of the screen, fixed or absolute, full width.
*   **Padding:** 40px (top) 60px (sides).
*   **Logo (Left):** 
    *   Icon: A stylized "M" in a folded ribbon style, color `#FF007A`.
    *   Text: "Movieto" in white, bold sans-serif, placed to the right of the icon.
*   **Nav Links (Center):** 
    *   Items: Home, Movies, Series, Category, Pricing, About.
    *   Active State: "Home" has a pink underline and pink text color.
    *   Spacing: 30px between items.
*   **Utility Icons (Right):**
    *   Search Icon (Magnifying glass).
    *   User Icon (Circle with person silhouette).
    *   Menu Icon (Hamburger - three horizontal lines).
    *   Color: White, 20px size.

## 4. Hero Content (Left-Aligned)
*   **Placement:** Centered vertically, offset to the left side (approx 10% from left edge).
*   **Headline:** "Unlimited movies, TV shows, and more" (White, spans 2-3 lines).
*   **Sub-headline:** "Find the latest and greatest movies and shows all available on Movieto." (Muted grey, max-width 450px).
*   **Call to Action (CTA) Container:** Flexbox, 20px gap.
    *   **Button 1 (Get Started):** 
        *   Background: `#FF007A`.
        *   Shape: Pill-shaped (border-radius: 50px).
        *   Padding: 15px 40px.
    *   **Button 2 (Show Plans):** 
        *   Background: `#252545`.
        *   Shape: Pill-shaped.
        *   Padding: 15px 40px.
        *   Border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1).

## 5. Background Image Grid (The "Poster Wall")
The background consists of several movie posters arranged in a tilted, overlapping grid. These should be placed behind the text using `z-index`.

### General Poster Properties:
*   **Shape:** Rectangular with rounded corners (12px radius).
*   **Rotation:** All posters are rotated exactly **-25 degrees**.
*   **Effect:** Apply a dark overlay or reduce opacity (approx 0.4 to 0.6) to ensure text readability.

### Specific Poster Placements (from top-left to bottom-right):
1.  **Top Left (Partial):** Poster for "Prey for the Devil". Features a close-up of a face with hands over the eyes.
2.  **Top Center:** Poster for "Smile". Features a creepy, wide-grinning mouth.
3.  **Top Right:** Poster for "Mr. Robot". Dark, features a hooded figure.
4.  **Middle Left:** Poster for "Wednesday". Features Wednesday Addams in a dark dress against a window.
5.  **Middle Right:** Poster for "Dune". Features a group of characters (Paul Atreides, etc.) in desert gear.
6.  **Bottom Left (Partial):** Poster for "M3GAN". Features the doll character's face looking forward.
7.  **Bottom Center:** Poster for "John Wick". Features Keanu Reeves in a suit, dark blue lighting.
8.  **Bottom Right:** Poster for "Aquaman". Features Jason Momoa underwater with a trident.

## 6. Visual Effects
*   **Overlay:** A subtle vignette effect (darker edges) to draw focus to the center.
*   **Glassmorphism:** The "Show Plans" button and the navigation bar could benefit from a slight `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)` to feel modern.
*   **Depth:** The posters in the background should have a soft drop-shadow to create layers.

## 7. Implementation Notes
*   **Responsive Design:** On mobile, the poster grid should be simplified or hidden, and the text should center-align.
*   **Asset Handling:** All posters should be high-resolution but compressed. They are backgroundless in the sense that they function as a texture, not as interactive elements.
*   **Hover States:** Nav links and buttons should have a slight scale-up (1.05x) or brightness increase on hover.
