# Design Specification: Multi-Panel Promotional Grid Layout

## 1. Overview & Global Theme
- **Layout Type:** 3x3 Grid Composite (9 distinct promotional sub-designs)
- **Primary Color Palette:** Deep Royal Blue (`#002080`), Electric Blue (`#0055FF`), White (`#FFFFFF`), with dark accent tones (`#111111`).
- **Design Style:** Modern, high-tech, e-commerce promotional banner aesthetic with dynamic typography and floating product shots.
- **Aspect Ratio:** Full screen / Edge-to-edge layout.

---

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Primary Fonts:** Heavy sans-serif geometric typefaces (similar to *Montserrat* or *Helvetica Bold*).
- **Hierarchy:** 
  - Main headers: Extra bold, uppercase, white or deep blue.
  - Subheaders/CTA text: Medium weight, clean readability.
- **Text Effects:** High-contrast text against backgrounds, subtle drop shadows used selectively on major headers for depth.

---

## 4. Execution Guidelines for Replication
1. **Grid Structure:** Create a strict 3x3 uniform grid with minimal or zero gutter spacing for a seamless wall-to-wall aesthetic.
2. **Color Consistency:** Ensure all panels share the exact same palette of royal blue, electric blue, and crisp white to unify the disparate product shots.
3. **Lighting & Shadows:** Apply realistic, soft drop shadows beneath all floating product renders to give them a tangible 3D pop-out effect against the flat blue backgrounds.
