# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Architectural Minimalist / Brutalist Luxury. Clean, high-contrast layouts paired with oversized typography and structural product imagery.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Extremely high whitespace ratio. Ample padding around headings and card sections (`py-16`, `px-8`), allowing typography and imagery to command full visual attention.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, borderless architecture with razor-thin hairline borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0` or `#1E293B`) for grids and card containers. Zero drop-shadows on containers; heavy reliance on pristine typography scale and crisp aspect-ratio media.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, minimalist monochrome icons, asymmetric photo/video overlays, interactive horizontal sliders.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `4px 12px`, Font size: `12px`, Font weight: `500`, Tracking: `0.08em`, Border radius: `9999px`, Background: `#F5F5F5`, Border width: `1px`, Border color: `#E5E5E5`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or custom SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-neutral-900 inline-block"></span>`.
