# Design System Specification: Aurient Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Theme
* **View:** Fullscreen single-viewport hero (`100vw`, `100vh`, `overflow: hidden`).
* **Background Color:** Base `#0D0C0A` (deep warm-tinted dark gray/black).
* **Color Palette:**
  * **Primary Text:** `#FFFFFF`
  * **Secondary / Muted Text:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`
  * **Accents / Button:** `#FFFFFF` (background), `#111111` (text)
  * **Line Accents:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`

---

## 2. Typography
* **Primary Font:** Inter or SF Pro Display
* **Fallback Fonts:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Helvetica Neue`, `sans-serif`
* **Hierarchy:**
  * **Brand Name:** 18px, Medium (500), tracking: `-0.01em`
  * **Nav Links:** 13px, Regular (400), tracking: `0`
  * **Hero Title:** 56px–64px, Semi-Bold (600), line-height: `1.08`, tracking: `-0.03em`
  * **Hero Subtitle:** 15px, Regular (400), line-height: `1.5`, max-width: `420px`
  * **Button Label:** 14px, Medium (500)
  * **Metric Values:** 24px, Semi-Bold (600), tracking: `-0.02em`
  * **Metric Labels:** 11px, Regular (400), color: muted

---

## 3. Background & Imagery
### Image Specification
* **Subject:** Profile view of a man with wavy, dark hair facing right, illuminated by golden-hour sunset light. Deep bokeh light spheres reflect across the foreground and lens.
* **Format:** Photorealistic asset with baked-in dark ambient background on the edges.
* **Placement:** Positioned on the right side (`50%` to `100%` viewport width), vertically centered to cover the right two-thirds.
* **Blend/Fade:** Radial or linear gradient mask applied on the left edge (`mask-image: linear-gradient(to right, transparent 0%, black 40%)`) smoothly merging into the pure `#0D0C0A` left side.

### Vector Graphic Overlay
* **Elements:** Two overlapping thin concentric circular wireframes.
* **Style:** `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
* **Placement:** Centered around the subject's head/forehead, bleeding off the top-right viewport.

---
