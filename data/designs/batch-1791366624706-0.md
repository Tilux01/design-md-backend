# Design Specification: Creamy Ice Cream Landing Page

This document provides the exact design specifications to build a pixel-perfect, full-screen clone of the provided ice cream landing page, omitting any outer presentation frames or background wrappers.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Variables

### Colors
*   **Primary Green (Background Main):** `#428B5E` (approximate flat green of the banner)
*   **Secondary Cream (Bottom Shape):** `#F7E5C3`
*   **Accent Red (Second Ice Cream Cup):** `#E04B4B`
*   **Text Light (Headings & Main Text):** `#FFFFFF`
*   **Text Dark (Footer & Secondary Elements):** `#2C3E35`
*   **Button Dark (Navigation / CTA):** `#1E2B23`
*   **Button Light:** `#FFFFFF`

### Typography
*   **Font Family:** Inter / SF Pro Display (Clean, modern sans-serif)
*   **Logo ("Creamy"):** Bold, serif/script style (similar to Playfair Display or custom branding).
*   **Headings ("Taste Joy in Every Bite"):** Bold Sans-Serif, large scale, extra-bold weight.
*   **Body Text:** Regular weight, 14px, high line-height for readability.

---

## 2. Layout Structure (Full Screen)

The layout uses a viewport-filling container with a split background: top-half green (`#428B5E`), bottom-half cream (`#F7E5C3`) mapped into a curved organic wave shape.

## 3. Component Specifications

### A. Navigation Bar
*   **Position:** Fixed top, aligned with padding (approx. 40px left/right).
*   **Logo:** Text "Creamy" in white, stylized font, top-left.
*   **Nav Links:** Centered pill-shaped container (`#1E2B23` background or dark translucent pill).
    *   Items: `HOME` (active, white pill), `MENU`, `ABOUT`, `CONTACT`.
*   **Icons (Right):**
    *   User profile icon (circle outline).
    *   Shopping bag icon with notification badge (`2`).

### B. Hero Section
*   **Left Content:**
    *   **Headline:** "Taste Joy in Every Bite" (White, `48px`–`56px`, bold).
    *   **Subtext:** "They aren't just about incredible sound, they're designed for your well-being. With immersive audio and noise cancellation." (White, opacity ~80%, `14px`, max-width ~300px).
    *   **Buttons:**
        *   Primary: `ORDER NOW` (Black/Dark pill, white text).
        *   Secondary: `SEE MENU ITEMS` (Outlined pill or lighter contrast).
    *   **Social Proof:**
        *   Overlapping avatars of 3 reviewers.
        *   Text: **10K+ Reviews** (Customers are satisfied).
*   **Right Content / Product Showcase:**
    *   **Size Selectors:** Small toggle pills `[50g]` `[100g]` `[500g]` positioned top-right of the ice cream area.
    *   **Product Display (Carousel Style):**
        *   **Center/Active:** Large "NICK'S" Mint Chokladchip tub (`270 calories`). Highlighted centrally on the cream wave crest.
        *   **Right (Background):** Red tub (Strawberry/Red flavor), slightly scaled down.
        *   **Far Right (Background):** Smaller tub, blurred/scaled down further.
    *   **Carousel Nav:** Left (`←`) and Right (`→`) small circular arrows centered below the main product.

### C. Footer Section
*   **Left:** Copyright text: `© 2025 All rights reserved` (Small, muted white/dark text depending on background contrast).
*   **Right:** Social link / creator tag: `@uiuxzaid` inside a pill button.
