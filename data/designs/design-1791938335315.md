# Design Specification: BBC Earth - Africa Landing Page

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Layout Type:** Full-screen Hero (100vh, 100vw).
*   **Background:** A high-contrast, moody landscape of an African savanna under a heavy, dark cloud-filled sky.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Text:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
    *   **Accent/Active Text:** `#FFFFFF`
    *   **Background Tones:** Earthy browns (`#8B5A2B`), muted oranges (`#D28D5D`), and deep slate blues/grays for the sky.
*   **Overlay:** A subtle dark gradient overlay at the top and bottom to ensure text readability against the image.

---

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font (Headings):** **Gill Sans MT** or **Granby**. 
    *   *Fallback:* 'Trebuchet MS', sans-serif.
*   **Secondary Font (Navigation/Body):** **Helvetica Neue** or **Arial**.
    *   *Fallback:* sans-serif.

### Text Styles:
1.  **Main Title (AFRICA):** 
    *   Font-size: `clamp(5rem, 15vw, 12rem)`
    *   Font-weight: 900 (Black/Heavy)
    *   Letter-spacing: `0.05em`
    *   Text-transform: Uppercase
    *   *Layering Note:* This text is positioned behind the elephant subjects but in front of the background landscape (Z-index layering).
2.  **Sub-heading (SEVEN WORLDS ONE PLANET):**
    *   Font-size: `1.2rem`
    *   Letter-spacing: `0.6em`
    *   Font-weight: 700
    *   Text-transform: Uppercase
3.  **Navigation Links:**
    *   Font-size: `0.75rem`
    *   Font-weight: 600
    *   Letter-spacing: `0.1em`
4.  **Body Text (Bottom Left):**
    *   Font-size: `0.8rem`
    *   Line-height: `1.4`
    *   Max-width: `300px`

---

## 3. Imagery & Assets

### A. Background Image
*   **Description:** A wide-angle shot of a dry African savanna. The ground consists of tall, golden-brown dried grass. The horizon line is roughly at the vertical center of the screen. The sky is filled with dramatic, dark, blue-gray storm clouds.
*   **Placement:** `background-size: cover; background-position: center;`

### B. Foreground Subject (The Elephants)
*   **Description:** A group of four African elephants walking from left to right.
    1.  **Lead Elephant (Left):** Largest, adult, tusks visible, trunk slightly curved forward.
    2.  **Second Elephant (Middle-Left):** Adult, slightly smaller, trunk reaching toward the ground.
    3.  **Baby Elephant (Middle-Right):** Small calf, walking closely behind the second adult.
    4.  **Trailing Elephant (Right):** Adult, walking with a steady gait.
*   **Technical Detail:** These must be high-resolution **transparent PNGs** (backgroundless) or masked layers. They are placed in front of the "AFRICA" text to create a 3D depth effect.
*   **Rotation:** 0 degrees (natural standing position).

---

## 4. Component Breakdown

### Header (Top Navigation)
*   **Left Section:** 
    *   Hamburger Menu Icon (three horizontal white lines).
    *   Links: "ABOUT", "WHERE TO WATCH".
*   **Center Section:** 
    *   **BBC Earth Logo:** The word "BBC" in three white squares followed by "earth" in a lowercase, thin sans-serif font.
*   **Right Section:** 
    *   Social Icons: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube (Small, white, circular or minimalist silhouettes).

### Main Content Area
*   **Vertical Centering:** The "SEVEN WORLDS ONE PLANET" and "AFRICA" text are centered vertically and horizontally.
*   **Layering Order (Bottom to Top):**
    1.  Landscape Background.
    2.  "AFRICA" Text.
    3.  Elephant PNGs.
    4.  Top/Bottom Navigation UI.

### Footer (Bottom Navigation)
*   **Bottom Left:** 
    *   Text: "Home to the greatest wildlife gatherings on Earth, but even in this land of plenty, wildlife faces huge challenges."
*   **Bottom Center:** 
    *   **Button:** "WATCH TRAILER".
    *   Style: Ghost button (Transparent background, 1px white border, white text).
    *   Padding: `12px 40px`.
*   **Bottom Right:** 
    *   **Pagination:** "← AFRICA  AUSTRALIA →".
    *   "AFRICA" is bold/active; "AUSTRALIA" is slightly dimmed (opacity 0.6).

---

## 5. Spacing & Alignment
*   **Container Padding:** `4vw` (Left and Right), `5vh` (Top and Bottom).
*   **Alignment:** 
    *   Navigation items are aligned to the top edge.
    *   Body text and pagination are aligned to the bottom edge.
    *   The "Watch Trailer" button is perfectly centered horizontally at the bottom.
