# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Modern FinTech Dark Mode with Soft Neomorphic / Glassmorphic Highlights and Warm Off-White Content Panels.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High information density balanced by generous boundary paddings and soft card segregation. Clean, structured layout with sharp focal points.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered elevation using a deep carbon textured background (`#0B0B0C`), dark slate sidebar (`#111113`), warm pale-cream content cards (`#F5F4EE`), and high-contrast charcoal cards (`#141416`). Subtle 1px translucent borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) with soft ambient drop-shadows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-contrast dark sidebar with subtle gradient active states, off-white container panels with rounded corners (`border-radius: 24px`), currency dropdown selector pills, rounded avatar stacks with interactive add triggers.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.02em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#FACC15`, text `#141416`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`
