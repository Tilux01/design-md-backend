# Wellina Supplement E-commerce Clone Design System & Specifications

This document outlines the exact design specifications, typography, colors, and layout structure required to create a pixel-perfect, full-screen clone of the provided Wellina e-commerce website.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
*   **Viewport:** Full-screen responsive layout. Designed for desktop viewports ($\ge 1440\text{px}$).
*   **Grid System:** 
    *   Max content width: Standardized container width (~1280px) with fluid margins.
    *   Product Grid: 4-column layout for product cards on the homepage.
*   **Background Colors:**
    *   Body Background: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
    *   Hero Background (Left): Muted Sage/Olive (`#A3B19B` approx.) combined with dark green tones (`#1B3B2B`).
    *   Footer/Deep accents: Dark Forest Green (`#142B20`).

---

## 2. Typography

### Font Families
*   **Primary Serif (Headings):** Playfair Display / Georgia (Fallback) — Used for large editorial headings ("Whole body health...", "Find your supplement", "Science-backed formulations").
*   **Sans-Serif (Body & Navigation):** Inter / Helvetica / system-ui — Used for navigation, product titles, metadata, and CTA buttons.

### Type Scale
*   **Hero Main Heading:** 
    *   Size: `56px` to `64px`
    *   Weight: 400 (Regular/Italicized accents)
    *   Line Height: `1.1`
*   **Section Heading ("Find your supplement"):**
    *   Size: `40px`
    *   Weight: 400 (Serif)
*   **Section Heading ("Science-backed formulations"):**
    *   Size: `36px`
    *   Weight: 400 (Serif)
*   **Product Titles:**
    *   Size: `14px` - `16px`
    *   Weight: 500 (Medium)
*   **Navigation Links:**
    *   Size: `13px` - `14px`
    *   Weight: 400

---

## 3. Color Palette

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code / RGB | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Dark** | Forest Green | `#142B20` | Navigation background, primary buttons, text. |
| **Accent Light** | Soft Sage | `#D8E2DC` / `#E8EDE6` | Product card backgrounds, button fills. |
| **Sale Badge** | Crimson / Rust | `#C84B31` | "Sale" tag on product cards. |
| **Best Seller** | Muted Green | `#6B8E23` / `#4A6B41` | "Best Sellers" badge. |
| **Text Primary** | Deep Charcoal | `#1A1A1A` | Main headers and body text. |
| **Text Muted** | Gray | `#7A7A7A` | Sub-labels, old prices, metadata. |

---

## 4. Component Specifications

### A. Navigation Bar (Header)
*   **Background:** Dark Forest Green (`#142B20`)
*   **Layout:** Flexbox, space-between.
*   **Left Section (Nav Links):** 
    *   Items: `Shop ▾`, `Collections ▾`, `Pages ▾`, `Features ▾`
    *   Color: White (`#FFFFFF`) with lowercase/subtle dropdown carets.
*   **Center Section (Logo):** 
    *   Text / Wordmark: **wellina** in an elegant custom lowercase serif typeface.
*   **Right Section (Action Icons):**
    *   Search icon (`🔍`)
    *   Account icon (`👤`)
    *   Cart icon (`🛒`) with a notification badge (`1` inside a solid circle).

---

### B. Hero Section
*   **Layout:** Split-screen asymmetric design.
    *   Left side: Solid muted olive tone with hand/product macro photography.
    *   Right side: Dark botanical palm leaf photography.
*   **Typography Overlay (Center):**
    *   Sub-header: `FORMULATIONS` (Uppercase, tracked out, small font size).
    *   Main Headline: *Whole body health starts in the gut.* ("in the gut" styled in italic serif).
*   **Call-to-Action Button:**
    *   Pill-shaped button: `Shop Now` with a trailing arrow icon inside a dark circle (`→`).
    *   Background: Light cream/yellowish-green (`#D4E09B`).
    *   Text/Icon color: Dark green (`#142B20`).
*   **Slider Pagination:**
    *   Indicator: `1/3` with a progress line track at the bottom center.
    *   Navigation arrows: Left (`<`) and Right (`>`) controls.

---

### C. Product Section ("Find your supplement")
*   **Section Header:**
    *   Title: *Find your supplement* (Serif, left-aligned).
    *   Filter Tabs (Right-aligned): Pill toggle buttons for `Best Sellers` (Active) and `New Arrivals` (Inactive).
*   **Product Card Design:**
    *   **Background:** Off-white/light gray container (`#F4F4F2`).
    *   **Badges:** 
        *   Sale badge: Red pill (`#C84B31`, white text) positioned top-left.
        *   Best Seller badge: Muted green pill positioned top-left.
    *   **Image Area:** Centered product bottle photography with clean drop shadows.
    *   **Product Details:**
        *   Category/Type: e.g., `Supplement` / `Vitamin` (Small, muted gray font).
        *   Product Name: e.g., `Daily Synbiotic` or `Daily Multivitamin` (Medium weight).
        *   Pricing: Current price bold (`$50.00` or `$200.00`), strikethrough for original price (`$232.00`).
    *   **Action:** Circular black shopping cart button (`🛒`) positioned at the bottom-right of each card.

---

### D. Section: "Science-backed formulations"
*   **Alignment:** Centered layout.
*   **Sub-header:** `FORMULATIONS` (Uppercase, small, tracked).
*   **Heading:** *Science-backed formulations* (Large serif).
*   **Description:** "This simple yet powerful supplement is here to support your long-term health and wellness." (Centered, max-width constrained).
*   **Cards Preview:** Edge-to-edge or rounded card grid showcasing lifestyle/ingredient imagery with label tags (`Proteins`, `Formulations`, `Stronger Cells`).

---

## 5. Interaction & State Guidelines
*   **Hover States:** 
    *   Buttons should slightly darken or scale (`transform: scale(1.02)`).
    *   Product cards should exhibit subtle elevation changes on hover.
*   **Carousel Elements:** Smooth CSS transitions for image sliding and pagination updates.
