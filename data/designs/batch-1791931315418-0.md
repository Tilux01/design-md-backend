# Design Specification: Dream Drone Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Layout Strategy
* **Layout**: Full-screen desktop landing page (100vw, 100vh), zero outer margin.
* **Theme**: Minimalist, dark matte aesthetic with high-contrast white elements.
* **Grid**: Split hero layout—visual asset on the left/center, textual copy and CTA aligned to the right.

---

## 2. Color Palette

| Token | Hex Value | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `bg-primary` | `#25272A` | Base surface background |
| `bg-surface-elevated` | `#1A1B1E` | Card surfaces & overlays |
| `text-primary` | `#FFFFFF` | Headlines, active links, CTA background |
| `text-secondary` | `#8C8E94` | Inactive links, subtitles, icons |
| `cta-text` | `#1E2022` | Button label text |
| `accent-glow` | `#1D2F4E` | Subtle blue interior rim lighting on the drone |

---

## 3. Typography

* **Primary Font**: `Samsung Sharp Sans` or `Poppins`
* **Fallback Stack**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`

### Hierarchy
* **Main Title (`H1`)**: 56px, Semi-Bold (600), `#FFFFFF`, letter-spacing: -0.5px.
* **Subtitle**: 14px, Regular (400), `#8C8E94`, line-height: 1.6.
* **Navigation Links**: 13px, Medium (500). Active: `#FFFFFF`; Inactive: `#8C8E94`.
* **Button Label**: 12px, Semi-Bold (600), `#1E2022`.
* **Video Label**: 12px, Medium (500), `#FFFFFF`.

---

## 4. Header / Navigation
* **Placement**: Fixed or sticky top bar, 40px padding (`top: 40px`, `inline: 60px`).
* **Left Nav Items**: Horizontal list (`gap: 40px`):
  * `Home` (Active, white)
  * `Products` (Muted)
  * `Store` (Muted)
  * `About` (Muted)
* **Right Profile Utility**: Flex row (`gap: 16px`, items-center):
  * Search icon: Magnifying glass glyph (14px, muted).
  * Profile avatar: Circular user icon (14px, muted).
  * Profile name: "Francis Snyder" (13px, muted) + small downward chevron.

---

## 5. Main Content Area

### Right Block: Copy & CTA
* **Position**: Centered vertically on the right half (`right: 10%`, `top: 38%`).
* **Title**: `Dream drone`
* **Description Block**:
  * Text:
    ```text
    Samsung's new drone, fantastic new technology
    let you experience the future
    ```
  * Alignment: Right-aligned text block matching button alignment.
* **CTA Button**:
  * Text: `View Details`
  * Shape: Full pill/capsule (`border-radius: 9999px`).
  * Dimensions: ~130px width, 36px height.
  * Styling: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E2022`, no border.

---

## 6. Visual Assets & Media

### Main Hero Image (Drone)
* **Description**: Matte dark-grey/black quadcopter with integrated circular propeller guards. Features four ducted rotors, "SAMSUNG" embossed on the center top body, and four low-profile landing legs. Subtle blue LED accents along the inner prop duct walls.
* **Format**: Cutout PNG with transparency, retaining a soft ground contact shadow directly beneath the feet.
* **Perspective**: 3/4 isometric perspective, angled towards the bottom-right.
* **Placement**: Absolute, occupying the center-left area (`left: 5%`, `bottom: 12%`, scaling to ~55vw).

### Video Thumbnail Card
* **Position**: Bottom-right corner (`bottom: 40px`, `right: 60px`).
* **Dimensions**: 160px width, 90px height, `border-radius: 8px`.
* **Visual**: Cropped, close-up macro shot of the drone propeller guard.
* **Overlay**: Semi-transparent dark overlay with an inline play button icon (`▶`) and text `Tutorial`.

---

## 7. Bottom Bar & Controls
* **Left**: Stylized minimalist logo mark (`R`) in muted gray (`bottom: 40px`, `left: 60px`).
* **Center**: Single circular power/control icon (circle with a top vertical tick), 16px, muted gray, centered horizontally at `bottom: 40px`.
