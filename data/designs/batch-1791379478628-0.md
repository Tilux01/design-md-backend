# Design Specification: 10 Figma Plugins Post

## 1. Overview & Layout Strategy
* **Aspect Ratio:** Vertical (approx. 9:16 story/reel format).
* **Canvas Size:** Full screen (no internal borders or background framing).
* **Background Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Visual Hierarchy:**
  1. **Top Badge:** "PLUGINS" pill element (top-left).
  2. **Hero 3D Illustration:** Asymmetrical arrangement of stylized 3D shapes mimicking the Figma brand geometry (center).
  3. **Typography Block:** "10 FIGMA PLUGINS" stacked headline (bottom-left).
  4. **Brand Watermark:** Minimalist infinity/logo icon (bottom-right).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
* **Primary Font Family:** Clean, geometric, high-contrast sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *SF Pro Display*, or *Plus Jakarta Sans*).
* **Fallback Fonts:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

### Text Element Breakdown:
1. **Top Badge ("PLUGINS"):**
   * **Font Size:** ~14px - 16px (Bold / 700)
   * **Color:** White (`#FFFFFF`)
   * **Container:** Pill-shaped background badge, Solid Black (`#000000`), generous horizontal padding.

2. **Headline ("10"):**
   * **Font Size:** ~72px - 84px (Black / 900)
   * **Color:** Vibrant Orange (`#E8763C` or `#F05A28`)

3. **Headline ("FIGMA PLUGINS"):**
   * **Font Size:** ~48px - 60px (Black / 900)
   * **Line Height:** Tight (approx. 0.95 - 1.0)
   * **Color:** Pitch Black (`#000000`)
   * **Letter Spacing:** Tightly tracked (negative letter spacing).

---

## 3. Color Palette
* **White (Background):** `#FFFFFF`
* **Pitch Black:** `#000000`
* **Orange (Number / Left Capsule):** `#F05A28` / `#EA6A47`
* **Peach/Light Pink (Right Capsule):** `#FFD1C1`
* **Purple (Left Mid Shape):** `#6B1CDD`
* **Blue (Sphere):** `#1D70EB`
* **Mint Green (Bottom Shape):** `#86F5A3`

---

## 4. Visual Elements & 3D Composition
* **Style:** Claymorphic/Matte 3D render with subtle texturing (soft grain/stipple effect) and soft directional lighting casting gentle shadows.
* **Composition:** Inspired by the Figma geometric shapes (overlapping pill, capsule, and sphere elements angled diagonally downward).
  * **Top Capsule:** Split horizontally into two colors (Orange on left, Peach on right) with a rounded, pill-like silhouette.
  * **Middle Purple Shape:** Half-capsule / dome shape pointing downward-right.
  * **Blue Sphere:** A clean, vibrant blue 3D sphere positioned to the right of the purple shape.
  * **Bottom Green Shape:** Soft mint-green curved form extending from beneath the purple element.

---

## 5. UI/Branding Elements
* **Bottom-Right Icon:** Minimalist rounded square badge containing a stylized infinity/loop icon in solid black (`#000000`) with white negative space.

---
