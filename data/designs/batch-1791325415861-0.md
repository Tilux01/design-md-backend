# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Enterprise Portfolio / Neo-Minimalist SaaS Showcase.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-contrast, spacious multi-layered interface compositions combining dark mode engineering surfaces (Permitify preview) with clean editorial whitespace, split-screen contrast blocks, and typography-first layouts.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered depth architecture utilizing crisp structural outlines (`1px solid`), deep black/slate background canvases, and high-contrast vibrant accent cards (red, blue, orange, and purple hues).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, monochrome structural wireframes, glowing accent status dots, high-contrast monospace technical annotations.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-red-500 inline-block"></span>`.
