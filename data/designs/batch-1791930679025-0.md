# Design Specification: EcuMoto Landing Page

## 1. Color Palette

* **Primary Accent:** `#FF6600` (Vibrant Orange)
* **Background Base:** Radial gradient from `#33373B` (center) to `#181A1B` (edges)
* **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
* **Text Secondary / Inactive:** `#8E9398` (Muted Gray)
* **UI Light Accent:** `#FFFFFF` (Solid White for menu button)
* **Shadow:** `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)` for vehicle floor shadow

---

## 2. Typography

* **Primary Font:** Montserrat, Poppins, or Inter (Geometric Sans-serif)
* **Fallback Fonts:** `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`
* **Hierarchy:**
  * **Brand Name:** 16px, Bold, Uppercase, Tracking +1px
  * **Nav Links:** 12px, Semi-Bold, Uppercase, Tracking +1.5px
  * **Vehicle Specs Title:** 14px, Bold, Capitalized
  * **Vehicle Specs Label:** 10px, Medium, Uppercase, Tracking +2px
  * **Slide Numbers:** 12px, Bold, Tracking +1px
  * **"GO" Button:** 16px, Extra-Bold, Uppercase

---

## 3. Layout Structure

* **Viewport:** Fullscreen (`100vw` × `100vh`), `overflow: hidden`.
* **Atmosphere:** Dark, smoky, desaturated cloud texture centered behind the motorcycle over the dark gray gradient.

## 4. Components & Positioning

### Header Navigation
* **Container:** Pinned top, full width, height `80px`, flexbox layout with horizontal padding (`48px`).
* **Logo (Left):** Minimalist motorcycle helmet icon in white/orange + text `ECU` (White) `MOTO` (Orange).
* **Nav Menu (Center):** 
  * Items: `INICIO` (Orange `#FF6600`), `TIENDA`, `BLOG`, `OFERTAS`, `CONTACTO` (Muted Gray).
  * Spacing: `24px` gap.
* **Utility Actions (Right):**
  * Outline icons: Wishlist (Heart), Search (Magnifier), User Profile, Cart.
  * Spacing: `16px` gap.
* **Hamburger Button (Top-Right Edge):**
  * White square block (`80px` × `80px`) flush to top-right corner.
  * Two thick horizontal black bars centered.

### Left Sidebar (Indicators & Socials)
* **Vertical Slide Numbers:** Pinned `left: 48px`, vertically centered.
  * Values: `001`, `002`, `003` (Orange `#FF6600`), `004`.
  * Vertical gap: `28px`.
* **Social Links (Bottom-Left):** Pinned `left: 48px`, `bottom: 32px`.
  * Horizontal row of Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube icons (`14px` height, muted white/gray).

### Center Stage (Motorcycle Asset)
* **Image Description:** Cutout of a 2019 KTM 390 Duke motorcycle with orange trellis frame, orange wheels, black and white tank fairings with bold orange "DUKE" typography.
* **Asset Format:** Transparent background PNG.
* **Placement:** Horizontally and vertically centered. Facing right, zero tilt/rotation.
* **Ground Shadow:** Soft, elliptical, dark drop shadow directly beneath both tires to anchor the bike to the floor.

### Right Sidebar (Specifications)
* **Container:** Pinned `right: 80px`, vertically centered.
* **List Items:** 4 spec items arranged vertically (`24px` spacing):
  1. **Duke 390** / `MODELO`
  2. **Sport** / `SUBTIPO`
  3. **Negro** / `COLOR`
  4. **2019** / `AÑO`
* **Bullet Indicators:** Each entry is preceded by a glowing orange radio-dot (solid orange center with faint outer orange stroke).

### Action Button ("GO")
* **Placement:** Absolute bottom-right corner (`bottom: 0`, `right: 0`).
* **Dimensions:** `80px` × `80px` square.
* **Style:** Solid orange background (`#FF6600`), centered bold white text "GO". No border-radius.
