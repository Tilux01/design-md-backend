# Comprehensive Design & Implementation Guide: Wix Studio "Nexa Digital Agency" Full-Screen Landing Page

This document provides a pixel-precise, comprehensive specification to clone the modern, futuristic web layout showcased in the Wix Studio promotional video. The design features a high-end dark mode layout styled with deep charcoal/black containers, neo-morphic floating glass panels, and volumetric 3D organic abstract gradients with metallic spherical elements.

---

## 1. Global Specifications & Layout Structure

### 1.1 Viewport & Root
- **Layout Model:** Full-screen viewport (`100vw` width, `100vh` height).
- **Background:** 
  - A blurred, ambient multi-tone gradient mesh containing deep forest green, olive, slate gray, and dark teal tones.
  - Recommended CSS:
    ```css
    body {
      margin: 0;
      padding: 0;
      width: 100vw;
      height: 100vh;
      overflow: hidden;
      background: linear-gradient(135deg, #2b3a32 0%, #17211c 50%, #111413 100%);
      font-family: var(--font-primary, sans-serif);
    }
    ```

### 1.2 Typography System
- **Primary Sans-Serif Font:** `Montserrat`, `Inter`, or system UI sans-serif.
- **Fallbacks:** `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
- **Text Hierarchy:**
  - **Hero Heading ("WE BUILD DIGITAL."):** 
    - Font Weight: `800` (Extra Bold)
    - Case: Uppercase
    - Line-height: `1.05` tight stacking.
  - **Nav Items / UI Labels:** 
    - Font Weight: `400` to `500` (Regular/Medium)
    - Size: Small (`12px` to `14px`)
  - **Body Copy:** 
    - Font Weight: `300` to `400` (Light/Regular)
    - Color: Light muted gray (`#A3B1AC` or `#B0BEC5`).

---

## 2. Layout Grid & Component Architecture

The interface is structured as a centralized asymmetric master container resting on the blurred ambient backdrop, divided into two primary structural panels:
1. **Left Brand Sidebar (Dark Charcoal Block)**
2. **Right Dynamic Hero Canvas (Glassmorphic Emerald/Slate Container)**

---

## 3. Component Breakdown & Precise Placement

### 3.1 Left Brand Sidebar (NEXA Digital Agency)
- **Container Positioning:** Absolute/Flex left section with a solid near-black background (`#0B0E0D`).
- **Width:** Approximately `18%` to `22%` of the inner master container.
- **Elements:**
  1. **Agency Vertical Logo:**
     - Text: "NEXA" in a bold, geometric vertical stack rotated or vertically aligned alongside "Digital Agency" in a smaller weight.
     - Color: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  2. **Follow Us Section:**
     - Label: `FOLLOW US` (Small, spaced uppercase tracking).
     - Subtitle text: *"By reaching out to connect with us, you're taking the first step toward unlocking the full potential of your online presence."*
     - Font size: `11px`, muted light gray (`#8A9A95`).
     - Social Media Icons: Minimalist line art/icons for LinkedIn (`in`), Facebook (`f`), and Instagram (`ig`) aligned horizontally in pure white.
  3. **CTA Button (Bottom Left):**
     - Shape: Pill/Capsule button with a black fill and fine white border.
     - Text: `Let's Transform Your Digital Presence` with a right-pointing arrow icon enclosed in a solid light-green circular badge (`#A3E635`).

---

### 3.2 Right Dynamic Hero Canvas
- **Container Positioning:** Fills the remaining right portion of the inner frame.
- **Background Styling:** Dark translucent dark-green glassmorphism with subtle inner borders and multi-layered gradient depth.

#### A. Top Navigation Bar
- **Position:** Absolute top right inside the main canvas container.
- **Items:**
  - **Pill Nav Tabs:** Centered or left-aligned pill-shaped items (`Home`, `Services`, `Projects`, `About Us`). 
    - Active tab (`Home`): Solid pill background (`#A3E635` light green) with dark text (`#000000`).
    - Inactive tabs: Transparent background with white text (`#FFFFFF`).
  - **Contact Us Button:** Outlined pill button positioned at the far right top (`Contact Us`).

#### B. Hero Typography & Copy
- **Main Header:** 
  - `WE BUILD DIGITAL.`
  - Color: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`), bold typography.
- **Sub-description Paragraph:** 
  - *"From web design and development to digital marketing strategies, we craft tailored solutions to drive your online success."*
  - Positioned directly below the main heading.

#### C. Floating Interactive / Badge Elements
- **"Learn More" Badge:**
  - A circular rotating badge graphic containing the text `"LEARN MORE LEARN MORE"` curved along the perimeter with a diagonal arrow icon pointing towards the center.

#### D. Bottom-Right Feature Box ("WHAT WE DO?")
- **Heading:** `WHAT WE DO?` (Small bold accent text).
- **Body Text:** *"We specialize in creating dynamic digital experiences tailored to your brand's needs"*
- **Team/Collaborator Avatars & Action:**
  - Overlapping circular portrait photos of three team members.
  - Text link: `Connect with our experts` accompanied by a diagonal arrow icon (`↗`).

---

## 4. Image Assets & Visual Assets Guide

> **Note on Assets:** All specified images are backgroundless/transparent cutouts or floating rendered elements composited into the scene.

### 4.1 Central 3D Volumetric Object (Hero Centerpiece)
- **Description:** A futuristic, high-end 3D abstract rendering. It features a glossy, reflective gold-chrome metallic sphere intersected by a fluid, organic Möbius strip-like ribbon flowing in deep forest green and iridescent olive-green tones.
- **Placement:** Centered prominently between the headline text area and the right-hand feature area, appearing to float in 3D space with soft drop shadows cast onto the background gradient.
- **Rotation/Angle:** Angled at approximately 45 degrees, catching a primary light source from the top-left, creating a bright specular highlight on the upper curve of the gold sphere.

### 4.2 Team Member Avatars (Bottom Right)
- **Description:** Three consecutive, overlapping circular portrait headshots of professional multi-cultural team members smiling toward the camera, styled with professional studio lighting against neutral backgrounds (cropped clean as circles with subtle white border strokes).
- **Placement:** Positioned immediately left of the "Connect with our experts" text link at the bottom-right of the hero canvas.
