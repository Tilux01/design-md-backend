# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Luxury, Dark-Mode Cinematic Showcase, High-End Art Portfolio & Studio Landing.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Generous framing with balanced asymmetric typography, cinematic photography integration, and intentional structural breathing room.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Deep dark immersive background layers (`#151515` to `#111111`) paired with subtle glassmorphic overlays, warm gold/amber glowing accents (`#D4A373`), and crisp high-contrast serif typography.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Cinematic lighting photography, asymmetric serif typography juxtaposition, high-tracking uppercase micro-labels, warm amber accent highlights, floating directional scroll pills.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `11px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Down Arrow (`↓`) -> Use `&#8595;` or inline SVG.
  - Bullet Point (`•`) -> Use `&#8226;`.
