# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Streetwear E-Commerce, High-Contrast Minimalist Luxury, Dark Mode Fashion Catalog.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio, asymmetric section separators, clean grid layouts, highly legible typograhic hierarchy emphasizing imagery and bold headings.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat structural sections separated by subtle high-contrast borders (`#262626`), minimal elevation via subtle inner highlights and clean grid partitioning.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, heavy editorial typography, split dark/neutral contrast sections, border-framed product cards.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: 4px 12px, font-size: 11px, font-weight: 700, tracking: 0.15em, border-radius: 9999px, background: #ffffff, text: #000000.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
