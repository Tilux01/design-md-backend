# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode FinTech SaaS, Industrial Neo-Metallic, High-End Enterprise Minimalist.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace with asymmetric composition, balancing massive typography on the left against a complex 3D industrial geometric sculpture on the right.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered obsidian gradients with specular metallic highlights, reflective brushed planes, and subtle radial purple/blue ambient illumination.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-contrast dark obsidian canvas, specular metallic lighting ramps, pill-shaped glass navigation bars, glowing orange primary action buttons.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Chevron Down (⌄) -> Use SVG path `M19 9l-7 7-7-7`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
