# Design System & Implementation Guide

This document outlines the exact design specifications for a full-screen, dark-themed eyewear product landing page.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Foundations

### Color Palette
*   **Background (Dark Neutral):** `#161618` (Deep charcoal, nearly black)
*   **Primary Accent (Orange/Gold):** `#F39C12` (Vibrant amber/gold gradient focal points)
*   **Text Primary (Light/White):** `#FFFFFF`
*   **Text Muted (Subtle Gray):** `#888888`
*   **Line/Border Color:** `#2C2C2E` (Subtle dark gray dividers)

### Typography
*   **Font Family:** Modern Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Roboto*, or *Helvetica Neue*)
*   **Headings / Display Text:**
    *   `G L A S S E S R E...`: Extra-light or light weight, uppercase, wide letter spacing (`letter-spacing: 0.15em`).
    *   Colors split: White for `GLASSES`, Orange (`#F39C12`) for `RE`.
*   **Body & UI Elements:**
    *   **New collection 2021:** 10px, uppercase, white.
    *   **Marcus Radland / Title:** 12px / 9px, white and muted gray.

---

## 2. Layout & Structure (Full Screen)

The layout uses a fluid grid centered across a responsive viewport with a dark radial/linear gradient background (`#1A1A1C` to `#111113`).

### Header (Navigation Bar)
*   **Position:** Absolute / Fixed top, spanning full width with padding (`40px` horizontal, `30px` vertical).
*   **Left Section:**
    *   Brand Logo: Custom rounded-rectangle badge in `#F39C12` featuring a bold white "G".
*   **Center Section:**
    *   *Empty (minimalist whitespace).*
*   **Right Section:**
    *   Hamburger Menu Icon (3 horizontal lines, white).
    *   Search Icon (Magnifying glass, white).

---

## 3. Component Specifications

### A. Hero Text Block
*   **Subtitle:** "New collection 2021" positioned above the central hero text.
*   **Main Hero Typography:**
    *   Content: `G L A S S E S R E`
    *   Styling: Large display font, layered centrally behind/over the product imagery.

### B. Product Imagery (Eyewear)
*   **Visual Asset:** High-contrast, studio-lit photo of sunglasses with gradient amber lenses (matching the `#F39C12` accent).
*   **Placement:** Prominently angled from the center-left extending across the middle ground.

### C. Carousel / Pagination Indicators
*   **Left Indicator:** `01` in white, paired with a vertical dividing line extending downwards.
*   **Right Indicator:** `125` indicating total pages/slides, aligned with horizontal slider rails.

### D. Designer / Team Credit (Bottom Right)
*   **Card Element:**
    *   Portrait photo of **Marcus Radland** (Head of Design).
    *   Text labels:
        *   "Marcus Radland" (White, medium weight).
        *   "Head of Design" (Muted gray, small).
    *   Signature graphic overlay.
    *   Background container anchored by an organic curved accent shape in `#F39C12`.

---
