# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise Financial SaaS Dashboard, modern minimalist data-dense UI.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High information density balanced by clean white cards, generous internal padding (`16px` to `24px`), and light grey background canvas.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean flat cards with subtle 1px border outlines (`#E2E8F0`), zero heavy drop shadows, relying on clear layout separation and subtle background contrast differences.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges with trend arrows, clean metric summary cards, donut charts with central text legends, miniature bar charts with gradient fills.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `2px 8px`, font-size `11px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-600`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Trend Arrow Up -> Use `&#9650;` or SVG icon component.
  - Sparkle / Waving Hand (👋) -> Use `&#128075;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
