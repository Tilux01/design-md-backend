# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Outdoor Minimalist / Neo-Brutalist E-Commerce.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-contrast, spacious layout with massive editorial typography offset against a clean, off-white background and stark monochromatic contrast elements.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, stark surface architecture with crisp black typography, accented by high-saturation amber CTA buttons and heavy industrial typography overlaps.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, overlapping 3D product compositions, editorial monochromatic photography with amber interactive accents, minimalist navigational geometry.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`, border width `1px` border-color `#F59E0B`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
