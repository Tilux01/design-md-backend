### 1.2 Typography System

* **Primary Heading Font**: `Syne`, `Clash Display`, or `Cabinet Grotesk`
  * *Fallback Stack*: `'Helvetica Neue'`, Arial, sans-serif
  * *Characteristics*: Wide geometric styling, light-to-medium letterforms, expanded uppercase display.
* **Body & UI Font**: `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, or `Geist Sans`
  * *Fallback Stack*: system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
  * *Characteristics*: High legibility, neutral geometric grotesque.

#### Type Scale Reference Table

| Role | Font Family | Size | Weight | Line Height | Tracking | Text Transform |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
