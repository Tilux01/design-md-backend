# UI Design Specification & Replication Blueprint

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport
- **Screen Presentation:** Fullscreen implementation (ignoring browser chrome and the surrounding vignette background shown in the preview).
- **Page Canvas Background:** Solid off-white/cream (`#F8F9F5` or `#FAFAFA`).
- **Grid Structure:** 
  - Centered max-width container (approx. 1200px – 1280px) with fluid horizontal padding (`clamp(24px, 5vw, 80px)`).
  - Asymmetric two-column main hero split: Left column for branding, typography, and CTAs (approx. 50-55% width); Right column for the primary featured product display (approx. 45-50% width).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
*Note: Since specific commercial web fonts may not be directly accessible via standard `@import`, fallback stacks are provided for exact cross-platform rendering.*

### Primary Heading Font (Display / Bold)
- **Visual Style:** Ultra-bold, uppercase, geometric sans-serif with slight humanist proportions (similar to *Anton*, *Bebas Neue*, or *Helvetica Neue Black Condensed*).
- **Used For:** Main hero headline ("SHOES SPEAK LOUDER THAN WORDS"), section kickers ("FEEL THE EXPERIENCE"), and navigation pills.
- **Font Family Stack:** 
  ```css
  font-family: 'Anton', 'Impact', 'Arial Black', sans-serif;
  ```
- **Line Height:** Tight (`1.05` to `1.1`).
- **Letter Spacing:** Normal to slightly tight (`-0.02em`).

### Body Font
- **Visual Style:** Clean, highly readable, neutral geometric sans-serif (*Inter*, *Roboto*, or *SF Pro Text*).
- **Used For:** Paragraph description ("Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet..."), navigation links, and small meta text.
- **Font Family Stack:** 
  ```css
  font-family: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;
  ```
- **Line Height:** Relaxed (`1.5` to `1.6`).

## 4. Component Breakdown

### A. Top Utility Bar
- **Background:** Solid Lime (`#C2F941`).
- **Height:** Slim bar (~32px to 36px).
- **Content:** Centered text links separated by vertical pipes (`|`):
  - `Find store` | `Help` | `Join us` | `Join us`
- **Typography:** Inter, Medium, `12px`, uppercase, dark charcoal text color.

### B. Header / Navigation Bar
- **Background:** Transparent / matching page background (`#FAFAFA`).
- **Layout:** Flexbox row with space-between alignment.
- **Left Element:** Nike Swoosh Logo (Classic black vector, height approx. 20px–24px).
- **Center Element:** Pill-shaped navigation menu container.
  - Background: White or subtle off-white border container with rounded pills.
  - Links: `Dynamic`, `Woman`, `Bestsellers`, `Our story`, `About us`.
- **Right Elements:** 
  - Hamburger menu icon (three horizontal lines).
  - **Create Account Button:** Pill-shaped button with lime border or light background hover state (`#C2F941`), dark bold text.

### C. Hero Section (Left Column)
- **Kicker / Subtitle:**
  - Text: `FEEL THE EXPERIENCE`
  - Typography: Anton / Bold Sans-serif, small size (`14px`), colored in muted lime-olive or dark charcoal with tracking.
- **Main Heading:**
  - Text: `SHOES SPEAK LOUDER THAN WORDS`
  - Typography: Anton, uppercase, massive scale (`clamp(40px, 5vw, 68px)`).
- **Body Copy:**
  - Text: `Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit. Ut elit tellus, luctus nec ullamcorper mattis, pulvinar dapibus leo.`
  - Typography: Inter, Regular, `15px`, color `#666666`, max-width ~420px.
- **Primary CTA Button ("Shop now"):**
  - Shape: Rounded capsule / pill button.
  - Background: Solid Lime (`#C2F941`).
  - Text: `Shop now` + Arrow/Circle icon.
  - Hover Effect: Slight scale up (`scale(1.03)`) or shadow enhancement.
- **Decorative Arrow / Indicator:**
  - A hand-drawn or clean vector thin curved arrow pointing downward from the CTA button toward the secondary elements.

### D. Hero Section (Right Column / Featured Product)
- **Product Display:**
  - Large high-resolution product photography of a running shoe (Blue/Black upper with electric lime accents and white swoosh).
  - Angle: Dynamic 45-degree floating isometric angle.
  - Shadow: Soft, diffused drop-shadow underneath to simulate floating elevation (`box-shadow: 0 30px 60px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)`).

### E. Bottom Product Thumbnail Carousel / Grid
- **Placement:** Bottom right quadrant, beneath the hero image.
- **Cards:** 3 distinct product variant cards.
  - Background: Light pastel-lime container backgrounds.
  - Content: Smaller product shots of sneakers in different colorways (Blue/Green, Black/White, Neon Yellow/Black).
  - Active State: One card is visibly selected/active with a glowing lime border or highlight treatment.

---

## 5. Animations & Interactive Behaviors

1. **Page Load / Entrance Animation:**
   - **Hero Text:** Fades up with a subtle translateY offset (`opacity: 0; transform: translateY(20px)` transitioning to `1` and `0` over `0.8s` cubic-bezier easing).
   - **Featured Shoe:** Slides in from the right with a soft spring physics easing, combined with a gentle floating CSS keyframe animation (`floating` loop up and down by 8px every 4 seconds).
2. **Hover States:**
   - **Navigation Items:** Smooth color transition to bold black or underline glow.
   - **Product Thumbnails:** Scaling up image slightly (`scale(1.05)`) on hover with smooth transition (`transition: transform 0.3s ease`).
   - **CTAs:** Background brightness shift on hover.
