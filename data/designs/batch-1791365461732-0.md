# Design Specification: Aloo Design Agency Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Reset

### Colors
*   **Background (Dark Navy):** `#1a202c` (Deep dark slate/blue)
*   **Text & Primary Logo (White):** `#ffffff`
*   **Accent Color (Orange/Coral):** `#ff5722` (Used for the vertical line, top border accent)
*   **Muted Text / Secondary (Light Gray/Blue):** `#8d99ae` (Used for navigation links, subtext, social links)

### Typography
*   **Font Family:** Inter, Roboto, or system sans-serif.
*   **Logo/Headline ("aloo"):** Extra bold, lowercase, sans-serif (`font-weight: 800`).
*   **Body & Navigation:** Medium weight, sans-serif (`font-weight: 500`).

---

## 2. Layout & Structure

*   **View:** Full-screen (`100vw` width, `100vh` height), responsive design.
*   **Container:** Centered dark navy card/screen layout with subtle depth shadow.

---

## 3. Component Breakdown

### Header / Navigation Bar
*   **Top Border Accent:** A thin horizontal line spanning the top width, transitioning from orange on the left to transparent/dark on the right.
*   **Top Left:** 
    *   Hamburger menu icon + "MENU" text in uppercase white.
*   **Top Center:** 
    *   Brand/Creator name: "Marketmold" in uppercase white.
*   **Top Right:** 
    *   "PLAY VIDEO" text link.

### Hero Section (Center)
*   **Main Title ("aloo"):** 
    *   Large, bold white lettering centered horizontally and vertically.
*   **Accent Line:** 
    *   A striking vertical orange line extending downward from the center of the "a" and "l" characters.
*   **Tagline:** 
    *   Positioned directly to the right of "aloo": `| design agency` in white/light gray.
*   **Watermark Background:** 
    *   A subtle, large-scale watermark of the word "aloo" duplicated in the background with extremely low opacity (`rgba(255,255,255,0.03)`).

### Left Sidebar / Vertical Text
*   Rotated text on the far left: `"FUTURE OF DESIGN"` written vertically, reading downwards in uppercase, muted gray.

### Bottom Left
*   Language switchers: **`EN`** (active, white) and **`SPN`** (inactive, muted).

### Bottom Right
*   Social media links: **Facebook**, **Twitter**, **Google+** in muted white/gray.

### Right Floating Elements
*   Vertical pagination/navigation indicators (Up arrow, vertical line, Down arrow) aligned vertically on the middle-right edge.
