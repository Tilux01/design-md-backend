# Design Specification: "Watch How Rolex Became Iconic"

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Setup
* **Aspect Ratio:** Vertical format (Designed for mobile/Stories/Reels - 9:16 aspect ratio).
* **Full Screen Mode:** The design spans $100\%$ width and $100\%$ height with zero padding/margins on the container.
* **Background:** 
  * **Color:** Off-white / light gray solid fill (`#F4F4F4` or `#EFEFEF`) with a very subtle vertical gradient to simulate studio lighting (slightly brighter in the center, soft vignette at the edges).

---

## 2. Typography & Font Stack
The design relies on a striking contrast between an elegant, editorial serif italic font and a bold, authoritative sans-serif or serif capital font.

### Font 1: Elegant Script / Italic Serif (for "watch how" and "became one of")
* **Primary Font:** *Playfair Display Italic*, *Baskerville Italic*, or *Didot Italic*.
* **Fallback Stack:** `Georgia, Cambria, "Times New Roman", serif`.
* **Style:** Italic, sentence case.
* **Color:** Dark charcoal (`#222222` or `#1A1A1A`).

### Font 2: Bold Serif/Sans-Serif Capitals (for "ROLEX" and "THE MOST ICONIC")
* **Primary Font:** *Cinzel Bold*, *Trajan Pro*, or *Montserrat Black*.
* **Fallback Stack:** `Impact, "Arial Black", sans-serif`.
* **Style:** Uppercase, wide tracking/letter-spacing.
* **Color:** Rolex Signature Green (`#006039` or `#004B2B`).

---

## 3. Visual Elements & Assets

### A. The Rolex Coronet (Crown Logos)
* **Position 1 (Top Left):** Angled at approximately $-30^\circ$, cropped off the top-left edge of the screen. Soft drop shadow giving a 3D cut-out/embossed paper effect.
* **Position 2 (Bottom Right):** Angled similarly, cropped off the bottom-right edge of the screen, mirroring the top element.
* **Color:** Solid neutral dark gray (`#707070` to `#888888`) with high-blur drop shadows to create depth.

### B. The Diagonal Design Sweep
* **Shape:** A sweeping, translucent green diagonal ribbon/stripe passing from the bottom-left through the center to the top-right.
* **Color:** Soft, muted Rolex green with transparency (`rgba(0, 96, 57, 0.25)`).
* **Layering:** Sits *behind* the central watch and text, but *above* the background canvas.

### C. The Central Product (Rolex Cosmograph Daytona)
* **Model Featured:** Yellow Gold Rolex Daytona with a deep emerald green dial and gold sub-dials.
* **Placement:** Exact dead-center of the composition ($X: 50\%, Y: 50\%$).
* **Angle:** Slightly tilted matching the diagonal flow.
* **Shadows:** Deep, realistic soft drop shadow beneath the watch to make it pop off the 2D background (floating effect).

---

## 4. Exact Layout & Hierarchy (Top to Bottom)

1. **Top-Left:** Cropped 3D Grey Crown logo.
2. **Upper-Middle Text:**
   * Line 1: `"watch how"` (Font 1, Italic, small-to-medium size, centered or slightly left-aligned).
   * Line 2: `"ROLEX"` (Font 2, Bold Uppercase, large size, Rolex Green).
3. **Center:** The Yellow Gold/Green Dial Rolex Daytona watch.
4. **Lower-Middle Text:**
   * Line 3: `"became one of"` (Font 1, Italic, small size).
   * Line 4: `"THE MOST ICONIC"` (Font 2, Bold Uppercase, large size, tightly tracked, Rolex Green).
5. **Bottom-Right:** Cropped 3D Grey Crown logo.

---

## 5. Animation & Motion Design (If implemented as a video/reel)

* **Initial State (0.0s):** The background fades in with a subtle zoom. The diagonal green stripe wipes across the screen from bottom-left to top-right.
* **The Watch (0.5s - 1.5s):** The gold watch scales up from 80% to 100% with a smooth spring physics ease-in-out, settling with a gentle float animation (sine wave bobbing up and down by 5-10 pixels).
* **Typography Animation (1.0s - 2.0s):** 
  * `"watch how"` fades up with a slight upward drift.
  * `"ROLEX"` letter-spacing expands slightly as it fades in.
  * `"became one of"` and `"THE MOST ICONIC"` fade in sequentially from bottom to top.
* **Ambient Lighting:** A subtle light sheen passes diagonally across the gold watch glass and casing every 3 seconds to simulate a live product studio shoot.
