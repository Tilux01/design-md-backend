# Design Specification: Piano "Digital Revenue Optimization" Landing Screen

This document provides the complete, pixel-accurate design specifications to reproduce the full-screen Piano landing page hero interface.

---

## 1. Canvas & Viewport Setup

* **Display Mode**: Full-bleed edge-to-edge viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`, `overflow: hidden`).
* **Base Background**: Atmospheric multi-stop radial gradient creating a soft, moody, cinematic vignette:
  * **Center Glow**: Neutral lilac-taupe (`#8D8896`) positioned slightly off-center (`50% 48%`).
  * **Mid Tone**: Muted dusty slate violet (`#75707F`).
  * **Outer Edges / Corners**: Deep smoky charcoal-mauve (`#56525E` blending into `#4A4652` at the periphery).
* **Atmospheric Noise**: Subtle 2–3% monochromatic grain overlay across the entire background to eliminate gradient banding and match the particulate texture of the central visual.

## 5. Central 3D Visual Asset (Particle Sphere)

### Visual Asset Description
* **Nature**: Completely **backgroundless (alpha transparent PNG/WebP)** floating 3D particulate celestial sphere.
* **Visual Characteristics**:
  * An intricate point-cloud / particulate volume consisting of hundreds of thousands of microscopic sand-like grains.
  * **Structure**: A porous, spherical celestial body with organic fissures, swirls, and planetary chasms running through its mantle, revealing hollows inside.
  * **Color / Lighting**:
    * Warm, radiant interior glow in tones of champagne, light peach, and soft rose gold (`#F4DACB` to `#E3BEA6`).
    * Peripheral particles scatter cool ambient daylight with silvery-lavender and pale ash tones (`#C2BDC9` to `#EBE7ED`).
    * Shadows in the deep crevices drop into muted violet-gray tones, blending seamlessly into the background gradient.
  * **Edge Falloff**: Delicate, fading particle dispersion at the outer perimeter; no hard vector edge.

### Asset Placement & Dimensions
* **Position**: Centered within the viewport (`position: absolute; top: 48%; left: 50%; transform: translate(-50%, -50%);`).
* **Scale**: Occupies approximately `56vw` to `60vw` in width and `65vh` to `70vh` in height, maintaining a 1:1 aspect ratio.
* **Z-Index**: Positioned behind the foreground typography and action buttons (`z-index: 10`).
