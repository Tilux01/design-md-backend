# Design Specification: Supercell Games Landing Page

## 1. General Layout & Canvas
- **Background:** Solid off-white/very light gray (`#F9F9F9`).
- **Dimensions:** Full-screen responsive width.
- **Padding:** 40px global horizontal padding for the header and footer elements.
- **Alignment:** Flexbox-based layout with a vertical stack (Header, Main Content, Footer).

## 2. Typography
- **Primary Font:** `Supercell-Magic` (or a custom bold, rounded sans-serif).
- **Fallback Fonts:** `Arial Rounded MT Bold`, `Helvetica`, `sans-serif`.
- **Text Colors:**
    - Primary Headings/Titles: Black (`#000000`).
    - Subtext/Developer Name: Dark Gray (`#666666`).
    - Genre Highlight: Bright Yellow (`#FFEB3B`).
    - Search Placeholder: Light Gray (`#A0A0A0`).

## 3. Header Section
- **Logo (Left):** The "SUPERCELL" logo. Blocky, stacked text, black.
- **Navigation (Left):** "Games" text. Font size: 24px, Weight: Bold.
- **Search Bar (Center):** 
    - **Shape:** Pill-shaped (fully rounded corners).
    - **Background:** Light gray (`#EEEEEE`).
    - **Border:** 1px solid `#E0E0E0`.
    - **Contents:** A small magnifying glass icon on the left, followed by placeholder text "Search games..".
- **Menu (Right):** 
    - Text "Menu" in 18px bold.
    - **Icon:** A 2x2 grid of four small squares (bento menu icon) placed to the right of the text.

## 4. Main Content: Game Cards
The cards are arranged in a horizontal flex row with `overflow-x: hidden` (or scrollable). Each card consists of a background container and a character image that breaks the top boundary.

### Card Base Styles
- **Shape:** Vertical rectangle with highly rounded corners (approx. 40px radius).
- **Shadow:** Soft drop shadow (`rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`) offset downwards.
- **Dimensions:** Approx 280px width x 400px height.
- **Text Alignment:** Bottom-left inside the card, padded by 25px.

### Card 1: Brawl Stars
- **Base Color:** Vibrant Red (`#E53935`).
- **Character Image:** 
    - **Description:** Nita (girl in a bear hat) riding on top of Bruce (a large red bear). The bear has its paws spread wide.
    - **Background:** Transparent (PNG).
    - **Placement:** Centered horizontally. The bear's lower body is inside the card, but the head and Nita overlap the top edge of the card significantly.
- **Text:** 
    - Title: "Brawl Stars" (White, Bold, 22px).
    - Subline: "Supercell Action" ("Action" is highlighted in Yellow).

### Card 2: Clash Royale
- **Base Color:** Royal Blue (`#1E88E5`).
- **Character Image:** 
    - **Description:** The Prince character riding a small pony. He holds a blue-and-white striped lance vertically.
    - **Background:** Transparent (PNG).
    - **Placement:** The pony is inside the card; the Prince's torso and the lance extend above the card boundary.
- **Text:** 
    - Title: "Clash Royale" (White, Bold, 22px).
    - Subline: "Supercell Strategy" ("Strategy" is highlighted in Yellow).

### Card 3: Clash of Clans

- **Character Image:**

    - **Background:** Transparent (PNG).
    - **Placement:** The Barbarian's legs are inside; his torso, sword, and the flying debris overlap the top and sides of the card.

    - Title: "Clash of Clans" (White, Bold, 22px).
    - Subline: "Supercell Strategy" ("Strategy" is highlighted in Yellow).

### Card 4: Boom Beach (Partial)

## 5. Footer Section
- **Social Links (Left):** 
    - "Facebook" and "Youtube" in plain black text, 16px. Spaced about 30px apart.
- **Pagination (Right):**
    - **Prev:** Left chevron `<` followed by "Prev".
    - **Next:** "Next" followed by right chevron `>`.
    - **Style:** Bold, black text, 18px.

## 6. Technical Implementation Details
- **Z-Index:** Character images must have a higher `z-index` than the card base to create the "pop-out" 3D effect.
- **Image Assets:** All character images must be high-resolution PNGs with no background.
- **Hover Effect (Recommended):** Cards should scale slightly (`scale(1.05)`) and the character image should move upward by 10px when hovered to enhance the 3D depth.
- **Spacing:** Use `justify-content: space-between` for the header and footer. Use `gap: 20px` for the card container.
