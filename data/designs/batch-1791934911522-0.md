# Design Specification: Hizamo Portfolio

---

## 1. Color Palette

* **Primary Background:** Warm off-white / Alabaster (`#F7F3EE`)
* **Primary Text:** Dark Charcoal (`#121212`)
* **Secondary Text:** Medium Muted Grey (`#606060`)
* **Accent / CTA:** Burnt Rust Orange (`#DE4B26`)
* **Badge / Accents Line:** Light Terracotta (`#D47A5B`)
* **Card Thumbnail Backgrounds:** Neutral dark studio tones (`#1E1E1E` to `#2A2A2A`)

---

## 2. Typography

* **Headings & Logo:** *Plus Jakarta Sans*, *Cabinet Grotesk*, or *Inter* (Weight: 700 / Bold).
  * *Fallback:* `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
* **Body & Navigation:** *Plus Jakarta Sans* or *Inter* (Weights: 400 Regular, 500 Medium).
  * *Fallback:* `Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif`

---

## 3. Global Layout & Structure

* **Page Container:** Full-width fluid container with horizontal padding (`px-12` to `px-20` / max-width `1440px`).
* **Grid:** 12-column layout base with flexible flexbox containers.

---

## 4. Header & Navigation

* **Left:** Brand logo text `"Hizamo"` (Font size: ~24px, Bold, `#121212`).
* **Center:** Navigation items horizontally centered:
  * `"Work"` (Active: Black text with a thick bottom border in `#DE4B26`).
  * `"About"` (Muted grey/black hover).
  * `"Contact"` (Muted grey/black hover).
* **Right:** Availability status:
  * Solid circular orange indicator (`#DE4B26`, ~8px diameter).
  * Text: `"Available for work"` (14px, Medium).

---

## 5. Hero Section

* **Layout:** Two-column split layout (55% content, 45% visual).

### Left Column
* **Headline:** `"Designer & \nCreative Developer"` (H1, ~64px–72px, bold, tight line-height `1.05`, `#121212`).
* **Subtext:** `"I craft digital experiences that are intuitive, engaging, and built to make an impact."` (~18px, `#606060`, max-width 400px, margin-top 24px).
* **CTA Button:**
  * Background: Solid Orange (`#DE4B26`).
  * Text: White (`#FFFFFF`), `"View My Work →"`.
  * Style: Rounded corners (`border-radius: 8px`), padding `12px 24px`.

### Right Column (Hero Visuals)
* **Background Shapes:**
  * A tall, soft-beige vertical oval mask/blob set behind the model.
  * A thin, large circular stroke outline in terracotta orange arcing across the background.
* **Model Image:**
  * **Type:** Backgroundless cutout (transparent PNG).
  * **Subject:** Young man with short dark beard, wire-rimmed round eyeglasses, black knit beanie, and a plain black t-shirt.
  * **Pose:** Front-facing, head slightly turned to the left, looking just off-camera.
  * **Placement:** Bottom-aligned with the hero section, layered over the oval shape.
* **Badge / Stamp:**
  * Circular badge located at the lower-right of the hero graphic.
  * Thin black border circle with circular uppercase text: `"• DESIGNING • DIGITAL EXPERIENCES"`.
  * Center icon: Black four-point sparkle/star.

---

## 6. Featured Work Section

### Section Header
* **Left:** Category tag `"FEATURED WORK"` (12px, tracking-widest, uppercase, `#606060`).
* **Right:** Link `"View All Projects →"` (14px, dark gray/black with orange arrow).

### Project Cards Grid
* **Grid:** 4-column layout (`grid-cols-4`, gap ~24px).
* **Card Components:**
  1. **Thumbnail:**
     * Aspect ratio roughly 16:11, rounded corners (`border-radius: 12px`).
     * Real-world studio mockups placed on textured stone/slate bases.
     * **Card 1:** Dark laptop mockup displaying *"Building brands that stand out."*
     * **Card 2:** Dark smartphone mockup displaying *"Wellness made simple."*
     * **Card 3:** Angled laptop mockup showing liquid metallic artwork and *"Performance drives results."*
     * **Card 4:** Vertical smartphone mockup displaying a mountain valley and *"Discover your next adventure."*
  2. **Metadata & Title (Below Thumbnail):**
     * Category label: 12px, Regular, `#606060` (e.g., "Brand Identity", "Web Design").
     * Project Name: 16px, Bold, `#121212` (e.g., "Northline Studio", "Mindful App").
     * Arrow: Small orange arrow (`→`, `#DE4B26`) bottom-right aligned in each card info block.
