# Puffer Jacket Landing Page Design Specification

This document provides the exact design specifications for cloning the provided orange-themed e-commerce landing page for **Jacket Masters**.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Layout

### 1.1 Color Palette
*   **Primary Background (Gradient):**
    *   Top/Center: Vibrant Warm Orange (`#E8691A` to `#F57C00`)
    *   Bottom/Corners (Vignette effect): Dark Burnt Orange / Brown (`#8B3A0F` to `#3D1702`)
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   Primary Headings & Key Prices: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
    *   Body Text & Subtitles: Soft Off-White (`#E0D8D0` with ~80% opacity)
    *   Strikethrough Price: Muted Orange/Gray (`#A08070`)
*   **UI Elements / Buttons:**
    *   Pill Navigation Background: Translucent Dark Brown (`rgba(40, 15, 5, 0.4)` with Backdrop Blur)
    *   Size Selector Circles:
        *   Default: Translucent Dark (`rgba(0,0,0,0.3)`) with white text.
        *   Active: Solid White (`#FFFFFF`) with dark text (`#2A1005`).

### 1.2 Typography
*   **Font Family:** Modern Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Montserrat*, or *Helvetica Neue*).
*   **Headings:** Bold / Extra Bold.
*   **Body:** Regular / Light.

---

## 2. Header & Navigation

### 2.1 Top Bar Layout
*   **Logo (Left):**
    *   Icon: Small square badge with "JM" monogram.
    *   Text: "JACKET MASTERS" in uppercase, tracked (letter-spaced), white font.
*   **Floating Navigation Pill (Center-Top):**
    *   Rounded pill container with blur effect.
    *   Items:
        1.  `PUFFER JACKET` (Active state indicated by solid background pill).
        2.  `ALL PRODUCTS`
        3.  `ABOUT US`
        4.  `CONTACT`
*   **Action Icons (Top Right):**
    *   Shopping Cart Icon (with badge/count indicator).
    *   Wishlist / Heart Icon.

---

## 3. Hero Section

### 3.1 Left Column (Content)
*   **Carousel Indicators:** Two small circular arrow buttons (`<` `>`) stacked horizontally above the headline.
*   **Main Headline:**
    *   Text: *"Stand out Without trying"*
    *   Style: Large, bold, multi-line title in pure white.
*   **Body Paragraph:**
    *   Text: *"It’s not just about staying warm. It’s about stepping outside and instantly feeling confident, comfortable, and completely yourself. Designed to elevate even the simplest outfit, this jacket wraps you in lightweight warmth."*
    *   Style: Medium-small font size, muted off-white, comfortable line-height.
*   **Call to Action Button:**
    *   Text: `"Get the look" >`
    *   Style: Solid white pill button with dark text and a right arrow icon.
*   **Social Links (Bottom Left):**
    *   Horizontal row of minimal icons: Instagram (`📷`), Facebook (`f`), Globe (`🌐`), Behance (`Be`).

### 3.2 Center Column (Featured Product Display)
*   **Main Product Image:**
    *   High-end 3D render/photo of the **Glossy Orange Puffer Jacket**.
    *   Features realistic reflections, high-definition stitching detail, and a soft shadow underneath grounded on the surface.
*   **Slogan Below Product:**
    *   Text: *"Confidence, wrapped in warmth"*
    *   Style: Italicized/subtle centered typography underneath the jacket shadow.

### 3.3 Right Column (Product Details & Purchasing)
*   **Pricing:**
    *   Current Price: `$149` (Large, bold white text).
    *   Original Price: `$199` (Smaller text, positioned below/beside with a horizontal strikethrough).
*   **Size Selector:**
    *   Label: `Choose your size:` (Small muted text).
    *   Options (Circular buttons): `36`, `38`, `40`.
*   **Thumbnail Preview (Bottom Right):**
    *   A small preview card/thumbnail of the dark/black variant of the jacket hovering near the bottom right corner.

---
