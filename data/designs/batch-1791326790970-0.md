# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: High-Tech Cyberpunk / Dark Mode Enterprise Tech.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Spacious dark-canvas presentation with deep obsidian backing, accented by vibrant cyan luminescent elements and atmospheric deep-space radial fog.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered obsidian panels featuring subtle 1px border glows (`rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.2)`), soft ambient cyan floor reflection, and deep atmospheric drop shadows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Luminescent cyan geometric grid blocks, ambient light bleeding, hyper-tracked uppercase typography, floor bounce lighting.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `4px 12px`, font-size: `0.75rem`, font-weight: `650`, tracking: `0.1em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.1)`, border: `1px solid rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;`
