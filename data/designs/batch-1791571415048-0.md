## 1. Global Styles & Design System

### 1.1 Typography
*   **Primary Font Family:** Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif
*   **Fallback Font Stack:** `sans-serif`
*   **Font Weights Used:** 
    *   Regular (400) - Body copy, descriptions, secondary navigation
    *   Medium (500) - Navigation labels, button text, card headers, pricing titles
    *   Semi-Bold (600) - Section headings, primary call-to-actions, pricing amounts

*   **Neutrals:**
    *   Primary Background: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
