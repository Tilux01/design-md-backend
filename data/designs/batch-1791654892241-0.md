# Design Specification: "Today" Screen Clone

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Settings
* **Target Viewport:** Full-screen mobile layout (fluid width, bounded to standard smartphone aspect ratios e.g., 390px × 844px / iPhone 14 dimensions).
* **Orientation:** Portrait.
* **Background Color:** Soft, very light lavender tint (`#F4F2FC` to `#F8F6FF`), featuring a very subtle radial gradient or flat pastel wash.
* **Overall Corner Radius (if rendered as a card container, otherwise full bleed):** `40px` outer phone shell margin, though the UI components fill the viewport edge-to-edge horizontally with safe-area padding.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
* **Primary Font Family:** **SF Pro Rounded** (iOS system font) or **Quicksand** / **Nunito** as open-source web-safe equivalents.
* **Fallback Font Stack:** `'Quicksand', 'Nunito', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

### Text Hierarchy:
1. **Header Title ("Today"):**
   * Font Size: `28px` – `32px`
   * Weight: Bold (`700`)
   * Color: Deep indigo/charcoal (`#2D2354`)
2. **Status/Subtitle Text ("Thriving (75%)"):**
   * Font Size: `14px`
   * Weight: Medium (`500`)
   * Color: Muted purple-grey (`#6E6390`)
3. **Card Titles / Metrics ("15 / 54 kcal", "5 habits"):**
   * Font Size: `16px` – `18px`
   * Weight: Semi-bold (`600`) / Bold (`700`)
   * Color: Dark slate (`#1F1A38`)

---

## 3. UI Components Breakdown

### A. Top Navigation Bar
* **Title:** "Today" aligned to the top-left with generous padding (`20px` from top status bar).
* **Action Icon (Top Right):** 
   * A pill-shaped or rounded-square icon button with a soft purple border/background containing a vertical settings/slider icon (`#E4E0F5` background, dark purple icon).

### B. Horizontal Date/Status Strip (Week Bar)
* Located directly below the main header.
* **Layout:** A horizontal scroll or flex row of 7 circular/pill-shaped status containers representing days of the week.
* **Item States:**
   * **Active/Selected Day (Middle items):** Filled with solid muted purple (`#7C6EE6`), featuring white text/icons and a subtle upward elevation.
   * **Inactive Days (Outer items):** Outlined pill shapes with light grey/purple borders and faint icons.
   * **Indicators:** Small circular dot indicators sit below each pill to denote task completion status (green/purple ticks or dots).

### C. Central Mascot Illustration Area
* **Placement:** Centered horizontally in the middle tier of the screen, sitting against a soft backdrop shape.
* **Backdrop Element:** An abstract, soft-edged pastel purple/lavender cloud/blob (`#E0D9F7`) floating behind the character, dotted with tiny white sparkles/stars.
* **Character Image Asset:**
   * **Description:** A cute, minimalist purple creature/otter with a white face patch, a black button nose/eye, and small stubby limbs. It is posed in a sitting position, angled slightly to the left, with its tail extended to the right. 
   * **Background:** Backgroundless (transparent PNG/SVG).
   * **Rotation/Placement:** Perfectly upright (`0°` rotation), centered directly above the "Thriving (75%)" label.
* **Status Label:** Directly below the illustration reads *"Thriving (75%)"* centered in medium purple weight.

### D. Bottom Metrics & Habits Cards (2-Column Grid)
* **Layout:** A responsive 2-column grid with a `12px` gap, padded horizontally (`16px` from screen edges).
* **Card Style:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or very pale translucent cards with smooth rounded corners (`cornerRadius: 24px`), soft drop shadows (`box-shadow: 0px 8px 24px rgba(124, 110, 230, 0.08)`).

#### Left Card (Calorie / Energy Tracker):
* **Top Header:** Small icon (flame/sparkle) + text label ("- kcal" or similar).
* **Main Metric:** **"15 / 54"** in large bold typography, followed by smaller unit text ("kcal").
* **Visual Aid:** A subtle linear progress bar or indicator line running across the bottom of the card.

#### Right Card (Habits Tracker):
* **Top Header:** Text label **"5 habits"** with an arrow or action indicator on the top right.
* **Secondary Row:** Text label **"To Do"** with a numeric count below (**"05:00"** or completion fraction), accompanied by a small clock or checklist icon.
