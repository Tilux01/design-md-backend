This document provides a comprehensive design specification for a high-fidelity music application. The design focuses on a sophisticated dark-mode aesthetic with high-contrast yellow accents and a unique vertical navigation paradigm.

---

# Design Specification: "VibeMusic" UI

## 1. General Theme & Color Palette
The application utilizes a "Neumorphic-Lite" style, combining soft shadows, rounded corners, and a high-contrast dark-to-light transition.

*   **Primary Background (Dark):** `#1E1F24` (Deep Charcoal/Dark Grey)
*   **Secondary Background (Light):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) - used for the lower content section on the Home screen.
*   **Accent Color (Yellow):** `#FFD700` to `#FFC107` (Bright Gold/Yellow).
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   Primary: `#FFFFFF` (White)
    *   Secondary: `#9A9A9A` (Medium Grey)
    *   Accent: `#FFD700` (Yellow)
    *   Dark Text (on white): `#000000` (Black)
*   **Shadows:** Soft drop shadows with a blur radius of 15-20px, using a 15% opacity black.

---

## 2. Typography
The design uses a clean, geometric Sans-Serif font family.

*   **Primary Font:** **Montserrat** or **Product Sans**.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Headers:** Bold, 24pt (e.g., "Music").
    *   **Vertical Labels:** Semi-Bold, 12pt, Uppercase, Letter spacing: 1.5px.
    *   **Song Titles (Large):** Bold, 22pt.
    *   **Song Titles (List):** Semi-Bold, 16pt.
    *   **Artist Names:** Regular, 12pt.
    *   **Lyrics:** Regular, 14pt, Line height: 1.6.

---

## 3. Screen 1: Discovery / Home Screen

### Layout Structure
*   **Full Screen:** 100vh / 100vw.
*   **Top Header:** Contains the word "Music" (Left) and a pill-shaped search bar (Right).
    *   *Search Bar:* Darker grey than background, rounded edges, magnifying glass icon on the right.

### Vertical Navigation Sidebar (Left)
*   **Placement:** Fixed to the left side, spanning from the top header to the white section.
*   **Content:** Vertical text rotated -90 degrees.
    *   "Recommend" (Active - Yellow indicator line next to it).
    *   "Album" (Inactive - Grey).
    *   "Style" (Inactive - Grey).

### Featured Card (Center Top)
*   **Image Description:** A rectangular card with heavily rounded corners (approx 30px). The background is a vibrant yellow. The image features a portrait of a young man (Andy Grammer) looking downward, obscured by a soft, white "smoke" or "cloud" effect at the bottom.
*   **Overlay Text:** "Don't Give Up" (White, Bold) and "Andy Grammer" (White, Small).
*   **Icon:** A small white play button icon inside a semi-transparent white circle, located at the bottom left of the yellow card.

### Collection Section (Bottom White Area)
*   **Shape:** A white container with a large top-left "cut-out" or rounded corner that tucks under the dark sidebar.
*   **Header:** Vertical text "Collection" rotated -90 degrees on the left edge.
*   **List Items:**
    1.  **Lost My Way:** Square image (rounded corners) showing a person from behind in a gallery. Text: "Lost My Way" (Black) / "David" (Grey).
    2.  **A Werewolf Boy:** Square image (rounded corners) showing a yellow wall with a bicycle. Text: "A Werewolf Boy" (Black) / "Carole" (Grey).

### Bottom Navigation Bar
*   **Icons:** 
    *   Left: Music/Home icon (Yellow).
    *   Center: Floating Action Button (FAB) - Yellow circle with a white play icon.
    *   Right: Profile/User icon (Grey).

## 4. Screen 2: Now Playing Screen

### Top Navigation
*   **Icons:** Back arrow (Left), Lyrics/Chat icon, Playlist icon, and Share icon (Right). All icons are thin-line white strokes.

### The Vinyl Player (Center)
*   **Image Description:** A large, realistic black vinyl record. In the center is a circular cropped version of the yellow Andy Grammer album art.
*   **Rotation:** The entire vinyl assembly should have a CSS rotation animation (slow, clockwise).
*   **Placement:** Centered horizontally, slightly towards the top half.

### Progress Bar (Right Side)
*   **Orientation:** Vertical.
*   **Visual:** A thin grey line with a white "thumb" or indicator.
*   **Timestamp:** "3:56" written vertically at the top of the bar.

### Song Info & Main Controls
*   **Title:** "Don't Give Up" (White, Large).
*   **Artist:** "- Andy Grammer -" (Grey, centered).
*   **Play Button:** A large yellow glowing circle with a white play triangle, positioned to the right of the song title.

### Lyrics Section (Bottom Center)
*   **Text:** 
    *   "I will fight I will fight for you" (White, High Opacity).
    *   "I always do until my heart is black and blue" (Grey, Lower Opacity).
    *   "And I will stay I will stay with you" (Grey, Faded/Gradient out).

### Bottom Playback Bar
*   **Icons:** Repeat icon, Skip Back, Skip Forward, Heart icon.
*   **Style:** Thin white line icons, evenly spaced across the bottom.

---

## 5. Image Specifications

1.  **Andy Grammer Portrait (Main):**
    *   **Source:** High-resolution portrait.
    *   **Treatment:** Yellow duotone filter.
    *   **Transparency:** The "smoke" at the bottom of the image should be a PNG with a transparent alpha channel to blend into the yellow background of the card.
    *   **Rotation:** 0 degrees.

2.  **Vinyl Record:**
    *   **Source:** Backgroundless PNG of a black vinyl record with grooves.
    *   **Center Hole:** Transparent.
    *   **Album Art Inlay:** Circular crop of the Andy Grammer portrait, placed exactly in the center of the vinyl.

3.  **List Thumbnails:**
    *   **Lost My Way:** 1:1 Aspect ratio, 15px border-radius.
    *   **A Werewolf Boy:** 1:1 Aspect ratio, 15px border-radius. Backgroundless yellow wall image.
