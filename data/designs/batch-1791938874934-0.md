# Design Specification: Netflix UI (House of Cards Edition)

## 1. Global Styles
*   **Background Color:** `#000000` (Pure Black).
*   **Primary Text Color:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
*   **Secondary Text Color:** `#B3B3B3` (Light Grey).
*   **Accent Color:** `#E50914` (Netflix Red).
*   **Font Family:** 
    *   **Primary:** `Netflix Sans` (Custom).
    *   **Fallback:** `Helvetica Neue`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Layout:** Full-screen, edge-to-edge (no margins on the main container).

---

## 2. Hero Section (Top 60% of Screen)

### Background Image
*   **Description:** A high-resolution cinematic shot of Frank Underwood (Kevin Spacey) and Claire Underwood (Robin Wright). 
*   **Composition:** Frank is centered-left, leaning against a dark wood door frame, arms crossed, wearing a dark suit, white shirt, and dark tie. Claire is to the right, slightly behind him, looking toward the left with a stern expression, wearing a dark dress.
*   **Lighting:** Dim, moody, warm interior lighting.
*   **Placement:** The image is right-aligned. The left side of the image fades into a solid black gradient to ensure text readability.

### Hero Metadata (Top Left Overlay)
*   **Netflix Branding:** "ORIGINAL NETFLIX" in all caps. Font size: Small. Color: Red (`#E50914`).
*   **Main Logo:** "HOUSE [US Flag Icon] of CARDS". 
    *   "HOUSE" and "CARDS" are in a bold, condensed sans-serif. 
    *   The US Flag icon replaces the "O" in "OF" or sits between the words. The flag is upside down (a show-specific detail).
*   **Rating Stars:** Five stars. The first four are red, the fifth is half-red/half-grey.
*   **Metadata Row:** 
    *   Year: "2014"
    *   Age Rating: "18" (contained in a small grey outlined box).
    *   Duration: "2 temporadas"
    *   Quality Badges: "HD" and "5.1" (each in a small rounded grey box).
*   **Release Info:** "Temporada 3 - 27 de fevereiro" in bold white text.
*   **Synopsis:** A paragraph of grey text (`#B3B3B3`). Text: "É verdade que o poder corrompe? O congressista Frank Underwood está para descobrir."

*   **Netflix Logo (Bottom Right):** The standard Netflix wordmark in red (`#E50914`). It is placed in the bottom right corner, acting as a watermark for the interface.

## 5. Detailed Image Descriptions (For Assets)

1.  **Hero Image (Frank/Claire):** Backgroundless on the left side (blends to black). Frank is looking directly at the camera. Claire is in profile/three-quarters view. No rotation.
2.  **House of Cards Thumbnail:** Frank Underwood sitting in a white marble chair, hands on armrests. Background is black. No rotation.
3.  **Arrested Development Thumbnail:** The Bluth family gathered in a tight group. Warm, yellow/orange lighting. No rotation.
4.  **Friends Thumbnail:** The cast standing together. The word "F·R·I·E·N·D·S" is written in its iconic font across the top in white. No rotation.
5.  **Mad Men Thumbnail:** Don Draper in a dark suit, silhouette style, against a white background with red accents. No rotation.
6.  **House MD Thumbnail:** Extreme close-up of Gregory House. The background is pure white, making the character pop. No rotation.

---

## 6. Interaction States (Selection)
*   **Focus State:** When a thumbnail is selected (e.g., the House of Cards thumbnail in the first row), it must have a **2px solid white border** and no rounded corners.
*   **Unfocused State:** Thumbnails have no border and a slight dimming effect (approx 80% brightness) compared to the focused item.
