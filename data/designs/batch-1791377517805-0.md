# Visual Design Specification & Implementation Blueprint

## 1. Global Layout & Typography
### Viewport & Canvas
- **Aspect Ratio / Framing:** Full-screen edge-to-edge layout (`100vw`, `100vh`). No outer borders or decorative containers.
- **Root Background:** Soft, plush beige/taupe wall color (`#dcdcdc` to `#cfcbc5` ambient gradient, base hex: `#d2cec9`).
- **Flooring Plane:** Muted warm-gray, high-density low-pile carpet (`#b8b2aa` to `#a8a29b` subtly textured feel).

### Typography System
- **Primary Font Family:** `"Playfair Display"`, Georgia, serif (used for sophisticated, elegant UI accents or text overlays if applicable).
- **Secondary Font Family:** `"Inter"`, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif (clean sans-serif for functional UI inputs).
- **Fallback Stack:** `serif` (for decorative elements) and `sans-serif` (for clean readability).

---

## 2. Color Palette & Lighting Tokens
- **Primary Base (Walls/Carpet):** `#D2CEC9` (Warm Taupe Gray)
- **Secondary Base (Bedding/Upholstery):** `#F7F4F0` (Off-White / Alabaster Cream)
- **Accent Soft Pinks:** 
  - Blush Silk/Pillow: `#F2C4CE`
  - Deep Heart Pillow: `#E6738C`
- **Metals & Finishes:** Polished Silver / Chrome (`#E8ECEF`), Warm Brass/Gold (`#D4AF37` for subtle chair legs).
- **Ambient Sunlight:** Golden hour warm rays (`#FFE5B4` with low-opacity warm amber glows hitting the upper wall and window grid).

---

## 3. Structural Components & Layout

### A. Vanity Station (Foreground Left)
- **Vanity Structure:** White glossy lacquer wood makeup desk with integrated glass top revealing organized interior cosmetic trays.
- **Hardware:** Small crystal/silver knob pulls on drawers.
- **Vanity Mirror:** Hollywood-style vertical mirror lined with large round glowing warm-white LED bulb fixtures spaced evenly vertically along the left and right borders.
- **Attached Shelving Unit:** White floating tiered shelving system mounted directly to the side of the vanity backing, holding miniature pink perfume bottles, candles, and decorative pink-and-white floral arrangement on top.
- **Vase & Florals:** Clear glass rectangular vase holding fresh peach-pink and coral-pink tulips (`#FFB3A7`) with vibrant green stems.
- **Vanity Chair:** Scalloped-edge shell-back upholstered chair in matching cream velvet fabric (`#F9F6F0`) supported by slim tapered gold metal legs.

### B. Bedding & Headboard Area (Mid-Ground/Background)
- **Headboard:** Tall tufted diamond-patterned upholstery in soft off-white/cream faux leather or velvet, featuring silver nailhead trim along the vertical edge.
- **Mattress & Daybed Setup:** Positioned snugly in the corner against two walls. Heavy layering of plush textiles:
  - Base skirt: Deeply fluted/pleated vertical cream bed skirt.
  - Main Duvet: Extremely thick, chunky hand-knitted bouclé or faux-fur ripple textured blanket in pristine off-white.
  - Accent Throws: Folded slate-gray woven throw blanket across the lower portion.
- **Pillow Arrangement:**
  - Back row: Large Euro pillows in matching textured corduroy/ribbed cream fabric.
  - Accent pillows: Assorted blush pink satin throw pillows and a standout vibrant pink plush heart-shaped pillow (`#E6738C`) right in the center.

### C. Window & Treatment (Background Right)
- **Window Frame:** Standard double-hung window with a classic exterior grid pattern casting geometric sunlight shadows across the room.
- **Curtains:** Dual-layer window treatment featuring crisp white sheer sheer-linen curtains overlaid with opaque pure white curtain panels hung from a brushed silver metal rod with grommet rings.

---

## 4. Animation & Interaction Specification

### Lighting & Sunlight Animation
- **Sunbeam Shift (`@keyframes sunDrift`):** 
  - Simulates late-afternoon sunlight moving across the bed and window grid.
  - Transition duration: `12s` ease-in-out infinite loop, shifting opacity between `0.4` and `0.7`.
- **Vanity Bulb Glow (`@keyframes softPulse`):**
  - Subtle breathing effect on the vanity mirror bulbs to emulate real warm-white incandescent filaments.
  - `box-shadow` scaling from `0 0 10px rgba(255, 220, 180, 0.6)` to `0 0 20px rgba(255, 220, 180, 0.9)`.
