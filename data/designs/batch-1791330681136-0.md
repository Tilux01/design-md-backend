# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Minimalist Solid UI
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio, balancing negative space with structured container padding.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat neutral surfaces with solid high-contrast borders and subtle depth cues.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, minimalist borders, high-contrast typography, status indicators.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#F3F4F6`, border width `1px` `#E5E7EB`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`

- **Framer Motion Variants Code**:
