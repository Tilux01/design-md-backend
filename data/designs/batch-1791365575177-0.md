# Design Specification: Broqqoli Multibrand Store

## 1. Global Styles & Foundations

### Color Palette
- **Dark Theme (Hero Section):**
  - Background: `#161616` (Deep Charcoal Black)
  - Darker Layer/Elements: `#1a1a1a` to `#1f1f1f`
  - Text & Accents: `#ffffff` (Pure White)
  - Muted Text: `#888888` (Medium Gray)
- **Light Theme (Product Section):**
  - Background: `#f4f4f4` (Light Gray/Off-White)
  - Text: `#161616` (Charcoal Black)
  - Muted Text: `#777777` (Muted Gray)

### Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** Inter, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif (Clean geometric sans-serif).
- **Brand Title ("BROQQOLI"):** Extra-bold, uppercase, wide letter-spacing.
- **Body & Navigation:** Medium and Regular weights, uppercase for headers/nav, sentence case for descriptions.

---

## 2. Layout & Structure

- **Structure:** Two primary vertical sections (stacked full-width screens/viewports).
  1. **Hero Section:** Dark background, featuring a brand landing page with a model, large typography, and navigation overlays.
  2. **Product Detail Section:** Light background, featuring a minimalist e-commerce product display (RAINS jacket).
- **Grid / Alignment:**
  - Strict absolute and flexbox alignment creating a border-framed feel.
  - Generous whitespace/padding along outer boundaries (~40px to 60px).

---

## 3. Section-by-Section Breakdown

### Section 1: Hero Landing (Dark Theme)

- **Background:** `#161616`
- **Decorative Elements:**
  - A large subtle geometric circle graphic behind the central model.
- **Top Header Bar:**
  - **Left:** Hamburger menu icon (`=`), links: `MEN`, `WOMAN`, `SPECIAL`.
  - **Far Left (Sidebar text):** Vertical stack containing `ABOUT`, `STORE`, `INFO`.
  - **Right:** Search icon (`Q`), `ACCOUNT`, `CART` with item badge (`3`).
  - **Far Right (Sidebar vertical categories):** `JACKETS / HOODIE / SHOES`.
- **Center Content:**
  - **Model Image:** Centered, showcasing a person wearing a black hoodie.
  - **Brand Title:** `BROQQOLI` in massive white bold letters overlaid across the lower-middle area. (Note: The "Q"s use custom slashed-circle zero/null symbols: `Ø`).
  - **Number Callout:** `— 94.` displayed prominently beside the title.
- **Bottom Bar:**
  - **Left:** `SOCIAL COMMUNITY:` followed by `FACEBOOK`, `INSTAGRAM`, `PINTEREST`.
  - **Center:** Style descriptors: `CLASSIC`, `URBAN STYLE`, `CASUAL`.
  - **Right:** Language selector: `ENG v`.

---

### Section 2: Product Detail (Light Theme)

- **Background:** `#f4f4f4`
- **Top Header Bar:**
  - **Left:** Hamburger menu icon (`=`).
  - **Right:** Search icon (`Q`), `ACCOUNT`, `CART` (`3`).
- **Left Column (Product Info):**
  - **Category:** `JACKET` (Small uppercase).
  - **Title:** `RAINS` (Large bold serif or heavy sans-serif).
  - **Description:** *"The men's jacket of the Danish brand Rains, modeled on the legendary M-51 park."*
  - **Price:** `$990 —`
  - **Bottom Left:** `SOCIAL COMMUNITY:` (`FACEBOOK`, `INSTAGRAM`, `PINTEREST`).
- **Center Column (Product Display):**
  - **Product Image:** High-resolution studio shot of a black waterproof RAINS long coat/jacket laid flat/centered on white/light gray background.
  - **Carousel Controls:** `< — >` beneath the image.
- **Right Column / Bottom Right:**
  - **Add to Cart Action:** `ADD TO CART +`
  - **Language Selector:** `ENG v` at bottom right.

---

## 4. Interactive Elements & States (Design Intent)
- **Buttons / Links:** Hover states should include slight opacity shifts or underline interactions.
- **Cart Badge:** Solid dark circle with white text (`3`).
- **Icons:** Minimalist 1px-2px stroke icons (Search, Hamburger, Arrows, Plus).
