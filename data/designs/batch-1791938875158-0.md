# Design Specification: Telecine Streaming Interface

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Layout Style:** Full-screen, immersive dashboard with a fixed sidebar and a scrollable main content area.
*   **Theme:** Dark mode with high-contrast yellow accents.
*   **Visual Style:** Modern, clean, utilizing "Glassmorphism" (background blurs and semi-transparent overlays).
*   **Corner Radii:** Consistent rounded corners across all elements (approx. `12px` to `20px`).

## 2. Color Palette
*   **Primary Accent:** `#FFD700` (Vibrant Yellow) - Used for the logo, active states, and primary buttons.
*   **Background Overlay:** `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)` - A dark, semi-transparent layer over the background image to ensure text readability.
*   **Sidebar Background:** `rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.8)` with a `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.
*   **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
*   **Text Secondary:** `#B3B3B3` (Light Gray) - Used for descriptions and inactive menu items.

## 3. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Montserrat** (Bold for titles, Regular/Medium for body).
*   **Alternative Font:** **Poppins** or **Inter**.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Sizes:**
    *   Hero Title: `48px` / `3rem` (Bold)
    *   Section Headings: `24px` / `1.5rem` (Semi-Bold)
    *   Body Text: `14px` / `0.875rem` (Regular)
    *   Sidebar Labels: `16px` / `1rem` (Medium)

## 4. Component Breakdown

### A. Background Layer
*   **Image:** A high-resolution, cinematic still from *The Garfield Movie (2024)*. 
*   **Description:** The image features a close-up of Garfield (an orange, slightly grumpy-looking cat) on the left and Odie (a yellow, wide-eyed, happy dog) on the right. The background behind them is a soft-focus green indoor environment.
*   **Treatment:** The image should be `fixed`, `object-fit: cover`, and have a `filter: blur(5px)` applied to the edges or a global overlay to prevent it from distracting from the UI.

### B. Sidebar Navigation (Left)
*   **Width:** Approx. `200px` to `240px`.
*   **Top Accent:** A solid yellow (`#FFD700`) horizontal bar at the very top left corner.
*   **Menu Items:** Vertical list with icons followed by text.
    *   **Home** (Active state: Yellow icon/text or indicator).
    *   **Movies**
    *   **Favorites**
    *   **Categories**
    *   **About**
*   **Bottom Section:** A **Settings** icon located at the bottom left.
*   **Icons:** Thin, minimalist line icons (e.g., Lucide or Feather Icons).

### C. Top Header
*   **Logo:** The text "Telecine" in the top left (next to the sidebar), styled in the primary yellow color, bold sans-serif.
*   **Right Controls:** 
    *   **Search Icon:** A simple magnifying glass icon.
    *   **Profile/Settings:** A circular yellow icon (representing a user avatar or quick settings).

### D. Hero Section (Featured Content)
*   **Placement:** Centered vertically in the upper half of the main viewport.
*   **Title:** "The Garfiled Movie 2024" (Note: The original image contains a typo; "Garfield" is the correct spelling).
*   **Description:** A paragraph of secondary gray text (approx. 3-4 lines) providing a synopsis of the movie.
*   **Action Buttons:**
    *   **Primary Button:** A large, rounded-rectangle yellow button (`#FFD700`) with black text (e.g., "Watch Now").
    *   **Secondary Button:** A circular button with a white outline and a "Play" triangle icon inside, placed immediately to the right of the primary button.

### E. "Popular" Section (Movie Carousel)
*   **Heading:** The word "Popular" in white, bold text.
*   **Navigation:** Two small circular buttons with left/right arrows on the far right of the section header.
*   **Movie Posters:** A horizontal row of cards. Each card has:
    1.  **Poster Image:** Vertical aspect ratio (approx. 2:3), rounded corners (`12px`).
    2.  **Title:** Small white text centered or left-aligned directly below the poster.
*   **Poster List (Left to Right):**
    1.  *The Garfield Movie 2024*: Garfield's face close-up.
    2.  *Despicable Me 4*: Gru and Minions on a dark blue background.
    3.  *Inside Out 2*: Joy and other emotions on a light blue background.
    4.  *Home*: The character Oh (purple alien) on an orange background.
    5.  *Under Paris*: A shark fin in dark water.
    6.  *IF*: A large purple furry creature.
    7.  *Kung Fu Panda 4*: Po in a fighting stance against a green jungle background.
    8.  *Madagascar*: The four main animal characters on a jungle background.

## 5. Image Asset Requirements
*   **Background Image:** Full-screen, backgroundless (no UI baked in), high-res 16:9 or 21:9 ratio.
*   **Movie Posters:** Individual `.jpg` or `.webp` files. They should be clean, without "Play" buttons or text overlays baked into the image itself (titles are handled by the UI).
*   **Icons:** SVG format for perfect scaling.

## 6. Layout Specifics (CSS Hints)
*   **Main Container:** `display: flex; height: 100vh; overflow: hidden;`
*   **Content Area:** `flex-grow: 1; padding: 40px; overflow-y: auto;`
*   **Glass Effect:** `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05); backdrop-filter: blur(10px); border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1);`
