# Design Specification: "Lunar Relic" Interactive Product Showcase

## 1. Overview & Creative Direction
This design is a high-end, immersive digital product showcase inspired by cinematic sci-fi and architectural product photography. The central focus is an iconic, pristine white sneaker suspended statically in a dramatic, dark environment surrounded by rugged, volcanic/lunar rock formations lit from within by a warm, subterranean glow.

*   **Aesthetic:** Sci-Fi Brutalism, Cinematic, High-Contrast, Minimalist Luxury.
*   **Color Palette:** Pitch Black (`#000000`), Dark Charcoal/Obsidian (`#111111`, `#1c1c1c`), Warm Amber/Magma (`#ff6b00`, `#ff3b00`), and Pure White (`#ffffff`).
*   **Layout:** Full-screen immersive viewport (`100vw` x `100vh`), centered hero product with atmospheric lighting.

---

## 2. Layout & Composition (Full-Screen Viewport)
The interface is entirely edge-to-edge with no padding or framing containers.

### Z-Index Layering:
1.  **Layer 0 (Background):** Solid absolute black (`#000000`).
2.  **Layer 1 (Environment 3D Scene):** Interactive 3D rock formations with subsurface scattering (warm glow).
3.  **Layer 2 (Hero Product):** Floating Nike Air Force 1 sneaker with soft drop-shadow mapping.
4.  **Layer 3 (UI Overlay):** Minimalist branding, typography, and interaction prompts (pointer-events: auto only on interactive elements).

## 4. Visual Elements & Assets Breakdown

### A. Background & Environment (3D / WebGL)
*   **Elements:** Jagged, low-poly/sculpted rock formations rendered using a tool like Three.js, Spline, or Cinema 4D.
*   **Lighting Physics:** 
    *   **Rim Lighting:** Cool white light hitting the top edges of the rocks to define silhouettes against the black void.
    *   **Emissive Glow:** Warm orange/amber light emanating from the cracks *behind* and *underneath* the rocks, simulating underground heat/magma.
*   **Atmosphere:** Subtle floating dust particles or micro-debris reacting to cursor movement (parallax effect).

### B. The Hero Product (Shoe)
*   **Subject:** White Nike Air Force 1 Low.
*   **Materials:** Leather texture with subtle roughness maps; metallic lace dubrae catching a specular highlight.
*   **Shadow:** Soft ambient occlusion beneath the shoe to anchor it realistically in mid-air.

---

## 5. Animation & Interaction Specifications

### A. Initial Load Animation (Entrance Sequence)
1.  **Fade-In (0s - 0.8s):** The pure black screen smoothly brings in the background glow.
2.  **Rock Assembly (0.4s - 1.2s):** The rock formations scale up from `0.95` to `1.0` with a subtle cubic-bezier easing (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
3.  **Shoe Float-In (0.8s - 1.8s):** The shoe slides down from the upper viewport, locking into its floating coordinate space with a gentle inertial bounce.

### B. Continuous Idle Animations
*   **Floating (Hover) Loop:** 
    *   *Property:* `transform: translateY(Y)`
    *   *Keyframes:* Sine wave oscillation, amplitude of `12px`, duration of `4.5s`.
*   **Subtle Rotation:** 
    *   *Property:* `transform: rotateZ(Z)`
    *   *Amplitude:* ±1.5 degrees over `6s`.

### C. Mouse / Gyroscope Parallax Interaction
*   **Desktop (Cursor Tracking):** 
    *   The camera shifts slightly based on mouse coordinates relative to the window center.
    *   Rocks move slower (deeper layer), the shoe tracks moderately, and UI elements remain fixed or feature a slight micro-parallax.
*   **Mobile (Device Orientation):** 
    *   Mapped to `deviceorientation` API for gyroscope-based depth tilting.
