# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist Dark Portfolio / High-End Creative Showcase.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio focusing around a bold typographic overlay masking a portrait subject. Clean, immersive, and striking.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Absolute zero-elevation flat canvas composition (`#111111`) with overlapping large typographic text layers behind and in front of the human subject portrait.

# 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

## Color Token Definitions
- **Primary Brand Color & Variants**: Cream/Beige text default (`#F4ECE1`), Muted Cream (`#E2D5C3`).
- **Secondary & Accent Colors**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Crisp Black (`#0B0B0B`).
- **Background & Surface Architecture**: Canvas background (`#111111`), Surface 1 (`#161616`).
- **Border & Divider Scale**: Outline stroke for wireframe lettering (`#F4ECE1` at 30% opacity).
- **Text Color Hierarchy**: Primary Cream (`#F4ECE1`), Secondary Muted (`#999999`), Inverse Black (`#0B0B0B`).
