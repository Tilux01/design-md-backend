# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Web3 AI SaaS & Robotic Tech Platform (Cyberpunk Minimalist / High-Tech Futuristic).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Spacious layout with prominent central hero focus, balanced card asymmetry, and deep immersive canvas backgrounds.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered translucent surfaces featuring dark obsidian frosted glass (`rgba(10, 14, 26, 0.6)`), crisp fine borders with magenta/pink neon inner glows (`rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.4)`), and intense volumetric ambient lighting behind 3D robotic elements.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient pink/magenta radial glows, glassmorphic cards with subtle gradient borders, floating metallic 3D entities, minimalist dark mode typography.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-pink-500 inline-block"></span>`.
