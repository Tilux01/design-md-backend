# Design Specification: "Jose Maria's Journal" Mobile App Interface

This document outlines the exact visual and structural specifications for cloning the two screens of the mobile journaling app shown in the provided media. 

---

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Color Palette
* **Background Color:** Light Warm Gray / Off-White (`#F2F1EF` or similar)
* **Primary Accent / Yellow:** Bright Sunny Yellow (`#FFC83B`)
* **Text Primary (Dark):** Dark Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`)
* **Text Secondary (Muted):** Muted Gray (`#707070` to `#8A8A8A`)
* **Card Backgrounds:** 
  * White (`#FFFFFF`) for distinct container boxes.
  * Soft Peach/Pink (`#FADBD8` approx.) for "Pause & reflect" card.
  * Soft Lavender (`#E8DAEF` approx.) for "Set Intentions" card.
* **Emotion Bar Colors:**
  * **Happy:** Yellow/Orange (`#FFC038`)
  * **Sad:** Deep Brown/Maroon (`#6E3830`)
  * **Calm:** Olive Green (`#7FA83E`)
  * **Anxious:** Muted Olive-Gray (`#686959`)

### Typography
* **Font Family:** A modern, humanist sans-serif (e.g., **Inter**, **SF Pro Display**, or **Plus Jakarta Sans**).
* **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
* **Font Weights & Sizes:**
  * **App Bar Greeting Name ("Hi, Jose Maria"):** ~24px, Bold.
  * **Screen Title ("My Journal"):** ~20px, Bold.
  * **Primary Stats Counter ("420"):** ~42px, ExtraBold/Black.
  * **Body/Subtext:** 12px – 14px, Regular to Medium.

---

## 2. Screen 1: Dashboard / Home View

### Header Section
* **Greeting:** "Hi, Jose Maria" aligned left, with a bold weight on the name.
* **Profile Avatar:** Circular image of a person smiling, positioned at the top right, approx. 36px diameter with a subtle border.

### Date Carousel (Horizontal Scroll)
* **Days displayed:** Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu (Highlighted with solid yellow circle), Fri, Sat, Sun.
* **Dates:** 7, 8, 9, **10** (active/selected with yellow circular background), 11, 12, 13.

### "My Journal" Section
* **Header Row:** Title "My Journal" on the left, "See all" link in muted gray on the right.
* **Main Card (Today - Morning):**
  * **Background:** Soft gradient ranging from pale yellow to deeper warm yellow (`#FFD166`).
  * **Text Content:**
    * "Let's start your day" (Bold, ~16px)
    * "Begin with a mindful morning reflections." (Regular, ~11px)
  * **Illustration (Foreground):** A cute, smiling cartoon sun character rising over rolling green hills with small trees and a grassy meadow. The image is vector-style, flat-art with warm daylight tones.
  * **Rotated Secondary Card:** A vertical preview card labeled "Evening" is visible on the right edge, indicating swipeability.

### "Quick Journal" Section
* **Header Row:** "Quick Journal" on the left, "See all" on the right.
* **Cards Grid (Horizontal scrollable or two-column):**
  * **Card 1 (Pause & reflect):**
    * Background: Pale pink/peach.
    * Icon: Tiny green sprout icon.
    * Title: "Pause & reflect"
    * Subtext: "What are you grateful for today?"
    * Tags at bottom: "Today", "Personal".
  * **Card 2 (Set Intentions):**
    * Background: Pale lavender.
    * Icon: Smiling yellow face emoji.
    * Title: "Set Intentions"
    * Subtext: "How do you want to feel?"
    * Tags at bottom: "Today", "Family".

### Bottom Navigation Bar
* **Background:** Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) spanning the bottom.
* **Items (Left to Right):**
  1. **Home:** Filled home icon with active indicator dot below, labeled "Home" (Dark active color).
  2. **Explore:** Compass/Globe outline icon, labeled "Explore".
  3. **Floating Action Button (Center):** Prominent circular yellow button with a white plus sign (`+`) floating slightly above the nav bar.
  4. **Journey:** Document/Journal outline icon, labeled "Journey".
  5. **Profile:** User profile outline icon, labeled "Profile".

---

## 3. Screen 2: Journal Analytics / Detail View ("My Journal")

### Top Bar
* **Back Button:** Circular white button containing a left chevron (`<`) on the far left.
* **Title:** Centered text "My Journal".
* **Menu Button:** Circular white button containing three horizontal dots (`...`) on the far right.

### Header & Stats
* **Large Counter:** "420" centered in massive bold typography.
* **Subtitle:** "Celebrate what made you smile today." centered underneath in muted gray.

### Emotions Breakdown Card
* **Container:** A clean white card with rounded corners (`border-radius: 24px`) spanning the content area.
* **Section Header:** "Emotions" (Bold) followed by subtext "Here are four core emotions for your journal".
* **Vertical Bar Chart:**
  * Four vertical pill-shaped bars representing percentages. Each bar has a filled colored section rising from the bottom against a light gray track.
  * **Bar 1 (Happy):** Filled with yellow up to **48%**. Label underneath: "Happy".
  * **Bar 2 (Sad):** Filled with dark maroon up to **33%**. Label underneath: "Sad".
  * **Bar 3 (Calm):** Filled with olive green up to **27%**. Label underneath: "Calm".
  * **Bar 4 (Anxious):** Filled with grayish-olive up to **40%**. Label underneath: "Anxious".

### Bottom Action Button
* **Button:** Full-width pill button with a bright yellow background (`#FFC83B`), containing centered black text: **"Create a New Journal"**.
