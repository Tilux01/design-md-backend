# Complete Design Specification (`design.md`)

This document provides the exact visual, structural, and technical specifications required to recreate the **"The Three Graces"** editorial landing page as a modern, full-screen interactive web application.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Aesthetic Vibe

* **Aesthetic:** Neoclassical High-Fashion / Art Gallery Editorial dark mode UI.
* **Mood:** Dramatic, luxurious, minimal, dark (chiaroscuro lighting), polished.
* **Layout Structure:** Single-viewport (`100vh`), centered hero layout with multi-layered depth (Background sculpture → Text overlay → Interactive floating accents).

---

## 2. Color Palette

| Token Name | Hex Code | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Canvas Background** | `#000000` or `#050505` | Absolute pitch-black main viewport background. |

## 3. Typography Specifications & Fallbacks

### A. Navigation Bar Text (`ART`, `ABOUT`, `VISIT`, `SHOP`, `SEARCH`)
* **Primary Font:** `Inter`, `Montserrat`, or `SF Pro Display`
* **Fallback Chain:** `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`
* **Font Weight:** `500` (Medium)
* **Font Size:** `12px` (0.75rem)
* **Letter Spacing:** `0.25em` (4px tracking)
* **Transform:** Uppercase

### B. Title Accent Text ("*The*")
* **Primary Font:** `Cormorant Garamond` (Italic) or `Playfair Display` (Italic)
* **Fallback Chain:** `Georgia`, `Baskerville`, `Bodoni MT`, `serif`
* **Font Weight:** `300` (Light Italic) or `400` (Regular Italic)
* **Font Size:** `26px` – `30px`
* **Letter Spacing:** `0.05em`
* **Style:** Lowercase with capital T, pure italic serif.

### C. Main Hero Heading ("T H R E E   G R A C E S")
* **Primary Font:** `Cinzel`, `Didot`, or `Bodoni 72`
* **Fallback Chain:** `Playfair Display`, `Times New Roman`, `serif`
* **Font Weight:** `400` (Regular)
* **Font Size:** `52px` – `64px` (Responsive scale)
* **Letter Spacing:** `0.38em` (Ultra-wide tracking)
* **Transform:** Uppercase

### D. Descriptive Body Paragraph
* **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Roboto`
* **Fallback Chain:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `sans-serif`
* **Font Weight:** `400` (Regular)
* **Font Size:** `11px` – `12.5px`
* **Line Height:** `1.6`
* **Letter Spacing:** `0.03em`
* **Text Alignment:** Center

## 4. Detailed Image Asset Descriptions

### Asset 1: Main Hero Sculpture Artwork
* **File Type:** High-resolution transparent background cutout (`PNG` or `WebP`).
* **Subject:** Antonio Canova’s *The Three Graces* marble sculpture (Euphrosyne, Aglaea, and Thalia).
* **Lighting Style:** Low-key chiaroscuro monochrome lighting (pure black and white). Deep shadows engulfing 70% of the statues' inner details, with stark rim lighting along the right edges, hair curls, shoulders, and arms.
* **Background Status:** **100% Backgroundless / Transparent cutout**.
* **Placement & Scale:**
  * **Position:** Centered horizontally (`left: 50%`, `transform: translateX(-50%)`).
  * **Vertical Alignment:** Positioned in the vertical center of the screen, extending from slightly below the header down to the middle section behind the heading text.
  * **Scale:** Width approx. `420px` – `500px` depending on screen height.
  * **Rotation/Transform:** `0deg` rotation, straight upright perspective.
* **Layering (Z-Index):** Positioned behind the text block overlay (`z-index: 10`), but in front of the background (`z-index: 0`).

### Asset 2: Floating Sphere Accent
* **Element Type:** Flat geometric vector circle or rendered light source.
* **Appearance:** Solid pure white sphere (`#FFFFFF`), crisp edge, no drop shadow or glow.
* **Dimensions:** `18px` × `18px` perfect circle (`border-radius: 50%`).
* **Placement:**
  * **Position:** Right-hand side of the sculpture.
  * **Relative Location:** Approximately `180px` to `200px` right of the center axis, level with the upper arm/back of the rightmost Grace figure.
* **Layering (Z-Index):** `z-index: 15` (Above background and artwork).

## 7. Responsive Rules & Interactivity Guidelines

1. **Full Viewport Fitting:** `overflow: hidden` on body/container prevents vertical scrolling; elements auto-scale via max-widths and relative sizing to fit laptops, tablets, and desktops perfectly in 1 view.
2. **Hover States:**
   * Nav items transition smoothly (`transition: color 0.3s ease`) from `#A3A3A3` to `#FFFFFF`.
3. **Typography Scaling:**
   * Headline scale down gracefully on screens `< 768px` from `64px` to `36px` while maintaining wide letter-spacing.
