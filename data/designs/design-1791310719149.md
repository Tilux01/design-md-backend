# Design System Specification: "Reimagined History" E-Commerce Landing Page

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
* **Mood & Archetype:** Minimalist luxury meets editorial storytelling. The aesthetic leans heavily into high-end artisanal curation (evoked by the candle imagery and restrained, modernist typography).
* **Visual Density:** Extremely low density with vast, deliberate whitespace usage to center focus on the hero product and typography.
* **Theme Balance:** Clean **Light Mode** core experience accented by a high-contrast solid black utility/author container in the bottom-left quadrant.
* **Texture & Depth:** Flat, clean white canvas (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle soft drop shadows beneath UI containers and product imagery to create a floating, modern spatial depth.

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
* **Container Structure:** Centered fixed-aspect window frame (representing a responsive desktop breakpoint at `1280px` max width).
* **Spacing Scale (8pt grid system):**
  * `8px` (`0.5rem`) - Icon padding, tight gaps
  * `16px` (`1rem`) - Element padding, small gutters
  * `32px` (`2rem`) - Section padding, card margins
  * `64px` (`4rem`) - Macro structural padding (Header gutters)
* **Section Sequence (Top to Bottom):**
  1. **Global Header:** Brand logo (`Si`), Country region navigation links, Search (`🔍`), and Hamburger Menu (`☰`).
  2. **Hero Main Stage:** Left-aligned editorial typography ("realreason." + "Reimagined History.") balanced by a floating product visual (artisanal bark-wrapped jar candle) on the right.
  3. **Floating Footer Dock:** Split-layout utility dock featuring an author profile/bio card on the left (dark surface), social action icons, audio/progress scrubber, carousel pagination dots, and slider navigation arrows (`<`, `>`).

---

## 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement

### A. Global Navigation
* **Alignment:** Flexbox `space-between`, vertical center aligned.
* **Height:** `80px` fixed header boundary.
* **Elements:** 
  * Left: Logo mark (`Si`) with `24px` font weight `800`.
  * Center-Left: Region selector links (Ghana, Canada, United States, United Arab Emirates) spaced with `32px` gaps. Color transition from muted gray (`#888`) to primary black (`#000`) on active/hover.
  * Right: Search icon and Hamburger menu icon enclosed in clean, borderless interactive targets (`32x32px`).

### B. Hero Product Stage
* **Visual Balance:** 45% Left (Typography block), 55% Right (Product Imagery).
* **Product Visual:** Realistic photographic asset featuring drop-shadow mechanics (`rgba(0,0,0,0.08) 0px 24px 48px`). Floating subtly against the pure white canvas.

### C. Bottom Utility Dock
* **Dimensions:** Full-width container inset by `40px` margins from the bottom frame. Split into two primary visual zones:
  * **Zone 1 (Author/Context Card):** Solid black background (`#080808`), rounded corners (`border-radius: 0px` inside a floating layout or `12px` integrated). Contains avatar image (circular, `36px`), author name ("JACKSON WAGNER"), and sub-copy. Includes minimalist Instagram and Share icons.
  * **Zone 2 (Carousel & Pagination Controls):** Right-aligned within the canvas. Features three pagination indicators (two inactive gray dots, one active black pill), left/right circular arrow navigation buttons (`border: 1px solid #E5E5E5`, `border-radius: 50%`, `36x36px`), and a textual `.more` link.

---

## 6. Motion Mechanics & Interactive States
* **Hover Transitions:** 
  * Buttons and interactive icons scale up smoothly (`scale(1.05)`) with a color shift from neutral border (`#E5E5E5`) to dark accent (`#000`).
  * Duration: `200ms`
  * Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` (Standard Material/Apple ease-out).
* **Carousel / Slide Transitions:**
  * Horizontal translation of hero content and product imagery using `cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)` over `450ms`.
* **State Management:**
  * Active pagination dots morph from circular (`8px`) to elongated pills (`20px x 8px`) upon selection.
