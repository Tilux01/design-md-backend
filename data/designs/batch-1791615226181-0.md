# Design Specification: "Believe Its True" Fullscreen Hero / Landing Experience

---

## 1. Overview & Aesthetic Direction

A cinematic, atmospheric, sci-fi landing page hero featuring a muted, desaturated landscape, ominous flying saucers (UFOs), and stark typographic contrast. 

* **Theme / Mood:** Moody, enigmatic, retro-futuristic, cinematic sci-fi.
* **Canvas:** Full viewport (`100vw × 100vh`), borderless, responsive edge-to-edge layout.
* **Compositional Principle:** Visual depth achieved through distinct layered planes:
  1. Base atmospheric sky gradient (far background).
  2. Floating cloud bank and distant UFOs (mid-background).
  3. Primary mothership UFO with soft volumetric light beam (midground).
  4. Sweeping dark terrain ridge with a lone human figure (foreground).
  5. High-contrast typography and minimalist UI controls (interface layer).

## 3. Typography System

### Primary Display Font (Headline)
* **Font Family:** `Cabinet Grotesk` (Extrabold / Heavy)
  * *Fallbacks:* `Neue Haas Grotesk Display Pro`, `Syne`, `Arial Black`, `sans-serif`
* **Style:** Lowercase only, very tight letter-spacing.
* **Weight:** 800 or 900
* **Size:** Fluid `clamp(3.5rem, 7vw, 6.5rem)`
* **Line Height:** `1.0`
* **Letter Spacing:** `-0.04em` (tight tracking)
* **Color:** `#1B1E1C`

### Body & Secondary UI Font
* **Font Family:** `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans`
  * *Fallbacks:* `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`
* **Weights:**
  * Regular: 400 (Body description)
  * Medium: 500 (Eyebrow, Links, Nav)
* **Sizes & Casing:**
  * **Top Tagline / Eyebrows:** `0.75rem` (12px), Uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.18em`
  * **Body Copy:** `0.85rem` (13.6px), Sentence case, `line-height: 1.5`, `letter-spacing: 0.02em`
  * **CTA Link (`MORE`):** `0.75rem` (12px), Uppercase, Bold/Semi-bold, `letter-spacing: 0.15em`
  * **Navigation Links (`ABOUT`, `CONTACT`):** `0.75rem` (12px), Uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.2em`
  * **Counter (`04/04`):** `0.75rem` (12px), Monospaced numerals, `letter-spacing: 0.1em`

## 4. Visual Assets & Image Layering Specifications

All photographic elements must be isolated transparent assets (PNG with smooth alpha channel or WebP) layered over the base canvas.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [Layer 5 - Z-Index 50]  UI & Text Elements (Header, Headline, Nav)     |
| [Layer 4 - Z-Index 40]  Lone Person Foreground Asset                    |
| [Layer 3 - Z-Index 30]  Dark Ridge/Ground Silhouette Asset              |
| [Layer 2 - Z-Index 20]  UFO Assets (Center, Left, Right) + Beam Layer  |
| [Layer 1 - Z-Index 10]  Cloud Bank Asset (Soft Alpha Mask)             |
| [Layer 0 - Z-Index 00]  Base Canvas Atmosphere (Sky Gradient)          |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 1. Base Sky Gradient (Background)
* **Type:** CSS Gradient / Base surface.
* **Specification:** Linear vertical gradient from top to bottom:
  * `0%`: `#9AA49F`
  * `55%`: `#B4BCB9`
  * `100%`: `#8E9893`

### 2. Cloud Bank Layer
* **Type:** Isolated transparent PNG / WebP.
* **Description:** Soft, photorealistic white/cream cumulus cloud formation stretching horizontally across the upper mid-section. Faded, wispy vapor edges on left and right sides.
* **Placement:**
  * `width`: ~65% of viewport width.
  * `height`: Auto (proportional).
  * `top`: ~18% from viewport top.
  * `left`: ~22% from viewport left edge.
* **Opacity:** 92%.

### 3. Center Mothership (Primary UFO)
* **Type:** Isolated transparent PNG / WebP.
* **Description:** Classic circular flying saucer, metallic dark pewter finish with radial symmetry. Features a raised central observation dome on top, a sharp rim flange, and a shadowy undercarriage with subtle mechanical panel seams. Light catches the upper edge from an overcast sky above.
* **Placement:**
  * `width`: ~24% of viewport width (approx. 320px–380px on standard desktop).
  * `top`: ~28% from viewport top.
  * `left`: 50% (horizontally centered using `transform: translateX(-50%)`).
  * `rotation`: `0deg` (level).
* **Volumetric Light Beam:**
  * A very faint, translucent white vertical cone gradient (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` to `transparent`) extending downwards from the center underbelly toward the hill surface.

### 4. Flanking UFOs (Secondary & Distant)
* **Left UFO:**
  * **Description:** Smaller saucer, matching design, angled slightly downward to the left (~-4° tilt).
  * **Placement:** `top: 34%`, `left: 25%`, `width: ~7.5%` of viewport width.
* **Right UFO:**
  * **Description:** Even smaller distant saucer, horizontal orientation.
  * **Placement:** `top: 31%`, `left: 68%`, `width: ~4%` of viewport width.

### 5. Ground / Hill Silhouette
* **Type:** Isolated PNG / SVG landscape layer.
* **Description:** A gentle dark grassy knoll / barren hill ridge running continuously across the entire width of the frame. The crest begins lower on the left (~64% of viewport height) and slopes gently downward toward the right (~67% of viewport height). The fill is nearly solid dark charcoal (`#161916`) with fine grass/rock edge detailing along the top crest.
* **Placement:**
  * `width`: `100vw`
  * `bottom`: `0`
  * `height`: ~38% of viewport height.

### 6. Lone Human Figure
* **Type:** Isolated cutout PNG.
* **Description:** Back-facing or three-quarter facing solitary person standing upright on the crest of the hill. Wearing a dark red/brick-red hooded cold-weather parka/anorak and dark charcoal pants.
* **Placement:**
  * Anchored directly on the ridge crest.
  * `left`: ~50.8% of viewport width (vertically aligned just off-center beneath the main UFO).
  * `top`: ~56.5% from viewport top (feet firmly on the ridge line).
  * `height`: ~6.5% to 7.5% of viewport height (small scale to emphasize vastness).

## 7. Responsive Behavior Guidelines

1. **Desktop / Ultrawide (>1440px):** Elements scale proportionally; max width constraints ensure content does not push too close to screen edges.
2. **Laptop / Tablet Landscape (1024px–1366px):** 
   * Typography scales down smoothly using `clamp()`.
   * The relative Z-ordering and positions remain consistent.
3. **Mobile & Portrait (<768px):**
   * UFOs stack vertically above the headline.
   * Headline size reduces to `clamp(2.5rem, 10vw, 3.8rem)`.
   * Eyebrow, headline, and paragraph collapse into a single centered column above the hill.
   * Lone figure remains on the hill crest beneath the typography.
