# Complete Design Specification Document (`design.md`)

This document outlines the visual layout, design system, component hierarchy, typography, colors, and image specifications for replicating the **Aeline** environmental organization website.

---

## 1. Design System & Global Styles

### 1.1 Color Palette
* **Brand Primaries:**
  * **Deep Charcoal (Text/Headings):** `#18211B` (Used for major headings and primary body elements)
  * **Forest Green (Primary Accent):** `#2D5D34` (Used for emphasized heading keywords, icons, and primary pill buttons)
  * **Vibrant Eco Lime / Chartreuse:** `#D2E669` (CTA buttons, newsletter container background)
  * **Dark Pine Green (Buttons):** `#2F432B` (Input submission buttons and primary interactive CTA)
* **Neutrals & Backgrounds:**
  * **Page Canvas Background:** `#F5F6F3` (Soft off-white / light neutral organic paper tint)
  * **Card Surface Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Solid pure white container cards)
  * **Muted Slate/Gray (Body & Descriptions):** `#637067`
  * **Subtle Borders & Lines:** `#E4E9E2`
* **Translucent & Frosted Glass (Overlays):**
  * **Glass Pill Card Background:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`

  * **Dark Glass Card Overlays:** `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(0,0,0,0) 30%, rgba(18,28,21,0.88) 100%)`

### 1.3 Geometry & Layout Principles
* **Full-Width Canvas:** The site sits on a full-screen light neutral canvas (`#F5F6F3`) with zero outer browser mockup frames.
* **Rounded Bento-Section Enclosures:** Sections are housed inside large, standalone rounded card panels with `border-radius: 28px` and `padding: 48px` to `64px`, separated by vertical gaps of `32px`.
* **Pill-Shaped Geometry:** Interactive buttons, inputs, badge tags, and stats containers feature pill rounding (`border-radius: 9999px`).
* **Shadows:**
  * **Card Shadow:** `0 8px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`
  * **Floating Glass Pill Shadow:** `0 12px 28px rgba(24, 33, 27, 0.08)`

## 4. Spacing, Responsiveness & Micro-Interactions

### 4.1 Spacing Rhythm
* **Section Padding:** `64px 48px` on desktop, `32px 20px` on mobile.
* **Component Gap:** Uniform `24px` to `32px` between grid items.
* **Text Gaps:** `8px` between badge and heading; `16px` between heading and body; `24px` between body and action buttons.

### 4.2 Interactive States
* **Buttons:**
  * Lime Button (`#D2E669`): Hover transitions to slightly darker lime (`#C2D858`), `transform: translateY(-1px)`.
  * Pine Green Button (`#2F432B`): Hover transitions to `#243421`.
  * Text Links ("Learn More →"): Hover moves the right arrow `4px` to the right with ease transition (`transition: transform 0.2s ease`).
* **Cards:**
  * Feature cards and Initiative cards: Subdued hover lift (`transform: translateY(-4px)`, shadow expands to `0 16px 36px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`).
  * Feature image zoom: Image inside card scales smoothly to `scale(1.04)` on card hover.

### 4.3 Breakpoints
* **Desktop (`≥ 1200px`):** Full 3-column / 4-column layouts as specified.
* **Tablet (`768px - 1199px`):**
  * Hero switches to a stacked vertical layout with stats bar below.
  * Mission feature cards switch to a 3-column horizontal scroll or 2-column grid.
  * Initiatives split grid shifts from side-by-side to stacked (Project cards first, Newsletter box below).
* **Mobile (`< 768px`):**
  * Navigation collapses to hamburger menu + "Get Involved" button.
  * Main headings scale down to `32px` (`2rem`).
  * Stats bar stacks into a 2x2 grid.
  * All card grids collapse to 1 column.
