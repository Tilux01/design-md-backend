# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Heritage & Scientific Genealogy (Warm, tactile archive style combined with clean, modern web UI).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Generous whitespace with structured asymmetric layout, framed by aged parchment textures and warm cream surfaces.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered paper surface hierarchy with soft sepia drop shadows (`0 8px 32px rgba(90, 70, 50, 0.08)`), thin muted borders, and subtle paper texture overlays.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, aged parchment backgrounds, vintage genealogical iconography, subtle vignette framing.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `4px 12px`, font-size: `11px`, font-weight: `600`, tracking: `0.08em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `rgba(30, 57, 50, 0.06)`, border: `1px solid rgba(30, 57, 50, 0.12)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-brand inline-block"></span>`.
