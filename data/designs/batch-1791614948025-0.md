# Design Specification: Atomic Heart – "POLYMER" Interactive Showcase

---

## 1. Overview & Aesthetic Direction
* **Aesthetic Archetype:** Soviet Retro-Futurism / Clean Industrial Sci-Fi HUD / High-Tech Laboratory Dossier.
* **Core Philosophy:** High-contrast, sterile white laboratory background juxtaposed with heavy black brutalist typography, refractive 3D fluid simulations, and subtle semi-transparent technical UI overlays.
* **Canvas Target:** Full-screen desktop browser experience ($1920 \times 1080\text{px}$ native baseline, fluid 100vw $\times$ 100vh viewport, edge-to-edge layout).

## 3. Typography System

### 3.1 Font Families
1. **Display / Hero Font (Brutalist Extended Sans):**
   * *Primary:* `Druk Wide Bold`, `Eurostile Extended Bold`, or `Syne Extra Bold`.
   * *Fallback:* `'Arial Black', 'Impact', sans-serif` (with `transform: scaleX(1.15)` or `letter-spacing: -0.02em`).
   * *Used for:* Main headline ("POL" / "MER"), "ATOMIC HEART" logo, "D. SECHENOV" badge text, "04%" HUD stat.
2. **Technical / Monospace HUD Font:**
   * *Primary:* `JetBrains Mono`, `Chakra Petch`, or `Space Mono`.
   * *Fallback:* `'Courier New', monospace`.
   * *Used for:* Numeric badges ("001.", "006."), "POLYMERIZATION...", "FEB. 21 TH", scroll indicator.
3. **UI & Dossier Body Font:**
   * *Primary:* `Inter`, `Neue Haas Grotesk`, or `Helvetica Neue`.
   * *Fallback:* `sans-serif`.
   * *Used for:* Navigation hierarchy, biography paragraphs, language selector.

### 3.2 Typographic Hierarchy
* **Giant Hero Text (`POL` / `MER`):**
  * Size: `clamp(7rem, 14vw, 15rem)`
  * Weight: 900 (Ultra Heavy / Black)
  * Line Height: `0.85`
  * Letter Spacing: `-0.03em`
  * Text Color: `#121214`
* **Brand Logo ("ATOMIC HEART"):**
  * Size: `1.5rem` (`24px`), stacked in two lines, centered.
  * Stencil/Distressed Industrial Bold styling.
* **Sub-Header ("FEB. 21 TH"):**
  * Size: `0.7rem` (`11px`), Bold, tracked out (`letter-spacing: 0.15em`).
* **Navigation Links (Right Rail):**
  * Size: `0.8rem` (`13px`), Bold/Medium uppercase.
  * Inactive tracking: `0.12em`, color: `#A0A0A3`.
  * Active item (`POLYMER ◂`): color: `#111111`, tracked out, bold with filled indicator triangle pointing left.
* **Technical Body Copy (Dossier):**
  * Size: `0.72rem` (`11.5px`), regular weight, line-height: `1.45`, color: `#6B6D71`.

## 4. Visual Assets & 3D Imagery Specifications

### 4.1 Central 3D Asset: Polymer Specimen & Dispenser Pedestal
* **Format & Alpha:** PNG-24 with transparent alpha channel or real-time WebGL/Three.js model.
* **Composition:**
  * **Top Section (Amorphous Polymer):** Highly turbulent, refractive, viscous fluid blob mimicking liquid glass / clear water with diamond-like facets and ambient caustic reflections. No colored tint; purely clear refraction with white specular highlights and soft grayish internal shadows.
  * **Floating Splatters:** Separate detached droplets of the same refractive liquid hovering around the perimeter (two droplets to the left of the main mass, one smaller droplet to the right).
  * **Middle Section (Tendril Cables):** 6 to 8 braided black synthetic hydraulic lines/cables extending downwards from the bottom of the polymer fluid into the mechanical base.
  * **Bottom Section (Dispenser Base):** Soviet-futuristic cylindrical containment capsule. Brushed matte silver/chrome metal housing with rounded bevels, embedded with dual curved brass/gold conduit pipes extending from the bottom rim.
* **Z-Index Layering:**
  * `z-index: 1`: Background canvas and light tech vectors.
  * `z-index: 2`: Text `"POL"` (left).
  * `z-index: 3`: **Central 3D Polymer Asset** (overlaps the right edge of 'O' and 'L').
  * `z-index: 4`: Text `"MER"` (positioned lower right, behind the rightmost droplet but layered alongside the lower tendrils).
  * `z-index: 5`: Foreground HUDs (`04%` glass card) and UI elements.

### 4.2 Dmitry Sechenov Portrait
* **Dimensions:** Approx. $110\text{px} \times 125\text{px}$.
* **Style:** High-contrast monochromatic / grayscale studio portrait.
* **Subject:** Middle-aged Soviet scientist with neat side-parted hair, trim mustache/beard, wearing a formal suit and necktie. Looking slightly left of camera.
* **Finish:** Crisp rectangular cut, no border radius, faint drop shadow (`rgba(0,0,0,0.08) 0px 4px 12px`).

### 4.3 Bottom-Right Teaser Video Thumbnail
* **Dimensions:** Width $\approx 220\text{px}$, Height $\approx 110\text{px}$.
* **Subject:** In-engine screenshot of Dr. Sechenov in a laboratory examining a glowing polymer vat.
* **Overlays:**
  * Translucent circular frosted play button in the center ($\approx 36\text{px}$ diameter) containing a small white triangle.
  * Attached to a black vertical sidebar tab labeled `• WATCH •`.

## 7. Interactive States & Micro-interactions

1. **Hover on Navigation Links (Right Rail):**
   * Non-active links transition from `#A0A0A3` to `#111111` with a slight horizontal translation of $-3\text{px}$ towards the left.
2. **Central 3D Polymer Simulation:**
   * Subtle continuous idle floating wave animation (undulating surface caustics).
   * Mouse parallax: Fluid mesh reacts slightly to cursor movement ($X/Y$ tilt of $\pm 5^\circ$).
3. **Scroll Action:**
   * Downward scroll triggers transition from Section 001 (`POLYMER`) to Section 002 (`NEURO-POLYMER` / next categorical topic), transitioning the large background typography and changing the central model.
4. **Video Widget Hover:**
   * Scaled up slightly ($1.03\times$), play button becomes solid white with black icon.
