# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist SaaS / Clean Enterprise Presentation Mockup.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with a warm, off-white neutral canvas (`#f7f5f2`). Focused content hierarchy, balanced container padding, and floating bottom cookie banner.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, minimalist card layout utilizing subtle surface differentiation (`#f0ece6` to `#ffffff`) with ultra-clean borders (`#e6e1da`) and zero heavy drop shadows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-end minimalist minimalism, pill-shaped interactive elements, subtle border treatments, floating notification drawers.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `13px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `-0.01em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#111111`, text color `#ffffff`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
