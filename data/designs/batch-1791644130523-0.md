# Design System & Specification Document (`design.md`)

## 1. Overview & Visual Direction
* **Theme:** Romantic, vintage-playful, cute presentation / greeting page.
* **Canvas Style:** Full-screen responsive viewport (100vw, 100vh) without browser margins or window frames.
* **Overall Aesthetic:** Soft cream background, vintage botanical floral illustrations flanking the sides, playfulness in the center via handwritten typography and a cute kawaii cat illustration.

## 6. Responsiveness & Screen Fitting

* **Desktop (> 1024px):** Full layout as described; side florals fully visible at 100% size.
* **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Side floral illustrations scale down to `200px` width and shift closer to outer edges (`left: 1%`, `right: 1%`).
* **Mobile (< 767px):**
  * Side floral illustrations opacity reduced or hidden to avoid overlapping the central text.
  * Nav bar converts to horizontal scroll or compact menu.
  * Buttons stack vertically or scale to `150px` width each side-by-side.
