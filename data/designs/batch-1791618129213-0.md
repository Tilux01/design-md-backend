# Design Specification (`design.md`)

---

## 1. Overview & Theme Architecture

A minimalist, high-end luxury e-commerce and portfolio landing page ("DIAMOND . JEWELRY"). The aesthetic is ultra-clean, architectural, and restrained, emphasizing expansive negative space, high-contrast micro-typography, and a central tactile 3D product focal point.

* **Target Viewport:** Fullscreen edge-to-edge (`100vw` × `100vh`, `overflow: hidden`).
* **Frame Stripping:** All external mockup bezels, device borders, outer canvas margins, and mockup drop shadows shown in the reference image are discarded. The UI surface expands to fill the entire browser window seamlessly.

## 3. Typography System

All typefaces use modern, geometric grotesques with tight tracking on headings and wide tracking on micro-labels.

### Font Families
1. **Primary Headline & Display:**
   * **Ideal:** `Syne` (Bold / ExtraBold) or `Monument Extended` (Regular / Bold)
   * **Fallback Stack:** `Neue Haas Grotesk Display Pro`, `Druk Wide`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial Black`, sans-serif
2. **Body, Navigation & Metadata:**
   * **Ideal:** `Inter` or `Space Grotesk`
   * **Fallback Stack:** `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `San Francisco`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif

* **Position:** Absolute, pinned bottom-left: `left: 56px`, `bottom: 52px`.

### 4.3. Bottom Center Carousel / Pagination

## 5. Hero Visual Asset Specification

The centerpiece is an organic 3D glass product sculpture/sconce dish.

```
       .-''''-.
     .'  _.._  '. <--- Folded/curled translucent glass lip
    /  .'    '.  \
   |  /   (•)  \  | <--- Concentric bowl & warm brass center stud
    \  '.____.'  /
     '.        .'
       '-....-'
```

### 5.1. Asset Visual Details
* **Subject:** An organic, asymmetrical blown-glass pebble dish or modern architectural wall sconce.
* **Top-Left Lip:** An undulating, folded-over lip with a crisp, refractive rim edge casting an interior shadow.
* **Core Cavity:** A deeply recessed smooth concave funnel tinted in graduated smokey-slate charcoal (`#4D5660` down to `#2A3036`).
* **Center Pivot/Stud:** A flush, cylindrical polished warm brass/brushed gold medallion (`#DFBD8C`) with a subtle top-lit directional specular highlight.
* **Material Finish:** Semi-translucent matte frosted glass / etched crystal with soft sub-surface scattering.
* **Format:** High-resolution transparent PNG or lossless WebP (strictly transparent background with clean alpha channels preserving soft exterior contact drop-shadows).

### 5.2. Size & Placement
* **Dimensions:** Approximately `460px × 460px` to `520px × 520px` at standard `1440 × 900` desktop resolution (approx. `35%` to `38%` of the viewport height).
* **Positioning:**
  * Horizontally centered (`left: 50%`, `transform: translate(-50%, -50%)`).
  * Vertically offset slightly upward (`top: 48%` to `49%`) to maintain optical weight balance above the bottom pagination dots.
* **Shadow:** Casts a wide, diffuse, ultra-soft ambient occlusion shadow toward the bottom-right onto the background surface (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`, blur radius `60px`).

## 6. Micro-Interactions & States

* **Nav Links (`PRODUCT`, `LAYOUT`, `DESIGN`, `LUSON`):**
  * Default: `#4A4D52`.
  * Hover: `#0A0A0A` with transition `color 0.25s ease`.
* **Icons (Search & Cart):**
  * Subtle hover scale `1.1` and stroke contrast enhancement.
* **Pagination Dots:**
  * Cursor pointer on inactive dots.
  * Hovering expands the dot scale to `1.25`.
* **Centerpiece:**
  * Subtle 3D parallax reaction (`translate3d` and micro-rotation of `±2deg` to `±4deg`) coupled to mouse movement across the desktop plane.
