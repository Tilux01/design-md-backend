# Design Specification: Ethan Park Portfolio (Hero Section)

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
- **View:** Full-screen viewport takeover (100vw, 100vh).
- **Core Stacking Layers (Z-index order from back to front):**
  1. **Base Background:** Solid dark slate-gray fill covering the entire viewport.
  2. **Background Portrait Image:** A low-contrast, moody portrait placed centrally, spanning vertically to anchor the composition.
  3. **UI Container / Header Bar:** Fixed horizontal bar spanning the top edge.
  4. **Typography Overlays:** Centralized title stack and bio paragraph container.
  5. **Footer Branding:** Large, bold hero text locked to the bottom foreground.

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary Background (Base):** `#1A2026` (Dark Slate Gray)
- **Image Overlay Tint:** Dark neutral shadow blending the silhouette into the background.
- **Primary Text Color (White):** `#FFFFFF`
- **Secondary UI/Text Color (Muted Gray):** `#CCCCCC` (for secondary labels/nav items)

---

## 3. Typography & Font Stack
### Primary Font Family (Hero Branding / Headings)
- **Style:** Heavy, geometric, bold sans-serif with a wide tracking.
- **Suggested Font Stack:** 
  - `Impact`, `Arial Black`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif.
- **Fallback Hierarchy:** If `Impact` or `Arial Black` is unavailable, use `Helvetica Neue` with a heavy `900` font weight, compressed letter-spacing adjustments, and capitalized text transforms.

### Secondary Font Family (Body / Navigation / Subtitles)
- **Style:** Clean, highly legible neo-grotesque sans-serif.
- **Suggested Font Stack:**
  - `Helvetica Neue`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, sans-serif.
- **Fallback Hierarchy:** Standard system sans-serif stack.

---

## 4. Element-by-Element Specifications

### A. Top Navigation Bar
- **Position:** Absolute / Fixed at top (Pinned `top: 0`, `left: 0`, `right: 0`).
- **Layout:** Flexbox, spaced evenly with `space-between`.
- **Padding:** `20px 40px` (responsive).
- **Items:**
  - Left: `ABOUT`
  - Center: `WORKS`
  - Right: `CONTACT`
- **Typography:** 
  - Font: Secondary Sans-Serif (`Helvetica Neue`), uppercase, letter-spacing: `2px`, weight: `700`.
  - Font Size: `14px`.
  - Color: `#FFFFFF`.

---

### B. Background Portrait Image
- **Description:** A high-contrast, moody photographic side-profile silhouette of a person facing right, wearing a dark cap and showing textured dreadlocks falling past the ear. The lighting highlights the jawline, nose, and cap brim while the rest of the body fades into the dark slate background.
- **Background Status:** The subject is silhouetted against a dark studio background (not strictly transparent/cutout, but blended seamlessly via low-light matching).
- **Placement:** Centered horizontally (`transform: translateX(-50%)`, `left: 50%`), pinned vertically to cover the central screen area.
- **Rotation / Transform:** No rotation; strictly neutral 0-degree side profile.
- **Z-Index:** Placed behind text layers, just above the solid base background.

---

### C. Central Title Stack
- **Position:** Absolutely positioned in the mid-section, overlaying the portrait.
- **Alignment:** Center-aligned text block.
- **Structure (Top to Bottom):**
  1. `CREATIVE DIRECTOR`
     - Font: Bold sans-serif, uppercase, slightly tracked out.
     - Color: `#FFFFFF`.
  2. `DIGITAL DESIGNER`
     - Font: Bold sans-serif, uppercase.
     - Color: `#FFFFFF`.
  3. `ART DIRECTION`
     - Font: Bold sans-serif, uppercase.
     - Color: `#FFFFFF`.

---

### D. Bio Paragraph Container
- **Position:** Placed just above the massive footer title, centered.
- **Max Width:** `700px` (constrained for readability).
- **Text Content:** 
  > "Ethan Park works at the intersection of design, strategy, and visual culture, balancing roles as a creative director, designer, and researcher. His approach blends clarity with experimentation, shaping identities and narratives that resonate across cultural and commercial landscapes with a refined, purpose-driven vision."
- **Typography:**
  - Font: Secondary Sans-Serif (`Helvetica Neue`).
  - Font Size: `13px` / Line-height: `1.5`.
  - Color: `#E0E0E0`.
  - Alignment: Centered.
- **Decorative Divider:** A thin horizontal line featuring a small diamond icon (`◆`) in the exact center, positioned directly above the main footer name.

---

### E. Footer Branding (Massive Name Plate)
- **Position:** Locked to the bottom edge (`bottom: 0`, `left: 0`, `right: 0`).
- **Background Container:** Solid black or ultra-dark panel wrapping the text to ensure absolute legibility against the background portrait.
- **Content:** `ETHAN PARK®`
- **Typography:**
  - Font: Primary Heavy Sans-Serif (`Impact` / `Arial Black`).
  - Font Size: Fluid/Responsive scale (approx. `12vw` to ensure it spans edge-to-edge horizontally).
  - Letter Spacing: Tight or slightly negative (`-1px` to `0px`).
  - Color: `#FFFFFF`.
  - Alignment: Center-aligned, filling the container width uniformly.
  - Trademark Symbol (`®`): Subscript/superscript scaled relative to the main text size, aligned cleanly to the top-right of the "K".

---

## 5. Responsive Behavior Rules
- **Desktop (>1024px):** Maintain exact multi-layered absolute spacing as described above.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** Scale down the bottom hero text (`ETHAN PARK®`) proportionally so it does not wrap awkwardly; reduce padding on the top navigation.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Stack the navigation into a hamburger menu or simplify inline; scale down body text to `11px` with increased line-height; reduce bottom title font size to fit viewport width without horizontal scrolling.
