# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Fintech Neo-Luxury / Dark Mode Glassmorphism with Editorial Minimalism.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio focusing attention on central interactive 3D credit card mockups and punchy value-prop typography.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered glass cards with fine border gradients (`rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`), backdrop blur (`blur-xl`), and warm ambient glow orbs behind the primary focal point.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient background radial warmth gradients, high-tracking uppercase pill badges with live pulsing indicators, frosted glass cards with specular top-edge highlights.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-orange-500 inline-block"></span>`.
