# Design Specification: OURA & CO. Website Clone

This document provides the exact design specifications, layout hierarchy, typography, color palette, and interactive behavior needed to build a pixel-perfect, full-screen clone of the provided OURA & CO. web page.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

- **View:** Full-screen layout (100% width, responsive scaling).
- **Layout Model:** CSS Grid and Flexbox.
- **Scroll Behavior:** Smooth scrolling (`scroll-behavior: smooth`).
- **Global Background Color:** 
  - Hero Section: Dark/Muted earthy backdrop overlaid on a high-end interior photograph.
  - Lower Content Sections: Warm Off-White/Cream (`#F2EFE9` or similar neutral warm tone).

---

## 2. Color Palette

| Color Role | Hex / Description | Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Dark (Text & Accents)** | `#2B231E` (Dark Espresso Brown) | Main headings, large numbers, logos, footer box backgrounds. |
| **Warm Off-White (Background)** | `#F2EFE9` | Lower section background, card backings. |
| **Pure White (Text)** | `#FFFFFF` | Hero headline text, navigation links, overlay button text. |
| **Muted Earthy Overlay** | `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)` to `0.4` | Darkens the hero background image for readability. |
| **Interactive Elements** | `#FFFFFF` with brown text | "GET MY FEELING" pill button. |

---

## 3. Typography & Font Stack

To achieve the editorial, high-end architectural feel of the site:

### **Primary Heading & Display Font (Serif)**
*Used for large titles: "WE DESIGN THE FEELING OF YOU", "OURA & CO.", and statistics numbers (4, 20, 62).*
- **Primary:** `Playfair Display`, `Cormorant Garamond`, or a bespoke Editorial Serif.
- **Fallback:** `Georgia`, `Times New Roman`, `serif`.
- **Weight:** Regular (400) to Bold (700).

### **Navigation & Body Font (Sans-Serif)**
*Used for navbar items, subheadings, and descriptive body text.*
- **Primary:** `Montserrat`, `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`.
- **Fallback:** `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
- **Weight:** Light (300) for body, Medium (500) for navigation.

## 5. Animations & Interactions (CSS / JS)

1. **Hero Image Motion:** 
   - A subtle horizontal pan/zoom effect (Ken Burns effect) on the background image.
2. **Ghost Figure Animation:**
   - The walking figure in the hero has a slight motion-blur/opacity effect (`opacity: 0.8; filter: blur(0.5px);`) to simulate movement.
3. **Hover States:**
   - Navigation links transition smoothly to a lighter opacity or underline on hover.
   - The `GET MY FEELING` button subtly inverts colors or scales up (`transform: scale(1.02);`) on hover.
   - The dark callout card on the bottom right slightly deepens its shadow or shifts tone on hover.
