# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Luxury Real Estate / Clean Mobile App UI (Dark & Light Glassmorphic Minimalism).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio focusing on immersive photography, clean typography scales, and modular card layout systems.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered frosted glass panels (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), crisp 1px subtle borders, deep soft shadows (`0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)`), and floating pill navigation bars.

# 2. Color Palette & Design Tokens

## Color Token Definitions
- **Primary Brand Color & Variants**: Default (`#111111`), Hover (`#222222`), Active (`#000000`), Subdued (`#666666`).
- **Secondary & Accent Colors**: Accent white/glow (`#FFFFFF`), success green (`#10B981`), price tag dark pill (`#1A1A1A`).
- **Background & Surface Architecture**: Canvas background (`#F5F2EC`), Surface 1 Cards (`#1E1E1E` / `#FFFFFF`), Surface 2 Modals/Bottom Nav (`rgba(15, 15, 15, 0.85)`), Surface 3 Floating Pills (`#FFFFFF` / `#111111`).
- **Border & Divider Scale**: Base border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), muted border (`#E5E5E5`), active glow border (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Text Color Hierarchy**: Primary text (`#111111` / `#FFFFFF`), Secondary/Muted text (`#888888`), Disabled text (`#CCCCCC`), Inverse text (`#FFFFFF`), Accent text (`#1A1A1A`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Frosted glass panels, high-contrast monochrome UI elements, exact iOS status bars, pill-shaped floating toolbars.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> `&#9679;`
