# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Futuristic Cyber-Organic Glassmorphism, Cinematic Sci-Fi Concept Art.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High ambient darkness ratio, centered focal interaction point, minimalist composition emphasizing negative space and glowing specular highlights.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Deep obsidian void background layered with chromatic metallic chrome hands, volumetric blue-violet atmospheric lighting, and central atomic energy spark.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-intensity specular reflections, chromatic light bleed (blue/violet), central energy singularity, metallic chrome shaders.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.1)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;`
