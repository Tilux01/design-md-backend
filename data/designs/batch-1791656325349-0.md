# Moonie App — Design System & Full-Screen Onboarding Specification

This document provides the exact UI/UX design specifications to create a pixel-perfect, full-screen clone of the **Moonie** sleep and relaxation mobile onboarding application.

---

### 1.2 Typography

*   **Primary Typeface:** **SF Pro Rounded** (iOS) or **Nunito** (Google Fonts).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, 'SF Pro Rounded', 'Nunito', 'Quicksand', sans-serif`
*   **Font Weights:** Regular (`400`), Medium (`500`), Semi-Bold (`600`), Bold (`700`).

#### Type Hierarchy
*   **Hero / Display (App Logo):** 36px | Bold (`700`) | Letter-spacing: `-0.5px`
*   **Screen Header 1 (H1):** 28px | Bold (`700`) | Line-height: 1.2 | Color: `#FFFFFF`
*   **Screen Header 2 (H2):** 20px | Semi-Bold (`600`) | Line-height: 1.3
*   **Subtitle / Prompt:** 14px | Medium (`500`) | Color: `#B8C7E0`
*   **Card Title / List Label:** 15px | Medium (`500`) | Color: `#FFFFFF`
*   **Button Text:** 16px | Semi-Bold (`600`) | Letter-spacing: `0.2px`
*   **Caption / Disclaimer:** 11px – 12px | Regular (`400`) | Color: `#B8C7E0`

### 1.3 Starfield Background Effect
Every screen (except the splash landscape) features an ambient starry night background:
*   Small 1px to 2.5px circular white stars (`opacity: 0.3` to `0.9`), randomly scattered.
*   Certain stars possess a faint soft 4-point sparkle crosshair (`opacity: 0.8`).
*   Positioning: Predominantly clustered in the top 60% of the screen.

## 3. Visual Assets & Illustrations Specification

### 3.1 Splash Night Scenery (Screen 1)
*   **Subject:** A tranquil night countryside landscape.
*   **Elements:**
    *   **Sky:** Transitioning from deep midnight navy at the top to a vibrant cyan-lit horizon.
    *   **Moon:** Large circular full moon, positioned upper-right, chalk-white with faint textured craters, surrounded by a soft, diffuse cyan-blue glow aura.
    *   **House / Cottage:** Silhouette of a small single-story country house with a gabled pitched roof, placed on a dark rolling hill. It has two glowing warm-white square windows on its front facade.
    *   **Hills & Fog:** Gentle curved horizon hills overlapping with low-altitude translucent cyan night mist/clouds resting in front of the house base.
*   **Placement:** Full-bleed background illustration occupying 100% of the screen.

### 3.2 Sleeping Fox Mascot (Screen 6 — Subscription)
*   **Subject:** A peaceful sleeping red fox illustration.
*   **Asset Properties:** Backgroundless (transparent PNG/SVG).
*   **Visual Details:**
    *   The fox is curled up in a crescent pose, eyes closed with relaxed curved eyelashes, light orange/ginger coat, cream/white underbelly and muzzle.
    *   It rests atop a mound of stylized foliage: deep emerald leaves, fan-shaped palm fronds, tiny star-shaped yellow night flowers, and miniature floating stardust specks.
*   **Placement:** Centered horizontally, directly below the "Moonie PLUS" brand mark. Dimensions: Approx. `220px × 140px`. No tilt/rotation (0°).

## 5. Animation & Interaction Guidelines

1.  **Selection Feedback:**
    *   Tapping any glass card or list item transitions `border-color` to `#F080C0` and animates `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(240, 128, 192, 0.4)` over `180ms ease-out`.
    *   Scale effect: Gentle `scale(0.98)` on press-down, returning to `1.0`.
2.  **Screen Transitions:**
    *   Horizontal slide navigation (`push`/`pop`) along the onboarding funnel with `300ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
3.  **Time Picker Physics:**
    *   Inertial scroll with soft haptic ticks upon crossing each hour/minute boundary. Center row triggers haptic feedback.
