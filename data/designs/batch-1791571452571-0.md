# UI/UX Specification: Smart Online Meditations Web Application

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
- **Canvas/Viewport:** Full-screen responsive layout centered within a light grey canvas background (`#f0f1f4`). The application container uses a clean, rounded white card (`#ffffff`) with subtle drop shadows and generous padding.
- **Grid System:** Asymmetric multi-column dashboard layout utilizing CSS Flexbox/Grid.
- **Border Radius:** Consistent use of soft, pill-shaped corners (`8px`, `12px`, `24px`, and fully rounded `9999px` badges).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
- **Primary Font Family:** Inter (Fallback: `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`).
- **Heading Styles:**
  - **Main Title ("Smart online meditations"):** ~32px, Bold (Weight: 700), Dark Navy (`#111827`).
  - **Section Headings ("Physiological state of users", "Smart Lessons #95"):** ~16px – 18px, Semi-bold (Weight: 600).
- **Body & Metadata Text:**
  - **Paragraphs/Descriptions:** 12px – 14px, Regular (Weight: 400), Muted Gray (`#4b5563`).
  - **Captions, Tags, & Dates:** 10px – 12px, Medium (Weight: 500).

---

## 3. Color Palette
- **Primary Accent / Interactive Blue:** `#2563eb` (Deep Royal Blue) / `#3b82f6` (Vibrant Blue).
- **Backgrounds:** 
  - Outer Canvas: `#f0f1f4`
  - App Dashboard: `#ffffff`
  - Soft Card Backgrounds / Light Tint: `#f8fafc` to `#f1f5f9`
- **Text Colors:**
  - Primary Headers: `#0f172a` (Slate 900)
  - Body Text: `#475569` (Slate 600)
  - Muted/Inactive: `#94a3b8` (Slate 400)
- **Functional Colors:**
  - Success / Active States: `#2563eb`
  - Border Separators: `#e2e8f0` (Light Gray)

---

## 4. Component-by-Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation & Header
- **Top Bar Left:** Hamburger menu icon (`three horizontal lines`), followed by pill navigation tabs:
  - Active Tab: **"Dashboard"** (Pill background: `#0f172a` solid dark, Text: White).
  - Inactive Tabs: **"Activity"**, **"Revitalize"**, **"Routine"** (White background with thin border, Dark text).
- **Top Bar Right:**
  - User profile avatar (Circular photographic thumbnail).
  - Utility icon buttons with subtle circular backgrounds (Video/Yoga icon, Audio/Sound icon).
  - Current day indicator badge (**"Tue"**).
  - Settings and grid widgets icons.
  - Dropdown: **"About Zen State"** with a chevron-down indicator.

- **Left Vertical Sidebar Navigation:**
  - Minimalist thin rail containing vector icon buttons (Home/Dashboard, Analytics/Flow chart, Yoga pose, Audio waves).
  - Active state highlighted with a blue accent bar or dot.
  - Bottom arrow navigation button.

---

### B. Hero Section ("Smart online meditations")
- **Copywriting Block:**
  - Title: *Smart online meditations*
  - Subtitle: *Using technology and expert instructors, we offer you the opportunity to dive into a world of serenity and mindfulness.*
- **Featured Banner Card:**
  - Soft rounded container with a light gray/slate background overlay.
  - **Image:** Photographic portrait of a woman in activewear practicing yoga, set against a calm, minimal background.
  - **Floating Badge (Top Left):** Circular badge text reading *"FREE LESSON // VIEW PART OF"*.
  - **Floating Accessory (Top Right):** Sleek modern black water bottle icon overlay.
  - **Action Button ("Join community"):** Vibrant royal blue pill button (`#2563eb`) with white text and a right chevron arrow `>`.

---

### C. Lesson Card ("Smart Lessons #95")
- **Card Container:** White background, subtle border, rounded corners.
- **Header:** Title *"Smart Lessons #95"* paired with a category tag badge *"Yoga"* (light background, rounded pill).
- **Description:** *"Let our advanced AI guide you through a session designed to rejuvenate your spirit and enhance your inner calm. Immerse yourself in the gentle flow of yoga postures."*
- **Progress Indicator:** 
  - Linear progress bar filled partially with solid blue.
  - Counter text: `94 / 315` accompanied by an external link/arrow icon.
- **Hashtags / Tags:** Pill badges with `#` prefix: `#health`, `#online` (blue accent), `#takecare`, `#meditations`.

---

### D. Physiological State & Analytics Widgets (Right Column Top)
- **Widget 1: Physiological state of users (Bar Chart)**
  - Card header with a small circular yoga thumbnail.
  - Vertical bar chart tracking days of the week (**Mon** through **Sun**).
  - Bars rendered in solid blue (`#3b82f6`), with specific data-point circular markers highlighting active peaks.
- **Widget 2: Free and New Lessons Banner**
  - Dark or styled promotional box featuring lesson info, meditation duration metrics (`↑ 29.5%`), interactive day selector pills (`Friday, Sep 28, 2023`), time slots (`07:00 AM`, `11:00 AM`), and a primary **"Take Lesson"** CTA button.

---

### E. Metrics, Circular Stats, & Bottom Widgets
- **Widget 1: Brainwave Patterns**
  - Features an outlined brain icon, quick `+` action button, title *"Brainwave patterns"*, numerical value `120,45`, and an audio frequency waveform visualizer line.
- **Widget 2: Circular Progress & Percentage**
  - Concentric circular stroke progress rings indicating completion data (`28 Sep`, `89.2%`, `20 Minutes`).
  - Tag: *"Yoga Energy Online"*.
- **Widget 3: Meditation Excellence (Line Chart)**
  - Card title: *"Meditation Excellence"* with a *"Pro AI - based Chart"* badge.
  - Rating badge: `★ 4.7`.
  - Smooth curved line chart mapping performance across calendar dates (**24 Fri** to **2 Sun**), with highlighted date indicator pills at the bottom.
- **Widget 4: AI Yoga Instructor Preview**
  - Card showcasing daily streak count (`3 Days`) and a photographic thumbnail of a yoga instructor holding a purple yoga mat.

3. **Yoga Instructor Photo (Bottom Widget):** Instructor standing confidently holding a rolled-up purple yoga mat in a clean, modern studio space.
4. **Water Bottle Graphic:** Product-style render of a sleek black ergonomic water bottle.
