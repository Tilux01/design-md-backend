# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Fantasy gaming landing page, ethereal Japanese folklore aesthetics, cinematic dark-fantasy sunrise/sunset atmosphere.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Immersive full-screen background illustration with structured asymmetric floating UI panels, high emphasis on atmospheric backdrop and clear readability zones.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered glassmorphic cards with subtle white/gold borders, frosted backdrop blurs (`blur(12px)` to `blur(20px)`), and warm amber/blue ambient glows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient pink/purple atmospheric gradients, glowing glassmorphic CTA buttons, stylized Japanese kanji typography overlay, floating petal particle accents.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:

  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-400 inline-block"></span>`
