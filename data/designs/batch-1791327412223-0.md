# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Cinematic Dark Fantasy / Editorial Gaming Landing Page with immersive atmospheric depth, high-contrast typography, and glassmorphic micro-surfaces.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Immersive full-viewport hero layout with a high spatial footprint, balancing expansive atmospheric background imagery with highly focused, dense typography blocks and floating glass cards.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered depth using semi-transparent dark glass surfaces (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), subtle gold/yellow accent borders, and atmospheric radial illumination orbs.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, atmospheric amber glow backdrops, frosted glassmorphism overlays, subtle noise grain textures, active pulse status dots.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `700`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-amber-400 inline-block animate-pulse"></span>`
