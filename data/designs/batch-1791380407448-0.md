# Comprehensive UI/UX & Technical Design Specification: Modeza E-Commerce Landing Page

---

## 1. Global Page Layout & Responsive Architecture

*   **Design Paradigm:** Minimalist, High-Fashion Editorial E-Commerce.
*   **Viewport Strategy:** Designed natively for desktop viewports (`max-width: 1440px` centered container with a full-width background surface).
*   **Grid System:** 12-column structural grid with structural gutters (`24px` on desktop).
*   **Background Surfaces:**
    *   **Primary Canvas:** Soft warm off-white/cream `#F7F5F0`.
    *   **Hero Accent Circle:** Deep burnt orange/rust radial splash `#D9531E` to `#FF7F32`.
    *   **Card Surfaces:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` with ultra-soft diffuse drop shadows (`0px 10px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`).

---

## 2. Typography System & Font Stack Specifications

### Primary Heading Font (Serif Editorial)
*   **Font Family:** `Cormorant Garamond`, `Playfair Display`, Georgia, serif.
*   **Usage:** Hero titles, section headings, numbers.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Cormorant Garamond', Georgia, serif;`

### Secondary UI Font (Clean Sans-Serif)
*   **Font Family:** `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif.
*   **Usage:** Navigation, body text, buttons, tags, metadata.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Inter', system-ui, sans-serif;`

---

## 3. Section-by-Section Design & Layout Specifications

### A. Navigation Header
*   **Layout:** Fixed or sticky top bar, horizontal flex layout with space-between alignment.
*   **Components:**
    *   **Brand Logo:** Left-aligned. Text "Modeza." in `Cormorant Garamond` (italicized dot accent).
    *   **Navigation Links:** Center-aligned (`Home`, `Shop`, `Collections`, `About`, `Lookbook`, `Contact`). Font size: `14px`, Weight: `500`, Color: `#1A1A1A`. Active link (`Home`) underlined or highlighted with warm copper `#C17D46`.
    *   **Utility Icons & CTA:** Right-aligned search icon (`🔍`), Cart counter badge (`🛍️ [2]`), and a primary pill-shaped button (`Shop Now`) with dark charcoal fill (`#1A1A1A`) and white text.

---

### B. Hero Section
*   **Layout:** Asymmetrical two-column hero. Left side typography/CTAs; Right side dynamic editorial image composition.
*   **Left Column Elements:**
    *   **Eyebrow Text:** `NEW COLLECTION 2026` (Uppercase, tracked out, `11px`, weight `600`, color `#C17D46`).
    *   **Main Hero Heading:** "Timeless Elegance" set in large serif typography (`64px`–`72px`). "Elegance" is italicized and styled in a warm golden-orange tone (`#D97736`).
    *   **Body Copy:** "Minimal designs. Maximum impact. Redefine your wardrobe with pieces that speak sophistication." (`15px`, line-height `1.6`, color `#6B6B6B`).
    *   **CTA Button Group:**
        *   Primary Button: "Explore Collection" (`#1A1A1A` background, white text, pill shape, trailing arrow icon).
        *   Secondary Button: "Watch Latest Show" (Transparent background, dark border, leading play icon).
    *   **Social Proof Badges:**
        *   Badge 1: `10K+ Happy Customers` with a shopping bag icon.
        *   Badge 2: `4.9 Customer Rating` with a filled star icon.
*   **Right Column Elements:**
    *   **Visual Graphic:** Cutout model image wearing neutral tone layered fashion pieces, positioned inside a dynamic organic brush-stroke / solid orange circle backdrop.
    *   **Trust Badge Overlay:** Top-right floating card: `✓ Premium Quality Assured`.

---

### C. Value Proposition Banner (Floating Feature Bar)
*   **Layout:** Single-row horizontal card overlapping the hero and category sections. White pill-shaped container with a subtle shadow.
*   **Grid Structure:** 4 equal columns divided by subtle vertical dividers:
    1.  **Free Shipping** (Truck Icon) — "On orders over $100"
    2.  **Easy Returns** (Refresh Icon) — "30-day return policy"
    3.  **Secure Payment** (Shield Icon) — "100% protected"
    4.  **24/7 Support** (Headset Icon) — "We're here to help"

---

### D. "Shop by Category" Section
*   **Header Row:**
    *   Title: "Shop by Category" (`Cormorant Garamond`, `36px`, italicized accent on "Category").
    *   Action Link: "View All Categories →" (Right-aligned, interactive copper hover state).
*   **Category Card Grid (4 Items):**
    1.  **Dresses:** Model in rust/orange midi dress. Subtext: `24 Items`.
    2.  **Tops:** Model in neutral blouse and trousers. Subtext: `18 Items`.
    3.  **Outerwear:** Model in beige trench coat and hat. Subtext: `20 Items`.
    4.  **Bottoms:** Model in denim/casual bottoms. Subtext: `16 Items`.
*   **Card Design Specifics:**
    *   Rounded vertical image cards with warm cream internal card backgrounds.
    *   Hover effect: Image zoom (`scale(1.03)`), arrow buttons slide into view.
    *   Carousel Navigation: Circular pagination arrows (`←` / `→`) positioned on the outer left and right edges.

---

### E. Promotional Banner ("Summer Sale")
*   **Layout:** Full-width asymmetrical promotional card split into two distinct visual zones.
*   **Left Zone (Content Area):**
    *   Eyebrow: "Summer Sale" (`#D97736`, uppercase).
    *   Heading: "Up to 50% Off" (`Cormorant Garamond`, `48px`).
    *   Description: "Refresh your style this season with our exclusive collection: limited time offer!"
    *   CTA: "Shop the Sale" button (`#1A1A1A` background).
*   **Right Zone (Visual Area):**
    *   Editorial model image wearing a floral summer dress, cropped inside a circular frame.
    *   Floating Seal: Circular badge overlapping the graphic reading "Limited Offer." rotated slightly for an organic feel.

## 5. Animation & Interaction Specifications

*   **Page Load / Entrance Animations:**
    *   **Hero Elements:** Staggered fade-in and slide-up animation (`opacity: 0` to `1`, `translateY: 20px` to `0`) with a `0.6s` cubic-bezier easing curve.
    *   **Hero Orange Circle:** Scale-up reveal from center (`transform: scale(0.95)` to `1`).
*   **Hover States:**
    *   **Buttons:** Background color shifts from `#1A1A1A` to `#D97736` with smooth transition (`transition: all 0.3s ease`).
    *   **Category Cards:** Image zooms smoothly (`transform: scale(1.05)` over `0.4s`), navigational arrow buttons gain an accent background tint.
*   **Carousel / Navigation Controls:**
    *   Clicking carousel arrows (`←` / `→`) smoothly translates the category card track by `320px` using CSS `scroll-behavior: smooth`.
