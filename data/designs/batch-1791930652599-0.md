# Design System & Implementation Guide: Creative Agency Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Colors
* **Page Dimensions:** Full viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), overflow hidden.
* **Base Background:** Off-white/light-gray (`#F3F3F3`).
* **Primary Accent (Teal):** `#007385`
* **Text Primary (Dark):** `#222222`
* **Text Muted (Gray):** `#777777`
* **Embossed Element Color:** `#FFFFFF` with drop shadow `0 10px 25px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`.

---

## 2. Typography
* **Primary Font (Headings & UI):** `Montserrat`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif.
  * *Navigation & Tags:* Weight 600, uppercase, letter-spacing `0.15em`, size `11px`.
  * *Body Headline:* Weight 700, size `28px`, line-height `1.2`.
* **Accent/3D Typography ("CREATIVE"):** `Futura`, `Montserrat`, or geometric sans-serif (Light / Weight 300), size `~18vw`, uppercase, pure white with soft, directional bottom-right shadows to produce a 3D cut-out effect.

---

## 3. Navigation Bar (Top)
* **Position:** Fixed at top, `padding: 30px 50px`, width `100%`, flexbox layout.
* **Left Links:** `HOME`, `ABOUT`, `PORTFOLIO`, `CONTACT` (horizontal, gap `32px`, dark gray text).
* **Right Elements:**
  * Counter: `01 / 03` (medium gray).
  * Search Icon: Minimalist magnifying glass.
  * Menu Icon: Two horizontal stacked bars (hamburger style).

---

## 4. Visual Imagery & Center Stage

### Background Typography
* **Text:** `"CREATIVE"`
* **Position:** Centered horizontally and vertically across the viewport behind the main hand graphic.

### Central Foreground Graphic
* **Description:** Cutout, backgroundless PNG of a stylized human forearm and hand.
  * **Color:** Matte deep-teal/petroleum blue (`#007385`).
  * **Pose:** Arm angles upward from bottom-left towards upper-right; fingers loosely curled inward.
  * **Detail:** Thick, glossy white paint pouring from top, draping across the fingers and dripping down the inner wrist.
* **Placement & Layering:**
  * Sits centrally, directly in front of the embossed "CREATIVE" text (overlapping letters `E`, `A`, `T`, `I`).
  * Base of forearm originates around 25% from the bottom-left edge.

---

## 5. UI Accents & Content Blocks

### Left Column Elements
* **Vertical Label:** `"Latest project"` rotated -90 degrees, paired with a thin vertical rule (`height: 40px`). Positioned mid-height, `30px` from the left edge.
* **Bottom-Left Callout:**
  * Tag: Small teal line accent + `"• LEARN MORE"` (`#007385`, `10px`).
  * Headline: `"We create beautiful"` / `"things for you"` (`26px`, bold).
  * Subtext: `"2018"` (`12px`, muted gray).

### Center-Bottom
* **Scroll Indicator:** Minimal downward-pointing chevron (`v`) centered at the bottom margin.

### Right Column Elements
* **Pointer Annotation:** Fine gray line linking from the forearm to a right-aligned text label: `"• CREATIVE AGENCY"`.
* **Social Icons:** Vertically stacked on the right border: Pinterest, Twitter, Facebook (`font-size: 14px`, muted gray).
* **Bottom-Right Video Card:**
  * Floating card (`~220px × 90px`), light gray/translucent backdrop.
  * Label: `"watch video"` (top-left of card).
  * Graphic: Model/silhouette portrait.
  * Play Button: Circular teal button with a centered white play triangle.
