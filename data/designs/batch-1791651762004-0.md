# Design Specification: AgentAI Website Clone

This specification provides a precise blueprint for recreating the provided landing page. It is structured to guide a frontend developer or UI/UX designer in building an exact clone, omitting device mockups or container frames to focus purely on the full-screen web experience.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

### Color Palette
*   **Background (Primary):** Deep Rich Black (`#080808` to `#000000` gradients)
*   **Accent Color (Vibrant Red-Orange):** `#FF3300` / `#FF2200` (Used for active states, glowing elements, key highlights, and CTA buttons)
*   **Text (Primary/White):** `#FFFFFF`
*   **Text (Secondary/Muted Gray):** `#888888`
*   **Card Backgrounds:** Dark charcoal translucent layers (`rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.7)` with subtle border strokes `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`)

### Typography & Fonts
*   **Primary Font Family:** Clean, geometric sans-serif (e.g., **Inter**, **Plus Jakarta Sans**, or **SF Pro Display**).
*   **Fallbacks:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Hierarchy:**
    *   **Navbar Links:** 13px, Medium weight, Uppercase/Sentence Case.
    *   **Hero Heading ("Your AI Sprint Team On Demand"):** Extra bold, massive scale (~56px–72px), tight line-height, gradient text effect transitioning from white to red-orange on specific keywords ("On Demand").
    *   **Body Copy:** 14px–16px, Regular weight, leading 1.6 for high readability.
    *   **Stat Numbers:** Large, bold display numbers (e.g., "230+", "120+").

## 3. Responsive Behavior & Interactions
*   **Hover States:** Cards should feature a subtle border glow (transitioning to brighter red/white opacity) on mouse hover.
*   **Buttons:** Scale up slightly (`transform: scale(1.02)`) or shift color saturation on hover.
*   **Fluid Layout:** On smaller screens (tablets/mobiles), stack the multi-column hero and service grids vertically while maintaining the dark immersive color palette and red accent lighting.
