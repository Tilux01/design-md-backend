This is a detailed `design.md` specification for recreating the "Music Space" player interface. This document focuses on a full-screen implementation, excluding the external phone frame.

---

# Design Specification: Music Space Player

## 1. General Theme & Layout
*   **Background Color:** Solid White (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Primary Accent Color:** Deep Royal Purple (`#482677`).
*   **Secondary Accent Color:** Soft Lavender/Pinkish-White (`#F3E5F5`) for icons on dark backgrounds.
*   **Layout Style:** Minimalist, vertical stack with a central organic wave element.

---

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** A monospaced or "typewriter" style sans-serif font. 
    *   *Recommended:* **"Space Mono"** (Google Fonts) or **"Courier Prime"**.
    *   *Fallbacks:* `Monaco`, `Consolas`, `Lucida Console`, `monospace`.
*   **Text Details:**
    *   **Header ("Music Space"):** Center-aligned, size ~18px, weight: 600.
    *   **Song Title ("Fly to the Moon"):** Left-aligned within the purple wave, size ~20px, weight: 700, color: `#FFFFFF`.
    *   **Artist Name ("Ed Peter"):** Left-aligned below the title, size ~14px, weight: 400, color: `#BDBDBD` (Light Grey/Lavender).
    *   **Time Indicators ("2:38", "4:58"):** Size ~12px, weight: 500, color: `#482677`.

---

## 3. Top Navigation Bar
*   **Placement:** Top of the screen, standard padding (approx. 20px from top/sides).
*   **Left Element:** Hamburger Menu icon (three horizontal lines). Color: `#482677`.
*   **Center Element:** Text "Music Space".
*   **Right Element:** Profile Picture. 
    *   *Description:* A small circular crop of a user’s face. The image should be centered in the circle. No border.

---

## 4. The Central Wave & Album Art
*   **The Wave Shape:**
    *   A large, organic, dark purple (`#482677`) shape that spans the full width of the screen.
    *   The top edge starts roughly 25% down the screen, dipping in the middle and rising on the right.
    *   The bottom edge is a deep "S" curve, ending higher on the left and lower on the right.
    *   **Shadow:** A soft drop shadow (`rgba(0,0,0,0.2)`) should be applied to the bottom edge of the wave to give it depth against the white background.
*   **Album Art (Image 1):**
    *   **Placement:** Circular container, positioned on the left side, vertically centered within the purple wave. It slightly overlaps the top and bottom curves of the wave.
    *   **Image Description:** A close-up photo of a male performer (Ed Peter) wearing a fedora hat and sunglasses, singing into a microphone. The lighting is stage-style with heavy red and blue neon tints.
    *   **Properties:** The image is a standard photo cropped into a perfect circle. It has a subtle inner glow or a thin dark purple stroke to blend it into the wave background.
*   **Action Icons:**
    *   Located to the right of the Artist Name.
    *   Icons: Download (arrow down to line), Shuffle/Repeat (intertwined arrows), and Heart (outline).
    *   Color: White or very light lavender.

---

## 5. Playback Controls & Progress
*   **Arc Progress Bar:**
    *   Instead of a straight line, the progress bar is a **thick, curved arc** (part of a circle) located in the lower half of the screen.
    *   **Color:** Deep Purple (`#482677`).
    *   **Progress Indicator:** A small circular "thumb" or bubble sits on the arc at the current time position. Inside this bubble is the text "2:38".
    *   **End Time:** The text "4:58" is placed at the very end of the arc on the right side.
*   **Main Controls:**
    *   Centered horizontally at the bottom of the screen.
    *   **Icons:** Previous (`|<<`), Pause (`||`), and Next (`>>|`).
    *   **Style:** Thick, bold glyphs in Deep Purple (`#482677`). The Pause icon consists of two vertical rounded rectangles.

---

## 6. Image Assets Summary
1.  **Profile Image:** Small, circular, backgroundless (or cropped to circle), featuring a person's headshot.
2.  **Album Art:** Circular, high-contrast stage photography. Red/Pink lighting. Features a man with a hat and microphone. No background transparency needed as it is contained within a circle.

---

## 7. Spacing & Measurements (Relative)
*   **Side Margins:** 10% of screen width.
*   **Wave Height:** Approximately 40% of the total screen height.
*   **Control Section:** Occupies the bottom 25% of the screen.
