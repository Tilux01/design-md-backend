# Design Specification: Japan Travel Hero UI

## 1. Overview & Layout
* **Layout Model**: Fullscreen viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), fixed overflow.
* **Composition**: Dark-themed hero landing page with a photographic background, minimal navigation, left-aligned typography, floating contextual card, right-aligned vertical scroll indicator, and bottom statistics bar.

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Primary Background**: `#0D1117` (Dark moody tone beneath image).
* **Text Primary**: `#FFFFFF` (Headings, active values, high-emphasis text).
* **Text Secondary**: `#B0B3B8` (Nav items, descriptions, labels).
* **Accent Color**: `#00D2D3` / `#38D9D9` (Cyan CTA button).
* **Surface Overlay**: `rgba(20, 24, 27, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)` (Info card).
* **Border/Track**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` (Scroll track, play button ring).

---

## 3. Typography
* **Primary Font**: `Montserrat`, sans-serif.
* **Secondary / Body**: `Inter`, sans-serif.
* **Fallback Stack**: `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.

### Type Scale
* **Main Title**: `Montserrat`, 64px–72px, 800 weight, uppercase, line-height 1.1, tracking `2px`.
* **Section Numbers / Stats**: `Montserrat`, 28px–32px, 700 weight, white.
* **Nav Links / Labels**: `Inter`, 12px–13px, 500 weight, uppercase, tracking `1.5px`.
* **Body / Card Text**: `Inter`, 11px–12px, 400 weight, line-height 1.6, secondary color.

---

## 4. Background Image Specification
* **Description**: Low-angle landscape photo of a traditional Japanese Buddhist temple (irimoya-style dark tiled roof, wooden eaves, illuminated front facade with circular crests) centered in the mid-ground. Foreground features a wide stone-paved courtyard leading to the entrance with a lone figure walking toward the temple. The background contains a stormy, dark overcast sky with dramatic cloud textures and modern high-rises faintly visible on the far right.
* **Type**: Photographic, non-transparent (contains its own dark moody lighting).
* **Placement**: Full-bleed background covering `100%` width and height, `object-fit: cover`, centered alignment. No rotation.

---

### 5.1 Header (Top Bar)
* **Padding**: `40px 60px`, flexbox space-between.
* **Logo (Left)**: "JAPAN." — Bold, tracking `3px`, white, `16px`.
* **Navigation (Right)**:
  * Links: `HOME`, `ABOUT`, `GALLERY`, `FEATURES` (spaced `32px` apart, `12px` font size).
  * Menu Icon: Two horizontal white bars (width: `20px`, gap: `5px`, height: `2px`).

### 5.2 Hero Section (Left-Aligned)
* **Position**: Absolute, left: `60px`, top: `35%`.
* **Title**:
  * Line 1: `ENDLESS`
  * Line 2: `DISCOVERY`
* **Video Action (Below Title, 32px offset)**:
  * Circular Play Button: 40px diameter, 1.5px white border, centered solid white triangle play icon.
  * Label: "WATCH MOVIE", `12px`, bold, uppercase, margin-left `12px`.
* **CTA Button (Below Play Action, 24px offset)**:
  * Cyan background (`#00D2D3`), white/dark text, rounded corners (`4px`), padding `10px 24px`, font size `11px`, bold uppercase.

### 5.3 Right Floating Info Card
* **Position**: Absolute, right: `120px`, top: `55%`, width: `260px`.
* **Style**: Translucent black box (`rgba(15, 20, 25, 0.7)`), 1px subtle border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), padding: `20px`.
* **Content**:
  * Title: "JAPAN" — `12px`, bold, uppercase, white.
  * Body: Brief geographic description, `11px`, color `#B0B3B8`, line-height `1.5`.

### 5.4 Vertical Scroll Indicator (Far Right)
* **Position**: Absolute, right: `40px`, top: `45%`, height: `200px`.
* **Elements**:
  * Top Label: `01` (`12px`, white, bold).
  * Track: `2px` wide vertical rule (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).
  * Active Thumb: Solid white segment filling upper 25% of track.
  * Bottom Label: `04` (`12px`, secondary text).

### 5.5 Footer (Bottom Bar)
* **Position**: Absolute, bottom: `40px`, left: `60px`, right: `60px`.
* **Left Sub-elements**:
  * Social Links: `INSTAGRAM`, `YOUTUBE` (spaced `24px` apart, `11px`, secondary color).
* **Right Sub-elements (Data Counters)**:
  * Row of 3 columns, spaced `40px` apart:
    * Col 1: `2,000` (value, `24px`, white, bold) / `Temples` (label, `11px`, secondary).
    * Col 2: `6,850` (value, `24px`, white, bold) / `Islands` (label, `11px`, secondary).
    * Col 3: `362` (value, `24px`, white, bold) / `Rivers` (label, `11px`, secondary).
