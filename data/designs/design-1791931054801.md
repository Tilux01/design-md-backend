# Design Specification: Duvera E-Commerce Checkout

## 1. Overview & Layout Architecture
A full-screen, dark-mode glassmorphic e-commerce product checkout interface.

* **Layout Mode**: Full-screen, two-column split layout with a unified top navigation bar.
* **Canvas Dimensions**: Responsive (Base: `1440px × 900px`).
* **Background**: Deep charcoal base (`#1A1718` to `#242022`) layered with two ambient radial blur gradients:
  * Warm Amber Glow: `rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.18)` positioned behind the central product display.
  * Soft Crimson Glow: `rgba(235, 47, 47, 0.12)` situated in the bottom right corner.

---

## 2. Typography
* **Primary Font**: `Poppins`, `Inter`, or `SF Pro Display`.
* **Fallback Stack**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
* **Type Hierarchy**:
  * **Brand Title ("Duvera")**: Semi-bold / 600, `24px`, `#FFFFFF`.
  * **Brand Subtitle**: Regular / 400, `12px`, `#A09D9E`.
  * **Section Headings ("Checkout")**: Medium / 500, `20px`, `#FFFFFF`.
  * **Price Accent ("290$")**: Semi-bold / 600, `20px`, `#F5A623`.
  * **Form Labels / Placeholders**: Regular / 400, `13px`, `#8E8B8C`.
  * **Data Rows & Balance**: Regular / 400, `14px`, `#B8B5B6` (values: `#E5E3E4`).
  * **Action Button ("Buy")**: Bold / 700, `16px`, `#FFFFFF`.
  * **Hint Text ("Roll Over Image...")**: Regular / 400, `11px`, `#6E6A6B`.

---

## 3. Header Navigation Bar
* **Placement**: Top row, spanning full width. Height: `64px`.
* **Left**:
  * Brand block: "Duvera" above "Ecommerce Store" (stacked vertically).
* **Right Elements** (Flex row, gap `12px`):
  * **Search Bar**: Fully rounded pill (`h: 40px`, `w: 180px`), translucent background (`rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`), border (`1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`). Left icon: Search lens (`16px`, `#B8B5B6`). Placeholder: "Search".
  * **Profile Button**: Rounded square (`40px × 40px`), pill radius, glass style with center user-avatar icon (`#E0DFDF`).
  * **Cart Button**: Matching glass container (`40px × 40px`) with shopping cart icon. Includes a small red notification indicator dot (`6px × 6px`, `#FF3B30`) aligned to the upper right.

---

## 4. Main Body Split

### A. Left Column: Product Showcase (60% Width)
* **Vertical Thumbnail Rail** (Left-aligned, gap `10px`):
  * 4 circular/squircle glass tiles (`44px × 44px`, `radius: 12px`):
    * Tile 1: Dual controller top-down view.
    * Tile 2: VR headset angled view.
    * Tile 3: Headset + strap accessory view.
    * Tile 4: Headset front faceplate view.
  * 1 Action Tile: `+` icon centered in matching glass pill.
* **Hero Product Area** (Centered):
  * **Image Asset**: Pair of matte-black VR motion controllers (Oculus/Meta Touch style).
  * **Asset Requirements**: Backgroundless transparent PNG.
  * **Arrangement & Rotation**:
    * Symmetrical inward orientation.
    * Left controller tilted approx. `-25°`, right controller tilted approx. `+25°`.
    * High-gloss tracking rings pointing upward; thumbstick faceplates facing viewer.
  * **Ground Shadow**: Diffused oval drop shadow (`blur: 32px`, `opacity: 0.5`) beneath handles.
* **Microcopy**: Centered below hero image: "Roll Over Image To Zoom In".

### B. Vertical Divider
* `1px` subtle separator line (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) between left and right sections.

### C. Right Column: Checkout Panel (40% Width)
* **Header Row**: "Checkout" left-aligned, "290$" right-aligned in amber.
* **Input Fields** (Style: `h: 44px`, border-radius `10px`, border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`, bg `rgba(255,255,255,0.03)`):
  1. **Cardholder Name**: Full-width field.
  2. **Card Number**: Full-width field. Houses credit card badge (Mastercard red/orange interlocking circles) on the right edge.
  3. **Row: Expiration & CVV**:
     * "Expiration" container: Split into two mini inputs: `MM` with dual stepper arrows (`⬍`), `/` divider, and `YY`.
     * "CVV" container: 3-4 digit input box.
* **Cost Breakdown**:
  * "balance amount:": `200$`
  * "Vat (21%):": `90$`
  * Horizontal hairline divider (`rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`).
  * "Total:": `290$`
* **Primary CTA ("Buy")**:
  * Dimensions: Full width, height `46px`, border-radius `12px`.
  * Background: Solid or subtle vertical gradient `#F5A623` to `#E08A00`.
  * Content: White bold text centered.

---

## 5. UI Elements & Styling Summary
* **Borders**: Thin translucent strokes (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `0.15`).
* **Glass Surface**: `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, background `rgba(40, 36, 38, 0.55)`.
* **Corner Radii**:
  * Inputs: `10px`
  * Buttons / Badges: `12px` to `999px` (fully rounded)
  * Main Card / Viewport container: `24px`
