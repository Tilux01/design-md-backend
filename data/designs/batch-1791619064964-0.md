# Design Specification: Reboot Landing Page

---

## 1. Overview & Aesthetic Architecture

* **Visual Style:** Minimalist, high-end Swiss modernism combined with Web3/AI tech aesthetics. Characterized by expansive white negative space, precision dot-matrix data visualization, crisp monochrome typography, and a single vibrant iridescent neon accent on the primary action button.
* **Layout Structure:** Full-viewport single-fold hero experience (`100vw` × `100vh`), structured into three vertical tiers:
  1. Top navigation bar
  2. Centered hero message with interactive CTA and background particle map
  3. Bottom edge-to-edge monochrome partner logo strip
* **Viewport Scope:** Full-screen web application (the outer gray mockup border and rounded preview frame in the reference image are omitted in favor of a 100% full-bleed web layout).

### 5.3. Primary CTA Pill Button & Iridescent Neon Glow
* **Button Metrics:**
  * Label: **"Join our World"**
  * Height: `42px`
  * Padding: `10px 24px`
  * Border radius: `9999px`
  * Background: Solid `#0A0A0A`
  * Text color: `#FFFFFF`
  * Font: `13px`, Medium
  * Position: `relative`
* **Underglow / Aura Effect:**
  * Located immediately behind/beneath the button via a pseudo-element (`::after` or dedicated blur container).
  * Size: Width `110%`, Height `100%`, centered under the button.
  * Shift: `top: 6px` to `10px` downward.
  * Gradient composition: A horizontal spectrum blend of Cyan (`#38BDF8`), Hot Pink (`#F43F5E`), Gold (`#FBBF24`), and Purple (`#A855F7`).
  * Filter: `filter: blur(16px) saturate(1.8);`
  * Opacity: `0.75`–`0.85`.
  * Yields a soft, radiant chromatic halo visible only below the bottom contour of the black capsule.

---

### 5.4. Background Asset: Dot-Matrix Topographic World Graphic

* **Asset Type:** Backgroundless transparent vector / high-res alpha PNG (`format: webp/svg/png`).
* **Description & Visual Content:**
  * A rasterized, horizontal dot-matrix world map representing continental landmasses.
  * Composed of thousands of individual circular dots organized in strictly horizontal parallel scanning lines.
  * **Left Wing:** Depicts the Western Hemisphere / Americas in dot clusters.
  * **Right Wing:** Depicts the Eastern Hemisphere / Eurasia & Africa in dot clusters.
  * **Center:** Dot density thins out or fades to 0% opacity behind the hero headline and CTA button to preserve textual readability.
  * **Dot Dynamics:** Dot diameters vary from `1.5px` (light, peripheral points) up to `4.5px` (dense interior landmass points). Dot colors are dark slate / soft charcoal (`rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.75)` to `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.25)`).
* **Placement & Dimensions:**
  * Position: `absolute; top: 50%; left: 50%; transform: translate(-50%, -50%);`
  * Width: `92vw` to `96vw` (spanning nearly the entire screen width).
  * Height: `auto` (approximately `480px` to `560px`).
  * Rotation: `0deg` (strictly horizontal scanline orientation).
  * Pointer-events: `none`.

## 6. Micro-Interactions & Hover Dynamics

* **Navigation Links:** Smooth opacity shift (`opacity: 1` $\to$ `opacity: 0.65`) on mouseover (`transition: opacity 200ms ease`).
* **Pill Buttons ("Sign in" & "Join our World"):**
  * Background hover: Transition to `#1F1F23`.
  * "Join our World" glow: Glow expands on hover (`filter: blur(20px)`, opacity increases to `0.95`, transform scale `1.05`).
* **Partner Logos:** Base state `#0A0A0A` with `opacity: 0.85`; hover state transitions to `opacity: 1.0` with `transform: translateY(-1px)`.
