# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist & Dark Mode Automotive Brand Identity.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-contrast, clean framing with generous negative space and rigid grid alignments. Focuses on typographic hierarchy, fine material detailing, and high-impact product imagery.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Monolithic flat dark surfaces (`#000000` canvas) with sharp borders (`#1F1F1F`), subtle linear gradient cards, and high-contrast color sample blocks.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-precision typographic specimens, meticulous color palette mapping with hex codes, multi-viewport automotive showcase grid.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255,255,255,0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-[#00C2B8] inline-block"></span>`.
