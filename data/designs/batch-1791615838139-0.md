## 5. Graphics & Visual Assets Specification

### Primary 3D Visual Asset (Hero Graphics)

* **Asset Type:** High-resolution transparent background PNG or WebP image with smooth alpha transparency channel (no background color baked into the graphic file).
* **Composition Overview:** A multi-layered, floating 3D abstract sphere composition featuring heavy glassmorphism, depth-of-field, refraction, and caustic effects.

#### Detailed Composition Elements (From Foreground to Background):

1. **Main Glass Refractive Sphere (Foreground - Left Center):**
   * **Size:** Occupies ~55% of the container width (`~300px`).
   * **Appearance:** Completely translucent, high-index glass ball with sharp, bright white edge reflections (fresnel light effect) at the top-left and bottom-right edges.
   * **Internal Refraction Effect:** Acts as a lens, showing a magnified, distorted, soft-blurred vibrant royal blue blob inside its top-right quadrant (refracting the blue sphere positioned behind it).

2. **Vibrant Blue Sphere (Mid-ground - Center Right):**
   * **Size:** Medium sphere (`~180px`).
   * **Positioning:** Situated directly behind the right side of the main glass sphere, partially overlapping.
   * **Color & Material:** Rich, saturated royal blue gradient (`#1E40AF` to `#3B82F6`) with smooth matte surface shading and soft ambient light reflections.

3. **Bottom Frosted Pearl Sphere (Foreground - Bottom Center/Right):**
   * **Size:** Medium-large sphere (`~240px`).
   * **Positioning:** Placed at the lower right corner, overlapping the bottom edge of the blue sphere and extending below the glass sphere.
   * **Color & Material:** Matte frosted white/pale blue ice texture (`#E2E8F0` to `#FFFFFF`) with light, soft shadows, giving it a 3D clay/porcelain sheen.

4. **Background Soft-Focus Spheres (Background - Top Right):**
   * **Top Small Sphere:** Smooth white matte sphere (`~60px`) floating near top center-right.
   * **Far Right Small Sphere:** Soft-focus glowing white/blue sphere (`~90px`) positioned at the upper right edge, creating visual depth through simulated camera blur (depth of field).

* **Overall Lighting & Shadows:**
  * Key Light Source: Top-Left soft studio lighting.
  * Shadows: Soft blue-tinted drop shadows under and between overlapping spheres; no heavy black shadows.
  * Rotation & Orientation: Frontal view with slightly tilted upward perspective (`5° to 10°` pitch angle).
