# Design Specification: Tame Impala Media Card

## 1. General Container
*   **Background:** Solid White (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Dimensions:** A landscape rectangular aspect ratio (approx. 16:9 or 3:2).
*   **Border Radius:** If used as a card, `24px` to `32px`. If full-screen, `0px`.
*   **Padding:** The design uses a "bleed" effect on the left for the vinyl and generous white space on the right.

---

## 2. Typography
The design utilizes a "Swiss Style" or International Typographic Style, characterized by cleanliness and readability.

*   **Primary Font:** **Inter** (Bold and Regular) or **Helvetica Neue**.
*   **Secondary Font:** **Arial** or **system-ui** (sans-serif).
*   **Font Fallback:** `Inter, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;`

### Text Styling:
1.  **Artist Name ("TAME IMPALA"):**
    *   **Weight:** Extra Bold / Black (Weight 800 or 900).
    *   **Case:** ALL CAPS.
    *   **Letter Spacing:** Slightly tight (`-0.02em`).
    *   **Line Height:** Tight (`0.9` to `1.0`). The "TAME" and "IMPALA" should be stacked vertically with minimal gap.
    *   **Color:** Pure Black (`#000000`).
2.  **Track Titles:**
    *   **Weight:** Medium (Weight 500).
    *   **Size:** Approximately 1/4 the size of the Artist Name.
    *   **Color:** Dark Grey to Black (`#1A1A1A`).
3.  **Time Durations:**
    *   **Weight:** Regular (Weight 400).
    *   **Size:** Same as track titles.
    *   **Color:** Light Grey (`#8E8E8E`).

---

## 3. The Vinyl Record (Left Element)
*   **Placement:** Vertically centered on the left edge. The record is "cut off," with only the right 60% of the disc visible.
*   **Shape:** Perfect circle.
*   **Color:** Deep Black (`#050505`).
*   **Texture:** Subtle concentric circular ridges (simulating vinyl grooves) using a very low-opacity white overlay or gradient.
*   **Center Label:**
    *   A smaller circle in the dead center of the record.
    *   **Background:** White (`#FFFFFF`).
    *   **Content:** A small "T" or a number "1" (Side A indicator) in a sans-serif font, centered.
*   **Rotation:** The record should be static, but for a "live" feel, a slow clockwise CSS rotation can be applied.

---

## 4. Tracklist Layout (Right Element)
The tracklist is positioned directly below the "TAME IMPALA" header.

*   **Structure:** Four horizontal rows.
*   **Row Spacing:** Even vertical padding between rows (approx `16px`).
*   **Column 1 (Thumbnail):**
    *   Small square image (`40px` x `40px`).
    *   Border radius: `4px`.
*   **Column 2 (Title):**
    *   Left-aligned, directly next to the thumbnail.
*   **Column 3 (Duration):**
    *   Right-aligned to the edge of the text container.

## 6. Detailed Alignment & Spacing
*   **Vertical Alignment:** The top of the "T" in "TAME" should align horizontally with the top curve of the visible vinyl record.
*   **Horizontal Alignment:** The right edge of the text ("IMPALA" and the time durations) should maintain a consistent margin from the right edge of the screen (approx `5%` of total width).
*   **Visual Balance:** The "negative space" (white area) between the vinyl record and the start of the text should be roughly equal to the width of the thumbnail images.

## 7. UI Elements (Top Right)
*   **Icons:** Small, thin-stroke icons for "Share," "Menu (three dots)," and "Close (X)."
*   **Color:** Light Grey (`#B3B3B3`).
*   **Placement:** Top right corner, arranged horizontally with `12px` spacing between them.
