# Design Specification: Floe Dashboard

## 1. Overall Layout & Architecture

- **Viewport**: Fullscreen (`100vw`, `100vh`), overflow-y scroll on main content.
- **Color Scheme**: Clean light-mode dashboard.
  - Background: `#FBFBFB` (Main canvas)
  - Sidebar Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Text Primary: `#111827`
  - Text Secondary / Muted: `#6B7280`
  - Accent / Star: `#FBBF24`
  - Alert Badge: `#EF4444`
  - Borders: `#E5E7EB`

---

## 2. Typography

- **Primary UI Font**: Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, or SF Pro Display.
  - *Fallback*: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.
- **Card Titles & Technical Labels**: Monospace (JetBrains Mono, Roboto Mono).
  - *Fallback*: `ui-monospace, "Courier New", monospace`.
- **Scale**:
  - Page Title: `24px` | Bold (`700`)
  - Section Titles: `18px` | Semi-bold (`600`)
  - Monospace Card Titles: `15px` | Regular (`400`)
  - Body / Nav: `14px` | Regular/Medium (`400`/`500`)
  - Metadata / Counts: `12px` | Regular (`400`)

---
