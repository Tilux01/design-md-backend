# UI/UX Specification & Design System: Coffee Mobile App Experience

## 1. Global Design System & Foundations

### 1.1. Color Palette (Warm Cream & Minimalist Espresso)
The entire aesthetic relies on a sophisticated, monochromatic palette of warm creams, soft beiges, and deep espresso browns, giving it an organic, artisanal coffee-house feel.

*   **Background (Primary Screen Fill):** Warm Cream Gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #FBF7F2 0%, #F3EAE1 100%)`
*   **Surface / Cards / Input Fields:** Soft Milk White / Translucent Cream `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` with subtle drop shadows.
*   **Primary Accent (Buttons & Active States):** Warm Caramel / Terracotta `rgba(205, 128, 96, 0.9)` to `rgba(180, 100, 70, 1.0)`.
*   **Text Primary (Headers & Headings):** Deep Espresso Brown `#3B2C23`.
*   **Text Secondary / Muted:** Soft Taupe / Muted Mocha `#8C7D73`.
*   **Borders & Dividers:** Very subtle warm borders `rgba(225, 210, 195, 0.6)`.
*   **Shadows (Soft Neumorphic / Skeuomorphic):** `0 12px 30px -10px rgba(100, 70, 50, 0.12)` for elevated cards and floaters.

### 1.2. Typography System & Fallbacks
*Note: The typography in the preview mimics a handwritten/organic sans-serif styling.*

*   **Primary Display / Headers (`h1`, `h2`):**
    *   *Primary Choice:* **"Caveat"** or **"Patrick Hand"** (Google Fonts) for organic, handwritten cafe menus.
    *   *Fallback Stack:* `Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif`.
*   **Body & UI Text (`p`, `span`, buttons):**
    *   *Primary Choice:* **"Quicksand"** or **"Nunito"** (Google Fonts) for rounded, friendly UI readability.
    *   *Fallback Stack:* `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

## 3. Asset Specifications & Image Guidelines

Since this layout requires a seamless, exact clone, all images must adhere to the following strict aesthetic rules:

1.  **Photography Style:** Commercial-grade, warm-toned food and beverage photography. Avoid harsh studio lighting; favor natural window light casting soft, directional shadows.
2.  **Color Grading of Images:** High exposure, warm color temperature (approx. 4500K–5200K), low contrast in shadows, creamy highlights.
3.  **Isolation:** All coffee cup images must have neutral, soft cream or beige backgrounds that blend seamlessly into the screen background gradient (`#FBF7F2`).
