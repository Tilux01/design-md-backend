# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Luxury Minimalist & High-End Fragrance Brand Presentation Board.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio with generous padding frames, emphasizing high-end typography and tactile product photography.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat dark-mode surface with crisp 1px borders, relying on high-contrast typography and authentic tactile photography rather than drop shadows or heavy blurs.

# 6. Exhaustive Component-by-Component Architectural Specs

## 1. Brand Header Section
- **Layout & Positioning**: Flexbox row, space-between alignment, relative positioning.
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Padding `32px 40px`, gap `24px`.
- **Borders & Radii**: Bottom border 1px solid `#2E2E2E`.
- **Typography**: 
  - Font Family: `"Cormorant Garamond", serif`
  - Font Size: `5.0rem` (80px)
  - Font Weight: `300 / Light`
  - Letter-Spacing: `0.08em`
  - Text-Transform: `uppercase`
  - Color: `#F7F2EA`

## 2. Typography Weight Matrix Bar
- **Layout & Positioning**: Flexbox row, space-between distribution across 5 weights (ExtraLight, Light, Regular, Medium, Bold).
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Padding `16px 40px`, border-top and border-bottom 1px solid `#2E2E2E`.
- **Typography**: 
  - Labels: Inter, 13px, uppercase, color `#9E9E9E`.
  - Specimen Alphabet (`abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz`): Inter, 13px, lowercase, color `#F7F2EA`.

## 3. Visual Showcase Grid (Space / Material / Object)
- **Layout & Positioning**: CSS Grid with 3 columns (`1fr 1fr 1fr`), explicit gap `16px`.
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Padding `24px 40px`.
- **Borders & Radii**: Border 1px solid `#2E2E2E`, inner imagery contained with sharp 0px corners.
- **Content Panels**:

  - Panel 2: "MATERIAL" (Macro shot of frosted glass and brushed gold collar)
  - Panel 3: "OBJECT / DETAIL" (Atomizer spray head and silk ribbon tie)
