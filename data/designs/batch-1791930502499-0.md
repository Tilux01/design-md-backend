# Design Specification: UN:LEASH Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Canvas Setup
* **Target Layout:** Fullscreen single-page dashboard/landing page (100vw × 100vh, `overflow: hidden`).
* **Theme:** Dark sci-fi/cyberpunk, industrial energy drink showcase.
* **Color Palette:**
  * Background: `#0B0D11` to `#13161C` (Dark carbon/slate).
  * Primary Text/Elements: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
  * Secondary/Muted Text: `#6E7683` (Cool gray).
  * Accents / Glows: `#00F0D0` to `#38E1FF` (Cyan/Electric Turquoise).
  * Card Background: `rgba(18, 22, 28, 0.65)` with backdrop blur.

---

## 2. Background & Graphic Layers
* **Base Texture:** Carbon fiber/micro-dot grid pattern over a subtle radial gradient (brightest near center-right).
* **Orbital HUD Rings:**
  * Thin, concentric circular vector rings centered around the product.
  * Line weight: 1px; color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
  * Outer arc accents in glowing cyan (`#00F0D0`) with technical nodes/markers.

---

## 3. Typography & Fallbacks
* **Primary Display/Headings:** `Industry Black`, `Barlow Condensed (700/800)`, or `Montserrat (800)`.
  * *Fallback:* `Impact, "Arial Black", sans-serif`.
* **Technical Labels & Navigation:** `Space Grotesk`, `Rajdhani`, or `Inter (600, uppercase)`.
  * *Fallback:* `system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`.
* **Data/Body Text:** `Inter` or `Roboto (400)`.
  * *Fallback:* `sans-serif`.

---

## 4. Layout Structure

### A. Navigation (Top Bar)
* **Position:** Fixed top, full width, padded (`padding: 40px 60px`).
* **Left:** Brand wordmark `UN:LEASH` (Font size: ~20px, bold, white).
* **Nav Menu:** Horizontal list offset toward the center-left:
  * Items: `HOME`, `ABOUT`, `DRINKS`, `CONTACT`.
  * Styling: Font size ~11px, tracking `0.15em`, muted gray (`#6E7683`).
  * Active state (`HOME`): White text with a small cyan horizontal indicator bar beneath.

### B. Hero Headline (Middle Left)
* **Eyebrow:** `POWER INITIATED` (9px, tracked out `0.25em`, cyan-gray).
* **Main Heading:**
  ```text
  UN:LEASH
  YOUR ENERGY
  ```
  * Size: ~56px–64px, uppercase, tight line-height (`0.95`), pure white.

### C. Central Product Feature (Image Asset)
* **Asset Description:** High-resolution 3D render of an aluminum energy drink can.
  * **Finish:** Matte/brushed gunmetal dark aluminum body, metallic silver pull-tab top.
  * **Transparency:** Must be backgroundless (isolated PNG/WebP).
  * **Placement & Orientation:** Centered slightly to the right of middle; tilted ~35° diagonally toward the upper-left.
  * **Visual Effects:** 3 to 4 luminous cyan energy loops spiraling dynamically around the can's body with soft particle glimmers.

### D. Interactive HUD Callouts & Orbit Nodes
* **"POWER" Node (Bottom-Left of Can):**
  * Small cyan `+` icon anchored to the lower-left arc.
  * Label: `POWER` (11px, white, bold) placed adjacent to the icon.
* **"ENERGY" Node (Bottom-Right of Can):**
  * Small cyan `+` icon positioned on the right arc line.
  * Label: `ENERGY` (11px, white, bold).
* **"HEALTH" Info Card (Top-Right):**
  * Small circular pip node connected to the outer orbit path.
  * Floating card: Dark semi-transparent rounded rectangle (`border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`).
  * Title: `HEALTH` (12px, bold, uppercase, white).
  * Description: 3–4 lines of small muted text (`#8A92A0`, ~10px font, reading details about guarana/seed extract).

### E. Social Links (Right Edge)
* **Position:** Vertical alignment along the far right margin.
* **Icons:** Facebook (`f`), Twitter (`bird`), Instagram (`camera`).
* **Style:** Stacked vertically, size ~14px, muted slate color (`#5A6270`), hover to cyan.

### F. Footer
* **Left:** Copyright text (`© 2017 UN:LEASH. ALL RIGHTS RESERVED.`), 8px, `#3C424E`.
* **Right:** Subtle watermark `UN:LEASH`, 8px, `#3C424E`.
