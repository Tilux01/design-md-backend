# Visual Design Specifications: Promotional Product Banner

## 1. Global Canvas & Background
*   **Dimensions:** Fluid/Responsive web banner ratio (approx. 16:9 landscape).
*   **Background Style:** Smooth, multi-stop vertical gradient transitioning from a vibrant sky blue at the top to a clean, pale cerulean blue at the base.
*   **Lighting/Atmosphere:** Airy, clinical-dermatological aesthetic with soft volumetric lighting radiating from the upper-left corner.
*   **Surface:** A clean, minimalist white matte studio shelf running horizontally across the lower third of the frame, blending seamlessly into the white foreground surface.

---

## 2. Typography & Text Hierarchy
*(Note: Text in the source image is heavily blurred/stylized; specifications below reflect standard e-commerce promotional copy placement and visual weight).*

### Primary Headline (Top Left)
*   **Content (Est.):** Brand name / Series title (e.g., "DERMA+").
*   **Font Family:** Modern Sans-Serif (Clean geometric, high legibility like Helvetica Neue or Inter).
*   **Weight:** Bold / Extra Bold.
*   **Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Alignment:** Left-aligned.

### Secondary Sub-headline (Upper Left, below primary)
*   **Content (Est.):** Product line description (e.g., "Advanced Barrier Care Formula").
*   **Font Family:** Modern Sans-Serif.
*   **Weight:** Regular / Medium.
*   **Color:** Soft Off-White / Pale Blue-White tint (`#E2EEF8`).
*   **Alignment:** Left-aligned.

### Body / Callout Text (Lower Left)
*   **Content (Est.):** Benefit statements / Ingredients highlights.
*   **Font Family:** Clean Sans-Serif.
*   **Weight:** Regular.
*   **Color:** White with 80% opacity.
*   **Line Height:** 1.5x for readability.

---

## 3. Product Arrangement & Composition
The products are arranged on the white surface in a balanced, stepped composition ranging from mid-ground to foreground-right.

### Product 1: Tall Cleanser / Treatment Tube (Center-Left)
*   **Type:** Tall, flat-top squeeze tube with a pump/cap base.
*   **Colorway:** White body with pastel pink and yellow accent labeling.
*   **Position:** Vertical orientation, positioned slightly behind the central cluster.
*   **Shadows:** Soft, diffused contact shadow underneath.

### Product 2: Yellow/Orange Serum Bottle (Center)
*   **Type:** Dropper bottle / pump serum.
*   **Colorway:** Translucent amber/yellow glass or packaging with a white pump top.
*   **Position:** Foreground, slightly left of center.

### Product 3: Compact Cream Jar (Center-Right)
*   **Type:** Small round cosmetic jar with a blue screw cap.
*   **Colorway:** White base with dark navy/blue cap and matching label band.
*   **Position:** Mid-ground, resting beside the serum bottle.

### Product 4: Wide Cream/Balm Tub (Right-Center)
*   **Type:** Medium-sized wide cosmetic tub/jar.
*   **Colorway:** White body with a prominent deep navy blue lid and blue-accented branding band.
*   **Position:** Mid-ground.

### Product 5: Large Pump Bottle (Far Right)
*   **Type:** Oversized lotion/body wash pump dispenser bottle.
*   **Colorway:** Solid rich cobalt blue with a matching pump mechanism.
*   **Position:** Foreground right, serving as a visual anchor and balancing the composition.
*   **Shadows:** Deep, soft directional shadow casting to the right.

---

## 4. UI Badges & Floating Elements
*   **Badge 1 (Top Right Circular Callout):**
    *   **Shape:** Pure white circular badge with a subtle drop shadow.
    *   **Content:** Dark blue text/percentage (e.g., "SAVE 15%").
    *   **Position:** Floating in the upper-right sky area.
*   **Badge 2 & 3 (Floating Left Benefit Bubbles):**
    *   **Shape:** Small, overlapping semi-transparent white speech bubble/circles with subtle drop shadows.
    *   **Content:** Icon or short text highlighting key features (e.g., "Dermatologist Tested").
    *   **Position:** Floating diagonally down the left side.

---

## 5. Lighting & Shadow Details
*   **Directional Light:** Soft, diffused light source originating from the top-left.
*   **Reflections:** Subtle specular highlights along the edges of the plastic pump bottles and cream jars to emphasize the clean, glossy packaging materials.
*   **Drop Shadows:** Contact shadows beneath all products feature a soft gradient fade, anchoring them realistically to the white surface without harsh edges.
