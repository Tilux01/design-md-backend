# Design Specification: Minimalist Circular Progress Slider

## 1. Overview & Layout Strategy

This design features a hyper-minimalist, skeuomorphic-digital hybrid UI component centered around a rounded rectangular card. The interface presents a smooth, rounded surface with a recessed track and a movable pill-shaped control knob (slider handle). 

* **Viewport & Background:** The entire layout occupies the full screen (`100vw` $\times$ `100vh`). The background is a soft, neutral off-white or light cool gray, creating a subtle contrast against the brighter white foreground card.
* **Foreground Card:** A large, rounded white panel occupies the bottom-left and center portion of the screen, featuring a generous border-radius on its top-right corner where it meets the slider track.
* **Component Placement:** The interactive slider control is positioned at the top edge intersection of the card, acting as an anchor where the carved groove begins.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Lighting (Effects)

The design heavily relies on soft neumorphic shadows and highlights to create a sense of tactile depth on a monochromatic canvas.

* **Background Base (`--bg-color`):** `#EAEAEA` to `#ECECEC` (Soft neutral light gray).
* **Card Base (`--card-color`):** `#F4F4F5` (Pure soft white).
* **Recessed Track (`--track-color`):** 
  * Inset shadow/depth: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)` inner shadow.
  * Groove stroke/depth: Soft inner bevel mimicking carved plastic.
* **Slider Pill Handle (`--handle-bg`):**
  * Base color: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white).
  * Lighting: Top-left specular highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), bottom-right drop shadow (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`).
* **Indicator Glow / Accent (`--accent-color`):** 
  * A warm, soft glowing gradient behind or inside the active state: `#FF8A3D` transitioning to `#FF521C` (vibrant soft orange/coral).
* **Icon & Cursor Color (`--icon-color`):** `#1A1A1A` (Near black/dark charcoal for crisp iconography).

---

## 3. Typography & Font Stack

While this specific UI utilizes minimal textual elements, standard system typography principles apply to any accompanying text or cursor interactions.

* **Primary Font:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` (San Francisco / Inter style grotesque sans-serif).
* **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, sans-serif`.
* **Weight:** Regular (400) to Medium (500) for UI labels.

---

## 4. Component Breakdown & Geometry

### A. The Slider Track (Groove)
* **Shape:** A smooth, quarter-circle curved groove (arc) that originates underneath the pill control and sweeps downwards along the curved corner of the card.
* **Dimensions:** 
  * Stroke width: ~`12px` to `16px`.
  * Corner radius matching the underlying white card.
* **Depth Effect:** Rendered using an inner stroke or SVG path with dual shadows (dark inner shadow on top/left, light highlight on bottom/right) to look physically pressed into the surface.

### B. The Control Pill (Thumb / Handle)
* **Shape:** Rounded pill (Capsule).
* **Dimensions:** Approximately `60px` width $\times$ `36px` height.
* **Border Radius:** `18px` (fully rounded caps).
* **Internal Elements:**
  * **Sun/Brightness Icon:** Located on the left side of the pill. A central solid circle with 8 radiating short hash marks (sunburst motif). Color: `#1A1A1A`.
  * **Active Fill / Indicator:** The right half of the pill reveals a warm orange/coral glow underneath a translucent or frosted white layer, indicating the current value/level.

### C. The Mouse Cursor
* **Type:** Standard pointer cursor with a custom drop shadow (`drop-shadow(0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.3))`).
* **Position:** Hovering precisely over the right edge of the pill handle, indicating an active drag/grab state.

---

## 5. Animation & Interaction Specifications

This UI implies fluid, physics-based micro-interactions:

1. **Dragging / Sliding State:**
   * **Pathfollowing:** When the user clicks and drags the pill handle, it smoothly follows the curved arc path along the card's border.
   * **Interpolation:** `transition: transform 0.15s cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1);` to ensure zero lag yet silky-smooth deceleration.
2. **Dynamic Lighting / Glow:**
   * As the slider moves along the track, the orange accent indicator expands or shifts hue/opacity proportionally to the value (0% to 100%).
3. **Hover & Active States:**
   * **Hover:** The pill handle subtly scales up (`transform: scale(1.02)`) with an intensified drop shadow.
   * **Active (Mousedown):** The pill compresses slightly (`transform: scale(0.98)`) to simulate physical depression into the surface.
