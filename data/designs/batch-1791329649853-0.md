# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise / Editorial Glassmorphic Minimalist with 3D Render Focus.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace, floating glass navbar, generous negative space around center 3D fluid sphere and typography.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Backdrop blur glassmorphic frosted pill container with subtle white borders and low-opacity dark surfaces.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Frosted glass panels, liquid ripples background, hyper-spaced typography, dark pill CTA button.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Navbar floating container with `rounded-full`, backdrop blur, and semi-transparent white backdrop.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;`
