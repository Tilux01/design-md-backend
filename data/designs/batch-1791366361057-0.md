## 1. Global & Architectural Styles

### Color Palette
- **Primary Red:** `#F94A42` (Used for banner calls-to-action, highlights, brand tags)

- **Text Primary (Dark Slate / Black):** `#1A1A1A` (Headings, brand logo, paragraph text)

- **Primary Brand / Headings (Sans-Serif Bold):** `"Montserrat", "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif`
