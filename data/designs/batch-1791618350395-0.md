# Design Specification (`design.md`)

This document defines the exact visual design, layout architecture, typography, color system, and asset requirements to create a pixel-perfect, fullscreen clone of the **CAMP/ SUMMER CAMP'21** landing page.

---

## 1. Design Overview & Visual Theme

- **Design Philosophy**: Minimalist, warm neo-morphism / glassmorphism blended with playful 3D claymorphic render art.
- **Canvas / Viewport**: Fullscreen single-page application layout (`100vw`, `min-h-screen`). Completely borderless with no surrounding device mockup frames.
- **Atmosphere**: Studio-lit, soft ambient shadows, warm off-white tones, glowing warm-coral accents, and clean geometric typography.

## 3. Typography System

### Primary Font Stack (Geometric Sans-serif)
- **Primary Choice**: **Gilroy** (Hairline / Light / SemiBold) or **Poppins** / **Plus Jakarta Sans**
- **System Fallback**: `Inter`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Helvetica Neue`, `sans-serif`

### 5.3. Hero Right: 3D Artwork & Interactive Badges

#### A. Main Campfire Artwork
- **Type**: Clean 3D asset with an alpha transparent background (no baked flat backdrop).
- **Subject Matter**:
  1. **Stylized Fire Flame**:
     - Modern claymorphic teardrop geometry with two fluid crests (a taller left crest and a secondary softer right hump).
     - Surface Finish: Matte clay, ultra-smooth sub-surface scattering with emissive warm core.
     - Internal color: Radiant warm white-yellow `#FFF9D2` at the core center.
     - Outward gradient: Soft transitions to radiant peach-orange `#FF8357`, darkening to solid coral `#FF5A36` along the exterior silhouette.
  2. **Campfire Logs**:
     - Three intersecting rounded cylindrical wooden logs stacked beneath the flame.
     - Texture: Smooth stylized matte chocolate brown (`#694537`).
     - End Faces: Flat cross-sections displaying carved concentric circular tree growth rings in lighter beige-brown (`#825745`).
     - Spatial Orientation:
       - Log 1: Base log angled downward towards the left front.
       - Log 2: Angled horizontally right behind Log 1.
       - Log 3: Supporting log under the flame.
  3. **Contact Shadow**:
     - Soft realistic diffuse ambient occlusion shadow grounding the logs onto the floor plane (`rgba(80, 40, 20, 0.15)` blurred over `60px`).
- **Placement**:
  - Right: `8%` from viewport edge.
  - Center vertical: Centered against the `SUMMER CAMP'21` headline.
  - Scale: Approximately `540px` wide by `560px` tall.

#### B. Floating Spheres (Embers)
- **Top Sphere**:
  - Diameter: `22px`.
  - Placement: Placed vertically above the center of the flame at `top: 12%`, `left: 71%`.
  - Texture: Smooth matte alabaster/clay sphere with soft top-left key lighting and ambient bottom reflection.
- **Lower Sphere**:
  - Diameter: `36px`.
  - Placement: Positioned directly above the right shoulder of the flame at `top: 24%`, `left: 76%`.
  - Texture: Matching pearly white matte sphere with realistic directional light casting downward.

#### C. Circular Text Stamp Badge
- **Content**: `JUNE 15 • AUGUST 08` arranged around a circular SVG path.
- **Diameter**: `96px`.
- **Placement**: Positioned to the upper right of the flame crest (`top: 32%`, `left: 85%`).
- **Styling**:
  - Text: Uppercase, `11px`, letter-spacing `+0.18em`, color `#111111`.
  - Dot: Solid bullet symbol `•` separating the two date stamps.
  - Motion: Continuous infinite clockwise rotation (`animation: spin 24s linear infinite`).

## 7. Responsiveness & Edge Rules

1. **Desktop Standard (`1280px` - `1920px`)**:
   - Matches the exact visual layout. Single screen height, no vertical scrollbar (`overflow-y: hidden`).
2. **Compact Laptops (`1024px` - `1279px`)**:
   - Campfire scales down by `15%`. Headline size drops to `clamp(48px, 5.5vw, 76px)`.
3. **Mobile & Tablet (`< 1024px`)**:
   - Layout stacks vertically. Campfire moves below headline and email capture.
   - Stats grid switches from 3 columns to single-column vertical stack with `margin-top: 40px`. Screen allows natural vertical scrolling.
