# Design Specification: "EQUALIZER" Neo-Cyberpunk Hero Interface

This document specifies the exact visual, structural, typographic, and stylistic guidelines to construct a pixel-accurate, full-screen implementation of the "EQUALIZER" hero interface.

---

## 1. Canvas & Atmosphere

* **Canvas Dimension**: Full viewport width and height (`100vw` × `100vh`), `overflow: hidden`.
* **Atmospheric Palette & Lighting**:
  * **Base Background**: Dark studio vignette transitioning from a deep navy blue (`#040a1c`) to a rich nocturnal cyan-blue (`#061a3b`).
  * **Left Rim Light (Magenta Glow)**: Radial gradient centered at approximately `15% X, 35% Y` with color `#e026c2` / `#c4159f` at ~55% opacity, softly fading outward into the deep blue base.
  * **Right Fill Light (Electric Cyan/Blue Glow)**: Diffused directional wash from the top-right / right-center (`#0d72d6` blending to `#02b3e4`) illuminating the studio wall and subject’s back.
  * **Floor Ground Reflection**: Subtle horizontal gradient along the lower third (`#071938` with soft contact shadows beneath the subject’s boots).

## 3. Typography & Glyphs

### 3.1 Display Font: Central Title (`EQUALIZER`)
* **Visual Style**: Ultra-futuristic, geometric, stencil/sliced sci-fi display font with segmented horizontal bars and disconnected strokes.
  * `E`: Formed by three independent horizontal rounded bars with angled inner ends.
  * `Q`: Clean oval with an offset, disconnected lower diagonal slash.
  * `U`: Minimalist rounded curved container.
  * `A`: Angular sans-crossbar upward chevron (`^` style).
  * `L`: Strict 90° geometric angle with clipped edges.
  * `I`: Clean vertical pill/slab bar.
  * `Z`: Sharp, segmented diagonal glyph.
  * `R`: Rounded bowl with a detached angled leg.
* **Primary Fonts**: *Anurati*, *Blanka*, *Ethnocentric*, or *Techno Sci-Fi*.
* **Web Fallback Chain**: `'Anurati', 'Blanka', 'Michroma', 'Orbitron', 'Syne', sans-serif`.
* **Sizing & Letter Spacing**:
  * Font Size: `clamp(4rem, 8vw, 8.5rem)`
  * Letter Spacing: `0.35em` to `0.45em`
  * Text Color: `#FFFFFF`
  * Drop Shadow: `0 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`

### 3.2 Display Sub-Heading (`She Can Occasionally See To An Enemy`)
* **Visual Style**: Clean, modern, high-tracking geometric sans-serif.
* **Font**: *Montserrat*, *Inter*, or *Sofia Pro*.
* **Web Fallback Chain**: `'Montserrat', 'Inter', -apple-system, sans-serif`.
* **Styling**:
  * Capitalization: Title Case / Capitalized Words
  * Font Size: `clamp(0.65rem, 0.9vw, 0.85rem)`
  * Weight: `500` (Medium)
  * Letter Spacing: `0.5em` (wide, airy tracking)
  * Text Color: `#FFFFFF`

### 3.3 UI Navigation, Card Copy & Micro-Typography
* **Primary Fonts**: *Inter*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, or *Montserrat*.
* **Navigation Links**:
  * Size: `11px` (`0.6875rem`)
  * Weight: `600` (Semi-Bold)
  * Case: `UPPERCASE`
  * Letter Spacing: `0.22em`
* **Card Title (`STRIKING DISCOVERY`)**:
  * Size: `10.5px`
  * Weight: `700` (Bold)
  * Case: `UPPERCASE`
  * Letter Spacing: `0.15em`
* **Card Body Text**:
  * Size: `9.5px` - `10px`
  * Weight: `400` (Regular)
  * Line Height: `1.45`
  * Color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`

## 4. Visual Subject (Hero Imagery)

* **Asset Type**: High-resolution cutout (isolated transparent PNG/WebP) or seamless studio composite photograph.
* **Character Profile**:
  * A female neo-ninja / cyber-samurai warrior in a deep, wide martial-arts stance.
  * **Headwear & Mask**: Traditional Asian conical straw hat (*kasa* / *coolie hat*) tilted forward, casting a deep shadow over the upper face. Black tactical lower-face mask covering the nose and mouth.
  * **Clothing**: Jet-black flowing martial arts kimono/gi with oversized draped sleeves, belted at the waist.
  * **Footwear**: Light slate-blue / cyan suede thigh-high stiletto boots reflecting the cyan rim light.
  * **Pose**: Low martial arts squat / lunging guard stance. Her right knee is bent deeply with foot forward; her left leg is fully extended toward the right edge of the frame. Her left hand is raised in an open-palm kung-fu warding gesture toward the right.
  * **Weapon**: High-tech glowing white lightsaber / neon katana held firmly in her right hand, pointed upward toward the top-left at roughly a 42-degree angle.
* **Positioning & Scale**:
  * Horizontally centered in the viewport.
  * Scale: The character’s silhouette occupies roughly 65% of the viewport height and 60% of the viewport width.
  * The katana blade reaches into the upper left magenta quadrant.
  * The extended left boot extends into the lower right quadrant.

### 5.1 Top Navigation Bar
* **Dimensions**: Height: `90px`, padding: `0 4.5vw`.
* **Display**: Flexbox, `justify-content: space-between`, `align-items: center`.
* **Left: Logo (`EQ`)**:
  * Custom vector logo mark:
    * `E`: Formed by three stacked, horizontally aligned pill bars.
    * `Q`: Adjacent circular ring with an offset diagonal tail.
  * Height: `24px`, Color: `#FFFFFF`.
* **Right: Menu Links**:
  * List: `HOME`, `ABOUT`, `SERVICES`, `WORK`, `CONTACT`.
  * Layout: Horizontal row, `gap: 2.75rem`.
  * Style: White text, uppercase, subtle opacity change on hover (`opacity: 0.7`).

### 5.2 Layered Hero Title & Character Stacking
To achieve the high-end 3D visual depth, layers must be ordered precisely:

* **Layer 1 (`z-index: 1`)**: Ambient dual-color background canvas.
* **Layer 2 (`z-index: 2`)**: **Central Title Text (`EQUALIZER`)**:
  * Centered vertically and horizontally across the screen.
  * Subtitle placed directly beneath the center letters ("U A L I"):
    * Text: `She Can Occasionally See To An Enemy`
    * Margin-top: `1.25rem`.
* **Layer 3 (`z-index: 3`)**: **Main Subject Image**:
  * The character’s torso, head, and thighs overlap the letters `U`, `A`, `L`, and `I`, while the sword slices diagonally across the `E` and `Q` area in the foreground.
* **Layer 4 (`z-index: 4`)**: Foreground UI components (Glass card, Navigation, Pagination, Social links).

### 5.3 Bottom-Left Glassmorphic Feature Card
* **Position**: Fixed/Absolute, `bottom: 40px`, `left: 4.5vw`.
* **Dimensions**:
  * Width: `clamp(280px, 22vw, 340px)`.
  * Min-height: `88px`.
* **Styling**:
  * Background: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12) 0%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03) 100%)`.
  * Backdrop filter: `blur(20px) saturate(160%)`.
  * Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.22)`.
  * Border-radius: `6px` (sharp, minimal rounding).
  * Padding: `16px 20px`.
  * Box-shadow: `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.3)`.
* **Content Structure**:
  * Two-column layout (`display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 16px;`).
  * **Left Column (Play Button)**:
    * Circular container: diameter `42px`.
    * Border: `1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`.
    * Center: Solid white equilateral play triangle (width: `12px`, pointing right).
  * **Right Column (Metadata)**:
    * **Header**: `STRIKING DISCOVERY` (bold, white, tracking `0.12em`, `margin-bottom: 5px`).
    * **Body**: `See Antoine Fuqua full list of movies and tv shows from their career. Find where to watch Antoine Fuqua's latest movies` (line-height: `1.4`, light slate white).

### 5.4 Right-Hand Vertical Pagination Dots
* **Position**: Absolute, `right: 3.5vw`, `top: 50%`, `transform: translateY(-50%)`.
* **Structure**: Vertical flexbox column (`flex-direction: column; gap: 16px; align-items: center;`).
* **Elements**: 5 circular pagination indicators.
* **Style**:
  * Size: `5px` × `5px` outer diameter.
  * Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`.
  * Fill: Transparent (hollow ring).
  * Active state (middle or top): Solid filled white (`#FFFFFF`) with a faint `0 0 8px #FFFFFF` glow.

### 5.5 Bottom-Right Social Micro-Nav
* **Position**: Absolute, `bottom: 45px`, `right: 4.5vw`.
* **Structure**: Horizontal flexbox (`display: flex; gap: 1.75rem; align-items: center;`).
* **Items**:
  * `IN` (LinkedIn)
  * `TW` (Twitter / X)
  * `YT` (YouTube)
* **Styling**:
  * Font: Inter/Montserrat, `11px`, bold, uppercase, color `#FFFFFF`.
  * Letter-spacing: `0.15em`.
  * Cursor: Pointer with hover color transition to electric cyan (`#0091ff`).

## 6. Lighting, Glow, and FX Specs

1. **Katana Saber Blade Glow**:
   * Pure white core bar: `box-shadow: 0 0 10px #fff, 0 0 20px #ff3df5, 0 0 40px #a13dff;`.
2. **Backdrop Vignette Overlay**:
   * Add a CSS radial gradient overlay across the entire screen:
     ```css
     background: radial-gradient(circle at 50% 50%, transparent 60%, rgba(2, 6, 18, 0.65) 100%);
     ```
3. **Film Grain (Optional Polish)**:
   * A delicate 2% monochromatic noise texture applied via `mix-blend-mode: overlay` to enhance the cinematic movie poster atmosphere.
