# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Modern Clean SaaS Analytics Dashboard with Soft Glassmorphism, Rounded Metrics Panels, and Subtle Pastel/Monochrome Tinting.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace density with generous padding (16px to 32px gaps) separating floating card containers, clean grid alignment, and crisp iconography.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered card depth utilizing flat neutral surfaces (`#FFFFFF` on `#F3F4F6`), subtle border definitions (`#E5E7EB`), rounded pills (`9999px`), and large curved card radii (`16px`/`20px`) with minimal drop shadows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Clean status indicators with soft gradients, floating interactive tooltips, pill-shaped dark primary navigation pills, minimalist vector sparklines.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
