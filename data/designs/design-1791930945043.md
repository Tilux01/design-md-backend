# Design Specification: LÄMPZ - Lumier Dark

A detailed layout and styling specification to clone the modern, dark-themed product landing page for **LÄMPZ**.

---

## 1. Color Palette

* **Primary Background:** `#1c212a` (Dark Slate / Charcoal)
* **Background Diagonal Overlays:** `#ffffff` at `3%` to `6%` opacity (linear diagonal light slashes angled at ~-35deg)
* **Accent Orange:** `#ff4e27` (Bright Coral/Tangerine — used for buttons, active indicators, cord loop)
* **Primary Text (Warm White):** `#f4ece1` (Cream/Warm Off-White)
* **Secondary Text (Pure White):** `#ffffff`
* **Muted / Inactive Text:** `#525b6a` (Medium Slate Grey)
* **Nav Button Fill:** `#252c37`

---

## 2. Typography

* **Primary Display Font:** `Montserrat` (Fallback: `Arial Black`, `system-ui`, `sans-serif`)
  * Heavy weights (`700`, `800`) for headers and logos.
* **Secondary Font (Body & Thin Titles):** `Inter` or `Helvetica Neue` (Fallback: `Arial`, `sans-serif`)
  * Thin weights (`200`, `300`) for secondary headers; Regular (`400`) for body.
* **Font Details:**
  * **Brand Logo:** `Montserrat`, 18px, Bold, Uppercase, letter-spacing: 2px.
  * **Main Title ("LUMIER"):** `Montserrat`, 64px, ExtraBold, Uppercase, Tracking: 1px.
  * **Sub Title ("DARK"):** `Inter`, 56px, ExtraLight (200), Uppercase, Tracking: 4px.
  * **Slider Numbers:** `Inter`, 14px, Bold (`03` active: 16px).
  * **Body Copy:** `Inter`, 12px, Regular, Line-height: 1.6.
  * **Rotated Social Links:** `Inter`, 11px, Regular, Tracking: 1.5px.

---

## 3. Screen Layout & Structure

Full-viewport desktop layout (`100vw` × `100vh`) with fixed elements and minimal scroll.

### 3.1 Background Layer
* Base fill: `#1c212a`.
* Two broad, semi-transparent diagonal geometric light bands angled at approximately `125deg`, cutting through the center and behind the lamp image.

### 3.2 Header (Top Bar)
* **Height:** 80px, edge-to-edge padding (0 48px).
* **Left:** Brand mark — minimalist smiley icon (`(:)`) followed by text `LÄMPZ`.
* **Right Elements (Flex row, gap 24px):**
  * Search icon (thin stroke, 18px, `#525b6a`).
  * "MENU" label (`#ffffff`, 12px, uppercase, tracking: 2px).
  * Menu toggle button: Rounded-rectangle (`42px × 42px`, fill `#252c37`), containing 3 horizontal white lines (hamburger icon).

### 3.3 Left Sidebar (Pagination Indicator)
* **Position:** Absolute/Fixed, left: 48px, vertically centered.
* **Layout:** Vertical flex column, gap: 20px.
* **Items:**
  * `01` (Muted `#525b6a`)
  * `02` (Muted `#525b6a`)
  * `— 03` (Active: `#ffffff`, preceded by an orange horizontal dash `#ff4e27` of width 18px)
  * `04` (Muted `#525b6a`)

### 3.4 Hero Content (Center-Left)
* **Position:** Left 24%, vertically centered (aligned above the bottom features).
* **Title:**
  * Line 1: `LUMIER` (Color: `#f4ece1`, Font-weight: 800).
  * Line 2: `DARK` (Color: `#ffffff`, Font-weight: 200).
* **CTA Button:**
  * Dimensions: `120px` width, `36px` height, full pill radius (`999px`).
  * Fill: `#ff4e27`.
  * Label: "DISCOVER", 10px, bold, uppercase, tracking: 1.5px, white.
  * Margin-top: 24px.

### 3.5 Bottom Information Columns
* **Position:** Fixed bottom: 48px, left: 30%, right: 20%.
* **Layout:** 2-column grid, gap: 64px.
* **Column 1:**
  * Title: "Light Style" (White, 13px, Semi-Bold).
  * Text: "This Lamp has a metallic silver and polished finish that is complemented well by its sheer snow shantung shade. With a pleasant design to any room." (Color: `#7c8798`, 11px).
* **Column 2:**
  * Title: "Minimalist" (White, 13px, Semi-Bold).
  * Text: "The tall, slim, curved arc has a brushed nickel finish with a white marble inner." (Color: `#7c8798`, 11px).

### 3.6 Right Sidebar (Social Links)
* **Position:** Absolute, right: 32px, vertically centered.
* **Orientation:** Rotated -90deg (bottom-to-top).
* **Content:** `Facebook  ——  Twitter  ——  Instagram`
* **Color:** `#525b6a`, 11px, with thin horizontal divider lines between names.

---

## 4. Hero Product Image Specification

* **Type:** Background-isolated transparent PNG.
* **Subject:** Matte black/charcoal designer desk lamp.
  * **Lamp Head:** Conical matte dark shade, interior reflector visible in off-white/light grey, angled downward-left (~45°).
  * **Hardware:** Rotary adjustment bolt/knob on joint, textured dark finish.
  * **Accent:** Vibrant orange loop handle/pull cord extending from the top back socket.
  * **Stem:** Smooth curved cylindrical arm bending downwards vertically along the right side.
* **Placement:**
  * Overlapping the right-center section.
  * Positioned from top: ~15% to bottom: 0, with the vertical stem exiting the bottom frame.
  * Scale: Approximately 55% of viewport width.
