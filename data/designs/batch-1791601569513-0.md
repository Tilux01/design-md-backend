# Design Specification (`design.md`): Iceland Discovery Hero Interface

This document specifies the complete, full-screen implementation of the provided UI design. All mockups, external frames, tilts, and canvas borders have been removed in favor of a responsive, full-screen (edge-to-edge, `100vw` × `100vh`) hero section.

---

## 1. Imagery & Asset Specifications

### 1.1 Primary Background Image
* **Description**: A sweeping, cinematic, high-resolution landscape photograph of the southern/central highlands of Iceland (resembling Landmannalaugar or Fjallabak). 
  * **Landscape Features**: Dramatic, steep volcanic mountain ridges covered in vibrant mossy green patches on deep black volcanic basalt. In the valley below, a calm glacial/volcanic crater lake with milky-blue/slate-grey water reflects soft diffuse light. Rolling ridges fade into a hazy, fog-laden overcast horizon.
  * **Color Profile**: Desaturated, moody Scandinavian palette—slate grey, charcoal black (`#1A1C18`), damp moss green (`#5F6F3A`), and pale mist grey/white sky (`#D5DAD8`).
  * **Asset Type**: Non-transparent, full-bleed photographic background (not backgroundless).
  * **Placement**: Full-bleed background across the entire viewport (`width: 100vw; height: 100vh; object-fit: cover; object-position: center 30%;`).

## 2. Color Palette & Surface System

### 2.1 Colors
* **Primary White**: `#FFFFFF` (pure solid white for active navigation, main headings, and CTA)
* **Secondary White/Muted**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` (secondary text, paragraphs, outlined controls)
* **Ghost Heading Grey**: `rgba(220, 225, 225, 0.35)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)` (for the frosted "ICE" text)
* **Dark Charcoal (Header text & icons)**: `#141517` / `#1F2022`
* **Frosted Tint**: `rgba(35, 42, 40, 0.45)` to `rgba(20, 24, 22, 0.65)`

### 2.2 Glassmorphism Surface (Left Hero Panel)
* **Dimensions**: Spans the full height below the navbar down to the viewport bottom (`height: calc(100vh - 72px)`), occupying `42%` of the viewport width (`width: 42vw; min-width: 480px`).
* **Background Fill**: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(60, 68, 65, 0.45) 0%, rgba(24, 28, 27, 0.70) 100%)`
* **Backdrop Filter**: `backdrop-filter: blur(32px) saturate(130%); -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(32px) saturate(130%);`
* **Borders**: 
  * Right edge: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`
  * Top / Left / Bottom: `none`

## 5. Component Breakdown & Styling

### 5.1 Top Navigation Bar
* **Position**: Sticky/Absolute top: `top: 0; left: 0; width: 100vw; height: 72px; z-index: 50;`
* **Background**: Solid clean white (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle shadow (`0 2px 10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`).
* **Inner Container**: Flexbox layout with space-between alignment; horizontal padding: `48px`.
* **Left Sub-group**:
  * Label: `"FOLLOW US"` (Font size: `12px`, Weight: `600`, tracking: `1.8px`).
  * Icon Group: Spacing `16px` to the right of label. Contains 4 outline icons spaced `14px` apart:
    1. **Instagram**: Standard camera glyph outline (`18px`).
    2. **YouTube**: Rounded rectangle with play triangle outline (`18px`).
    3. **Telegram**: Paper airplane glyph outline (`18px`).
    4. **WhatsApp**: Chat bubble with handset glyph outline (`18px`).
  * Icon Color: Solid dark charcoal (`#1F2022`).
* **Right Sub-group**:
  * Navigation links horizontally aligned with `36px` spacing:
    1. `DESTINATIONS`
    2. `DEALS`
    3. `SPECIAL OFFERS` *(note: UI renders literal "SECIAL OFFERS")*
    4. `ABOUT US`
  * Text Style: `#1F2022`, font-weight `600`, font-size `12.5px`, tracking `1.5px`.

---

### 5.2 Split Hero Wordmark ("ICE" & "LEND")
* **Concept**: The country name ("ICELAND", stylized as "ICELEND") is fractured into two overlapping typographic blocks positioned asymmetrically:
  1. **"ICE" (Left, Blurred Domain)**:
     * Positioned inside the frosted glass pane.
     * Horizontal alignment: Center-right of the left frosted container (`margin-right: 15%`).
     * Vertical alignment: Approximately `40%` down from the top bar.
     * Visual effect: Translucent greyish-white fill with subtle inset/drop-shadow effect (`text-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35); color: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.40);`).
  2. **"LEND" (Right, Sharp Landscape Domain)**:
     * Positioned outside the glass pane, directly over the dark mountain landscape.
     * Horizontal alignment: Starts near the right edge of the frosted panel (`left: 44vw`).
     * Vertical alignment: Shifted downward compared to "ICE" (`top: 52%` from viewport top).
     * Color: Crisp, opaque solid white (`#FFFFFF`).
     * Visual effect: High-contrast drop shadow for separation against the background (`text-shadow: 0 8px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`).

---

### 5.3 Left Content Section (Tour Info & Controls)
Contained within the lower portion of the left frosted glass panel, padded `48px` from left and bottom edges:

1. **Tour Number & Pagination**:
   * Number: `"01"` in a light geometric font.
   * Carousel Dots: Positioned directly below `"01"`:
     * 4 horizontal circular dots (`gap: 6px; margin-top: 10px;`).
     * Dot 1 (Active): Solid white circle (`width: 7px; height: 7px; background: #FFFFFF; border-radius: 50%;`).
     * Dots 2, 3, 4 (Inactive): Hollow circular outlines (`width: 7px; height: 7px; border: 1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7); border-radius: 50%;`).

2. **Tour Description Block**:
   * Placed to the right of the `"01"` counter (aligned horizontally with `"01"`).
   * Title: `"DISCOVER TOURS"` (`font-size: 16px; font-weight: 600; letter-spacing: 2px; color: #FFFFFF; margin-bottom: 12px;`).
   * Paragraph Copy:
     > *"Iceland is an amazing country. This is a country of beautiful waterfalls, geysers, active volcanoes and icebergs. These are long beaches of black sand and blue, black, azure icebergs."*
   * Dimensions: `max-width: 310px; line-height: 1.6; color: rgba(235, 240, 240, 0.65); font-size: 11.5px;`.

3. **Call-to-Action (CTA)**:
   * Positioned below the description block (`margin-top: 36px;`).
   * Text: `"VIEW ALL TRIPS"` (White `#FFFFFF`, font size `12px`, tracking `2px`).
   * Graphic Line with Arrow:
     * Next to the text, a clean white horizontal rule extending `140px` to `160px`.
     * Terminated with a rightward triangular arrowhead (`→` stroke vector).
     * Rule styling: `height: 1.5px; background: #FFFFFF; opacity: 0.85;`.

## 6. Motion & Interactive Details (Implementation Notes)
* **Background Zoom**: Subtle Ken Burns zoom (`transform: scale(1.03)` over 20s) adds atmospheric depth to the landscape.
* **Pagination Interaction**: Clicking the dots transitions the counter (`01` -> `02`), updates the description text, and smoothly fades the landscape image.
* **CTA Hover**: Hovering over `"VIEW ALL TRIPS"` causes the horizontal line and arrowhead to slide right by `8px` (`transition: transform 0.3s ease`).
