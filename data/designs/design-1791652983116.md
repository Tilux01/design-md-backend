# UI/UX Specification: "MOYA Matcha & Tea" Mobile App

This document serves as a complete, pixel-for-pixel visual and structural design specification for the **MOYA Matcha & Tea** mobile application, derived directly from the provided multi-screen layout. 

---

## 1. Global Design System & Foundations

### 1.1 Color Palette
*   **Deep Forest Green (Primary Brand & Splash Screen Background):** 
    *   Hex: `#38483B`
    *   Usage: Splash screen background, primary accents, bottom navigation highlights.
*   **Warm Cream / Oat (Secondary Brand & App Interface Background):** 
    *   Hex: `#F4F0E6` (Approximate)
    *   Usage: Main application background, card container fills, secondary modal elements.
*   **Dark Charcoal / Soft Black (Typography & Primary Icons):** 
    *   Hex: `#1E241F`
    *   Usage: Main headings, product titles, prices, active icons.
*   **Muted Gray / Olive Tint (Secondary Text & Borders):** 
    *   Hex: `#787C75`
    *   Usage: Subheadings, descriptive copy, divider lines, inactive navigation icons.
*   **Pure White / Off-White (Containers):** 
    *   Hex: `#FFFFFF`
    *   Usage: Inner product card backings, quantity selector backgrounds.

### 1.2 Typography
*   **Primary Brand Font (Logos & Large Headers):**
    *   *Family:* **Cinzel** or **Didot** (High-contrast, elegant modern serif with sharp geometric or classic terminals).
    *   *Fallback Stack:* `Didot, "Bodoni MT", "Cinzel", "Times New Roman", serif`
    *   *Weight:* Regular (400) to Semi-Bold (600). Uppercase tracking is heavily utilized (`letter-spacing: 0.15em`).
*   **Secondary UI Font (Body, Navigation, Prices, Buttons):**
    *   *Family:* **Montserrat** or **Inter** (Clean, highly legible geometric sans-serif).
    *   *Fallback Stack:* `Montserrat, Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
    *   *Weights:* Light (300) for descriptions, Regular (400) for general body, Medium (500) for buttons and labels, Bold (700) for pricing and section titles.

## 2. Screen-by-Screen Breakdown

---

### Screen 1: Splash / Loading Screen

#### Layout & Structure
*   **Background:** Solid Deep Forest Green (`#38483B`), edge-to-edge.
*   **Alignment:** Perfectly centered vertical layout with an organic watermark illustration at the lower-middle portion.

#### Component Breakdown
1.  **Brand Logo:**
    *   **Text:** `MOYA`
    *   **Font:** Serif (`Cinzel` style), uppercase, spaced heavily.
    *   **Subtext:** `MATCHA & TEA`
    *   **Spacing Line/Icon:** A delicate, minimalist minimalist leaf-line art icon sitting directly beneath the subtext.
2.  **Bottom Text & Loading State:**
    *   **Copy:** `PREMIUM MATCHA.` / `MINDFUL MOMENTS.`
    *   **Font:** Sans-serif, uppercase, tracking wide. Color: Cream/White with low opacity.
    *   **Loading Bar:** A thin horizontal track line spanning approximately 40% of the screen width, filled with an active progress indicator pill on the left edge.
    *   **Label:** `LOADING...` in tiny uppercase sans-serif.
