# Design Specification: RENTRA Portfolio / Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
- **View:** Full-screen viewport (`100vw` width, `100vh` height), immersive hero layout.
- **Scroll:** Single-page view layout.
- **Theme/Color Palette:** Monochrome (Black & White).
  - Background base: Off-white/light gray gradient or atmospheric photographic background.
  - Text & Branding elements: Solid black (`#000000`) and subtle gray borders (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)`).

---

## 2. Background Imagery & Visual Assets
- **Background Image:** 
  - **Description:** A dramatic, cinematic black-and-white photograph of a high-performance sports/race car taking a sharp turn on a track or wet asphalt road. Heavy atmospheric fog/mist surrounds the car with visible motion blur emphasizing speed and motion. The road features a clean, curving white lane line leading the viewer's eye into the center.
  - **Placement:** Full-screen background covering the entire viewport (`width: 100%`, `height: 100%`, `object-fit: cover`).
  - **Rotation/Effects:** Monochromatic filter applied; high contrast between the dark asphalt and the misty white upper background.
- **Logo Graphic:**
  - **Description:** A bold, abstract monogram logo representing the letter "R". It features geometric, solid black blocks with a dynamic, curved cutout reminiscent of speed, aerodynamics, or a road curve.
  - **Placement:** Centered horizontally, positioned directly above the main brand name "RENTRA".
  - **Background:** Backgroundless (transparent SVG/PNG).

---

## 3. Typography & Text Elements

### A. Main Branding
- **Brand Name:** `RENTRA`
- **Font Family:** Geometric Sans-Serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Syne*, *Space Grotesk*, or *Helvetica Neue*).
- **Fallback:** `sans-serif`.
- **Styling:** Uppercase, heavy/bold weight, wide letter-spacing.
- **Placement:** Horizontally centered, directly below the logo graphic.

### B. Navigation / Service Categories
- **Items:** 
  1. `UI/UX DESIGN ↗`
  2. `BRAND IDENTITY ↗`
  3. `PRODUCT DESIGN ↗`
  4. `WEB DESIGN ↗`
- **Font Family:** Clean sans-serif (e.g., *Inter* or *Helvetica*).
- **Fallback:** `sans-serif`.
- **Styling:** Uppercase, regular to medium weight, small text size. Each item includes a thin top border separator and an upward-right diagonal arrow (`↗`).
- **Placement:** Distributed in a horizontal row across the lower middle section of the screen, above the footer metadata.

### C. Footer Metadata
- **Left Footer Text:** `VIKAS MORVADIYA`
- **Right Footer Text:** `uiuxdesigner`
- **Font Family:** Sans-serif.
- **Fallback:** `sans-serif`.
- **Styling:** Uppercase (left) and lowercase/branded styling (right), muted contrast for subtle UI hierarchy.
- **Placement:** Fixed near the bottom corners of the viewport (Left: bottom-left corner; Right: bottom-right corner).

---

## 4. Spacing, Alignment & Grid System
- **Alignment:** Centralized focal point for branding elements (`logo` + `RENTRA`), with peripheral UI elements anchored to the bottom navigation bar and corners.
- **Responsive Behavior:** 
  - On desktop, service categories sit in a clean 4-column horizontal flex row with spacing.
  - On mobile/tablet, the service categories stack vertically while maintaining the top border lines and right-aligned arrows.
