# Design Specification: Biorg Multivitamins App Interface

## 1. Overall Layout & Global Styles

### Background & Color Palette
*   **Primary Background:** Deep black (`#0a0a0a` or `#000000`), dominating the outer container and the lower half of the mobile screen.
*   **Secondary Background (Header/Card):** Light gray/off-white (`#f2f2f2` or `#ececec`), forming the rounded-bottom top container of the phone screen that houses the product mockup.
*   **Accent Color (Lime Green):** Vibrant lime/chartreuse (`#b5f542` or `#c5ff4a`), used for price text, product branding subtitles, and active states.
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   Pure White (`#ffffff`) for primary headers, titles, and main UI icons.
    *   Muted Gray (`#888888`) for secondary body text, ingredient subtotals, and inactive bottom navigation icons.
    *   Dark Charcoal (`#1a1a1a`) for text inside light-themed interactive buttons (e.g., the add icon `+` button).

### Typography & Fonts
*   **Primary Font Family:** Clean, geometric sans-serif (such as **Inter**, **SF Pro Display**, or **Plus Jakarta Sans**).
*   **Fallbacks:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Text Hierarchy:**
    *   **Project / Meta Labels (Top Bar):** Uppercase, tracking/letter-spacing wide, small font size (~10-12px), muted gray.
    *   **App Header Time & Status:** Standard system font, 14px, bold, white.
    *   **Main Product Title ("Daily Formula"):** Heavy bold sans-serif, large display scale (~32px), white.
    *   **Subtitles & Descriptions:** Regular weight, ~14px, off-white/gray, leading (line-height) relaxed.
    *   **Price Tag:** Bold, lime green, ~20px.

---

## 2. Header / Meta Bar (External to Phone Screen)
Positioned at the very top of the desktop presentation layout in tracking-spaced uppercase:
*   **Top-Left:** `PROJECT: BIORG`
*   **Top-Middle-Left:** `PRODUCTS: BRANDING, APP, WEBSITE`
*   **Center Logo:** A minimalist, stylized lowercase handwritten-style serif letter **"b"** in white.
*   **Top-Middle-Right:** `DESIGNERS: BY.SHIVA X UXPOLLY`
*   **Top-Right:** `YEAR: 2026`

## 3. Mobile Device Screen Specifications

### Dimensions & Structure
*   **Device Type:** iOS Smartphone mockup (represented with rounded corners and a top dynamic island/notch area).
*   **Status Bar:** 
    *   Time: `9:41` (Left-aligned)
    *   System Icons: Cellular signal, Wi-Fi, and Battery level indicators (Right-aligned, pure white).

### Top Section (Light Background Container)
*   **Shape:** Rounded rectangle occupying the top ~40% of the screen with an off-white background (`#f2f2f2`).
*   **Navigation / Back Arrow:** 
    *   A left-pointing chevron icon (`<`) enclosed in a solid white circular button with a soft drop shadow, positioned centrally-left.
*   **Product Mockup Image:**
    *   **Description:** A photorealistic, dark olive-green tinted glass jar with a wide black ribbed screw-on cap. Inside the translucent glass, dark green multivitamin capsules are faintly visible. The jar features a minimalist centered logo (white lowercase "b") and tiny subtext reading "Multivitamins / Daily Formula" printed horizontally across the label.
    *   **Transparency:** The jar image is backgroundless (isolated with transparent padding).
    *   **Placement & Rotation:** Centered horizontally within the light container, standing straight upright (0-degree rotation).

### Middle Section (Dark Background Container)
*   **Category Pill:** 
    *   A small, pill-shaped dark gray badge with rounded borders centered just below the jar container, containing the word **"Complex"** in white text.
*   **Product Title:**
    *   **"Daily Formula"** in large, bold, pure white 32px typography.
*   **Product Description:**
    *   *Text:* "Essential vitamins and minerals to support energy, immunity, and overall well-being."
    *   *Styling:* Centered alignment, muted gray, regular weight, max-width constrained to fit nicely within the mobile margins.
*   **Pricing & Action Row:**
    *   **Price:** `$35.99` in bright lime green, bold font weight.
    *   **Add Button (`+`):** A solid white circular button containing a bold black plus (`+`) sign, positioned to the right of the price.

### Bottom Section (Ingredients & Navigation)
*   **Section Header:**
    *   *Label:* "Key Ingredients" (Left-aligned, white, small heading).
    *   *Indicator:* A small horizontal grab-handle bar centered above the ingredients list.
*   **Ingredients List Items:**
    *   **Item 1:**
        *   Title: **Vitamin D3** (White, bold)
        *   Value/Unit: **2000 IU** (Right-aligned, muted gray)
        *   Separator: A thin horizontal dividing line spanning the width of the card.
    *   **Item 2:**
        *   Title: **Zinc** (White, bold)
        *   Value/Unit: **15 mg** (Right-aligned, muted gray)
        *   Separator: A thin horizontal dividing line.
*   **Bottom Navigation Bar (Floating Dock):**
    *   **Background:** Semi-transparent dark frosted glass (`rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.8)` with backdrop blur).
    *   **Shape:** Rounded pill bar spanning horizontally across the bottom of the screen.
    *   **Icons (Left to Right):**
        1.  **Home Icon:** Outline of a house (Inactive, muted gray).
        2.  **Capsule/Pill Icon:** Outline of a medication capsule (Inactive, muted gray).
        3.  **Calendar/Grid Icon:** Outline of a calendar grid (Inactive, muted gray).
        4.  **Shopping Bag Icon (Active):** Solid white circular container housing a black shopping bag icon, elevated slightly above the dock bar to signify the current active screen/state.
        5.  **Settings/Gear Icon:** Outline of a gear (Inactive, muted gray).

Outside the main mobile mockup frame at the bottom right:
