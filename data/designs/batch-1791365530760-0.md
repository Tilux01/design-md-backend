# Light Bulb Landing Page Design Specification

## 1. Overall Layout & Structure
- **View:** Full-screen layout (Edge-to-edge).
- **Theme:** Dark Mode, modern minimalist design style.
- **Grid Layout:** 
  - **Left Area:** Large product imagery (Light Bulb) dominating the left side.
  - **Right Area:** Editorial text content with typography hierarchy, numbers, and horizontal separator.
  - **Bottom Footer Bar:** Social media links and a down-arrow navigation trigger.
  - **Header:** Brand logo top-left, interactive Menu top-right.

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Background (Base):** Dark Charcoal Gray (`#212427` or similar dark gradient backdrop).
- **Primary Accent (Orange):** Vibrant safety orange (`#F35B24`) used for:
  - Number "01"
  - Arrow buttons (social links and bottom-right scroll button)
  - Mail icon accent
- **Primary Text (White):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) used for main headings ("Be The Light.") and Menu.
- **Secondary Text (Muted Gray):** Light gray (`#A0A4A8`) used for paragraph descriptions and social links.
- **Background Elements (Subtle):** Vertical background text ("Light Bulb.") in low-opacity gray (`#3A3E42`).

---

## 3. Typography
*Font Family (Clean Sans-Serif like Inter, Roboto, or Helvetica):*

- **Header / Logo:** Custom stylized monogram icon (top-left).
- **Menu:** 
  - Text: `Menu` (Uppercase, small, tracking/letter-spacing applied).
  - Icon: 4-dot menu grid symbol (`⠇` or custom 2x2 grid dots) in orange/white.
- **Vertical Background Text:** 
  - Content: `Light Bulb.`
  - Style: Vertical text orientation, large bold sans-serif, low opacity.
- **Section Number:**
  - Content: `01`
  - Style: Bold sans-serif, vibrant orange (`#F35B24`), large size.
- **Main Heading:**
  - Content: `Be The Light.`
  - Style: Clean white sans-serif, medium-large display weight.
- **Body Paragraph:**
  - Content: *"The filament is protected from oxidation with a glass or fused quartz bulb that is filled with inert gas or a vacuum. In a halogen lamp, filament evaporation is slowed by a chemical process that redeposits metal vapor onto the filament, thereby extending its life."*
  - Style: Muted gray, regular weight, small legible font size, controlled line-height.

---

## 4. Component Breakdown & Positioning

### Header Section
- **Top Left:** Logo symbol (White minimalist rounded icon with geometric cuts).
- **Top Right:** Menu interaction button featuring a 4-dot icon followed by the word "Menu".

### Hero / Main Content Area
- **Left Column:**
  - High-res product photography of a vintage Edison-style bulb with glowing orange filaments against a neutral gray studio background.
- **Center/Right Column:**
  - Vertical watermark text running alongside the content: `Light Bulb.`
  - Section marker: `01` placed above a subtle horizontal divider line.
  - Heading: `Be The Light.`
  - Paragraph text directly below the heading.

### Footer Bar
- **Bottom Left:** Mail/Contact icon with an orange accent box.
- **Bottom Center:** Social links with small orange triangle arrow indicators:
  - `▶ facebook`
  - `▶ instagram`
  - `▶ pinterest`
- **Bottom Right:** 
  - A solid orange square action button containing a white downward-pointing arrow (`↓`).

---

## 5. Interactive States & Behaviors (For Implementation)
- **Hover Effects:** Social links and Menu should feature subtle opacity shifts or color highlights.
- **Scroll/Navigation:** The bottom-right orange arrow button functions as a "Next Section" or scroll trigger.
