# Design Specification: Y-3 Adidas Yohji Yamamoto Product Page

A full-screen, dark-mode luxury e-commerce product landing page.

---

## 1. Color Palette

* **Base Background:** `#222326` (Subtle radial gradient brightening slightly toward center `#2C2D32`)
* **Primary Accent:** `#FF3B4E` (Vibrant Coral/Red)
* **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (Solid White)
* **Text Secondary / Muted:** `#7A7D84` (Medium Slate Gray)
* **Watermark / Background Graphics:** `#1C1D20` / Opacity-reduced Charcoal

---

## 2. Typography

* **Primary Font (Headings, UI, Body):** *Montserrat* or *Inter*
  * *Fallbacks:* `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`
  * *Weights:* 400 (Regular), 600 (Semi-bold), 800 (Extra-bold)
* **Signature Font:** Custom Yohji Yamamoto vector signature
  * *Fallback Font:* *Autography*, *Caveat*, `cursive`

---

## 3. Layout & Structure

Full-screen viewport layout (`100vw`, `100vh`) with fixed margins (approx. `48px` padding).

### Top Navigation Bar
* **Left:** 
  * Hamburger Menu: Three horizontal white bars (`20px` width, `2px` height, `4px` gap).
  * Brand Logo: Two-line text block:
    * `BRAND` (White, Bold, uppercase, `13px`)
    * `MARKET` (Accent Red `#FF3B4E`, Bold, uppercase, `13px`)
* **Right:** 
  * Shopping Cart Icon: Minimalist shopping cart glyph in muted white/gray.
  * Badge: Top-right corner solid red notification dot (`6px` diameter).

### Left Sidebar / Micro-UI
* **Pagination Dots (Center-left vertical alignment):**
  * 5 vertical dots spaced `12px` apart.
  * Dot 1 (Active): Solid red `#FF3B4E` with an outer ring or slightly enlarged (`8px`).
  * Dots 2–5: Inactive muted gray dots (`4px`).
* **Bottom-Left Area:**
  * Numeric Indicator: `027` (White, Bold, `18px`).
  * Social Links (Directly below): `FB`, `TW`, `IN` (Muted gray `#7A7D84`, uppercase, `11px`, horizontal layout).

---

## 4. Main Stage & Image Assets

### Hero Product (Foreground - Left/Center)
* **Asset Description:** High-top black avant-garde Y-3 sneaker, complete profile facing right. Features technical fabrics, neoprene collar, chunky sculpted midsole, and minimal heel branding.
* **Format:** Backgroundless PNG (cutout).
* **Placement:** Vertically centered, left of canvas center.
* **Effects:** Soft, dispersed black drop shadow directly beneath the sole on the floor.

### Graphic Composition (Right Half)
* **Watermark Typography:** Giant `Y-3` logo set in ultra-bold sans-serif, dark charcoal tint (`#1D1E22`), filling roughly 50% of the screen height.
* **Secondary Shoe Cutout:**
  * **Asset Description:** Backgroundless cutout showing a close-up/rear-three-quarter perspective of the sneaker heel and sole.
  * **Placement:** Overlaid inside and extending through the right half of the giant `Y-3` graphic.
* **Secondary Text:**
  * Text: `adidas | YOHJI YAMAMOTO` in white medium-weight sans-serif (`14px`), centered horizontally over the right graphic.
* **Vector Signature:**
  * Japanese designer Yohji Yamamoto's handwritten signature rendered in pure white vector stroke, layered across the lower segment of the right shoe/watermark.

---

## 5. Bottom Action Bar

Aligned along the lower-third, horizontally spanning below the footwear:

* **Product Title (Left):**
  * Line 1: `Y-3 ADIDAS`
  * Line 2: `YOHJI YAMAMOTO`
  * Style: White, Extra-Bold, uppercase, `22px`, tight line-height.
* **Add to Cart CTA (Center):**
  * Style: Pill-shaped button (`border-radius: 9999px`).
  * Background: Solid accent red (`#FF3B4E`).
  * Label: `ADD TO CART` (White, bold, `12px`, uppercase, generous horizontal padding).
* **Price Tag (Right of Button):**
  * Text: `$ 555`
  * Style: Muted grayish-white (`#9CA0AA`), Bold, `22px`.
