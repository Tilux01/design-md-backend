# Design Specification: Vision-Stream Music Interface

## 1. Core Visual Language
*   **Theme:** Glassmorphism / Spatial UI.
*   **Background:** The application should sit on a subtle, dark-to-light neutral gradient or a soft-focus abstract background to allow the glass effect to pop.
*   **Surface Material:** 
    *   **Main Container:** Semi-transparent white/grey (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
    *   **Backdrop Blur:** `40px` to `60px`.
    *   **Border:** `1px` solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` to create a "glass edge" highlight.
    *   **Shadow:** Large, soft ambient occlusion shadow (`0 20px 50px rgba(0,0,0,0.3)`).
*   **Corner Radius:** High rounding. Main container: `40px`. Buttons/Pills: `20px` to `full`.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **SF Pro Display** (Apple's system font).
*   **Secondary/Fallback:** **Inter**, **Roboto**, or a clean **Sans-Serif**.
*   **Weights:** 
    *   Regular (400) for secondary info and lists.
    *   Medium (500) for navigation items.
    *   Bold (700) for "Sia" and "Discover".
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   Primary: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
    *   Secondary: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` (Muted White for play counts and durations).

## 3. Layout Structure

### A. The Sidebar (Left)
*   **Width:** ~20% of the container.
*   **Header:** "Discover" (Large, Bold, White).
*   **Dropdown:** "Artist" with a downward chevron.
*   **Navigation List:**
    *   Items: Popular, Discography, Featuring Sia, Fans also like, Appears on.
    *   **Active State (Popular):** A pill-shaped background with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` and a small lightning bolt icon.
*   **Floating Icon Dock:** A vertical pill detached to the far left containing four icons: Home, Clock, Rocket, and Heart.

### B. The Hero Section (Artist Profile)
*   **Background:** A darker, recessed glass panel (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1)`).
*   **Verified Badge:** Small blue circle with a white checkmark, followed by "Verified artist" in small caps.
*   **Artist Name:** "Sia" in very large, bold font.
*   **Stats:** "45,337,113 monthly listeners" in secondary text color.
*   **Artist Image:**
    *   **Subject:** A high-resolution profile shot of the artist Sia.
    *   **Characteristics:** She is wearing her iconic platinum blonde bob wig with bangs covering the eyes. She is facing left, showing her profile.
    *   **Placement:** Right-aligned within the hero section.
    *   **Masking:** The image must be **backgroundless (PNG)**, blending seamlessly into the glass panel. The bottom of the image should softly fade out or be clipped by the hero container.
*   **Action Bar:**
    *   **Play Button:** Bright Green (`#32D74B`), pill-shaped, black text/icon.
    *   **Follow Button:** Translucent pill with white border.
    *   **Secondary Links:** "Go to artist radio" and "Share" in plain white text.

### C. Content Section (Popular Songs)
*   **Header:** "Popular" (Medium Bold).
*   **List Item Structure:**
    *   **Column 1:** Index number (1, 2, 3).
    *   **Column 2:** Album Art (Small rounded square, `40px`).
    *   **Column 3:** Song Title (White, Bold).
    *   **Column 4:** Play Count (Muted White, right-aligned).
    *   **Column 5:** Duration (Muted White, right-aligned).
    *   **Column 6:** Heart Icon (Outline).
*   **Song Data:**
    1. Unstoppable | 958,283,458 | 3:37
    2. Dusk 'Til Dawn (feat Sia) - Radio Edit | 1,458,283,452 | 3:59
    3. Chandelier | 1,458,283,452 | 3:31

### D. Floating Player Bar (Bottom)
*   **Position:** Centered at the bottom, detached from the main panel.
*   **Shape:** Horizontal pill with high blur.
*   **Left Section:** Playback controls (Previous, Pause, Next) in white.
*   **Center Section:** 
    *   **Now Playing Card:** A smaller, slightly darker glass pill inside the bar.
    *   **Thumbnail:** Square art for "Muddy Feet".
    *   **Text:** "Muddy Feet (feat Sia)" (White) / "Miley Cyrus, Sia" (Muted).
*   **Right Section:** Shuffle icon, Volume icon, and AirPlay/Devices icon.

## 4. Iconography
*   **Style:** Thin-stroke line icons (approx. 1.5px to 2px stroke width).
*   **Library Recommendation:** **Lucide Icons** or **Phosphor Icons**.
*   **Specific Icons Needed:**
    *   Home, History (Clock), Explore (Rocket), Heart (Nav).
    *   Chevron Down (Artist dropdown).
    *   Lightning Bolt (Popular).
    *   Verified Checkmark.
    *   Play (Solid), Follow (User+), Share.
    *   Skip Back, Pause, Skip Forward.
    *   Shuffle, Speaker/Volume, Screen Mirroring (AirPlay).

## 5. Image Specifications
1.  **Artist Hero Image:** 
    *   **Description:** Sia profile, platinum blonde bob, facing left.
    *   **Format:** Transparent PNG. 
    *   **Rotation:** 0 degrees, but positioned so the gaze leads into the text.
2.  **Album Thumbnails:**
    *   **Unstoppable:** Dark/Black background with a central white figure.
    *   **Dusk 'Til Dawn:** Abstract light/sky blue and white clouds.
    *   **Chandelier:** Dark interior room with a spotlight.
    *   **Muddy Feet:** Blue-toned artistic shot (Miley Cyrus).

## 6. Interaction States (Hover/Active)
*   **Buttons:** Increase background opacity by 10% on hover.
*   **List Items:** A subtle white glow or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` background highlight on hover.
*   **Play Button:** Slight scale increase (`scale(1.05)`) on hover.
