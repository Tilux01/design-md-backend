# Design Specification: "Dream trip" Aero Bicycle Landing Page

## 1. Canvas & Global Styles
- **Theme:** Minimalist, dark-mode futuristic aero product showcase.
- **Background Color:** Dark slate navy `#2B3144`.
- **Canvas Sizing:** Full viewport width and height (`100vw`, `100vh`), overflow hidden.

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary Background:** `#2B3144`
- **Card/Component Dark Accents:** `#222736`
- **Text Primary (Headings, Active):** `#FFFFFF`
- **Text Secondary (Body text, Inactive):** `#8C93A8`
- **Teal / Cyan Accent (CTA, Seatpost glow, Active dots):** `#00C2D6`
- **Subtle White Borders/Dividers:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

---

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Display Font (Headings):** *Poppins*, *Outfit*, or *Gilroy* (Bold 700 / ExtraBold 800).
  - *Fallback:* `system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`
- **Body & Controls Font:** *Inter* or *DM Sans* (Regular 400, Medium 500).
  - *Fallback:* `Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif`
- **Sizes & Weights:**
  - **Hero Heading ("Dream trip"):** `56px`, Weight 700, Letter spacing `-0.5px`.
  - **Body Copy:** `13px`, Weight 400, Line height `1.6`, Color `#8C93A8`.
  - **Price ("$ 899.350"):** `24px`, Weight 600, Color `#FFFFFF`.
  - **Nav Links / Labels:** `11px`, Weight 600, Uppercase, Tracking `1.5px`.
  - **Specs / Pagination:** `11px - 14px`, Weight 500.

---

## 4. Layout Structure

### A. Navigation Bar (Top)
- **Container:** Full width, `padding: 32px 64px`, flexbox space-between.
- **Left:** Monoline bicycle wireframe icon in white (`width: 32px`).
- **Center:** Navigation items horizontally aligned with `gap: 40px`.
  - Links: `HOME`, `BICYCLE`, `EQUIP`, `DEALER`, `CLUB` (Color: `#8C93A8`, Active: `#FFFFFF`).
- **Right:** Utility icons (Search magnifying glass, Bell/Cart icon) in `#FFFFFF`, size `18px`, `gap: 24px`.

---

### B. Left Rail (Social Icons)
- **Position:** Fixed / Absolute left rail (`left: 40px`, vertically centered below header).
- **Items:** Facebook (`f`), Twitter (`t`), Google (`G`) stacked vertically, size `14px`, color `#8C93A8`, `gap: 20px`.

---

### C. Main Content Area (Left Column)
- **Placement:** Absolute/Grid aligned left (`left: 12%`, `top: 36%`, `max-width: 380px`).
- **Accent Indicator:** Thin vertical teal bar (`2px × 24px`, `#00C2D6`) positioned slightly above and to the left of the text block.
- **Headline:** `Dream trip` (Color `#FFFFFF`).
- **Description:** "High quality 3K fabric carbon fiber front fork, reduce body weight and enhance shock absorption effect, aerodynamic three knife modeling design."
- **Price Tag:** `$ 899.350` (Margin top: `20px`).
- **CTA Button:**
  - **Text:** `PAY FOR  >` (Uppercase, 12px, Bold, `#FFFFFF`).
  - **Background:** Solid teal `#00C2D6`.
  - **Dimensions & Style:** `padding: 12px 28px`, border-radius `4px`, flat (no shadow).

---

### D. Hero Product Asset (Center-Right)
- **Subject:** 3D side-profile render of a matte-black aero road bicycle facing left.
- **Attributes:**
  - **Format:** High-resolution transparent background (PNG/WebP).
  - **Features:** Solid matte-black front disc wheel with red hub cap; solid aero carbon frame; rear solid disc wheel; integrated aero handlebars; bright cyan/teal illuminated accent band beneath the seatpost collar.
  - **Ground Contact:** Subtle horizontal ground line and soft diffuse contact shadow underneath the tires.
- **Placement:** Positioned across the center to right-half of the screen (`width: ~55vw`, bottom aligned near `18%`).

---

### E. Vertical Pagination (Far Right)
- **Position:** Right-aligned rail (`right: 64px`, vertically centered).
- **Items:** Stacked vertically:
  - `01` (Muted gray)
  - `02 ○` (Small hollow circle indicator)
  - `03 ◉` (Active: `#00C2D6` ring/dot indicator with bold white text)
  - `04 ○` (Small hollow circle indicator)
