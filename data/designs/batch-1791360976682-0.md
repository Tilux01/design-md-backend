# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise SaaS with Editorial Softness & Editorial Dark Vignette.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio in navigation and header zones; tight, data-dense card and pill layouts with generous outer negative space framing.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered dark architectural surfaces featuring soft radial vignettes, multi-stop backdrop blurs (`backdrop-blur: 16px`), and high-contrast pill components elevated against raw structural backgrounds.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient radial blur vignettes, frosted glassmorphism overlays, subtle grain/noise textures, financial indicator chips with status pips.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, border width `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
