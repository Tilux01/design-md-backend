# Design Specification: Glassmorphic Smart Home OS

## 1. Global Theme & Visual Style
*   **Design Language:** Glassmorphism (Frosted Glass).
*   **Background:** The UI is a full-screen overlay. The "background" is the live camera feed of the room.
*   **Surface Material:** 
    *   **Fill:** White (`#FFFFFF`) with 15% to 25% opacity.
    *   **Backdrop Blur:** 30px to 50px (Gaussian blur).
    *   **Border:** 1.5px solid white (`#FFFFFF`) with 30% opacity (creates a "rim light" effect).
    *   **Corner Radius:** 32px for main panels; 20px for internal widgets.
*   **Shadows:** Soft, large-spread ambient occlusion shadows (Black, 10% opacity, 40px blur).

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `SF Pro Display` (or `Inter` as a web fallback).
*   **Secondary Font:** `SF Pro Text` (or `Roboto` for smaller UI elements).
*   **Fallbacks:** `System-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Color:** 
    *   Primary Text: Dark Charcoal (`#2D2D2D`) at 90% opacity.
    *   Secondary/Muted Text: Dark Gray (`#555555`) at 70% opacity.
    *   Input/Placeholder: Gray (`#888888`).

## 3. Layout Structure

### A. Top Header Bar
*   **Position:** Top center, floating.
*   **Dimensions:** ~90% width of the main panel.
*   **Elements (Left to Right):**
    1.  **Profile Avatar:** Circular image of a Black man in a suit. Background-less, centered in a dark circle.
    2.  **Greeting:** "Welcome, Daniel" (Semi-bold, 18pt).
    3.  **Voice Search Bar:** Pill-shaped, semi-transparent dark fill. Contains a glowing blue/purple "Siri-style" orb icon on the left. Placeholder text: "Can you open the curta....."
    4.  **Mode Toggle:** Pill-shaped container. "Light" (active, white background, sun icon) and "Dark" (inactive, moon icon).
    5.  **Notification Bell:** Circular button, semi-transparent, containing a white bell icon.

### B. Left Navigation Rail
*   **Position:** Vertically centered on the far left.
*   **Style:** Narrow vertical pill-shaped glass panel.
*   **Icons (Top to Bottom):**
    *   Home (Active, white circle background)
    *   Lightning Bolt (Energy)
    *   Layers/Stack
    *   Notification Bell
    *   Clock/Timer
    *   Camera/Video
    *   Settings (Gear)
    *   Chevron Down (Expand)

### C. Main Widget Grid (Central Panel)
The main panel is a large rounded rectangle containing two rows of widgets.

#### Row 1 (Top):
1.  **YouTube Widget:** 
    *   Search bar at top with red YouTube logo and microphone icon.
    *   Three bottom tabs: Home, Shorts, Subscriptions (Thin line icons).
2.  **Smart TV Widget:** 
    *   Title "Smart TV" with a toggle switch (ON).
    *   App Icons: Netflix (N), Prime Video (Blue), HBO (Black), and a "+" button for more.
3.  **Music Player (Yahweh):**
    *   Album Art: Square image of a female singer in a white dress, dark background.
    *   Track Info: "Yahweh" (Bold), "Onyinye Nnodim" (Subtext).
    *   Controls: Progress bar, Repeat, Back, Play/Pause, Next, Shuffle.

#### Row 2 (Middle):
1.  **Reminders:** List style. Items: "Call Gardener", "Buy Groceries". Large number "2" in the corner.
2.  **Battery/Status:** Four circular rings showing charge levels for: VR Headset, Phone, Watch, and Controllers. Green accent color for active charge.
3.  **App Shortcuts (Center):** 
    *   Icons: AirDrop (Blue), Messages (Green), Mail (Blue/Red badge '8'), Notes (Yellow).
    *   List below: SharePlay, Copy, Print, Duplicate (with corresponding thin-line icons).
4.  **Air Conditioner:** 
    *   Title "Air Conditioner" with green toggle (ON).
    *   Large Temp: "16°C" (Bold, 48pt).
    *   Controls: Up/Down chevrons, Mode: "Cold", Auto turn-on/off times, Wind speed bar graph.

#### Row 3 (Bottom):
1.  **Smart Light:** Large square widget with a green toggle switch at the bottom.
2.  **Vacuum Cleaner:** Large square widget with a white toggle switch (OFF) at the bottom.

### D. Bottom Room Navigation
*   **Position:** Bottom center, floating pill.
*   **Tabs:** "Living Room" (Active, white pill background), "Bedroom", "Kitchen", "Dining Room", "Play Room".
*   **Action:** A "+" button on the far right for adding new rooms.

## 4. Asset & Icon Descriptions

### Icons
*   **Style:** Minimalist line art (Linear icons). 
*   **Stroke Weight:** 1.5px to 2px.
*   **Color:** Dark Charcoal (`#2D2D2D`).
*   **Backgrounds:** All icons should be background-less (transparent PNG/SVG) unless they are inside a specific active-state circle.

### Specific Images
1.  **User Profile:** High-resolution portrait of a man, circular crop, no background (transparent).
2.  **Voice Orb:** A spherical gradient, glowing. Colors: Electric Blue (`#00D4FF`) blending into Deep Purple (`#8A2BE2`). 0-degree rotation.
3.  **Album Art:** 1:1 Square aspect ratio. High contrast image of a performer.
4.  **App Icons (Netflix, etc.):** Standard brand logos, rounded corners (4px radius), centered within their respective 40x40px containers.
5.  **Device Icons (Battery Widget):** 
    *   Headset: Side profile of a VR HMD.
    *   Phone: Vertical rectangle with home button.
    *   Watch: Square face with strap lugs.
    *   Controllers: Two small circles with sticks.

---

## 5. Interaction States
*   **Hover:** Increase border opacity to 60% and slightly increase backdrop-blur.
*   **Active/Toggle ON:** Accent color is Emerald Green (`#4CD964`).
*   **Inactive/Toggle OFF:** Light Gray (`#D1D1D6`).
*   **Text Selection:** Light Blue highlight (`#007AFF` at 20% opacity).
