# Design Specification: Atelier Arc — Spatial Architectural Portfolio

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Design Language

The design is a high-end, immersive spatial UI inspired by visionOS and modern editorial architectural portfolios. It marries rugged natural topography with ultra-refined glassmorphism, precise modern typography, and a 3D cylindrical gallery arc.

* **Aesthetic Theme:** Spatial Minimalist / Desert Modernism / Translucent Glassmorphism.
* **Viewport Target:** Fullscreen viewport (`100vw` × `100vh`), borderless, responsive canvas with fixed/pinned interactive UI layers.
* **Spatial Depth Model:** Single z-axis depth hierarchy where background photography recedes, typography floats in mid-depth, and an interactive 3D cylindrical card carousel wraps horizontally around the viewer in the lower foreground.

The carousel simulates a physical curved display panel wrapping cylindrically around the user.

* **Perspective Container:**
  * `perspective: 1400px;`
  * `perspective-origin: 50% 50%;`
  * `transform-style: preserve-3d;`
* **Card Dimensions:**
  * Width: `210px` to `240px` (Desktop)
  * Height: `290px` to `330px` (Aspect Ratio ~ `1 : 1.38`)
  * Corner Radius: `24px` smooth radius (`border-radius: 24px; overflow: hidden;`)
* **Arc Geometry Matrix (Left to Right across 7 cards):**
  1. **Card 1 (Far Left, partially off-canvas):**
     * `transform: rotateY(32deg) translateZ(-80px) translateX(-20px);`
     * Visual: Deep angle showing outer lateral face.
  2. **Card 2 (Mid-Left):**
     * `transform: rotateY(20deg) translateZ(-25px);`
  3. **Card 3 (Center-Left):**
     * `transform: rotateY(9deg) translateZ(5px);`
  4. **Card 4 (Dead Center):**
     * `transform: rotateY(0deg) translateZ(15px);`
     * Faces flat and closest to viewer.
  5. **Card 5 (Center-Right):**
     * `transform: rotateY(-9deg) translateZ(5px);`
  6. **Card 6 (Mid-Right):**
     * `transform: rotateY(-20deg) translateZ(-25px);`
  7. **Card 7 (Far Right, partially off-canvas):**
     * `transform: rotateY(-32deg) translateZ(-80px) translateX(20px);`
* **Card Frame Styling:**
  * Outer stroke: `1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`.

## 7. Motion, Depth & Interaction Guidelines

### 7.1 Interactive States
1. **Nav Links:**
   * Default: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`
   * Hover: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 1.0)`, slight scale `scale(1.04)`
   * Transition: `all 0.25s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`
2. **Action Buttons:**
   * "Portfolio" Hover: Brightness increase, subtle shadow expansion (`box-shadow: 0 12px 28px rgba(0,0,0,0.35)`).
   * "Approach" Hover: Background becomes `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.36)`.
3. **Carousel Card Interactivity:**
   * Hover on individual card: Card translates forward along its local normal vector (`translateZ(30px)`), luminous border brightens to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`, outer glow intensifies.
   * Drag/Swipe Scroll: Dragging along the X-axis rotates the entire cylindrical carousel group around the Y-axis, bringing adjacent cards into the center focus point with smooth inertial damping.
