# UI/UX Specification: Elastos Light Wallet Dashboard

---

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Architecture

*   **View Mode:** Full-screen immersive viewport (100vw × 100vh), occupying the entire browser canvas without external mockup frames, shadows, or rounded exterior device cards.
*   **Base Background:** Dark, multi-layered monochromatic gradient surface (`#111318` to `#181b22`) featuring subtle, sweeping fluid organic curves (`#1f242e` to `#161921`) that flow diagonally across the layout to create spatial depth.
*   **Typography Engine:** 
    *   **Primary Font Family:** Inter, Roboto, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif.
    *   **Fallback Font Chain:** `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
    *   **Font Characteristics:** Modern geometric sans-serif with exceptional legibility at small tracking values, clean numerals, and balanced tracking (letter spacing).

## 4. Imagery & Iconography Guidelines

*   **Logo/Geometric Elements:** Rendered purely via vector paths (SVG) utilizing crisp geometric strokes and minimal fills.
*   **QR Code:** Represented as a standard scannable 2D matrix barcode graphic placeholder.
*   **Hardware Ledger Icon:** A minimalist vector depiction of a hardware security token device with an LCD screen slot and button indicator.
*   **System Icons:** Universal stroke-based iconography (chevrons, copy clipboards, QR symbols, social logos) rendered at a uniform stroke-width (1.5px to 2px) in matching neutral gray (`#8a919e`) or active white (`#ffffff`).
