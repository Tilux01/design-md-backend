# NEURA Landing Page Design Specification

## 1. Global Styles

### 1.1 Color Palette
*   **App Background:** `#F4F4F6` (Very light gray)
*   **Background Shape (Blob):** `#E5E5E8` (Slightly darker, soft gray used for the large background shape linking the cards)
*   **Card Backgrounds (Right side):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white)
*   **Primary Text:** `#1A1A1A` (Near black for headings and strong emphasis)
*   **Secondary Text:** `#666666` (Medium gray for paragraphs, sub-labels, and navigation)
*   **Accents:**
    *   Button/Icon Fill: `#000000` (Pure black)
    *   Star Rating: `#FBBF24` (Golden yellow)
    *   Robot LED Glow: `#60A5FA` (Light glowing blue)

### 1.2 Typography
The design uses a clean, geometric, modern sans-serif typeface. 
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter`, `SF Pro Display` (Apple), or `Helvetica Now`.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif.`
*   **Weights Used:**
    *   Bold (700): Main headline, card titles, large numbers (X1).
    *   Medium (500): Navigation links, button text, small caps eyebrows.
    *   Regular (400): Body copy, descriptions, small specs.

### 1.3 Layout & Grid
*   **Layout Style:** Full screen, 100vh, hidden overflow (no scrolling intended for this specific hero view).
*   **Structure:** A top navigation bar, followed by a 3-column layout (Left Content, Center Hero Image, Right Content/Widgets).
*   **Z-Index Hierarchy:** 
    1. Base Background (`#F4F4F6`)
    2. Large irregular soft-gray background shape.
    3. Center Robot Image (overlaps the background shape).
    4. Text, UI Cards, and Navigation (Top-most layer).

## 3. Image Descriptions (For Assets)

### 3.1 Main Hero Image (The Robot)
*   **Type:** Raster graphic (PNG or WEBP).
*   **Background:** **MUST BE BACKGROUND-LESS (100% Transparent).**
*   **Description:** A hyper-realistic, high-fidelity 3D render of a futuristic humanoid robot shown from the mid-thigh up. 
    *   *Colors & Materials:* The primary chassis is glossy, pristine white plastic/metal paneling. The joints, neck, and under-chassis are made of matte black, heavily detailed mechanical parts and synthetic muscle structures.
    *   *Head/Face:* The head is sleek and aerodynamic. The "face" is a solid, smooth, opaque black visor spanning the front. Across the center of the black visor is a single, glowing, horizontal light-blue slit (resembling an LED eye line). On the side of the head (the "ear" area), there is a circular indentation with a glowing light-blue ring.
    *   *Body Details:* The right pectoral plate has the word "NEURA" printed in small, dark gray, modern sans-serif letters. The left shoulder/bicep plate has the designation "X1" printed in large dark gray text, with the words "PEOPLE IDEAS PROGRESS" stacked in very small text beneath it. On the right lower chest plate, there is a small, horizontal glowing light-blue slit.
    *   *Pose & Placement:* The robot is standing tall, projecting confidence. Its body is angled slightly to its left (viewer's right), but the head is turned slightly back toward the viewer. The arms hang naturally at the sides, revealing intricate black finger mechanisms on the hands. 
    *   *Lighting:* Studio-style lighting, soft shadows, highlighting the glossy reflections on the white armor and the deep shadows of the mechanical joints.
*   **Positioning in layout:** Centered horizontally. Anchored to the bottom of the viewport. No rotation.

### 3.2 Testimonial Avatars
*   **Type:** Raster graphics (JPG/PNG).
*   **Description:** Three separate, small, circular cropped headshots of smiling, diverse, professional-looking humans (e.g., one woman with dark hair, one man with short hair, one woman with blonde hair). 
*   **Positioning:** Placed in a horizontal row, with standard CSS negative margin (e.g., `margin-left: -8px`) applied to the 2nd and 3rd image to create a slight overlapping effect.

### 3.3 Icons (System Assets)
*All icons should be flat, monochromatic SVGs.*
*   **Search:** Standard magnifying glass outline.
*   **Globe:** Standard grid globe outline.
*   **Arrow Right:** Solid small arrow inside a circular button.
*   **Star:** 5-point star, filled with gold/yellow.
*   **Brain:** Outline of a human brain with a gear/circuit pattern inside.
*   **Height:** Outline of a human silhouette next to a vertical measurement line.
*   **Weight:** Outline of a standard gym weight/kettlebell.
*   **Battery:** Outline of a battery cylinder, partially filled.
