## 6. Animation & Interaction Specifications

### Page Load & Entry Animations (Framer Motion / CSS Keyframes)
1.  **Card Container Entry:**

3.  **Hero Image Float:**
    *   The central ice cream cone illustration exhibits a continuous subtle floating animation (up and down by `8px` over a `4s` infinite loop with an ease-in-out curve).
4.  **Interactive States:**
    *   **Buttons:** Scale up slightly (`scale(1.05)`) and elevate box-shadow on hover.
