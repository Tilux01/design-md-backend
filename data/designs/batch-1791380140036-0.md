# Design Specification: "Свитер повседневный" (Casual Sweater) Product Page

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport
- **Canvas / Viewport:** Full-screen responsive layout (designed for mobile/portrait aspect ratio, e.g., 9:16).
- **Background Color:** Solid greige / warm neutral gray (`#C8C3BE` or similar soft muted taupe).
- **Split Screen Effect:** The left half of the screen features a frosted glass / blur overlay (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with a semi-transparent light layer, creating a distinct editorial layout separation.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Font Family
- **Main Font:** Clean, modern Sans-Serif (Geometric/Humanist style, resembling *Montserrat*, *Inter*, or *Helvetica Neue*).
- **Fallback Stack:** `sans-serif`, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto.

### Text Hierarchy & Styles

#### Top Navigation Tags
- **Content:** `Новая коллекция` | `Демисезон` | `Тренд`
- **Font Size:** Extra small (~11px–12px).
- **Font Weight:** Regular to Medium (400–500).
- **Color:** White / Off-White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Letter Spacing:** Wide tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.05em`).

#### Main Title Section
- **Heading ("Свитер"):**
  - **Font Size:** Large display size (~48px–56px).
  - **Font Weight:** Bold / Heavy (~700).
  - **Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - **Line Height:** Tight (`1.0`).
- **Subheading ("повседневный"):**
  - **Font Size:** Medium-large (~24px–28px).
  - **Font Weight:** Light / Regular (~300–400).
  - **Color:** Light grey/white with slight transparency (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).

#### Feature Block ("Принт")
- **Header ("Принт"):**
  - **Font Size:** Medium (~16px).
  - **Font Weight:** Semi-bold (~600).
  - **Color:** White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - **Margin Bottom:** Small gap above body text.
- **Body Text:**
  - *"Шелкография гарантирует долговечность рисунка -он не потрескается и не выцветет даже после множество стирок"*
  - **Font Size:** Small (~12px–13px).
  - **Font Weight:** Regular (~400).
  - **Line Height:** Generous (`1.4` to `1.5`).
  - **Color:** Off-white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`).
- **Tag ("\[ new ]"):**
  - **Font Size:** Small (~11px).
  - **Font Weight:** Regular (~400).
  - **Color:** Light muted white.

#### Section Header ("Широкий размерный ряд")
- **Font Size:** Medium (~15px–16px).
- **Font Weight:** Medium (~500).
- **Color:** White (`#FFFFFF`).

#### Bottom Description / Slogan
- *"Комфорт, стиль и качество - всё, что нужно для уверенного городского образа!"*
- **Font Size:** Small (~11px–12px).
- **Font Weight:** Light / Regular (~400).
- **Color:** Light muted white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).

#### Material Composition Callouts (Right Side)
- **Numbers ("92%", "8%"):**
  - **Font Size:** Massive display size (~54px–64px).
  - **Font Weight:** Light / Thin (~300).
  - **Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Labels ("хлопок", "шерсть"):**
  - **Font Size:** Small (~12px–13px).
  - **Font Weight:** Regular (~400).
  - **Text Transform:** Lowercase.
  - **Color:** White / light gray.

---

## 3. UI Elements & Components

### Icons
- **Favorite / Wishlist Icon:**
  - **Shape:** Minimalist outline heart icon.
  - **Position:** Top right corner.
  - **Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - **Size:** ~20px × 20px.

### Thumbnail Gallery Component
- **Layout:** Horizontal flex row containing 3 image thumbnails.
- **Image Styling:** 
  - Rounded corners (`border-radius: 6px` or `8px`).
  - Object-fit cover.
  - Subtle drop shadow or border to separate from the background.
- **Content:** Different angles/poses of the model wearing the same blue sweater and cream trousers.

---

## 4. Imagery & Media
- **Background Hero Image:** 
  - High-resolution editorial portrait of a blonde female model wearing a pastel blue quarter-zip fleece/sweater and cream-colored wide-leg trousers with drawstrings.
  - Positioned prominently on the right half, bleeding across the center line.
- **Lighting / Tone:** Soft, professional studio lighting with warm, muted color grading.

---

## 5. Animations & Interactions (Recommended for Development)
- **Entrance Animation:**
  - **Fade-In & Slide-Up:** Text elements on the left panel stagger in sequentially with a smooth cubic-bezier easing (`0.25, 1, 0.5, 1`) over 0.6s.
  - **Blur Transition:** The frosted glass overlay fades in alongside the text.
- **Hover / Tap States:**
  - **Thumbnails:** Scale up slightly (`transform: scale(1.05)`) with a smooth 0.2s transition when tapped/hovered, switching the main hero image to the selected thumbnail view.
  - **Heart Icon:** Scale pulse animation on tap with a fill color transition to solid white/red depending on state.
