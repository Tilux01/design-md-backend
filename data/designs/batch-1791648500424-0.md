# Design Specification Document (`design.md`)

## 1. Project Overview & Aesthetic
* **Theme:** High-contrast, cinematic, dark-mode landing page / hero section.
* **Style:** Bold editorial typography meets dramatic monochrome portraiture.
* **Aspect Ratio / Framing:** Full-screen responsive viewport (`100vw` × `100vh`), edge-to-edge layout with zero container borders, shadows, or outer frames.
* **Overall Color Tone:** Deep black (`#000000`) and pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle gray muted UI accents.

## 3. Typography System

### Primary Display Font (Hero Title "DRAMA OWNS")
* **Style:** Ultra-condensed, heavy, tall display sans-serif with tight kerning.
* **Font Family:** `Bebas Neue`, `Oswald`, `Impact`, `Anton`
* **Fallback Stack:** `"Bebas Neue", "Oswald", "Impact", "Arial Narrow", sans-serif-condensed, sans-serif`
* **Text Properties:**
  * Case: `UPPERCASE`
  * Font Weight: `900` / `Heavy` / `Bold`
  * Letter Spacing: `-0.02em` (tight)
  * Line Height: `0.9` or `1.0`
  * Size: Dynamic viewport width scaled (~`13vw` to `15vw`)

### Secondary Font (Header Navigation, Subtitles, Footer)
* **Style:** Clean, geometric/grotesque sans-serif.
* **Font Family:** `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica Neue`
* **Fallback Stack:** `"Inter", "Roboto", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`
* **Text Properties:**
  * **Logo ("DRAMA OWNS"):** Upper case, `font-weight: 800`, `font-size: 18px`, `letter-spacing: 0.05em`.
  * **Nav Links ("Home", "About", "Contact"):** Sentence case, `font-weight: 500`, `font-size: 15px`, `letter-spacing: 0.02em`.
  * **Subtitles ("Join For More...", "Presented By..."):** Title case, `font-weight: 700`, `font-size: 14px`, `letter-spacing: 0.01em`.
  * **Footer handle ("t.me/..."):** `font-weight: 600`, `font-size: 13px`.

## 4. Layering & Z-Index Hierarchy

The 3D overlapping effect is the visual core of this design. It requires 4 distinct stacking contexts:

1. **`z-index: 1` — Background Layer:** Pure black `#000000` canvas.
2. **`z-index: 2` — Giant Display Text:** The white text `DRAM` on the left and `OWNS` on the right (or `DRAMA` and `OWNS` split with central spacing).
3. **`z-index: 3` — Central Subject Cutout:** Backgroundless portrait image of the subject, positioned to overlap the middle letters (`A`/`O`) while letting the remaining outer text show behind his shoulders.
4. **`z-index: 4` — Foreground UI Layer:** Top Header navigation bar, side subtitles, and bottom footer links/icons.

## 5. Image Specification & Placement

### Subject Image Asset Details
* **Type:** Cutout portrait (PNG format with a 100% transparent background; no background tint, no borders).
* **Color Mode:** High-contrast Black & White (Monochrome).
* **Subject Matter:** Upper torso/chest-up portrait of South Korean actor Song Joong-ki (in character as *Vincenzo Cassano*).
* **Attire:** Dark tailored formal suit jacket, crisp white collared dress shirt, dark matte tie, and a visible folded white pocket square on his left chest pocket (viewer's right).
* **Pose & Expression:**
  * Facing straight forward toward the camera.
  * Head slightly tilted back/upward, looking down toward the lens with a stern, confident expression.
  * Rotation: `0deg` (perfectly upright alignment).
* **Lighting Style:** Dramatic studio key light from the upper-right side of the image (viewer's left), casting bright white highlights along his right jawline, cheek, neck, and shoulder, while keeping the left side of his suit and face in deep shadow.

### Image Positioning & Alignment
* **Horizontal Alignment:** Exactly centered (`left: 50%`, `transform: translateX(-50%)`).
* **Vertical Alignment:** Bottom edge anchored to the bottom edge of the viewport (`bottom: 0`).
* **Scale:** Extends from the bottom edge up to roughly `82%` of the viewport height, ending just beneath the top navigation bar.
* **Text Occlusion:** The head and neck sit directly over the central space between `DRAM` and `OWNS`, overlapping the inner edges of the letters.
