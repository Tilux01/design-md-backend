# Comprehensive UI/UX Design Specification & Replication Guide

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport Configuration
- **Target Display:** Fullscreen responsive web interface (designed within a 16:9 aspect ratio container, scaling to `100vw` × `100vh`).
- **Background Color (Primary):** Pale sage green (`#D5DEC8` or `rgb(213, 222, 200)`).
- **Secondary Accent Color (Dark/Black):** Deep charcoal black (`#141614` or `rgb(20, 22, 20)`) used for all primary typography, UI lines, and structural blocks.
- **Grid Structure:** Modular HUD (Heads-Up Display) wireframe grid. Faint plus (`+`) crosshair coordinate markers distributed evenly across the canvas on an invisible 12-column grid system. Diagonal hazard stripes appear subtly in the top-left structural column behind the large vertical title.

---

## 2. Typography & Font Specifications

### Primary Font Families
- **Display / Structural Font:** Bold, geometric, uppercase sans-serif (resembling *Rajdhani*, *Share Tech Mono*, or *Syncopate*). Used for heavy UI titles, numerical lists, and section markers.
- **Body / Metadata Font:** Clean, modern monospace sans-serif (resembling *JetBrains Mono*, *Share Tech Mono*, or *Courier New* as fallback). Used for descriptions, subtitles, and interactive links.

### Font Styles & Fallbacks
1. **Title / Vertical Brand Text (`ALIEN`)**:
   - **Font:** `Syncopate, 'Rajdhani', sans-serif`
   - **Weight:** Bold (700)
   - **Transform:** Uppercase, vertically rotated (90 degrees or stacked letterform blocks).
2. **HUD Body Text (`Cyber Alien is a survival game...`)**:
   - **Font:** `'Share Tech Mono', monospace, sans-serif`
   - **Size:** 11px–12px
   - **Line Height:** 1.4
   - **Color:** `#141614`

---

## 3. Header Section (Top Navigation)

### Left Area
- **Logo:** Minimalist text block reading `LOGO` in a wide tracking, bold sans-serif font. Positioned at `top: 40px`, `left: 120px`.

### Center Area
- **Navigation Links:** Horizontal alignment (`NEWS +`, `TRAILER +`, `ACTORS +`).
  - **Styling:** Monospace, uppercase, 12px, tracking-wide. The `+` symbol acts as an interactive node indicator.

### Right Area
- **Language Switcher:** `RUS | ENG` (with `ENG` highlighted in bold).
- **Hamburger Menu Icon:** Two thick horizontal bars positioned on the far right (`top: 40px`, `right: 120px`).

---

## 4. Left Sidebar & Structural UI Elements

- **Vertical Numeric Pagination (`01` to `06`):**
  - **Position:** Fixed along the left margin (`left: 120px`, centered vertically).
  - **Styling:** Small monospace numbers stacked vertically. Item `03` is highlighted with an enclosing square bounding box to indicate the current active state.
- **Branding & Identifiers:**
  - **Large Vertical Title:** `ALIEN` rendered vertically on the mid-left canvas. Color is semi-transparent dark charcoal overlaying the pale green background with a subtle stencil/cutout aesthetic, accompanied by the small tag `13` directly above it.
  - **Sub-heading:** `2077` rendered in a heavy, wide geometric font placed just beneath the lower-left section of the `ALIEN` title.
- **Interactive Widgets (Bottom Left):**
  - **QR Code Block:** A crisp black-and-white scannable QR code enclosed in a thin square border, accompanied by coordinate crosshairs (`+`).
  - **Action Button:** A minimal bordered button reading `PRE ORDER NOW` with a small `+` marker.
  - **Social Links:** `TWITTER` and `INSTAGRAMM` aligned horizontally at the bottom left margin.

---

## 5. Center Hero Area & Imagery

### Central 3D Asset (Hero Render)
- **Image Description:** A high-end 3D render of a biomechanical cybernetic alien creature. The entity features an intricate, skeletal-metallic exoskeleton with exposed wiring, segmented armor plating, a polished chrome skull/helmet structure, and a glowing neon-green optical sensor/eye. 
- **Pose & Placement:** The creature is crouched defensively atop a jagged, weathered rock formation in the center-right of the viewport.
- **Background Layer:** The image asset is **backgroundless** (isolated render integrated directly into the sage green canvas). No rotation applied to the subject, but it overlaps structural grid lines to create spatial depth.

### Right Text Block
- **Description Box:** A rectangular panel positioned to the right of the central creature.
- **Content:** 
  > `"CYBER ALIEN" IS A SURVIVAL GAME SET IN A WORLD WHERE ADVANCED TECHNOLOGY MERGES WITH AN EXTRATERRESTRIAL THREAT. YOU WILL FACE ADAPTIVE LIFEFORMS FROM THE DEPTHS OF SPACE, EVOLVED THROUGH CYBERNETIC IMPLANTS. EXPLORE DERELICT STATIONS, WIELD HIGH-TECH WEAPONRY, AND UNCOVER HIDDEN TECHNOLOGIES TO CONFRONT CREATURES CAPABLE OF HACKING SYSTEMS AND CONTROLLING MACHINES. YOUR MISSION IS NOT JUST TO SURVIVE, BUT TO UNRAVEL THE MYSTERY OF THE ALIEN-TECHNOLOGY SYMBIOSIS, WHERE EVERY ENCOUNTER COULD BE YOUR LAST.`
- **Styling:** Fully justified, small monospace font, bounded by subtle HUD crosshair indicators at its corners.

## 6. Footer & Bottom UI Elements

- **Center Indicator:** 
  - Text: `SCROLL` accompanied by a downward vertical tracking line indicator positioned directly below the central rock mount (`bottom: 60px`).
- **Bottom-Right Media / Video Thumbnail Widget:**
  - **Thumbnail Description:** A cinematic still showing a close-up shot of the cybernetic alien head inside a dark corridor environment.
  - **Overlay:** A prominent white play icon (`▶`) centered inside a semi-transparent dark button overlay.
  - **Placement:** Anchored to the bottom-right corner of the canvas, neatly aligned with the grid boundaries.
