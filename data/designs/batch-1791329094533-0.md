# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise SaaS & Data Analytics Dashboard with high visual hierarchy, clean borders, minimalist cards, and modern financial indicators.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High information density balanced by generous inner card padding (16px to 24px) and subtle soft grey/white container surfaces (`#FFFFFF` on `#F8FAFC`).
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat surface architecture relying on ultra-fine 1px borders (`#E2E8F0`) and subtle interior containers rather than heavy drop shadows, paired with a distinct rounded app canvas (`rounded-2xl`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-precision SVG charts, donut breakdowns, thin 1px crisp borders, subtle brand accent highlights (`#F95738`), high-contrast tabular figures.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `2px 8px`, font-size: `12px`, font-weight: `500`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `#FFF1EE`, text color: `#F95738`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Up Arrow (↑) -> Use `&#8593;` or SVG
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
