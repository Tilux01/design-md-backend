# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise Life Sciences / Editorial Minimalist.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with a clear division of information. Minimalist framing with a central abstract 3D visual hero element.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, pure white canvas surfaces with hairline borders (`#e2e8f0`) framing interactive buttons and content blocks.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Minimalist structural framing, hairline borders, clean typography, medical/scientific aesthetic.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#f1f5f9`, border width `1px` solid `#e2e8f0`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Arrow / Caret (⌄) -> Use `&#8964;` or inline SVG chevron.
