# Design Specification: Dark Neumorphic Music Player

## 1. Core Theme & Visual Style
*   **Style:** Dark Neumorphism / Skeuomorphism 2.0.
*   **Atmosphere:** Premium, tactile, and minimalist.
*   **Background:** The application should be full-screen with no external borders or device frames.
*   **Surface Logic:** Elements appear to be extruded from or recessed into the background material using dual shadows (light and dark).

## 2. Color Palette
*   **Main Background:** `#1a1b1e` (Deep Charcoal/Matte Black)
*   **Accent Color (Active):** `#ff4d26` (Vibrant Orange-Red)
*   **Primary Text:** `#e0e0e0` (Off-white/Light Grey)
*   **Secondary Text:** `#757575` (Medium Grey)
*   **Shadow - Dark (Bottom-Right):** `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`
*   **Shadow - Light (Top-Left):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`

## 3. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` (or `SF Pro Display`).
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `Oxygen`, `Ubuntu`, `Cantarell`, `Fira Sans`, `Droid Sans`, `Helvetica Neue`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Title (Now Playing):** 14px, Medium, Letter spacing: 0.5px.
    *   **Song Title (Main):** 24px, Bold.
    *   **Artist Name:** 14px, Regular, Opacity: 0.6.
    *   **List Item Title:** 16px, Medium.
    *   **List Item Subtitle:** 12px, Regular.

## 4. Layout Structure

### Screen A: Now Playing (Main View)
1.  **Header Bar:** 
    *   Left: Recessed circular button with a "Back" chevron.
    *   Center: "NOW PLAYING" text in uppercase.
    *   Right: Recessed circular button with a "List/Menu" icon.
2.  **Album Art Container:**
    *   A large, perfectly circular element centered in the upper half.
    *   **Visual Effect:** The circle is recessed (inner shadow) into the background.
    *   **Image:** A circular, backgroundless (masked) image. 
        *   *Description:* A dark, abstract, 3D render featuring jagged, crystalline, or volcanic rock textures with glowing red/orange embers or lava-like veins flowing through the cracks.
        *   *Placement:* Centered perfectly within the recessed circle. No rotation.
3.  **Song Info:**
    *   "Low Life" (Title) centered below the art.
    *   "Future ft. The Weeknd" (Artist) centered below the title.
4.  **Playback Progress:**
    *   A horizontal track spanning 80% of the width.
    *   **Track:** Recessed thin line.
    *   **Progress:** A glowing orange line (`#ff4d26`) indicating elapsed time.
    *   **Thumb:** A small, raised orange circle at the current position.
    *   **Timestamps:** Small text at both ends of the track (e.g., 1:05 / 4:20).
5.  **Controls:**
    *   **Previous/Next:** Circular buttons, slightly raised from the surface.
    *   **Play/Pause (Center):** A larger, raised circular button. The button face is the accent color (`#ff4d26`). The "Pause" icon is white.

### Screen B: Playlist View
1.  **Header:** Similar to Screen A, but text reads "NEXT UP".
2.  **Mini-Visual:** A smaller version of the recessed circular album art at the top center.
3.  **List Items:**
    *   Each row contains: Title (Top), Artist (Bottom), and an Action icon (Right).
    *   **Active State:** The current playing song ("Low Life") has a background highlight that is slightly lighter than the main background, with a small orange play button icon on the far right.
    *   **Inactive State:** Flat text with a small recessed dot or arrow on the right.

## 5. Component Details (Neumorphic Math)

### Raised Elements (Buttons)
*   **Outer Shadow 1:** `10px 10px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)` (Bottom-Right)
*   **Outer Shadow 2:** `-10px -10px 20px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` (Top-Left)
*   **Border-Radius:** 50% for circles, 20px for cards.

### Recessed Elements (Track/Art Container)
*   **Inner Shadow 1:** `inset 6px 6px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)` (Top-Left)
*   **Inner Shadow 2:** `inset -6px -6px 12px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` (Bottom-Right)

## 6. Image Asset Specifications
*   **Album Art:** 
    *   **Format:** PNG with transparency (circular mask).
    *   **Content:** Abstract dark matter/rock with glowing red internal light.
    *   **Placement:** Centered in the top-middle of the screen.
*   **Icons:**
    *   **Style:** Thin-line vector icons (Linear).
    *   **Color:** `#e0e0e0` for standard, white for the active play button.
    *   **Placement:** Centered perfectly within their respective circular button housings.

## 7. Interaction States
*   **Button Press:** When a button is clicked, the shadows should invert (change from `box-shadow` to `inset box-shadow`) to simulate the button being physically pressed into the surface.
*   **Accent Glow:** The orange play button and progress bar should have a subtle outer glow (`drop-shadow`) of the same color to simulate light emission.
