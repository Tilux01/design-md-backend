# Puffer Jacket Landing Page Design Specification

## 1. Overall Layout & Canvas
- **Canvas:** Full-screen layout with an immersive, gradient-driven visual hierarchy.
- **Background Gradient:** A rich, dynamic orange gradient transition starting from a bright saturated top-left/center and deepening into warm, burnt sienna/dark brown tones along the bottom and right edges (`#ff6600` to `#803300` / `#2a0c00` color stops).
- **Border Radius & Shadow:** The UI sits inside a rounded container with subtle drop shadows (`border-radius: 20px` approx., with heavy drop-shadow framing onto the background surface).

---

## 2. Navigation Bar (Header)
- **Positioning:** Fixed at the top-center.
- **Logo (Left):**
  - **Icon:** Small square mark (`JM`).
  - **Text:** "JACKET MASTERS" in uppercase, clean white, sans-serif font with tracked-out spacing.
- **Center Nav Pill:**
  - Floating pill-shaped semi-transparent dark background (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)` with backdrop blur).
  - **Links:** 
    1. `PUFFER JACKET` (highlighted/active state with a pill fill matching the container shape)
    2. `ALL PRODUCTS`
    3. `ABOUT US`
    4. `CONTACT`
- **Right Action Icons:**
  - Shopping Cart icon
  - Heart / Wishlist icon

---

## 3. Hero Section (Left Column)
- **Carousel Controls:**
  - Left & Right chevron navigation arrows inside subtle circular buttons.
- **Headings:**
  - **Main Title:** "Stand out Without trying"
    - "Stand out" (White, bold, large scale)
    - "Without trying" (Soft warm white/orange tint)
- **Paragraph Copy:**
  - *"It’s not just about staying warm. It’s about stepping outside and instantly feeling confident, comfortable, and completely yourself. Designed to elevate even the simplest outfit, this jacket wraps you in lightweight warmth."*
  - Font: Clean sans-serif, muted white/off-white for high readability against the orange gradient.
- **Call-to-Action (CTA):**
  - **Button:** "Get the look" with a right chevron arrow (`>`).
  - Pill-shaped white background with dark text and crisp alignment.

---

## 4. Product Display (Center Column)
- **Hero Product Image:**
  - High-resolution 3D-rendered glossy orange puffer jacket.
  - Positioned centrally with a soft radial shadow underneath to anchor it to the surface.
- **Subtext below product:**
  - *"Confidence, wrapped in warmth"* (Italicized subtitle, centered).

---

## 5. Pricing & Options (Right Column)
- **Pricing Block:**
  - **Current Price:** `$149` (Large, bold white text)
  - **Original Price:** `$199` (Strikethrough, muted tone)
- **Size Selector:**
  - **Label:** "Choose your size:"
  - **Buttons:** Circular size selection pills (`36`, `38`, `40`).
    - Active size (`36`) styled with a white fill and dark text.
    - Inactive sizes styled with dark transparent borders/backgrounds.
- **Thumbnail Preview (Bottom Right):**
  - Small preview card showing an alternate colorway (dark/black puffer jacket).

---

## 6. Footer Elements (Bottom Left)
- **Social Media Icons:**
  - Instagram (`📷`), Facebook (`f`), Globe/Website (`🌐`), Behance (`Be`).
  - Styled cleanly as subtle icons aligned horizontally at the bottom-left corner.
