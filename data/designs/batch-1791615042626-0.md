# Design System & Interface Specification: Spatial Furniture Studio ("Sofa cake")

---

## 1. Executive Overview

This document specifies the exact visual design, layout architecture, typography, color palette, 3D staging, and component hierarchy for the full-screen spatial furniture studio and inventory interface.

The design features a high-depth visual interface split between an organic fluid showcase hero on the left and a minimalist catalog/storehouse inventory on the right, punctuated by floating glassmorphic and elevation-pill control decks.

### 5.2 3D Spatial Staging & Asset Specifications

All assets are backgroundless (transparent alpha PNG or real-time 3D models with transparent background) distributed across depth layers ($Z$-index).

#### Asset 1: Central Velvet Couch ("Sofa cake") — Focus Layer ($Z$-index: 10)
*   **Visual Description:** Minimalist modern 2-seater barrel/capsule sofa upholstered in plush royal purple/violet micro-velvet material. Features a continuous curved backrest that loops seamlessly into rounded cylindrical armrests. Single thick, flat-top rounded bench seat cushion. Supported by 4 minimalist slender dark charcoal/matte black cylindrical peg legs.
*   **Lighting & Shader:** Diffuse soft studio lighting from the top-left, casting subtle ambient occlusion between cushion folds and a deep, soft contact shadow directly underneath.
*   **Placement & Perspective:**
    *   Positioned directly bridging the seam between the purple blob and the right white pane (`left: 28%` to `48%`, `top: 32%`).
    *   Perspective: Straight-on frontal view with a slight $+5^\circ$ downward tilt.
    *   Focus: **Tack sharp** (0px blur).

#### Asset 2: Floating Tiered Round Stool ("Worktop") — Foreground Layer ($Z$-index: 20)
*   **Visual Description:** Multi-tiered stacked disc stool / mini side table.
    *   Top Tier: Terracotta-orange rounded dome/disc.
    *   Middle Tier: Creamy ivory/soft-yellow disc.
    *   Base Tier: Deep navy-indigo cylindrical platform.
    *   Legs: Three tapered wooden tripod legs in warm coral/natural tone.
*   **Placement & Camera Effect:**
    *   Positioned foreground-left (`left: 22%`, `top: 42%`), partially masking the left corner of the sofa.
    *   Effect: **Heavy Depth-of-Field Foreground Blur** (Gaussian blur: `8px` to `12px`), simulating camera proximity.

#### Asset 3: Floating Pillow ("Couch capsule") — Foreground Top Layer ($Z$-index: 20)
*   **Visual Description:** Plump, square-format decorative throw cushion in vibrant tangerine/coral orange (`#FF6F48`).
*   **Placement & Camera Effect:**
    *   Floating high in the upper-mid canvas (`left: 32%`, `top: 4%`), overlapping the top-right apex of the purple background blob.
    *   Rotation: Tilted approximately `22deg` clockwise.
    *   Effect: **High Motion / Depth Blur** (Gaussian blur: `14px`), conveying suspension and zero-gravity float.

## 6. Micro-Interactions & Animation Standards

*   **Floating 3D Dynamics:**
    *   Foreground assets (blurred orange cushion and tiered stool) utilize subtle idle levitation via CSS keyframe translation: `translateY(-4px)` to `translateY(+4px)`, duration `4s`, timing function `ease-in-out` infinite loop.
*   **Shelf Row Hover State:**
    *   Transition: `all 0.25s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
    *   Background shifts from transparent/light tint to `#FFFFFF`.
    *   Box shadow elevates to `0 8px 20px rgba(117, 66, 245, 0.08)`.
    *   Plus button (`+`) rotates `90deg` on hover with color shifting to `#7542F5`.
*   **Action Pill Submit Button:**
    *   Hover: Scale `1.05`, background color shifts to `#1B1728`.
    *   Active/Press: Scale `0.96`.
