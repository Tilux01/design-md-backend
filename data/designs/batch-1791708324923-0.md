# Presentation Design - Comprehensive UI/UX Specification

## Global Styles & Page Setup

*   **View/Canvas Setup:** Full-screen responsive presentation layout displayed against a pitch black background (`#000000`).
*   **Typography Hierarchy:**
    *   **Primary Font:** Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.
    *   **Fallback Font:** Arial, sans-serif.
    *   **Font Weights Used:** Regular (400), Medium (500), Semi-Bold (600), Bold (700).
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   `Background Color (Canvas):` `#000000` (Pure Black)
    *   `Card Dark Background:` `#161616` to `#1c1c1c` (Dark charcoal/grey gradients)
    *   `Card Light Background:` `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
    *   `Brand Primary/Accent:` `#FF6B00` or `#FF5722` (Vibrant Orange)
    *   `Primary Text (Dark Mode):` `#FFFFFF` (White)
    *   `Secondary Text (Dark Mode):` `#999999` to `#A0A0A0` (Muted Grey)
    *   `Primary Text (Light Mode):` `#111111` (Dark Charcoal)
