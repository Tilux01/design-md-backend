# URBAN FIT - E-Commerce Landing Page Design Specification

## 1. Global Styles & Typography

### Fonts
* **Primary Brand & Headings:** `Montserrat` (Fallback: `sans-serif`)
* **Body Text & Navigation:** `Inter` (Fallback: `sans-serif`)
* **Styling Notes:** 
  * Header logo (`URBAN FIT`) uses tracked-out, bold uppercase sans-serif.
  * Hero main headings use ultra-bold, heavy-weight typography (`Montserrat Black` or `900` weight) with tight line-height.

### Color Palette
* **Primary Dark (Backgrounds & Text):** `#111111` (Near Black)
* **Primary Light (Backgrounds):** `#F4F1EC` (Warm Cream / Off-White)
* **Accent/Highlight (Discount badges):** `#D4AF37` / `#C8A235` (Muted Gold)
* **Card Backgrounds (Products):** `#F0ECE5` (Light Stone)
* **Subtle Gray (Borders & Icons):** `#D1CDC7`
* **Pure White:** `#FFFFFF`

---

## 2. Header & Navigation

* **Layout:** Fixed/Sticky top banner, full-width with generous horizontal padding.
* **Left Section:** 
  * Logo text: `URBAN FIT` (Bold, uppercase, tracked/letter-spaced).
* **Center Section (Nav Links):** 
  * `NEW IN` | `MEN` | `WOMEN` | `ACCESSORIES` | `SALE`
  * Typography: Medium weight, uppercase, small font size (approx. 13-14px).
* **Right Section (Icons):** 
  * Search icon (magnifying glass)
  * Account icon (user outline)
  * Cart icon (shopping bag with item badge `0`)

---

## 3. Hero Section

* **Layout:** Split layout (Left: Text & CTA, Right: Imagery).
* **Background:** Warm cream `#F4F1EC`.
* **Left Content:**
  * Small category tag: `NEW COLLECTION` (Uppercase, small, tracked, thin horizontal accent line above/beside).
  * Main Headline (Two lines, mixed weights/colors):
    * Line 1: `SUMMER` (Black, ultra-bold, massive scale)
    * Line 2: `ESSENTIALS` (Muted beige/bronze, ultra-bold, massive scale)
  * Subtext / Callout: `—— DROP NOW LIVE ——`
  * Primary Button: `SHOP NOW →` (Dark rectangular button with white text and arrow icon).
* **Right Content:**
  * High-fashion lifestyle photography featuring two models in streetwear hoodies (one black bucket hat + black hoodie, one beige hoodie).
  * **Overlay Element (Top Right):** Circular badge with dark background (`#111111`), gold/white text:
    * `UP TO`
    * `50%`
    * `OFF`
* **Sub-Hero Value Proposition Bar:**
  * Light gray/stone container below hero with 4 horizontal columns:
    1. 🔥 **TRENDING NOW** — Hot Right Now
    2. ⭐ **BEST SELLERS** — Top Picks
    3. 📦 **NEW ARRIVALS** — New Styles Added
    4. 🚚 **FAST DELIVERY** — Across The World

## 5. Promotional & Banner Section

* **Layout:** 2-column split banner.
* **Left Banner (Limited Time Offer):**
  * Background: Dark charcoal/black (`#161616`).
  * Content: Tag icon + `LIMITED TIME OFFER` + `UP TO 50% OFF` in bold typography.
* **Right Banner (Free Shipping):**
  * Background: Warm beige (`#EAE3D9`).
  * Content: Globe icon + `FREE SHIPPING WORLDWIDE` + `ON ORDERS OVER $75`.

---

## 6. "Shop The Look" Carousel / Gallery Section

* **Section Header:** `SHOP THE LOOK` with horizontal divider line and `VIEW MORE →` link on the far right.
* **Layout:** Horizontal multi-column image gallery showing full-outfit lifestyle shots mirroring the models from the hero section.

---

## 7. Footer / Trust Badges Bar

* **Layout:** 4-column feature list at the very bottom.
* **Items:**
  1. 🛡️ **SECURE PAYMENT** — 100% Protected
  2. 🎧 **24/7 SUPPORT** — We're Here To Help
  3. 🔄 **EASY RETURNS** — 30 Days Return
  4. 🏆 **PREMIUM QUALITY** — Best Quality Products

---

## 8. Animations & Interactive States (UI/UX)

* **Hover Effects:**
  * Navigation links: Subtle opacity change or underline reveal.
  * Primary Buttons (`SHOP NOW`): Slight scale up or background color shift to dark gray.
  * Product Cards: Image zoom on hover (scale: 1.05) with smooth CSS transition (`transition: transform 0.3s ease`).
  * Icons (Cart, Search, User): Scale up slightly on hover.
* **Page Load / Scroll Animations:**
  * Fade-in-up animation for hero text and images (`opacity: 0; transform: translateY(20px);` transitioning to `1` and `0`).
  * Lazy loading for product and gallery grid imagery.
