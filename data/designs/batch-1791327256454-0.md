# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Futuristic Minimalist Luxury / Automotive Concept UI.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace, centered focus, asymmetrical framing designed to highlight the central vehicle render.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean matte white architectural surfaces with soft ambient occlusion shadows and subtle reflective lighting gradients.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-end automotive studio lighting, hyper-realistic reflections, minimal floating HUD elements, glowing amber light strips.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `6px 16px`, font-size: `12px`, font-weight: `600`, tracking: `0.05em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `rgba(255,255,255,0.9)`, border width: `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-orange-500 inline-block"></span>`.
