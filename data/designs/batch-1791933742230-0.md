# Design Specification: Minimalist Fashion Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Palette
Full-screen, minimalist editorial landing page with asymmetric layering, high-contrast typography, and organic accents.

### Color Palette
- **Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Primary Text:** `#1A1A1A` (Charcoal Black)
- **Secondary / Meta Text:** `#8C8C8C` (Muted Grey)
- **Accent Green:** `#4A704A` / `#567A57` (Olive / Forest Sage)
- **Clothing / Card Neutral:** `#F4F2EE` (Off-white / Warm Cream)

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
- **Primary Font Family:** Geometric Sans-Serif (`Gilroy`, `Montserrat`, or `Futura`).
- **Fallback Font Stack:** `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.

### Hierarchy
- **Nav Links:** 11px, Bold / Medium, Uppercase, Tracking `0.15em`, `#1A1A1A`.
- **Large Backdrop Numerals ("2020"):** ~160px–180px, Extra Bold, Accent Green (`#567A57`), rotated 90° clockwise.
- **Main Heading ("Minimal-ist"):** 72px–80px, Bold, Line height `0.95`, `#1A1A1A`.
- **Section Heading ("Fashion culture"):** 36px–40px, Semi-Bold, Line height `1.05`, `#1A1A1A`.
- **Subheadings ("Living with less", "Get back to basics"):** 14px–16px, Semi-Bold, `#1A1A1A`.
- **Body Text:** 10px–11px, Regular, Line height `1.6`, `#8C8C8C`.
- **Vertical Caption:** 9px, Regular, Rotated 90° clockwise, `#8C8C8C`.

---

## 3. Layout Grid & Structure
- **Viewport:** 100vw × 100vh, fixed/unscrollable desktop layout (`overflow: hidden`).
- **Outer Padding:** `40px 60px`.

### Header (Top Row)
- **Left Navigation (`top: 40px, left: 60px`):** Horizontal list (`gap: 32px`):
  - `NEW ARRIVALS`, `BEAUTY`, `CULTURE`, `LIVING`
- **Right Utilities (`top: 40px, right: 60px`):**
  - Minimal line icons (`20px × 20px`, `stroke-width: 1.5px`): Shopping Bag, Search Icon (`gap: 24px`).

---

## 4. Key Components & Coordinates

### Left Column
- **Slide Indicator:** "01" at `top: 25%`, `left: 60px`, 11px, Medium, `#1A1A1A`.
- **Year Tag:** "2020" at `top: 45%`, `left: 60px`, 11px, Semi-Bold, `#1A1A1A`.
- **Headline Layer:**
  - Placed at `left: 14%`, `top: 44%`.
  - Stacked: "Minimal" over "-ist". Partially tucks behind the center model cutout.
- **Body Copy Block:**
  - Located at `left: 60px`, `bottom: 12%`, `max-width: 260px`.
  - Header: "Living with less"
  - Copy: "Act Minimalist fashion is defined by one major principle: keep it simple! Streamlined shapes, a small selection of colors and even a bare minimum (gasp!) amount of clothing in your closet."
  - Pagination marker: Three muted dots below text.

### Center Focal Image
- **Description:** Studio cut-out (transparent background, no framing) of a fair-skinned woman with a blunt platinum blonde bob looking downward to the left. She wears a minimal draped ivory high-neck coat/smock and holds lush, deep-green foliage/broad leaves behind her shoulders.
- **Layering & Placement:**
  - Position: Horizontal center (`left: 48%`, `transform: translateX(-50%)`), anchored at screen bottom (`bottom: 0`).
  - Height: ~78vh to 82vh.
  - Z-Index: Sits above the background vertical "2020" text and the word "Minimal", but below nav elements.

### Right Column & Accents
- **Vertical Backdrop "2020":**
  - Placed around `left: 55%` to `60%`, `top: -5%`.
  - Rendered vertically (rotated `90deg` clockwise), oversized, clipped at viewport edge, `#567A57`.
- **Rotated Caption:**
  - Placed at `right: 80px`, `top: 25%`.
  - Text: "Simplicity is the key to pinpointing this style." (rotated `90deg` clockwise).
- **Secondary Card (Inset):**
  - Placed at `right: 80px`, `top: 45%`.
  - Dimensions: `160px × 110px`, rectangular image of draped, folded ivory/silk fabric texture.
- **Bottom Callout & Graphic:**
  - Placed at `right: 22%`, `bottom: 15%`.
  - Title: "Fashion" / "culture" stacked.
  - Subtitle: "Get back to basics".
  - Graphic: Thick inverted U-shape / arch icon (`w: 50px, h: 40px`, stroke/fill `#4A704A`) placed to the right at `right: 80px`, `bottom: 15%`.
