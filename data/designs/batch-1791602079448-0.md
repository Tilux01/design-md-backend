# Design System & Implementation Specification (`design.md`)

## 1. Executive Summary & Page Architecture

This document defines the complete visual, layout, and functional specifications for recreating the **"AROUND THE WORLD: NORWAY MOUNTAINS"** landing page. 

The layout is built as a **full-screen viewport landing page (100vh / 100vw)** with a **50/50 split-screen hero structure**:
*   **Left Section (50% Width):** Frosted ambient glass overlay featuring brand mark, hero typography with texture masking, quote, sub-navigation CTA, and graphic icons.
*   **Right Section (50% Width):** Crisp, high-resolution photography showcase displaying a Norwegian mountain fjord, overlaid with primary navigation header, custom vertical pagination indicator, and carousel controls.

## 4. Imagery & Asset Specifications

### 1. Main Right-Pane Background Image
*   **Description:** A sweeping, high-angle aerial landscape photograph of Norway’s fjords (resembling Kvalvika Beach or Lofoten Islands). On the right side, dramatic, steep, dark granite mountain peaks with light patches of snow and moss rise into a moody, cloud-filled sky. On the lower center-left, a golden sand beach curves smoothly into clear, dark turquoise Atlantic water.
*   **Type:** Raster Image (`.webp` or `.png`).
*   **Background:** Fully opaque photographic asset.
*   **Dimensions / Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 or custom cropped to fill 50% screen width × 100% screen height (`object-fit: cover`).
*   **Placement:** Right screen half (spanning x-axis `50vw` to `100vw`, y-axis `0` to `100vh`).
*   **Rotation:** `0 deg`.

### 2. Title Text Texture Fill Asset (Left Side Text Mask)
*   **Description:** A close-up texture image of sunlit, warm golden-brown rock faces and jagged mountain terrain under direct warm sunlight.
*   **Type:** Seamless pattern or high-res texture image (`.jpg` / `.webp`).
*   **Usage:** Applied strictly as a background clip (`background-clip: text`) onto the left portion of the "NORWAY MOUNTAINS" title text.

### 3. Header Center Top Line-Art Logo
*   **Description:** Minimalist vector icon depicting geometric mountain peaks outlined in thin gold/bronze lines.
*   **Type:** Vector (`.svg`).
*   **Background:** Backgroundless (Transparent).
*   **Dimensions:** Width ~`70px`, Height ~`35px`.
*   **Placement:** Straddling the upper-center split boundary, positioned at `top: 30px`.
*   **Rotation:** `0 deg`.

### 4. Left Hero Accent Mountain Logo
*   **Description:** Dark silhouette mountain range graphic featuring 3 overlapping geometric peaks.
*   **Type:** Vector (`.svg`).
*   **Background:** Backgroundless (Transparent).
*   **Color:** Charcoal / Dark Brown (`#2B241F`).
*   **Dimensions:** Width ~`110px`, Height ~`40px`.
*   **Placement:** Positioned on the left pane above the "NORWAY" text block, left-aligned with the body text margin.
*   **Rotation:** `0 deg`.

### Top Navigation Bar
*   **Left Brand Mark:**
    *   Text stacked vertically: "AROUND" on line 1, "THE WORLD" on line 2.
    *   Position: Absolute, `top: 40px`, `left: 50px`.
    *   Color: Dark charcoal `#1E1B18`.
*   **Center Top Icon:**
    *   Position: Absolute, `top: 32px`, `left: 50%` (centered horizontally).
*   **Right Menu Links:**
    *   Horizontal list: `HOME`, `ABOUT US`, `TOURS`, `CONTACTS`, `SIGN IN`.
    *   Gap: `36px` between items.
    *   Position: Absolute, `top: 40px`, `right: 50px`.
    *   Color: `#FFFFFF` with soft text-shadow for outdoor background visibility.

### Central Split Title ("NORWAY MOUNTAINS")
*   **Visual Logic:**
    *   The title spans across the center divide line of the screen.
    *   The left half of the title ("NOR" + top of "MOU") sits over the frosted glass background and is filled with the **warm rock texture mask**.
    *   The right half of the title ("WAY" + right edge of "TAINS") sits over the landscape photo and is filled with **solid pure white (`#FFFFFF`)**.
*   **Alignment:** Vertically centered on the page (`top: 42%`), overflowing the center screen axis.

### Left Body Content Panel
*   **Category Tag:**
    *   Text: "AMAZING TOUR"
    *   Positioned directly above the main title, with generous tracking (`3px`).
*   **Quote Text:**
    *   Text: *"Travel leaves you speechless and then makes you a better storyteller."*
    *   Max Width: `320px`.
    *   Position: `40px` below the "MOUNTAINS" title line.
*   **CTA Link:**
    *   Text: "LEARN ABOUT THE MOUNTAINS OF NORWAY"
    *   Style: Bold, white text with a solid 1px white underline offset by `6px`.
    *   Position: `24px` below quote text.

### Right Controls & Pagination Elements
1. **Vertical Slider Navigation Bar (Right Edge):**
   * **Location:** Positioned vertically at `right: 40px`, centered vertically (`top: 50%`, `transform: translateY(-50%)`).
   * **Structure:** A thin vertical line (`1px` width, `120px` height) with 3 diamond indicators (`◇`).
   * **Active Indicator:** Highlighted active diamond framed in a slightly larger square outline.
2. **Carousel Arrow Controls (Bottom Right):**
   * **Location:** Positioned at `bottom: 40px`, `right: 50px`.
   * **Structure:** Two square buttons (`44px` × `44px`) side-by-side with a `10px` gap.
   * **Appearance:** Warm semi-transparent dark bronze (`rgba(88, 77, 65, 0.85)`).
   * **Icons:** Single stroke left arrow (`←`) and right arrow (`→`) in white (`#FFFFFF`).

## 6. Interactive Behavior & Transitions

*   **Navigation Links Hover State:**
    *   Opacity transitions from `0.85` to `1.0`.
    *   Subtle white underline scales in from center (`transition: transform 0.3s ease`).
*   **Carousel Arrow Buttons Hover State:**
    *   Background shifts to `rgba(115, 101, 86, 0.95)`.
    *   Arrow icon translates slightly (`-2px` left on prev, `+2px` right on next).
*   **CTA Link Hover State:**
    *   Underline expands in thickness or changes color tone toward warm bronze (`#E2AA72`).
*   **Slide Switch Animation:**
    *   Right image fades with a smooth cross-dissolve (`0.6s ease-in-out`).
    *   Text texture fill in title updates to match respective new destination imagery.
