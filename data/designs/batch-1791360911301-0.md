# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Fine Art & Museum Curator Dark Minimalist.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-end editorial layout with deep negative space framing a dramatic central chiaroscuro sculpture composition. Focus is concentrated on high-contrast neoclassical imagery and letter-spaced typography.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Single-layer immersive viewport (`100vw`/`100vh`) with absolute depth achieved solely through directional lighting, soft ambient background drop-off, and layered subtle white/monochrome atmospheric highlights.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase navigation labels, chiaroscuro lighting simulation, atmospheric point-light highlights (moon indicator), minimal divider strokes.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: N/A for this minimalist fine-art layout (replaced by active navigation underline indicator).
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Bullet / Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-white inline-block"></span>`.
