# Design Specification: Music Stream App UI

## 1. Global Styles
*   **Background:** Solid Black (`#000000`).
*   **Accent Color 1 (Lime):** `#D4E95E` (Used for active states and primary buttons).
*   **Accent Color 2 (Purple):** `#C499F3` (Used for the primary featured card).
*   **Text Primary:** White (`#FFFFFF`) at 100% opacity.
*   **Text Secondary:** Light Grey (`#A0A0A0`) at 70-80% opacity.
*   **Corner Radius:** 
    *   Cards: `32px`
    *   Buttons/Chips: `20px`
    *   Profile/Thumbnails: `50%` (Circular) or `16px` (Rounded Square).

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `SF Pro Display`.
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `System UI`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Weights & Sizes:**
    *   **Greeting ("Hi, Samantha"):** `28px`, Semi-bold.
    *   **Section Headers ("Curated & trending"):** `22px`, Medium.
    *   **Card Titles:** `20px`, Bold.
    *   **List Titles:** `16px`, Medium.
    *   **Subtitles/Metadata:** `12px`, Regular.
    *   **Chips:** `14px`, Medium.

---

## 3. Header Section
*   **Status Bar:** Standard iOS layout (Time at 9:41, Signal, Wi-Fi, Battery icons).
*   **Background Glow:** A soft, radial gradient behind the profile picture. Colors: Deep Purple (`#4B0082`) fading into black.
*   **Profile Image:** 
    *   **Description:** A circular portrait of a young woman with dark hair wearing headphones, looking directly at the camera. Neutral background.
    *   **Placement:** Top left, `20px` padding from edges.
*   **Action Icons:** 
    *   **Search & Heart:** Two circular buttons on the top right. 
    *   **Style:** Dark grey background (`#1A1A1A`), `1px` subtle border, white thin-stroke icons.

---

## 4. Filter Chips (Horizontal Scroll)
*   **Layout:** A horizontally scrollable row below the greeting.
*   **Active State ("All"):** Background `#D4E95E`, Text Black (`#000000`).
*   **Inactive States ("New Release", "Trending", "Top"):** Background `#1A1A1A`, Text White, `1px` border.
*   **Spacing:** `8px` gap between chips.

---

## 5. Featured Card: "Discover weekly"
*   **Background:** A solid light purple (`#C499F3`) base with a subtle inner shadow for depth.
*   **Dimensions:** Full width minus `40px` horizontal padding.
*   **Image Content:**
    *   **Description:** A high-quality photo of a woman with headphones looking upwards. The image has a blue/pink neon lighting effect.
    *   **Placement:** Right-aligned within the card. The image is **backgroundless** (cut out) so the purple card color shows behind her head.
    *   **Rotation:** 0 degrees, but the subject's head is tilted back.
*   **Text Content:**
    *   **Title:** "Discover weekly" (Black, Bold).
    *   **Description:** "The original slow instrumental best palylists." (Black, Regular, `14px`). Note: Keep the typo "palylists" if a literal clone is required.
*   **Controls:**
    *   **Play Button:** Large dark purple circle with a white play triangle.
    *   **Secondary Icons:** Heart, Download (arrow in circle), and More (three dots). Thin black strokes.

---

*   **Header:** Title on the left, "See all" text button on the right in light grey.
*   **List Items:**
    1.  **Starlit Reverie:**

        *   **Metadata:** "By Budiarti • 8 Songs".

    2.  **Midnight Confessions:**
        *   **Thumbnail:** Square with `16px` radius. Image of a person with eyes closed, blue/purple lighting.
    3.  **Lost in the Echo:** (Partially visible at the bottom).

---

## 7. Floating Navigation Bar
*   **Effect:** Frosted glass (Backdrop-filter: `blur(20px)`) with a dark semi-transparent background (`rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.8)`).
*   **Shape:** Pill-shaped, centered at the bottom.
*   **Icons:**
    *   **Home (Active):** Contained in a Lime Green (`#D4E95E`) rounded square. Black house icon.
    *   **Music Library, Shuffle, Bookmark, Settings:** Thin white stroke icons distributed evenly.
*   **Placement:** Floating approximately `20px` above the bottom edge of the screen.

---

## 8. Visual Effects & Transitions
*   **Glassmorphism:** Applied to the navigation bar and the "Discover weekly" card controls.
*   **Gradients:** The top of the screen features a subtle mesh gradient of purple and dark blue to break the solid black background.
*   **Shadows:** Soft drop shadows on the floating navigation bar to give it height (Z-index).
