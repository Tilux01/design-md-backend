# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist, Modern E-commerce & Clean SaaS Hybrid.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace focus, organized asymmetric grid cards with generous padding, highly breathable layout sections.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered surface architecture featuring clean white canvas (`#FFFFFF`), subtle light gray card surfaces (`#F8FAFC`), crisp 1px neutral borders (`#E2E8F0`), and pill-shaped interactive floating action elements.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, floating ambient accent elements, card micro-indicators, border-radius continuity.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.1)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
