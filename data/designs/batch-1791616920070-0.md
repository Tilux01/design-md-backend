# Design Specification: Silica Gel Editorial Landing Page

This document provides a comprehensive, pixel-precise specification to build a 1:1 full-screen clone of the UI design. Outer presentation borders, rounded bezel mockups, and exterior studio backgrounds have been omitted to define a borderless, full-screen viewport layout.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Geometry

### 1.1 Viewport Partition
The layout is split horizontally into two distinct color zones across a standard 100vw × 100vh canvas:
* **Upper Hero Zone (`68vh` height):** 
  * Background Color: Soft Lilac Gray (`#E1DFE6`)
  * Houses top navigation, central hero typography, and 3D visual showcase.
* **Lower Content Shelf (`32vh` height):**
  * Background Color: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
  * Houses secondary editorial blocks, descriptions, and quick action icons.

### 1.2 Elevation & Layer Hierarchy (Z-Index Map)
* `z-index: 1`: Background canvas split (`#E1DFE6` upper, `#FFFFFF` lower).
* `z-index: 5`: Central hero typography (`SILICA GEL .`).
* `z-index: 10`: Backgroundless 3D visual element (overlaps upper canvas and hero typography).
* `z-index: 20`: Lower shelf editorial text columns and bottom-right utilities.
* `z-index: 30`: Left floating gradient feature card (spans across both upper and lower zones).
* `z-index: 40`: Top navigation bar (sticky/fixed at screen top).
* `z-index: 50`: Carousel navigation buttons (floating on the edge of the card).

## 4. Key Visual Asset (3D Render)

### Asset Specifications
* **Type:** Cutout visual asset (completely transparent background / PNG or WebP).
* **Placement:**
  * Horizontal Alignment: Anchored to the right half of the canvas (`right: 0`, spanning ~52% of total screen width).
  * Vertical Alignment: Sits at `top: 18vh` to `top: 72vh`, subtly crossing the boundary between the lilac top canvas and the bottom white shelf.
* **Visual Composition:**
  * **Spheres (3 total):** Three porous, fine-grained matte foam/pumice spheres with an earthy peach/coral tint (`#EB8A7A`).
    * *Sphere 1 (Top-Right):* Partially obscured by the white florets.
    * *Sphere 2 (Center-Left):* Fully exposed, medium size (~200px diameter on 1080p).
    * *Sphere 3 (Bottom-Right):* Large sphere (~240px diameter), nestled underneath the decorative white cluster.
  * **Curled Cluster:** A dense, organic assemblage of white/bone porcelain ribbons, curled shavings, and paper-flower spiral rosettes. It wraps organically around the spheres, cascading downward from top right towards the bottom shelf.
  * **Lighting:** Diffuse daylight from top-left, generating soft self-shadowing without harsh specular highlights.

## 6. Micro-Interactions & States

1. **Navigation Links:**
   * Default: `#32273F`
   * Hover: Opacity reduction to `0.6`, subtle underline transition.
2. **Search Input Bar:**
   * Focus state: Subtle 1px outline in `#8879A0`, placeholder text fades out.
3. **Carousel Buttons:**
   * Hover (Left Button): Scale `1.05`, background transitions to `#F5F4F8`.
   * Hover (Right Button): Scale `1.05`, background transitions to `#4C3969`.
4. **Social & Utility Icons:**
   * Hover: Micro-scale (`transform: translateY(-2px)`), color shifts toward `#645579`.
5. **3D Visual Parallax (Optional Enhancement):**
   * Floating peach spheres subtly react to mouse cursor movement (subtle multi-axis translate `±12px`).
