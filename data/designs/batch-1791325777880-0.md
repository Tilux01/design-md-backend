# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Neural SaaS, High-Tech Immersive AI, Deep Space Data Visualization.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio framing a central immersive 3D particle entity; generous padding across screen edges with asymmetrical metric callouts.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Ultra-flat translucent surface layers with subtle backdrop blurs (`blur-md`), hairline borders (`1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and soft ambient glow orbs simulating depth.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient particle simulations, floating metric telemetry data points with crosshair coordinate lines, glassmorphism containers.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.7)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-blue-400 inline-block"></span>`.

- **Motion Principles**: Smooth cubic-bezier easing (`[0.16, 1, 0.3, 1]`), buttery parallax scrolling for particle depth, subtle ambient pulse on status indicators.
- **Framer Motion Variants Code**:
