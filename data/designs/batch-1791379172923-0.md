# Design Specification: "Immersive." Mountain Experience Clone

This document outlines the exact design and implementation specifications for creating a pixel-perfect, full-screen clone of the interface shown in the media.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport

*   **Mode:** Fullscreen (Edge-to-Edge). No container framing, window borders, or padding.
*   **Background Color (Fallback):** `#E5E0D8` (Warm beige/sand tone to match the atmospheric haze).
*   **Overflow:** `hidden` (preventing any scrollbars).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

The design relies on a clean, modern, wide-tracked grotesque sans-serif style reminiscent of editorial high-fashion or architecture web interfaces (e.g., Swiss/International Typographic Style).

### Primary Font:
*   **Font Family:** `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, or `Arial`, fallback to `sans-serif`.
*   **Style:** Regular / Light (300-400 weight).
*   **Letter Spacing (Tracking):** Highly expanded (`0.15em` to `0.3em`).
*   **Text Transform:** Uppercase.

---

## 3. Visual Components & Layout Structure

### A. Background / Hero Asset
*   **Element:** High-resolution photographic or 3D-rendered grayscale image of a mountain range emerging from mist/clouds.
*   **Styling:**
    *   `object-fit: cover;`
    *   `width: 100vw; height: 100vh;`
    *   Monochrome / Low-saturation grading with warm undertones matching the overall beige palette.

### B. Header Navigation
Positioned absolutely at the top of the viewport with generous side padding.

*   **Left Element:** Minimalist brand or title text (e.g., partially visible `"TFORT"` / truncated title).
    *   **Font Size:** `14px`
    *   **Tracking:** Wide (`0.2em`)
    *   **Color:** Low-opacity white / light gray (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`) to blend over the image.
*   **Center Section:** Subtle navigation links or spacing (optional/minimalist).
*   **Right Elements:** Minimalist icon/menu indicators (e.g., small dot/menu symbol or profile indicator).
    *   **Color:** Matching left text (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`).

### C. Large Typography Overlays (Parallax / Section Titles)
Faint, large-scale watermark-style typography layered across the mid-ground of the mountain.

*   **Content:** Fragmented words overlaid horizontally (e.g., `TFORT` on the left, trailing into middle characters).
*   **Styling:**
    *   **Opacity:** Very subtle (`opacity: 0.15` to `0.25`).
    *   **Blend Mode:** `overlay` or `soft-light` to integrate naturally with the mountain textures.
    *   **Font Size:** Fluid scaling (approx. `6vw` to `10vw`).

---

## 4. Animations & Interactions

### A. Parallax Mouse Move Effect
*   **Behavior:** As the cursor moves across the screen, the background mountain layer and foreground typography shift subtly in opposite directions to create depth.
*   **Implementation:** Track mouse coordinates `(clientX, clientY)` and apply a subtle `transform: translate(x, y)` via requestAnimationFrame.

### B. Transition / Load State
*   **Initial Load:** Fade-in from a solid `#E5E0D8` background with the mountain image scaling down slightly from `1.05` to `1.0` over `1.2s` with an `ease-out` cubic-bezier curve.
```css
transition: transform 1.2s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), opacity 0.8s ease;
```
