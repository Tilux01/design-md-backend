# Design Specification: Space Education Landing Page (Planet Earth)

This document provides the exact specifications to build a pixel-perfect, full-screen clone of the provided space education landing page. All presentation frames, window mockups, and outer background shadows from the source image are omitted; the implementation should occupy the full viewport width and height (`100vw` × `100vh`).

---

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas

*   **View Mode:** Fullscreen (`width: 100vw; height: 100vh; overflow: hidden;`)
*   **Background Color:** Deep space black/dark navy `#0A0D14` as the solid base.
*   **Ambient Effects:** Subtle starfield speckles scattered randomly across the background with varying opacities (`0.2` to `0.8`) and sizes (`1px` to `2px`).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Fonts
*   **Logo (`spaceedu`):** Sans-serif, lowercase, medium weight with generous letter-spacing.
*   **Navigation & UI Elements:** Clean sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*, or system fallback), uppercase with wide tracking for navigation items and buttons.
*   **Headings (`PLANET` / `EARTH`):** 
    *   `PLANET`: Small, uppercase, tracked-out sans-serif.
    *   `EARTH`: Ultra-large, high-contrast serif typeface (resembling *Playfair Display*, *Bodoni*, or *Times New Roman*), uppercase, tracking tightly spaced.
*   **Body Copy:** Highly legible, lightweight sans-serif, centered text alignment.

### Fallback Stack
```css
font-family: 'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif; /* For main headers */
font-family: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif; /* For UI, navigation, and body */
```

---

## 3. Header & Navigation

Positioned at the top with generous padding from the viewport edges (`padding: 24px 48px;`).

*   **Logo (`spaceedu`):**
    *   **Position:** Top-left.
    *   **Styling:** White text, lowercase, font-weight: 500. The dot (`.`) or trailing segment may use an accent color (cyan/teal `#00F0FF`).
*   **Navigation Links:**
    *   **Position:** Top-right, horizontally aligned.
    *   **Items:** `Planets`, `Trailer`, `Tickets`, `Blog`.
    *   **Active State:** `Planets` has an active state indicated by a solid bottom accent line (cyan/teal `#00F0FF`, height: `2px`, width matching the text).
*   **Enroll CTA Button:**
    *   **Position:** Far right of the navigation row.
    *   **Styling:** Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 50px`), white solid background, dark text (`#0A0D14`), font-weight: 600, padding: `8px 20px`.
*   **Divider Line:**
    *   A subtle, full-width horizontal rule running directly below the navigation bar (`border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).

---

## 4. Hero Content Section

Centered vertically and horizontally within the viewport (excluding header space).

*   **Pre-header (`PLANET`):**
    *   **Text:** `PLANET`
    *   **Styling:** Uppercase, small font size (~12-14px), letter-spacing: `4px`, color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
*   **Main Header (`EARTH`):**
    *   **Text:** `EARTH`
    *   **Styling:** Massive serif typography (~80-110px), uppercase, white, tracking: `2px`.
    *   **Accent Line:** A short cyan/teal line centered horizontally directly beneath the word `EARTH` (width: `~60px`, height: `2px`, color: `#00F0FF`).
*   **Body Copy:**
    *   **Text:** *"Learn more about this fascinating miracle that we call our home, Planet Earth. Course enrollment starts today. Early Bird tickets typically last a week, don't miss out!"*
    *   **Styling:** Max-width: `550px`, centered alignment, font-size: `14px`, line-height: `1.6`, color: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`.
*   **Primary CTA Button (`GET STARTED`):**
    *   **Position:** Centered below the body copy.
    *   **Styling:** Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 50px`), semi-transparent dark background with a crisp white or glowing cyan border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`), white text, uppercase, letter-spacing: `1px`, padding: `12px 32px`, box-shadow: `0 0 20px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)`.

---

## 5. Imagery & Visual Assets

### 1. Planet Earth (Hero Foreground / Bottom Anchor)
*   **Description:** A detailed, high-resolution rendering of Planet Earth viewed from space, dominated by swirling white cloud formations, deep blue oceans, and illuminated continental landmasses. The top half of the globe catches a bright solar glare/highlight at the upper-right edge.
*   **State:** Backgroundless (transparent PNG/WebP cutout).
*   **Placement:** Anchored at the bottom center of the viewport, overlapping the lower section of the layout. 
*   **Dimensions/Transform:** Large scale (`width: ~70vw` to `80vw`), overflowing slightly off the bottom edge of the screen to create depth.

### 2. Planet Venus (Left Carousel Preview)
*   **Description:** A hazy, pale green-grey spherical planetary body with subtle atmospheric banding.
*   **State:** Backgroundless cutout.
*   **Placement:** Positioned mid-left, partially cut off by the left edge of the screen.
*   **Label:** Directly below or next to the asset, the word `VENUS` appears in small, uppercase, spaced-out white text (`font-size: 11px`, `letter-spacing: 2px`).

### 3. Planet Mars (Right Carousel Preview)
*   **Description:** A rusty reddish-orange spherical planetary body showing dark surface patches and a glowing sunlit limb on its right edge.
*   **State:** Backgroundless cutout.
*   **Placement:** Positioned mid-right, partially cut off by the right edge of the screen.
*   **Label:** Directly below or next to the asset, the word `MARS` appears in small, uppercase, spaced-out white text (`font-size: 11px`, `letter-spacing: 2px`).

### 4. Scroll Indicator Icon
*   **Description:** A minimal downward-pointing chevron arrow (`↓`).
*   **State:** Contained inside a small dark circular badge with a subtle border or solid dark background.
*   **Placement:** Centered horizontally near the absolute bottom of the screen, just overlapping the upper atmosphere of Planet Earth.
