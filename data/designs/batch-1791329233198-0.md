# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise Fintech SaaS, modern dashboard interface.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace utilization with structured card containers, organized hierarchy, and clean separation between navigation, sidebar, and primary workspace.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat card layout utilizing subtle borders (`#E2E8F0`), muted background canvases (`#F8FAFC`), and clean surface layering with soft shadows (`0 4px 6px -1px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, emerald active indicators, clean rounded surface containers, avatar status dots.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
