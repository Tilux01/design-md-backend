# Visual Design Specification: Strawberry Milkshake Interaction Screen

This document details the exact design specifications for recreating the mobile UI screen shown in the provided media. The layout is designed to be full-screen, clean, minimalist, and centered around an interactive/animated strawberry milkshake aesthetic.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Specifications

*   **Target Aspect Ratio:** Mobile Portrait (approx. 9:16)
*   **Background:** 
    *   **Outer canvas (screen edge):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or very light off-white.
    *   **Central Content Card/Container:** A rounded-rectangle container with a solid soft pink fill (`#FFD1DC` or a warm pastel pink `#[FFC0CB]` with rounded corners of roughly `24px` to `32px` radius).
*   **Full-Screen Implementation:** Edge-to-edge layout where the soft pink content area fills the central viewport horizontally with consistent margins (~16px padding on left/right).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

*   **Primary Font Family:** Clean, geometric, rounded sans-serif (e.g., **Quicksand**, **Nunito**, or **SF Pro Rounded**).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Quicksand', 'Nunito', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif;`
*   **Text Hierarchy:**
    *   *Top Indicator / Tag:* Small rounded pill badge at the top-left of the pink container. Text is dark green/sage or deep pink inside a contrasting lighter pill. Font weight: Semi-bold, size: `12px`.

---

## 3. Visual Assets & Imagery (Description & Placement)

All decorative elements floating above the milkshake are **backgroundless (transparent PNGs)**, placed via absolute positioning, and rotated dynamically to create a dynamic falling/floating effect.

### A. Central Glass & Straw
*   **Description:** A tall, slender, frosted/glass-textured tumbler filled with light pink/white milkshake. A bright red/white striped straw protrudes from the top-right of the glass.
*   **Placement:** Centered horizontally near the bottom half of the pink container.
*   **Rotation:** Upright (0 degrees).

### B. Falling/Floating Strawberries (Backgroundless PNGs)
*   **Description:** Whole and halved fresh red strawberries with bright green leafy caps.
*   **Placement & Rotation Map:**
    *   *Top-Center cluster:* 1 whole strawberry angled slightly right (~15° rotation).
    *   *Top-Right:* 1 sliced strawberry showing inner red texture, tilted left (~-30° rotation).
    *   *Upper-Left:* 1 whole strawberry tilted right (~45° rotation).
    *   *Mid-Left:* 1 strawberry resting near the edge, angled downwards (~-15° rotation).
    *   *Mid-Right (near glass):* 1 whole strawberry hovering near the top rim of the glass (~20° rotation).
    *   *Bottom-Right:* 1 strawberry slice resting at the base of the glass.
    *   *Bottom-Left:* 1 strawberry half partially submerged/resting at the base corner.

### C. Floating Mint Leaves / Small Accents (Backgroundless PNGs)
*   **Description:** Small, vibrant green single mint leaves.
*   **Placement:** Scattered sparsely among the strawberries (one near the top-left cluster, one near the center-right above the glass) to add contrast against the pink backdrop.

---

## 4. Interactive Elements & User Actions

*   **Finger / Touch Indicator:** A dark silhouette/gloved hand indicator pointing and interacting with the mid-right side of the screen, simulating a tap or swipe gesture on the liquid/strawberries.
*   **Bottom Navigation / Action Bar:** Clean white space at the bottom of the screen containing subtle button outlines or minimal pagination dots for app navigation.
