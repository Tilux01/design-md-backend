# My Journal App UI Design Specification

## Global Styles & Tokens

### Color Palette
- **Primary / Accent (Yellow/Orange):** `#FFB938` (Used for active states, gradients, primary buttons, and the "Happy" emotion bar)
- **Background (Light Mode):** `#F4F3EF` (App background and card base)
- **Card Backgrounds:** 
  - Pure White: `#FFFFFF` (Used for popup containers and the emotions panel on the second screen)
  - Light Pink (Pause & Reflect): `#FDE4E1`
  - Light Purple (Set Intentions): `#E9E7FE`
- **Text Colors:**
  - Primary Dark Text: `#1C1C1E` (Headings, active dates, core numbers)
  - Secondary Gray Text: `#6E6E73` / `#8A8A8E` (Subtitles, unselected dates, labels)
- **Emotion Bar Colors:**
  - Happy (Yellow): `#FFB938`
  - Sad (Brown): `#783C30`
  - Calm (Green): `#7C9B3C`
  - Anxious (Slate Gray): `#6C7365`

### Typography (San Francisco / System UI Sans-Serif)
- **App Bar / Greeting:** 24px, Bold, `#1C1C1E`
- **Section Headers ("My Journal", "Quick Journal"):** 18px, Semi-Bold, `#1C1C1E`
- **Body / Subtitles:** 14px, Regular, `#6E6E73`
- **Big Metric Counter ("420"):** 48px, Bold, `#1C1C1E`

---

## Screen 1: Home Dashboard (`/home`)

### 1. Top Navigation Bar
- **Greeting:** "Hi, Jose Maria" (Bold, 24px)
- **User Avatar:** Circular profile picture located at the top-right corner.

### 2. Horizontal Date Selector
- **Days displayed:** Mon to Sun
- **Dates displayed:** 7 (Mon) to 13 (Sun, matching the active state)
- **Active State (`Thu 10`):** Circular background filled with `#FFB938`, text color `#FFFFFF` or dark `#1C1C1E`.
- **Inactive States:** Transparent background with gray text.

### 3. My Journal Section
- **Header:** "My Journal" with "See all" link on the right.
- **Morning Card (Active):**
  - Background: Gradient from bright yellow (`#FFD15C`) to warm orange (`#FF9F2E`).
  - Illustration: Sunrise over green hills with a cute smiling sun character.
  - Text: 
    - "Let's start your day" (Bold, 18px)
    - "Begin with a mindful morning reflections." (Regular, 12px)
- **Evening Card (Preview/Overflow):**
  - Positioned partially on the right edge (`overflow-x`).
  - Background: Muted beige (`#DCD6CD`).
  - Vertical Text: "Evening".

### 4. Quick Journal Section
- **Header:** "Quick Journal" with "See all" link.
- **Card 1: Pause & Reflect**
  - Background: `#FDE4E1` (Light Pink)
  - Icon: Leaf/sparkle icon (`#3A7D44`)
  - Title: "Pause & reflect" (Semi-bold, 14px)
  - Description: "What are you grateful for today?" (12px)
  - Tags: `Today` (white pill), `Personal` (pink outlined pill)
- **Card 2: Set Intentions**
  - Background: `#E9E7FE` (Light Purple)
  - Icon: Smiling face emoji (`😊`)
  - Title: "Set Intentions" (Semi-bold, 14px)
  - Description: "How do you want to feel?" (12px)
  - Tags: `Today` (white pill), `Family` (purple outlined pill)

### 5. Bottom Navigation Bar
- **Background:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Items:**
  - 🏠 **Home** (Active, dark filled home icon with label)
  - 🧭 **Explore** (Outline compass icon)
  - ➕ **Add / Action Button:** Centered floating circular button filled with `#FFB938` containing a white `+` icon.
  - 📖 **Journey** (Outline journal icon)
  - 👤 **Profile** (Outline user profile icon)

## Screen 2: Journal Analytics / Detail (`/journal-detail`)

### 1. Top Navigation Bar
- **Back Button:** Circular container with a `<` chevron.
- **Title:** "My Journal" (Centered, 16px, Semi-Bold)
- **Menu Button:** Circular container with `...` (three dots).

### 2. Counter Header Section
- **Metric Number:** `420` (Bold, 48px)
- **Subtitle:** "Celebrate what made you smile today." (Regular, 14px, `#6E6E73`)

### 3. Emotions Breakdown Card
- **Container:** Rounded white card (`#FFFFFF`) covering the lower half.
- **Section Title:** "Emotions" (Bold, 16px)
- **Subtitle:** "Here are four core emotions for your journal" (12px, `#8A8A8E`)
- **Vertical Progress / Bar Chart:**
  - Four vertical pill-shaped bars representing levels/percentages.
  - **Happy:** Filled 48% with `#FFB938`, labeled "Happy" below.
  - **Sad:** Filled 33% with `#783C30`, labeled "Sad" below.
  - **Calm:** Filled 27% with `#7C9B3C`, labeled "Calm" below.
  - **Anxious:** Filled 40% with `#6C7365`, labeled "Anxious" below.

### 4. Bottom Action Area
- **Primary Button:** 
  - Text: "Create a New Journal"
  - Background: `#FFB938`
  - Shape: Fully rounded pill button (`border-radius: 28px`)
  - Text Color: `#1C1C1E` (Semi-bold)
