# NeoVision — Full UI/UX Design Specification (`design.md`)

This document provides the complete, pixel-accurate design and implementation specifications for the **NeoVision** website. It is tailored for front-end developers, UI designers, and design-system engineers to recreate this exact dark-mode, futuristic tech aesthetic without missing any component, layout detail, or visual asset.

---

## 1. Global Theme & Aesthetic Direction

* **Visual Identity:** Cyberpunk-minimalism, high-end editorial sci-fi, cinematic dark mode, luxury spatial-computing tech.
* **Canvas Style:** Full-width, full-bleed presentation (`100vw`). Pitch-black base canvas (`#080808` to `#0A0A0A`) complemented by low-contrast dark charcoal cards (`#121212` to `#161616`), razor-thin borders, and greyscale photography with stark directional rim lighting.
* **Background Decor:** Smooth, faint, low-opacity bezier curves (light grey strokes with `10%` to `15%` opacity) sweeping across the lower quadrants to create spatial depth.

## 3. Typography Specification

### Primary Display Font (Headlines)
* **Primary Choice:** `Syne` (Weights: `700 Bold`, `800 Extra-Bold`) or `Clash Display`
* **Fallback Stack:** `Space Grotesk`, `Neue Montreal`, `Monument Extended`, `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `sans-serif`
* **Characteristics:** Wide geometric proportion, all-caps presentation, tight tracking (`letter-spacing: -0.01em` to `0.02em`).

### Secondary Body & Interface Font
* **Primary Choice:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter` (Weights: `400 Regular`, `500 Medium`, `600 Semi-Bold`)
* **Fallback Stack:** `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`
* **Characteristics:** Clean, tall x-height, neutral legibility at small sizes.

### Type Hierarchy Scale
* **Section Header (H2):** Display Sans, Uppercase, `44px` – `52px`, Line-height: `1.1`, Weight: `700`, Color: `#FFFFFF`.
* **Sub-Section Header / Story Title (H3):** Sans-serif, Sentence case, `22px` – `26px`, Line-height: `1.3`, Weight: `600`, Color: `#FFFFFF`.
* **Card Title:** Sans-serif, Sentence case, `18px` – `20px`, Line-height: `1.3`, Weight: `600`, Color: `#FFFFFF`.
* **Body Text:** Inter, `13px` – `14px`, Line-height: `1.6`, Weight: `400`, Color: `#9E9E9E`.
* **Microcopy & Metadata:** Inter, `11px` – `12px`, Line-height: `1.4`, Weight: `400`, Color: `#707070`.
* **Action Links ("Learn More"):** Inter, `12px` – `13px`, Line-height: `1.2`, Weight: `500`, Color: `#FFFFFF`.

## 6. Background Decorative Lines Specification

* **Type:** SVG Vector paths, stroke width `1px` to `1.5px`.
* **Stroke Color:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
* **Path Trajectory:**
  * Starts at the bottom-left corner (`X: 0%, Y: 92%`), curves gently upwards across the center (`X: 55%, Y: 84%`), and arcs downwards to the bottom-right edge (`X: 100%, Y: 88%`).
  * A second concentric path tracks slightly offset (`~40px` above), fading smoothly at both canvas edges.

## 7. Interactive States & Micro-interactions

* **Pill Badges (`Digital`, `Reality`, `Next`):**
  * Hover: `border-color: rgba(255,255,255,0.4)`, `background: rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`, cursor pointer.
* **Service Cards:**
  * Hover: Subtle vertical lift (`translateY(-4px)`), border shifts to `rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`, smooth transition (`300ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
* **"Learn More" Links:**
  * Hover: Underline decoration or text color brightens from `#CCCCCC` to `#FFFFFF` with slight rightward translation (`translateX(3px)`).
* **Testimonial Cards:**
  * Hover: Background brightens from `#121212` to `#161616`, avatar outer ring glows with soft white shadow (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`).

---

## 8. Responsive Breakpoint Behavior

* **Desktop (`≥ 1024px`):** Full 3-column service grid, 3-column feature row, 2-column testimonial section as documented above.
* **Tablet (`768px – 1023px`):**
  * Service grid shifts to a 2-column or horizontal scrollable track.
  * Feature showcase stacks with vertical order: Category tags → Feature Image → Article copy.
  * Testimonial section switches to a single column (Intro on top, cards stacked underneath).
* **Mobile (`< 768px`):**
  * Service cards stack into a single column (`width: 100%`).
  * Headlines scale proportionally (`H2` becomes `32px – 36px`).
  * Horizontal margins reduce to `20px` to maximize screen real estate.
