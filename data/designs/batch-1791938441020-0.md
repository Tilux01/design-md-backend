This design specification document outlines the requirements for a high-fidelity, full-screen clone of the "Dhaka Movies" XR interface.

# Design Specification: Dhaka Movies XR Interface

## 1. Global Styles & Visual Language
*   **Theme:** Dark Mode / Glassmorphism.
*   **Background:** The application should be full-screen with a background blur effect (frosted glass) that allows the underlying environment to peek through.
*   **Border Radius:** All primary containers use a large corner radius (approx. `40px`). Secondary elements (posters) use `20px`.
*   **Accent Color:** Neon Green (`#84FF00`) used for the primary Play button and active states.
*   **Text Color:** Primary text is White (`#FFFFFF`). Secondary/Description text is Light Grey (`#B3B3B3`).

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Inter** or **SF Pro Display**.
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:**
    *   Titles/Headings: Bold (700).
    *   Navigation/Buttons: Medium (500).
    *   Descriptions: Regular (400).

## 3. Layout Structure

### 3.1. Floating Side Navigation (Left)
A vertical pill-shaped floating bar located to the left of the main window.
*   **Background:** Semi-transparent dark glass.
*   **Icons (Top to Bottom):**
    1.  **Home Icon:** Housed in a lime-green glowing square (Active state).
    2.  **Search Icon:** Magnifying glass (Outline).
    3.  **TV Icon:** Rectangle with two antennae (Outline).
    4.  **Shopping Bag Icon:** (Outline).
    5.  **Settings Icon:** Gear (Outline).

### 3.2. Top Navigation Bar (Internal)
*   **Logo:** Left-aligned. A circular lime-green icon with lowercase "dm" in black, followed by "Dhaka Movies" in white bold text.
*   **Menu Items:** Center-aligned. "Movies", "TV Series", "Games", "Kids". (Font size: 16px, Weight: 500).
*   **User Controls:** Right-aligned.
    *   **Notification Bell:** Housed in a circular glass button.
    *   **Profile Pill:** A horizontal pill containing the name "Megan" and a circular profile photo of a woman with dark hair and a red top.

### 3.3. Hero Section (Main Feature)
*   **Content:** "Black Adam" movie.
*   **Background Image:** A wide-format poster featuring Dwayne Johnson (Black Adam) in the center, flanked by Hawkman, Dr. Fate, Cyclone, and Atom Smasher. The lighting is dramatic with blue and gold highlights.
*   **Overlay Elements:**
    *   **Title:** "DC BLACK ADAM" logo in metallic silver/gold styling.
    *   **Description:** Three lines of white text describing the plot.
    *   **Action Buttons:**
        *   "More info": Pill-shaped, semi-transparent glass.
        *   "Add to Favourite": Pill-shaped, semi-transparent glass with a heart icon.
*   **Central Play Button:** A large, vibrant neon-green circle with a black "Play" triangle in the center. It has a soft outer glow.

### 3.4. Recent Update Sidebar (Right)
A vertical column titled "Recent Update" with a "See all" link in green.
*   **Card 1:** Poster for "Edge of Tomorrow" (labeled "LIVE. DIE. REPEAT."). Features Tom Cruise and Emily Blunt in exo-suits. Includes a heart icon overlay in the top right.
*   **Card 2:** Poster for "Tenet". Features two men in suits, one inverted, against a blue/grey architectural background. Includes a heart icon overlay.

### 3.5. New Trending Row (Bottom)
A horizontal row titled "New Trending" with a "See all" link in green.
*   **Poster 1 (Mortal Kombat):** Features Sub-Zero and Scorpion facing off. Intense blue and orange lighting.
*   **Poster 2 (X-Men Apocalypse):** Ensemble cast featuring Oscar Isaac as Apocalypse in the center, surrounded by X-Men.
*   **Poster 3 (1917):** Features a soldier running through a battlefield with "1917" in large orange numbers in the background.
*   **Poster 4 (Oblivion):** Features Tom Cruise standing on a cliff edge overlooking a futuristic waterfall and a crashed ship.

## 4. Image Specifications & Placement

1.  **Profile Image:** 
    *   *Description:* Portrait of a woman (Megan), smiling, dark hair, wearing a red shirt.
    *   *Placement:* Top right profile pill.
    *   *Format:* Circular crop, no background (integrated into UI).

2.  **Hero Image (Black Adam):**
    *   *Description:* High-resolution cinematic shot. The characters are cut out from their original backgrounds to create a layered depth effect against the dark UI. Dwayne Johnson's face is the focal point.
    *   *Rotation:* 0 degrees.
    *   *Transparency:* Bottom and edges are feathered/gradient-faded into the dark background of the UI container.

3.  **Movie Posters (Trending & Recent):**
    *   *Description:* Standard 2:3 or 16:9 aspect ratio thumbnails.
    *   *Transparency:* None (Fully opaque).
    *   *Overlays:* Each poster has a small, semi-transparent glass circle in the top right corner containing a white heart icon (outline).

4.  **Play Button Icon:**
    *   *Description:* A 2D vector play triangle, solid black.
    *   *Placement:* Perfectly centered within the neon green circle.

---

## 5. Interaction Elements (Footer)
*   **Pagination:** Located at the very bottom center. Four small dots. The second dot is white (active), the others are grey (inactive).
*   **Floating Browser Bar:** At the very top, a browser address bar showing "dhakamovies.com" with standard Safari-style controls (Back, Forward, Share, Plus, Tabs). This bar is semi-transparent glass.
