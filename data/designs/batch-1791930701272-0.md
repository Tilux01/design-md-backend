# Design Specification: Aer "Work Anywhere" Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Layout Architecture
- **Canvas Size**: 100vw × 100vh (Full screen, no window frames).
- **Base Grid**: Fixed left sidebar (60px width) + Fluid main content area.
- **Theme**: Dark, minimalist, technical apparel aesthetic.

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Background Primary**: `#1F2226` (Dark Charcoal)
- **Background Accent / Sidebar**: `#181A1D` (Matte Deep Slate)
- **Card Background**: `#25282E`
- **Text Primary**: `#FFFFFF` (100% white)
- **Text Secondary**: `#8A8E95` (Muted gray)
- **Text Inactive / Dividers**: `#3D4148` (Low-contrast gray)

---

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Font**: `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, `-apple-system`, `sans-serif`
- **Scale**:
  - **Hero Title**: 48px, Bold (700), Line-height: 1.1
  - **Category Index (`/ 01`)**: 16px, Semi-Bold (600)
  - **Body / Subtext**: 13px, Regular (400), Line-height: 1.5
  - **Navigation Links**: 13px, Regular (400)
  - **Section Titles (EVENTS, NEWS)**: 11px, Bold (700), Tracking: +1.5px (Uppercase)
  - **Metadata & Social Links**: 11px, Medium (500)

---

## 4. Components & Structural Layout

### A. Left Rail (Sidebar)
- **Dimensions**: Fixed 60px wide, 100vh height.
- **Top**: Hamburger icon (3 horizontal lines, 18px wide, white).
- **Bottom**: Vertical text social links (`Fb`, `In`, `Tw`), stacked bottom-to-top, rotated -90deg or vertically aligned, spaced 20px apart. Color: `#8A8E95`.

### B. Top Navigation Bar
- **Dimensions**: 100% width of the main canvas, 80px height, flex row, vertically centered.
- **Left**: Brand logo text **Aer** (24px, Bold, `#FFFFFF`).
- **Right (Nav Links)**:
  - Links: `Home`, `Products`, `About`, `Locations`, `Cart`.
  - Color: `#8A8E95`; Active state (`Home`): `#FFFFFF` with a 2px horizontal underline.
  - Far Right: Search icon (magnifying glass, 16px, `#FFFFFF`).

### C. Hero Section
- **Central Visual**: Full-height atmospheric background image (see Image Specifications).
- **Left Content Panel (Overlay)**:
  - Position: 120px from top, 40px from left rail.
  - Number indicator: `/ 01` (White).
  - Active line: `Work Collection` (White) with an active underline.
  - Inactive list: `Active Collection`, `Travel Collection` (Color: `#50555E`, stacked vertically, 10px spacing).
- **Right Content Panel (Overlay)**:
  - Position: Right-of-center, vertically centered.
  - Headline:
    ```text
    Work
    Anywhere
    ```
  - Subtitle:
    ```text
    Introducing the Work Collection, a line of minimalist
    bags designed for a new generation specifically for
    the modern professional
    ```
  - Style: `#8A8E95`, link-styled underlines under key phrases.

### D. Bottom Information Bar
- **Dimensions**: Spans the lower 25% of the viewport; dark background `#181A1D`.
- **Three-Column Grid**:
  1. **Events Column (Left)**:
     - Header: `EVENTS`
     - Two horizontal sub-columns:
       - Item 1: `Pop-up @ Steven Alan Fillmore` / `SF — Sat, August 12 / 5–8PM`
       - Item 2: `Pop-up @ Steven Alan Brooklyn` / `NYC — Sat, August 12 / 5–8PM`
     - Bottom-left pagination: `...` indicator.
  2. **Thumbnail Image (Center)**:
     - Embedded vertical photo preview (see Image Specifications).
  3. **News Column (Right)**:
     - Header: `NEWS`
     - Body: "A fresh take on our Active Collection, just in time for spring. Available in black."
     - Action link: `Read More` (underlined).

---

## 5. Imagery & Asset Specifications

1. **Main Hero Image (Center)**:
   - **Description**: Back view of a person wearing a dark hooded jacket and a technical black commuter backpack. Lighting is moody, low-key, directional from the right, with dark shadows blending seamlessly into `#1F2226`.
   - **Treatment**: Integrated dark background (not backgroundless), centered horizontally, 0° rotation.

2. **Bottom Thumbnail Image**:
   - **Description**: Full-body or 3/4 side profile of a man wearing a backpack against a neutral concrete architectural backdrop.
   - **Treatment**: Cropped vertical rectangle, sharp corners, no border, 0° rotation.
