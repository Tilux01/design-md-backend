# Design Specification: Automotive Media Interface (Dark Mode)

## 1. General Theme & Layout
*   **Background:** Solid deep midnight navy to black gradient (e.g., `#080A0F` to `#000000`).
*   **Overall Layout:** A single-page application (SPA) layout divided into a main content area (left 65%) and a sidebar (right 35%), wrapped in a global top status bar and a global bottom control bar.
*   **Visual Style:** Glassmorphism/Neumorphism hybrid. High contrast, rounded corners (approx. `24px` for main containers, `12px` for smaller elements).

## 2. Color Palette
*   **Primary Accent:** Vibrant Sky Blue (`#4A89F3`) - used for active states and primary buttons.
*   **Background (Main):** `#0B0D14` (Deep Charcoal/Navy).
*   **Background (Secondary/Cards):** `#161B22` with a subtle `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)`.
*   **Text (Primary):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
*   **Text (Secondary):** `#8A8D91` (Cool Grey).
*   **Success/Battery:** `#4CD964` (Bright Green).

## 3. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Inter** or **SF Pro Display**.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Heading 1 (Album Title):** `36px`, Semi-Bold, Letter-spacing: `-0.5px`.
    *   **Heading 2 (Section Titles):** `18px`, Medium, Color: Secondary Text.
    *   **Body Text:** `14px`, Regular, Line-height: `1.5`, Color: Secondary Text.
    *   **Button Text:** `14px`, Semi-Bold.
    *   **Metadata (Stats):** `12px`, Regular, Color: Secondary Text.

## 4. Component Breakdown

### A. Top Status Bar
*   **Left:** User profile section. Small circular avatar (`32px`) followed by "Hi, Rosen" in `14px` White.
*   **Center:** Digital clock (`15:20`) in `14px` White.
*   **Right:** System icons (Signal strength, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Battery). The battery icon is green (`#4CD964`).

### B. Hero Section (Featured Album)
*   **Content:**
    *   Title: "Thank U, Next"
    *   Stats Row: "12 Songs • 571 Favorites • 24.1k View" (Small text inside a subtle dark pill container).
    *   Description: 3-4 lines of justified text about Ariana Grande and Republic Records.
    *   **Primary Button:** "Play Now" - Rounded pill, Blue background, white text, includes a "Play" icon inside a white circle.
    *   **Secondary Button:** Heart icon button, dark circular background.
*   **Main Image (The Artist):**
    *   **Subject:** Ariana Grande.
    *   **Properties:** High-resolution, **backgroundless (PNG)**.
    *   **Pose:** Facing slightly left, looking at the camera, high ponytail with silver/platinum hair.
    *   **Placement:** Positioned behind the text elements but in front of the background, centered-right of the left panel. The bottom of the image should fade/blend into the background.

### C. Top Albums Section
*   **Header:** "Top Albums" in `18px` Secondary Text.
*   **Grid:** 4 horizontal cards.
*   **Card Style:** Rounded square images (`120px x 120px`).
*   **Labels:** Title (White, `14px`) and Artist (Grey, `12px`) stacked below the image.
*   **Images Needed:**
    1.  *Just Look Up:* Ariana Grande (Cloudy/Blue aesthetic).
    2.  *Poker Face:* Lady Gaga (Blue/Teal water aesthetic).
    3.  *This Is Acting:* Sia (Face with two-toned wig).
    4.  *Let Somebody Go:* Selena Gomez (Red/Pink aesthetic).

### D. Playlists Sidebar (Right)
*   **Header:** "Play Lists" in `18px` Secondary Text.
*   **List Items:** 5 rows.
*   **Active State:** The second item ("Please don't go") is highlighted with a Blue (`#4A89F3`) background, white text, and a Pause icon.
*   **Inactive State:** Transparent background, white text for title, grey for artist, play icon on the far right.
*   **Avatars:** Small circular thumbnails (`36px`) for each song.

### E. Mini-Player (Bottom Right)
*   **Container:** Darker floating card with a subtle border.
*   **Content:**
    *   Square album art on the left.
    *   Song Title ("Please don't go") and Artist ("Brown Boy").
    *   Progress Bar: Thin line with a blue progress indicator.
    *   Controls: Previous, Play/Pause, Next, and Heart icons.

### F. Global Bottom Navigation
*   **Style:** Dark semi-transparent bar.
*   **Icons (Left to Right):**
    1.  Car/Vehicle Settings.
    2.  Steering Wheel/Drive Mode.
    3.  Navigation/Arrow.
    4.  Temperature Left (`25°`).
    5.  Fan/Climate Icon.
    6.  Temperature Right (`25°`).
    7.  App Grid/Menu.
    8.  Phone/Dialer.
    9.  Music/Media (Active).

## 5. Image Asset Specifications

1.  **Main Feature Image:**
    *   **Description:** Ariana Grande, silver/white hair in a high ponytail, wearing a dark top.
    *   **Format:** Transparent PNG.
    *   **Crop:** Waist-up.
    *   **Rotation:** 0 degrees, but the subject is angled slightly.
2.  **Album Thumbnails:**
    *   **Shape:** Rounded Rectangles (`16px` radius).
    *   **Aspect Ratio:** 1:1.
3.  **Icons:**
    *   **Style:** Thin-line vector icons (Linear).
    *   **Color:** White or Light Grey.

## 6. Interaction Notes
*   **Hover/Active States:** Any interactive element (buttons, list items) should transition to `#4A89F3` or increase opacity.
*   **Scrolling:** The "Top Albums" section should support horizontal touch-swipe. The "Play Lists" should support vertical scrolling if the list exceeds the height.
