# Visual Design Specification: Animated Owl Character Concept

## 1. Overview & Art Direction
- **Subject:** A 3D digital art rendering of a stylized anthropomorphic owl wearing round spectacles and a teal hoodie.
- **Style:** Modern 3D animated character design (similar to Pixar/DreamWorks style). Highly textured, soft lighting, focus on tactile materials (soft feathers, fleece fabric).
- **Aspect Ratio & Framing:** Full-screen composition, isolated on a clean, solid white/off-white background. The UI elements from the original carousel post (progress bars, watermark, arrow) are excluded as requested.

---

## 2. Character Anatomy & Styling

### 2.1 Head & Facial Features
- **Head Shape:** Rounded, slightly flattened top with a pair of small tufted feather "horns" at the crown.
- **Feathers:** 
  - Layered, overlapping feather clumps covering the body.
  - Color palette: Deep periwinkle to slate gray/purple with lighter lavender-gray tips on the crown and back.
  - Facial disc: Concentric rings of fine, soft white and pale blue feathers radiating outward from the eyes.
- **Glasses:** 
  - Thin, round wire-rimmed spectacles in a muted bronze/copper finish.
  - Situated on the side of the head, resting over the prominent facial disc.
- **Eyes:** Large, yellow-amber iris partially visible behind the glasses.

### 2.2 Torso & Clothing
- **Garment:** An oversized, long-sleeved pullover hoodie.
- **Color:** Soft, muted teal / turquoise-green with a matte, fabric finish.
- **Texture:** Fine loop-wheel cotton fleece texture (visible ribbing knit on the cuffs and bottom hem).
- **Details:** 
  - A structured hood resting flat against the upper back with a visible thickness.
  - Dropped shoulder seams conforming to the owl’s rounded posture.
  - The hem sits neatly just above the lower tail feathers.

### 2.3 Lower Body & Limbs
- **Wings/Arms:** Folded neatly at the sides, emerging from the sleeves, ending in primary feathers that resemble fingers/claws.
- **Tail:** A fan of stiff, layered purple-gray feathers protruding from beneath the hoodie hem.
- **Legs & Feet:** 
  - Short, scaly avian legs in a warm brownish-gray tone.
  - Sharp black talons gripping the white ground plane in a natural, stable stance.

---

## 3. Environment & Lighting

- **Background:** Pure neutral white (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle soft contact shadow beneath the feet to ground the character.
- **Lighting Setup:** 
  - **Key Light:** Soft, diffused light coming from the front-left, highlighting the texture of the feathers and the fabric of the hoodie.
  - **Rim/Back Light:** Subtle edge lighting defining the silhouette against the white background.
- **Depth of Field:** Slight falloff toward the back tail feathers to create subtle focal depth.
