# Design Specification: M Music Landing Page (Clone)

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport
- **Screen Architecture:** Full-screen responsive viewport (100vw × 100vh), designed for desktop/tablet screens.
- **Background Color:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) spanning the entire viewport.
- **Design Philosophy:** Minimalist, high-contrast, editorial layout combining large typography, bold grid alignment, and structural black offset headers.

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary / Dominant:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Text / Structural Black:** Deep Black / Charcoal (`#111111`)
- **Muted Elements:** Medium Gray (`#777777`)
- **Accent Color (Dots):** Muted Gold / Mustard (`#D4AF37` / `#C5A059`)

---

## 3. Typography & Fonts
### Font Family Strategy
- **Primary Heading Font:** High-contrast serif display font with sharp, traditional terminals (e.g., *Bodoni Moda*, *Playfair Display*, or *Didot*).
- **Body / UI Font:** Clean geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*, or *Montserrat*).
- **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Bodoni Moda', 'Playfair Display', Didot, serif;` for headers; `font-family: 'Inter', sans-serif;` for body text.

### Text Specifications
- **Logo ("M" / "MUSIC"):** 
  - "M": ~24px serif, bold.
  - "MUSIC": ~8px sans-serif, letter-spaced (tracking: `3px`).
- **Top Navigation ("Brands.", "Models."):** 
  - ~12px sans-serif, uppercase/sentence-case mix, white text over black background blocks.
- **Main Hero Typography ("M U S I C"):**
  - Massive editorial serif heading, ultra-tight tracking, partially masked or layered behind the foreground acoustic guitar image.
  - Size: Responsive, approx. `12vw` to `16vw`.
- **Price Block ("$300 USD"):**
  - "$300": ~36px bold serif/sans hybrid.
  - "USD": ~10px sans-serif uppercase tracking.
- **Product Subtitle ("Acoustic"):**
  - ~14px bold sans-serif.
- **Placeholder Description ("Moecenas ac purus..."):**
  - ~11px regular sans-serif, line-height 1.4, muted gray (`#555555`).
- **Call to Action ("BUY NOW"):**
  - ~10px bold uppercase sans-serif with a solid underline tracking (`2px`).
- **Footer Socials ("INS FB LIN"):**
  - ~10px sans-serif, uppercase, tracking `2px`.
- **Copyright Footer ("© 2018 MUSIC"):**
  - Rotated 90 degrees vertically, placed on the far right edge, ~9px sans-serif.

---

## 4. Header & Navigation Structure
- **Top-Left Logo Area:**
  - Placed at absolute coordinates (approx. `top: 40px`, `left: 60px`).
  - Stacked "M" logo with "MUSIC" text below it.
- **Top-Right Navigation Box:**
  - A solid black rectangular block (`background-color: #111111`) pinned to the top-right header area.
  - Contains two navigation links: **"Brands."** and **"Models."** in white text.
- **Hamburger Menu Icon:**
  - Positioned to the far right of the black header block (3 horizontal lines).

---

## 5. Central Hero Section
- **Typography Masking Effect:**
  - The word **"MUSIC"** is rendered in huge scale across the middle of the screen.
  - The letters **"S"** and **"I"** and parts of **"C"** sit behind the guitar neck/body, creating a layered depth effect.
- **Image Integration (Acoustic Guitar):**
  - **Description:** A high-contrast monochrome (black and white) photograph of an acoustic guitar shot vertically. It features the headstock with tuning pegs near the top center, a long fretted neck cutting downward through the letter "S" and "I", and the upper portion of the guitar body with the soundhole resting near the bottom center.
  - **Asset Properties:** Backgroundless (isolated on white or masked out).
  - **Placement:** Anchored vertically down the center-right of the composition. No rotation; perfectly straight vertical alignment.

---

## 6. Left Sidebar / Product Details Block
- **Vertical Divider Line:** A thin 1px vertical rule separating the price from the description block.
- **Price Component:** Displays `$300` stacked above `USD` in clean editorial layout.
- **Product Information:**
  - Title: `Acoustic`
  - Body copy placeholder text.
  - Link: `BUY NOW` with a fine bottom border.
- **Social Links:** `INS FB LIN` positioned horizontally at the lower-left margin.

---

## 7. Right-Side UI Controls
- **Pagination / Carousel Dots:**
  - Vertical stack of three circular indicator dots positioned on the middle-right edge of the screen.
  - Top dot: Black (active).
  - Middle dot: Gold/Mustard accent.
  - Bottom dot: Muted gray/inactive.
- **Vertical Copyright Text:**
  - Rotated vertically (`transform: rotate(90deg)`) running along the right edge: `© 2018 MUSIC`.

---

## 8. Responsive & Interactive Behavior
- **Hover States:**
  - Navigation items ("Brands.", "Models.") slightly dim or shift opacity on hover.
  - "BUY NOW" underline expands or darkens on hover.
- **Scalability:** 
  - The central "MUSIC" typography scales fluidly using CSS `clamp()`.
  - The guitar image scales proportionally while maintaining its vertical anchor point.
