# UI Design System & Component Specification

## 1. Global Styles & Foundations

### 1.1 Color Palette
- **Backgrounds:**
  - App Background / Outer Frame: `#2c2c2f` (Dark Charcoal)
  - Main Window Background: `#ffffff` (Pure White)
  - Sidebar Background: `#3a393e` (Muted Dark Gray)
  - Secondary Card / Container Background: `#f4f4f6` (Light Gray)
- **Interactive & Accent Colors:**
  - Active Nav / Highlight / Play Button Yellow: `#d3ea48` (Neon Chartreuse/Lime)
  - Banner Primary Color Gradient: `#421d05` (Deep Bronze/Brown) transitioning to `#c74219` (Burnt Orange) and `#cf6112` (Vibrant Orange)
- **Text Colors:**
  - Primary Headers & Titles: `#1a1a1a` (Near Black)
  - Secondary Text & Metadata: `#75757a` (Medium Gray)
  - Sidebar Active State Text: `#1a1a1a`
  - Sidebar Inactive State Text: `#b3b3b8` (Light Grayish-White)
- **Borders & Dividers:**
  - Subtle Separator Lines: `#e5e5ea` (Light Divider Gray)

### 1.2 Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- **Fallback Stack:** System UI Sans-Serif
- **Type Scale:**
  - **Window Title / Top Navigation:** 14px, Medium (500)
  - **Sidebar Section Headings:** 11px, Bold (700), Uppercase
  - **Sidebar Item Links:** 13px, Regular (400) / Medium (500) for active
  - **Main Section Headings ("Popular artists", "Recently played"):** 16px, Bold (700)
  - **Banner Title ("BLINDING LIGHT"):** 28px, Extra Bold (800), Uppercase, Letter-spacing: tight
  - **Banner Subtext / Description:** 12px, Regular (400)
  - **Card Title / Track Names:** 13px, Semi-Bold (600)
  - **Artist Names / Metadata:** 11px, Regular (400) to Medium (500)

---

## 2. Layout & Structure

- **View Mode:** Full-screen responsive viewport (100vw × 100vh) matching the internal layout architecture of the reference UI.
- **Grid Layout:** 
  - **Left Sidebar:** Fixed width (~220px), running vertically down the left side.
  - **Main Content Area:** Flexible grid occupying the remaining horizontal space, divided into a primary left content panel and a secondary right-side "Now Playing" sidebar panel.
  - **Bottom Floating Player Bar:** Fixed at the bottom center of the viewport, spanning horizontally with rounded corners.

---

  - **Utility Controls:** Shuffle/Random icon, Loop/Repeat icon, and Volume control speaker icon.
