# Design Specification: Volterra S9 Landing Page

This document provides exact, comprehensive design and development guidelines to build a pixel-perfect, clean, high-end electric vehicle landing page based on the Volterra S9 concept design.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

### Colors
*   **Backgrounds**:
    *   Page Main Canvas: Light gray-white (`#F5F5F7`)
    *   Hero Content/Card Wrapper: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a very soft radial glow or gradient.
    *   Subtle Dividers & Borders: Thin light gray (`#E5E5E7` / `rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`)
*   **Typography**:
    *   Primary Headings & Bold Text: Deep obsidian black (`#0E0E0E`)
    *   Body Text & Secondary Info: Cool charcoal (`#515154`)
    *   Muted Labels / Subtexts: Slate gray (`#86868B`)
*   **Accents**:
    *   Interactive State Hover: Pure Black (`#000000`)
    *   Indicator Lights: Soft neon cyan/blue (`#00E5FF`) on chargers

### Typography & Fonts
*   **Primary Font**: `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` as the primary sans-serif web font.
*   **Fallback Font stack**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
*   **Font Weights**:
    *   Heavy Headings: `800` (Extra Bold) or `700` (Bold)
    *   Subtitles & Medium UI: `500` (Medium)
    *   Body text: `400` (Regular)

### Layout Grid & Spacing
*   **Page Container**: `max-width: 1440px` centered.
*   **Horizontal Margins**: `80px` on desktop, scaling down dynamically for tablet / mobile devices.
*   **Default Card Roundedness**: `border-radius: 24px` for large content panels, `12px` for small list items.

## 2. Header / Navigation Bar

A slim, clean, single-row navigation bar pinned to the top.

*   **Layout**: Flexbox container (`justify-content: space-between`, `align-items: center`).
*   **Height**: `80px`.
*   **Left Section**:
    *   Dropdown Button: A small solid black circle (`width: 36px, height: 36px`) containing a white chevron pointing downwards.
    *   Navigation Links: Flex row with `gap: 32px`. Links: `Vehicles`, `Technology`, `Ownership`, `Support`. Font-size: `14px`, medium weight, color: `#515154`.
*   **Center Section**:
    *   Logo: Text `"VOLTERRA"`. Uppercase, tracking (letter-spacing) set to `0.4em`. Bold, font-size: `16px`.
*   **Right Section**:
    *   Search Icon: A clean outline magnifying glass icon.
    *   CTA Button: `"Book a test drive →"`. Black capsule shape (`border-radius: 20px`), white text, small scale horizontal padding (`16px` px, `10px` py), font-size: `13px`.

## 3. Hero Section (Electric Performance)

This is a premium, high-impact section containing the main tagline, product hero render, and performance specs.

### Background Structure
The background is split diagonally or features a soft angled curve transitioning from the top-left pure white background down to a light silver-gray background at the bottom right.

### Text & Call-To-Action (Left Column)
*   **Main Tagline**: 
    ```text
    Electric
    performance,
    made simple.
    ```
    *   Font Size: `64px` (or dynamic `4vw`), extra bold, line-height `1.05`, deep obsidian black.
*   **Product Subtitle**:
    `The Volterra S9 combines exhilarating performance with everyday ease.`
    *   Font Size: `16px`, regular weight, color: `#515154`. Spacing: `24px` top margin.
*   **Buttons (Inline Stack)**:
    *   *Primary CTA*: `"Book a test drive →"` - Pill-shaped button, black background, white text. Font-weight: bold.
    *   *Secondary Link*: `"Explore S9 →"` - Text link in deep black, thin underline or clean arrow, font-weight: regular, font-size: `14px`.

### Hero Image (Right Column)
*   **Placement**: Positioned to overlap the transition background, filling the right side of the screen.
*   **Subject**: **The Volterra S9 Coupe**
    *   *Angle*: Low, wide front-three-quarter perspective facing slightly left.
    *   *Color*: Brilliant metallic white with a glossy finish.
    *   *Key visual elements*:
        *   Slim, futuristic horizontal LED headlamps.
        *   Deep lower black splitter/aerodynamic intake vents.
        *   Clean bonnet with a subtle integrated minimalist wing logo centered.
        *   Panoramic black glass canopy/roofline.
        *   Sleek dark alloy wheels with bright, high-contrast red brake calipers visible behind the spokes.
        *   Natural soft studio shadow directly underneath the car body, reflecting softly onto the light gray floor plane.

### Key Performance Specs (Bottom Bar)
A clean, horizontal row containing 3 key specification points separated by light vertical divider lines.

*   **Grid Style**: 3 equal columns, height ~80px.
*   **Spec 1: Acceleration**:
    *   Icon: Speedometer outline.
    *   Label: `0 - 100 km/h` (Muted, uppercase, `11px`).
    *   Value: `3.1 s` (Bold, `28px`).
*   **Spec 2: Range**:
    *   Icon: Battery vertical gauge with level bars.
    *   Label: `Estimated range` (Muted, uppercase, `11px`).
    *   Value: `600 km` (Bold, `28px`).
*   **Spec 3: Power**:
    *   Icon: Stylized electric lightning bolt.
    *   Label: `Max power` (Muted, uppercase, `11px`).
    *   Value: `520 kW` (Bold, `28px`).
