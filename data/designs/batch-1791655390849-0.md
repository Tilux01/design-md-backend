# Comprehensive UI/UX Design Specification: E-Commerce Audio App ("With new sounds")

This specification provides a complete blueprint for recreating the presented e-commerce mobile application interface. It outlines three distinct application states/screens within a unified design system.

---

## 1. Global Design System & Foundation

### Color Palette
*   **Primary Background (Base):** Soft Peach/Pink (`#F4DFD8` to `#F6E3DC`)
*   **Surface Containers / Cards:** Light Dusty Rose/Tint (`#F1CDC2` or translucent white/pink overlays)
*   **Primary Text (Dark):** Deep Charcoal/Black (`#1A1A1A`)
*   **Secondary Text (Muted):** Medium Taupe/Gray (`#7D7572`)
*   **Accent/Interactive Elements (Dark):** Pitch Black (`#111111`) for primary active states and checkout buttons.
*   **Color Swatches (Product Variants - Screen 3):**
    *   Dark Blue/Navy (`#1B365D`)
    *   Mint Green (`#A8DADC`)
    *   White/Off-White (`#F1F1F1`)
    *   Coral/Orange-Red (`#E0533C`)

### Typography
*   **Font Family Stack:** 
    *   *Primary:* **SF Pro Display** / **Inter** / **Helvetica Neue** (Modern sans-serif, clean geometry).
    *   *Fallback:* `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
*   **Type Scale:**
    *   **Screen Titles (Hero):** 36px–42px, Bold (Weight: 700), Tight Line Height (1.0–1.1).
    *   **Section Headers:** 18px–20px, Semi-Bold (Weight: 600).
    *   **Body Text:** 13px–14px, Regular (Weight: 400).
    *   **Labels / Buttons:** 11px–12px, Medium (Weight: 500).

---

## 2. Screen-by-Screen Layout & Components

---

### SCREEN 1: Onboarding / Splash / Hero Screen ("With new sounds")

#### Layout Structure
*   **Full-bleed container** with a warm peach gradient background.
*   **Top Bar:**
    *   Pill-shaped badge floating at the top left (`20 new arrivals` with a small flame/fire icon).
    *   Pill style: White/Translucent background, dark text, rounded edges (border-radius: 20px).
*   **Hero Visuals:**
    *   Large, immersive image of rose-gold over-ear headphones floating diagonally across the screen.
    *   Play button indicator: A circular glassmorphism play button with concentric ripple rings placed centrally in the upper right of the image container.
*   **Typography Block (Bottom Left):**
    *   Subtitle: *"old tracks •"* in small uppercase/sentence-case text.
    *   Main Headline: *"With new sounds"* in massive, bold, stacked white typography with high contrast against the headphones.

#### Image Assets (Screen 1)
*   **Hero Product Image:** 
    *   *Description:* High-resolution product shot of over-ear headphones in a metallic rose-gold/pink finish, featuring mesh ear cushions.
    *   *Background State:* Backgroundless (transparent PNG/WebP).
    *   *Placement & Transformation:* Positioned centrally/diagonally. Rotated approximately -25 to -30 degrees, extending from the upper-right down to the bottom-left edge.

---

### SCREEN 2: Home / Catalog Dashboard ("New arrivals")

#### Layout Structure
*   **Header Section:**
    *   Search Bar: Rounded white pill input box spanning the top width. Features a magnifying glass icon on the left and placeholder text `"search"`.
    *   User Avatar: Circular profile picture located on the top right of the search bar row.
*   **Main Title Section:**
    *   Header: *"New arrivals"* (Large, bold, 2-line layout).
    *   Action/Filter Icon: Three-line horizontal filter icon aligned to the right of the title.
*   **Category Filter Pills:**
    *   Horizontal scrollable row of filter chips.
    *   Active Pill: Black background with white text (`All`).
    *   Inactive Pills: Transparent background with dark borders/text (`Headphones`, `Speakers`).
*   **Product Grid (2x2 Layout):**
    *   Rounded rectangular cards (light peach background, subtle soft shadows).
    *   **Card 1 (Airpods Pro):** Image of white wireless earbuds in charging case, label *"Airpods Pro"*, price `"$499.00"`.
    *   **Card 2 (Speakers):** Image of a dark spherical smart speaker, label *"Speakers"*, price `"$359.00"`.
    *   **Card 3 (Headphones):** Image of rose-gold over-ear headphones, label *"Headphones"*, price `"$650.00"`.
    *   **Card 4 (Earphones):** Image of wired/wireless earbuds, label *"Earphones"*, price `"$60.00"`.
*   **Floating Navigation / Cart Bar:**
    *   A floating dark pill-shaped bottom bar (`$1080.00` with a shopping bag icon and a white notification dot) overlaid at the bottom center.

#### Image Assets (Screen 2)
*   **Grid Product Images:**
    *   *Airpods Pro:* White charging case with open lid and earbuds inside. Backgroundless. Centered in card.
    *   *Speakers:* Spherical dark blue mesh smart speaker. Backgroundless. Centered in card.
    *   *Headphones:* Rose-gold over-ear headphones viewed from the front. Backgroundless. Centered in card.
    *   *Earphones:* White wired/wireless earbuds laid out flat. Backgroundless. Centered in card.

---

### SCREEN 3: Product Detail View ("Apple Airpods")

#### Layout Structure
*   **Top Navigation:**
    *   Minimalist status/action dots on the top-left and a menu/hamburger icon on the top-right.
*   **Product Display Showcase:**
    *   Dual product visual arrangement: Front view of the rose-gold over-ear headphones alongside a side-profile view of the right ear cup.
*   **Color Variant Selector:**
    *   Row of four circular color swatch buttons housed inside a light rounded container.
    *   Active indicator: A thin dark outer border ring surrounding the selected blue swatch.
*   **Product Information Card (Bottom Sheet Style):**
    *   White/Cream rounded container filling the lower portion of the screen.
    *   **Product Title:** *"Apple Airpods"* (Bold, 24px).
    *   **Description:** *"A mesh textile wraps the ear cushions to provide pillow-like softness"* (Muted gray text, 13px).
    *   **Footer Action Bar:**
        *   Price: `"$499.00"` (Large, bold).
        *   Primary Button: Dark pill-shaped button reading `"Add to cart"` with a shopping bag icon on the right.

#### Image Assets (Screen 3)
*   **Main Product Showcase:**
    *   *Image 1 (Left):* Over-ear headphones facing forward, metallic rose-gold headband and ear cups. Backgroundless.
    *   *Image 2 (Right):* Standalone right ear cup rotated to show the side profile and headband attachment mechanism. Backgroundless.
    *   *Placement:* Positioned in the upper half of the screen against the peach backdrop with soft drop shadows creating depth.
