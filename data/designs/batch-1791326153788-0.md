# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Futuristic Clean-Tech / Dark Mode Glassmorphism & IoT SaaS.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High visual breathing room with modular card-based containers, balanced generous inner card padding, and distinct high-contrast status markers.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered dark charcoal-to-black surfaces (`#0E1113`, `#14171A`, `#1C2126`) featuring subtle 1px border highlights, neon radial green glows (`#2AF598` / `#00F2FE`), and deep ambient shadows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges with neon status indicator dots, concentric SVG circular meters, frosted glassmorphism overlays, and deep charcoal ambient shading.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(42, 245, 152, 0.1)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(42, 245, 152, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-[#2AF598] inline-block shadow-[0_0_8px_#2AF598]"></span>`.
