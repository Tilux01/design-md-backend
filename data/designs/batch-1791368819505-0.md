# Design Specification: ShopMate E-commerce Landing Page

## Global Styles & Tokens

### Color Palette
- **Primary Color:** `#5B8C51` (Sage Green - used for CTA buttons, badges, highlights)
- **Primary Hover/Dark:** `#4A7342` (Darker Sage)
- **Background Main:** `#FAF8F5` (Warm Cream / Off-White)
- **Background White:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Text Primary (Dark):** `#111111`
- **Text Secondary (Muted):** `#555555` / `#777777`
- **Border/Divider Color:** `#E5E5E5`

### Typography
- **Font Family:** Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- **Scale:**
  - Hero Title: `48px` / `56px` line-height, Bold (`700`)
  - Section Headers: `24px` / `28px`, Semi-bold (`600`)
  - Body / Subtitles: `14px` / `16px`, Regular (`400`) / Medium (`500`)
  - Small / Captions: `12px`, Regular (`400`)

---

## Component Specifications

### 1. Hero Section
- **Layout:** Two-column split layout (Left: Text & CTA, Right: Product showcase graphic).
- **Background:** Soft gradient / solid warm cream (`#FAF8F5`).
- **Badge:** "NEW ARRIVALS" pill badge (Green text, light background, uppercase, 11px font).
- **Heading:** "Discover The Best Products for You"
- **Subtext:** "Explore our wide range of high-quality products at affordable prices. Shop now and enjoy the best deals!"
- **CTA Group:**
  - Primary Button: "Shop Now →" (Sage green background, white text, rounded corners).
  - Secondary Button: "Explore Deals" (White background, dark border, dark text).
- **Social Proof Micro-Element:** Circular overlapping avatar thumbnails + "Trusted by 10,000+ Happy Customers" text.
- **Hero Graphic Composition:**
  - Olive green backpack as central backdrop.
  - Beige over-ear headphones floating on left.
  - Potted succulent in white planter on right.
  - Floating on a soft white circular platform/pedestal.

---

### 2. Feature/Value Props Bar
- **Layout:** 4-column horizontal grid with icons and concise text.
- **Items:**
  1. **Free Shipping** — On orders over $50
  2. **Secure Payment** — 100% secure payment
  3. **Easy Returns** — 30 days return policy
  4. **24/7 Support** — Dedicated support
- **Styling:** Light grey/beige circular icon containers with minimalist outline icons, paired with stacked bold title and muted description text.

---

### 3. Shop by Categories
- **Header:** "Shop by Categories" (Left-aligned) with "View All Categories →" link (Right-aligned).
- **Layout:** 6-column circular category avatar grid.
- **Items:**
  1. Electronics (Headphones icon)
  2. Fashion (Green t-shirt icon)
  3. Home & Kitchen (Armchair icon)
  4. Beauty (Perfume bottle icon)
  5. Sports (Sneaker icon)
  6. Accessories (Watch icon)
- **Card Style:** Circular image preview with clean white background, subtle drop shadow, and centered category label underneath.

---

### 4. Best Selling Products
- **Header:** "Best Selling Products" with "View All Products →" link.
- **Layout:** 5-column product grid card layout.
- **Product Card Elements:**
  - White card container with subtle border.
  - Favorite/Wishlist heart icon (Top right).
  - Product Image thumbnail.
  - Product Title (Bold, 14px).
  - Rating Stars + Review Count e.g., `★ 4.5 (128)`.
  - Pricing (Current price in bold dark text, original crossed-out price in muted grey).
  - "Add to Cart" button (Sage green background, shopping cart icon + text, full width of card).
- **Featured Products in this section:**
  1. Smart Watch Series 5 — `$89.99` (was `$129.99`)
  2. Wireless Headphones — `$59.99` (was `$69.99`)
  3. Travel Backpack — `$39.99` (was `$59.99`)
  4. Running Shoes — `$49.99` (was `$79.99`)
  5. Luxury Perfume — `$29.99` (was `$49.99`)

---

### 5. Promotional Banner ("Up to 50% Off")
- **Layout:** Full-width split banner (Left: Promo copy, Right: Shopping bag & decor).
- **Background:** Soft gradient warm beige/cream.
- **Badge:** "Special Offer"
- **Heading:** "Up to 50% Off"
- **Description:** "Limited time offer on selected items. Hurry up and grab the best deals!"
- **CTA Button:** "Shop the Sale →"
- **Graphic Side:** Kraft paper shopping bag with "50% OFF" green sticker tag, accompanied by a potted plant and minimalist leaf shadows on the background wall.

---

### 6. Testimonials Section ("What Our Customers Say")
- **Header:** "What Our Customers Say" (Centered).
- **Layout:** 3-column testimonial card grid with left/right carousel pagination arrows.
- **Card Elements:**
  - White background container with subtle shadow.
  - Large quotation mark graphic (`“`).
  - Review feedback text.
  - Customer avatar (circular), Name (Bold), and 5-star rating (`★★★★★`).
- **Content Examples:**
  - *John D.:* "Amazing products and fast delivery! ShopMate is my go-to store for all my needs."
  - *Sarah M.:* "Great quality at affordable prices. The customer support is also very responsive."
  - *Michael T.:* "Very happy with my purchase. Highly recommend ShopMate to everyone!"

---

### 7. Footer
- **Layout:** 4-column layout + bottom branding.
- **Columns:**
  1. **Brand Column:** Logo ("ShopMate" with shopping bag icon) + Short brand value proposition.
  2. **Quick Links:** Home, Shop, Categories.
  3. **Customer Service:** Track Order, Returns & Refunds, Shipping Policy.
  4. **Newsletter Signup:**
     - Title: "Subscribe to our newsletter"
     - Subtitle: "Get the latest updates on new products and upcoming sales."
     - Input field + "Subscribe" button.
- **Divider & Bottom spacing:** Clean, ample padding with consistent typography hierarchy.
