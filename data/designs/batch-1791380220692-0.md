# Design Specification: PlayStation 5 DualSense "Cosmic Red" Promotional Poster

This document provides the exact technical and visual specifications to recreate the provided promotional poster as a pixel-perfect digital layout (HTML/CSS or canvas-based vector layout), optimized for full-screen display.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Properties

* **Dimensions:** Full-screen responsive (Designed at a vertical 4:5 aspect ratio, e.g., `1080px × 1350px`).
* **Background Type:** Two-tone split composition (Upper vertical gradient + Lower diagonal surface).
* **Upper Background Gradient:** 
  * Linear gradient from **Deep Crimson Red** (`#300002`) at the top to **Bright Crimson Red** (`#9c030d`) at the bottom.
* **Lower Surface ("Stage"):**
  * Solid vibrant red (`#c90614`) angled podium surface in the lower third, creating depth with a sharp drop shadow under the controller.
* **Lighting/Atmosphere:** Dramatic studio lighting with a strong overhead radial highlight directly behind the center controller and text.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

All text styling must match the high-end, editorial aesthetic of official PlayStation marketing materials.

### **Font Stack Recommendations:**
* **Primary Editorial Serif (Headings, Body Copy):** `Playfair Display`, `Didot`, or fallback `Georgia, serif`.
* **Primary Sans-Serif (Branding, Features):** `Montserrat`, `Helvetica Neue`, or fallback `Arial, sans-serif`.

---

### **Layer 1: Top Header Features (Micro-Text)**

* **Content:**
  * `DualSense` (Left)

  * **Letter Spacing:** Wide tracking (`1.5px`).
