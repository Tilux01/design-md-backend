# Design Specification: Modern Gaming Dashboard

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Orientation:** Full-screen landscape (16:9 aspect ratio recommended).
*   **Theme:** Dark Cinematic / Glassmorphism.
*   **Background:** A full-screen, high-resolution image with a heavy Gaussian blur (approx. 20px) and a dark semi-transparent overlay (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`) to ensure text readability.
    *   **Background Image Description:** A cinematic wide shot of a dusty, sunlit interior of a wooden barn or saloon (Western aesthetic). Features vertical wooden beams and a red-and-white striped flag hanging in the upper center background.
*   **Padding:** Global horizontal padding of `5%` and vertical padding of `4%`.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` (Sans-Serif).
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `Oxygen`, `Ubuntu`, `Cantarell`, `"Open Sans"`, `"Helvetica Neue"`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Styles:**
    *   **Heading (Game Title):** 36pt, Bold, White, Letter spacing: -0.02em.
    *   **Body Text (Description):** 14pt, Regular, Light Gray (`#CCCCCC`), Line height: 1.6.
    *   **Navigation/Labels:** 12pt, Medium, White, Uppercase/Sentence case.
    *   **Button Text:** 11pt, Bold, Uppercase.

## 3. Header Section
*   **Placement:** Top of the screen, spanning full width with global padding.
*   **User Profile (Top Left):**
    *   **Avatar:** Small circular image (approx. 32px) showing a stylized character headshot.
    *   **Username:** "rodrigodesign" in white, medium weight.
    *   **Status:** Small green dot next to "Online" in a smaller, lighter font.
*   **Navigation Menu (Top Center):**
    *   **Items:** Home, My Games, Store, Library, Settings.
    *   **Active State:** "My Games" is encapsulated in a pill-shaped, semi-transparent white capsule (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`) with a slight backdrop blur.
    *   **Inactive State:** White text with 60% opacity.
*   **Clock (Top Right):**
    *   **Text:** "9:41 AM" in white, medium weight.

## 4. Game Carousel (Center)
*   **Placement:** Vertically centered, spanning the width of the screen.
*   **Behavior:** A horizontal scrolling list of cards with a slight "focus" scale effect.
*   **Card Design:**
    *   **Dimensions:** Portrait aspect ratio (approx. 2:3).
    *   **Border Radius:** 12px.
    *   **Shadow:** Soft outer glow/drop shadow.
*   **Game Tiles (Left to Right):**
    1.  **The Last of Us Part II (Focused):** Darkened/Selected state. Image: Ellie playing guitar in a dark forest.
    2.  **Far Cry 5:** Image: A group of cultists sitting at a long table in a field (The Last Supper parody).
    3.  **Unidentified Game:** Dark, silhouette-heavy image of a character in a hallway.
    4.  **Red Dead Redemption 2:** Image: Arthur Morgan in a red-tinted frame holding a revolver.
    5.  **PUBG:** Image: Character in a white shirt and level 3 helmet walking away from an explosion.
    6.  **Valorant:** Image: The character Jett in a blue/purple stylized background with "VALORANT" text.
    7.  **Call of Duty: Warzone:** Image: Soldiers standing in front of a crashed plane with "WARZONE" text.

## 5. Game Details Section (Bottom Left)
*   **Placement:** Positioned below the carousel, aligned to the left margin.
*   **Content:**
    *   **Title:** "The Last of Us Part II" (Large, White).
    *   **Description:** A 3-4 line paragraph describing the game's narrative (Ellie's journey of revenge in a post-apocalyptic Seattle). Text is constrained to approx. 40% of screen width.
*   **Buttons:**
    *   **Primary (RESUME):** Rectangular with slight rounding (4px). Background: Light Gray/White. Text: Black.
    *   **Secondary (WATCH TRAILER):** Rectangular with slight rounding (4px). Background: Transparent with a 1px white border. Text: White.

## 6. Footer Navigation (Bottom Center)
*   **Placement:** Centered at the very bottom of the screen.
*   **Icons:** Three minimalist white icons arranged horizontally with 30px spacing.
    1.  **Home Icon:** A simple house outline.
    2.  **Tag Icon:** A price tag shape (representing Store).
    3.  **Grid Icon:** Four small squares (representing Library).
*   **Indicator:** A very small white dot/dash below the active icon (Home).

## 7. Image Asset Specifications
*   **Background:** `bg_saloon_blurred.jpg` - Full bleed, no transparency, blurred.
*   **Game Posters:** High-quality 600x900px vertical posters.
*   **Icons:** SVG format, white stroke, 2px thickness.
*   **Rotation:** All elements are 0-degree rotation (flat UI).
*   **Transparency:** Use `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)` on the navigation pill and buttons for a premium glass effect.
