# Design System & Technical Specification: HoneyDrop Landing Page

This document provides an exact design specification (`design.md`) to recreate the **HoneyDrop** hero landing page as a full-screen, edge-to-edge web design. 

---

## 1. Canvas & Global Page Layout

* **Viewport:** Full screen (`100vw` × `100vh`), no wrapper frame, border, or outer mockup background.
* **Base Background Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
* **Container Max Width:** `1280px` (centered horizontally with `margin: 0 auto; padding: 0 40px`).
* **Grid Layout:** 2-column hero split (`50%` left content area, `50%` right visual display area).

---

## 2. Color Palette

### Primary & Accent Colors
* **Brand Orange (Primary/CTA):** `#FF641A` (Vibrant warm orange)
* **Brand Orange Gradient (CTA Hover/Fill):** `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF7A00 0%, #E04E00 100%)`
* **Headline Orange ("Natural"):** `#EE5D26`
* **Headline Multi-color Gradient ("Honey"):** 
  * Linear Gradient angle: `90deg` (Left to Right)
  * Color stops: `#3B82F6` (0% - Bright Cyan/Blue) → `#EC4899` (50% - Vivid Pink) → `#A855F7` (100% - Soft Purple)

### Neutral Colors
* **Primary Text (Dark Charcoal):** `#23262F`
* **Body / Muted Text:** `#777E90`
* **Nav Links Hover:** `#14171A`
* **Background Circle Glow:** `#FFE3C2` (Soft warm peach/yellow with `opacity: 0.8`)

### UI Accent Colors
* **Profile Avatar Icon Background:** `#FCD34D` (Warm bright yellow)
* **Scroll Mouse Icon Outline:** `#A0AEC0`

* **Font Family:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Poppins` or `Outfit` (Modern, rounded geometric sans-serif).

font-family: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', 'Poppins', 'Outfit', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;

## 4. Header / Navigation Bar

### Structural Layout
* **Height:** `80px`
* **Flexbox Configuration:** `display: flex; align-items: center; justify-content: space-between;`
* **Padding:** `0 40px`

### Left: Brand Logo Area
* **Icon:** Stylized bee/honeycomb mark. Two rounded orange drops forming bee wings with a dark brown body outline (`#EE5D26` and `#23262F`). Size: `28px × 28px`.
* **Brand Name:** "HoneyDrop" (Sans-serif, bold, `20px`, dark charcoal `#23262F`). Spacing: `8px` gap between icon and text.

### Center: Navigation Links
* **Links:** `Shop`, `Categories`, `FAQ's`, `About`, `Contact`
* **Spacing:** `32px` gap between links.
* **Text Style:** `14px`, medium weight, `#777E90`. First letter capitalized, "FAQ's" with apostrophe.

### Right: Utility Icons & Profile
* **Spacing:** `20px` gap between items.
* **Search Icon:** Outline magnifying glass (`20px`, dark grey stroke).
* **Cart Icon:** Outline shopping bag (`20px`, dark grey stroke).
* **Profile Avatar:** Circular container (`36px × 36px`), filled with yellow (`#FCD34D`), containing a stylized 3D/vector 2D character avatar head.

## 5. Hero Content (Left Section)

### Alignment & Layout
* **Padding Top:** `80px`
* **Width:** `460px` max-width.
* **Flex Layout:** Vertical stack (`flex-direction: column; align-items: flex-start;`).

### Components Breakdown
1. **Headline:**
   * Line 1: `Natural` (Orange `#EE5D26`, size `52px`, font-weight `800`).
   * Line 2: `Honey` (Text fill gradient `linear-gradient(90deg, #3B82F6, #EC4899, #A855F7)`, size `88px`, font-weight `900`).
2. **Body Text:**
   * Text string: `"HoneyDrop honey is produced in kerala by bees that pollinate naturally"`
   * Note: "kerala" is explicitly lowercase as per original composition.
   * Margin Top: `24px`.
   * Max Width: `380px`.
3. **Primary CTA Button ("Shop now"):**
   * Text: "Shop now" (White `#FFFFFF`, `14px`, SemiBold).
   * Background: Solid orange `#FF641A` with subtle gradient and smooth box-shadow (`0px 10px 20px rgba(255, 100, 26, 0.25)`).
   * Dimensions: Padding `14px 32px`.
   * Border Radius: `8px` (Slightly rounded rectangle).
   * Margin Top: `32px`.

## 6. Hero Graphics & Visual Assets (Right Section)

### Background Glow Element
* **Description:** A large soft warm yellow-orange circular shape positioned behind the jar graphics to create depth.
* **Dimensions:** `480px × 480px` circular element (`border-radius: 50%`).
* **Color:** Soft pastel warm amber (`#FFE3C2`).
* **Placement:** Centered behind the 3D Honey Jar asset.

### Image Asset 1: Main 3D Honey Jar & Scene (Backgroundless PNG)
* **Format Requirement:** Transparent PNG (no background).
* **Positioning:** Placed centrally over the soft background circle, overlapping slightly into the lower center area.
* **Rotation:** Tilted approximately **30° to 35° clockwise**.
* **Visual Description & Details for Asset Rendering/Selection:**
  * **Jar Container:** Clear glass jar rendered in high-gloss 3D.
  * **Jar Lid:** Ice-blue/light turquoise metallic frosted cap at the top left opening.
  * **Blue Seal Tag:** A vibrant royal blue (`#2A70E0`) brand security strip extending from the lid down the right side of the jar.
  * **Jar Label/Lower Half:** Bright vibrant magenta/pink (`#EC1C82`) glossy wrapper with subtle white fluid line graphics wrapping around the lower body.
  * **Contents:** Rich, glossy golden-amber organic honey visible inside the glass.

### Image Asset 2: Floating 3D Dipper Spoon
* **Format Requirement:** Integrated inside the backgroundless transparent PNG or floating absolute layer.
* **Position & Angle:** Top-left relative to the jar lid, angled downwards.
* **Visual Description:** A wooden/amber dipper dripping thick, glossy, dark golden honey downward into the open top of the jar.

### Image Asset 3: 3D Bee Character
* **Format Requirement:** Backgroundless transparent element.
* **Placement:** Positioned at the bottom-left base of the tilted jar (overlapping the jar's lower left corner).
* **Visual Description:** Cute cartoon 3D honey bee facing left. Black and yellow striped body, large white glossy eyes, tiny translucent wings, black antennae.

## 7. Floating Decorative & UI Elements

### 1. Dark 3D Floating Hexagon
* **Type:** Decorative geometry icon.
* **Color:** Dark charcoal / matte black (`#23262F`).
* **Placement:** Floating in the lower-middle gap between the text block and the jar graphic (approx. `x: 45%`, `y: 68%` from top left of page).
* **Size:** `24px × 24px`.

### 2. Scroll Indicator Icon
* **Type:** Mouse scroll indicator.
* **Placement:** Bottom-center of the full page layout (`bottom: 30px`, horizontally centered).
* **Visual Description:** Thin, rounded pill outline representing a computer mouse (`20px × 32px`, border `2px solid #A0AEC0`, radius `12px`), containing a small vertical scroll wheel tick mark (`2px × 6px`, `#A0AEC0`) centered inside at the top.
