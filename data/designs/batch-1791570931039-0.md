# Design Specification: Stodio Agency Landing Page

This document provides a comprehensive design specification for recreating the Stodio agency landing page. It focuses on layout, typography, color palette, imagery, and component structures to achieve a pixel-perfect, full-screen clone.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

### Color Palette
*   **Primary Dark (Backgrounds & Text):** `#0D0D0D` (Deep Charcoal/Black)
*   **Primary Light (Backgrounds):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
*   **Secondary Text (Muted):** `#7A7A7A` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` on dark sections; `#888888` on light sections.
*   **Accent Red/Coral (Brand Highlights):** `#FF3B30` or `#E63946` (Used for tags like "Next-Gen Design Agency", "Who we are", bullet points, buttons).
*   **Border/Divider Grays:** `#E0E0E0` (Light mode dividers), `rgba(255,255,255,0.15)` (Dark mode borders).

### Typography Stack
If exact proprietary web fonts are unavailable, use the following fallback stack:
*   **Primary Heading Font (Sans-Serif, Bold, Geometric):** 
    *   *Primary:* `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, or `Space Grotesk`
    *   *Fallback:* `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`
*   **Body Font:** 
    *   *Primary:* `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `-apple-system`
    *   *Fallback:* `sans-serif`

## 2. Section-by-Section Breakdown

### Section 1: Hero Section (Dark Theme)

*   **Background:** Full-width dark background (`#0D0D0D`) with a high-contrast, moody photographic overlay on the right side (blending into darkness on the left).
*   **Navigation Bar:**
    *   **Logo:** Left-aligned text "Stodio®" with a distinctive red/coral dot or icon.
    *   **Nav Links:** Center/Left-aligned links: `Home`, `Studio`, `Work` (with a small red badge reading "25"), `Careers`, `Blog`, `Contact`.
    *   **CTA Button:** Right-aligned pill button reading "Book A Intro ↗" with a circular avatar thumbnail inside the button on the left.
*   **Hero Content:**
    *   **Small Tag:** Red square bullet followed by uppercase text: `+ Next-Gen Design Agency`.
    *   **Main Headline:** Large, bold, multi-line typography: 
        > Next-Gen Design Agency for Growing Brands.
    *   **Bottom Left Subtext:** Inline list items: `+ Define`, `+ Design`, `+ Development`.
    *   **Bottom Right Subtext:** Description of services: `Branding`, `Mobile & Web App Design for Startups and Giants`.
    *   **Action Buttons:** Two pill-shaped buttons: 
        1. Red filled button: `View Projects ↗`
        2. White outlined button: `Reach Out ↗`

---

### Section 2: Social Proof / Client Logos (White Theme)

*   **Background:** Clean white background (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Layout:** Single horizontal row featuring a descriptive label on the left and minimalist monochrome brand logos stretching across.
*   **Content:**
    *   **Label (Left):** Small uppercase text: `★ WE'VE DONE 500+ ENTERPRISE AND BUSINESS CONSULTING.`
    *   **Logos (Right to Left):** Simplified, grayscale versions of recognizable partner brands (e.g., Red Bull, JD, XBOX, HubSpot).

---

### Section 3: "Who We Are" Section (White Theme)

*   **Background:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Layout:** Asymmetric editorial layout with a strong bold statement on the left and an interactive-feeling arrow link on the right.
*   **Content:**
    *   **Tag:** Red square bullet followed by `Who we are`.
    *   **Headline:** Large impact text spanning multiple lines, transitioning from bold black to muted gray:
        > We build search-first digital systems to help category **leaders** lead their industries.
    *   **Action Link:** Right-aligned text with an arrow: `→ ABOUT THE STUDIO`.

---

### Section 4: Portrait Image Gallery

*   **Layout:** A horizontal grid of 4 distinct portrait photographs placed side-by-side, stretching across the page width.
*   **Images Description:**
    1.  **Card 1:** A portrait of a man with facial hair, dramatically lit with bold red/magenta ambient lighting on the left side of his face against a dark background.
    2.  **Card 2:** A close-up portrait of a woman with red hair looking directly at the camera, featuring a subtle horizontal motion-blur/shutter-drag effect across her face.
    3.  **Card 3:** A portrait of a bald man wearing a mustard-yellow button-down shirt, captured with a stylistic motion-blur effect against a neutral studio backdrop.
    4.  **Card 4:** A vibrant, warm-toned artistic photograph featuring warm golden and deep red lighting casting abstract human silhouettes and motion.

---

### Section 5: "By The Numbers" Statistics Section

*   **Background:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Layout:** 4-column statistical grid separated by subtle dotted horizontal divider lines.
*   **Columns:**
    1.  **Stat:** `1%`
        *   **Title:** Expert-Vetted
        *   **Description:** Recognized in the top 1% of freelancers for consistent quality, trust, and expertise.
    2.  **Stat:** `30+`
        *   **Title:** Clients served
        *   **Description:** From startups to giants - each treated like our only one.
    3.  **Stat:** `100%`
        *   **Title:** Success Score
        *   **Description:** All 5-star reviews. No compromises. No "just okay."
    4.  **Stat:** `8+`
        *   **Title:** Years of expertise
        *   **Description:** Deep experience in UX, branding, and growth-driven design for real-world products.

---

### Section 6: "Services" Section Transition

*   **Background:** Transitions back to deep dark mode (`#0D0D0D`).
*   **Header:**
    *   **Tag:** Red square bullet + `Services`.
    *   **Main Title:** `Our [Icon/Badge] Services` (featuring a colorful abstract 3D graphic or badge inline).
    *   **Description (Right aligned):** `We craft high-impact digital experiences through strategic design, seamless coding, and creative thinking.`
