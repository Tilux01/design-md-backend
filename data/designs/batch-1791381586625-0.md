# Comprehensive Design Specification: Mary's Chrome Hero Section

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Strategy
* **Viewport:** Full-screen immersive layout (`width: 100vw`, `height: 100vh`, `overflow: hidden`).
* **Canvas Background:** Solid soft pastel blush pink (`#FCECEB` or `rgb(252, 236, 235)`).
* **Centering Container:** A responsive flex/grid container centering the primary red card with generous surrounding padding.

---

## 2. Typography System & Fallbacks
The interface simulates Google-style modern geometric sans-serif typography (reminiscent of *Product Sans* or *Google Sans*).

* **Primary Font Family:** `'Google Sans', 'Product Sans', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
* **Text Styles & Hierarchy:**
  * **"Mary's" / "Chrome" Header Text:**
    * **Font Size:** Fluid scaling, desktop target ~`64px` (`4rem`).
    * **Font Weight:** Bold (`700`).
    * **Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
    * **Letter Spacing:** Tight, `-0.02em`.
  * **Card Captions ("Crochet lessons"):**
    * **Font Size:** `14px` (`0.875rem`).
    * **Font Weight:** Medium (`500`).
    * **Color:** Dark Slate/Charcoal (`#202124`).
  * **Domain Labels ("diy.com", "youtube.com"):**
    * **Font Size:** `11px` (`0.6875rem`).
    * **Font Weight:** Regular (`400`).
    * **Color:** Muted Grey (`#5F6368`).

---

## 3. Component Breakdown & Spatial Architecture

### A. Main Red Container Banner
* **Dimensions:** Max-width ~`920px`, aspect ratio-driven or fixed height (~`480px`).
* **Background:** Vibrant Google Red (`#EA4335` or `#E53935`).
* **Border Radius:** Highly rounded corners (`40px` to `48px`).
* **Drop Shadow:** Soft, multi-layered diffuse shadow for elevation (`0px 20px 40px rgba(234, 67, 53, 0.25)`).
* **Internal Layout:** Split-screen or absolute-positioned collage layout. The text `"Mary's"` sits on the left side, `"Chrome"` sits on the right side, bridged centrally by the profile avatar.

### B. Central Profile Avatar (Hero Focal Point)
* **Shape:** Perfect circle (`border-radius: 50%`).
* **Size:** ~`180px` to `200px` diameter.
* **Border:** Thick solid white border (`4px` to `6px` solid `#FFFFFF`) acting as a separator from the red backdrop.
* **Positioning:** Absolute centering horizontally within the red container, slightly offset vertically to overlap the top/middle edge.
* **Content:** High-resolution portrait of an older woman with short silver/grey hair, wearing a patterned collared shirt under a brown cardigan.

### C. Floating Collage Elements (Cards & Assets)
All floating elements use subtle drop shadows (`0px 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`) and rounded corners (`16px` to `24px`) to mimic modern card UI (Pinterest/Chrome UI style).

1. **Top-Left Crochet Tutorial Card:**
   * **Dimensions:** ~`160px` width, portrait orientation.
   * **Content:** Image of hands crocheting white yarn with a gold crochet hook.
   * **Footer:** White background card containing text `"Crochet lessons"`, `"diy.com"`, and a small red `"Visit"` pill button.
   * **Rotation:** Slight counter-clockwise tilt (~`-4deg`).

2. **Top-Right Yellow Knit Sweater:**
   * **Asset:** Cutout/floating image of a textured, geometric cable-knit yellow sweater.
   * **Positioning:** Overlapping the top-right corner of the red banner.
   * **Rotation:** Slight clockwise tilt (~`6deg`).

3. **Top-Right Search/Address Bar Pill:**
   * **Shape:** Capsule pill shape (`border-radius: 50px`).
   * **Background:** White (`#FFFFFF`).
   * **Content:** Red Chrome icon (`#EA4335`) on the left with an empty text input area.

4. **Bottom-Left Blank UI / Bookmark Card:**
   * **Dimensions:** Square-ish white card (`~180px` width/height).
   * **Content:** Soft pink placeholder blocks simulating a saved UI list or quick-access bookmark tile.

5. **Bottom-Right Crochet Video Card:**
   * **Dimensions:** ~`180px` width, portrait orientation.
   * **Content:** Image of hands working with grey textured yarn.
   * **Footer:** White card containing `"Crochet lessons - Part 1"`, `"youtube.com"`, and a red pill button reading `"Add to watch later"`.

---

## 4. Carousel Pagination Indicator (Footer)
Positioned centrally at the bottom of the screen, below the main container.
* **Layout:** Flex row with `12px` gap.
* **Elements:** Four circular indicator dots (`10px` diameter each).
  * **Inactive Dots:** Transparent with a crisp grey outline (`border: 2px solid #BDC1C6`).
  * **Active Dot:** Solid Google Red (`background-color: #EA4335`).

---

## 5. Animation & Micro-Interaction Specifications

### A. Entrance Animation (Staggered Fade & Float)
* **Duration:** `1.2s` total.
* **Timing Function:** `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (Smooth Apple/Google-style ease-out).
* **Sequence:**
  1. **Red Background Banner:** Scales up from `0.95` opacity `0` with a smooth translateY offset (`+30px` to `0`).
  2. **Central Avatar:** Pops in with a spring scale effect (`scale(0.8)` -> `scale(1.05)` -> `scale(1)`).
  3. **Floating Cards & Assets:** Float in from their respective diagonal directions with staggered delays (`0.1s` intervals) to create a dynamic depth-of-field parallax feel.

### B. Idle / Hover Micro-Interactions
* **Floating Cards:** Subtle infinite floating keyframe animation (`translateY` oscillation of `±6px` over a `4s` duration, staggered per card).
* **Hover State (Cards):** Scale up slightly (`scale(1.03)`) with an intensified drop shadow (`0px 16px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.16)`).
* **Pagination Dots:** Smooth width/scale transition on state change.
