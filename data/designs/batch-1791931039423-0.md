# Design Specification: S'WATCH "Fossil Gen 5" Product Showcase

## 1. Overview & Layout Structure

A full-screen luxury e-commerce landing page featuring an asymmetric layout, high-contrast typography, and an organic curved background cutout.

*   **Grid**: 12-column grid or flexible 3-column layout (Left: Product info, Center: Hero product cutout, Right: Secondary previews & controls).
*   **Viewport**: 100vw × 100vh, `overflow: hidden`.
*   **Backdrop Structure**:
    *   **Base Layer**: Deep chocolate brown (`#1F1311`) with a subtle radial gradient behind the text.
    *   **Center Mask/Cutout**: Symmetrical hourglass shape spanning vertically, revealing a warm camel/beige backdrop (`#B89574`) directly behind the hero watch.

---

## 2. Typography

*   **Primary Display Font**: Montserrat, Syne, or Poppins (Heavy/Black weights).
    *   *Fallback*: Arial Black, Impact, sans-serif.
*   **Body & Nav Font**: Inter or Helvetica Neue (Regular, Medium).
    *   *Fallback*: system-ui, sans-serif.

### Type Hierarchy
*   **Logo (`S'WATCH`)**: Montserrat Bold, 16px, uppercase, letter-spacing: 2px.
*   **Nav Links**: Inter Medium, 11px, uppercase, letter-spacing: 2px, muted tan/white.
*   **Hero Title Line 1 ("Fossil")**: Montserrat Black, ~120px–140px, solid white (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Hero Title Line 2 ("Gen 5")**: Montserrat Black, ~120px–140px, outlined text (`-webkit-text-stroke: 2px #FFFFFF`, fill: transparent).
*   **Price ("$359.00")**: Inter Medium, 28px, white (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Buttons / CTA ("Add to cart", "EXPLORE")**: Inter Regular, 13px, letter-spacing: 1.5px.

---

## 3. Color Palette

*   **Dark Espresso (Primary BG)**: `#1E1210`
*   **Warm Camel/Tan (Accent Cutout)**: `#B89574`
*   **Pure White (Primary Text/Accents)**: `#FFFFFF`
*   **Muted Tan/Taupe (Secondary Text/Borders)**: `#8F7E73`
*   **Subtle Radial Glow**: `#36211C` (centered behind hero text)

---

## 4. Component Breakdown

### Header (Top Bar)
*   **Left**: Logo `S'WATCH`, followed by horizontal links `SHOP` and `SEARCH` (gap: 32px).
*   **Right**: `CART (0)` label and a 2-line minimalist hamburger icon (gap: 20px).
*   **Padding**: `32px 48px`.

### Left Sidebar / Indicator
*   **Language Switcher**: Vertical text track (`EN`, `IT`, `FR`) rotated 90° or stacked vertically, positioned along the far-left margin, vertically centered. Active language (`EN`) in white; inactive in muted taupe.

### Hero Section (Left Content)
*   **Text Block**: Left-aligned, vertically centered.
    *   "Fossil"
    *   "Gen 5"
    *   "$359.00" (spaced 24px below title).
*   **Bottom Preview Card**:
    *   Square thumbnail (`120px × 80px`), `border-radius: 12px`, positioned bottom-left.
    *   Contains lifestyle wrist-shot image.
*   **Bottom Explore Action**:
    *   Text: `EXPLORE ↓` located adjacent to the preview card.

### Center Stage (Hourglass Cutout & Hero Asset)
*   **Hourglass Mask**: Smooth bezier curve pinching inwards from top and bottom centers to create a waist behind the watch. Fill: `#B89574`.
*   **Main Product Asset**: Vertically centered, overlapping both top and bottom dark boundaries.
*   **CTA Link**: `Add to cart` centered at the bottom pinch of the tan hourglass.

### Right Panel (Carousel & Navigation)
*   **Mini Product Card**:
    *   Centered vertically on the right side.
    *   Pill-shaped rounded border (`stroke: 1px #8F7E73`, radius: `24px`, dimensions: ~`100px × 140px`).
    *   Micro arrow indicators (`←`, `→`) flanking top-right and bottom-left of the card.
*   **Next Action Button**:
    *   Bottom-right corner.
    *   Circular stroke button (`44px` diameter) with right arrow icon (`→`).

---

## 5. Image & Asset Specifications

### 1. Main Hero Watch
*   **Type**: Backgroundless PNG (Alpha transparent).
*   **Description**: High-end luxury gold watch with brushed gold/woodgrain presidential link bracelet, fluted gold bezel, brown dial, diamond hour markers, and day-date complications.
*   **Placement**: Centered within the hourglass cutout.
*   **Orientation**: Fully vertical (0° rotation), front-facing.
*   **Dimensions**: Scaled to occupy ~85% of viewport height.

### 2. Secondary Preview Watch
*   **Type**: Backgroundless PNG.
*   **Description**: Front-facing two-tone stainless steel and gold watch with Jubilee bracelet and silver dial.
*   **Placement**: Centered inside the right-hand pill card.
*   **Dimensions**: ~`70px × 110px`.

### 3. Lifestyle Thumbnail (Bottom-Left)
*   **Type**: Standard photograph (JPEG/WebP).
*   **Description**: Close-up shot of hands interacting with a dark smartwatch screen outdoors.
*   **Placement**: Inside the bottom-left rounded thumbnail container.
