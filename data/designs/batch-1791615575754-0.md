# Design System & Implementation Specification: "Sofa Cake" Storehouse Dashboard

---

## 1. Aesthetic Vision & Design Philosophy

The interface is a hybrid of **Clean Scandinavian Minimalist Dashboard UI** and **Playful 3D Claymorphism / Depth-of-Field Product Showcase**. 

* **Atmosphere:** Airy, modern, futuristic retail/inventory management.
* **Spatial Concept:** Two primary zones—an immersive 3D spatial staging area on the left and a structured tabular inventory on the right—tied together by floating glass/pill navigation islands.
* **Depth Simulation:** Heavy use of foreground defocus (bokeh / tilt-shift blur) creating tangible physical depth between the background canvas, focal products, and the viewer plane.

## 5. 3D Asset & Visual Imagery Specifications

### A. Focal Product: "Couch Cake" (Sharp Center Stage)
* **Description:** A minimalist, plush contemporary 2-seater settee/loveseat with exaggerated, bulbous rounded contours. The backrest curls continuously into low, rounded armrests. The seat cushion is thick, curved, and deep. It rests on two visible thin, matte-black cylindrical stiletto legs on the right base.
* **Color / Texture:** Matte royal velvet/clay in deep violet (`#602BF8` lit with `#8B5CF6`).
* **Format & Background:** Backgroundless, clean alpha channel transparent PNG / WebP.
* **Position & Scale:** Positioned overlapping the border between the purple hero blob and the right white area. Scaled to approximately `360px` wide.
* **Depth/Focus:** **100% sharp focus** (zero blur). Casts a faint, diffuse ground shadow (`rgba(72, 45, 150, 0.15)`) trailing softly down-right.

### B. Foreground Blurred Elements (Depth of Field Simulation)
To achieve the cinematic macro tilt-shift look, two elements exist on a higher z-index in the foreground with heavy defocus blur:

1. **Floating Orange Cushion (Top Center-Left):**
   * **Description:** A plump, squarish 3D velvet throw pillow in bright tangerine orange (`#FF6433`), angled in mid-air at a ~35° roll and -20° pitch.
   * **Focus Treatment:** Heavy camera blur (`filter: blur(12px) to blur(16px)`).
   * **Position:** Floats near top center edge (`top: 5%`, `left: 32%`).
2. **Foreground Stepped Stool / "Worktop" (Lower Mid-Left):**
   * **Description:** A 3D round tripod stool/side-table featuring a dome-stepped top: a bright red-orange top disc, a middle yellow disc tier, a deep indigo/violet base ring, and three outward-angled natural beech wood legs.
   * **Focus Treatment:** Intense foreground lens blur (`filter: blur(14px) to blur(18px)`).
   * **Position:** Floats in front of the lower boundary of the purple sofa (`bottom: 30%`, `left: 24%`).

### C. Right Column Inventory Thumbnails (Crisp, Backgroundless Miniatures)
All thumbnails are isolated on transparent backgrounds and fit within a `44px × 44px` bounding container:

1. **Worktop Thumbnail:**
   * **Description:** Isometric full view of the tripod stool. Multi-tier circular sandwich top (coral/orange, sunshine yellow, navy base) supported by three splayed blonde-wood peg legs. Perfectly crisp and unblurred.
2. **Couch Capsule Thumbnail:**
   * **Description:** Isometric 3D square scatter cushion/pillow in satin tangerine orange (`#FF6433`), angled diagonally. Crisp specular highlight on top corner.
3. **Couch Cake Thumbnail:**
   * **Description:** Miniature identical isometric rendition of the large focal purple sofa, facing front-right.

### D. User Avatar (Top Right Corner)
* **Description:** High-gloss 3D yellow emoji-style sphere (`#FFB938`), rendering a minimalist happy face (two tiny bead eyes, soft curved mouth).
* **Placement:** Anchored to top-right corner, `40px` from right viewport boundary, `48px` from top. Size: `36px × 36px`.

## 8. Micro-Interactions & Animation Guidelines

1. **Table Row Hover:**
   * Transition `200ms ease`.
   * Row background subtly tints to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
   * Action symbol (`+`) transforms `scale(1.15)` with color shifting to `#7847FF`.
2. **Defocus Parallax Effect (Interactive):**
   * Mouse movement produces subtle, multi-plane parallax:
     * *Layer 4 (Foreground blurred items):* Moves `±15px` opposite to cursor.
     * *Layer 3 (Sharp sofa):* Moves `±5px` with cursor.
     * *Layer 1 (Purple blob):* Moves `±2px` with cursor.
3. **Floating Center Island Hover:**
   * Pill elevates slightly on hover (`translateY(-2px)`), shadow softens and spreads by `+4px`.
   * Dark right-arrow button reacts on click with `scale(0.95)` press feedback.
