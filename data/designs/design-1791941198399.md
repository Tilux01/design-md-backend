# Design System & Implementation Guide: OneBank Dashboard

---

## 1. Global Setup & Typography

### Base Colors
- **Primary Brand / Header Purple:** `#9E86E2`
- **Primary Accent Purple:** `#7C5CE5`
- **Soft Background Tint:** `#F4F5FB`
- **White / Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Text Primary:** `#14171F`
- **Text Secondary / Muted:** `#8D90A0`
- **Border / Divider:** `#ECEEF5`
- **Dark Elements / Buttons:** `#141416`

### Typography
- **Primary Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Fallbacks:** Inter, SF Pro Display, system-ui, sans-serif
- **Scale:**
  - Header Title / Display: 22px – 26px, Bold (700)
  - Card Headings & Large Figures: 20px – 24px, Bold (700)
  - Section Titles / Subheads: 15px – 17px, SemiBold (600)
  - Body / Labels: 13px – 14px, Medium (500)
  - Micro / Captions: 10px – 12px, Regular (400) or SemiBold (600)

---

## 2. Layout Structure

Full-screen viewport layout (`100vw`, `100vh`) with three primary sections:
1. **Top Navigation Bar:** Full-width purple header strip.
2. **Left Icon Sidebar:** Vertical app switcher dock.
3. **Main Dashboard Grid:** Split into Workspace (left 70%) and Cards/Sidebar Panel (right 30%).

---

## 3. Top Navigation Bar

- **Dimensions & Style:** Height `68px`, background `#9E86E2`, padding `0 28px`. Flex container, space-between.
- **Left:**
  - Logo: "onebank" in bold white text with a 45° upper-right arrow icon (`↗`).
- **Center Navigation Tabs:** Horizontal pill-style list:
  - *Dashboard (Active):* Semi-translucent white pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`), 4-square grid icon + text.
  - *Statistics, My Wallet, Transfers, Messages:* White text at 85% opacity, paired with line icons.
- **Right Controls:**
  - White gear (settings) icon.
  - Notification bell with a white counter badge (`2`).
  - Circular avatar (36px): smiling woman, enclosed in a thin white border with a micro dropdown chevron (`˅`).

---

## 4. Left Icon Dock

- **Dimensions & Style:** Width `76px`, background `#FFFFFF`, vertical flex column spanning from beneath the top bar to the bottom edge.
- **Dock Icons (Top-to-Bottom):**
  - App drawer icon (9 dots).
  - "TD" bank circle logo.
  - "m" Monobank circular icon (Black circle, white text, active status indicated by a purple pill indicator at the left border).
  - Cross/Close icon ("×") in gray circle.
  - Octagonal star banking logo.
  - Plus ("+") icon: Solid black circle with white plus.
- **Bottom:** Exit/logout door-arrow icon pointing left.

## 6. Right Sidebar Panel

### Your Cards Section
- **Header:** "Your cards" with counter `3`, plus `<` and `>` circular navigation arrows.
- **Card Stack Presentation:**
  - Under-cards: Dark graphite card and soft-gray card rotated and layered behind.
