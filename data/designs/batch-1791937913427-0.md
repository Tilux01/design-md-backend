# Design Specification: Media Detail View (Zootropolis)

## 1. Global Styles
*   **Background Color:** Deep Charcoal / Midnight Black (`#0f1014`)
*   **Primary Text Color:** Pure White (`#ffffff`)
*   **Secondary Text Color:** Silver/Light Grey (`#cacaca`)
*   **Accent Color (Active State):** Pure White with a bottom border highlight (`#ffffff`)
*   **Font Family:** 
    *   *Primary:* **Avenir Next** (Bold, Medium, and Regular weights).
    *   *Fallback:* **San Francisco** (macOS/iOS), **Roboto** (Android), **sans-serif**.
*   **Border Radius:** 4px to 6px for all content cards and buttons.

---

## 2. Hero Header Section
This section occupies the top 60% of the viewport.

### A. Background Image (Hero)
*   **Content:** A high-resolution cinematic still from *Zootropolis*. 
*   **Visuals:** Nick Wilde (a red fox) is on the right, wearing a light green short-sleeved button-down shirt and brown trousers, looking down at a smartphone in his hand. Judy Hopps (a rabbit) is in her blue police uniform, positioned to the right of Nick, leaning forward and pulling his arm as if leading him toward a case. The background is a blurred city street with warm-toned, European-style buildings.
*   **Treatment:** A linear gradient overlay is applied. 
    *   *Top:* Transparent.
    *   *Bottom:* Transitions to solid `#0f1014` to blend into the content area.
*   **Placement:** Full width, top-aligned.

### B. Title Logo
*   **Content:** The "Disney Zootropolis" wordmark.
*   **Placement:** Top-left, with significant padding (approx. 40px from top and left).
*   **Details:** "Disney" is in the classic script font, small, centered above the main title. "ZOOTROPOLIS" is in a custom, bold, slightly rounded sans-serif font with a subtle 3D bevel effect.

### C. Metadata Row
*   **Content:** `[PG] 1h 48m • 2016 • HD • AD • CC • 5.1`
*   **Styling:** Small caps or slightly reduced font size (12px-14px). Grey text (`#cacaca`).
*   **Icons:** The rating [PG] and technical specs (HD, CC, etc.) are enclosed in thin-bordered rectangles.

### D. Genre Tags
*   **Content:** `Kids • City • Family • Comedy • Animation`
*   **Styling:** Same as metadata row, positioned directly below it.

### E. Primary Action Buttons
*   **Play Button:** 
    *   Large, full-width (or wide) white button (`#ffffff`).
    *   Text: "▶ PLAY" in black (`#000000`), bold, all-caps.
*   **Secondary Buttons:** 
    *   Three circular or icon-only buttons below the Play button: "Add to List" (+ icon), "Trailer" (screen icon), and "Download" (down arrow icon).
    *   Label text is small and placed directly under the icons.

### F. Synopsis
*   **Content:** "A rookie cop rabbit and wily fox team up to crack a case."
*   **Styling:** Regular weight, 16px, white text. Maximum width of 50% of the screen to prevent long line lengths.

---

## 3. Tab Navigation
*   **Items:** `SUGGESTED`, `EXTRAS`, `DETAILS`
*   **Styling:** All-caps, bold, letter-spaced.
*   **Active State:** "SUGGESTED" is highlighted with a thick white underline (approx 3px height) that spans the width of the text.
*   **Inactive State:** Greyed out (`#8f8f8f`).

---

## 4. Content Grid (Suggested Section)
A 2-column grid layout with horizontal and vertical gutters of 10px.

### Image Card Specifications:
All images are **backgroundless** (meaning they are full-bleed posters within their containers) and have a **16:9 aspect ratio**.

1.  **Strange World:** Left column, row 1. Features the main cast in front of a swirling orange/pink portal.
2.  **Kung Fu Panda (Disney+ Version):** Right column, row 1. Features Po (the panda) in a dynamic martial arts pose against a purple/blue background with a Disney+ logo in the corner.
3.  **Wreck-It Ralph:** Right column, row 2 (partially visible). Features Ralph (large man in orange overalls) and Vanellope against a blue background.
4.  **Raya and the Last Dragon:** Left column, row 2. Features Raya in her hat, close-up, with a teal/green background.
5.  **Moana:** Center/Bottom. Features Moana and Maui on their boat against a bright blue ocean background.
6.  **Tangled:** Right column, row 3. Features Rapunzel and Flynn Rider in the forest.

---

## 5. Global Navigation Bar (Bottom)
*   **Background:** Translucent black (`rgba(0,0,0,0.8)`) with a background blur effect (backdrop-filter).
*   **Icons:**
    1.  **Home:** House icon (Active/White).
    2.  **Search:** Magnifying glass icon.
    3.  **Downloads:** Downward arrow into a tray icon.
    4.  **Profile:** User avatar (circular image).
*   **Styling:** Icons are stacked vertically above their respective text labels.

---

## 6. Technical Implementation Notes
*   **Status Bar:** The top of the screen shows system icons (Time: 8:49 AM, Wi-Fi, Battery) in white.
*   **Safe Areas:** Ensure the bottom navigation bar respects the device's home indicator (the thin white bar at the very bottom).
*   **Transitions:** The hero image should have a slight parallax effect upon scrolling, and the Tab underline should animate horizontally when switching tabs.
