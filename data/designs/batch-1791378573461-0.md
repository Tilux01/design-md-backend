# UI/UX Specification & Replication Guide: VERA "SKIN NOURISH" Product Landing Page

This document provides a pixel-for-pixel specification to recreate the e-commerce product display page shown in the media. 

---

## 1. Global Page Layout & Structure

*   **View Type:** Full-screen responsive desktop/tablet display (centered layout).
*   **Background Color:** Soft, desaturated icy blue/cyan gradient or solid hex.
*   **Grid System:** Centered flex column layout containing a Top Navigation Bar, a Hero Section with a 3D/Product Showcase, and a massive background typography element.

### Color Palette
*   **Background (Canvas):** `#E4EEF2` (Soft cool grey-blue)
*   **Primary Text & Logo:** `#1A1A1A` (Near Black)
*   **Subtle Text & Navigation:** `#4A4A4A` (Dark Charcoal)
*   **Background Watermark Typography (`NOURISH`):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)` or a very faint tint of `#CAD7DF` with a subtle drop shadow or overlay blend mode.
*   **Jar Primary Color (Green):** `#1E513A` (Deep Forest/Olive Green)
*   **Jar Cap Color:** `#363837` (Dark Charcoal Grey / Matte finish)

---

## 2. Typography Specification

### Primary Brand Font (Logo & Headings)
*   **Font Family:** **Didot** or **Bodoni Moda** (High-contrast, modern serif).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `Didot, Bodoni Moda, "Times New Roman", Times, serif`
*   **Usage:**
    *   **Logo (`VERA`):** `24px` to `28px`, Letter spacing: `4px` to `6px`, Uppercase, Bold/Regular weight.
    *   **Watermark (`NOURISH`):** Extra large display size (approx. `22vw` to `25vw`), Uppercase, letter spacing: `8px`. Positioned centrally behind the product jar.

### Navigation & UI Font (Body & Links)
*   **Font Family:** **Inter** or **Helvetica Neue** (Clean, neutral geometric sans-serif).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
*   **Usage:**
    *   **Nav Links (`Home`, `Shop`, `About Us`, `Blog`):** `13px` or `14px`, Font Weight: `400` (Regular), Color: `#222222`.
    *   **Action Icons (Wishlist/Profile):** Unicode/SVG symbols accompanied by standard UI font text (`Profile`).

---

## 3. Section-by-Section Breakdown

### A. Top Navigation Bar
*   **Position:** Fixed or absolute at the top of the viewport (`padding: 24px 48px`).
*   **Left Group (Links):** 
    *   `Home`, `Shop`, `About Us`, `Blog` arranged horizontally with a `32px` gap. Active state/hover effects shift color subtly or underline.
*   **Center Group (Brand Logo):**
    *   `VERA` centered precisely or anchored centrally in the header grid. Font: *Didot* with wide tracking.
*   **Right Group (User Actions):**
    *   Wishlist icon (heart outline), Cart/Bag icon (shopping bag), and "Profile" text link.

### B. Hero Section (Product Showcase)
*   **Container:** Flexbox centered horizontally and vertically (`justify-content: center; align-items: center;`).
*   **Background Typography Element (`NOURISH`):**
    *   Placed absolute/z-index behind the product jar. 
    *   Text is horizontally centered across the screen.
    *   Opacity: ~`35%` to blend cleanly into the icy blue background.
*   **Product Jar (Centerpiece):**
    *   **Visual Asset:** A 3D render or high-resolution product mockup of a cylindrical cosmetic jar.
    *   **Jar Body:** Deep forest green with a subtle metallic/satin gradient highlight reflecting off the surface.
    *   **Jar Label:** A rectangular sage/dark-green sticker featuring white serif/sans-serif text layout:
        *   `VERA`
        *   `SKIN NOURISH`
        *   Subtitle/volume indicators below.
    *   **Jar Cap:** Wide, cylindrical matte dark-grey/black lid with a sharp top edge bevel and realistic shadow casting downward onto the body.
    *   **Shadow:** A soft, ambient drop shadow underneath the base of the jar to anchor it realistically to the canvas.

---

## 4. Animation & Interaction Specifications (As seen in media workflow)

Since the media demonstrates an interactive browser/trackpad environment:
1.  **3D Tilt / Parallax Effect:** 
    *   The product jar supports a subtle mouse-move or scroll-triggered gyroscope/tilt effect (using libraries like `Three.js` or CSS `transform: perspective(1000px) rotateX() rotateY()`).
2.  **Page Load Transitions:**
    *   **Fade-in:** The background canvas loads first, followed by a staggered fade-in of the top navigation (`opacity: 0 -> 1`, `translateY: -10px -> 0`).
    *   **Watermark & Jar Reveal:** The large `NOURISH` text scales up slightly from `0.98` to `1.0`, while the jar scales up with a smooth cubic-bezier easing curve (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
