# Air Jordan 6 Retro SP Product Page Design Specification

## 1. Global Styles & Variables

### 1.1 Color Palette
*   **Background (Dark):** `#1e212b` (Deep charcoal/navy slate)
*   **Background (Accent/Pink):** `#ea334e` (Vibrant hot pink/red)
*   **Primary Text (White):** `#ffffff`
*   **Secondary Text (Muted Gray):** `#8d939e`
*   **Accent/Button Red:** `#e7304b`
*   **Star Rating Gold:** `#f3b719`
*   **Dark Neutral Overlay:** `#171923` (used for large background typography)

### 1.2 Typography
*   **Font Family:** Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
*   **Navigation Links:** 11px, Medium (500), Uppercase, Letter-spacing: 0.5px
*   **Product Price:** 22px, Bold (700)
*   **Product Title ("Air Jordan 6 Retro SP"):** 42px, Bold (700), Line-height: 1.1
*   **Body Copy:** 12px, Regular (400), Line-height: 1.5, Color: Muted Gray
*   **Button Text ("ADD TO CART"):** 10px, Bold (700), Uppercase, Letter-spacing: 1px

---

## 2. Layout & Structure

*   **View:** Full-screen responsive viewport (100vw × 100vh)
*   **Container Layout:** Asymmetrical split-screen hero section
    *   **Left Side (Dark Theme):** ~55% width, houses navigation, product info, description, CTA, and social links.
    *   **Right Side (Pink/Red Theme):** ~45% width, houses hero product image and video trailer trigger.
*   **Background Typography:** Large faint watermark number "6" positioned centrally across the split backdrop.

---

*   **Position:** Fixed top, padding: 30px horizontal
*   **Left Element:** Nike Swoosh logo (White, ~32px width)

    *   `WOMEN`
    *   `KIDS`
    *   `CUSTOMIZE`
    *   *Hover state:* Opacity change to 100% (default inactive links are muted gray).
*   **Right Elements:**
    *   Search icon + "Search" text (Muted gray)
    *   User profile icon (Small circular avatar/icon)
    *   Cart icon with item badge counter (`0`)

### 3.2 Product Details (Left Panel)
*   **Price:** `$225` (White, bold)
*   **Title:** `Air Jordan 6 Retro SP` (White, stacked in 2 lines: "Air Jordan 6" / "Retro SP")
*   **Rating:** 5 solid gold stars (`★★★★★`) sized around 12px.

    > Originally released the year MJ won his first professional championship, the Air Jordan 6 Retro SP features a sculpted midsole for cushioned stability, with overlays designed to abstractly depict the number 23.

    *   Background: `#ea334e` (Hot pink)
    *   Border-radius: 4px (slightly rounded pill or rectangle)
    *   Padding: 10px 20px
    *   Text: White, uppercase, bold.

### 3.3 Hero Image & Visuals (Right Panel)
*   **Background Shape:** Diagonal split slicing from top-right to bottom-left using `#ea334e`.
*   **Product Image:**
    *   Subject: Air Jordan 6 Retro SP (Silver/Grey/Infrared colorway).

    *   Position: Bottom right corner of the pink section.

*   **Hover Effects:** Navigation items and buttons should feature smooth transition opacity/scale shifts (e.g., `transition: all 0.2s ease-in-out`).
