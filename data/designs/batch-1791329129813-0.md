# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise SaaS & Financial Analytics Dashboard.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-density data presentation balanced with generous card padding (`24px`), distinct panel grouping, and soft borders to avoid visual fatigue.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat multi-surface card architecture. Surface background uses very pale off-white (`#F8FAFC`), elevated cards use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle 1px gray borders (`#E2E8F0`) and minimal drop-shadows.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Clean status indicators with emerald green pulses, high-contrast metric difference badges (`+12% vs last year`), hover tooltip cards with financial breakdowns.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `2px 8px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#ECFDF5`, text color `#059669`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;`
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
