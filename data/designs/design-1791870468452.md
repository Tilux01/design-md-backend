# Design Specification: AIVISION STUDIO Landing Page (Dark Mode)

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
* **Viewport:** Full-screen responsive layout optimized for desktop and tablet display.
* **Orientation:** Landscape / Tablet frame mockup centered within the viewport.
* **Background:** Smooth gradient transitioning from dark navy blue at the top/center to a deeper azure/indigo blue at the outer edges (`#0F1B33` to `#0B132B`).
* **Container (Mockup):** 
  * Rounded rectangle container with a sleek dark bezel (`#0A0F1D`).
  * Corner radius: `24px`.
  * Subtle outer glow and drop shadow to give depth against the background.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
* **Primary Font Family:** Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.
* **Fallback Strategy:** If Google Fonts / system sans-serif fails, fallback strictly to standard web-safe sans-serif fonts.

### Text Hierarchy:
1. **Brand / Logo ("AIVISION STUDIO"):**
   * Weight: Bold (`700`)
   * Size: `14px` / `16px` (Stacked two-line layout)
   * Color: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)

2. **Hero Heading ("Revolutionize Video Content with AI"):**
   * Weight: Bold (`800`)
   * Size: `36px` to `48px` (Fluid responsive)
   * Line Height: Tight (`1.1`)
   * Color: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)

3. **Hero Subtext / Body Copy:**
   * Weight: Regular (`400`)
   * Size: `13px`
   * Color: Light Gray / Off-white (`#A0AEC0`)
   * Max-Width: ~320px

4. **Navigation & Buttons:**
   * Weight: Medium (`500`)
   * Size: `12px` to `14px`
   * Color: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)

---

## 3. Color Palette
* **Background Primary:** `#0A0F1D` (Deep dark blue/black)
* **Surface Cards / Panels:** Translucent dark glassmorphism (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`)
* **Borders / Strokes:** Subtle cyan/blue translucent borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`)
* **Accent Primary:** Electric Blue / Cyan (`#00E5FF` to `#3B82F6`) used for highlights and gradient buttons.
* **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF`
* **Text Secondary:** `#94A3B8`

---

## 4. Header & Navigation Section
* **Logo Position:** Top-left corner. Displays "AIVISION" on line one and "STUDIO" on line two in bold white uppercase text.
* **Theme Toggle (Dark Mode Switch):**
   * Positioned immediately to the right of the logo.
   * Pill-shaped container with label "Dark" and a crescent moon glyph icon inside a circular badge.
* **Action Buttons (Top-Right):**
   * **Log In:** Text link / ghost button with transparent background and white text.
   * **Sign Up:** Solid white pill button with dark text (`#0A0F1D`), highly prominent.

---

## 5. Hero Section (Left Column)
* **Heading:** "Revolutionize Video Content with AI"
* **Description:** "At AI Video Creations, we bring your ideas to life through the power of artificial intelligence."
* **Primary Interactive Elements:**
  * **Swipe to Get Started:** 
    * Pill-shaped button featuring a circular arrow icon `>` on the left and text "Swipe to Get Started" right-aligned.
    * Semi-transparent glass background with a subtle border.
  * **Choose Your Style:**
    * Centered prominent action button below the hero area.
    * Styled with a vibrant blue gradient fill (`#2563EB` to `#1D4ED8`) and rounded pill edges.

---

## 6. Visual Element: 3D Glass Bubbles (Center Illustration)
* **Image Description:** A cluster of transparent, glossy 3D glass spheres/bubbles floating centrally between the text and sidebar cards. 
* **Characteristics:**
  * **Backgroundless:** Yes, rendered with alpha transparency over the blue gradient background.
  * **Refraction & Lighting:** Features realistic specular highlights, chromatic aberration (subtle pink/cyan light refractions along the edges), and depth of field blur (larger bubbles in the foreground are sharper, background bubbles are softly blurred).
  * **Placement & Rotation:** Centered horizontally in the mid-section overlap area. Rotated naturally with random organic spacing (no strict geometric grid).

---

## 7. Sidebar Cards Section (Right Column)

### Card 1: "Transform Your Content Today"
* **Container:** Frosted glass panel with rounded corners (`16px`) and subtle cyan border glow.
* **Content:**
  * Heading: "Transform Your Content Today" (Bold, White, `14px`)
  * Body: "Join thousands of creators who are leveraging AI to produce captivating videos." (Muted text, `11px`)
  * **"Join Us" Button:** Pill button with blue gradient fill.
  * **Avatar Group:** A cluster of small circular profile pictures overlapping at the bottom right of the card, representing active community creators.

### Card 2: "Contact Us"
* **Container:** Matching frosted glass panel below Card 1.
* **Content:**
  * Heading: "Contact Us" (Bold, White, `14px`)
  * Body: "Have questions? Need assistance? Our support team is here to help." (Muted text, `11px`)
  * **"Support" Button:** Pill-shaped outline/ghost button with centered white text.
