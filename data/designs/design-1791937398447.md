# Design Specification: The New Circus Streaming Interface

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Background:** The entire application should be full-screen (100vw/100vh). The background is a full-bleed, high-contrast, desaturated image of a cornfield at dusk, heavily blurred (approx. 20px Gaussian blur) to create depth.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Red:** `#E50914` (used for buttons and rating tags).
    *   **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
    *   **Text Secondary:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` (Off-white/Grey).
    *   **Glassmorphism:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` with a `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)` and a `1px` solid border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
*   **Typography:**
    *   **Primary Heading Font:** A heavy, condensed Sans-Serif. 
        *   *Recommendation:* **"Anton"** or **"Bebas Neue"**.
        *   *Fallback:* `Impact, sans-serif-condensed, sans-serif`.
    *   **Secondary/Body Font:** A clean, geometric Sans-Serif.
        *   *Recommendation:* **"Montserrat"** or **"Inter"**.
        *   *Fallback:* `Arial, sans-serif`.

---

## 2. Header & Navigation (Top)
*   **Logo (Top Left):** 
    *   Text: "WC" (WolvesClub).
    *   Style: Bold, stylized sans-serif. 
    *   Placement: 40px from top, 40px from left.
*   **Social Bar (Top Center-Left):**
    *   Icons: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube.
    *   Container: White pill-shaped background with a "cut-out" notch effect from the top edge.
    *   Placement: Centered vertically with the logo, shifted slightly left of the center.
*   **Search (Top Center-Right):**
    *   Icon: Magnifying glass.
    *   Placement: Mirrored to the right of the social bar.

---

## 3. Sidebar (Left)
*   **Menu Toggle:** Three horizontal lines (hamburger icon) located below the logo.
*   **Vertical Indicator:** A thin white vertical line (approx 60px height).
*   **Pagination Dots:** Four small circles arranged vertically. The second dot from the top is larger and filled (active state), the others are hollow outlines.
*   **Vertical Text:** The word "movies" rotated -90 degrees, placed near the bottom left.

---

## 4. Main Title Section (Center-Left)
*   **Sub-headline:** "THEY WON'T MAKE YOU LAUGH"
    *   Font: Secondary Font, Bold, Uppercase.
    *   Size: Approx 18px.
    *   Letter Spacing: Wide (approx 2px).
*   **Main Title:** "THE NEW CIRCUS"
    *   Font: Primary Heading Font (Condensed).
    *   Size: Extremely large (approx 120px).
    *   Line Height: 0.9 (Tight).
    *   Structure: Stacked vertically ("THE" / "NEW" / "CIRCUS").
*   **Action Buttons:**
    *   **Play Button:** Solid Primary Red pill, white text "PLAY".
    *   **Download Button:** Transparent pill with a 2px white border, white text "DOWNLOAD".

## 5. Featured Movie Card (Right)
*   **Container:** 
    *   Dimensions: Approx 45% width, 70% height.
    *   Styling: Rounded corners (30px), glassmorphic border, subtle drop shadow.
*   **Card Image (Backgroundless/Integrated):**
    *   **Description:** A group of four menacing clowns standing in a cornfield. 
    *   **Lead Clown (Right Foreground):** Closest to the viewer, tilted slightly left. Features high-detail white cracked face paint, exaggerated black arched eyebrows, a large round red nose, and a wide, jagged-tooth grin. Wearing a grey ruffled Victorian-style circus collar with red vertical tassels hanging down.
    *   **Background Clowns:** Three additional clowns receding into the cornfield, blurred slightly more than the lead clown to create depth.
    *   **Placement:** The image should fill the card container, with the lead clown's face positioned in the lower right quadrant of the card.
*   **Card Content (Top & Bottom Overlay):**
    *   **Title:** "THE NEW CIRCUS" (Smaller version of main title).
    *   **Metadata Row:** "2023 | [+21] | 2h 5min | Violence". 
        *   Note: "[+21]" is inside a solid red square badge.
    *   **Description:** "If you meet them in the middle of the countryside, you will become their entertainment." (Left-aligned, Secondary Font).
    *   **Card Controls (Bottom):**
        *   **Watch Trailer:** Red pill button with a "Play" triangle icon. Text: "WATCH TRILER" (Note: maintain the original's typo "TRILER" if an exact clone is required).
        *   **Bookmark Icon:** Red square button with rounded corners and a white bookmark outline.
        *   **Expand Icon:** Red circular button on the far right with a white diagonal arrow icon.

## 7. Technical Implementation Notes
*   **Image Assets:**
    *   `bg-main.jpg`: The blurred cornfield.
    *   `clowns-group.png`: The sharp image of the clowns. This should be a high-resolution PNG with the edges masked to fit the card's rounded corners.
*   **Layout Engine:** Use **CSS Grid** for the main layout (Sidebar | Content | Card) and **Flexbox** for the internal button and header alignments.
*   **Z-Index Management:** 
    *   Background Image: `z-index: 1`
    *   Main Text/Sidebar: `z-index: 10`
    *   Featured Card: `z-index: 20`
