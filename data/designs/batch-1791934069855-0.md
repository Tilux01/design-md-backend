# Design System Specification: AgencyPro Hero Section

## 1. Overview & Layout Architecture
- **Screen Dimension**: 100vw × 100vh (Full-screen viewport, no scroll).
- **Layout Type**: 2-column asymmetric split layout overlaid with absolute navigation and metrics.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Background Base: `#0F1012` to `#1B1D21` (smooth dark charcoal radial gradient).
  - Text Primary: `#FFFFFF`
  - Text Secondary: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`
  - Glass Surfaces: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` fill, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` 1px border.

---

## 2. Typography
- **Primary Serif (Headline)**:
  - Font: `Cormorant Garamond`, `Playfair Display`, fallback: `Georgia, serif`.
  - Style: Semi-bold (600), high optical contrast, line-height 1.15.
- **Secondary Sans-Serif (Body, UI, Stats)**:
  - Font: `Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, fallback: `system-ui, sans-serif`.
  - Style: Regular (400), Medium (500), Bold (700), clean geometric tracking.

---

## 3. Background & Imagery
- **Hero Artwork**:
  - **Description**: Monochromatic, high-contrast, black-and-white 3D render of an abstract female profile facing left. The skin texture is smooth matte porcelain. Lighting is cinematic chiaroscuro from the right, leaving the back and center in deep shadow.
  - **Position**: Absolute, pinned to the right half (`right: 0`, `top: 0`, `width: 55vw`, `height: 100vh`), seamlessly blending into the left dark background via a vertical gradient seam.
  - **Format**: High-resolution raster/PNG with integrated shadow-to-dark gradient background.

---

## 4. Components & Structure

### A. Navigation Bar
- **Placement**: Fixed top, width 100%, height 72px, padding: `0 48px`, flex alignment.
- **Left**:
  - Logo: Upward zigzag trend icon (`~20px`) + "AgencyPro" text (Sans-serif, 18px, weight 600).
- **Right (Frosted Header Bar)**:
  - Container: Glassmorphic capsule/bar (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
  - Links: "About", "Blog", "Contact" (Sans-serif, 13px, regular, muted white).
  - Button: "Get Started" (Glass pill, 13px, weight 500, padding: `8px 18px`, border: `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`).

### B. Left Content Column
- **Position**: Centered vertically, offset left (`left: 64px`, `max-width: 520px`).
- **Badge**:
  - Pill shape: Translucent gray background (`rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`), 4px radius.
  - Content: Small rocket icon + "Digital Growth Agency" (12px, regular).
- **Main Heading**:
  - Content: "Build Powerful Brands<br/>That Drive Growth"
  - Styling: Serif, 52px, letter-spacing -0.5px, color `#FFFFFF`.
- **Sub-headline**:
  - Content: "We help ambitious businesses grow with strategic branding, stunning websites, and high-converting digital results."
  - Styling: Sans-serif, 14px, lineHeight 1.5, color `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`, margin-bottom 32px.
- **Call-to-Action Buttons**:
  - Container: Horizontal flex, 12px gap.
  - "Start Your Project": Glass button, light grey translucent fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`), border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`, radius 4px, font 13px bold.
  - "View Our Work": Matching secondary glass button style.

### C. Right Metric Cards
- **Position**: Fixed bottom-right corner (`right: 64px`, `bottom: 48px`, `width: 380px`).
- **Card Specs**:
  - Stack: 3 vertical cards, 12px gap.
  - Appearance: Translucent fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, backdrop blur 10px, radius 4px, padding: `16px 20px`.
  - Layout: Flexbox between value (left) and label (right).
- **Metric Content**:
  1. `250` (32px Bold Sans) — `PROJECTS COMPLETED` (10px Uppercase Sans, muted).
  2. `98%` (32px Bold Sans) — `98% CLIENT SATISFACTION` (10px Uppercase Sans, muted).
  3. `8+` (32px Bold Sans) — `YEARS EXPERIENCE` (10px Uppercase Sans, muted).
