# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Modern Enterprise Financial SaaS & Dashboard Glassmorphism.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-density operational layout with structured cards, tight paddings (`12px` to `20px`), and clean grid gutters optimized for data-rich analytics.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-layered card architecture featuring frosted translucent surfaces (`bg-white/90` or dark-mode equivalents), crisp 1px subtle borders (`#E2E8F0`), and soft ambient drop shadows (`0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, frosted glass panels, neon indicator bars, live status pills.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `0.75rem`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(37,99,235,0.1)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
