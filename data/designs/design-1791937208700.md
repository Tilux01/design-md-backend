This document provides a comprehensive design specification for cloning the "Dhaka Movies" Spatial UI interface. The design follows a modern "Glassmorphism" aesthetic, characteristic of visionOS, focusing on depth, translucency, and vibrant accents.

---

# Design Specification: Dhaka Movies Spatial Interface

## 1. Visual Theme & Style
*   **Overall Style:** Glassmorphism / Frosted Glass.
*   **Background:** The interface is designed to be viewed over a dynamic environment. For a web/app clone, use a dark, blurred cinematic background (e.g., a library or theater) with a `backdrop-filter: blur(40px)`.
*   **Corner Radius:** High rounding across all elements. 
    *   Main Window: `40px`
    *   Buttons/Cards: `20px`
    *   Sidebar: `50px` (Pill shape)

## 2. Color Palette
*   **Primary Accent:** `#A3FF00` (Neon Lime Green) - Used for the logo, active icons, play buttons, and "See all" links.
*   **Surface (Glass):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` with a white `1px` border at `0.1` opacity to simulate a glass edge.
*   **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
*   **Text Secondary:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` (Off-white/Gray).
*   **Shadows:** Soft, large-spread shadows to create depth (`0px 20px 50px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`).

## 3. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `SF Pro Display` (Apple system font).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `Inter`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:**
    *   Logo: Bold
    *   Headings: Semi-bold
    *   Body/Description: Regular
*   **Sizes:**
    *   Hero Title: `48px`
    *   Section Headers: `20px`
    *   Nav Links: `16px`
    *   Metadata/Description: `14px`

## 4. Layout Structure

### A. Global Navigation Sidebar (Left)
*   **Position:** Floating pill-shaped vertical bar to the left of the main content.
*   **Background:** Dark translucent glass (`rgba(0,0,0,0.4)`).
*   **Icons:**
    1.  **Home (Active):** Solid Neon Green house icon inside a rounded square background.
    2.  **Search:** Thin white outline magnifying glass.
    3.  **TV:** Thin white outline television set.
    4.  **Movies:** Thin white outline shopping bag/briefcase.
    5.  **Settings:** Thin white outline gear icon.

### B. Header (Top)
*   **Logo:** Left-aligned. Icon is a green circle with "dm" in black lowercase serif font. Text "Dhaka Movies" in white semi-bold.
*   **Nav Links:** Center-aligned. "Movies", "TV Series", "Games", "Kids". Active state: White text; Inactive: Secondary text.
*   **User Controls:** Right-aligned. 
    *   **Notification Bell:** Outlined icon inside a circular glass button.
    *   **Profile:** Pill-shaped button containing the name "Megan" and a circular avatar (Headshot of a woman with dark hair, smiling, backgroundless/neutral background).

### C. Hero Section (Main Feature)
*   **Content:** "Black Adam"
*   **Background Image:** A high-resolution cinematic wide-shot. Center-left features Dwayne Johnson (Black Adam) looking forward. Right side features Hawkman with golden wings spread and Dr. Fate in a gold helmet.
*   **Overlay Elements:**
    *   **Title Logo:** "DC BLACK ADAM" in metallic, chiseled 3D typography.
    *   **Description:** 3 lines of text describing the Egyptian Gods and the 5000-year imprisonment.
    *   **Buttons:** Two pill-shaped glass buttons. "More info" (Info icon) and "Add to Favourite" (Heart icon).
    *   **Play Button:** Large central floating element. A vibrant Neon Green circle (`#A3FF00`) with a black "Play" triangle in the center. It has a soft outer glow.

### D. Recent Update (Right Sidebar)
*   **Header:** "Recent Update" (Left) | "See all" (Neon Green, Right).
*   **Cards:** Vertical stack of two cards.
    1.  **Card 1:** Poster for "Edge of Tomorrow" (Live. Die. Repeat.). Features Tom Cruise and Emily Blunt in exo-suits running through a battlefield.
    2.  **Card 2:** Poster for "Tenet". Features a man in a suit (John David Washington) split by a central line, representing time inversion.
*   **Card Details:** Each card has a small white outline heart icon in the top-right corner.

### E. New Trending (Bottom Row)
*   **Header:** "New Trending" (Left) | "See all" (Neon Green, Right).
*   **Grid:** Horizontal scroll of 4 posters.
    1.  **Mortal Kombat:** Features Sub-Zero and Scorpion facing off with a blue/orange contrast.
    2.  **X-Men Apocalypse:** Ensemble cast poster with Oscar Isaac (Apocalypse) in the center and X-Men below him.
    3.  **1917:** Features a soldier (George MacKay) running through a field with explosions and the large numbers "1917" in the background.
    4.  **Oblivion:** Features Tom Cruise standing on a cliff edge overlooking a massive waterfall and a ruined futuristic landscape.
*   **Card Details:** Rounded corners (`20px`), heart icon in the top right of each poster.

## 5. Asset Descriptions

### Image Assets
1.  **Hero Image (Black Adam):** Cinematic landscape. The characters should be cut out or blended so the text on the left remains legible against a darker area of the image.
2.  **Mortal Kombat Poster:** Portrait orientation. High contrast, dark background.
3.  **X-Men Poster:** Portrait orientation. Blue/Purple color grade.
4.  **1917 Poster:** Portrait orientation. Earthy tones (browns, oranges).
5.  **Oblivion Poster:** Portrait orientation. Cool tones (blues, whites).
6.  **Avatar:** Circular crop of a professional female headshot.

### Icons
*   **Style:** Linear/Outline for inactive states; Solid for active states.
*   **Stroke Weight:** `1.5px` to `2px`.
*   **Source:** Similar to `Lucide Icons` or `Heroicons`.

---

## 6. Technical Effects (CSS/Implementation)
*   **Backdrop Blur:** `backdrop-filter: blur(25px) brightness(80%);`
*   **Border Gradient:** To achieve the "glass" edge, use a linear gradient border from `rgba(255,255,255,0.2)` at the top-left to `rgba(255,255,255,0.05)` at the bottom-right.
*   **Hover States:** When hovering over movie cards, increase the `brightness` by 10% and apply a slight `scale(1.02)` transform.
*   **Play Button Glow:** `box-shadow: 0 0 30px rgba(163, 255, 0, 0.4);`
