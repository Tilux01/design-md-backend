# Smartpad Home Automation Dashboard — Design Specification (`design.md`)

This document provides the complete, production-ready design specification required to implement an exact, pixel-perfect clone of the **Smartpad** smart home dashboard.

---

## 1. Global Canvas & Viewport

* **Display Mode**: Full-screen application (100vw × 100vh), removing the outer mock-up frame and the external purple decorative gradient entirely.
* **Canvas Background Color**: `#F6F4EF` (warm off-white / porcelain cream).
* **Global Text Anti-Aliasing**: Subpixel & Grayscale enabled (`-webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased`).
* **Layout Structure**: Single viewport, non-scrolling dashboard layout divided into a two-column desktop grid:
  * **Left Column (Data & Controls)**: ~52%–55% width, padded interior.
  * **Right Column (3D Isometric Room View)**: ~45%–48% width, bleeding directly to the right and bottom edges.

## 5. Right Section: 3D Isometric Cutaway Room (Image Asset Spec)

### 5.1 Image Asset Specification
* **Format**: High-resolution 32-bit PNG or WebP with full **Alpha Channel Transparency** (strictly no background box or square container outline).
* **Integration**: Bleeds seamlessly onto the `#F6F4EF` canvas.
* **Anchor & Alignment**:
  * Pinned to `right: 0`, `bottom: 0`.
  * Width: `48%` to `50%` of overall screen width.
  * Vertical overflow: Anchors flush to the bottom edge, with the roof eave sloping down from the top right quadrant.

### 5.2 Visual Content & Scene Composition
* **Perspective / Projection**: Axonometric / Isometric architectural cutaway (approximately 30° elevation angle).
* **Architecture / Structure**:
  * A single-room loft cutaway under an angled, pitched gable roof.
  * **Roof**: Outer terracotta-red clay tiles with a clean, light-colored overhang and exposed warm wood support rafters underneath.
  * **Walls**:
    * Interior walls rendered in warm sunlight yellow / light amber tones (`#FDE68A` to `#F59E0B` ambient radiance).
    * Exterior section cut/facade in clean chalky white/off-white with visible architectural depth.
  * **Flooring**:
    * Warm golden teak/oak hardwood planks extending outwards into an open-air balcony/cantilever ledge towards the lower-left.
* **Interior Furniture & Accents**:
  1. **Bed**:
     * Low wooden bed frame with warm timber headboard along the back wall.
     * Crisp white mattress duvet, folded neatly.
     * Olive green bed throw / runner blanket folded across the lower half.
  2. **Rug**:
     * Circular lemon-yellow woven area rug placed on the wooden floor alongside the bed.
  3. **Window**:
     * Left interior wall features a tall, vertical two-pane sash window framed in white wood, with soft diffused daylight flooding in.
  4. **Lighting**:
     * Warm amber global illumination inside the room, suggesting a comfortable, cozy atmosphere.
* **Interactive HUD Hotspot Marker**:
  * **Position**: Hovering floating in space near the center-right interior wall above the bed.
  * **Visual**: A semi-translucent circular glowing aura (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`) with a white circular inner disc and a subtle plus sign `+` at the center (`dimension: 32px × 32px`). This represents the interactive light fixture / smart entity anchor.

## 6. Interaction States & Transitions

1. **Room Tabs (`Bed`, `Living`, `Dining`, `Study`)**:
   * Hover: Inactive tabs transition from `#B2B3BA` to `#60626B` (`transition: color 0.2s ease`).
   * Active: Transition underline position smoothly using CSS transform.
2. **Candela Dial**:
   * Draggable / rotatable knob altering the Candela numerical value dynamically between `1` and `10`.
   * Outer amber glow expands / intensifies proportionally as Candela value increases.
3. **Day / Night Toggle**:
   * Clicking `"Night"` slides the indigo active pill from left to right, changes background ambient colors subtly to deep navy, and shifts the 3D room lighting from warm golden day to moody cool blue night illumination.
4. **Appliance Rail**:
   * Clicking appliance icons switches the active context in the central panel (e.g., selecting Thermostat updates the dial to Temperature controls `°C`).
