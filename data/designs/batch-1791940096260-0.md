# Design Specification: TuneHive Music App

## 1. Global Theme & Color Palette
The design follows a **Dark-Mode Aesthetic** with a monochromatic dark olive base and high-contrast neon accents.

*   **Primary Background:** `#1A1D16` (Deep Olive Black)
*   **Secondary Background (Cards/Pills):`#2A2F24`
*   **Accent Color (Primary):** `#C5FF29` (Neon Lime/Electric Green)
*   **Accent Color (Glow):** `rgba(197, 255, 41, 0.3)`
*   **Text - Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
*   **Text - Secondary:** `#A1A1A1` (Cool Grey)
*   **Text - Muted:** `#5C6353` (Dark Sage)

## 2. Typography
The UI uses a modern, geometric Sans-Serif typeface.
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Poppins`
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`

### Text Styles:
*   **Heading 1 (Splash):** 32px, Bold, Tracking -1%, Leading 120%.
*   **Heading 2 (Section Titles):** 18px, Semi-Bold.
*   **Body Text:** 14px, Regular, Leading 150%.
*   **Label/Small:** 12px, Medium.

---

## 3. Screen 1: Splash / Onboarding
**Layout:** Vertical Flex, centered content.

*   **Top Bar:** Standard iOS/Android status bar icons in white.
*   **Hero Image:** 
    *   **Description:** A high-fidelity 3D render of over-ear headphones. 
    *   **Color:** Glossy Lime Green (`#C5FF29`).
    *   **Placement:** Centered horizontally, occupying the top 50% of the screen.
    *   **Rotation:** Rotated approximately 15 degrees clockwise on the Y-axis to show depth.
    *   **Background:** No background (transparent PNG).
*   **Content Area:**
    *   **Title:** "Start Your Sonic Journey" (Left-aligned).
    *   **Description:** 2-3 lines of muted text explaining the app's value.
*   **Bottom Action Bar:**
    *   **Button:** A wide pill-shaped button.
    *   **Left Element:** A circular neon green icon with a black "Next" arrow.
    *   **Center Element:** Text "Turn on your music" in white.
    *   **Right Element:** A small, hollow headphone icon (`#A1A1A1`).

---

## 4. Screen 2: Home / Discovery
**Layout:** Vertical Scrollable View.

*   **Header:**
    *   **Left:** Circular profile image (32x32px) featuring a stylized avatar.
    *   **Center:** "Good Morning, Antony Doe" (White, 16px).
    *   **Right:** Search icon (Magnifying glass) inside a subtle dark circle.
*   **Categories Section:**
    *   **Label:** "Select Categories" (White, Semi-bold).
    *   **Pills:** Horizontal scrolling list.
    *   **Active Pill:** Background `#C5FF29`, Text `#000000`.
    *   **Inactive Pills:** Background `#2A2F24`, Text `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Popular Songs Section:**
    *   **Label:** "Popular Songs" with a "View All" link on the right.
    *   **Cards:** Square cards (approx 120px).
    *   **Image 1:** Abstract orange/fire visual.
    *   **Image 2:** Dark purple "MUSIC" typography art.
    *   **Image 3:** Red-toned portrait.
*   **New Collection Section:**
    *   **Cards:** Larger rectangular cards.
    *   **Image:** A backgroundless photo of a male singer holding a vintage microphone, positioned slightly off-center to allow text overlay.
*   **Bottom Navigation Bar:**
    *   **Style:** Floating dark pill (`#1A1D16`) with a slight outer glow.
    *   **Icons:** Home, Search, Heart, Profile.
    *   **Active State:** The Home icon is encased in a solid Neon Lime (`#C5FF29`) circle.

---

## 5. Screen 3: Now Playing
**Layout:** Full-screen immersive view.

*   **Background Image:**
    *   **Description:** A warm, high-contrast photo of a man and woman looking at each other closely.
    *   **Placement:** Full screen, top-aligned.
    *   **Overlay:** A heavy linear gradient from `transparent` at the top to `#1A1D16` (100% opacity) at the bottom (starting at the 50% mark).
*   **Header:**
    *   **Left:** Back arrow icon.
    *   **Right:** Triple-dot "Options" icon.
*   **Playback Controls (The "Dial"):**
    *   **Outer Ring:** A large, thin circular arc acting as the progress bar.
    *   **Progress Indicator:** A small neon green dot on the arc.
    *   **Volume/Status Bar:** A horizontal neon green bar just above the main button.
    *   **Main Play Button:** 
        *   Large circular button with a heavy neon green outer glow.
        *   Icon: Pause (two vertical bars) in black.
    *   **Secondary Controls:** Skip back/forward icons placed to the left and right of the main dial in white.
*   **Metadata:**
    *   Track name and Artist name are centered above the dial in white and muted green respectively.

---

## 6. UI Components & Effects
*   **Corner Radius:** All cards and buttons use a `24px` to `32px` radius for a soft, modern look.
*   **Glows:** Use `box-shadow: 0px 0px 15px rgba(197, 255, 41, 0.4)` for active buttons and the playback dial.
*   **Glassmorphism:** Navigation bars and category pills should have a slight backdrop-filter (blur) if placed over images.
*   **Icons:** Thin-stroke (Linear) icons, approximately 2px weight.
