# Design Specification: Spacing & Grid Modernist Landing Interface

---

## 1. Overview & Aesthetic Direction

This interface is a minimalist, Swiss-inspired modernist product showcase with atmospheric ambient depth. It combines generous whitespace, heavy typographic contrast, structural framing lines, and vibrant, blurred chromatic mesh lighting. 

* **Display Mode**: Edge-to-edge full screen (`100vw` × `100vh` viewport minimum).
* **Grid Style**: Asymmetric split-screen with a fixed narrow left utility sidebar and a modular content canvas.
* **Atmospheric Theme**: Pristine white/off-white background with soft, out-of-focus chromatic blooms (aurora/mesh gradients) floating in the canvas periphery.

  The central artwork represents abstract mathematical spacing, focal depth, and networked nodes:
  1. **Base Gradient**: Full bleed saturated blend starting with royal violet (`#6B21A8`) at the top, transitioning through vivid pink-fuchsia (`#D946EF`), into glowing peach-orange (`#FB7185`) at the mid-right edge, and dark ultraviolet (`#3B0764`) in the bottom left.

     * A pure white (`#FFFFFF`) solid circular disc (`~84px` diameter) positioned near upper-center.
     * Behind and trailing down-right from this disc is a stepped, semi-transparent kinetic blur/trail effect showing directional movement across the gradient field.
  4. **Connected Metric Nodes (Bottom-Right)**:
     * Two small solid white circular dots (`~14px` diameter each).
     * Connected by a crisp, thin 1.5px solid white line spanning diagonally at approx. `45°`.
     * Surrounding each white dot is a concentric translucent circular halo/glow (`~48px` diameter, `20%` white opacity) representing magnetic grid snapping and spacing radius.

## 6. Interaction & Responsive Behaviors

* **Pill Buttons & Links**: Hover state shifts `Download now` to `#262626` background; nav links transition smoothly to `#0D0D0D` color.
* **Control Buttons**: The circular dismiss (`×`) and minimize (`—`) buttons exhibit a scale transform (`1.05`) and background tint (`#F7F7F7`) on cursor hover.
* **Screen Scaling**:
  * At viewport widths below `1100px`, the three-column layout wraps into a two-tier structure, preserving the left utility sidebar.
  * High-DPI screens maintain razor-sharp 1px border lines via standard subpixel hairline rendering (`border: 0.5px solid #EEEEEE` or CSS variable border widths).
