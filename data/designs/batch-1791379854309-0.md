# Design Specification: Gaming Studio Landing Page (`rinagrim.wixstudio.io`)

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

### Color Palette
*   **Primary Dark/Background:** `#0d1117` (Deep dark slate/night blue representing mountains at night)
*   **Sun/Glow Accent:** `#f0a500` (Warm amber/gold)
*   **Text Primary:** `#ffffff` (Pure white)
*   **Text Secondary (Subtitles/Body):** `#d1d5db` (Light cool gray)
*   **Glassmorphic Navigation Background:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` with backdrop blur (`blur(12px)`)
*   **Border/Outline Color:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`

### Typography & Fonts
*   **Headings (Logo & Hero Title):** 
    *   *Font Family:* **"Cinzel"**, **"Trajan Pro"**, or fallback to **`serif`** (Must be a high-contrast, majestic, classic fantasy serif font with sharp terminals).
*   **Navigation & Body Copy:** 
    *   *Font Family:* **"Montserrat"**, **"Inter"**, or fallback to **`sans-serif`** (Clean, geometric, highly legible at small sizes).

---

## 2. Layout & Structure

### A. Navigation Bar (Header)
*   **Position:** Fixed or sticky top navigation.
*   **Container Style:** Centered, floating glassmorphic pill shape (`border-radius: 50px`).
*   **Nav Links:** 
    *   `ABOUT`
    *   `PROJECTS`
    *   `CAREERS`
    *   `FAQ`
    *   `CONTACT`
*   **Link Styling:** Uppercase, tracking (letter-spacing: `1.5px`), font-size: `13px`, font-weight: `500`, color: `#ffffff`. Hover state adds a subtle underline or brightness shift.

### B. Hero Section
*   **Layout:** Full-screen viewport (`100vh`), centered flex/grid container.
*   **Background Visuals:**
    *   Layered composition: Dark starry night sky, dramatic layered mountain silhouettes in the foreground/midground, and a glowing cinematic sun/portal directly behind the main typography.
*   **Carousel / Navigation Arrows:** 
    *   Subtle thin chevron arrows (`<` and `>`) placed on the middle-left and middle-right edges of the screen to indicate slide navigation.

### C. Typography Content
*   **Main Headline:**
    *   Text: **"BUILDING WORLDS, CRAFTING DREAMS!"**
    *   Style: Serif, large display size (`clamp(2.5rem, 5vw, 4.5rem)`), centered, uppercase, heavy drop shadow for contrast against the bright sun background.
*   **Subheadline:**
    *   Text: *"Dive into a world where imagination knows no bounds. Our team of visionary designers, developers and artists collaborate seamlessly to create games that transcend expectations."*
    *   Style: Sans-serif, max-width `600px`, centered, line-height `1.6`, color `#d1d5db`, font-size `14px`.

### D. Call to Action (CTA) Button
*   **Text:** **"JOIN OUR TEAM"**
*   **Style:** 
    *   Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 30px`).
    *   Background: Semi-transparent dark glass with a solid white or subtle gold border.
    *   Hover State: Background fills with solid white or gold (`#f0a500`), text color shifts to dark `#0d1117` with smooth transition (`0.3s ease`).

---

## 3. Animations & Interactions

*   **Page Load / Entrance Animation:**
    *   Hero text and background elements fade in with a slight upward translate (`transform: translateY(20px)` to `0`).
    *   Duration: `1.2s` with an `ease-out` timing function.
*   **Hover States:**
    *   Navigation items scale up slightly or increase text opacity on hover.
    *   CTA button triggers a smooth scaling effect (`scale(1.05)`) and color inversion.
*   **Background Parallax (Optional Polish):**
    *   Subtle mouse-move parallax effect where the mountain layers shift slightly slower than the cursor to create a 3D depth effect.
