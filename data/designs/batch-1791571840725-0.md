# Design Specification: "intelly" Medical Dashboard

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas

### Viewport & Scaling
- **Target Viewport:** Desktop application interface, optimized for 1440px width and above.
- **Layout Model:** Fixed-width container layout with a persistent dark sidebar on the left, a main content area in the center/left, and an interactive right-hand sidebar for calendar and timeline scheduling.
- **Full-Screen Implementation:** The UI spans the entire viewport width (`100vw`) and height (`100vh`) with a light background canvas and no outer device bezel frames.

### Color Palette
- **Canvas/Background:** Soft cream / warm off-white (`#F8F4EE` or `#F5F2EB`).
- **Primary Sidebar Background:** Deep charcoal / near black (`#141414` or `#121212`).
- **Accent Colors (Cards & Modules):**
  - **Patients Card (Yellow/Gold):** `#F3DE7C` base with darker yellow chart bars (`#D1B745`).
  - **Visits Summary (Pink/Magenta):** `#F3B2C9` base with deep magenta text and line charts (`#A83868`).
  - **By Condition (Green/Olive):** `#A3BA7C` base with dark olive green text.
  - **Sessions (Soft Blue):** `#9DBCE2` base with deep blue accents.
  - **Patient List Items (Soft Pastels):** Light pink (`#F7CEE4`), light blue (`#D4E4F7`), light peach (`#FBE2D1`), light green (`#D9F0D9`).
- **Typography Colors:**
  - Primary Headings: Deep charcoal (`#1A1A1A`).
  - Secondary Text: Muted gray-brown (`#6B6661`).
  - Interactive/Link States: Soft blue (`#4A7BC7`).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Font Family
- **Font Stack:** `"Inter", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
- **Fallback Strategy:** System UI sans-serif stack ensuring native rendering across macOS, Windows, and Linux.

### Type Scale & Hierarchy
- **Main Heading ("Good morning, Dr.Olivia"):** 
  - Size: `28px` / `32px` (Bold, `font-weight: 700`)
  - Color: `#1A1A1A`
- **Section Headers ("Patients:", "Visits summary:", "Patient's list", "Visit details"):**
  - Size: `18px` / `20px` (Semi-bold, `font-weight: 600`)
- **Card Metrics & Numbers:**
  - Size: `16px` to `22px` (Medium/Bold, `font-weight: 600`)
- **Body & Metadata Text:**
  - Size: `12px` to `14px` (Regular/Medium, `font-weight: 400` / `500`)
  - Color: `#5A5550`

---

## 3. Sidebar Navigation (Left Panel)

- **Background:** `#141414`
- **Width:** Fixed `240px`
- **Top Brand Area:**
  - Logo Text: `"intelly"` in a clean, modern geometric sans-serif typeface, lowercase.
  - Indicator: Small circular pink badge/dot on the right of the logo text.
- **Navigation Sections:**
  - **General Category Label:** Small uppercase muted text (`#7A7A7A`), `10px`.
  - **Nav Items (Icon + Label):**
    - 📊 Dashboard (Active state indicated by background highlight or distinct white text)
    - 📅 Schedule
    - 👥 Patients
    - 📈 Statistics & reports
    - 🎓 Education
    - 🔖 My articles
  - **Tools Category Label:** Small uppercase muted text.
  - **Tools Items:**
    - 💬 Chats & calls
    - 💳 Billing
    - 📁 Documents base
    - ⚙️ Settings
  - **Bottom Action:**
    - 🚪 Log out (positioned at the bottom with a distinct margin offset).

---

## 4. Top Header & Search Bar

- **Search Bar:**
  - Pill-shaped container with a light neutral background (`#ECE6DC`).
  - Left icon: Magnifying glass (`🔍`).

- **Border Radius:**

- **Images & Graphics:** All decorative charts, graph lines, and geometric status indicators are rendered as vector graphics or responsive containers maintaining precise color fills matching the specified palette.
