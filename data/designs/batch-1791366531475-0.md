# Product Landing Page Design Specification

## Global Styles

### Color Palette
- **Background (Base):** `#23252b` (Dark charcoal/grey)
- **Primary Text:** `#ffffff` (White)
- **Secondary / Muted Text:** `#70737d` (Medium grey)
- **Accent (Brand/CTA):** `#ff3b50` (Bright red-orange)
- **Accent Dark (Transparent Overlay/Backgrounds):** `#181a1f`

### Typography
- **Font Family:** Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif (Clean sans-serif)
- **Logo (Brand Market):**
  - "BRAND": `14px`, Bold (700), White (`#ffffff`), letter-spacing: `1px`
  - "MARKET": `14px`, Bold (700), Accent Red (`#ff3b50`), letter-spacing: `1px`
- **Product Title:**
  - `24px` / `28px` line-height, Bold (700), White (`#ffffff`)
- **Price:**
  - `18px`, Medium (500), Muted grey (`#70737d`)
- **Carousel Pagination / Meta (027):**
  - `14px`, Medium (500), White (`#ffffff`)
- **Social Links (FB, TW, IN):**
  - `10px`, Medium (500), Muted grey (`#70737d`)
- **CTA Button Text:**
  - `11px`, Bold (700), White (`#ffffff`), uppercase, letter-spacing: `0.5px`

---

## Layout & Structure

### Viewport Container
- **Dimensions:** Full-width viewport (`100vw` width, `100vh` height), centered flex/grid container with dark ambient background.
- **Border Radius:** `0px` (Full screen)

---

## Header Section

- **Position:** Absolute / Fixed at top, padding: `24px 40px` (scaled relative to full screen).
- **Left Element (Hamburger & Logo):**
  - Hamburger Menu Icon (`3 lines`): White (`#ffffff`), `16px` width, spacing: `16px` right to logo.
  - Brand Logo: Stacked or inline typography "BRAND MARKET" (BRAND in white, MARKET in red).
- **Right Element (Cart):**
  - Shopping Cart Icon: Circular container (`32px x 32px`), background color `#2b2d34`, icon color `#ffffff`.
  - Notification Badge: Small red dot (`8px x 8px`) with white number `1` inside, positioned top-right of the cart icon.

---

## Main Content Area

### Left Column / Product Details (Bottom-Left Alignment)
- **Position:** Absolute, anchored near bottom-left (`left: 80px`, `bottom: 60px`).
- **Elements:**
  1. **Product Name:** 
     - "Y-3 ADIDAS" (Line 1)
     - "YOHJI YAMAMOTO" (Line 2)
     - Font Size: `24px`, Weight: `700`, Color: `#ffffff`
  2. **Product Index:**
     - `"027"` displayed above social links, Size: `14px`, Color: `#ffffff`
  3. **Social Media Links:**
     - Horizontal layout: `FB`, `TW`, `IN`
     - Font Size: `10px`, Color: `#70737d`, spacing: `12px`

### Central Product Showcase (Hero)
- **Hero Image (Shoe):**
  - High-detail product render of the **Y-3 Yohji Yamamoto High-Top Sneaker** (Black colorway with white sole accents, utility strap details).
  - Positioned centrally, casting a subtle soft drop shadow onto the dark surface below.
- **Background Typography (Watermark):**
  - Massive background text `"Y-3"` styled in dark, subtle charcoal (`#1d1f25`) occupying the right-center background behind the secondary shoe angle/elements.
- **Secondary Image / Graphic Overlay (Right):**
  - Secondary semi-transparent cropped view of the shoe heel/branding overlapping the background typography.
  - Overlay Tag: Pill-shaped badge reading `adidas | YOHJI YAMAMOTO` in white text on a dark backdrop.
  - Signature Graphic: White handwritten script signature of *"Yohji Yamamoto"* positioned dynamically beneath the badge.

---

## Interactive Elements & Controls

### Left Pagination Dots
- **Position:** Vertical stack, placed mid-left (`left: 40px`, centered vertically).
- **Structure:** 
  - 5 vertical dots representing carousel items.
  - Active dot: Red (`#ff3b50`), slightly larger/highlighted.
  - Inactive dots: Muted grey (`#70737d`).

### Call to Action (CTA) Button ("ADD TO CART")
- **Position:** Aligned to the right of the product title block (`bottom: 60px`, `left: 360px`).
- **Dimensions:** Height: `36px`, Padding: `0 24px`, Border-radius: `18px` (Pill shape).
- **Background:** Accent Red (`#ff3b50`).
- **Text:** `"ADD TO CART"` (`11px`, Bold, White).

### Price Display
- **Position:** Directly to the right of the CTA button.
- **Text:** `"$ 555"` (`18px`, Medium, Muted Grey `#70737d`).
