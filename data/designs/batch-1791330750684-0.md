# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist & Technical UI Showcase.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with an asymmetric editorial grid layout. Designed to emphasize clean geometric typography alongside complex mathematical visualization (DNA wave patterns).
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, single-surface architectural layout on a pristine neutral background with crisp high-contrast dark typography.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-contrast editorial typography, monochrome vector wave graphics, high-tracking pill badges.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#0A0A0A`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
