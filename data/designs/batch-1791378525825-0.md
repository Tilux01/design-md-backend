## 1. Global Layout & Structure (Full Screen Viewport)

### 1.1 Responsive Breakpoints & Viewport Framework
- **Primary Design Canvas:** Desktop 1440px viewport (optimized for fluid scale down to 1024px tablet, and 375px mobile).
- **Page Container Max-Width:** `1280px` centered with automated side margins (`0 auto`).
- **Background Engine:**
  - **Dark Zones:** Pitch black/dark charcoal (#0d0e12) providing dramatic contrast for macro food photography.
  - **Light Zones:** Clean, breathable pure white (#ffffff) or near-white surface (#fbfbfd) framing clean editorial copy.

### 1.2 Layout Grid Engine
- **Columns:** 12-column fluid grid system with a standard gutter width of `24px` on desktop and `16px` on mobile.
- **Vertical Rhythm (Whitespace Strategy):**
  - Section vertical padding: `120px` top and bottom for major focal sections.
  - Element spacing: Controlled via an 8pt baseline scale (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `48px`, `64px`, `96px`).

## 4. Component-by-Component Blueprint

### 4.1 Global Navigation Bar (Header)
- **Dimensions:** Fixed or Sticky top header, height `80px`.
- **Layout Structure:**
  - **Left:** Brand Logo (Icon mark + "sushi" typographic logo in custom vector format, primary color `#f15a24`).
  - **Center:** Navigation Links (`Home`, `Menu`, `About`, `Chefs`, `Reservation`, `Contact`). Font: 14px, medium weight, color `#a0a3af` changing to `#ffffff` on hover. Active indicator: subtle underline or dot.
  - **Right:** Primary Action Button ("Book a Table" or "Order Now"). Pill shape, background `#f15a24`, text color `#ffffff`.
- **Interactions:** Hover states scale buttons by `1.02` transform scale with smooth ease-out transitions (`0.2s`).

---

### 4.2 Hero Section (Dark Surface)
- **Layout:** Two-column split layout.
- **Left Column (Content):**
  - Main Heading: *“Hush Modern Restaurant”*
  - Supporting Copy: 2-3 lines of descriptive editorial copy introducing the culinary art style.
  - Call-to-Action: Primary pill button ("Explore Menu").
- **Right Column (Visual Montage):**
  - Dynamic overlapping layout showcasing high-resolution macro food photography of salmon nigiri, dragon rolls, and raw fish plates on black slate platters.
  - Subtle graphic splashes (textured brush strokes or dynamic paint splatters) framing the edges of the imagery.

---

### 4.3 Section: Curated Menu / Featured Plates
- **Section Heading:** *"Our Menu Selections"*
- **Layout Grid:** 3-column card layout on a dark background.
- **Card Specs:**
  - **Background:** Surface Dark (`#16181f`) with rounded corners (`16px` border-radius).
  - **Image container:** Aspect ratio 4:3, featuring rounded top borders.
  - **Content wrapper:** Contains Item Title (H3), Ingredients subtext, Price indicator in Vibrant Coral (`#f15a24`), and an add/view interaction button.
- **Interactions:** Cards lift upward by `4px` with a subtle drop shadow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)`).

### 4.4 Section: Interactive Customizer ("Make to Order Your Sushi Creation")
- **Background:** Dark slate backdrop (`#0d0e12`).
- **Layout:** Split layout featuring instructional copy on the left and a live-rendering product display on the right (plate of custom sushi rolls styled with microgreens and wooden chopsticks).
- **Features:** Clean typographic guidance, step-by-step indicator tags, and a prominent call-to-action button to initiate the customizer wizard.

### 4.5 Section: Light Theme Integration / Contact Details
- **Background Transition:** Shifts cleanly to pure white (`#ffffff`) or light off-white.
- **Layout:** Multi-column layout housing contact cards, operating hours, interactive form elements, and tabular lists.
- **Footer Section:** Dark background returns to anchor the page bounds, featuring social media channels, copyright information, and secondary navigation links.

## 5. Animation, Motion, and Micro-Interactions

### 5.1 Scroll-Triggered Transitions (Framer Motion / CSS Scroll-Driven)
- **Hero Reveal:** Elements fade up (`opacity: 0` to `1`, `translateY: 30px` to `0`) with a staggered delay of `0.15s` per element.
- **Image Parallax:** Macro food images scale slightly on scroll (`scale(1.0` to `1.05`) to create depth.

### 5.2 Hover States & Feedback
- **Buttons:** Background color shifts `10%` lighter on hover; scale transform `transform: scale(1.03)`.
- **Navigation Links:** Color transition from secondary muted gray (`#a0a3af`) to pure white (`#ffffff`) with a `0.2s ease` transition duration.
- **Cards:** Elevation shift using smooth CSS `box-shadow` property interpolation.
