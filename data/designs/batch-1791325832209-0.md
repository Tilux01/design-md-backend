# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Cinematic Editorial / Gothic Cinematic Platform (Vigil app interface, featuring atmospheric stone masonry textures, moonlit night sky, warm torchlight glow, and high-end multimedia publication layout).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Highly balanced atmospheric density. Features immersive background photography/renders overlaid with clean glassmorphic panels, generous padding, and razor-sharp typographic hierarchy.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-layered depth using deep obsidian backgrounds (`#0B0E14`), translucent dark glass surfaces (`rgba(11, 14, 20, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), subtle burnished bronze borders, and warm golden radial lighting gradients (`rgba(255, 170, 85, 0.15)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient atmospheric fog vignettes, high-tracking cinematic uppercase headers, subtle amber accent borders, glassmorphic dropdown cards, and interactive chapter cards.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `6px 14px`, font-size: `12px`, font-weight: `600`, tracking: `0.1em`, border-radius: `9999px`, background: `rgba(14, 18, 25, 0.8)`, border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Sparkle / Portal Indicator (⎔ / ✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-brand inline-block"></span>`.
