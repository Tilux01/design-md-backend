# Design Specification: "GLOW" NFT Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport
* **Display Mode:** Full-screen responsive web design (100% width, 100% viewport height).
* **Base Background Color:** Deep teal/petrol blue gradient `#051C24` at the bottom/edges transitioning into a slightly lighter petrol green `#0A2D38` at the center.
* **Layout Structure:** 
  * **Header:** Fixed or absolute positioning at the top with logo on the far left and navigation links on the far right.
  * **Hero Section:** Centered composition featuring a large split-text typography ("GL" and "OW"), a central circular graphic element with rotating text, and a 3D humanoid character intersecting the layers.
  * **Footer/Side Elements:** Minimalist filler text block on the bottom-left; pagination dots/indicators on the bottom-right.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Heading Font (GL & OW)
* **Font Family:** Geometric, ultra-bold, futuristic sans-serif (e.g., *Montserrat (Black)*, *Orbitron (Black)*, or *Syne (Extra Bold)* with custom letter modifications).
* **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Orbitron', 'Montserrat', sans-serif;`
* **Style:** Uppercase, heavy weight (900), wide tracking.
* **Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).

### Secondary/Circular Text Font
* **Font Family:** Clean, geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Space Grotesk*, or *Syne*).
* **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Space Grotesk', 'Inter', sans-serif;`
* **Style:** Uppercase, tracked out, curved along an SVG path in a circle.
* **Color:** Semi-transparent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`).

### Body & Navigation Font
* **Font Family:** Modern humanist sans-serif (e.g., *Inter* or *Roboto*).
* **Fallback Stack:** `font-family: 'Inter', sans-serif;`
* **Weight:** Regular (400) for body text; Medium (500) for navigation.
* **Color:** Muted light grey (`#A3B8C2`).

## 4. Hero Section & Central Composition

### A. Background Graphic Elements
* **Central Ring:** 
  * A thin, elegant white/light-cyan circular stroke (`border` or `SVG path`).
  * **Circular Text:** The phrase *"ENTER GLOW - THE FUTURE OF DIGITAL ART & COLLECTIBLES"* loops along the top and bottom of the circular path in a smaller, tracked-out font.

### B. Split Typography ("GL" and "OW")
* **Placement:** Positioned horizontally across the center, partially overlaid by the central character graphic.
* **Left Text ("GL"):** Aligned towards the left-center of the screen, just overlapping the left edge of the central circle.
* **Right Text ("OW"):** Aligned towards the right-center, overlapping the right edge of the central circle.

### C. Central Character Image (Visual Description & Placement)
* **Description of Image:** A high-end 3D digital render of a futuristic humanoid/cyborg character. The character has a smooth, metallic teal/blue head and face, almond-shaped glowing red/pink eyes, and pointed organic-mechanical ears. The neck and upper torso feature dark, reflective metallic plating accented with glowing neon-green lights on the collarbone area and fiery orange-yellow energy/light trails that sweep up and around the head like horns or a glowing halo crown.
* **Image Properties:**
  * **Background:** Transparent (PNG or WebP format with alpha channel).
  * **Placement:** Dead center of the viewport, vertically and horizontally.
  * **Layering (Z-Index):** Positioned *in front* of the circular text ring and *behind* or *intersecting* the "GL" and "OW" typography layers to create a 3D depth effect.
  * **Rotation/Transform:** Upright, facing directly forward.
