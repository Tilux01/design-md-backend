# System Design Specification: Jason Martin Portfolio Website

This document provides the exact frontend design specifications to implement a high-fidelity, pixel-perfect clone of the three-screen portfolio website for **Jason Martin, Graphic Designer**.

---

## 1. Global Specifications

### 1.1 Canvas & Layout
* **Dimensions**: Full-screen sections intended for a single-page application (SPA) with snap-scrolling (e.g., `scroll-snap-type: y mandatory`) or as individual pages. Each section must take exactly `100vh` and `100vw`.
* **Container Padding**: 
  * Left and Right: `8%` to `10%` to keep the content aligned perfectly across sections.
  * Top: Dedicated space for the fixed navigation header (`~80px` height).

### 1.2 Color Palette
* **Primary Dark Background**: `#0b0e14` (Deep slate charcoal black).
* **Text / Light Accents**: `#ffffff` (Pure White).
* **Secondary / Muted Text**: `#8a95a5` (Muted cool-gray).
* **Primary Cyan Accent**: `#00a8e8` (Vibrant cyan/light blue used for active states, underlines, and highlight circles).
* **Timeline Circle Dark**: `#1a2332` (Muted dark blue-gray circle background for non-active timeline badges).

### 1.3 Typography
* **Primary Headings & Accent Text**: Montserrat
  * *Alternative / Fallbacks*: System-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.
* **Body Text**: Inter or Roboto (for clean legibility at smaller sizes).
* **Font Weights**:
  * Light: `300`
  * Regular: `400`
  * Medium: `500`
  * Bold: `700`
  * Black: `900`

## 2. Global Header & Navigation
This header persists at the top of all three sections.

* **Layout**: Flexbox container (`justify-content: space-between; align-items: center;`).
* **Height**: `80px`.
* **Left-aligned Links**:
  * Horizontal list of navigation items: `Home`, `About`, `Resume`, `Portfolio`.
  * **Active State**: The active link for the current section is colored in **Primary Cyan Accent** (`#00a8e8`), while inactive items are colored in **Muted Cool-Gray** (`#8a95a5`) or **Pure White** with a transition effect on hover.
* **Right-aligned Contact Info**:
  * Phone number: `774-555-3021` accompanied by a small phone icon outline (or unicode phone symbol `📞` styled to match). Text color: **Muted Cool-Gray** (`#8a95a5`).

## 3. Section 1: Hero / Home Page

### 3.1 Background Image
* **Description**: A highly professional, low-light image of a dark modern workspace.
* **Key Elements**:
  * A dark wooden desk.
  * An glowing monitor on the right showing a vibrant mountain landscape with a cyan/blue tone.
  * A metallic laptop (MacBook style), a white wireless keyboard, and a mouse in front of it.
  * A small potted succulent plant next to the monitor.
  * A desk lamp casting soft downlight.
* **Implementation**: Background cover (`background-size: cover; background-position: center;`). Apply a dark overlay with `linear-gradient(rgba(11, 14, 20, 0.85), rgba(11, 14, 20, 0.85))` to ensure high text contrast.

### 3.2 Hero Text & CTA Block
* **Positioning**: Center-left vertical alignment (`display: flex; flex-direction: column; justify-content: center;`).
* **HTML Structure**:
  * `<h1>` containing:
    * `<span>JASON</span>` (Font size: `4.5rem`, Weight: `500`, Color: `#ffffff`, Letter-spacing: `normal`).
    * `<br>`
    * `<span>MARTIN</span>` (Font size: `6.5rem`, Weight: `900`, Color: `#ffffff`, Letter-spacing: `1px`, Line-height: `0.9`).
  * `<h2>Graphic Designer</h2>` (Font size: `1.8rem`, Weight: `400`, Color: `#ffffff`, Letter-spacing: `12px`, Text-transform: `uppercase`, Margin-top: `15px`).
* **CTA Buttons**:
  * Flex row with a `20px` gap.
  * **Button 1 (Resume)**: Rounded capsule shape (`border-radius: 50px`). Transparent background with a thin white border (`1px solid #ffffff`). Color: `#ffffff`.
  * **Button 2 (Portofolio)**: Identical styling to Button 1. Note the literal spelling on the mockup: `"Portofolio"` (or correct to `"Portfolio"` depending on preference).

### 3.3 Bottom-Left Social Icons

* **Icons (White, height ~18px, spaced 20px apart)**:

## 6. Interaction & Responsive Behaviors

### 6.1 Animations
* **Hover Transitions**:
  * Nav links: Transition color from `#8a95a5` to `#ffffff` over `0.2s` ease.
  * CTA buttons: Invert colors on hover (solid white background with dark `#0b0e14` text).
  * Skill sliders: Tooltips showing precise percentages (e.g., "95%") appear directly above the knob when hovered.

### 6.2 Responsive Design Breakpoints
* **Desktop (Default)**: 3-column layout on the Resume page.
* **Tablet (1024px and below)**:
  * Resume Page converts to a 2-column layout (Column 1 and Column 2 side-by-side, Column 3 wraps beneath).
  * Hero Section scales typography sizes down by `15%`.
* **Mobile (768px and below)**:
  * Navigation links compress into a responsive mobile menu or hamburger button.
  * All 3-column resume components stack into a single column (`width: 100%`).
  * About section portrait collapses to be a background element or stacks vertically beneath the text.
