# Design Specification: MyPlayAI Music Interface

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Overall Container:** Full-screen application.
*   **Theme:** Hybrid. A dark-themed sidebar (Glassmorphism/Frosted Glass effect) paired with a clean, light-themed main content area.
*   **Border Radius:** Highly rounded corners (24px to 32px) for all major containers and cards.
*   **Background:** The application itself should be full-screen. The sidebar uses a semi-transparent dark charcoal, while the main content area is solid white.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `SF Pro Display`.
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:** 
    *   Regular (400) for secondary text and metadata.
    *   Medium (500) for navigation items.
    *   Bold (700) for headers and "Now Playing" titles.
*   **Colors:**
    *   Primary Text (Light Mode): `#1A1A1A`
    *   Secondary Text (Light Mode): `#757575`
    *   Sidebar Text: `#FFFFFF` (Active) / `#A0A0A0` (Inactive)
    *   Accent Color: `#D4E95E` (Lime Green)

## 3. Sidebar (Left Navigation)
*   **Width:** Approximately 20% of screen width.
*   **Background:** Dark Charcoal (`#2C2C2C`) with a subtle blur effect.
*   **Top Left:** Three window control dots (Red, Yellow, Green) horizontally aligned.
*   **Section 1: Library**
    *   Header: "Library" (White, Semi-bold, 18px).
    *   Items: Browse, Songs, Albums, Artists, Radio.
    *   **Active State:** "Browse" has a pill-shaped background (`#3D3D3D`) and a Lime Green (`#D4E95E`) dot/indicator.
*   **Section 2: My Music**
    *   Header: "My music" (White, Semi-bold, 18px).
    *   Items: Recently Played, Favorite Songs, Local File.

## 4. Main Content Area (Center)
*   **Background:** Solid White (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Top Navigation Bar:**
    *   Breadcrumbs: `Artists > Top 2023` (Light Gray).
    *   Center Tabs: "New Releases" (Active - Black text), "New Feed", "Shuffle Play".
    *   Right Icons: Search (Magnifying glass), Settings (Gear), Notifications (Bell), Profile Picture (Circular, 32px).

### A. Featured Banner (Curated Playlist)
*   **Background:** A horizontal gradient ranging from deep burnt orange (`#8B4513`) to a lighter copper.
*   **Content:**
    *   Label: "CURATED PLAYLIST" (Small, uppercase, white).
    *   Title: "BLINDING LIGHT" (Large, Bold, White).
    *   Description: 2-line summary about vivid emotions.
    *   Stats: Heart icon, "83,012 Likes", "18 Songs, 39 min 43 sec".
*   **Image Asset:** A high-resolution cutout of **The Weeknd** wearing red suit and sunglasses. He is positioned on the right side of the banner, facing left. The image is integrated into the banner with a soft shadow.

### B. Popular Artists Section
*   **Header:** "Popular artists" with a "See all" link on the right.
*   **Layout:** Five circular avatars with names underneath.
*   **Image Descriptions:**
    1.  **Tyler, The Creator:** Black and white photo, wearing a light-colored ushanka hat.
    2.  **21 Savage:** Black and white high-contrast profile shot, looking down.
    3.  **6ix9ine:** Color photo, rainbow hair visible, face tattoos, white hoodie.
    4.  **Travis Scott:** Color photo, head tilted back, white shirt, dark background.
    5.  **OBLADAET:** Color photo, wearing a white t-shirt, indoor setting with warm lighting.

### C. Recently Played Section
*   **Header:** "Recently played" with "See all" link.
*   **Table Layout:**
    *   Row 1: "Mr. Right Now" by Metro Boomin. Duration: 3:21. Plus icon at end.
    *   Row 2: "Many Men" by 21 Savage. Duration: 2:29. Plus icon at end.
*   **Thumbnail:** Small square album art (24px radius).

## 5. Right Sidebar (Now Playing)
*   **Container:** Light gray/off-white card with a subtle drop shadow.
*   **Header:** "Now Playing" with a soundwave icon.
*   **Main Album Art:** Large square (approx 250px) with 24px border radius.
    *   **Image Description:** A cinematic shot of a person in a bright red jumpsuit walking towards a building entrance with a green-tinted door. The building is beige/grey concrete.
*   **Track Info:** "Snowfall" (Bold) by "Oneheart" (Regular).
*   **Queue List:**
    1.  "Therapy" - Oneheart (2:02)
    2.  "Apathy" - Tezce (2:02)
    3.  "Look I'm here" - Oneheart (2:02)

## 6. Bottom Player Bar
*   **Background:** Dark Charcoal (`#2C2C2C`) with 40px border radius. Floating above the bottom edge.
*   **Left Side:** Small album art (Snowfall), Track Name, Artist Name.
*   **Center Controls:**
    *   Back, Play/Pause (Lime Green Circle), Forward.
    *   **Visualizer:** A dynamic yellow-green (`#D4E95E`) waveform representing the audio frequencies.
*   **Right Side:** Timestamp (03:12), Shuffle icon, Repeat icon, Volume icon.

## 7. Iconography & Assets
*   **Icons:** Use a thin-stroke SVG library (like Lucide or Feather Icons).
*   **Images:** 
    *   All artist avatars must be perfectly circular (`border-radius: 50%`).
    *   All album art must have a consistent `border-radius: 12px`.
    *   The "The Weeknd" banner image is a **transparent PNG cutout** layered over the orange gradient background.
    *   The profile picture (top right) is a circular crop of a female face.

## 8. Color Palette Summary
*   **Accent Lime:** `#D4E95E`
*   **Sidebar Dark:** `#2C2C2C`
*   **Main White:** `#FFFFFF`
*   **Text Primary:** `#1A1A1A`
*   **Text Muted:** `#8E8E8E`
*   **Banner Gradient:** `linear-gradient(90deg, #632E07 0%, #B35D1E 100%)`
