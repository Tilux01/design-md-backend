# Design System & Specification: Minimalist Fashion Landing Page

## 1. Overview & General Layout
* **Canvas Style**: Edge-to-edge, minimalist, warm editorial aesthetic.
* **Layout Grid**: 12-column responsive grid with ~80px horizontal padding on desktop.
* **Background**: Clean solid off-white (`#FBFBFB` or `#FFFFFF`).

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Primary Background**: `#FDFDFD`
* **Text / Primary Dark**: `#222222`
* **Text / Secondary Muted**: `#707070`
* **Borders / Separators**: `#E0E0E0`
* **Accent / Dark Navy (Clothing)**: `#1F2E3D`

---

## 3. Typography
* **Primary Sans-Serif**: `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, Arial, sans-serif.
  * **Weights**: 300 (Light), 400 (Regular), 600 (Semi-Bold), 700 (Bold).
* **Hierarchy**:
  * **Brand Logo**: 20px, Bold (700), uppercase tracking +1.5px.
  * **Nav Links**: 13px, Regular (400), muted dark.
  * **Hero Header ("Autumn Vibes")**: 56px–64px, Light to Regular (300–400), line-height 1.15.
  * **Section Header ("Utero's Philosophy")**: 28px–32px, Regular (400).
  * **Body / Taglines**: 13px–15px, Regular (400), line-height 1.6, muted `#707070`.

---

## 4. Header / Navigation
* **Placement**: Top fixed/sticky bar, 70px–80px height.
* **Left**: Brand logo text `"UTERO"`.
* **Center**: Horizontal menu links (`Home`, `Shop`, `About Us`, `Blog`), spaced 24px apart.
* **Right**: Icons for Search, User Profile, and Cart (with badge/text showing item counter and balance, e.g., `$0.00 / 0`).

---

## 5. Hero Section (Split Layout)

### Left Column: Text & Interaction
* **Category Tag**: 
  * Text: `"AUTUMN / COLLECTION / 2021"`
  * Style: 11px, uppercase, stacked/spaced lines, color `#8A8A8A`.
* **Headline**: 
  * Text: `"Autumn\nVibes"`
  * Style: Large, light weight, two lines.
* **Body Copy**: 
  * Text: `"Casual Style - Pure design. 100% natural textiles guaranteed all-day comfort."`
* **CTA Button**: 
  * Label: `"Shop Now"`
  * Style: Transparent fill, 1px solid `#CCCCCC` border, rounded corners (radius: 6px), 12px text, 10px 24px padding.
* **Pagination**: Small muted index indicator (`01` or pagination dot) placed lower left.

### Right Column: Feature Portrait
* **Mask / Geometry**: 
  * Custom curved frame: Flat at top and right; wide circular bottom-left radius (`border-bottom-left-radius: 50%` or SVG path mask).
* **Image Description**:
  * **Subject**: Young male model leaning casually with left hand raised behind his head, directly facing camera.
  * **Attire**: Dark slate-blue / navy knit crewneck sweater.
  * **Background**: Studio light-gray backdrop matching page tones (not transparent, but softly blended).
  * **Rotation / Orientation**: 0° (vertical orientation, chest-up crop).

---

## 6. Secondary Section: Brand Philosophy (Split Layout)

### Left Column: Circular Feature Visual
* **Mask / Geometry**: Perfect circle crop (`border-radius: 50%`), ~320px diameter, positioned partially in frame.
* **Image Description**:
  * **Subject**: Waist-level shot of a person carrying minimalist shopping bags or natural-fiber garments.
  * **Attire**: Earth-toned, warm beige/camel wool jacket with textured beige/white packaging.
  * **Lighting**: Natural warm daylight.

### Right Column: Copy
* **Sub-label**: Thin horizontal rule or small category kicker.
* **Headline**: `"Utero's Philosophy"`, 32px regular weight.
* **Tagline/Quote**: `"There's More Than Equal Fashioned"` (or brand statement), 14px, muted gray.
