# Design Specification: Wireless Headphones Landing Page

## 1. Overview & Theme
A dark-mode, minimalist e-commerce landing page featuring high-end wireless headphones.
- **Background**: Solid deep black (`#070707`) with a subtle glossy ground reflection.
- **Base Grid**: 2-column layout (Left: Product info & micro-catalog; Right: Large hero 3D visual).

---

## 2. Color Palette
- **Background**: `#070707`
- **Primary Text**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Secondary/Muted Text**: `#8E8E8E`
- **Accent/Button Background**: Dusty Rose / Muted Mauve (`#B87D7D`)
- **Accent Button Text**: `#2A1616`
- **Borders & Dividers**: `#1F1F1F`

---

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Font**: `Montserrat` or `Poppins`
- **Fallback Font**: `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `sans-serif`
- **Styles**:
  - **Logo**: 13px, Bold, Uppercase, tracked (`letter-spacing: 2px`).
  - **Nav Links**: 11px, Semi-bold, Uppercase, tracked (`letter-spacing: 1.5px`).
  - **Hero Title**: 56px–64px, Line-height 1.1. Line 1 semi-bold (`Headphones`), Line 2 extra-bold (`Wireless`).
  - **Hero Subtitle**: 12px, Regular, muted grey, max-width ~380px.
  - **Button**: 11px, Bold, Uppercase, tracked (`letter-spacing: 1.5px`).
  - **Section Label**: 10px, Bold, Uppercase, tracked (`letter-spacing: 2px`).
  - **Micro-Catalog**: 11px Bold for price (`#FFFFFF`); 9px Regular for labels (`#8E8E8E`).

---

## 4. Navigation Bar
- **Position**: Top, full width, padded (`40px 60px`).
- **Left**: Circle-dot icon followed by `PLAYING / MARKET`.
- **Right Center**: Nav links horizontal list with equal gaps: `INICIO`, `TIENDA`, `CATEGORÍAS`, `BLOG`, `CONTACTO`.
- **Far Right**: Minimal shopping basket icon (white stroke).

---

## 5. Hero Section (Left Column)
- **Content Alignment**: Left-aligned, vertical spacing ~24px.
- **Header**:
  ```text
  Headphones
  Wireless
  ```
- **Description**:
  > "Mejores efectos de sonido y una mejor experiencia auditiva. Tiene 3 modos de ecualización con los que puede cambiar libremente."
- **CTA Button**:
  - Label: `IR A LA TIENDA`
  - Dimensions: ~130px × 40px, sharp corners (border-radius: `0px` to `2px`).
  - Color: Dusty Rose (`#B87D7D`).

---

## 6. Featured Products (Bottom-Left)
- **Top Border**: Full-width thin horizontal line (`#1F1F1F`) spanning across the bottom section.
- **Label**: `ARTÍCULOS DESTACADOS` placed right above items.
- **Product Row**: 3 items aligned horizontally.
  - **Item 1**: Thumbnail (Rose Gold) | Text: `$55,00` / `Oro rosa`
  - **Item 2**: Thumbnail (Navy/Blue) | Text: `$65,00` / `Blue`
  - **Item 3**: Thumbnail (Purple/Magenta) | Text: `$55,00` / `Púrpura`

---

## 7. Imagery & Assets

### Main Hero Image
- **Description**: Premium metallic rose gold/bronze over-ear headphones with soft-padded earcups, textured fabric headband, and polished metal hinges.
- **Format**: Backgroundless (PNG/WebP with alpha channel).
- **Placement & Rotation**:
  - Right hemisphere of the screen, vertically centered.
  - Angled in a three-quarter perspective (~35° rotation), tilted slightly forward.
  - Bottom area features a soft vertical mirror reflection fading into the `#070707` floor.

### Thumbnail Images
- **Description**: Three isolated front-facing miniature headphones matching each colorway (`Oro rosa`, `Blue`, `Púrpura`).
- **Placement**: Scaled to ~40px wide, positioned left of their respective price tags.

---

## 8. Footer & Utility Elements
- **Floating Chat Support**: Bottom-right corner (`bottom: 30px`, `right: 40px`), thin white outline of a WhatsApp/chat dialogue bubble.
