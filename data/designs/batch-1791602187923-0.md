## 7. Interactive Micro-Interactions
1. **Cards:** On hover, light feature cards transition background from `#F5F5F5` to `#EBEBEB` with a `200ms ease` animation. The top-right arrow (`↗`) translates `2px` right and `-2px` top.
2. **Buttons:** Pill buttons scale down slightly (`scale(0.98)`) on active click and invert colors on hover (`#0D0D0D` background becomes white text, and vice versa).
3. **Smooth Scroll:** Enabled globally for navigation links.
