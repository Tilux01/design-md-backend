# Bayt Travel - UI Design Specification

## 1. Global Styles

### 1.1. Color Palette
*   **Hero Gradient (Sky):** A smooth vertical gradient.
    *   Top: Deep, muted grayish-purple/mauve (approx. `#6A5C64` or heavily darkened peach).
    *   Middle: Warm, glowing orange/peach (approx. `#F59B71`).
    *   Bottom: Soft, pale peach/off-white (approx. `#FDF1EA`). This bottom color continues solidly down the rest of the page.
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   `#FFFFFF` (White) - Used in the top navigation.
    *   `#111111` (Near Black) - Main headings, bold text in the search bar.
    *   `#777777` (Medium Gray) - Subtext in the search bar, navigation inactive states.
*   **UI Elements:**
    *   `#FFFFFF` (White) - Search bar background, card overlay pills.
    *   `#000000` (Black) - Search action button.
    *   `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` - Semi-transparent white for borders/dividers in the header.

### 1.2. Typography
*   **Primary Font (Sans-Serif):** Used for navigation, UI elements, search bar, and buttons.
    *   *Recommended:* `Inter`, `Poppins`, or `SF Pro Display`.
    *   *Fallback:* `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Secondary Font (Serif):** Used exclusively for section headers to give a premium, editorial feel.
    *   *Recommended:* `Playfair Display`, `Lora`, or `Merriweather`.
    *   *Fallback:* `Georgia`, `Times New Roman`, `serif`.

### 1.3. Layout Constraints
*   **Viewport:** Full width (`100vw`), Full height (`100vh`).
*   **Max-Width Container:** The content (Navigation, Search Bar, Card Grid) is constrained to a central column (approx. `1200px` to `1440px` wide) with equal padding on the left and right (approx. `5%` or `80px`).

## 2. Component Breakdown

### 2.1. Header / Navigation (Absolute/Fixed over Hero)
*   **Placement:** Top of the screen, spanning full width. There is a thin, subtle white border (`1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`) separating the header from the hero area below it.
*   **Layout:** Flexbox, `justify-content: space-between`, vertically centered.
*   **Left Section:**
    *   **Logo Text:** "Bayt Travel" (Font: Sans-serif, Regular/Medium, White, approx 18px).
    *   **Divider:** A vertical, thin white line (`1px` width, approx `24px` height, opacity 50%).
    *   **Primary Links:**
        *   "Visitors" (Includes a small, white, downward-pointing chevron icon next to it).
        *   "Apartments"
        *   "Where to go"
        *   "Contact Us"
        *   *Styling:* Sans-serif, Light/Regular weight, approx 14px, White color with slight opacity (approx 80%). Generous spacing (gap) between links.
*   **Right Section:**
    *   **Text Link:** "Become a host" (Matches primary link styling).
    *   **Language/Region Icon:** A backgroundless white outline icon of a globe.
    *   **User Menu Pill:** A rounded rectangle (pill shape) with a thin white outline.
        *   Contains: A backgroundless white hamburger menu icon (three horizontal lines) on the left, and a small circular user avatar placeholder (grayish circle with a white silhouette) on the right.

### 2.2. Hero Section
*   **Background:** The gradient mentioned in 1.1.
*   **Hero Image (Cityscape):**
    *   **Description:** A high-quality photo of a modern city skyline (Doha, Qatar). It features tall, uniquely shaped skyscrapers (one twisting, one bullet-shaped).
    *   **Placement/Rotation:** Placed on the left side of the screen, extending slightly past the center. It sits behind the search bar. Rotation is 0 degrees (standard).
    *   **Style:** The image is *not* backgroundless; however, it fades smoothly at the bottom into the pale peach background color of the webpage, and fades at the top into the darker sky gradient. It also features a misty/foggy effect at the base of the buildings.
*   **Floating Search Bar:**
    *   **Placement:** Centered horizontally, overlapping the transition between the hero image and the solid background of the section below.
    *   **Container:** Large pill shape (fully rounded corners, e.g., `border-radius: 99px`), solid White (`#FFFFFF`) background, subtle soft drop shadow (`box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`).
    *   **Layout:** Flexbox, divided into 4 main input sections and 1 action button. Sections are separated by very subtle, vertical light gray divider lines.
    *   **Inputs (from left to right):**
        1.  **Location:** Backgroundless gray outline map-pin icon. Bold Text: "Qatar". Subtext: "Choose the destination".
        2.  **Check in:** Backgroundless gray outline calendar icon. Bold Text: "Check in". Subtext: "Add date".
        3.  **Check out:** Backgroundless gray outline calendar icon. Bold Text: "Check out". Subtext: "Add date".
        4.  **Visitors:** Backgroundless gray outline user/group icon. Bold Text: "Visitors". Subtext: "Add guests".
    *   **Input Styling:** Icons left-aligned. Text stacked (Bold primary text on top, smaller, lighter gray subtext on bottom).
    *   **Action Button:** A solid Black (`#000000`) circle placed on the far right inside the white pill. Inside the circle is a backgroundless white icon of a triangle pointing right (resembling a play button or a stylized arrow).

### 2.3. Content Section (Below Hero)
*   **Background:** Solid soft pale peach/off-white (`#FDF1EA`), seamlessly continuing from the bottom of the hero gradient.
*   **Section Header:**
    *   **Title:** "Recomended Places to Stay" *(Note: spelled exactly as in the image with one 'm', though grammatically it should be 'Recommended')*.
    *   **Typography:** Secondary Font (Serif), approx 28px-32px, Dark Gray/Black (`#111111`).
    *   **Action:** A small pill-shaped button on the far right reading "See All". It has a transparent background, a thin dark gray outline, and dark gray sans-serif text.
*   **Card Grid:**
    *   **Layout:** Horizontal flex or CSS Grid. The image shows 4 cards, with the 4th card partially cut off on the right edge, implying horizontal scrolling or a wider grid. Gap between cards is approx `20px`.
    *   **Card Structure (Uniform for all):**
        *   **Shape:** Rectangle with slightly rounded corners (approx `border-radius: 16px`).
        *   **Size:** Aspect ratio is roughly square or slightly taller than wide.
        *   **Images:** Each card has a full-cover background image (Not backgroundless).
            *   *Card 1 Image:* A luxury villa/house exterior at dusk with a vibrant, surreal pink/peach sky.
            *   *Card 2 Image:* A modern two-story luxury home exterior at twilight, warmly lit from inside, with a deep blue/purple sky.
            *   *Card 3 Image:* Abstract/modern interior or exterior detail, very light gray/white tones, looks like a sleek modern space.
            *   *Card 4 Image:* A darker interior shot showing a room with framed, illuminated artwork/posters on a dark wall.
        *   **Card Overlays (Absolute positioning inside the card):**
            *   **Top Left (Price):** A small white pill (`border-radius: 99px`) containing tiny sans-serif text.
                *   Card 1: "$1,300 / night"
                *   Card 2: "$1,200 / night"
                *   Card 3: "$170 / night"
                *   Card 4: "$120 / night"
                *   *Styling:* Bold price, lighter weight "/ night", dark text on white background.
            *   **Top Right (Favorite):** A small white perfect circle containing a tiny, backgroundless, light gray outline of a heart icon.
