# Design Specification: Cinematic Gaming Dashboard

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Theme:** Dark Cinematic / Glassmorphism.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Text:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
    *   **Secondary Text:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` (Muted White)
    *   **Accent Color (Selection):** `rgba(100, 149, 237, 0.5)` (Cornflower Blue glow/border)
    *   **Background Overlays:** `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)` (Linear gradients to ensure text readability)
*   **Typography:**
    *   **Primary Font:** **Inter** or **Segoe UI**.
    *   **Fallback Fonts:** System-ui, San Francisco, Roboto, Arial, sans-serif.
    *   **Weights:** Regular (400), Semi-Bold (600), Bold (700).

## 2. Layout Structure
The interface is a full-screen `1920x1080` (16:9) layout with no external borders or frames.

### A. Background Layer
*   **Image Description:** A high-resolution, cinematic wide-shot from the game *Death Stranding*. It features the protagonist (Sam Porter Bridges) in a dark, atmospheric environment. He is wearing a tactical suit and holding a glowing orange "Bridge Baby" (BB) pod in his arms. The lighting is moody, with the primary light source coming from the orange pod.
*   **Placement:** Full-screen cover (`background-size: cover`).
*   **Overlay:** A subtle dark gradient overlay from the bottom-left to the top-right to ensure the white text in the foreground remains legible.

### B. Header (Top Bar)
*   **User Profile (Top Left):** 
    *   Circular avatar image (approx. 40px diameter).
    *   Username text to the right of the avatar in Semi-Bold white.
*   **System Icons (Top Center-Right):** 
    *   Three minimalist icons (Search/Magnifying glass, Notifications/Bell, Settings/Gear).
*   **Clock (Top Right):** 
    *   Digital time (e.g., "12:57 PM") in Regular weight, aligned to the right margin.

### C. Navigation Menu (Sub-Header)
*   **Items:** Home, Store, **My games**, Apps, Library, Settings.
*   **Active State:** "My games" is encapsulated in a pill-shaped, semi-transparent grey button (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`) with white text.
*   **Inactive State:** Muted white text, no background.

### D. Game Carousel (Center)
A horizontal row of rectangular tiles with rounded corners (approx. 8px radius).
1.  **Tile 1 (Selected - Death Stranding):** 
    *   **Visual:** Portrait art of a female character with dark hair. 
    *   **Effect:** Scaled slightly larger than others. Surrounded by a soft blue outer glow and a thin blue border.
2.  **Tile 2 (Folder/Grid):** 
    *   A 2x2 grid containing four smaller game icons (Red Dead Redemption 2, Forza, etc.).
3.  **Tile 3 (Wasteland 3):** 
    *   Backgroundless PNG of a soldier in power armor against a blue/dark background.
4.  **Tile 4 (Ori and the Will of the Wisps):** 
    *   Art featuring a glowing white creature in a forest.
5.  **Tile 5 (Sekiro):** 
    *   Art featuring a shinobi with a sword.
6.  **Tile 6 (Hellblade):** 
    *   Close-up portrait of a female character with blue war paint.

### E. Game Details (Bottom Left)
Positioned directly below the selected "Death Stranding" tile.
*   **Title:** "Death Stranding" in large, Bold white font (approx. 32px).
*   **Stats Row:**
    *   **Time played:** "2h 11m" (Label in muted white, value in white).
    *   **Progress:** "2%" (Label in muted white, value in white).
    *   **Last Trophy:** A small trophy icon followed by the text "Master Builder".

### F. Footer (Bottom Left Corner)
Minimalist action prompts using controller-style icons:
*   (A) Play
*   (X) Search
*   (Options Icon) Menu

## 3. Image Assets & Specifics
*   **Background Image:** Must be a "clean" high-res still from *Death Stranding*. Sam should be positioned slightly to the right of the center to allow the "Game Details" text to breathe on the left.
*   **Game Tiles:** 
    *   All tiles should have a 1:1 or 4:5 aspect ratio.
    *   **Backgroundless Images:** The *Wasteland 3* and *Sekiro* tiles should use images where the character is the focus, with the background of the tile being a dark, solid, or blurred color to maintain the "floating" UI look.
*   **Selection Glow:** The glow around the active tile should use a `box-shadow` with a spread of 15px-20px using the color `rgba(100, 149, 237, 0.6)`.

## 4. Interaction Notes
*   **Focus:** When moving between tiles, the blue glow and the "Game Details" text should update dynamically to match the highlighted game.
*   **Transparency:** All UI panels (the navigation pill and game tiles) should utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)` to give a premium glass effect against the cinematic background.
