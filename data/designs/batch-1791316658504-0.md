# Design System & Technical Specification

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
* **Primary Mood:** Minimalist high-end tech, precision engineering, experimental neural-AI research.
* **Brand Archetype:** Elite deep-tech / Autonomous Intelligence house ("NeuralNova").
* **Visual Density:** Ultra-spacious, high-breathing-room whitespace balance with hyper-focal visual anchors.
* **Theme Balance:** Clean Light Mode surface container enclosed within an immersive deep-space/starfield ambient background. High contrast monochrome typography balanced with a striking hyper-glossy metallic blue central element.

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
* **Viewport Max-Width:** `1280px` centered container with rounded corner shell (`border-radius: 24px`).
* **Header / Nav Layout:** Flexbox row, space-between alignment.
  * Left: Brand logomark + wordmark (`gap: 10px`).
  * Right: Nav items cluster (`gap: 32px`).
* **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous whitespace. Navbar padding: `32px 48px`.

---

## 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Special Glyphs
* **Logo Mark:** Custom geometric italicized polygon glyph (`✦`-derived optical node) paired with crisp geometric wordmark `NeuralNova`.
* **Visual Anchor:** Central hyper-reflective liquid-chrome blue orb (`border-radius: 50%`) with high-gloss caustic reflections and ambient depth mapping.

## 8. Accessibility (a11y) & WCAG Contrast Audit
* **Primary Text (`#0F0F10`) on Light Surface (`#FAFAFA`):** Contrast ratio **16.4:1** (Exceeds WCAG AAA).
* **Muted Navigation Links (`#6B7280`) on Light Surface (`#FAFAFA`):** Contrast ratio **4.6:1** (Exceeds WCAG AA).
* **Focus State:** 
  ```css
  a:focus-visible, button:focus-visible {
    outline: 2px solid #004EEB;
    outline-offset: 4px;
    border-radius: 4px;
  }
  ```
