# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Neutral Clean Gray Interface / Minimalist System Canvas.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace focus, emphasizing neutral surface separation, balanced padding, and structural clarity.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat neutral gray canvas background with precise 1px subtle border definitions and minimal depth layering.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient glow orbs, subtle borders, neutral background tones.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `0.75rem`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.1)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.

