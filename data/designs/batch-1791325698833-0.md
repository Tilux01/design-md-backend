# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Ultra-Dark Glassmorphic Generative AI Workspace (Clean, Immersive, Spatial, Futuristic SaaS).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace-to-content ratio focusing optical attention entirely on the central generative conversational interface and ambient luminescent focal nodes.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-layered depth achieved through dark achromatic tonal steps, subtle inner borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), multi-stage backdrop blurring (`blur(24px)`), and top-down atmospheric spotlight gradients simulating overhead illumination.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Top-down atmospheric lighting gradients, glassmorphism surface panels, high-precision vector monoline icons, radial ambient spotlights.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding (`6px 12px`), font-size (`12px`), font-weight (`500`), tracking (`normal`), border-radius (`9999px`), background translucency (`rgba(255,255,255,0.05)`), border width (`1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`).
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Arrow Up (↑) -> Use `&#8593;` or inline SVG arrow.

# 7. Motion Mechanics, Framer Motion & CSS Animations
- **Motion Principles**: Spring physics for interactive elements (`stiffness: 300, damping: 25`), smooth cubic-bezier easing (`0.16, 1, 0.3, 1`) for workspace fade-ins.

