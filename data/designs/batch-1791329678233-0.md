# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: High-End FinTech Glassmorphism & Enterprise SaaS Platform.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace focus, organized into modular viewport cards with generous interior padding and glowing ambient floating orbs to emphasize spatial depth and high-tech product positioning.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered translucent surfaces featuring soft white and light-blue frosted glass panels (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), subtle crisp 1px borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`), and deep ambient drop-shadows (`box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges with pulsing status dots, ambient 3D gradient coin/sphere tokens floating across cards, translucent frosted glass overlays, and smooth subtle curved vector ribbons.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding: `6px 14px`, Font-size: `0.75rem`, Font-weight: `600`, Tracking: `0.08em`, Border-radius: `9999px`, Background translucency: `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08)`, Border width: `1px solid rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) $\rightarrow$ Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) $\rightarrow$ Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) $\rightarrow$ Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
