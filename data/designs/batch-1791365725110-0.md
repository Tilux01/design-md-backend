# Design Specification: Coffee Flavours Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Reset
- **Viewport**: Full-screen, responsive, centered design (max-width container simulating a modern web presentation, but scaling to full viewport width).
- **Background Palette**: 
  - Left panel: Soft greyish-beige (`#E3E1DE` to `#D6D3CF`) with a subtle angled striped/slatted shadow texture.
  - Right panel: Clean warm cream/off-white (`#F2EFEA`).
  - Overall canvas background (outside the design): Neutral dark gray (`#777777` to `#888888`) with subtle drop shadow for presentation realism.
- **Typography**:
  - Headings / Branding / Titles: Elegant serif typeface (e.g., *Playfair Display*, *Cinzel*, or *Bodoni*), high contrast, dark charcoal/black (`#1A1A1A`).
  - Body Text / Navigation / Buttons: Clean sans-serif typeface (e.g., *Inter*, *Roboto*, or *Helvetica*), lowercase for specific UI elements, muted dark grey (`#555555`).

---

## 2. Layout Structure
- **Grid / Split Screen**: 2-column layout dividing the viewport cleanly down the middle (50% / 50% split on desktop):
  - **Left Side**: Visual display area featuring a top-down product photography composition (coffee cup, spoon with powder, scattered beans, dried autumn leaf).
  - **Right Side**: Interactive content area featuring branding links, vertical slider pagination, product title, description text, call-to-action button, and navigation arrows.

---

## 3. Detailed Component Breakdown

### Left Panel (Visual & Branding)
- **Top-Left Header**:
  - Logo icon: Stylized coffee bean / steam graphic in black.
  - Brand name: `"coffee flavours"` in lowercase bold sans-serif.
- **Vertical Bottom-Left Text**:
  - Small, rotated vertical text reading `© Slider Revolution` in light grey/dark charcoal.
- **Imagery & Composition** (Top-Down Flat Lay on textured grey surface):
  - **Coffee Cup**: White ceramic cup filled with latte/cappuccino, showing crema texture, angled slightly to the right with its handle facing right.
  - **Spoon**: Silver teaspoon positioned to the left of the cup, bowl of the spoon filled with brown coffee powder/sugar.
  - **Autumn Leaf**: Dried, crinkled golden-brown leaf positioned near the top-center.
  - **Coffee Beans**: Dark roasted coffee beans scattered artfully around the cup and spoon (top-left, bottom-left, bottom-center, and bottom-right).
  - **Shadow Effect**: Diagonal soft light casting striped/slatted shadows across the entire left composition from top-right to bottom-left.

### Right Panel (Content & Navigation)
- **Top-Right Social Navigation**:
  - Minimalist social media icons aligned to the top right:
    - Facebook (`f`)
    - Instagram (`📷` / camera icon)
    - YouTube (`▶` / play icon)
  - Color: Dark charcoal (`#1A1A1A`).

- **Vertical Flavor Carousel / Pagination (Center-Left of Right Panel)**:
  - Slide counter: `"01 / 05"` stacked vertically with a diagonal slash.
  - Interactive thumbnail indicator dots/circles (5 total, aligned vertically):
    - Active dot: Outlined/bordered circle with a preview inside.
    - Inactive dots: Solid muted brownish/grey circles representing different coffee variants.

- **Main Content Block (Centered vertically)**:
  - **Title**: `"Latte"` in large, elegant serif font, sentence case, bold.
  - **Description**: 
    - Text: *"Latte is a coffee drink made with espresso and steamed milk. The term comes from the Italian caffè e latte, caffellatte or caffellatté, which means "coffee & milk"."*
    - Style: Small sans-serif, muted grey, comfortable line-height for readability.

- **Call-to-Action (CTA) Button**:
  - Text: `"ORDER NOW"` in uppercase sans-serif, bold, tracked-out spacing.
  - Background: Muted warm tan/brown (`#C8B19B` or `#D4C2B0`).
  - Shape: Rectangular with sharp or very slightly rounded corners, centered beneath the description.

- **Bottom-Right Navigation Arrows**:
  - Minimalist vertical arrows (Up arrow `↑`, Down arrow `↓`) stacked vertically in light grey/charcoal to indicate scrolling or changing slides.

---

## 4. Interactions & States (For Frontend Implementation)
- **Hover States**:
  - Social icons and navigation arrows slightly darken or shift opacity on hover.
  - CTA button darkens slightly on hover.
  - Flavor dots transition smoothly when switching between slides (01 through 05).
- **Responsive Behavior**:
  - On smaller screens (tablets/mobiles), stack the layout vertically (Visual on top, content below) or scale the split-screen layout dynamically while maintaining the core typography and asset proportions.
