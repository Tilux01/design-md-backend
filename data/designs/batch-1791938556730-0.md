# Design Specification: Supercell Games Dashboard

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Overall Layout:** A clean, minimalist "Full Screen" dashboard. The main content is centered vertically and horizontally within the viewport.
*   **Background:** Solid white (`#FFFFFF`). No external borders or frames; the UI should bleed to the edges of the browser window.
*   **Corner Radius:** A consistent soft aesthetic using a `24px` border-radius for all primary containers (cards, search bar).

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** A modern, clean Sans-Serif like **"Inter"** or **"Roboto"**.
    *   *Fallback:* `system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Logo Font:** A custom, heavy, blocky slab-serif font (Supercell brand font).
    *   *Fallback:* `Impact` or `Arial Black` with tight letter spacing.
*   **Text Hierarchy:**
    *   **Logo:** Uppercase, Bold, Black, `24px`.
    *   **Nav Links ("Games", "Menu"):** Semi-bold, Dark Gray (`#333333`), `16px`.
    *   **Card Titles:** Bold, White (`#FFFFFF`), `18px`.
    *   **Card Subtitles:** Regular, White with 80% opacity, `12px`.
    *   **Footer Links/Nav:** Medium, Gray (`#666666`), `14px`.

## 3. Color Palette
*   **Background:** `#FFFFFF`
*   **Search Bar:** `#F5F5F5` (Light Gray)
*   **Card 1 (Brawl Stars):** `#E53935` (Vibrant Red)
*   **Card 2 (Clash Royale):** `#1E88E5` (Bright Blue)
*   **Card 3 (Clash of Clans):** `#2D2926` (Deep Charcoal/Brown)
*   **Card 4 (Boom Beach):** `#7E57C2` (Muted Purple)

---

## 4. Component Breakdown

### A. Header
*   **Logo (Left):** "SUPERCELL" stacked in two lines.
*   **Navigation (Left-Center):** The word "Games" placed next to the logo.
*   **Search Bar (Center):** 
    *   Width: `250px`, Height: `40px`.
    *   Background: `#F5F5F5`.
    *   Border-radius: `20px`.
    *   Content: A small magnifying glass icon on the left, followed by placeholder text "Search games..." in light gray.
*   **Menu (Right):** The word "Menu" followed by a `2x2` grid icon (four small squares).

### B. Game Card Carousel
The cards are arranged in a horizontal row with even spacing (approx. `30px` gap).

**Individual Card Structure:**
*   **Base Container:** 
    *   Dimensions: Width `220px`, Height `280px`.
    *   Border-radius: `24px`.
    *   Shadow: Soft drop shadow (`rgba(0,0,0,0.1) 0px 10px 20px`).
*   **Text Content (Bottom):** 
    *   Positioned at the bottom of the card with `20px` padding.
    *   Line 1: Game Title (e.g., "Clash Royale").
    *   Line 2: "Supercell | [Genre]" (e.g., "Supercell Strategy").
*   **Image Placement (The "Pop-out" Effect):**
    *   Images must be **backgroundless (transparent PNGs)**.
    *   They are positioned `absolute` relative to the card.
    *   The bottom of the character sits inside the card, but the top half (heads/weapons) extends **above** the top boundary of the colored card container.

### C. Footer
*   **Social Links (Left):** "Facebook" and "Youtube" spaced `20px` apart.
*   **Navigation Controls (Right):**
    *   Text: `< Prev` and `Next >`.
    *   Styling: Dark gray text, clickable.

## 5. Image Descriptions & Placement

All images are high-quality 3D renders with a slight perspective.

1.  **Brawl Stars Character (Nita & Bruce):**
    *   **Description:** A young girl (Nita) with a red animal hat riding on the back of a large, stylized red bear (Bruce). The bear is facing forward with a slightly grumpy expression.
    *   **Placement:** Centered on the Red card. The bear's feet are hidden by the card's bottom, and Nita's head extends above the card top.
    *   **Rotation:** 0 degrees (facing forward).

2.  **Clash Royale Character (The Prince):**
    *   **Description:** A knight in golden armor with a blue plume on his helmet, riding a small brown pony. He holds a long blue-and-white striped lance.
    *   **Placement:** Centered on the Blue card. The lance extends diagonally toward the top-left, breaking the top boundary significantly.
    *   **Rotation:** Slightly angled (3/4 view) toward the right.

3.  **Clash of Clans Character (The Barbarian):**
    *   **Description:** A muscular man with yellow hair and a large yellow mustache, wearing a brown kilt. He is mid-swing with a heavy iron sword. Small wooden debris/splinters fly around him.
    *   **Placement:** Centered on the Dark Charcoal card. The sword and his raised arm extend above the card.
    *   **Rotation:** Angled toward the left, creating a sense of motion.

4.  **Boom Beach Character (Dr. T):**
    *   **Description:** A villainous character in a light purple lab coat and dark goggles, looking over his shoulder with a devious grin.
    *   **Placement:** Centered on the Purple card (partially off-screen to the right).
    *   **Rotation:** Facing right, looking back toward the center.

## 6. Interactions (Optional but Recommended)
*   **Hover Effect:** When hovering over a card, the character image should scale up slightly (`transform: scale(1.05)`) and the card should lift (`translateY(-10px)`).
*   **Active State:** The current card in focus could have a slightly glowier shadow.
