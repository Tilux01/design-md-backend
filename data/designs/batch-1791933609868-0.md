# Design Specification: Minimalist Designer Portfolio

---

## 1. Typography

* **Headings (Display/H1/H2):** 
  * Font: *Playfair Display* or *Instrument Serif*
  * Fallbacks: `Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif`
  * Weights: 400 (Regular), 600 (Semi-Bold)
  * Character: Elegant, editorial serif with high contrast.
* **Body, Subtitles & Navigation:**
  * Font: *Inter* or *Plus Jakarta Sans*
  * Fallbacks: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`
  * Weights: 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (Semi-Bold)
  * Letter-spacing: `-0.01em` on body, `+0.05em` on uppercase tags.

---

## 2. Color Palette

* **Background Primary:** `#FAF9F6` (Warm off-white)
* **Background Surface (Cards):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white)
* **Text Primary:** `#161616` (Deep charcoal/black)
* **Text Secondary:** `#6F6F6F` (Neutral medium gray)
* **Text Muted:** `#A0A0A0` (Light gray)
* **Accent Color:** `#5B67CA` to `#6E78D8` (Soft indigo/periwinkle used on "impact." and decorative elements)
* **Hero Background Glows:** Overlapping blurred circles of `#D8DCF8` (soft blue) and `#EBD8F8` (soft lavender)
* **Borders & Dividers:** `#EAEAEA`

---

## 3. Global Styles & UI Elements

* **Buttons:**
  * *Primary:* Dark fill (`#161616`), white text, fully rounded (`border-radius: 9999px`), padding `12px 24px`, right arrow icon (`→`).
  * *Secondary / Outline:* Transparent fill, dark text, border `1px solid #161616` or no border with icon (`▶ WATCH INTRO`).
* **Pills / Filter Tabs:** Fully rounded (`border-radius: 9999px`), padding `8px 18px`. Active state has `#161616` background and white text; inactive state has transparent/light gray styling.
* **Card Elevation:** Very soft box-shadow `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`.

---

## 4. Desktop Layout Structure

### A. Navigation Bar
* **Positioning:** Full-width, padding `28px 80px`, flex row, aligned center, space-between.
* **Left:** Logo text `"A."` (Serif font, Bold, 24px).
* **Center:** Nav links (14px, uppercase/medium): `HOME`, `ABOUT`, `WORK`, `SERVICES`, `BLOG`, `CONTACT`. Spacing: `32px`.
* **Right:** Dark CTA button `"LET'S TALK →"` (13px, uppercase, rounded).

---

### B. Hero Section
* **Grid:** 2-column split (Left: ~55%, Right: ~45%).
* **Left Content Area:**
  * Sub-heading: `"HELLO, I'M ALEX"` (12px, uppercase, tracked, `#5B67CA`).
  * Main Headline: `"I design and build digital experiences that make an impact."`
    * Size: ~56px–64px, Serif font.
    * The word `"impact."` is styled in `#5B67CA`.
  * Sub-text: 2 lines, ~16px, `#6F6F6F`, max-width `460px`.
  * Button Group: Flex row; gap `20px`.
    * Button 1: `"VIEW MY WORK →"` (Filled dark).
    * Button 2: `"▶ WATCH INTRO"` (Text button with play icon).
  * Bottom-left element: Vertical text or icon with `"SCROLL DOWN"` indicator.

* **Right Content Area:**
  * Decorative Backing: Soft blurred lavender/blue radial gradients layered behind the subject.
  * Image Placement: Model aligned to bottom-right edge of the viewport.
  * Floating Card ("Available for Freelance"):
    * Position: Absolute, bottom-right over the subject's left chest/shoulder.
    * Styling: White rounded container (`16px` radius), padding `16px 20px`, soft drop shadow.
    * Content: Status dot (green/gray) + `"AVAILABLE FOR FREELANCE"`, short copy `"Let's work together on your next project."`, link `"GET IN TOUCH →"`.

---

### C. Selected Projects Section
* **Header:**
  * Category tag: `"MY WORK"` (12px, uppercase, gray).
  * Title: `"Selected Projects"` (Serif, ~36px) on left.
  * Right link: `"VIEW ALL PROJECTS →"` (13px, uppercase).
* **Filter Bar:** Horizontal pill buttons: `All`, `Web Design`, `Branding`, `UI/UX`, `Development`.
* **Projects Grid:** 3 columns × 2 rows.
  * Card dimensions: 16:10 aspect ratio preview image, rounded corners (`12px`), light outer border.
  * Details below image:
    * Project Name (16px, font-weight 600, `#161616`).
    * Category (13px, `#6F6F6F`).
  * Cards visible:
    1. *Nexus – Business Website* (Dark mode layout)
    2. *Haven – Interior Studio* (Minimalist beige architectural site)
    3. *Gusto – Restaurant* (Dark culinary UI)
    4. *Pure Skincare – eCommerce* (Clean cosmetic product layout)
    5. *Crafted – Agency* (Minimal editorial typography layout)
    6. *Taskly – Dashboard UI* (Pastel SaaS app interface)

---

## 5. Mobile Layout Adaptations (Max Width: 430px)

### Mobile Hero Screen
* Header: Logo `"A."` on left, hamburger menu (three horizontal lines) on right.
* Content flow: Stacked vertically. Headline scaled down to ~32px. Buttons stacked or wrapped.
* Subject portrait placed at bottom, partially cropped.

### Mobile Projects Screen
* Horizontal scrolling pill filters.
* 1-column project card stack instead of 3-column grid.

### Mobile About/Metrics Screen
* Metric cards stacked vertically with light circular icon containers:
  * `5+` Years Experience
  * `50+` Projects Completed
  * `30+` Happy Clients
