# UGMONK Clone - Design Specification & Layout Architecture

## 1. Global Styles & Design Tokens

### Color Palette
- **Primary Background (Header/Hero):** `#c89f89` (Warm Muted Terracotta / Clay)
- **Content Background:** `#ffffff` (Pure White)
- **Primary Text (Dark):** `#222222` (Soft Black / Charcoal)
- **Secondary Text (Navigation/Links):** `#ffffff` (White on Hero) / `#666666` (Gray on White)
- **Product Card Background:** `#f7f7f7` (Very Light Gray / Off-white)
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#e0e0e0` (Light Gray)

### Typography
- **Font Family:** System Sans-Serif (`-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, sans-serif)
- **Brand Logo:** Uppercase, letter-spacing: `0.15em`, weight: `600`
- **Nav Links:** Uppercase / Capitalized, size: `13px`, tracking: `0.05em`
- **Product Titles:** Size: `13px`, weight: `400`, color: `#333333`
- **Product Prices:** Size: `13px`, weight: `400`, color: `#555555`

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Components

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| TOP NAVIGATION BAR (Brand, Links, Utility Icons)                |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| HERO SECTION (Wooden Hanger Rod + 10 Hanging T-Shirts + Text)   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| TAB NAVIGATION (Mens / Womens / Objects)                        |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
| PRODUCT GRID (3 Rows x 4 Columns = 12 Product Cards)            |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 3. Detailed Component Breakdown

### A. Top Navigation Bar (Header)
- **Background:** Transparent overlaying the Hero background (`#c89f89`).
- **Layout:** Flexbox, space-between alignment with horizontal padding (`max-width: 1200px`, centered).
- **Left:** Brand Logo (`UGMONK`) in bold white uppercase text.
- **Center:** Navigation links (`Men`, `Women`, `Objects`, `Journal`) in white.
- **Right:** Utility links (`Login`, `Help`, `Contact us`) and Shopping Cart Icon (`🛒`).

### B. Hero Section
- **Background:** `#c89f89` (Solid warm clay tone).
- **Height:** ~450px.
- **Elements:**
  - **Wooden Hanger Rod:** Horizontal cylinder spanning full width with drop shadows to simulate depth.
  - **Hanging T-Shirts (10 pcs):** Realistic black plastic hangers holding folded/draped t-shirts in various colors (Dark Green, Rust, White, Charcoal, Blue, Heather Gray, Olive, etc.).
  - **Call to Action (Left aligned):**
    - Heading: "New Essential Tees" (White, `24px`)
    - Sub-link: "Learn more →" (White, with arrow icon)

### C. Category Tab Bar
- **Background:** `#ffffff`
- **Alignment:** Centered text links with active bottom border indicator under "Mens".
- **Items:** `Mens` (Active, bold with subtle bottom line), `Womens`, `Objects`.

### D. Product Grid (Catalog)
- **Grid Setup:** CSS Grid, 4 columns, gap: `24px`.
- **Max Width:** `1200px` (centered).

#### Product Card Anatomy:
1. **Image Container:**
   - Background: `#f7f7f7` (Light neutral card background).
   - Image: Centered, high-resolution product shot on plain white background.
   - Aspect Ratio: Square (`1:1`).
2. **Product Details (Below Image):**
   - **Title:** e.g., `Men's Essential Tee (Port)`
   - **Price:** `$30.00`

## 5. Responsive Behavior
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 4-column product grid, full horizontal nav.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 2 or 3-column product grid, compressed spacing.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 2-column product grid, collapsible hamburger menu for navigation.
