# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist Coffee Roaster Platform (Dark Mode, tactile luxury, artisanal craft meets modern web design).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace focus with expansive imagery grids, cinematic hero typography, and generous section padding (48px to 96px).
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Deep obsidian base canvas (`#0A0A0A`) with subtle dark glass surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`), thin 1px borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and soft atmospheric lighting effects.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, ambient cyan/blue glow orbs, dark glassmorphism cards, subtle background noise textures, interactive image hover zoom.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`, border width `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-cyan-400 inline-block"></span>`.
