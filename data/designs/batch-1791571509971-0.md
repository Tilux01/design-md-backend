# Design Specification: PUZZLE Learning Management System (LMS) Dashboard

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

*   **View Type:** Full-screen responsive application layout.
*   **Structure:** Two-tier architectural design:
    *   **Top Dark Container (`#101728`):** Houses the primary branding, global navigation, active course cards, and the weekly activity chart.
    *   **Bottom Light Container (`#F4F5F7` / White Cards):** Houses detailed metrics (`Your progress` chart, recommended courses (`You may like it`), and the schedule (`Upcoming` calendar + list)).
*   **Border Radius:** Smooth, modern rounded corners (approx. `16px` to `24px` for outer sections; `12px` to `16px` for internal cards).
*   **Spacing:** Generous whitespace (padding/margins between `16px` and `24px` for internal modules).

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

*   **Primary Font Family:** Inter (Fallback: `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`).
*   **Font Weights:**
    *   **Regular (400):** Body copy, timestamps, axis labels, sub-descriptions.
    *   **Medium (500):** Navigation items, course titles, table/card headers.
    *   **Bold (600 / 700):** Key metrics, percentages, primary section titles (`You courses`, `Your progress`, `Upcoming`).
*   **Text Hierarchy:**
    *   **Main Section Titles:** ~18px–20px, Bold.
    *   **Card Titles:** ~14px–16px, Semi-bold.
    *   **Body / Metadata:** ~12px, Regular/Medium.
    *   **Microcopy (Tags, Grades):** ~10px–11px.

---

## 3. Color Palette

### Dark Theme Section (Top)
*   **Background:** Deep Midnight Navy (`#101728`)
*   **Text (Primary):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
*   **Text (Secondary/Muted):** Light Slate Gray (`#8A94A6`)
*   **Course Card Backgrounds:**
    *   *Web Development:* Very dark slate with a white card container (`#FFFFFF` text inside, distinct dark card wrapper).
    *   *Motion Design:* Soft Peach / Warm Cream (`#FCEADE`) with dark text.
    *   *English Level C1:* Soft Lavender / Periwinkle (`#D8D9F3`) with dark text.

### Light Theme Section (Bottom)
*   **Page Background:** Light Gray / Off-White (`#F4F5F7`)
*   **Card Backgrounds:** Solid White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle shadow separation.
*   **Accent Colors:**
    *   *Primary Blue:* `#3B82F6` (Used for progress fills, active chart states, highlight dots).
    *   *Warning/Orange Accent:* `#F59E0B` (Used for secondary graph lines and indicators).
    *   *Success/Active Pill:* `#10B981` (implied indicators).

---
