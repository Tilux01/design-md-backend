# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Cinematic Luxury / Dark-Mode Creative Portfolio.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-impact editorial whitespace with atmospheric photographic immersion. Focuses on asymmetry, large hero typography, and subtle muted framing tokens.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered obsidian backdrop, soft ambient lighting overlays on photography, ultra-thin borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and floating pill containers.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Cinematic contrast ratios, glowing micro-indicators, vertical text orientation details, high-tracking uppercase accent metadata.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.1em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> `&mdash;`
  - Arrow Down (↓) -> `&#8595;` or inline SVG
  - Status Dot (●) -> `<span class="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-brand inline-block"></span>`
