# Design Specification: Kawasaki Ninja H2 R Landing Page

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

### Color Palette
- **Background (Primary):** `#0a0a0a` (Deep black/charcoal)
- **Accent Color:** `#10b981` (Bright emerald green) - Used for buttons, labels, and active navigation indicators.
- **Text Color (Primary):** `#ffffff` (Pure white)
- **Text Color (Secondary):** `#a1a1aa` (Muted gray for descriptions and metadata)
- **Background Watermark/Text:** `#1f1f23` (Very dark gray for large background typography)

### Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** Inter / Sans-serif (Clean, geometric, modern grotesque style)
- **Font Sizes & Weights:**
  - **Logo:** Bold, uppercase, tracking-wide.
  - **Hero Title ("H2 R"):** Extra-bold, large scale.
  - **Navigation/Menu Items:** Medium, uppercase, `text-sm`.
  - **Spec Numbers:** Large, bold (`text-xl` to `text-3xl`).
  - **Spec Labels:** Small, uppercase (`text-xs`), green color.
  - **Body Copy:** Regular, `text-sm`, line-height relaxed.

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Grid

- **Layout Type:** Full-screen immersive single-page layout with a dark theme. Fixed positioning for key navigational anchors (top, bottom, left, right rails).
- **Orientation:** Vertical stack / Multi-column hybrid (Optimized for desktop/tablet viewports).

---

### A. Top Navigation Bar
- **Position:** Fixed to the top.

  - `MODELS` (with active green indicator line next to it)

  - `SERVICE`
- **Styling:** Uppercase, spaced tracking, hover states highlight in green.

### B. Hero Section / Central Showcase

  - Large background typography reading vertically/diagonally: `H2 R` and `2023` in faint dark gray (`#151515`).

  - Realistic studio-lit render of the **Kawasaki Ninja H2 R** motorcycle angled dynamically facing the top-right/center.
