# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Luxury Minimalist Dark Mode, automotive heritage concept portfolio.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Extremely high whitespace ratio, floating glassmorphic panels layered over atmospheric monochrome studio backdrops, generous padding and clear focal framing.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-tier glassmorphism utilizing translucent white/gray surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `0.15`), delicate 1px borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`), subtle backdrop blurring (`blur(16px)`), and deep ambient dark gradients.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges, frosted glass containers, monochromatic automotive backdrop photography, subtle interactive sliders.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, border width `1px` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-amber-400 inline-block"></span>`.
