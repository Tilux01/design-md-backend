# Design Specification: Homeluxe Real Estate App (UI/UX Clone)

## 1. Global & Architectural Overview
- **Layout Structure:** Three-panel showcase displaying concurrent app states (Home Feed, Property Details, and Bookings Dashboard). 
- **Design Paradigm:** Modern Neo-Morphic Minimalist / Clean Luxury Real Estate UI.
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Primary Base / Background:** Off-White / Cream (`#F5F4F0` or `#FAF9F5`)
  - **Surface Cards:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle soft drop-shadows.
  - **Primary Dark (Text & Buttons):** Deep Charcoal / Off-Black (`#1A1A1A`)
  - **Accent / Interactive:** Warm Forest Green (`#1E2C1F` / `#223523`) for primary CTAs and active bottom-nav indicators.
  - **Muted Text / Secondary:** Medium Gray (`#7B7B7B`, `#8C8C8C`)
  - **Interactive Badges / Highlights:** Soft Amber / Gold (`#D4AF37` / `#C68A3B`) for ratings and specific status tags.
- **Typography Engine:**
  - **Primary Font Family:** Inter, sans-serif (Fallback: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`).
  - **Weights Utilized:** Regular (400), Medium (500), Semi-Bold (600), Bold (700).
