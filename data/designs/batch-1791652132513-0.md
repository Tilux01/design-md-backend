# Design Specification: Imagica Landing Page Clone

This document provides a comprehensive design specification for recreating the provided Imagica landing page interface. The output is intended for full-screen implementation (ignoring the surrounding mockup frame/shadows), capturing every detail of typography, layout, color palette, and visual assets.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

*   **View Mode:** Full-screen responsive viewport (remove the outer presentation container/shadows).
*   **Base Background:** Solid near-black (`#030305`) overlaid with a dynamic abstract background image.
*   **Layout Flow:** Single-page hero section with a fixed/floating header navigation bar at the top, centered typography in the vertical/horizontal middle, and a bottom action link.

---

## 2. Color Palette

*   **Primary Background:** `#030305` (Deep Black/Navy)
*   **Glassmorphism Header Fill:** Semi-transparent dark overlay (`rgba(10, 10, 15, 0.4)` to `rgba(20, 20, 30, 0.6)`) with a frosted backdrop-filter blur.
*   **Primary Text (Headings):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) and off-white/light gray gradient tone (`#E2E8F0`).
*   **Secondary Text (Nav & Sub-copy):** `#94A3B8` (Muted Slate Gray).
*   **Accent Glows (from imagery):** Electric Blue (`#1E3A8A` to `#3B82F6`), Deep Violet (`#4C1D95`), and subtle hints of Magenta/Red edge lighting.
*   **Buttons:**
    *   *Get Early Access (Outline):* Transparent fill, thin white/gray rounded border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`), white text.
    *   *Login (Solid):* Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) solid pill shape with dark text (`#000000`).

---

## 3. Typography & Fonts

### Font Family Strategy
*   **Primary Font:** Inter (or SF Pro Display as a macOS fallback system font).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

### Text Hierarchy & Styles

1.  **Navigation Links:**
    *   Size: `14px`
    *   Weight: Regular (`400`)
    *   Color: `#94A3B8` (Hover: `#FFFFFF`)
    *   Tracking/Letter-spacing: Normal.

2.  **Eyebrow / Sub-heading ("BUILD A NO-CODE AI APP IN MINUTES"):**
    *   Size: `12px` - `13px`
    *   Weight: Semi-bold (`600`)
    *   Color: `#94A3B8`
    *   Letter-spacing: Wide (`1.5px` to `2px`)
    *   Text-transform: Uppercase.

3.  **Main Hero Heading ("A new way to think and create with computers"):**
    *   Size: `56px` (Desktop) / Fluid scaling (`clamp(36px, 5vw, 64px)`)
    *   Weight: Medium to Semi-bold (`500` - `600`)
    *   Line-height: Tight (`1.1`)
    *   Color: Graded white-to-light-gray (`#FFFFFF` fading slightly toward the bottom).

4.  **Call to Action Button ("Get early access"):**
    *   Size: `14px`
    *   Weight: Medium (`500`)
    *   Color: `#FFFFFF`

5.  **Bottom Action Link ("Play Video Demo"):**
    *   Size: `13px`
    *   Weight: Regular (`400`)
    *   Color: `#94A3B8` (with hover state brightening to `#FFFFFF`).

---

## 4. Component Breakdown & Placement

### A. Navigation Bar (Header)
*   **Position:** Fixed or sticky at the top, horizontally centered with generous padding (e.g., `24px` top margin, constrained max-width or spanning full width with inner padding).
*   **Container Style:** Floating pill / capsule navbar with a blurred glassmorphism background and a subtle thin border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).
*   **Left Element:** Logo
    *   Icon: Circular vector graphic with a small protruding node (imagica signature icon).
    *   Text: "imagica" in a modern lowercase geometric sans-serif font, white, bold.
*   **Center Elements:** Navigation Links
    *   Items: `Product`, `How it works`, `Features`, `Mission`, `Company`.
    *   Spacing: Evenly spaced with ~`24px` to `32px` gaps.
*   **Right Elements:** Actions
    *   `Enterprise` link (styled like nav links).
    *   `Login` button: Solid white pill button with black text, rounded corners (`9999px`), padding around `8px 20px`.

### B. Hero Section (Center Content)
*   **Alignment:** Flex column, vertically and horizontally centered in the lower-middle portion of the screen.
*   **Content Stack:**
    1.  **Eyebrow Text:** `BUILD A NO-CODE AI APP IN MINUTES`
    2.  **Main Headline:** `A new way to think and create with computers` (split across 2 lines).
    3.  **Primary CTA Button:**
        *   Shape: Pill button with rounded borders.
        *   Text: `Get early access`
        *   Border: Thin white outline (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`).
        *   Padding: `12px 32px`.
        *   Hover effect: Slight background fill transition (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).

### C. Footer / Bottom Secondary Action
*   **Position:** Absolute positioned near the bottom center of the viewport, below the hero button.
*   **Content:** `Play Video Demo` (simple text link with optional subtle play icon prefix).

---

## 5. Background Image Asset Specification

Instead of generating this complex fluid graphic via code, use a high-resolution raster image (`.webp` or `.png`) with the following characteristics:

*   **Asset Type:** Abstract 3D rendered fluid metallic/glass ribbon surface.
*   **Transparency:** Backgroundless / transparent PNG or seamlessly blended into `#030305`.
*   **Color Profile:** Deep rich blues, dark indigos, electric blue reflections, and faint warm rim-lighting (red/magenta highlights on the right curve).
*   **Composition & Placement:**
    *   Positioned absolutely to cover `100%` width and height of the viewport (`object-fit: cover`).
    *   **Rotation/Flow:** Sweeping diagonal curves starting from the top-left, looping downward across the center, and curving upward toward the right side, creating a dynamic depth-of-field effect that naturally frames the central hero text.
    *   **Blending Mode:** `normal` or `screen`/`luminosity` over the dark base background to ensure the text remains fully legible with strong contrast.
