# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise SaaS, Modern B2B Product Dashboard.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High structural cleanliness with balanced whitespace; card-based modular layout with generous internal padding and distinct visual grouping.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean flat white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) layered over a soft off-white canvas (`#F8F8F7` / `#F3F3F2`), enhanced with subtle grey borders (`#E5E7EB`) and soft contextual floating elements with deep shadow projection (e.g. tooltip cards with dark surfaces `#111827`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Floating tab switcher pill bars, clean border separation, soft green financial indicators, status tags.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#1E1E1E` (active) / `#E5E7EB` (inactive).
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
