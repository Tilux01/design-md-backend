# Design & Layout Specification: Invoice Dashboard

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas

- **Viewport Orientation:** Horizontal / Desktop landscape.
- **Background Styling:** 
  - A smooth, multi-stop radial/linear gradient transitioning from a pale mint green (`#D4F0E4`) on the top-left to a soft lavender/periwinkle (`#D2C9F4`) on the top-right and right edge.
  - Overall texture is clean, modern, and minimal with soft rounded container cards layered on top.
- **Window Padding:** Consistent internal padding of approximately `24px` to `32px` from the outer edges.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

- **Primary Font Family:** Clean, modern geometric sans-serif (e.g., **Inter**, **SF Pro Display**, or **Plus Jakarta Sans**).
- **Fallback Stack:** `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
- **Type Hierarchy:**
  - **Main Invoice Amount (`$ 68,575.00`):** `48px – 56px`, Bold/Semibold.
  - **Section Titles ("Invoice lines", "Activity", "Upcoming"):** `18px – 20px`, Semibold.
  - **Card Headers / Product Names:** `14px – 15px`, Medium/Bold (`#111` or dark charcoal).
  - **Body / Metadata / Labels (Account, Invoice Number, Status):** `11px – 13px`, Regular/Medium, muted gray (`#666` or `#777`).

---

## 3. Header Section

- **Logo:** 
  - Bold, lowercase brand mark `sf.` in the top-left corner using a heavy sans-serif weight, accompanied by navigational pill buttons (`[::]` and `[←]`).
- **Page Title:** 
  - "Invoice" in medium weight, positioned next to the navigation controls.
- **Top-Right Action Buttons:**
  - Pill-shaped buttons with subtle white/translucent backgrounds and soft shadows:
    1. **"Issue Credit"** (includes a credit card icon).
    2. **"Edit"** (includes a pencil/edit icon).
    3. **"Delete"** (includes a trash icon).
    4. **Avatar Icon:** Circular profile picture placeholder featuring a person's photo.
    5. **Search / Utility Icon:** Circular button containing a search magnifying glass.

---

## 4. Financial Summary & Status Bar

- **Total Amount Display:**
  - Displays **`$ 68,575 .00`** in large prominent typography. The currency symbol `$` is slightly smaller, aligned with the baseline or top of the integer figures.
- **Metadata Badges:**
  - **Account:** Small icon + text reading `Ohana Inc.`
  - **Invoice Number:** `#` symbol + text reading `INV-4905`.
  - **Status:** Circular check/status icon + text reading `Posted`.
- **Progress / Payment Breakdown Bar:**
  - **Paid Segment:** Bright pastel green pill with a checkmark icon and text `\$ 25,000`.
  - **Credits Segment:** Soft pastel yellow pill with a wallet/card icon and text `\$ 10,000`.
  - **Balance Segment:** Gray/neutral pill with an hourglass icon and text `\$ 38,575`.
  - **Dotted Timeline/Progress Indicator:** A dashed/dotted progress bar representing remaining days (`Days Outstanding: 8 Days`).
  - **Primary CTA Button:** Dark black, pill-shaped button on the far right reading **`Pay Invoice`**.

---

## 5. Main Content Area (Split Layout)

The application body is divided into two primary columns/cards: **Invoice lines** (left, taking ~70% width) and **Activity** (right, taking ~30% width).

### Left Section: Invoice Lines
- **Sub-Navigation Tabs:**
  - Pill tabs: **Invoice lines** (active, white background), **Details**, **Docs**, **Notes**.
- **Item Count:** 
  - Text: `67 Items` alongside a search bar input (`Search items`).
- **Grid Layout for Product Cards:**
  - A responsive CSS Grid containing product cards. Each card features:
    - **Product Image:** A clean thumbnail image of the hardware item (e.g., iPhone 14 Pro, MacBook Pro 13, MacBook Air M1, iMac 27", iPhone 15). *See Section 6 for image descriptions.*
    - **Product Title & Price:** Bold product title with the unit/item price directly below (e.g., `iPhone 14 Pro` / `$ 850`).
    - **Quantity & Location:** Bottom row showing quantity (`20 Qty`) and store reference (`Store 2`), alongside an overflow options button (`...`).

### Right Section: Activity
- **Header:** Title **Activity** with a badge counter `12 Activities`.
- **Action Toolbar:** 
  - Row of circular icon buttons (Calendar, Plus icons, User icons, Phone, Mail).
- **Upcoming Section:**
  - **Card 1 (Purple Theme):** 
    - Header/Time: `12 Feb at 8pm`.
    - Title: `Send Payment Reminder`.
    - Description/Metadata: `Jossi Johnson sent a payment reminder.` Includes a small user avatar and a diagonal arrow action button.
  - **Card 2 (Yellow Theme):**
    - Header/Time: `13 Feb at 12pm`.
    - Title: `Call about the contract`.
    - Description/Metadata: `Brian Carpenter · Google meets`. Includes a user avatar and a diagonal arrow action button.
