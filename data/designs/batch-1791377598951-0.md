## 4. Animation Specifications (Interactive Clone)

If implemented as an interactive digital wall or portfolio interface:

1.  **Media Player Bar:** Slides down smoothly from the top viewport on page load (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, duration: `0.6s`).
2.  **Hanging Bags Physics:** A subtle CSS keyframe or physics simulation where hovering over a bag causes a gentle sway (rotation: `-2deg` to `2deg`, duration: `1.5s` infinite alternate).
3.  **Watermark Fade-In:** Fades in with a slight delay (`0.8s`) after the core background loads.
