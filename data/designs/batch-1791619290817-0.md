# Design Specification: Dark Neomorphic E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard

---

## 1. System Overview & Aesthetic

* **Theme:** High-end Dark Mode / Futuristic Glassmorphism with Cyberpunk / Neon Accents.
* **Canvas Style:** Fullscreen dark canvas filling `100vw` by `100vh` without outer mockups, borders, or simulated desktop framing.
* **Lighting Model:** Ambient dark lighting with localized colored glows (deep indigo, electric violet, cyan) emanating from behind the central product display and functional buttons.
* **Surface Materials:** Semi-transparent dark surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` to `blur(24px)` and fine `1px` subtle borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).

* **Viewport Bounds:** Fullscreen `100vw × 100vh`, `overflow: hidden`, padding: `24px 32px`.
* **Grid Template:**
  * **Top Bar:** Fixed height `56px`, horizontal flexbox distribution (`justify-content: space-between`).
  * **Main Body:** Flexible grid with 3 vertical zones:
    1. Left: Info column (`320px` wide) + Left floating icon sidebar (`56px` wide).
    2. Center: Sneaker canvas stage (flexible width `1fr`).
    3. Right: Secondary KPI & Media widgets (`340px` wide).
  * **Bottom Bar:** Fixed height `52px`, horizontal flexbox containing reports and utility buttons.

### 5.4 Central Hero Graphic: The Nike Air Max 720 Sneaker

#### Image Description, Orientation & Composition
* **Subject:** A hyper-detailed, photorealistic, backgroundless (transparent PNG cutout) rendering of a **Nike Air Max 720 "Navy Blue / Deep Royal"** lifestyle running shoe.
* **Orientation & Perspective:**
  * 3/4 angled three-quarter perspective, facing front-left.
  * The toe box points toward the lower-left quadrant at roughly a 35-degree downward angle.
  * The elevated heel and collar point toward the upper-right quadrant.
  * The lateral (outer) profile and the upper tongue are fully visible.
* **Material & Structural Details:**
  * **Upper:** Molded synthetic technical mesh in midnight dark navy with sculpted, organic wave-like structural ribs wrapping around the side panels.
  * **Midsole & Cushioning Unit:** Signature 360-degree full-length transparent Air Max 720 unit—a tall, bulbous, glossy polyurethane bubble extending around the entire perimeter of the sole. Glossy deep violet and metallic blue light reflections highlight the pressurized air chamber.
  * **Accents:** Matte deep blue swoosh accents embossed near the lower lateral guard and tongue; dark indigo woven lacing system neatly tied along the throat.
* **Placement & Dimensions:**
  * Positioned in the central visual plane, slightly off-center to the right, overlapping the visual boundary between the stats and the right media rail.
  * Approximate visual span: `520px` width by `480px` height.
  * Floating weightlessly without a heavy floor shadow, lit by an ambient violet-indigo backlight coming from behind the heel counter.

## 6. Micro-Interactions, States & Motion Blueprint

### 6.1 Hover & Active States
* **Pill Buttons:**
  * Hover: `transform: translateY(-2px)`, `filter: brightness(1.1)`.
  * Active: `transform: scale(0.97)`.
* **Sidebar Icons:**
  * Inactive icons have opacity `0.45`; on hover, opacity increases to `1.0` with a soft purple glow filter.
* **Thumbnail Selectors:**
  * Clicking an alternate thumbnail triggers a smooth crossfade of the central 3D sneaker asset and updates the price/title metrics.

### 6.2 Data Particle Orb Animation
* The spherical particle cloud in the "2019 YEAR" KPI card continuously rotates along its Y and Z axes at `0.15 rad/sec` with subtle particle pulsation to communicate live real-time ingestion of sales queries.
