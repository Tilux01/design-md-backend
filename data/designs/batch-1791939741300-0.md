This document provides a comprehensive design specification for cloning the provided music player UI. The design follows a minimalist, high-contrast, "Soft-UI" (Neumorphic) aesthetic characterized by rounded shapes, subtle shadows, and a clean monochrome palette with vibrant image accents.

---

# Design Specification: Minimalist Arch Music Player

## 1. Global Styles

### 1.1 Color Palette
*   **Application Background:** `#000000` (Pure Black)
*   **Main Interface Container:** `#F8F8F8` (Off-White/Light Grey)
*   **Primary Text/Icons:** `#000000` (Pure Black)
*   **Secondary Text:** `#A0A0A0` (Medium Grey)
*   **Shadows (Dark):** `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`
*   **Shadows (Light/Highlight):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 1.0)` (Used for neumorphic depth on the off-white surface)

### 1.2 Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` (or `Montserrat`)
*   **Fallback Fonts:** `San Francisco`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica Neue`, `sans-serif`
*   **Weights:**
    *   **Bold (700):** Used for track titles and main headers.
    *   **Medium (500):** Used for artist names and durations.
    *   **Regular (400):** Used for secondary UI elements.

### 1.3 Shapes & Depth
*   **Main Container:** Large corner radius (approx. `40px`).
*   **The "Arch" (Image Container):** A rectangle with the bottom two corners having a border-radius equal to 50% of the container width, creating a perfect semi-circle bottom.
*   **Buttons:** Circular with subtle drop shadows to create a "raised" effect.

---

## 2. Asset Descriptions (Images)

### 2.1 Hero Artwork
*   **Content:** A stylized, high-contrast digital illustration. It features a dark silhouette of a person with hair tied in a bun, shown in profile. The silhouette is set against a dark teal/navy background. Vibrant, diagonal streaks of glowing red and orange light pass behind the silhouette, providing a sense of motion and color contrast.
*   **Format:** The image itself should be a high-resolution rectangular asset.
*   **Masking:** The image must be masked by the "Arch" container (see Section 3.1). It is **not** backgroundless; the dark teal background of the illustration should fill the entire Arch container.
*   **Placement:** Centered at the top of the interface. In the "Now Playing" view, the image is slightly scaled up compared to the "Playlist" view.

---

## 3. Screen 1: Playlist View

### 3.1 Header Section
*   **Container:** The Arch container (inverted U-shape) occupies the top 40% of the screen.
*   **Overlay Text:** 
    *   **Title:** "Not you" (White, Bold, ~24px), centered horizontally near the bottom of the Arch.
    *   **Subtitle:** Artist Name (White, Medium, ~14px, slightly transparent), centered directly below the title.

### 3.2 Track List Section
*   **Layout:** A vertical list of tracks starting below the Arch.
*   **List Item Structure:**
    *   **Left Side:** Track Title (Black, Medium, ~16px).
    *   **Right Side:** Duration (Grey, Regular, ~14px).
    *   **Spacing:** Generous vertical padding between items (~25px).
*   **List Content:** 5-6 visible tracks before the bottom control bar.

### 3.3 Mini-Player (Bottom Bar)
*   **Placement:** Fixed at the very bottom of the white container.
*   **Controls:**
    *   **Left:** Small "Shuffle" icon.
    *   **Center Group:** Previous track icon, **Play/Pause Toggle** (Black circular button with white icon), Next track icon.
    *   **Right:** Small "Repeat" icon.

---

## 4. Screen 2: Now Playing View

### 4.1 Header Section
*   **Container:** The Arch container is larger here, occupying the top 50% of the screen.
*   **Overlay Text:** Same as the Playlist view ("Not you"), but positioned slightly higher within the arch.

### 4.2 Progress Indicator (The Arc)
*   **Shape:** A thin, light-grey circular stroke that perfectly follows the curve of the bottom of the Arch container, extending slightly upwards on both sides.
*   **Active Track:** A black stroke following the same path, representing elapsed time.
*   **Thumb (Handle):** A small, white circular knob with a subtle drop shadow, positioned at the current playback point on the arc.
*   **Time Display:** Current timestamp (e.g., "01:36") in Black, Medium, ~16px, centered directly below the bottom-most point of the arc.

### 4.3 Main Controls
*   **Layout:** Centered horizontally below the time display.
*   **Play Button:** A large, circular black button. It features a significant drop shadow (`box-shadow: 0 10px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.2)`) to appear elevated. The "Play" triangle icon is white and centered.
*   **Skip Buttons:** "Previous" and "Next" icons are placed to the left and right of the Play button. They are black and sit flat on the surface (no shadow).

### 4.4 Navigation/Utility Icons
*   **Top Left:** "Back" arrow icon.
*   **Top Right:** "Share" or "Options" icon.
*   **Bottom Left:** "Volume" or "Speaker" icon.
*   **Bottom Right:** "Playlist/Menu" icon (three horizontal lines).
*   *Note: All utility icons are thin-stroke black icons.*

---

## 5. Technical Implementation Notes

*   **Shadows:** To achieve the "Soft-UI" look, use two shadows on the buttons: a dark shadow on the bottom-right and a very faint white highlight on the top-left.
*   **Transitions:** When moving from Screen 1 to Screen 2, the Arch container should smoothly expand downward, and the tracklist should fade out as the large controls fade in.
*   **Masking:** Use `overflow: hidden` and `border-radius` on the Arch container to ensure the artwork fits the U-shape perfectly.
