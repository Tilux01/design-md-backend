# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Minimalist E-Commerce & Product Showcase Landing Page.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with a clean, centered container layout focusing user attention directly on the hardware product presentation and feature annotations.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Flat, clean surface architecture utilizing a crisp white canvas background (`#FFFFFF`) with high-contrast dark typography and subtle, sharp product shadow layering.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-contrast annotation callout lines, hand-drawn aesthetic connector arrows, clean minimalist typography, vivid orange hardware contrast.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `14px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(255, 92, 0, 0.1)`, border width `1px`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-orange-500 inline-block"></span>`.
