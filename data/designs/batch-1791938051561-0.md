# Design Specification: House M.D. Landing Page Clone

## 1. Global Styles & Theme
*   **Background Color:** Deep Midnight Blue to Black gradient (`#02080E` to `#000000`).
*   **Primary Accent Color:** Cyan / Electric Blue (`#00AEEF`) used for icons, active states, and highlights.
*   **Secondary Accent:** Muted Grey/Blue (`#5D707F`) for secondary text and borders.
*   **Typography:**
    *   **Headings/Logo:** Sans-serif, bold, wide tracking. 
        *   *Primary:* **Futura PT** or **Montserrat**. 
        *   *Fallback:* Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif.
    *   **Body/Stats:** Clean, geometric sans-serif. 
        *   *Primary:* **Roboto** or **Inter**.
        *   *Fallback:* San Francisco, Segoe UI, sans-serif.
    *   **Quotes:** Serif for a sophisticated look.
        *   *Primary:* **Playfair Display** or **Georgia**.

---

## 2. Header & Navigation
*   **Logo (Top Left):** A square border containing a bold, white letter "H".
*   **Navigation (Top Center):** Uppercase links: HOME, ABOUT, EPISODES, CAST, GALLERY.
    *   *Style:* Small font size (approx 12px), wide letter spacing, white color with 70% opacity.
*   **Social Icons (Top Right):** Instagram, Twitter, and YouTube icons in white.
*   **Layout:** Flexbox with `justify-content: space-between`, padding: `40px 60px`.

---

## 3. Hero Section (Top Fold)
*   **Main Subject Image:** 
    *   *Description:* A high-resolution portrait of Dr. Gregory House (Hugh Laurie). He is leaning forward, chin resting on his hands which are clasped over the handle of his wooden cane. His expression is intense and piercing.
    *   *Placement:* Right-aligned, filling the right 60% of the screen.
    *   *Background:* The image should be **backgroundless (PNG)** or have a soft glow behind the head. The bottom of the image should fade into the black background using a linear gradient mask.
*   **Main Title ("HOUSE M.D."):**
    *   "HOUSE" is in large, white, bold uppercase letters. The "H" is enclosed in a square stroke box.
    *   "M.D." is smaller, placed to the right of "HOUSE".
    *   *Detail:* A cyan EKG heart-rate line runs horizontally underneath "HOUSE M.D."
*   **Tagline:** "EVERYBODY LIES" in small, spaced-out cyan text above the main title.
*   **Description Text:** Short 3-line paragraph on the left. White text, 60% opacity.
*   **Call to Action:** A circular "Play" icon (cyan outline) followed by the text "WATCH TRAILER" in cyan.

---

## 4. Show Info Section (Middle)
*   **Graphic (Left):** 
    *   *Description:* A top-down anatomical scan of a human brain. 
    *   *Style:* Cyan/Blue neon "X-ray" style. It should be a **backgroundless PNG**.
    *   *Placement:* Centered vertically within the section, left side.
*   **About Text (Center):**
    *   Header: "ABOUT THE SHOW" in cyan.
    *   Body: A paragraph describing the show's premise. White text, high readability.
*   **Statistics (Right):**
    *   Vertical stack: "8 SEASONS", "177 EPISODES", "2004 - 2012 ON AIR".
    *   *Style:* Large white numbers, smaller cyan labels.

---

## 5. Episode Explorer (Bottom)
*   **Header:** "EXPLORE EPISODES" (Left) and "VIEW ALL EPISODES" (Right, smaller).
*   **Episode Cards (Grid):** 4 cards displayed horizontally.
    *   **Card 1 (Pilot):** Image of House sitting in his office chair.
    *   **Card 2 (No Reason):** Close-up of House's face looking distressed.
    *   **Card 3 (One Day, One Room):** A wide shot of a hospital corridor with a silhouette of a person.
    *   **Card 4 (House's Head):** Close-up of House's face looking sideways.
*   **Card Details:** 
    *   Top left of card: Episode number (e.g., 501).
    *   Below number: Episode title in uppercase.
    *   Hover effect: A cyan play button overlay.

---

## 6. Footer / Quote Section
*   **Quote (Bottom Left):** 
    *   Text: "If nobody hates you, you're doing something wrong."
    *   Style: Italicized, white, 80% opacity. Large quotation mark icon in the background.
*   **Background Element:** 
    *   *Description:* A massive, low-opacity (5%) letter "H" spanning the height of the footer.
*   **Bottom Right Text:** "FOLLOW THE TRUTH" in small, spaced-out cyan text.

---

## 7. Technical Implementation Notes
*   **Layout:** Use CSS Grid for the overall page structure and Flexbox for the navigation and stats.
*   **Image Masks:** Use `mask-image: linear-gradient(to bottom, black 80%, transparent 100%)` on the hero image to ensure a seamless transition into the rest of the page.
*   **Interactivity:** 
    *   Navigation links should have a cyan underline on hover.
    *   Episode cards should slightly scale up (1.05x) and increase brightness on hover.
*   **Full Screen:** The body should have `margin: 0; overflow-x: hidden;` to ensure the design touches all edges of the viewport.
