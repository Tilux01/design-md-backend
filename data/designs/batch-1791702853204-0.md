# Design Specification Document (`design.md`)

## 1. Overview & Visual Identity

This document defines the exact visual design specification for a modern, high-end agency / corporate portfolio page. The aesthetic is strictly minimalist, architectural, and editorial—characterized by clean grid lines, strong typography contrast, ample negative space, and a refined neutral monochrome palette.

---

## 2. Global Canvas & Page Layout Structure

The layout utilizes a full-screen, viewport-filling (`100vw` × `100vh`) application structure anchored by an architectural grid delineated by subtle 1px solid structural dividing lines.

### Structure Breakdown
* **Viewport Dimensions:** Full screen edge-to-edge layout (`100vw` / `100vh`).
* **Grid Lines / Borders:** All internal panel dividers are `1px solid #D0D0D0` (or `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.15)`).
* **Overall Background:** Uniform off-white / light slate gray (`#EBEBEB` or `#E8E8E8`).
* **Text & Dark Accents:** Jet Black (`#000000` / `#111111`) and Muted Gray (`#666666`).


### A. Fixed Left Sidebar
* **Width:** `80px` fixed (or `5.5vw` on high-res displays).
* **Position:** Fixed to the left edge spanning 100% height (`0px` to top/bottom).
* **Border:** Right vertical border (`1px solid #D0D0D0`).
* **Sub-divisions:**
  * **Top Square (Logo Container):** Height equal to Sidebar Width (`80px × 80px`). Bottom border line separates it from the lower sidebar area.
  * **Lower Sidebar Area:** Spans remaining height. Contains social media icons pinned to the bottom left corner.

### B. Top Header Navigation
* **Height:** `80px` fixed (matching the top logo container height).
* **Position:** Top edge, spanning from `left: 80px` to `right: 0`.
* **Border:** Bottom horizontal border (`1px solid #D0D0D0`).
* **Padding:** `0 60px`.
* **Content Placement:** Flexbox row (`align-items: center`, `justify-content: space-between`).

### C. Main Content Canvas
* **Position:** Spans remaining screen viewport (`top: 80px`, `left: 80px`, `width: calc(100vw - 80px)`).
* **Padding:** `60px 80px 80px 80px`.
* **Grid Columns inside Main Canvas:**
  * **Col 1 (Display Number):** `width: 25%` (~200px - 260px).
  * **Col 2 (Section Heading):** `width: 30%` (~280px - 340px).
  * **Col 3 (Body Text):** `width: 45%` (~400px - 550px).

## 6. Detailed Component Specifications

### A. Sidebar Component
1. **Logo Square (Top):**
   * Dimensions: `80px × 80px`.
   * Centered Content: Backgroundless vector geometric logo.
   * **Logo Image/Icon Description:** A clean, hollow black circle ring icon with a stroke width of `3px` and diameter of `36px`. Background is completely transparent.
2. **Social Media Links (Bottom):**
   * Positioned vertically near the bottom edge (`bottom: 30px`).
   * Centered horizontally inside the sidebar (`width: 100%`).
   * **Icons Stack (Top to Bottom):**
     1. *Globe/Dribbble Icon:* Vector line globe icon (`18px × 18px`), dark `#000000`, transparent background.
     2. *Instagram Icon:* Outline camera icon (`18px × 18px`), dark `#000000`, transparent background.
     3. *Twitter/X Icon:* Glyph icon (`16px × 16px`), dark `#000000`, transparent background.
   * Vertical Spacing between social icons: `24px`.

### B. Top Navigation Bar Component
1. **Left Nav Group:**
   * Contains 4 sequential menu items rendered horizontally with `40px` gap spacing between items.
   * **Item Structure:** Small number prefix stacked above or preceding the title label (e.g., `01.` placed above or next to `Services`).
   * **Nav Items List:**
     * `01. Services` (Active State: Bold text `#000000`, with a thick `3px` solid black bar directly underneath the header border).
     * `02. Work` (Inactive: Standard weight).
     * `03. Company` (Inactive: Standard weight).
     * `04. Careers` (Inactive: Standard weight).

2. **Right Action Group ("Contact"):**
   * Positioned at the far right of the top bar.
   * Contains "Contact" text link paired with a circular icon button.
   * **Button Details:**
     * Diameter: `32px`.
     * Shape: Perfect circle (`border-radius: 50%`).
     * Fill Color: Solid Black (`#000000`).
     * Icon: Right-pointing arrow `→` in pure white (`#FFFFFF`), centered horizontally and vertically inside the black circle.

### C. Main Content Header Section
1. **Section Sub-header Label:**
   * Text: `FROM START TO FINISH`
   * Margin Bottom: `80px` spacing before the content grid begins.

### D. Main Content Hero Grid Section
1. **Column 1: Section Index Number**
   * Content: `01`
   * Typography: Extra bold display size (`180px`), tight line height (`0.85`). Alignment top-left.

2. **Column 2: Title Block**
   * **Accent Divider Line:** A `2px solid #000000` line spanning horizontally above Columns 2 and 3. Positioned `20px` above the title.
   * **Title:** `Unlocking the opportunity`
     * Width/Wrapping: Max-width ~220px, wrapping across 2 lines.
     * Line height: `1.15`.
   * **Category Subtitle:** `Business strategy`
     * Spacing: `24px` top margin below the title text.

3. **Column 3: Body Copy Block**
   * Consists of two long-form narrative paragraphs providing deep project/service context.
   * **Paragraph 1:**
     > "Our projects always begin by gaining a deep understanding of your business goals, customer needs, applicable technologies, the surrounding space, and team structure. We meet in person with key stakeholders—executives, engineers, data scientists, product and design teams—to immerse ourselves in your business. The PS user-centered methodology begins with comprehensive customer research as a way to inform the design process. This qualitative inquiry unlocks insights, opportunities, and solutions businesses typically haven't considered, and resulting personas ensure we empathize with the people we're designing for. We land on an informed hypothesis about where to focus the work to ensure the end product is feasible to build based on your team's strengths, is user-friendly and beautiful, and drives adoption and scale."
   * **Paragraph 2 (Repeat/Secondary copy block):**
     > Identical structural block below Paragraph 1 with `24px` top margin spacing.

## 7. Interactive States & Micro-interactions

* **Navigation Links:**
  * Active link (`01. Services`) displays an indicator bar at the baseline.
  * Inactive links display a subtle hover transition (`color: #000000` on hover with opacity shift from `0.6` to `1.0` over `0.2s`).
* **Contact Button Hover:**
  * The black circle button scales slightly (`transform: scale(1.05)`) and arrow slides `2px` right on hover.
* **Social Media Icons:**
  * Opacity transitions from `0.6` to `1.0` on hover.

---

## 8. Layout Constraints & Responsive Scaling

* **Breakpoint (Desktop):** Above `1024px` — Grid maintains full 3-column architecture as specified.
* **Breakpoint (Tablet):** `768px - 1023px` — Column 1 (`01`) stacks above Column 2 and Column 3. Main padding adjusts to `40px`.
* **Breakpoint (Mobile):** `< 767px` — Sidebar converts to a collapsed menu bar at top. Grid falls back to a 1-column layout stacking Number -> Title -> Paragraphs sequentially.
