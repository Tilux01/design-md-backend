# Design Specification: Bombardier Global 6000 Configurator

## 1. General Theme & Layout
*   **Background:** A solid, light neutral gray (`#E5E5E5`). The design is full-screen, edge-to-edge.
*   **Corner Radii:** Most containers use a large border-radius (approx. `24px` to `32px`) to maintain a soft, modern aesthetic.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   Primary Background: `#E5E5E5`
    *   Card Backgrounds: `#FFFFFF` (White) and `#A5B8CE` (Muted Blue-Gray)
    *   Primary Text/Icons: `#000000` (Black)
    *   Active State: `#000000` (Black background with white text)
    *   Secondary Text: `#8E8E8E` (Medium Gray)

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** A clean, geometric Sans-Serif. **Outfit**, **Montserrat**, or **Inter** are the closest matches.
*   **Heading Font:** The "Interior" and "Bombardier" headings use a bold, slightly wide geometric sans-serif (e.g., **Lexend** or **Syne**).
*   **Fallbacks:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.

## 3. Top Navigation Bar
*   **Position:** Centered at the top, floating.
*   **Logo:** A black circle on the far left containing the letters "BO" in white bold sans-serif.
*   **Tabs:** A pill-shaped container with four options:
    1.  **Aircraft:** Icon of a plane + text.
    2.  **Interior (Active):** Black pill background, white text/icon. Icon is a lounge chair.
    3.  **Exterior:** Icon of a plane wing + text.
    4.  **Summary:** Icon of a document + text.
*   **Styling:** Semi-transparent white background or light gray border, subtle drop shadow.

## 4. Main Viewport (The Aircraft)
*   **Image 1 (Main Jet):** 
    *   **Description:** A high-resolution 3D render of a white Bombardier Global 6000. It is shown from a side profile, slightly angled towards the viewer. The image must be **backgroundless (transparent PNG)**.
    *   **Details:** The plane has a gold/bronze pinstripe along the fuselage. The registration "N381D" is visible on the tail engine.
    *   **Cutaway:** The front section of the fuselage is "cut away" to reveal the interior seating (a white/blue lounge area).
    *   **Placement:** Centered vertically and horizontally in the upper 2/3 of the screen.
    *   **Stairs:** A mobile boarding stair unit is attached to the open main door.
*   **Floating Controls:**
    *   **Top Center (above plane):** Four circular white buttons with thin gray borders containing icons for: Zoom, Seat Selection, User/Profile, and Settings.
    *   **Left Side (vertical):** Two circular white buttons for "Camera/Video" and "360 View".
    *   **Bottom Left:** "Sections" label followed by a pill-shaped slider. Numbers 1-4. "1" is active (black circle).
    *   **Bottom Right:** Small left/right arrow buttons for rotating the view.

## 5. Bottom Left Card (Jet Info)
*   **Background:** Muted Blue-Gray (`#A5B8CE`).
*   **Content:**
    *   **Top Left:** "13" (Large white text) over "Passengers" (Small, low-opacity white text).
    *   **Top Right:** A white circular button with a diagonal arrow (external link icon).
    *   **Center Image (Image 2):** 
        *   **Description:** A smaller, full-body transparent PNG of the Bombardier Global 6000 from a front-quarter perspective, flying slightly upward.
        *   **Placement:** Centered within the card.
    *   **Bottom:** "Bombardier" (Bold, large white text) and "Global 6000" (Small, light white text) below it.

## 6. Bottom Right Card (Floorplan)
*   **Background:** White (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Title:** "Floorplan" (Large black bold text).
*   **Top Right Icons:** Three icons in a row: "Grid/Layout," "View Toggle," and "Settings."
*   **Floorplan Diagram:**
    *   A top-down, simplified architectural schematic of the jet interior.
    *   **Sections labeled:** "Entertainment Suite," "Main Lounge," "Private Stateroom," and "En-Suite."
    *   **Visuals:** Gray blocks representing seats, sofas, and tables. 
    *   **Accents:** The far left and far right of the diagram feature diagonal "hazard" stripe patterns in light gray to indicate non-habitable zones (cockpit/tail).

## 7. Interactive Elements & States
*   **Hover States:** All buttons should have a slight scale-up effect or a subtle darkening of the background.
*   **Active States:** The "1" in sections and the "Interior" tab in the top nav use a solid black background (`#000000`) with white content (`#FFFFFF`).
*   **Shadows:** Use extremely soft, large-radius box shadows (`rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`) on the two bottom cards to give them depth against the light gray background.

## 8. Image Assets Requirements
1.  **Main_Jet_Cutaway.png:** Transparent background, side profile, high-detail shadows on the ground, cutaway revealing interior.
2.  **Mini_Jet_Flight.png:** Transparent background, angled perspective, no landing gear visible.
3.  **Icons:** A consistent stroke-based icon set (like **Feather Icons** or **Lucide**) with a consistent `2px` stroke weight.
