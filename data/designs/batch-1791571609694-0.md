# Design Specification: Laboratory Management Dashboard

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
* **View:** Full-screen responsive web application dashboard.
* **Layout Structure:**
  * **Top Navigation Bar:** Minimalist header with a left-aligned search/brand area, central icons, and a right-aligned profile/notification zone showing total revenue.
  * **Main Content Area:** A padded container (`padding: 24px` to `32px`) featuring a light background.
  * **Grid Layout:** 
    * Top row: A wide **Test Request Overview** card (approx. 70% width) paired with a smaller **Financial/Summary** card on the right (approx. 30% width).
    * Bottom row: Split layout containing **Equipment Utilization** on the left and **Staff for Today's Shift** on the right.

---

## 2. Color Palette

* **Backgrounds:**
  * Application Canvas: Light Gray-Blue (`#F4F5F7` or `rgb(244, 245, 247)`)
  * Card Backgrounds: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle border radii.
  * Overview Dashboard Box: Soft Mint/Sage Tint (`#EBF2EF` or similar low-saturation green-gray).
* **Text Colors:**
  * Primary Headings / Dark Text: Dark Slate Gray (`#1E293B` or `#111827`)
  * Secondary Text / Labels: Muted Gray (`#64748B` or `#9CA3AF`)
* **Accent & Status Colors:**
  * Active/Primary Pill (Purple): Soft Lavender-Purple background (`#EDE9FE`) with Dark Purple text (`#7C3AED`).
  * Indicator/Chart Highlights (Lime Green): Soft Lime (`#BEF264` or `#A3E635`).
  * Inactive/Empty Dots (Light Gray): `#E2E8F0`.

---

## 3. Typography & Font Stack

* **Font Family:** Modern Sans-Serif (Optimized for dashboard legibility).
  * *Primary Stack:* `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `"Segoe UI"`, `Roboto`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, sans-serif.
  * *Fallback:* If custom fonts cannot be loaded, the system falls back to standard platform sans-serif fonts.
* **Typography Hierarchy:**
  * **Dashboard Title / Section Headers:** 
    * Size: `18px` - `20px`
    * Weight: Semi-bold (`600`)
  * **Metric Numbers (Large - e.g., "88%", "$567.8..."):** 
    * Size: `28px` - `36px`
    * Weight: Bold (`700`)
  * **Sub-labels / Table Data:** 
    * Size: `12px` - `14px`
    * Weight: Regular (`400`) to Medium (`500`)
