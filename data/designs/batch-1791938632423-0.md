This `design.md` provides a technical specification for recreating the provided audiobook interface. The goal is a high-fidelity, full-screen responsive web application using modern CSS (Flexbox/Grid).

# Design Specification: "Origin" Audiobook Interface

## 1. Global Styles
*   **Background:** Solid `#0F1115` (Deep Charcoal/Near-Black). The design should be edge-to-edge (full screen) with no outer margins or frames.
*   **Text Color:** Primary: `#FFFFFF` (White); Secondary: `#9499A1` (Muted Grey).
*   **Accent Color:** `#D4B996` (Muted Gold/Tan) used for the primary button and active states.
*   **Cursor:** Default, with `pointer` for all buttons and book covers.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Serif (Headings):** *Playfair Display* or *Libre Baskerville*.
    *   *Fallback:* Georgia, Times New Roman, serif.
*   **Primary Sans-Serif (UI/Body):** *Inter* or *Montserrat*.
    *   *Fallback:* System-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif.

## 3. Layout Structure
The interface is divided into two main areas:
1.  **Left Sidebar (Navigation):** Fixed width (approx. 80px).
2.  **Main Content Area:** Flexible width, containing the Hero section and the Suggestions footer.

---

## 4. Component Details

### A. Sidebar (Left)
*   **Width:** 80px.
*   **Background:** `#090A0C` (slightly darker than main background).
*   **Items:**
    *   **Top Logo:** A stylized white strawberry or fruit-shaped icon.
    *   **Nav Icons:** Search (magnifying glass), Home (house), Library (books), Settings (gear).
    *   **Active State:** The "Home" icon is white. To its right, a vertical gold bar (`#D4B996`) 3px wide and 30px tall indicates the current selection.
    *   **Spacing:** Icons are vertically centered with high padding between them.

### B. Hero Section (Main Content)
*   **Padding:** 60px top, 80px left.
*   **Book Title:** "Origin"
    *   **Font:** Serif, 48px, Weight 500, Color `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Metadata Row:**
    *   **Content:** "by Dan Brown | Narrated by Paul Michael"
    *   **Font:** Sans-serif, 14px, Color `#9499A1`.
*   **Stats Row:**
    *   **Content:** 4.5 Stars icon | 13 hrs 30 mins.
    *   **Font:** Sans-serif, 14px, Color `#9499A1`.
*   **Description Paragraph:**
    *   **Content:** A block of justified or left-aligned text (approx. 4 lines).
    *   **Font:** Sans-serif, 15px, Line-height 1.6, Color `#9499A1`. Max-width: 500px.

### C. Action Buttons
*   **Primary Button ("LISTEN NOW"):**
    *   **Background:** `#D4B996` (Gold).
    *   **Text:** Dark `#0F1115`, Bold, All-caps.
    *   **Icon:** Small black "Play" triangle to the left of the text.
    *   **Border-radius:** 2px.
*   **Secondary Button ("+ ADD TO WISHLIST"):**
    *   **Background:** Transparent with a 1px border of `#45484D`.
    *   **Text:** White, All-caps, 12px.
    *   **Hover State:** Border turns white.

### D. Featured Artwork (Right Side)
*   **Image Description:** The cover for "Origin" by Dan Brown. It features a dark blue/teal background. In the center is a white, glowing 3D spiral/vortex. The text "DAN BROWN" is at the top in a gold serif font, and "ORIGIN" is at the bottom in the same style.
*   **Placement:** Positioned to the right of the text.
*   **Styling:** 
    *   **Size:** Approx 350px width.
    *   **Shadow:** A heavy "Drop Shadow" (`rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`) shifted to the bottom-right to give it a 3D "floating" effect off the dark background.
    *   **Rotation:** 0 degrees (perfectly vertical).

---

## 5. Bottom Section ("More Suggestions")
*   **Heading:** "MORE SUGGESTIONS"
    *   **Font:** Sans-serif, 12px, Bold, All-caps, Color `#45484D`.
    *   **Margin-bottom:** 20px.
*   **Book Grid:** A horizontal row of 5 book covers.
    *   **Cover 1:** Daniel Silva - "The Other Woman". Red background, black silhouette of a woman.
    *   **Cover 2:** Stephen King - "The Outsider". Grey/monochrome, a dark figure standing in a misty forest.
    *   **Cover 3:** "Betrayal" - Blue/Electric aesthetic, a silhouette of a person running toward a bright light.
    *   **Cover 4:** "The Silent Corner" - Dark red/brown tones, featuring a stylized eye or circular motif.
    *   **Cover 5:** Steve Berry - "The Lost Order". Dark brown/parchment style, featuring a lantern or compass.
*   **Cover Styling:** All covers should be backgroundless (no frames), rectangular (approx 2:3 aspect ratio), and have a slight 5px border-radius.

---

## 6. Technical Implementation Notes
*   **Flexbox:** Use `display: flex` for the main container to separate the Sidebar and Main Content.
*   **Transitions:** Add a `0.3s ease` transition to the book covers on hover (slight scale up `1.05`) and the buttons.
*   **Responsive:** On smaller screens, the Featured Artwork should move above the text or disappear, and the Suggestions grid should become scrollable horizontally.
