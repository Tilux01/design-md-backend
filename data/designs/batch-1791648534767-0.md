# Design Specification (`design.md`) — Mercury Landing Page

This document provides exact, comprehensive design specifications for replicating the **Mercury** landing page hero section. This specification is designed for a full-screen web application (100vw × 100vh / full browser viewport), excluding any surrounding presentation mockups or background borders present in the source preview.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Page Architecture

* **Target Experience:** High-end, dark-mode, developer-focused AI SaaS platform landing hero.
* **Viewport Layout:** Fullscreen web application (`100vw` × `100vh` minimum height).
* **Color Scheme:** Deep dark/near-black theme with high-contrast white typography, muted gray secondary elements, and dynamic dot-grid lighting effects.
* **Layout Structure:**
  * **Top Navigation Bar:** Fixed or sticky top bar spanning 100% width.
  * **Main Hero Container:** Centered vertically and horizontally within the remaining viewport.
  * **Background Graphic System:** Dynamic canvas/SVG dot-matrix grid covering the entire viewport background.
  * **Bottom Footer Banner:** Centered social proof text pinned towards the bottom.

## 4. Visual Assets & Graphical Elements

### 4.1 Background Canvas (Dot-Matrix Pattern)
* **Type:** Vector/Canvas interactive background graphic (Backgroundless overlay sitting behind UI elements).
* **Grid Layout:** Uniform array of circular dots spaced in a regular 2D grid matrix across 100% width and height.
* **Dot Specifications:**
  * **Base Dot Diameter:** `2px`
  * **Grid Spacing:** `24px` interval on both X and Y axes.
  * **Illumination Pattern:**
    * Base dots are dim (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
    * Concentric/Curved field lines of dots are illuminated brightly (`#FFFFFF` at `0.85` opacity and slightly enlarged to `3px` or `3.5px` diameter).
    * Dynamic curve shapes: Two sweeping parabolic arches framing the hero text block (one sweeping down from the top-left to center-left, another sweeping up on the right side).
    * Subdued orange/coral glowing dots (`#FF5533`) localized in the center-left area behind the text "Learn to Vibe Code".
* **Placement:** Positioned at `z-index: 0`, spanning absolute full viewport (`top: 0, left: 0, width: 100vw, height: 100vh`).

### 4.2 Icons
* **Arrow Right Icon (`→`):**
  * **Style:** Clean, thin line arrow.
  * **Dimensions:** `14px × 14px`.
  * **Color:** Matches adjacent text color (`#000000` inside primary CTA, `#A1A1AA` inside secondary CTA).
  * **Placement:** Inline, placed to the right of the button text with a `6px` gap.

## 6. Micro-Interactions & Motion Behavior

1. **Nav Bar Hover Effects:**
   * Nav links transition color from `#A1A1AA` to `#FFFFFF` over `200ms ease`.
   * Primary navigation CTA ("Join Waitlist") subtly scales to `1.02` or brightens background to `#FFFFFF` on hover.

2. **Hero CTAs:**
   * Primary Button ("Join Waitlist →"): Light shadow illumination on hover; arrow translates right by `3px` (`transition: transform 200ms ease`).
   * Secondary Button ("Book a Call →"): Text brightens to `#FFFFFF`, arrow translates right by `3px`.

3. **Background Grid Particle Effect:**
   * **Parallax/Cursor Tracking:** The highlighted curve/field lines in the dot matrix subtly shift and distort away from or towards the mouse cursor position across the screen.
   * **Pulse Animation:** Illuminated dots along the energy curves pulse gently in opacity (`0.5` to `0.9`) with a slow looping animation (`duration: 4s ease-in-out`).

## 7. Responsive Breakpoint Adaptation

* **Desktop (1024px+):** Exact layout as specified above.
* **Tablet (768px – 1023px):**
  * Hero Title Size: Reduced to `48px`.
  * Hide center navigation menu; replace with a minimal mobile menu icon or keep only right CTA.
* **Mobile (< 768px):**
  * Hero Title Size: Reduced to `36px`.
  * Paragraph Size: `14px`, max-width `90%`.
  * CTA Button Group: Stacked vertically (`flex-direction: column`, width `100%` for primary CTA).
  * Padding Side: `20px`.
