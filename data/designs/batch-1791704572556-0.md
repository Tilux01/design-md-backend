# Design System & Implementation Specification: Jack Evans Portfolio

A complete, pixel-accurate design specification for replicating the full-page editorial portfolio of **JACK EVANS** (Freelance Photographer & Filmmaker).

---

## 1. Creative Direction & Visual Language

- **Style:** Minimalist, high-contrast brutalist-editorial portfolio.
- **Theme Dynamics:** Alternating stark full-width color blocks—switching rhythmically between deep matte black (`#0A0A0A`) and clean studio off-white (`#F5F5F5`).
- **Core Visual Drivers:**
  - Oversized display typography juxtaposed with tiny micro-labels (captions, coordinates, tags).
  - Clean hairline divider rules (`1px`).
  - High-grain, atmospheric cinematic photography.

## 3. Typography System

### Font Families
- **Display & Section Headers:** `Syne` or `Neue Haas Grotesk Display Pro`  
  *Fallback Stack:* `"Helvetica Neue", "Arial Black", Arial, sans-serif`  
  *Characteristics:* Tight tracking, high geometric weight, heavy structural uppercase glyphs.
- **Body & Editorial Copy:** `Inter` or `Helvetica Neue`  
  *Fallback Stack:* `Arial, sans-serif`  
  *Characteristics:* High legibility, neutral grotesk feel.
- **Micro-labels & Metadata:** `Space Mono` or `Inter` (All Caps, wide letter-spacing)  
  *Fallback Stack:* `Courier New, monospace` or `Arial, sans-serif`

### Type Scale

| Role | Font Family | Size | Weight | Line Height | Letter Spacing | Transform |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hero Title** | Display | `7vw` / `80px–110px` | 800 (Bold) | `0.9` | `-0.03em` | UPPERCASE |

## 4. Global Layout & Structural Grid

- **Canvas Mode:** Edge-to-edge full width (`100vw`).
- **Inner Content Max-Width:** `1440px` centered with variable horizontal padding:
  - Desktop: `padding-inline: 48px`
  - Tablet: `padding-inline: 32px`
  - Mobile: `padding-inline: 20px`
- **Section Transitions:** Hard vertical color seams (no blur gradients or soft shadows).

## 7. Interactive Behaviors & Micro-interactions

1. **Navigation Links:**
   - Default: `opacity: 0.7`.
   - Hover: `opacity: 1.0`, smooth `0.2s ease` transition.
2. **Text Action Links (`WORK WITH ME BOOK NOW ->`, `CONTACT ───>`):**
   - Default: Static underline or line arrow.
   - Hover: Right arrow translates `+4px` horizontally (`transform: translateX(4px)`).
3. **Gallery Image Cards:**
   - Default: Static scale `1.0`.
   - Hover: Image slightly scales to `1.03` with an inner zoom effect, overflow hidden.
4. **Form Inputs:**
   - Default: Bottom border `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.3)`.
   - Focus: Bottom border `#0A0A0A` (`1.5px`), `outline: none`.
5. **Submit Button:**
   - Default: Background `#0A0A0A`, text `#FFFFFF`.
   - Hover: Background `#262626`, cursor `pointer`.
   - Active: Scale `0.98`.
