# Design Specification: Nike Hero Landing Page

## 1. Overall Layout & Architecture
* **Viewport:** Fullscreen (100vw, 100vh), overflow hidden.
* **Layout Grid:** 2-column asymmetric layout.
  * **Left Sidebar:** ~7% width, fixed solid black background (`#111111`).
  * **Main Content Area:** ~93% width, solid white/off-white background (`#FAFAFA`).

---

## 2. Color Palette
* **Primary Dark:** `#111111` (Sidebar, main titles, active text)
* **Background Light:** `#FFFFFF` to `#FAFAFA`
* **Muted Text / Accents:** `#8A8A8A` (Body text, inactive links)
* **Giant Watermark Gray:** `#D8D8D8`
* **Divider Lines:** `#CCCCCC`

---

## 3. Typography & Fallbacks
* **Primary Display/Headings:** `Futura`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, sans-serif (Geometric, Bold/Heavy, uppercase).
* **Body/Navigation:** `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, sans-serif (Light to Medium, uppercase with wide letter-spacing).
* **Font Specs:**
  * **Main Title ("NIKE"):** ~56px, Extra Bold, tracking `1px`.
  * **Watermark ("07"):** ~380px–420px, Heavy sans-serif, color `#D8D8D8`.
  * **Navigation Links:** ~12px, Medium, letter-spacing `3px`.
  * **Body Paragraph:** ~11px, Regular, line-height `1.5`, max-width `280px`.
  * **Sub-Nav ("SHOES"):** ~13px, Bold, tracking `3px`.
  * **Pagination ("07"):** ~32px, Bold. Inactive ("01", "09"): ~24px, Regular.

---

## 4. UI Component Breakdown

### A. Left Sidebar
* **Background:** Black (`#111111`).
* **Content:** White Nike Swoosh icon positioned at the top center (~30px from top).

### B. Header Navigation
* **Position:** Top of the main area, aligned horizontally.
* **Left Links:** `MEN`, `WOMEN`, `COLLECTIONS` placed ~60px from the sidebar, spaced ~40px apart.
* **Right Icon:** Minimal hamburger menu (two horizontal lines) pinned to the far right (~40px padding).

### C. Left Content Column
* **NIKE Heading:** Upper-left quadrant (~120px from top).
* **Description:** 3-line placeholder text below title.
* **"EXPLORE" Button:**
  * Border: `1px solid #111111`
  * Padding: `8px 24px`
  * Typography: 10px, uppercase, tracking `2px`
  * Background: Transparent
* **Category Selector (Vertical):**
  * Placed ~80px below the button.
  * Stacked vertically: `NEW`, `▶  SHOES  ◀`, `SALE`.
  * Inactive items in muted gray; active item in solid black with subtle filled triangle carousels.

### D. Center-Right Hero Visual
* **Background Graphic:** Giant number "07" positioned centered-right.
* **Hero Shoe Image:**
  * **Description:** Lateral profile of a Nike running shoe. Features a black mesh upper, white midsole, dark maroon/burgundy heel accent and outsole trim, and a prominent white swoosh on the quarter panel.
  * **Asset Specs:** PNG format with transparent background (no outer frame/backdrop).
  * **Orientation:** Facing left at a ~10° downward forward tilt.
  * **Layering:** Shoe weaves through the number "0" (front foot in front, ankle collar masked/behind the curve of "0") and overlaps the diagonal stem of the "7".
  * **Shadow:** Soft, diffused horizontal contact drop shadow directly beneath the sole.

### E. Bottom Pagination Bar
* **Position:** Bottom center/right aligned horizontally across the main canvas.
* **Elements (Left to Right):**
  1. Left arrow `◀`
  2. Muted text `01`
  3. Thin horizontal line (~100px, `#CCCCCC`)
  4. Active text `07` (large, bold, `#111111`)
  5. Thin horizontal line (~100px, `#CCCCCC`)
  6. Muted text `09`
  7. Right arrow `▶`
