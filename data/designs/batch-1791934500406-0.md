# Design System & Implementation Specification: Axel Morgan Portfolio

## 1. Global System & Typography

### Color Palette
* **Primary Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
* **Secondary Background:** `#F8F8F8` (Light Gray Card Fills)
* **Dark Background (Footer):** `#080808` (Off-black)
* **Primary Text:** `#0D0D0D` (Charcoal Black)
* **Secondary Text:** `#666666` (Muted Gray)
* **Brand Accent:** `#E52E1D` (Vibrant Vermilion Red)
* **Borders / Dividers:** `#E5E5E5`

### Typography
* **Primary Display/Headlines:** `Impact`, `Bebas Neue`, or `Oswald` (Compressed/Condensed Sans-Serif, Extra Bold, Uppercase).
  * *Fallback:* `Arial Narrow`, `sans-serif`.
* **Body & UI Text:** `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, or `Arial` (Clean Neo-Grotesque Sans-Serif).
  * *Weights:* 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 700 (Bold).

---

## 2. Layout & Components Breakdown

### 2.1 Navigation Bar
* **Layout:** Full-width flex container; height `80px`; padded `0 48px`.
* **Left:** Logo `AXEL MORGAN` with an inline red dot (`#E52E1D`) at the end. Bold uppercase.
* **Center:** Horizontal navigation menu (`HOME`, `ABOUT`, `SERVICES`, `WORK`, `CLIENTS`, `TESTIMONIALS`, `CONTACT`). Font size: `12px`, letter-spacing: `1px`, muted black.
* **Right:** Button `LET'S TALK ↗`. Solid black fill, sharp rectangular edges, white text, uppercase `12px`.

---

### 2.2 Hero Section
* **Grid:** 2-column layout (approx. 55% / 45%).
* **Left Column:**
  * Sub-headers: `AXEL MORGAN / CREATIVE DIRECTOR` (left) and `BASED IN NEW YORK` (right-aligned above main header). Font size `11px`, bold uppercase.
  * Main Headline: `CREATIVE` (Line 1), `DIRECTION` (Line 2). Font size `120px+`, extreme condensed bold, uppercase.
  * Graphical Overlay: Hand-drawn red brush/crayon scribble underlining `DIRECTION`.
  * Supporting Copy: 2-line bio (`14px`, line-height `1.5`, muted black).
  * CTA Button: `VIEW MY WORK ↗` (Black rectangle, white text).
* **Right Column:**
  * Visual Block: Solid red rectangle (`#E52E1D`) base.
  * Image: Cutout portrait of a man looking forward-left with stubble and dark crewneck. Monochromatic/desaturated black-and-white treatment with transparent background, overlapping the red rectangle layer.
* **Right Edge (Floating):**
  * Vertical text reading `• AVAILABLE FOR FREELANCE` rotated 90° clockwise.

---

### 2.3 About Section
* **Tag:** `• ABOUT ME` (Red bullet, `11px` bold uppercase).
* **3-Column Grid:**
  1. **Left (Story):** Bold headline (`20px`), two short body paragraphs, digital handwritten script signature (`Axel Morgan`), followed by text signature `Axel Morgan / Creative Director`.
  2. **Center (Metrics):** Massive `12+` (`72px`, condensed black), red underline, label `YEARS OF EXPERIENCE`. Below: 4-row list with bold metrics (`250+`, `80+`, `25+`, `12`) and gray labels.
  3. **Right (Image):** Grayscale architectural photo of modern brutalist concrete structures. A dynamic diagonal red paint stroke covers the lower-right corner.

---

### 2.4 Services Section
* **Tag:** `• SERVICES`
* **Layout:** 5-column grid with thin linear icons:
  1. Square icon: `BRAND STRATEGY`
  2. Eye icon: `CREATIVE DIRECTION`
  3. Asterisk/Burst icon: `CAMPAIGN DEVELOPMENT`
  4. Team icon: `TEAM LEADERSHIP`
  5. Top-right arrow icon: `DIGITAL EXPERIENCES`
* **Typography:** Titles in bold uppercase `13px`; descriptions in muted gray `12px`.

---

### 2.5 Skills Section
* **Layout:** 2-column split (1:3 ratio).
* **Left Column:** Tag `• SKILLS` and stacked headline `SKILLS THAT DRIVE IMPACT`.
* **Right Column:** Two parallel columns of progress bars.
  * Left: Brand Strategy (95%), Creative Direction (95%), Art Direction (90%).
  * Right: Campaign Development (90%), Team Leadership (85%), Digital Design (80%).
  * Bar Style: Ultra-thin light gray track, active track filled in solid red (`#E52E1D`). Percentages right-aligned.

---

### 2.6 Featured Work Section
* **Header:** Tag `• FEATURED WORK` on the left; Filter links on the right (`ALL WORK`, `BRANDING`, `CAMPAIGNS`, `DIGITAL`, `EXPERIENCES`).
* **Cards (4-column grid):**
  1. **North Edge:** Dark charcoal background, centered white typographic logo. Subtitle: `NORTH EDGE / Branding`.
  2. **Wildhorn:** Coral red background with large bold vertical typography `BOLD BY NATURE` and an amber cosmetic bottle centered. Subtitle: `WILDHORN / Campaign`.
  3. **Gymferno:** High-contrast B&W fitness portrait with overlay text `Disciplin. Over Motivation`. Subtitle: `GYMFERNO / Campaign`.
  4. **Kion Studio:** Solid black card with massive white sans-serif text `KION.`. Subtitle: `KION STUDIO / Digital Experience`.

---

### 2.7 Trust & Achievements
* **Tag:** `• TRUSTED BY / ACHIEVEMENTS`
* **Client Logos:** Single horizontal row featuring flat black vector logos: Nike, Adidas, Samsung, Coca-Cola, Netflix, Spotify, Visa, Adobe.
* **Awards Grid:** 4-column horizontal list separated by subtle vertical borders:
  * Awwwards (Honorable Mention)
  * CSS Design Awards (Special Kudos)
  * Communication Arts (Excellence Award)
  * The Webby Awards (Official Honoree)

---

### 2.8 Testimonials Section
* **Tag:** `• WHAT CLIENTS SAY`
* **Cards:** 3-column equal-width cards with off-white background (`#F8F8F8`) and light inner padding.
* **Card Elements:**
  * Red quotation mark icon `“` top-left.
  * Quote body text (`13px`, medium weight).
  * Client Name (Bold `12px`) and Title/Company (`11px`, muted gray).

---

### 2.9 Pre-Footer Call to Action
* **Layout:** Full-width white section with right-edge graphic asset.
* **Graphic Asset:** Distressed red grunge/spray paint texture bleeding inward from the far right edge.
* **Content:**
  * Left Headline: `LET'S CREATE SOMETHING` (Black), `EXTRAORDINARY.` (Red). Massive condensed typography.
  * Right: Supporting copy `Have a project in mind...?` and black CTA button `GET IN TOUCH ↗`.

---

### 2.10 Footer
* **Background:** Deep Black (`#080808`), white/light-gray text.
* **4-Column Layout:**
  1. **Brand:** `AXEL MORGAN •`, short summary, linear social icons (Instagram, LinkedIn, Behance, Mail).
  2. **Navigation:** Stacked vertical list of main page anchors.
  3. **Services:** Stacked vertical list matching services offerings.
  4. **Contact:** Email (`hello@axelmorgan.com`), phone number, location (`New York, NY`).
* **Bottom Bar:** Subdued copyright notice at bottom right (`© 2024 Axel Morgan. All rights reserved.`).
