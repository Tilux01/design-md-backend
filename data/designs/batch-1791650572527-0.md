# Comprehensive UI/UX Design Specification & Replication Blueprint

This document serves as the absolute blueprint for creating an exact, pixel-perfect clone of the provided dark-mode SaaS landing page ("SEOtalos.com").

## 1. Global Design System & Foundations

### 1.1 Color Palette
*   **Deep Space Black (Primary Background):** `#050505` to `#0A0A0C` (Rich, near-black with a subtle cool undertone). Use linear gradients running vertically from `#050505` to `#0F0F13` to create atmospheric depth.
*   **Surface Cards & Modals:** `#121216` with a semi-transparent border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
*   **Pure White (Primary Text/Icons):** `#FFFFFF`
*   **Muted Gray (Secondary Text):** `#9CA3AF` (Tailwind `gray-400`)
*   **Dark Muted Gray (Tertiary/Subtle Text):** `#6B7280` (Tailwind `gray-500`)
*   **Interactive Accent (Primary Buttons):** `#FFFFFF` background with `#000000` text, transitioning to an off-white/glow on hover.
*   **Subtle Glows & Accents:** Soft white lighting effects emanating from abstract geometric shapes (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` radial gradients).

### 1.2 Typography & Fallbacks
*   **Primary Font Family:** **Inter** (Modern, clean, highly legible sans-serif used heavily in tech SaaS products).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
*   **Typography Hierarchy:**
    *   **Hero Heading:** 56px–64px, Line-height: 1.1, Letter-spacing: -0.03em, Weight: 700 (Bold).
    *   **Section Heading (H2):** 36px–42px, Line-height: 1.2, Letter-spacing: -0.02em, Weight: 600 (Semi-bold).
    *   **Card / Subsection Title:** 20px–24px, Line-height: 1.3, Weight: 600.
    *   **Body Text:** 15px–16px, Line-height: 1.5, Weight: 400 (Regular).
    *   **Captions / Badges:** 12px–13px, Line-height: 1.4, Weight: 500 (Medium).

### 1.3 Global Layout & Grid
*   **Layout Type:** Full-screen vertical scrolling single-page architecture (`width: 100%`).
*   **Max Content Width:** `1200px` centered with auto margins, utilizing responsive padding (`24px` mobile, `80px` desktop sides).
*   **Visual Aesthetics:** Grid lines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`) faintly visible in the background running vertically and horizontally to give a blueprint/engineering aesthetic.
