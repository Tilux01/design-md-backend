# Design Specification: Tone Studio Hero

## 1. Color Palette

* **Background Canvas:** `#CFC7BE` (Warm Muted Greige)
* **Primary Text & Dark Accents:** `#2C2B29` (Charcoal Slate)
* **Watermark Lettering:** `#C0B7AC` (Subtle low-contrast Taupe)
* **Pill Outlines & Sub-elements:** `#696660` (Muted Bronze/Grey)
* **Light Accents / Shirt:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)

---

## 2. Typography

* **Logo ("tone"):**
  * *Font:* Custom Extended Geometric Display (e.g., *Ethnocentric*, *Microgramma*, or *Syne Extra Bold*).
  * *Fallback:* `sans-serif` (letter-spacing: `0.05em`, horizontal scale: `1.2`).
* **Headline ("YOUR STYLE STARTS FROM THE TOP"):**
  * *Font:* *Druk Wide Bold*, *Monument Extended*, or *Pangram Pangram Formula*.
  * *Fallback:* `Impact`, `Arial Black`, `sans-serif`.
  * *Specs:* Uppercase, heavy weight, leading: `0.9`, tracking: `-0.02em`.
* **Secondary UI & Badges:**
  * *Font:* *Aktiv Grotesk*, *Neue Haas Grotesk*, or *Inter*.
  * *Fallback:* `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
  * *Specs:* Medium / Regular, sizes `11px` to `13px`.

---

## 3. Layout & Structure

Full-screen viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`), relative layout with layered z-indexes.

### Layer Order:
1. `z-0`: Greige Base Background (`#CFC7BE`)
2. `z-10`: Massive Stylized Watermark Typography
3. `z-20`: Central Cutout Model Image
4. `z-30`: Foreground UI (Badges, Floating Buttons, Headlines, Navigation)

---

## 4. Components & Content

### 4.1 Header (Top)
* **Container:** Full width, padding: `24px 32px`, flex row, space-between.
* **Brand Logo (Left):** "tone" in lowercase wide geometric type (`font-size: ~20px`).
* **Nav Buttons (Right):** Three rounded pill-shaped buttons grouped together:
  * Labels: `New Arrivals`, `Collections`, `Custom Order`.
  * Style: Transparent background, `1px` border (`#2C2B29`), rounded-full, `padding: 6px 16px`, `font-size: 11px`.

### 4.2 Top-Left Micro Badge
* **Location:** `top: 140px`, `left: 120px` (above watermark, near cap visor).
* **Components:**
  * Circle button: `36px` diameter, background `#2C2B29`, icon `↗` (white, thin).
  * Label: "Drop new styles" (stacked in two lines, `10px`, dark charcoal).

### 4.3 Background Watermark Graphic
* **Text:** Stylized geometric forms (resembling "STAGE" or "TONE").
* **Style:** Oversized, angular, high-tech glyphs with stencil cutouts. Tone-on-tone tint, spans horizontally behind the model.

### 4.4 Model Imagery
* **Asset Description:** High-resolution transparent cutout (PNG) of a man in profile facing left.
  * Features: Bald/shaved head, dense salt-and-pepper beard, detailed dark neck tattoo, clear acetate eyeglasses.
  * Apparel: Neutral tan/khaki unstructured 5-panel cap, clean white crew-neck t-shirt.
* **Placement:**
  * Anchored to bottom-center.
  * Height: Approx. `85-90vh`.
  * Horizontal Position: Aligned slightly right of center; chest extends across the lower right frame.

### 4.5 Floating Interactive Widget (Right)
* **Location:** Right-center (`top: 48%`, `right: 18%`).
* **Design:** Dark circular badge (`#3A3937`), diameter: `~160px`.
* **Content:** Centered white play triangle (`▶`, `12px`), flanked horizontally by low-opacity white text: `More` (left) and `About` (right) at `9px`.

### 4.6 Bottom-Left Hero Content
* **Location:** `bottom: 40px`, `left: 40px`, max-width `450px`.
* **Upper Capsule:** Pill border (`1px solid #2C2B29`), text: "Your head deserves the right fit", `font-size: 11px`.
* **Headline:** Two-column visual layout:
  * Line 1: `YOUR STYLE`
  * Line 2: `STARTS FROM`
  * Line 3: `THE TOP`
  * Font-size: `~52px`, ultra-bold, uppercase, tightly stacked.

### 4.7 Bottom-Right Hero Content
* **Location:** `bottom: 40px`, `right: 40px`, text-align right.
* **Paragraph:**
  * Text: *"limited drop, hand-crafted in small batches by our team at tone studio."*
  * Style: `font-size: 10px`, max-width: `140px`, lowercase, line-height: `1.3`.
* **Pill Buttons:**
  * Labels: `New Drop`, `Collections`.
  * Positioned below paragraph, matching header pill styling (`padding: 4px 14px`, `10px` font).
