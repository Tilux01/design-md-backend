# UI/UX Specification & Design System Clone: "Coffee" E-Commerce Landing Page

This document provides a comprehensive, pixel-level specification to recreate the website displayed on the laptop screen in the media. All physical laptop frames, bevels, and ambient backgrounds are omitted in favor of a **full-screen viewport implementation**.

---

## 1. Global Design Tokens & Typography

### Color Palette
*   **Primary Dark (Text & Dark Accents):** `#1A1612` (Rich Espresso Black)
*   **Background Base (Light Cream):** `#F4F0EB` (Warm Oat/Cream)
*   **Surface White (Cards & Buttons):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
*   **Secondary Text / Subdued Elements:** `#6E655D` (Muted Taupe)
*   **Accent / Hover State (Warm Amber):** `#C88A58` (Caramel/Coffee Gold)
*   **Border / Divider Subtlety:** `#E3DCD3` (Soft Stone)

### Typography Stack
The design relies on a modern, elegant serif for hero headlines and a clean, legible geometric sans-serif for body copy and navigation.

*   **Primary Heading Font (Serif):** 
    *   *Family:* `Playfair Display`, `Didot`, `Georgia`, `serif`
    *   *Usage:* Hero titles, major section headers.
*   **Body & Navigation Font (Sans-Serif):** 
    *   *Family:* `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`
    *   *Usage:* Navigation links, body paragraphs, buttons, metadata.

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation Header
*   **Layout:** Fixed or sticky top bar, full-width with generous horizontal padding (`max-width: 1280px` centered).
*   **Background:** Semi-transparent frosted glass over the hero imagery (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background: rgba(26, 22, 18, 0.3)`).
*   **Left Element (Logo):** 
    *   Text: **"Coffee"**
    *   Font: Serif, italicized or stylized, color `#FFFFFF` over the dark hero.
*   **Center Elements (Nav Links):**
    *   Items: `Home`, `About`, `Menu`, `Contact`
    *   Font: Sans-serif, 14px, weight 400, color `#FFFFFF`.
*   **Right Elements (Actions):**
    *   Search icon and a primary CTA Pill Button (`#FFFFFF` background, `#1A1612` text).

---

### B. Hero Section
*   **Layout:** Split-column or layered full-width banner with an atmospheric background image of a modern, warm coffee shop interior (blurred context).
*   **Overlay:** Dark gradient overlay (`linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(26,22,18,0.7) 0%, rgba(26,22,18,0.2) 100%)`) to ensure text legibility.
*   **Content (Left Aligned):**
    *   *Tagline / Eyebrow:* Small uppercase sans-serif text in gold/amber (`#C88A58`).
    *   *Main Headline:* 
        *   Text: **"Cerrenciling turkiffee"** (or contextual brand headline).
        *   Font: `Playfair Display`, size `clamp(3rem, 5vw, 5.5rem)`, weight `700`, color `#FFFFFF`. Line-height: `1.1`.
    *   *Body Paragraph:* Multi-line descriptive text explaining the brand's artisanal coffee craft. Font: `Inter`, 16px, color `#E3DCD3`, max-width `450px`.
    *   *CTA Button:* 
        *   Text: "Read More" (with right-arrow or minimalist icon).
        *   Style: Pill-shaped, background `#F4F0EB`, text color `#1A1612`, padding `12px 28px`, font-weight `500`.

*   **Hero Visuals (Right Side / Center Composition):**
    *   A high-end double-walled glass cup filled with a latte (distinct crema art on top) resting on a rustic wooden table.
    *   Scattered roasted coffee beans resting dynamically around the base of the cup.
    *   A secondary ceramic white espresso cup and saucer in the mid-background.

---

### C. Carousel / Pagination Indicators
*   **Position:** Centered horizontally near the bottom of the hero/banner container.
*   **Design:** 4 minimalist dots. The active dot is wider (pill-shaped) and colored `#C88A58` (Amber); inactive dots are circular and translucent white (`rgba(255,255,255,0.4)`).

---

### D. Secondary Content Section (Below the Fold)
*   **Background:** Solid warm cream (`#F4F0EB`).
*   **Layout:** Multi-column grid featuring contextual content blocks (e.g., "Coffee Coffee", imagery cards, and descriptive excerpts).
*   **Cards:**
    *   Background: `#FFFFFF`.
    *   Border-radius: `12px`.
    *   Subtle box shadow: `0px 10px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`.
    *   Contained imagery paired with bold sans-serif subheadings (`18px`, `#1A1612`) and short descriptive snippets (`14px`, `#6E655D`).

---

## 3. Micro-Interactions & Animations

1.  **Hero Image & Text Entry (On Page Load):**
    *   Fade-in up animation (`opacity: 0 -> 1`, `transform: translateY(20px) -> translateY(0)`) with a staggered delay for the headline, paragraph, and CTA button (Duration: `0.8s`, Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
2.  **Button Hover States:**
    *   CTA pills undergo a smooth background color transition to the amber accent (`#C88A58`) with text shifting to pure white (`#FFFFFF`). Transition: `all 0.3s ease`.
3.  **Carousel / Slider Transition:**
    *   Smooth horizontal sliding or cross-dissolve animation between featured products/slides when navigation dots are clicked.
4.  **Card Hover Effect:**
    *   Subtle elevation lift (`transform: translateY(-4px)`) and shadow intensification on secondary content cards.
