# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Dark Mode Neo-Fintech & Glassmorphic Mobile Dashboard (High-end consumer banking and asset management).
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High structural contrast combining dark ultra-sleek financial card previews with crisp white/light gray mobile interface containers. Balanced margins, deliberate asymmetric card stacking, and controlled component spacing.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered depth using stark pure blacks (`#0B0B0E`), frosted translucent white cards (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), deep shadows (`0 12px 36px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)`), and saturated vibrant violet/purple accent branding (`#7C3AED`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Floating overlapping payment cards with precise skew/tilt effects, dual-tone contrast surfaces, ultra-smooth border radii (`24px` to `36px`), and vibrant purple call-to-action pills.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.02em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.1)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.2)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG path.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span className="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
