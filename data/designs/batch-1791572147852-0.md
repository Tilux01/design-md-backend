# UI/UX Specification & Replication Guide

This document provides the exact design specifications for transforming the **"BEFORE"** mobile application screen into the **"AFTER"** mobile application screen. 

---

## 1. Global & Layout Changes

| Element | Before State | After State (Target) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Device Frame** | Older iOS shape (Standard rounded rectangle with home indicator bar) | Modern iOS shape (Dynamic Island notch, pill indicator, status bar icons) |
| **App Theme / Header Background** | Light teal-to-white gradient | Solid deep forest green (`#0e5043`) extending through the header and promo banner |
| **Status Bar** | Dark icons on pale background | White text/icons (`941`, cellular, wifi, battery) |

---

## 2. Typography & Font Stack

*   **Primary Font Family:** `Inter`, `SF Pro Display`, or `Roboto` (Sans-serif)
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`

### Typography Hierarchy
*   **Screen Title / Location Dropdown:** 14px, Medium (Weight: 500), White (`#FFFFFF`)
*   **Search Placeholder:** 14px, Regular (Weight: 400), Muted Gray (`#8A9A95`)
*   **Promo Banner Header:** 18px – 20px, Bold (Weight: 700), White (`#FFFFFF`)
*   **Promo Banner Body:** 11px, Regular (Weight: 400), Light Gray (`#D1DCD8`)
*   **Section Headers ("Service Categories", "Popular Services"):** 16px, Semi-bold (Weight: 600), Dark Slate (`#111111`)
*   **"View all >" Links:** 12px, Medium (Weight: 500), Muted Green/Gray (`#667872`)
*   **Card Titles:** 14px, Semi-bold (Weight: 600), Dark Slate (`#111111`)
*   **Card Subtitles / Pricing:** 12px, Regular/Medium, Dark Gray (`#555555`)
*   **Navigation Bar Labels ("Home"):** 12px, Medium (Weight: 500)

---

## 3. Component-by-Component Breakdown

### A. Header Section
*   **Location Dropdown:** 
    *   Icon: Location pin (white)
    *   Text: "Your location" (smaller, muted) + "San Antinoe, Tx" with a chevron-down arrow.
*   **Profile Avatar:** 
    *   *Added in "After":* Circular profile picture featuring a person's portrait with a soft glowing teal border placed on the top right next to the notification bell.
*   **Search Bar:**
    *   Pill-shaped input with a white background (`#FFFFFF`).
    *   Search magnifying glass icon on the left.
    *   Placeholder text: `Search for a service..`

### B. Promotional Banner ("YOUR SOLUTION, ONE TAP AWAY!")
*   **Background Container:** Solid deep teal (`#0c4a3e`) with rounded corners (~16px).
*   **Left Side (Text & CTA):**
    *   Heading: "YOUR SOLUTION, ONE TAP AWAY!" (All caps, bold white).
    *   Subtext: "Seamless, Fast & Reliable Services at Your Fingertips".
    *   Button: Pill-shaped white button with dark text reading **"Explore"**.
*   **Right Side (Illustration):**
    *   Isometric 3D illustration depicting professionals performing maintenance/home repairs (e.g., HVAC repair technician and painter working in a cutaway room).

### C. Service Categories Section
*   **Header Row:** 
    *   Left: "Service Categories"
    *   Right: "View all >"
*   **Structural Redesign (Before vs. After):**
    *   *Before:* Vertical grid icons with text labels stacked underneath (Hairdresser, Cleaning, Painting).
    *   *After:* Clean horizontal card layout (2x2 grid shown):
        *   Each card features a white background, rounded border, a colored icon on the left (e.g., Hairdryer, Paintbrush, Cleaning tool, Chef hat), category title in the middle, and a chevron arrow (`>`) aligned to the right.

### D. Popular Services Section
*   **Header Row:**
    *   Left: "Popular Services"
    *   Right: "View all >"
*   **Content Cards:**
    *   Horizontal or vertical image-preview cards displaying service providers at work (e.g., cleaning, cooking preparation).
    *   Ratings badge (e.g., `★ 4.5 (123 Reviews)`).
    *   Service title and price range (e.g., `$25 - $30`).

### E. Bottom Navigation Bar
*   **Background:** Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) with a top border/shadow.
*   **Items (Left to Right):**
    1.  **Home:** Active state featuring a rounded dark-green pill background (`#0c4a3e`) enclosing a white home icon and the text "Home".
    2.  **Calendar/Bookings:** Outline icon (gray).
    3.  **Chat/Messages:** Outline speech bubble icon (gray).
    4.  **Profile/Settings:** Outline user profile icon (gray).
*   **System Bar:** Standard iOS black horizontal home indicator bar at the absolute bottom.
