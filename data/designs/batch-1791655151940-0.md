# DESIGN SPECIFICATION: Dreamy Social/Journaling App UI

## 1. Global Styles & Foundations

### Color Palette
*   **Backgrounds / Gradients:**
    *   Primary Background Gradient: Linear gradient shifting from a soft lavender (`#D4E0F0`) to a warm pastel pink/peach (`#E8D0E0`).
*   **Card Backgrounds (Frosted Glass / Glassmorphism):**
    *   `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` with a backdrop blur of `blur(20px)` and a subtle 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`.
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   Primary Headings: Deep Slate/Navy (`#2C3E50` or `#1E293B`)
    *   Secondary/Body Text: Muted Slate (`#64748B`)
    *   Accent Text / Highlights: Soft gold/yellow (`#F59E0B`) or lavender highlights.
*   **Interactive Elements / Buttons:**
    *   Solid White or translucent pill buttons with soft drop shadows (`box-shadow: 0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`).

### Typography
*   **Primary Font Family:** *Inter* or *SF Pro Display* (clean, modern sans-serif).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Font Weights & Sizes (Estimated):**
    *   **Screen Title (H1):** 32px, Bold, tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em`).
    *   **Section Headers / Card Titles:** 16px – 18px, Semi-Bold / Medium.
    *   **Body Text:** 14px, Regular, line-height 1.5.
    *   **Captions/Metadata:** 12px, Regular/Medium.

## 2. Screen 1: Dashboard / Feed View (Left)

### Layout Structure
*   **Layout Type:** Vertical scrollable feed with a multi-column staggered/masonry or grid layout.
*   **Top Navigation Bar:**
    *   **Left:** App logo/avatar (circular white icon placeholder).
    *   **Right:** Search or settings icon (subtle white circular icon).
*   **Header Section:**
    *   **Title:** "The Journal" (or similar phrasing), large bold white/slate typography.
    *   **Filter Tabs:** Horizontal pill-shaped filters underneath the title (e.g., *All, Trend, Audio, Images*). The active tab ("All") is highlighted in solid yellow/gold (`#FACC15`) with rounded pill geometry.

### Content Grid (Cards)
The content is broken down into various aspect-ratio cards styled with glassmorphism:
1.  **Top-Left Card (Green/Teal Theme):**
    *   *Background:* Soft sage green gradient.
    *   *Content:* Dark text layout with a bold headline and short description. Features a subtle white floating icon/badge in the bottom corner.
2.  **Top-Right Card (Thought of the Day):**
    *   *Background:* Translucent white/blue frosted glass.
    *   *Content:* Text-heavy card titled "Thought of the Day" with muted paragraph text.
3.  **Middle-Left Card (Warm Yellow/Cream):**
    *   *Background:* Soft warm beige/yellow gradient.
    *   *Content:* Elegant serif or script header text ("...life...").
4.  **Middle-Right Card (Photo Card):**
    *   *Image Content:* A photographic card showing a person outdoors in a natural landscape setting. The image is cropped to a rounded rectangle matching the card radius.
5.  **Bottom Cards:**
    *   Dark/moody photo card on the bottom-left with a circular icon overlay (play/audio indicator).
    *   Pink-tinted card on the bottom-right.

## 3. Screen 2: Detail / Thought View (Right)

### Layout Structure
*   **Header:**
    *   **Title:** "Thought of the day" centered at the top in large, bold typography with a subtle drop shadow.
    *   **Navigation Elements:** Back arrow on the top left, share/options icon on the top right.
*   **Floating Modal / Content Sheet:**
    *   A prominent white rounded container (`border-radius: 24px`) overlaying the lower portion of the screen.
    *   *Padding:* Generous internal padding (~24px).
*   **Modal Header:**
    *   Small category tag/date at the top left of the card.
    *   Close/Minimize icon (`X` or chevron) on the top right.
*   **Modal Body Text:**
    *   **Heading:** Bold primary text string introducing the thought.
    *   **Paragraph:** Multi-line justified text discussing a reflective topic (e.g., mindfulness, daily inspiration). High contrast for readability against the white background.
*   **Media / Attachment Section (Inside Modal):**
    *   Two distinct visual elements side-by-side or horizontally scrollable at the bottom of the card:
        1.  *Image 1:* A vibrant abstract illustration featuring warm red/orange fluid shapes on a light pink background (backgroundless aesthetic, clean edges).
        2.  *Image 2:* A patterned texture swatch (subtle geometric grid or woven fabric texture).
*   **Bottom Action Bar:**
    *   A centered pill-shaped button at the very bottom of the screen (e.g., "Add to favorites" or "Close") styled as a solid white container with soft shadow.
