# Design Specification: "Hello There" Interactive Landing Page

This document provides a comprehensive, production-ready design specification to clone the visual design and layout of the provided landing page. All outer operating system chrome, browser toolbars, scrollbars, and window framing icons have been omitted; this specification describes the full-screen canvas layout.

---

## 1. Overall Visual Style & Theme

* **Aesthetic Tone:** Whimsical, cozy, vintage-storybook romanticism paired with playful, handwritten web elements.
* **Base Background:** A uniform, warm, natural off-white/parchment background across the entire viewport.
* **Layout Structure:** Full-screen single viewport (`100vw`, `100vh`) with fixed top navigation, a centered vertical content card, and two flanking vintage botanical watercolor illustrations.

## 3. Typography & Hierarchy

### 3.1 Font Families & Fallbacks
1. **Primary Display Script (Headline):**
   * **Target Font:** A heavy, organic brush-marker display script with irregular, hand-lettered contours.
   * **Recommended Google Fonts:** `Permanent Marker`, `Caveat Brush`, or `Sedgwick Ave`.
   * **CSS Fallback:** `'Permanent Marker', 'Caveat Brush', 'Comic Sans MS', cursive`.

2. **Secondary Casual Hand-script (Subtitle & Navigation):**
   * **Target Font:** A monoline, warm, natural handwritten script.
   * **Recommended Google Fonts:** `Patrick Hand`, `Gaegu`, or `Gochi Hand`.
   * **CSS Fallback:** `'Patrick Hand', 'Gaegu', 'Comic Neue', cursive, sans-serif`.

3. **Button Text (Clean Rounded Grotesk):**
   * **Target Font:** A geometric, friendly, bold rounded sans-serif.
   * **Recommended Google Fonts:** `Nunito` (Weight: 800), `Quicksand` (Weight: 700), or `Fredoka` (Weight: 600).
   * **CSS Fallback:** `'Nunito', 'Quicksand', 'Arial Rounded MT Bold', sans-serif`.

### 3.2 Type Scale & Styling

* **Top Navigation Links:**
  * Font Family: Secondary Casual Hand-script (`Patrick Hand`)
  * Size: `15px` (`0.9375rem`)
  * Weight: `600` (Semi-Bold)
  * Letter Spacing: `0.3px`
  * Text Color: `var(--color-nav-text)`

* **Main Heading ("Hello there \n _!"):**
  * Font Family: Primary Display Script (`Permanent Marker`)
  * Size: `64px` (`4rem`)
  * Line Height: `1.1`
  * Alignment: Center
  * Text Color: `var(--color-text-primary)`
  * Visual Note: The text is on two lines: line 1 is `"Hello there"`, line 2 is `"-!"` (or `"_!"` with a low underscore and exclamation point).

* **Prompt / Subtitle ("do you want to see your gift?"):**
  * Font Family: Secondary Casual Hand-script (`Patrick Hand`)
  * Size: `20px` (`1.25rem`)
  * Weight: `500` / `600`
  * Text Transform: Lowercase
  * Text Color: `var(--color-text-secondary)`
  * Margin: `32px 0 24px 0`

* **Button Labels ("YES PLEASE", "NO THANKS"):**
  * Font Family: Button Rounded Grotesk (`Nunito` 800)
  * Size: `14px` (`0.875rem`)
  * Letter Spacing: `1.5px`
  * Text Transform: Uppercase
  * Text Color: `var(--color-text-primary)`

## 4. Layout & Grid Architecture

* **Container:** `width: 100vw`, `height: 100vh`, `overflow: hidden`, `position: relative`, `background-color: var(--color-bg-canvas)`.
* **Arrangement Strategy:**
  * **Header:** Absolute positioned at the top right.
  * **Center Stage (Hero):** Centered horizontally and vertically using flexbox (`display: flex; flex-direction: column; align-items: center; justify-content: center;`).
  * **Flanking Botanical Elements:** Fixed/Absolute positioned on the left and right flanks, maintaining responsiveness via percentage-based positioning.

* **Positioning:**
  * Top: `24px`

  * Display: Horizontal flex (`flex-direction: row; gap: 24px; align-items: center;`)

  * Hover state: `color: var(--color-border-burgundy)`

### 5.2 Center Hero Group

A single vertically aligned stack with centered children:

#### A. Mascot Sticker (Cat Illustration)
* **Visual Description:**
  * A minimalist, whimsical hand-drawn 2D cartoon white kitten/cat standing upright.
  * Thin black outlines with solid white body fill and completely transparent surrounding background (PNG/SVG).
  * The kitten has its eyes closed in a happy crescent shape, tiny pink blushed cheeks, and small rounded ears with pink inner details.
  * Small paws rest gently against its belly.
  * Directly above the kitten's head floats a small, soft pink-red heart (`#F4A6AC`).
* **Dimensions:**
  * Width: ~`90px`
  * Height: ~`110px`
  * Margin Bottom: `18px`

#### B. Main Title
* **HTML Structure:**
  ```html
  <h1 class="main-title">
    Hello there<br />
    _!
  </h1>
  ```
* **Styling:**
  * Text Alignment: `center`
  * Margin: `0 0 16px 0`

#### C. Subtitle
* **Content:** `"do you want to see your gift?"`
* **Styling:**
  * Text Alignment: `center`
  * Margin: `16px 0 28px 0`

#### D. Action Buttons Row
* **Display:** Horizontal flex (`flex-direction: row; gap: 20px; justify-content: center;`)
* **Button Specifications (Both Buttons):**

  * **Background:** `var(--color-btn-bg)` (`#ECE7E0`)
  * **Padding:** `12px 38px`
  * **Min Width:** `180px`
  * **Text Alignment:** Center
  * **Box Shadow:** Optional micro-shadow: `0 2px 4px rgba(108, 17, 21, 0.04)`

    * `background-color: var(--color-btn-bg-hover)` (`#E0DAD0`)

  * **Active/Click Interaction:**
    * `transform: translateY(1px)`

### 5.3 Decorative Botanical Illustrations

Both illustrations are vintage watercolor botanical prints isolated with **transparent backgrounds (alpha PNGs)**.

#### Left Botanical Illustration: Red Ranunculus / Peony
* **Visual Description:**
  * Classical 18th/19th-century botanical watercolor etching of deep carmine red double-bloom flowers (Ranunculus or Camellia/Peony).
  * Shows two prominent dark crimson blossom heads, one smaller emerging bud beneath them, realistic muted olive-green veined leaves, and a clean single stem curving slightly towards the bottom-left corner.
  * Isolated, transparent background with no clipping box or border.
* **Placement & Size:**
  * Position: `absolute`
  * Left: `clamp(30px, 8vw, 120px)`
  * Top: `50%`
  * Transform: `translateY(-48%)`
  * Width: `clamp(200px, 18vw, 290px)`
  * Height: Auto (maintaining natural aspect ratio ~1:1.4)
  * Rotation: `0deg` to `-3deg` (slight organic tilt)
  * Z-index: `1`

#### Right Botanical Illustration: Pink Rose of Sharon / Hibiscus
* **Visual Description:**
  * Classical botanical watercolor engraving of dusty blush-pink Hibiscus / Rose of Sharon blossoms.
  * Shows one fully opened light salmon/dusty-rose flower with a dark ruby-red throat/eye and delicate yellow stamen, accompanied by an unopened pink bud to the right, realistic serrated green leaves, and a straight woody green stem.
  * Isolated, transparent background.
* **Placement & Size:**
  * Position: `absolute`
  * Right: `clamp(30px, 8vw, 120px)`
  * Top: `50%`
  * Transform: `translateY(-42%)`
  * Width: `clamp(180px, 16vw, 260px)`
  * Height: Auto (maintaining natural aspect ratio ~1:1.3)
  * Rotation: `0deg`
  * Z-index: `1`

## 6. Responsive Behavior & Breakpoints

* **Desktop Wide (`> 1200px`):**
  * Standard layout as shown in the media. Both botanicals have generous breathing room (`80px+` clearance from center block).
* **Tablet / Small Laptop (`768px - 1199px`):**
  * Reduce botanical width to `150px - 180px`.
  * Decrease horizontal margins for botanicals so they do not overlap center text.
  * Reduce headline font size to `48px`.
* **Mobile (`< 768px`):**
  * Nav bar collapses to a subtle hamburger menu or horizontal scroll list.
  * Left and right botanical illustrations move to subtle background watermarks (`opacity: 0.25; z-index: 0; pointer-events: none;`) or reposition above and below the main card to avoid overlapping the central interactive content.
  * Button layout switches to `flex-direction: column; width: 100%; max-width: 240px;` with full touch targets (`min-height: 48px`).
