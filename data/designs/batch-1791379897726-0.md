# Design Specification: Audi R8 V10 Performance Poster

## 1. Global Layout & Document Structure
- **Canvas / Viewport:** Full-screen responsive poster layout.
- **Orientation:** Portrait (3:4 ratio standard poster format).
- **Primary Color Palette:**
  - **Background (Off-White):** `#F4F5F7`
  - **Diagonal Accent Bar (Deep Red/Crimson):** `#990000` to `#7A0000` gradient or solid rich motorsport red.
  - **Typography & Details (Dark Charcoal/Black):** `#111111`
  - **Subtext & Secondary Details (Muted Gray):** `#777777`
  - **Highlight (Pure White):** `#FFFFFF`

---

## 2. Background & Diagonal Design Element
- **Base Canvas:** Solid light gray/off-white (`#F4F5F7`).
- **Diagonal Brand Stripe:** 
  - A prominent diagonal red band cuts from the top-left quadrant down through the bottom-right.
  - Angle: Approximately -30 degrees from horizontal.
  - Serves as a dynamic visual anchor holding the rear car view, center profile car, and specification footer.

---

## 3. Typography & Font Stack
*(Note: To replicate this precise editorial aesthetic, clean, geometric sans-serif typefaces are required.)*

### Primary Typeface Family: **Helvetica Neue / Inter / Montserrat**
- **Fallback Stack:** `Inter, Montserrat, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`

#### Specific Text Elements:
1. **Header Labels (Top Left & Center & Right):**
   - Text: `POSTER NO. 101` / `AUDI SPORT` / `R8 PERFORMANCE`
   - Size: 10px - 12px uppercase.
   - Tracking/Letter-spacing: Wide (`0.15em`).
   - Color: `#111111`.

2. **Hero Vertical Title (Right Side):**
   - Text: `R8 V10`
   - Orientation: Vertical reading direction (rotated 90 degrees or stacked characters depending on implementation). *In this poster, it runs vertically downward on the right side.*
   - Style: Bold, heavy geometric sans-serif outline or semi-transparent solid white/light gray block.
   - Color: `#E2E4E8` (subtle contrast against the off-white background).

3. **Descriptive Paragraph (Center Footer inside Red Bar):**
   - Text: *"THE AUDI R8 V10 PERFORMANCE COMBINES BREATHTAKING PERFORMANCE WITH EVERYDAY USABILITY. ENGINEERED WITH AUDI SPORT DNA, BUILT FOR THOSE WHO DEMAND PERFECTION."*
   - Font Size: 10px, uppercase, centered alignment.
   - Color: `#FFFFFF`.

4. **Technical Specs Footer (Bottom inside Red Bar):**
   - Labels: `ENGINE:`, `POWER:`, `0-100:`, `TOP SPEED:`
   - Values: `5.2L NATURALLY ASPIRATED V10`, `620 HP`, `3.1 SEC`, `331 KM/H`
   - Font Size: 11px labels, 14px bold values.
   - Color: `#FFFFFF`.

## 4. Imagery & Visual Assets

### Asset 1: Rear-End Car Crop (Top Left)
- **Subject:** Rear profile of a Nardo Gray Audi R8 V10 Performance, showing taillights, active exhaust, and "R8 V10 PERFORMANCE" badge.
- **Framing:** Angled crop nested neatly inside the upper-left boundary of the red diagonal stripe.
- **Effects:** Clean drop shadow separating it from the background.

### Asset 2: Full Side Profile Car (Center)
- **Subject:** Complete side profile of the Nardo Gray Audi R8 V10 Performance on a reflective white studio floor.
- **Key Details:** Carbon side blades, red brake calipers, black multi-spoke wheels, aerodynamic rear wing.
- **Positioning:** Placed horizontally overlapping the diagonal red stripe and light background.
- **Ground Effect:** Subtle soft drop-shadow / soft reflection underneath the tires to ground the vehicle.

---

## 5. UI / Decorative Elements
- **Target Reticle / Tech Brackets:** 
  - Thin white dashed/solid corner crop marks framing the descriptive paragraph and performance stats box in the lower red section. Gives a high-tech, blueprint/spec-sheet aesthetic.
- **Audi Branding:**
  - **Four Rings Logo:** Classic interconnected rings in solid black (`#111111`) placed at the bottom-left alongside the `Audi Sport` wordmark featuring the signature red parallelogram accent.

---

## 6. Animation Specifications (Interactive / Web Version)
If translating this static poster into an interactive web experience:
1. **Initial Load / Entrance:**
   - **Diagonal Red Stripe:** Scales in or slides down swiftly from the top-left at `-30 deg` using `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` over `1.0s`.
   - **Car Imagery:** Fades in and slides up slightly from the bottom (`translateY(20px)` to `0`) with a `0.8s` delay.
   - **Vertical Title (`R8 V10`):** Fades in with a letter-spacing expansion effect from `0.05em` to `0.15em`.
2. **Hover Interactions:**
   - **Spec Box:** Subtle border glow or slight elevation change when hovered.
   - **Car Profile:** Parallax micro-movement reacting to cursor coordinates (subtle 3D tilt).
