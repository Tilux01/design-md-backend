# UI Layout Specification: Monochrome Neumorphic Dashboard

## Global Configuration & Screen Setup
- **Background**: Dark charcoal / black gradient texture (`#111111` to `#222222`) simulating a physical matte surface.
- **Layout Strategy**: Absolute full-screen canvas with a multi-pane, bento-grid layout. All outer frames/borders of the main container are omitted to fill the screen edge-to-edge.
- **Color Palette**: 
  - **Base Background**: Dark charcoal (`#2a2a2a` / `#1f1f1f`)
  - **Cards & Panes**: Muted grays (`#363636`, `#444444`)
  - **Interactive Elements / Accents**: Light grays / Off-white (`#cccccc`, `#ffffff`)
  - **Text / Icons**: White (`#ffffff`) and translucent white.

---

## 1. Left Vertical Navigation Rail
- **Position**: Fixed left, spanning nearly the full height of the screen.
- **Width**: Narrow vertical bar (~80px).
- **Styling**: Rounded vertical card with a dark inset background (`#1e1e1e`).
- **Components (Top to Bottom)**:
  - **Top Item**: Circular button with a white 4-pointed star/sparkle icon inside (`#3a3a3a`).
  - **Middle Items**: A vertical stack of 6 small rounded pill/square icons acting as nav links (`#4a4a4a`).
  - **Lower Item**: Small single rounded icon square near the bottom.
  - **Bottom Item**: Larger circular profile/avatar placeholder button (`#aaaaaa`).

---

## 2. Central Main Content Area (Left-Center)
- **Position**: Occupies the top-left to middle region of the screen, just right of the nav rail.
- **Styling**: Large rounded container (`border-radius: 24px`), filled with a medium-dark gray surface (`#424242`).
- **Components**:
  - **Center Icon/Avatar**: A large central profile graphic consisting of a floating white circle (head) and a sweeping body shape (`#ffffff`).
  - **Floating Action Pill (Bottom-Left)**: A small white circular icon button overlapping the lower-left edge.
  - **Input / Action Bar (Bottom)**: A horizontal rounded pill spanning the bottom width with a dark "+" circle button on the left edge and a text placeholder line.

---

## 3. Right Stacked Menu / Cards (Right Column, Top)
- **Position**: Top-right corner of the layout.
- **Layout**: Vertical stack of 4 distinct components/cards.
- **Components**:
  1. **Top Pill**: A simple horizontal header pill (`#4a4a4a`).
  2. **Card 1 (Back)**: Medium gray card with a small indicator circle on the left edge.
  3. **Card 2 (Middle)**: Medium-light gray card with a small indicator circle.
  4. **Card 3 (Foreground)**: Lightest gray active card (`#b0b0b0`) with a small circular icon on the left edge.

---

## 4. Bottom-Center Dashboard Widget Card
- **Position**: Placed horizontally below the main central content area.
- **Styling**: Rounded rectangular card (`#333333`).
- **Components**:
  - **Header**: Small horizontal pill at the top-left (`#4e4e4e`).
  - **Grid / Columns**: 
    - Left column: 3 vertical line placeholders (text rows) with an outline style.
    - Middle column: 3 vertical rounded pill placeholders.
    - Right column: 3 stacked horizontal pill buttons (`#4e4e4e`).
  - **Footer**: A subtle dashed/solid separator line near the bottom edge.

---

## 5. Bottom-Right Multi-Layer Card Widget
- **Position**: Bottom-right corner.
- **Styling**: Rounded rectangular container (`#383838`).
- **Components**:
  - **Header**: Small pill and a circular settings/action button in the top-right corner.
  - **Visual Preview**: A layered, offset card stack overlapping the bottom-left corner with gradient/flat gray tones (`#505050`, `#606060`, `#707070`).

---

## Typography & Iconography Rules
- **Icons**: Minimalist, flat vector shapes using pure white (`#ffffff`) or light gray (`#cccccc`).
- **Borders & Shadows**: Soft outer drop shadows and subtle inner highlights to create a tactile, neumorphic depth effect across all panels.
