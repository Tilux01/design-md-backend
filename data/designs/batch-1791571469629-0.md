# UI Design Specification: Starbucks Web and Mobile Concept

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

### Background & Viewport
- **Background Color:** A soft, desaturated mint/sage green background (`#E6ECE8` approximately) sits behind the core white UI card, creating a subtle contrast.
- **Main Container:** A large, rounded white card (`#FFFFFF`) occupies the central viewport, acting as the primary website canvas.
- **Drop Shadow:** A soft, diffused, multi-layered drop shadow (`0px 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`) is applied to the main container, giving it a floating card effect.
- **Layout Type:** Desktop/Tablet web layout featuring an embedded, floating mobile-responsive product configuration modal overlapping the center-left.

---

## 2. Header & Navigation (Desktop)

### Top Bar Elements
- **Brand Logo:** 
  - Positioned at the top-left (`x: 100px`, `y: 250px`).
  - Features the iconic circular Starbucks Siren logo in classic green and white.
- **Navigation Links:**
  - Placed horizontally to the right of the logo.
  - Links: `COFFEE`, `TEA`, `MENU`.
  - **Typography:** Sans-serif, uppercase, tracked/letter-spaced.
- **Search Bar:**
  - Centered horizontally within the top navigation area.
  - Consists of a thin horizontal border line with placeholder text `SEARCH` and a small magnifying glass icon on the right side.
- **Right Utilities:**
  - **My Basket:** Accompanied by a shopping bag icon and red notification dot.
  - **Hamburger Menu Icon:** Three horizontal lines (`☰`) at the far top-right.

### Rewards Banner Section
- Located just beneath the main navigation on the left side:
  - **Text:** "STARBUCKS REWARDS" in bold uppercase.
  - **Action Link:** "GET MORE INFO" in smaller, uppercase text with an underline style.

---

## 3. Hero Section

- **Headline:**
  - Text: *"The happiest hour of the year"*
  - **Styling:** Bold, dark forest green (`#0C3B2E`), heavy weight, tight line-height, left-aligned.
- **Subtext:**
  - Text: *"Sign up to get exclusive access to deals on drinks this holiday season."*
  - **Call to Action (CTA):** "SEND ME MAGIC" right-aligned beneath the subtext in a smaller, prominent font style.
- **Hero Background Graphic:**
  - A subtle, large, semi-transparent soft mint circular abstract blob positioned behind the typography and hero images to add depth.

---

## 4. Product Grid (Web Background)

- **Card Structure:**
  - Four identical product cards are arranged in a horizontal row across the lower portion of the web interface.
  - **Background:** Light gray-cream rounded rectangular container (`#F4F4F2`).
- **Card Content:**
  - **Product Image:** A realistic photographic render of a Starbucks "Midnight Mint Mocha Frappuccino" topped with whipped cream and chocolate drizzle, sitting on a transparent background.
  - **Product Title:** "Midnight Mint Mocha Frappuccino" (two-line wrap, dark gray/black, medium weight).
  - **Nutritional Info:**
    - Label: `Calories`
    - Label: `Total Fat 19g` (with percentage `29%` aligned right).
    - Label: `- Saturated Fat 13g` (with percentage `65%` aligned right).
    - Label: `Trans Fat 0.5g`

---

## 5. Floating Mobile Modal (Overlay Component)

- **Positioning:** Overlaps the bottom-center/left of the main web interface, simulating a mobile app view embedded in the desktop design.
- **Container Styling:**
  - **Background:** Rich dark forest green gradient (`#144030` to `#0B2B1F`).
  - **Border Radius:** Highly rounded corners (approx. 24px).
  - **Pattern Overlay:** Subtle geometric or star-like pattern watermark subtly visible across the green background.
- **Carousel Controls:**
  - Left and right chevron arrows (`<` and `>`) placed vertically centered on the edges of the image area to switch products.
- **Product Display:**
  - A prominent photographic render of the "Midnight Mint Mocha Frappuccino" in a clear takeaway cup with a green straw.
- **Product Title:**
  - "Midnight Mint\nMocha\nFrappuccino" in large, bold white typography.
- **Form Controls (Dropdowns):**
  - **Size:** Label "Size" with a dropdown selector (`Select a size ∨`).
  - **Milk:** Label "Milk" with a dropdown selector (`Select a milk type ∨`).
  - **Drink:** Label "Drink" with a dropdown selector (`Drink type ∨`).
- **Primary CTA Button:**
  - Full-width white pill-shaped button.
  - Text: "ADD TO BASKET" in dark green, bold, centered uppercase.

---

## 6. Typography Specifications

| Element | Font Family (Primary) | Fallback Stack | Weight | Size (Desktop/App) | Color |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hero Title** | `SoDo Sans` / `Helvetica Neue` | `Arial, sans-serif` | Bold (700) | 36px / 42px | `#0C3B2E` (Forest Green) |
| **Nav Links** | `SoDo Sans` | `Arial, sans-serif` | Medium (500) | 12px | `#222222` |
| **Card Title** | `SoDo Sans` | `Arial, sans-serif` | Semi-Bold (600) | 14px | `#333333` |
| **Mobile Title** | `SoDo Sans` | `Arial, sans-serif` | Bold (700) | 28px | `#FFFFFF` |
| **Body / Specs** | `SoDo Sans` | `Arial, sans-serif` | Regular (400) | 11px / 12px | `#666666` (Web) / `#CCCCCC` (Mobile) |

*Note: Starbucks natively uses proprietary fonts such as **SoDo Sans**. In web development environments where custom brand fonts are unavailable, fallback to `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, or system sans-serif fonts.*

---

## 7. Color Palette

- **Primary Green (Brand):** `#006241` (Starbucks Green)
- **Dark Forest Green (Hero/Modal):** `#0C3B2E` / `#144030`
- **Background Mint:** `#E6ECE8`
- **Card Background (Web):** `#F4F4F2`
- **Card Background (White):** `#FFFFFF`
- **Text Dark:** `#222222`
- **Text Muted:** `#767676`
- **Notification Red:** `#D9381E`
