# Design Specification: Blvck Tumbler Landing Page

This document provides the complete design specifications to create a full-screen, pixel-perfect HTML/CSS implementation of the Blvck Tumbler landing page shown in the media.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Layout

### 1.1 Viewport & Container
- **Layout Type:** Full-screen viewport (`100vw` width, `100vh` height).
- **Background Color:** Dark Charcoal / Matte Black (`#1c1e22` or `rgb(28, 30, 34)`).
- **Z-Index Strategy:** Clean layering with absolute positioning for decorative vectors, typography, and interactive cards.

### 1.2 Typography & Fonts
- **Primary Headings:** Sans-serif, bold, geometric style (e.g., *Helvetica Neue*, *Montserrat*, or *Inter* with `font-weight: 700`).
- **Body & Captions:** Clean sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Roboto*, or *Arial* with `font-weight: 300` or `400`).
- **Color Palette:**
  - **Off-White / Cream:** `#f4eae1` (used for main headings).
  - **Vibrant Orange:** `#ff6b00` (used for accent circles, buttons, and brand tags).
  - **Muted Gray:** `#8a8d91` (used for paragraphs and secondary text).

---

## 2. Component Breakdown

### 2.1 Navigation Bar (Top)
- **Position:** Absolute, top-left and top-right, aligned with consistent horizontal padding.
- **Left Side:**
  - **Brand Logo:** A small red/orange location/pin icon followed by the text `"GRIDS."` in uppercase white tracking.
- **Right Side:**
  - **Hamburger Menu Icon:** Three horizontal lines representing the navigation drawer (`#ffffff`).

### 2.2 Hero Section (Left & Center)
- **Main Heading:**
  - **Text:** 
    ```
    Blvck
    Tumbler
    ```
  - **Styling:** Large scale, stacked text in off-white (`#f4eae1`), prominent line-height, bold weight.
- **Left Info Card ("Black Lifestyle Lovers"):**
  - **Position:** Absolute, positioned towards the lower-left area.
  - **Background:** Semi-transparent dark overlay box (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)` or slightly lighter than the background with backdrop blur).
  - **Title:** `"Black Lifestyle Lovers."` (White, medium weight).
  - **Body Copy:** `"Experience what it feels like drinking your favourite beverage out of our premium, all-black mug that was designed with you in mind."` (Muted gray, small text size, leading line-height).
- **Bottom CTA ("Explore Now"):**
  - **Position:** Bottom-left corner.
  - **Design:** A solid vibrant orange horizontal rectangle (`#ff6b00`) with the text `"EXPLORE NOW"` inside in white tracking.

### 2.3 Central Imagery (The Product)
- **Main Tumbler Image:**
  - **Description:** A high-resolution, matte-black travel tumbler sitting centrally on a reflective surface. It features a lid with a matching black straw inserted vertically. Subtle debossed or dark-grey typography reading `"ALL BLACK EVERYTHING"` is visible on the center of the cup body.
  - **Type:** Backgroundless product mockup (transparent PNG or WebP).
  - **Positioning:** Centered horizontally, anchored to the bottom edge of the screen.
- **Decorative Orange Ring:**
  - **Description:** A clean, vector stroke circular ring in vibrant orange (`#ff6b00`) positioned behind the top right portion of the tumbler and overlapping the straw area.
  - **Rotation/Placement:** Perfectly circular, positioned around coordinates `X: 52%, Y: 32%`.

### 2.4 Secondary Section (Right Side)
- **Faded Background Typography:**
  - **Text:** `"Blvck"` and `"Tumbler"` stacked vertically in extremely large, low-opacity dark gray text positioned in the upper right background.
- **Right Info Card ("The design"):**
  - **Position:** Absolute, positioned towards the lower-right area.
  - **Background:** Semi-transparent dark overlay box matching the left info card.
  - **Title:** `"The design."` (White).
  - **Body Copy:** `"Lorem ipsum dolor makes our Blvck Tumbler unique and different. Let's watch the video."` (Muted gray).
  - **Interactive Element:** A circular play button icon featuring a dark background with an orange triangular play symbol inside, positioned on the right edge of the card.
- **Navigation Controls:**
  - **Right Arrow (`→`):** A minimalist white right-pointing arrow positioned mid-right on the screen for carousel/slider pagination.
  - **Credit Tag:** Small uppercase `"CREDIT"` text placed statically at the bottom right corner.

---

## 3. Responsive Behavior & Implementation Notes
- **Scalability:** Use CSS Grid and Flexbox for macro-layouts so elements scale gracefully from laptop displays to ultra-wide screens.
- **Images:** Ensure the tumbler asset is isolated with a transparent background to blend seamlessly into the `#1c1e22` canvas.
