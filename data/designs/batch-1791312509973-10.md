# Design System Specification: Nature Production (ORA)

---

## 1. Design Philosophy & Overall Aesthetic
* **Primary Mood:** Modern, minimalist, editorial, eco-conscious, high-contrast, avant-garde digital showcase.
* **Visual Density:** Clean and airy, utilizing heavy vertical grid separation and intentional asymmetry to create focal tension.
* **Whitespace Balance:** High. Generous negative space balances a dominant left-aligned focal anchor (monochrome photography) with an editorial right-hand typography block.
* **Theme Balance:** Split layout. A dark-mode hero container on the left anchors the brand identity, while the right section transitions into a stark light-mode editorial canvas with delicate structural columns.

---

* **Primary Color (Dark Anchor):** `#111111` (Hero Background)

  * **Brand Green (P in PRODUCTION):** `#2E7D32`
  * **Brand Blue (C in PRODUCTION):** `#1565C0`

  * **Brand Cyan/Teal (N in PRODUCTION):** `#00838F`

  * **Light Canvas Base:** `#FFFFFF`

  * **Primary Heading:** `#1A1A1A`

---

## 3. Typography System & Hierarchy
* **Recommended Font Families:**
  * **Headings / Brand:** *Helvetica Neue*, *Inter*, or *Neue Haas Grotesk* (Sans-serif, geometric-grotesque)
  * **Body Copy:** *Inter*, *SF Pro Text*, or *Roboto*
* **Scale Breakdown:**
  * **Brand Monogram (ORA):** 16px, Weight: 700, Letter-spacing: `0.25em` (Vertical orientation)
  * **Navigation Items (Studio, Concept, Contact):** 12px, Weight: 400, Letter-spacing: `0.05em`
  * **H1 / Feature Heading (Nature.):** 56px–72px, Weight: 700, Line-height: `1.0`, Letter-spacing: `-0.02em`
  * **Sub-heading (PRODUCTION):** 24px, Weight: 600, Letter-spacing: `0.3em`
  * **Body Copy (Protect it):** 11px, Weight: 400, Line-height: `1.5`, Letter-spacing: `0`
  * **Pagination / Nav Arrows (`<`, `>`):** 20px, Weight: 300

---

## 4. Layout Architecture & Spatial Grid
* **Container Max-Width:** 1440px fluid container with fixed aspect ratio framing.
* **Grid System:** 6-column vertical structural grid with fine hairline border dividers (`1px` solid `rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`).
* **Section Sequence:**
  1. **Left Panel (2 columns):** Dark background, brand identifier (`O R A`), hero imagery (subject breaking out of the container boundary), interactive toggle (`+`).
  2. **Right Content Area (4 columns):** Light background, top navigation links, central editorial typography, editorial copy block, carousel pagination controls, and social metadata.
* **Z-Index Hierarchy:**
  * Background Canvas: `z-index: 0`
  * Image / Subject Layer (Overflowing boundary): `z-index: 10`
  * Typography & UI Controls: `z-index: 20`
  * Modals / Overlays: `z-index: 50`

---

## 5. Component Blueprint & Element Placement
* **Hero Image Frame:**
  * Bleeds across the dark-to-light boundary to create depth and breaking-plane composition.
  * Drop Shadow: `0px 24px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)` cast onto the light background.
* **Action Triggers & Buttons:**
  * **Circular Action Badge (`+`):** `32px × 32px`, border-radius: `50%`, background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, backdrop-filter: `blur(8px)`, text color: `#FFFFFF`.
  * **Pagination Chevrons (`<`, `>`):** Minimalist vector arrows, click targets `40px × 40px`, stroke-width: `1.5px`, color: `#1A1A1A`.
  * **Pagination Dots:** Active state indicated by a double-ring bullet indicator (`8px` diameter).
* **Navigation Architecture:**
  * Header links aligned to top-right grid anchors (`Studio.`, `Concept.`, `Contact.`).
  * Social handles (`v`, `t`, `in`) anchored vertically in the bottom-right grid column.

---

## 6. Motion Mechanics, 3D & Mobile Gestures
* **Animation Triggers:**
  * **Page Load / Entrance:** Staggered fade-in and upward translation (`transform: translateY(20px)` to `0`) for editorial typography and image layers.
  * **Hover States:** Structural grid line opacity shifts from `0.08` to `0.25` on active column hover.
  * **Carousel Transition:** Horizontal slide with cubic-bezier easing (`cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`).
* **Transition Durations:**
  * Micro-interactions (Buttons, Links): `200ms ease-in-out`
  * Page / Slide Transitions: `600ms cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`
* **Mobile Native Behaviors:**
  * Collapse multi-column grid into a single-column stacked layout.
  * Swipe left/right gestures mapped to carousel index steps with rubber-band resistance at boundaries.
