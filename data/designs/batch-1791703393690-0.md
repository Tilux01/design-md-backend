# Design Specification: RIDEQUEST Luxury Transport Platform

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Design Language

The design is an **Editorial Luxury Mobility Bento-Grid** combining brutalist structure with ultra-high-end executive minimalism. It relies on razor-thin grid borders, stark contrast shifts between deep obsidian dark-mode sections and surgical alabaster light-mode sections, technical telemetry metadata, and architectural geometric masks (perfect circles, pill stadium shapes, and tall arched portals).

---

## 2. Color Palette & Theming

### 2.1 Dark Surface Palette (Hero & Features & Footer)
* **Background Dark Primary:** `#0C0D0E` (Deep obsidian black)
* **Background Dark Elevated / HUD:** `rgba(25, 27, 30, 0.65)` (Frosted backdrop with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`)
* **Text Dark Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (100% white)

* **Text Light Secondary:** `#686C73` (Slate graphite)

## 3. Typography System

### 3.1 Font Families & Fallback Cascade
* **Primary Display & Body Font:**
  * *Target Font:* `Syne`, `PP Neue Montreal`, or `Cabinet Grotesk`
  * *Universal Modern Fallback:* `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `sans-serif`
  * *Characteristics:* Neo-grotesque sans-serif with geometric precision, tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`), and sharp vertical cuts.
* **Technical Monospace / Sub-label Font:**
  * *Target Font:* `Space Mono`, `PP Supply Mono`, or `JetBrains Mono`
  * *Universal Modern Fallback:* `ui-monospace`, `SFMono-Regular`, `Menlo`, `Monaco`, `monospace`
  * *Usage:* Used for `/ABOUT`, `/NOW`, telemetry numbers, percentages, and micro-labels.

## 4. Layout Architecture & Structural Grid

* **Container Model:** Edge-to-edge full viewport grid layout (`100vw`). No outer drop shadows, no device framing, no rounded outer container.
* **Dividing Lines:** Continuous grid lines via `border-right: 1px solid` and `border-bottom: 1px solid` forming a cohesive bento matrix.
* **Layout Structure (Desktop 3-Column Asymmetric Grid):**
  * **Left Column:** `22%` width (Hero vertical branding, "WHO WE ARE?", App download badge, "WHY CHOOSE RIDE-QUEST?").
  * **Middle Column:** `53%` width (Hero centerpiece, About manifesto, Promo video card, Fleet charts, Safety card, How-it-works arch).
  * **Right Column:** `25%` width (Aviation radar card, Team avatars, Electric car circle card, Driver profile, 24/7 CTA).

## 6. Micro-Interactions & Motion Choreography

1. **Grid Borders:** Fixed 1px hairline rendering (`border: 1px solid var(--grid-border)`).
2. **Hover States on Action Pills:**
   * Light badges invert to dark surfaces on hover (`transition: all 0.25s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
   * Red CTA `"CONTACT US •"` scales up slightly (`transform: scale(1.03)` with soft glow box-shadow `0 8px 24px rgba(255, 59, 48, 0.35)`).
3. **Circular Video & Image Masks:**
   * Image within the `"Meet and greet"` pill banner expands with a subtle slow zoom (`transform: scale(1.05)`) when hovered.
   * Play button pulse on idle (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.4)` expanding to `0 0 0 12px rgba(255,255,255,0)`).
4. **Trajectory Lines:** SVGs with `stroke-dasharray` and animated `stroke-dashoffset` to simulate real-time transport telemetry.
