# Design Specification: Fitness App Interface Clone

This document outlines the complete design, layout, typography, and asset specifications to create a pixel-perfect, full-screen clone of the provided fitness application interface. 

---

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

*   **Viewport & Background:** 
    *   **Full-screen layout:** The design spans the entire viewport (`100vw` width, `100vh` height).
    *   **Background:** A large hero image (`Hero Background`) covers the entire screen as a full-bleed backdrop. A dark overlay or atmospheric grading gives it a muted, high-contrast athletic editorial feel.
*   **Central Content Window (The App UI Frame):**
    *   The interface elements are grouped inside a centralized, rounded-corner container (resembling a tablet or desktop web-app window) with a dark charcoal/obsidian background (`#1A1A1E`).
    *   **Border Radius:** Smooth, large corner radii (`approx. 24px` to `32px`).
    *   **Grid Structure:** The app container is divided into two primary columns/sections:
        1.  **Left Sidebar/Content Column:** Contains navigation links, a video/article preview tile, a "Recommended" editorial feature card, and bottom pagination/footer text.
        2.  **Right Hero/Action Column:** Contains a search/action bar at the top, a dynamic central action area with an athlete action shot, an "Add to cart" overlay button, and a promotional product card ("Discover new product").

---

## 2. Typography & Font Stack

*   **Primary Font Family (Headings & UI):** A clean, geometric, or neo-grotesque sans-serif typeface with sharp modern aesthetics (similar to **Inter**, **SF Pro Display**, or **Helvetica Neue**).
*   **Secondary/Accent Font (Body & Descriptive text):** A highly legible, modern sans-serif.

### Fallback Font Stack:
```css
font-family: 'Inter', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;
```

### Typography Hierarchy:
1.  **Navigation Links ("A-News", "Training", "Programs", etc.):**
    *   *Weight:* Regular / Medium (~400–500)
    *   *Size:* Small (~12px – 14px)
    *   *Color:* Light grey/white (`#E0E0E0`), active state highlighted in solid white.
2.  **Main Headings ("The Community Lift-Off showcased..."):**
    *   *Weight:* Bold (~700)
    *   *Size:* Large display (~24px – 28px)
    *   *Color:* Dark charcoal/black (`#111111`) inside the light yellow card.
3.  **Body Text / Descriptions:**
    *   *Weight:* Regular (~400)
    *   *Size:* Small (~11px – 13px)
    *   *Line-height:* 1.4 – 1.5
    *   *Color:* Muted dark grey (`#555555`).
4.  **Footer Callout ("Train smart, recover harder, live better"):**
    *   *Weight:* Medium (~500)
    *   *Size:* Medium (~16px)
    *   *Color:* Light grey/off-white with high readability against the background.

---

## 3. Component-by-Component Breakdown

### A. Top Navigation Bar (Inside App Container)
*   **Left Links:** Horizontal list of navigation items: `A-News`, `Training`, `Programs`, `Nutrition`, `Shop`, `Docs`.
*   **Search Bar:** Centered pill-shaped search input featuring a magnifying glass icon and placeholder text: `Search workouts`. Background is dark translucent grey (`#2A2A2E`).
*   **Action Buttons (Right):**
    *   A circular pill button with a `+` icon and the text `Create post`. The plus icon is housed inside a solid light-yellow/lime circle (`#D2FF6C`).

---

### B. Left Column Elements

#### 1. Article/Video Preview Card (Top Left)
*   **Background Image:** Action shot of an athlete in a gym setting, slightly blurred/darkened with a gradient overlay.
*   **Overlay Text:** 
    *   Title: *“How to Build Strength Without Burning Out”* (Bold white, ~14px).
    *   Sub-caption text in smaller muted grey.
*   **Interactive Icon:** A diagonal arrow icon (`↗`) inside a small dark circular badge positioned at the bottom right of the image tile.

#### 2. Quick Stat/Metric Widgets (Middle Left)
*   **Timer Widget:** A dark square tile displaying `30` (large bold numbers) with the label `minutes` underneath.
*   **Recovery Icon Widget:** A dark square tile featuring a minimalist bottle/icon representing recovery metrics or supplements, with the label `recovery` underneath.

#### 3. "Recommended" Editorial Feature Card (Bottom Left)
*   **Background Color:** Solid pale pastel yellow / cream (`#FAF6C8` or `#F9F7D9`).
*   **Header:** Small uppercase label `Recommended` paired with a three-dot menu icon (`•••`) on the top right.
*   **Headline:** *"The Community Lift-Off showcased an inspiring lineup of everyday athletes"* in bold, large dark text.
*   **Body Copy:** Descriptive paragraph detailing the event (*"From first-time lifters to seasoned pros..."*).
*   **Button:** A pill-shaped button labeled `Read more` with a dark solid outline and white/cream interior.
*   **Bottom Bar:** 
    *   Left: *“The truth about supplements”*
    *   Right: *“Next”* with a diagonal arrow icon (`↗`) inside a circular button.

---

### C. Right Column Elements

#### 1. Top Action Overlay
*   **Add to Cart Button:** A pill-shaped button reading `Add to cart` with a light green/lime circular badge containing a `+` icon (`#D2FF6C`).

#### 2. Main Hero Image (Right Panel)
*   **Description:** A dynamic, full-bleed action photograph of a male athlete with braided hair and sunglasses, wearing a white ribbed tank top, black shorts, white socks, and white sneakers, caught mid-motion/jumping.
*   **Placement & Rotation:** Occupies the entire right half of the internal app frame as a backdrop container.

#### 3. Product Promo Card ("Discover new product")
*   **Container:** A floating dark card (`#111115`) positioned in the lower right corner of the right column with rounded corners.
*   **Visual Content:** High-end product photography of a black athletic shaker bottle/flask with red accent details, isolated against a dark background (**backgroundless product render**).
*   **Text & Action:**
    *   Heading: *“Discover new product”* (White text).
    *   Button: Pill-shaped `Shop now` button at the bottom of the card.

---

## 4. Background & Global Footer Elements

*   **Global Background Image:** A large, cinematic editorial photograph of an athlete in motion (matching the person in the app interface), spanning the entire background viewport with a soft, moody desaturated tone.
*   **Bottom-Right Footer Text:**
    *   *“Train smart, recover harder, live better”* (Bold, light grey, ~18px).
    *   *“Personal project created to sharpen my design skills and push them to a more polished, professional level.”* (Smaller, muted grey, ~12px).
    *   Placement: Fixed/aligned to the bottom-right corner of the global screen, sitting below the main app container.
