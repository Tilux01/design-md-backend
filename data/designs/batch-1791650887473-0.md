# Design Specification: "Glitch" Future Fashion E-Commerce Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Structure
* **Viewport & Canvas:** 
  * Fullscreen display with a dark, immersive background.
  * The main content sits inside an integrated, floating rounded-rectangle UI container with a thick white border, soft drop shadows, and deep dark-gray/black internal surface areas.
* **Color Palette:**
  * **Background (Global):** Deep charcoal/near-black (`#111113`).
  * **Container Base:** Dark slate/black (`#141416`).
  * **Borders & UI Framework:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  * **Accent Color (Neon/Sci-Fi Orange):** Electric orange/amber (`#FF6B00` / `#FF8800`) used on the helmet visors, weapon details, and product callouts.
  * **Typography Color:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for primary headings, light gray (`#A0A0A5`) for secondary/subtext.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts
* **Primary Display Font (Futuristic Sans):** 
  * *Style:* Geometric, sci-fi, rounded-edge tech display font with distinctive custom glyphs (e.g., stylized 'S', 'A', 'N' in words like "GLITCH" and "FASHION").
  * *Fallback Stack:* `font-family: 'Orbitron', 'Eurostile', 'Rajdhani', sans-serif;`
* **Body / Subtext Font:**
  * *Style:* Clean, legible, modern grotesk sans-serif.
  * *Fallback Stack:* `font-family: 'Inter', 'SF Pro Display', system-ui, sans-serif;`
* **Typography Hierarchy:**
  * **Brand Logo ("GLITCH"):** Uppercase, wide tracking, custom rounded tech lettering.
  * **Hero Headline ("FUTURE FASHION, REDEFINED."):** Extra-large, heavy, bold geometric tracking. Line breaks explicitly set after "FASHION,".
  * **Product Labels ("NEUROVIBE MK-I", "VOIDBLADE", "SHADOW CIRCUIT JACKET", "CRIMSON PHANTOM"):** Small, uppercase, letter-spaced, white.
  * **Pricing ("$230", "$120", "$450"):** Medium weight, legible numeric styling in white.

---

## 3. Header & Navigation Section
* **Logo Top-Left:**
  * Contained within a pill-shaped white-bordered badge.
  * Text reads: `GLITCH` in futuristic outlined/solid tech font.
* **Top-Right Utilities:**
  * Three floating circular/pill UI buttons positioned horizontally:
    1. **Search Button:** Circular dark button with a white magnifying glass icon.
    2. **Cart/Bag Button:** Circular dark button with a shopping bag/basket icon.
    3. **Contact Pill:** Wide rounded pill button featuring a mail envelope icon and the text `Contact`.

---

## 4. Hero Section & Central Visual
* **Background Watermark:**
  * Large, faint outlined text watermark reading `GLITCH` positioned centrally in the dark background box behind the main character model.
* **Hero Headline (Left Column):**
  * Positioned toward the bottom-left of the inner container.
  * Content:
    ```
    FUTURE
    FASHION,
    REDEFINED.
    ```
* **Call-to-Action (Top-Right of Inner Box):**
  * A white rounded capsule button floating above the character shoulder.
  * Text: `SHOP NOW` (Black text on white background, uppercase).

---

## 5. Interactive Product Hotspots & Annotations
The interface features thin white indicator lines terminating in glowing white dots pointing to specific equipment/clothing parts on the central model:

1. **Neurovibe MK-I (Top Left):**
   * *Label text:* `NEUROVIBE MK-I` (with `$230` below it).
   * *Position:* Points to the glowing orange visor rim of the helmet.
2. **Voidblade (Center Right):**
   * *Label text:* `VOIDBLADE` (with `$120` below it).
   * *Position:* Points to the dual hilt/scabbard mounted on the character's back.
3. **Shadow Circuit Jacket (Lower Right):**
   * *Label text:* `SHADOW CIRCUIT JACKET` (with `$450` below it).
   * *Position:* Points to the textured collar/shoulder armor of the teal jacket.

## 6. Secondary Product Card (Bottom Right)
* **Card Container:**
  * A rounded-rectangle dark card positioned in the bottom-right corner of the inner container, bordered in white.
* **Product Image Inside Card:**
  * *Image Description:* A cyberpunk character model from the chest up, wearing a bright crimson-red tactical jacket (`CRIMSON PHANTOM`) with white Japanese kanji lettering printed across the chest, paired with a white sci-fi tactical helmet.
  * *Placement:* Fills the left/central part of the mini card.
* **Product Details:**
  * *Name:* `CRIMSON PHANTOM` (White, uppercase, small).
  * *Price:* `$450` (White).
* **Navigation Arrows:**
  * Two small circular buttons with left (`←`) and right (`→`) arrows located at the top-right of the mini product card for carousel navigation.
