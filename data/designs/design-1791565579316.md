# UI Design & Implementation Specification: Dashboard

## 1. Overview & Layout Strategy
This specification details a pixel-perfect clone of the "Lordbank" dashboard interface. 

- **Color Palette & Themes:** The design adopts a modern, clean, soft-themed aesthetic dominated by **Mint Green (`#65D193` / `#82DF9D`)** for primary accents/success states, **Dark Slate/Navy (`#1E3140`, `#1C2B36`, `#16222B`)** for cards and structural elements, and a **Light Off-White/Gray (`#F4F7F6`, `#FFFFFF`)** canvas background.
- **Grid Structure:** The viewport is divided into two primary asymmetrical columns:
  - **Left Section (approx. 72% width):** Header greeting, profile spotlight card, weekly hours breakdown, team composition widgets, and working hours analytics.
  - **Right Section (approx. 28% width):** Payout/Salary activity feed (`Salaries and incentive`) and a bottom structural breakdown widget displaying payroll metrics (Basic salary, Performance, Gifts, and Take-home pay).
- **Global Constraints:** Eliminate all mockup browser chrome (no outer window frame, address bars, or OS-level drop shadows). Render directly as a responsive full-screen web viewport.

---

## 2. Typography & Fallback Strategy

The UI uses a geometric sans-serif typeface family characterized by rounded terminals and high legibility at small sizes (similar to **Plus Jakarta Sans**, **Inter**, or **SF Pro Display**).

```css
font-family: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;
```

### Type Hierarchy:
*   **Page Title ("Good morning Jhon"):** `font-size: 28px`, `font-weight: 700`, `letter-spacing: -0.5px`, `color: #16222B`.
*   **Card Headings:** `font-size: 16px`, `font-weight: 600`, `color: #16222B`.
*   **KPI / Metric Numbers (e.g., `46,5`, `120`, `$2.540.00`):** `font-size: 32px` to `42px`, `font-weight: 700`.
*   **Body & Subtext:** `font-size: 12px` to `14px`, `font-weight: 400`/`500`, `color: #7B8C9B` (muted slate gray).
