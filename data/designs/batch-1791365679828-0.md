# Future Website Clone Design System & Specifications

This document outlines the precise specifications to create a pixel-perfect clone of the "Future" web interface shown in the image.

---

## 1. Global Styles & Foundations

### Color Palette
*   **Primary Background:** `#0B0C10` (Deep obsidian black with very subtle violet undertones)
*   **Secondary/Card Background:** `#12131A` (Used for frosted glass panels and footer info blocks)
*   **Primary Text & Highlights:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white)
*   **Muted Body Text:** `#8C8F99` (Cool light gray)
*   **Accent Color:** `#2B54FF` (Electric futuristic blue)
*   **Interface Borders/Dividers:** `#21232D` (Subtle dark gray separating structural columns)

### Typography
*   **Font Family:** Geometric, futuristic sans-serif (e.g., *Rajdhani*, *Orbitron*, or *Syncopate* for headings; *Inter* for body copy).
*   **Logo (`N.JEY`):** Bold, spaced-out sans-serif.
*   **Main Hero Title (`FUTURE`):** Ultra-wide geometric sans-serif, uppercase, heavy letter-spacing.
*   **Navigation Menu:** Uppercase, medium weight, small tracking.

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Grid

*   **Viewport:** Fullscreen (100vw × 100vh), zero margins/padding on the root body.
*   **Layout Engine:** CSS Grid / Flexbox with vertical column dividers (simulating a modular HUD UI layout).
*   **Columns (Left to Right):**
    1.  **Far Left Column (Branding & Navigation Indicator):** Contains the logo and vertical pagination dots.
    2.  **Center-Left Column (Hero Copy & CTA):** Contains the subheadline, main title, body paragraph, and "LET'S GO" button. Highlighted by a vertical blue accent block.
    3.  **Center-Right Column (3D Character Render & Annotations):** The focal point featuring the cyborg rendering and technical callout lines ("ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE", "NO EMOTION").
    4.  **Far Right Column (Social/Platform Links):** Vertical icon list (`Vk`, `Tw`, `In`, `Be`).

---

## 3. Component Specifications

### A. Header & Navigation
*   **Logo:** Top-left corner (`N.JEY`), white text.
*   **Nav Links:** Centered horizontally at the top (`HOME`, `ABOUT`, `WORKS`, `SERVICES`, `CONTACTS`).
    *   *State:* Inactive links are muted gray (`#6B6F7B`), active or hovered links are white with a subtle glow or underline indicator.
*   **Language Switcher / Settings:** Top-right (`ENG`, menu grid icon).

### B. Hero Section
*   **Pre-header Label:** `HERE AND NOW` prefixed with a solid `#2B54FF` vertical blue rectangle.
*   **Main Heading:** `FUTURE`
    *   *Styling:* Large display size, distinct geometric letterforms (note the open/modern terminals on letters like 'T' and 'R').
*   **Body Copy:** 
    > "People who think about the future, about how to improve their lives, and take action in this direction, have a plan of action."
    *   *Color:* `#8C8F99`, small clean typography, left-aligned.
*   **Call-to-Action (CTA):** 
    *   *Button Text:* `LET'S GO`
    *   *Style:* Outlined box button with thin white/gray border, transparent background, uppercase text.

### C. Visual Centerpiece (Cyborg Render)
*   **Image:** High-contrast 3D render of a dark chrome/obsidian cyborg head and shoulder bust.
*   **HUD Annotations:** Thin leader lines pointing to the model:
    *   Upper right: `ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE`
    *   Lower left: `NO EMOTION`
*   **Bottom Feature Cards:** Two semi-transparent frosted cards overlaying the bottom of the 3D render:
    *   **Card 1:** `TECHNOLOGY` + micro-copy placeholder.
    *   **Card 2:** `INNOVATION` + micro-copy placeholder.
    *   *Background:* `rgba(18, 19, 26, 0.7)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(10px)`.

### D. Sidebars
*   **Left Edge:** A vertical stack of 5 pagination/slide indicator dots (first dot active/white, rest dim).
*   **Right Edge:** Vertical social/platform abbreviations (`Vk`, `Tw`, `In`, `Be`) aligned vertically.

---
