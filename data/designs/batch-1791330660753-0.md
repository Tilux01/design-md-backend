# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Clean Enterprise B2B SaaS, Editorial Minimalist, Technical Authority.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio with generous container padding and editorial typography scaling. Focused on clarity, readability, and structural balance.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean flat white surfaces paired with subtle border definitions (`#E2E8F0`) and high-contrast dark accents. Zero heavy drop-shadows on cards; relies on structural borders and crisp typography hierarchy.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Clean monochrome minimalism, high-end tech illustration, balanced typographic contrast, precise border structures.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.05em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#0F172A`, text color `#FFFFFF`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content to prevent rendering glitches.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or SVG icon component.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-emerald-500 inline-block"></span>`.
