# Design System & Implementation Spec (`design.md`)

## 1. Color Palette
* **Background Primary:** `#0A0A0A` (Deep Matte Black)
* **Background Secondary:** `#141414` (Dark Charcoal)
* **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
* **Text Secondary:** `#737373` (Medium Muted Gray)
* **Accent Red:** `#E5383B` / `#FF334B` (Vibrant Crimson Red)
* **Border/Outline:** `#262626` (Subtle Dark Gray)

---

## 2. Typography
* **Primary Font:** `Inter`, `Helvetica Neue`, Arial, sans-serif.
* **Weights & Hierarchy:**
  * **Brand/Logo:** 700 (Bold), `20px`, Tracking: Normal (`Bike.`).
  * **Headlines (H1):** 800 (Extra Bold), `48px`–`64px`, Uppercase, Tracking: `0.05em`.
  * **Subtitles / Eyebrow:** 600 (Semi-Bold), `12px`–`14px`, Uppercase, Tracking: `0.15em`, Color: `#737373`.
  * **Body Text:** 400 (Regular), `14px`, Line Height: `1.6`, Color: `#8C8C8C`.
  * **Navigation & Buttons:** 500 (Medium), `13px`, Uppercase, Tracking: `0.1em`.

---

## 3. Global Layout & Components

### A. Navigation Bar
* **Position:** Fixed or absolute top, `100%` width, `padding: 32px 64px`.
* **Left:** Text logo `"Bike."` in white.
* **Right:** 
  * Inline links: `ABOUT`, `COLLECTION`, `SPECS`.
  * Primary CTA: Solid red pill button (`#E5383B`) with white uppercase text `"CONTACT"`.

### B. Main Hero Section (Full-Screen)
* **Dimensions:** `100vw` × `100vh`, dark vignette overlay (`#0A0A0A`).
* **Visual Asset (Center-Left):**
  * **Description:** Macro studio photograph of a matte-black road bike cockpit. Focuses on curved drop handlebars wrapped in textured black tape, clamp, and stem.
  * **Lighting:** High-contrast dramatic rim lighting highlighting curves against deep black shadows.
  * **Background:** Seamless integrated dark gradient (fades to `#0A0A0A`).
  * **Placement:** Occupies 60% of the left viewport, centered vertically, angled slightly downward to the right.
* **Content Block (Right Aligned):**
  * **Position:** Right side, vertical center (`padding-right: 80px`, `max-width: 420px`).
  * **Eyebrow:** `"THIS BIKE"`
  * **H1:** `"IS LIFE"`
  * **Body:** 2–3 lines of muted descriptive copy.
  * **CTA Button:** Outlined rectangular button, border `1px solid #E5383B`, text `"SEE MORE"` in white or red.

### C. Pagination / Carousel Controls
* **Position:** Bottom center/right.
* **Indicator:** Numeric counter (e.g., `01 / 03`) with active indicator highlighted via a solid red block badge.
