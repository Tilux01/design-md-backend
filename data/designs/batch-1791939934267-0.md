# Design Specification: Minimalist Circular Media Player

## 1. General Layout & Theme
*   **Orientation:** Portrait (Mobile Full Screen).
*   **Background:** A soft vertical linear gradient. The background color should dynamically match the primary hue of the album art.
    *   **Screen 1 (Brown):** Top: `#8D5B3E` to Bottom: `#4B2C1D`.
    *   **Screen 2 (Green):** Top: `#5B7A68` to Bottom: `#2D3D34`.
    *   **Screen 3 (Blue):** Top: `#536D7A` to Bottom: `#2A353B`.
*   **Padding:** 24px horizontal padding for all elements.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` (Semi-Bold for Titles, Regular for Metadata).
*   **Secondary Font:** `Montserrat` or `System-UI`.
*   **Fallbacks:** `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Text Colors:**
    *   Primary: White (`#FFFFFF`) at 100% opacity.
    *   Secondary: White (`#FFFFFF`) at 60% opacity (used for artist names and timestamps).

## 3. Header Section (Top Bar)
*   **Left Element:** Chevron Down icon (width: 20px, stroke: 2px).
*   **Center Element:** Text "Indie Rock Road Trip". Font size: 12px, Letter spacing: 0.5px, Uppercase/Title case.
*   **Right Element:** Horizontal Ellipsis (three dots) icon.
*   **Placement:** 40px from the top of the screen.

## 4. Track Information
*   **Song Title:** Font size: 22px, Weight: 600 (Semi-bold), Color: `#FFFFFF`. Centered.
*   **Artist Name:** Font size: 14px, Weight: 400, Color: `#FFFFFF` (60% opacity). Centered, 8px below the title.
*   **Timestamps:** 
    *   Format: `00:00 | 00:00`
    *   Font size: 11px, Monospace-style font recommended for alignment.
    *   Placement: 30px below the artist name.

## 5. Central Visual (Circular Progress & Album Art)
*   **Outer Ring (Progress Bar):**
    *   Diameter: Approx. 65% of screen width.
    *   Stroke Width: 2px.
    *   Base Ring Color: White at 10% opacity.
    *   Active Progress Color: A soft gold/beige (`#E5C094`).
    *   **The Knob:** A solid circle (8px diameter) in the active progress color, positioned at the current time head.
*   **Album Art (Inner Circle):**
    *   Diameter: 85% of the Outer Ring’s diameter.
    *   Shape: Perfect circle (no border-radius, use `overflow: hidden` on a circular container).
    *   **Image Descriptions:**
        *   **Image 1 (The Runner):** A high-contrast, moody photograph. In the center is a dark, silhouette-like structure resembling a cross or a wooden post draped in dark fabric. The background is a field of orange/brown dried flowers or tall grass under a hazy, overcast sky. No background transparency.
        *   **Image 2 (Ylang Ylang):** A painterly, atmospheric scene. A silhouette of a person sits at a piano on a wet, reflective beach at twilight. The sky is a muted teal/green. A small, bright light source (a lamp) sits atop the piano. No background transparency.
        *   **Image 3 (A Moment Apart):** A cinematic wide shot. Two figures stand on a dark, rocky cliffside overlooking a horizon at dusk. Floating above them in the center is a white, glowing wireframe icosahedron (geometric 3D shape). The color palette is deep blue and grey. No background transparency.

## 6. Primary Playback Controls
*   **Layout:** Horizontal flex row, centered.
*   **Icons (Left to Right):**
    1.  **Minus Circle:** Small, thin stroke. (Dislike/Remove).
    2.  **Skip Back:** Standard media icon.
    3.  **Play Button:** A large white solid circle (approx 60px diameter) with a centered dark triangle (play icon) matching the background's darkest hue.
    4.  **Skip Forward:** Standard media icon.
    5.  **Heart:** Small, solid heart icon (Like).
*   **Spacing:** Evenly distributed; Play button is the focal point.

## 7. Footer Controls (Bottom Bar)
*   **Layout:** Horizontal flex row, centered.
*   **Icons (Left to Right):**
    1.  **Shuffle:** Two crossing arrows.
    2.  **Queue:** Three horizontal lines (hamburger style).
    3.  **Volume:** Speaker icon with small waves.
    4.  **Repeat:** Two arrows forming a circle.
*   **Style:** All icons are white at 60% opacity, approx 18px in size.
*   **Placement:** 40px from the bottom of the screen.

## 8. Visual Effects
*   **Drop Shadows:** The circular album art has a very subtle, large-radius outer glow/shadow matching the theme color to give it depth.
*   **Transitions:** If implemented, the background gradient should cross-fade smoothly when the track changes.
*   **Icon Stroke:** All icons (except the Play button) should use a "Thin" or "Light" weight (approx 1.5px to 2px stroke width).
