# Design Specification: FilScreen Dashboard

## 1. Global Styles
*   **Background:** The main application background is a solid, very light off-white/grey (`#F4F5F9`).
*   **Border Radius:** A consistent high-rounding strategy is used. Main containers and cards use `24px` to `32px`. Buttons and small tags use `12px` to `16px`.
*   **Spacing:** The layout follows a strict grid with roughly `24px` to `32px` of padding between major sections.
*   **Shadows:** Soft, diffused drop shadows are used on the main container to lift it from the background. Cards have very subtle `0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)` shadows.

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Inter** or **Poppins** (Sans-serif).
*   **Fallback Stack:** `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Weights:** 
    *   Regular (400) for secondary text and descriptions.
    *   Medium (500) for navigation items.
    *   Semi-Bold (600) for section headers.
    *   Bold (700) for movie titles and logo.
*   **Colors:**
    *   Heading/Active: Dark Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`).
    *   Body/Secondary: Muted Grey (`#7E7E7E`).
    *   Accent: Royal Purple (`#6366F1`).

## 3. Layout Structure
The interface is a three-column layout:
1.  **Left Sidebar (Navigation):** ~18% width.
2.  **Main Content (Center):** ~57% width.
3.  **Right Sidebar (Activity/Search):** ~25% width.

---

## 4. Left Sidebar (Navigation)
*   **Logo:** "FilScreen" in Bold Sans-serif. "Fil" is in Dark Charcoal, "Screen" is in the accent Purple (`#6366F1`).
*   **Navigation Links:** Vertical list with icons (approx. 20px size).
    *   **Home (Active):** Background is a light lavender tint (`#EEF2FF`). Text and icon are Purple.
    *   **Categories, Favorites, Downloads, Friends, Community, History:** Default state is Muted Grey text/icon.
    *   *Note:* "Downloads" and "Community" have a small purple notification dot to the right.
*   **Bottom Links:** "Settings" and "Log out" are positioned at the bottom, separated by a large vertical gap from the main nav.

---

## 5. Main Content (Center)

### 5.1 Top Navigation Tabs
*   A horizontal row of pill-shaped buttons.
*   **New (Active):** Purple background with white text.
*   **Series, Movies, TV shows:** Transparent background with Muted Grey text.

### 5.2 Hero Banner (Dune: Part Two)
*   **Image:** A wide cinematic shot of two characters (Paul Atreides and Chani) standing on a desert dune against a sunset orange/brown sky. One character holds a knife upward.
*   **Overlay Elements:**
    *   **Tags:** Two pill tags at the top left: "Science fiction" and "Adventure" (Semi-transparent white background).
    *   **Rating:** Top right pill tag: "4.8 ★" (Gold star).
    *   **Text Content:** Large white title "Dune: Part Two". Below it, a two-line description in white, slightly transparent text.
    *   **Buttons:** 
        *   "Watch" button: Purple background, white text, play icon.
        *   "Heart" button: Circular, semi-transparent white, containing a heart icon.
    *   **Social Proof:** Bottom right shows three small circular friend avatars overlapping, followed by the text "Friends are watching" in small white font.

### 5.3 Grid Sections ("Trending now" & "Coming soon")
*   **Section Headers:** Bold text with a small emoji (e.g., 🔥 for Trending).
*   **Movie Cards:**
    *   **Aspect Ratio:** Roughly 4:5 vertical cards with `24px` border radius.
    *   **Card Header:** A semi-transparent rating tag (e.g., "4.8") on the top left and a heart icon on the top right.
    *   **Card Footer:** Title in white bold text, followed by genre in smaller regular text.
*   **Specific Images (Trending):**
    1.  **Oppenheimer:** A man in a hat (Cillian Murphy) centered against a circular orange/fire explosion background.
    2.  **The Witcher:** A man with long white hair (Geralt) and a woman (Ciri) in a dark, moody blue/grey forest setting.
    3.  **The Marvels:** A woman (Captain Marvel) glowing with golden energy against a cosmic purple/pink background.

## 6. Right Sidebar

### 6.1 Search Bar
*   A wide, pill-shaped input field.
*   Light grey background (`#F0F0F0`).
*   Left-aligned "Search" placeholder text and a magnifying glass icon on the right.
*   To the right of the search bar: A notification bell icon and a circular user profile picture.

### 6.2 "Continue watching" Section
*   **List Items:** Horizontal layout.
*   **Thumbnail:** Small rounded rectangle (`16px` radius). Contains a play button icon centered on a semi-transparent overlay.
*   **Metadata:** Title in bold, Genre/Category below, and Year (e.g., 2023) at the bottom.
*   **Images:**
    1.  **Barbie:** Bright pink background with a blonde woman in a car.
    2.  **The Hunger Games:** A woman (Katniss) in profile against a dark, warm-toned background.
    3.  **Iron Man:** A man (Tony Stark) in a high-tech suit against a dark workshop background.

### 6.3 "Friends activity" Section
*   **List Items:** Circular avatar on the left, text on the right.
*   **Text Format:** "[Name] is now watching [Movie Title]" (Movie title is in a slightly darker or bolder font).
*   **Avatars:** High-resolution headshots of diverse people.
    1.  **James Banistor:** Watching "Stranger Things".
    2.  **Jenna Barbera:** Watching "You".
    3.  **Sara Cameron:** Watching "The Witcher".
    4.  **Jonathan Paul:** Watching "Dune".

---

## 7. Image Asset Requirements
*   **Backgroundless Elements:** All icons (Home, Heart, Play, Search, Bell, Settings) should be SVG format with no background.
*   **Avatars:** All user and friend avatars are perfect circles (`border-radius: 50%`).
*   **Posters:** Movie posters should be high-quality, center-cropped to fit the rounded containers.
*   **Hero Image:** The "Dune" image requires a subtle dark gradient overlay at the bottom to ensure white text remains legible.
