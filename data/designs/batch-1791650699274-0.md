# Design Specification: Beat Records Website

## 1. Global Styles & Theme

*   **Color Palette:**
    *   Primary Background: Dark Charcoal / Near Black (`#111111` to `#161616`) with subtle ambient red/purple undertones.
    *   Accent Color: Crimson Red (`#D92525` or similar vivid red) used for highlights, active navigation states, and specific card accents.
    *   Text Color (Primary): Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
    *   Text Color (Secondary): Muted Gray (`#999999` to `#AAAAAA`).
    *   Borders/Dividers: Dark translucent gray lines (`rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`).

*   **Typography:**
    *   **Primary Font Family:** Inter / Montserrat (Sans-serif, clean, geometric).
        *   *Fallback:* `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
    *   **Headings / Brand:** Bold uppercase heavy sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat Black or Anton for large display text).
    *   **Scale:**
        *   Hero Title ("BEAT RECORDS"): ~64px–72px, Bold, Uppercase.
        *   Section Titles ("RECORDS", "ARTISTS", "BEAT ME"): ~32px–40px, Bold, Letter-spacing wide.
        *   Navigation Links: ~14px, Medium.
        *   Body / Descriptions: ~12px–14px, Regular, Line-height 1.6.

*   **Layout & Structure:**
    *   **Type:** Full-screen single-page layout (scrolling sections).
    *   **Max Width:** Centered container max-width around 1200px for content blocks, with fluid edge-to-edge dark backgrounds.

---
