# Design Specification (`design.md`)

This document provides the exact design guidelines, layout specifications, typography, color palette, and asset details required to recreate the provided graphic post.

---

## 1. Canvas Dimensions & Aspect Ratio

* **Target Aspect Ratio:** `4:5` (Standard vertical social feed format).
* **Base Reference Resolution:** `1080px × 1350px` (or scaled proportionally, e.g., `1440px × 1800px` for high-density displays).
* **Bleed / Safe Margin:** `0px` outer framing (full-bleed presentation). All structural text elements maintain a minimum `60px` to `80px` inset from edges.

---

## 3. Typography System

### Font Family
* **Primary Typeface:** **Gilroy** or **Neue Haas Grotesk Display** (Geometric Neo-Grotesque with tight kerning and wide weight range).
* **Fallback Stack:**
  ```css
  font-family: "Gilroy", "Neue Haas Grotesk", "Inter", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;
  ```

### Font Hierarchy & Styles

1. **Header & Footer Meta Labels** (`Design`, `Confiança`, `Samuel`):
   * Size: `18px` (`~1.3%` of canvas height).
   * Weight: `300` (Light) or `400` (Regular).
   * Tracking / Letter-spacing: `+0.5px` to `+1px`.
   * Case: Title Case.

2. **Username Tag (`@osamuelreis_`)**:
   * Size: `12px` – `13px`.
   * Weight: `500` (Medium).
   * Tracking: `0px`.

3. **Left Headline Block** (`Se você / não confia / no seu / trabalho,`):
   * Size: `36px` – `40px`.
   * Line Height: `1.15` – `1.2`.
   * Alignment: Left-aligned.
   * Weight breakdown:
     * `Se você`: Light (`300`)
     * `não confia`: Light (`300`)
     * `no seu`: Light (`300`)
     * `trabalho,`: Extra Bold / Black (`800` or `900`)

4. **Right Headline Block** (`por que / o cliente / confiaria?`):
   * Size: `64px` – `72px` (approx. 1.7× larger than left block).
   * Line Height: `1.05` – `1.1`.
   * Alignment: Left-aligned.
   * Weight breakdown:
     * `por que`: Hairline / Thin (`200` or `300`), all lowercase.
     * `o cliente`: `o` in Thin (`200`), `cliente` in Bold (`700` or `800`).
     * `confiaria?`: Heavy Bold / Black (`900`), with question mark.

## 5. Detailed Asset Specifications

### A. Central Subject Image
* **Format:** Transparent Cutout PNG (background fully removed).
* **Description:** 
  * A young adult male with short, textured dark brown hair, thin pencil mustache, and black round/semi-hexagonal frame glasses.
  * **Pose:** Seated, leaning slightly forward, hands clasped together in front of the chest with fingers interlaced.
  * **Clothing & Accessories:** Plain matte-black crew-neck t-shirt, matte-black ring bands on left and right fingers, black silicone strap smartwatch on the left wrist, and a black fabric wristband with white lettering (`...SOU CAMPEÃO...`) on the right wrist.
  * **Expression:** Calm, focused, direct eye contact with the lens.
* **Lighting & Treatment:** Soft overhead-front studio softbox lighting, crisp edge definition, natural skin tones with warm highlights, deep black clothing with subtle fabric texture.
* **Placement:**
  * Horizontal Alignment: Centered (`left: 50%`, `transform: translateX(-50%)`).
  * Vertical Alignment: Anchored to the bottom edge (`bottom: 0`).
  * Size: Covers roughly `82%` to `85%` of total canvas height. Top of hair sits at approximately `20%` down from the top edge.

### B. User Avatar (Inside Social Pill)
* **Format:** Circular thumbnail (`~18px × 18px`).
* **Content:** Micro portrait matching the subject's face/headshot.

## 6. Layout & Spatial Positioning

### Header Row
* **Vertical Position:** `top: 70px` (`~5.2%` of height).
* **Horizontal Structure:** 3-column distribution across the canvas width:
  * Left: `Design` positioned at `left: 95px`.
  * Center: `Confiança` positioned at `left: 50%`, `transform: translateX(-50%)`.
  * Right: `Samuel` positioned at `right: 95px`.

### Decorative Geometry (Backdrop Arc Box)

* **Stroke:** `1.2px` solid `#B4B4B8` (approx. 40% opacity).
* **Dimensions:** Width `~480px`, Height `~350px`.
* **Border Radius:** `border-top-left-radius: 40px`, `border-top-right-radius: 40px`, bottom borders open or hidden behind shoulders.
* **Placement:** Centered horizontally behind the head. Top edge positioned at `y ≈ 230px`.
