# Design Specification: Dhaka Movies Interface (Glassmorphic Clone)

## 1. Overall Aesthetic & Layout
*   **Design Style:** Modern Glassmorphism (Frosted Glass).
*   **Theme:** Dark Mode.
*   **Layout:** Full-screen application window with a fixed side navigation bar, a top browser-style utility bar, and a main content area organized into a hero section, a right-hand sidebar, and a bottom trending row.
*   **Corner Radii:** The main application window and all internal cards/buttons use significant rounding (approx. `24px` to `40px` for main containers, `12px` to `16px` for smaller elements).
*   **Background:** The UI itself is a semi-transparent dark grey `rgba(15, 15, 15, 0.75)` with a heavy `backdrop-filter: blur(40px)`. 

## 2. Typography
*   **Primary Font:** **Inter** (Variable). This provides the clean, modern, high-legibility look seen in the UI.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `Inter, "SF Pro Display", "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Font Weights:**
    *   **Bold (700):** Movie titles ("BLACK ADAM"), Section headers ("Recent Update").
    *   **Medium (500):** Navigation links, button text, user name.
    *   **Regular (400):** Description text, metadata, URL bar text.
*   **Colors:**
    *   **Primary Text:** White (`#FFFFFF`) at 100% opacity.
    *   **Secondary Text:** Light Grey (`#B0B0B0`) for descriptions and metadata.
    *   **Accent Text:** Lime Green (`#A3FF00`) for "See all" links and active states.

## 3. Color Palette
*   **Primary Background:** `rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.8)` (Frosted).
*   **Accent Color:** Lime Green (`#A3FF00`) - used for the logo accent, active icons, and the primary play button.
*   **Surface Color (Cards/Buttons):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` with a thin `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
*   **Icon Color:** White (`#FFFFFF`) or Lime Green (`#A3FF00`) for active states.

## 4. Component Details

### 4.1 Top Browser Utility Bar
*   **Placement:** Top edge, full width, slightly more transparent than the main body.
*   **Elements:** 
    *   **Left:** Window controls (standard browser icons), Back/Forward arrows.
    *   **Center:** A pill-shaped URL bar containing the text `dhakamovies.com` with a refresh icon on the right.
    *   **Right:** Share icon, Plus/Add icon, and a Tab-view icon.

### 4.2 Side Navigation Bar
*   **Placement:** Left side, vertical pill-shaped container.
*   **Background:** Darker translucent grey `rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.5)`.
*   **Icons (Top to Bottom):**
    1.  **Home:** A house icon inside a lime green circle (Active state).
    2.  **Search:** A standard magnifying glass outline.
    3.  **TV:** A stylized television screen outline.
    4.  **Movies:** A shopping bag/briefcase outline.
    5.  **Settings:** A standard gear/cog outline.

### 4.3 Main Header
*   **Logo (Top Left):** "dm" in a lime green circle followed by "Dhaka Movies" in white bold text.
*   **Navigation Links (Center):** "Movies", "TV Series", "Games", "Kids". White text, medium weight, spaced evenly.
*   **User Section (Top Right):**
    *   **Notification Bell:** A white outline bell icon inside a circular translucent button.
    *   **User Profile:** A pill-shaped button containing a circular avatar image of a woman (Megan) and the text "Megan" next to it.

### 4.4 Hero Section (Featured Content)
*   **Background Image:** A high-resolution composite poster for "Black Adam". 
    *   **Description:** Features Dwayne Johnson's face prominently in the center, flanked by Hawkman (right) and other Justice Society members. The image has a dark, moody blue and gold color grade with lightning effects. It is integrated into the background of the hero area with a soft fade at the edges.
*   **Content Overlay (Left-aligned):**
    *   **Title Logo:** The stylized "DC BLACK ADAM" logo in metallic silver/gold.
    *   **Description:** Three lines of white text at roughly 80% opacity describing the movie's plot.
    *   **Buttons:** Two pill-shaped buttons: "More info" and "Add to Favourite" (which includes a heart icon).
*   **Central Play Button:**
    *   **Placement:** Exactly centered within the hero section.
    *   **Design:** A large, vibrant lime green (`#A3FF00`) circle containing a solid black "play" triangle. It has a slight outer glow of the same color.

### 4.5 Recent Update Sidebar (Right)
*   **Header:** "Recent Update" (White) with a "See all" link (Lime Green) on the right.
*   **Movie Cards (Vertical Stack):**
    1.  **Card 1:** Image from "Edge of Tomorrow" (labeled "LIVE. DIE. REPEAT."). Features Tom Cruise and Emily Blunt in exo-suits. A small heart icon sits in the top right corner.
    2.  **Card 2:** Image from "Tenet". Features two men in suits walking through a futuristic corridor. A small heart icon sits in the top right corner.

### 4.6 New Trending Section (Bottom)
*   **Header:** "New Trending" (White) with a "See all" link (Lime Green) on the right.
*   **Movie Cards (Horizontal Row):** Four vertical posters, each with a small heart icon in the top right.
    1.  **Mortal Kombat:** Features Sub-Zero and Scorpion facing off. Title text at the bottom.
    2.  **X-Men Apocalypse:** Features the ensemble cast (En Sabah Nur, Mystique, Professor X). Title text at the bottom.
    3.  **1917:** Features two soldiers running through a battlefield with a large "1917" in the background.
    4.  **Oblivion:** Features Tom Cruise standing on a cliff edge overlooking a ruined Earth with a waterfall.

## 5. Image Specifications
*   **Backgrounds:** All movie poster images should be high-resolution (minimum 1080p source) and cropped to fit their respective containers (approx. 2:3 aspect ratio for trending, 16:9 for recent updates).
*   **Transparency:** All images within the UI should have a very subtle `0.9` opacity to allow the background blur to slightly influence their tone, maintaining the glassmorphic theme.
*   **User Avatar:** A clear, circular headshot of a person, centered within its 1:1 aspect ratio container.

## 6. Placement & Spacing
*   **Main Container Padding:** `32px` on all sides.
*   **Gap between Sections:** `40px` vertical spacing between the Hero section and the Trending section.
*   **Card Spacing:** `20px` horizontal gap between cards in the Trending row.
*   **Alignment:** All text elements in the Hero section are left-aligned to a common vertical axis. The Play button is the only element centered both vertically and horizontally within its specific container.
