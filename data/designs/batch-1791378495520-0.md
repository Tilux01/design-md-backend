# UI/UX Specification: Nike E-Commerce Landing Page

## 1. Global Layout & Structure

*   **View Mode:** Fullscreen, responsive web layout (desktop-first design translated to fluid scaling).
*   **Split-Screen Concept:** The layout is divided vertically into two primary zones:
    *   **Left Zone (Neutral):** Soft off-white/light gray background (`#ececee` or `#f0f0f2`) taking up roughly 50% of the viewport width.
    *   **Right Zone (Accent):** Vibrant orange/coral background (`#f16b3f` to `#eb6136`) taking up the remaining 50%.
*   **Centerpiece:** A high-top Nike basketball shoe positioned dynamically on the boundary line between the two background zones, utilizing drop shadows to create 3D depth.

---

## 2. Typography & Fonts

### Primary Fonts
*   **Brand / Display (Vertical Text):** 
    *   *Family:* `Bebas Neue`, `Anton`, or `Futura Condensed Extra Bold` (Geometric, ultra-bold sans-serif with narrow tracking).
    *   *Fallback:* `Impact`, `Arial Black`, `sans-serif`.
*   **UI / Navigation / Body:** 
    *   *Family:* `Montserrat`, `Inter`, or `Helvetica Neue`.
    *   *Fallback:* `Arial`, `sans-serif`.

### Typography Stylesheet Rules
*   **Navigation Links (MEN, WEMON [typo in source: WOMEN], shop, COLLECTIONS):**
    *   Font-size: `12px` / `0.75rem`
    *   Letter-spacing: `2px` to `3px` (Uppercase)
    *   Font-weight: `500` (Medium)
*   **Vertical Brand Wordmark ("NIKE"):**
    *   Font-size: `120px` to `160px` (Responsive clamp)
    *   Orientation: Vertical (rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise or stacked vertically). In this visual, it reads vertically from bottom to top: `N I K E`.
    *   Font-weight: `900` (Black)
*   **Slogan ("just do it...."):**
    *   Font-size: `14px`
    *   Font-style: Italic or stylistic script/sans hybrid, lowercase with trailing dots.
*   **Numeric Pagination Indicators (1, 2, 3, 4):**
    *   Font-size: `14px`
    *   Font-weight: `600`
*   **"next >" / "DISCOVER new":**
    *   Font-size: `11px`
    *   Letter-spacing: `1.5px`
    *   Uppercase.

---

## 3. Color Palette

*   **Background (Left):** `#EDEDED` (Soft Light Gray)
*   **Background (Right):** `#EE6337` (Vibrant Nike Orange)
*   **Text & Icons (Left):** `#111111` (Deep Rich Black)
*   **Text & Icons (Right):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) & `#1A1A1A` (Dark Charcoal for UI boxes)
*   **Shoe Accent Colors:** Black, Orange, White, Red-Orange gradient on the midsole air-unit.

---

## 4. Component Breakdown & Positioning

### A. Left Section (Light Side)
1.  **Top-Left Logo:** 
    *   The iconic Nike "Swoosh" in vibrant orange/red (`#E44D26`).
2.  **Top Navigation:**
    *   Items: `MEN` | `WEMON` (Note: Typo in original design, render as `WOMEN` or keep exact match `WEMON`).
3.  **Vertical Carousel Indicators (Left-Center):**
    *   Four left-pointing chevrons (`<<<<`) stacked vertically indicating scroll/carousel direction.
4.  **Main Vertical Typography ("NIKE"):**
    *   Positioned centrally on the left panel, running vertically.
    *   Color: Black (`#111111`).
5.  **Bottom Slogan:**
    *   "just do it...." placed directly beneath the vertical wordmark in lowercase italics.
6.  **Bottom-Left Vertical Tag:**
    *   Vertical text rotated upwards: `DISCOVER` / `new` separated by a vertical dividing line.

---

### B. Right Section (Orange Side)
1.  **Top Navigation:**
    *   Items: `shop` | `COLLECTIONS` | Search Icon (`🔍`).
    *   Positioned on the top right, aligned with the left navigation.
2.  **Product Showcase (Hero Image):**
    *   A high-top futuristic Nike sneaker (Black upper with bright orange swoosh, grey panels, strapped collar, and visible red-tinted Air Max sole unit).
    *   Positioned overlapping the center dividing line with a heavy soft shadow cast onto the orange background to create depth.
3.  **Right-Side Pagination & Controls:**
    *   A vertical pill-shaped widget on the middle-right containing:
        *   `1`, `2`, `3`, `4` (Numbered pagination blocks; box `1` is active with a rounded dark container border).
        *   `next >` link below number 3.
        *   A group of four left-pointing chevrons (`<<<<`) stacked vertically near the bottom of this stack.
        *   A black square action button with a white `+` icon at the bottom of the widget (Add to cart / Quick view).
4.  **Color Swatches (Bottom Center-Right):**
    *   A row of small colored Nike Swoosh icons representing available colorways:
        *   Black, Purple, Pink, Blue, Teal/Green.
    *   Label below swatches: *"Avialable in Different Colors"* (Note: typo in source, recommend correcting to *"Available in Different Colors"*).

---

## 5. Animations & Interactive States (Micro-Interactions)

1.  **Page Load Animation (Entrance):**
    *   **Background:** Split panes slide in from left and right.
    *   **Shoe Hero Image:** Fades in with a scale-up effect (`scale: 0.9` -> `1`) accompanied by a smooth drop-shadow expansion.
    *   **Text Elements:** Staggered fade-up entrance for navigation items and the vertical "NIKE" text.
2.  **Hover States:**
    *   **Nav Links (`MEN`, `SHOP`, etc.):** Slight color shift or underline indicator scaling from 0% to 100%.
    *   **Pagination Numbers (`1`, `2`, `3`, `4`):** Background fill transitions smoothly to dark charcoal (`#1A1A1A`) with white text.
    *   **Color Swatches:** Scale up slightly (`scale: 1.2`) on hover with a subtle glowing aura matching the swatch color.
    *   **Action Button (`+`):** Rotates 90 degrees on hover.
3.  **Carousel / Slide Transition:**
    *   Clicking pagination numbers (`1`-`4`) or `next >` triggers a slide transition where the hero shoe cross-fades and translates horizontally while color swatches and metadata update dynamically.
