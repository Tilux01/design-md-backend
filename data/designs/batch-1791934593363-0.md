# Design Specification: Aaron Mitchell Portfolio

---

## 1. Global Styles & Theming

### 1.1 Color Palette
* **Background Primary:** `#FBFBFA` (Soft warm off-white)
* **Background Secondary / Cards:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white)
* **Background Neutral / Muted:** `#F0F1EB` (Soft sage-gray for stats bar and CTA card)
* **Text Primary:** `#1C1F1C` (Deep charcoal)
* **Text Muted:** `#6F756D` (Medium slate-gray)
* **Accent / Olive Green:** `#70806E` (Muted sage green for buttons and badges)
* **Accent Hover:** `#5F6E5D` (Darkened sage)
* **Border Color:** `#E3E5DF` (Subtle divider gray)

### 1.2 Typography
* **Primary Serif (Headings):**
  * *Primary:* `Playfair Display`, `Cormorant Garamond`, or `Editorial New`
  * *Fallback:* `Georgia`, `Baskerville`, `serif`
  * *Style:* Light/Regular weights, elegant high-contrast editorial look.
* **Secondary Sans-Serif (Body & UI):**
  * *Primary:* `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans`
  * *Fallback:* `system-ui`, `-apple-system`, `sans-serif`
  * *Style:* Regular (400), Medium (500), Semi-bold (600).
* **Eyebrow / Sub-tags:** Uppercase, tracked (`letter-spacing: 0.15em`), font size: `11px–12px`.

## 3. Hero Section

### 3.1 Content (Left Column, ~55% width)
* **Eyebrow:** `DIGITAL DESIGN THAT CONNECTS` (Uppercase, muted).
* **Headline (H1):** *"I design digital experiences that are intuitive, intelligent and impactful."* (Large serif, charcoal).
* **Body Text:** Short bio paragraph summarizing user-centered design value proposition.
* **CTA Buttons:**
  * *Primary:* Solid olive green button with label `VIEW MY WORK →`.
  * *Secondary:* Outlined white/gray pill button with label `LET'S WORK TOGETHER →`.
* **Decorative Accents:** Dotted vertical lines and small circuit-style geometric coordinates on the far-left edge.

### 3.2 Visuals (Right Column, ~45% width)
* **Hero Image:**
  * **Type:** Backgroundless cutout (PNG/WebP with transparent background).
  * **Description:** Mid-shot portrait of a man with short dark hair, beard, and dark-rimmed glasses wearing a white t-shirt and open olive-green button-up shirt, looking slightly off-camera to the left.
  * **Backdrop Element:** Positioned directly behind the man is a white/silver humanoid robotic android bust facing the same direction, blended subtly into a soft circular light-gray background halo.
  * **Corner Tag:** Top-right text overlay reading `AI > DESIGN / HUMAN > IMPACT` next to a subtle plus (`+`) crosshair and grid of dots.

## 6. Metrics & Stats Ribbon

* **Container:** Full-width block in pale sage-gray (`#F0F1EB`) with subtle technical circuit-line watermarks on left and right edges.
* **Grid:** 4 evenly spaced columns separated by subtle vertical divider lines.
* **Metrics:**
  * `80+` — Projects Completed (User profile icon above)
  * `50+` — Happy Clients (Smiley icon above)
  * `6+` — Years Experience (Medal ribbon icon above)
  * `20+` — Industries Served (Globe wireframe icon above)
* **Font:** Numbers are large-scale serif; labels are small-scale sans-serif.

## 8. Process Section ("How I Work")

* **Header:** Eyebrow `MY PROCESS`, H2 `How I Work`.
* **Layout:** 4-step horizontal process timeline connected by horizontal dashed gray lines.
* **Steps:**
  1. `01 / Discover` (Search/magnifier icon) — Goal alignment & challenges.
  2. `02 / Design` (Pencil icon) — Usability, clarity, concepts.
  3. `03 / Develop` (Code brackets icon `</>`) — Clean, efficient code.
  4. `04 / Launch` (Rocket icon) — Testing, refinement, and launch.
* **Format:** Circular numbered icon badge at the top, bold phase title, 2-line explanation.
