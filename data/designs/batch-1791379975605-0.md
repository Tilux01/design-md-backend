# Design Specification: "PROMPTS" Social Media Creative

## 1. Global Layout & Canvas Specifications
* **Aspect Ratio:** 4:5 vertical (Standard Instagram/LinkedIn post format).
* **Color Space:** sRGB.
* **Layout Principle:** Asymmetrical, high-contrast, modern "future-editorial" style. Layered composition where the central futuristic vehicle physically intersects and breaks through the background typography and foreground elements.

---

## 2. Background Layer
* **Type:** Photography / Raster Asset.
* **Content:** Dramatic, high-altitude skyscape.
  * **Upper Zone:** Deep, rich royal blue sky with light, scattered cirrus clouds.
  * **Lower Zone:** Dense, volumetric white-to-light-grey cumulus/stratocumulus cloud deck rolling horizontally across the bottom third.
* **Lighting/Atmosphere:** Sunlit from the upper right, casting soft natural diffusion across the clouds. A subtle layer of fine atmospheric digital dust/noise overlays the entire canvas for depth.

---

## 3. Typography Specifications

### A. Main Headline ("PROMPTS")
* **Text String:** `PR   MPTS` (The letter 'O' is replaced by a massive graphic vector stroke).
* **Font Family:** Ultra-bold, condensed sans-serif (e.g., *Impact*, *Bebas Neue*, *Anton*, or *Montserrat Black* with negative letter-spacing).
* **Styling:** 
  * All caps.
  * Heavy vertical weight.
* **Color:** Deep Navy / Midnight Blue (`#0F172A` or matching the darkest tones of the car's carbon fiber).
* **Placement:** Centered horizontally in the upper-middle canvas, positioned directly *behind* the flying vehicle.

### B. Secondary Heading ("QUE TRANSFORMAM IDEIAS EM VISUAIS ABSURDOS")
* **Font Family:** Clean geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Helvetica Neue*, or *SF Pro Display*).
* **Styling:** Medium weight, tracked out (wide letter-spacing).
* **Color:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with high legibility against the sky.
* **Layout Structure:** Two-line lockup positioned to the right of the vehicle:
  * Line 1: `QUE     TRANSFORMAM     IDEIAS`
  * Line 2: `EM          VISUAIS          ABSURDOS`

### C. Creator Attribution Badge
* **Components:** 
  * Circular avatar thumbnail (Natã Olivar, smiling portrait with red backdrop).
  * Text lockup: 
    * `Design by` (small, light grey, sans-serif)
    * `NATÃ OLIVAR` (bold white, sans-serif) + Blue verification checkmark icon (`✓`).
    * `@olivar.dsgn` (medium grey, sans-serif).
* **Container:** Pill-shaped translucent backing (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`) with a subtle border and soft drop shadow.
* **Placement:** Top-center, aligned above the main headline.

### D. Sub-Copy (Bilingual Callouts)
* **English Text:**
  * Flag Icon: 🇺🇸
  * Content: `PROMPTS THAT WILL SURPRISE YOUR CUSTOMERS.`
* **Spanish Text:**
  * Flag Icon: 🇪🇸
  * Content: `PROMPTS QUE SORPRENDERÁN A TUS CLIENTES.`
* **Font Family:** Clean geometric sans-serif (*Inter* / *Arial*).
* **Styling:** Bold labels, regular body, sentence case. Left-aligned in the lower-left quadrant.

### E. Carousel Indicator
* **Text String:** `ARRASTA PRO LADO ↗`
* **Styling:** Uppercase, tracked out, housed inside a thin rectangular border box with rounded corners and an arrow icon.
* **Placement:** Lower-center.

### F. Save Reminder (Bottom Right)
* **Text String:** 
  * `JÁ SALVA PRA`
  * `NÃO PERDER.`
* **Font Family:** Bold condensed sans-serif or tracked-out heavy geometric sans.
* **Styling:** Right-aligned, crisp white, high contrast against the cloud base.

### G. Lorem Ipsum Footer Block
* **Text String:** Standard multi-line *Lorem Ipsum* placeholder text block justified along the bottom edge.
* **Font Family:** Monospace or micro-sans-serif (*Courier New* or *Inter 8pt*).
* **Color:** Muted light grey with high transparency.

---

## 4. Central Graphic Element (The Vehicle)
* **Asset Type:** 3D Render / Concept Hypercar.
* **Design Language:** Futuristic, aerodynamic, aggressive hypercar with visible forged carbon fiber texture, glowing X-shaped LED front headlights (intense warm orange/red), and specialized vertical-flight thrusters/fins.
* **Perspective / Angle:** Dynamic three-quarter ventral shot, angled downwards as if plunging vertically or banking sharply through the clouds.
* **Compositional Depth:** 
  * The rear wing and upper chassis overlap *in front* of the main typographic headline (`PR_MPTS`).
  * The bright red vector loop (`O`) loops organically behind and around the upper chassis of the car, acting as a bridge between flat typography and 3D space.
* **Lighting Effects:** Real-time reflections of the sky and clouds on the glossy carbon bodywork; emissive red glow radiating from the front headlamps onto the surrounding cloud vapor.

---

## 5. Graphic Accent Vectors
* **The "O" Vector Loop:** 
  * A fluid, hand-drawn vector ribbon stroke that forms the missing 'O' in "PROMPTS".
  * **Color:** Vibrant neon coral-red (`#FF4D30`).
  * **Layering:** Intersects the car structure—passing behind the rear wing and weaving forward.

---

## 6. Animation Specifications (For Motion / Reel / Video Versions)
* **Intro (0.0s - 1.0s):**
  * **Camera:** Subtle 3D parallax zoom-out, moving slightly away from the vehicle.
  * **Vehicle:** Gentle hovering float, engine light pulses (throbbing orange glow on the headlights).
  * **Typography:** Main background text (`PROMPTS`) fades up from 0% to 100% opacity with a scale-down effect.
* **Looping Elements:**
  * **Clouds:** Slow, continuous horizontal drift from right to left across the bottom layer to simulate altitude wind.
  * **Atmospheric Dust:** Random floating particle specks drifting upward across the camera lens.
  * **Swipe Indicator:** Subtle pulsing or flashing border on the `ARRASTA PRO LADO` badge to encourage user interaction.
