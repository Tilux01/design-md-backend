# UI Component Specifications

## Overall Layout & Structure
- **Root Container:** Full-screen layout with a multi-column e-commerce dashboard style.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary Accent: Deep Purple (`#6C5CE7` family) for banners, highlights, and primary call-to-action buttons.
  - Backgrounds: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for card containers, very light grey (`#F8F9FA`) for the main workspace background.
  - Text Colors: Dark charcoal (`#2D3436`) for primary headings/text, muted grey (`#636E72`) for secondary text/subtitles, and soft red/pink (`#E84393`) for promotional badges.

---

## Component Breakdowns

### 1. Left Sidebar Navigation
- **Container:** Vertical panel fixed to the left.
- **Items:** A vertical list of text-only or icon-with-text navigation links (e.g., Dashboard, Products, Orders, Analytics, Settings).
- **Styling:** Subtle hover states with light grey backgrounds; active items marked with a purple text accent or left indicator border.

### 2. Promotional Banner (Top Header)
- **Container:** Full-width horizontal hero banner spanning the central content area.
- **Styling:** Gradient background transitioning from soft violet to magenta pink.
- **Content:** White promotional typography and a rounded pill-shaped CTA button on the left side.

### 3. Category / Icon Row
- **Container:** Horizontal scrollable row below the top banner.
- **Items:** Circular icon avatars with small text labels underneath (e.g., Categories, Deals, New Arrivals).

### 4. Product Grids
- **Container:** Multi-column grid layout containing product cards.
- **Card Design:**
  - **Thumbnail:** Square image container with rounded top corners.
  - **Badges:** Small rectangular tag overlay in the top-left corner (e.g., Sale, Hot).
  - **Typography:** 
    - Product title in 2-line truncated bold text.
    - Price in bold primary text, with optional strikethrough original price in muted grey.
  - **Layout:** Uniform spacing (approx. 12-16px gap) between cards.

### 5. Right Sidebar (Cart / Summary Panel)
- **Container:** Vertical panel fixed to the right side of the screen.
- **Sections:**
  - **Item List:** Thumbnail image, product name, quantity selector, and price per item.
  - **Summary Breakdown:** Subtotal, shipping, and total calculation rows.
  - **Action Button:** Full-width rounded primary button ("Checkout" or "Proceed to Pay") at the bottom in solid purple.
