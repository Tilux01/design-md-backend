# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist, Architectural Interior Studio Brand Identity Kit.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High-end minimalist layout with generous negative space, precise typographic hierarchy, structured grid alignment, and calm neutral palette punctuated by organic tactile warmth.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean flat surfaces using pure tonal contrast (`#0B0B0C`, `#F4F1EA`, `#C4A484`, `#7A8F7A`, `#3D3A6B`) without heavy drop-shadows or complex glassmorphism, prioritizing print-editorial visual clarity translated to digital design systems.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase captions, minimal luxury editorial cards, precise grid layouts, tone-on-tone color chips with direct hex annotation.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `px-3 py-1`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#1A1A1C`, border `1px solid #2A2A2D`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before`.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-clay inline-block"></span>`.

## Component 1: Brand & Typography Header Bar
- **Layout & Positioning**: Flexbox, `row`, `justify-between`, `align-center`, sticky top 0, `z-index: 50`.
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Padding `24px 32px`, gap `24px`.

- **Typography**: Outfit, font-weight `300` / `400`, letter-spacing `-0.03em`, text color `#F4F1EA`.

## Component 2: Typography Variant Selector Cards
- **Layout & Positioning**: 5-column CSS Grid (`grid-template-columns: repeat(5, minmax(0, 1fr))`), gap `12px`.
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Padding `16px 20px`, background `#111113`, border `1px solid #2A2A2D`, radius `4px`.
- **Typography**: Title in 12px Medium uppercase (`Outfit`, tracking `0.08em`, `#9CA3AF`), preview alphabet in 14px Regular (`#F4F1EA`).

## Component 3: Spatial Visual Showcase Grid
- **Layout & Positioning**: 3-column asymmetric Grid (`1.2fr 1fr 1fr`), gap `16px`.
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Padding `0px`, height `auto`, radius `4px`, overflow `hidden`.
- **Borders**: Border `1px solid #2A2A2D`.

## Component 4: Color Swatch Palette Bar
- **Layout & Positioning**: 5-column Flex/Grid row layout (`Obsidian`, `Plaster`, `Clay`, `Sage`, `Indigo`), gap `16px`.
- **Box Model & Spacing**: Card padding `24px`, min-height `120px`, radius `4px`.
- **Typography**: Swatch titles in 14px Medium (`#F4F1EA` or `#0B0B0C`), hex codes in 12px Mono (`#9CA3AF` or dark muted).
