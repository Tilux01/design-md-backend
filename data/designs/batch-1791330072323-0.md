# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Premium 3D Portfolio / Dark Mode Glassmorphism with deep cybernetic purple and obsidian tones.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High atmospheric whitespace with a central focal element (3D interactive vault icon) and balanced peripheral floating assets.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Multi-layered depth via deep blacks (`#0B0813`), semi-transparent obsidian surfaces, high-end metallic sheen highlights, and vibrant neon purple/gold ambient backlighting.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: Ambient violet/amber underglow, high-contrast metallic edge highlights, volumetric lighting simulation on 3D artifacts.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `4px 12px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.1)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;`.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-violet-400 inline-block"></span>`.
