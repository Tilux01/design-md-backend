This document provides a detailed design specification for cloning the provided Netflix user interface. The goal is to achieve a pixel-perfect layout, focusing on typography, spacing, and asset placement.

---

# Design Specification: Netflix UI Clone

## 1. Global Styles & Layout
*   **Background:** Solid black (`#000000`).
*   **Layout Type:** Full-screen, fixed-height viewport.
*   **Primary Container:** A vertical flexbox or grid layout divided into a Sidebar (left) and Main Content Area (right).
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   Primary Background: `#000000`
    *   Primary Text: `#FFFFFF` (White)
    *   Secondary Text/Metadata: `#B3B3B3` (Light Grey)
    *   Accent Red (Netflix Brand): `#E50914`
    *   Badge Backgrounds: Semi-transparent grey/black.

## 2. Sidebar (Navigation)
*   **Width:** Approximately 5% of the screen width.
*   **Background:** Transparent to solid black gradient (left to right).
*   **Items (Top to Bottom):**
    *   **Search Icon:** Thin-stroke magnifying glass.
    *   **Home Icon:** House silhouette (Active state: Red vertical bar indicator on the far left).
    *   **New/Hot Icon:** TV screen icon.
    *   **Movies/TV Icon:** Film strip icon.
    *   **Categories Icon:** Plus sign inside a square.
    *   **My List Icon:** Plus sign.
    *   **Profiles Icon:** Square with rounded corners containing a simple smiley face.
*   **Placement:** Centered vertically within the sidebar strip.

## 3. Hero Section (Feature Title)
*   **Background Image:** 
    *   **Description:** A wide-angle shot of a man in a dark hoodie and dark pants, seen from behind, climbing over a stone balustrade. The Eiffel Tower is visible in the background against a twilight/dusky sky with orange and blue hues.
    *   **Placement:** Covers the top 70% of the screen.
    *   **Effect:** A heavy bottom-to-top black gradient fade to ensure the text remains legible and transitions into the content rows below.
*   **Typography & Branding:**
    *   **Netflix Logo:** Small red "N" followed by "FILM" in white, uppercase, tracking (letter-spacing) set to ~0.2em.
    *   **Title:** "VJERAN TOMIC" (Small, white, uppercase) above "THE SPIDER-MAN OF PARIS" (Large, white, bold, uppercase).
    *   **Metadata Row:** 
        *   "New" (Green text: `#46D369`).
        *   "2023", "16+", "1h 26m" (White text).
        *   "VISION" and "5.1" badges (White text inside thin white rectangular borders).
    *   **Description Text:** 
        *   **Content:** "In his own words, the burglar behind the 2010 robbery of the Paris Museum of Modern Art tells how he pulled off the biggest art heist in French history."
        *   **Style:** Maximum width of 40% of the screen. Color: `#FFFFFF`. Line height: 1.4.

## 4. Content Rows ("Only on Netflix")
*   **Row Header:** "Only on Netflix" (White, Bold, ~1.5rem).
*   **Card Dimensions:** Aspect ratio of approximately 2:3 (vertical posters).
*   **Card Details:**
    1.  **Spider-Man of Paris:** Poster matching the hero background.
    2.  **Unsolved Mysteries:** A skull partially buried in dark soil/roots.
    3.  **Who Killed Jill Dando?:** A vintage television set showing a woman with blonde hair in a yellow suit.
    4.  **Beckham:** A close-up of Victoria and David Beckham.
    5.  **Next in Fashion:** A woman with vibrant pink/green floral headwear.
    6.  **Old Dads:** Two men (Bill Burr and Bokeem Woodbine) looking forward; one in a grey shirt, one in a blue button-down.
    7.  **Predators:** A close-up of a cheetah's face/eye.
*   **Badges:** Each card features a small red "N" logo in the top-left corner. Several cards feature a "Recently Added" red banner at the bottom.

## 5. Typography Specification
*   **Primary Font:** **Netflix Sans**. This is a proprietary geometric sans-serif.
*   **Fallback Stack:** `Inter, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`.
*   **Font Weights:**
    *   **Regular:** 400 (Description text).
    *   **Medium:** 500 (Metadata).
    *   **Bold:** 700 (Main Title, Row Headers).
*   **Letter Spacing:** Titles should have slightly tightened kerning (-0.02em), while small metadata (like "FILM") should have expanded tracking (+0.1em).

## 6. Image Asset Descriptions (For Development)
*   **Hero Image:** High-resolution, landscape orientation. The subject (climber) is positioned slightly to the right of the center to allow text clearance on the left.
*   **Poster Images:** All posters are background-filled (no transparency). They should have a slight border-radius (approx 4px).
*   **Netflix "N" Logo:** A backgroundless (transparent PNG) red ribbon icon forming the letter "N". It appears in the top left of every "Netflix Original" thumbnail.
*   **Recently Added Banner:** A solid red (`#E50914`) rectangle spanning the bottom width of the thumbnail, with "Recently Added" centered in white, bold, all-caps text.

## 7. Spacing & Alignment
*   **Left Margin:** The text in the Hero section and the start of the horizontal scroll rows must align vertically (approx. 60px from the sidebar).
*   **Row Gaps:** 10px between posters.
*   **Vertical Padding:** Approx. 40px between the Hero description and the "Only on Netflix" row header.
