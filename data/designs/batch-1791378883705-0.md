# Design Specification: Cinematic Earth in Space

This document serves as the exact design blueprint for recreating the visual media. It outlines layout, color palette, typography, lighting effects, and animations to ensure a pixel-perfect, full-screen clone.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Viewport
* **Display Mode:** Full-screen (`width: 100vw; height: 100vh;`).
* **Frame & Borders:** Completely omitted. No container margins, padding, or decorative borders.
* **Aspect Ratio:** Responsive, designed to adapt cleanly to ultrawide, widescreen, and standard displays without letterboxing.
* **Overflow:** `overflow: hidden` to prevent scrollbars.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Lighting

### Background (Space)
* **Deep Space Base:** `#020408` (Near-black, very dark navy).
* **Nebula/Stardust Highlight:** Deep muted purples (`#180d26` to `#2d1a45`) blended softly into the upper-left corner with high blur and low opacity (`opacity: 0.4`).

### Earth
* **Oceans:** Deep royal blue (`#0f2a4a` in shadowed zones, `#1b4975` in mid-tones).
* **Landmasses:** Earthy greens and browns dusted with pale yellows and whites in illuminated zones (`#c2b280`, `#8fbc8f`).
* **City Lights (Night side / Terminator line):** Bright warm amber/yellow (`#ffdf80` and `#ffaa33`), clustered densely across North America and partially visible points southward.
* **Atmospheric Glow (Haze):** Electric cyan/sky blue (`#70b5ff`) forming a soft rim around the curvature of the planet facing the light source.

### Lens Flare / Light Source
* **Primary Flare Origin (Sun/Light):** Off-screen or just past the upper-left curve.
* **Horizontal Streak / Anamorphic Flare:** 
  * Core: Bright white (`#ffffff`).
  * Glow/Bloom: Cyan/Electric blue (`#1ca3ec`) extending horizontally across the lower edge of the screen.

---

## 3. Typography & Fonts
*(Note: While the primary image does not feature prominent UI typography, any accompanying overlay text should adhere to these specifications to match the sci-fi cinematic aesthetic).*

* **Primary Font (Headings / UI):** 
  * `Rajdhani` or `Orbitron` (Clean, geometric, futuristic sans-serif with squared edges).
  * **Fallback stack:** `font-family: 'Rajdhani', 'Orbitron', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif;`
* **Secondary Font (Body / Data):** 
  * `Inter` or `Roboto Mono` (For crisp, highly legible metadata or readouts).
  * **Fallback stack:** `font-family: 'Inter', 'Roboto Mono', sans-serif;`
* **Weights:** Light (300) for subheadings, Bold (700) for primary titles.
* **Letter Spacing:** Wide tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.15em` to `0.25em`) to evoke a high-tech aerospace HUD feel.

---

## 4. Layering & Composition (Z-Index Architecture)

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐ z-index: 3 (Top)
│ Anamorphic Lens Flare / Bottom Glow Streaks   │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────┤ z-index: 2
│ Planet Earth (Rotating 3D Sphere / Texture)   │
├───────────────────────────────────────────────┤ z-index: 1
│ Deep Space & Nebula / Dust Background         │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘ z-index: 0 (Base)
```

1. **Layer 1 (Background):** Solid dark space gradient with low-opacity purple/magenta nebula clouds in the top left. Subtle, scattered starfield (varying opacity dots: 1px to 2px).
2. **Layer 2 (Earth):** A high-resolution 3D sphere rendered with realistic day/night textures. 
   * Positioned predominantly on the right-hand side of the frame, taking up roughly 60–70% of the viewport width.
   * Features a sharp day/night terminator running vertically down the North American continent, highlighting glowing city lights on the dark side.
3. **Layer 3 (FX / Lighting):** A wide anamorphic lens flare cutting horizontally across the bottom portion of the canvas, casting a blue ambient reflection.

---

## 5. Animation & Motion Design

To capture the cinematic quality of the source media, the following animations should be applied in code (CSS or WebGL/Three.js):

* **Earth Rotation:**
  * **Property:** `transform: rotateY()`
  * **Duration:** Slow, continuous loop (e.g., `120s` per full revolution).
  * **Easing:** `linear`.
* **Atmospheric Shimmer / Glow:**
  * Subtle pulsing of the atmospheric rim light using a CSS keyframe or shader oscillation (opacity shifting between `0.8` and `1.0` over a `4s` sine wave).
* **Lens Flare Drift:**
  * Micro-movements of the bottom blue flare streak to simulate camera hand-held or subtle optical drift (`transform: translateX()` oscillating by `±5px` over `6s`).
* **Starfield Twinkle:**
  * Random opacity flickering on 10–15% of background stars to simulate atmospheric refraction or distance shimmer.
