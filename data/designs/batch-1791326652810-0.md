# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: High-end 3D Digital Art Portfolio & Glassmorphism Showcase.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: Extremely generous whitespace emphasizing a central 3D sculptural asset against a clean editorial gradient backdrop.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered translucent glass elements with high-index chromatic refraction, soft ambient floor reflections, and subtle diffuse shadows (`0 20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-index refraction shaders, iridescent dispersion highlights (pink/cyan fringes), soft caustic ground reflections.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-indigo-500 inline-block"></span>`.
