# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Neo-Minimalist / Technical Design Studio Portfolio.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace ratio featuring an expansive grid background, highly focused typographic hierarchy, and floating interactive elements.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Clean flat cards with subtle 1px structural borders and soft multi-layered drop shadows (`box-shadow: 0 20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`).

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-contrast geometric grid background, floating capsule navigation, tactile squircle glyph containers, editorial uppercase tracking metadata.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 16px`, font-size `11px`, font-weight `500`, tracking `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background `#1C1C1C`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `&#9679;` or `<span class="w-2 h-2 rounded-full bg-[#FF5500] inline-block"></span>`.
