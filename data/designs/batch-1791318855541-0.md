# 1. Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Blueprint
- **Primary Mood & Archetype**: Editorial Minimalist Dark Mode, Premium E-Commerce Showcase.
- **Visual Density & Whitespace Ratio**: High whitespace with generous section padding (`space-16`), balanced content grouping, and carefully controlled typographic scale to maintain an upscale publication look.
- **Surface Elevation Model**: Layered glassmorphism utilizing dark charcoal backgrounds (`#121212` to `#181818`), subtle 1px translucent borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), and soft drop shadows for floating UI pills and product cards.

# 5. AI Design Traits, Micro-Badges & Symbol Safety
- **AI Visual Traits**: High-tracking uppercase pill badges with subtle dot indicators, floating glow highlights on interactive states, border-glow focus rings, editorial serif headings paired with clean sans-serif body copy.
- **Pill Badge Specification**: Padding `6px 14px`, font-size `12px`, font-weight `600`, letter-spacing `0.08em`, border-radius `9999px`, background translucency `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border width `1px` solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Symbol & Glyph Safety Mapping**:
  - Em-dash (—) -> Use `&mdash;` or CSS `::before` content.
  - Sparkle / Star (✦) -> Use `&#10022;` or inline SVG icon.
  - Status Dot (●) -> Use `<span class="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-brand inline-block"></span>`.
