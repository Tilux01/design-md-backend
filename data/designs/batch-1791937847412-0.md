# Design Specification: WolvesClub Streaming "Halloween Special"

## 1. Global Layout & Theme
*   **Layout Style:** Full-screen immersive hero layout. The design should fill the entire viewport (`100vh`, `100vw`).
*   **Background:** The background is a composite of a dark, textured base layer and a centered, high-definition character image. 
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Background:** `#0a0a0a` (Deep Black/Grey).
    *   **Accent 1 (Horror):** `#E8914B` (Muted Orange/Amber).
    *   **Accent 2 (Humor):** `#63C7C7` (Muted Teal/Cyan).
    *   **Text (Primary):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
    *   **Text (Secondary/Muted):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` (Light Grey).
    *   **Overlays:** Linear gradients from black (`#000000`) at the edges to transparent in the center to focus on the character.

## 2. Typography
*   **Main Title Font:** 
    *   **Style:** Ultra-condensed, bold, distressed/textured Sans-Serif.
    *   **Recommended:** *Bebas Neue* (with a custom texture overlay) or *Impact*.
    *   **Fallback:** `Arial Narrow`, `sans-serif`.
    *   **Properties:** `text-transform: uppercase`, `line-height: 0.85`, `letter-spacing: -2px`.
*   **Body & UI Font:**
    *   **Style:** Clean, modern Geometric Sans-Serif.
    *   **Recommended:** *Inter*, *Montserrat*, or *Roboto*.
    *   **Fallback:** `Helvetica`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.
*   **Genre Header (Center Top):** 
    *   **Style:** Stylized, sharp, "Metal" or "Gothic" inspired font for "HORROR"; Rounded, playful font for "HUMOR".

## 3. Component Breakdown

### A. Navigation Bar (Top)
*   **Logo (Left):** "WC" in a stylized, sharp-edged font.
*   **Nav Links:** "Home", "Category", "Series", "Movies". Font size: `14px`, weight: `500`.
*   **Genre Indicator (Center):** Large, semi-transparent text (e.g., "HORROR") acting as a stylistic anchor.
*   **Search Bar (Right):** Rounded capsule shape. Semi-transparent background (`rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`). Contains a magnifying glass icon and placeholder text "Search your episode...".
*   **Profile Icon:** A simple circular outline containing a user silhouette icon.

### B. Left Content Area (Hero Info)
*   **Sub-header:** Small caps, e.g., "HORROR SERIE:".
*   **Main Title:** Massive vertical text. 
    *   *Note:* In "The Skin Maker 2", the "2" is significantly larger and colored in the theme accent (Orange), placed behind or overlapping the text.
*   **Add to Favorites Button:** 
    *   **Style:** Outline button with a star icon. 
    *   **Border Color:** Matches the theme accent (Orange or Teal).
    *   **Text:** "Add to favorites" in small caps.

### C. Right Content Area (Details & Media)
*   **Metadata Row:** Year (e.g., 2023), Age Rating (boxed, e.g., `+18`), Duration, and Genre.
*   **Description:** A paragraph of justified or left-aligned text. Font size: `13px`, line-height: `1.5`.
*   **Action Buttons:**
    *   **Play:** Solid white button with a black play triangle.
    *   **Watch Trailer:** Transparent button with a white border.
*   **"What's Next?" Section:** 
    *   Heading in all caps.
    *   Two horizontal thumbnails with rounded corners (`10px`). These represent upcoming content.

### D. Footer
*   **Copyright (Left):** "© 2023 WolvesClub Studio. All rights reserved." (Small, muted text).
*   **Social Icons (Center):** Facebook, Instagram, YouTube icons in a row.
*   **Close Icon (Floating):** A small "X" in a circle located near the bottom right of the main content area.

## 4. Image Descriptions & Placement

### Image 1: "The Skin Maker 2" (Horror)
*   **Character:** A man sitting forward on a stool. He wears a pale, stitched leather mask with small eye holes, a dark button-down shirt, and a heavy leather apron. His hands are clasped between his knees.
*   **Background:** A dark, rustic workshop filled with dozens of glowing white pillar candles of varying heights. The lighting is warm and flickering.
*   **Placement:** Centered, slightly lower third. No rotation. 
*   **Transparency:** Background is fully integrated (not backgroundless).

### Image 2: "Skin Walker: Hunting" (Horror)
*   **Character:** A tall, slender humanoid creature with a deer's head and large antlers. It is covered in dark, matted fur or mossy rags.
*   **Background:** The creature is standing knee-deep in dark, still water. The atmosphere is foggy and monochromatic (grey/green tones).
*   **Placement:** Centered. The antlers extend upwards toward the navigation bar.
*   **Transparency:** Background is fully integrated.

### Image 3: "See You At The Grave" (Humor)
*   **Character:** A skeleton (Grim Reaper) wearing a tattered black hooded robe. The skeleton is leaning forward with a wide, laughing jaw, holding up a "peace sign" (V-sign) with its bony hand.
*   **Background:** A misty, sun-drenched graveyard at dawn or dusk. The light is hazy and golden-white.
*   **Placement:** Centered. The hand gesture overlaps the main title text on the left.
*   **Transparency:** Background is fully integrated.

## 5. Technical Implementation Notes
*   **Background Images:** Should be set to `background-size: cover; background-position: center;`.
*   **Text Shadows:** Use subtle black text shadows on white text to ensure readability against complex backgrounds.
*   **Transitions:** Implement a smooth `0.5s` cross-fade transition when switching between the different movie/series screens.
*   **Responsive Design:** On smaller screens, the layout should stack vertically: Character Image top, Title middle, Description/Buttons bottom. The "What's Next" thumbnails should be hidden or moved to a horizontal scroll at the very bottom.
